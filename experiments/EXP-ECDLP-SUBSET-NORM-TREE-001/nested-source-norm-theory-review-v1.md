### Assumptions

- Every source polynomial splits over `K` with exactly the registered distinct
  roots.
- Branch triples are ordered.
- `Theta(B^2)` penultimate modules use balanced `d_i=Theta(B)`.
- Branch idempotents form an exact target-dependent partition.

### Evidence so far

- Root-only D2-identity handling and finite-child predicates are exact.
- `S=O` is correctly included in the finite norm; the `R=O` route is separate.
- Guarded denominators are units only on selected components, and the primary
  note now requires global-unit or fraction-free execution.
- Sequential resultants are exact determinant norms over the product rings.
- `G_I=G_L*G_R` and `R_I=R_L*R_R` are exact for finite nodes.
- Sequential one-source elimination that emits its intermediate lands in a
  balanced two-source module of dimension `Theta(B^2)`.
- Dense and black-box counts are interface widths, not universal lower bounds.

### Required revisions applied
