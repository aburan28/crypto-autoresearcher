# Experiment contract: exact subset-norm tree

## Status and execution boundary

`CONJECTURE`, `HEURISTIC`, `MODEL-BOUND`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

This contract authorizes paper algebra and symbolic object census only. It
authorizes no implementation, development run, or canonical run. Source work
requires theory, literature, benchmark, and red-team `GO` records, a clean
frozen commit, and explicit development authorization. Canonical execution is
a separate user authorization boundary.

## Candidate

Compile a fixed curve and coordinate factor base into exact subtree
nonemptiness operators for

```text
D2 intersect (Q-D3).
```

A balanced D2 subset-product tree needs only a root-to-leaf sequence of exact
predicates to locate one relation. The proposed non-generic mechanism is an
exact block-Hankel, approximant, or low-displacement representation of the
target-translated D3 norm. The tree itself is not the mechanism.

## Claim tiers

### Tier A: fixed-curve online improvement

Explicit D3 advice and its witness dictionary are allowed and charged. The
candidate must still beat exact D2 scanning in operations, traffic, and live
state. Report offline work, advice, memory bandwidth, supported targets, and
success probability. Total Tier A advice is capped at the actual canonical bytes
of `O(B^3)` explicit D2+D3 advice. Larger advice is a separately named tier with
an equal-byte comparator. Tier A cannot be called a one-instance exponent result.

### Tier B: compressed fixed-curve compiler

Total advice, including terminal D3 witness lift, must be `o(B^3)`, target live
state must be `o(B^2)`, online work must be `o(B^2)`, and peak preprocessing
workspace must be `o(B^3)`. Recomputed D3 support, one pointer per D3 point, or
an auxiliary supported-target table counts as D3 advice. A finite `0.8x` ratio
is only a continuation screen; Tier B needs an upper-confidence B-slope below 3.

### Eventual one-instance claim

At `B=n^(1/5)`, advice, advice writes, factor-base construction, compiler work,
peak preprocessing workspace, an actual high-confidence relation-attempt batch,
sparse linear algebra, and individual descent must each fit a total
`o(B^2.5)` path. Attempt count uses uniform support and rank-dependent yield as
specified in `notes/ecdlp_relation_preprocessing_accounting_20260718.md`.
Explicit D3 preprocessing already fails this stronger gate.

## Exact semantics

Use distinct oriented affine D2 points, the registered irreducible quadratic,
canonical extension serialization, and the identity sentinel in `theory.md`.
Charge every extension operation in base-field operations and bytes. Each D2
leaf stores or exactly derives one deterministic pair witness while audit data
retains point multiplicity. For node `I`, the finite-finite predicate is

```text
gcd(M_I,C_Q) has positive degree
```

or equivalently its resultant is zero. A positive root-to-leaf trace returns a
D2 point `R`; terminal lift returns a registered D3 witness for `Q-R`.

With `o_2,o_3` denoting identity support, full support is

```text
Hit(Q) = Hit_ff(Q)
         or (o_2 and Q in D3)
         or (o_3 and Q in D2).
```

Use deterministic priority for overlapping routes. The empty translated
product is one, and specialization must account exactly for the degree drop
when `Q in D3` without an undisclosed membership oracle.

The output is five signed public factor identifiers, independently replayed on
the curve. A Boolean existence answer, a D2 point without its D3 witness, or a
target accepted only by an oracle support filter is incomplete.

Any coordinate formula for `Q-S` must enumerate and certify the complete typed
translation cover: `Q=O`, `S=O`, `S=Q` yielding `O`, `S=-Q` yielding doubling,
and ordinary translation. Guards, denominator inverses, saturation, and mutation
controls are part of the operator definition.

Tier B has a simple exact terminal-lift baseline that uses no D3 dictionary:
after obtaining `S=Q-R`, scan every signed factor `P`, probe `S-P` in the already
charged D2 dictionary, and return `P` plus its deterministic pair witness. Charge
`Theta(B)` group operations, probes, traffic, identity/sign/repetition handling,
and independent replay. Any proposed lift must be compared with this baseline.

## Proposed operator census

Before implementation, give an exact formula for every node operator:

1. input and output coefficient modules;
2. target-independent matrix, polynomial, or transposed map;
3. target specialization rule;
4. displacement equation or approximant relation;
5. generator rank, displacement-kernel dimension, every boundary datum needed to
   identify the operator, nonzeros, and stored coefficients;
6. exact zero-test and false-positive/false-negative semantics;
7. terminal witness-lift data;
8. root-to-leaf operation, traffic, and live-state sum;
9. hereditary restriction from every parent operator to each child subset;
10. exact certificates for positive and negative predicates, including identity
    and target-dependent degree-drop cases.

Analyze the root first. Child operators are unauthorized if the root
specialization already constructs a D2-length target vector or has dense
matched-control rank.

### Frozen first root family

`root-operator-preflight-v1.md` instantiates the companion commutator
`Delta_J(T)=J*T-T*J` and the direct explicit-approximant interface. The exact
generator rank is zero, but `ker(Delta_J)=K[J]` has dimension `N2`; identifying
the target through the frozen power-basis interface materializes the forbidden
`N2`-coefficient remainder. The direct explicit-factor interface instead
processes `N3` translated factors. Independent theory, benchmark, and red-team
reviews mark only these interfaces `REJECTED_SCOPED`. Do not construct their
child operators. Scalar norms, nested source-level resultants, alternate
displacement operators, source-recursive circuits, and batch specialization are
new families requiring fresh ledgers.

### Frozen second root family

`nested-source-norm-preflight-v1.md` proves exact ordered-source norm semantics
without constructing distinct D3. It rejects only explicitly expanded CRT or
dense Sylvester/Macaulay interfaces, sequential resultants that emit their
two-source quotient output, direct `Theta(B^3)` tuple streams, and standard
product-basis black-box determinant vectors. Independent theory, benchmark, and
red-team reviews mark these interfaces `REJECTED_SCOPED`.

The scalar-only transposed norm remains `REVISE`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. It must
dimension selector construction, every internal vector space and oracle
boundary, target state and traffic, exact child values or updates, positive and
negative certificates, and the charged terminal lift. A scalar final output does
not satisfy this census by itself.

## Forbidden displacement

The positive path may not hide cost in:

- a materialized translated D3 polynomial `C_Q`;
- all `Theta(B^2)` D2 membership or remainder values;
- a target bit, coefficient, row, cache entry, or pointer per D2 leaf;
- target-specific preprocessing or a supported-target table;
- an unreported explicit D3 witness dictionary in Tier B;
- a probabilistic node predicate without exact fail-closed replay;
- audit scalar indices used by the candidate;
- an uncharged exact fallback.

FFT, multipoint evaluation, modular composition, and generic resultant code may
serve as cold baselines. Emitting every value does not satisfy the candidate.

## Controls

### Positive controls

- Planted D2+D3 witnesses at the leftmost and rightmost leaves and across every
  tree depth.
- Targets with an identity D2 or D3 component.
- Exhaustive 5- to 7-bit intersections and witness replay.

### Negative controls

- No-hit targets whose root predicate must reject.
- Altered target, leaf encoding, orientation, node product, and terminal D3
  witness; independent replay must reject each.
- A cardinality-, collision-, orientation-, and degree-matched synthetic random
  D3 set, labeled strictly as an operator-rank null because it need not have
  source witnesses.
- A dense random structured matrix with the same dimensions as each proposed
  node operator.

### Same-map controls

Randomize accepted non-pole sources under fixed map parameters, branch
cardinalities, x-collision policy, D2 tree shape, target schedule, and node
algorithm. Rebuild genuine D2/D3 witnesses from this factor base for end-to-end
support, output, and rank controls. Coordinate advantage must survive this
map-preserving comparison.

### Target schedule

- Uniform targets come from preregistered scalar seeds independent of source
  data, advice, tree order, and audit witnesses.
- Planted targets blind the compatible leaf index until candidate output is
  frozen.
- Hit, no-hit, identity, doubling, and ordinary translation cardinalities are
  exact and aggregated conjunctively.
- The D2-scan comparator receives the same advice and a frozen optimal registered
  scan order.

## Baselines

- exact D2 scan against an explicit D3 dictionary;
- complete D2+D3 translator with its full operation and traffic vector;
- materialized D4 fixed-curve compiler;
- equal-advice fixed-base BSGS;
- constructive generic preprocessing with offline work and advice separated;
- normalized rho using the same curve formulas and valid automorphisms.

D2 scanning is output-equivalent. BSGS, generic preprocessing, and rho solve a
stronger problem and are end-to-end kill screens.

For equal-byte BSGS, treat candidate advice as a byte cap and minimize total
work over every table size `1<=m<=M_B`; do not force the comparator to consume
the cap. For constructive generic preprocessing, use its separate complete
record size, restrict `m` to the cited construction's valid range, and minimize
total work over that range. At each fixed valid `m`, charge

```text
T_G=soft-Theta(sqrt(epsilon_DLP*n/m))
P_G=soft-Theta(sqrt(epsilon_DLP*n*m)).
```

Generic oracle queries, coordinate field operations, and traffic are separate
metrics. Full formulas are frozen in
`notes/ecdlp_relation_preprocessing_accounting_20260718.md`.

## Required reporting

For every curve, family, level, node size, target kind, and control, report:

- exact `N_j` supports, formal multiset ceilings, witness multiplicities,
  uniform `epsilon_rel`, `R_req`, rank-dependent `eta_r`, and confidence `alpha`;
- irreducible quadratic, canonical extension bytes, and exact base-field cost
  of every extension operation;
- product-tree build operations, coefficients, bytes, and traffic;
- each operator's dimensions, displacement equation, rank, generators,
  nonzeros, and construction work;
- target specialization and node zero-test field operations;
- positive and negative node calls and complete root-to-leaf path cost;
- terminal membership and three-witness lift work;
- advice bytes, target live bytes, RSS, reads, writes, and bandwidth;
- success probability, supported-target count, false decisions, and retries;
- independent five-id witness verification;
- actual one-target, `K=B`, `K=16B`, and `K=A_(1-alpha)` costs;
- generator fixedness, field modulus, curve model, special structure, and every
  amortization denominator.

Report generic preprocessing with `S_bits`, complete generic-record conversion,
online and preprocessing generic-group queries, and arbitrary-target DLP success.
Report coordinate field operations and traffic separately. Do not insert
relation probability, field work, bytes, or batch sharing into the generic
`S*T^2` theorem, and do not describe a relation compiler as violating a DLP
lower bound unless a full arbitrary-target DLP pipeline has the same boundary.

## Zero-run gate

Implementation remains unauthorized unless all pass:

1. the oriented affine encoding, resultant/gcd predicate, full identity formula,
   degree-drop semantics, deterministic witness choice, and self-reduction are
   exact;
2. the coordinate translation operator covers `Q=O`, `S=O`, `S=Q`, `S=-Q`,
   and ordinary routes with exact guards and certificates;
3. every resident, persistent, streamed, and regenerated object has a dimension
   in `B` and `log p`;
4. the root-node operator is written explicitly and has a proved exact
   displacement or approximant relation;
5. target specialization does not emit `Theta(B^2)` values or construct
   `C_Q`;
6. the root representation restricts hereditarily to every child subset without
   rebuilding a D2-length target object;
7. candidate-generated positive and negative decisions have exact fail-closed
   certificates; the exhaustive oracle supplies no bit, branch, witness, or cache;
8. the sum of operator advice and peak preprocessing workspace has upper B-slope
   below 3 for Tier B;
9. a positive terminal predicate returns a D3 witness within the same advice
   claim and is compared with the exact `Theta(B)` D2-lift baseline;
10. a symbolic path beats D2 online scanning and includes all root-to-leaf
    nodes, no-hit queries, traffic, and certificate work;
11. no forbidden displaced state appears.

If item 4 or 5 fails at the root, record a scoped negative for the frozen
operator family. Do not build the remaining tree.

## Development functional gate

1. Candidate and exhaustive oracle agree on every node and target in tiny
   controls; the oracle supplies no candidate decision, path, witness, or cache.
2. Candidate returns the same complete hit/no-hit decision as exact D2 scanning.
3. Every returned five-leaf witness independently sums to the target.
4. All mutation controls fail and all positive controls pass.
5. Target-independent advice is byte-identical across target schedules.
6. Uniform, planted, hit, no-hit, identity, doubling, and ordinary target
   cardinalities are realized exactly with blinded planted leaves.

## Development continuation gate

Tier A or B may continue only if both seeds at every size pass all of:

1. the functional gate with zero fallback;
2. online operations, reads, writes, and live bytes each at most `0.8` of exact
   D2 scanning for inversion weights `10,50,100`;
3. at least one complete operator-representation, boundary-data, nonzero, or
   traffic measure at most `0.8` of the matched random control at every tree
   level without support loss; displacement rank alone cannot pass;
4. Tier A total advice is at most the actual canonical `O(B^3)` D2+D3 comparator
   advice and charges all offline work, bytes, and bandwidth;
5. Tier B advice and peak preprocessing workspace are each at most `0.8` of
   compressed explicit D3 at development sizes and have upper-confidence B-slope
   below 3, including terminal lift and regenerated state;
6. preprocessing plus actual `K=B`, `K=16B`, and `K=A_(1-alpha)` work,
   certificates, output, uniform support, and rank yield beat the
   output-equivalent comparator conjunctively;
7. measured crossover `K_star` is at most `A_(1-alpha)`;
8. slopes do not worsen toward the exact comparator.

This is permission for a larger noncanonical census, not an exponent claim. A
Tier A pass cannot promote Tier B or the one-instance premise.

## Proof and disproof tracks

### Proof track

- Derive a fresh implicit scalar-norm, nested source-resultant, alternate-
  displacement, or batch-specialization equation from coordinate addition.
- Bound generator rank and the root-to-leaf sum for actual coordinate supports.
- Prove exact terminal D3 witness lift under Tier B advice.
- Extend a passing compiler through relation rank, sparse linear algebra, and
  individual descent.

### Disproof track

- Show a proposed displacement generator is non-identifying or needs dense
  boundary data even when its rank is small.
- Show exact zero testing requires all D2 remainders or a dense translated D3
  polynomial.
- Show terminal witness lift recreates explicit D3 advice.
- Compare against matched random supports and stronger generic preprocessing.
