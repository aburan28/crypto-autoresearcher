# Root operator benchmark review v1

## Handoff: companion-displacement accounting

### Claim or task

Audit the dimensions and costs of the frozen root representations.

### Status

`GO` for the scoped accounting negative. This is not a lower bound against
other implicit norm or source-structured representations.

### Assumptions

- `N2=Theta(B^2)` and `N3=Theta(B^3)` only in the measured collision-light
  specialization.
- Scan costs are worst-case charged baselines, not universal lower bounds.

### Evidence so far

- Dense companion remainder: `Theta(N2)` extension words and traffic for the
  frozen first-column interface.
- Direct translated factors: exactly `N3-epsilon_+(Q)` explicit factors.
- Sequential scalar norm: `O(1)` live accumulator and naive `Theta(N2*N3)`
  evaluation; explicit fast multipoint consumes all `N3` explicit inputs.
- Tier A D2 scan: worst-case `Theta(N2)` probes with charged D3 advice.
- Fixed root advice is `Theta(N2)` for the registered explicit representation.
- Exceptional membership scans and exact terminal lift are each worst-case
  `Theta(B)` and fit Tier B individually.
- The dense remainder fails strict `o(B^2)` target work/state; explicit D3
  advice fails strict `o(B^3)`, and regenerating its explicit factor stream
  online fails `o(B^2)`.

### Failure modes

Every cost above is tied to its stated interface. None is an information-
theoretic bound on alternate target-parametric circuits.

### Next concrete action

Preserve the family as `REJECTED_SCOPED`; require a fresh accounting review for
an implicit scalar-norm or nested-resultant successor.
