# Analysis: source-orbit quotient locator

## Handoff: source-orbit quotient result

### Claim or task

Test whether the source-only x-coordinate quotient of suffix sums `R2+R3`
reduces typed five-term locator work after exact member lifting.

### Status

`NEGATIVE RESULT`, `TOY-EVIDENCE`, `MODEL-BOUND`. The quotient is an exact
full-budget correctness mechanism, but it has no accepted 4- or 8-class
strict gate on either fresh curve. It is not an ECDLP break or an exponent
improvement.

### Assumptions

- The curves, factor bases, progression, and relation transcripts are the
  deterministic fresh fixtures generated by the committed typed-five harness.
- The affine residual `g_Q(X)` is zero exactly at `X=Q` because its multiplier
  is a field nonsquare.
- A suffix class is the set of source pairs whose sum has one affine x
  coordinate. This identifies the elliptic negation orbit, including repeated
  source representations.
- The quotient predicate is
  `h_Q(P,S) = g_Q(P+S) * g_Q(P-S)`. A zero is lifted against every member of
  the class using the original predicate before a witness or relation row is
  accepted.
- The baseline is the original full-predicate typed locator run on the same
  fixture. Its operation vectors are recorded rather than replaced by a
  scalar score.

### Evidence so far

- Generator `RUN-TT-SOURCE-ORBIT-QUOTIENT-001` is `completed_valid`, bound to
  commit `c49531b034a044d81a7e623b1442d194190f05fe`, with raw-result SHA-256
  `8bcb199d601393e4ea962dbf77750d10b4382c3fcb6fc8e1d79311f66bb806fc`.
- The generator used seeds `271828` and `161803`, curves `p=15667` and
  `p=15683`, four factor-base families, and fresh relation transcripts.
- Each curve has 100 suffix pairs and 55 x-orbit classes. The full quotient
  scan therefore evaluates 907,500 and 990,000 quotient entries, versus
  1,650,000 and 1,800,000 original predicate entries: exactly 55% of the
  original predicted-entry count.
- The full quotient budget has exact support, held-out support, valid
  witnesses, and full quotient row rank `55/55` on every family and curve.
  Candidate relation ranks are family-dependent (`5-11` on p15667 and
  `9-11` on p15683), so full support is not being promoted to universal rank.
- No 4-class or 8-class budget passes exact support, held-out support, and
  full relation rank on either curve for any family.
- Matched Pollard rho solves every target, with `211,901` total group
  operations across both curves.
- The target-independent source-orbit cache retains 60,500 entries and about
  22.92 MB on p15667, or 66,000 entries and about 24.76 MB on p15683. This
  cache, original lift cache, source construction, lift operations,
  reconstruction, witnesses, and matrix work are included in charged vectors.
- Against the same full original-predicate comparator, charged field
  multiplications fall from approximately `141.1-153.9M` to
  `101.2-110.3M`, while charged point-add calls rise from `112,300-122,500`
  to `123,628-135,100` and charged inversions rise by about ten percent.
  The field-multiplication reduction is therefore not a net generic win in
  this mixed operation model.
- Independent verifier `RUN-TT-SOURCE-ORBIT-QUOTIENT-005` is
  `completed_valid`, bound to commit `ddf732a236c925ba6a4b710849623f89853e638a`,
  with raw-result SHA-256
  `83d99922c19de488af4690b95d0b1b7d9bd05ce9da488e3dcbbeafed18f8dd74`.
  It independently regenerates the fixtures, recomputes the affine curve
  class partition, checks class digests and entry counts, validates lifted
  witnesses, and checks the rho certificates.

### Failure modes

- The strict sub-full gate fails before any asymptotic interpretation: 4 and
  8 classes do not cover the required support and held-out witnesses.
- An x-coordinate quotient saves predicate entries but requires two source
  additions per class. The source cache makes this cost visible; omitting it
  would create a false positive.
- The affine x partition is representation-specific and does not imply a
  reusable algebraic index at cryptographic dimensions.
- The baseline comparator is a matched toy implementation, not a theorem or
  a cryptographic-size rho benchmark.
- No individual logarithm recovery, sparse linear algebra scaling, target
  descent at cryptographic size, or fixed-curve S/T frontier result follows.

### Next concrete action

Do not repeat scalar suffix re-ranking. Test a source-derived recursive-state
operator that computes both signs through a shared differential/projective
addition primitive, or a circuit-contracted row-space basis that avoids
materializing one two-addition orbit state per prefix/class. Require the same
full lift, held-out, rank, memory, bandwidth, and matched-rho gates.
