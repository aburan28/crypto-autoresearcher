# Experiment Contract: 16-bit projective shared-sign sweep

## Hypothesis

The shared-Z Jacobian source operator and its homogeneous `Z^12` zero-locator preserve the three-curve `source_prf_x` signal at the next field-size scale, while remaining cheaper than affine orbit and original affine comparators under explicit inversion-weight models.

## Null hypothesis

The 14-bit signal is a small-parameter artifact: the projective locator loses support or rank, the source-only scale no longer preserves reconstruction, the negative control behaves identically, or downstream relation costs and memory erase the arithmetic advantage.

## Parameters

- field/curve family: two fresh deterministic ordinary prime-order curves at 16 field bits
- seeds: `97531`, `86420`
- families: `source_prf_x` (candidate) and `random_x` (negative control)
- dimensions: generated by the existing deterministic fixture builder at occupancy `0.5`
- orbit budgets: `64`, `96`, `full`
- inversion weights: `10`, `50`, `100`, `200` field multiplications per inversion
- comparators: same-fixture naive affine orbit quotient and original affine full predicate

## Metrics

- full and sub-full exact support, held-out coverage, witness validity, and rank
- charged field multiplications, inversions, point additions, source cache bytes, and lift queries
- weighted arithmetic cost `field_multiplications + w * field_inversions` for each registered `w`
- relation equation count, matrix rank, matrix operation counters, witness operations, and matched rho operations
- wall time, CPU time, peak RSS, and raw artifact hashes

## Positive control

Full projective support, witnesses, and rho must pass on both curves. `source_prf_x` must pass the full predeclared sub-full gate on both curves, and projective weighted arithmetic cost must be below both affine comparators for every registered inversion weight.

## Negative control

`random_x` is analyzed under the same budgets, comparator construction, and verification path. Its failures are retained; no selector tuning is permitted after seeing the results.

## Success criterion

This is a larger-scale practical signal only if the independent verifier passes all arithmetic, zero-equivalence, support, witness, rank, comparator, and rho checks. It is stronger if `source_prf_x` passes the same sub-full budget on both fresh curves and the weighted advantage survives downstream relation accounting.

## Falsification criterion

Any projective mismatch, target-dependent scaling, full-support failure, rank loss, failed negative-control accounting, weighted cost reversal, or verifier failure falsifies the 16-bit extension for this representation.

## Claim boundary

This is medium-toy fixed-curve evidence. It does not claim a generic prime-field ECDLP break, an asymptotic exponent improvement, deployed-key recovery, or cryptographic-scale superiority to Pollard rho. Individual-log descent remains a separate gate beyond the relation/rank accounting in this experiment.
