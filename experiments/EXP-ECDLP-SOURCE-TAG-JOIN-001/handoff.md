- Calling exact `D2+D3` a non-generic or exponent-breaking result.

- Evaluating x-only roots without recovering signs and five exact leaves.
- Using `random_scalar` constructor scalars as public provenance.

  gate.
- Building a resultant solver before degree/template storage survives a census.
