### Failure modes

- A one-GiB parser ceiling is not a peak-memory budget, and post-parse JSON
  node checks do not bound parser materialization.
- Input-controlled replay caps are not a trusted verifier-work budget.
- Pre-primary expansion, graph construction, replay, proof, and cache work need
  explicit per-phase and aggregate ceilings.
- The standalone oracle must derive the frozen curve, B, caps, and expected
  transcript rather than following emitted envelope values.
- A complete V6 protocol can still reveal that canonical B8 exact closure is
  infeasible inside any acceptable future execution envelope.
- A finite family PASS would remain a protocol signal, not an ECDLP attack or
  exponent improvement.
