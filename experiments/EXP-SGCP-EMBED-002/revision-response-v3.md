## Exact matrix policy

V2 allowed a mostly exact matrix and bounded residual gaps. V3 does not. All
168 rows and all 672 cap cells must be exact in both primary support and the
complete secondary objective. One unresolved cell makes the whole matrix
invalid and the mathematical outcome `INCONCLUSIVE`.

This stricter rule avoids exact-only selection bias. It also means V3 may fail
as an execution design if `B=8` cells cannot close under the frozen node and
role budgets. Such a failure narrows the optimizer design, not the coordinate
predicate hypothesis.

## Accounting boundary

V3 proves only deterministic structural-cell and nested-byte receipts. It does
not claim complete operation accounting, peak memory, memory bandwidth, or
fixed-curve preprocessing advantage. Observational producer wall times remain
inside rows but are not independently reconstructed. A future trusted runner
must measure both canonical roles externally.
