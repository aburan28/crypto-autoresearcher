the dedicated non-Groebner generalized-root problem but do not supply its
solver, while Jochemsz-May is heuristic for this multivariate EC circuit. The
first explicit tensor-box family remains a scoped negative; composed rational
maps and a genuinely new dedicated decoder remain open.
