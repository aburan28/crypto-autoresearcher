# First-power tensor-box lattice preflight v1

## Handoff: generalized-root first-power shift family

### Claim or task

Instantiate one bounded-root shift family for the exact five-source circuit and
test its symbolic feasibility at `B=p^(1/5)`.

### Status

`NEGATIVE RESULT`, `MODEL-BOUND`, paper-only.

The frozen first-power tensor-box family unconditionally violates the
preprocessing-size gate when materialized explicitly. Its determinant-volume
heuristic also supplies no positive recovery slack, but does not prove the
absence of exceptional short combinations. This does not rule out higher-power,
support-adapted, composition-tower, implicit, or other dedicated root-set
operators. No implementation or experiment was run.

### Assumptions

- One fixed feasible addition-branch pattern is used.
- The number `E` of leaf and gate equations is constant in `B`.
- `d_i=deg(M_b_i)=Theta(B)` and `X_i=Theta(B)` for all five sources.
- Rational-map degrees are constant.
- Every non-source coordinate, slope, and inverse variable is an arbitrary
  field residue with bound `Y_j=Theta(p)`.
- `p=Theta(B^5)`.
- Membership polynomials are explicitly expanded and coefficient-dense for the
  `Theta(B^6)` nonzero count; sparse or implicit storage is outside that count.
- The standard modular coefficient-lattice norm condition is used; complete
  multivariate recovery would still require an independent-equation heuristic.

### Frozen shift family

Let `F_1,...,F_E` be every equation of the fixed branch pattern, including
source membership, map and denominator equations, curve equations, and selected
addition equations. Define

```text
A_box = {alpha in N^5 : 0<=alpha_i<d_i}
A_0   = |A_box| = product_i d_i = Theta(B^5).
```

Let `S` be the union of monomials appearing in `t^alpha*F_j`. The coefficient
lattice is generated by

```text
{t^alpha*F_j : alpha in A_box, 1<=j<=E}
union
{p*mu : mu in S},
```

using centered integer coefficient lifts.

For a column `mu=t^gamma*z^beta`, scale by

```text
W_(gamma,beta)
  = product_i X_i^gamma_i * product_j Y_j^beta_j
  = Theta(B^|gamma| * p^|beta|).
```

### Dimension census

Constant circuit and map degrees do not offset the five degree-`Theta(B)`
membership polynomials:

```text
C = |S|                         = Theta(B^5)
raw generator count R=C+E*A_0  = Theta(B^5).
```

For dense coefficient-form membership polynomials,

```text
Z_lat = Theta(B^6).
```

Sparse or implicit membership coefficients can lower the explicit nonzero
count, but not the `Theta(B^5)` column count of this all-shifts coefficient
lattice. At `p=B^5`, merely materializing its columns and generators already
costs `Theta(p)`, far above the required `o(B^2.5)` preprocessing and workspace
path. This is the unconditional negative for the frozen formulation.

### Determinant and recovery inequality

Let `r` be the rank modulo `p` of shifted equation rows on the `C` columns.
Before variable scaling, the coefficient lattice has determinant `p^(C-r)`.
After scaling,

```text
det(L) = p^(C-r)
         * product_(t^gamma*z^beta in S) B^|gamma|*p^|beta|.
```

Define average source and full-field monomial degrees

```text
dbar_t = (1/C) * sum_S |gamma|
dbar_z = (1/C) * sum_S |beta|.
```

Then

```text
det(L)^(1/C)
  = B^(5*(1-r/C)+dbar_t+5*dbar_z).
```

A returned modular polynomial with `s` terms is guaranteed by the standard
sufficient criterion to vanish over the integers at the sought root when its
scaled Euclidean norm is below `p/sqrt(s)`. Since `s<=C`, the stronger
`p/sqrt(C)` threshold is a conservative sufficient screen. It is not a necessary
condition. Even optimistically using `det(L)^(1/C)` as the shortest-vector scale
and ignoring reduction loss gives the determinant-volume heuristic

```text
5*(1-r/C)+dbar_t+5*dbar_z
  < 5-(1/2)*log_B(C)
  = 2.5+o(1).
```

The multiplier box has `dbar_t=Theta(B)`. Therefore the slack

```text
sigma_B
  = 2.5-(5*(1-r/C)+dbar_t+5*dbar_z)
  = -Theta(B).
```

It remains negative even in the optimistic case `r=C` and `dbar_z=0`. This says
that the volume heuristic supplies no positive recovery prediction; determinant
volume does not lower-bound the shortest vector, so exceptional sparse vectors
or syzygies are not excluded. Locally, the monic source term already scales as
`X_i^d_i=B^Theta(B)`, while the available modulus is only `p=B^5`.

### Full-field obstruction

In the frozen formulation, unrestricted coordinates receive full-field bounds.
Every surviving full-field degree adds approximately five to the B-exponent; a
linear term such as `D(t_i)*x_i` already carries one factor `p=B^5`. Combinations
that eliminate every full-field variable escape this local heuristic and need
separate accounting. The explicit `Theta(B^5)` materialization negative remains
even with `dbar_z=0`. Any elimination-first successor must separately prove that
it does not reconstruct the forbidden D2-sized target eliminant.

### Narrow scoped conclusion

For the explicitly materialized first-power tensor-box coefficient lattice
containing all `product_i d_i=Theta(B^5)` shifts, the dimensions alone violate
the `o(B^2.5)` preprocessing gate. With dense expanded membership polynomials,
the representation also has `Theta(B^6)` nonzeros. Its determinant-volume
estimate has average scaled source exponent `Theta(B)`, so the standard
sufficient modular norm screen supplies no positive recovery slack. This does
not exclude exceptional short combinations or differently represented,
support-adapted, higher-power, elimination-based, or implicit solvers.

### Mathematically distinct successor

An exact composition-tower fiber lift would represent each source set by a
constant-degree chain

```text
u_(i,k+1)=g_(i,k)(u_(i,k)), 0<=k<L, L=Theta(log B),
```

with inverse-edge labels transporting every leaf to a public factor id. Process
the addition circuit level by level and retain only target-compatible fibers.
If `W(B)` is maximum exact active-frontier width, a necessary one-instance
per-attempt gate is

```text
soft-O(W(B)) = o(B^1.5),
```

with an immediate kill at `Omega(B^2)`. No suitable tower is currently known
for the arbitrary-prime `x_interval` source set; p-1 or auxiliary-isogeny towers
remain separately restricted families.

### Failure modes

- Exceptional short combinations could exist despite the failed determinant
  estimate; this is not a theorem against all lattices.
- Sparse or implicit `M_b` lowers explicit nonzeros; a different support-adapted
  column representation is outside this negative.
- Combinations that eliminate all full-field variables require a separate
  determinant and object-dimension ledger.
- Composition lifting fails if no compact tower exists or its exact frontier
  grows to D2 scale.

### Next concrete action

Do not implement or tune this shift family. Derive the subset-norm root operator
or a composition-tower frontier equation and stop immediately if target state or
frontier width reaches `Omega(B^2)`.
