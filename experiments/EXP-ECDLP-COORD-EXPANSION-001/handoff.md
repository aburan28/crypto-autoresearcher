## Handoff: Point-only coordinate expansion profile

### Claim or task

Test whether frozen coordinate factor bases combine high exact genuinely-new
five-fold support with separate `D2` and `D3` compression relative to
coordinate-matched public nulls.

### Status

HYPOTHESIS

### Assumptions

- Generated ordinary prime-order toy curves only.
- The candidate compiler and online query receive points only.
- The subgroup-log, D4/D5, and split censuses are diagnostic and fully charged.
- Support does not imply independent relations, matrix rank, or descent.

### Evidence so far

- The first development scaffold was rejected because its query used subgroup
  scalars, its sign-complete sizing ignored cancellation classes, and its
  first-witness order was biased.
- The revised point-only compiler has unit-tested exact `D2`/`D3` support,
  canonical reduced-form counts, `D5_new`, split hits, and witness recovery.
- No interpretation-eligible development sweep has run yet.

### Failure modes

- Fewer than 31 null draws.
- Inverse-pair cancellation artifacts.
- Diagnostic DLP tables misreported as attack advice.
- Scan-order or coordinate-chart dependence.
- Support-mass mistaken for packed functional advice.
- Positive support promoted without witness, cost, rank, or descent.

### Next concrete action

Run the 31-null Stage-A development falsifier, then commission independent
protocol and result review before preserving any evidence claim.
