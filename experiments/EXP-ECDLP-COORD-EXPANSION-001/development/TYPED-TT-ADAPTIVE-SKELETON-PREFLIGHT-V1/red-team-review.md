# Red-Team Review: TYPED-TT-ADAPTIVE-SKELETON-PREFLIGHT-V1

## Verdict

Accept as a stronger toy observation than the rank-budgeted predecessor. Do not promote it to a cryptanalytic result.

## Objections and responses

1. **The plateau rule is heuristic.** Correct. Full validation confirms the frozen rows but does not prove that a future independent direction cannot occur after the stop.

2. **The prefix order is tuned to the same frozen family.** Correct. Diagonal-first ordering must be tested against fresh curves, randomized orders, and adversarially permuted factor bases.

3. **The validation phase still enumerates full tensors.** Correct. Construction and validation are explicitly separated. The construction ratio is not an end-to-end attack cost.

4. **The construction imports helper code from the prior source-aware prototype.** Correct. An independent arithmetic implementation and a circuit-native RCB evaluator are required before treating the result as robust.

5. **No relation or individual-log witness is produced.** Correct. Exact locator reconstruction is only a prerequisite for relation collection and descent.

6. **The fixed-curve advice frontier is not yet measured.** Correct. Basis rows, pivot columns, inverse cross matrix, coefficient traffic, and retained target support must all be charged in bytes and bandwidth.

## Required follow-up

Run fresh-seed adaptive and adversarial-order controls, bind the selected-column operator to typed relation rows, measure quotient rank and held-out descent, and compare complete offline/online costs against materialized D4 and optimized rho.
