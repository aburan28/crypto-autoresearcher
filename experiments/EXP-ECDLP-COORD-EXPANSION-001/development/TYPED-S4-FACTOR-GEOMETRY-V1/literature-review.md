# Literature Review: Finite-Field Zero Reporting

Reviewer task `019fafb4-b3f4-7322-ad83-7a5f6435b31e` searched primary
literature for an index serving the exact rank-24/48 RCB factors.

## Status

`OPEN`, with a `NEGATIVE RESULT` for direct application of known generic
orthogonal-vector, incidence, matrix-multiplication, and indexing results.
This is not an impossibility theorem for named elliptic coordinates.

## Closest Exact Results

Williams and Yu give exact finite-field orthogonal-vector and Hopcroft
algorithms. Their `O(n d^(k(p-1)))` bound over `F_(p^k)` is useful in small
characteristic, not here where `p` grows like `B^5` and `d` is 24-48. Their
arbitrary-field output-sensitive method reports nonzero products, but this
matrix has `B^5-O(1)` nonzeros.

- [Williams-Yu, Finding Orthogonal Vectors in Discrete Structures](https://people.csail.mit.edu/virgi/6.s078/papers/OV-finite.pdf)

Real fixed-dimensional Hopcroft algorithms use order and geometric cuttings
that do not transfer to finite fields. Even their formal balanced-block cost,
`B^(5-4/d)`, is far above the required online `B`.

Erickson's real partition-model space/query lower bounds are useful barrier
analogies, but they are not finite-field lower bounds.

- [Erickson, Space-Time Tradeoffs for Emptiness Queries](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/S0097539798337212)

Boolean OV indexing and probabilistic-polynomial nearest-neighbor algorithms
depend on coordinatewise disjointness or Boolean Hamming predicates. They do
not represent cancellation in a growing prime field without recreating the
degree-`p-1` Fermat indicator.

- [Goldstein-Lewenstein-Porat, Orthogonal Vectors Indexing](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ISAAC.2017.40)
- [Alman-Williams, Probabilistic Polynomials and Hamming Nearest Neighbors](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.05106)

## DLP Barrier Comparators

Generic fixed-generator preprocessing obeys
`S T^2 = Omega_tilde(epsilon q)`. The desired
`S<B^2.5`, `T near B`, `q=B^5` regime lies below that frontier, so any
success must exploit named coordinates outside the model.

- [Corrigan-Gibbs-Kogan, The Discrete-Logarithm Problem with Preprocessing](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~henrycg/pubs/eurocrypt18discrete/)

The structured generic-group model requires an actual structured partial
operation and justified density. A coordinate predicate or low-rank
factorization alone does not instantiate it.

- [Corrigan-Gibbs-Henzinger-Wu, The Structured Generic-Group Model](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/384)

## Elliptic Recurrence Lead

Stange proves exact nonlinear elliptic-net recurrences for points generated by
fixed elliptic points. Thus `P0+iD` has reusable recurrence structure, but the
theory does not itself locate sparse zeros.

- [Stange, Elliptic Nets and Elliptic Curves](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.1316)

Lauter and Stange relate elliptic divisibility sequences to ECDLP and record
the coordinate identity

`W(n-1)W(n+1)/W(n)^2 = x(P)-x([n]P)`.

This supports a concrete progression-axis recurrence test but supplies no
generic prime-field zero index.

- [Lauter-Stange, ECDLP and Equivalent Hard Problems for EDS](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.0728)

## Cryptanalytic Neighbors

Summation polynomials, Weil-descent index calculus, and rational-map factor
bases solve related decomposition systems, but no cited method gives the
required sparse-zero index over ordinary prime fields.

- [Semaev, Summation Polynomials](https://eprint.iacr.org/2004/031.pdf)
- [Diem, Extension-Field ECDLP](https://www.math.uni-leipzig.de/~diem/preprints/dlp-ell-curves.pdf)
- [Petit-Kosters-Messeng, Rational-Map Factor Bases](https://www.iacr.org/archive/pkc2016/96140156/96140156.pdf)
- [Renes-Costello-Batina, Complete Addition Formulas](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/complete-addition-formulas-for-prime-order-elliptic-curves/)

No primary source was found that uses the tensor rank of an elliptic addition
law to locate relations.

## Bottom Line

No matched known algorithm provides all of:

`P,S,M=o(B^2.5)` and total target work `B^(1+o(1))`

for high-characteristic factors with sides `B^2` and `B^3` and only
`O(1)` expected zeros.

Expected sparse output does not help a classical output-sensitive algorithm
when the computed product is dense. Explicitly scanning `V` already costs
`B^3`.

## Novelty Boundary

- rank factorization as OV: standard;
- applying it to the exact RCB norm tensor: `NOVELTY-UNVERIFIED`;
- elliptic nets for generating the progression: known;
- an implicit block-product zero index using shared elliptic-net state: no
  matched source found and remains a defensible novel experiment.

## Next Experiment

For dyadic blocks `C` of four-`R` tuples, define diagnostically

`G_C(i)=product_{r_tuple in C} h(i,r_tuple)`.

Then `G_C(i)=0` exactly when block `C` contains a witness for progression
index `i`.

Measure Berlekamp-Massey order, Hankel rank, exact elliptic-net recurrence
residuals, common annihilators across targets and sibling blocks, shared-state
bytes, and exact witness descent. Controls must include true elliptic-net
sequences, a synthetic planted low-order recurrence, random rank factors, and
matched random `A`.

Promotion requires a constructible shared node operator below `B^2.5` state
and expected root specialization plus witness descent near `B`. Explicit
`B^3/B^4` node materialization, target-specific annihilators, or linear
complexity `Theta(B)` is a scoped negative.
