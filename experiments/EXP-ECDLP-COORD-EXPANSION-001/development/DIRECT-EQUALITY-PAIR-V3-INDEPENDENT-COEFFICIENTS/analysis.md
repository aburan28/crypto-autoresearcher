> ordered cut-2/cut-3 suffix coordinate vector generated by the frozen
> complete-addition circuit has an exact independent reconstruction in the

This closes the current coefficient-provenance and shared-rank-code

- a proof that dimensions 24/12 are representation-minimal;
- an intrinsic simultaneous-zero reporting algorithm;
- compression below explicit `Theta(B^3)` cut-2 state;
- multiplicity-aware resultant or squarefree-reduction cost;
- build/query memory traffic or many-target amortization;
- relation rank, final descent, asymptotic scaling, or deployment relevance.

The verifier independently implements the same published complete-addition
formula. Agreement is exact implementation evidence, not a formal proof of
the formula or a representation-independent theorem.
