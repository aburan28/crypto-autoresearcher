## Ingested — the Anthropic cryptanalysis release (user-supplied)

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team published two cryptanalytic results found with
Claude Mythos Preview on 2026-07-28. Four documents, four entries. **None of this
material bears on the ECDLP**, and every entry says so explicitly; it is ingested
for the methodology and as target-profile exemplars.

| ID | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| KN-LIT-7592 | `hawk_key_recovery.pdf` (Straznickas, Weis) | HAWK-`n` key recovery reduces to exact-SVP in dimension `n/2+1`; `2^150 → 2^108` (HAWK-512), `2^288 → 2^182` (HAWK-1024) in the spec's own gate-count model, with an end-to-end HAWK-256 recovery. Unconditional and deterministic — *stronger* than the target profile requires. The engine is an **unused second Galois involution** `τ: ζ ↦ -ζ` where all prior module-LIP work used complex conjugation |
| KN-LIT-7593 | `aes_mobius_bridge.pdf` (Nasr, Carlini) | 7-round AES-128: `2^99 → 2^89.3–2^91.4` at fixed `2^105` data, by making the MitM fingerprint invariant to a *second* key byte. Ingested mainly for §5 — a six-rung **validation ladder for a claim that cannot be run** |
| KN-LIT-7594 | Blog post | Program-level: costs (~60h, ~$100k, ~10^9 output tokens per result), autonomy, follow-ons (LEA, Serpent-128, Salsa20, Poseidon, SHA-1 — all unpublished), and the **1:4 discovery-to-validation time ratio** |
| KN-LIT-7595 | `aes_mobius_bridge_cot.pdf` | The published discovery transcript. **Primary methodology source**; the direct origin of `KN-TECH-056` and `docs/inventor-protocol.md` |
