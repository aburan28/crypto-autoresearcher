## A low-yield week, and why

This is the smallest ingest of the recent gathers (5, against 11–13 in the three
preceding runs). That is a property of the window, not of the screen, and the reason is
worth recording because it will recur:

**ePrint's `/days/7` lists papers *updated* in the last seven days, not papers
*posted*.** The 2026-07-27 and 2026-07-28 gathers ran over overlapping windows and
already ingested the on-topic items that resurface here on a metadata bump. Of the 67
ePrint entries, **19 were already in the corpus by identifier**, and they included

## Cross-post, not ingested

`arXiv:2607.25552` "Refined Humbert Invariants in Supersingular Isogeny Degree
Analysis" (Kırımlı, Korpal) survived identifier dedup on the arXiv side but is the
**same paper** as `iacr:2025/1605`, already in the corpus as **KN-LIT-1462**. No new
entry was created.

The arXiv identifier is **not** recorded on KN-LIT-1462: records are immutable and
corrections supersede rather than overwrite, and adding a cross-reference did not
warrant a supersession. Flagging for the Coordinator: the corpus currently has no
mechanism for attaching a second identifier to an existing entry when a paper appears
on both ePrint and arXiv, and this will keep happening. It is a real gap in the dedup
path — an arXiv-first ingest of a paper already held under its ePrint number would
**not** have been caught by identifier matching, only by the topical check.

That topical check is what caught it here, and it is worth keeping in the procedure:
grepping the corpus for distinctive title terms ("Humbert", "SuperSolver",
"Delfs") surfaced three duplicates that identifier matching alone would have found only
because the ids happened to be present in cross-referencing prose.
