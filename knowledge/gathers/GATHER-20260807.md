# GATHER-20260807 — sweep of the local downloads directory

Run 2026-08-07 on user request: *"review the research papers in
`/Volumes/SSD990/downloads` and translate into knowledge corpus format"*.

This is the first **local-file sweep**: instead of retrieving from arXiv/IACR by
query, the corpus was screened against the full contents of a long-accumulated local
PDF library (2,392 unique files after deduplication of `(N)`-suffixed duplicates).
The goal was to find research papers sitting on disk that the corpus had never
ingested — the mirrors of the online sweeps, where the retrieval boundary is the
filesystem rather than a search API.

**2,392 unique PDFs screened; 8 records written** — all `KN-LIT`, all new IDs minted
with random 6-hex suffixes (`KN-LIT-1b6203`, `KN-LIT-be0bfd`, `KN-LIT-0cbb26`,
`KN-LIT-fb9929`, `KN-LIT-fd27c2`, `KN-LIT-f37d84`, `KN-LIT-0471e7`, `KN-LIT-91b680`),
each verified free with `tools/allocate_id.py --check` before writing.

## Method

1. Extracted first-page text of every unique PDF with `pdftotext` into
   `/Volumes/SSD990/llm/tmp/opencode/pdfmeta/`.
2. Built a metadata table (title/authors/arXiv id/year/abstract) for the files
   that parsed, and ran the arXiv/DOI/title match against the corpus's `KN-LIT`
   body text: **1,373 of 2,014 parsed keys are already covered** by existing
   corpus entries (several under truncated or re-segmented titles).
3. Remaining "not covered" keys were filtered for relevance (slides, talk
   abstracts, lecture notes, exams, obituaries, and out-of-domain files —
   e.g. peptide chemistry, astronomy — were excluded from entry-writing).
4. Each genuine candidate was cross-checked by direct grep of distinctive
   phrases against `knowledge/literature/*.md`; false candidates dropped when a
   phrase already lived in a corpus entry (`Fast change of level` →
   `KN-LIT-3789`; `Maps between curves` → `KN-LIT-4850`; Hasse–Witt II →
   `KN-LIT-3054`; `small discrete logarithms faster` → `KN-LIT-3060`).

## Notes on scope

- The downloads library is dominated by **already-ingested** material: the
  corpus was seeded from the same collection in earlier passes, so the hit
  rate on novelty is low by construction. The real value of this sweep was the
  handful of stragglers (four Bos-era implementation manuscripts, one
  ANTS-XV submission, the Bernstein coprime-factoring preprint) that the
  thematic sweeps missed because they live only on disk.
- Several downloads are talk slides or school-note files (`ecc2017school-*`,
  `facthacks-29C3`, `session4-mcgrew`, `NXP_PQC_EW_EU25`, ICERM talk
  abstracts, `eddsa` teaching notes, `2WC00_*` course pages, an exam
  cover-sheet). These are **not** literature records and were excluded; if the
  program wants talk/lecture material in the corpus, that is a schema change
  (`KN-TALK`), not a literature entry.
- Obituary/memorial files (`2020-07-01-ANTS-PeterMontgomery`,
  `2021-June-ARITH-PeterMontgomery`) are biographical notices, not research
  papers; excluded for the same reason.
- Out-of-domain PDFs (peptide/protein chemistry, astrophysics, large PDF
  corpora) were screened and excluded; they carry no ECDLP signal.
