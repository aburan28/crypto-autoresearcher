# GATHER-20260809 — second local sweep of `/Volumes/SSD990/downloads`

Run 2026-08-09 on user request: *"review all the new files in this directory and use
it to inspire new novel ideas for the crypto-autoresearcher goals."*

This is the **second local-file sweep**, following
[GATHER-20260807](GATHER-20260807.md). That sweep screened 2,392 unique PDFs; the
downloads tree has since grown to 9,465 entries. This run therefore screened only the
**delta**, and — unlike the first sweep, which was a coverage exercise — it was run as
an *idea-generation* pass: sources were selected for what they could falsify or
propose, not for corpus completeness.

## Method

1. Listed files with mtime after 2026-08-08, excluding AppleDouble `._*` sidecars:
   **845 files**, of which **277 unique documents** after collapsing `" (N)"`-suffixed
   duplicate downloads (`.pdf`, `.ps`, `.ppt`).
2. Extracted the first three pages of each with `pdftotext` (270 of 277 produced
   usable text; the 7 failures are PostScript files with no `.pdf` twin).
3. Screened the extracted text against an ECDLP-relevance regex (discrete log, index
   calculus, Semaev, Weil descent/restriction, summation polynomial, decomposition,
   Gröbner, point counting, isogeny, Kani, higher-dimensional, theta, Kummer,
   hyperelliptic, Jacobian, pairing, Pollard, GHS, trace zero, endomorphism, division
   polynomial, elliptic net, Mordell, descent, smoothness, factor base), requiring at
   least three distinct hits: **26 candidates**.
4. Read the candidates and cross-checked each distinctive mechanism by grep against
   `knowledge/` and `ideas/` before minting anything.

## What the delta actually is

The new material is **not** a set of new preprints. It is a bulk mirror of workshop and
school material, dominated by:

- **ECC 2007 (Dublin)** — Silverman, Smith, Diem, Chao–Momose, Gaudry, Lange, Hess,
  Thériault, Ritzenthaler, Lercier, Satoh, Weng, Teske, Frey, Rück, Chao.
- **ECC 2009** and **ECC 2018 (Osaka)** — Jao, Morrison, Gaudry point counting, CSIDH.
- **ECC 2024 (Bordeaux school + workshop)** — Kunzweiler on higher-dimensional
  isogenies, SQIsign2D, FESTA, Tibouchi, pyecsca, plus exercise sheets.
- A mirror of a Stanford cryptography publications page (`bfibe`, `latticehibe`,
  `ssl-timing`, `no_rsa_red`, …), largely out of scope for the ECDLP program.
- Non-research files: conference photographs, banquet images, archived programme HTML,
  and empty `missing_url_record*.html` placeholders from the crawl.

Most of the ECDLP-adjacent items are already covered — the corpus carries 812 records
mentioning Semaev, 420 mentioning point counting, and 89 mentioning Robert. Five
sources were genuinely uncovered and were written up.

## Notes on scope and honesty

- No experiment ran. No hypothesis status changed. Only the Coordinator may approve a
  contract or change an official state.
- The two scoped negatives are filed as `KN-TECH` **applicability-boundary** records,
  not as `KN-FIND` internal findings. That is deliberate and schema-enforced: an
  internal finding must carry `internal_refs` into the ledger (an evidence or decision
  record), and a literature-derived derivation produced by an unreviewed sweep has no
  such anchor. Promoting either to `KN-FIND` is a Coordinator action after review, not
  a sweep action.
- The three ideas are deliberately cheap and two of them are **discriminators over
  evidence this program already holds** rather than new constructions. `ECDLP-IDEA-435`
  is the highest-value item in this sweep precisely because its expected outcome is a
  lane closure.
- Every complexity figure quoted from the new sources (`Õ(q^{2-2/g})`,
  `Õ(q^{2-2/(d-2)})`, `Õ(q^{4/3})`, `Õ(q)`) is the original authors' **heuristic** claim
  for curves over `F_q` with a proper subfield available. None of it transfers to prime
  fields, and the two new `KN-LIT` records state that boundary explicitly rather than
  leaving it to be inferred.
- `citation_verified: read` on the five new `KN-LIT` records means the file on disk was
  opened and its text extracted during this sweep. That is a narrower and more
  defensible claim than the corpus-wide marking questioned by `KN-OPEN-3f7a21`, and it
  is the standard used here.
- Deliberately not ingested: photographs, banquet images, archived programme HTML,
  empty crawl placeholders, and the Stanford publications mirror, which is
  out-of-domain for this program.
