# GATHER-20260801 — themed sweep: algebraic number theory and abstract algebra

Run 2026-08-01 on user request: *"gather the latest research from algebraic number
theory, abstract algebra and other relevant fields and enrich the knowledge base."*

This is a **themed** gather, not the usual recency-only sweep. The window since the
last gather ([`GATHER-20260729`](GATHER-20260729.md)) is three business days and, on
inspection, near-empty for this program — so the sweep was widened along two axes the
previous gathers never covered: **abstract-algebra arXiv categories** (`math.RA`,
`math.GR`, `math.RT`, never previously swept) and **topic-targeted retrieval** across
all of 2026 on algorithmic algebraic number theory.

**781 unique items screened; 20 records written** — 17 `KN-LIT`, 1 `KN-TECH`, 2
`KN-OPEN`, plus one ledger correction. **Two of the 17 are repairs of existing corpus
defects**, not new discoveries: one supersedes a corrupt citation, the other ingests an
ECDLP paper that three previous gathers skipped on a false-positive dedup. Both are
recorded in `ledger/corrections/CORR-20260801-003.yaml`.

## A near-empty recency window, and why

The 2026-07-29 .. 08-01 window contributed **almost nothing**, and the reason is worth
recording because it will recur:

- **ePrint `/days/7` is dominated by re-surfaced papers.** Of 63 entries, most of the
  on-topic ones were already in the corpus: `iacr:2026/916` (KN-LIT-1607),
  `iacr:2026/443` (KN-LIT-7582), `iacr:2026/906` (KN-LIT-7583), `iacr:2025/1605`
  (KN-LIT-1462), `iacr:2026/465` (KN-LIT-7596), `iacr:2025/1137` (KN-LIT-7612),
  `iacr:2026/1516` (KN-LIT-7580), `iacr:2026/171` (KN-LIT-1885, under its arXiv id).
  The residue is FHE, MPC, consensus, SNARKs, and AI-security. **One exception —
  `iacr:2026/1244` — was not in the corpus at all**; see below.
- **arXiv `cs.CR` for the window was 58/58 off-topic** — AI security, federated
  learning, watermarking, backdoors, LLM jailbreaks. Not one algebraic paper.
- **arXiv `math.NT` for the window was analytic, not algorithmic** — partitions,
  Diophantine approximation, `L`-function moments, Goldbach. Two items were worth a
  second look (`2607.27034` Kuroda's class number formula, `2607.26915` splitting
  Brauer classes by genus-one curves); neither has algorithmic content and neither was
  ingested.

**The yield came almost entirely from the topic-targeted queries over 2026**, not from
the last three days. For a themed request this is the right trade, and future gathers
serving a topic (rather than a date) should be built the same way.

## Correction 1: a false-positive dedup dropped the sweep's only ECDLP paper

`GATHER-20260729`'s "Already ingested" table lists `iacr:2026/1244` — *Resource
Estimation of the Distributed Quantum Algorithm for the Elliptic Curve Logarithm
Problem* — as **KN-LIT-7563**, and rejects it on that basis.

**KN-LIT-7563 is a different paper.** It is Wesolowski's *The supersingular isogeny
problem in time and memory `p^{1/3+o(1)}`*, and its `eprint` field is `null`. Four
searches confirm the paper was never ingested: no entry holds `iacr:2026/1244` as an
identifier; a title search for "Distributed Quantum Algorithm for the Elliptic Curve"
returns nothing; an author search for "Khajeian" returns nothing; the string
`2026/1244` occurs in exactly **one** place in the corpus — KN-LIT-7600 line 50, in
prose repeating the same mis-citation.

**Three consecutive gathers (0726, 0727, 0729) repeated the exclusion.** The paper is
the only item either that window or this one turned up whose title contains "Elliptic
Curve Logarithm Problem", and it gives qubit and Toffoli counts for a 256-bit curve —
i.e. it is the single most on-topic item any recent sweep encountered.

Ingested here as [[KN-LIT-7657]]. KN-LIT-7600's parenthetical mis-citation is
**documented and left in place**: amending prose inside an immutable record would be a
silent substantive rewrite.

The lesson generalizes past this one row: **identifier dedup fails in both directions.**
It gives false negatives on cross-posts (three found today, below) and the
hand-written "already ingested" tables that compensate for it give false positives that
silently drop papers. Every dedup decision in this gather was backed by both an
identifier check and a distinctive-title grep.

## Correction 2: KN-LIT-2414 superseded

`iacr:2026/185` was **already in the corpus** as [[KN-LIT-2414]], under a corrupt
citation produced by the 2026-07-24 bulk PDF-scrape pass:

- `title` = an ANTS XVII conference banner concatenated with a truncated paper title;
- `authors` = the text fragment `"APPLICATION TO THE"` — **not a person**;
- `year`, `venue`, and all three identifier fields `null`.

That record cannot support a novelty check or a citation, and identifier-based dedup
could never have found it. Per `knowledge/README.md`, corrections supersede:
[[KN-LIT-7642]] carries the correct citation, KN-LIT-2414 now has
`superseded_by: KN-LIT-7642` plus a header note, and **its body is left unedited**.

**Flagging for the Coordinator:** this is unlikely to be the only such record. The
2026-07-24 pass scraped PDFs at scale, and one spot-collision in a 17-item sweep
suggests a non-trivial population of entries with unparseable titles, fragment authors,
and null identifiers. A cheap audit — count `knowledge/literature/` entries with all
three identifiers null *and* an author field that is not a plausible personal name —
would size the problem. **Not run here**; this gather makes no claim about how many
exist. Carried as `OI-1` in `CORR-20260801-003`.

## Cross-post dedup — the gap is now confirmed three times

`GATHER-20260729` flagged that the corpus has no mechanism for attaching a second
identifier when a paper appears on both ePrint and arXiv. This sweep hit it **three
more times**, all caught by title matching and none by identifier matching:

| Paper | Held as | Also appears as | Caught by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spectral Theory of Isogeny Graphs and Quantum Sampling | KN-LIT-1885 (`arXiv:2602.02263`) | `iacr:2026/171` | title grep |
| The SQInstructor | KN-LIT-1919 (`arXiv:2603.09899`) | `iacr:2026/493` | title grep |
| Linear Code Equivalence via Plücker Coordinates | **newly ingested** KN-LIT-7652 | both `iacr:2026/495` and `arXiv:2603.09869` | title + author match |

KN-LIT-7652 records **both** identifiers in its frontmatter, since it was created with
both in hand. The two pre-existing entries were **not** amended: records are immutable,
and adding a cross-reference does not warrant a supersession.

The procedural conclusion, unchanged from 2026-07-29 and now better evidenced:
**identifier dedup is insufficient — every candidate must also be grepped by
distinctive title terms.** That is what caught all three here. An arXiv-first ingest of
a paper already held under its ePrint number would otherwise pass straight through.

## Provenance

All 20 knowledge entries are `confidence: reported`, `citation_verified: web`. **No
full texts were read.** Every claim is relayed from an ePrint abstract page or an arXiv
API abstract retrieved 2026-08-01, and every entry's "Not verified here" section says so
and enumerates what was not checked. Citations were confirmed directly against the
primary record — ePrint `citation_title`/`citation_author` metadata, or the arXiv API —
for all 17 literature entries.

Every literature entry states explicitly whether it bears on the ECDLP. **Exactly one
does: [[KN-LIT-7657]]**, and only as a quantum resource estimate whose stated figures
are per-node. Eleven are cost-model, methodology, or adjacent-problem entries; five
([[KN-LIT-7643]], [[KN-LIT-7645]], [[KN-LIT-7646]], [[KN-LIT-7642]], [[KN-LIT-7655]])
touch machinery the program actually runs, all at the level of constants rather than
exponents.

No experiment was run. No evidence or decision record is created or modified. No
hypothesis status changes. `KN-OPEN-024` and `KN-OPEN-025` are questions, not claims,
and neither proposes an attack. `CORR-20260801-003` records two measured provenance
defects and takes no research position; it follows the `CORR-20260729-003` precedent of
a side-branch session documenting what it measured without exercising Coordinator
authority.
