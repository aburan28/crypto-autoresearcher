# KN-FIND-860118 — the corpus carries an uncorroborated "ML-KEM is broken" claim that this program refuted two weeks ago and never recorded

**Status:** internal finding, recorded to correct an active hazard in the corpus.
**Added:** 2026-08-05.
**Cross-references:** `KN-LIT-1743` (the literature entry this corrects).
**Rule 12:** UNMET and UNWAIVED. This entry changes no `EV-*` status and asserts
no evidence tier. It records a discrepancy between the corpus and two artifacts
already in this repository.

## The hazard

`knowledge/literature/KN-LIT-1743.md` relays, under **Key claims (as reported)**:

> This means that ML-KEM, Falcon, Hawk, NTRU-HPS, and NTRU-HRSS with all
> standardized parameter sets are broken under quantum attack.

The entry does scope itself — it is filed under "as reported", carries a
**Not verified here** section, and calls the paper peripheral to the ECDLP
program. But a reader consulting the corpus for ML-KEM today meets that sentence
with **no cross-reference to the objection this program had already produced**,
and the objection is not in any ledger record. That is the hazard: the program's
own refutation is invisible from the place a reader would look.

## What this program already found, on 2026-07-22

Committed at `dabe36167`, in
`coordination/goals/GOAL-MLKEM-001/batches/BATCH-002/tasks/TASK-20260722-207/structure_report.yaml`
(lines 397-408), verbatim:

> The advertised algorithm takes a module basis and outputs a generator of its
> determinant ideal. No checked reduction maps the target `b = A s + e` to `s`;
> the determinant-ideal output is unchanged when `b` and `s` change with `A`
> fixed. It therefore cannot certify the claimed key recovery as written.
>
> `survives: false`
> `reason: output_invariant_is_secret_independent`

Also stated at
`.../BATCH-001/tasks/TASK-20260722-201/baseline_map.md` lines 261-266 and
`.../TASK-20260722-207/derivation_note.md` lines 523-527.

**No ledger record cites it.** Verified: `grep -rl
"output_invariant_is_secret_independent" ledger/` returns nothing. The finding
sat in task artifacts for two weeks without promotion, which is exactly the
failure a knowledge corpus exists to prevent.

## What a 2026-08-05 scouting check added

Run against `IDEA-20260805-e805b6`, **unreviewed**, artifacts in session
scratchpad only and **not archived in this repository**. It is recorded here as
corroboration of the 2026-07-22 finding, not as evidence in its own right.

It read the actual paper — `arXiv:2605.17412v2`, retrieved 2026-08-05 — rather
than a summary. Note that `KN-LIT-1743` names a local copy at
`downloads/2605.17412v1.pdf` and **that path does not exist in this repository**;
the entry was seeded from v1, and the paper is now at v2.

It verified computationally that `det_R M(A) = (q^d)` and
`[R^{2d} : M(A)] = q^{nd}` for every `A`: one distinct determinant ideal over 200
draws at each of n = 4, 8, 16 with d = 2 and q = 3329, planted-versus-null
identical 200/200, with `det_R B = ±X^t · q^d` exactly at n up to 256 and
d in {2,3,4}. Cross-checked three ways, including a disjoint code path using no
ring arithmetic that matched the index modulo four primes at dimension 2048.

It cited the specific steps: Algorithm 1 takes `B` and returns `(g_0, γ)` with
`A` and `t` absent from the inputs and the secret absent from the output; §2.2
eq. (2) defines the only ML-KEM module lattice in the paper; and the paper
performs this very computation **for Falcon** at Proposition 6.3 (A3), finds the
determinant ideal `(q)` has trivial log-embedding, and therefore **switches
target** to the first Gram-Schmidt ideal `(f)` — but never runs the check on
ML-KEM's lattice and never switches target for it. For Falcon, Hawk and NTRU the
paper supplies an explicit map back to the secret; **for ML-KEM it supplies
none.**

## What is and is not closed

**Closed:** the ML-KEM instantiation of the pipeline *as written in v2*, on the
paper's own structure.

**Not closed, and explicitly not claimed:** Parts I-III's mathematics, which may
each be correct; the tower-PIP algorithm as number theory; the principal-ideal
family generally; and the Falcon / Hawk / NTRU extensions, which target `(f)`
rather than a determinant ideal and are structurally different — the argument
here does not reach them.

**A correction to `IDEA-20260805-e805b6`'s own mechanism**, recorded because the
proposal is now on the record: graded cells localise the cause to the **q-ary
modulus, not the rank** — rank-1 without modulus gave 191/200 distinct ideals,
rank-2 without modulus 200/200, both q-ary cells 1. The proposal offered rank
reduction as a second independent obstruction; rank is not doing the work.

## Why this is recorded as a finding and not as evidence

Neither source is a reviewed evidence record. The 2026-07-22 artifact was
produced under a batch; the 2026-08-05 check was Coordinator scouting with no
independent review, and its own executor declined to propose a corpus change,
correctly leaving that to the Coordinator. Two independent routes reaching the
same conclusion is worth recording; it is not worth an evidence tier, and this
entry claims none.

## The lesson, which is about the corpus and not about ML-KEM

A refutation that lives only in a task artifact does not protect anyone. This
program spent two weeks with a "standardized schemes are broken" sentence in its
literature corpus and the objection to it sitting three directories away, in a
file no novelty check reads. The knowledge-promotion gate exists for exactly
this and it did not fire, because the objection was a by-product of a batch
about something else rather than that batch's declared finding.
