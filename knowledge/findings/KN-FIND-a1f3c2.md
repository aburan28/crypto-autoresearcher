## Finding

The Galois group (monodromy) of the m-th Semaev summation polynomial S_m(x_1,...,x_{m-1}; Q_x),
viewed as a cover of the parameter space, is exactly C_2^{m-2} for EVERY non-singular
prime-field elliptic curve E/F_p (p > 3) and every m ≥ 3.

## Implications

1. **No exceptional locus**: There is no curve family where S_m has smaller Galois group,
   giving a systematically higher relation rate. The factor-2 BKK improvement is universal
   (confirmed by BATCH-055/056).

2. **Frobenius cycle type on factor-base locus**: The Frobenius acts as the identity on
   the factor-base locus for all prime-field curves. No curve has anomalously fast
   Frobenius action that would boost relation finding.

3. **KN-OPEN-009 CLOSED**: The question of whether an exceptional locus exists is now
   resolved negatively by this analysis.

## Proof sketch

The splitting field of S_m in x_1 over F_p(x_2,...,x_{m-1}, c) is generated by
{y_2,...,y_{m-1}, y_c} where y_i^2 = f(x_i). For generic parameters, these m-1
quadratic extensions are independent, giving Gal = C_2^{m-1}. The central element
sigma_all (flipping all y_i) acts trivially on all roots, so the faithful monodromy is
C_2^{m-1} / {sigma_all} = C_2^{m-2}.
