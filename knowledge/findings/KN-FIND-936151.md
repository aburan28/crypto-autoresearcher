## 1. The finding

Nagao ePrint 2015/984 Definition 5 gives the **true** first fall degree `d_F` of
a generator set, and Definition 6 the **fake** `d'_F`, computed modulo the field
equations `S_fe = {X_j^p - X_j}`. His **Lemma 4** asserts the one-directional
inequality

```
    d_F  <=  d'_F
```

and every route from a measured fake degree to a statement about a true degree
runs through it. 2015/984 asserts Lemma 4 without proof, derives it from its
"Lemma 3 ([11])", attributes that to Nagao 2013/549, and declines to reproduce
the proof ("Proof of this Lemma is complicated and not constructive", :507).

> **FALSE as a universal statement.** Under the **equality** reading of condition
> (1) of Definitions 5 and 6, the universally quantified
> `d_F <= d'_F` over finite generator sets `F ⊂ F_2[X_1..X_N]` is **false**, and
> it is false under **every** placement of `S_fe` in either system.

### The two witnesses, in full

Both are exact finite arithmetic over `F_2`, computable in milliseconds, and
checkable by anyone without trusting any agent in this program.

| | witness A | witness B |
|---|---|---|
| ring | `F_2[X, Y]` | `F_2[X_1, X_2, X_3]` |
| `f_1` | `X^2 Y` | `X_1X_2 + X_3` |
| `f_2` | `XY + X` | `X_1X_3 + X_2` |
| `d'_F` (fake) | 2 | 2 |
| `d_F` (true) | 3 | 3 |
| kills | both true-side placements (outside under both readings; inside under equality) | both true-side placements |
| shape | 2 variables, **not** multilinear | 3 variables, multilinear |
| produced by | `TASK-20260916-9da6e0`, a blind reader, from the definitions | `tools/lemma4_inside_form.py`, written earlier and independently |

**The two are genuinely different objects** — different variable counts, and one
is multilinear while the other is not — and that is recorded so they cannot
silently collapse into one witness counted twice.

**The mechanism, in one sentence.** `f_1 = X^2 Y` is **not reduced** mod `S_fe`:
the fake world sees `XY` at degree 2 where the true world sees degree 3, and the
inequality fails by exactly that difference.

**It is not pathological.** 94 of 400 random multilinear pairs at `N = 3`
violate the inequality — **23.5%**, seed 20260916, binomial 95% interval about
`[19.5%, 28.0%]`. Zero of the 400 had `d_F < d'_F`.

**Replication, and what kind it is.** Each witness is computed by **two
implementations written without sight of each other**: a blind reader's
`recheck.py` in an independent session, and the Coordinator's
`tools/lemma4_inside_form.py` from an earlier session, which that reader never
read. Per AGENTS.md "Review architecture" this is a **blind re-derivation**
rather than a replication, so the agreement is evidence about the *quantity* and
not about either implementation — which is precisely what recomputing from a
producer's own artifacts structurally cannot buy. Each implementation also
carries its own internal oracle: `lemma4_inside_form.py` cross-checks its
eliminator against literal cofactor enumeration (80/80 agreement), and the
reader certifies its lower bounds by two ring homomorphisms rather than by
search. Pinned by 15 tests (`tools/test_lemma4_inside_form.py`, 15/15 pass).

## 2. Inertness — why the naming in older records is unsafe to reuse

> **Adjoining `S_fe` to the FAKE system leaves `d'_F` unchanged.**

One line from Definition 6 as printed: every member `X_j^p - X_j` given its own
multiplier `h_j` contributes `h_j(X_j^p - X_j) ≡ 0 mod S_fe`, identically, for
every `h_j`. Definition 6 states all three of its conditions mod `S_fe`, so such
a member contributes `deg = -∞` to condition (1) and nothing to conditions (2)
and (3). All three conditions are unchanged, and so is the minimum over
witnesses. Verified exhaustively on witness A under **both** readings of
condition (1), and on 300 random multilinear pairs at `N = 3` with **zero**
disagreements. Structurally, `first_fall_degree` drops generators that reduce to
zero, which is inertness realised in code rather than asserted.

**The consequence that bites, and the reason this section exists.** Because the
fake side is inert, an "inside form" and an "outside form" distinguished *on the
fake side* are **one statement**, not two. Any record that draws the
inside/outside distinction on the fake side is drawing a distinction that does
not exist, and one half of it was already known false.

**Where the distinction genuinely lives is the TRUE side**, and
`CORR-20260916-96f47d` drew it there and drew it **correctly**: *"Lemma 4 puts
`d_F` on `{f_1..f_M}` with the field equations OUTSIDE the left-hand system."*
Four later committed documents restated that on the fake side and so
misdescribed Lemma 4 as printed (the drift is catalogued in
`DEC-20260916-87fc5c` and in the closing checkpoint
`ledger/goals/GOAL-SEMBIN-fcb7a2/checkpoints/BATCH-e0a0c1.closing.yaml`). None
of them is edited; all are immutable. **The rule this finding exports: state
which SIDE a placement is on, every time.** Lemma 4 as printed puts `S_fe`
*inside* the fake system and *outside* the true one.

## 4. The forward value: the inference is RECOVERABLE per instance, by U-4

**The useful content of this entry is not that a lemma is dead.** It is that the
inference the lemma was carrying can be re-earned, instance by instance, at no
asymptotic cost.

The derivation from 2013/549's Lemma 2 to Lemma 4 needs two hypotheses that are
stated in **neither** paper:

- **`U-3`** — the field equations must be **members of the true system** whose
  `d_F` is claimed. Lemma 4 as printed leaves the true side as `{f_1..f_M}`
  alone, so `U-3` fails.
- **`U-4`** — the witness realising `d'_F` must **lose no total degree under
  reduction mod `S_fe`**; equivalently `max_i deg(g_i f_i) <= d'_F` on the
  **unreduced** products. `U-4` does not follow from Definition 6, which bounds
  only the *reduced* product degree, and cannot be closed by Lemma 2, whose
  bound is relative to `deg R` where `deg R` is itself governed by the unreduced
  product degree one step upstream. **`U-4` is the entire distance between what
  Lemma 2 gives and what Lemma 4 claims.**

> **The recovery rule.** An inference "`d'_F` was measured at `v`, therefore
> `d_F <= v`" may no longer be discharged by **citing Lemma 4 as a theorem**. It
> may be discharged **at an instance** by recording the unreduced total degree of
> each product `g_i f_i` of the realising fake witness and checking
> `max_i deg(g_i f_i) <= d'_F` there. Where that holds, the consequence stands —
> and is then reported as discharged by **that bookkeeping**, not by Lemma 4.

**Both papers already do exactly this in their own applications**, by explicit
bookkeeping (`deg g_i <= 1` on already-reduced generators): 2015/984's only use
of Lemma 4 — the proof of its Lemma 6 — puts the union on the **true** side and
absent from the fake side, the reverse of Lemma 4's printed placement and the
sound direction; and 2013/549 never states Lemma 4 at all, stating its estimate
over `n + N` equations, which makes the field equations members by construction.
**So the published uses are unaffected by this finding. A consumer that
transcribed the lemma literally is not.** Operationalised as `NA-1` of
`DEC-20260916-87fc5c`.

**The obstruction read as a resource.** `d_F - d'_F` *is* the degree lost to
reduction, so the same gap that breaks the citation is the hypothesis of the
repair: an instrument that records unreduced product degrees discharges `U-4`
directly. Independently, the 23.5% violation rate is itself worth having — it
turns "how often does the fake degree mislead" into a measurable property of a
family rather than a yes/no question about a lemma, which is the better-posed
question.

## 5. Boundaries — these travel with the finding

1. **`p = 2` only.** Every witness is over `F_2`. Nothing is checked at
   `p >= 3`, where Proposition 2's bound is `3p+1`.
2. **`N <= 3` variables.** Nothing is checked at the `N` of any descended
   system. `claim_tier: toy`, and that is honest: a counterexample to a
   universally quantified statement is complete at any scale, but it is also the
   exact reason this entry says nothing about EQS4 at `n ∈ [7,12]`.
3. **The equality reading of condition (1)** — see §3. Void under the printed
   `>=`.
4. **The transfer is NOT established and is NOT assumed.** From "the universal
   statement is false" to "a given consumer's inference is broken" requires that
   the consumer's instances be among the **violating** instances. Whether
   `EXP-SEMBIN-4fa22c`'s EQS4 instances are is unknown, and is why this finding
   moves no hypothesis and no experiment status.
5. **Zero runs, zero measurements.** `EV-SEMBIN-1ca3c8` carries empty
   `run_ids` and empty `experiment_ids` and says so at length. The backing is a
   derivation carried out by machine, not a sampled observation.

## 6. What this forecloses, and what it does not

**Foreclosed.** Citing `d_F <= d'_F` as a theorem, anywhere in this program.
There is now **no placement of the field equations** under which a universally
quantified `d_F <= d'_F` is unrefuted at the equality convention:

| placement | status |
|---|---|
| `S_fe` outside the true system (= Lemma 4 as printed, by inertness) | **FALSE** |
| `S_fe` inside the true system | **FALSE** |
| `S_fe` inside or outside the **fake** system | **not a distinction** (inertness) |

**Not foreclosed** — and the most valuable single answer now available is the
first of these:

1. **Does Nagao's own application chain discharge `U-4` at the instances
   Proposition 5 covers?** Both published uses discharge it by explicit
   bookkeeping, but no reader has been asked whether EQS4 inherits that — EQS4
   is reached through Definition 8 and footnote 6's omitted proof, which is
   where the chain is thinnest. A `yes` restores the inference at those
   instances **without needing Lemma 4 at all** (`NA-4` of
   `DEC-20260916-87fc5c`).
2. **`p >= 3`**, entirely untouched.
3. **Structured summation-polynomial systems**, where the violation rate of a
   random family says nothing.
4. **Nagao's intent on condition (1)**, which this text cannot settle.
