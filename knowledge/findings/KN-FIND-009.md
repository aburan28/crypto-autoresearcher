## What is established, and at what level

**Certificate level (level 1, `docs/claims-and-verification.md`).** Let `E` be a
`j = 0` curve over `F_p` with `p ≡ 1 mod 3`, let `G = <P>` have prime order `N`,
and let the eigenvalue coordinates be the multiset `{1, λ₂, λ₃}` with `λ₂, λ₃` the
two roots of `L² + L + 1 ≡ 0 mod N` — the eigenvalue source frozen in
`EXP-ENDO-001`. Vieta gives `1 + λ₂ + λ₃ ≡ 0 mod N` **identically**, so the
integer vector `(1,1,1,0,…,0)` lies in the witness lattice `W_r(k)` for **every**
rank `r ≥ 3` and **every** `k`, and the ∞-norm first minimum of `W_r` is
**exactly 1** — against a Minkowski-style prediction of about `N^{1/(2r)}`.
Computed instance: `p = 4294967539`, `y² = x³ + 7`, `N = 613557433`,
`λ₂ = 290635917`, `λ₃ = 322921515`, predicted `N^{1/6} = 29.150…`, true value `1`;
the on-curve form `P + φ(P) + φ²(P) = O` replays to the point at infinity — a
**true relation that yields no discrete logarithm**. The existence hypothesis
`N ≡ 1 mod 3` is *forced*, not assumed: `φ` acts on a prime-order group as a
scalar of order 3, and `λ = 1` would force `N | Norm(ζ₃ − 1) = 3`.

**Derivation level (level 2).** Three further statements, checkable on paper and
instantiated on the same toy instance:

1. For prime `N`, the `k`-independent sublattice of `W_r` — the intersection over
   all `k ∈ [1, N−1]` — is exactly `R_r × R_r`, where
   `R_r = {c ∈ Z^r : Σ c_i λ_i ≡ 0 mod N}`, and every vector of it has `B = 0`, so
   a `k = −A/B` recovery yields nothing. Any **two distinct** values of `k` pin
   this. **`N` prime is load-bearing**: over a composite modulus a nonzero
   non-invertible `B` recovers `k mod N/gcd(B,N)` — Pohlig–Hellman, partial
   information rather than none.
2. Writing down a basis of `W_r(k)` for any `r ≥ 1` is polynomial-time
   **equivalent** to solving the instance. The reduction is deterministic and uses
   **no LLL**: `W_r(k)` is the kernel of a character of index `N`, so the
   annihilator group is cyclic of order `N`, generated by
   `u = (λ₁,…,λ_r, kλ₁,…,kλ_r)`; compute the one-dimensional nullspace of the
   basis matrix over `Z/N`, normalise at the first coordinate, and read `k` off
   coordinate `r+1`. Verified against a randomly scrambled `W₂` basis in which `k`
   appeared in no entry, recovering `k` with `[k]P = Q`. (An LLL-plus-`−A/B`
   route is *not* unconditional: LLL can return a vector with `B = 0`, and does on
   `W₃`.)
3. The **governing invariant** is the existence of a relation `Σ c_i λ_i ≡ 0
   mod N` of ∞-norm at most `X = ⌈N^{1/(2r)}⌉` — *relation-freeness at a
   threshold* — and **not** the `Z`-rank of `End(E)`. `R_r` has determinant `N` in
   dimension `r`, so its generic first minimum is about `N^{1/r}`, the square of
   `X`: a relation short enough to matter is a measure-zero accident, and the CM
   triple `{1, λ, λ²}` is exactly that accident.

## Boundaries

- **Scope.** The frozen eigenvalue source only, ranks `r ≥ 3` only, prime `N`
  only. **Not** a statement about the curve family and **not** about rank: on the
  same `j = 0` curves, `φ₃ = a + b·φ` is a genuine endomorphism, evaluable as
  `[a]P + [b]φ(P)` and passing an on-curve eigenvalue check, whose relation vector
  `(a,b,−1)` is generically long — so **genuine, curve-backed, non-degenerate
  `r = 3` and `r = 4` cells exist**.
- **The same degeneracy is not peculiar to `j = 0`.** It is a Vieta/automorphism
  artifact of using every efficiently computable scalar as a coordinate: at
  `j = 1728` four distinct efficiently computable scalars `{1, i, −1, −i}` exist
  (verified on a real curve), and norm-1 congruence vectors exist there too. The
  frozen `j = 1728` cells escape only because that contract draws them
  synthetically.
- **Toy scale.** One 33-bit `p` with a 30-bit `N`, plus a 28-bit `j = 1728`
  instance. No crypto-scale validation; the identity's generality rests on the
  derivation, not on the instance.
- **Zero runs.** `EXP-ENDO-001` was never executed. Nothing here is a measurement,
  and neither heuristic of that contract was validated or falsified by execution.
- **Not a cryptanalytic result.** No attack, no attack improvement, no hardness
  result, no lower bound, no closure, no impossibility claim. No exponent moves in
  either direction and no promotion gate is addressed. The `k` recovered above was
  *chosen*, not solved.
- **The endomorphism lane is not closed.** `W_r` as parametrised in `EXP-ENDO-001`
  is barred; endomorphism lattices as a class are not. Preprocessing/advice
  models, multi-target/batch DLP, interval and partial-information settings, and
  the supersingular rank-4 case are all untouched.
- **Independence is session-level and implementation-level only** — not
  model-level — and item 3's positive half (that relation-free cells are typical)
  is **unmeasured**: no SVP was run on a curve-backed alternative triple.

## A retraction this entry exists partly to record

The originating Coordinator record inferred a *genuine rank bound* from the
`Z`-rank of `End(E)` and concluded that a repaired sweep would have only two
points, `r = 1` and `r = 2`. **That reasoning is retracted** (`DEC-20260728-005`,
R1): rank deficiency guarantees *some* relation, never a *short* one. The
conclusion survives for a different reason — a rescued `r = 3` cell is a
`Z`-linear reparametrisation of the `r ≤ 2` lattice and adds no endomorphism
content — but the published premise was false.

Equally retracted: the claim that the `k`-independent sublattice is *useless*.
`R₂ = {(c₁,c₂) : c₁ + c₂λ ≡ 0 mod N}` **is the GLV decomposition lattice**, whose
short vectors of norm about `√N` split `k = k₁ + k₂λ` and underlie the
automorphism-quotient rho walk (`KN-TECH-018`, `KN-LIT-041`, `KN-LIT-042`). The
sublattice called information-free is the useful one in the literature.

## Why it matters to this program

It is a reusable **experiment-design law**, not merely a dead lane. Any future
contract that augments a search object with endomorphism eigenvalues must
pre-register **relation-freeness at threshold `X = ⌈N^{1/(2r)}⌉`** as its
genuine/synthetic criterion — one SVP in dimension `r`, seconds of integer
arithmetic — instead of a rank count, and must state its algorithm's position
against Pollard rho and BSGS in **time and memory** before freezing. The withdrawn
instrument cost about `2^{r+1}√N` time with `2^r√N` memory, Pareto-dominated by
BSGS at every rank including `r = 1` and by rho absolutely on memory, with no
time–memory point at which it wins; that fact was already committed in the
proposal the contract was derived from, and was rediscovered at review. See
`KN-TECH-018` and `KN-TECH-020` for surrounding technique context and
`KN-LIT-041`/`KN-LIT-042` for the correct, constant-factor way endomorphisms help.
