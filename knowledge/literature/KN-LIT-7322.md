## Contribution
We construct a two-round Schnorr-based signature scheme (DWMS) by delinearizing two pre-commitments supplied by each signer. DWMS is a secure signature scheme in the algebraic group model (AGM) and the random oracle model (ROM) under the assumption of the hardness of the one-more discrete logarithm problem and the 2-entwined sum problem that we introduce in this paper.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our new m-entwined sum problem tweaks the k-sum problem in a scalar field using the associated group.
- We prove the hardness of our new problem in the AGM assuming the hardness of the discrete logarithm problem in the associated group.
- We believe that our new problem simplifies the security proofs of multisignature schemes that use the delinearization of commitments.
