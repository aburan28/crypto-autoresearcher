## Contribution
**Verifying Classic McEliece**: examines the role of **formal methods in
post-quantum standardisation** — using Classic McEliece as the case study for a
broader argument about what standardisation processes should require.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Formal verification is applicable to Classic McEliece's specification and implementation.
- A position on what role formal methods should play in PQC standardisation.
