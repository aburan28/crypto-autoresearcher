## Contribution
Considers E = Q(ζm) a power-of-two cyclotomic field with maximal totally real
subfield K = Q(ζm + ζm^{-1}). Building on Chevignard et al. (Eurocrypt 2025),
which reduces module-LIP for rank-two module lattices over O_E to the
norm-reduced Principal Ideal Problem (nrdPiP) in a quaternion algebra, this
paper derives two consequences: (1) a dimension reduction for SVP instances
of the class used by the HAWK signature scheme, and (2) an improved
key-recovery approach for HAWK that exploits a trace-zero representation of
the cyclotomic order to shrink the lattice dimension (reported as roughly
n/2 + 1 compared with the naive dimension-n reduction).

## Key claims (as reported)
- Module-LIP over rank-2 module lattices over O_E reduces to nrdPiP in a
  quaternion algebra (recap of Chevignard et al., Eurocrypt'25).
- Dimension-reduction corollary: HAWK secret-key recovery reduces to SVP in
  dimension ≈ n/2 + O(1) (diagonal lattice over the trace-zero subspace),
  improving the naive reduction to dimension n.
- Concretely targets the HAWK parameter sets (NIST Round-4 PQC signature
  candidates).

## Relevance
- Directly relevant to the program's lattice spine: HAWK is a NIST Round-4
  PQC signature; module-LIP-to-quaternion reductions mirror the lattice side
  of the isogeny/quaternion endomorphism analog. The trace-zero technique is
  a generic reduction dual to the program's own NTRU-lattice security
  arguments.
