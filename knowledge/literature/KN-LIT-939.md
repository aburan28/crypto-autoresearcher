## Contribution
Mordell curves over a number field K are elliptic curves of the form y 2 = x3 + c, where c ∈ K \ {0}. Let p ≥ 5 be a prime number, K a number field such that [K : Q] ∈ {2p, 3p} and let E be a Mordell curve defined over K.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We classify all the possible torsion subgroups E(K)tors for all Mordell curves E defined over Q when [K : Q] ∈ {2p, 3p}.
