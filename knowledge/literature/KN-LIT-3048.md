## Contribution
We present a new definition of computationally binding commitment schemes in the quantum setting, which we call “collapse-binding”. The definition applies to string commitments, composes in parallel, and works well with rewinding-based proofs.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We give simple constructions of collapse-binding commitments in the random oracle model, giving evidence that they can be realized from hash functions like SHA-3.
- We evidence the usefulness of our definition by constructing three-round statistical zero-knowledge quantum arguments of knowledge for all NP languages.
