## Contribution
In an inner-product functional encryption scheme, the plaintexts are vectors and the owner of the secret key can delegate the ability to compute weighted sums of the coefficients of the plaintext of any ciphertext. Recently, many inner-product functional encryption schemes were proposed.

## Key claims (as reported)
- However, none of the known schemes are secure against chosen ciphertext attacks (IND-FE-CCA).
- We present a generic construction of IND-FE-CCA inner-product functional encryption from projective hash functions with homomorphic properties.
- We show concrete instantiations based on the DCR assumption, the DDH assumption, and more generally, any Matrix DDH assumption.
