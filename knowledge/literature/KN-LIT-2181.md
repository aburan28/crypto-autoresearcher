## Contribution
We propose the first polynomial time algorithm for the braid DiffieHellman conjugacy problem (DHCP) on which the braid key exchange scheme and the braid encryption scheme are based [9]. We show the proposed method solves the DHCP for the image of braids under the Lawrence-Krammer representation and the solutions play the equivalent role of the original key for the DHCP of braids.
