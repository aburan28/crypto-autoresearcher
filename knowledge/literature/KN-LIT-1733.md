## Contribution
For an abelian variety X and α ∈ Br(X), we propose a new invariance IndSH (α) that refines the known period index relations. It is closely related to the geometry of X , the Gm -gerbe over X that corresponds to α: we study the minimal trivializing isogenies for X via its μn -lifts and the 1−twisted semihomogeneous vector bundles on X .

## Key claims (as reported)
- As an application, we show that the period index conjecture holds true for products of elliptic curves of any dimension.
