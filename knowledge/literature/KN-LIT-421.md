## Contribution
Assuming the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, we design a deterministic algorithm that, given a prime p and positive integer m = o(p1/2 (log p)−4 ), outputs an elliptic curve E over the finite field Fp for which the cardinality of E(Fp ) is divisible by m. The running time of the algorithm is mp1/2+o(1) , and this leads to more efficient constructions of rational functions over Fp whose image is small relative to p.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also give an unconditional version of the algorithm that works for almost all primes p, and give a probabilistic algorithm with subexponential time complexity.
