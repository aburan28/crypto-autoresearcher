## Contribution
An elliptic curve E defined over a p-adic field K with a p-isogeny φ : E → E ′ comes equipped with an invariant αφ/K that measures the valuation of the leading term of the formal group homomorphism Φ : Ê → Ê ′ . We prove that if K/Qp is unramified and E has additive, potentially supersingular reduction, then αφ/K is determined by the number of distinct geometric components on the special fibers of the minimal proper regular models of E and E ′ .
