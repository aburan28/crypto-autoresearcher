## Contribution
We prove that a balanced 8-round Feistel network is indifferentiable from a random permutation, improving on previous 10-round results by Dachman-Soled et al. and Dai et al. Our simulator achieves security O(q 8 /2n ), similarly to the security of Dai et al.

## Key claims (as reported)
- For further comparison, Dachman-Soled et al. achieve security O(q 12 /2n ), while the original 14-round simulator of Holenstein et al. achieves security O(q 10 /2n ).
