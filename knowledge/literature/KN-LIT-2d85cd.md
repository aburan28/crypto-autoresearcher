## Contribution
**Low-Reiter**: the Niederreiter encryption scheme implemented for embedded
microcontrollers — early evidence that code-based cryptography could run on
constrained hardware at all.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Niederreiter encryption is implementable on embedded microcontrollers.

## Relevance to this program
Held with [[KN-LIT-4915]] (MicroEliece) as the 2009-2010 pair that established
embedded feasibility, fifteen years before the current wave. The interval
between "shown possible" and "shown practical" in this literature is long, and
worth remembering when judging how quickly a promising direction should be
expected to produce deployable results.
