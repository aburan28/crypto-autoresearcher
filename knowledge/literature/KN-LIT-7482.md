## Contribution
We present a realization of an LFSM that utilizes an LFSR. This is based on a well-known fact from linear algebra.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This structure is used to show that a previous attempt at using a CA in place of an LFSR in constructing a stream cipher did not necessarily increase its security.
- We also give a general method for checking whether or not a nonlinear filter generator based on an LFSM allows reduction to one that is based on an LFSR and which is vulnerable to Anderson information leakage.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
