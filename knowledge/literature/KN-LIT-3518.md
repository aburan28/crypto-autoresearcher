## Contribution
) Jonathan Katz Dept. of Computer Science, University of Maryland, College Park, MD (Work done while at Columbia University) jkatz@cs.umd.edu Abstract. We describe efficient protocols for non-malleable (interactive) proofs of plaintext knowledge for the RSA, Rabin, Paillier, and El Gamal encryption schemes.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also highlight some important applications of these protocols: – Chosen-ciphertext-secure, interactive encryption.
- In settings where both parties are on-line, an interactive encryption protocol may be used.
- We construct chosen-ciphertext-secure interactive encryption schemes based on any of the schemes above.
- In each case, the improved scheme requires only a small overhead beyond the original, semantically-secure scheme. – Password-based authenticated key exchange.
