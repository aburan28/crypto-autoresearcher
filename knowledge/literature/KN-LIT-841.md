## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve defined over a number field F with good ordinary reduction at all primes above p, and let F∞ be a finitely ramified uniform pro-p extension of F containing the cyclotomic Zp -extension Fcyc . Set F (n) be the n-th layer (n) of the tower, and Fcyc the cyclotomic Zp -extension of F (n) .

## Key claims (as reported)
- We study the growth of (n) the rank of E(F ) by analyzing the growth of the λ-invariant of the Selmer group (n) over Fcyc as n → ∞.
- This method has its origins in work of A.
- Cuoco, who studied 2 Zp -extensions.
- Refined estimates for growth are proved that are close to conjectured estimates.
