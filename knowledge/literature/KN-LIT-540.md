## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve over Q. Let p be a prime of good reduction for E.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Then, for a prime p 6= l, the Frobenius automorphism associated to p (unique up to conjugation) acts on the l-adic Tate module of E.
- The characteristic polynomial of the Frobenius automorphism has rational integer coefficients and is independent of l.
- Its splitting field is called the Frobenius field of E at p.
- Let E1 and E2 be two elliptic curves defined over Q that are non-isogenous over Q and also without complex multiplication over Q.
