## Contribution
We present a technique for delegating a short lattice basis that has the advantage of keeping the lattice dimension unchanged upon delegation. Building on this result, we construct two new hierarchical identity-based encryption (HIBE) schemes, with and without random oracles.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The resulting systems are very different from earlier lattice-based HIBEs and in some cases result in shorter ciphertexts and private keys.
- We prove security from classic lattice hardness assumptions.
