## Contribution
Since Crypto 2004, hash functions have been the target of many attacks which showed that several well-known functions such as SHA-0 or MD5 can no longer be considered secure collision free hash functions. These attacks use classical cryptographic techniques from block cipher analysis such as differential cryptanalysis together with some specific methods.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Among those, we can cite the neutral bits of Biham and Chen or the message modification techniques of Wang et al.
- In this paper, we show that another tool of block cipher analysis, the boomerang attack, can also be used in this context.
- In particular, we show that using this boomerang attack as a neutral bits tool, it becomes possible to lower the complexity of the attacks on SHA-1.
