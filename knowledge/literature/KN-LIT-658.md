## Contribution
Let E/Q be an elliptic curve of conductor N , let p > 3 be a prime where E has good ordinary reduction, and let K be an imaginary quadratic field satisfying the Heegner hypothesis. In 1987, Perrin-Riou formulated an Iwasawa main conjecture for the Tate–Shafarevich group of E over the anticyclotomic Zp -extension of K in terms of Heegner points.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we give a proof of Perrin-Riou’s conjecture under mild hypotheses.
- Our proof builds on Howard’s theory of bipartite Euler systems and Wei Zhang’s work on Kolyvagin’s conjecture.
- In the case when p splits in K, we also obtain a proof of the Iwasawa–Greenberg main conjecture for the p-adic L-functions of Bertolini–Darmon–Prasanna.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
