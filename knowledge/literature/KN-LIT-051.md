## Contribution
A comprehensive survey of ~ten years of lattice cryptography, centered on the SIS
and LWE problems (KN-LIT-049, KN-LIT-050), their ring/module variants, and the
worst-case-to-average-case hardness foundations, plus the cryptographic
constructions built on them.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Standard reference/tutorial map of the field (encryption, signatures, advanced
  primitives, security reductions); presents no new hardness results.
- IACR ePrint 2015/939 is the freely available version.

## Relevance to this program
The best single entry-point to the POST-QUANTUM lattice landscape that is ADJACENT
to this repo's ECDLP mission. Recorded so the corpus has an authoritative map of
the quantum-resistant assumptions and schemes (KN-TECH-021, KN-TECH-022,
KN-TECH-023) that contrast with the (quantum-broken) ECDLP the program studies.
