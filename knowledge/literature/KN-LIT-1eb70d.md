## Contribution
Application of **Omura's iterative decoding method** to the cryptanalysis of the
McEliece system. Omura's method is the ancestor credited by Clark and Cain
([[KN-LIT-93e8d7]]) for the ISD idea in the coding-theory literature, largely
independently of Prange's formulation.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Omura's iterative decoding applied as a cryptanalytic method against McEliece.

## Relevance to this program
Held for provenance. The ISD idea has **two independent origins** — Prange in
the cyclic-code literature ([[KN-LIT-6a786b]]) and Omura in the decoding
literature — and the field spent years rediscovering connections between them.

This program treats that as an argument for the novelty-check discipline it
already runs: independent rediscovery is the normal case, not the exception,
and a proposal's originality claim must be checked against the corpus before it
is asserted.
