## Contribution
The references [9, 3, 1] treat timing attacks on RSA with CRT and Montgomery’s multiplication algorithm in unprotected implementations. It has been widely believed that exponent blinding would prevent any timing attack on RSA.

## Key claims (as reported)
- At cost of significantly more timing measurements this paper extends the before-mentioned attacks to RSA with CRT when Montgomery’s multiplication algorithm and exponent blinding are applied.
- Simulation experiments are conducted, which confirm the theoretical results.
- Effective countermeasures exist.
- In particular, the attack efficiency is higher than in the previous version [12] while large parts of both papers coincide.
