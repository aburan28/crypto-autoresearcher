## Contribution
Given two semistable elliptic surfaces over a curve C defined over a field of characteristic zero or finitely generated over its prime field, we show that any compatible family of effective isometries of the Néron-Severi lattices of the base changed elliptic surfaces for all finite separable maps B → C arises from an isomorphism of the elliptic surfaces. Without the effectivity hypothesis, we show that the two elliptic surfaces are isomorphic.
