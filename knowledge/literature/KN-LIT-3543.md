## Contribution
We present a new protocol for efficient distributed computation modulo a shared secret. We further present a protocol to distributively generate a random shared prime or safe prime that is much more efficient than previously known methods.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This allows one to distributively compute shared RSA keys, where the modulus is the product of two safe primes, much more efficiently than was previously known.
