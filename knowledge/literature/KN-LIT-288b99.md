## Contribution
**Statistical decoding of codes over `F_q`** — extending the statistical
decoding approach from binary codes to general finite fields.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Statistical decoding generalises to codes over `F_q`.

## Relevance to this program
Held as part of the statistical-decoding trajectory documented on
[[KN-LIT-b66899]]. Non-binary generalisation is also the setting where
[[KN-LIT-1321dc]] later found a structural improvement invisible in the binary
case — a reminder that generalising an algorithm sometimes **reveals** rather
than merely extends.
