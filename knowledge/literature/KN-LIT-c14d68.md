## Contribution

F4 with a Hilbert-series-driven choice of how many S-polynomials to generate per degree, plus pair selection favouring small leading terms. It cuts zero reductions and matrix sizes and beats M4GB and Magma F4 single- and multi-core. It is the current best engineered F4 for MQ; not ECDLP-specific.

## Key claims (as reported)

- New Fukuoka MQ Challenge records: Type VI over F_31 with m = 21, 22, 23, 24.

## Relevance to this program

Efficient F4 variant for MQ systems (2026); engineering baseline for the in-house F4/GPU-F4 work.
