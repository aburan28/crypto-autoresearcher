## Key claims (as reported)
- We show how to generalize lattice reduction algorithms to module lattices.
- Specifically, we reduce γ-approximate ModuleSVP over module lattices with rank k ≥ 2 to γ 0 -approximate ModuleSVP over module lattices with rank 2 ≤ β ≤ k.
- To do so, we modify the celebrated slide-reduction algorithm of Gama and Nguyen to work with module filtrations, a highdimensional generalization of the (Z-)basis of a lattice.
- The particular value of γ that we achieve depends on the underlying number field K, the order R ⊆ OK , and the embedding (as well as, of course, k and β).
