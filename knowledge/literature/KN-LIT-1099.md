## Contribution
Let K be a number field and l ≥ 5 a prime number. Mazur and Rubin introduced the notion of diophantine stability for a variety X/K at a prime l.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that there is a positive density set of elliptic curves E/Q of rank 1 such that E/K is diophantine stable at l.
- This has implications for Hilbert’s Tenth Problem over OK .
- This problem asks whether there exists an algorithm that decides in finite time whether a finite system of Diophantine equations over OK has a solution.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
