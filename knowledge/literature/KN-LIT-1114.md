## Contribution
We characterise genus 3 complex smooth hyperelliptic curves that contain two additional involutions as curves that can be build from five points in P1 with a distinguished triple. We are able to write down explicit equations for the curves and all their quotient curves.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that, fixing one of the elliptic quotient curve, the Prym map becomes a 2:1 map and therefore the hyperelliptic Klein Prym map, constructed recently by the first author with A.
- Ortega, is also 2:1 in this case.
- As a by-product we show an explicit family of (1, d) polarised abelian surfaces (for d > 1), such that any surface in the family satisfying a certain explicit condition is abstractly nonisomorphic to its dual abelian surface.
