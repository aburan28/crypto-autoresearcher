## Why this record exists

GFPN's symmetrization-aware Gaudry ladder (`IDEA-20260920-a639a1` /
`EXP-GFPN-05ff43`) needs the Joux–Vitse (n−1)-point / F4-remake method as the
m=4 fallback when m=5 FGLM times out, and as the literature anchor for
oracle-assisted SDHP (`IDEA-20260920-73dc35`). Under AGENTS.md rule 9 those
uses require a frozen, read source.

## What was read

Full extracted text of ePrint 2010/157 (`paper_fulltext.md`). Variation of
Gaudry/Diem index calculus for E(F_{q^n}); relations obtained on E(F_{p^5});
F4 variant removing reductions to zero; elementary-symmetric symmetrization;
application to oracle-assisted static Diffie–Hellman on a ~130-bit field.
