## Contribution
This paper studies functional-graph-based (second) preimage attacks against hash combiners. By exploiting more properties of functional graph, we find an improved preimage attack against the XOR combiner with a complexity of 25n/8 , while the previous best-known complexity is 22n/3 .

## Key claims (as reported)
- Moreover, we find the first generic second-preimage attack on Zipper hash with an optimal complexity of 23n/5 .

Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
