## Contribution
In 1996, Coppersmith introduced two lattice reduction based techniques to find small roots in polynomial equations. One technique works for modular univariate polynomials, the other for bivariate polynomials over the integers.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Since then, these methods have been used in a huge variety of cryptanalytic applications.
- Some applications also use extensions of Coppersmith’s techniques on more variables.
- However, these extensions are heuristic methods.
- In the present paper, we present and analyze a new variation of Coppersmith’s algorithm on three variables over the integers.
