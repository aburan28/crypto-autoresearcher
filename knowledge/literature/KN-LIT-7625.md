## Contribution
Completes the Honda–Tate classification: every conjugacy class of Weil
q-numbers arises as the Frobenius of some simple abelian variety over F_q.
Together with Tate’s injectivity (KN-LIT-7624), this gives a bijection
between F_q-isogeny classes of simple abelian varieties and conjugacy classes
of Weil q-numbers.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Surjectivity of A ↦ π_A onto Weil q-numbers up to conjugacy.
- Classification is stated in terms of Frobenius endomorphisms of type (A_0)
  (Weil numbers of order a for k_a = F_{p^a}).
- Applications indicated in the paper include consequences for endomorphism
  algebras; the full endomorphism-ring / isomorphism-type refinement is
  Waterhouse (KN-LIT-7626).

## Relevance to this program
The existence half of “which isogeny classes exist over F_q.” Anchors
enumerations of ordinary/supersingular classes, CM discriminants, and any
proposal that assumes a Weil polynomial is realized by a curve or AV.
Does not produce discrete logs; it constrains which groups can appear.
