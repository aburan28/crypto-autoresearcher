## Contribution
We consider the geometric map C, called Cayleyan, associating to a plane cubic E the adjoint of its dual curve. We show that C and the classical Hessian map H generate a free semigroup.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We begin the investigation of the geometry and dynamics of these maps, and of the geometrically special elliptic curves: these are the elliptic curves isomorphic to cubics in the Hesse pencil which are fixed by some endomorphism belonging to the semigroup W(H, C) generated by H, C.
- We point out then how the dynamic behaviours of H and C differ drastically.
- Firstly, concerning the number of real periodic points: for H these are infinitely many, for C they are just 4.
- Secondly, the Julia set of H is the whole projective line, unlike what happens for all elements of W(H, C) which are not iterates of H.
