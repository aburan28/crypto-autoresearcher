## Contribution
Let p be an odd prime. We study Mazur’s conjecture on the growth of the Mordell– Weil ranks of an elliptic curve E/Q over Zp -extensions of an imaginary quadratic field, where p is a prime of good reduction for E.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In particular, we obtain criteria that may be checked through explicit calculation, thus allowing for the verification of Mazur’s conjecture in specific examples.
