## Contribution
For an elliptic curve E over a finite field we define the point sequence (Pn ) recursively by Pn = θ(Pn−1 ) = θn (P0 ) with an endomorphism θ ∈ End(E) and with some initial point P0 on E. We study the distribution and the linear complexity of sequences obtained from (Pn ).
