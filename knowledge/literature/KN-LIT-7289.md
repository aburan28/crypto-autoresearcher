## Contribution
We study twists of the Burkhardt quartic threefold over non-algebraically closed base fields of characteristic different from 2, 3, 5. We show they all admit quartic models in projective four-space.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We identify a Galois-cohomological obstruction that measures if a given twist is birational to a moduli space.
- This obstruction has implications for the rational points on these varieties.
- As a result, we see that all possible 3-level structures can be realized by abelian surfaces, whereas Kummer 3-level structures that group theoretically may be admissible, may not be realizable over certain base fields.
- We give an example of a Burkhardt quartic over a bivariate function field whose desingularization has no rational points at all.
