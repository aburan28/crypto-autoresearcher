## Contribution
We consider a setting where users store their encrypted documents on a remote server and can selectively share documents with each other. A user should be able to perform keyword searches over all the documents she has access to, including the ones that others shared with her.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The contents of the documents, and the search queries, should remain private from the server.
- This setting was considered by Popa et al.
- (NSDI ’14) who developed a new cryptographic primitive called Multi-Key Searchable Encryption (MKSE), together with an instantiation and an implementation within a system called Mylar, to address this goal.
