## Contribution
We present a new construction based on modular groups. A novel element of our construction is to embed each input into a sequence of matrices with determinant ±1, the product of which yields the desired mac.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We analyze using the invertibility and the arithmetic properties of the determinants of certain types of matrices; this may be of interest in other applications.
- Performance results on our preliminary implementations show the speed of our mac is competitive with recent fast mac algorithms, achieving 0.5 Gigabytes per second on a 1.06 GHz Celeron.
