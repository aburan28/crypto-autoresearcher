## Contribution
Given a family of products of elliptic curves over a rational curve defined over a number field K, and assuming that there exists no isogeny between the pair of elliptic curves in the generic fiber, we establish an upper bound for the number of special fibers with height at most B where the two factors are isogenous. Our proof provides an upper bound that is dependent on K, the family, and the height B.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Furthermore, by introducing a slight modification to the definition of the height of the parametrizing family, we prove a uniform bound depends solely on the degree of the family, the field K, and B.
- Based on the uniformity, and the fact that the idea of using Heath-Brown type bounds on covers and optimizing the cover to count rational points on specific algebraic families has not been exploited much yet, we hope that the paper serves as a good example to illustrate the strengths of the method and will inspire further exploration and application of these techniques in related research.
