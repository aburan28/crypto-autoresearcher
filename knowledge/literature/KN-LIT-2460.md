## Contribution
In this paper, we present new algorithms for the field arithmetic layers of supersingular isogeny Diffie-Hellman; one of the fifteen remaining candidates in the NIST post-quantum standardization process. Our approach uses a polynomial representation of the field elements together with mechanisms to keep the coefficients within bounds during the arithmetic operations.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We present timings and comparisons for SIKEp503 and suggest a novel 736-bit prime that offers a 1.17× speedup compared to SIKEp751 for a similar level of security.
