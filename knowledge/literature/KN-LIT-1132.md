## Contribution
For two supersingular elliptic curves E and E ′ defined over Fp2 , let [E × E ′ ] be the superspecial abelian surface with the principal polarization {0} × E ′ + E × {0}. We determine local structure of the vertices [E × E ′ ] in the (l, l)-isogeny graph of principally polarized superspecial abelian surfaces where either E or E ′ is defined over Fp .
