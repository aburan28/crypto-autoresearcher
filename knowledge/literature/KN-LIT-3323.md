## Contribution
We investigate the possibility of cryptographic primitives over nonclassical computational models. We replace the traditional finite field Fn∗ with the infinite field Q of rational numbers, and we give all parties unbounded computational power.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also give parties the ability to sample random real numbers.
- We determine that secure signature schemes and secure encryption schemes do not exist.
- We then prove more generally that it is impossible for two parties to agree upon a shared secret in this model.
- This rules out many other cryptographic primitives, such as Diffie-Hellman key exchange, oblivious transfer and interactive encryption.
