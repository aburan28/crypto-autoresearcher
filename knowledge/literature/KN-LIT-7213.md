## Contribution
Viable cryptosystem designs must address power analysis attacks, and masking is a commonly proposed technique for defending against these side-channel attacks. It is possible to overcome simple masking by using higher-order techniques, but apparently only at some cost in terms of generality, number of required samples from the device being attacked, and computational complexity.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We make progress towards ascertaining the significance of these costs by exploring a couple of attacks that attempt to efficiently employ second-order techniques to overcome masking.
- In particular, we consider two variants of secondorder differential power analysis: Zero-Offset 2DPA and FFT 2DPA.
