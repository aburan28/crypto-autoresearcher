## Contribution
In this paper we combine methods from additive combinatorics and Diophantine geometry to study the generalised sum-product phenomenon in algebraic groups. As an application of this circle of ideas, we resolve a conjecture of Bremner on arithmetic progressions in coordinates of elliptic curves, along with various other generalisations studied in the literature.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also prove a uniform Bourgain–Chang-type sum-product estimate for general 1dimensional algebraic groups G over C.
- Using these ideas, we provide an alternative solution to a problem of Bays–Breuillard.
- Furthermore, we show an Elekes–Szabó type result in the same setting for sets with small doubling, improving upon an earlier result of Bays– Breuillard when G is not Ga .
- Our power saving here can be shown to be quantitatively optimal.
