## Contribution
The Mutual Information Analysis (MIA) is a generic sidechannel distinguisher that has been introduced at CHES 2008. This paper brings three contributions with respect to its applicability to practice.

## Key claims (as reported)
- First, we emphasize that the MIA principle can be seen as a toolbox in which different (more or less effective) statistical methods can be plugged in.
- Doing this, we introduce interesting alternatives to the original proposal.
- Second, we discuss the contexts in which the MIA can lead to successful key recoveries with lower data complexity than classical attacks such as, e.g. using Pearson’s correlation coefficient.
- We show that such contexts exist in practically meaningful situations and analyze them statistically.
