## Contribution
Let OK be a Henselian discrete valuation domain with field of fractions K. Assume that OK has algebraically closed residue field k.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Let E/K be an elliptic curve with additive reduction.
- The semi-stable reduction theorem asserts that there exists a minimal extension L/K such that the base change EL /L has semi-stable reduction.
- It is natural to wonder whether specific properties of the semi-stable reduction and of the extension L/K impose restrictions on what types of Kodaira type the special fiber of E/K may have.
- In this paper we study the restrictions imposed on the reduction type when the extension L/K is wildly ramified of degree 2, and the curve E/K has potentially good supersingular reduction.
