## Contribution
In the 1960’s, Birch proved that the traces of Frobenius for elliptic curves taken at random over a large finite field is modeled by the semicircular distribution (i.e. the usual Sato-Tate for non-CM elliptic curves). In analogy with Birch’s result, a recent paper by Ono, the author, and Saikia proved that the limiting distribution of the normalized Frobenius traces Aλ (p) of a certain family of K3 surfaces Xλ with generic Picard rank 19 is the O(3) distribution.
