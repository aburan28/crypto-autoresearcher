## Contribution
Introductory chapter of the CUP volume *Topics in Computational Number Theory
inspired by Peter L. Montgomery*, tracing the impact of Peter Montgomery's
work — Montgomery multiplication, the Montgomery ladder, Montgomery's ECM
variant, PH/P-1 style factorization, and its application to ECDLP/ECC
computations — and framing the volume's chapters that expand the theme.

## Key claims
- Advertised as the volume's intro; contains cross-references to the other
  chapters.
- Montgomery's modular multiplier remains the bedrock of high-speed finite
  field arithmetic used in ECC and factorization.

## Relevance
- Survey/context for Montgomery-based arithmetic in the program; relevant to
  the baseline/arithmetic cost domain (Montgomery multiplication is one of
  the program's low-level cost quantities). No ECDLP claims.
