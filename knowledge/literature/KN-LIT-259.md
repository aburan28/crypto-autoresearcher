## Contribution
By carrying out a rational transformation on the base curve CP1 of the Seiberg-Witten curve for N = 2 supersymmetric pure SU(2)-gauge theory, we obtain a family of Jacobian elliptic K3 surfaces of Picard rank 17. The isogeny relating the Seiberg-Witten curve for pure SU(2)-gauge theory to the one for SU(2)-gauge theory with Nf = 2 massless hypermultiplets extends to define a Nikulin involution on each K3 surface in the family.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that the desingularization of the quotient of the K3 surface by the involution is isomorphic to a Kummer surface of the Jacobian variety of a curve of genus two.
- We then derive a relation between the Yukawa coupling associated with the elliptic K3 surface and the Yukawa coupling of pure SU(2)-gauge theory.
