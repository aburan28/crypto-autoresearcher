## Contribution
This note gives a simple algorithm for the following effectivity problem: given a genus 2 curve X together with a nonconstant map π : X → E to an elliptic curve, determine an elliptic curve E ′ and a map π ′ : X → E ′ independent of π. Equivalently, we compute the complementary elliptic factor in the decomposition of Jac(X) up to isogeny.

## Key claims (as reported)
- While the problem has been studied extensively, and more general ones have been solved by deep and powerful techniques, we are not aware of a reference for the simple explicit procedure described here.
