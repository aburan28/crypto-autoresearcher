## Contribution
Let p be a prime lager than 3. Let k be a number field, which does not contain the subfield of Q(ζp2 ) of degree p over Q.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Suppose that E is an elliptic curve defined over k.
- We prove that the existence of a counterexample to the local-global divisibility by p2 in E, assures the existence of a k-rational point of exact order p in E.
- Using the Merel Theorem, we then shrunk the known set of primes for which there could be a counterexample to the local-global divisibility by p2 .
