## Contribution
Let K = Q( −3) or Q( −1) and let Cn denote the cyclic group of order n. We study how the torsion part of an elliptic curve over K grows in a quadratic extension of K.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In the case E(K)[2] ≈ C1 we investigate how a given torsion structure can grow in a quadratic extension and the maximum number of extensions in which it grows.
- We also study the torsion structures which occur as the quadratic twist of a given torsion structure.
- In order to achieve this we examine nisogenies defined over K for n = 15, 20, 21, 24, 27, 30, 35.
