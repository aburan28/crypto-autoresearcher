## Contribution
For a prime p and a rational elliptic curve E/Q , set K = Q(E[p]) to p denote the torsion field generated by E[p] := ker{E − → E}. The class group ClK is a module over Gal(K/Q).

## Key claims (as reported)
- Given a fixed odd prime number p, we study the average nonvanishing of certain Galois stable quotients of the mod-p class group ClK /p ClK .
- Here, E varies over all rational elliptic curves, ordered according to height.
- Our results are conditional, since we assume that the p-primary part of the Tate-Shafarevich group is finite.
- Furthermore, we assume predictions made by Delaunay for the statistical variation of the p-primary parts of Tate-Shafarevich groups.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
