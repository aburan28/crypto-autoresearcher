## Contribution

Instead of the full Macaulay matrix at the degree of regularity, uses specific submatrices that are provably sufficient, effectively working at a fractional degree between two integers. Under a mild generalised semi-regularity assumption (checked experimentally) this smooths the XL/F4 cost curve and avoids heavily overdetermined matrices.
