## Contribution
We introduce new modulus scaling techniques for transforming a class of primes into special forms which enables efficient arithmetic. The scaling technique may be used to improve multiplication and inversion in finite fields.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We present an efficient inversion algorithm that utilizes the structure of scaled modulus.
- Our inversion algorithm exhibits superior performance to the Euclidean algorithm and lends itself to efficient hardware implementation due to its simplicity.
- Using the scaled modulus technique and our specialized inversion algorithm we develop an elliptic curve processor architecture.
- The resulting architecture successfully utilizes redundant representation of elements in GF (p) and provides a low-power, high speed, and small footprint specialized elliptic curve implementation.
