## Contribution
The CM class number one problem for elliptic curves asked to find all elliptic curves defined over the rationals with non-trivial endomorphism ring. For genus-2 curves it is the problem of determining all CM curves of genus 2 defined over the reflex field.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We solve the problem by showing that the list given in Bouyer–Streng [3, Tables 1a, 1b, 2b, and 2c] is complete.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
