## Contribution
We prove that there exist infinitely many elliptic curves over Q(i) with jinvariant 1728 and rank exactly 2 which are not obtained by base change from Q. The rank of each such curve is determined via 2-isogeny descent, and the existence of infinitely many such curves follows from Tao’s constellation theorem for Gaussian primes.
