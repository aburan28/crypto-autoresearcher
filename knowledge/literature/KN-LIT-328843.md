## Contribution

Parallel-rho ECDLP engines for prime-field curves on the COPACOBANA FPGA cluster, with cost estimates for larger (Certicom-size) instances. Bos-Kaihara-Montgomery (SHARCS 2009) quote it as 3.97e7 and 2.08e7 iterations/s for 96- and 128-bit generic prime fields on a ~US$10,000, 120-FPGA machine.

## Key claims (as reported)

- 3.97e7 it/s (96-bit) and 2.08e7 it/s (128-bit) per COPACOBANA, as quoted by Bos-Kaihara-Montgomery 2009.

## Relevance to this program

COPACOBANA special-purpose hardware for ECDLP; historical FPGA cost baseline.
