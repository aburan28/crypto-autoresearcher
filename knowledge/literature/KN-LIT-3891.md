## Contribution
The most efficient collision attacks on members of the SHA family presented so far all use complex characteristics which were manually constructed by Wang et al. In this report, we describe a method to search for characteristics in an automatic way.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This is particularly useful for multi-block attacks, and as a proof of concept, we give a two-block collision for 64-step SHA-1 based on a new characteristic.
- The highest number of steps for which a SHA-1 collision was published so far was 58.
- We also give a unified view on the expected work factor of a collision search and the needed degrees of freedom for the search, which facilitates optimization.
