## Contribution
We consider an arbitrary int-amplified surjective endomorphism f of a normal projective variety X over C and its f −1 -stable prime divisors. We extend the early result in [32, Theorem 1.3] for the case of polarized endomorphisms to the case of intamplified endomorphisms.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Assume further that X has at worst Kawamata log terminal singularities.
- We prove that the total number of f −1 -stable prime divisors has an optimal upper bound dim X + ρ(X), where ρ(X) is the Picard number.
- Also, we give a sufficient condition for X to be rationally connected and simply connected.
- Finally, by running the minimal model program (MMP), we prove that, under some extra conditions, the end product of the MMP can only be an elliptic curve or a single point.
