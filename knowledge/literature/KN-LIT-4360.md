## Contribution
We address the problem of polynomial time factoring RSA moduli N1 = p1 q1 with the help of an oracle. As opposed to other approaches that require an oracle that explicitly outputs bits of p1 , we use an oracle that gives only implicit information about p1 .

## Key claims (as reported)
- Namely, our oracle outputs a different N2 = p2 q2 such that p1 and p2 share the t least significant bits.
- Surprisingly, this implicit information is already sufficient to efficiently factor N1 , N2 provided that t is large enough.
- We then generalize this approach to more than one oracle query.
