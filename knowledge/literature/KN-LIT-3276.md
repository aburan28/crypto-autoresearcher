## Contribution
At PKC 2003 Paeng, Jung, and Ha proposed a lattice based public key cryptosystem(PJH). It is originated from GGH, and designed as a hybrid of GGH and NTRUEncrypt in order to reduce the key size.

## Key claims (as reported)
- They claimed that PJH is secure against all possible attacks, especially against lattice attacks.
- However, in this paper, we present a key recovery attack, based on lattice theory, against PJH.
- The running time of our attack is drastically short.
- For example, we could recover all secret keys within 10 minutes even for the system with n = 1001 on a single PC.
