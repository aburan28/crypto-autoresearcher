## Contribution
Integral attacks are well-known to be effective against bytebased block ciphers. In this document, we outline how to launch integral attacks against bit-based block ciphers.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This new type of integral attack traces the propagation of the plaintext structure at bit-level by incorporating bit-pattern based notations.
- The new notation gives the attacker more details about the properties of a structure of cipher blocks.
- The main difference from ordinary integral attacks is that we look at the pattern the bits in a specific position in the cipher block has through the structure.
- The bit-pattern based integral attack is applied to Noekeon, Serpent and present reduced up to 5, 6 and 7 rounds, respectively.
