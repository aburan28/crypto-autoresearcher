## Contribution
Constructing practical and provably secure group signature schemes has been a very active research topic in recent years. A group signature can be viewed as a digital signature with certain extra properties.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Notably, anyone can verify that a signature is generated by a legitimate group member, while the actual signer can only be identified (and linked) by a designated entity called a group manager.
- Currently, the most efficient group signature scheme available is due to Camenisch and Lysyanskaya [CL02].
- It is obtained by integrating a novel dynamic accumulator with the scheme by Ateniese, et al.
- In this paper, we construct a dynamic accumulator that accumulates composites, as opposed to previous accumulators that accumulated primes.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
