## Contribution
Byzantine Agreement (BA) among n players allows the players to agree on a value, even when up to t of the players are faulty. In the broadcast variant of BA, one dedicated player holds a message, and all players shall learn this message.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In the consensus variant of BA, every player holds (presumably the same) message, and the players shall agree on this message.
- BA is the probably most important primitive in distributed protocols, hence its efficiency is of particular importance.
- BA from scratch, i.e., without a trusted setup, is possible only for t < n/3.
- In this setting, the known BA protocols are highly efficient (O(n2 ) bits of communication) and provide information-theoretic security.
