## Contribution
Khazad is a new block cipher initially proposed as a candidate to the NESSIE project. Its design is very similar to Rijndael, although it is a 64-bit block cipher.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we propose a new attack that can be seen as an extension of the Square attack.
- It takes advantage of redundancies between the round key derivation and the round function, and also exploits some algebraic observations over a few rounds.
- As a result, we can break 5 rounds of Khazad faster than exhaustive key search.
- This is the best known cryptanalytic result against Khazad.
