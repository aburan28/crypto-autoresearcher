## Contribution
Let A be an abelian variety over a number field K. An identity between the L-functions L(A/Ki , s) for extensions Ki of K induces a conjectural relation between the Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer quotients.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove these relations modulo finiteness of X , and give an analogous statement for Selmer groups.
- Based on this, we develop a method for determining the parity of various combinations of ranks of A over extensions of K.
- As one of the applications, we establish the parity conjecture for elliptic curves assuming finiteness of X (E/K(E[2]))[6∞ ] and some restrictions on the reduction at primes above 2 and 3: the parity of the Mordell-Weil rank of E/K agrees with the parity of the analytic rank, as determined by the root number.
- We also prove the p-parity conjecture for all elliptic curves over Q and all primes p: the parities of the p∞ -Selmer rank and the analytic rank agree.
