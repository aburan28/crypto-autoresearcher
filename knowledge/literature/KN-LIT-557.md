## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve over Q. Let p be an odd prime and ι : Q ֒→ Cp an embedding.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Let K be an imaginary quadratic field and HK the corresponding Hilbert class field.
- For a class group character χ ∈ c ClK , let Q(χ) be the field generated by Im(χ) and pχ the prime of Q(χ) above ClK such that the χp determined via ιp .
- Under mild hypotheses, we show that the number of χ ∈ c isotypic Tate–Shafarevich group X(E/HK )χ is finite with trivial pχ -part increases with the absolute value of the discriminant of K.
- We also show an analogous result simultaneously for X(E/HK )χ and the analytic Sha of a GL2 -type abelian variety over Q.
