## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve defined over rational field Q and N be a positive integer. Now ME (N ) denotes the number of primes p, such that the group Ep (Fp ) is of order N .

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that ME (N ) follows Poisson distribution when an average is taken over a large class of curves.
