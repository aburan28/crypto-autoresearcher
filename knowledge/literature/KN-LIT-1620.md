## Contribution
Let A/Q be a modular abelian variety of analytic rank 0. If G is a non-trivial finite abelian group such that all prime factors of |G| are sufficiently large in terms of A, we show that there are infinitely many G-extensions F/Q such that A(F ) is finite.

## Key claims (as reported)
- When A is a rational elliptic curve of analytic rank zero with no exceptional primes, or the product of two such curves, the same conclusion holds without any assumptions on |G|.
- Our proof relies on new simultaneous non-vanishing results for twisted central L-values of even-weight holomorphic newforms.
- These results are obtained via novel constructions related to horizontal p-adic Lfunctions and are of independent interest.
