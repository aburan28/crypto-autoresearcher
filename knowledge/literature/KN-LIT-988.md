## Contribution
Given a number field F0 that contains no Hilbert class field of any imaginary quadratic field, we show that under GRH there exists an effectively computable constant B := B(F0 ) ∈ Z+ for which the following holds: for any finite extension L/F0 whose degree [L : F0 ] is coprime to B, one has for all elliptic curves E/F0 that the L-rational torsion subgroup E(L)[tors] = E(F0 )[tors]. This generalizes a previous result of González-Jiménez and Najman [GJN20, Theorem 7.2.i] over F0 = Q.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Towards showing this, we also prove a result on relative uniform divisibility of the index of a mod-l Galois representation of an elliptic curve over F0 .
- Additionally, we show that the main result’s conclusion fails when we allow F0 to have rationally defined CM, due to the existence of F0 -rational isogenies of arbitrarily large prime degrees satisfying certain congruency conditions.
