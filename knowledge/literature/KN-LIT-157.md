## Contribution
This note contains an elementary discussion of the Arakelov intersection theory of elliptic curves. The main new results are a projection formula for elliptic arithmetic surfaces and a formula for the “energy” of an isogeny between Riemann surfaces of genus 1.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The latter formula provides an answer to a question originally posed by Szpiro.
