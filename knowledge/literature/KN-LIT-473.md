## Contribution
We unconditionally determine IQ (d), the set of possible prime degrees of cyclic Kisogneies of elliptic curves with Q-rational j-invariants and without complex multiplication over number fields K of degree ≤ d, for d ≤ 7, and give an upper bound for IQ (d) for d > 7. Assuming Serre’s uniformity conjecture, we determine IQ (d) exactly for all positive integers d.
