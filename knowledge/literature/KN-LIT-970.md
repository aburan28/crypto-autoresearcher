## Contribution
Recently, there has been great interest towards constructing efficient zero-knowledge proofs for practical languages. In this work, we focus on proofs for threshold relations, in which the prover is required to prove knowledge of witnesses for k out of ` statements.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The main contribution of our work is an efficient and modular transformation that starting from a large class of Σ-protocols and a corresponding threshold relation Rk,` , provides an efficient Σ-protocol for Rk,` with improved communication complexity w.r.t. prior results.
