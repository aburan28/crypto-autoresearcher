## Contribution
Let A be a simple abelian variety of dimension g defined over a finite field Fq with Frobenius endomorphism π. This paper describes the structure of the group of rational points A(Fqn ), for all n ≥ 1, as a module over the ring R of endomorphisms which are defined over Fq , under certain technical conditions.

## Key claims (as reported)
- If [Q(π) : Q] = 2g and R is a Gorenstein ring, then A(Fqn ) ∼ = R/R(π n − 1).
- This includes the case when A is ordinary and has maximal real multiplication.
- Otherwise, if Z is the center of R and (π n − 1)Z is the product of invertible prime ideals in Z, then A(Fqn )d ∼ = R/R(π n − 1) where d = 2g/[Q(π) : Q].
- Finally, we deduce the structure of A(Fq ) as a module over R under similar conditions.
