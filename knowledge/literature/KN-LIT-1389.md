## Contribution
Vlăduţ characterized in 1999 the set of finite fields k such that all elliptic curves defined over k have a cyclic group of rational points. Under the conjecture of infinitely many Mersenne primes, this set is infinite.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In these notes we prove that there is no a finite field k such that all the simple abelian varieties defined over k of dimension g > 1 have a cyclic group of rational points.
