## Contribution
Mazur’s isogeny theorem states that if p is a prime for which there exists an elliptic curve E/Q that admits a rational isogeny of degree p, then p ∈ {2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 37, 43, 67, 163}. This result is one of the cornerstones of the theory of elliptic curves and plays a crucial role in the proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this expository paper, we overview Mazur’s proof of this theorem, in which modular curves and Galois representations feature prominently.
