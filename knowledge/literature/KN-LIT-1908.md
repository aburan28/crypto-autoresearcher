## Contribution
Let E/Q be an elliptic curve and let ρE : Gal(Q/Q) → GL(2, Z) representation attached to E. We describe and implement an algorithm to compute the image of ρE in GL(2, b Z) (up to conjugation) for an elliptic curve E/Q with complex multiplication (CM) and j-invariant not 0 or 1728.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In the process, we prove certain entanglement results between division fields of elliptic curves over Q with CM.
