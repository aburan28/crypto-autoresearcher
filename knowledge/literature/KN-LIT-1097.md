## Contribution
Let E/Q be an elliptic curve and let p be an odd prime of good reduction for E. Let K be an imaginary quadratic field satisfying the classical Heegner hypothesis and in which p splits.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The goal of this paper is two-fold: (1) We formulate a p-adic BSD conjecture for the p-adic L-function LBDP introduced by p Bertolini–Darmon–Prasanna [BDP13].
- (2) For an algebraic analogue FpBDP of LBDP , we show that the “leading coefficient” part of p our conjecture holds, and that the “order of vanishing” part follows from the expected “maximal non-degeneracy” of an anticyclotomic p-adic height.
- In particular, when the Iwasawa–Greenberg Main Conjecture (FpBDP ) = (LBDP ) is known, p our results determine the leading coefficient of LBDP at T = 0 up to a p-adic unit.
- Moreover, p by adapting the approach of Burungale–Castella–Kim [BCK21], we prove the main conjecture for supersingular primes p under mild hypotheses.
