## Contribution
We prove many new cases of the Inverse Galois Problem for those simple groups arising from orthogonal groups over finite fields. For example, we show that the finite simple groups Ω2n+1 (p) and PΩ+ 4n (p) both occur as the Galois group of a Galois extension of the rationals for all integers n ≥ 2 and all primes p ≥ 5.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We obtain our representations by studying families of twists of elliptic curves and using some known cases of the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture along with a big monodromy result of Hall.
