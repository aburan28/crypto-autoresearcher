## Contribution
The Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) provides three levels of security: 128, 192, and 256 bits. Given a desired level of security for the AES, this paper discusses matching public key sizes for RSA and the ElGamal family of protocols.

## Key claims (as reported)
- For the latter both traditional multiplicative groups of finite fields and elliptic curve groups are considered.
- The practicality of the resulting systems is commented upon.
- Despite the conclusions, this paper should not be interpreted as an endorsement of any particular public key system in favor of any other.
