## Contribution

Lecture material introducing principally polarised abelian varieties (PPAVs) of
dimension `> 1` and isogeny computation between them, i.e. the machinery that the
post-SIDH literature (Kani-style embeddings, SQIsign2D, FESTA) is built on.

Read from the local corpus copies `downloads/2-01-ecc-higherdim-handout.pdf`
(school handout), `downloads/3-01-ecc-workshop-kunzweiler.pdf` (workshop talk
"Isogeny Computations in Higher Dimensions"), and `downloads/ecc-higherdim-ex.pdf`
(exercises).

## Key content (as reported)

- Dimension classification: all 1-dimensional PPAVs are elliptic curves; in
  dimension 2 the PPAVs are products of elliptic curves and Jacobians of genus-2
  curves; in dimension 3, products and Jacobians of genus-3 curves.
- Genus-2 arithmetic: Mumford representation `(a, b)` with `0 <= deg b < deg a <= 2`
  and `a | (f - b^2)`; Cantor's algorithm as composition (`a' = a_1 a_2`) followed
  by reduction (`a = monic((f - b'^2)/a')`, `b = -b' mod a`).
- Torsion: `E[m] ≅ (Z/mZ)^2` versus `Jac(C)[m] ≅ (Z/mZ)^4` for `p ∤ m`. The 2-torsion
  of `Jac(C)` is generated by pairs of Weierstrass points `D_ij`, with an explicit
  Weil-pairing rule `e_2(D_ij, D_kl) = -1` exactly when `|{i,j} ∩ {k,l}| = 1`.
- `Jac(C)` as a variety: Cassels–Flynn give `Jac(C) = V(F_1,...,F_72) ⊂ P^15`
  (16 coordinates), which is stated as impractical for explicit computation. The
  **Kummer surface** `K = Jac(C)/<±1> = V(F) ⊂ P^3` is a quartic with 15
  singularities and only 3 coordinates, retaining "remnants of the group
  structure: multiplications, isogenies".
- The Kummer surface is presented as the exact dimension-2 analogue of x-only
  elliptic arithmetic: `π(P) = x_1`; `π(P + Q)` is *not* well defined in general
  (only when `2Q = 0`), while `π(2P) = 2π(P)` is.

## Relevance

- Supplies the current primary reference for the higher-dimensional representation
  the corpus has been tracking under `Kani` and `Robert` tags, in teaching form
  with the explicit coordinate costs (16 vs 3 coordinates) that any representation
  proposal must charge.
- The Kummer/x-only analogy is the dimension-2 counterpart of active idea
  `ECDLP-IDEA-158` (nonfaithful x-only WNU signed lift); the recorded fact that
  pseudo-addition is well defined only for a known difference is the standing
  cost this program must charge to any `±1`-quotient relation-search proposal.
- The obvious "apply the SIDH-break machinery to ECDLP" route is closed by this
  program in `KN-TECH-3b593f` on a degree/torsion-budget count: the Kani-style
  recovery needs torsion advice of modulus exceeding the unknown map's degree,
  and the ECDLP "map" `[m]` has degree `m^2 ≈ p^2`.
- `LITERATURE-DERIVED`, teaching material; no complexity claim and no prime-field
  consequence.
