## Contribution

Encodes the relation-search (PDP) step of index calculus on twisted Edwards curves over prime fields as QUBO and solves it on D-Wave. The FGHR symmetries give many equivalent solutions, which makes annealing easier. Follow-up: Dzierzkowski, "The generalized method of solving ECDLP using quantum annealing", IJET 2025, doi:10.24425/ijet.2025.153544, which removes the complete-arithmetic requirement.
