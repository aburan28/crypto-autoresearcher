## Contribution
We construct a short group signature scheme. Signatures in our scheme are approximately the size of a standard RSA signature with the same security.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Security of our group signature is based on the Strong Diffie-Hellman assumption and a new assumption in bilinear groups called the Decision Linear assumption.
- We prove security of our system, in the random oracle model, using a variant of the security definition for group signatures recently given by Bellare, Micciancio, and Warinschi.
