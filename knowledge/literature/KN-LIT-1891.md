## Contribution
We study complex subvarieties in certain non-Kähler holomorphically symplectic manifolds X, called the Bogomolov-Guan manifolds. Let E be an elliptic curve, L an ample line bundle on E, S ⊂ E 2 a complex curve, and p1 , p2 the corresponding projections of S to E.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The curve S is called a quasi-diagonal if p∗1 L ⊗ p∗2 L−1 is a torsion line bundle.
- We show that there are at most countably many quasidiagonals for any (E, L).
- Using the quasi-diagonals, we classify the projective subvarieties in the Bogomolov-Guan manifold.
- The BogomolovGuan manifold is equipped with a Lagrangian fibration π : X → CP n .
