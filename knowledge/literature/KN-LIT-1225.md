## Contribution
For any prime p > 3 and rational p-integers b, c with c(b3 − 27c) 6≡ 0 (mod p) let Vp (x2 + bx + xc ) be the residue-counts of x2 + bx + xc modulo p as x runs over 1, 2, . . . , p − 1. In this paper, we reveal the connection between Vp (x2 + bx + xc ) and the number of points on certain elliptic curve over the field Fp .
