## Contribution
We describe a cryptanalysis of a public-key encryption scheme based on the polynomial reconstruction problem, published at Eurocrypt 2003 by Augot and Finiasz. Given the public-key and a ciphertext, we recover the corresponding plaintext in polynomial time.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our technique is a variant of the Berlekamp-Welsh algorithm.
- We also describe a cryptanalysis of the reparation published by the authors on the IACR eprint archive, using a variant of the previous attack.
- Both attacks are practical as given the public-key and a ciphertext, one recovers the plaintext in a few minutes on a single PC.
- Key-Words: Cryptanalysis, Augot and Finiasz cryptosystem, Polynomial Reconstruction Problem, Reed-Solomon codes.
