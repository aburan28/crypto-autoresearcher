## Contribution
Let p be an odd prime number, E an elliptic curve defined over a number field. Suppose that E has good reduction at any prime lying above p, and has supersingular reduction at some prime lying above p.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we construct the plus and the minus Selmer groups of E over the cyclotomic Zp -extension in a more general setting than that of B.D.
- Kim, and give a generalization of a result of B.D.
- Kim on the triviality of finite Λ-submodules of the Pontryagin duals of the plus and the minus Selmer groups, where Λ is the Iwasawa algebra of the Galois group of the Zp -extension.
