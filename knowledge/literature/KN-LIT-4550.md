## Contribution
It is often claimed that the security of theoretical quantum key distribution (QKD) is guaranteed by the laws of physics. However, this claim is content-free if the underlying definition of theoretical QKD is not actually compatible with the laws of physics.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This paper observes that (1) the laws of physics pose serious obstacles to the security of QKD and (2) these laws are ignored in all QKD “security proofs”.
