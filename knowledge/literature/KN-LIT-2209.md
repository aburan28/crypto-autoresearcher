## Contribution
We propose the first construction for achieving adaptively secure functional encryption (FE) for poly-sized circuits (without complexity leveraging) from indistinguishability obfuscation (iO). Our reduction has polynomial loss to the underlying primitives.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We develop a “punctured programming” approach to constructing and proving systems where outside of obfuscation we rely only on primitives realizable from pseudo random generators.
- Our work consists of two constructions.
- Our first FE construction is provably secure against any attacker that is limited to making all of its private key queries after it sees the challenge ciphertext.
- (This notion implies selective security.) Our construction makes use of an we introduce called puncturable deterministic encryption (PDE) which may be of independent interest.
