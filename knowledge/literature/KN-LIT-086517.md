## Contribution

Revisits the lambda (kangaroo) method for discrete logarithms known to lie in an interval, including its parallel versions and the heuristic analysis (the "Monopoly" analogy) behind the standard ~2*sqrt(N) average cost. Kangaroo methods are what the secp256k1 "puzzle" solvers run. Parallel-kangaroo analysis and experiments: E. Teske, "Computing discrete logarithms with the parallelized kangaroo method", Discrete Appl. Math. 130:61-82 (2003), doi:10.1016/S0166-218X(02)00590-5.

## Key claims (as reported)

- About 2*sqrt(N) group operations for an interval of width N (heuristic; proved (2+o(1))sqrt(N) by Montenegro-Tetali 2009).

## Relevance to this program

The kangaroo (lambda) method and its parallelisation; every interval-DLP GPU solver (JeanLucPons Kangaroo, RCKangaroo) descends from it.
