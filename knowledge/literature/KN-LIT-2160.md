## Contribution
We introduce and analyze a side-channel attack on a straightforward implementation of the RSA key generation step. The attack exploits power information that allows to determine the number of the trial divisions for each prime candidate.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Practical experiments are conducted, and countermeasures are proposed.
- For realistic parameters the success probability of our attack is in the order of 10–15 %.
