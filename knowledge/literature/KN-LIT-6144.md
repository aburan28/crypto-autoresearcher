## Contribution
We aim to understand, formalize and provably achieve the goals underlying the core key-ratcheting technique of Borisov, Goldberg and Brewer, extensions of which are now used in secure messaging systems. We give syntax and security definitions for ratcheted encryption and key-exchange.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We give a proven-secure protocol for ratcheted key exchange.
- We then show how to generically obtain ratcheted encryption from ratcheted key-exchange and standard encryption.
