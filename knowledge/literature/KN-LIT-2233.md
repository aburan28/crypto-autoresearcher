## Contribution
At Eurocrypt ’02 Cramer and Shoup [7] proposed a general paradigm to construct practical public-key cryptosystems secure against adaptive chosen-ciphertext attacks as well as several concrete examples. Among the others they presented a variant of Paillier’s [21] scheme achieving such a strong security requirement and for which two, independent, decryption mechanisms are allowed.
