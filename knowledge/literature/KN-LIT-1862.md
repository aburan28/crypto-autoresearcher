## Contribution
Let E be a non-isotrivial elliptic curve over a global function field Fq (t) of characteristic coprime to 2 and 3. Under some explicit conditions, we determine the secondary terms for the first moments of prime Selmer groups of cyclic prime twist families of E over Fq (t).
