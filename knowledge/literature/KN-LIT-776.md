## Contribution
In this note we prove that for every integer d ≥ 1, there exists an explicit constant Bd such that the following holds. Let K be a number field of degree d, let q > max{d − 1, 5} be any rational prime that is totally inert in K and E any elliptic curve defined over K such that E has potentially multiplicative reduction at the prime q above q.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Then for every rational prime p > Bd , E has an irreducible mod p Galois representation.
- This result has Diophantine applications within the “modular method”.
- We present one such application in the form of an Asymptotic version of Fermat’s Last Theorem that has not been covered in the existing literature.
