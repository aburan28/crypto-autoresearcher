## Contribution
In this paper we present exchange-equivalence attacks which is a new cryptanalytic attack technique suitable for SPN-like block cipher designs. Our new technique results in the first secret-key chosen plaintext distinguisher for 6-round AES.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The complexity of the distinguisher is about 288.2 in terms of data, memory and computational complexity.
- The distinguishing attack for AES reduced to six rounds is a straightforward extension of an exchange attack for 5-round AES that requires 230 in terms of chosen plaintexts and computation.
- This is also a new record for AES reduced to five rounds.
- The main result of this paper is that AES up to at least six rounds is biased when restricted to exchangeinvariant sets of plaintexts.
