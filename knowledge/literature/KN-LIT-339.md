## Contribution
We provide formulas for traces of pth Hecke operators in level 1 in terms of values of finite field 2 F1 -hypergeometric functions, extending previous work of the author to all odd primes p, instead of only those p ≡ 1 (mod 12). We first give a general level 1 trace formula in terms of the trace of Frobenius on a family of elliptic curves, and then we draw on recent work of Lennon to produce level 1 trace formulas in terms of hypergeometric functions for all primes p > 3.
