## Contribution
This paper provides the first efficient, standard-model, fullysecure schemes for some related and challenging forms of public-key encryption (PKE), namely deterministic and hedged PKE. These forms of PKE defend against subversion of random number generators, an end given new urgency by recent revelations on the nature and extent of such subversion.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We resolve the (recognized) technical challenges in reaching these goals via a new paradigm that combines UCEs (universal computational extractors) with LTDFs (lossy trapdoor functions).
- Crucially, we rely only on a weak form of UCE, namely security for statistically (rather than computationally) unpredictable sources.
- We then define and achieve unique-ciphertext PKE as a way to defend against implementation subversion via algorithm-substitution attacks.
