## Contribution
We propose a multi-party computation (MPC) protocol over Z2k secure against actively corrupted majority from somewhat homomorphic encryption. The main technical contributions are: (i) a new efficient packing method for Z2k -messages in lattice-based somewhat homomorphic encryption schemes, (ii) a simpler reshare protocol for leveldependent packings, (iii) a more efficient zero-knowledge proof of plaintext knowledge on cyclotomic rings Z[X]/ΦM (X) with M being a prime.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Integrating them, our protocol shows from 2.2x upto 4.8x improvements in amortized communication costs compared to the previous best results.
- Our techniques not only improve the efficiency of MPC over Z2k considerably, but also provide a toolkit that can be leveraged when designing other cryptographic primitives over Z2k .
