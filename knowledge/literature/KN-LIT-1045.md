## Contribution
Consider the family of elliptic curves En : y 2 = x3 + n2 , where n varies over positive cubefree integers. There is a rational 3-isogeny φ from En to Ên : y 2 = x3 − 27n2 and a dual isogeny φ̂ : Ên → En .

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that for almost all n, the rank of Selφ (En ) is 0, and the rank of Selφ̂ (Ên ) is determined by the number of prime factors of n that are congruent to 2 mod 3 and the congruence class of n mod 9.
