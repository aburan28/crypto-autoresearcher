## Contribution
Proves, under the point-decomposition index-calculus framework, subexponential
(and in regimes polynomial-ish) complexity statements for ECDLP over families
of finite fields GF(q^n) as n grows in a controlled relation to q.

## Key claims (as reported)
- For suitable families of extension fields, ECDLP can be solved in
  subexponential expected time — a genuine complexity separation from generic
  square-root methods, in the extension-field regime.
- Results are asymptotic over families; they do not target fixed prime fields.

## Relevance to this program
Marks the boundary of what is *proven* for index calculus. Reinforces that the
program's prime-field questions are open precisely where Diem/Gaudry results
do not reach. Guards against over-claiming: a toy prime-field measurement is
not evidence about these asymptotic families.
