## Contribution
We develop techniques for constructing trapdoor functions (TDFs) with short image size and advanced security properties. Our approach builds on the recent framework of Garg and Hajiabadi [CRYPTO 2018].

## Key claims (as reported)
- As applications of our techniques, we obtain – The first construction of deterministic-encryption schemes for blocksource inputs (both for the CPA and CCA cases) based on the Computational Diffie-Hellman (CDH) assumption.
- Moreover, by applying our efficiency-enhancing techniques, we obtain CDH-based schemes with ciphertext size linear in plaintext size. – The first construction of lossy TDFs based on the Decisional DiffieHellman (DDH) assumption with image size linear in input size, while retaining the lossiness rate of [Peikert-Waters STOC 2008].
- Prior to our work, all constructions of deterministic encryption based even on the stronger DDH assumption incurred a quadratic gap between the ciphertext and plaintext sizes.
- Moreover, all DDH-based constructions of lossy TDFs had image size quadratic in the input size.
