## Contribution
A simple heuristic approach to the conjugacy problem in braid groups is described. Although it does not provide a general solution to the latter problem, it demonstrates that various proposed key parameters for braid group based cryptographic primitives do not offer acceptable cryptographic security.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We give experimental evidence that it is often feasible to reveal the secret data by means of a normal PC within a few minutes.
