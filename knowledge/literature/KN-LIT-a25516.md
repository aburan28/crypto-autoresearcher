## Reported result

The authors estimate class-group-action T-gate complexity for CSIDH-512 decreasing from 2^52.6 to 2^51.7, and, for a specified memory budget and four-way permutation model, find collimation arity four best among evaluated choices. With Peikert-style hidden-shift algorithms they report at least 85% lower T-gate count for solving CSIDH-512.

## Scope and evidence boundary

The percentages and exponents refer to quantum T-gate resource estimates under a chosen algorithm and memory model. They do not describe measured quantum hardware, or a classical attack on SQIsign.

## Research follow-up

Compare time-depth, number of logical qubits and classical memory, not T gates alone; retain the target CSIDH parameterization.
