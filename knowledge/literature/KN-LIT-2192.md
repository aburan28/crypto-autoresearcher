## Contribution
We present a practical cryptanalysis of WalnutDSA, a digital signature algorithm trademarked by SecureRF. WalnutDSA uses techniques from permutation groups, matrix groups and braid groups, and is designed to provide post-quantum security in lightweight IoT device contexts.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The attack given in this paper bypasses the E-MultiplicationTM and cloaked conjugacy search problems at the heart of the algorithm and forges signatures for arbitrary messages in approximately two minutes.
- We also discuss potential countermeasures to the attack.
