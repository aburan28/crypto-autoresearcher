## Contribution
In 2017, Ward Beullens et al. submitted Lifted Unbalanced Oil and Vinegar (LUOV)[4], a signature scheme based on the famous multivariate public key cryptosystem (MPKC) called Unbalanced Oil and Vinegar (UOV), to NIST for the competition for post-quantum public key scheme standardization. The defining feature of LUOV is that, though the public key P works in the extension field of degree r of F2 , the coefficients of P come from F2 .

## Key claims (as reported)
- This is done to significantly reduce the size of P .
- The LUOV scheme is now in the second round of the NIST PQC standardization process.
- In this paper we introduce a new attack on LUOV.
- It exploits the "lifted" structure of LUOV to reduce direct attacks on it to those over a subfield.
