## Contribution
Constructs Lubin–Tate formal groups over the ring of integers of a local
field and uses their torsion to give an explicit description of the totally
ramified abelian extensions (explicit local class field theory). Isolates
the local, formal-group half of classical complex multiplication.

## Key claims (as reported)
- For a uniformizer π and residue cardinality q, power series f ≡ πT
  (mod deg 2) and f ≡ T^q (mod π) determine a formal A-module whose
  torsion generates the ramified abelian extensions.
- Endomorphisms [a]_f of the formal group realize the local Artin map on
  units.
- Later deformation theory of Lubin–Tate formal groups is the source of
  Lubin–Tate moduli spaces used in p-adic Langlands and chromatic homotopy;
  that deformation layer is not in this short note.

## Relevance to this program
Canonical reference for formal-group CM over local fields. Distinguishes
proposals that invoke “formal complex multiplication” or Lubin–Tate torsion
from (i) global CM of elliptic curves (Deuring / KN-LIT-075) and (ii) the
anomalous additive transfer (KN-TECH-033), which uses the *elliptic* formal
group logarithm rather than Lubin–Tate local CFT. Forecloses treating
Lubin–Tate as a new ECDLP mechanism without a stated reduction.
