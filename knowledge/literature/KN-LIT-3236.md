## Contribution
We describe a cryptanalysis of the GGH15 multilinear maps. Our attack breaks the multipartite key-agreement protocol in polynomial time by generating an equivalent user private key; it also applies to GGH15 with safeguards.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also describe attacks against variants of the GGH13 multilinear maps proposed by Halevi (ePrint 2015/866) aiming at supporting graph-induced constraints, as in GGH15.
