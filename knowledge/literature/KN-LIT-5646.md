## Contribution
We present a new, simple candidate broadcast encryption scheme for N users with parameter size poly(log N ). We prove security of our scheme under a non-standard variant of the LWE assumption where the distinguisher additionally receives short Gaussian pre-images while avoiding zeroizing attacks.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This yields the first candidate optimal broadcast encryption that is plausibly postquantum secure, and enjoys a security reduction to a simple assumption.
- As a secondary contribution, we present a candidate ciphertext-policy attribute-based encryption (CP-ABE) scheme for circuits of a-priori bounded polynomial depth where the parameter size is independent of the circuit size, and prove security under an additional non-standard assumption.
