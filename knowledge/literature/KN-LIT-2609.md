## Contribution
We look at iterated power generators si = sei−1 mod N for a random seed s0 ∈ ZN that in each iteration output a certain amount of bits. We show that heuristically an output of (1 − 1e ) log N most significant bits per iteration allows for efficient recovery of the whole sequence.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This means in particular that the Blum-Blum-Shub generator should be used with an output of less than half of the bits per iteration and the RSA generator with e = 3 with less than a 13 -fraction of the bits.
- Our method is lattice-based and introduces a new technique, which combines the benefits of two techniques, namely the method of linearization and the method of Coppersmith for finding small roots of polynomial equations.
- We call this new technique unravelled linearization.
