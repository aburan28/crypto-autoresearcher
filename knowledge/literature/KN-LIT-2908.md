## Contribution
The number field sieve takes time L1.901···+o(1) on a generalpurpose computer with L0.950···+o(1) bits of memory; here L is a particular subexponential function of the input size. It takes the same time on a parallel trial-division machine, such as Cracker or TWINKLE, of size L0.950···+o(1) .
