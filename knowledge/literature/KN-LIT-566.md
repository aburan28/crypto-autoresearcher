## Contribution
If E is an elliptic curve with a point of order two, then work of Klagsbrun and Lemke Oliver shows that the distribution of dimF2 Selφ (E d /Q) − dimF2 Selφ̂ (E ′d /Q) within the quadratic twist family tends to the discrete normal distribution N (0, 12 log log X) as X → ∞. We consider the distribution of dimF2 Selφ (E d /Q) within such a quadratic twist family when dimF2 Selφ (E d /Q) − dimF2 Selφ̂ (E ′d /Q) has a fixed value u.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Specifically, we show that for every r, the limiting probability that dimF2 Selφ (E d /Q) = r is given by an explicit constant αr,u .
- The constants αr,u are closely related to the u-probabilities introduced in Cohen and Lenstra’s work on the distribution of class groups, and thus provide a connection between the distribution of Selmer groups of elliptic curves and random abelian groups.
- Our analysis of this problem has two steps.
- The first step uses algebraic and combinatorial methods to directly relate the ranks of the Selmer groups in question to the dimensions of the kernels of random F2 -matrices.
