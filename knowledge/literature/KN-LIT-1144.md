## Contribution
Let E be a non CM elliptic curve defined over Q. There is an isogeny-torsion graph associated to E and there is also a Galois representation ρ E ,l ∞ : Gal(Q/Q) → GL2 (Zl ) associated to E for every prime l.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this article, we explore the relation between these two objects when l = 3.
- More precisely, we give a classification of 3-adic Galois images associated to vertices of the isogeny-torsion graph of E .
