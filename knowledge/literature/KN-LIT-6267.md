## Contribution
In a recent paper Mohassel and Franklin study the efficiency of secure two-party computation in the presence of malicious behavior. Their aim is to make classical solutions to this problem, such as zero-knowledge compilation, more efficient.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The authors provide several schemes which are the most efficient to date.
- We propose a modification to their main scheme using expanders.
- Our modification asymptotically improves at least one measure of efficiency of all known schemes.
- We also point out an error, and improve the analysis of one of their schemes.
