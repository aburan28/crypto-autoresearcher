## Contribution
The BBCRS scheme is a variant of the McEliece public-key encryption scheme where the hiding phase is performed by taking the inverse of a matrix which is of the form T + R where T is a sparse matrix with average row/column weight equal to a very small quantity m, usually m < 2, and R is a matrix of small rank z ⩾ 1. The rationale of this new transformation is the reintroduction of families of codes, like generalized Reed-Solomon codes, that are famously known for representing insecure choices.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We present a key-recovery attack when z = 1 and m is chosen between 1 and 1 + R + O( √1n ) where R denotes the code rate.
- This attack has complexity O(n6 ) and breaks all the parameters suggested in the literature.
