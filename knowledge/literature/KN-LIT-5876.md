## Contribution
We construct a lattice-based predicate encryption scheme for multidimensional range and multi-dimensional subset queries. Our scheme is selectively secure and weakly attribute-hiding, and its security is based on the standard learning with errors (LWE) assumption.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Multi-dimensional range and subset queries capture many interesting applications pertaining to searching on encrypted data.
- To the best of our knowledge, these are the first lattice-based predicate encryption schemes for functionalities beyond IBE and inner product.
