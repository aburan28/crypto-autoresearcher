## Contribution
The aim of this paper is to study certain family of elliptic curves {XH }H defined over a number field F arising from hyperplane sections of some cubic surface X/F associated to a cyclic cubic extension K/F . We show that each XH admits a b d 3-isogeny φ over F and the dual Selmer group S (φ) (X H /F ) is bounded by a kind of unit/class groups attached to K/F .

## Key claims (as reported)
- This is proven via certain rational function on the elliptic curve XH with nice property.
- We also prove that the Shafarevich-Tate group d b X(X H /Q)[φ] coincides with a class group of K as a special case.
