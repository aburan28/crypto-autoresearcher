## Contribution
We describe an algorithm for computing, for all primes p ≤ X, the trace of Frobenius at p of a hypergeometric motive over Q in time quasilinear in X. This involves computing the trace modulo pe for suitable e; as in our previous work treating the case e = 1, we combine the Beukers–Cohen– Mellit trace formula with average polynomial time techniques of Harvey and Harvey–Sutherland.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The key new ingredient for e > 1 is an expanded version of Harvey’s “generic prime” construction, making it possible to incorporate certain p-adic transcendental functions into the computation; one of these is the p-adic Gamma function, whose average polynomial time computation is an intermediate step which may be of independent interest.
- We also provide an implementation in Sage and discuss the remaining computational issues around tabulating hypergeometric L-series.
