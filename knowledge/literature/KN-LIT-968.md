## Contribution
In [DKY2], it was conjectured that there is a uniform bound B, depending only on the degree d, so that any pair of holomorphic maps f, g : P1 → P1 with degree d will either share all of their preperiodic points or have at most B in common. Here we show that this uniform bound holds for a Zariski open and dense set in the space of all pairs, Ratd × Ratd , for each degree d ≥ 2.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The proof involves a combination of arithmetic intersection theory and complex-dynamical results, especially as developed recently by Gauthier-Vigny [GV], Yuan-Zhang [YZ1], and Mavraki-Schmidt [MS].
- In addition, we present alternate proofs of the main results of DeMarco-Krieger-Ye in [DKY1, DKY2] and of Poineau in [Po].
- In fact we prove a generalization of a conjecture of Bogomolov–Fu–Tschinkel [BFT] in a mixed setting of dynamical systems and elliptic curves.
