## Contribution
The group signature scheme [1], ACJT for short, is popular. In this paper we show that it is not secure.

## Key claims (as reported)
- It does not satisfy exculpability.
- The group manager can sign on behalf of any group member.
- The drawback found in the scheme shows that some inductions are not sound, though they are prevalent in some so-called security proofs.
