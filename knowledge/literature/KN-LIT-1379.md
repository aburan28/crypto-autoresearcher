## Contribution
We recall and define various kinds of supersingular l-isogeny graphs and precise graph isomorphism with a corresponding quaternion l-ideal graph. In particular, we introduce the notion of double-orientations on supersingular elliptic curves and study the structure of double-oriented supersingular l-isogeny graphs.
