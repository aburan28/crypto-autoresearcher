## Contribution

Uses an efficiently computable endomorphism to shrink the factor base for elliptic curves and genus-2 Jacobians over F_{q^n}. Operations on the factor base cost more, but experiments show a net gain over generic attacks for certain families.

## Key claims (as reported)

- Net gain vs generic attacks for certain families (numbers in paper).

## Relevance to this program

Index calculus on hyperelliptic Jacobians with effective endomorphisms; endomorphism-accelerated decomposition (cf. GGMP 2020 for subfield curves).
