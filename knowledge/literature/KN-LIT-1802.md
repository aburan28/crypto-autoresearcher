## Contribution
In this paper we consider the sequences (Ψn (P))n≥0 , (Φn (P))n≥0 and (Ω n (P))n≥0 of values of the translated division polynomials of an elliptic curve E/K evaluated at a point P ∈ E(K)2 . We prove that these sequences are purely periodic when K is a finite field.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Then we use the periodicity properties of these sequences to prove that certain subsequences of these sequences are Zp -Cauchy.
- Finally, we use this result to prove analogous results for Somos 4 and Somos 5 sequences.
