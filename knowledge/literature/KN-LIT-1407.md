## Contribution
Elkies ([Elk87, Elk89]) proved the infinitude of supersingular primes for elliptic curves over real number fields. We generalize Elkies’ result to some abelian fourfolds in Mumford’s families ([Mum69, §4]), and more generally, to certain families of Kuga-Satake abelian varieties.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The proof relies on the study of local deformation spaces at closed points of the integral model of a Hodge-type Shimura variety, based on the work of Madapusi [MP16], and on the analysis of real points of a Shimura curve, based on the work of Shimura [Shi75].
