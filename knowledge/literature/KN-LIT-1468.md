## Contribution
For certain families of elliptic curves admitting a rational isogeny of prime degree l, we establish a central limit theorem for the Tamagawa ratio and derive bounds on its average value. By using the Tamagawa ratio to bound the size of the l-isogeny Selmer group from below, we show that for l ∈ {2, 3, 5, 7, 13}, there exist elliptic curves with arbitrarily large l-Selmer groups.
