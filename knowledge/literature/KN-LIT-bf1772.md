## Contribution

Complete classification, in odd characteristic, of elliptic curves C_0/k_d with (2,...,2)-coverings C/k of P^1 subject to GHS, for prime extension degrees d = 2, 3, 5, 7. Diem showed GHS fails for prime d >= 11, so this list is complete. Checking whether a given degree-5 curve such as ecGFp5 lies in one of the d = 5 classes is a concrete, cheap test.

## Key claims (as reported)

- Complete list for d in {2,3,5,7}; 2013/487 found 17 new classes beyond 10 known ones (genus 1-3, isogeny condition).

## Relevance to this program

Classification of odd-characteristic curves vulnerable to GHS, including extension degree 5: the quick check that ecGFp5/EcMasFp5 are not in a weak class (GOAL-GFPN-380702).
