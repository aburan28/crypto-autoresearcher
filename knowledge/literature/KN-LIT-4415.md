## Contribution
In this paper we present new and more accurate estimates of the biases of the linear approximation of the FSM of the stream cipher SNOW 2.0. Based on improved bias estimates we also find a new linear distinguisher with bias 2−86.9 that is significantly stronger than the previously found ones by Watanabe et al.
