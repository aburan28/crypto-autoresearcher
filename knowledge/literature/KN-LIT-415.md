## Contribution
In this note we show that any supersingular abelian variety is isogenous to a superspecial abelian variety without increasing field extensions. The proof uses minimal isogenies and the Galois descent.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We then construct a superspecial abelian variety which not directly defined over a finite field.
- This answers negatively to a question of the author [J.
- Alg., 2013] concerning of endomorphism algebras occurring in Shimura curves.
- Endomorphism algebras of supersingular elliptic curves over an arbitrary field are also investigated.
