## Contribution
In this article, we show that the quartic Diophantine equations x4 ± pqy 4 = ±z 2 and x4 ± pqy 4 = ±iz 2 have only trivial solutions for some primes p and q satisfying conditions p = −1. Here we have found the torsion of the two p ≡ 3 (mod 8), q ≡ 1 (mod 8) and q families of elliptic curves to find the solutions of given Diophantine equations.
