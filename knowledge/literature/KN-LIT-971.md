## Contribution
A prime number p is said to be irregular if it divides the class number of the p-th cyclotomic field Q(ζp ) = Q(Gm [p]). In this paper, we study its elliptic analogue for the division fields of an elliptic curve.

## Key claims (as reported)
- More precisely, for a prime number p ≥ 5 and a positive integer n, we study the p-divisibility of the class number of the pn -division field Q(E[pn ]) of an elliptic curve E of the form y 2 = x3 − (s4 + t2 )x.
- In particular, we construct a certain infinite subfamily consisting of curves with novel properties that they are of Mordell-Weil rank 1 and the class numbers of their pn -division fields are divisible by p2n .
- Moreover, we can prove that these division fields are not isomorphic to each other.
- In our construction, we use recent results obtained by the first author.
