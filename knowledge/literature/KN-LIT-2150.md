## Contribution
In this paper we present a new lattice construction for a lattice based partial key exposure attack for the RSA cryptography. We consider the situation that the RSA secret key d is small and a sufficient amount of the LSBs (least significant bits) of d are known by the attacker.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that our lattice construction is theoretically more efficient than known attacks proposed in [2, 7].
