## Contribution
A formula is given for the dimension of the Selmer group of the rational three-isogeny of elliptic curves of the form y 2 = x3 +a(x−b)2 . The formula is in terms √ of the three-ranks of the quadratic number √ fields Q( a) and Q( −3a) and various aspects of the arithmetic of these number fields.
