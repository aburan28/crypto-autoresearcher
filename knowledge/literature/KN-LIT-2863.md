## Contribution
We propose an algorithm that, given an arbitrary N of un1 known factorization and prime e ≥ N 4 +ε , certifies whether the RSA e function RSAN,e (x) := x mod N defines a permutation over Z∗N or not. The algorithm uses Coppersmith’s method to find small solutions of polynomial equations and runs in time O(ε−8 log2 N ).

## Key claims (as reported)
