## Contribution
Let K be a number field and r an integer. Given an elliptic curve E, defined over K, we consider the problem of counting the number of degree two prime ideals of K with trace of Frobenius equal to r.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Under certain restrictions on K, we show that “on average” the number of such prime ideals with norm less than or equal to x satisfies an asymptotic identity that is in accordance with standard heuristics.
- This work is related to the classical Lang-Trotter conjecture and extends the work of several authors.
