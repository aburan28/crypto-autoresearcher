## Contribution
Let K be a number field. We consider a local-global principle for elliptic curves E/K that admit (or do not admit) a rational isogeny of prime degree l.

## Key claims (as reported)
- For suitable K (including K = Q), we prove that this principle holds for all l ≡ 1 mod 4, and for l < 7, but find a counterexample when l = 7 for an elliptic curve with j-invariant 2268945/128.
- For K = Q we show that, up to isomorphism, this is the only counterexample.
