## Contribution
The ground-breaking results of Wang et al. have attracted a lot of attention to the collision resistance of hash functions. In their articles, Wang et al. give input differences, differential paths and the corresponding conditions that allow to find collisions with a high probability.

## Key claims (as reported)
- However, Wang et al. do not explain how these paths were found.
- The common assumption is that they were found by hand with a great deal of intuition.
- In this article, we present an algorithm that allows to find paths in an automated way.
- Our algorithm is successful for MD4.
