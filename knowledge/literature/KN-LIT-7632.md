## Contribution

Survey / summer-school notes on the mathematics of isogeny-based cryptography:
elliptic curves, isogenies, ordinary and supersingular graphs, and the main
cryptographic constructions. Mentions Teske among early isogeny-based ideas
(alongside Couveignes and Rostovtsev–Stolbunov).

## Key claims (from fetched arXiv PDF)

- Accessible path through ECC → pairings → isogenies for post-quantum and
  classical isogeny applications.
- Covers isogeny graphs and cryptographic systems built on them.
- Useful modern exposition of weak isogeny-class / path-to-weak-curve themes
  (meet-in-the-middle path search threat model for trapdoor secrecy).

## Relevance to GOAL-ECTD-001

Background reference for ordinary/supersingular isogeny geometry and for the
public MITM path-recovery threat a trapdoor generator must defeat.
