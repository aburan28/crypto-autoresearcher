## Contribution
The (2, 3, 7) triangle group is known to be associated with a quaternion algebra A/K ramified at two of the three real places of K = Q(cos 2π/7) and unramified at all other places of K. This triangle group and its congruence subgroups thus give rise to various Shimura curves and maps between them.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We study the genus-1 curves X0 (3), X1 (3) associated with the congruence subgroups Γ0 (3), Γ1 (3).
- Since the rational prime 3 is inert in K, the covering X0 (3)/X (1) has degree 28, and its Galois closure X (3)/X (1) has geometric Galois group PSL2 (F27 ).
- Since X (1) is rational, the covering X0 (3)/X (1) amounts to a rational map of degree 28.
- We compute this rational map explicitly.
