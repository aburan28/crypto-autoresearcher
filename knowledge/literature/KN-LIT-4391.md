## Contribution
Anonymous attribute-based credentials (ABCs) are a powerful tool allowing users to authenticate while maintaining privacy. When instantiated from structure-preserving signatures on equivalence classes (SPS-EQ) we obtain a controlled form of malleability, and hence increased functionality and privacy for the user.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Existing constructions consider equivalence classes on the message space, allowing the joint randomization of credentials and the corresponding signatures on them.
- In this work, we additionally consider equivalence classes on the signingkey space.
- In this regard, we obtain a signer-hiding notion, where the issuing organization is not revealed when a user shows a credential.
- To achieve this, we instantiate the ABC framework of Fuchsbauer, Hanser, and Slamanig (FHS, Journal of Cryptology ’19) with a recent SPS-EQ scheme (ASIACRYPT ’19) modified to support a fully adaptive NIZK from the framework of Couteau and Hartmann (CRYPTO ’20).
