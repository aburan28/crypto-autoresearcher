## Contribution
Thanks to a new upper bound, we study more precisely the nonlinearities of Maiorana-McFarland’s resilient functions. We characterize those functions with optimum nonlinearities and we give examples of functions with high nonlinearities.

## Key claims (as reported)
- But these functions have a peculiarity which makes them potentially cryptographically weak.
- We study a natural super-class of Maiorana-McFarland’s class whose elements do not have the same drawback and we give examples of such functions achieving high nonlinearities.
