## Contribution
The ring signature allows a signer to leak secrets anonymously, without the risk of identity escrow. At the same time, the ring signature provides great flexibility: No group manager, no special setup, and the dynamics of group choice.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The ring signature is, however, vulnerable to malicious or irresponsible signers in some applications, because of its anonymity.
- In this paper, we propose a traceable ring signature scheme.
- A traceable ring scheme is a ring signature except that it can restrict “excessive” anonymity.
- The traceable ring signature has a tag that consists of a list of ring members and an issue that refers to, for instance, a social affair or an election.
