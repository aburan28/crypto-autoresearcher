## Contribution
The paper shows that some of elliptic curves over finite fields of characteristic three of composite degree are attacked by a more effective algorithm than Pollard’s ρ method. For such an elliptic curve E, we construct a Cab curve D on its Weil restriction in order to reduce the discrete logarithm problem on E to that on D.

## Key claims (as reported)
- And we show that the genus of D is small enough so that D is attacked by a modified form of Gaudry’s variant for a suitable E.
- We also see such a weak elliptic curve is easily constructed.
