## Contribution
An optimised quantum implementation of ISD targeting **scalable quantum
resources** — companion to the same authors' hybrid-decoding paper
([[KN-LIT-4388b3]]), on the implementation rather than the trade-off side.

## Key claims (as reported)
- An optimised quantum implementation of ISD.
- Framed against scalable quantum resources, i.e. asking how the cost behaves as the device grows rather than assuming an idealised machine.
