## Contribution
In Crypto 97, a public key cryptosystem based on the closest vector problem was suggested by Goldreich, Goldwasser and Halevi [4]. In this paper, we propose a public key cryptosystem applying representations of polynomials to the GGH encryption scheme.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Its key size is much smaller than the GGH system so that it is a quite practical and efficient lattice based cryptosystem.
