## Contribution
In this work, we introduce and construct D-restricted Functional Encryption (FE) for any constant D ≥ 3, based only on the SXDH assumption over bilinear groups. This generalizes the notion of 3-restricted FE recently introduced and constructed by Ananth et al.

## Key claims (as reported)
- (ePrint 2018) in the generic bilinear group model.
- A D = (d + 2)-restricted FE scheme is a secret key FE scheme that allows an encryptor to efficiently encrypt a message of the form M = (x, y, z).
- Here, x ∈ Fd×n and y, z ∈ Fn p p .
- Function keys can be issued for a function f = ΣI=(i1 ,..,id ,j,k) cI · x[1, i1 ] · · · x[d, id ] · y[j] · z[k] where the coefficients cI ∈ Fp .
