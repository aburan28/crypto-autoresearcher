## Contribution
The goal of steganography is to pass secret messages by disguising them as innocent-looking covertexts. Real world stegosystems are often broken because they make invalid assumptions about the system’s ability to sample covertexts.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We examine whether it is possible to weaken this assumption.
- By modeling the covertext distribution as a stateful Markov process, we create a sliding scale between real world and provably secure stegosystems.
- We also show that insufficient knowledge of past states can have catastrophic results.
