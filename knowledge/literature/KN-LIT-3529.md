## Contribution
We construct efficient authentication protocols and messageauthentication codes (MACs) whose security can be reduced to the learning parity with noise (LPN) problem. Despite a large body of work – starting with the HB protocol of Hopper and Blum in 2001 – until now it was not even known how to construct an efficient authentication protocol from LPN which is secure against man-in-the-middle (MIM) attacks.
