## Contribution
Key escrow is inherent in identity-based encryption (IBE). A curious key generation center (KGC) can simply generate the user’s private key to decrypt a ciphertext.

## Key claims (as reported)
- However, can a KGC still decrypt if it does not know the intended recipient of the ciphertext?
- We answer by formalizing KGC anonymous ciphertext indistinguishability (ACI − KGC).
- We find that all existing pairing-based IBE schemes without random oracles, whether receipt-anonymous or not, do not achieve KGC onewayness, a weaker notion of ACI − KGC.
- In view of this, we first show how to equip an IBE scheme by Gentry with ACI − KGC.
