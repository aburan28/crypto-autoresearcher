## Contribution
Let K be an imaginary quadratic field, and let OK,f be an order in K of conductor f ≥ 1. Let E be an elliptic curve with CM by OK,f , such that E is defined by a model over Q(jK,f ), where jK,f = j(E).

## Key claims (as reported)
- It has been shown by the author and Lozano-Robledo that Gal(Q(jK,f , E[N ])/Q(jK,f )) is only abelian for N = 2, 3, and 4.
- Let p be a prime and let n ≥ 1 be an integer.
- In this article, we bound the commutator subgroups of Gal(Q(E[pn ])/Q) and classify the maximal abelian extensions contained in Q(E[pn ])/Q.
