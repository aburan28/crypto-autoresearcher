## Contribution
We prove that the Hessian transformation of elliptic curves, both as an action on j-invariants and on the Hesse pencil, is a Lattès map, namely it ascends to a degree-3 endomorphism ψ of a prescribed elliptic curve E. This result provides a powerful tool to investigate the dynamics of the Hessian transformation, which inherits its symmetries from ψ.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In particular, we show that, over arbitrary fields of characteristic different from 2 and 3, the Hessian functional graphs can be completely determined in terms of the action of ψ on the twists of E.
- When the underlying field is finite, we specialize our results to provide a complete classification of Hessian functional graphs.
- In such a case, we also present a practical way to compute iterated Hessians.
