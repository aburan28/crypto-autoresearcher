## Contribution
Let {nP + Q}n≥0 be a sequence of points on an elliptic curve defined over a number field K. In this paper, we study the denominators of the x-coordinates of this sequence.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove that, if Q is a torsion point of prime order, then for n large enough there always exists a primitive divisor.
- Later on, we show the link between the study of the primitive divisors and the Lang-Trotter conjecture.
