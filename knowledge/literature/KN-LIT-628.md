## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve defined over a number field K with supersingular reduction at all primes of K above p. If K∞ /K is a Zp -extension such that E(K∞ )[p∞ ] is finite and H 2 (GS (K∞ ), E[p∞ ]) = 0, then we prove that the Λ-torsion subgroup of the Pontryagin dual of Selp∞ (E/K∞ ) is pseudoisomorphic to the Pontryagin dual of the fine Selmer group of E over K∞ .
