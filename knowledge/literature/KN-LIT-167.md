## Contribution
Let E/k(T ) be an elliptic curve defined over a rational function field of characteristic zero. Fix a Weierstrass equation 2 for E.

## Key claims (as reported)
- For points R ∈ E(k(T )), write xR = AR /DR with relatively prime polynomials AR (T ), DR (T ) ∈ k[T ].
- The sequence {DnR }n≥1 is called the elliptic divisibility sequence of R.
- Let P, Q ∈ E(k(T )) be independent points.
- We conjecture that  deg gcd(DnP , DmQ ) is bounded for m, n ≥ 1, and that gcd(DnP , DnQ ) = gcd(DP , DQ ) for infinitely many n ≥ 1.
