## Contribution
In this paper, we obtain bounds for the Mordell-Weil ranks over cyclotomic extensions of a wide range of abelian varieties defined over a number field F whose primes above p are totally ramified over F/Q. We assume that the abelian varieties may have good non-ordinary reduction at those primes.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our work is a generalization of [6], in which the second author generalized Perrin-Riou’s Iwasawa theory for elliptic curves over Q with supersingular reduction ([10]) to elliptic curves defined over the above-mentioned number field F .
- On top of non-ordinary reduction and the ramification of the field F , we deal with the additional difficulty that the dimensions of the abelian varieties can be any number bigger than 1 which causes a variety of issues.
- As a result, we obtain bounds for the ranks over cyclotomic extensions Q(μpmax(M,N )+n ) of the Jacobian varieties of ramified hyperelliptic curves y 2p among others.
