## Contribution
We present a novel construction that applies the Ciphertext-Policy AttributeBased Encryption paradigm in an original symmetric framework, where also the encryptor needs to have enough attributes to be able to produce a ciphertext for a given policy. The scheme is built from minimal assumptions on collision-resistant hash functions and pseudorandom functions, exploiting the properties of linear secret sharing and polynomial interpolation.
