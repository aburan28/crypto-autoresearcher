## Contribution
We initiate a study of on-line ciphers. These are ciphers that can take input plaintexts of large and varying lengths and will output the ith block of the ciphertext after having processed only the first i blocks of the plaintext.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Such ciphers permit length-preserving encryption of a data stream with only a single pass through the data.
- We provide security definitions for this primitive and study its basic properties.
- We then provide attacks on some possible candidates, including CBC with fixed IV.
- Finally we provide a construction called HCBC which is based on a given block cipher E and a family of AXU functions.
