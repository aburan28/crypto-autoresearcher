## Contribution
Vertical Sato-Tate states that the Frobenius trace of a randomly chosen elliptic curve over Fp tends to a semicircular distribution as p → ∞. We go beyond this statement by considering ′ the number of elliptic curves Nt,p with a given trace t over Fp and characterizing the 2-dimensional ′ distribution of (t, Nt,p ).

## Key claims (as reported)
- In particular, this gives the distribution of the size of isogeny classes of elliptic curves over Fp .
- Furthermore, we show a notion of stronger convergence for vertical Sato-Tate which states that the number of elliptic curves with Frobenius trace in an interval of length pε converges to the expected amount.
- The key step in the proof is to truncate Gekeler’s infinite product formula, which relies crucially on an effective Chebotarev’s density theorem that was recently developed by Pierce, Turnage-Butterbaugh and Wood.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to the isogeny cluster: bears on supersingular/isogeny-based constructions and the isogeny-path problems that compete with and inform ECDLP work.
