## Contribution
We discuss many analogy points with the elliptic curves. more precisely, we study the characteristic polynomial of a Drinfeld module of rank 2 and use it to calculate the number of isogeny classes for such modules. To cite this article: Mohamed-Saadbouh Mohamed-Ahmed , C.
