## Contribution
We investigate commitment schemes whose messages, keys, commitments, and decommitments are elements of bilinear groups, and whose openings are verified by pairing product equations. Such commitments facilitate efficient zero-knowledge proofs of knowledge of a correct opening.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show two lower bounds on such schemes: a commitment cannot be shorter than the message and verifying the opening in a symmetric bilinear group setting requires evaluating at least two independent pairing product equations.
- We also present optimal constructions that match the lower bounds in symmetric and asymmetric bilinear group settings.
