## Contribution
Let Eλ be the Legendre family of elliptic curves with equation Y 2 = X(X − 1)(X − λ). Given a curve C, satisfying a condition on the degrees of some of its coordinates and parametrizing m points P1 , . . . , Pm ∈ Eλ and n points Q1 , . . . , Qn ∈ Eμ and assuming arXiv:2409.01408v3 [math.NT] 23 Oct 2025 that those points are generically linearly independent over the generic endomorphism ring, we prove that there are at most finitely many points c0 on C, such that there exists an isogeny φ : Eμ(c0 ) → Eλ(c0 ) and the m+n points P1 (c0 ), . . . , Pm (c0 ), φ(Q1 (c0 )), . . . , φ(Qn (c0 )) ∈ Eλ(c0 ) are linearly dependent over End(Eλ(c0 ) ).
