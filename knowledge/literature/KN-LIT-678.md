## Contribution
We describe the automorphism groups of finite p-groups arising naturally via Hessian determinantal representations of elliptic curves defined over number fields. Moreover, we derive explicit formulas for the orders of these automorphism groups for elliptic curves of j-invariant 1728 given in Weierstrass form.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We interpret these orders in terms of the numbers of 3-torsion points (or flex points) of the relevant curves over finite fields.
- Our work greatly generalizes and conceptualizes previous examples given by du Sautoy and Vaughan-Lee.
- It explains, in particular, why the orders arising in these examples are polynomial on Frobenius sets and vary with the primes in a nonquasipolynomial manner.
