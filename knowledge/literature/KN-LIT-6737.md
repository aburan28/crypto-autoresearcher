## Contribution
We study the problem of finding solutions to linear equations modulo an unknown divisor p of a known composite integer N . An important application of this problem is factorization of N with given bits of p.

## Key claims (as reported)
- It is well-known that this problem is polynomial-time solvable if at most half of the bits of p are unknown and if the unknown bits are located in one consecutive block.
- We introduce an heuristic algorithm that extends factoring with known bits to an arbitrary number n of blocks.
- Surprisingly, we are able to show that ln(2) ≈ 70% of the bits are sufficient for any n in order to find the factorization.
- The algorithm’s running time is however exponential in the parameter n.
