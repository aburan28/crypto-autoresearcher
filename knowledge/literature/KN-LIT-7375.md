## Contribution
A unique signature scheme has the property that a signature σPK (m) is a (hard-to-compute) function of the public key PK and message m, for all, even adversarially chosen, PK. Unique signatures, introduced by Goldwasser and Ostrovsky, have been shown to be a building block for constructing verifiable random functions.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Another useful property of unique signatures is that they are stateless: the signer does not need to update his secret key after an invocation.
- The only previously known construction of a unique signature in the plain model was based on the RSA assumption.
- The only other previously known provably secure constructions of stateless signatures were based on the Strong RSA assumption.
- Here, we give a construction of a unique signature scheme based on a generalization of the Diffie-Hellman assumption in groups where decisional Diffie-Hellman is easy.
