## Contribution
An abelian variety A/K is heavenly at l if the extension K(A[l∞ ])/K(μl∞) is both pro-l and unramified away from l. It is known that for a fixed quadratic field K, the number of K-isomorphism classes of heavenly elliptic curves is finite, even running over all primes l.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove a complementary result, that for a fixed prime l ≥ 7, there are only finitely many such classes, even running over all quadratic fields.
- This naturally raises the question of whether to expect a finiteness result when both K and l are allowed to vary.
- We demonstrate similarities in the behavior of heavenly elliptic curves and elliptic curves with complex multiplication, in terms of their Frobenius traces modulo l.
- We determine the complete list of heavenly elliptic curves defined over quadratic fields with complex multiplication and with irrational j-invariant (up to isomorphism).

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
