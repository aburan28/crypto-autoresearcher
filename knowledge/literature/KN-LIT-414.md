## Contribution
Let E be a semistable elliptic curve over Q. We prove that if E has nonsplit multiplicative reduction at at least one odd prime or split multiplicative reduction at at least two odd primes, then rankZ E(Q) = 1 and #X(E) < ∞ =⇒ ords=1 L(E, s) = 1.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also prove the corresponding result for the abelian variety associated with a weight two newform f of trivial character.
- These, and other related results, are consequences of our main theorem, which establishes criteria for f and Hf1 (Q, V ), where V is the padic Galois representation associated with f , that ensure that ords=1 L(f, s) = 1.
- The main theorem is proved using the Iwasawa theory of V over an imaginary quadratic field to show that the p-adic logarithm of a suitable Heegner point is non-zero.
