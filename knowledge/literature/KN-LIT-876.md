## Contribution
We investigate the isogeny graphs of supersingular elliptic curves over F p2 equipped with a d-isogeny to their Galois conjugate. These curves are interesting because they p are, in a sense, a generalization of curves defined over F p , and there is an action of the ideal class group of Q( −dp) on the isogeny graphs.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We investigate constructive and destructive aspects of these graphs in isogeny-based cryptography, including generalizations of the CSIDH cryptosystem and the Delfs–Galbraith algorithm.

## Relevance to this program

Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
