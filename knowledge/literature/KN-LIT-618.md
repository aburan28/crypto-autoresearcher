## Contribution
The paper uses Iwasawa theory at the prime p = 2 to prove non-vanishing theorems for the value at s = 1 of the complex L-series of certain quadratic twists of the Gross family of elliptic curves √ with complex multiplication by the field K = Q( −q), where q is any prime ≡ 7 mod 8.
