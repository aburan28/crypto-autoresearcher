## Contribution
This paper presents differential attacks on Simon and Speck, two families of lightweight block ciphers that were presented by the U.S. National Security Agency in June 2013.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We describe attacks on up to slightly more than half the number of rounds.
- While our analysis is only of academic interest, it demonstrates the drawback of the intensive optimizations in Simon and Speck.
