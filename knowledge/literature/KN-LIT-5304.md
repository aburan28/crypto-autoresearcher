## Contribution
We use the invariant theory of binary quartics to give a new formula for the Cassels-Tate pairing on the 2-Selmer group of an elliptic curve. Unlike earlier methods, our formula does not require us to solve any conics.

## Key claims (as reported)
- An important role in our construction is played by a certain K3 surface defined by a (2, 2, 2)-form.
