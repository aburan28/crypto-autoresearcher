## Contribution
In recent years there have been several attempts to build white-box block ciphers whose implementations aim to be incompressible. This includes the weak white-box ASASA construction by Bouillaguet, Biryukov and Khovratovich from Asiacrypt 2014, and the recent space-hard construction by Bogdanov and Isobe from CCS 2015.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this article we propose the first constructions aiming at the same goal while offering provable security guarantees.
- Moreover we propose concrete instantiations of our constructions, which prove to be quite efficient and competitive with prior work.
- Thus provable security comes with a surprisingly low overhead.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
