## Contribution
In this note, we study the resonance variety of rank-two vector bundles over an elliptic curve. Our approach is based on analyzing the flattening stratification of the resonance, as introduced in [AS25].

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also investigate the linear section of the Grassmann variety Gr(2, n) from which the resonance is constructed through the lens of its corresponding flattening stratification.
