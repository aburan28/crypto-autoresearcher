## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve over a field K and l a prime. There exists an elliptic curve E ∗ related to E by an isogeny (rational map that is also a group homomorphisms) of degree l if and only Φl (X, j(E )) = 0, where Φl (X, Y ) is the traditional modular polynomial.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Moreover, the modular polynomial gives the coefficients of E ∗ , together with parameters needed to build the isogeny explicitly.
- Since the traditional modular polynomial has large coefficients, many families with smaller coefficients can be used instead, as described by Elkies, Atkin and others.
- In this work, we concentrate on the computation of the family of modular polynomials introduced by Charlap, Coley and Robbins.
- It has the advantage of giving directly the coefficients of E ∗ as roots of these polynomials.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to the isogeny cluster: bears on supersingular/isogeny-based constructions and the isogeny-path problems that compete with and inform ECDLP work. Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
