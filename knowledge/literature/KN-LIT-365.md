## Contribution
Let p and q be odd prime numbers with q−p = 2, the φ−Selmer groups, Shafarevich-Tate groups ( φ− and 2−part ) and their dual ones as well the Mordell-Weil groups of elliptic curves y 2 = x(x ± p)(x ± q) over imaginary quadratic number fields of class number one are determined explicitly in many cases.
