## Contribution
We describe a security-preserving construction of a random permutation of domain size N from a random function, the construction tolerating adversaries asking all N plaintexts, yet employing just Θ(lg N ) calls, on average, to the one-bit-output random function. The approach is based on card shuffling.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The basic idea is to use the sometimes-recurse transformation: lightly shuffle the deck (with some other shuffle), cut the deck, and then recursively shuffle one of the two halves.
- Our work builds on a recent paper of Ristenpart and Yilek.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
