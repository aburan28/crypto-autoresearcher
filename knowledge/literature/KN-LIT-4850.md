## Contribution
Let X and Y be curves over a finite field. In this article we explore methods to determine whether there is a rational map from Y to X by considering L-functions of certain covers of X and Y and propose a specific family of covers to address the special case of determining when X and Y are isomorphic.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also discuss an application to factoring polynomials over finite fields.
