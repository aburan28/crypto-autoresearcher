## Contribution

Variant of Semaev's original prime-field approach that reduces the required Groebner-basis computations to a single basis computation, outperforming both Semaev's method and PKM16 in their experiments.

## Key claims (as reported)

- One Groebner basis instead of many (experimental comparison in paper).
