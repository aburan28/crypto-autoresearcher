## Contribution
Nowadays it is well known that randomness may fail due to bugs or deliberate randomness subversion. As a result, the security of traditional public-key encryption (PKE) cannot be guaranteed any more.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Currently there are mainly three approaches dealing with the problem of randomness failures: deterministic PKE, hedged PKE, and nonce-based PKE.
- However, these three approaches only apply to different application scenarios respectively.
- Since the situations in practice are dynamic and very complex, it’s almost impossible to predict the situation in which a scheme is deployed, and determine which approach should be used beforehand.
- In this paper, we initiate the study of hedged security for noncebased PKE, which adaptively applies to the situations whenever randomness fails, and achieves the best-possible security.
