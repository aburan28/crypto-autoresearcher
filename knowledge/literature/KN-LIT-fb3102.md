## Contribution
The original **statistical decoding algorithm** for general linear block codes:
decide error positions by a statistical test over many low-weight parity checks,
instead of searching for an error-free information set.

## Key claims (as reported)
- A statistical alternative to combinatorial decoding.

## Relevance to this program
The origin of the arc traced on [[KN-LIT-b66899]] — an idea judged uncompetitive
for two decades that became, after reformulation as a dual attack on LPN, one of
the two serious attack paradigms against random linear codes.

This program treats it as the standing counterexample to closing a direction on
the basis of its first formulation's performance.
