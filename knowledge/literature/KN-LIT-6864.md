## Contribution
We present the first chosen-ciphertext secure public-key encryption schemes resilient to continuous tampering of arbitrary (efficiently computable) functions. Since it is impossible to realize such schemes without a self-destruction or key-updating mechanism, our proposals allow for either of them.

## Key claims (as reported)
- As in the previous works resilient to this type of tampering attacks, our schemes also tolerate bounded or continuous memory leakage attacks at the same time.
- Unlike the previous works, our schemes have efficient instantiations.
- We also prove that there is no secure digital signature scheme resilient to arbitrary tampering functions against a stronger variant of the continuous tampering attack, even if it has a self-destruction mechanism.
