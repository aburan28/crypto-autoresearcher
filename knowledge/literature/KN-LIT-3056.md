## Contribution
We present an unconditional CRT algorithm to compute the modular polynomial Φl (X, Y ) in quasi-linear time. The main ingredients of our algorithm are: the embedding of l-isogenies in smooth-degree isogenies in higher dimension, and the computation of m-th order deformations of isogenies.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We provide a proof-of-concept implementation of a heuristic version of the algorithm demonstrating the practicality of our approach.
