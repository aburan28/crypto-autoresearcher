## Contribution
We propose the first identity-based encryption (IBE) scheme that is (almost) tightly secure against chosen-ciphertext attacks. Our scheme is efficient, in the sense that its ciphertext overhead is only seven group elements, three group elements more than that of the state-of-theart passively (almost) tightly secure IBE scheme.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our scheme is secure in a multi-challenge setting, i.e., in face of an arbitrary number of challenge ciphertexts.
- The security of our scheme is based upon the standard symmetric external Diffie-Hellman assumption in pairing-friendly groups, but we also consider (less efficient) generalizations under weaker assumptions.
