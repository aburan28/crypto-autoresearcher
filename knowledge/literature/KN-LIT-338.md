## Contribution
In 2000, Galbraith and McKee heuristically derived a formula that estimates the probability that a randomly chosen elliptic curve over a fixed finite prime field has a prime number of rational points. We show how their heuristics can be generalized to Jacobians of curves of higher genus.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We then elaborate this in genus g = 2 and study various related issues, such as the probability of cyclicity and the probability of primality of the number of points on the curve itself.
- Finally, we discuss the asymptotic behavior for g → ∞.
