## Contribution
In the 1970s, Serre proved that the adelic index of a non-CM elliptic curve over a number field is finite. More recently, Zywina conjectured the complete set of adelic indices for such curves over Q.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this article, we prove that Zywina’s conjecture is true for the family of non-CM elliptic curves over Q that admit a nontrivial rational cyclic isogeny.
- This strengthens a result of Lemos that resolved Serre’s uniformity question for the same family of curves.
- Our proof proceeds by analyzing a collection of modular curves associated with each prime isogeny degree, using recent advances on l-adic images, isogeny-torsion graphs, and computations of models and rational points.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to the isogeny cluster: bears on supersingular/isogeny-based constructions and the isogeny-path problems that compete with and inform ECDLP work. Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
