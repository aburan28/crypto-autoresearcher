## Contribution
The cube attack is a powerful cryptanalytic technique and is especially powerful against stream ciphers. Since we need to analyze the complicated structure of a stream cipher in the cube attack, the cube attack basically analyzes it by regarding it as a blackbox.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Therefore, the cube attack is an experimental attack, and we cannot evaluate the security when the size of cube exceeds an experimental range, e.g., 40.
- In this paper, we propose cube attacks on non-blackbox polynomials.
- Our attacks are developed by using the division property, which is recently applied to various block ciphers.
- The clear advantage is that we can exploit large cube sizes because it never regards the cipher as a blackbox.
