## Contribution
Due to recent breakthroughs in hash functions cryptanalysis, some new hash schemes have been proposed. G RINDAHL is a novel hash function, designed by Knudsen, Rechberger and Thomsen and published at FSE 2007.

## Key claims (as reported)
- It has the particularity that it follows the R IJNDAEL design strategy, with an efficiency comparable to SHA-256.
- This paper provides the first cryptanalytic work on this new scheme.
- We show that the 256-bit version of G RINDAHL is not collision resistant.
- With a work effort of approximatively 2112 hash computations, one can generate a collision.
