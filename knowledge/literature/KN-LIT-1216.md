## Contribution
We show that two natural cycle classes on the moduli space of compact type stable maps to a varying elliptic curve agree. The first is the virtual fundamental class from Gromov-Witten theory, and the second is the Torelli pullback of the special cycle on Ag of principally polarized abelian varieties admitting an elliptic isogeny factor.
