## Contribution
On-Line Authenticated Encryption (OAE) combines privacy with data integrity and is on-line computable. Most block cipher-based schemes for Authenticated Encryption can be run on-line and are provably secure against nonce-respecting adversaries.

## Key claims (as reported)
- But they fail badly for more general adversaries.
- This is not a theoretical observation only – in practice, the reuse of nonces is a frequent issue1 .
- In recent years, cryptographers developed misuse-resistant schemes for Authenticated Encryption.
- These guarantee excellent security even against general adversaries which are allowed to reuse nonces.
