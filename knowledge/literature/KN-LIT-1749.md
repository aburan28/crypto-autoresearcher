## Contribution
We show that Mordell curves with arithmetic progressions in the ycoordinate of length 7 have not been ruled out by previous work, and we give non-isomorphic families of Mordell curves with y-arithmetic progressions of length 6. We also construct parametric families of elliptic curves of moderate rank, with subfamilies possessing rational points in arithmetic progression.
