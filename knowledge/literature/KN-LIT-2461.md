## Contribution
We give a new proof of the existence of public-coin concurrent zeroknowledge arguments for NP in the plain model under standard assumptions (the existence of one-to-one one-way functions and collision-resistant hash functions), which was originally proven by Goyal (STOC’13). In the proof, we use a new variant of the non-black-box simulation technique of Barak (FOCS’01).

## Key claims (as reported)
- An important property of our simulation technique is that the simulator runs in a straight-line manner in the fully concurrent setting.
- Compared with the simulation technique of Goyal, which also has such a property, the analysis of our simulation technique is (arguably) simpler.
