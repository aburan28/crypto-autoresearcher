## Contribution
Let K be a number field. Using the modular method, we prove asymptotic results on solutions of the Diophantine equation x4 − y 2 = z p over K, assuming some deep but standard conjectures of the Langlands programme when K has at least one complex embedding.

## Key claims (as reported)

- When modularity of elliptic curves over K is known, for example when K is real quadratic or the r-layer of the cyclotomic Z2 -extension of Q, effective asymptotic results hold.
