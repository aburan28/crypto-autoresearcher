## Contribution
We study the behavior of Selmer groups of an elliptic curve E/Q in finite Galois extensions with prescribed Galois group. Fix a prime l ≥ 5, a finite group G with #G = ln , and an elliptic curve E/Q with Sell (E/Q) = 0 and surjective mod-l Galois representation.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that there exist infinitely many Galois extensions F/Q with Galois group Gal(F/Q) ≃ G for which the l-Selmer group Sell (E/F ) also vanishes.
- We obtain an asymptotic lower bound for the number M(G, E; X) of such fields F with absolute discriminant |∆F | ≤ X, proving that there is an explicit constant δ > 0 such that 1 M(G, E; X) ≫ X ln−1 (l−1) (log X)δ−1 .
- The asymptotic for M(G, E; X) matches the conjectural count for all G-extensions F/Q for which |∆F | ≤ X, up to a power of log X.
- This demonstrates that Selmer stability is not a rare phenomenon.
