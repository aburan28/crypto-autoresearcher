## Contribution
We continue the study of statistical zero-knowledge (SZK) proofs, both interactive and noninteractive, for computational problems on point lattices. We are particularly interested in the problem GapSPP of approximating the ε-smoothing parameter (for some ε < 1/2) of an n-dimensional lattice.
