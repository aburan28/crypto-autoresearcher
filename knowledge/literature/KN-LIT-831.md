## Contribution
Several discrete geometry problems are closely related to the arithmetic theory of elliptic curves defined on the rational fields Q. In this paper we consider the θ-congruent number for (θ = π3 and 2π 3 ) and tiling number n.

## Key claims (as reported)
- For the case that n ⩾ 2 is square-free odd integer, we determine all n such that the Selmer rank of elliptic curve En, π3 : y 2 = x(x−n)(x+3n) or/and En, 2π : y 2 = x(x+n)(x−3n) is zero.
- From this, we provide several series 3 π 2π of non θ-congruent numbers for θ = 3 and 3 , and non tiling numbers n with arbitrary many of prime divisors.
