## Contribution
We provide the first constructions of SNARGs for BatchNP and P based solely on the sub-exponential Decisional Diffie Hellman (DDH) assumption. Our schemes achieve poly-logarithmic proof sizes.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We obtain our results by following the correlation-intractability framework for secure instantiation of the Fiat-Shamir paradigm.
- The centerpiece of our results and of independent interest is a new construction of correlation-intractable hash functions for “small input” product relations verifiable in TC0 , based on sub-exponential DDH.
