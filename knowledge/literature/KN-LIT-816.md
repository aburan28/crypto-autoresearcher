## Contribution
We study the simultaneous reductions at several supersingular primes of elliptic curves with complex multiplication. We show – under additional congruence assumptions on the CM order – that the reductions are surjective (and even become equidistributed) on the product of supersingular loci when the discriminant of the order becomes large.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This variant of the equidistribution theorems of Duke and Cornut-Vatsal is an(other) application of the recent work of Einsiedler and Lindenstrauss on the classification of joinings of higher-rank diagonalizable actions.
