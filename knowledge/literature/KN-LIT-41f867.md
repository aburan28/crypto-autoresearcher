## Contribution
An early cryptanalytic study of the McEliece public-key cryptosystem, presented
at Crypto 1988 — among the first serious attempts to attack the system after
its 1978 publication.

## Key claims (as reported)
- A cryptanalytic attack on McEliece, without breaking it at its proposed parameters.

## Relevance to this program
Marks the start of the sustained cryptanalytic attention that makes McEliece's
survival meaningful. A hard problem's credibility comes from **failed attacks
by people who tried hard**, and this is the first of that record for
code-based encryption.

The same standard applies to anything this program concludes about the ECDLP:
the value of a negative result is proportional to the seriousness of the effort
that produced it, which is why evidence records must state their budget and
test boundary.
