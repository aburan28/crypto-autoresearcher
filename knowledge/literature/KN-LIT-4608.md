## Contribution
With chosen-IV chosen texts, David Wagner has analyzed the multiple modes of operation proposed by Eli Biham in FSE’98. However, his method is too unrealistic.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We use only known-IV chosen texts to attack many triple modes of operation which are combined with cascade operations.
- 123 triple modes are analyzed with complexities less than E.
- Our work shows that the securities of many triple modes decrease when the initial values are exposed.
