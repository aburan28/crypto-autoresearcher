## Contribution
We investigate some aspects of the m-division field K(E [m]), where E is an elliptic curve defined over a field K with char(K) 6= 2, 3 and m is a positive integer. When m = pr , with p ≥ 5 a prime and r a positive integer, we prove K(E [pr ]) = K(x1 , ζp , y2 ), where {(x1 , y1 ), (x2 , y2 )} is a generating system of E [pr ] and ζp is a primitive p-th root of the unity.

## Key claims (as reported)
- If E has a K-rational point of order √ p, then K(E [pr ]) = K(ζpr , m1 a), with a ∈ K(ζpr ) and m1 |pr .
- In addition, when K is a number field, we produce an upper bound to the logarithmic height of the discriminant of the extension K(E [m])/K, for all m ≥ 3.
- As a consequence, we give an explicit effective version of the hypotheses of the local-global divisibility problem in elliptic curves over number fields.
