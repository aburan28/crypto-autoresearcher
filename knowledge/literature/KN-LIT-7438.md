## Contribution
We provide the first standard model construction for a powerful class of Universal Computational Extractors (UCEs; Bellare et al. Crypto 2013) based on indistinguishability obfuscation.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our construction suffices to instantiate q-query correlation-secure hash functions and to extract polynomially many hardcore bits from any one-way function.
- For many cryptographic primitives and in particular for correlation-secure hash functions all known constructions are in the random-oracle model.
- Indeed, recent negative results by Wichs (ITCS 2013) rule out a large class of techniques to prove the security of correlation-secure hash functions in the standard model.
- Our construction is based on puncturable PRFs (Sahai und Waters; STOC 2014) and indistinguishability obfuscation.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
