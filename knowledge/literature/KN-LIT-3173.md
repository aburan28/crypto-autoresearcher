## Contribution
A recent paradigm put forth by Boyle et al. (CCS 2018, Crypto 2019) showed how pseudorandom correlation generators (PCG) can be used to generate large amounts of useful forms of correlated (pseudo)randomness, using minimal interactions followed solely by local computations, yielding silent secure two-party computation protocols.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This can be extended to N-party using programmable PCG’s.
- Previous works constructed very efficient (non-programmable) PCG’s for correlations such as random oblivious transfers.
- However, the situation is less satisfying for random oblivious linear evaluations (OLE’s), their generalisation over large fields.
- The state-of-the-art work of Boyle et al.
