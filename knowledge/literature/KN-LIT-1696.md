## Contribution
Let X be a geometrically irreducible smooth projective curve over a field k. Yamazaki et al. define a biadditive symmetric pairing ⟨−, −⟩ on the torsion subgroup of the Picard group Pic(X) with values in k× ⊗ Q/Z.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The intrinsic subgroup Pic(X)is tors is the kernel of this pairing.
- When X is an elliptic curve E, we can identify E ≃ Pic0 (E).
- We classify E(k)is tors in purely algebraic terms for many elliptic curves over an arbitrary field k.
- We give a generalization of the analytic methods of Yamazaki et al. from Q to an arbitrary field k ⊂ C.
