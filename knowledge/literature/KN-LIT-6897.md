## Contribution
In this paper, we improve the recent rebound and start-fromthe-middle attacks on AES-like permutations. Our new cryptanalysis technique uses the fact that one can view two rounds of such permutations as a layer of big Sboxes preceded and followed by simple affine transformations.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The big Sboxes encountered in this alternative representation are named Super-Sboxes.
- We apply this method to two second-round SHA-3 candidates Grøstl and ECHO, and obtain improvements over the previous cryptanalysis results for these two schemes.
- Moreover, we improve the best distinguisher for the AES block cipher in the known-key setting, reaching 8 rounds for the 128-bit version.
