## Contribution
A closed Riemann surface S is called a generalized Fermat curve of type (p, n), where p, n ≥ 2 are integers, if it admits a group H  Znp of conformal automorphisms so that S /H is an orbifold of genus zero with exactly n + 1 cone points, each one of order p. It is known that S is a fiber product of (n − 1) classical Fermat curves of degree p and, for (p − 1)(n − 1) > 2, that it is a non-hyperelliptic Riemann surface.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, assuming p to be a prime integer, we provide a decomposition, up to isogeny, of the Jacobian variety JS as a product of Jacobian varieties of certain cyclic p-gonal curves.
- Explicit equations for these p-gonal curves are provided in terms of the equations for S .
- As a consequence of this decomposition, we are able to provide explicit positive-dimensional families of closed Riemann surfaces whose Jacobian variety is isogenous to the product of elliptic curves.
