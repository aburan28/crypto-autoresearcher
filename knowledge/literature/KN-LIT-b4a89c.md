## Contribution

A transfer for E/F_{q^3}: an F_q-rational (l,l,l)-isogeny from the Weil restriction to the Jacobian of a genus-3 curve over F_q, followed by index calculus there. It is a cover-type attack that needs no covering map and works for some prime-order curves.

## Key claims (as reported)

- ECDLP in some prime-order E(F_{q^3}) in O~(q).

## Relevance to this program

Cover attacks for some prime-order curves over F_{q^3} via isogenies to genus-3 Jacobians (O~(q)); cover-attack baseline for extension-field curves.
