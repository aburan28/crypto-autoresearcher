## Contribution
For a quadratic field K without rationally defined complex multiplication, we prove that there exists of a prime pK depending only on K such that if d is a positive integer whose minimal prime divisor is greater than pK , then for any extension L/K of degree d and any elliptic curve E/K, we have E (L)tors = E (K)tors . By not assuming the GRH, this is a generalization of the results by Genao, and Gonález-Jiménez and Najman.
