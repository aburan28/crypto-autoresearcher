## Contribution
We study the problem of secure two-party computation in the presence of a trusted setup. If there is an unconditionally UC-secure protocol for f that makes use of calls to an ideal g, then we say that f reduces to g (and write f v g).

## Key claims (as reported)
- Some g are complete in the sense that all functions reduce to g.
- However, almost nothing is known about the power of an incomplete g in this setting.
- We shed light on this gap by showing a characterization of f v g for incomplete g.
- Very roughly speaking, we show that f reduces to g if and only if it does so by the simplest possible protocol: one that makes a single call to ideal g and uses no further communication.
