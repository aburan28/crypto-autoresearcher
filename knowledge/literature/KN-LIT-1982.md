## Contribution
We revisit the question of constructing an ideal cipher from a random oracle. (Journal of Cryptology, 2014) proved that a 14-round Feistel network using random, independent, keyed round functions is indifferentiable from an ideal cipher, thus demonstrating the feasibility of such a transformation.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Left unresolved is the number of rounds of a Feistel network that are needed in order for indifferentiability to hold.
- We improve upon the result of Coron et al. and show that 10 rounds suffice.
