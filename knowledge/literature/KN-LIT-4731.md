## Contribution
Digital Signatures are ubiquitous in modern computing. One of the most widely used digital signature schemes is ECDSA due to its use in TLS, various Blockchains such as Bitcoin and Etherum, and many other applications.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Yet the formal analysis of ECDSA is comparatively sparse.
- In particular, all known security results for ECDSA rely on some idealized model such as the generic group model or the programmable (bijective) random oracle model.
- In this work, we study the question whether these strong idealized models are necessary for proving the security of ECDSA.
- Specifically, we focus on the programmability of ECDSA’s “conversion function” which maps an elliptic curve point into its x-coordinate modulo the group order.
