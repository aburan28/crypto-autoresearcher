## Contribution
Let E/Q be an elliptic curve, P ∈ E(Q) be non-torsion, and (Dn ) be the associated elliptic divisibility sequence. We study when a product k Y Dni i=1 can be a ρ-th power, where ρ is a fixed prime.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our obstructions concern the incidence of prime divisors among the indices ni , rather than the prime factorisation of individual EDS terms.
- Assuming that D1 is divisible by 2 or 3, we show that sufficiently large primes l occurring as simple largest prime divisors of the indices, with ni /l B-smooth whenever l | ni , must occur in ρ-balanced incidence blocks.
- In particular, for a finite family of such primes, the associated incidence rows are disjoint, linearly independent over Fρ , and satisfy |Λ∗ | ≤ ⌊k/ρ⌋.
