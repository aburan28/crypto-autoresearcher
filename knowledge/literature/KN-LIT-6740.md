## Contribution
We present two new algorithms for solving norm equations over global function fields with at least one infinite place of degree one. The first of these is a substantial improvement of a method due to Gaál and Pohst, while the second approach uses index calculus techniques and is significantly faster asymptotically and in practice.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Both algorithms incorporate compact representations of field elements which results in a significant gain in performance compared to the Gaál-Pohst approach.
- We provide Magma implementations, analyze the complexity of all three algorithms under varying asymptotics on the field parameters, and provide empirical data on their performance.
