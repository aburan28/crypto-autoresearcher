## Contribution
We provide the first constructions of non-interactive zeroknowledge and Zap arguments for NP based on the sub-exponential hardness of Decisional Diffie-Hellman against polynomial time adversaries (without use of groups with pairings). Central to our results, and of independent interest, is a new notion of interactive trapdoor hashing protocols.
