## Contribution
Let E/Q be an elliptic curve and p a rational prime of good ordinary reduction. For every imaginary quadratic field K/Q satisfying the Heegner hypothesis for E we have a corresponding line in E(K) ⊗ Qp , known as a shadow line.

## Key claims (as reported)
- When E/Q has analytic rank 2 and E/K has analytic rank 3, shadow lines are expected to lie in E(Q) ⊗ Qp .
- If, in addition, p splits in K/Q, then shadow lines can be determined using the anticyclotomic p-adic height pairing.
- We develop an algorithm to compute anticyclotomic p-adic heights which we then use to provide an algorithm to compute shadow lines.
- We conclude by illustrating these algorithms in a collection of examples.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
