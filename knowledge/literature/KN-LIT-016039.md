## Reported result

The authors refine the dual distinguishing inequality when the first minimum is sufficiently large and sample from a chosen lower-dimensional dual of a primal-lattice projection rather than the full dual lattice. Their feasible-parameter enumeration reports a 26.1–43.7-bit improvement relative to the original provable dual attack framework.

## Scope and evidence boundary

The comparison is to the original *provable dual* framework. The abstract does not establish a 26–44-bit drop against the best heuristic ML-KEM attack, or a break of any standardized parameter set.

## Research follow-up

Recompute a same-game comparison against current heuristic and provable baselines with discrete Gaussian sampling, preprocessing, memory and bit-operation costs separated.
