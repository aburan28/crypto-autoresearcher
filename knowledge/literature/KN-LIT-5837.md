## Contribution
This paper presents a practical cryptanalysis of the Identification Scheme proposed by Patarin at Crypto 1996. This scheme relies on the hardness of the Isomorphism of Polynomial with One Secret (IP1S), and enjoys shorter key than many other schemes based on the hardness of a combinatorial problem (as opposed to numbertheoretic problems).

## Key claims (as reported)
- Patarin proposed concrete parameters that have not been broken faster than exhaustive search so far.
- On the theoretical side, IP1S has been shown to be harder than Graph Isomorphism, which makes it an interesting target.
- We present two new deterministic algorithms to attack the IP1S problem, and we rigorously analyze their complexity and success probability.
- We show that they can solve a (big) constant fraction of all the instances of degree two in polynomial time.
