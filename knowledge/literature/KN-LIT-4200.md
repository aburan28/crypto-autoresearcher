## Contribution
Exponent splitting is a classical technique to protect modular exponentiation against side-channel attacks. Although it is rarely implemented due to efficiency reasons, it is widely considered as a highlysecure solution.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Therefore it is often used as a reference to benchmark new countermeasure proposals.
- In this paper, we make new observations about the statistical behavior of the splitting of the exponent.
- We look at the correlations between the two shares, and show an important imbalance.
- Later, we show how to use this imbalance in higher-order attacks (mostly based on address-bit, safe-error and fault analysis).
