## Contribution
Let F be a number field and p ≥ 7 a rational prime. We obtain a simple descent criterion characterising those projective Galois representations ρ : GF → PGL2 (Fp ) for which the corresponding twist Xρ (p) of the principal modular curve of level p is defined over Q.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also give a more concrete version of this result for representations which arise from elliptic curves over cyclic number fields.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
