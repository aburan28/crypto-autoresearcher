## Contribution

Low-memory parallel collision algorithm with tame and wild sets (the "Gaudry-Schost algorithm") for the 2-dimensional DLPs arising in genus-2 point counting (Matsuo-Chao-Tsujii). It became the standard tool for multi-dimensional and interval DLPs and is the basis of the Galbraith-Ruprai equivalence-class interval algorithm. Follow-ups: Galbraith-Ruprai, IMACC 2009, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-10868-6_22; Wu-Zhuang, DCC 90:107-119 (2022), doi:10.1007/s10623-021-00966-5; Mukhopadhyay, eprint 2023/160.

Low-memory parallel birthday algorithm for (multi-dimensional) interval DLP; the reference for tame/wild set designs that are not kangaroos.
