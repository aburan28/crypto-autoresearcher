## Contribution
Side channels provide additional information to skilled adversaries that reduce the effort to determine an unknown key. If sufficient side channel information is available, identification of the secret key can even become trivial.

## Key claims (as reported)
- However, if not enough side information is available, some effort is still required to find the key in the key space (which now has reduced entropy).
- To understand the security implications of side channel attacks it is then crucial to evaluate this remaining effort in a meaningful manner.
- Quantifying this effort can be done by looking at two key questions: first, how ‘deep’ (at most) is the unknown key in the remaining key space, and second, how ‘expensive’ is it to enumerate keys up to a certain depth?
- We provide results for these two challenges.
