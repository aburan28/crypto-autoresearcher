## Contribution

Upper bounds on the maximal Groebner-basis degree of homogenised affine semi-regular systems, and a characterisation of the first degree fall. Compares Buchberger-like runs on affine vs homogenised systems. The companion papers give explicit Hilbert-Poincaré series and study genericness of semi-regularity (Fröberg-type conjectures). This supplies the modern solving-degree toolkit for estimating F4/F5 cost on decomposition systems.
