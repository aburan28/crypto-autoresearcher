## Contribution
In undeniable signature schemes, zero-knowledgeness and non-transferability have been identified so far. In this paper, by separating these two notions, we show the first 3-move confirmation and disavowal protocols for Chaum’s undeniable signature scheme which is secure against active and concurrent attacks.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our main observation is that while the signer has one public key and one secret key, there exist two witnesses in the confirmation and disavowal proofs of Chaum’s scheme.
