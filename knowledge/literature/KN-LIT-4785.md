## Contribution
Lossy encryption was originally studied as a means of achieving efficient and composable oblivious transfer. Bellare, Hofheinz and Yilek showed that lossy encryption is also selective opening secure.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We present new and general constructions of lossy encryption schemes and of cryptosystems secure against selective opening adversaries.
- We show that every re-randomizable encryption scheme gives rise to efficient encryptions secure against a selective opening adversary.
- We show that statistically-hiding 2-round Oblivious Transfer implies Lossy Encryption and so do smooth hash proof systems.
- This shows that private information retrieval and homomorphic encryption both imply Lossy Encryption, and thus Selective Opening Secure Public Key Encryption.
