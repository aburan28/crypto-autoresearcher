## Contribution
Recently the braid groups were introduced as a new source for cryptography. The group operations are performed efficiently and the features are quite different from those of other cryptographically popular groups.

## Key claims (as reported)
- As the first step to put the braid groups into the area of pseudorandomness, this article presents some cryptographic primitives under two related assumptions in braid groups.
- First, assuming that the conjugacy problem is a one-way function, say f , we show which particular bit of the argument x is pseudorandom given f (x).
- Next, under the decision Ko-Lee assumption, we construct two provably secure pseudorandom schemes: a pseudorandom generator and a pseudorandom synthesizer.
