## Contribution
A shuffle is a permutation and rerandomization of a set of ciphertexts. Among other things, it can be used to construct mix-nets that are used in anonymization protocols and voting schemes.

## Key claims (as reported)
- While shuffling is easy, it is hard for an outsider to verify that a shuffle has been performed correctly.
- We suggest two efficient honest verifier zero-knowledge (HVZK) arguments for correctness of a shuffle.
- Our goal is to minimize round-complexity and at the same time have low communicational and computational complexity.
- The two schemes we suggest are both 3-move HVZK arguments for correctness of a shuffle.
