## Contribution

Rigorous collision bounds for the additive (r-adding) walks actually used for ECDLP, via Hildebrand-type mixing-time estimates for random walks on finite abelian groups.

## Key claims (as reported)

- Bounds within a sqrt(log|G|) factor of the birthday bound O(sqrt|G|).

## Relevance to this program

Rigorous collision bound for additive rho within √(log|G|) of the birthday bound. Cite when discussing whether table/additive walks (including the ECC2K-130 'table walk') are provably random enough.
