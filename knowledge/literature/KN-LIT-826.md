## Contribution
Investigating a conjecture of Zannier, we study irreducible subvarieties of abelian schemes that dominate the base and contain a Zariski dense set of torsion points that lie on pairwise isogenous fibers. If everything is defined over the algebraic numbers and the abelian scheme has maximal variation, we prove that the geometric generic fiber of such a subvariety is a union of torsion cosets.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We go on to prove fully or partially explicit versions of this result in fibered powers of the Legendre family of elliptic curves.
- Finally, we apply a recent result of Galateau-Martı́nez to obtain uniform bounds on the number of maximal torsion cosets in the Manin-Mumford problem across a given isogeny class.
- For the proofs, we adapt the strategy, due to Lang, Serre, Tate, and Hindry, of using Galois automorphisms that act on the torsion as homotheties to the family setting.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to the isogeny cluster: bears on supersingular/isogeny-based constructions and the isogeny-path problems that compete with and inform ECDLP work.
