## Contribution
In this paper, we prove that for each number field F there exists a uniform bound on the prime levels p of elliptic curves E/F for which F (E[p]) = F (ζp ). Under the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, we also give uniform bounds on p for which F (E[p])/F is abelian, provided that F has no rational complex multiplication.

## Key claims (as reported)
- These are generalizations of results of González-Jiménez and Lozano-Robledo [GJLR16] to general number fields.
