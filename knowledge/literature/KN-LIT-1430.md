## Contribution
Let a, b > 0 be coprime integers. Assuming a conjecture on Hecke eigenvalues along binary cubic forms, we prove an asymptotic formula for the number of primes of the form ax2 + by 3 with x ≤ X 1/2 and y ≤ X 1/3 .

## Key claims (as reported)
- The proof combines sieve methods with the theory of real quadratic fields/indefinite binary quadratic forms, the Weil bound for exponential sums, and spectral methods of GL(2) automorphic forms.
- We also discuss applications to elliptic curves.
