## Contribution
Existing protocols for non-interactive multi-party key exchange either (1) support a bounded number of users, (2) require a trusted setup, or (3) rely on knowledge-type assumptions. We construct the first non-interactive key exchange protocols which support an unbounded number of parties and have a security proof that does not rely on knowledge assumptions.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our non-interactive key-exchange protocol does not require a trusted setup and extends easily to the identity-based setting.
- Our protocols suffer only a polynomial loss to the underlying hardness assumptions.
