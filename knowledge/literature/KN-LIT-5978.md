## Contribution
Hopper, John Langford, and Luis von Ahn Computer Science Department, Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh PA 15213, USA {hopper,jcl,biglou}@cs.cmu.edu Abstract. Informally, steganography is the process of sending a secret message from Alice to Bob in such a way that an eavesdropper (who listens to all communications) cannot even tell that a secret message is being sent.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this work, we initiate the study of steganography from a complexity-theoretic point of view.
- We introduce definitions based on computational indistinguishability and we prove that the existence of one-way functions implies the existence of secure steganographic protocols.
