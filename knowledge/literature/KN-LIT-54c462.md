## Contribution

For generalised GLS binary curves E/F_{q^l} (q = 2^n), the GLS endomorphism induces an efficient endomorphism on the GHS-descended genus-g Jacobian over F_q, giving a factor-n speed-up of index calculus there. Backed by an explicit computation. It is the most recent practical binary-field Weil-descent ECDLP computation found.

## Key claims (as reported)

- DL in a prime-order subgroup of a GLS curve over F_{2^{5*31}} (155-bit field) in ~1,035 CPU-days (Magma).

## Relevance to this program

GLS endomorphism speeds GHS descent; a prime-order subgroup over F_{2^{5·31}} broken in ~1,035 CPU-days with Magma. Recent practical Weil-descent data point.
