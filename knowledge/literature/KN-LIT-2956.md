## Contribution
We analyse the hash function family based on walks in LPS Ramanujan graphs recently introduced by Charles et al. We present an algorithm for finding collisions that runs in quasi-linear time in the length of the hashed value.

## Key claims (as reported)
- A concrete instance of the hash function is considered, based on a 100-digit prime.
- A short collision is given, together with implementation details.
