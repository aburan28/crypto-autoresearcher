## Contribution

Reviews Petit et al. 2016 and Kudo et al. 2018, proposes a "pseudo-index calculus" for prime-field ECDLP, and concludes that no subexponential method currently exists over prime fields. The companion 2025/015 makes strong claims about finite-field index calculus. Both are unrefereed preprints whose claims were not independently assessed here; treat with caution.

## Key claims (as reported)

- No sub-rho result.
