## Contribution
Let d ≥ 1 be an integer and let p be a rational prime. Recall that p is a torsion prime of degree d if there exists an elliptic curve E over a degree d number field K such that E has a K-rational point of order p.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Derickx, Kamienny, Stein and Stoll [5] have computed the torsion primes of degrees 4, 5, 6 and 7.
- We verify that the techniques used in [5] can be extended to determine the torsion primes of degree 8.
