## Contribution
For positive integers K and L, we introduce and study the notion of K-multiplicative dependence over the algebraic closure Fp of a finite prime field Fp , as well as L-linear dependence of points on elliptic curves in reduction modulo primes. One of our main results shows that, given non-zero rational functions φ1 , . . . , φm , ̺1 , . . . , ̺n ∈ Q(X) and an elliptic curve E defined over the rational numbers Q, for any sufficiently large prime p, for all but finitely many α ∈ Fp , at most one of the following two can happen: φ1 (α), . . . , φm (α) are K-multiplicatively dependent or the points (̺1 (α), ·), . . . , (̺n (α), ·) are L-linearly dependent on the reduction of E modulo p.

## Key claims (as reported)
- As one of our main tools, we prove a general statement about the n intersection of an irreducible curve in the split semiabelian variety Gm m × E with the algebraic subgroups of codimension at least 2.
- As an application of our results, we improve a result of M.
- Chang and extend a result of J.
- Voloch about elements of large order in finite fields in some special cases.
