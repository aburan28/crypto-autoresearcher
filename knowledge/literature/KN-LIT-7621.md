## Contribution
Standard textbook treatment of the arithmetic of elliptic curves: Weierstrass
geometry, the formal group, curves over finite/local/global fields, the
Mordell–Weil theorem, and the Néron–Tate canonical height. The second edition
adds computational material (Schoof point counting, Miller pairing, Lenstra
ECM) and brief ECC remarks.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Every elliptic curve carries a formal group law (Ch. IV) whose logarithm
  controls the p-adic and anomalous additive transfers.
- The Mordell–Weil group of an elliptic curve over a number field is finitely
  generated (Ch. VIII).
- The Néron–Tate canonical height is a positive-definite quadratic form on
  E(K)/E(K)_tors, compatible with the height pairing used in Gross–Zagier
  and in Xedni-style lattice lifts (Ch. VIII).
- These are textbook theorems; confidence is `established` for the classical
  statements, not for any crypto-scale complexity claim.

## Relevance to this program
The missing classical spine behind formal-group / anomalous attacks
