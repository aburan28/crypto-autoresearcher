## Contribution
A shuffle is a permutation and re-encryption of a set of ciphertexts. Shuffles are for instance used in mix-nets for anonymous broadcast and voting.

## Key claims (as reported)
- One way to make a shuffle verifiable is to give a zero-knowledge proof of correctness.
- All currently known practical zero-knowledge proofs for correctness of a shuffle rely on interaction.
- We give the first efficient non-interactive zeroknowledge proof for correctness of a shuffle.
