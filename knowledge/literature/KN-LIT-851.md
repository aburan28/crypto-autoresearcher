## Contribution
For the elliptic curves Eσ2D : y 2 = x3 + σ2Dx , which has 2-isogeny curve Eσ2D : y 2 = x3 − σ8Dx, σ = ±1, D = pe11 pe22 · · · penn , where pi are different odd prime numbers and ei = 1 or 3, we demonstrate that Tamagawa numbers of these elliptic curves are always one or zero by the use of matrix in finite field F2 . The specific number depends on the value of σ.

## Key claims (as reported)
- By our proofs of these results, we find a method to quickly sieve a part of the elliptic curves with Mordell-Weil rank zero or rank one in this form as an application.
