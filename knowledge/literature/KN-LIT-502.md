## Contribution
There are several formulas for the number of orbits of the projective line under the action of subgroups of GL2 . We give an interpretation of two such formulas in terms of the geometry of elliptic curves, and prove a more general formula for a large class of congruence subgroups of Bianchi groups.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our formula involves the number of walks on a certain graph called an isogeny volcano.
- Underlying our results is a complete description of the group of extensions of a pair of CM elliptic curves, and of a pair of lattices in a quadratic field.
