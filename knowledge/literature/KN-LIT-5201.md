## Key claims (as reported)
- (TCC 2020) showed how to obtain a non-interactive zero-knowledge (NIZK) argument for NP from a succinct non-interactive argument (SNARG) for NP.
- In particular, their work demonstrates how to leverage the succinctness property from an argument system and transform it into a zero-knowledge property.
- In this work, we study a similar question of leveraging succinctness for zero-knowledge.
- Our starting point is a batch argument for NP, a primitive that allows a prover to convince a verifier of T NP statements x1 , . . . , xT with a proof whose size scales sublinearly with T .
