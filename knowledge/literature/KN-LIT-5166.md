## Contribution
Non-committing encryption (NCE) introduced by Canetti et al. (STOC ’96) is a central tool to achieve multi-party computation protocols secure in the adaptive setting.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Recently, Yoshida et al.
- (ASIACRYPT ’19) proposed an NCE scheme based on the hardness of the DDH problem, which has ciphertext expansion O(log λ) and public-key expansion O(λ2 ).
- In this work, we improve their result and propose a methodology to construct an NCE scheme that achieves constant ciphertext expansion.
- Our methodology can be instantiated from the DDH assumption and the LWE assumption.
