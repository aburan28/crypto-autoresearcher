## Contribution
The ultimate goal of designing a symmetric-key cryptographic primitive often can be formulated as an optimization problem. So far, these problems mainly have been solved with trivial algorithms such as brute force or random search.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that a more advanced and equally versatile class of search algorithms, called metaheuristics, can help to tackle optimization problems related to design of symmetrickey primitives.
- We use two nature-inspired metaheuristics, simulated annealing and genetic algorithm, to optimize in terms of security the components of two recent cryptographic designs, SKINNY and AES-round based constructions.
- The positive outputs of the optimization suggest that metaheuristics are non-trivial tools, well suited for automatic design of primitives.
