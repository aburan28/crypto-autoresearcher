## Contribution
Let P and Q be non-zero integers. The Lucas sequence {Un (P, Q)} is defined by U0 = 0, U1 = 1, Un = P Un−1 − QUn−2 (n ≥ 2).

## Key claims (as reported)
- For each positive integer n ≤ 7 we describe all Lucas sequences with (P, Q) = 1 having the property that Un (P, Q) is a perfect square.
- The arguments are elementary.
- We also find all Lucas sequences such that U8 (P, Q) is a perfect square.
- This reduces to a number of problems of similar type, namely, finding all points on an elliptic curve defined over a quartic number field subject to a “Q-rationality” condition on the X-coordinate.
