## Contribution
Gennaro, Gentry, Parno and Raykova proposed an efficient NIZK argument for Circuit-SAT, based on non-standard tools like conscientious and quadratic span programs. We propose a new linear PCP for the Circuit-SAT, based on a combination of standard span programs (that verify the correctness of every individual gate) and highdistance linear error-correcting codes (that check the consistency of wire assignments).

## Key claims (as reported)
- This allows us to simplify all steps of the argument, which results in significantly improved efficiency.
- We then construct an NIZK Circuit-SAT argument based on existing techniques.
