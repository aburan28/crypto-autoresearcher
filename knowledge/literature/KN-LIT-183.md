## Contribution
We present a deterministic and explicit algorithm for the computation of the endomorphism rings of supersingular elliptic curves. Given any prime characteristic p, the algorithm returns a list of pairs (E, {1, e1 (E), e2 (E), e3 (E)}) for all supersingular elliptic curves E over Fp , where the second coordinate is a base of EndFp (E) ⊗ Q.
