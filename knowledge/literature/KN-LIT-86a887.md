## Contribution

Bounds the last fall degree of zero-dimensional systems obtained by Weil descent, the rigorous route (unlike FFD heuristics) to bounding the Groebner-basis cost of summation-polynomial systems. Follow-up: M.-D. A. Huang, "On the last fall degree of Weil descent polynomial systems", FFA 73:101836 (2021), doi:10.1016/j.ffa.2021.101836.
