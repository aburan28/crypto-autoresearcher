## Contribution
We study modularity properties of generating series of logarithmic Gromov–Witten invariants of elliptic fibrations relative to singular fibers. Motivated by predictions from Vafa–Witten theory, we conjecture that such generating series are mock modular forms.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove this conjecture for a large class of invariants of the rational elliptic surface mirror to P2 , relative to a cycle of nine rational curves.
- The proof uses a correspondence between log Gromov–Witten invariants of the mirror and Vafa–Witten invariants of P2 established in previous work joint with Bousseau, together with known mock modularity results on the Vafa–Witten side.
