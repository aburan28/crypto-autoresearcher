## Contribution
Classifies the types of multiplier (endomorphism) rings of elliptic function
fields, establishing the *Deuring correspondence*: supersingular elliptic curves
over F_{p^2} correspond to maximal orders in the quaternion algebra ramified
exactly at p and infinity, while ordinary curves correspond to orders in
imaginary quadratic fields (CM).

## Key claims (as reported)
- The supersingular-curve <-> quaternion-maximal-order dictionary is the origin of
  all later endomorphism-ring-based isogeny cryptography (KN-LIT-072, KN-LIT-073,
  KN-LIT-074).
- A 1941 arithmetic result, foundational and independent of cryptography.

## Relevance to this program
The algebraic foundation on which endomorphism rings, quaternion orders, CM, and

(CM, volcano orientation, RQ-ISO-001, ISO-AR) the program studies. The
