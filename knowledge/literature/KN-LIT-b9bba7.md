## Relevance to this program
The distinction between an attack that is *analysed* and one that is *run* is
the same one this program draws between a derivation and a certificate
(`docs/claims-and-verification.md`). An executed attack producing a recovered
key is the strongest claim tier available; an analysed attack is a weaker one,
however convincing the analysis.

Held as a reminder that this program's own strongest results should be the ones
it actually ran.
