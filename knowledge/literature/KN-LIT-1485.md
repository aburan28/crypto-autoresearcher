## Contribution
In this paper, we discuss refined Humbert invariants, introduced by Kani in [26]. We show that in the contexts of SQISign and CSIDH, under GRH, the computational isogeny problem is polynomially equivalent to the computational refined Humbert invariant problem.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This includes an algorithm to compute the basis of a maximal order of a quaternion algebra given its norm form.
- Finally, we also review what is known in the literature about computing refined Humbert invariants.
