## Contribution
We present a quantum polynomial time attack against the GMMSSZ branching program obfuscator of Garg et al. (TCC’16), when instantiated with the GGH13 multilinear map of Garg et al.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This candidate obfuscator was proved secure in the weak multilinear map model introduced by Miles et al.
- Our attack uses the short principal ideal solver of Cramer et al.
- (EUROCRYPT’16), to recover a secret element of the GGH13 multilinear map in quantum polynomial time.
- We then use this secret element to mount a (classical) polynomial time mixed-input attack against the GMMSSZ obfuscator.
