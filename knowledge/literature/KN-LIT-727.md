## Contribution
Let p be an odd prime number, and let E be an elliptic curve defined over a number field F ′ such that E has semistable reduction at every prime of F ′ above p and is supersingular at at least one prime above p. Under appropriate hypotheses, we compute the Akashi series of the signed Selmer groups of E over a Zdp -extension over a finite extension F of F ′ .

## Key claims (as reported)
- As a by-product, we also compute the Euler characteristics of these Selmer groups.
