## Contribution
In PKC 1999, Fujisaki and Okamoto showed how to convert any public key encryption (PKE) scheme secure against chosen plaintext attacks (CPA) to a PKE scheme which is secure against chosen ciphertext attacks (CCA) in the random oracle model. Surprisingly, the resulting CCA secure scheme has almost the same efficiency as the underlying CPA secure scheme.
