## Contribution
For a lattice Λ in the complex plane, let KΛ be the field of Λ-elliptic functions. For two relatively prime integers p (respectively q) greater than 1, consider the endomorphisms ψ (resp. φ) of KΛ given by multiplication by p (resp. q) on the elliptic curve C/Λ.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove that if f (resp. g) are complex Laurent power series that satisfy linear difference equations over KΛ with respect to φ (resp. ψ) then there is a dichotomy.
- Either, for some sublattice Λ′ of Λ, one of f or g belongs to the ring KΛ′ [z, z −1 , ζ(z, Λ′ )], where ζ(z, Λ′ ) is the Weierstrass zeta function, or f and g are algebraically independent over KΛ .
- This is an elliptic analogue of a recent theorem of Adamczewski, Dreyfus, Hardouin and Wibmer (over the field of rational functions).
