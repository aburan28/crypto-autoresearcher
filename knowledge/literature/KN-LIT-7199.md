## Contribution
As a building block, gadgets and associated algorithms are widely used in advanced lattice cryptosystems. The gadget algorithms for power-of-base moduli are very efficient and simple, however the current algorithms for arbitrary moduli are still complicated and practically more costly despite several efforts.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Considering the necessity of arbitrary moduli, developing simpler and more practical gadget algorithms for arbitrary moduli is crucial to improving the practical performance of lattice based applications.
- In this work, we propose two new gadget sampling algorithms for arbitrary moduli.
- Our first algorithm is for gadget Gaussian sampling.
- It is simple and efficient.

## Relevance to this program
