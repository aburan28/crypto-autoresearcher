## Contribution
Impossible differential cryptanalysis has shown to be a very powerful form of cryptanalysis against block ciphers. These attacks, even if extensively used, remain not fully understood because of their high technicality.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Indeed, numerous are the applications where mistakes have been discovered or where the attacks lack optimality.
- This paper aims in a first step at formalizing and improving this type of attacks and in a second step at applying our work to block ciphers based on the Feistel construction.
- In this context, we derive generic complexity analysis formulas for mounting such attacks and develop new ideas for optimizing impossible differential cryptanalysis.
- These ideas include for example the testing of parts of the internal state for reducing the number of involved key bits.
