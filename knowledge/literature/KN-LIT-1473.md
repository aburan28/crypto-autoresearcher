## Contribution
In this article we study Tamagawa numbers of elliptic curves defined over Q that have isogenies or torsion points. More precisely, our aim is either to bound the number of primes that can divide their Tamagawa numbers or, when such a bound is not possible, to find infinite subfamilies whose Tamagawa numbers are as small as possible.
