## Relevance to GOAL-ECTD-001 (pointer)

Under GRH, small-degree ordinary isogeny graphs are expanders, so ECDLP
difficulty is nearly uniform across an endomorphism-ring level. A viable
trapdoor needs a sparse weak set, likely across a hard vertical/cross-level
barrier rather than a broadly weak horizontal family.
