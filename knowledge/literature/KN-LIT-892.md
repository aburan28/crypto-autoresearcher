## Contribution
There has been a lot of effort to construct good quantum codes from the classical error correcting codes. Constructing new quantum codes, using Hermitian self-orthogonal codes, seems to be a difficult problem in general.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, Hermitian self-orthogonal codes are studied from algebraic function fields.
- Sufficient conditions for the Hermitian selforthogonality of an algebraic geometry code are presented.
- New Hermitian self-orthogonal codes are constructed from projective lines, elliptic curves, hyper-elliptic curves, Hermitian curves, and Artin-Schreier curves.
