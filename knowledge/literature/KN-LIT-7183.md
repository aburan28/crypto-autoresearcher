## Contribution
We study the problem of determining the groups that can arise as the torsion subgroup of an elliptic curve over a fixed quadratic field, building on work of Kamienny-Najman, Krumm, and Trbović. By employing techniques to study rational points on curves developed by Bruin and Stoll, we determine the possible √ torsion subgroups of elliptic curves over quadratic fields Q( d) for all squarefree d with |d| < 800, improving on the previously known range of −5 < d < 26.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We use our computations to study the validity of a conjecture of Granville concerning how many twists of a given hyperelliptic curve admit a nontrivial rational point.
