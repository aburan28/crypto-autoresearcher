## Contribution
In this paper we present a simpler construction of a publickey encryption scheme that achieves adaptive chosen ciphertext security (CCA2), assuming the existence of trapdoor permutations. We build on previous works of Sahai and De Santis et al. and construct a scheme that we believe is the easiest to understand to date.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In particular, it is only slightly more involved than the Naor-Yung encryption scheme that is secure against passive chosen-ciphertext attacks (CCA1).
- We stress that the focus of this paper is on simplicity only.
