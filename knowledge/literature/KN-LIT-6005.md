## Contribution
In this work, we present efficient public-key encryption schemes resilient against linear related key attacks (RKA) under standard assumptions and in the standard model. Specifically, we obtain encryption schemes based on hardness of factoring, BDDH and LWE that remain secure even against an adversary that may query the decryption oracle on linear shifts of the actual secret key.
