## Contribution
Let E and E ′ be two elliptic curves over a number field. We prove that the reductions of E and E ′ at a finite place p are geometrically isogenous for infinitely many p, and draw consequences for the existence of supersingular primes.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This result is an analogue for distributions of Frobenius traces of known results on the density of Noether-Lefschetz loci in Hodge theory.
- The proof relies on dynamical properties of the Hecke correspondences on the modular curve.
