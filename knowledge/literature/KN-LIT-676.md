## Contribution
We describe a systematic investigation into the existence of congruences between the mod p torsion modules of elliptic curves defined over Q, including methods to determine the symplectic type of such congruences. We classify the existence and symplectic type of mod p congruences between twisted elliptic curves over number fields, giving global symplectic criteria that apply in situations where the available local methods may fail.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We report on the results of applying our methods for all primes p ≥ 7 to the elliptic curves in the LMFDB database, which currently includes all elliptic curves of conductor less than 500 000.
- We also show that while such congruences exist for each p ≤ 17, there are none for p ≥ 19 in the database, in line with a strong form of the Frey-Mazur conjecture.
