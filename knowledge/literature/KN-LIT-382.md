## Contribution
Improving over an earlier construction by Kaye and Zalka [1], in [2] Maslov et al. describe an implementation of Shor’s algorithm, which can solve the discrete logarithm problem on ordinary binary elliptic curves in quadratic depth O(n2 ). In this paper we show that discrete logarithms on such curves can be found with a quantum circuit of depth O(log2 n).

## Key claims (as reported)
- As technical tools we introduce quantum circuits for F2n -multiplication in depth O(log n) and for F2n -inversion in depth O(log2 n).
