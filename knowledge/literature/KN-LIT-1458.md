## Contribution
For certain symmetric isogeny λ : A → A∨ of abelian varieties over a global field F , B. Rains put an orthogonal quadratic structure on H1 (AF , A[λ]) and realize the Selmer group Selλ (A) as an intersection of two maximal isotropic subspaces of H1 (AF , A[λ]).

## Key claims (as reported)
- With this understanding of Selmer groups, they expect to model the Selmer groups of elliptic curves and Jacobian varieties of hyperelliptic curves as the intersections of random maximal isotropic subspaces of orthogonal spaces.
- We extend this phenomenon to abelian varieties with multiplication and discuss the Shafarevich-Tate groups.
