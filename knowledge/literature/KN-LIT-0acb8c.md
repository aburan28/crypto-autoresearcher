## Contribution

Proves an upper bound on the time to construct a Groebner basis in terms of the degree of regularity of the leading forms. It bounds that degree w.h.p. for sufficiently overdetermined systems over any finite field, so such systems are solvable in polynomial time w.h.p. Relevant to over-determined (n-1 trick) decomposition systems.

## Key claims (as reported)

- Sufficiently overdetermined random systems: polynomial time w.h.p.
