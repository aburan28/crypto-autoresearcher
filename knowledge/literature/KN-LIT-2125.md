## Contribution
A collision search for a pair of n-bit unbalanced functions (one is R times more expensive than the other) is an instance of the meet-in-the-middle problem, solved with the familiar standard algorithm that follows the tradeoff T M = N , where T and M are time and memory complexities and N = 2n . By combining two ideas, unbalanced interleaving and van Oorschot-Wiener parallel collision search, we construct an alternative algorithm that follows T 2 M = R2 N , where M ≤ R.
