## Contribution
We prove a p-converse to the theorem of Gross–Zagier and Kolyvagin for elliptic curves E/Q at primes p > 3 of multiplicative reduction. Two key ingredients in the argument are an extension to this setting of a p-adic formula of Bertolini–Darmon–Prasanna obtained in our earlier work [Cas18a], and an exceptional zero formula for Heegner points.
