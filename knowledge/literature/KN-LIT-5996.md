## Contribution
We propose a general framework for differential and linear cryptanalysis of block ciphers when the block is not a bitstring. We prove piling-up lemmas for the generalized differential probability and the linear potential, and we study their lower bounds and average value, in particular in the case of permutations of Fp .

## Key claims (as reported)
- Using this framework, we describe a toy cipher, that operates on blocks of 32 decimal digits, and study its security against common attacks.
