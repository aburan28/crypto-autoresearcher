## Contribution
Let F be a number field unramified at an odd prime p and F∞ be the Zp -cyclotomic extension of F . Generalizing Kobayashi plus/minus Selmer groups for elliptic curves, Büyükboduk and Lei have defined modified Selmer groups, called signed Selmer groups, for certain non-ordinary Gal(F /F )-representations.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In particular, their construction applies to abelian varieties defined over F with good supersingular reduction at primes of F dividing p.
- Assuming that these Selmer groups are cotorsion Zp [[Gal(F∞ /F )]]modules, we show that they have no proper sub-Zp [[Gal(F∞ /F )]]-module of finite index.
- We deduce from this a number of arithmetic applications.
- On studying the Euler-Poincaré characteristic of these Selmer groups, we obtain an explicit formula on the size of the Bloch-Kato Selmer group attached to these representations.
