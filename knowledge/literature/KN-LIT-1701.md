## Contribution
The aim of this paper is to study the variation under quadratic twists of the analytic Iwasawa invariants of Sprung’s sharp/flat 2-adic L-functions for elliptic curves over Q with good supersingular reduction at 2. Under the hypothesis that the μ-invariant vanishes, we obtain an explicit difference formula for the sharp/flat λ-invariants.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This formula gives a supersingular analogue of Matsuno’s formula in the good ordinary case.
- As an application, following the method of Hatley–Ray, we obtain asymptotic lower bounds for the number of quadratic twists with prescribed sharp/flat 2-adic Iwasawa λ-invariant.
