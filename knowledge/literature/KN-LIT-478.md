## Contribution
Let E be a rational elliptic curve, and K be an imaginary quadratic field. In this article we give a method to construct Heegner points when E has a prime bigger than 3 of additive reduction ramifying in the field K.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The ideas apply to more general contexts, like constructing Darmon points attached to real quadratic fields which is presented in the appendix.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
