## Contribution
**The complexity of information set decoding** — an analysis of ISD as a
computational problem, from the direction of what the method fundamentally
costs rather than how to implement it faster.

## Relevance to this program
Asking what a *method* costs at its best, rather than what the current best
implementation costs, is the "method ceiling" check that section 8 of
`docs/inventor-protocol.md` (`KN-TECH-080`) requires of proof-oriented
proposals in this program **before** expensive experiments are approved.

This is an early published instance of that check, and it is worth citing as
precedent when the coordinator asks a proposal for its method ceiling.
