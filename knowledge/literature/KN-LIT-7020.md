## Contribution
We study a class of problems called Modular Inverse Hidden Number Problems (MIHNPs). ­ The basic ¡ problem in this ¢® class is the following: Given many pairs xi , msbk (α + xi )−1 mod p for random xi ∈ Zp the problem is to find α ∈ Zp (here msbk (x) refers to the k most significant bits of x). We describe an algorithm for this problem when k > (log2 p)/3 and conjecture that the problem is hard whenever k < (log2 p)/3.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that assuming hardness of some variants of this MIHNP problem leads to very efficient algebraic PRNGs and MACs.
