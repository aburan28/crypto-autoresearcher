## Contribution

Reformulates the ECDLP as intersections of quadric hypersurfaces, an alternative to summation polynomials, solved with F4. The authors do not claim speed over Shor or rho.

## Key claims (as reported)

- F4 experiments for primes < 500,000.

## Relevance to this program

Geometric/linear-algebra reformulation of ECDLP (ePrint 2019/1059); another 'new representation' proposal that does not change the exponent.
