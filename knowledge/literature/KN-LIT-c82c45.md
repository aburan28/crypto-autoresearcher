## Contribution

Applies Gaudry's abelian-variety index calculus to the trace-zero subgroup of E(F_{q^n}), treating the practically relevant degrees 3 and 5 with a prototype implementation and comparison to other algorithms. Relevant to any design (like ecGFp5) whose security relies on degree-5 extension-field hardness.
