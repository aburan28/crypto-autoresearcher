## Contribution

First sharp analysis of a non-trivial birthday attack: proves that Pollard's kangaroo method solves g^x = h for x uniform in [a,b] in expected (2+o(1))sqrt(b-a) steps, and that the bound holds for step sizes that are powers of any fixed n, resolving a conjecture of Pollard.
