## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve defined over Q. For a quadratic number field K and an odd prime number p, let L be a Zp -extension of K.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove that E(L)tors = E(K)tors when p > 5.
- It enables us to classify the groups that can be realized as the torsion subgroup E(L)tors , by using the classification of torsion subgroups over the quadratic fields.
