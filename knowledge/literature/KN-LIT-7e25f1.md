## Contribution

Two new ideas for collision-based multi-user attacks built on vOW parallel collision search. First, a graph on user keys whose edges are chain merges: once it is connected, all keys fall at once. Second, precomputation after which individual keys are learned cheaply. Applies to multi-target ECDLP and to symmetric constructions.
