## Contribution
Proves that the Néron–Tate height of a Heegner point on a modular elliptic
curve is proportional to the central derivative of the associated Rankin
L-series. When the analytic rank is one, this produces a rational point of
infinite order and supplies the height side of the Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer
prediction in that case.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Canonical heights of Heegner points equal (up to explicit factors) L'(f,1)
  for the attached newform / Rankin–Selberg L-series.
- For analytic rank 1, the Heegner construction yields a non-torsion
  Mordell–Weil generator.
- Keywords recorded by the primary index: Jacobian of the modular curve,
  modular elliptic curve, class-number problem, heights of Heegner points,
  Rankin L-series, BSD.

## Relevance to this program
Landmark linking modular curves, L-derivatives, and Néron–Tate heights.
Any ECDLP proposal that claims to extract discrete-log information from
Heegner points, central L-derivatives, or “BSD heights” is `known` relative
to this theorem’s existence — the open question is whether any such arithmetic
gives a sub-rho *computational* advantage on cryptographic finite-field
curves, which this paper does not address.
