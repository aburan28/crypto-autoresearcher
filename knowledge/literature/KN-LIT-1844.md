## Contribution
The classical Néron-Ogg-Shafarevich criterion characterises good reduction of an elliptic curve E over a p-adic field via the action of inertia on the l-adic Tate module. However, the action of inertia on E[l] is not sufficient to distinguish between potentially good and multiplicative reduction, and the action on Tl (E) is not sufficient to determine the Kodaira type.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We remedy this situation by endowing E[l] with a distance function that records the p-adic distances between the x-coordinates of the points.
- We show that, equipped with this additional structure, E[l] determines the Kodaira type of the elliptic curve.
- In the case of residue characteristic 2, we assume that E does not have potentially good reduction of type In∗ .

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
