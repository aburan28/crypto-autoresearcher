## Relevance to this program
Part of the record that ISD's development was driven **as much by coding theory
as by cryptanalysis**. Techniques crossed between the two communities in both
directions for years.

That is a live argument for this program's corpus policy: material from an
adjacent field with no immediate cryptanalytic framing is worth holding,
because the transfer direction is not predictable in advance.
