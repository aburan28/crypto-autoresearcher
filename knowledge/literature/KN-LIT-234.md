## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve over Q without complex multiplication, and which is not isogenous to a curve with non-trivial rational torsion. For each prime p of good reduction, let |E(Fp )| be the order of the group of points of the reduced curve over Fp .

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove in this paper that, under the GRH, there are at twin least 2.778CE x/(log x)2 primes p such that |E(Fp )| has at most 8 prime factors, counted with multiplicity.
- This improves previous results of Steuding & Weng [18] and Murty & Miri [13].
- This is also the first result where the dependence on twin the conjectural constant CE appearing in the twin prime conjecture for elliptic curves (also known as Koblitz’s conjecture) is made explicit.
- This is achieved by sieving a slightly different sequence than the one of [18] and [13].
