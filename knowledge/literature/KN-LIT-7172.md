## Contribution
There is a well-known class of message authentication systems guaranteeing that attackers will have a negligible chance of successfully forging a message. This paper shows how one of these systems can hash messages at extremely high speed—much more quickly than previous systems at the same security level—using IEEE floating-point arithmetic.
