## Contribution
MD5 is one of the most widely used cryptographic hash functions nowadays. It was designed in 1992 as an improvement of MD4, and its security was widely studied since then by several authors.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The best known result so far was a semi free-start collision, in which the initial value of the hash function is replaced by a non-standard value, which is the result of the attack.
- In this paper we present a new powerful attack on MD5 which allows us to find collisions efficiently.
- We used this attack to find collisions of MD5 in about 15 minutes up to an hour computation time.
- The attack is a differential attack, which unlike most differential attacks, does not use the exclusive-or as a measure of difference, but instead uses modular integer subtraction as the measure.
