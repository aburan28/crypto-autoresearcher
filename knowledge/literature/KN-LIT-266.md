## Contribution
We present a class of wild matrix problems (representations of boxes), which are “brick-tame,” i.e. only have oneparameter families of bricks (representations with trivial endomorphism algebra). This class includes several boxes that arise in study of simple vector bundles on degenerations of elliptic curves, as well as those arising from the coadjoint action of some linear groups.
