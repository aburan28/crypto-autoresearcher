## Contribution
The security analysis of Keccak, the winner of SHA-3, has attracted considerable interest. Recently, some attention has been paid to the analysis of keyed modes of Keccak sponge function.

## Key claims (as reported)
- As a notable example, the most efficient key recovery attacks on Keccak-MAC and Keyak were reported at EUROCRYPT’15 where cube attacks and cubeattack-like cryptanalysis have been applied.
- In this paper, we develop a new type of cube distinguisher, the conditional cube tester, for Keccak sponge function.
- By imposing some bit conditions for certain cube variables, we are able to construct cube testers with smaller dimensions.
- Our conditional cube testers are used to analyse Keccak in keyed modes.
