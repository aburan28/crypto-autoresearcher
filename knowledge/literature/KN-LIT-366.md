## Contribution
We present two algorithms that, given a prime l and an elliptic curve E/Fq , directly compute the polynomial Φl (j(E), Y ) ∈ Fq [Y ] whose roots are the j-invariants of the elliptic curves that are l-isogenous to E. We do not assume that the modular polynomial Φl (X, Y ) is given.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The algorithms may be adapted to handle other types of modular polynomials, and we consider applications to point counting and the computation of endomorphism rings.
- We demonstrate the practical efficiency of the algorithms by setting a new point-counting record, modulo a prime q with more than 5,000 decimal digits, and by evaluating a modular polynomial of level l = 100,019.
