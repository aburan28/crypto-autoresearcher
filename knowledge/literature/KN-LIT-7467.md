## Contribution
Verifiably encrypted signatures (VES) are signatures encrypted by a public key of a trusted third party and we can verify their validity without decryption. This paper proposes a new VES scheme which is secure under the decisional linear (DLIN) assumption in the standard model.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also propose new obfuscators for encrypted signatures (ES) and encrypted VES (EVES) which are secure under the DLIN assumption.
- All previous efficient VES schemes in the standard model are either secure under standard assumptions (such as the computational DiffieHellman assumption) with large verification (or secret) keys or secure under (non-standard) dynamic q-type assumptions (such as the q-strong Diffie-Hellman extraction assumption) with short verification keys.
- Our construction is the first efficient VES scheme with short verification (and secret) keys secure under a standard assumption (DLIN).
- As by-products of our VES scheme, we construct new obfuscators for ES/EVES based on our new VES scheme.
