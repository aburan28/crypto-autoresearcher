## Contribution
We propose a new basic trapdoor `IC (`-Invertible Cycles) of the mixed field type for Multivariate Quadratic public key cryptosystems. This is the first new basic trapdoor since the invention of Unbalanced Oil and Vinegar in 1997. `IC can be considered an extended form of the well-known Matsumoto-Imai Scheme A (also MIA or C ∗ ), and share some features of stagewise triangular systems.
