## Contribution
We construct a polynomial commitment scheme with constant (i.e., independent of the degree) sized evaluation proofs and logarithmic (in the degree) verification time in the transparent setting. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first result achieving this combination of properties.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We build our scheme from an inner product commitment scheme with constant-sized proofs but with linear verification time.
- To improve the verification time to logarithmic for polynomial commitments, we prove a new extremal combinatorial bound.
- Our constructions rely on groups of unknown order instantiated by class groups.
- We prove security of our constructions in the Generic Group Model.
