## Contribution
We show that every pair of 2-torsion elements in the Tate-Shafarevich group of an elliptic curve are visible in the same abelian surface. This was previously only known for a single 2-torsion element.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our result explains some of the original observations on visibility in the paper of Cremona and Mazur.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
