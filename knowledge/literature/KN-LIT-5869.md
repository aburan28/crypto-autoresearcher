## Contribution
The block cipher GOST 28147-89 was the Russian Federation encryption standard for over 20 years, and is still one of its two standard block ciphers. GOST is a 32-round Feistel construction, whose security benefits from the fact that the S-boxes used in the design are kept secret.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In the last 10 years, several attacks on the full 32-round GOST were presented.
- However, they all assume that the S-boxes are known.
- When the S-boxes are secret, all published attacks either target a small number of rounds, or apply for small sets of weak keys.
- In this paper we present the first practical-time attack on GOST with secret S-boxes.
