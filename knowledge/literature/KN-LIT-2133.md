## Contribution
In [6], Biryukov presented a new methodology of stream cipher design, called leak extraction. The stream cipher LEX, based on this methodology and on the AES block cipher, was selected to phase 3 of the eSTREAM competition.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The suggested methodology seemed promising, and LEX, due to its elegance, simplicity and performance was expected to be selected to the eSTREAM portfolio.
- In this paper we present a key recovery attack on LEX.
- The attack requires about 236.3 bytes of key-stream produced by the same key (possibly under many different IVs), and retrieves the secret key in time of 2112 simple operations.
- Following a preliminary version of our attack, LEX was discarded from the final portfolio of eSTREAM.
