## Contribution
Via a novel application of Iwasawa theory, we study Hilbert’s tenth problem for number fields occurring in Zp -towers of imaginary quadratic fields K. For a odd prime p, the lines (a, b) ∈ P1 (Zp ) are identified with Zp -extensions Ka,b /K.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Under certain conditions on K that involve explicit elliptic curves, we identify a line (a0 , b0 ) ∈ P1 (Z/pZ) such that for all (a, b) ∈ P1 (Zp ) with (a, b) 6≡ (a0 , b0 ) (mod p), Hilbert’s tenth problem has a negative answer in all finite layers of Ka,b .
- Using results of Kriz–Li and Bhargava et al., we demonstrate that for primes p = 3, 11, 13, 31, 37, a positive proportion of imaginary quadratic fields meet our criteria.
