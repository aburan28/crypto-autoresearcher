## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve defined over Q, and let p be an odd prime at which E has good ordinary reduction. Consider the p-primary Selmer group Selp∞ (Q∞ , E) over the cyclotomic Zp -extension of Q, and denote its (algebraic) μ-invariant by μp (E).

## Key claims (as reported)
- Let ρ̄E,p : Gal(Q̄/Q) → GL2 (Z/pZ) be the Galois representation arising from the action of the absolute Galois group on the p-torsion of E.
- Greenberg conjectured that if ρ̄E,p is reducible, then there exists a rational isogeny E → E ′ of p-power degree such that μp (E ′ ) = 0.
- In this article, we investigate this conjecture by establishing sufficient Galois-theoretic criteria for its validity, formulated in terms of the representation ρ̄E,p .
- Our approach relies on a fundamental result of Coates and Sujatha concerning the fine Selmer group.
