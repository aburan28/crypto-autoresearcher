## Contribution
In [Yin], the author constructed some CM points on the 2i elliptic curves Epi : y 2 = x3 + p4 for primes p ≡ 4, 7 mod 9 and i = 1, 2, which give rational points on the curves x3 + y 3 = pi . This solves the 4, 7 cases of Sylvester’s conjecture.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we prove the explicit Gross-Zagier formula relating the height of our CM points and the derivative of the L-functions of Epi .
