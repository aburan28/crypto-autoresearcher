## Contribution
Let p be an odd prime number and let K be an imaginary quadratic field in which p is split. Let f be a modular form with good reduction at p.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We study the variation of the Bloch–Kato Selmer groups and the Bloch–Kato–Shafarevich–Tate groups of f over the anticyclotomic Zp -extension K∞ of K.
- In particular, we show that under the generalized Heegner hypothesis, if the localization of the generalized Heegner cycle attached to f at one of the primes above p is primitive and certain local conditions hold, then the Pontryagin dual of the Selmer group of f over K∞ is free over the Iwasawa algebra.
- Consequently, the Bloch–Kato–Shafarevich–Tate groups of f vanish.
- This generalizes earlier works of Matar and Matar–Nekovář on elliptic curves.
