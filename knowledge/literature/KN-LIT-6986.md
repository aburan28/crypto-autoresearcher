## Contribution
We give the first demonstration of the cryptographic hardness of the Goldreich-Goldwasser-Micali (GGM) function family when the secret key is exposed. We prove that for any constant  > 0, the GGM family is a 1/n2+ -weakly one-way family of functions, when the lengths of secret key, inputs, and outputs are equal.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Namely, any efficient algorithm fails to invert GGM with probability at least 1/n2+ – even when given the secret key.
- Additionally, we state natural conditions under which the GGM family is strongly one-way.
