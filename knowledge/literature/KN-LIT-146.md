## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve having complex multiplication by a given quadratic order of an imaginary quadratic field K. The field of definition of E is the ring class field Ω of the order.

## Key claims (as reported)
- If the prime p splits completely in Ω, then we can reduce E modulo one the factors of p and get a curve E defined over Fp .
- The trace of the Frobenius of E is known up to sign and we need a fast way to find this sign.
- For this, we propose to use the action of the Frobenius on torsion points of small order built with class invariants à la Weber, in a manner reminiscent of the Schoof-Elkies-Atkin algorithm for computing the cardinality of a given elliptic curve modulo p.
- We apply our results to the Elliptic Curve Primality Proving algorithm (ECPP).
