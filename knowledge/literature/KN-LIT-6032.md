## Contribution
Solving a system of multivariate polynomials over a finite field is a promising problem in cryptography. Recently, Sakumoto et al. proposed public-key identification schemes based on the quadratic version of the problem, which is called the MQ problem.

## Key claims (as reported)
- However, it is still an open question whether or not it is able to build efficient constructions of public-key identification based on multivariate polynomials of degree greater than two.
- In this paper, we tackle the cubic case of this question and construct public-key identification schemes based on the cubic version of the problem, which is called the MC problem.
- The MQ problem is a special case of the MC problem.
- Our schemes consist of a protocol which is zero-knowledge argument of knowledge for the MC problem under the assumption of the existence of a non-interactive commitment scheme.
