## Contribution
We introduce the novel notion of a Proof of Human-work (PoH) and present the first distributed consensus protocol from hard Artificial Intelligence problems. As the name suggests, a PoH is a proof that a human invested a moderate amount of effort to solve some challenge.

## Key claims (as reported)
- A PoH puzzle should be moderately hard for a human to solve.
- However, a PoH puzzle must be hard for a computer to solve, including the computer that generated the puzzle, without sufficient assistance from a human.
- By contrast, CAPTCHAs are only difficult for other computers to solve — not for the computer that generated the puzzle.
- We also require that a PoH be publicly verifiable by a computer without any human assistance and without ever interacting with the agent who generated the proof of human-work.
