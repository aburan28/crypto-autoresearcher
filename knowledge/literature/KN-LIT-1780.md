## Contribution
The first purpose of this paper is to give the fnite transcendence of Frobenius traces for elliptic curves over Q without the assumption of complex multiplication (CM). This result generalizes the previous work by Luca and Zudilin, who obtained similar transcendence results specifically for the CM case.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The second purpose is to give the finite transcendence of Frobenius traces for several principally polarized abelian varieties over Q, by using Luca–Zudilin’s method.
