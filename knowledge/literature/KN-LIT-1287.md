## Contribution
We refine and generalize the results of K. Howe on principal polarizations on products of abelian varieties over finite fields.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Firstly, we study the reasons for the absence of an irreducible principal polarization in the isogeny class of the product of an ordinary and a supersingular abelian variety.
- Secondly, we provide a necessary condition for the existence of a principal polarization on an abelian variety in the isogeny class of the product of a geometrically simple abelian surface and an elliptic curve.
- As an application, we prove that this abelian threefold or its quadratic twist is a Jacobian.
