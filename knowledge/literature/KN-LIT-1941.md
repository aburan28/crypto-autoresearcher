## Contribution
In this paper, we consider two infinite parametric families of elliptic curves defined over Q ′ given by the equations Ea,b : y 2 = x3 − a2 x + b2 and Ea,b : y 2 = x3 − a2 x + b6 , where a, b ∈ N satisfy certain mild conditions. We prove that the torsion group of Ea,b (Q) is trivial and the Mordell-Weil ranks of both ′ Ea,b (Q) and Ea,b (Q) are at least 3 for infinitely many choices of a and b by using the Néron-Tate height √ of a rational point and by exploiting the unit group of the ring of integers of Q( 3).

## Key claims (as reported)
- This is an extension of the results of Brown-Myres and Fujita-Nara where lower bounds of the ranks were provided under the assumption that a = 1 or b = 1.
- Also, our families of elliptic curves vastly generalize the curves recently investigated by Hatley and Stack.
