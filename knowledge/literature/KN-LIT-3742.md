## Contribution
We propose a generalization of exTNFS algorithm recently introduced by Kim and Barbulescu (CRYPTO 2016). The algorithm, exTNFS, is a state-of-the-art algorithm for discrete logarithm in Fpn in the medium prime case, but it only applies when n = ηκ is a composite with nontrivial factors η and κ such that gcd(η, κ) = 1.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our generalization, however, shows that exTNFS algorithm can be also adapted to the setting with an arbitrary composite n maintaining its best asymptotic complexity.
