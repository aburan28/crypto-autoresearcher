## Contribution
We describe a short signature scheme which is existentially unforgeable under a chosen message attack without using random oracles. The security of our scheme depends on a new complexity assumption we call the Strong Diffie-Hellman assumption.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This assumption has similar properties to the Strong RSA assumption, hence the name.
- Strong RSA was previously used to construct signature schemes without random oracles.
- However, signatures generated by our scheme are much shorter and simpler than signatures from schemes based on Strong RSA.
- Furthermore, our scheme provides a limited form of message recovery.
