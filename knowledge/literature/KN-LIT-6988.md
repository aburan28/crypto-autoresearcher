## Contribution
In this paper we propose a new Delay-PUF architecture that is expected to solve the current problem of Delay-PUF that it is easy to predict the relation between delay information and generated information. Our architecture exploits glitches that behave non-linearly from delay variation between gates and the characteristic of pulse propagation of each gate.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We call this architecture Glitch PUF.
- In this paper, we present a concrete structure of Glitch PUF.
- We then show the evaluation results on the randomness and statistical properties of Glitch PUF.
- In addition, we present a simple scheme to evaluate Delay-PUFs by simulation at the design stage.
