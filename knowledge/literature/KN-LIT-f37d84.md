## Contribution
Considers spaces of modular forms attached to definite orthogonal groups of
low even rank and nontrivial level, equipped with Hecke operators defined
through Kneser neighbours. Reviews algorithms that compute with these spaces,
investigates endoscopy with theta series (theorem of Rallis), and exhibits
examples and conjectures. Two applications: (1) counts of Kneser neighbours
expressed through coefficients of classical or Siegel modular forms
(complementing Chenevier–Lannes), and (2) new instances of Eisenstein
congruences of Ramanujan-type.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Algorithms for computing Hecke operators/theta on definite orthogonal
  modular forms.
- Counts of Kneser neighbours relate to classical/Siegel coefficient
  functions; Eisenstein congruences obtained computationally.
