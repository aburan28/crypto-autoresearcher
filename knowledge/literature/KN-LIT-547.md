## Contribution
We explain a method for computing the Cassels-Tate pairing on the 3-isogeny Selmer groups of an elliptic curve. This improves the upper bound on the rank of the elliptic curve coming from a descent by 3-isogeny, to that coming from a full 3-descent.

## Key claims (as reported)
- One ingredient of our work is a new algorithm for solving cubic norm equations, that avoids the need for any S-unit computations.
- As an application, we show that the elliptic curves with torsion subgroup of order 3 and rank at least 13, found by Eroshkin, have rank exactly 13.
