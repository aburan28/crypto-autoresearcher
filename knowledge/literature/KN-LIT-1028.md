## Contribution
For an elliptic curve with complex multiplication over a number field, the p∞ –Selmer rank is even for all p. Česnavičius proved this using the fact that E admits a p-isogeny whenever p splits in the complex multiplication field, and invoking known cases of the p-parity conjecture. We give a direct proof, and generalise the result to abelian varieties.
