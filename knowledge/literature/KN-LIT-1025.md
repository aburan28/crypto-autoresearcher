## Contribution
The aim of the inverse Galois problem is to find extensions of a given field whose Galois group is isomorphic to a given group. In this article, we are interested in subgroups of GL2 (Z/nZ) where n is an integer.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We know that, in general, we can realize these groups as the Galois group of a given number field, using the torsion points on an elliptic curve.
- Specifically, a theorem of Reverter and Vila gives, for each prime n, a polynomial, depending on an elliptic curve, whose Galois group is GL2 (Z/nZ).
- In this article, we generalize this theorem in several directions, in particular for n non necessarily prime.
- We also determine a minimum for the valuations of the coefficients of the polynomials arising in our construction, depending only on n.
