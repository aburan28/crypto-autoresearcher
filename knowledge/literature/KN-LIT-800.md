## Contribution
The computation of the order of Frobenius action on the `-torsion is a part of Schoof-Elkies-Atkin algorithm for point counting on an elliptic curve E over a finite field Fq . The idea of Schoof’s algorithm is to compute the trace of Frobenius t modulo primes ` and restore it by the Chinese remainder theorem.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Atkin’s improvement consists of computing the order r of the Frobenius action on E[`] and of restricting the number t (mod `) to enumerate by using the formula t2 ≡ q(ζ + ζ −1 )2 (mod `).
- Here ζ is a primitive r-th root of unity.
- In this paper, we generalize Atkin’s formula to the general case of abelian variety of dimension g.
- Classically, finding of the order r involves expensive computation of modular polynomials.
