## Contribution
The defect of a curve over a finite field is the difference between the number of rational points on the curve and the Weil–Serre bound for the curve. We present a construction for producing genus-4 double covers of genus2 curves over finite fields such that the defect of the double cover is not much more than the defect of the genus-2 curve.

## Key claims (as reported)
