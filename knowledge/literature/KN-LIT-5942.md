## Contribution
Recently, proof of space (PoS) has been suggested as a more egalitarian alternative to the traditional hash-based proof of work. In PoS, a prover proves to a verifier that it has dedicated some specified amount of space.

## Key claims (as reported)
- A closely related notion is memory-hard functions (MHF), functions that require a lot of memory/space to compute.
- While making promising progress, existing PoS and MHF have several problems.
- First, there are large gaps between the desired space-hardness and what can be proven.
- Second, it has been pointed out that PoS and MHF should require a lot of space not just at some point, but throughout the entire computation/protocol; few proposals considered this issue.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
