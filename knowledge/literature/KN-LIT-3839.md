## Contribution
We describe two improvements to Gentry's fully homomor- phic scheme based on ideal lattices and its analysis: we provide a more aggressive analysis of one of the hardness assumptions (the one related to the Sparse Subset Sum Problem) and we introduce a probabilistic decryption algorithm that can be implemented with an algebraic circuit of low multiplicative degree. Combined together, these improvements lead to a faster fully homomorphic scheme, with a e O(λ3.5 ) bit complexity per elementary binary add/mult gate, where λ is the security parameter.

## Key claims (as reported)
- These improvements also apply to the fully homomorphic schemes of Smart and Vercauteren [PKC'2010] and van Dijk et al.
