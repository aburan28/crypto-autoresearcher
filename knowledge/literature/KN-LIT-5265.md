## Contribution
We show that indistinguishability obfuscation (IO) for all circuits can be constructed solely from secret-key functional encryption (SKFE). In the construction, SKFE need to be able to issue a-priori unbounded number of functional keys, that is, collusion-resistant.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our strategy is to replace public-key functional encryption (PKFE) in the construction of IO proposed by Bitansky and Vaikuntanathan (FOCS 2015) with puncturable SKFE.
- Bitansky and Vaikuntanathan introduced the notion of puncturable SKFE and observed that the strategy works.
- However, it has not been clear whether we can construct puncturable SKFE without assuming PKFE.
- In particular, it has not been known whether puncturable SKFE is constructed from ordinary SKFE.
