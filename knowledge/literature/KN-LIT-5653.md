## Contribution
Hypothesis tests have been used in the past as a tool in a cryptanalytic context. In this paper, we propose to use this paradigm and define a precise and sound statistical framework in order to optimally mix information on independent attacked subkey bits obtained from any kind of statistical cryptanalysis.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In the context of linear cryptanalysis, we prove that the best mixing paradigm consists of sorting key candidates by decreasing weighted Euclidean norm of the bias vector.
