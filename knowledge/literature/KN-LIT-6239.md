## Contribution
The GGH15 multilinear maps have served as the foundation for a number of cutting-edge cryptographic proposals. Unfortunately, many schemes built on GGH15 have been explicitly broken by so-called “zeroizing attacks,” which exploit leakage from honest zero-test queries.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The precise settings in which zeroizing attacks are possible have remained unclear.
- Most notably, none of the current indistinguishability obfuscation (iO) candidates from GGH15 have any formal security guarantees against zeroizing attacks.
- In this work, we demonstrate that all known zeroizing attacks on GGH15 implicitly construct algebraic relations between the results of zero-testing and the encoded plaintext elements.
- We then propose a “GGH15 zeroizing model” as a new general framework which greatly generalizes known attacks.
