## Contribution
We report on the design of Scream, a new software-efficient stream cipher, which was designed to be a “more secure SEAL”. Following SEAL, the design of Scream resembles in many ways a block-cipher design.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The new cipher is roughly as fast as SEAL, but we believe that it offers a significantly higher security level.
- In the process of designing this cipher, we re-visit the SEAL design paradigm, exhibiting some tradeoffs and limitations.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
