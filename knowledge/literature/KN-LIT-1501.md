## Contribution
Half a century ago Manin showed that given a number field k and a rational prime l, there exists a uniform bound for the order of cyclic l-power isogenies between two non-CM elliptic curves over k. We generalize this to certain 2-dimensional families of abelian 3-folds with multiplication by an imaginary quadratic field.
