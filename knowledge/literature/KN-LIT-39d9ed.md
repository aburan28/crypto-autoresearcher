## Contribution

An explicit, constructive `(2,2,2)`-isogeny `φ : J_H → J_X` from the Jacobian of
a *hyperelliptic* genus-3 curve `H/F_q` to the Jacobian of a genus-3 curve `X`
that is, with overwhelming probability, *non-hyperelliptic* (a plane quartic
`C ⊂ P^2`). Because `φ(Q) = [m] φ(P)` whenever `Q = [m]P`, the DLP transfers,
and the transferred instance is attacked by a strictly cheaper index calculus.

## Key claims (as reported)

- Index-calculus cost separates the two genus-3 geometries: `Õ(q^{4/3})` on
  hyperelliptic genus-3 Jacobians (Gaudry–Thomé–Thériault–Diem) versus `Õ(q)` on
  non-hyperelliptic ones (Diem). `H` and `C` are never isomorphic, so an
  algorithm cannot simply be ported; a **homomorphism** is what moves the problem.
- Oort–Ueno: every 3-dimensional PPAV is isomorphic over `F_{q^2}` to a Jacobian.
  Hence quotients of `J_H` by small subgroups are again Jacobians, and a generic
  such quotient is non-hyperelliptic.
- For almost all kernels no explicit construction of the isogeny is known. The
  talk supplies a general construction for the **tractable subgroups**
  `S = <[W_1 - W_2], [W_3 - W_4], [W_5 - W_6], [W_7 - W_8]> ≅ (Z/2Z)^3`
  generated by differences of the eight Weierstrass points of `H : y^2 = F(x,z)`.
- Mechanism: choose a trigonal `g : P^1 → P^1` with `g(W_i) = g(W_{i+1})` for each
  generator of `S`. Recillas' **trigonal construction** applied to the canonical
  map `π : H → P^1` and to `g` yields a genus-3 curve `X` with a 4-to-1 map
  `f : X → P^1`; Donagi–Livné give the isogeny `φ : J_H → J_X` with `ker φ = S`.
  The four points of `f^{-1}(Q)` are the four ways of splitting
  `(g∘π)^{-1}(Q) = {P_1,P_2,P_3, ι(P_1), ι(P_2), ι(P_3)}` into complementary
  triples.

## What is structurally new here relative to the corpus

The corpus already carries Recillas/Prym realisation as a *rejected* idea
(`ECDLP-IDEA-061`, merged into `TRANSFER-H008/PO96` and active `002`). The
distinct content of this source is not "realise a Prym as a Jacobian" but that
**the kernel is selected from the ramification/branch data of the base map**, so
the isogeny is computed by a construction on `P^1` rather than by Vélu/Kohel on
the curve, and the target lands in a moduli stratum with a *different index-calculus
exponent*. That is a representation change that moves the exponent, not the group.

## Relevance

- Motivates `ECDLP-IDEA-434`: within a fixed prime-field isogeny class the group
  and its order are constant, so the analogous question is whether the *Semaev
  decomposition yield* is an isogeny-class invariant or a curve invariant. That
  question is a new axis for `KN-FIND-007` (yield conservation under factor-base
  geometry), which was established at fixed curve.
- The genus-1 specialisation of the tractable-subgroup mechanism is closed by
  this program in `KN-TECH-3b593f`: on an elliptic curve the Weierstrass points
  *are* `E[2]`, so the Weierstrass-difference subgroup is `E[2]` itself and the
  quotient is an ordinary 2-isogeny with no exponent change.
- No breakthrough content and no prime-field consequence. Applies to genus-3
  Jacobians over `F_q`; `LITERATURE-DERIVED`.
