## Contribution
In this work, we propose a new public-key cryptosystem whose security is based on the computational intractability of the following problem: Given a Mersenne number p = 2n − 1 , where n is a prime, a positive integer h , and two n -bit integers T, R , decide whether their exist n -bit integers F, G each of Hamming weight less than h such that T = F · R + G modulo p .
