## Contribution
We prove that the family IF0 of elliptic curves over number fields that are geometrically isogenous to an elliptic curve with F0 -rational j-invariant is typically bounded in torsion. Under an additional uniformity assumption, we also prove that the family Id0 of elliptic curves over number fields that are geometrically isogenous to an elliptic curve with degree d0 j-invariant is typically bounded in torsion.
