## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve without CM that is defined over a number field K. For all but finitely many nonarchimedean places v of K there is the reduction E(v) of E at v that is an elliptic curve over the residue field k(v) at v.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The set of v’s with ordinary E(v) has density 1 (Serre).
- For such v the endomorphism ring End(E(v)) of E(v) is an order in an imaginary quadratic field.
- We prove that for any pair of relatively prime positive integers N and M there are infinitely many nonarchimedean places v of K such that the discrim∆(v) inant ∆(v) of End(E(v)) is divisible by N and the ratio N is relatively prime to N M .
- We also discuss similar questions for reductions of abelian varieties.
