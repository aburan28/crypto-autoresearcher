## Contribution

Two low-storage interval-DLP algorithms: a four-kangaroo method and a Gaudry-Schost-based method, with experiments matching the heuristics. The ePrint version corrects the proof of Theorem 6.

## Key claims (as reported)

- 4-kangaroo: (1.715+o(1))sqrt(N); Gaudry-Schost variant: (1.661+o(1))sqrt(N); baseline vOW kangaroo (2+o(1))sqrt(N).

## Relevance to this program

Four-kangaroo and Gaudry–Schost refinements (~1.71√N) for interval DLP; baseline for interval-search engineering.
