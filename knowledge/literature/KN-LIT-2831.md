## Contribution
This paper analyzes the exact extent to which 0 and ∞ cause trouble in Montgomery’s fast branchless formulas for x-coordinate scalar multiplication on elliptic curves of the form by 2 = x3 + ax2 + x. The analysis shows that some multiplications and branches can be eliminated from elliptic-curve primality proofs and from elliptic-curve cryptography.
