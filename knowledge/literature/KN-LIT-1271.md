## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve defined over Q with good ordinary reduction at a prime p ≥ 5, and let F be an imaginary quadratic field. Under appropriate assumptions, we show that the Pontryagin dual of the fine Mordell-Weil group of E over the Z2p -extension of F is pseudo-null as a module over the Iwasawa algebra of the group Z2p .
