## Contribution
In Private Information Retrieval (PIR), a client wishes to access an index i from a public n-bit database without revealing any information about i. Recently, a series of works starting with the seminal paper of Corrigan-Gibbs and Kogan (EUROCRYPT 2020) considered PIR with client preprocessing and no additional server storage.
