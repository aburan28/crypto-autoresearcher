## Contribution
We give a function F (d, n, p) such that if K/Qp is a degree n field extension and A/K is a d-dimensional abelian variety with potentially good reduction, then #A(K)[tors] ≤ F (d, n, p). Separate attention is given to the prime-to-p torsion and to the case of purely additive reduction.

## Key claims (as reported)
- These latter bounds are applied to classify rational torsion on CM elliptic curves over number fields of degree at most 3, on elliptic curves over Q with integral j (recovering a theorem of Frey), and on abelian surfaces over Q with integral moduli.
- In the last case, our efforts leave us with 11 numbers which may, or may not, arise as the order of the full torsion subgroup.
- The largest such number is 72.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
