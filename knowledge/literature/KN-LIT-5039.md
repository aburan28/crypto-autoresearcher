## Contribution
Differential cryptanalysis is a well-known statistical attack on block ciphers. We present here a generalisation of this attack called multiple differential cryptanalysis.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We study the data complexity, the time complexity and the success probability of such an attack and we experimentally validate our formulas on a reduced version of PRESENT.
- Finally, we propose a multiple differential cryptanalysis on 18-round PRESENT for both 80-bit and 128-bit master keys.
