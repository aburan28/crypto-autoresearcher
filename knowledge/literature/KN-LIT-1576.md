## Contribution
A field in which the (logarithmic) Weil height is bounded from below by a strictly positive constant is said to have the Bogomolov property (property (B)). Given a normalized eigenform f ∈ Sk (Γ0 (N )) Amoroso and Terracini [AT25] proved (B) for the field “cut out” by the adelic representation associated to f under some assumptions on f , generalizing the earlier work of Habegger [Hab13] on elliptic curves.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper we extend this result to the case of normalized eigenforms with nontrivial nebentypus character.
- We also introduce the notion of ADZ field, inspired by earlier work of Amoroso, David, and Zannier [ADZ14], exhibiting a class of fields in which property (B) is preserved under (arbitrary) composition.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
