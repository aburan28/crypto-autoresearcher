## Key claims (as reported)
- Rational secret sharing is a fundamental primitive at the intersection of cryptography and game theory.
- In essence, a dealer wishes to engineer a communication game that, when rationally played, guarantees that each of the players learns the dealer’s secret.
- Yet, all solutions so far were quite inefficient and relied on the players’ beliefs and not just on their rationality.
- After providing a more complete definition of this problem, we exhibit a very efficient and purely rational solution to it with a verifiable trusted channel.
