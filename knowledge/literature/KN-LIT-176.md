## Contribution
We work out the complete descent via 4-isogeny for a family of rational elliptic curves with a rational point√of order 4; such a family is of the form y 2 + x y + a y = x3 + a x2 where −a ∈ Q× . In the process we exhibit the 4-isogeny and the isogenous curve, explicitly present the principal homogeneous spaces, and discuss examples by computing the rank.
