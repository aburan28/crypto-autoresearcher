## Contribution
Given an elliptic curve with Weierstrass equation y 2 = f (x), and a positive definite binary quadratic form Q(u, v). We show that there are infinitely many d in the set represented by the quadratic forms in the genus of Q such that the twisted elliptic curve dy 2 = f (x) has analytic rank one.
