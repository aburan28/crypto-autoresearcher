## Contribution
We develop a graph-theoretic algorithm to compute the φ-Selmer group of the elliptic curve Eb : y 2 = x3 + bx over Q(i), where b ∈ Z[i] and φ is a degree 2 isogeny of Eb . We associate to Eb a weighted graph Gb , whose vertices are the odd Gaussian primes dividing b, and whose edge weights are determined by the quartic residue symbol between pairs of these primes.

## Key claims (as reported)
- By applying our algorithm, we explicitly compute the φ-Selmer group of Eb when b is a product of inert primes, and we construct several infinite families of elliptic curves over Q(i) with trivial Mordell–Weil rank.
