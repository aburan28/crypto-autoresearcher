## Contribution
Multidimensional linear attacks are one of the most powerful variants of linear cryptanalytic techniques now. However, there is no knowledge on the key-dependent capacity and data complexity so far.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Their values were assumed to be close to the average value for a vast majority of keys.
- This assumption is not accurate.
- In this paper, under a reasonable condition, we explicitly formulate the capacity as a Gamma distribution and the data complexity as an Inverse Gamma distribution, in terms of the average linear probability and the dimension.
- The capacity distribution is experimentally verified on the 5-round PRESENT.
