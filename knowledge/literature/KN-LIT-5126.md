## Contribution
This paper proposes several approaches to improve the collision attack on MD4 proposed by Wang et al. First, we propose a new local collision that is the best for the MD4 collision attack.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Selection of a good message difference is the most important step in achieving effective collision attacks.
- This is the first paper to introduce an improvement to the message difference approach of Wang et al., where we propose a new local collision.
- Second, we propose a new algorithm for constructing differential paths.
- While similar algorithms have been proposed, they do not support the new local collision technique.
