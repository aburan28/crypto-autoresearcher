## Contribution
We show that, up to a natural equivalence relation, the only non-trivial, non-identity holomorphic maps Conf n C → Conf m C between unordered configuration spaces, where m ∈ {3, 4}, are the resolving quartic map R : Conf 4 C → Conf 3 C, a map Ψ3 : Conf 3 C → Conf 4 C constructed from the inflection points of elliptic curves in a family, and Ψ3 ◦R. This completes the classification of holomorphic maps Conf n C → Conf m C for m ≤ n, extending results of Lin, Chen and Salter, and partially resolves a conjecture of Farb.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also classify the holomorphic families of elliptic curves over Conf n C.
- To do this we classify homomorphisms between braid groups with few strands and PSL2 Z, then apply powerful results from complex analysis and Teichmüller theory.
- Furthermore, we prove a conjecture of Castel about the equivalence classes of endomorphisms of the braid group with three strands.
