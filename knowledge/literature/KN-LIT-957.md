## Contribution
We prove non-trivial upper bounds for general bilinear forms with trace functions of bountiful sheaves, where the supports of two variables can be arbitrary subsets in Fp of suitable sizes. This essentially recovers the Pólya– Vinogradov range, and also applies to symmetric powers of Kloosterman sums and Frobenius traces of elliptic curves.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In the case of hyper-Kloosterman sums, we can beat the Pólya–Vinogradov barrier by combining additive combinatorics with a deep result of Kowalski, Michel and Sawin on sum-products of Kloosterman sheaves.
- Two Sato–Tate distributions of Kloosterman sums and Frobenius traces of elliptic curves in sparse families are also concluded.
