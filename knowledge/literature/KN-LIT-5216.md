## Contribution
We construct, under standard hardness assumptions, the first nonmalleable commitments secure against quantum attacks. Our commitments are statistically binding and satisfy the standard notion of non-malleability with respect to commitment.

## Key claims (as reported)
- (λ)-round classical protocol, assuming the existence of post-quantum one-way functions.
- Previously, non-malleable commitments with quantum security were only known against a restricted class of adversaries known as synchronizing adversaries.
- At the heart of our results is a new general technique that allows to modularly obtain non-malleable commitments from any extractable commitment protocol, obliviously of the underlying extraction strategy (black-box or non-black-box) or round complexity.
- The transformation may also be of interest in the classical setting.
