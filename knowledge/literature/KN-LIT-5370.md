## Contribution
We show equivalence between the existence of one-way functions and the existence of a sparse language that is hard-on-average w.r.t. some efficiently samplable “high-entropy” distribution. In more detail, the following are equivalent: – The existence of a S(·)-sparse language L that is hard-on-average with respect to some samplable distribution with Shannon entropy h(·) such that h(n) − log(S(n)) ≥ 4 log n; – The existence of a S(·)-sparse language L ∈ NP, that is hard-onaverage with respect to some samplable distribution with Shannon entropy h(·) such that h(n) − log(S(n)) ≥ n/3; – The existence of one-way functions. where a language L is said to be S(·)-sparse if |L ∩ {0, 1}n | ≤ S(n) for all n ∈ N.
