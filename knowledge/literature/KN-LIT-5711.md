## Contribution
Password-based authenticated key exchange (PAKE) allows two parties with a shared password to agree on a session key. In the last decade, the design of PAKE protocols from lattice assumptions has attracted lots of attention.

## Key claims (as reported)
- However, existing solutions in the standard model do not have appealing efficiency.
- In this work, we first introduce a new PAKE framework.
- We then provide two realizations in the standard model, under the Learning With Errors (LWE) and Ring-LWE assumptions, respectively.
- Our protocols are much more efficient than previous proposals, thanks to three novel technical ingredients that may be of independent interests.
