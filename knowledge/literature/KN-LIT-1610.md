## Contribution
We show that up to isomorphism there are exactly twenty pairs (C, E), where C is a genus-2 curve over C, where E is an elliptic curve over C, and where for every integer n > 1 there is a map of degree n from C to E. We also show that the intersection of the Humbert surfaces Hn2 , where n ranges from 2 to 1811, is empty.
