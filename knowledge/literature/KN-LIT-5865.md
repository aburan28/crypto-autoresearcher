## Contribution
This paper addresses the security of symmetric cryptosystems in the blockwise adversarial model. At Crypto 2002, Joux, Martinet and Valette have proposed a new kind of attackers against several symmetric encryption schemes.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we first show a generic technique to thwart blockwise adversaries for a specific class of encryption schemes.
- It consists in delaying the output of the ciphertext block.
- Then we provide the first security proof for the CFB encryption scheme, which is naturally immune against such attackers.
