## Contribution
We study the Iwasawa theory of a CM elliptic curve E in the anticyclotomic Zp extension D∞ of the CM field K, where p is a prime of good, supersingular reduction for E. Our main result yields an asymptotic formula for the corank of the p-primary Selmer group of E along the extension D∞ /K.
