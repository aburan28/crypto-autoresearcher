## Contribution
Multivariate Quadratic Public Key Schemes (MQPKS) attracted the attention of researchers in the last decades for two reasons. First they are thought to resist attacks by quantum computers and second, most of the schemes were broken.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The latter may be the reason why implementations are rare.
- This work investigates one of the most promising member of MQPKS and its variants, namely UOV, Rainbow and enTTS.
- UOV resisted all kinds of attacks for 13 years and can be considered one of the best examined MQPKS.
- We describe implementations of UOV, Rainbow and enTTS on an 8-bit microcontroller.
