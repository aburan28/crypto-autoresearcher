## Contribution
We present the impossible differential cryptanalysis of the block cipher XTEA[7] and TEA[6]. The core of the design principle of these block ciphers is an easy implementation and a simplicity.

## Key claims (as reported)
- But this simplicity dose not offer a large diffusion property.
- Our impossible differential cryptanalysis of reduced-round versions of XTEA and TEA is based on this fact.
- We will show how to construct a 12-round impossible characteristic of XTEA.
- We can then derive 128-bit user key of the 14round XTEA with 262.5 chosen plaintexts and 285 encryption times using the 12-round impossible characteristic.
