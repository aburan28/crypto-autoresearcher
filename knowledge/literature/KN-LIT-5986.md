## Contribution
We propose a new unidirectional proxy re-encryption scheme based on the hardness of the LWE problem. Our construction is collusionsafe and does not require any trusted authority for the re-encryption key generation.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We extend a recent trapdoor definition for a lattice of Micciancio and Peikert.
- Our proxy re-encryption scheme is provably CCA-1 secure in the selective model under the LWE assumption.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
