## Contribution
Classically, selective-opening attack (SOA) has been studied for randomized primitives, like randomized encryption schemes and commitments. The study of SOA for deterministic primitives, which presents some unique challenges, was initiated by Bellare et al.

## Key claims (as reported)
- (PKC 2015), who showed negative results.
- Subsequently, Hoang et al.
- (ASIACRYPT 2016) showed positive results in the non-programmable random oracle model.
- Here we show the first positive results for SOA security of deterministic primitives in the standard (RO devoid) model.
