## Contribution
Let E be a rational elliptic curve. We generalize a theorem due to Avcı[Avc26b], which asserts that for any quadratic field K and prime p > 5, the equality E(K)tors = E(L)tors holds for every Zp -extension L/K.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we consider the setting where the Zp -extension L is replaced by the compositum K∞ of all Zp -extensions of K.
- Under this new setting, we prove the analogous statement for p = 5, and further provide partial results for the remaining primes p = 3 and p = 2. arXiv:2607.13514v1 [math.NT] 15 Jul 2026 Contents
