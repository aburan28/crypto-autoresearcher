## Contribution
Homomorphic secret sharing (HSS) allows n clients to secret-share data to m servers, who can then homomorphically evaluate public functions over the shares. A natural application is outsourced computation over private data.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this work, we present the first plain-model homomorphic secret sharing scheme that supports the evaluation of polynomials with degree higher than 2.
- Our construction relies on any degree-k (multi-key) homomorphic encryption scheme and can evaluate degree-((k + 1)m − 1) polynomials, for any polynomial number of inputs n and any sub-logarithmic (in the security parameter) number of servers m.
- At the heart of our work is a series of combinatorial arguments on how a polynomial can be split into several low-degree polynomials over the shares of the inputs, which we believe is of independent interest.
