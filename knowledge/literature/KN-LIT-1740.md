## Contribution
In an article published a few years before the modularity of elliptic curves over Q was proved, Mazur [Maz91] looked at modularity as a purely complex analytic phenomenon, defining a notion of an elliptic curve over Q having a hyperbolic uniformisation of arithmetic type. Such an elliptic curve (of conductor N , say) is necessarily geometrically modular, i.e. a quotient of the jacobian of the modular curve X0 (N ), by a morphism defined over Q.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We extend these ideas to elliptic curves over totally real fields of odd degree, using Shimura curves for quaternion algebras split at all finite places and one real place.
- In particular, we prove that the existence of a hyperbolic uniformisation of arithmetic type would imply geometric modularity.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
