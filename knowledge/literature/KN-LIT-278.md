## Contribution
In this work, we determine all Lattès maps which are Belyi morphisms. It turns out that in the generic case, i.e. when the automorphism group is Z/2Z, the corresponding family of Lattès maps are Belyi morphisms if and only if the isogeny is multiplication by two.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This family form a continuous family of Belyi maps.
- Elliptic curves with complex multiplication also determine a family over Z of Belyi morphisms.
- We give the explicit formulas for the first few Belyi morphisms when the curve has complex multiplication by 3rd root of unity.
