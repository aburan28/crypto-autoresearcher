## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve over Q with prime conductor p. For each non-negative integer n we put Kn := Q(E[pn ]).

## Key claims (as reported)
- The aim of this paper is to estimate the order of the p-Sylow group of the ideal class group of Kn .
- We give a lower bound in terms of the Mordell-Weil rank of E(Q).
- As an application of our result, we give an example such that p2n divides the class number of the field Kn in the case of p = 5077 for each positive integer n.
