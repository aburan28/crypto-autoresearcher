## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve defined over Q without complex multiplication. The field F generated over Q by all torsion points of E is an infinite, nonabelian Galois extension of the rationals which has unbounded, wild ramification above all primes.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove that the absolute logarithmic Weil height of an element of F is either zero or bounded from below by a positive constant depending only on E.
- We also show that the Néron-Tate height has a similar gap on E(F ) and use this to determine the structure of the group E(F ).

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
