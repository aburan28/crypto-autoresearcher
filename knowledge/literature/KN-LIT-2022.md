## Contribution
We describe a hardware device for supporting the sieving step in integer factoring algorithms like the quadratic sieve or the number field sieve. In analogy to Bernstein’s proposal for speeding up the linear algebra step, we rely on a mesh of very simple processing units.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Manufacturing the device at moderate cost with current hardware technology on standard wafers with 200 mm or 300 mm diameter should not provide any major obstacle.
- A preliminary analysis of the parameters for factoring a 512-bit number with the number field sieve shows that the design considered here might outperform a TWINKLE device.
