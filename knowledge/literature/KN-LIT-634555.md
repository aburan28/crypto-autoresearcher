## Contribution

Combines GHS Weil descent (E/F_{p^6} to a genus-3 curve over F_{p^2}) with Nagao-style under-determined decomposition, sieving and right-kernel linear algebra. It gives a working attack on a curve over a sextic extension that looked secure. This remains the flagship practical index-calculus ECDLP computation.

## Key claims (as reported)

- DL in a 149-bit prime-order subgroup of E(F_{p^6}) (p ~ 2^25) in about one month real time, ~110,000 CPU-hours (relation search ~62 h on 1024 cores; Lanczos ~28.5 days on 64 cores). A 160-bit group is estimated at ~200 years on one core.

## Relevance to this program

Cover + decomposition IC made practical: a 149-bit subgroup of E(F_{p^6}) in ~110,000 CPU-hours (the largest practical algebraic ECDLP computation found). Baseline for any 'practical IC record' claim.
