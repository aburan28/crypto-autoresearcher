## Contribution
Let S2 be the p-primary second Morava stabilizer group, C a supersingular elliptic curve over Fp , O the ring of endomorphisms of C, and l a topological generator of × × Z× p (respectively Z2 /{±1} if p = 2). We show that for p > 2 the group Γ ⊆ O[1/l] of quasi-endomorphisms of degree a power of l is dense in S2 .

## Key claims (as reported)
- For p = 2, we show that Γ is dense in an index 2 subgroup of S2 .
