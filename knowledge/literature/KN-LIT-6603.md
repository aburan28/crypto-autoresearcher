## Contribution
There has been a lot of recent progress in constructing efficient zero-knowledge proofs for showing knowledge of an ~ s with small coefficients satisfying A~ s = ~t. For typical parameters, the proof sizes have gone down from several megabytes to a bit under 50KB (Esgin et al., Asiacrypt 2020).

## Key claims (as reported)
- These are now within an order of magnitude of the sizes of lattice-based signatures, which themselves constitute proof systems which demonstrate knowledge of something weaker than the aforementioned equation.
- One can therefore see that this line of research is approaching optimality.
- In this paper, we modify a key component of these proofs, as well as apply several other tweaks, to achieve a further reduction of around 30% in the proof output size.
- We also show that this savings propagates itself when these proofs are used in a general framework to construct more complex protocols.

## Relevance to this program
