## Contribution
Let p and q be distinct prime numbers, with q ” 1 pmod 12q. Let N be a positive integer that is coprime to pq.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove a formula relating the Hasse–Weil zeta function of the modular curve X0 pqN qFq to the Ihara zeta function of the p-isogeny graph of supersingular elliptic curves defined over Fq equipped with a Γ0 pN q-level structure.
- When N “ 1, this recovers a result of Sugiyama.
