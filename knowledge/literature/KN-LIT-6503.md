## Contribution
Code-based cryptography is often viewed as an interesting “Post-Quantum” alternative to the classical number theory cryptography. Unlike many other such alternatives, it has the convenient advantage of having only a few, well identified, attack algorithms.

## Key claims (as reported)
- However, improvements to these algorithms have made their effective complexity quite complex to compute.
- We give here some lower bounds on the work factor of idealized versions of these algorithms, taking into account all possible tweaks which could improve their practical complexity.
- The aim of this article is to help designers select durably secure parameters.

## Relevance to this program
