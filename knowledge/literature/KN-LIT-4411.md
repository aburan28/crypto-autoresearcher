## Contribution
Identity-based cryptography is form of public-key cryptography that does not require users to pre-compute key pairs and obtain certificates for their public keys. Instead, public keys can be arbitrary identifiers such as email addresses.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This means that the corresponding private keys are derived, at any time, by a trusted private key generator.
- The idea of signcryption is to provide a method to encrypt and sign data together in a way that is more efficient than using an encryption scheme combined with a signature scheme.
- We present an identity-based signcryption solution that we believe is the most efficient, provably-secure scheme of its type proposed to date.
- Our scheme admits proofs of security in the random oracle model under the bilinear Diffie-Hellman assumption using the definitions proposed by Boyen.
