## Contribution
We give an alternative derivation of (N, N )-isogenies between fast Kummer surfaces which complements existing works based on the theory of theta functions. We use this framework to produce explicit formulæ for the case of N = 3, and show that the resulting algorithms are more efficient than all prior (3, 3)-isogeny algorithms.
