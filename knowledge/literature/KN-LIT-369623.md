## Contribution

Proves a birthday paradox for self-intersections of Markov chains. Applied to rho with a random-oracle partition, a collision occurs in Theta(sqrt|G|) steps w.h.p., and the parallel distinguished-point version on J processors needs Theta(sqrt|G|/J) steps. These were the first correct-order bounds that do not assume i.i.d. steps. Refinement: S. Kijima, R. Montenegro, "Collision of random walks and a refined analysis of attacks on the discrete logarithm problem", PKC 2015, LNCS 9020, pp. 127-149, doi:10.1007/978-3-662-46447-2_6.

## Key claims (as reported)

- Theta(sqrt|G|) steps; Theta(sqrt|G|/J) with J processors.

## Relevance to this program

Rigorous Θ(√|G|) bound for rho with a random-oracle partition; the reference for any claim that a walk 'provably' collides within the birthday bound. Heuristic walk constants measured by this program do not contradict or improve it.
