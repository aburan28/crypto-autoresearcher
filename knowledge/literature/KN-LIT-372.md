## Contribution
Let p be a prime and let C be a genus one curve over a number field k representing an element of order dividing p in the Shafarevich-Tate group of its Jacobian. We describe an algorithm which computes the set of D in the Shafarevich-Tate group such that pD = C and obtains explicit models for these D as curves in projective space.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This leads to a practical algorithm for performing explicit 9-descents on elliptic curves over Q.
