## Contribution
We study l-isogeny graphs of ordinary elliptic curves defined over Fq with an added level structure. Given an integer N coprime to p and l, we look at the graphs obtained by adding Γ0 (N ), Γ1 (N ), and Γ(N )-level structures to volcanoes.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Given an order O in an imaginary quadratic field K, we look at the action of generalised ideal class groups of O on the set of elliptic curves whose endomorphism rings are O along with a given level structure.
- We show how the structure of the craters of these graphs is determined by the choice of parameters.
