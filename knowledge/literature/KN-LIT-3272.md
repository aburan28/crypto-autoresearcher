## Contribution
In the conference PKC’98, Shin et al. proposed a dedicated hash function of the MD family. In this paper, we study the security of Shin’s hash function.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We analyze the property of the Boolean functions, the message expansion, and the data dependent rotations of the hash function.
- We propose a method for finding the collisions of the modified Shin’s hash function and show that we can find collisions with probability 2−30 .
