## Contribution
Let f : P1 → P1 be a map of degree > 1 defined over a function field k = K(X), where K is a number field and X is a projective curve over K. For each point a ∈ P1 (k) satisfying a dynamical stability condition, we prove that the Call-Silverman canonical height for specialization ft at point at , for t ∈ X(Q) outside a finite set, induces a Weil height on the curve X; i.e., we prove the existence of a Q-divisor D = Df,a on X so that the function t 7→ ĥft (at ) − hD (t) is bounded on X(Q) for any choice of Weil height associated to D.

- We also prove a local version, that the local canonical heights t 7→ λ̂ft ,v (at ) differ from a Weil function for D by a continuous function on X(Cv ), at each place v of the number field K.
- These results were known for polynomial maps f and all points a ∈ P1 (k) without the stability hypothesis [21, 14], and for maps f that are quotients of endomorphisms of elliptic curves E over k and all points a ∈ P1 (k) [32, 29].
