## Contribution
This paper compares the XL algorithm with known Gröbner basis algorithms. We show that to solve a system of algebraic equations via the XL algorithm is equivalent to calculate the reduced Gröbner basis of the ideal associated with the system.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Moreover we show that the XL algorithm is also a Gröbner basis algorithm which can be represented as a redundant variant of a Gröbner basis algorithm F4 .
- Then we compare these algorithms on semi-regular sequences, which correspond, in conjecture, to almost all polynomial systems in two cases: over the fields F2 and Fq with q n.
- We show that the size of the matrix constructed by XL is large compared to the ones of the F5 algorithm.
- Finally, we give an experimental study between XL and the Buchberger algorithm on the cryptosystem HFE and find that the Buchberger algorithm has a better behavior.
