## Contribution
We prove several results on torsion points and Galois representations for complex multiplication (CM) elliptic curves over a number field containing the CM field. One result computes the degree in which such an elliptic curve has a rational point of order N , refining results of Silverberg [Si88], [Si92].

## Key claims (as reported)
- Another result bounds the size of the torsion subgroup of an elliptic curve with CM by a nonmaximal order in terms of the torsion subgroup of an elliptic curve with CM by the maximal order.
- Our techniques also yield a complete classification of both the possible torsion subgroups and the rational cyclic isogenies of a K-CM elliptic curve E defined over K(j(E)).
