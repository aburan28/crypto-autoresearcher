## Contribution
In the recent years, side channel analysis has received a lot of attention, and attack techniques have been improved. Side channel analysis of second order is now successful in breaking implementations of block ciphers supposed to be effectively protected.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This progress shows not only the practicability of second order attacks, but also the need for provably secure countermeasures.
- Surprisingly, while many studies have been dedicated to the attacks, only a few papers have been published about the dedicated countermeasures.
- In fact, only the method proposed by Schramm and Paar at CT-RSA 2006 enables to thwart second order side channel analysis.
- In this paper, we introduce two new methods which constitute a worthwhile alternative to Schramm and Paar’s proposal.
