## Contribution
For small odd primes p, we prove that most of the rational points on the modular curve X0 (p)/wp parametrize pairs of elliptic curves having infinitely many supersingular primes. This result extends the class of elliptic curves for which the infinitude of supersingular primes is known.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We give concrete examples illustrating how these techniques can be explicitly used to construct supersingular primes for such elliptic curves.
- Finally, we discuss generalizations to points defined over larger number fields and indicate the types of obstructions that arise for higher level modular curves.
