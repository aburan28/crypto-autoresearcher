## Contribution
In a recent work the authors prove the effective asymptotic Fermat’s Last Theorem for the infinite family of fields Q(ζ2r+2 )+ where r ≥ 0. A crucial step in their proof is the following conjecture of Kraus.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Let K be a number field having odd narrow class number and a unique prime λ above 2.
- Then there are no elliptic curves defined over K with conductor λ and a Krational point of order 2.
- In this note we give a new elementary proof of Kraus’ conjecture that makes use only of basic facts about elliptic curves, Tate curves and Tate modules.
