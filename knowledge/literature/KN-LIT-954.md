## Contribution
Let p ě 5 be a prime number and E{Q an elliptic curve with good supersingular reduction at p. Under the generalized Heegner hypothesis, we investigate the p-primary subgroups of the Tate– Shafarevich groups of E over number fields contained inside the anticyclotomic Zp -extension of an imaginary quadratic field where p splits.
