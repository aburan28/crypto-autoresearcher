## Contribution
Let l be an odd prime, and suppose E is an elliptic curve defined over the rational numbers Q. If E has an l-torsion point, then there has been significant work done on characterizing the l-divisibility of the global Tamagawa number of E.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we consider elliptic curves that are l-isogenous to elliptic curves with an l-torsion point and study the l-divisibility of their global Tamagawa numbers.
