## Contribution
Infinite groups have been used for cryptography since about twenty years ago. However, it has not been so fruitful as using finite groups.

## Key claims (as reported)
- An important reason seems the lack of research on building a solid mathematical foundation for the use of infinite groups in cryptography.
- As a first step for this line of research, this paper pays attention to a property, the so-called right-invariance, which makes finite groups so convenient in cryptography, and gives a mathematical framework for correct, appropriate use of it in infinite groups.
