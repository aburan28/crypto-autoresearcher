## Contribution
The general trend in semiconductor industry to separate design from fabrication leads to potential threats from untrusted integrated circuit foundries. In particular, malicious hardware components can be covertly inserted at the foundry to implement hidden backdoors for unauthorized exposure of secret information.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This paper proposes a new class of hardware Trojans which intentionally induce physical side-channels to convey secret information.
- We demonstrate power side-channels engineered to leak information below the effective noise power level of the device.
- Two concepts of very small implementations of Trojan side-channels (TSC) are introduced and evaluated with respect to their feasibility on Xilinx FPGAs.
- Their lightweight implementations indicate a high resistance to detection by conventional test and inspection methods.
