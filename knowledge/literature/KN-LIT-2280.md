## Contribution
The Cramer-Shoup cryptosystem for groups of prime order is a practical public-key cryptosystem, provably secure in the standard model under standard assumptions. This paper extends the cryptosystem for groups of unknown order, namely the group of quadratic residues modulo a composed N .

## Key claims (as reported)
- Two security results are: In the standard model, the scheme is provably secure if both the Decisional Diffie-Hellman assumption for QRN and the factorisation assumption for N hold.
- In the random oracle model, the scheme is provably secure under the factorisation assumption by a quite efficient reduction.
