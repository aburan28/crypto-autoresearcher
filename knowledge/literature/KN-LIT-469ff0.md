## Contribution

Cost analysis for the binary-field Certicom challenges over F_2^131 and F_2^163 (Koblitz and random curves) on ASICs, FPGAs, CPUs and the Cell. It covers step-function and distinguished-point choices and finds normal bases attractive for Koblitz challenges on ASIC/FPGA.
