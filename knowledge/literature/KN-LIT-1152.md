## Contribution
Let p be an odd prime and q = pr , r ≥ 1. For positive integers n, let n Gn [· · · ]q denote McCarthy’s p-adic hypergeometric functions.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this article, we prove an identity expressing a 4 G4 [· · · ]q hypergeometric function as a sum of two 2 G2 [· · · ]q hypergeometric functions.
- This identity generalizes some known identities satisfied by the finite field hypergeometric functions.
- We also prove a transfomation that relates n+2 Gn+2 [· · · ]q and n Gn [· · · ]q hypergeometric functions.
- Next, we express the trace of Frobenius of elliptic curves in terms of special values of 4 G4 [· · · ]q and 6 G6 [· · · ]q hypergeometric functions.
