## Contribution
Given a complex quasiprojective curve B and a non-isotrivial family E of elliptic curves over B, the p-torsion E[p] yields a monodromy representation ρE [p] : π1 (B) → GL2 (Fp ). We prove that if ρE [p] ∼ = ρE 0 [p] then E and E 0 are isogenous, provided p is larger than a constant depending only on the gonality of B.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This can be viewed as a function field analog of the Frey–Mazur conjecture, which states that an elliptic curve over Q is determined up to isogeny by its ptorsion Galois representation for p > 17.
- The proof relies on hyperbolic geometry and is therefore only applicable in characteristic 0.
