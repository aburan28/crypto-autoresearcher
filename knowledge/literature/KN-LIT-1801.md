## Contribution
We introduce a p-adic L-function LA/L associated to an ordinary elliptic curve A over a global function field K of characteristic p together with a Zdp -extension L/K, d = 0 allowed, unramified outside a finite set of places where A has ordinary (good ordinary or multiplicative) reductions. This LA/L is characterized by its interpolation of the special values of twisted Hasse-Weil L-functions, we show that it satisfies the desired functional equation and specialization formula in connection with the characteristic ideal of the dual p∞ -Selmer group of A/L.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The Iwasawa main conjecture having LA/L as the analytic side is proven in several cases.
- In the d ≥ 3 case, the conjecture holds for A/L if and only if it holds for all intermediate Z2p -extensions A/L′ belonging to a given non-empty Zariski open subset of the Grassmannian Gr(d − 2, d)(Zp ).
