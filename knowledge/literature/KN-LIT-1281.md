## Contribution
We give a characterization of the codomain [l]E(k) of the multiplication-by-l map [l] in the case of elliptic curves over a field k of characteristic 6= 2, 3 with l-torsion E[l] = hW1 , W2 i fully defined over k, for primes l different from the characteristic. We show that a point Q ∈ E(k) lies in [l]E(k) if and only if hW1 (−Q) and hW2 (−Q) are lpowers of k, where hW1 and hW2 are functions on E with divisor div(hWi ) = lWi −lP∞ .

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our characterization leads to an effective procedure to find pre-images of [l] by solving an order l system of linear equations and computing a polynomial gcd.
