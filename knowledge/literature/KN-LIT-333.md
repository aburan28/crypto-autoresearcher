## Contribution
Let K be a number field, and let E be an elliptic curve over K. A famous result by Faltings of 1983 can be reformulated for elliptic curves as follows: if S is a set of primes of good reduction for E having density one, then the K-isogeny class of E is determined by the function p → #E(kp ), which maps a prime in S to the size of the group of points over the residue field.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we prove that it suffices to look at the radical of the size.

Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
