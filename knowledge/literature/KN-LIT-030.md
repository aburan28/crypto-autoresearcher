## Contribution
The canonical graduate text on finite-group representation theory. Develops
character theory, Maschke's theorem (complete reducibility when char = 0 or
char does not divide |G|), the orthogonality relations, and the decomposition of
a representation into *isotypic components* with the associated canonical
projectors / group-algebra idempotents.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Any G-representation over a non-modular field splits as a direct sum of
  isotypic blocks; the projectors onto them are explicit averages over G
  (character idempotents).
- Foundations folded in: Maschke, Math. Ann. 52:363-368, 1899
  (doi:10.1007/BF01476165) -- complete reducibility; Wedderburn, "On
  Hypercomplex Numbers," Proc. LMS s2-6(1):77-118, 1908
  (doi:10.1112/plms/s2-6.1.77) -- a semisimple algebra is a product of matrix
  algebras (block structure of C[G]); Fulton-Harris, GTM 129, 1991
  (doi:10.1007/978-1-4612-0979-9) -- explicit S_n irreducibles via Young
  symmetrizers.

## Relevance to this program
Supplies the exact machinery for the program's equivariant index-calculus
candidate (RQ-EQJ-001, EXP-EQJ-001): decompose the Semaev fiber-product relation
space under G = S_{m-1} semidirect (Z/2)^{m-1} into isotypic blocks via exact
idempotents, then harvest/solve per block. Wedderburn's block structure is what
turns G-equivariance into independent smaller linear-algebra blocks; whether the
non-trivial blocks carry a useful share of relations (vs the trivial block that
FHJRV symmetrization, KN-LIT-004, already exploits) is the measured question
(KN-OPEN-002 neighborhood).
