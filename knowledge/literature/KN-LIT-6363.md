## Contribution
We announce the classification of Sato–Tate groups of abelian threefolds over number fields; there are 410 possible conjugacy classes of closed subgroups of USp(6) that occur. We summarize the key points of the “upper bound” aspect of the classification, and give a more rigorous treatment of the “lower bound” by realizing 33 groups that appear in the classification as maximal cases with respect to inclusions of finite index.
