## Contribution
Despite their popularity, lattice reduction algorithms remain mysterious cryptanalytical tools. Though it has been widely reported that they behave better than their proved worst-case theoretical bounds, no precise assessment has ever been given.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Such an assessment would be very helpful to predict the behaviour of lattice-based attacks, as well as to select keysizes for lattice-based cryptosystems.
- The goal of this paper is to provide such an assessment, based on extensive experiments performed with the NTL library.
- The experiments suggest several conjectures on the worst case and the actual behaviour of lattice reduction algorithms.
- We believe the assessment might also help to design new reduction algorithms overcoming the limitations of current algorithms.
