## Contribution
Becker and Wille derived a lower bound of only one round for multi-party contributory key agreement protocols. Up until now no protocol meeting this bound has been proven secure.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We present a protocol meeting the bound and prove it is secure in Bellare and Rogaway’s model.
- The protocol is much more efficient than other conference key agreement protocols with provable security, but lacks forward secrecy.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
