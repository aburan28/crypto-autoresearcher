## Contribution
We generalize the notion of twisting endomorphisms, first defined by [7], to the setting of O-oriented supersingular elliptic curves. We give an algorithm to find supersingular elliptic curves over Fp with a twisting endomorphism of prime degree l, and we use it to compute a basis of their full endomorphism rings.
