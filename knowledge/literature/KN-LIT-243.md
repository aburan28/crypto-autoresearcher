## Contribution
Consider an elliptic curve defined over an imaginary quadratic field K with good reduction at the primes above p ≥ 5 and has complex multiplication by the full ring of integers OK of K. In this paper, we construct p-adic analogues of the Eisenstein-Kronecker series for such elliptic curve as Coleman functions on the elliptic curve.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We then prove p-adic analogues of the first and second Kronecker limit formulas by using the distribution relation of the Kronecker theta function.
