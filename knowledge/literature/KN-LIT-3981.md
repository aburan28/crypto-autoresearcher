## Contribution
We construct a new group signature scheme using bilinear groups. The group signature scheme is practical, both keys and group signatures consist of a constant number of group elements, and the scheme permits dynamic enrollment of new members.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The scheme satisfies strong security requirements, in particular providing protection against key exposures and not relying on random oracles in the security proof.
