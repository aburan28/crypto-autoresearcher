## Contribution
The Wirtinger integral is one of the integral representations of the Gauss hypergeometric function. Its integrand can be regarded as a multivalued function on an elliptic curve.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we study an analogue of the Wirtinger integral on a hyperelliptic curve of genus two, introduced by Mizutani and Watanabe.
- We investigate the associated twisted homology and cohomology groups using the hyperelliptic involution and intersection forms.
