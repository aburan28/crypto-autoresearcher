## Contribution
Let K = Q( −p) be a quadratic field for an odd prime p. We show that there exist infinitely many primes p for which no elliptic curve E/Q has torsion subgroup Z/2Z × Z/2N Z over K for N = 5, 6.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also prove that there exist infinitely many primes p for which there are infinitely many elliptic curves E/Q with this torsion structure, conditional on the parity conjecture.
- Using these results, we obtain new torsion classification results over Kummer extensions of cyclotomic fields and over composites of Zp -extensions of number fields, refining and extending our previous work.
