## Contribution
This paper is a continuation of our earlier work, in which, we described a Las Vegas algorithm to solve the elliptic curve discrete logarithm problem. The Las Vegas algorithm reduces the elliptic curve discrete logarithm problem to finding a zero minor in a matrix.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Using intersection poset of a hyperplane arrangement, we develop an algorithm to find a zero minor in a rectangular matrix.
- Our methods are elementary.
- We discuss the complexity of our algorithm, success probability and provide implementation details.
- We also provide simulation details.
