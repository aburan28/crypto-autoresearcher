## Contribution
Let K/F be a finite Galois extension of number fields and σ be an absolutely irreducible, self-dual representation of Gal(K/F ). Let p be an odd prime and consider two elliptic curves E1 , E2 with good, ordinary reduction at primes above p and equivalent mod-p Galois representations.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this article, we study the variation of the parity of the multiplicities of σ in the representation space associated to the p∞ -Selmer group of Ei over K.
- We also compare the root numbers for the twist of Ei /F by σ and show that the p-parity conjecture holds for the twist of E1 /F by σ if and only if it holds for the twist of E2 /F by σ.
- We also express Mazur-Rubin-Nekovář’s arithmetic local constants in terms of certain local Iwasawa invariants.

## Relevance to this program
