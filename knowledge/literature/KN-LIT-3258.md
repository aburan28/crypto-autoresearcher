## Contribution
Sober-t32 is a candidate stream cipher in the NESSIE competition. Some new attacks are presented in this paper.

## Key claims (as reported)
- A Guess and Determine attack is mounted against Sober-t32 without the decimation of the key stream by the so-called stuttering phase.
- Also, two distinguishing attacks are mounted against full Sober-t32.
- These attacks are not practically feasible, but they are theoretically more efficient than exhaustive key search.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
