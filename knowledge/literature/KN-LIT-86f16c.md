## Contribution

Uses kangaroo methods, rather than enumeration, to finish side-channel attacks on DL-based systems when b key bits remain unknown; with many targets in one group, precomputation (Bernstein-Lange) lowers the cost further. It also extends to key-rank estimation with probabilistic bit knowledge.

## Key claims (as reported)

- 2^{b/2} instead of 2^b for b unknown bits; 2^{b/3} with precomputation over many targets.

## Relevance to this program

Kangaroo with partial key information from side channels; interval-DLP application context.
