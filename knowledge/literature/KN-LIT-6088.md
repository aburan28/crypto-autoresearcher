## Contribution
Fully-homomorphic encryption (FHE) enables computation on encrypted data while maintaining secrecy. Recent research has shown that such schemes exist even for quantum computation.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Given the numerous applications of classical FHE (zero-knowledge proofs, secure two-party computation, obfuscation, etc.) it is reasonable to hope that quantum FHE (or QFHE) will lead to many new results in the quantum setting.
- However, a crucial ingredient in almost all applications of FHE is circuit verification.
- Classically, verification is performed by checking a transcript of the homomorphic computation.
- Quantumly, this strategy is impossible due to no-cloning.
