## Contribution
We introduce a new cryptographic primitive called the blind coupon mechanism (BCM). In effect, the BCM is an authenticated bit commitment scheme, which is AND-homomorphic.

## Key claims (as reported)
- It has not been known how to construct such commitments before.
- We show that the BCM has natural and important applications.
- In particular, we use it to construct a mechanism for transmitting alerts undetectably in a message-passing system of n nodes.
- Our algorithms allow an alert to quickly propagate to all nodes without its source or existence being detected by an adversary, who controls all message traffic.
