## Contribution
In this article, we produce infinite families of non-congruent numbers in the residue class of 1, 2, and 3 modulo 8 with arbitrarily many triples or quadruples prime factors. In short, we use Monsky matrix to show that the 2-Selmer rank of the corresponding congruent number elliptic curve is zero.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also establish some quantitative results to conclude that each such family contains infinitely many non-congruent numbers.

Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
