## Contribution
Broadcast encryption (BE) schemes allow a sender to securely broadcast to any subset of members but requires a trusted party to distribute decryption keys. Group key agreement (GKA) protocols enable a group of members to negotiate a common encryption key via open networks so that only the members can decrypt the ciphertexts encrypted under the shared encryption key, but a sender cannot exclude any particular member from decrypting the ciphertexts.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we bridge these two notions with a hybrid primitive referred to as contributory broadcast encryption (CBE).
- In this new primitive, a group of members negotiate a common public encryption key while each member holds a decryption key.
- A sender seeing the public group encryption key can limit the decryption to a subset of members of his choice.
- Following this model, we propose a CBE scheme with short ciphertexts.
