## Contribution
Signature schemes from the RSA assumption are very important because of their highly reliable security. Despite their importance, only a few digital signature schemes from the RSA assumption are currently known.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Thus, improvement of efficiency in this area seems to be very important.
- In this paper, we propose various signature schemes from the RSA assumption.
- First, we propose a scheme that simultaneously provides the shortest signatures and public key length among the known schemes.
- Compared with the known best schemes, the signature size is the same as that of the scheme proposed recently by Hofheinz, Jager, and Kiltz, whereas the public key size is about the half that of the Hohenberger-Waters scheme.
