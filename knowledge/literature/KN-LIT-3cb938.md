## Contribution

Studies simplified splitting of a binary-curve point into 2 or 3 factor-base points and gives algorithms around 2^{n/3}, which "in some sense violate the generic group model". The companion 2016/704 proposes highly saturated clique-structured equation systems aiming to lower the regularity degree (speculative, no complexity result).

## Key claims (as reported)

- ~2^{n/3} for 2- and 3-way splitting variants (not full ECDLP).

## Relevance to this program

Point splitting with summation polynomials on binary curves (and a Semaev 2015 commentary). Additional voice in the Semaev-2015 debate; check before publishing the in-house Semaev 2015 audit.
