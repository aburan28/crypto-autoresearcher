## Contribution
Recently there has been a huge emphasis on constructing cryptographic protocols that maintain their security guarantees even in the presence of side channel attacks. Such attacks exploit the physical characteristics of a cryptographic device to learn useful information about the internal state of the device.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Designing protocols that deliver meaningful security even in the presence of such leakage attacks is a challenging task.
