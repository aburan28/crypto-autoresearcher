## Contribution
Let A be an abelian variety defined over a number field K, E/K be an elliptic curve, and φ : A → E m be an isogeny defined over K. Let P ∈ A(K) be such that φ(P ) = (Q1 , . . . , Qm ) with RankZ (⟨Q1 , . . . , Qm ⟩) = 1.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We will study a divisibility sequence related to the point P and show its relation with elliptic divisibility sequences.
