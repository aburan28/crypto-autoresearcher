## Contribution
Standard monograph on Néron models: smooth group schemes of finite type over
a Dedekind base that uniquely extend the Néron mapping property of an abelian
variety over the generic fiber. Treats construction via Grothendieck’s
algebraic geometry and the relation to the relative Picard functor for
Jacobians.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Néron models exist for abelian varieties over the fraction field of a
  Dedekind domain (after Néron; constructed here in modern language).
- The special-fiber identity component and component group encode reduction
  type; for elliptic curves this recovers Kodaira–Néron types.
- Second part of the book relates Néron models of Jacobians to the relative
  Picard functor.

## Relevance to this program
Textbook anchor for reduction, component groups, and integral models —
prerequisites for any proposal that lifts finite-field ECDLP instances to
characteristic zero (Xedni / height lattices), invokes Néron models of
Jacobians, or claims information from bad-reduction fibers. Forecloses
treating “Néron model” as a novel object.
