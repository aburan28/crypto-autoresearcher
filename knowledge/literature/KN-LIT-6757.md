## Contribution
Hyperelliptic Curve Cryptosystems (HECC) are competitive to elliptic curve cryptosystems in performance and security. Recently efficient scalar multiplication techniques using a theta divisor have been proposed.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Their application, however, is limited to the case when a theta divisor is used for the base point.
- In this paper we propose efficient and secure scalar multiplication of a general divisor for genus 2 HECC over IF2m .
- The proposed method is based on two novel techniques.
- One is divisor decomposition technique in which a general divisor is decomposed into two theta divisors.
