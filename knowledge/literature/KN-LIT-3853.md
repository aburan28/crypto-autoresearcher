## Contribution
This paper describes an extremely efficient squaring operation in the so-called ‘cyclotomic subgroup’ of F× , for q ≡ 1 mod 6. Our q6 result arises from considering the Weil restriction of scalars of this group from Fq6 to Fq2 , and provides efficiency improvements for both pairingbased and torus-based cryptographic protocols.
