## Contribution
In this paper, we propose a new general construction to reduce the public key size of McEliece cryptosystems constructed from automorphism-induced Goppa codes. In particular, we generalize the ideas of automorphism-induced Goppa codes by considering nontrivial subsets of automorphism groups to construct Goppa codes with a nice block structure.

## Key claims (as reported)
- By considering additive and multiplicative automorphism subgroups, we provide explicit constructions to demonstrate our technique.
- We show that our technique can be applied to automorphisminduced Goppa codes based cryptosystems to further reduce their key sizes.
