## Contribution
We construct a family of Poisson structures of hydrodynamic type on the loop space of CP n−1 . This family is parametrized by the moduli space of elliptic curves or, in other words, by the modular parameter τ .

## Key claims (as reported)
- This family can be lifted to a homogeneous Poisson structure on the loop space of Cn but in order to do that we need to upgrade the modular parameter τ to an additional field τ (x) with Poisson brackets {τ (x), τ (y)} = 0, {τ (x), za (y)} = 2πi za (y) δ′ (x − y) where z1 , ..., zn are coordinates on Cn .
- These homogeneous Poisson structures can be written in terms of an elliptic r-matrix of hydrodynamic type.
