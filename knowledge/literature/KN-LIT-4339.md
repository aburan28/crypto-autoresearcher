## Contribution
We propose a fully functional identity-based encryption scheme (IBE). The scheme has chosen ciphertext security in the random oracle model assuming an elliptic curve variant of the computational DiffieHellman problem.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our system is based on the Weil pairing.
- We give precise definitions for secure identity based encryption schemes and give several applications for such systems.
