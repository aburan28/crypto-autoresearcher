## Contribution
Homomorphic signatures (HS) allows the derivation of the signature of the message-function pair (m, g), where m = g(m1 , . . . , mK ), given the signatures of each of the input messages mk signed under the same key. Multi-key HS (M-HS) introduced by Fiore et al.

## Key claims (as reported)
- (ASIACRYPT’16) further enhances the utility by allowing evaluation of signatures under different keys.
- The unforgeability of existing M-HS notions assumes that all signers are honest.
- We consider a setting where an arbitrary number of signers can be corrupted, called unforgeability under corruption, which is typical for natural applications (e.g., verifiable multi-party computation) of M-HS.
- Surprisingly, there is a huge gap between M-HS (for arbitrary circuits) with and without unforgeability under corruption: While the latter can be constructed from standard lattice assumptions (ASIACRYPT’16), we show that the former likely relies on non-falsifiable assumptions.
