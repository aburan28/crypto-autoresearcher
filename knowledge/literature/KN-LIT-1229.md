## Contribution
Let c < 3p/16 be a prime or c = 1. Let E be a Z[ −cp]oriented supersingular elliptic curve defined over Fp2 .

## Key claims (as reported)
- There exists a cisogeny from E to E p with kernel G ⊂ E[c].
- Given an Eichler order corresponding to the endomorphism ring End(E, G) = {θ ∈ End(E) : θ(G) ⊆ G}, we can compute a ternary quadratic form with discriminant p by solving two square roots in Fc , and the ternary quadratic form corresponds to a maximal order O ∼ = End(E) in Bp,∞ by Brandt–Sohn correspondence.
