## Contribution
We prove that the module learning with errors (M-LWE) problem with arbitrary polynomial-sized modulus p is classically at least as hard as standard worst-case lattice problems, as long as the module rank d is not smaller than the number field degree n. Previous publications only showed the hardness under quantum reductions.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We achieve this result in an analogous manner as in the case of the learning with errors (LWE) problem.
- First, we show the classical hardness of M-LWE with an exponential-sized modulus.
- In a second step, we prove the hardness of M-LWE using a binary secret.
- And finally, we provide a modulus reduction technique.
