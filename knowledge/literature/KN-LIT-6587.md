## Contribution
We present a new method for computing the scalar multiplication on Koblitz curves. Our method is as fast as the fastest known technique but requires much less memory.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We propose two settings for our method.
- In the first setting, well-suited for hardware implementations, memory requirements are reduced by 85%.
- In the second setting, well-suited for software implementations, our technique reduces the memory consumption by 70%.
- Thus, with much smaller memory usage, the proposed method yields the same efficiency as the fastest scalar multiplication schemes on Koblitz curves.
