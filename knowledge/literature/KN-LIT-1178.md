## Contribution
Let l be a prime number and let E and E ′ be l-isogenous elliptic curves defined over a finite field k of characteristic p 6= l. Suppose the groups E(k) and E ′ (k) are isomorphic, but E(K) 6≃ E ′ (K), where K is an l-power extension of k.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In a previous work we have shown that, under mild rationality hypotheses, the case of interest is when l = 2 and K is the unique quadratic extension of k.
- In this paper we study the likelihood of such an occurrence by fixing a pair of 2-isogenous elliptic curves E, E ′ over Q and asking for the proportion of primes p for which E(Fp ) ≃ E ′ (Fp ) and E(Fp2 ) 6≃ E ′ (Fp2 ).
