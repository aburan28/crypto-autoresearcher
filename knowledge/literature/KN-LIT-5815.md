## Contribution
The braid group with its conjugacy problem is one of the recent hot issues in cryptography. At CT-RSA 2001, Anshel, Anshel, Fisher, and Goldfeld proposed a commutator key agreement protocol (KAP) based on the braid groups and their colored Burau representation.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Its security is based on the multiple simultaneous conjugacy problem (MSCP) plus a newly adopted key extractor.
- This article shows how to reduce finding the shared key of this KAP to the list-MSCPs in a permutation group and in a matrix group over a finite field.
- We also develop a mathematical algorithm for the MSCP in braid groups.
- The former implies that the usage of colored Burau representation in the key extractor causes a new weakness, and the latter can be used as a tool to investigate the security level of their KAP.
