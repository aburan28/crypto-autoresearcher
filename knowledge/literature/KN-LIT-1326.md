## Contribution
To answer a question about the distribution of products of elliptic curves in isogeny classes of abelian surfaces defined over finite fields, we compute specific orbital integrals in the group GSp4 . More precisely, we compute integrals over the orbits of elements in the subgroup GL2 ×det GL2 .

## Key claims (as reported)
- As a first step towards a complete solution of the problem, this article contains explicit computations for arbitrary orbital integrals of spherical functions over this subgroup, and also compute orbital integrals over GSp4 in a large number of cases.
