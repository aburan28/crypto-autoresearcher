## Relevance to this program
The middle of the statistical-decoding arc described on [[KN-LIT-b66899]] — the
stage at which the approach was being judged and, by the standards of the time,
found wanting. Fifteen years later the reformulated version became a serious
attack.

Held specifically as the **negative-assessment step that turned out to be
non-final**. When this program deprioritises a direction, rule 9 requires it to
record the evidence, budget, test boundary and a revisit condition — and this
arc is why: a well-founded "not competitive" verdict about a formulation is
routinely mistaken for a verdict about the idea.
