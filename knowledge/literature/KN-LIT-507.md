## Contribution
Given an elliptic curve E over Q, a celebrated conjecture of Goldfeld asserts that a positive proportion of its quadratic twists should have analytic rank 0 (resp. We show this conjecture holds whenever E has a rational 3-isogeny.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also prove the analogous result for the sextic twists of j-invariant 0 curves (Mordell curves).
- To prove these results, we establish a general criterion for the non-triviality of the p-adic logarithm of Heegner points at an Eisenstein prime p, in terms of the relative p-class numbers of certain number fields and then apply this criterion to the special case p = 3.
- As a by-product, we also prove the 3-part of the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture for many elliptic curves of j-invariant 0.
