## Contribution
Let E/Q be an elliptic curve given by an integral Weierstrass equation. Let P ∈ E(Q) be a point of infinite order, and let (Bn )n≥1 be the elliptic divisibility sequence generated by P .

## Key claims (as reported)
- This paper is concerned with a question posed in 2007 by Everest, Reynolds and Stevens: does (Bn )n≥1 contain only finitely many perfect powers?
- We answer this question positively under the following three additional assumptions: (i) P is non-integral; (ii) ∆(E) > 0, where ∆(E) is the discriminant of E; (iii) P ∈ E0 (R), where E0 (R) denotes the connected component of identity.
- Our method makes use of Galois representations attached to elliptic curves defined over totally real fields, and their modularity.
- We can deduce the same theorem without assumptions (ii) and (iii) provided we assume some standard conjectures from the Langlands programme.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
