## Contribution
We prove that it is impossible to construct perfect-complete quantum public-key encryption (QPKE) with classical keys from quantumly secure one-way functions (OWFs) in a black-box manner, resolving a long-standing open question in quantum cryptography. Specifically, in the quantum random oracle model (QROM), no perfect-complete QPKE scheme with classical keys, and classical/quantum ciphertext can be secure.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This improves the previous works which require either unproven conjectures or imposed restrictions on key generation algorithms.
- This impossibility extends to QPKE with quantum public key in natural settings, which is tight to all known QPKE constructions with quantum public key.
