## Contribution
In this paper we propose efficient two-round k-out-of-n oblivious transfer schemes, in which R sends O(k) messages to S, and S sends O(n) messages back to R. The computation cost of R and S is reasonable.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The choices of R are unconditionally secure.
- For the basic scheme, the secrecy of unchosen messages is guaranteed if the Decisional Diffie-Hellman problem is hard.
- When k = 1, our basic scheme is as efficient as the most efficient 1-out-of-n oblivious transfer scheme.
- Our schemes have the nice property of universal parameters, that is each pair of R and S need neither hold any secret key nor perform any prior setup (initialization).
