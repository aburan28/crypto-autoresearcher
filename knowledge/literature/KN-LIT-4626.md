## Contribution
We propose a new authentication protocol that is provably secure based on a ring variant of the learning parity with noise (LPN) problem. The protocol follows the design principle of the LPN-based protocol from Eurocrypt’11 (Kiltz et al.), and like it, is a two round protocol secure against active attacks.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Moreover, our protocol has small communication complexity and a very small footprint which makes it applicable in scenarios that involve low-cost, resourceconstrained devices.
- Performance-wise, our protocol is more efficient than previous LPN-based schemes, such as the many variants of the Hopper-Blum (HB) protocol and the aforementioned protocol from Eurocrypt’11.
- Our implementation results show that it is even comparable to the standard challenge-and-response protocols based on the AES block-cipher.
- Our basic protocol is roughly 20 times slower than AES, but with the advantage of having 10 times smaller code size.
