## Contribution
It is shown that, under some mild technical conditions, representations of prime numbers by binary quadratic forms can be computed in polynomial complexity by exploiting Schoof’s algorithm, which counts the number of Fq -points of an elliptic curve over a finite field Fq . Further, a method is described which computes representations of primes from reduced quadratic forms by means of the integral roots of polynomials over Z.
