## Contribution
Algebraic cryptanalysis usually requires to find solutions of several similar polynomial systems. A standard tool to solve this problem consists of computing the Gröbner bases of the corresponding ideals, and Faugère’s F4 and F5 are two well-known algorithms for this task.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we present a new variant of the F4 algorithm which is well suited to algebraic attacks of cryptosystems since it is designed to compute Gröbner bases of a set of polynomial systems having the same shape.
- It is faster than F4 as it avoids all reductions to zero, but preserves its simplicity and its computation efficiency, thus competing with F5.
