## Key claims (as reported)
- The OAEP encryption scheme was introduced by Bellare and Rogaway at Eurocrypt ’94.
- It converts any trapdoor permutation scheme into a public-key encryption scheme.
- OAEP is widely believed to provide resistance against adaptive chosen ciphertext attack.
- The main justification for this belief is a supposed proof of security in the random oracle model, assuming the underlying trapdoor permutation scheme is one way.
