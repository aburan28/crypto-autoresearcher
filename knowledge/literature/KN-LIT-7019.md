## Contribution
All known multilinear map candidates have suffered from a class of attacks known as “zeroizing” attacks, which render them unusable for many applications. We provide a new construction of polynomialdegree multilinear maps and show that our scheme is provably immune to zeroizing attacks under a strengthening of the Branching Program Un-Annihilatability Assumption (Garg et al., TCC 2016-B).

## Key claims (as reported)
- Concretely, we build our scheme on top of the CLT13 multilinear maps (Coron et al., CRYPTO 2013).
- In order to justify the security of our new scheme, we devise a weak multilinear map model for CLT13 that captures zeroizing attacks and generalizations, reflecting all known classical polynomial-time attacks on CLT13.
- In our model, we show that our new multilinear map scheme achieves ideal security, meaning no known attacks apply to our scheme.
- Using our scheme, we give a new multiparty key agreement protocol that is several orders of magnitude more efficient that what was previously possible.
