## Contribution
For a, b, c ∈ Q, the exceptional set associated to the Gauss hypergeometric function 2 F1 (a, b, c; z) is defined by arXiv:2607.16331v1 [math.NT] 15 Jul 2026 E(a, b, c) := {z ∈ Q | 2 F1 (a, b, c; z) ∈ Q}. In this paper, the exceptional sets E(a, b, c) are determined explicitly for each 2 F1 (a, b, c; z) whose monodromy group is an arithmetic triangle group in Takeuchi’s class I.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The description is obtained via hypergeometric–modular identities together with transcendence results for periods of abelian varieties due to Wüstholz, and classical result of Schneider on algebraic values of j-invariant of elliptic curves with complex multiplication.
