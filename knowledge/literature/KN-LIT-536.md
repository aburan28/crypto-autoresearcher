## Contribution
Given a smooth projective curve C defined over Q and given two elliptic surfaces E1 −→ C and E2 −→ C along with sections Pi , Qi of Ei (for i = 1, 2), we prove that if there exist infinitely many t ∈ C(Q) such that for some integers m1,t , m2,t , we have that [mi,t ](Pi )t = (Qi )t on Ei (for i = 1, 2), then at least one of the following conclusions must hold: either (i) there exists a nontrivial isogeny ψ : E1 −→ E2 and also there exist nontrivial endomorphisms φi of Ei (for i = 1, 2) such that φ2 (P2 ) = ψ(φ1 (P1 )); or (ii) Qi is a multiple of Pi for some i = 1, 2. A special case of our result answers a conjecture made by Silverman.
