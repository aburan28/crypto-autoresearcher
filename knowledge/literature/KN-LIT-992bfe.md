## Contribution

Many-core FPGA architecture for parallel rho on prime-field curves, used to estimate the monetary cost of Certicom ECCp-131 on FPGA clusters; concludes it is arguably infeasible on FPGAs in reasonable time.

## Key claims (as reported)

- "Almost 300%" speed-up over prior FPGA state of the art; ECCp-131 cost tables in the full text.
