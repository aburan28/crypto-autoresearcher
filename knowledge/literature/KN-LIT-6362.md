## Contribution
We consider the distribution of normalized Frobenius traces for two families of genus 3 hyperelliptic curves over Q that have large automorphism groups: y 2 = x8 + c and y 2 = x7 − cx with c ∈ Q∗ . We give efficient algorithms to compute the trace of Frobenius for curves in these families at primes of good reduction.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Using data generated by these algorithms, we obtain a heuristic description of the Sato-Tate groups that arise, both generically and for particular values of c.
- We then prove that these heuristic descriptions are correct by explicitly computing the Sato-Tate groups via the correspondence between Sato-Tate groups and Galois endomorphism types.
