## Contribution
In this article, we study how to compute the number of K-rational points with a given j-invariant on an arbitrary modular curve. As an application, for each positive integer n, we determine the list of possible numbers of cyclic n-isogenies an elliptic curve over some number field can admit.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Similarly, for an odd prime power pk , we calculate the possible values for the number of points above some j-invariant on Cartan modular curves Xs (pk ), Xns (pk ) and their normalizers.
- Combining known results about images of Galois representations of CM elliptic curves with our work, we also devise a simple algorithm to determine the number of rational CM points on any modular curve.
