## Contribution
In CRYPTO 2019, Gohr shows that well-trained neural networks can perform cryptanalytic distinguishing tasks superior to traditional differential distinguishers. Moreover, applying an unorthodox key guessing strategy, an 11-round key-recovery attack on a modern block cipher Speck32/64 improves upon the published state-of-the-art result.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This calls into the next questions.
- To what extent is the advantage of machine learning (ML) over traditional methods, and whether the advantage generally exists in the cryptanalysis of modern ciphers?
- To answer the first question, we devised ML-based key-recovery attacks on more extended round-reduced Speck32/64.
- We achieved an improved 12-round and the first practical 13-round attacks.
