## Contribution
We continue work of Gekeler and others on elliptic curves over Fq (T ) with conductor ∞ · n where n ∈ Fq [T ] has degree 3. Because of the Frobenius isogeny there are infinitely many curves in each isogeny class, and we discuss in particular which of these curves is the strong Weil curve with respect to the uniformization by the Drinfeld modular curve X0 (n).

## Key claims (as reported)
- As a corollary we obtain that the strong Weil curve E/Fq (T ) always gives a rational elliptic surface over Fq .
