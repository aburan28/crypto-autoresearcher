## Contribution
This note presents two attacks against COMET, a secondround candidate in the NIST lightweight cryptography standardization process. The first attack uses a long message to detect the use of weak keys, whereas the second attack focuses on the resistance of COMET against slide attacks.

## Key claims (as reported)
- These attacks do not invalidate the security claims of the designers.
