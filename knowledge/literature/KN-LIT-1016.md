## Contribution
The modularity of elliptic curves always intrigues number theorists. Recently, Thorne had proved a marvelous result that for a prime p, every elliptic curve defined over a p-cyclotomic extension of Q is modular.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The method is to use some automorphy lifting theorems and study non-cusp points on some specific elliptic curves by Iwasawa theory for elliptic curves.
- Since the modularity of elliptic curves over real quadratic was proved, one may ask whether it is possible to replace Q with a real quadratic field K.
- Following Thorne’s idea, we give some assumptions first and prove the modularity of elliptic curves over the Zp -extension of some real quadratic fields.
