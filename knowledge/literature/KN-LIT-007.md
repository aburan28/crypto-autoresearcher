## Contribution
The GHS attack: transfers ECDLP on a curve over a binary composite field
GF(2^n) to a hyperelliptic-curve DLP over a subfield via Weil descent, where
index calculus applies — weakening specific curve/field combinations.

## Key claims (as reported)
- For certain composite binary fields, Weil descent yields a computationally
  easier DLP instance than generic methods, breaking those specific ECDLP
  instances.
- Applies to structured field extensions, not to prime fields or generic
  curves.

## Relevance to this program
Historical anchor for Weil-descent techniques feeding the summation-polynomial
line. Scope is narrow (structured binary fields); a proposal invoking Weil
descent over prime fields is not covered by GHS and must be assessed fresh.
