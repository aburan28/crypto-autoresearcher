## Contribution
We apply autocorrelation and Walsh coefficients for the investigation of correlation immune and resilient Boolean functions. We prove new lower bound for the absolute indicator of resilient functions that improves significantly (for m > (n − 3)/2) the bound of Zheng and Zhang [18] on this value.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove new upper bound for the number of nonlinear variables in high resilient Boolean function.
- This result supersedes the previous record.
- We characterize all possible values of resiliency orders for quadratic functions and give a complete description of quadratic Boolean functions that achieve the upper bound on resiliency.
- We establish new necessary condition that connects the number of variables, the resiliency and the weight of an unbalanced nonconstant correlation immune function and prove that such functions do not exist for m > 0.75n−1.25.
