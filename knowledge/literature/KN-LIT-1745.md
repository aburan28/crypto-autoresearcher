## Contribution
We study moduli spaces of Higgs bundles with two poles on an elliptic curve. We describe all singular fibers of the Hitchin map, including the nilpotent cone.

## Key claims (as reported)
- To achieve this, we consider a modular map that lifts Higgs bundles with five poles on the Riemann sphere to Higgs bundles on the elliptic curve.
- This map is a two-sheeted covering and we analyze its Galois involution.
- We prove that the modular map is surjective and determine its ramification locus.
- In particular, we also obtain an explicit description of the singular locus of the moduli space.
