## Contribution
We show how to extract an arbitrary polynomial number of simultaneously hardcore bits from any one-way function. In the case the one-way function is injective or has polynomially-bounded pre-image size, we assume the existence of indistinguishability obfuscation (iO).

## Key claims (as reported)
- In the general case, we assume the existence of differing-input obfuscation (diO), but of a form weaker than full auxiliary-input diO.
- Our construction for injective one-way functions extends to extract hardcore bits on multiple, correlated inputs, yielding new D-PKE schemes.
- Of independent interest is a definitional framework for differing-inputs obfuscation in which security is parameterized by circuit-sampler classes.
