## Contribution
Recently, Aaronson et al. (arXiv:2009.07450) showed that detecting interference between two orthogonal states is as hard as swapping these states.

## Key claims (as reported)
- While their original motivation was from quantum gravity, we show its applications in quantum cryptography.
- We construct the first public key encryption scheme from cryptographic non-abelian group actions.
- Interestingly, the ciphertexts of our scheme are quantum even if messages are classical.
- This resolves an open question posed by Ji et al.
