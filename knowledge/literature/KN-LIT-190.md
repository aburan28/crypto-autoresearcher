## Contribution
Using degree N isogenies of elliptic curves, we produce a spectrum Q(N ). This spectrum is built out of spectra related to tmf .

## Key claims (as reported)
- At p = 3 we show that the K(2)local sphere is built out of Q(2) and its K(2)-local Spanier-Whitehead dual.
- This gives a conceptual reinterpretation a resolution of Goerss, Henn, Mahowald, and Rezk.
