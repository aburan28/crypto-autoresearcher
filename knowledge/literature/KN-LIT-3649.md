## Contribution
We construct practical and efficient zero-knowledge arguments with sublinear communication complexity. The arguments have perfect completeness, perfect special honest verifier zero-knowledge and computational soundness.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our zero-knowledge arguments rely on two-tiered homomorphic commitments for which pairing-based constructions already exist.
- As a concrete application of our new zero-knowledge techniques, we look at the case of range proofs.
- To demonstrate a committed value belongs to a specific 1 N -bit integer interval we only need to communicate O(N 3 ) group elements.
