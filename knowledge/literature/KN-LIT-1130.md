## Contribution
We study the action of the Galois group G of a finite extension K/k of number fields on the points on an elliptic curve E. For an odd prime p, we aim to determine the structure of the p-adic completion of the Mordell-Weil group E(K) as a Zp [G]-module only using information of E over k and the completions of K.
