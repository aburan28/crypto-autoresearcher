## Contribution
Round-reduced variants of AES are widely used as building blocks in the design of cryptographic schemes. The study of non-random properties and distinguishers for round-reduced AES has always been an important research topic.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The longest known secret-key distinguishers on AES cover 6 rounds.
- Related differences and related differentials were introduced by the designers of AES in 2009, but research in this direction remains limited.
- In this paper, we provide a new perspective on related differences through exchange and shift operations.
- Based on related differentials, we present new non-random properties and secret-key distinguishers for up to 7 rounds of AES.
