## Contribution
We develop a new method for the computation of (3, 3)-isogenies between principally polarized abelian surfaces. The idea is to work with models in P8 induced by a symmetric level-3 theta structure.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this setting, the action of three-torsion points is linear, and the isogeny formulas can be described in a simple way as the composition of easy-to-evaluate maps.
- In the description of these formulas, the relation with the Burkhardt quartic threefold plays an important role.
- Furthermore, we discuss generalizations of the idea to higher dimensions as well as different isogeny degrees.
