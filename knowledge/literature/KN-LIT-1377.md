## Contribution
Let N > 1 and let ΦN (X, Y ) ∈ Z[X, Y ] be the modular polynomial which vanishes precisely at pairs of j-invariants of elliptic curves linked by a cyclic isogeny of degree N . In this note we study the divisibility of the coefficients of ΦN (X + J, Y + J) for certain algebraic numbers J, in particular J = 0 and other singular moduli.

## Key claims (as reported)
- It turns out that these coefficients are highly divisible by small primes at which J is supersingular.
