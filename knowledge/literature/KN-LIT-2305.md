## Contribution
Access Control Encryption (ACE) is a novel paradigm for encryption which allows to control not only what users in the system are allowed to read but also what they are allowed to write. The original work of Damgård et al.

## Key claims (as reported)
- [DHO16] introducing this notion left several open questions, in particular whether it is possible to construct ACE schemes with polylogarithmic complexity (in the number of possible identities in the system) from standard cryptographic assumptions.
- In this work we answer the question in the affirmative by giving (efficient) constructions of ACE for an interesting class of predicates which includes equality, comparison, interval membership, and more.
- We instantiate our constructions based both on standard pairing assumptions (SXDH) or more efficiently in the generic group model.
