## Contribution
We generalize results of Alladi, Dawsey, and Sweeting and Woo for Chebotarev densities to general densities of sets of primes. We show that if K is a number field and S is any set of prime ideals with natural density δ(S) within the primes, then X μ(a) − lim = δ(S), X→∞ N(a) 2≤N(a)≤X a∈D(K,S) where μ(a) is the generalized Möbius function and D(K, S) is the set of integral ideals a ⊆ OK with unique prime divisor of minimal norm lying in S.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Our result can be applied to give formulas for densities of various sets of prime numbers, including those lying in a Sato-Tate interval of a fixed elliptic curve, and those in Beatty sequences such as ⌊πn⌋.
