## Contribution

Decomposition (index calculus) in Jacobians of hyperelliptic curves over F_{q^n} using Riemann-Roch spaces instead of summation polynomials. With sieving, it is what Joux-Vitse combined with GHS for their F_{p^6} record. Related Nagao preprints: 2010/511 (formalising decomposition attacks), 2013/548, 2013/549.

## Key claims (as reported)

- See Vitse 2017 for why its asymptotic complexity is optimal among known decomposition techniques.

## Relevance to this program

Decomposition attack on hyperelliptic Jacobians over extension fields (Riemann–Roch-based). The Riemann–Roch decomposition encodings the crypto repo tried on ECC2K-130 (RR solver panel) are in this line.
