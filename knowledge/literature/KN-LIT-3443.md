## Contribution
We extend the Tiny-OT two party protocol of Nielsen et al (CRYPTO 2012) to the case of n parties in the dishonest majority setting. This is done by presenting a novel way of transferring pairwise authentications into global authentications.

## Key claims (as reported)
- As a by product we obtain a more efficient manner of producing globally authenticated shares, in the random oracle model, which in turn leads to a more efficient two party protocol than that of Nielsen et al.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
