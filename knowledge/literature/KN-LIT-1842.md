## Contribution
We show that, conditional on Zywina’s effective version of the Serre uniformity conjecture, there is a natural way to parameterize non-CM Q-rational points on all modular curves in terms of the rational points on finitely many modular curves. Our proof refines Zywina’s work to give a (conditional) parameterization of the images of adelic Galois representations of elliptic curves.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In particular, we show that there are 41 j-invariants of elliptic curves whose associated Galois image does not vary in an infinite family.
- Using our explicit parameterization, we show that all rational points on all modular curves arise from the geometry of modular curves in a formal sense, confirming a philosophy of Mazur and Ogg.
