## Contribution
MISTY1 is a block cipher designed by Matsui in 1997. It was well evaluated and standardized by projects, such as CRYPTREC, ISO/IEC, and NESSIE.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we propose a key recovery attack on the full MISTY1, i.e., we show that 8-round MISTY1 with 5 FL layers does not have 128-bit security.
- Many attacks against MISTY1 have been proposed, but there is no attack against the full MISTY1.
- Therefore, our attack is the first cryptanalysis against the full MISTY1.
- We construct a new integral characteristic by using the propagation characteristic of the division property, which was proposed in 2015.
