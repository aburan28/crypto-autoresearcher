## Contribution
We describe a polynomial-time cryptanalysis of the (approximate) multilinear map of Coron, Lepoint and Tibouchi (CLT). The attack relies on an adaptation of the so-called zeroizing attack against the Garg, Gentry and Halevi (GGH) candidate multilinear map.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Zeroizing is much more devastating for CLT than for GGH.
- In the case of GGH, it allows to break generalizations of the Decision Linear and Subgroup Membership problems from pairing-based cryptography.
- For CLT, this leads to a total break: all quantities meant to be kept secret can be efficiently and publicly recovered.
