## Contribution
We study a graded Lie algebra arising from the Galois action on the pro-p fundamental group of a once-punctured elliptic curve with complex multiplication. Among other things, we provide a minimal generating set of the rationalized Lie algebra under suitable assumptions.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The proof is based on a slight variant of the theory of weighted completion of profinite groups developed by Hain and Matsumoto.
