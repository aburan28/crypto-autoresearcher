## Contribution
In this paper we investigate the security of 5-round AES against two different attacks in an adaptive setting. We present a practical key-recovery attack on 5-round AES with a secret s-box that requires 232 adaptively chosen ciphertexts, which is as far as we know a new record.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In addition, we present a new and practical key-independent distinguisher for 5-round AES which requires 227.2 adaptively chosen ciphertexts.
- While the data complexity of this distinguisher is in the same range as the current best 5-round distinguisher [14], it exploits new structural properties of 5-round AES.
