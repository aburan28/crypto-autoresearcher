## Contribution
The rectangle attack has shown to be a very powerful form of cryptanalysis against block ciphers. Given a rectangle distinguisher, one expects to mount key recovery attacks as efficiently as possible.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In the literature, there have been four algorithms for rectangle key recovery attacks.
- However, their performance vary from case to case.
- Besides, numerous are the applications where the attacks lack optimality.
- In this paper, we investigate the rectangle key recovery in depth and propose a unified and generic key recovery algorithm, which supports any possible attacking parameters.
