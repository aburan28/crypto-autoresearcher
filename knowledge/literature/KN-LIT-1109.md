## Contribution
Let A and A′ be nonzero abelian varieties defined over a number field k such that Hom(A, A′ ) = 0. Under the Generalized Riemann hypothesis for motivic L-functions attached to A and A′ , we show that there exists a prime p of k of good reduction for A and A′ at which the Frobenius traces of A and A′ are nonzero and differ by sign, and such that the norm Nm(p) is Ok,g,g′ (log(2N N ′ )2 ), where N and N ′ respectively denote the absolute conductors of A and A′ .

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also make the dependence of the big-O constant on k and the dimensions g, g ′ of A, A′ explicit up to an effectively computable absolute constant.
- Our method extends that of Chen, Park, and Swaminathan who considered the case in which A and A′ are elliptic curves.
