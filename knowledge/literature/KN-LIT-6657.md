## Contribution
This work presents a novel low-cost optoelectronic setup for time- and spatially resolved analysis of photonic emissions and a corresponding methodology, Simple Photonic Emission Analysis (SPEA). Observing the backside of ICs, the system captures extremly weak photoemissions from switching transistors and relates them to program running in the chip.

## Key claims (as reported)
- SPEA utilizes both spatial and temporal information about these emissions to perform side channel analysis of ICs.
- We successfully performed SPEA of a proof-of-concept AES implementation and were able to recover the full AES secret key by monitoring accesses to the S-Box.
- This attack directly exploits the side channel leakage of a single transistor and requires no additional data processing.
- The system costs and the necessary time for an attack are comparable to power analysis techniques.
