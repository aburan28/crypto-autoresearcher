## Contribution
We construct a signature scheme that is proved secure, without random oracles, under the strong RSA assumption. Unlike other efficient strong-RSA based schemes, the new scheme does not generate large prime numbers during signing.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The public key size and signature size are competitive with other strong RSA schemes, but verification is less efficient.
- The new scheme adapts the prefix signing technique of Hohenberger and Waters (CRYPTO 2009) to work without generating primes.
