## Contribution
ISOGENY GRAPHS AND MODULAR CURVES arXiv:2509.15214v1 [math.NT] 18 Sep 2025 JUN BO LAU, TRAVIS MORRISON, ELI ORVIS, GABRIELLE SCULLARD, AND LUKAS ZOBERNIG Abstract. We introduce a “non-orientable” variation of Serre’s definition of a graph, which we call an abstract isogeny graph.

## Key claims (as reported)
- These objects capture the combinatorics of the graphs G(p, l, H), the l-isogeny graphs of supersingular elliptic curves with H-level structure.
- In particular they allow for the study of non-backtracking walks, primes, and zeta functions.
- We prove an analogue of Ihara’s determinant formula for the zeta function of an abstract isogeny graph.
- For B1 (N ) ⊆ H ⊆ B0 (N ) and p > 3, we use this formula to relate the Ihara zeta function of G(p, l, H) to the Hasse-Weil zeta functions of the modular curves XH,Fl and XH×B0 (p),Fl .
