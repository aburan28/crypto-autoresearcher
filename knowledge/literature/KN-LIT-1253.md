## Contribution
We study relatively minimal surfaces equipped with a strongly isotrivial elliptic fibration in positive characteristic by means of the notion of equivariantly normal curves introduced and developed recently by Brion in [Bri24a; Bri24b]. Such surfaces are isomorphic to a contracted product E ×G X, where E is an elliptic curve, G is a finite subgroup scheme of E and X is a G-normal curve.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Using this description, we compute their Betti numbers to determine their birational classes.
- This allows us to complete the classification of maximal automorphism groups of surfaces in any characteristic, extending the result in characteristic zero obtained in [Fon24b].
- When G is diagonalizable, we compute additional invariants to study the structure of their Picard schemes.
