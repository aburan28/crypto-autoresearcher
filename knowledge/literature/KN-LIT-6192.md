## Contribution
We study two open problems proposed by Wagner in his seminal work on the generalized birthday problem. First, with the use of multicollisions, we improve Wagner’s k-tree algorithm that solves the generalized birthday problem for the cases when k is not a power of two.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The new k-tree only slightly outperforms Wagner’s k-tree.
- However, in some applications this suffices, and as a proof of concept, we apply the new 3-tree algorithm to slightly reduce the security of two CAESAR proposals.
- Next, with the use of multiple collisions based on Hellman’s table, we give improvements to the best known time-memory tradeoffs for the k-tree.
- As a result, we obtain the a new tradeoff curve T 2 · M lg k−1 = k · N .
