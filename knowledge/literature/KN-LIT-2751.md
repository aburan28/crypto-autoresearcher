## Contribution
We propose to use blockchains to achieve MPC which does not require the participating parties to be online simultaneously or interact with each other. Parties who contribute inputs but do not wish to receive outputs can go offline after submitting a single message.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In addition to our main result, we study combined communication- and state-complexity in MPC, as it has implications for the communication complexity of our main construction.
- Finally, we provide a variation of our main protocol which additionally provides guaranteed output delivery.
