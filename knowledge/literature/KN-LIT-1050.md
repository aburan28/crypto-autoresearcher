## Contribution
An elliptic K3 surface with two II∗ fibers is called the Inose surface. In this paper, we give a method to find a section of an Inose surface corresponding to an isogeny of general degree between two elliptic curves.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In particular, we show examples of bases of the Mordell-Weil lattices of Inose surfaces arising from isogenies of degrees 5 and 6.

## Relevance to this program
