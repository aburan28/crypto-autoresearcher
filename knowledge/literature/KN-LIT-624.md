## Contribution
Let l and p ≥ 3 be distinct prime numbers. Let E/Ql be an elliptic curve with p-torsion module Ep .

## Key claims (as reported)
- Let Ql (Ep ) be the p-torsion field of E.
- We provide a complete description of the degree of the extension Ql (Ep )/Ql .
- As a consequence, we obtain a recipe to determine the discriminant ideal of the extension Ql (Ep )/Ql in terms of standard information on E.
