## Contribution
Efficiently computable homomorphisms allow elliptic curve point multiplication to be accelerated using the Gallant-Lambert-Vanstone (GLV) method. We extend results of Iijima, Matsuo, Chao and Tsujii which give such homomorphisms for a large class of elliptic curves by working over Fp2 and demonstrate that these results can be applied to the GLV method.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In general we expect our method to require about 0.75 the time of previous best methods (except for subfield curves, for which Frobenius expansions can be used).
- We give detailed implementation results which show that the method runs in between 0.70 and 0.84 the time of the previous best methods for elliptic curve point multiplication on general curves.
