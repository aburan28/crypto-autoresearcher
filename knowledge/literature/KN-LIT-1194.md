## Contribution
We show that the authentication protocol [IEEE Internet Things J., 2023, 10(1), 867-876] is not correctly specified, because the server cannot complete its computations. To revise, the embedded device needs to compute an extra point multiplication over the underlying elliptic curve.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also find the protocol cannot provide anonymity, not as claimed.
- It can only provide pseudonymity.
