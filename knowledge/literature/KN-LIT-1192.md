## Contribution
We provide a formula for the order of the Tate–Shafarevich group of elliptic curves over dihedral extensions of number fields of order 2n, up to 4th powers and primes dividing n. Specifically, for odd n it is equal to the order of the Tate–Shafarevich group over the quadratic subextension.

## Key claims (as reported)
- A similar formula holds for even n.
