## Contribution
Proves a threshold sum-product estimate in prime fields: for A subset of F_p with
p^delta < |A| < p^{1-delta}, max(|A+A|, |A*A|) >= c|A|^{1+epsilon} for some
epsilon = epsilon(delta) > 0. A set that is neither tiny nor almost all of F_p
cannot be near-closed under both addition and multiplication.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Threshold (qualitative epsilon > 0) sum-product; derives a Szemeredi-Trotter-
  type incidence bound, Erdos distance, and finite-field Kakeya applications.
- Large-subset quantitative sharpening: Garaev, "The sum-product estimate for
  large subsets of prime fields," Proc. AMS 136(8):2735-2739, 2008
  (doi:10.1090/S0002-9939-08-09386-6, arXiv:0706.0702).
