## Contribution
We compute the first murmurations for elliptic curves over function fields Fq (t): oscillatory patterns in average Frobenius traces that separate rank-0 from rank-1 curves, with z-scores up to 256. For the family ED : y 2 = x3 + x + D(t) with D monic squarefree of degree 5, we enumerate 534,745 curves across q = 7, 11, 13 with exact BSD invariants.

## Key claims (as reported)
- All L-polynomials factor into cyclotomic polynomials—a weight-2 consequence of the Weil conjectures and Kronecker’s theorem, independent of CM.
- Since |X| = L(1/q) in this family (a consequence of BSD with trivial torsion and Tamagawa numbers), the |X| modulation of murmurations is entirely a composition effect: different |X| strata have different mixtures of L-polynomial types, and hence different mean traces.
- This yields an exact reweighting identity for the |X|-stratified murmuP ration density: Ms (d, q) = − λ fλ,s pd (λ), where λ ranges over cyclotomic types, fλ,s is the type composition of the |X| = s stratum, and pd (λ) is the degree-d power sum of the unitarized roots.
- Within each |X| stratum, joint cells—distinct L-polynomial types sharing the same |X|—show that the murmuration profile carries arithmetic information strictly finer than |X| alone.
