## Contribution
Geyer and Jarden proved several results for torsion points of elliptic curves defined over the fixed field by finitely many elements in the absolute Galois group of a finitely generated field over the prime field in its algebraic closure. As an analogue of these results, this paper studies torsion points of Drinfeld modules defined over the fixed field by finitely many elements in the absolute Galois group of a finitely generated function field in its algebraic closure.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We prove some results which are similar to those of Geyer and Jarden.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
