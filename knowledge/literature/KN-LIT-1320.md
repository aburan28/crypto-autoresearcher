## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve over a field K and l a prime. There exists an elliptic curve E ∗ related to E by an isogeny of degree l only if Φtl (X, j(E )) = 0, where Φtl (X, Y ) is the traditional modular polynomial.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Moreover, Φtl gives the coefficients of E ∗ , together with parameters needed to build the isogeny explicitly.
- Since Φtl has very large coefficients, many families with smaller coefficients can be used instead, as described by Elkies, Atkin and others.
- In this work, we concentrate on the computation of the family of modular polynomials introduced by Fricke and more recently used by Charlap, Coley and Robbins.
- In some cases, the resulting polynomials are small, which justifies the interest of this study.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to the isogeny cluster: bears on supersingular/isogeny-based constructions and the isogeny-path problems that compete with and inform ECDLP work. Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
