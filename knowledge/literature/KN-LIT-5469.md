## Contribution
In many practical settings, participants are willing to deviate from the protocol only if they remain undetected. Aumann and Lindell introduced a concept of covert adversaries to formalize this type of corruption.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In the current paper, we refine their model to get stronger security guarantees.
- Namely, we show how to construct protocols, where malicious participants cannot learn anything beyond their intended outputs and honest participants can detect malicious behavior that alters their outputs.
- As this construction does not protect honest parties from selective protocol failures, a valid corruption complaint can leak a single bit of information about the inputs of honest parties.
- Importantly, it is often up to the honest party to decide whether to complain or not.
