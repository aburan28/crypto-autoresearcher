## Contribution
Attribute-based cryptography has emerged in the last years as a promising primitive for digital security. For instance, it provides good solutions to the problem of anonymous access control.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In a ciphertextpolicy attribute-based encryption scheme, the secret keys of the users depend on their attributes.
- When encrypting a message, the sender chooses which subset of attributes must be held by a receiver in order to be able to decrypt.
- All current attribute-based encryption schemes that admit reasonably expressive decryption policies produce ciphertexts whose size depends at least linearly on the number of attributes involved in the policy.
- In this paper we propose the first scheme whose ciphertexts have constant size.
