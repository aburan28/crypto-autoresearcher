## Contribution
The elliptic curve Ek : y 2 = x3 + k admits a natural 3-isogeny φk : Ek → E−27k . We compute the average size of the φk -Selmer group as k varies over the integers.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Unlike previous results of Bhargava and Shankar on n-Selmer groups of elliptic curves, we show that this average can be very sensitive to congruence conditions on k; this sensitivity can be precisely controlled by the Tamagawa numbers of Ek and E−27k .
