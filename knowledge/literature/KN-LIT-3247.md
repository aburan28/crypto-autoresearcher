## Contribution
In this paper, we investigate the difficulty of one of the most relevant problems in multivariate cryptography – namely MinRank – about which no real progress has been reported since [19, 9]. Our starting point is the Kipnis-Shamir attack [19].

## Key claims (as reported)
- We first show new properties of the ideal generated by Kipnis-Shamir’s equations.
- We then propose a new modeling of the problem.
- Concerning the practical resolution, we adopt a Gröbner basis approach that permitted us to actually solve challenges A and B proposed by Courtois in [8].
- Using the multi-homogeneous structure of the algebraic system, we have been able to provide a theoretical complexity bound reflecting the practical behavior of our approach.
