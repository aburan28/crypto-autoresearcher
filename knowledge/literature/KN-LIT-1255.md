## Contribution
We present the first Key Policy Attribute-Based Encryption (KP-ABE) scheme employing isogeny-based cryptography through class group actions, specifically utilizing the Csi-FiSh instantiation and pairing groups. We introduce a new assumption, denoted Isog-DLin, which combines the isogeny and DLin assumptions.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We propose the following constructions: a small universe KP-ABE and a large universe KP-ABE under the Isog-DBDH assumption, and a small universe KP-ABE under the Isog-DLin assumption.
- In these constructions, the master key is designed to be secure against quantum computer attacks, while the ciphertext remains secure against classical computer attacks.
- This duallayered approach ensures robust security across classical and quantum computational paradigms, addressing current and potential future cryptographic challenges.
