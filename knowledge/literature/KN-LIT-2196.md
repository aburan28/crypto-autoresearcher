## Contribution
We study Pseudorandom Number Generators (PRNGs) as used in practice. We first give a general security framework for PRNGs, incorporating the attacks that users are typically concerned about.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We then analyze the most popular ones, including the ANSI X9.17 PRNG and the FIPS 186 PRNG.
- Our results also suggest ways in which these PRNGs can be made more efficient and more secure.
