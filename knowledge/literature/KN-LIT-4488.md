## Contribution
We construct an indistinguishability obfuscation (IO) scheme for all polynomial-size circuits from constant-degree graded encoding schemes, assuming the existence of a subexponentially secure pseudorandom generator computable by constant-degree arithmetic circuits, and subexponential hardness of the Learning With Errors (LWE) problem. Previously, all candidate general purpose IO schemes rely on polynomialdegree graded encoding schemes.
