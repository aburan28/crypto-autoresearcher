## Contribution
It is known that any isogeny graph consisting of ordinary elliptic curves over Fq with q = p or p2 has √ a special structure, called a volcano graph. We have a bound h < log2 4q of a height h of the 2-volcano graph.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we improve the bound on a height of 2-volcano graphs over Fq .
- In case q = p2 , we 1 show a tighter bound h ≤ 2 ⌊log2 p⌋ + 2.
- In case q = p, we also show that a good bound for each prime p can be computed by using our proposed techniques.
