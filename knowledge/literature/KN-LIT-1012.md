## Contribution
Let p be an odd prime and F∞ be a Zp -extension of a number field F . Given an elliptic curve E over F , we study the structure of the fine Selmer group over F∞ .

## Key claims (as reported)
- It is shown that under certain conditions, the fine Selmer group is a cofinitely generated module over Zp and furthermore, we obtain an upper bound for its corank (i.e., the λ-invariant), in terms of various local and global invariants.
