## Key claims (as reported)
- Succinct arguments allow a prover to convince a verifier of the validity of any statement in a language, with minimal communication and verifier’s work.
- Among other approaches, lattice-based protocols offer solid theoretical foundations, post-quantum security, and a rich algebraic structure.
- In this work, we present some new approaches to constructing efficient lattice-based succinct arguments.
- Our main technical ingredient is a new commitment scheme based on vanishing polynomials, a notion borrowed from algebraic geometry.
