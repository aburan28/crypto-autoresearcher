## Contribution
Improved quantum information set decoding, refining the quantum-ISD asymptotics
established by Kachigar–Tillich ([[KN-LIT-80202e]]) — quantum walk and
Grover-based speedups applied to the MMT/BJMM list-construction steps.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Better quantum ISD complexity than the previous quantum ISD results.
- Asymptotic, in the standard quantum-query/circuit model of that line.

## Relevance to this program
Held as the second step of the quantum-ISD asymptotic line, whose overall
message this program treats as a calibration point: **iterated quantum
improvements on a well-studied classical algorithm moved the exponent by a
small amount and did not threaten parameters.** That is the realistic prior for
what a quantum speedup on a mature algorithm buys.

**Does not bear on the ECDLP.**
