## Contribution
Canonical modern reference on abelian varieties: group varieties, dual
abelian variety, polarizations and the Riemann forms / theta structure,
and the basic cohomological toolkit. The second edition adds appendices by
Ramanujam and Manin.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Dual abelian variety and Poincaré bundle exist; polarizations correspond to
  ample line bundles giving the Rosati involution on End^0.
- Theta groups and Heisenberg representations control the geometry of
  polarized AVs — the algebraic substrate of later isogeny / Kani-embedding
  constructions.
- Textbook / monograph status; not a complexity paper.

## Relevance to this program
Foundation for duals, polarizations, and principally polarized Jacobians
appearing in higher-dimensional isogeny attacks (KN-TECH-026) and in any
