## Contribution
Broadcast Encryption with optimal parameters was a longstanding problem, whose first solution was provided in an elegant work by Boneh, Waters and Zhandry [?]. However, this work relied on multilinear maps of logarithmic degree, which is not considered a standard assumption.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Recently, Agrawal and Yamada [?] improved this state of affairs by providing the first construction of optimal broadcast encryption from Bilinear Maps and Learning With Errors (LWE).
- However, their proof of security was in the generic bilinear group model.
- In this work, we improve upon their result by providing a new construction and proof in the standard model.
- In more detail, we rely on the Learning With Errors (LWE) assumption and the Knowledge of OrthogonALity Assumption (KOALA) [?] on bilinear groups.
