## Contribution
Under GRH, any element in the multiplicative group of a number field K that is globally primitive (i.e., not a perfect power in K ∗ ) is a primitive root modulo a set of primes of K of positive density. For elliptic curves E/K that are known to have infinitely many primes p of cyclic reduction, possibly under GRH, a globally primitive point P ∈ E(K) may fail to generate any of the point groups E(kp ).

## Key claims (as reported)
- We describe this phenomenon in terms of an associated Galois representation ρE/K,P : GK → GL3 ( b Z), and use it to construct non-trivial examples of global points on elliptic curves that are locally imprimitive.

Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
