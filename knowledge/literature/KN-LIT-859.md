## Contribution
Let Ld be the Lattès map associated to the multiplication-by-d endomorphism of an elliptic curve E defined over a finite field Fq . We determine the density δ(Ld , q) of periodic points for Ld in P1 (Fq ).

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that the periodic point densities δ(Ld , q n ) converge as n → ∞ along certain arithmetic progressions, and compute simple explicit formulas for δ(Ll , q) when l is a prime and E belongs to a special family of supersingular elliptic curves.
