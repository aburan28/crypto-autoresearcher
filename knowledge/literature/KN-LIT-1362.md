## Contribution
We classify all instances of the condition ap (f ) ≡ x mod λ being related to a congruence on the prime p, where ap (f ) denotes the pth Fourier coefficient of a classical normalised cuspidal eigenform f and λ is a prime in the number field generated by the Fourier coefficients of f . This classification is done in terms of the (projective) image of the mod λ Galois representation associated with f and extends work by Swinnerton-Dyer.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We highlight that for x = 0, this condition is more often implied by a congruence on the prime p than the general value of ap (f ) mod λ.
- Finally, we illustrate various instances of these congruences through examples from the setting of weight 2 newforms attached to rational elliptic curves.
