## Contribution
Let {E(p,q) } represents a family of elliptic curves over Q as defined by the Weierstrass equation E(p,q) : y 2 = x3 − p2 x + q 2 , where p and q are prime numbers exceeding five. Previous research established that the {E(p,q) } had a rank of at least two for all p, q > 5 and two independent points.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This research implies that the two points can be extended to a basis for the family {E(p,q) }.
