## Contribution

Theoretical and experimental study of the first-fall-degree assumption for the systems in Petit-Quisquater. In some cases the assumption seems to hold and gives improved binary-ECDLP complexities; in other cases it is unlikely to hold and would have "very unexpected consequences".

## Key claims (as reported)

- Mixed verdict on FFDA.

## Relevance to this program

Generalised first-fall-degree assumptions and their limits; part of the FFD dispute (with HKY 2015, Kosters–Yeo).
