## Contribution
Let p > 3 be a prime and E be a supersingular elliptic curve defined over Fp2 . Let c be a prime with c < 3p/16 and G be a subgroup of E[c] of order c.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The pair (E, G) is called a supersingular elliptic curve with level-c structure, and the endomorphism ring End(E, G) is isomorphic to an Eichler order with level c.
- We construct two kinds of Eichler orders Oc (q, r) and Oc′ (q, r ′ ) with level c.
- Interestingly, we prove that each Oc (q, r) or Oc′ (q, r ′ ) can represent a primitive reduced binary quadratic form √ with discriminant √ 1+ −cp −16cp or −cp respectively.
- If a curve E is Z[ −cp]-oriented or Z[ 2 ]-oriented, then we prove that End(E, G) is isomorphic to Oc (q, r) or Oc′ (q, r ′ ) respectively.
