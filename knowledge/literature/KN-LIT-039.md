## Contribution
An expository, historically framed account of the Chebotarev density theorem:
in a Galois extension, the Frobenius conjugacy classes of unramified primes are
equidistributed across conjugacy classes of the Galois group in proportion to
their size.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Frobenius equidistribution generalizes Dirichlet's theorem and governs prime
  splitting/factorization patterns.
- Standard accessible reference for the theorem (function-field / cover analogue:
  Frobenius cycle-type statistics equidistribute according to the geometric
  monodromy group).

## Relevance to this program
The equidistribution principle behind the program's monodromy-census candidates
(round-2 A1 "Chebotarev census," C2 "imprimitive-monodromy resolvent";
EXP-MONO-001, KN-OPEN-009): predict the relation/split rate of the m-th Semaev
summation cover a priori from the Galois/monodromy group of the cover, replacing
the assumed uniform/quasirandom rate. If the geometric monodromy is the full
symmetric/wreath group (generic expectation), the census yields only the
quasirandom rate; an exceptional locus with smaller monodromy would give
curve-specific rate deviations.
