## Contribution

Official status of the 1997 ECC challenges. Level I 131-bit and all Level II (163-359-bit) challenges are unsolved. Resource figures for the solved 109-bit challenges are given as quoted by Wenger-Wolfger 2014. Public GPU efforts on ECCp-131 exist (e.g. github.com/brichard19/certicom-challenge, HIP/MPI rho) but publish no throughput.

## Key claims (as reported)

- Solved: 79-bit (Dec 1997), 89-bit (Feb 1998), 97-bit (Sep 1999), ECCp-109 (Nov 2002; ~10,000 computers, 549 days), ECC2-109 (Apr 2004; ~2,600 computers, ~510 days), ECC2K-108 (~9,500 PCs, 126 days). Unsolved: 131, 163, 191, 239, 359-bit.

## Relevance to this program

The Certicom ECC Challenge ladder (ECC2K-130, ECCp-131, ECC2-131 open at the 131-bit level). Parameters in this program's challenge files and cairn's examples/certicom-ecdlp come from a 2016 archive snapshot.
