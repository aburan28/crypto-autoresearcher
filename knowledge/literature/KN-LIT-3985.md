## Contribution
Two main computational problems serve as security foundations of current fully homomorphic encryption schemes: Regev’s Learning With Errors problem (LWE) and HowgraveGraham’s Approximate Greatest Common Divisor problem (AGCD). Our first contribution is a reduction from LWE to AGCD.

## Key claims (as reported)
- As a second contribution, we describe a new AGCD-based fully homomorphic encryption scheme, which outperforms all prior AGCD-based proposals: its security does not rely on the presumed hardness of the so-called Sparse Subset Sum problem, and the bit-length of a ciphertext is only e O(λ), where λ refers to the security parameter.
