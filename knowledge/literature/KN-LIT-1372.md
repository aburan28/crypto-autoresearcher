## Contribution
We study monogenity of pure cubic number fields by means of Selmer groups of certain elliptic curves. A cubic number field with discriminant D determines a unique nontrivial F3 -orbit in the first cohomology group of the elliptic curve E D : y 2 = 4x3 + D with respect to a certain 3-isogeny φ.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Orbits corresponding to monogenic fields must lie in the soluble part of the Selmer group S φ (E D / Q), and this gives a criterion to discard monogenity.
- From this, we can derive bounds on the number of monogenic cubic fields in terms of the rank of the elliptic curve.
- We can also determine the monogenity of many concrete pure cubic fields assuming GRH.
