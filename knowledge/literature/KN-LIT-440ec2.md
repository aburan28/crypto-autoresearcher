## Contribution

Proves upper bounds on the first fall degree of polynomial systems obtained by Weil descent of low-degree univariate/multivariate systems, and places the ECDLP systems inside this framework. It is the theoretical backbone of the FFD-based subexponential heuristics, which HKY 2015 and Kosters-Yeo later showed can be misleading.

## Key claims (as reported)

- FFD bounds independent of n for fixed input degree (qualitative).

## Relevance to this program

First fall degree of Weil-descended systems is bounded independently of n under the stated conditions (the HFE-style phenomenon). The CERTBIN degree-4 refutations (KN-FIND-5a8d3e, -c3a917) and the crypto repo's 'first fall degree 3' measurements should be read against it before any mechanism claim.
