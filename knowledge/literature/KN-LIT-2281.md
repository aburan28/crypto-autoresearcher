## Contribution
We give a simple and efficient construction of a verifiable random function (VRF) on bilinear groups. Our construction is direct.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In contrast to prior VRF constructions [14, 15], it avoids using an inefficient Goldreich-Levin transformation, thereby saving several factors in security.
- Our proofs of security are based on a decisional bilinear Diffie-Hellman inversion assumption, which seems reasonable given current state of knowledge.
- For small message spaces, our VRF’s proofs and keys have constant size.
- By utilizing a collision-resistant hash function, our VRF can also be used with arbitrary message spaces.
