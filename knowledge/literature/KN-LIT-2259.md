## Contribution
Assume that distributions X0 , X1 (respectively Y0 , Y1 ) are dX (respectively dY ) indistinguishable for circuits of a given size. It is well known that the product distributions X0 Y0 , X1 Y1 are dX +dY indistinguishable for slightly smaller circuits.

## Key claims (as reported)
- However, in probability theory where unbounded adversaries are considered through statistical distance, it is folklore knowledge that in fact X0 Y0 and X1 Y1 are dX + dY − dX · dY indistinguishable, and also that this bound is tight.
- We formulate and prove the computational analog of this tight bound.
- Our proof is entirely different from the proof in the statistical case, which is non-constructive.
- As a corollary, we show that if X and Y are d indistinguishable, then k independent copies of X and k independent copies of Y are almost 1 − (1 − d)k indistinguishable for smaller circuits, as against d · k using the looser bound.
