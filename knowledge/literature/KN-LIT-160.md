## Contribution
In his letter [Ser96], J.-P. Serre proves that the systems of Hecke eigenvalues given by × modular forms (mod p) are the same as the ones given by locally constant functions A× → F̄p , B /B where B is the endomorphism algebra of a supersingular elliptic curve.

## Key claims (as reported)
- After giving a detailed exposition of Serre’s result, we prove that the systems of Hecke eigenvalues given by Siegel modular forms (mod p) of genus g are the same as the ones given by algebraic modular forms (mod p) on the group GUg (B), as defined in [Gro99] and [Gro98].
- The correspondence is obtained by restricting to the superspecial locus of the moduli space of abelian varieties.
