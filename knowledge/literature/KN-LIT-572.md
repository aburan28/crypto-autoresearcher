## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve over Q such that EndQ̄ (E) = Z and which admits a non-trivial cyclic Q-isogeny. We prove that, for p > 37, the residual mod p Galois representation ρ̄E,p : GQ → GL2 (Fp ) is surjective.
