## Contribution
Seminal works by Birch and Ihara gave formulas for the mth power moments of the traces of Frobenius endomorphisms of elliptic curves over Fp for primes p ≥ 5. Recent works by Kaplan and Petrow generalized these results to the setting of elliptic curves that contain a subgroup isomorphic to a fixed finite abelian group A.
