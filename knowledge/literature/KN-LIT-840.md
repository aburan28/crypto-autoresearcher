## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve over an imaginary quadratic field K, and p be an odd prime such that the residual representation E[p] is reducible. The μ-invariant of the fine Selmer group of E over the anticyclotomic Zp -extension of K is studied.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We do not impose the Heegner hypothesis on E, thus allowing certain primes of bad reduction to decompose infinitely in the anticyclotomic Zp -extension.
- It is shown that the fine μ-invariant vanishes if certain explicit conditions are satisfied.
- Further, a partial converse is proven.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
