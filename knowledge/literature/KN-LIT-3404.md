## Contribution
For RSA, May showed a deterministic polynomial time equivalence of computing d to factoring N (= pq). On the other hand, Takagi showed a variant of RSA such that the decryption algorithm is faster than the standard RSA, where N = pr q while ed = 1 mod (p − 1)(q − 1).

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper, we show that a deterministic polynomial time equivalence also holds in this variant.
- The coefficient matrix T to which LLL algorithm is applied is no longer lower triangular, and hence we develop a new technique to overcome this problem.
