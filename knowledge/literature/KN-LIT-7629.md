## Contribution
Graduate textbook developing modular forms and modular curves X_0(N), X_1(N)
as Riemann surfaces and algebraic curves; Hecke operators; Jacobians of
modular curves; Eichler–Shimura; and Galois representations attached to
eigenforms, culminating in statements of the Modularity Theorem.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Modular curves are moduli spaces of elliptic curves with level structure;
  their Jacobians carry the abelian varieties attached to newforms.
- Eichler–Shimura relates Hecke eigenvalues to Frobenius traces of the
  associated abelian varieties mod p.
- States modularity in several equivalent forms and sketches number-theoretic
  applications (without proving Wiles–Taylor–Breuil–Conrad–Diamond).
