## Contribution
In this paper we present a new practical key-recovery attack on the SFLASH signature scheme. SFLASH is a derivative of the older C∗ encryption and signature scheme that was broken in 1995 by Patarin.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In SFLASH, the public key is truncated, and this simple countermeasure prevents Patarin’s attack.
- The scheme is well-known for having been considered secure and selected in 2004 by the NESSIE project of the European Union to be standardized.
- However, SFLASH was practically broken in 2007 by Dubois, Fouque, Stern and Shamir.
- Their attack breaks the original (and most relevant) parameters, but does not apply when more than half of the public key is truncated.
