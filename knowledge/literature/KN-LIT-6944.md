## Contribution
We describe an algorithm, AGM-X0 (N ), for point counting on elliptic curves of small characteristic p using p-adic lifts of their invariants associated to modular curves X0 (N ). The algorithm generalizes the contruction of Satoh [10], SST [11], and Mestre [9].

## Key claims (as reported)
- We describe this method and give details of its implementation for characteristics 2, 3, 5, 7, and 13.
