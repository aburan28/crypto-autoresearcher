## Contribution
We introduce and study arithmetic spin structures on elliptic curves. We show that there is a unique isogeny class of elliptic curves over Fp2 which carries a unique arithmetic spin structure and provides a geometric object of weight 1/2 in the sense of Deligne and Grothendieck.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This object is thus a candidate for Q(1/4).
