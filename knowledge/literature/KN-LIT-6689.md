## Contribution
In this work, we explore the question of simultaneous privacy and soundness amplification for non-interactive zero-knowledge argument systems (NIZK). We show that any δs −sound and δz −zero-knowledge NIZK candidate satisfying δs + δz = 1 − , for any constant  > 0, can be turned into a computationally sound and zero-knowledge candidate with the only extra assumption of a subexponentially secure public-key encryption.
