## Contribution
We show how regulator constants of a finitely generated Z[G]-module can be related to G-cohomology, where G is a finite group. We then derive consequences of such relation for modules naturally arising in number theory, such as ring of integers and units of number fields, K-theory groups of ring of integers and Mordell-Weil groups of elliptic curves.
