## Contribution
Double-block Hash-then-Sum (DbHtS) MACs are a class of MACs that aim for achieving beyond-birthday-bound security, including SUM-ECBC, PMAC_Plus, 3kf9 and LightMAC_Plus. Recently Datta et al.

## Key claims (as reported)
- (FSE’19), and then Kim et al.
- (Eurocrypt’20) prove that DbHtS constructions are secure beyond the birthday bound in the single-user setting.
- However, by a generic reduction, their results degrade to (or even worse than) the birthday bound in the multi-user setting.
- In this work, we revisit the security of DbHtS MACs in the multi-user setting.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
