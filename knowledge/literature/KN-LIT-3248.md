## Contribution
We investigate the security of a generalization of HFE (multivariate and odd-characteristic variants). First, we propose an improved version of the basic Kipnis-Shamir key recovery attack against HFE.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Second, we generalize the Kipnis-Shamir attack to Multi-HFE.
- The attack reduces to solve a MinRank problem directly on the public key.
- This leads to an improvement of a factor corresponding to the square of the degree of the extension field.
- We used recent results on MinRank to show that our attack is polynomial in the degree of the extension field.
