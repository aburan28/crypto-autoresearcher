## Why this entry matters to this program

**It supplies the definition the corpus did not have.** Every proposal in the
`RQ-DREG-bd6c86` batch records that the definition of last fall degree "was not
obtained", and `KN-OPEN-7f0511` catalogues four inconsistent first-fall-degree
definitions. This paper gives all four invariants precisely and, uniquely,
*relates* them with proofs.

**The full text was read**, not the abstract: 16 pages, extracted from the PDF
on 2026-09-16. That distinguishes this record from `KN-LIT-7605`,
`KN-LIT-7607` and `KN-LIT-a2f7b3`, which are abstract-level.

## Definitions (section 1)

- **Def 1.3, first fall degree.** `d_ff(F) = min{d : Syz(F^top)_d / Triv(F^top)_d ≠ 0}`, in `B = F_q[x]/(x_i^q − x_i)`; `Triv` generated by Koszul syzygies `f_i e_j − f_j e_i` and field-equation syzygies `f_i^{q−1} e_i`.
- **Def 1.4, degree of regularity.** `d_reg(F) = min{d ≥ 0 : (F^top)_d = R_d}`.
- **Def 1.5, last fall degree.** `V_{F,i}` = smallest `k`-vector space containing `F ∩ R_{≤i}` and closed under multiplication by `g ∈ R_{≤ i − deg f}`; `d_F = min{d : f ∈ V_{F,max{d,deg f}} for all f ∈ (F)}`.
- **Eq. (1).** For a degree-compatible order, `V_{F,d} = rowsp(M_d)` (proved in Gorla–Müller–Petit). Cor. 1.6: that row space is order-independent.
- **Def 2.5–2.6, degree fall.** `d_f = min{d : f ∈ V_{F,d}} ≥ deg f`; a degree fall iff `d_f > deg f`.

## Main results

- **Thm 2.8.** `d_F = max{d_f : f ∈ (F), d_f > deg f}` — the last fall degree *is* the largest degree at which a degree fall occurs.
- **Thm 2.9.** Same, restricted to elements of a Gröbner basis for a degree-compatible order.
- **Thm 3.1 (main).** `sd_σ(F) = max{d_F, max.GB.deg_σ(F)}` for degree-compatible `σ`. An equality: the reduced-GB degrees and the degree falls are the *only* two ingredients of the solving degree. `d_F` is independent of order and coordinates (Rmk 3.2).
- **Cor 3.3.** `sd_σ(F) = max{d_{g_1}, …, d_{g_s}}` over a reduced Gröbner basis.
- **Thm 5.1.** `F` contains the field equations ⟹ `sd(F) ≤ reg(F^h)`.
- **Thm 5.2 (Semaev–Tenti, quoted).** `F = {f_1..f_r} ∪ field equations`; if `d_reg(F) ≥ max{q, deg f_i}` then `sd(F) ≤ 2 d_reg(F) − 2`.
- **Thm 5.3.** `d_reg(F) ≤ reg(F^h)`.
- **§4.** The first fall degree may be **arbitrarily larger or smaller** than any of the other invariants; its relation to the solving degree is heuristic only.

## The correction it forces on this program

The paper's introduction states plainly that Huang–Kosters–Yeo bound the
complexity of **their own algorithm** by the last fall degree, and that
Gorla–Müller–Petit later showed the last fall degree is a **lower bound for the
solving degree**. This program had recorded the opposite direction. See
`CORR-20260916-0c9c0a`.
