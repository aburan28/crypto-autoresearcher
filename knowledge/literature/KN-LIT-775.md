## Contribution
Under suitable, fairly weak hypotheses on an elliptic curve E/Q and a primitive non-trivial Dirichlet character χ, we show that the algebraic L-value L (E, χ) at s = 1 is an algebraic integer. For instance, for semistable curves L (E, χ) is integral whenever E admits no isogenies defined over Q.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Moreover we give examples illustrating that our hypotheses are necessary for integrality to hold.
