## Contribution
New families of maximum distance separable (MDS) codes are constructed from elliptic curves by exploiting their group structures. In contrast to classical constructions based on divisors supported at a single rational point, the proposed approach employs divisors formed by multiple distinct points constituting a maximal subgroup of the curve.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The resulting √ codes achieve parameters approaching the theoretical upper bound (q + 1 + ⌊2 q⌋)/2 and include non Reed-Solomon (RS) arXiv:2509.04247v2 [cs.IT] 26 Oct 2025 MDS codes.
- The inequivalence of these codes to RS codes is established through an explicit analysis on the rank of the Schur product of their generator matrices.
- These results extend the known parameter range of elliptic MDS codes and provide additional evidence supporting the tightness of existing upper bounds for algebraic geometry MDS codes.
