## Contribution
This paper describes an attack on cryptographic devices called Differential Behavioral Analysis (or DBA). This is an hybrid attack between two already powerful attacks: differential power analysis (DPA) for the statistical treatment and safe-error attack for the fault type.

## Key claims (as reported)
- DBA, simulated on an algorithmic model of AES appears to be very efficient.
- The attacker is able to recover the entire secret key with byte-wise “stuck-at” faults injected repetitively.
- A theorical as well as a more realistic approach are presented.
