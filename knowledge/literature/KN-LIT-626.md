## Contribution
Let B be a simple CM abelian variety over a CM field E, p a rational prime. Suppose that B has potentially ordinary reduction above p and is self-dual with root number −1.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Under some further conditions, we prove the generic non-vanishing of (cyclotomic) p-adic heights on B along anticyclotomic Zp -extensions of E.
- This provides evidence towards Schneider’s conjecture on the non-vanishing of p-adic heights.
- For CM elliptic curves over Q, the result was previously known as a consequence of work of Bertrand, Gross–Zagier and Rohrlich in the 1980s.
- Our proof is based on non-vanishing results for Katz p-adic L-functions and a Gross–Zagier formula relating the latter to families of rational points on B.
