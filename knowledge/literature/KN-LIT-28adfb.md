## Contribution
A further improvement of the **work factor** in attacking McEliece's
cryptosystem — one step in the incremental sequence of ISD refinements running
from Lee–Brickell ([[KN-LIT-10be29]]) and Stern ([[KN-LIT-fb9047]]) through to
Canteaut–Chabaud ([[KN-LIT-f390dc]]).
