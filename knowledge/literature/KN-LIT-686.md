## Contribution
We prove that many representations ρ : Gal(K/K) → GL2 (F3 ), where K is a CM field, arise from modular elliptic curves. We prove similar results when the prime p = 3 is replaced by p = 2 or p = 5.

## Key claims (as reported)
- As a consequence, we prove that a positive proportion of elliptic curves over any CM field not containing a 5th root of unity are modular.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
