## Contribution
Let R be a complete discrete valuation ring with fraction field K and perfect residue field k of characteristic p > 0. Let E/K be an elliptic curve with a K-rational isogeny of prime degree l.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this article, we study the possible Kodaira types of reduction that E/K can have.
- We also prove some related results for elliptic curves over Q.
