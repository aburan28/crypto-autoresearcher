## Contribution
We consider the problem of checking whether an elliptic curve defined over a given number field has complex multiplication. We study two polynomial time algorithms for this problem, one randomized and the other deterministic.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The randomized algorithm can be adapted to yield the discriminant of the endomorphism ring of the curve.
