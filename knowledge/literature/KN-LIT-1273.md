## Contribution
Let E/Q be an elliptic curve and let p ≥ 5 be a prime of good supersingular reduction. We generalize results due to Meng Fai Lim proving Kida’s formula and integrality results for characteristic elements of signed Selmer groups along the cyclotomic Zp -extension of weakly ramified base fields K/Qp .
