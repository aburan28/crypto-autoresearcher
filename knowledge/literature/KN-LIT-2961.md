## Contribution
Copy-protection is the task of encoding a program into a quantum state to prevent illegal duplications. A line of recent works studied copy-protection schemes under “1 → 2 attacks”: the adversary receiving one program copy can not produce two valid copies.

## Key claims (as reported)
