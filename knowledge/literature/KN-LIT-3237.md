## Contribution
We introduce a new approach for cryptanalysis of key agreement protocols based on noncommutative groups. Our approach uses functions that estimate the distance of a group element to a given subgroup.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We test it against the Shpilrain-Ushakov protocol, which is based on Thompson’s group F , and show that it can break about half the keys within a few seconds on a single PC.
