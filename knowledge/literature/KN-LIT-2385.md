## Contribution
Recently, algebraic attacks were proposed to attack several cryptosystems, e.g. AES, LILI-128 and Toyocrypt.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This paper extends the use of algebraic attacks to combiners with memory.
- A (k, l)-combiner consists of k parallel linear feedback shift registers (LFSRs), and the nonlinear filtering is done via a finite automaton with k input bits and l memory bits.
- It is shown that for (k, l)-combiners, nontrivial canceling relations of degree at most dk(l+1)/2e exist.
- This makes algebraic attacks possible.
