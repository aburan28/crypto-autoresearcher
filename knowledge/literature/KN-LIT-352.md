## Contribution
Let E be a non-CM elliptic curve defined over Q. For each prime p of good reduction, E reduces to a curve Ep over the finite field Fp .

## Key claims (as reported)
- For a given squarefree polynomial f (x, y), we examine the sequences fp (E) := f (ap (E), p), whose values are associated with the reduction of E over Fp .
- We are particularly interested in two sequences: fp (E) = p + 1 − ap (E) and fp (E) = ap (E)2 − 4p.
- We present two results towards the goal of determining how often the values in a given sequence are squarefree.
- First, for any fixed curve E, we give an upper bound for the number of primes p up to X for which fp (E) is squarefree.
