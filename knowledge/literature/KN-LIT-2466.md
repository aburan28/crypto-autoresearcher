## Contribution
The XSL “algorithm” is a method for solving systems of multivariate polynomial equations based on the linearization method. It was proposed in 2002 as a dedicated method for exploiting the structure of some types of block ciphers, for example the AES and Serpent.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Since its proposal, the potential for algebraic attacks against the AES has been the source of much speculation.
- Although it has attracted a lot of attention from the cryptographic community, currently very little is known about the effectiveness of the XSL algorithm.
- In this paper we present an analysis of the XSL algorithm, by giving a more concise description of the method and studying it from a more systematic point of view.
- We present strong evidence that, in its current form, the XSL algorithm does not provide an efficient method for solving the AES system of equations.
