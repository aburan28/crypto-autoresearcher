## Contribution
We investigate fine Selmer groups for elliptic curves and for Galois representations over a number field. More specifically, we discuss Conjecture A, which states that the fine Selmer group of an elliptic curve over the cyclotomic extension is a finitely generated Zp -module.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The relationship between this conjecture and Iwasawa’s classical μ = 0 conjecture is clarified.
- We also present some partial results towards the question whether Conjecture A is invariant under isogenies.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to the isogeny cluster: bears on supersingular/isogeny-based constructions and the isogeny-path problems that compete with and inform ECDLP work. Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
