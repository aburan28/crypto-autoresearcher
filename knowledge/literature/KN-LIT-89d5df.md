## Contribution
A compact `GF(2)` **systemizer** (Gaussian elimination to systematic form) and
optimised **constant-time hardware sorters** for Classic McEliece key
generation. Constant-time sorting is required because the permutation is
generated by sorting secret values, and a data-dependent sort leaks it.

## Key claims (as reported)
- A compact GF(2) systemizer for hardware.
- Constant-time hardware sorting networks suited to key generation.

## Relevance to this program
The **constant-time sorting** requirement is the interesting part: a mundane
operation becomes security-critical because its input is secret, and the
standard efficient algorithm for it is disqualified.

That is a generalisable hazard. Security properties attach to operations by
virtue of what flows through them, not by virtue of what the operation is —
which is the same reason the Goppa-polynomial *load* was attackable
([[KN-LIT-6e1eb5]]) while the arithmetic around it was hardened.

**Does not bear on the ECDLP.**
