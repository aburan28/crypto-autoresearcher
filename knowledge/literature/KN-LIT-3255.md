## Contribution
This paper presents several multiset and boomerang attacks on Safer++ up to 5.5 out of its 7 rounds. These are the best known attacks for this cipher and significantly improve the previously known results.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The attacks in the paper are practical up to 4 rounds.
- The methods developed to attack Safer++ can be applied to other substitutionpermutation networks with incomplete diffusion.
