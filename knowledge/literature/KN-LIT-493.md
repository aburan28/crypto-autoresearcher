## Contribution
Let E be an elliptic curve, K1 its Kummer curve E/{±1}, E 2 its square product, and K2 the split Kummer surface E 2 /{±1}. The addition law on E 2 gives a large endomorphism ring, which induce endomorphisms of K2 .

## Key claims (as reported)
- With a view to the practical applications to scalar multiplication on K1 , we study the explicit arithmetic of K2 .
