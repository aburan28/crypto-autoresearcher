- Similarly, the fastest alleged algebraic attack on AES due to Courtois, Pieprzyk, Murphy and Robshaw uses a MQ system over GF (28 ).
- At present very little is known about practical solvability of such systems of equations over GF (2k ).
- The XL algorithm for Eurocrypt 2000 was initially studied over GF (p), and only recently in two papers presented at CT-RSA’02 and ICISC’02 the behaviour of XL is studied for systems of equations over GF (2).
