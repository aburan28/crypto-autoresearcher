## Contribution
Let C be a smooth projective curve defined over Q, let π : E −→ C be an elliptic surface and let σP1 , σP2 , σQ be sections of π (corresponding to points P1 , P2 , Q of the generic fiber E of E). We obtain a precise characterization, expressed solely in terms of the dynamical relations between the points P1 , P2 , Q with respect to the endomorphism ring of E, so that there exist infinitely many λ ∈ C(Q) with the property that for some nonzero integers m1,λ , m2,λ , we have that [mi,λ ](σPi (λ)) = σQ (λ) (for i = 1, 2) on the smooth fiber Eλ of E.
