## Key claims (as reported)
- NTRU is a very efficient public-key cryptosystem based on polynomial arithmetic.
- Its security is related to the hardness of lattice problems in a very special class of lattices.
- This article is motivated by an interesting peculiar property of NTRU lattices.
- Namely, we show that NTRU lattices are proportional to the so-called symplectic lattices.
