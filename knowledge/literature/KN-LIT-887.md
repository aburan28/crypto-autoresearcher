## Contribution
By Mazur’s Torsion Theorem, there are fourteen possibilities for the non-trivial torsion subgroup T of a rational elliptic curve. For each T , such that E may have additive reduction at a prime p, we consider a parameterized family ET of elliptic curves with the property that they parameterize all elliptic curves E/Q which contain T in their torsion subgroup.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Using these parameterized families, we explicitly classify the Kodaira-Néron type, the conductor exponent and the local Tamagawa number at each prime p where E/Q has additive reduction.
- As a consequence, we find all rational elliptic curves with a 2-torsion or a 3-torsion point that have global Tamagawa number 1.
