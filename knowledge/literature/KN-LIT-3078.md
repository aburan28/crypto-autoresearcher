## Contribution
Concurrent non-malleable zero-knowledge (NMZK) considers the concurrent execution of zero-knowledge protocols in a setting where the attacker can simultaneously corrupt multiple provers and veri ers. Barak, Prabhakaran and Sahai (FOCS'06) recently provided the rst construction of a concurrent NMZK protocol without any set-up argument proof assumptions.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Their protocol, however, is only computationally sound (a.k.a., a concurrent NMZK construction of a concurrent NMZK ).
- In this work we present the rst without any set-up assump- tions.
- Our protocol requires poly(n) rounds assuming one-way functions, or Õ(log n) rounds assuming collision-resistant hash functions.
- As an additional contribution, we improve the round complexity of concurrent NMZK arguments based on one-way functions (from poly(n) to Õ(log n)), and achieve a near linear (instead of cubic) security reductions.
