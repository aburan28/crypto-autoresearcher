## Contribution
We introduce a broad lattice manipulation technique for ex- pressive cryptography, and use it to realize functional encryption for access structures from post-quantum hardness assumptions. Speci cally, we build an e cient key-policy attribute-based encryption scheme, and prove its security in the selective sense from learning-witherrors intractability in the standard model.
