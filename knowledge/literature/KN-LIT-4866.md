## Contribution
Recent years have seen considerable interest in lightweight cryptography. One particular consequence is a renewed study of meet-inthe-middle attacks, which aim to exploit the relatively simple key schedules often encountered in lightweight ciphers.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In this paper we propose a new technique to extend the number of rounds covered by a meet-inthe-middle attack, called a match box.
- Furthermore, we demonstrate the use of this technique on the lightweight cipher KATAN, and obtain the best attack to date on all versions of KATAN.
- Specifically, we are able to attack 153 of the 254 rounds of KATAN32 with low data requirements, improving on the previous best attack on 115 rounds which requires the entire codebook.
