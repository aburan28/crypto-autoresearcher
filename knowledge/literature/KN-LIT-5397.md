## Contribution
Secret-sharing is one of the most fundamental primitives in cryptography, and has found several applications. All known constructions of secret sharing (with the exception of those with a pathological choice of parameters) require access to uniform randomness.

## Key claims (as reported)
- However, in practice it is extremely challenging to generate a source of uniform randomness.
- This has led to a large body of research devoted to designing randomized algorithms and cryptographic primitives from imperfect sources of randomness.
- Motivated by this, Bosley and Dodis (TCC 2007) asked whether it is even possible to construct a 2-out-of-2 secret sharing scheme without access to uniform randomness.
- In this work, we make significant progress towards answering this question.
