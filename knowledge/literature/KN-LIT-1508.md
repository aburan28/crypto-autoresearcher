## Contribution
We consider the family of elliptic curves Ea,b : y 2 = x3 + a(x − b)2 with a, b ∈ Z. These elliptic curves have a rational 3-isogeny, say φ.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We give an upper and a lower bound on the rank of the φ-Selmer group of Ea,b over K := Q(ζ3 ) in terms of the 3-part of the ideal class group of certain quadratic extension of K.
- Using our bounds on the Selmer groups, we construct infinitely many curves in this family with arbitrary large 3-Selmer rank over K and no non-trivial K-rational point of order 3.
- We also show that for a positive proportion of natural numbers n, the curve En,n /Q has root number −1 and 3-Selmer rank = 1.
