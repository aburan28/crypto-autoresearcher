## Contribution
We describe plausible lattice-based constructions with properties that approximate the sought-after multilinear maps in hard-discretelogarithm groups, and show an example application of such multi-linear maps that can be realized using our approximation. The security of our constructions relies on seemingly hard problems in ideal lattices, which can be viewed as extensions of the assumed hardness of the NTRU function.
