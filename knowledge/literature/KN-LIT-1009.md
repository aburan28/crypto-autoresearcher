## Contribution
The Hilbert class polynomial HO (x) ∈ Z[x] attached to an order O in an imaginary quadratic field K is the monic polynomial whose roots are precisely the distinct j-invariants of elliptic curves over C with complex multiplication by O. Let p be a prime inert in K and strictly greater than | disc(O)|.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We show that the number of Fp -roots of HO (x) (mod p) is either zero or | Pic(O)[2]| by exhibiting a free and transitive action of Pic(O)[2] on the set of Fp -roots of HO (x) (mod p) whenever it is nonempty.
- We also provide a concrete criterion for the existence of Fp -roots.
- A similar result was first obtained by Xiao et al.
- [XLD] and generalized much further by Li et al.
