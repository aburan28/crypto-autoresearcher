## Contribution
We show that if the quadratic twist family of a given elliptic curve over Fq [t] with Char(Fq ) ≥ 5 has an element whose N éron model has a multiplicative reduction away from ∞, then the average p-Selmer rank is p + 1 in large q-limit for almost all primes p.
