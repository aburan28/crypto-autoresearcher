## Contribution
In this paper, a new tool searching for impossible differentials is presented. Our tool can detect any contradiction between input and output differences.

- This is natural for ciphers with bit-wise diffusion like PRESENT, while finding such impossible differentials for ciphers with word-wise diffusion is novel.
