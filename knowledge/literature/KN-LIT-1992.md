## Contribution
Generalizing results of Stroeker and Top we show that the 2-ranks of the TateShafarevich groups of the elliptic curves y 2 = (x + k)(x2 + k2 ) can become arbitrarily large. We also present a conjecture on the rank of the Selmer groups attached to rational 2-isogenies of elliptic curves.
