## Contribution
This paper initiates the study of the provable security of authenticated encryption (AE) in the memory-bounded setting. Recent works – Tessaro and Thiruvengadam (TCC ’18), Jaeger and Tessaro (EUROCRYPT ’19), and Dinur (EUROCRYPT ’20) – focus on confidentiality, and look at schemes for which trade-offs between the attacker’s memory and its data complexity are inherent.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Here, we ask whether these results and techniques can be lifted to the full AE setting, which additionally asks for integrity.
- We show both positive and negative results.
- On the positive side, we provide tight memory-sensitive bounds for the security of GCM and its generalization, CAU (Bellare and Tackmann, CRYPTO ’16).
- Our bounds apply to a restricted case of AE security which abstracts the deployment within protocols like TLS, and rely on a new memory-tight reduction to corresponding restricted notions of confidentiality and integrity.
