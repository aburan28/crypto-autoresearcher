## Contribution
We generalize Elkies’s method, an essential ingredient in the SEA algorithm to count points on elliptic curves over finite fields of large characteristic, to the setting of p.p. abelian surfaces. Under reasonable assumptions related to the distribution of Elkies primes, we obtain improvements over Schoof’s method in two cases.

## Key claims (as reported)
- If the abelian surface A over Fq has RM by a eF (log4 q) fixed quadratic field F , we reach the same asymptotic complexity O as the SEA algorithm up to constant factors depending on F .
- If A is defined over a number field, we count points on A modulo sufficiently many primes 6 e in O(log q) binary operations on average.
- Numerical experiments demonstrate the practical usability of our methods.
