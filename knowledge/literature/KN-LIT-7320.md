## Contribution
One fundamental complexity measure of an MPC protocol is its round complexity. Asharov et al. recently constructed the first threeround protocol for general MPC in the CRS model.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Here, we show how to achieve this result with only two rounds.
- We obtain UC security with abort against static malicious adversaries, and fairness if there is an honest majority.
- Additionally the communication in our protocol is only proportional to the input and output size of the function being evaluated and independent of its circuit size.
- Our main tool is indistinguishability obfuscation, for which a candidate construction was recently proposed by Garg et al.
