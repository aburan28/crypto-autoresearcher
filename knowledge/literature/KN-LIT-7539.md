## Contribution
Zero correlation linear cryptanalysis is a novel key recovery technique for block ciphers proposed in [5]. It is based on linear approximations with probability of exactly 1/2 (which corresponds to the zero correlation).

## Key claims (as reported)
- Some block ciphers turn out to have multiple linear approximations with correlation zero for each key over a considerable number of rounds.
- Zero correlation linear cryptanalysis is the counterpart of impossible differential cryptanalysis in the domain of linear cryptanalysis, though having many technical distinctions and sometimes resulting in stronger attacks.
- In this paper, we propose a statistical technique to significantly reduce the data complexity using the high number of zero correlation linear approximations available.
- We also identify zero correlation linear approximations for 14 and 15 rounds of TEA and XTEA.
