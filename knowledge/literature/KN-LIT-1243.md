## Contribution
We use Heegner points to prove the existence of nontorsion rational points on the elliptic curve y 2 = x3 + D for any rational number D = a/b such that a and b are squarefree integers for which 6, a, and b are pairwise relatively prime, a ≡ b (mod 4), √ |a||b|−1 ≡ 5 or 7 (mod 9), and hK is odd, where K := Q( 3 D). In particular, we show that under these assumptions, the elliptic curve with equation y 2 = x3 + D has algebraic rank 1 and the elliptic curve with equation y 2 = x3 −D has algebraic rank 0.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This follows from our new expression for the fundamental unit of OK in terms of the class number hK and the norm of a special value of a modular function of level 6, for any integer D relatively prime to 6, not congruent to ±1 (mod 9), for which no exponent in its prime factorization is a multiple of 3.
- This expression is an analogue of a theorem of Dirichlet in 1840 relating the fundamental unit of a real quadratic field to its class number and a product of cyclotomic units.
