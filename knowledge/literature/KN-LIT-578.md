## Contribution
For an abelian variety A over a number field F , we prove that the average rank of the quadratic twists of A is bounded, under the assumption that the multiplication-by-3 isogeny on A factors as a composition of 3-isogenies over F . This is the first such boundedness result for an absolutely simple abelian variety A of dimension greater than one.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In fact, we exhibit such twist families in arbitrarily large dimension and over any number field.
- In dimension one, we deduce that if E/F is an elliptic curve admitting a 3-isogeny, then the average rank of its quadratic twists is bounded.
- If F is totally real, we moreover show that a positive proportion of twists have rank 0 and a positive proportion have 3-Selmer rank 1.
- These results on bounded average ranks in families of quadratic twists represent new progress towards Goldfeld’s conjecture – which states that the average rank in the quadratic twist family of an elliptic curve over Q should be 1/2 – and the first progress towards the analogous conjecture over number fields other than Q.
