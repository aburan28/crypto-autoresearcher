## Contribution
In this paper, we present new collision search attacks on the hash function SHA-1. We show that collisions of SHA-1 can be found with complexity less than 269 hash operations.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This is the first attack on the full 80-step SHA-1 with complexity less than the 280 theoretical bound.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
