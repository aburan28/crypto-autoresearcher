## Contribution
Given a sixth power free integer a, let Ea be the elliptic curve defined by y 2 = x3 + a. We prove explicit results for the lower density of sixth power free integers a for which the 3-isogeny induced Selmer group of Ea over Q(μ3 ) has dimension ≤ 1.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The results are proven by refining the strategy of Davenport–Heilbronn, by relating the statistics for integral binary cubic forms to the statistics for 3-isogeny induced Selmer groups.
