## Contribution
We establish a joint distribution result concerning the fractional part of αpθ for θ ∈ (0, 1), α > 0, where p is a prime satisfying a Chebotarev condition in a fixed finite Galois extension over Q. As an application, for a fixed non-CM elliptic curve E/Q, an asymptotic formula is given for the number of primes at the extremes of the Sato-Tate measure modulo a large prime l.

## Key claims (as reported)
- These are precisely the primes p for which the Frobenius √ trace ap (E) satisfies the congruence ap (E) ≡ [2 p] mod l.
- We assume a zero-free region hypothesis for Dedekind zeta functions of number fields.
