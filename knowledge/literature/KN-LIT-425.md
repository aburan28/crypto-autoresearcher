## Contribution
Let E/Q be an elliptic curve of conductor N , and let K be an imaginary quadratic field such that the root number of E/K is −1. Let O be an order in K and assume that there exists an odd prime p, such that p2 || N , and p is inert in O.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Although there are no Heegner points on X0 (N ) attached to O, in this article we construct such points on Cartan non-split curves.
- In order to do that we give a method to compute Fourier expansions for forms on Cartan non-split curves, and prove that the constructed points form a Heegner system as in the classical case.
