## Contribution
Invariant subspace attacks were introduced at CRYPTO 2011 to cryptanalyze PRINTcipher. The invariant subspaces for PRINTcipher were discovered in an ad hoc fashion, leaving a generic technique to discover invariant subspaces in other ciphers as an open problem.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Here, based on a rather simple observation, we introduce a generic algorithm to detect invariant subspaces.
- We apply this algorithm to the CAESAR candidate iSCREAM, the closely related LS-design Robin, as well as the lightweight cipher Zorro.
- For all three candidates invariant subspaces were detected, and result in practical breaks of the ciphers.
- A closer analysis of independent interest reveals that these invariant subspaces are underpinned by a new type of self-similarity property.

## Relevance to this program
Relevant to pairing-based reductions and endomorphism speedups (MOV/Frey-Rück special cases, GLV/GLS) that bound which curve classes are safe baselines.
