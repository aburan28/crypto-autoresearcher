## Contribution
A Huff curve over a field K is an elliptic curve defined by the equation ax(y 2 − 1) = by(x2 − 1) where a, b ∈ K are such that a2 6= b2 . In a similar fashion, a general Huff curve over K is described by the equation x(ay 2 − 1) = y(bx2 − 1) where a, b ∈ K are such that ab(a − b) 6= 0.
