## Contribution
We consider the problem of increasing the threshold parameter of a secret-sharing scheme after the setup (share distribution) phase, without further communication between the dealer and the shareholders. Previous solutions to this problem require one to start off with a non-standard scheme designed specifically for this purpose, or to have communication between shareholders.

## Key claims (as reported)
- In contrast, we show how to increase the threshold parameter of the standard Shamir secret-sharing scheme without communication between the shareholders.
- Our technique can thus be applied to existing Shamir schemes even if they were set up without consideration to future threshold increases.
- Our method is a new positive cryptographic application for lattice reduction algorithms, inspired by recent work on lattice-based list decoding of Reed-Solomon codes with noise bounded in the Lee norm.
- We use fundamental results from the theory of lattices (Geometry of Numbers) to prove quantitative statements about the information-theoretic security of our construction.
