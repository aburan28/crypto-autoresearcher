## Contribution
“Lapin” is a new RFID authentication protocol proposed at FSE 2012. “Ring-LPN” (Ring-Learning-Parity-with-Noise) is a new computational problem proposed in the same paper; there is a proof relating the security of Lapin to the difficulty of Ring-LPN.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This paper presents an attack against Ring-LPN-512 and Lapin-512.
- The attack is not practical but nevertheless violates specific security claims in the FSE 2012 paper.
