## Contribution
We present a quantum position verification scheme in the random oracle model. In contrast to prior work, our scheme does not require bounded storage/retrieval/entanglement assumptions.

## Key claims (as reported)
- We also give an efficient position-based authentication protocol.
- This enables secret and authenticated communication with an entity that is only identified by its position in space.
