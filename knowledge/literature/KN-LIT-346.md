## Contribution
We show that a character sum attached to a family of 3isogenies defined on the fibers of a certain elliptic surface over Fp relates √ to the class number of the quadratic imaginary number field Q( −p). In this sense, this provides a higher-dimensional analog of some recent class number formulas associated to 2-isogenies of elliptic curves.
