## Contribution
We prove that E(K)tors = E(Q)tors for all elliptic curves E defined over Q and all number fields K of degree d, where d is not divisible by a prime ≤ 7. Using this fact, we determine all the possible torsion groups E(K)tors over number fields K of prime degree p ≥ 7.
