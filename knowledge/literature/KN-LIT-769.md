## Contribution
The hardness of the Learning with Errors (LWE) problem is by now a cornerstone of the cryptographic landscape. In many of its applications the so called “LWE secret” is not sampled uniformly, but comes from a distribution with some min-entropy.

## Key claims (as reported)
- This variant, known as “Entropic LWE”, has been studied in a number of works, starting with Goldwasser et al.
- However, so far it was only known how to prove the hardness of Entropic LWE for secret distributions supported inside a ball of small radius.
- In this work we resolve the hardness of Entropic LWE with arbitrary long secrets, in the following sense.
- We show an entropy bound that guarantees the security of arbitrary Entropic LWE.
