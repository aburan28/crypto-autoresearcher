## Contribution
We use Iwasawa theory, at a prime p inert in a quadratic imaginary field K, to study the arithmetic properties of mock plectic invariants for elliptic curves of rank two. More precisely, under some minor technical assumptions, we prove that the non-vanishing of the mock plectic invariant QK attached to an elliptic curve E/Q of even analytic rank ran (E/K) ⩾ 2, and with multiplicative reduction at p, implies that the p-Selmer rank rp (E/K) equals 2.

## Key claims (as reported)
- The proof rests on one inclusion of Perrin-Riou’s Heegner point main conjecture for elliptic curves with multiplicative reduction at p which we obtain using bipartite Euler systems.
