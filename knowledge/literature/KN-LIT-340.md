## Contribution
In this paper we classify the elliptic fibrations on K3 surfaces which are the double cover of a blow up of P2 branched along rational curves and we give equations for many of these elliptic fibrations. Thus we obtain a classification of the van Geemen–Sarti involutions (which are symplectic involutions induced by a translation by a 2-torsion section on an elliptic fibration) on such a surface.

## Key claims (as reported)
- Each van Geemen–Sarti involution induces a 2-isogeny between two K3 surfaces, which is described in this paper.
