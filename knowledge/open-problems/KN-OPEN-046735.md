## Statement

The native first-fall definition of Caminata-Gorla (Definition 1.3,
arXiv:2112.05579) grades the syzygy slots of the top forms in
B = F_q[w,x,y,z]/(w^q, x^q, y^q, z^q) by deg(b_i) + deg(f_i), modulo the
module generated by the Koszul vectors and f_i^(q-1) e_i. The source audit
(experiments/EXP-PFDR-845d33/source-audit.md, retrieved 2026-09-06,
provenance: retrieved) records the operative gap verbatim:

> UNRESOLVED INTERFACE: Example 4.4 includes w^q among top forms although it
> is zero in B. The retrieved definition does not explicitly grade that zero
> generator, state whether to retain its prequotient degree, or reconcile
> taking top forms before/after passage to B. Retaining original slot weights
> is plausible but is not assumed here. This is an implementation
> prerequisite unresolved by this Executor, not a defect or contradiction
> established in the paper.

**WHAT IS OPEN:** does Definition 1.3 admit a well-posed slot grading when the
generator set (as in Example 4.4) contains w^q = 0 with no explicit zero-slot
degree, and what top-before-quotient convention does the source intend — is
the zero image of a slot a WEIGHTED slot (retaining its prequotient degree) or
a DELETED slot? The answer determines whether the native first-fall meter of
EXP-PFDR-a76b91 (and its predecessor EXP-PFDR-845d33) is well-posed at all,
and therefore whether the pre-registered control triples (3,5,5) at q=3 and
(3,9,9) at q=5 can ever be measured against the source.

## Why it blocks groups 1 and 3

- Group 1 (native q=3 and q=5 fixtures) requires the paper's native
  first-fall definition with its trivial-syzygy convention before any
  arithmetic. If the grading of the w^q slot is not well-posed, no degree
  value d_ff (or the dependent d_lf / solving-degree bounds) can be
  certified, and the frozen protocol prescribes "record unmeasured" — which is
  exactly what RUN-PFDR-20260907-587a55 recorded for both fixtures.
- Group 3 (the finite universal claims "forall native systems sd=d_ff" and
  "forall native systems sd-d_ff<=1") requires a CERTIFIED q=3 native witness
  to refute each claim; "otherwise return inconclusive". No certified native
  witness can exist until the grading is well-posed, so
  RUN-PFDR-20260907-d934ea returned inconclusive for both claims with
  certified_witness null.
- No degree estimate was substituted anywhere; the source predictions remain
  control predictions, never observations.

## Candidate convention (to verify, NOT adopted)

docs/first-fall-audit-repair.md proposes an explicit implementation
convention:

> A concrete proposed resolution is to form top forms in the ordinary
> polynomial ring first and then map them into the truncated ring, preserving
> each original generator degree as the weight of its slot in the graded free
> module. A zero image then has an explicitly weighted slot, rather than
> needing an intrinsic degree assigned to the zero polynomial. The quotient
> by the declared trivial syzygy module must use the same weights. Ordinary
> last-fall computation remains in the ordinary ring, without adding field
> equations.

The document is explicit that this is "a proposed explicit implementation
convention, not a new source theorem or an approved native measurement", and
that the successor "must document its agreement with Definitions 1.3/1.5 and
Examples 4.2/4.4 of Caminata-Gorla ... In particular, check the treatment of
zero slots against the displayed syzygies before freezing code."

Associated first-nontrivial-degree check argument (recorded as a candidate to
verify, not an established result): at q=3 and q=5 the first nontrivial
degree of the meter under the proposed convention is 3, witnessed by the
vector y*e1 - x*e2 (nontrivial in the graded free module), and the two
candidate treatments of the zero slot — retaining the prequotient weight q
versus deleting the slot — give the same first nontrivial degree for these two
controls. Running this check against the source's displayed syzygies is part
of the resolution work, not a premise of it.

## Consequence rule

Until this interface is resolved against the source under Coordinator
authority: native fixtures record **unmeasured**, no degree estimate is
substituted, and no successor contract may run native arithmetic on the
assumption that either candidate treatment is correct. The resolution must be
an additive amendment to a frozen contract, never a rewrite of the historical
records (EXP-PFDR-845d33, EXP-PFDR-a76b91, and their receipts are immutable).
