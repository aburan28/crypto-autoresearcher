## Q2 (answered for generic monodromy; what stays open)

The generic monodromy of the Semaev summation cover is determined, for p > 3
and every nonsingular E/F_p, on both bases (KN-FIND-bdcd60): (Z/2)^{m-2}
regular over the ordered base, (Z/2)^{m-2} semidirect S_{m-1} over the
symmetric base for m >= 4, with an exact Frobenius rule on unramified
F_p-fibres. No curve has a different generic monodromy. KN-OPEN-009's
"full versus exceptional" dichotomy is therefore not exhaustive for this
cover. Not settled here: the monodromy of specializations to proper closed
subvarieties of the base (it can shrink there, as KN-FIND-bdcd60's PT-2 object
shows), and anything about relation rates, which is Q1.
