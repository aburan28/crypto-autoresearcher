# Tier-ladder reachability mock: duplication audit

## Scope of this audit

This audit covers only the proposed synthetic, typed graph interface in
`specification.yaml`. It does not compare the draft to live goals, actual
research lanes, backlog records, external literature, or previous reachability
tools, because those sources were not read in this task.

## Novelty and duplication status

`inconclusive`.

The generic concepts of graphs, ranges, reachability, and typed input refusal
are not asserted to be novel. No claim is made that this mock contract is
distinct from a repository tool, a prior decision process, or published work.
No `known` or `adaptation` status is claimed because no source was retrieved or
compared.

## Distinguishing boundary if later comparison is authorized

Any later duplication comparison must bind exact source paths and hashes, and
must distinguish this draft by all of the following properties:

1. Its inputs are wholly synthetic and include no live record locator.
2. Its classification alphabet has no state-changing output.
3. Its no-state-change witness rejects retirement, approval, priority, and
   execution effects structurally.
4. Its range labels describe mock nodes only and make no portfolio ranking or
   feasibility assertion.

Until that comparison exists, these properties describe the proposed contract
only; they are not evidence of novelty or non-duplication.

## Pareto and state-of-the-art fields

`dominated_by: inconclusive` and `sota_delta: unassessed` because neither a
repository-wide comparison nor literature review was conducted. This draft
contains no time, memory, data, query, asymptotic, or cryptanalytic cost claim.
