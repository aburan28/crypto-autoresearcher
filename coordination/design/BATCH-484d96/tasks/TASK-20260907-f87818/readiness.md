## Counts and discriminating observations

Each nonzero prime domain uses 26 scalar-constraint panel families: 12 positive, four incompatible, and ten wrong-target. Across three order variants this gives `100*26*3 = 7,800` scalar cells for r=101 and `240*26*3 = 18,720` for r=241. Adding 12 malformed and one zero fixture per prime gives 7,813 and 18,733 cases, totaling **26,546**.

The reference is required to visit every exponent of each scalar cell: `7,800*100 + 18,720*240 = 5,272,800` exponent visits. The positive panels' candidate lengths sum to `5*n/2+2` per exponent and order; the informative wrong-target panels sum to `n/2+2`. Thus a correct run predicts 509,040 positive candidate-equality evaluations and 103,440 wrong-target evaluations, totaling 612,480 in the production verification service. The reference's separate work and zero-boundary checks are excluded from that last total and counted separately. These are design-derived expected counts, not measured outputs.

Five panels deliberately expose product-of-moduli coverage errors: P02, P03, P04, P07 and P11. Their total is `340*5*3 = 5,100` positive cells. The control tests whether the measurement machinery can actually detect the proposed artifact, rather than merely checking the correct implementation against its own formula. The brute-force reference does not compute candidates from the production M or CRT logic.

## Remaining uncertainty and later scope

The gcd compatibility rule, coset enumeration, primitive-element test, zero branch and finite controls are fully specified. Parent validation may still identify a schema defect; that would be an administrative correction to this draft before snapshot, not an unreported change to a frozen experiment.

The complete group mechanism is preserved prospectively: certify a residue against each supplied token, combine residues with generalized CRT, search the residual exponent using a specified baby/giant construction, and verify on the original Q. Its real implementation and cost remain unmeasured. Token subgroup membership or agreement with a residue does not prove that a supplied nonlinear power belongs to the original target. A later group protocol must establish supply correctness, source provenance, costs, group fixtures, success and memory/data accounting and independent review. These unresolved transfer requirements must not be recast as failures of the arithmetic hypothesis or as impossibility of the wider approach.

The finite comparator table does not reconstruct the complete published ECC frontier. Its low-memory row uses a parent-read internal decision, not a newly retrieved primary low-memory paper. The historical idea's illustrative exponent arithmetic is preserved in the original proposal; the new hypothesis deliberately claims no new exponent or ordinary-ECDLP improvement.
