## Why this is the highest-value action identified

The published method for every elliptic-curve rank record since 2006 is:
specialise a rank-17 elliptic K3 fibration over Q(t) and sieve each
specialisation for points outside the specialised generic subgroup. The ladder is
17 + 11 = 28 and 17 + 12 = 29 on the same surface.

2006 Elkies wrote that he would post formulas "after I have streamlined the
formulas, which as I have them look needlessly complicated" and never did.
Verified absent from arXiv:0709.2908, his Harvard pages (rk28_1.html is HTTP 404,
and the Wayback availability API returns no snapshot for it at either hostname),
ANTS-XIV, and Dujella's tables, which cite only the listserv post plus "personal
communication, 2006, 2009".

Those personal-communication citations are the evidence that asking works.
