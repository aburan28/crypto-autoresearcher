## Deviations from the task card, all recorded rather than absorbed

1. **A second, larger scan stratum was added mid-task** (`RUN-...-010`, spread 57-74). Reason:
   the first ceiling>=12 family that produced a certified rank-12 fibre came from the sparsely
   sampled large-spread stratum, so the small-spread exhaustive stratum was not where the
   rank-carrying families live. This widens the tuple range searched; it does NOT widen the
   reported best-tuple claim, and §2/§3 of the report are computed over the union.
   It is *not* the "widen tuple size until something works" move the task card forbade: the
   envelope-vs-tuple relationship is reported over the whole union and the result is still a miss
   at every cell.
2. **RUN-002 is invalid and is retained.** See `runs/VALIDITY-CORRECTION-RUN-002.md`. The
   admissibility condition it exposed was not in the approved contract; it is reported as a
   finding rather than treated as a nuisance.
3. **RUN-009 failed on a bad CLI flag** and is retained as `invalid_measurement`.
4. **RUN-012 was superseded by RUN-013** for the deliverables: RUN-012's build predated the merge
   of the spread-57-74 scan into deliverable 1. Both run records are kept; only RUN-013's output
   is the deliverable.
5. **Three runs hit their declared time budget** (005, 006, 011) and PARI `alarm` fired three
   times. Recorded as `resource_exhaustion` / infrastructure outcomes in the run records and in
   report §8. They mean the searched fibre set is a subset of the available one, so the reported
   minima are upper bounds on this method's reach.
6. **The admissibility coefficient was FITTED, then verified.** `[x^5 T^2] r = (24/5)P5 - 2 P2 P3`
   was obtained by solving for two unknown coefficients from two tuples in the 2-dimensional
   space of translation-invariant symmetric degree-5 invariants, then checked exactly on further
   tuples and against the symbolic `deg_x r` on 400 random tuples (0 mismatches) and on both
   published tuples. It is used only to PRE-FILTER the enumeration; the scan additionally
   re-checks `deg_x_r == 4` symbolically per family and refuses otherwise, so no result depends
   on the fitted formula being right.
7. **The generic rank of a family is never asserted.** The task card and H-ECQ-8b600d speak of
   "generic rank 11"; this task records the number of rational sections and the family's own
   Shioda-Tate ceiling, and reports only CERTIFIED ranks of specialisations. Where the report
   says a family "cannot span rank 11", that is a statement about the generic rank bounded by the
   ceiling, with the specialisation caveat stated inline.
8. **The null ladder's rungs are not perfectly matched in content.** They are generated by
   different parameter draws, so their P2 distributions differ; the comparison is therefore
   reported inside the log-P2 window all four rungs cover, with per-rung regressions alongside,
   rather than as a difference of raw medians. Raw per-rung summaries are also recorded.

## Certificate discipline

Every certified rank in `best_candidates.json` was produced twice: once in the search stage
(`search_rank.py` / `certify_candidates.py`) and once again, from scratch, in
`build_deliverables.py`, by a separate invocation of `exact_certify.py` on the exhibited points.
Agreement is recorded per record in `rank_lower_bound_agrees_with_search_stage`. The certifier is
independent of the code that produced the points. `RUN-...-001` and `-003` and `-008` and `-010`
are pure measurement runs and carry `certificate.kind: none`; the certifying runs carry
`certificate.kind: rank_lower_bound`.

## Two reporting defects in the run records, recorded rather than repaired

Run records are immutable, so these are stated here and not edited in place.

1. **`certificate.kind` is wrong in six manifests.** `run_harness.py` defaults the field to
   `rank_lower_bound` unless `RUN_CERT_KIND=none` is set, and that environment variable was set
   only for runs 001, 002 and 003. Runs **008 and 010 are pure measurement runs** (tuple scans,
   no rank claim) and their manifests nevertheless say `kind: rank_lower_bound`; run **009**
   produced no result at all and says the same; run **007** (the null ladder) certifies ranks
   only in its `certified_at_envelope_argmin` block and is otherwise a measurement run. The
   correct values are `none` for 008, 009 and 010, `none` for the measurement part of 007, and
   `rank_lower_bound` for 004, 005, 006, 011, 012, 013 and for 007's certification block. No
   deliverable depends on the field.
2. **`results/` was partially deduplicated after the runs finished.** All ten intermediate JSONs
   there were byte-identical (verified by sha256) to the `raw-result.json` of the run that
   produced them, so the task package carried every large artifact twice and came to 402 MB. The
   six that had NOT been committed were deleted; the four that the orchestrating session had
   already committed in flight were RESTORED and left alone, because deleting a committed blob
   frees nothing and only invites an audit question. `results/README.md` records the sha256 of
   every canonical copy and the exact `cp` commands to restore any of them before re-running
   `build_deliverables.py`. **No run directory was touched.** The package is 329 MB.
