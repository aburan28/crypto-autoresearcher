Stamped 2026-08-01T22:45Z (UTC epoch 1785624347).

Claim: for MINI-AES-64, a plaintext set active in cells drawn from the two
ShiftRows-diagonals DIAG0={0,5,10,15} and DIAG1={4,9,14,3} is BALANCED AFTER
5 ROUNDS when the number of active cells na >= 7 (2^28 texts).

Mechanism: after round 1 the active cells occupy state columns 0 and 1; after
round 2 the set is exactly an affine GF(2)-subspace of dimension 4*na (each
state column varies in a linear image of the free cells, and the level-0 ->
level-2 map is a bijection on the active coordinates). Three further rounds
have algebraic degree at most deg(S)^3 = 3^3 = 27 because the 4-bit SubCells
map has degree 3. A function of degree < d sums to zero over any affine
subspace of dimension d, so 4*na >= 28 forces a zero sum at level 5.

Therefore: na=7 and na=8 predicted BALANCED at level 5; na=5 (dim 20) and
na=6 (dim 24) predicted NOT guaranteed (they hold only if the true 3-round
degree is below 20 resp. 24). Level 6 is predicted NOT balanced at any na<=8,
since 4 rounds bound the degree by 3^4=81 > 64 = the whole block.

THIS IS THE STEP THAT DOES NOT TRANSFER TO AES-128: the AES 8-bit S-box has
degree 7, so the same argument needs 7^3 = 343 < dim, i.e. a subspace larger
than the 128-bit block. The 5-round balance is a property of 4-bit cells.

NAMED ERROR IN THE ARGUMENT. The step "after round 2 the set is an affine
subspace" requires each active LEVEL-1 STATE COLUMN to range over ALL 2^16 of
its values, so that round 2's SubCells acts on a product of full cell ranges
and preserves the product structure. That holds only when a whole ShiftRows
diagonal is active. At na=7, DIAG1 has 3 of 4 cells active, so level-1 column 1
ranges over a proper 12-bit subspace, round-2 SubCells destroys the affine
structure, and the degree bound must instead be applied from level 1 -- four
rounds, 3^4 = 81 > 28. The bound then gives nothing.

CORRECTED PREDICTION, registered now, before the na=8 sweep is run: the
argument is repaired only at na=8, where BOTH diagonals are fully active,
level-1 columns 0 and 1 each range over all 2^16 values, level 2 is an affine
subspace of dimension 32, and three further rounds have degree <= 27 < 32.
na=8 (2^32 texts) is therefore predicted BALANCED at level 5 and not at level 6.
The corrected prediction costs a factor 16 in data: 2^32, not 2^28.
