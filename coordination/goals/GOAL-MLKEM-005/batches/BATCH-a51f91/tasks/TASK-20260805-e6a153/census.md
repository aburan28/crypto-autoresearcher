## Scope restriction, stated before the table

`M` is **the number of ciphertexts decapsulable under one ML-KEM encapsulation key**.
This artifact reports `M` **and nothing derived from it**: there is no bit figure, no
`log2 M`, and no `G` anywhere in it. That is a task-card constraint, not an oversight.

Each row is one of three kinds, and the distinction matters for the counts below:

- `deployment_mode` -- a standardised or deployed way of using an ML-KEM key. These are what the distribution is over.
- `governing_clause` -- a normative document that constrains many modes (FIPS 203, SP 800-227, SP 800-57, the TLS hybrid reuse clause, the CFRG ML-KEM security-considerations draft). Reported because they are where a number would have to live, and mostly do not.
- `scope_note` -- a recorded absence: a mode the task card names that carries no ML-KEM `M` at all, and why.

## Distribution

> Across the 14 deployment-mode rows sourced here, 6 fix M = 1 (R09, R12, R14, R20, R21, R22) and the remaining 8 state no count bound at all (R06, R10, R15, R16, R17, R18, R19, R23) -- and the largest numeric M stated by ANY of the 24 rows, ephemeral or static, is 1, because every static row bounds the key in time or by a rate-limiting SHOULD and never by a number of ciphertexts.

### Does any standardised mode exceed 2^20?

No retrieved specification states any numeric M greater than 1, so none states an M above 2^20. Symmetrically, and this must be reported with equal weight: none of the 8 count-unstated modes forbids one either. The absence is an absence of a stated number in both directions; it is not a sourced ceiling and must not be read as one.

The single load-bearing structural finding: **for every static/reused row, the
specification bounds the key in TIME (certificate validity, cryptoperiod, rotation
recommendation) or by a rate-limiting SHOULD, and never by a count of ciphertexts.**
Where a specification does delegate a count -- draft-ietf-tls-hybrid-design-16 Sec. 2,
'the number of reuses of a KEM public key abides by any bounds in the specification of
the KEM' -- the delegation terminates at FIPS 203, which states no such bound (R01).
Converting any of the time bounds into a count would require a decapsulation rate that
no retrieved specification supplies; inventing one and reporting it would be an
estimate presented as a source, which AGENTS.md rule 9 forbids. So it is not done here.

## Controls

There is no contrast, comparator, surrogate or ablation in this task: it is a
single-arm documentary census with no measured quantity, no treatment and no null
object to construct. `KN-TECH-1a5b7e` modes 1/2/3/5 are therefore **inapplicable by
construction**, and no null is invented in order to look rigorous. What does stand in
for a control is the negative full-text search: for every row whose `M` is recorded as
`not stated`, the search terms used to establish the absence are named in the
derivation column, so the absence is checkable rather than asserted.

## What this census does not say

- It does not state a value of `G`, a block size, or any bit figure. Those are out of scope for this task by construction.
- It does not convert a cryptoperiod or a certificate validity period into a ciphertext count.
- It does not claim that any deployment achieves, or fails to achieve, a large `M`. It reports what the specifications state, and where they state nothing it says so.
- It asserts nothing about ML-KEM's security. Rule 12 is unmet and unwaived; no break is claimed.
