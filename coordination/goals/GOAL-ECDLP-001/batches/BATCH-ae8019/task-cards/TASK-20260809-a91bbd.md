   are undefined. FITREC02 carries 49152 arm-fit records with alpha and beta
   interval endpoints only; there is no gap record kind, representative,

   gap values and their selected endpoints.
2. **REV-CANONICAL-SCHEDULE-008** (critical) — COMPSCH2, PROVRC02, and ALIASv02
   admit multiple byte streams under reasonable implementations because their
   complete grammars are absent.
3. **REV-RESOURCE-CONSUMER-009** (critical) — The zero-residual resource ledger
   omits per-arm sorts and quantile endpoints, independent rank and
   retained-index comparison, S3 bootstrap index consumption, and gap storage.
4. **REV-PACKAGE-FINALIZATION-010** (critical) — S4 final package attestation is
   causally self-referential: it produces the logs, provenance, and index that
   purportedly contain verification, final measurements, validity, and hashes
   of themselves.
5. **REV-MUTATION-LAYER-011** (major) — Mutation enclosure, offsets, retained and
   rebound digests, and ALIASv02 parsing are not frozen, so conforming
   enclosures can reach different first errors.
