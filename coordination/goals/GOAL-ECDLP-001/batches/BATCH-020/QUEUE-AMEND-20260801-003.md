## Why

`ledger/decisions/DEC-20260731-020.yaml` — the id the BATCH-020 queue issued for
this batch's ledger decision — **already exists, is tracked, and is committed**,
and its content belongs to a different goal: the **GOAL-FAEST-001 BATCH-001**
decision (`decision: admit_ideation_to_experiment_design`; `target_ids`
IDEA-20260731-019 / IDEA-20260731-002 / IDEA-20260731-003, GOAL-FAEST-001,
RQ-FAEST-001, EV-FAEST-001; `decided_at: '2026-07-31'`).

Ledger records are immutable and IDs are never reused (AGENTS.md rule 4;
CLAUDE.md conventions), and TASK-20260731-048 is explicitly forbidden to touch
FAEST. **The FAEST record was therefore not overwritten, edited, moved, renamed,
or staged.** It was read only far enough to identify what occupied the path. It
is listed under `do_not_stage` in the TASK-20260731-048 ledger commit receipt.

The resolution follows `ledger/corrections/CORR-20260801-001.yaml`, where the
same collision class on this branch (origin/main having independently taken
DEC-20260731-009..012) was resolved by renumbering **our** record —
DEC-20260731-010 → DEC-20260731-019 — and never the other party's. Applying that
precedent, the BATCH-020 ledger decision is filed at the next free id,
**DEC-20260801-003**. DEC-20260731-021..024 and DEC-20260801-001..002 are also
taken.

## Recurring-defect note (for a future batch, not for repair here)

This is the **third** DEC-id collision in this campaign — DEC-20260731-010 → 019,
the DEC-20260731-009..012 remap, and now DEC-20260731-020. The recurring cause is
structural: this branch and `origin/main` allocate `DEC-YYYYMMDD-NNN` ids from
the same date-scoped sequence independently, with no shared reservation, so any
two concurrent lines of work on the same date collide. It is a harness defect
worth naming and fixing in a future batch (a branch-scoped or goal-scoped
allocation rule, or a pre-write collision check in `tools/validate_ledger.py`).
It is deliberately **not** fixed inside this archive: an archival task repairs its
own declared set, not the id-allocation scheme.
