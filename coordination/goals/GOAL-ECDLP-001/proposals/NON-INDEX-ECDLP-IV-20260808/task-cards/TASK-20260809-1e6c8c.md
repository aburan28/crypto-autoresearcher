## Objective

Independently determine whether the version-2 protocol repair is complete
enough for Coordinator approval, without running either experiment and without
interpreting any mathematical result.

## Required checks

1. Confirm that Arm A has three distinguishable policies: ordinary rho,
   negation-only rho, and full verified automorphism quotient.
2. Confirm identical instance, subgroup, seed, retry, success, certificate, and
   charged-cost bindings across the Arm-A policies.
3. Confirm that the proposed controls can distinguish an automorphism signal
   from a generic walk, relabeling, cycle, or accounting artifact.
4. Confirm that Arm B freezes candidate enumeration before target-side solve
   outcomes and charges one-shot and amortized costs separately.
5. Audit the explicit degree-2/degree-4 boundary against the existing
   `harness/isogeny_class.py` implementation. A missing even-degree formula must
   remain an implementation limitation, never a silent exclusion or negative
   result.
6. Confirm exact implementation paths, command interfaces, artifact paths,
   budgets, stopping rules, invalidation rules, and independent review
   dependencies are all named before approval.
7. Confirm that no field in either amendment changes an already executed run;
   both `affected_runs` lists must remain empty.
8. Confirm that the proposed successor task cards encode exclusive producer
   and archive scopes, the Arm-A-to-Arm-B snapshot dependency, exact artifact
   paths, and a machine-dispatch integration blocker for the immutable v1 queue.

## Deliverable

Write `protocol_review.yaml` beneath the task write scope with:

- `verdict: approve | revise | reject`;
- every blocking defect with an exact field/path;
- the cheapest repair for each defect;
- a statement that no experiment was executed;
- the independent runtime/model receipt and policy provenance.

The review must be independent of the Coordinator that authored the repair and
must use `review-adversarial` at `xhigh` with `independent_session_required: true`.
