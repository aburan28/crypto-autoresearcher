The adjacent binary-digit experiment owns Thue-Morse and Rudin-Shapiro signs of the ordinary integer representative of an F_p coordinate, its A(v)/q_maj instrument, fixtures and matched-marginal controls. The present proposal instead concerns precision-r coordinates of prime-to-p lifted points, lift correctness and the degree/character interface. Those mathematical objects are distinct. Repeating the binary panel would duplicate adjacent work; proving the precision-two interface would not. No outcome from the adjacent experiment is asserted here.

Indispensable revisions to the original proposal:

1. Freeze the Weierstrass lift, public subgroup order, digit convention and cost of statistic T. A polynomial-time lift does not make every arbitrary T polynomial-time. Use n-torsion, rather than exact order n, at the identity.
2. Remove the implication from computability to bounded degree. Its own claim D discloses that gap, so other passages cannot present the statistical transport as achieved.
3. Separate ordinary digits, Witt coordinates and whole-Witt characters. Freeze the exact observable-to-character identity before bounding it.
4. Distinguish the ordinary Serre-Tate canonical lift in Voloch-Walker from an arbitrary good-reduction lift in b4e6eb. Their pointwise agreement on the required prime-to-p subgroup needs an explicit matching-model argument; it does not identify arbitrary global sections.
5. A full-curve character theorem does not automatically control hybrid subgroup sums or q_maj. Bucketing, marginal imbalance, Fourier expansion, nontriviality, exceptional points and subgroup density all need explicit accounting before that interface is admitted.
6. Retire the anomalous prime-to-p non-generator control: its only possible point is the identity. Freeze symbolic known-false controls instead.
7. A disagreement between two implementations first identifies a verification defect. It is not by itself a refutation of a theorem. Preserve invalid observations and diagnose before interpretation.
8. Greater evaluation cost alone does not establish worse total rho cost when iteration dynamics differ. Conversely, redundant information does not imply computational uselessness.
9. An eventual measurement proposal needs exact parameters, an achievable enumeration budget, matched-marginal nulls, multiplicity accounting and inference criteria. Toy agreement cannot close the family; a flat observation need not automatically be an artifact.
