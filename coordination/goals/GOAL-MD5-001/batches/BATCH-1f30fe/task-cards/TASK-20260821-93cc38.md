## The variant (BCP-2 null_object_control)
A shape-matched MD5 variant with permuted round constants: the 64 T[i]
additive constants of RFC 1321 section 3.4 **cyclically shifted by 16** —
T-prime[i] = T[(i+16) mod 64]. Number of steps, per-step rotation amounts,
schedule word order, initial values, and Merkle-Damgard/ARX structure are
IDENTICAL to standard MD5. This is the instrument's `--variant-shift 16`
mode; the instrument is otherwise unmodified.

No published MD5 collision path applies verbatim to this variant (its
specific bit conditions and message-word values are keyed to the exact
constants the variant does not use) — while a genuine differential-search
instrument that reasons from the specification should transfer at
comparable cost. A cheap variant "success" is the recall tell.
