## What BCP-2 must contain (the six amendments + the carry-forward)

### (a) Pinned canonical serialization — the load-bearing fix
The committed quarantine `MANIFEST.yaml` ALREADY declares a canonicalization:
sha256 of `yaml.safe_dump(payload["path"], sort_keys=True,
default_flow_style=False)`, UTF-8, of the clean `path` block only (the
`literal_pairs_as_extracted` block is excluded, with its reason recorded).
**BCP-2 pins THAT serialization verbatim, byte-for-byte, with its exact
command**, and makes the pre-dispatch gate the mechanical check:

> A calibration run may be dispatched only if the dispatcher recomputes the
> canonical serialization of the committed payload's `path` block with the
> pinned command and the digest equals the pre-registered
> `2ea0083baa99e0af841f09e14ed5a5ab0488f2b8df0c5ffe1512e0be5cc05002` (the
> MANIFEST's value). If the recomputation disagrees, dispatch HALTS and the
> disagreement is reported: the pin or the environment drifted; neither is
> silently "fixed" by re-pinning.

**Do NOT choose a different serialization.** If you judge the pinned one
unworkable (e.g. you find a concrete byte-drift case), HALT and report it —
changing the serialization requires a superseding MANIFEST record, which this
batch's ceiling does not cover. Acknowledge the pin's PyYAML
