## Verdict and review boundary

**`DISSENT` on freeze/execution readiness; `CONCUR` on the additive design-only scope and no-execution ceiling.**

The exact prime arithmetic itself survives the first check: `5*2^248-1` has
bit length 251 and `27*2^500-1` has bit length 505, so the declared 32/64-byte
field widths and 65/129-byte curve IDs are consistent with those expressions.
The source pointer at line 248 also contains both expressions. The direct
failures are downstream width equations and the missing operational semantics,
not the prime bit-length labels.

I reviewed only the supplied snapshot tree at
`59f0700234c94ac5eb211e01f03ba517f247e9a7`, whose parent is
`3da590eafab28cefe25db26232ef510d9b4443a7`. The snapshot changes exactly the
four declared snapshot paths. The three producer/specification SHA-256 values
in `snapshot-receipt.json` match the bytes at that commit. I did not treat the
current worktree `HEAD` (`7a5762fddcae1d61c9655da87f66fb8f99b4c4da`) as evidence.
The receipt's `commit_sha: null` and `pending_post_commit` status remain an
operational provenance caveat for the Coordinator's archival work; they are
not a scientific failure or a reason to edit the immutable receipt.

The predecessor Validator and Red Team findings were read. The successor does
close several of their earlier objections, particularly the Fp² basis,
finite `L_steps`, typed branch-specific null declarations, C key/statistic
fields, and the random-set/non-SSI separation. The objections above are
residual defects found in the successor itself, including two arithmetic
contradictions that were not present merely as predecessor ambiguity.

No experiment, Stage 0/Stage 1 arithmetic run, cryptographic computation,
synthetic diagnostic, parser-based scientific check, or cost measurement was
executed. The proposed mutations in this report are static counterexamples to
the contract, not observations. No input, specification, queue, ledger, or
predecessor artifact was edited.
