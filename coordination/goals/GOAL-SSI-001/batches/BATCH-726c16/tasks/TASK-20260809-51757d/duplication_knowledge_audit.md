not claim that the SSI advice-frontier route has been executed, supported,

are read-only inputs. The new contract intentionally does not copy their v1,

`experiments/EXP-SSI-16649/inputs/INCUMBENT-FOE-v1.yaml`, whose pending status

replaced by a claim in the specification; it requires a separately archived,
independently verified source measurement.

whether these declarations are sufficient and internally consistent.

or missing incumbent is an operational or admission condition, not negative
cryptanalytic evidence.
