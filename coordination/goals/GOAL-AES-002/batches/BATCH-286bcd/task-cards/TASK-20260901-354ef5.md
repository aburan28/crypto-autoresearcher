## What to produce

`c2-paired-validation-design.yaml`, following `templates/research-records.md`'s
`experiment` schema exactly (copy the field names; do not invent new ones):

- **`preregistered_prediction`**: quantity = the full empirical distribution of
  h_r (Hamming distance between the two trajectories' full state) at each
  round r; formula = predicted convergence to Binomial(128, 1/2) by round ~2-4;
  source = the full-diffusion argument in object-enumeration.yaml's C2 entry,
  cited by path, stated at REDUCED confidence per the H-AES-77230c precedent
  named above.
- **Two arms, both explicit**:
  1. REAL CIPHER: instrumented (non-AES-NI) AES-128/192/256, two independently
     uniform random keys per sample, fixed plaintext, h_r recorded at every
     round boundary.
  2. MATCHED NULL CONTROL: identical instrumentation, with the real key
     schedule REPLACED by independent uniformly random round-key sequences
     (the whitening key RK[0] preserved, per the D-4 lesson C2's own
     minimal_test_sketch names).
- **Sensitivity anchor**: round 1, where a single-round-key XOR difference
  should produce a LARGE, easily detected deviation from the null — this is
  the check that the instrument has any sensitivity at all.
- **`invalidation_rules`**: the round-count DECAY CHECK, stated as an explicit,
  checkable rule and not merely mentioned: the excess |h_r distribution − null|
  MUST decay monotonically with round for BOTH the real cipher and the matched
  null arm. A quantity that does not decay is the canonical artifact tell
  (docs/inventor-protocol.md section 3) and invalidates the run's interpretation
  if it occurs.
- **`scale_relevance`**: tier `toy` per RQ-AES-002 R7 default; state the sample
  count (N = 2^16 to 2^24 independent (K,K′) pairs per round, per C2's own
  minimal_test_sketch) and the wall-clock estimate derived from the RANK 3
  envelope receipt's 1-core pure-Python throughput figure (11061.1924
  evals/s/core), cited by path — do NOT re-measure the envelope here.
- **`tail_checks`**: name at least one extreme-sample consistency check (e.g.
  the smoothest/most-deviant sampled h_r against its predicted tail
  probability under Binomial(128,1/2)), per docs/target-result-profile.md A7's
  pattern.
- **`budget`**: a concrete `wall_clock_seconds_per_run`, `maximum_runs`, and
  `maximum_memory_gb` estimate, scoped to this environment's measured envelope
  (4 cores, no AES-NI confirmed working, per the RANK 3 receipt).
- **`heuristic_under_test`**: name the implicit heuristic being tested ("AES's
  data path behaves, with respect to paired-independent-key Hamming distance,
