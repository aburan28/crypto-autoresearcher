## (a) CM-1 — the frozen cost model

Fix ALL SIX elements (a)–(f) of RQ-AES-002 `claim_tier_ceiling` R2, explicitly
and separately for AES-128, AES-192 and AES-256:

- **(a) UNIT.** One full AES-k encryption of one block (10/12/14 rounds) on the
  pinned FIPS-197-verified implementation, stating whether the key schedule is
  amortised or charged. Attack work counted in S-box lookups, table accesses,
  XORs or "operations" is converted into this unit by a STATED CONVERSION, or
  the comparison is void.
- **(b) SUCCESS CONVENTION.** Average-case 2^(k-1) or worst-case 2^k, declared
  ONCE and applied to BOTH sides. Mixing conventions manufactures a factor of 2,
  which is inside the margin any plausible result here would have.
- **(c) SUCCESS PROBABILITY.** An attack succeeding with probability p is charged
  at cost/p, and p is measured or derived, never assumed.
- **(d) DATA.** Charged in queries and in their kind (known / chosen plaintext /
  chosen ciphertext / adaptive). The definitional reference needs about two known
  pairs; an attack needing 2^60 chosen plaintexts is not "faster" on a single
  scalar and must be compared on the full Pareto frontier of time, memory, data
  and success probability.
- **(e) MEMORY.** Stated always, beside time. A time-only comparison is
  incomplete and is rejected.
- **(f) PRECOMPUTATION AND VERIFICATION.** Charged end to end, including the cost
  of confirming a candidate key.

CM-1 is FROZEN on commit and states that in its own text, together with its role
as a pre-dispatch gate for every later cryptanalytic card of this campaign.

**Write it to be attacked.** It is the primary target of the independent
