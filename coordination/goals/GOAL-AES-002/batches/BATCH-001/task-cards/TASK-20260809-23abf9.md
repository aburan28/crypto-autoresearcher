### (a) Cost model CM-1, frozen and independently reviewable

CM-1 fixes ALL SIX elements (a)-(f) of RQ-AES-002 `claim_tier_ceiling` R2, for EACH of AES-128, AES-192 and AES-256:

- **(a) UNIT** — one full AES-k encryption of one block at 10/12/14 rounds on the pinned FIPS-197-verified implementation, stating explicitly whether the key schedule is amortised or charged, with a STATED CONVERSION from S-box lookups, table accesses, XORs or "operations" into that unit. Without the conversion the comparison is void.
- **(b) SUCCESS CONVENTION** — average-case `2^(k-1)` or worst-case `2^k`, declared ONCE and applied to BOTH sides. Mixing conventions manufactures a factor of 2, which sits inside any plausible margin here.
- **(c) SUCCESS PROBABILITY** — an attack succeeding with probability `p` is charged at `cost/p`, and `p` is measured or derived, NEVER assumed.
- **(d) DATA** — charged in queries AND in their kind (known / chosen plaintext / chosen ciphertext / adaptive). The reference needs about two known pairs; an attack needing large chosen data is compared on the FULL PARETO FRONTIER of time, memory, data and success probability, never on a single scalar.
- **(e) MEMORY** — stated always, beside time. A time-only comparison is incomplete and is rejected.
- **(f) PRECOMPUTATION AND VERIFICATION** — charged end to end, including the cost of confirming a candidate key.

CM-1 also states, in its own text, that it is FROZEN AT COMMIT: any later change requires a versioned `protocol_amendment` on its own rationale, because a cost model adjusted after a margin is observed is a success criterion changed after observing an outcome. CM-1 names what a red team should attack in it — TASK-20260809-33a159 is dispatched specifically to attack it, and a cost model naming no attack surface is not a cost model.
