## Concrete next candidate

The proposed contract asks one uniform question: do the declared facts establish a nonzero scalar restriction that maps C to itself? Its twelve cards include:

- Four positive controls, including a valid restriction despite a nonscalar ambient matrix, a non-diagonal ambient matrix, or an ambient kernel.
- Four negative controls for non-invariance, zero scalar, origin-shifting translation and a whole-space scalar overclaim.
- Four underdeclared controls, each with one named missing premise and two fully described completions with opposite answers.

These are explicit additive groups and vector spaces over F_5, with F_2 appearing only in the characteristic control. They are analogues for scope reasoning. No elliptic-curve realization or CM descent statement is assumed. The field control detects a characteristic-dependent premise; it does not prove rational descent of a CM map.
