## G30-010: Execute the H-FB3-001 structured factor-base geometry battery (EXP-FB3-001)
- **Cluster:** 04 FACTORBASE
- **Type:** measurement
- **Long-term chain:** This goal decides whether ANY structured factor-base geometry yields an exponent-relevant (growing-with-N) decomposition advantage. A growth signal gates a larger-N confirmation arm and feeds base selection for the campaign-critical CLM-ECDLP-RELATION-COLLECTION claim (which gates any sub-rho end-to-end cost model); a decisive family-wide KILL permanently retires the six-geometry F3 family and protects the program from re-proposing geometry arms (anti-duplication rule i).
- **Mechanism:** Run the already-approved, pre-registered EXP-FB3-001 battery: six structured geometries (high_bit_interval H018, small_height H019, mixed_two_base H020, coset_union H021, greedy_optimized H022, asymmetric_sizing H004) against a matched random base of identical size on the same curve and same targets, paired not pooled. Per-cell permutation null on base labelings (>=100 draws) separates geometry signal from size-matched noise; one Holm-Bonferroni correction covers the full 8-cell family including the already-run H016 (QR) and H017 (small-multiples) prior cells, which enter the accounting but are NOT re-run. The greedy base is selected on a training half and evaluated on held-out targets (each target seen at most once during optimization).
- **Quantified prediction:** Under the null (the H028/H032 pattern), all geometry yield ratios sit ~1 within bootstrap CIs and permutation bands at every N. Alive criterion (the hypothesis direction): at least one geometry with Holm-adjusted yield-ratio CI excluding 1 AND effect slope vs log2 N excluding 0 in the alive direction — concretely ratio >=1.5 at N=2^18 with fitted slope >=+0.15 per doubling of N across 2^14..2^18, CI-separated from 0.
- **Test boundary / scope:** >=4 generic curves, N in {2^14, 2^16, 2^18} core arms (n<=18), m=3, >=300 random targets per cell, seeds {1,2,3,4}; the optional confirmation arm up to n<=24 fires ONLY on a growth signal, per H-FB3-001 interpretation_limits (toy n<=24 honesty ceiling; a growth signal at toy scale triggers confirmation, never a crypto claim).
- **Primary metric:** yield ratio (structured / matched random) per (geometry, N) with bootstrap CI and permutation-null band / **Secondary metrics:** effect slope vs log N per geometry; Holm-adjusted family significance; coverable-target fraction (H017 starvation diagnostic); typed-decomposition mix (mixed_two_base); greedy train/eval transfer gap; per-size exponent fit (asymmetric_sizing).
- **Controls:** Matched random base, same size, same curve, same targets per cell; permutation null >=100 draws per cell; same-curve/same-target pairing across geometries; H016/H017 prior cells included in Holm accounting without re-running (no re-sorting of existing pools); greedy train/held-out separation with leakage invalidating the greedy arm.
- **Falsification criteria:** All geometry ratios ~1 within CIs/permutation bands at every N, OR every nonzero effect constant across N (fails the growth clause) -> scoped KILL of the F3 family, "no signal seen at reachable scale," with family-wise correction reported.
- **Negative-outcome value:** A family-wise KILL decisively closes all six geometry arms plus the two prior cells, so the Coordinator stops commissioning factor-base-geometry variants and redirects budget to representation (G30-011), exceptional-curve (G30-012), and non-geometry frontiers; a constant-but-nonzero effect is recorded as "interesting structure, not alive" and likewise does not feed base selection.
- **Budget:** wall_clock 24h, memory 8 GiB, max_runs 96
- **Stopping rule:** The full pre-registered cell list runs regardless of per-cell outcomes (no early-stop fishing); censored cells are reported. A geometry infeasible at matched size on a curve is recorded infeasible for that cell, not null. At most ONE confirmation arm (n<=24) per surviving geometry before any alive claim; post-hoc geometry variants are exploratory and cannot satisfy the success criterion.
- **Harness dependency:** To be commissioned: the H016 decomposition-counting harness exists only as one-off sources plus the results format in inputs/h100_session/h016_base_yield.json — it must be reconstructed as a reusable in-repo module (bootstrap CIs, permutation null, growth arms) together with the generalized EXP runner (harness gap 3); h022_greedy_base.py exists only as a sketch and must be implemented with enforced train/eval separation.
- **Claim tier:** toy
- **Dependencies:** none (H016/H017 prior cells already run; EXP-FB3-001 specification.yaml approved per DEC-20260717-002)

## G30-011: Representation census — Edwards / Jacobi-intersection / Hessian vs short Weierstrass at matched instances
- **Cluster:** 04 FACTORBASE
- **Type:** measurement
- **Long-term chain:** Directly addresses KN-OPEN-003. If any curve model or symmetry yields lower-degree or sparser decomposition systems at matched instances, it gates a genuine solving-cost reduction for the point-decomposition stage, which gates the end-to-end cost model (KN-OPEN-006) needed before any sub-rho claim; if all models are invariant, it extends the EV-REP-001/002 model-invariance barrier to the untested models and symmetrized forms, protecting the program from future representation re-proposals.
- **Mechanism:** For matched instances — the same underlying curve (constructed to admit each model) and the same factor-base point set, addressed in model-native coordinates — build the m=3 decomposition ideal in short Weierstrass (Semaev), twisted Edwards, Jacobi-intersection, and Hessian models, in BOTH raw and symmetrized form (negation-map quotient / FHJRV-style variable reduction, the explicitly unresolved confound of EV-REP-002). Measure summation-polynomial total degree, term count (sparsity, never before recorded), symmetrized variable/equation reduction, and Gröbner cost per cell. New ground relative to the closed H-REP-001 scope (native Edwards vs Weierstrass d_reg at m<=3, d<=8): Jacobi-intersection and Hessian models, symmetrization gain, and sparsity statistics — none previously measured.
- **Quantified prediction:** Symmetrization reduces effective variables/equations by ~2x at matched d in every model (structural, expected to replicate KN-LIT-004 constant-factor gains). Model-native summation-polynomial degree is expected invariant (the H-REP-001 pattern), but term counts differ by model. Positive result: at least one model whose SYMMETRIZED system solves with GB wall-time CI-separated >=1.5x faster than symmetrized Weierstrass at matched (curve, base, d), with the gap non-decreasing across d in {6, 8, 12}.
- **Test boundary / scope:** Curves constructed to admit each model (order-4 torsion for Edwards/Jacobi-intersection, rational 3-torsion for Hessian); p in {2^12..2^20}; >=3 seeds and >=3 coefficient-variance replicates per model; d in {4, 6, 8, 12}; m=3 census, plus a capped m=4 pilot at d<=6. Toy tier throughout.
- **Primary metric:** per-PDP GB wall-time ratio (model symmetrized / Weierstrass symmetrized) at matched instance and d / **Secondary metrics:** summation-polynomial total degree; term count (sparsity) per model; symmetrized variable/equation reduction factor; GB basis size and max-degree proxy (NOT d_reg; the d_reg proxy limitation of harness/semaev.py applies); trivial-ideal incidence.
- **Controls:** Same curve and same factor-base point set across models (base membership defined model-independently); >=3 coefficient-variance replicates per model to separate a genuine representation effect from random coefficient noise (the KN-OPEN-003 guard); trivial-ideal control distinguishes "no decomposition" from a cheap solve; GB timeouts recorded as failed_infrastructure, never as evidence (AGENTS rule 5). No re-measurement of the closed H-REP-001 cell (native Edwards d_reg, m<=3, d<=8) except as a pinned replication control.
- **Falsification criteria:** All symmetrized model systems within CI of symmetrized Weierstrass at every d (full model invariance), OR any gap constant / non-growing in d (constant-factor only, the FHJRV/IC-5 verdict) -> scoped kill of representation as a solving-exponent lever for the tested models.
- **Negative-outcome value:** Closes KN-OPEN-003 for the three major alternative models plus symmetrization at toy scope; the program then fixes Weierstrass Semaev cost as the canonical PDP baseline for all downstream cost models and stops spending on representation search, redirecting to G30-010 geometry results and the KN-OPEN-006 structured-linear-algebra route.
- **Budget:** wall_clock 12h, memory 8 GiB, max_runs 60
- **Stopping rule:** Pre-registered (model x d x seed) cell list runs in full; model-infeasible cells recorded infeasible, not null. The m=4 pilot is capped at 2h total and starts only after the m=3 census completes; any model whose summation-polynomial construction cannot be verified against planted decompositions is dropped with a recorded reason, not silently skipped.
- **Harness dependency:** To be commissioned: (a) harness gap 2 — measure_sm_decomposition for m=3,4 with factor-base ideal systems (extends harness/semaev.py beyond the current measure_s3_decomposition); (b) a new pure-Python/sympy model module constructing Edwards/Jacobi-intersection/Hessian models and their summation polynomials with planted-decomposition self-checks (prior REP work used one-off Sage scripts; harness is Sage-free, gap 5 noted).
- **Claim tier:** toy
- **Dependencies:** G30-026 (hard dep: this census requires measure_sm_decomposition for m=3,4, listed as harness dependency (a)); complements G30-010 (both feed the PDP stage of the KN-OPEN-006 end-to-end cost model); replication-control cell references closed EXP-REP-001/002 artifacts without re-running them

## G30-012: Exceptional-curve sieve — Chebotarev/monodromy and S-unit predicates with fresh-curve held-out controls

- **Type:** attack-probe
- **Long-term chain:** If an a priori computable arithmetic predicate identifies curve subfamilies with measurably higher decomposition rates on FRESH curves, it gates a weak-target selection layer for relation collection (which gates reduced-cost attacks on predicate-selected instances and sharpens where the D2 full-monodromy quasirandomness barrier does and does not hold); a null closes the predicate-based curve-selection attack surface at tested scope — the conversion of A1/C2 nulls into two-sided D2 barrier evidence is owned by G30-009, whose empirical arm this goal's record feeds.
- **Mechanism:** Two-phase sieve designed against the closed ECFG failure mode (every frozen graph-feature selector failed fresh-window held-out controls). Phase 1 (calibration): on a training cohort of toy ordinary prime-order curves, consume G30-007's certified full-wreath curve set and per-curve Frobenius cycle-type histograms plus G30-008's resolvent outputs (extra-block-system certificates and factorization patterns) as predicate inputs, computing in-house only the S-unit smoothness statistics of curve arithmetic data (A3) — then FREEZE at most 3 pre-registered selection predicates, each with a stated predicted held-out lift. A predicate that was fit to the training cohort and re-tuned after seeing held-out data is void by construction.
- **Quantified prediction:** Two-sided, pre-registered: under the exceptional-family hypothesis, predicate-selected families appear at rate >=1/20 with held-out decomposition-rate lift >=1.3x over matched uniform fresh curves, CI-separated; under the full-wreath generic expectation, all selected-cohort deviations from G30-007's certified histograms stay within sampling noise and every held-out lift CI includes 1. - **Primary metric:** held-out yield lift (decomposition rate of predicate-selected fresh curves / matched uniform fresh-curve controls) / **Secondary metrics:** exceptional-family incidence rate; deviation from the full-monodromy Chebotarev prediction in Weil-floor units; predicate transfer gap (training lift minus held-out lift — the selector-fragility diagnostic); positive/negative control pass rates.
