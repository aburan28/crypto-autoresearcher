## G30-013: PO-transfer-007 probe — label-conditioned smoothness on cyclic covers X_d: z^d = h(P), fully charged
- **Cluster:** 05 RELATIONS
- **Type:** attack-probe
- **Long-term chain:** PO-transfer-007 is the last open auxiliary-cover frontier. This goal either (a) produces the first fully charged sub-rho relation engine, which would gate a scaling study toward medium-tier instances, or (b) closes the entire auxiliary-cover/correspondence transfer family (TRANSFER-NR-003..052, PO-transfer-001..007 lineage), redirecting all RELATIONS effort to counting-law and barrier work (G30-014, D1/D2/D3).
- **Mechanism:** For E/F_p in the prime-order subgroup, fix d ∈ {2, 3} and rational functions h ∈ F_p(E) of low degree. On the cyclic cover X_d: z^d = h(P), a relation event is a Norm_{X_d/E} value that factors completely over a factor base B. The open hypothesis is that conditioning on the d-th-power character label χ(h(P)) raises smoothness probability above the unconditioned rate (label-conditioned smoothness), and that cover rank is reusable across targets with blind descent. The probe measures the conditioned-vs-unconditioned smoothness ratio α, then charges everything: cover construction and h evaluation (preprocessing), rank/linear algebra, per-target descent, and memory, converting to group-op equivalents.
- **Quantified prediction:** a label-conditioned smoothness advantage exists but is small: α ∈ [1.2, 2.0] for at least one (d, h) cell at 12–16 bits; despite this, the fully charged end-to-end cost ratio vs matched rho is ≥ 3x and ≤ 46x (i.e., better than or comparable to PO67's 46–246x but still super-rho). No cell achieves ratio < 1.
- **Test boundary / scope:** toy curves at 8/10/12/14/16 bits via harness/toycurve.py, seeds 1–4, prime-order subgroup only; d ∈ {2, 3}; h from a frozen menu of ≤ 4 low-degree rational functions fixed before any run; factor base |B| = 14 at 8–12 bits, scaled to ~p^{1/2}/ln p at 14–16 bits; ≤ 2 independent instances per (bit size, seed).
- **Primary metric:** fully charged end-to-end cost ratio vs matched rho (group-op equivalents, identical instances and seeds) / **Secondary metrics:** conditioned-vs-unconditioned smoothness ratio α per cell; relation yield per trial; measured matrix rank vs |B| (rank-deficiency check); preprocessing group-op equivalents; peak RSS; distribution (median and IQR) of the ratio across seeds, never best-run.
- **Controls:** matched Pollard rho (harness/rho.py, Teske 32-branch) on every identical instance; unconditioned smoothness rate as the within-experiment control for α; fresh-window held-out instances (labels frozen before the held-out window is opened) to block selector fragility; trivial-ideal control on the decomposition side so "no relation" is distinguished from "cheap solve"; cover-fiber multiplicity netted out (each success and each trial counted with its fiber weight).
- **Falsification criteria:** the direction is killed if (i) α ≤ 1.05 across all (d, h) cells and seeds (no label-conditioning effect), or (ii) the best fully charged ratio is ≥ 46x (no improvement over the PO67 precedent), or (iii) measured rank saturates below |B| − 2 in every cell (reusable-rank premise fails). Conversely, any single verified cell with fully charged ratio < 1 falsifies the predicted negative and triggers mandatory independent review-xhigh review before any promotion.
- **Negative-outcome value:** a negative closes PO-transfer-007 and, with it, the whole auxiliary-cover transfer family; the Coordinator can then reject_scoped the correspondence-transfer research direction and reallocate cluster 05 budget to G30-014 (counting laws) and the barrier-theory track (D1/D2/D3). A positive (ratio < 1) changes the campaign plan to commission a medium-tier scaling ladder.
- **Budget:** wall_clock 16h, memory 8 GiB, max_runs 120
- **Stopping rule:** stop when every (bit size, seed, d, h) cell has both a completed_valid cover run and its matched rho control, OR when 16h wall clock is exhausted, OR when two consecutive bit sizes show α ≤ 1.05 with ratio ≥ 10x (early closure); timeouts/cancellations are reported as censored, never as evidence against the mechanism.
- **Harness dependency:** existing toycurve.py + rho.py + runner.py; consumes the end-to-end cost-model module (harness gap #4) owned and commissioned by G30-001 — this goal does NOT commission it — converting Gröbner/LA and descent cost into rho-comparable group-op equivalents; plus a norm-factorization/symbolic-cover script promoted from one-off Sage experiment style into a reusable module.
- **Claim tier:** toy
- **Dependencies:** HARD dependency on G30-001 (sole owner of the end-to-end GOE cost model, harness gap #4); shares the resulting cost-model module with G30-015 as a consumer only, never as a co-commissioner; no dependency on other clusters' goal IDs.
- **Revision note (2026-07-23 review):** Removed wording commissioning the end-to-end cost model and replaced it with a hard dependency on G30-001, the sole GOE cost-model owner, to eliminate duplicate commissioning across goals.

## G30-014: B1 AG-code enumerator calculus — exact relation-count law validated against brute force at toy scale
- **Cluster:** 05 RELATIONS
- **Type:** measurement
- **Long-term chain:** every sub-rho index-calculus chain (relation collection → rank → descent → end-to-end) currently lacks an exact relation-count law; B1 (round-2 conjecture) would supply one via AG-code weight enumerators and the MacWilliams transform. This goal validates the calculus itself before any attack claim: a validated counting law gates CLM-ECDLP-RELATION-COLLECTION (currently not_attempted) and gives the D2 barrier theorem (quasirandom relation rates) a precise, falsifiable quantitative form.
- **Mechanism:** Model the Semaev summation + factor-base relation process as punctured evaluation AG codes on E/F_p: relation events in a sampling window are codewords of a specified weight, so per-window relation counts are weight-enumerator coefficients, and the MacWilliams transform of the dual code yields an exact count law including the chronic rank-saturation effect (14/16 at |B| = 16). Compute each toy cell's relation counts two independent ways: (i) exhaustive brute-force enumeration of all relation events, and (ii) the closed-form enumerator calculus, then compare exactly.
- **Quantified prediction:** the enumerator calculus reproduces brute-force relation counts with relative deviation < 1e-9 (exact law, deviations only from floating rounding) in every computable cell at 8–14 bits, and reproduces the observed 14/16 rank saturation as an enumerator identity with zero free parameters.
- **Test boundary / scope:** toy curves at 8/10/12/14 bits, seeds 1–4, prime-order subgroup; factor base |B| ∈ {8, 12, 14, 16}; summation degrees m ∈ {2, 3} (S_2/S_3 explicit in harness/semaev.py); all windows enumerated exhaustively — no sampling; ≤ 2 instances per (bit size, seed).
- **Primary metric:** maximum absolute relative deviation between enumerator-predicted and brute-force relation counts across all cells / **Secondary metrics:** predicted vs measured rank of the relation matrix; enumerator computation time vs brute-force time (the calculus's computational edge); fraction of cells where the calculus is evaluable at all with the commissioned module.
- **Controls:** brute-force exhaustive enumeration is the ground-truth control; an MDS/Reed–Solomon sanity cell (where the weight distribution is known in closed form) validates the enumerator implementation itself; hold-out: the calculus parameters (code length, dimension, designed distance) are frozen before the 14-bit cells are enumerated.
- **Falsification criteria:** the B1 conjecture is killed within toy scope if (i) any evaluable cell shows relative deviation > 1e-6 after rounding audit, or (ii) the calculus provably requires input (e.g., a full weight distribution) that is not computable at 14 bits within budget — i.e., the "exact law" is exact only in principle, or (iii) the rank-saturation identity fails to reproduce 14/16 in any |B| = 16 cell.
- **Negative-outcome value:** a failure closes the B1 round-2 direction and tells the Coordinator to route the relation-counting-law need to A3 (Mason–Stothers/Evertse S-unit bounds) or to treat D2 (full wreath monodromy forcing quasirandom rates) as the default working assumption; a success green-lights B1 as the counting engine for any future relation-collection claim, replacing ad-hoc empirical counts.
- **Budget:** wall_clock 10h, memory 4 GiB, max_runs 60
- **Stopping rule:** stop when all frozen cells have paired enumerator + brute-force results with a deviation table, OR at 10h wall clock, OR immediately upon the first deviation > 1e-6 confirmed by re-run under a new RUN id (early falsification); censored cells are listed explicitly.
- **Harness dependency:** existing toycurve.py, semaev.py (S_2/S_3), runner.py; commissions one new module harness/agcode.py (weight-enumerator + MacWilliams utilities in pure Python/sympy) — small, within current pure-Python harness style, no Sage required.
- **Claim tier:** toy
- **Dependencies:** none (independent of G30-013/G30-015); its output is an input to any future activation of CLM-ECDLP-RELATION-COLLECTION (active-cartography claim matrix).

## G30-015: Split-Jacobian projected smoothness — a genus-2 correspondence relation engine that never factors through x-line Semaev
- **Cluster:** 05 RELATIONS
- **Type:** attack-probe
- **Long-term chain:** the program needs at least one relation engine whose relations are not x-line Semaev summations, because every closed family so far shares that representation. A split-Jacobian engine (open claim P1546) either survives the full-cost gate — gating a scaling study and a candidate route to CLM-ECDLP-SUBRHO-END-TO-END — or completes the correspondence-transfer closure, strengthening the D1/D2 barrier hypotheses that protect the program from false positives.
- **Mechanism:** Construct genus-2 curves C/F_p with explicitly split Jacobian Jac(C) ~ E × E' (gluing along controlled kernels), where E is the target curve. Relations are principal divisors div(f), f ∈ F_p(C), whose Abel–Jacobi class projected onto the E-factor lands in the E factor base (projected smoothness). No x-line Semaev summation polynomial is ever formed; the relation source is the genus-2 function field. From day one, all costs are charged: genus-2 divisor arithmetic, relation collection, matrix rank/linear algebra, the Jac(C) → E projection descent, target descent, and memory, in group-op equivalents.
- **Quantified prediction:** the genus penalty dominates: projected-smoothness relation density is measured within a factor 2 of its theoretical estimate, but the fully charged end-to-end cost ratio vs matched rho is ≥ 10x in every cell at 12–20 bits, with no sub-rho cell; ratio grows with bit size (adverse toy scaling, exponent > 1 measured on the 12/16/20-bit ladder).
- **Test boundary / scope:** toy E at 12/16/20 bits, seeds 1–4, prime-order subgroup; ≤ 3 split-Jacobian constructions per instance, frozen before runs; factor base on E sized ~p^{1/2}/ln p; genus-2 arithmetic via a commissioned reusable module (promoting the relevant one-off Sage experiment scripts); ≤ 2 instances per (bit size, seed).
- **Primary metric:** fully charged end-to-end cost ratio vs matched rho (group-op equivalents, identical instances) / **Secondary metrics:** projected-smoothness relation density vs theoretical estimate; relation yield per genus-2 function trial; measured rank vs factor-base size; preprocessing and descent shares of total cost; median/IQR distribution across seeds.
- **Controls:** matched Pollard rho on every identical instance; theoretical projected-smoothness density as the within-experiment control for the density claim; multiplicity netted (E × E' fiber pairs counted once, never credited twice); held-out fresh instances with constructions frozen in advance; every claimed solve carries an independently verified certificate (k*P == Q re-checked by harness code).
- **Falsification criteria:** the engine is killed if (i) measured projected-smoothness density is statistically indistinguishable from zero at 16 bits (binomial test, α = 0.01, on the frozen trial count), or (ii) fully charged ratio ≥ 100x in every 16-bit cell (worse than the worst closed correspondence family merits revisiting), or (iii) rank saturation prevents a single completed descent across all cells. Any verified cell with ratio < 1 falsifies the predicted negative and triggers mandatory independent review-xhigh review before any promotion.
- **Negative-outcome value:** a negative closes P1546 and completes the correspondence-transfer closure (bielliptic, Plücker, Prym, Kummer, Hesse, and now split-Jacobian), giving the Coordinator evidence of strength ≥ replicated to promote a KN-FIND barrier entry and to shift RELATIONS budget to representation-change (KN-OPEN-003) and counting-law (G30-014) work; a positive reopens exactly one correspondence engine with a measured cost profile.
- **Budget:** wall_clock 24h, memory 16 GiB, max_runs 80
- **Stopping rule:** stop when every frozen cell has a paired engine + rho result or an explicit censored/invalid status, OR at 24h wall clock, OR when the 12- and 16-bit ladders both show ratio ≥ 50x with zero-density-compatible yields (early closure); infrastructure failures are failed_infrastructure, never evidence.
- **Harness dependency:** consumes (1) the end-to-end cost-model module (harness gap #4) owned and commissioned by G30-001 — this goal does NOT commission it; commissions (2) a reusable genus-2 divisor-arithmetic / Abel–Jacobi projection module (Sage-backed; also commissions harness gap #5, Sage version capture in manifests), (3) the generalized EXP runner (harness gap #3) since this experiment family does not fit hard-coded run.py.
- **Claim tier:** toy
- **Dependencies:** HARD dependency on G30-001 (sole owner of the end-to-end GOE cost model, harness gap #4); benefits from G30-014's counting law for its density control but does not block on it; no other-cluster goal-ID dependencies.
- **Revision note (2026-07-23 review):** Removed cost-model commissioning wording and repointed the cost-model dependency from G30-013 to G30-001, the sole GOE cost-model owner.
