## G30-019: ISOWALK-C1 — non-backtracking isogeny-walk collision exponent vs birthday
- **Cluster:** 07 TRANSFER
- **Type:** measurement
- **Long-term chain:** Isogeny-walk collision search is the only open expander-based route to beating the generic birthday/rho boundary (KN-OPEN-005); a measured sub-birthday exponent would gate a "usable-collision" follow-up (collision → chain of isogenies → descent), which gates any non-generic end-to-end attempt. A birthday-confirmed result closes the expander-walk lane permanently and protects the program from constant-factor wins being misread as exponent wins.
- **Mechanism:** Build toy ordinary ℓ-isogeny graphs (ℓ ∈ {2,3}) over prime fields by enumerating j-invariants per trace class (toycurve-style point counting) and adding edges by factoring the ℓ-th modular polynomial Φ_ℓ(j, X) over F_p — all feasible in pure Python/sympy at these sizes, no Sage required. From fixed seeded start vertices, run non-backtracking walks (no immediate edge reversal) and record steps-to-first-collision T(N) across graph sizes N spanning ≥ 4 doublings. Fit the exponent α in T ≈ c·N^α by least squares on log–log across sizes, with per-size distribution over ≥ 64 seeded walks (distributions, not best runs). Compare against (i) a backtracking walk on the same graphs and (ii) uniform random-pair sampling (true birthday reference, α = 1/2 by construction). Collisions are counted once per unordered vertex pair — no multiplicity crediting across seeds or start points.
- **Quantified prediction:** α = 0.50 ± 0.03 for the non-backtracking walk (spectral gap improves only the constant, c_NB/c_BT ∈ [1, 2]) — i.e. the expected outcome is constant-only improvement, confirming the birthday boundary at toy scale.
- **Test boundary / scope:** Primes of 12/14/16/18 bits selected to admit ordinary isogeny classes of size ≥ 50 (prospectively frozen prime list, 3 primes per bit size); ℓ ∈ {2,3}; expected graph sizes N ∈ [~10^2, ~10^4]; walk budget capped at 8√N steps per walk (censored walks recorded as censored, never as failures or as collisions); seeds 1–64 per cell. Claim tier toy: no instance exceeds 18 bits.
- **Primary metric:** fitted collision exponent α (non-backtracking walk, all sizes pooled, with 95% CI) / **Secondary metrics:** constant ratio c_NB/c_BT; censored-walk fraction; per-size T distribution (median/IQR); spectral proxy (second eigenvalue bound from edge expansion on the two largest graphs); backtracking-walk α as internal consistency check.
- **Controls:** Uniform random-pair sampling on the identical vertex sets as the α = 1/2 reference; backtracking walk as the matched no-spectral-gap baseline; the same prime/class sets used for both walk types; instrument-validation control: planted-collision sanity run on a small complete graph where T is known exactly. Held-out rule: the two largest bit sizes are fitted only after the α fit on smaller sizes is frozen, guarding against post-hoc exponent tuning.
- **Falsification criteria:** The sub-birthday hope is falsified if the lower 95% CI bound on α ≥ 0.48. The constant-only claim is falsified — and the lane reopens — if α ≤ 0.45 holds across at least two size doublings with censored fraction < 20%. The run is invalid (not negative) if the planted-collision control or the uniform-sampling reference deviates from theory by > 10%.
- **Negative-outcome value:** On birthday confirmation (predicted), the Coordinator closes ISOWALK-C1 and the expander-walk speedup lane at tested scope, records the measured (α, c) law as a KN-FIND entry, supplies dedup evidence against future expander-walk re-proposals (cf. GROWTH-SL2-C1 adjacency), and redirects TRANSFER budget to G30-020/G30-021 and PO-transfer-007. On unexpected sub-birthday, commission a replication cell plus a usable-collision definition review before any attack framing — that is a critical finding requiring independent review-xhigh review.
- **Budget:** wall_clock 8h, memory 4 GiB, max_runs 60
- **Stopping rule:** Stop at the first of: (a) all 4 bit sizes × 3 primes × 2 walk types × 64 seeds completed (or cleanly censored under the 8√N cap); (b) α ≤ 0.45 verified on the two smallest sizes (early reopen trigger — halt, preserve artifacts, report for replication); (c) instrument-validation control fails (halt as invalid, fix instrument, re-run under new RUN ids); (d) budget exhausted — report censored cells explicitly.
- **Harness dependency:** NEW capability (to be commissioned): a pure-Python toy ℓ-isogeny graph builder (trace-class enumeration via harness/toycurve.py counting + hard-coded Φ_2, Φ_3 modular-polynomial evaluation), plus an instrument-locating step for the ledger-referenced `phase20_isogeny_walk*.sage.py` (if found in an external archive, copy into this repo and register under runner.py; absence is recorded). Generalized EXP runner (gap 3 / G30-025) if available; otherwise a bounded one-off driver under runner.py immutability. No Sage required for the fallback path.

## G30-020: A2 boundary theorem — exact characterization of toy no-transfer and the minimal structure permitting cross-curve advice
- **Cluster:** 07 TRANSFER
- **Type:** barrier
- **Long-term chain:** Isogeny-class-amortized preprocessing is the only known route to sub-rho *multi-target* economics: advice built once per class, reused across curves. Certifying the exact no-transfer boundary either yields a minimal enabling structure (gating a preprocessing-advice attack cell, which gates multi-target end-to-end claims) or a scoped theorem that closes the A2 lane and stops recurring re-proposals of class-amortized advice.
- **Mechanism:** Two phases on toy isogeny classes (trace-grouped, prime-order, ordinary — extending EXP-ISADV-001's find_isogeny_class machinery to larger fields and deeper windows). Phase 1 (measurement): replicate EXP-ISADV-001 at 10/12/14/16-bit primes with window W ∈ {8, 32, 128} and ≥ 6 class members, adding the honest transported-advice condition: advice planted on E0 is pushed through explicit low-degree isogenies φ (kernels enumerated at toy size) and re-keyed on x(φ(P)); hit rate is compared to the baseline |advice|/p with transport cost fully charged. Phase 2 (barrier census): enumerate a parameterized family of DLP-free keying structures — coordinate functions, Frobenius-trace keys, isogeny-class-invariant predicates, low-degree rational functions of x (degrees ≤ 8), and transported-window keys — and test each for cross-curve alignment above baseline net of keying cost. A labeled scalar-encoding "cheating" advice (keyed on k itself) is included as the positive control proving the instrument CAN detect transfer when it exists; it is never admitted as a claim.
- **Quantified prediction:** Every DLP-free keying structure in the census has |hit rate − baseline| ≤ 3/√samples (statistical noise) at all sizes; the positive control separates from baseline by ≥ 10× noise. Direction: no-transfer is confirmed, and the census supports the boundary statement "every successful alignment map at toy scale factors through the scalar lattice" (i.e., is DLP-equivalent at comparable size).
- **Test boundary / scope:** Ordinary prime-order classes at 10/12/14/16 bits (prospectively frozen prime list, ≥ 2 primes per size), class size ≥ 4 members, samples ≥ 2000 per condition (vs EXP-ISADV-001's 372, shrinking noise below the observed |Δ| ≈ 0.1 ambiguities), seeds 1–8 per cell. ℓ-isogenies for transport: ℓ ∈ {2, 3, 5} only. No supersingular, twist, or invalid-curve instances.
- **Primary metric:** max over DLP-free candidate structures of (charged transported-advice hit rate − baseline |advice|/p), per size / **Secondary metrics:** positive-control separation; per-structure hit-rate distribution across seeds; transport cost in group-op equivalents per structure; sensitivity of the result to window W and class size.
- **Controls:** Matched baseline = uniform-x negative control and relabeled out-of-window control on the same curve (EXP-ISADV-001 conventions, retained); scalar-encoding advice as labeled positive control; fresh-window rule: evaluation targets drawn from windows disjoint from any window used to select or tune keying structures; isogeny-class-invariant predicates (order/trace/CM) included as known-null controls per closed failure reason 6 — they must show zero transfer, else the instrument is invalid.
- **Falsification criteria:** The no-transfer boundary is falsified — and A2 reopens — if any DLP-free structure shows charged hit rate ≥ baseline + 5/√samples on ≥ 2 sizes with fresh-window controls passing. The census is inconclusive (not negative) if the positive control fails to separate or if a known-null invariant predicate shows transfer, indicating instrument failure.
- **Negative-outcome value:** On confirmation (predicted), the Coordinator records a scoped barrier: cross-curve advice at toy scale requires scalar-lattice information (DLP-equivalent), closing the A2 lane and the class-amortized preprocessing direction; the census + noise-law is promoted to a KN-FIND entry that serves as dedup evidence against future advice-transfer proposals. On falsification, the identified minimal structure gates a charged multi-target preprocessing-advice attack cell — a critical finding requiring independent review-xhigh review before any promotion.

- **Stopping rule:** Stop at the first of: (a) full matrix (4 sizes × W ∈ {8,32,128} × 8 seeds × all census structures) completed; (b) any DLP-free structure meets the reopen criterion on 2 sizes (halt, preserve artifacts, trigger replication); (c) positive or known-null control fails (halt as invalid, repair instrument, new RUN ids); (d) budget exhausted.
