## G30-007: Chebotarev census of the m-th Semaev summation cover at toy scale
- **Cluster:** 03 MONODROMY
- **Type:** measurement
- **Long-term chain:** Round-2 A1 bets that exceptional curves with sub-wreath summation-cover monodromy might admit structured (non-quasirandom) relation behavior; this census is the gate: it measures whether exceptional curves exist at toy scale and how often, which gates whether A1-style sieving (and any C2 block-structure hope) has any target population at all before anyone spends attack budget on it.
- **Mechanism:** For each toy curve E/F_p (short Weierstrass, prime-order subgroup, from harness/toycurve.py) and m in {3, 4}, specialize the summation polynomial S_m(x_1,...,x_{m-1}, a) at a large uniform sample of target x-coordinates a in the prime field, factor the resulting univariate polynomials over F_p, and record the factorization-degree pattern (the Frobenius cycle type of that fiber). Accumulate per-curve histograms and compare against the Chebotarev prediction for the full wreath group (for m=3: C2 wr S3 of order 48 acting on the 8 roots of the m-th summation cover; null model computed exactly by enumerating group cycle types). Curves whose histogram deviates beyond the quasirandom fluctuation envelope are flagged exceptional; a held-out confirmatory sample re-measures flagged curves on fresh specializations.
- **Quantified prediction:** Under the generic-expectation null, >= 90% of toy curves show total-variation distance < 0.05 between empirical and full-wreath histograms at 2×10^4 specializations per curve; exceptional-curve rate < 5%. A1-relevant positive: a reproducible exceptional subpopulation with TV > 0.15 on confirmatory resample.
- **Test boundary / scope:** Toy curves at 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 bits; 40 curves per bit size (seeded, seeds 100–139); m=3 primary, m=4 at 8–12 bits only; 2×10^4 specializations per (curve, m); confirmatory resample of all flagged curves at an independent 2×10^4 specializations.
- **Primary metric:** total-variation distance between empirical cycle-type histogram and exact full-wreath Chebotarev prediction / **Secondary metrics:** per-cycle-type max absolute deviation, exceptional-curve rate, confirmatory-replication rate of flags, per-specialization factorization cost.
- **Controls:** (i) exact group-enumerated wreath null (not an asymptotic approximation); (ii) a synthetic positive control constructed by thinning the specialization stream to a curve with forced rational subgroup structure, verifying the census detects a known deviation (detector sanity); (iii) held-out confirmatory resample for every flag — a flag that does not replicate is recorded as noise, not as an exceptional curve; (iv) uniform specialization sampling throughout, so the uniform-target selector barrier does not apply and no selector is credited.
- **Falsification criteria:** (a) the detector fails its positive control (instrument invalid — fix before any interpretation); (b) zero curves replicate TV > 0.15 across the full 200-curve sample at any bit size, which kills the A1 "sieve exceptional curves" premise at toy scale; (c) observed deviations shrink with sample size consistent with pure sampling noise (converging to the wreath prediction).
- **Negative-outcome value:** A null census closes the "find exceptional curves then exploit them" branch of A1 at toy scale and redirects the cluster's remaining budget to G30-009 (barrier) instead of attack-probe work; a positive census licenses exactly one follow-on attack goal (structured relation behavior on certified exceptional curves) and tells it the target density.
- **Budget:** wall_clock 8h, memory 4 GiB, max_runs 30
- **Stopping rule:** Stop after all 200 curves × census + confirmatory resamples complete, or at wall_clock exhaustion with >= 60% of cells complete (report censored cells); stop early only if the positive control fails (instrument repair, one re-run allowed within max_runs).
- **Harness dependency:** NEW module harness/monodromy.py (univariate S_m specialization + sympy factorization census; S_3 explicit from semaev.py, S_4 via the existing resultant construction); benefits from gap 3 (generalized EXP runner) for cell registration. No Sage required.
- **Claim tier:** toy
- **Dependencies:** none (feeds G30-008 and G30-009)

## G30-008: Imprimitive-monodromy resolvent test of the summation cover
- **Cluster:** 03 MONODROMY
- **Type:** attack-probe
- **Long-term chain:** C2 bets that the summation cover may admit imprimitive block structure beyond the defining wreath, which would let relation finding decompose into block-level then within-block stages; this resolvent test is the gate: it certifies whether any extra block system exists at toy scale and whether two-stage search actually cuts cost, which gates all block-structured relation finding before further C2 budget is spent; G30-009 is the barrier sibling whose proof (or replicated counterexample) decides whether any monodromy-structure route, including this one, can beat generic bounds at all.
- **Mechanism:** C2 conjectures the m-th summation cover's monodromy may be imprimitive beyond the defining wreath structure, i.e. admit a nontrivial block system that would let relation finding decompose into block-level then within-block stages. Test: compute block-system resolvents for the S_m cover at toy scale — for m=3, the degree-8 cover of the x-line admits resolvents for each candidate imprimitivity chain; factor the resolvent over F_p (and over the function field where feasible via coefficient specializations) and detect rational factors certifying a block system. For every curve with a certified extra block structure, run a pilot two-stage relation search (block-resolved membership first, within-block refinement second) with fully charged cost, against matched rho on the same instances.
- **Quantified prediction:** Null (wreath-maximal): fraction of random toy curves with a certified extra block system < 1% (expected 0 in the 200-curve sample). C2-positive branch: if extra imprimitivity exists, two-stage relation search on those curves shows >= 20% relation-collection cost reduction vs single-stage at fixed success rate — still far above rho end-to-end (predicted >= 10x rho), so the probe tests mechanism existence, not a rho break.
- **Test boundary / scope:** Same 200-curve ladder as G30-007 (8–16 bits, seeds 100–139) to allow joint analysis; m=3 primary; m=4 restricted to 8–10 bits; pilot two-stage search only on certified-positive curves, 10 instances per positive curve, seeds fixed per instance.
- **Primary metric:** number of curves with an independently verifiable extra block-system certificate / **Secondary metrics:** resolvent degree and factorization pattern, two-stage vs single-stage relation-collection cost ratio (positive curves only), end-to-end charged cost vs matched rho (group-op equivalents), certificate verification pass rate.
- **Controls:** (i) trivial-resolvent control: the defining wreath block system (the known C2 wr S3 imprimitivity) must be detected by the resolvent machinery on every curve — failure = invalid instrument; (ii) matched rho baseline (harness/rho.py, same seeds, same subgroup) on every pilot instance, full preprocessing + factorization + search cost charged, no multiplicity crediting (each relation trial counted once); (iii) any G30-007 exceptional curves analyzed as a separate stratum so a generic-curve conclusion is not contaminated.
- **Falsification criteria:** (a) resolvent machinery fails to recover the defining wreath block system (instrument invalid); (b) zero extra block systems certified across all 200 curves — C2's decomposition premise closed at toy scale for m=3,4; (c) certified block systems exist but two-stage search shows no cost reduction beyond resolvent-overhead noise (|ratio - 1| < 0.05), killing the exploitability half of C2.
- **Negative-outcome value:** Outcome (b) closes C2 at toy scale and removes "block-structured relation finding" from the round-2 frontier, feeding G30-009's barrier statement as corroborating evidence; outcome (c) keeps C2 theoretically open but de-prioritizes it below any direction with a measurable effect; a positive licenses exactly one scoped follow-on (scaling the two-stage search across bit sizes with the gap-4 cost model).
- **Budget:** wall_clock 10h, memory 8 GiB, max_runs 25
- **Stopping rule:** Stop after resolvent computation on all 200 curves (m=3) plus m=4 subset, plus pilots on all certified positives; hard stop at wall_clock exhaustion; at most one instrument repair + re-run if the trivial-resolvent control fails.
- **Harness dependency:** NEW module harness/resolvent.py (block-system resolvent construction + factorization; sympy-based at m=3, may need the gap-5 Sage environment capture if m=4 resultants exceed sympy capacity); gap 4 (end-to-end cost model to rho-comparable group-op equivalents) required before any cost-ratio claim is recorded.
- **Claim tier:** toy
- **Dependencies:** G30-001 (hard dep: no "× rho" cost-ratio number may be recorded before the GOE cost ruler exists); stratified analysis uses G30-007 outputs if available; cost claims additionally require the gap-4 cost model
- **Revision note (2026-07-23 review):** Restored the missing Long-term chain template field and added G30-001 as a hard dependency so no rho cost-ratio figure can be recorded before the GOE cost ruler exists.

## G30-009: Wreath-monodromy barrier theorem — prove or refute quasirandom relation rates (D2)
- **Cluster:** 03 MONODROMY
- **Type:** barrier
- **Long-term chain:** D2 is the program's cheapest possible breakthrough substitute: if full wreath monodromy provably forces quasirandom relation rates, then no monodromy-based structure (A1 sieving, C2 decomposition) can beat generic bounds, permanently closing a large hypothesis family and protecting the program from false positives; if the proof fails at a specific gap, that gap is the sharpest well-localized attack surface the program has, gating a targeted successor goal.
- **Mechanism:** Two-track. Track 1 (theory): attempt a rigorous proof that for a prime-field curve whose m-th summation cover has full wreath monodromy, Chebotarev equidistribution forces the relation-hit process (smooth factorizations of specialized summation fibers into the factor base) to be quasirandom up to o(1) bias — i.e. relation probabilities match the uniform-residue prediction, with explicit error terms at toy field sizes. Track 2 (empirical check): on the curves G30-007 certifies as full-wreath, measure actual relation-hit rates against the quasirandom prediction over the toy ladder and test whether deviations fall inside the theorem's predicted error envelope.
- **Quantified prediction:** Theorem provable within budget at the "effective Chebotarev + second-moment" level, with error envelope O(p^{-1/2}) relative bias; empirical relation-hit TV distance from quasirandom < 0.05 at 12–16 bits and decreasing with bit size. Refutation branch: exhibit one full-wreath-certified curve with relation-hit TV > 0.15 replicating on held-out seeds, or an irreducible proof gap with a constructed mechanism exploiting it.
- **Test boundary / scope:** Empirical arm restricted to full-wreath-certified curves from G30-007 at 8–16 bits (expected >= 180 of 200); relation-hit census of 10^4–10^5 specialized fibers per curve, factor base sized as in EXP-SEMAEV-001 convention (14 elements at 8–12 bits, scaled by p^{1/4} rule at 14–16 bits); seeds disjoint from G30-007 census seeds. Theory arm scoped to prime-field short-Weierstrass, m=3 (m=4 stated as conditional extension).
- **Primary metric:** proof status (proved | proved-with-gap | refuted-by-counterexample | inconclusive) against a predefined theorem statement frozen before execution / **Secondary metrics:** empirical relation-hit TV vs bit size (slope), max single-curve deviation, fraction of certified curves inside the predicted error envelope, error-term tightness (predicted vs observed envelope ratio).
- **Controls:** (i) theorem statement and error-envelope formula frozen in the experiment record before any empirical data is examined (no post-hoc envelope fitting); (ii) empirical arm uses only independently certified full-wreath curves, relation trials counted once each, trivial-ideal control distinguishes "no relation" from cheap solve per harness rules; (iii) held-out seed set for every flagged deviation — a deviation that does not replicate is noise; (iv) no cost claim is made or needed: this goal compares distributions, not attack cost, so rho baseline is not applicable (quasirandom uniform-residue model is the baseline).
- **Falsification criteria:** For the barrier: one replicated full-wreath counterexample (relation-hit TV > 0.15 on held-out seeds) refutes the theorem as stated and converts the goal into an attack-direction result. For the refutation branch: if all observed deviations are within the frozen envelope across >= 95% of certified curves and the proof closes without an unhandled case, the refutation branch is dead and A1/C2 are closed for prime-field summation covers at the tested generality.
- **Negative-outcome value:** "Proved" is itself the high-value negative: it closes A1 and C2 (as D2 is designed to do), removes the entire monodromy-structure family from the active frontier, and redirects cluster budget to directions D2 does not cover (e.g. B1 code-enumerator or D1/D3 barrier work). "Proved-with-gap" localizes the only surviving monodromy attack surface and spawns exactly one successor goal aimed at that gap. "Inconclusive" after budget exhaustion defers the family rather than silently leaving it open.
- **Budget:** wall_clock 12h, memory 4 GiB, max_runs 15
- **Stopping rule:** Stop at the first of: (a) complete proof with independent review-ready write-up; (b) replicated counterexample certified; (c) empirical arm complete on all certified curves with the proof arm's gap explicitly enumerated; (d) wall_clock exhaustion — record as inconclusive with censored cells, never as evidence for or against the math.
- **Harness dependency:** Existing toycurve.py + rho.py (instance generation) and G30-007's census module for certification input; NEW module harness/relation_rate.py (fiber factorization + factor-base smoothness census) — reuses gap-2-style factor-base ideal machinery only for labeling, not for Gröbner solving; gap 3 (generalized runner) for cell registration.
- **Claim tier:** toy (theory arm: proof applies at stated generality, but empirical corroboration is toy-tier only)
- **Dependencies:** G30-007 (supplies full-wreath-certified curve set and census machinery); G30-008 result, if available, sharpens the theorem's scope statement
