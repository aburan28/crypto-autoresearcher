## Falsifiable hypothesis

For the five-source prime-field elliptic relation fiber at a fixed target `R`, there is a target-local nonlinear apolar functional whose truncated Hankel operator has a flat extension of sub-rho construction size and whose multiplication algebra, joint primary decomposition, and nilpotent local algebras recover every accepted signed factor-base source tuple with exact multiplicity and scheme structure. The operation must be constructed directly from compact public elliptic equations, not from enumerated sources, a scalar-linear source atomizer, or either explicit or implicit reconstruction of the P1513 common norms.

## Mechanism-new operation

The new operation is **target-local nonlinear apolar flat extension with a source-biconditional multiplication algebra**. Given only `(E,F,R)` and the compact recursive-`S3` equations, it would construct a nonlinear truncated dual functional `Lambda_R`, prove flatness of its moment/Hankel operator, and decompose its multiplication algebra—by joint eigendecomposition on reduced fibers and joint primary/nilpotent decomposition on nonreduced fibers—to exact signed members of `F` with multiplicity.

This is not the scalar-linear Ulrich/Chow kernel atomizer closed by P1512 and IDEA-115. It is also not P1513's `common_norm(T,F,H)`, specialize-then-multiply, explicit norm expansion, dense fiber-product gcd, fixed-point truncated resultant, algebraic relation-matrix modular composition, or a Kedlaya-Umans implementation of those routes. The removal theorem must prove that the apolar functional is assembled target-locally by an operation independent of, and not reducible to, either common norm, while preserving sources and multiplicities in `N^(1/2-epsilon)` time and memory for some fixed `epsilon>0`.

The immutable P1514 producer receipt and its concurrent V2 correction close supplied-moment flat extension and supplied-sequence border bases as constructors, and show that the frozen direct source-moment enumerators miss the complete gate. The independent append-only audit further corrects their scope: reusable and streamed meet-in-the-middle decks have different campaign-time/memory tradeoffs, and the cited theorem gives `k=5B-3` as a sufficient cutoff, not a compulsory minimum. Only the frozen theorem-guaranteed dense Macaulay instantiation at that cutoff, with `binomial(5B+2,5)=Theta(B^5)` coordinates, is closed. Adaptive early stabilization, a smaller fiber-specific cutoff, sparse or multihomogeneous constructions, and an arbitrary structured target-local moment oracle remain open. On nonreduced fibers, exact multiplicity requires the joint primary decomposition and nilpotent local algebra, not joint eigenvalues alone; a one-functional interface is valid only for a faithful Gorenstein fiber or after a fully charged multi-functional replacement across every exceptional stratum.

## Assumptions

1. `E/F_p` has a public prime-order subgroup `<P>` of order `N=p^(1+o(1))`, the target is `Q=[x]P`, and the public factor base `F` has size `B=N^beta` with fixed five-source arity.
2. A projective recursive-`S3` presentation includes signs, infinity, repeated points, nonreduced fibers, and a scalar-blind target parameter.
3. The target-local coordinate ring either admits a faithful truncated apolar functional because the admitted fiber is Gorenstein, or uses a fully charged multi-functional replacement; its flat rank is output-sensitive and fully charged rather than assumed constant or replaced by the degree of a universal source deck.
4. The functional and every required moment are computable from the compact equations without a source table, `Theta(B^3)` specialization image, explicit degree-`Theta(B^3)` norm, or dense quotient module.
5. On reduced fibers, joint multiplication spectra and eigenspaces invert biconditionally to all signed factor-base tuples. On nonreduced fibers, a full joint primary decomposition and nilpotent local algebra, or a proved equivalent with charged local-space dimensions, recovers exact multiplicity and exceptional-fiber certificates.
6. Construction, coefficient growth, flatness tests, multiplication matrices, source output, relation verification, factor-log linear algebra, blind descent, candidates, and peak memory are charged.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Pollard rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time with constant-state memory; BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Let `B=N^beta`; one-time apolar derivation cost time/memory `N^a,N^a_m`; compact serialized recipe and working state `N^c,N^c_m`; reciprocal relation and target densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`; target-local flat-extension, multiplication, source inversion, and exact-verification time/working-memory per query `N^k,N^k_m`; relation-source and target ambiguity exponents `o,u`; and factor-log linear-algebra time/memory `N^ell,N^ell_m`. Then the complete-path exponents are

`lambda=max(a,c,beta+delta+k+o,ell,delta_t+k+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,c_m,k_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

For the P1513 normalization `N=Theta(r^5)`, every constructor, circuit-to-moment conversion, flatness witness, multiplication algebra, source splitter, and output stage must be `O(r^(5/2-epsilon))`; an `Omega(r^3)` time stage is already above rho. In the P1514 two-plus-three meet-in-the-middle controls, precomputing and indexing a reusable `B^3` deck costs `Theta(B^3)` total time and memory, while a streamed scan can retain `Theta(B^2)` working memory but costs `Theta(B^3)` time per target and `Theta(B^4)=Theta(N^0.8)` across `Theta(B)` relation queries. Direct `B^5` enumeration either precomputes `Theta(B^5)` time/state once or rescans to `Theta(B^6)` campaign time. For the separate frozen dense Macaulay instantiation at the theorem's sufficient cutoff `k=5B-3`, both the indexed coordinate payload and its direct materialization are `Theta(B^5)=Theta(N)`, before linear algebra; this is not a lower bound for a smaller adaptive, sparse, or multihomogeneous route. Constant density, `Theta(B)` query count, constant fiber rank, and full-output-rank normalizations used in these controls are favorable `heuristic` and `model-bound` assumptions, not theorems. Both formulas charge failed targets, `B+sigma` rows, coefficient bit lengths, primary decomposition, nilpotent local blocks, source output, and verification. Toy timings do not establish asymptotic exponents.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Flat extension reveals a finite algebra only after enough moments are available. The P1514 evidence proves that the standard supplied-moment and supplied-sequence decoders do not construct those moments. Its direct controls fail either at reusable `B^3` time/state, streamed `B^4` campaign time, precomputed `B^5` time/state, or rescanned `B^6` campaign time. The independent audit narrows the Macaulay conclusion to the theorem-guaranteed instantiation at the sufficient cutoff `k=5B-3`, which exposes `Theta(B^5)` coordinates; the cited theorem does not make that cutoff a universal minimum. Rank can also equal scheme length, non-Gorenstein fibers need more than one functional, and nonreduced multiplicity needs primary and nilpotent local data. P1512 closes target-independent scalar-linear atoms; the P1513 screens close their standard norm/common-factor routes. No present theorem supplies a distinct structured target-local moment constructor, but adaptive early stabilization, smaller fiber-specific cutoffs, and sparse or multihomogeneous routes remain unclosed.

## Proof track

Give an algebraic identity producing `Lambda_R` directly from the compact recursive-`S3` circuit; prove flatness and a reduced joint-spectrum/source biconditional; prove a fully charged joint primary decomposition, nilpotent local algebra, and exact length/source biconditional on every nonreduced, repeated, signed, and infinity stratum; prove the construction neither computes nor requires either P1513 norm or their common factor; and derive a fixed `epsilon>0` with `lambda,mu<=1/2-epsilon` through factor-log calibration and blind descent. An implementation is inadmissible until this theorem is independently checked.

## Disproof track

Reduce the proposed functional construction to any P1512 scalar-linear atomizer or P1513 screened route; prove that the needed moment set, flat rank, coefficient payload, multiplication matrices, or complete source output is `Omega(r^(5/2))`; exhibit a scalar-blind pair of fibers with identical truncated dual data but different sources; or show `lambda>=1/2` or `mu>=1/2` under charged construction and output.

## Positive and negative controls

- Planted zero-dimensional Gorenstein schemes with known reduced and nonreduced points, where moments are supplied independently and flat extension must recover every source.
- The same planted schemes with one missing moment, a colliding multiplication spectrum, or permuted source labels to force a completeness failure.
- Exhaustive toy elliptic five-source fibers, including repeated points, signs, infinity, and nilpotents, compared against direct enumeration.
- P1512 scalar-linear Chow matrices and each P1513 screened common-norm route as explicit negative controls, with identical coefficient, output, and memory accounting.
- Random dense zero-dimensional systems matched for degree and Hilbert function to detect an uncharged elliptic-specific shortcut.
- Blind known-log and unknown-log targets, matched rho and BSGS baselines, and an independent source/scalar verifier.
