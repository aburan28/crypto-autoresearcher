## Likely fatal obstruction

Transposed power projection accelerates a linear functional only after the quotient algebra and functional are supplied.  The type-II collision norm has the full period/resultant payload; differentiating it does not localize a source for free.  Every audited bisection either expands D period terms, builds a dense resultant, performs orbit BSGS, or makes rho-scale membership queries.
