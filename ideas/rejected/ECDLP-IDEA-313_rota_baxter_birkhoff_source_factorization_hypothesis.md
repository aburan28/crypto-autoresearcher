## Falsifiable hypothesis

Elliptic factor decompositions can be represented as characters of a connected filtered Hopf algebra valued in a Rota–Baxter algebra, so that algebraic Birkhoff or Atkinson factorization canonically separates an endpoint character into primitive source factors and returns exact signed factor points for reusable relations and blind target descent below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **compile endpoint relation forests into a Hopf-algebra character, choose a Rota–Baxter projection, and apply recursive Birkhoff/Atkinson factorization so primitive counterterm pieces are lifted to exact factor points**. The new mathematical operation is the Rota–Baxter split plus convolution factorization, not a solver substitution or dense resultant. For a fixed admissible projection under the standard completeness and filtration hypotheses, algebraic Birkhoff factorization is unique relative to the supplied character; that uniqueness does not reveal the leaves that generated it. A forest-faithful character or primitive basis embeds the source circuit, so the proposal merges with IDEAs 108, 176, 183, 185, and 191.

## Assumptions

1. A compact connected filtered Hopf algebra represents every signed factor decomposition without one generator, tree, or word per source tuple.
2. A public target-uniform Rota–Baxter operator canonically separates exact elliptic factor sources rather than a chosen regular/singular grading.
3. Birkhoff factors and primitive terms lift biconditionally to exact signed factor points on all collision, multiplicity, and sign strata.
4. Hopf basis construction, coproducts, convolution recursion, projection, primitive output, relation rank, factor logs, descent, verification, time, and peak memory are charged.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

With setup `N^a,N^a_m`, factor base `N^beta`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, one Rota–Baxter/Birkhoff factorization/inverse `N^q,N^q_m`, rank gain `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor-log completion `N^ell,N^ell_m`,

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

`q` includes Hopf-character compilation, coproduct and convolution recursion, projection, exact primitive lifting, and verification; `o` includes every primitive, word, forest, and ambiguous point tuple. Rho has expected time exponent `1/2` and negligible memory; BSGS has time and memory exponents `1/2`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

For a fixed admissible Rota–Baxter projection and the standard completeness/filtration hypotheses, algebraic Birkhoff factorization uniquely decomposes an already supplied character relative to that data; it separates regular and singular components, not hidden combinatorial leaves. Constructing the character from a source forest, or giving a primitive basis that names factor points, supplies the missing source circuit. Changing the supplied projection or Hopf representation can change the primitive presentation and proposed point lift, while no canonical endpoint-derived choice is supplied. Thus the operation reorganizes source data and does not create a point-faithful inverse.

## Proof track

Construct a compact target-uniform Hopf compiler and canonical Rota–Baxter operator, prove an all-strata biconditional from Birkhoff primitives to exact signed factors, and prove independent relation rank, factor logs, blind descent, plus complete `lambda,mu<=0.45` including basis and output growth.

## Disproof track

Exhibit distinct source forests or primitive words with the same accessible character and Birkhoff factors, show that changing the admissible projection changes the point lift without changing the endpoint, or prove any source-faithful Hopf basis/character/output has exponent `0.50` or worse.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a supplied small connected filtered Hopf algebra and labelled character must reproduce the known recursive Birkhoff factors.
- Negative: characters under different supplied admissible projections or representations of the same endpoint must not be interpreted as a canonical exact point source.
- Baselines: explicit forest enumeration, a dense resultant, IDEAs 108/176/183/185/191, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with an independent all-strata source biconditional, 1,000 verified relation rows and 100 blind descents per large size, and both complete exponents at most `0.45`.
- Falsify if the source forest/word is supplied to the character, primitive lifts depend on an external projection or representation choice, any source stratum fails, or basis/state/output cost reaches exponent `0.50`.
- Exponents in `(0.45,0.50)` are inconclusive and non-promoting.
