The screened operation is **derive absorbing subalgebras of the induced relation template, recurse through cyclic-term quotients, and lift terminal blocks to exact signed point sources**.  Absorption is a different universal-algebra decomposition from WNU bounded width, but it starts from the same explicit finite template and polymorphism operations as IDEA-122/146/158. parameter change, same-field isogeny variant, explicit large-prime/source table, post-hoc selector,
dense resultant, or relation-only certificate receives no mechanism credit.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Absorption theory assumes the finite relation template and its polymorphisms.  Explicit induced-template access is B^5; an implicit support query is the original source problem.  The prime-order elliptic group is affine and has no useful proper absorbing subuniverse, while a sparse factor base is not closed under the required operations.  Any artificial absorbing source domain therefore imports the missing source language or sign lift.

## Proof track

Give an implicit endpoint-uniform template oracle, a proper absorption chain outside the faithful-addition no-go, an exact all-sign lift, and complete exponents at most 0.45.

## Disproof track

Derive faithful addition, show every absorption chain is trivial/affine, reduce template access to source membership, find one lost sign stratum, or derive either complete exponent at least 0.50.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive control: manufactured finite Taylor templates with known absorbing chains and independently enumerated source lifts.
- Negative controls: faithful addition templates, explicit B^5 tables, IDEA-122, IDEA-146, IDEA-158, IDEA-167, P1480, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

Reopening requires implicit template access of exponent at most 0.45, a nontrivial absorption theorem, exact all-source recall with zero false sources, full factor-log rank, blind descent, and complete lambda and mu at most 0.45.  Explicit membership tables, affine collapse, lost signs, or exponent at least 0.50 falsifies this version.
