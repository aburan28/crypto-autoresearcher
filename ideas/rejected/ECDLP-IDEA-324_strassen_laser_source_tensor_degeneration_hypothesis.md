## Likely fatal obstruction

Laser methods bound aggregate tensor contraction through degenerations that may cancel or merge individual monomials. Exact ECDLP descent needs one labelled six-source witness on every admitted stratum. Border rank alone does not certify exact finite-field zero/nonzero behavior or a source witness; Bini-style exactification can repair an approximate bilinear computation only after its interpolation degree, precision/height, and every label are charged. Those costs or tags can materialize the original support.
