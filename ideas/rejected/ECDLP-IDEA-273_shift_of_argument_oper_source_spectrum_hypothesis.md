## Falsifiable hypothesis

The finite-field source fiber can be compiled target-uniformly into a compact representation on which a shift-of-argument commutative subalgebra has simple joint spectrum indexed by source points.  Recovering the oper or joint eigenvalue associated with `Q` would return factor points or `x` below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **compile source equations into a Lie-theoretic module, diagonalize the quantum shift-of-argument/Gaudin Hamiltonians, and invert the associated oper spectrum to a source point**.  This is not merely swapping polynomial solvers: it changes the representation and seeks a canonical commuting family.  But the module, highest weight, evaluation sites, and cyclic vector needed to label every factor tuple are not generated by the endpoint.  A point-faithful module or eigenbasis has dimension/output comparable to the source deck, and a compact spectrum without source labels gives invariants rather than a canonical section.  It therefore merges with spectral/character separation and materialized-product negatives once state is charged.

## Assumptions

1. Public source equations and target determine a compact Lie algebra, module, evaluation parameters, and cyclic vector without enumerating solutions.
2. The shift-of-argument subalgebra has simple spectrum in bijection with exact source tuples for all relevant strata.
3. The corresponding oper/eigenvalues can be computed and inverted to factor points with sub-rho output and ambiguity.
4. Compilation, module dimension, Hamiltonians, eigenvectors, opers, output, factor logs, descent, verification, time, and peak memory are charged.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Pollard rho has expected time exponent `1/2`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `1/2`.  Let setup time and memory be `N^a,N^a_m`, factor-base size be `N^beta`, reciprocal relation and target success densities be `N^delta,N^delta_t`, one compile/spectrum/oper/inverse attempt cost `N^q,N^q_m`, independent-rank gain be `N^r`, spectral output be `N^o`, inverse ambiguity be `N^u`, and factor-log completion be `N^ell,N^ell_m`. `lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

Every module basis vector, weight, evaluation site, Hamiltonian entry, eigenpacket, oper coefficient, failed inverse, source branch, factor log, verifier step, and live byte is charged.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Shift-of-argument results describe spectra after a representation and its weight data are supplied.  They do not manufacture a point-faithful representation from an endpoint.  To index all ECDLP factor tuples, the compiled module or its weight/eigenbasis must distinguish the same source deck the algorithm is meant to recover; compressing it destroys injectivity or leaves high-multiplicity eigenpackets.  Even a simple spectrum is only useful if the target selects an eigenline and the oper can be inverted to factors, both missing source-section operations.

## Proof track

Construct a target-uniform compact source module with simple spectrum, prove target eigenline selection and exact oper-to-factor inversion, and certify complete exponents at most `0.45`.

## Disproof track

Exhibit spectral collisions, prove point-faithful module/state dimension at least `N^0.50`, show target eigenline selection imports source advice, show oper inversion is source solving, or derive either complete exponent at least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive control: a supplied small highest-weight module with known simple shift-of-argument spectrum and labelled eigenvectors.
- Negative controls: source-label permutation, compressed modules with repeated spectra, random cyclic vectors, direct source tables, additive character matrices, dense resultants, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

Reopening requires a target-uniform module of exponent at most `0.45`, injective spectrum, exact all-strata oper inversion, blind descent, and complete `lambda,mu<=0.45`.  Spectral collisions, source-labelled modules, module/output/state at least `N^0.50`, missing target eigenline selection, or either exponent at least `0.50` falsifies this version.
