## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **factor the serial Semaev/addition predicate into low-arity relations, prove a weighted `B`-unit elimination order, evaluate supplied factors with InsideOut, and backtrack provenance to exact sources**. This is directly owned by `ECDLP-IDEA-117`, with IDEAs 120, 135, and 141 covering the same supplied transition/join/provenance grammar. Fractional FAQ width alone is dimensionless: each source-index domain has size `B`, whereas a field-state domain has size `p=B^5`, and factor construction/access/output sizes must be normalized separately. The gain exists only after low-arity factor tables or neighbor iterators are supplied; materializing central transitions or provenance restores `B^3` traffic. IDEA-266 remains a generic elimination control, not a new owner.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

With setup `N^a,N^a_m`, factor base `N^beta` where `beta=1/5`, reciprocal relation and target densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, factor construction/access plus one weighted InsideOut evaluation excluding source emission `N^q,N^q_m`, independently verified rank amortization `N^r`, separately charged exact-source/provenance output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor-log linear algebra `N^ell,N^ell_m`,

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

Factor generation and provenance are input costs, not free database access. A fractional-width number promotes only with a receipt listing every domain and factor cardinality/access exponent in `B`-units; any field-state domain costs `p=B^5` unless represented more compactly with proof. Here `0<=r<=o`: rank credit cannot exceed independently verified emitted-row output. The fixed `beta=0.20` gate requires `beta+delta+q-r+o<=0.45`, online `delta_t+q+o+u<=0.25`, all setup/state/factor-log terms at most `0.45`, and at least `B` independent rows. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50` and memory exponent `0`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

InsideOut exploits a factorization that is already supplied. For elliptic addition, the exact transition relation or neighbor iterator is the missing source-fiber generator. If factors are materialized, central joins/provenance have `B^3` or worse traffic; if only endpoint polynomials are supplied, FAQ evaluation is an aggregate elimination backend and exact source backtracking remains unsolved.
