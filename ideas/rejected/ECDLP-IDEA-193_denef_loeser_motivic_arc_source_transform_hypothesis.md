## Falsifiable hypothesis

The endpoint-specialized elliptic source-fiber scheme admits a target-uniform arc/contact-locus transform whose motivic components specialize biconditionally to every exact signed factor-base tuple. If computable compactly over the benchmark finite fields, the transform would produce complete known-log relations and fresh masked-target descents below rho and BSGS without enumerating the source fiber.

## Mechanism-new operation

The proposed operation is **source-fiber arc-space lifting, contact-order stratification, and Denef-Loeser motivic integration/change-of-variables followed by exact component-to-source specialization**. It is mechanism-new only if motivic data computed from the endpoint separates labeled smooth tuples, not merely singularity types, classes, or counts, and if specialization returns exact factor points without a component or arc family per source. Finite-field point counting, dense elimination, source-indexed cylinders, increasing jet depth after seeing answers, and generic solving are controls.

Independent semantic preflight is negative in scope. Motivic measure deliberately identifies sets with the same geometric class and local arc behavior; generic all-distinct smooth source tuples can share that behavior. Refining cylinders until each tuple is biconditionally identified installs a component/contact condition or arc family for each source, recovering the original output deck. No checked literature supplies the missing finite-field labeled-source inverse.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Pollard rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` expected time with constant-sized state; BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Let fiber construction, lifting/resolution, and frozen jet setup have time and memory exponents `a,a_m`; let reciprocal relation and masked-target densities be `N^delta,N^delta_t`; let one motivic/contact query plus specialization cost `N^q,N^q_m`; let exact source output and target ambiguity have exponents `o,u`; and let factor-log algebra have exponents `ell,ell_m`. The complete exponents are

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

Every equation, resolution chart, jet coefficient, cylinder, contact stratum, motivic term, specialization branch, exact tuple, failed endpoint, and verification is charged.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Motivic measure collapses points and cylinders with the same geometric class, and generic all-distinct smooth tuples have indistinguishable local arc type. Those invariants can count or classify strata without labeling their points. Enforcing biconditional exact source return requires one separating component, contact condition, sufficiently deep jet, or arc family per tuple; its construction and output recreate the source fiber. Characteristic-zero resolution and specialization add another unproved, potentially dominant layer.

## Proof track

Define a uniform finite-field-compatible transform, prove that bounded arc/contact data is biconditional with every exact signed factor tuple on every smooth and singular stratum, prove no source-indexed cylinders or full resolution deck is used, and derive complete blind-descent exponents `lambda,mu<=0.45`.

## Disproof track

Exhibit source-distinct smooth tuples with identical bounded motivic/contact data, prove exact separation needs one cylinder or jet branch per tuple, show specialization loses labels or multiplicities, require nonuniform resolution/source advice, or derive `max(lambda,mu)>=0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive control: published characteristic-zero arc/contact-locus calculations for singularities with independently known components.

- Negative control: source-indexed cylinders, unbounded jet depth, dense eliminants, full point counts, rho, BSGS, known-log endpoints, and fresh blind masked targets.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

This version is a scoped negative. A distinct successor requires a public finite-field-compatible transform, 100% source/multiplicity recall and zero false tuples on preregistered toy strata, invariant output under frozen presentations, no source-indexed cylinders, and formal `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
