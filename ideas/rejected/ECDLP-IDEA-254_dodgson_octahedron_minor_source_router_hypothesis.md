minor lattice, propagate condensation identities, and reverse the unique vanishing path
to five signed source rows**.  This is more specific than merely evaluating a supplied
determinant, but it merges with the P1539 locator interface and IDEA-052/077/243 once the

## Likely fatal obstruction

Dodgson condensation propagates contiguous solid minors of one ordered matrix.  A
transversal chooses one row independently from each colour block, so its natural index
space is a five-dimensional Cartesian deck, not a two-dimensional adjacent-minor grid.
Creating a grid that preserves row identity seeds at least the pair/triple catalogues the
method was meant to avoid.  Desnanot–Jacobi also divides by a central minor exactly where
zero-minor localization is singular; perturbing that zero preserves a value identity but
not a canonical exact source path.

## Proof track

Give an explicit target-uniform embedding and division-free recurrence, prove that its
seed/state sizes are within the promotion rectangle, and prove a biconditional all-strata
reverse map to signed rows with complete exponents at most `0.45`.

## Disproof track

Prove that any source-faithful seed contains `Omega(B^2)` pair or `Omega(B^3)` triple
states, exhibit two different transversals with the same recurrence trace, or show that
zero-safe branching or either complete exponent is at least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Negative controls: randomly permuted colour rows, planted zero central minors, source-label erasure, IDEA-052, IDEA-077, IDEA-243, P1539, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

state, any division-by-zero advice, or an established exponent at least `0.50` falsifies
