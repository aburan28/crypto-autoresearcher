## Falsifiable hypothesis

Associative elliptic addition admits a target-uniform Cambrian congruence whose unique sortable or Tamari-minimal representative is computable from an endpoint alone and whose labeled leaves are exactly every signed factor-base source tuple. The canonical source tree would supply complete known-log relations and fresh masked-target descent below rho and BSGS without enumerating the endpoint fiber.

## Mechanism-new operation

The proposed operation is **Cambrian/Tamari congruence reduction of elliptic addition trees followed by canonical sortable-tree leaf extraction**. It would be mechanism-new only if the public endpoint determines a compact congruence class and the canonical representative restores exact factor identities, not merely a parenthesization of an already supplied word. Supplying the leaves first, enumerating all bracketed factor words, choosing a post-hoc sortable representative, or changing only the tree balance is a control.

The current form is merged/rejected. Associativity identifies different parenthesizations of the same supplied leaves but does not invert an endpoint into those leaves. Tamari and Cambrian normal forms canonicalize word/tree representatives inside a known congruence class. Adding enough labels for the normal form to recover point identities reinstates the source deck; endpoint-only exact composable labels also meet the constant-or-injective boundary recorded by the current bucket-congruence theorem.

3. For known-log endpoints `R_j=[r_j]P`, compute each unique sortable or Tamari-minimal representative without enumerating its source words.
4. Decode every canonical tree to exact signed factor points; verify all sums and preserve repeats, cancellations, infinity, congruence collisions, misses, multiplicities, and output.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Pollard rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` expected time with constant-sized state; BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Let endpoint-congruence setup have time and memory exponents `a,a_m`; let reciprocal relation and target densities be `N^delta,N^delta_t`; let one canonicalization and leaf query cost `N^q,N^q_m`; let source output and target ambiguity have exponents `o,u`; and let factor-log algebra have exponents `ell,ell_m`. The complete exponents are

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

Every bracketed word, rotation, congruence branch, point label, decoded leaf, miss, and verification is charged; a materialized source-tree family contributes to `a`, `q`, or `o`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Tamari rotation and Cambrian sorting change or select the parenthesization/order class of an already specified word; they do not turn an elliptic sum endpoint into the word's factor-point identities. Endpoint-only labels compatible with exact global addition are too coarse to separate sources or injectively retain the whole deck. Thus an unlabeled normal form loses leaves, while a labeled one presupposes or enumerates the source fiber.

## Proof track

Define an endpoint-derived nonhomomorphic congruence with a compact canonical representative, prove a biconditional between its leaves and all exact signed source tuples on every stratum, prove it does not encode a source deck, and derive `lambda,mu<=0.45` for complete blind descent.

## Disproof track

Prove canonicalization is invariant only under parenthesization of supplied leaves, exhibit source-distinct tuples with the same endpoint normal form, reduce leaf labels to explicit source indexing, trigger the constant-or-injective composability boundary, or derive `max(lambda,mu)>=0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive control: published Cambrian lattices and sortable representatives for supplied Coxeter words.
- Positive control: toy elliptic addition words with deliberately supplied leaves and all binary parenthesizations.
- Negative control: source-distinct words with identical endpoints, and label erasure/permutation under fixed tree shapes.
- Negative control: balanced-tree parameter changes, explicit source-tree enumeration, rho, BSGS, known-log endpoints, and fresh blind masked targets.
