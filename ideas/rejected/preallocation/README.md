generation but failed operation-plus-information-flow deduplication before canonical
allocation. They are evidence, not additional hypothesis IDs, registry entries, or
dispatchable contracts. Contract snapshots use `.yaml.txt` deliberately so contract
discovery cannot execute them.

perturbation and `304` Fourier–Deligne drafts. Canonical IDs `302` and `304` remain the
Propp–Wilson and Jeffrey–Kirwan records in the parent directory.
