## Likely fatal obstruction

Mandell's theorem consumes the entire cochain algebra of a supplied space. For a finite disconnected source fiber, the degree-zero idempotents needed to decide exact nonemptiness under arbitrary restrictions encode all components, essentially the source list. Point-label ambiguity alone is not fatal because bisection would suffice if endpoint-only exact cochains existed. This meets IDEAs 069, 176, 298, 317, and 402 at the cochain-to-exact-predicate boundary.
