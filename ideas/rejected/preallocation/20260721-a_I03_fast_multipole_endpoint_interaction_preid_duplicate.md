## Likely fatal obstruction

FMM gains come from analytic separation and controlled approximation of a supplied geometric source set. Prime-field equality and common factors are exact, the x-coordinate catalogue has no compatible distance ordering, and any source-faithful near/far partition begins with the missing source interaction graph. Exactifying expansions restores full rank or explicit near traffic.
