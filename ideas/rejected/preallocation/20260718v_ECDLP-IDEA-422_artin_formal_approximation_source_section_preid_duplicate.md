## Falsifiable hypothesis

An endpoint-only system for the restricted signed five-factor fibre admits a compact compatible formal section; Artin approximation algebraizes that section uniformly over every deck restriction, after which charged bisection returns exact occurrences below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **construct a restriction-compatible formal section in the completed local ring and apply Artin approximation to replace it by an algebraic section with the same finite jet before lifting the section to five labelled occurrences**. This is not Hensel lifting, a solver substitution, or a new coordinate choice: the claimed operation is formal-to-algebraic source selection.

## Assumptions

1. Endpoint equations alone define the completed local problem and its restrictions.
2. A compatible formal section exists without first choosing a hidden source sheet.
3. One target-independent approximation order preserves exact existence and singleton identity on all signed, repeated, vertical, tangent, and infinity strata.
4. The algebraized section is canonical enough to reuse on fresh scalar-blind targets.
5. Formal construction, approximation order, branch ambiguity, output, rank, factor logs, descent, bit complexity, and peak memory are charged.

## Complete factor-base-to-target-descent path

1. Freeze the curve, signed decks, completed local rings, restriction schedule, approximation order, branch convention, and verifier without target fitting.
2. Construct endpoint-only formal state within `B^(9/4+o(1))`, without storing source occurrences or a completion oracle.
3. For each known-log restricted target, algebraize a compatible section, decide exact nonemptiness, and use charged `O(log B)` bisection plus singleton verification to recover five occurrences.
4. Collect at least `B` independent verified rows, charging failed restrictions and branch ambiguity, then solve all required factor logs.
5. Reuse unchanged state for fresh scalar-blind `Q+[t]P` targets.
6. Substitute factor logs, remove `t`, retain every algebraization branch, and verify `[x]P=Q`.
7. Charge formal construction, approximation, output, rank, factor logs, descent, verification, bit time, and peak memory.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal relation and target densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query work excluding output `N^q,N^q_m`, verified rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

Here `0<=r<=o`; setup/state must be at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`, a complete fresh restricted query at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`, and promotion requires both exponents at most `0.45`. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Artin approximation begins with a formal solution. In this application, selecting a formal branch that survives every restriction is already selecting the hidden source sheet. The theorem approximates rather than canonically discovers that branch, gives no uniform exact-label precision bound, and may leave source-sized branch ambiguity. This meets IDEAs 049, 125, 193, 275, and 408 at the missing-section boundary.

## Proof track

Construct the formal section from endpoint coefficients alone, prove a uniform finite order preserves exact restricted existence and labels on every stratum, then prove the complete descent and exponent gates.

## Disproof track

Exhibit two source fibres with identical retained completed state but different restricted existence, prove any compatible section contains a source idempotent, or show required precision/branch count exceeds a gate.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied smooth toy families with a planted unique formal branch must algebraize and replay exactly.
- Negative: equal finite jets with different higher-order branches, relabelled sheets, repeated roots, empty child restrictions, signed charts, and fresh targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 049/125/193/275/408, explicit source enumeration, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only formal construction, exact all-strata restriction stability, `1,000` independently verified rows at each of two largest toy sizes, `100` blind descents, the state/query caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-bearing formal coefficient, one equal-state/different-existence pair, one wrong singleton, supergate precision or ambiguity, or either complete exponent at least `0.50`.
- Correct algebraization of a supplied toy section is only a control.
