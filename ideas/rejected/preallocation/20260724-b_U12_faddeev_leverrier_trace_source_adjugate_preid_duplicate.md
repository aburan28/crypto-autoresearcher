## Falsifiable hypothesis

Traces of powers of an endpoint-defined relation operator determine a characteristic polynomial
and adjugate whose residues isolate exact signed factor-base occurrences. Faddeev–LeVerrier recursion
would turn public trace aggregates into point sources and complete blind descent with exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

The native operation recursively converts traces of matrix powers into characteristic coefficients
and an adjugate. It counts as ECDLP-new only if traces are endpoint-computable without source
enumeration and the adjugate has a canonical point-labelled inverse. Trace reconstruction after
a source matrix is supplied is an invariant/backend substitution.

## Assumptions

1. Public endpoints define compact exact traces without enumerating relation states.
2. Trace powers retain restricted signed occurrence identity.
3. Adjugate entries/residues canonically map to factor points under all strata.
4. Trace computation, divisions, factoring, replay, rank, logs, and descent satisfy both caps.
5. Operator and trace rules are target-independent and scalar-blind.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `beta=1/5`, setup/state are `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities
`N^delta,N^delta_t`, trace/adjugate/replay work `N^q,N^q_m`, rank credit
`N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity/failure `N^u`, and logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`.
Use `lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)` and
`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`, `0<=r<=o`; require both `<=0.45`,
state `<=B^(9/4+o(1))`, fresh work/workspace `<=B^(5/4+o(1))`; rho/BSGS are `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Power traces and characteristic coefficients are similarity invariants and discard basis/source
labels. Computing exact traces of source incidence either enumerates closed walks/source events or
uses the missing predicate. An adjugate of a compact aggregate matrix cannot distinguish fibres
that share all trace moments.

## Proof track

Prove endpoint-only trace compilation, injective restriction-uniform trace/adjugate semantics,
all-strata point inverse, full rank/log/descent, and complete sub-rho costs.

## Disproof track

Construct isospectral/equal-trace operators with different sources, expose source-event enumeration
in trace computation, or show adjugate/source replay/complete exponent reaches `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied diagonalizable labelled matrices with externally known eigenbasis/source map.
- Negative: similar/isospectral matrices, repeated eigenvalues, trace-moment collisions, small-characteristic division failures, exceptional fibres, and fresh targets.
- Baselines: spectral IDEA-001, moment IDEAs 053/259, Berkowitz, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- Correct traces, coefficients, adjugate, or one relation are controls only.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with exact trace compiler/inverse theorems, zero collisions/errors, all characteristic cases, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-enumerated trace, equal-trace source collision, noninvertible division, cap breach, missed/false occurrence, or exponent `>=0.50`.
