## Falsifiable hypothesis

Represent each partial-sum compatibility choice as a node label and encode elliptic consistency by a metric pairwise energy. Alpha-expansion min-cut moves would find a zero-energy exact signed source labeling, and restricted reruns would replay relation rows and fresh blind descents below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

Alpha-expansion simultaneously moves any subset of variables to one label via a graph cut and gives multiplicative approximation guarantees for metric energies. In the theorem's nonnegative metric domain, `OPT=0` is therefore preserved by the guarantee. It counts only if the pairwise energy graph is constructed endpoint-only, zero energy is biconditional with a complete source tuple, and a zero-energy labeling has a point-faithful inverse.
