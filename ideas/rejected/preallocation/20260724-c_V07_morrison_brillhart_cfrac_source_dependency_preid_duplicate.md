## Falsifiable hypothesis

Continued-fraction convergents of a public endpoint-derived quadratic irrational
produce smooth residues whose parity dependencies preserve exact signed
factor-base occurrences. CFRAC collection and dependency solving would yield full
relation rank and 100 fresh blind descents with complete exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

The native CFRAC operation streams convergents for a supplied integer, factors
associated small residues over a supplied prime base, and combines parity vectors
into a congruence of squares. ECDLP novelty requires the streamed residues to arise
from endpoints without source enumeration and the parity dependency to replay
elliptic occurrences rather than only certify a square.

## Assumptions

1. Endpoints compile one compact quadratic irrational with source-faithful convergents.
2. Smooth residue factors preserve occurrence identity, signs, repetitions, and restrictions.
3. Parity dependencies retain enough orientation to reconstruct exact elliptic tuples.
4. Continued fractions, smoothness trials, linear algebra, replay, logs, and descent meet both caps.
5. Neither the factor base nor convergent choice is fitted to hidden sources or targets.

## Likely fatal obstruction

CFRAC's convergents and factor vectors belong to a supplied integer-factoring
instance. Translating a factor occurrence into an elliptic source requires a
source-bearing encoding before collection. Parity dependencies intentionally forget
multiplicity modulo two and return a square certificate, not the signed point tuple
needed for factor logs and descent.

## Proof track

Prove an endpoint-only quadratic-irrational compiler, source-faithful smooth factors,
injective dependency replay including multiplicities, sufficient independent rank,
blind descent, and both complete cost caps.

## Disproof track

Hold convergents/parity vectors fixed while changing exact fibres, expose one
source-labelled factor, produce a parity collision, or show complete cost `>=0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied CFRAC instances with externally labelled smooth factors.
- Negative: equal parity vectors/different occurrences, repeated factors, nonsmooth residues, dependent rows, empty fibres, and fresh targets.
- Baselines: IDEAS 053/117/157/280, quadratic sieve, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- A correct square dependency or factor is relation-only evidence.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with exact compiler/replay theorems, zero occurrence errors, charged smoothness, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, and both exponents `<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-bearing convergent factor, parity-source collision, rank failure, cap breach, or exponent `>=0.50`.
