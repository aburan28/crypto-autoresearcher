## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query `N^q,N^q_m`, verified-rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`, with `0<=r<=o`.

Require setup/state `<=B^(9/4+o(1))`, total fresh online restriction/bisection/replay/verification `<=B^(5/4+o(1))`, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Elias–Fano compresses an already sorted list. Listing exact accepting endpoints plus occurrence satellites is the missing source catalogue; omitting satellites makes predecessor answers insufficient for replay. Even a `B^2` pair-key list leaves a `B^3=N^0.60` triple scan. This merges with IDEAs `053/120/350/374` and pre-ID wavelet-tree `B01`.
