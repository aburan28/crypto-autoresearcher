## Falsifiable hypothesis

Every dyadically restricted signed five-factor relation can be compiled from endpoint data into a uniformly bounded-degree geometrically irreducible finite-field variety whose explicit Lang–Weil lower bound is positive exactly when the finite restricted fiber is nonempty. Charged `O(log B)` positive-parent/negative-child bisection plus singleton verification then recovers a relation below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **replace a finite restricted deck fiber by a uniformly controlled geometrically irreducible algebraic family, use an explicit Lang–Weil point-count lower bound as an exact nonempty bit, and self-reduce the finite source by dyadic restrictions**. On audit, Lang–Weil is only a point-count backend for the exact restricted-fiber compiler already required by IDEAs 156, 197, and 379; it is not a new source-information operation.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query work excluding output `N^q,N^q_m`, verified rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

With `0<=r<=o`, setup/state must be at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`; the total fresh online restriction/bisection sequence plus singleton verification must be at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`; and promotion needs `lambda<=0.45` and `mu<=0.45`. Here `q` charges every `O(log B)` positive-parent/negative-child query and singleton verification, while `o` charges final tuple or direct output. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Finite deck membership cuts the relation to a zero-dimensional, highly reducible, or degree-growing set. A positive-dimensional irreducible envelope contains many nondeck points and is eventually positive independently of the rare finite source; it is not biconditional with the restricted deck fiber. A zero-dimensional exact encoding restores reducible components, source-bearing degree, or explicit finite membership. Lang–Weil gives an asymptotic aggregate estimate with an error term and cannot distinguish empty from a rare singleton. Thus this is a point-estimate backend substitution for IDEAs 156, 197, and 379 plus the P1553 exact-existence interface, not a mechanism-new record.

## Proof track

Construct endpoint-only uniformly bounded restricted varieties, prove geometric irreducibility and an explicit positive lower bound iff the finite source fiber is nonempty for every chart, then certify charged bisection and the complete descent gates.

## Disproof track

Exhibit one restriction producing a reducible/zero-dimensional/high-degree fiber, a nonempty singleton below the Lang–Weil error, an envelope point outside the deck, or compiler/bisection cost above the caps.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied geometrically irreducible varieties with certified dominant main terms must return correct positive-parent/negative-child existence bits.
- Negative: empty and singleton zero-dimensional fibers, reducible unions, high-degree finite-membership encodings, envelope false positives, chart exceptions, restrictions, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 019/051/156/197/379, direct finite-deck enumeration, Ax–Katz aggregate divisibility, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only uniformly bounded geometry, exact signed/chart-complete restricted existence including singleton fibers, `1,000` independent rows, `100` blind descents, total bisection/query caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one irreducibility/degree failure, one empty/nonempty bound collision, one source-bearing deck compiler, cap violation, or either exponent at least `0.50`.
- Correct point counts on supplied toy varieties are only controls.
