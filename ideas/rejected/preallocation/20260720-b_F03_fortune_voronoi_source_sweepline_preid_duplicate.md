## Falsifiable hypothesis

Embed partial elliptic sums as public planar sites so target translations become queries in a Voronoi diagram. Fortune's beach-line events would expose the nearest compatible pair/singleton cells, replay exact sources, and yield relations plus fresh blind descent below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

Fortune's sweepline transforms supplied planar sites into a Voronoi diagram using site events, circle events, a beach line, and an event queue. It counts only if a DLP-free finite-field-to-ordered-plane embedding preserves exact elliptic compatibility and occurrence labels, and nearest-cell location is biconditional with restricted source existence. Building sites from source tuples is a control.

## Assumptions

1. A target-independent ordered planar embedding covers every signed and exceptional source stratum exactly.
2. Site generation, coordinate precision, events, beach-line updates, restrictions, point location, replay, rank, logs, descent, time, and memory are charged.
3. Nearest-site identity exactly decides relation existence rather than geometric proximity.
4. Voronoi cells preserve occurrence ancestry under duplicates and arbitrary restrictions.
5. One diagram serves known-log and fresh scalar-blind targets without scalar coordinates or target rebuilding.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Let n be site count, C_site site-construction work, C_pred exact event/geometry predicate cost, Q_R restrictions, and C_inv inversion. Fortune's supplied planar-site diagram costs O(n log n) predicates and O(n) state. Set a=log_N(n(C_site+C_pred log n)), a_m=log_N(n+M_embed), q=log_N(Q_R(T_rebuild+T_locate+C_inv)+T_replay), and q_m=log_N(n+M_locate+M_inv). With beta=1/5 and the common delta,delta_t,r,o,u,ell,ell_m exponents:

lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)

mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u), 0<=r<=o.

Require setup/state <=B^(9/4+o(1)), complete fresh work <=N^(0.25+o(1))=B^(5/4+o(1)), lambda,mu<=0.45, and four increasing-B one-sided 95% upper bounds. Rho and BSGS remain 0.50.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Prime-field elliptic addition has no canonical DLP-free Euclidean order or metric making nearest sites equivalent to exact five-sum compatibility. Faithful sites are partial-source tuples; coarse sites merge incompatible ancestry. Restrictions delete sites and generally require rebuilding the diagram. This merges with IDEA-356/359 and pre-ID E06.

## Proof track

Give an endpoint-only planar embedding and exact all-strata nearest-cell/source biconditional stable under restrictions, then close every diagram, replay, rank, and descent cost.

## Disproof track

Find two source occurrences with the same embedded site but different completions, an order/precision dependency on scalar coordinates, or a restriction requiring source-sized rebuild.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied Euclidean sites with a unique planted nearest labelled source.
- Negative: colliding sites, cocircular events, field-order mutations, empty/singleton restrictions, exceptional and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEA-356/359, pre-ID E06, explicit pair sites, P1553 R4, rho, BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only sites, exact restriction-stable nearest/source inversion, rank d_FB, 100 blind descents, both caps, and lambda,mu<=0.45.
- Falsify on one source-derived site, one nearest/source mismatch, target-specific rebuild, cap violation, or complete exponent >=0.50.
