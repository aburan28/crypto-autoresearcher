The proposed operation is WDVV source recursion, not a dense resultant or generic associative solver.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Gromov–Witten classes start from a target space and marked insertions, while WDVV returns equality among aggregate boundary contributions. Supplying insertions or enough classes to identify arbitrary source occurrences imports the source; associativity alone has no canonical point inverse. This meets IDEAs 072, 087, 128, 287, and 305.

## Proof track

Construct a bounded endpoint-only CohFT, prove WDVV recursion decides exact restricted existence and returns labels on all strata, then certify full descent.

## Disproof track

Identify a source-bearing insertion, construct equal WDVV data with different occurrences, or prove boundary/class state or recursion exceeds a cap.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied toy CohFTs with planted semisimple quantum products and known insertions.
- Negative: equal invariants with permuted markings, nonsemisimple products, boundary automorphisms, empty restrictions, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 072/087/128/287/305, explicit source products, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates
