## Falsifiable hypothesis

The restricted five-factor endpoint relation compiles to a compact oper whose Miura reduction decides exact nonemptiness under every restriction; charged `O(log B)` dyadic bisection plus singleton verification then recovers original deck occurrences below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **compile the endpoint relation as an oper, choose a Borel/Miura reduction, factor the scalar differential operator into first-order Cartan factors, and invert their residues or eigenlines to five occurrence labels**. Audit shows this is a narrower supplied-operator factorization theorem inside the endpoint-to-integrable-operator-to-factor/spectrum-to-occurrence flow already owned by IDEAs 273 and 385, not a mechanism-new source channel.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query work excluding output `N^q,N^q_m`, verified rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

With `0<=r<=o`, setup/state must be at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`; the total fresh online restriction/bisection sequence plus singleton verification must be at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`; and promotion needs `lambda<=0.45` and `mu<=0.45`. Here `q` charges every `O(log B)` positive-parent/negative-child query and singleton verification, while `o` charges final tuple or direct output. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Drinfeld–Sokolov theory starts from a supplied oper or connection. Compiling one whose charged state decides exact nonemptiness for every restricted elliptic fiber imports source factorization or a completion oracle. Weyl/gauge nonuniqueness alone is not fatal because bisection would suffice after an exact predicate. The operation and information flow are already represented by IDEAs 273 and 385, with IDEAs 108, 180, and 305 nearby; Miura factorization is therefore a pre-ID theorem substitution rather than a new record.

## Proof track

Construct an endpoint-only compact oper, prove chart-complete exact restricted existence from its Miura state, recover labels by charged bisection and singleton verification, then certify the complete descent gates.

## Disproof track

Show oper coefficients contain source symmetric functions, exhibit equal retained oper/Miura state for restrictions with different existence bits, or prove factorization state above the caps.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied low-rank opers with planted Miura factors and fixed gauge must factor and replay exactly.
- Negative: Weyl-related reductions, repeated residues, gauge-equivalent connections, restrictions, signed branches, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 108/180/273/305/385, explicit factor dictionaries, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only oper construction, chart-complete exact restricted existence, charged `O(log B)` bisection and singleton verification, `1,000` independent rows, `100` blind descents, caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-bearing coefficient, one equal-state/different-existence collision, missing exact reduction, cap violation, or either exponent at least `0.50`.
- Correct Miura factorization of a supplied toy oper is only a control.
