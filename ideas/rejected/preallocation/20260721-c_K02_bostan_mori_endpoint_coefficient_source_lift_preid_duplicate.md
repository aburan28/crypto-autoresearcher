## Falsifiable hypothesis

Compile restricted signed source fibres into one target-independent rational generating function, use Bostan–Mori even/odd denominator reduction to extract exact endpoint coefficients, and self-reduce each positive coefficient to an exact signed occurrence below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The native operation repeatedly multiplies a rational denominator by its sign-flipped copy and selects parity coefficients to obtain a distant coefficient in quasi-linear polynomial work. It counts only if the rational function is endpoint-derived, restriction-stable, source-biconditional, and retains a charged coefficient-to-occurrence inverse; coefficient extraction from a supplied recurrence is a control.

## Assumptions

1. A degree at most `B^(9/4+o(1))` rational series represents every restriction without enumerating source tuples.
2. Coefficient zero is exactly restricted-source emptiness over the base field, with no modular cancellation.
3. Parity reduction preserves signed occurrence ancestry or a bounded exact lift.
4. Restricted numerators and denominators update within `B^(5/4+o(1))` work.
5. The same compiled series supports relation and fresh masked targets.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Bostan–Mori accelerates access to a coefficient of a rational function already supplied. Constructing a source-biconditional denominator can materialize the `B^5` source product, while a compact symmetric coefficient counts or sums fibres and loses point identity. Over `F_p`, a zero coefficient can also be cancellation rather than emptiness. Restoring occurrence tags makes the coefficient vector or numerator source-sized.

## Proof track

Derive a compact endpoint-only rational identity, prove zero iff empty for every restriction and all strata, provide a bounded exact occurrence inverse, and charge every polynomial operation.

## Disproof track

Audit coefficient origins and degrees, search for modular cancellation and same-coefficient distinct fibres, and falsify on source-sized series construction, target rebuilding, or ancestry loss.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied rational recurrences with nonnegative labelled coefficients and planted source backpointers.
- Negative: cancellation pairs over `F_p`, duplicate occurrences, high-order generic recurrences, empty restrictions, and fresh targets.
- Baselines: direct recurrence, Lagrange–Good, holonomic telescoping, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

Promote only with a proved endpoint-only compact series, exact zero semantics, four sizes, zero errors, exact all-strata lifts, full rank, 100 fresh blind descents, both caps, and 95% upper bounds `lambda,mu<=0.45`. Falsify on source materialization, coefficient cancellation, missing lift, false answer, or exponent at least `0.50`.
