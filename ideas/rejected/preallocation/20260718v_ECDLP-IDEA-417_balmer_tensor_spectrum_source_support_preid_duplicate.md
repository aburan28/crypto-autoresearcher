## Falsifiable hypothesis

An endpoint-constructed tensor-triangulated category for restricted relations has a compact Balmer spectrum whose support coordinates decide exact nonemptiness for every restriction; charged `O(log B)` bisection plus singleton verification then recovers one five-factor source below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **form a tensor-triangulated relation category, classify prime thick tensor ideals, compute object supports in the Balmer spectrum, intersect target supports, and lift a spectral point to factor occurrences**. The new operation is tensor-prime support, not ordinary eigenvalue or Fourier spectrum substitution.

2. A subgate finite presentation of the relevant spectrum is exact under every restriction.
3. Every relation object is proved tensor-nilpotence-free in the strong sense that it is nonzero exactly when its Balmer support is nonempty; support intersection preserves rare point existence and fits query/memory caps.
4. Spectrum/support state decides exact restricted existence; a direct spectral inverse is optional because charged bisection and singleton verification are sufficient.
5. Category construction, ideal tests, spectrum, support, lift, output, rank, logs, descent, verification, bit time, and memory are charged.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query work excluding output `N^q,N^q_m`, verified rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

With `0<=r<=o`, setup/state must be at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`; the total fresh online restriction/bisection sequence plus singleton verification must be at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`; and promotion needs `lambda<=0.45` and `mu<=0.45`. Here `q` charges every `O(log B)` positive-parent/negative-child query and singleton verification, while `o` charges final tuple or direct output. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Balmer's construction starts from a supplied tensor category. Moreover, empty support certifies tensor-nilpotence, not necessarily the zero relation object; a nonzero tensor-nilpotent object is therefore an immediate false-empty counterexample unless the endpoint compiler proves the extra reducedness property. A category and prime tests that decide exact nonemptiness for every restricted relation already encode incidence or a completion oracle, while exact-existence-faithful supports can be source-sized. Absence of a point inverse alone is not fatal because bisection would suffice. This meets IDEAs 086, 127, 163, 187, and 407 at the categorical-support-to-exact-predicate boundary.

## Proof track

Construct an endpoint-only finite category and spectrum, prove chart-complete exact restricted existence, recover labels by charged bisection and singleton verification, then certify the complete descent gates.

## Disproof track

Show one object/morphism/ideal test is source-bearing, exhibit a nonzero tensor-nilpotent relation object or equal retained supports for restrictions with different existence bits, or prove categorical state above the caps.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied finite reduced tensor categories with explicitly known prime spectra, proved support/nonzero equivalence, and uniquely labelled atoms must support-test exactly.
- Negative: a nonzero tensor-nilpotent object, equal supports for nonisomorphic objects, gauge-equivalent labels, arbitrary restrictions, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 086/127/163/187/407, explicit category tables, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates
