## Likely fatal obstruction

The canonical extension starts with a local system or connection. A connection that distinguishes arbitrary relation sheets imports the source cover; its residues are defined only up to gauge/conjugacy and do not canonically name sheets. Point-faithful eigenbases restore source-sized data. This meets IDEAs 030, 114, 180, 228, and 288.
