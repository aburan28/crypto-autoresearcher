## Falsifiable hypothesis

Endpoint-derived restricted-fibre values over a compact Boolean lattice admit Kennes fast
Möbius inversion to isolate signed source atoms, returning exact relation tuples and fresh
target descents with complete exponents below `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

The fast Boolean-lattice transform converts all supplied subset values to exact Möbius
coefficients. It counts only if endpoint data construct the required subset payload inside
the caps and atomic coefficients replay occurrences. Transforming an enumerated table is a control.

## Assumptions

1. The relevant restriction lattice has sub-rho represented size.
2. Every input value is endpoint-derived without Query2P1 or source enumeration.
3. Atomic coefficients are biconditional with signed occurrence tuples.
4. Negative fibres, restrictions, signs, multiplicity, and exceptional strata are exact.
5. Target-independent state works unchanged on known-log and blind targets.

## Complete factor-base-to-target-descent path

1. Freeze `B=N^(1/5)`, restriction universe, subset indexing, transform schedule, strata, signs, and verifier.
2. Construct target-independent state within `B^(9/4+o(1))`, with no supplied subset table, source catalogue, dense resultant, or target advice.
3. For each relation target, derive every charged input value, transform, isolate an atom, replay signed occurrences, and verify the point sum.
4. Collect `max(d_FB+32,1000)` verified rows, achieve rank `d_FB`, and solve all logs.
5. Reuse identical state for 100 fresh `R=Q+[t]P` targets, replay occurrences, compute `x=sum epsilon_i log_P(A_i)-t mod N`, and verify `[x]P=Q`.
6. Charge the full subset domain, transforms, zeros, outputs, retries, rank, factor logs, blind descent, bits, and live memory.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `beta=1/5`, let setup/state be `N^a,N^a_m`; relation and target reciprocal
densities be `N^delta,N^delta_t`; query/workspace be `N^q,N^q_m`; verified-rank
credit be `N^r`; output be `N^o`; ambiguity/amplification be `N^u`; and factor-log
time/memory be `N^ell,N^ell_m`.
`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)` and
`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`, `0<=r<=o`. Here `a,q` include constructing
all subset-indexed values. Promotion requires `lambda,mu<=0.45`, the `B^(9/4)`
setup cap, and `B^(5/4)` fresh cap. Pollard rho expected time and BSGS time/memory
have exponent `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Fast Möbius inversion accelerates arithmetic on a supplied `2^m` array. Constructing the
endpoint-conditioned values is the missing restricted predicate; materializing them is
exponential or restores `B^3` traffic. Coefficients can aggregate many occurrence tuples.

## Proof track

Prove a sub-rho endpoint constructor for the complete transform payload and a source-faithful
atomic inverse across every restriction and curve stratum.

## Disproof track

Find one supplied coefficient, exponential lattice, equal transform/different sources,
lost occurrence, target rebuild, or complete exponent `>=0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a supplied toy subset function with a unique labelled Möbius atom.
- Negative: dense transforms, cancelling source families, empty fibres, repeated signs, and blind targets.
- Baselines: explicit subset convolution, IDEAs 185/199, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.
- Controls are toy and model-bound.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with exact all-strata subset values, atomic replay, four sizes, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on supplied payload, source cancellation, domain/cap failure, or exponent `>=0.50`.
