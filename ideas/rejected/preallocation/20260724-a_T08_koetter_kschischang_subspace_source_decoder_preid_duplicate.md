## Falsifiable hypothesis

Each exact signed relation fibre has a public scalar-blind Grassmannian codeword, and the endpoint
determines a received subspace within bounded injection/subspace distance. Koetter–Kschischang
minimum-distance/interpolation decoding would recover an exact occurrence subspace, then factor
logs and 100 blind descents below rho.

## Mechanism-new operation

The native representation treats a transmitted packet span, rather than ordered packets, as the
codeword and decodes a supplied received subspace under the subspace metric. It counts only if the
endpoint compiles a compact received subspace whose decoded basis has a canonical all-strata
inverse to signed factor-base occurrences. Supplying a rank/subspace word and then using a
Sudan-style decoder merges with IDEA-150 and loses occurrence provenance.

## Assumptions

1. A compact target-independent Grassmannian encoding represents all signed source tuples.
2. Endpoint-only computation returns a received subspace inside the decoding radius.
3. The decoded subspace has a canonical basis-to-occurrence inverse despite basis invariance.
4. Extension-field dimension, basis traffic, interpolation, output, and rank-metric costs meet both caps.
5. The same representation is scalar-blind, full-rank across relations, and reusable for fresh masks.

## Likely fatal obstruction

A subspace code deliberately quotients away packet ordering and basis identity, precisely the data
needed to replay signed factor-base occurrences. Restoring a canonical occurrence basis requires
source labels or a dictionary. Moreover, representing `B` positions over a sufficiently large
extension can expose quadratic base-field state, repeating IDEA-150's cost obstruction.

## Proof track

Prove a compact endpoint received-subspace compiler, restriction-uniform distance bound, canonical
all-strata occurrence inverse, full rank/logs, blind descent, and complete sub-rho costs.

## Disproof track

Exhibit two occurrence tuples spanning the same subspace, a basis-dependent inverse, source-sized
extension state, missed stratum, or complete exponent at least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied random-network subspace-code words with correctable injected errors/erasures.
- Negative: same-span different-occurrence tuples, basis changes, rank-deficient spaces, repeated points, empty fibres, exceptional charts, and fresh targets.
- Baselines: IDEA-150/231/223/206, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- A decoded subspace or rank certificate remains toy/model-bound evidence.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with public compiler/canonical-inverse theorems, zero errors at four sizes/all strata, miss probability at most `2^-80`, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, both caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one same-span source collision, basis ambiguity, representation/cap breach, or exponent `>=0.50`.
