## Likely fatal obstruction

Hu–Tucker needs the ordered leaves and their weights, which are the source catalogue and target distribution. It optimizes expected path length, while exact ECDLP needs all-negative and fresh-target guarantees. Rare witnesses can receive deep codes, and any leaf backpointer exists only after its source occurrence was supplied.

## Proof track

Derive endpoint-only ordered cells and exact target-uniform weights, prove worst-case restriction and source-lift bounds, and complete relation/descent accounting.

## Disproof track

Audit leaf and weight provenance; mutate the target distribution, concentrate mass away from a singleton witness, and charge worst-case paths and rebuilds.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied ordered symbols with stable weights and labelled leaves.
- Negative: rare singleton symbols, shifted distributions, duplicate keys, empty intervals, adversarial deep leaves, and fresh targets.
- Baselines: balanced binary search, Elias–Fano, B-tree, PATRICIA, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
