## Likely fatal obstruction

The structure accelerates queries only after keys are inserted. A key that distinguishes exact elliptic sources is the source catalogue in integer form; endpoint-only coarse keys collide and lose ancestry. The `O(log log U)` query does not credit the `B^3` or `B^5` key-generation cost. This merges with IDEAs `120/350/352/374` and C01.
