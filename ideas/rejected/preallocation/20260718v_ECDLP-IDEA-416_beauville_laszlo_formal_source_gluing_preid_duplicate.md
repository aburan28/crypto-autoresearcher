## Falsifiable hypothesis

The restricted five-factor source family is reconstructible from compact endpoint-only data on a punctured complement and a formal neighborhood, with a bounded transition map whose Beauville–Laszlo gluing decides exact nonemptiness for every restriction; charged `O(log B)` bisection plus singleton verification then recovers a source below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **represent the relation source by a bundle or module on the complement and formal completion, glue them through an isomorphism on the punctured overlap, and evaluate a distinguished global section to recover occurrences**. The proposed new step is formal patching with a point section, distinct from toric fiber-product algebra gluing.

## Assumptions

1. Both local objects and their overlap isomorphism are endpoint-constructed without source charts or relation enumeration.
2. The patching element `f` is a non-zero-divisor and the completed module is `f`-regular (or the proved vector-bundle/global variant applies); formal depth, ranks, coefficients, and transition state fit the frozen caps.
3. Gluing commutes exactly with arbitrary dyadic restrictions and fresh targets.
4. The glued object decides exact restricted existence; a canonical global section is optional because charged bisection and singleton verification are sufficient.
5. Local construction, completion, transition, gluing, section evaluation, output, rank, logs, descent, verification, bit time, and memory are charged.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query work excluding output `N^q,N^q_m`, verified rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

With `0<=r<=o`, setup/state must be at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`; the total fresh online restriction/bisection sequence plus singleton verification must be at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`; and promotion needs `lambda<=0.45` and `mu<=0.45`. Here `q` charges every `O(log B)` positive-parent/negative-child query and singleton verification, while `o` charges final tuple or direct output. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Beauville–Laszlo begins with local modules and an overlap isomorphism that already contain the hidden relation data. Making the glued object decide exact nonemptiness under arbitrary restrictions imports source charts, completion oracles, or transition matrices of source scale. The absence of a canonical section alone is not fatal because bisection would suffice after an exact predicate. This meets IDEAs 086, 091, 161, 216, and 354 at the local-data-to-exact-predicate boundary.

## Proof track

Construct endpoint-only compact local objects and transition data, prove chart-complete exact restricted existence after gluing, recover labels by charged bisection and singleton verification, then certify the complete descent gates.

## Disproof track

Show one local trivialization or transition entry is source-bearing, exhibit equal retained glued state for restrictions with different existence bits, or prove formal/update state above the caps.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied finite modules with known complement/completion data and a unique labelled global section must glue and replay exactly.
- Negative: identical glued modules with different section labels, non-glueable local data, transition-gauge ambiguity, restrictions, signed branches, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 086/091/161/216/354, explicit source charts, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only local data, chart-complete exact restricted existence after gluing, charged `O(log B)` bisection and singleton verification, `1,000` independent rows, `100` blind descents, caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-bearing local datum, one equal-state/different-existence collision, failed restriction base change, cap violation, or either exponent at least `0.50`.
- Correct gluing of a supplied toy module is only a control.
