## Falsifiable hypothesis

Endpoint-derived reaction coordinates and umbrella potentials partition signed source space into
overlapping strata whose biased ensembles expose exact elliptic-relation fibres. Exact
unbiasing and occurrence replay would yield full-rank relations and 100 blind descents with
complete time and memory exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

Umbrella sampling alters a supplied energy by bias potentials along a supplied reaction coordinate,
then reweights overlapping biased ensembles to estimate the original distribution. It counts only
if endpoints construct coordinates and biases without source incidence, exact relation strata have
proved coverage, and sampled configurations retain signed occurrence identity. Biasing an explicit
source energy is a control.

## Assumptions

1. A compact public reaction coordinate separates exact relation fibres from nonrelations.
2. Bias potentials and overlap windows are target-independent and do not encode source tables.
3. Window mixing, overlap, normalization, reweighting error, and relation hits satisfy caps.
4. Configurations replay signs, multiplicities, and exceptional elliptic strata exactly.
5. Frozen windows and biases apply unchanged to fresh scalar-blind masked targets.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Umbrella sampling requires a reaction coordinate and bias that identify otherwise rare regions.
A coordinate isolating exact elliptic source fibres encodes the missing predicate; a coarse
coordinate mixes empty and nonempty fibres. Reweighting recovers aggregate probabilities, not
occurrence identities, and every additional window multiplies source-state and mixing costs.

## Proof track

Prove endpoint-only reaction coordinates/biases, restriction-uniform exact window coverage,
subcap mixing and overlap, occurrence replay, full rank/logs, blind descent, and complete costs.

## Disproof track

Construct equal reaction-coordinate histograms with different source fibres, expose a source-trained
bias, show nonoverlap or false relation strata, lose occurrences, or reach complete exponent at
least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a supplied toy energy with labelled rare states and overlapping exact windows.
- Negative: equal-coordinate different-source fibres, disjoint windows, false low-energy strata, empty fibres, shuffled labels, exceptional strata, and blind targets.
- Baselines: annealing, replica exchange, Wang–Landau, Metropolis-Hastings, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- Smooth reweighting or barrier crossing is only toy/model-bound evidence.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with exact public coordinate/bias/lift theorems, zero semantic errors at four sizes/all strata, overlap and miss bounds, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, both caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one coordinate collision, source-trained bias, window gap, cap breach, or complete exponent `>=0.50`.
