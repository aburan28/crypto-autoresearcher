## Falsifiable hypothesis

Attach F5 signatures to endpoint-derived summation-polynomial generators so that redundant reductions are removed while exact source provenance survives, yielding verified relation rows and blind descent below rho/BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

F5 orders module signatures and rejects reductions predicted to be syzygetic. It counts only if an elliptic-specific signature operation reduces construction of the source-faithful ideal or its endpoint-conditioned state, rather than merely changing the Gröbner backend after dense Macaulay data exist.

4. Restrictions and target changes do not require rebuilding dense bases.

## Likely fatal obstruction

F5 removes some reductions to zero after a polynomial system and monomial representation are supplied. It does not lower the relation ideal's degree/density, construct target-dependent source incidence, or make normal-form roots source-labelled. Therefore it is a solver substitution under the stated deduplication rule.

## Proof track

## Disproof track

## Positive and negative controls

- Negative: same Hilbert series under F4/Buchberger, shuffled source labels, singular/exceptional strata, and fresh targets.
- Baselines: existing Gröbner records, subresultants, dense resultants, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates
