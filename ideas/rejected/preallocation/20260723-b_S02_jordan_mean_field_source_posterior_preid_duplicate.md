## Falsifiable hypothesis

Signed factor-base decompositions admit an endpoint-derived graphical model whose posterior has a
restriction-stable low-order variational representation. Coordinate mean-field updates and
certified decimation would return exact occurrences often enough for full factor logs and 100
fresh descents with complete time and memory exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

Mean-field variational inference replaces a supplied joint distribution by an optimized product
or low-order family using expectations of log factors. It counts only if endpoints compile those
factors without source truth tables, the variational family certifies exact zero/nonzero
restrictions, and decimation replays occurrence-distinct signed sources. Approximate inference on
a materialized factor graph is a representation control.

## Assumptions

1. Public endpoint factors are compact, exact, target-independent, and source-free.
2. A proved variational gap separates empty restrictions from every nonempty source fibre.
3. Coordinate updates, normalization, precision, and convergence satisfy both cost caps.
4. Factorized marginals preserve correlations needed for signs, repeats, and exceptional strata.
5. One frozen representation works on known-log rows and fresh scalar-blind masks.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Mean-field inference assumes the joint factors and deliberately discards correlations. Exact
elliptic source fibres are sparse and correlation-defined; equal one-variable marginals can come
from different signed occurrences or from empty versus cancelling pseudomass. Restoring exact
restriction semantics requires the full source factor graph or Query2P1-equivalent state.

## Proof track

Derive source-free endpoint factors and prove restriction-uniform exact variational support,
correlation-preserving decimation, all-strata replay, full rank/logs, blind descent, and sub-rho
costs.

## Disproof track

Give equal factorized marginals with different exact fibres, a false variational fixed point, a
source-bearing factor, exponential correlation order, target-dependent retraining, or complete
exponent at least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied tree/product toy models with one labelled assignment and exact mean-field posterior.
- Negative: parity-correlated assignments, empty/singleton fibres with equal marginals, loopy pseudomass, repeated strata, shuffled labels, and blind targets.
- Baselines: sum-product, Bethe, bucket elimination, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- ELBO or marginal accuracy is only toy/model-bound evidence.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with an endpoint-only factor theorem, zero restriction/source errors at four sizes/all strata, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, both caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one false nonzero/zero, lost correlation or occurrence, source factor, cap violation, or complete exponent `>=0.50`.
