## Falsifiable hypothesis

An endpoint-derived ladder of softened elliptic-relation energies permits replica exchanges that
cross source-fibre barriers in sub-rho time. The cold replica then yields exact signed
occurrences with sufficient density for full factor logs and 100 blind target descents under
complete time and memory exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

Replica exchange runs several supplied equilibrium systems at different temperatures and swaps
configurations with an energy-dependent acceptance rule. It counts only if public endpoints
compile every energy and temperature without source incidence, a mixing/hit theorem reaches exact
relations, and the cold state has a charged occurrence lift. Tempering an explicit source search
is a control.

## Assumptions

1. The energy ladder is target-independent, endpoint-only, and has exact relation ground states.
2. Replica count, spacing, local transitions, swap rates, mixing, and restarts satisfy both caps.
3. High-temperature exploration does not erase signs, multiplicities, or exceptional strata.
4. Cold-replica hits lift to labelled factor-base occurrences with negligible false acceptance.
5. One frozen ladder applies unchanged to fresh scalar-blind masked targets.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Replica exchange improves traversal only after a meaningful energy landscape and local moves are
supplied. An exact energy separating relation fibres from nonrelations is the missing predicate;
a softened energy can mix while giving no exact hit guarantee. Multiplying replicas also charges
the same source-state, evaluation, and memory obstruction at every temperature.

## Proof track

Prove endpoint-only energy construction, relation-gap and ladder-overlap bounds, subcap mixing,
exact lift, full-rank relation collection, blind descent, and complete exponents below `0.45`.

## Disproof track

Show equal energy histograms with different source fibres, a source-dependent move/energy, vanishing
swap overlap, torpid cold mixing, lost signs, or complete exponent at least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a supplied finite spin-style toy landscape with one labelled ground-state relation.
- Negative: first-order bottlenecks, flat energies, equal histograms/different sources, empty fibres, shuffled labels, exceptional strata, and blind targets.
- Baselines: annealing, Metropolis-Hastings, exact coupling, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- Swap acceptance or equilibration is only toy/model-bound evidence.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with public energy/lift theorems, zero semantic errors at four sizes/all strata, total miss probability at most `2^-80`, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, both caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-bearing energy/move, false cold hit, cap breach, or complete exponent `>=0.50`.
