## Falsifiable hypothesis

An endpoint-derived recursive rule system for five-term decompositions can be Magic-Set
rewritten by a public target binding so bottom-up evaluation derives only target-relevant signed
occurrences. The rewritten program completes factor logs and fresh descent below exponent `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

Magic Sets rewrites recursive logical rules to propagate query bindings and suppress irrelevant
facts. It counts only if the original rules and extensional facts are endpoint-derived without
source enumeration, rewritten facts retain exact empty-fibre semantics, and a proof trace replays
signed point occurrences. Rewriting a supplied decomposition program is a control.

## Assumptions

1. Rules/facts are target-independent, scalar-blind, and within the setup cap.
2. Binding propagation is exact on all exceptional strata and restrictions.
3. Derived facts have charged signed provenance and bounded multiplicity.
4. Recursion depth, adornment blowup, duplicates, and all-negative evaluation are charged.
5. One frozen program serves known-log relation targets and fresh masks.

## Complete factor-base-to-target-descent path

1. Freeze `B=N^(1/5)`, rule language, adornments, evaluation order, restrictions, provenance,
   exceptional strata, and verifier.
2. Compile endpoint-only target-independent facts/rules within `B^(9/4+o(1))`; forbid source
   tuple tables, scalar residues, target fitting, and hidden `Query2P1` calls.
3. For each known-log target, apply the target binding, evaluate rewritten rules, replay one
   signed proof trace, and verify the elliptic sum.
4. Collect `max(d_FB+32,1000)` verified rows, require rank `d_FB`, solve every factor log, and
   charge compilation, facts, recursion, duplicates, traces, failures, and sparse linear algebra.
5. Reuse the frozen program for 100 fresh `R=Q+[t]P`, compute
   `x=sum epsilon_i log_P(A_i)-t mod N`, and verify `[x]P=Q`.
6. Charge rule/fact state, target-specific derived facts, bit complexity, rank, logs, and memory.

For `beta=1/5`, setup/state are `N^a,N^a_m`; relation/target reciprocal densities
`N^delta,N^delta_t`; evaluation/workspace `N^q,N^q_m`; rank credit `N^r`; proof output

setup/state `<=B^(9/4+o(1))`, and fresh work/workspace `<=B^(5/4+o(1))`.
Rho expected time and BSGS time/memory have exponent `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

The extensional facts and rule joins already encode source compatibility. Magic Sets reduces
irrelevant derivation after a target binding but does not manufacture the endpoint predicate;
proof provenance can be as large as the omitted source search.

## Proof track

Construct endpoint-only rules/facts, prove rewrite equivalence with exact negatives and signed
traces under restrictions, and bound the complete relation/descent exponents.

## Disproof track

Trace one fact or rule body to source enumeration, find an incomplete/false derivation,
unbounded adornment/provenance, restriction rebuild, or exponent `>=0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied toy Datalog rules with one labelled derivation.
- Negative: irrelevant recursion, duplicate proofs, empty fibres, equal heads/different sources,
  repeated signed points, and blind targets.
- Baselines: Courcelle/InsideOut/decomposable circuits, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- Rewrite correctness is only toy/model-bound evidence.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only facts, zero semantic errors at four sizes, bounded traces,
  full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on supplied facts, one semantic error, cap violation, or exponent `>=0.50`.
