## Falsifiable hypothesis

Endpoint equations admit a compact source-faithful factor graph whose solution clusters are
captured by survey-propagation messages. Certified survey decimation would return exact signed
occurrences densely enough for full factor logs and 100 fresh descents with complete time and
memory exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

Survey propagation passes distributions over cavity messages on a supplied sparse constraint
graph and heuristically decimates variables using cluster statistics. It counts only if endpoints
compile the graph without source tables, survey zeros/nonzeros are exact for all restrictions,
and decimation replays occurrence-distinct signed sources. Surveying a materialized source CSP is
a solver control.

## Assumptions

1. The endpoint factor graph is compact, sparse, target-independent, and source-free.
2. The relevant ECDLP fibre satisfies a proved cluster/cavity model rather than random-SAT heuristics.
3. Survey updates, convergence, precision, decimation, backtracking, and failure satisfy caps.
4. Cluster surveys preserve signs, repeated occurrences, and exceptional elliptic strata.
5. Frozen graph/messages generalize unchanged from known-log rows to fresh scalar-blind masks.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Survey propagation consumes a sparse factor graph and is heuristic even on its native random-SAT
regime. The elliptic relation graph's exact factors and source labels are the missing object;
cluster surveys aggregate assignments and can report biased messages on empty or atypical fibres.
Making decimation exact under arbitrary restrictions restores the full source CSP or Query2P1.

## Proof track
