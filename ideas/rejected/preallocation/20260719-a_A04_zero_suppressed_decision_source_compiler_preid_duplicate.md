## Likely fatal obstruction

ZDD compression is output-sensitive to a supplied family and variable ordering. Compiling the accepting family requires the exact relation predicate or enumerated source sets; adversarial relation families have exponential diagrams, and target updates can rebuild the whole object. This merges with IDEAs 120/337/371/377/397 at the operation-plus-information-flow boundary.
