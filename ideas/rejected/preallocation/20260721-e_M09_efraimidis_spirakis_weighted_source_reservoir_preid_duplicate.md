## Mechanism-new operation

Efraimidis-Spirakis weighted reservoir sampling assigns random priorities to supplied weighted items in one pass. It counts only if the stream and exact weights are endpoint-derived and the reservoir gives exact all-restriction existence plus occurrence replay within charged error; applying the native method to a supplied graph, sequence, matrix, distribution, score array, or source catalogue is a duplicate/control.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Weighted sampling still begins with an explicit source stream and weights. Endpoint-derived weights cannot distinguish the unknown relation without computing completion information, and a bounded reservoir can miss a unique valid tuple. Certifying empty support requires exhaustive coverage or the missing exact predicate. This is a parameterized reservoir control rather than a new mathematical operation.
