## Falsifiable hypothesis

The endpoint relation admits a compact clause encoding for which Chaff's two-watched-literal scheme makes restricted propagation and source recovery sufficiently output-sensitive to collect full-rank relations and solve 100 blind masked targets below rho/BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation stores two watched literals per clause and revisits a clause only when a watched literal becomes false, moving the watch or propagating/conflicting. It counts only if clauses are endpoint-derived, watch movement preserves exact source provenance, and the full compiler/search cost—not merely propagation delay—beats the gates.

## Assumptions

1. A compact exact source-faithful endpoint CNF already exists.
2. Watch lists are constructed without explicit source incidence beyond the caps.
3. Propagation plus a frozen complete search policy is uniformly sub-rho.
4. Satisfying assignments decode all signs, occurrences, and exceptional strata.
5. Target-independent clause/watch state is reusable for fresh masks.

1. Freeze curve, `B=N^(1/5)`, factor base/decks, exceptional strata, CNF, watch initialization/movement, complete branch policy, restrictions, masks, and verifier.
2. Build target-independent clauses/watch lists within `B^(9/4+o(1))`; forbid explicit source products, target-fitted clauses, log labels, dense resultants, and Query2P1.
3. For known-log `R`, instantiate endpoint literals, charge every watched-list traversal and branch, decode actual `(A_i,epsilon_i)`, and verify the point sum.

6. Charge compilation, watch storage/moves, propagation, branching, restrictions, outputs, verification, densities, rank, factor solve, bit operations, and peak memory.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

With `B=N^(1/5)`, setup/state `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query/workspace `N^q,N^q_m`, rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, amplification `N^u`, and factor-log costs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, charge

`lambda=max(a,1/5+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,1/5)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,1/5+o,ell_m,u)`, `0<=r<=o`.

Require `lambda,mu<=0.45`, setup/state `<=B^(9/4+o(1))`, and fresh work/workspace `<=B^(5/4+o(1))`. Rho and BSGS remain exponent `0.50`; amortized propagation cannot hide compilation, branches, or memory.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Two watched literals change the constant/amortized cost of propagation on an explicit clause database. They do not construct the clauses, make propagation complete, reduce the source-variable domain, or replay occurrence labels. Charging the CNF compiler and branch tree restores the existing obstruction, so this is an implementation control explicitly excluded by the semantic dedup rule.

## Proof track

Prove an endpoint-only clause compiler and complete watch-driven search whose total construction, branching, replay, and descent—not just propagation—satisfy both exponent gates.

## Disproof track

Identify the first source-derived clause/watch entry, a case with cheap propagation but exponential branching, an undecodable model, or any total exponent at least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a supplied toy CNF comparing naive scans with verified two-watch propagation.
- Negative: identical watch counts with different model sources, empty/singleton fibres, hard branch trees, repeated occurrences, and blind targets.
- Baselines: direct clause scanning, DPLL/CDCL, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- All controls are toy/model-bound; a speedup is not promotion evidence.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only on four-size exactness, charged compiler/search/replay, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, resource caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one supplied clause catalogue, branch-tree cap breach, lost source, or complete exponent at least `0.50`.
- Propagator correctness and validator success are not breakthroughs.
