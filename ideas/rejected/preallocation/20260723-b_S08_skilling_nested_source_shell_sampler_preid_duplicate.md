## Falsifiable hypothesis

An endpoint-derived likelihood orders signed source candidates so that nested likelihood shells
shrink to exact elliptic-relation fibres after sub-rho constrained sampling. Retained live points
would replay occurrences densely enough for full factor logs and 100 blind descents with complete
time and memory exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

Nested sampling repeatedly removes the lowest-likelihood point from a supplied live population
and replaces it with a sample constrained above that likelihood, thereby estimating prior mass
and evidence. It counts only if endpoints supply likelihood and exact constrained sampling without
source membership, and terminal live points replay signed occurrences. Evidence estimation over
an explicit source catalogue is a control.

## Assumptions

1. A public likelihood has exact relation states in its highest shells with a proved gap.
2. Sampling within each hard likelihood constraint avoids source enumeration and target leakage.
3. Live-point count, shrinkage error, multimodality, restarts, and hit probability satisfy caps.
4. Retained states have an exact all-strata lift to signed factor-base occurrences.
5. The likelihood and constrained sampler are frozen and reusable for fresh masked targets.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Nested sampling's core primitive is sampling exactly within a likelihood superlevel set. If that
set isolates valid elliptic sources, the primitive is Query2P1 plus source generation under a new
name. If likelihood is approximate, shell mass/evidence does not identify occurrences, and rare
valid modes can be lost permanently when the live population misses them.

## Proof track

Prove an endpoint-only likelihood and exact constrained sampler, restriction-uniform shell gap,
subcap rare-mode coverage, all-strata replay, full rank/logs, blind descent, and sub-rho costs.

## Disproof track

Give equal evidence/shell masses with different sources, a source-bearing constraint, missed rare
mode, false high-likelihood state, occurrence loss, or complete exponent at least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a supplied finite likelihood with broad labelled terminal modes and exact constrained sampling.
- Negative: isolated rare modes, equal evidence/different support, false high-likelihood states, empty fibres, shuffled labels, exceptional strata, and blind targets.
- Baselines: slice sampling, Metropolis-Hastings, trace estimators, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- Accurate evidence or posterior mass is only toy/model-bound evidence.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with exact public likelihood/constrained-sampler/lift theorems, zero errors at four sizes/all strata, total miss probability at most `2^-80`, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, both caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one missed valid shell, false terminal state, source constraint, cap breach, or complete exponent `>=0.50`.
