## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **compile endpoint relations to an oriented-matroid chirotope, realize it by a pseudosphere arrangement, locate a target tope/covector, and lift its signed support to exact elliptic occurrences**. This is topological representation plus tope inversion, not ordinary linear matroid intersection.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Useful chirotope signs require the very source-incidence or scalar-oriented coordinates sought. Topological representation preserves covectors of that supplied chirotope, not original occurrence identities; a point-faithful ground set is the source deck. This meets IDEAs 082, 137, 212, 256, and 257.

## Proof track

Construct a bounded public chirotope from endpoints, prove exact restriction-tope equivalence and labelled lift on all strata, then certify full descent.

## Disproof track

Show one chirotope sign depends on hidden sources, produce identical covector data with permuted occurrences, or prove rank/ground-set state exceeds a gate.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied realizable toy oriented matroids with planted colourful circuits.
- Negative: reorientations, nonrealizable chirotopes, repeated elements, same covectors with relabelled ground set, empty restrictions, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 082/137/212/256/257, explicit determinant predicates, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only chirotope, exact all-strata return, `1,000` verified rows, `100` blind descents, caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-dependent sign, covector label collision, wrong restriction, cap violation, or exponent at least `0.50`.
- Correct pseudosphere representation of a supplied toy matroid is only a control.
