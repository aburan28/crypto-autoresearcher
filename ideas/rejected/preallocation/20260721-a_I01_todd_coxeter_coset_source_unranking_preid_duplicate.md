## Likely fatal obstruction

Todd–Coxeter propagates consequences of supplied relators. An endpoint presentation that distinguishes every restricted source must already encode compatibility and occurrence labels, while the resulting table can have one row per source orbit. Coincidences compress names after source information exists; they do not construct the missing `Query2P1` answers.

## Proof track

Derive a bounded-index endpoint presentation and restriction subgroups directly from the addition law, prove exact all-strata coset-to-source lift, and bound every table row and coincidence within the complete exponents.

## Disproof track

Trace every generator, relator, subgroup word, and table row to its origin; falsify if any requires a source edge, precomputed tuple, `Query2P1`, an index-sized table, or loses occurrence labels.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied finite presentations with known small-index subgroups and labelled coset representatives.
- Negative: presentations with large index, adversarial coincidences, duplicate source words, empty restrictions, and shuffled lifts.
- Baselines: Knuth–Bendix, explicit source graph traversal, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates
