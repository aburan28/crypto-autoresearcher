## Likely fatal obstruction

The method starts from supplied geometric objects and conflict predicates. Exact finite-field addition is not a low-dimensional Euclidean proximity problem; faithful conflict objects are source partial sums. A complete cutting with complete conflict lists can preserve a rare singleton, but constructing and retaining those lists restores the source incidence and full output cost. This merges with IDEAs `326/344/348/359/393` and B08.
