## Falsifiable hypothesis

The restricted endpoint relation admits a compact public algebra whose subdirectly irreducible quotients decide exact nonemptiness of every restriction; charged `O(log B)` dyadic bisection and singleton verification then recover one five-factor relation below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **construct an endpoint relation algebra, compute separating congruences, embed it subdirectly into irreducible quotients, solve compatible quotient coordinates, and lift their intersection to source occurrences**. This is distinct from Birkhoff–von Neumann convex decomposition: the new operation is universal-algebraic factorization by congruences.

## Assumptions

1. The carrier, operations, and congruence tests are built from endpoints without source incidence or Query2P1.
2. A subgate family of irreducible quotients separates every restricted source occurrence.
3. Coordinate compatibility and recombination fit the query and memory caps.
4. Quotient compatibility decides exact restricted existence; a canonical direct lift is optional because charged bisection and singleton verification are sufficient.
5. Algebra construction, congruence search, quotient tables, recombination, output, rank, logs, descent, verification, bit time, and memory are charged.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query work excluding output `N^q,N^q_m`, verified rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

With `0<=r<=o`, setup/state must be at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`; the total fresh online restriction/bisection sequence plus singleton verification must be at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`; and promotion needs `lambda<=0.45` and `mu<=0.45`. Here `q` charges every `O(log B)` positive-parent/negative-child query and singleton verification, while `o` charges final tuple or direct output. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Birkhoff's joint subdirect embedding is injective on the supplied algebra; the theorem is not defective by merging its elements. The missing work is an endpoint-only algebra/compiler, an effective compact family of irreducible factors, a solver for membership in the subdirect image, and an exact arbitrary-restriction oracle within the caps. Supplying those data materializes incidence or a completion oracle. Noncanonical point lift alone is not fatal because bisection would suffice. This meets IDEAs 122, 167, 184, 314, and 383 at the supplied-algebra-to-exact-predicate boundary.

## Proof track

Construct an endpoint-only compact algebra, prove a bounded quotient family decides exact restricted existence, recover labels by charged bisection and singleton verification, then certify the complete descent gates.

## Disproof track

Show one carrier operation or congruence test requires source incidence, exhibit equal retained quotient state for restrictions with different existence bits, or prove quotient state above the caps.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied finite algebras with known irreducible factors and uniquely labelled elements must decompose and recombine exactly.
- Negative: isomorphic quotients with different generator labels, congruence-colliding occurrences, signed restrictions, rare sources, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 122/167/184/314/383, explicit relation algebras, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only algebra construction, exact restriction-stable existence, charged `O(log B)` bisection and singleton verification, `1,000` independent rows, `100` blind descents, caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-bearing operation, one equal-quotient/different-existence collision, inexact restriction handling, cap violation, or either exponent at least `0.50`.
- Correct decomposition of a supplied toy algebra is only a control.
