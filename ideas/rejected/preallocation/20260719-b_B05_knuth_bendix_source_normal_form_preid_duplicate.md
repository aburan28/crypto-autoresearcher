## Likely fatal obstruction

The rewrite rules that distinguish an accepting source word already encode source incidence. With only group laws, all decompositions reduce to the same endpoint and provenance is erased; enriching rules restores the source catalogue, while completion may diverge or explode. This merges with IDEAs `084/120/154/189/371`.
