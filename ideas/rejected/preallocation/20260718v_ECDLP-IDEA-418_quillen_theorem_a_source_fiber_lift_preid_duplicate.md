## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query work excluding output `N^q,N^q_m`, verified rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

With `0<=r<=o`, setup/state must be at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`; the total fresh online restriction/bisection sequence plus singleton verification must be at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`; and promotion needs `lambda<=0.45` and `mu<=0.45`. Here `q` charges every `O(log B)` positive-parent/negative-child query and singleton verification, while `o` charges final tuple or direct output. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Building comma categories for the source functor enumerates or decides the hidden decompositions. Theorem A begins with that supplied functor and proves homotopy equivalence; it does not construct an endpoint-only exact restricted-existence predicate. For finite discrete fibers, the component data needed to make the predicate exact is already the source list. Lack of a canonical pointwise inverse alone is not fatal because bisection would suffice. This meets IDEAs 073, 172, 181, 314, and 402 at the homotopy-to-exact-predicate boundary.

## Proof track

## Disproof track

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied finite categories with an explicit terminal object and unique labelled lift must contract and replay exactly.
- Negative: contractible categories with automorphic objects, equivalent categories with relabelled objects, disconnected discrete fibers, restrictions, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 073/172/181/314/402, explicit comma categories, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates
