## Likely fatal obstruction

Constructing a finite complex with one distinguishable dual cell per occurrence materializes the source fibre. Stable duality preserves stable homotopy information and pairings, not cell labels; equivalent complexes can have the same dual data and different labelled occurrences. This meets IDEAs 181, 220, 251, 298, and 402.
