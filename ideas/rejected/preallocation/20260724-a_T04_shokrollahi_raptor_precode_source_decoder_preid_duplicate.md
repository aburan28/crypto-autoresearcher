## Falsifiable hypothesis

A target-independent outer precode transforms signed factor-base occurrences into intermediate
symbols from which endpoint-derived LT checks retain a stable ripple. Raptor decoding would recover
exact relation tuples with setup/state and online costs below the P1553 caps, then complete factor
logs and fresh blind descent.

## Mechanism-new operation

Raptor codes concatenate a supplied high-rate precode with supplied LT-style sparse output checks.
The ECDLP operation would require an endpoint-only precode/check compiler whose intermediate
symbols preserve exact occurrences and whose inactivation solve remains below the caps. Adding a
precode after a source graph exists changes representation and decoding schedule but does not
construct the graph or hidden sources.

## Assumptions

1. A compact scalar-blind precode maps occurrences without source-sized generator state.
2. Endpoint output checks name intermediate neighbors without enumerating relations.
3. Precode and LT decoding preserve signs, repeats, ordering, and exceptional strata.
4. Inactivation/dense residual solves, overhead, retries, and output are fully charged.
5. The frozen construction yields full-rank rows and exact fresh masked descents.

With `beta=1/5`, charge setup/state `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities
`N^delta,N^delta_t`, precode/check/decode/replay `N^q,N^q_m`, rank credit
`N^r`, output `N^o`, overhead/failure `N^u`, and factor logs
`N^ell,N^ell_m`. Use

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`, `0<=r<=o`. Require `lambda,mu<=0.45`,
setup/state `<=B^(9/4+o(1))`, and online work/workspace `<=B^(5/4+o(1))`.
Pollard rho expected time and BSGS time/memory remain exponent `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Both Raptor layers encode an already identified source word. A precode can redistribute erasures
but cannot create endpoint-to-occurrence semantics. If intermediate checks are source-free they
carry no relation witness; if conditioned on valid sources, their neighbor graph and precode state
already contain the missing solution. Inactivation may also restore dense source-scale work.

## Proof track

Prove a compact public precode/check compiler, exact all-strata unprecode, bounded inactivation,
full rank/logs, blind descent, and complete exponents below `0.45`.

## Disproof track

Expose source-bearing precode columns or check neighbors, equal encoded streams with different
fibres, dense residual dimension above the cap, or complete exponent at least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: standard supplied Raptor erasure instances with known intermediate symbols.
- Negative: source-free checks, equal-stream different-source fibres, ripple stalls, dense residuals, duplicate occurrences, empty restrictions, and fresh targets.
- Baselines: LT/cuckoo/Kahn peeling, IDEA-132, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- Linear-time native decoding remains toy/model-bound evidence for ECDLP.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with public precode/check theorems, zero errors at four sizes/all strata, miss probability at most `2^-80`, bounded residuals, full rank/logs, 100 blind descents, both caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on source-labelled construction, one inverse ambiguity, residual/cap breach, or exponent `>=0.50`.
