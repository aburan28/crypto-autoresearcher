## Falsifiable hypothesis

Form an implicit endpoint-incidence matrix, sample actual rows and columns to build a CUR skeleton, and use its interpretable source representatives to decide exact restricted target existence and replay a signed tuple. The skeleton, rank solve, and blind descent meet the frozen gates.

## Mechanism-new operation

The native operation approximates a supplied matrix by selected original columns/rows and a small linking matrix. It counts only if entries and leverage/sampling access are endpoint-derived and the approximation preserves every rare exact relation.

## Assumptions

1. The endpoint-incidence matrix has a uniform low-rank/skeleton theorem below `B^(9/4)`.
2. Entry/sampling access does not materialize the matrix or query hidden source existence.
3. Approximation error is zero on exact support, with source labels and all restrictions preserved.
4. Construction, sampling, pseudoinverse, failures, output, rank, logs, blind descent, and memory are charged.
5. No post-hoc successful rows/columns, source table, or approximate-only promotion is admitted.

## Complete factor-base-to-target-descent path

1. Freeze curve, signed decks, charts, matrix grammar, sampler, restrictions, and verifier.
2. Build target-independent CUR state within `B^(9/4+o(1))` without reading/materializing source incidence.
3. On known-log targets, decide exact restricted existence and replay/verify five source occurrences.
4. Collect `B` independent rows and solve factor-base logs, retaining misses/dependencies.
5. Reuse state on `Q+[t]P`, recover a tuple, remove `t`, and verify `[x]P=Q`.
6. Charge entry access, sampling, decompositions, negative queries, output, rank, logs, descent, bit precision, and memory.

## Likely fatal obstruction

CUR begins with a matrix and preserves it only approximately. The implicit exact-entry oracle is Query2P1/source incidence, and a singleton relation can lie outside sampled rows/columns while remaining below Frobenius error. This merges with IDEAs `231/324/341/347/373`.

## Proof track

Prove a special-family exact skeleton theorem with endpoint-only entry/sampling access, all-strata replay, and complete descent costs.

## Disproof track

Plant one rare nonzero outside the skeleton, expose a source-bearing entry/leverage oracle, or charge matrix access/precision above a cap.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a supplied exactly low-rank matrix whose planted nonzero lies in sampled representatives.
- Negative: low-norm singleton perturbations, same CUR approximation/different support, no-hit restrictions, charts, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs `231/324/341/347/373`, explicit incidence matrix, Query2P1, rho, BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with zero-error support preservation, `1,000` independent rows, `100` blind descents, and all caps/exponents.
- Falsify on one missed exact nonzero, supplied entry oracle, target-fitted sampling, or exponent `>=0.50`.
