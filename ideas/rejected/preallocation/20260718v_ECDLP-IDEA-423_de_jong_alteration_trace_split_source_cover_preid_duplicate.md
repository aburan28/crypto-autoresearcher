## Falsifiable hypothesis

A bounded-degree endpoint-derived alteration makes the restricted relation family semistable and splits its sheets into compact idempotents; trace/norm descent then yields an exact restriction-stable source return below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **replace the relation family by a proper generically finite semistable alteration, separate alteration sheets by idempotents, and descend a chosen sheet through trace/norm to labelled elliptic occurrences**. The proposed operation is alteration-mediated sheet splitting, not a same-field isogeny or parameter change.

## Assumptions

1. The alteration is constructed from endpoint equations without enumerating source sheets.
2. Its degree, charts, exceptional strata, and descent data satisfy the setup cap.
3. Sheet idempotents decide exact nonemptiness for every restriction and all strata.
4. Trace/norm descent preserves occurrence labels rather than only their symmetric aggregate.
5. Alteration construction, degree, charts, ramification, output, ambiguity, logs, descent, time, and memory are charged.

## Likely fatal obstruction

An alteration regularizes a supplied family but does not canonically label its hidden fibre. Constructing sheet idempotents encodes the finite source set; tracing them down aggregates sheets, while retaining enough descent data to reverse the trace restores source-sized state. This meets IDEAs 010, 087, 159, 216, and 408 at the cover-section boundary.

## Proof track

Give an endpoint-only bounded-degree alteration, prove exact restriction-stable sheet separation and label-preserving descent on every stratum, then certify full-path exponents.

## Disproof track

Prove alteration degree or chart state scales with the source fibre, find trace-equal differently labelled sheets, or show one restriction forces rebuilding or source enumeration.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied toy nodal families with planted labelled normalization sheets.
- Negative: trace-equal relabelled covers, ramified/repeated sheets, exceptional divisors, empty restrictions, and fresh targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 010/087/159/216/408, normalization, explicit covers, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with exact endpoint construction, bounded degree/charts, `1,000` verified rows, `100` blind descents, caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-bearing sheet idempotent, trace collision, missed stratum, rebuild, cap violation, or exponent at least `0.50`.
- A correct alteration or trace identity on supplied toy data is only a control.
