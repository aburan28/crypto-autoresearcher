## Likely fatal obstruction

KZ theory starts with marked points, representations, and a path. Those inputs are the proposed source tuple and its ancestry. The universal associator transports supplied bracketings but contains no elliptic occurrence dictionary; truncation loses exactness and exact coefficients/path branches exceed the charged interface. This meets IDEAs 069, 108, 178, 189, and 407.

## Proof track

## Disproof track
