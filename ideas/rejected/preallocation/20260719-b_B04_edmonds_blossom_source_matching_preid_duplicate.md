## Likely fatal obstruction

Blossom shrinking begins after compatibility edges are supplied. A natural `2+3` graph materializes a `B^3` side or equivalent edge oracle, and perfect matchability is not inherently five-way group equality. This merges with IDEAs `223/345/382/392/396` and pre-ID Dulmage–Mendelsohn `A02`.

## Proof track

Give a compact endpoint-only graph and prove relation iff matchability, restriction stability, occurrence replay, and complete sub-rho descent.

## Disproof track

Expand one edge to a source query, preserve matching while deleting the planted relation, or exhibit graph/search cost beyond the caps.
