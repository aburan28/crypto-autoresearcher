## Mechanism-new operation

The native minimum-rotation operation finds the lexicographically least cyclic shift of a supplied string. Booth's published shortcut has acknowledged counterexamples, so only a separately verified no-shortcut reference form is admissible as a positive control. The operation counts only if the serialization is endpoint-derived without listing sources, cyclic equivalence is relation-faithful, and the canonical word retains every occurrence needed for replay; applying it to a supplied graph, sequence, matrix, distribution, score array, or source catalogue is a duplicate/control.
