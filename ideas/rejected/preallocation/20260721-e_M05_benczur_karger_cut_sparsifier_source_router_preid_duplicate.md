## Falsifiable hypothesis

## Mechanism-new operation

Benczur-Karger sparsification samples edges of a supplied capacitated graph to preserve cut values approximately. It counts only if edges and strengths are endpoint-derived, all exact source-existence bits survive sampling, and retained cuts return occurrence-labelled witnesses; applying the native method to a supplied graph, sequence, matrix, distribution, score array, or source catalogue is a duplicate/control.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Sampling probabilities require the explicit compatibility graph and edge strengths, which are already source incidence. Multiplicative cut preservation is not an exact labelled-witness guarantee: two sparsifiers can preserve all cuts within tolerance while retaining different relation routes, and cut values do not identify a five-source tuple. Low-strength edges sampled with probability one do not repair that information-flow mismatch. Arbitrary restrictions can also invalidate strengths. This merges with existing graph-sparsification controls.
