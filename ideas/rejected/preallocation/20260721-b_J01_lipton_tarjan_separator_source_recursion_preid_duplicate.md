## Likely fatal obstruction

Lipton–Tarjan separates a graph only after its vertices and edges exist. The exact elliptic compatibility graph has source-labelled pair and middle transitions; constructing it restores `B^2` pair traffic and potentially `B^3` source traffic. Collapsing equal endpoints may make a smaller planar-looking quotient but loses repeated occurrence ancestry, and generic compatibility graphs need not be planar.

## Proof track

Give an endpoint-only planarization with bounded crossings and separator boundary, prove restriction stability and exact all-strata lifts, and bound construction plus every negative query inside the complete exponents.

## Disproof track
