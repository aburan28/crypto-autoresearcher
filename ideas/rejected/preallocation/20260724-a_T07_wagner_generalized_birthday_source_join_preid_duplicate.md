## Falsifiable hypothesis

Signed factor-base occurrences admit a public multilevel projection such that Wagner's merge tree
filters partial elliptic sums with near-random balanced buckets while preserving every exact
relation. The reduced lists would yield full-rank rows and blind descent with complete time and
memory exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

Wagner's generalized birthday algorithm merges explicit lists while imposing successively more
zero-coordinate constraints. It counts only if the endpoint supplies a scalar-blind, exact,
restriction-stable projection that reduces source traffic rather than an explicit large-prime
table or post-hoc bucket selector. Merely changing list count, bucket width, or merge schedule is a
parameter/solver substitution for the established birthday and explicit-list lanes.

## Assumptions

1. Partial elliptic sources expose independent public coordinates suitable for balanced staged filtering.
2. Filters are one-sided exact on signs, repeats, order, infinity, tangencies, and all strata.
3. List construction, bucket traffic, false collisions, replay, and output fit both caps.
4. Filtering preserves enough independent rows and has a proved target hit density.
5. The same projections and tables are target-independent and support 100 fresh blind descents.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `beta=1/5`, let setup/state be `N^a,N^a_m`; reciprocal densities
`N^delta,N^delta_t`; leaf/projection/merge/replay work `N^q,N^q_m`; rank credit
`N^r`; list/output `N^o`; false-collision/failure `N^u`; and factor logs
`N^ell,N^ell_m`. Charge
`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)` and
`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`, `0<=r<=o`. Require `lambda,mu<=0.45`,
setup/state `<=B^(9/4+o(1))`, and fresh work/workspace `<=B^(5/4+o(1))`.
Pollard rho expected time and BSGS time/memory remain exponent `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Wagner gains by materializing and filtering multiple random lists in coordinates compatible with
addition. Generic elliptic points do not expose enough independent exact linear coordinates.
Hashing coordinates gives false collisions requiring full point checks; source-preserving
backpointers and leaf lists restore the explicit large-list traffic prohibited by the prompt.

## Proof track

Prove an endpoint/public partial-sum projection with balanced stages, no false-negative exact
relations, compact replay, full rank/logs, blind descent, and complete sub-rho costs.

## Disproof track

Show projection collisions erase source semantics, a stage needs an explicit large list or
post-hoc selector, target buckets exceed caps, or complete exponent reaches `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied random xor-list instances satisfying Wagner's independence model.
- Negative: correlated elliptic lists, adversarial bucket imbalance, false coordinate collisions, duplicate occurrences, empty restrictions, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEA-057, Schroeppel–Shamir, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.
- A zero-sum collision or valid row remains toy/model-bound evidence.
