For elliptic source variables, exact target-aware factors either enumerate source incidence or elimination creates dense intermediate tables with width at least the meet/partial-sum state. OR/sum messages are aggregates; retaining arg witnesses under all restrictions restores the lost source catalogue. This merges with factorization, knowledge-compilation, and dense-elimination lanes.
