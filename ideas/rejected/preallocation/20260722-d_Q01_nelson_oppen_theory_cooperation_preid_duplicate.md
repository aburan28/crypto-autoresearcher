## Falsifiable hypothesis

For a generic prime-order curve, combine target-independent decision procedures for elliptic
addition, factor-base membership, signs, and subset restrictions by exchanging only public
equalities. The cooperation decides exact restricted five-source existence and returns a
signed occurrence for relations and 100 fresh masked targets with complete time and memory
exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

The native operation purifies a mixed formula, invokes decision procedures for disjoint
theories, and propagates shared-variable equalities until a fixed point. It counts here only
if the component procedures and exchanged equalities are constructed from public endpoints
without a source catalogue or decomposition oracle, and a positive result replays actual
signed factor-base occurrences. Combining already source-aware solvers is a control.

## Assumptions

1. The elliptic, finite-field, membership, sign, and restriction theories have complete component procedures inside the caps.
2. Purification introduces no source-index constants or scalar-bearing equalities.
3. Equality exchange is sound and complete on all exceptional addition strata.
4. A satisfying combination retains a charged inverse to signed occurrences.
5. Byte-identical target-independent state serves relation targets and fresh masked targets.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Nelson–Oppen assumes the component decision procedures. A complete factor-base-membership
or elliptic-source component already implements Query2P1 or enumerates the source fibre;
without it, equality exchange only combines incomplete consequences and cannot certify
empty restrictions or recover occurrences.

## Proof track

subcap equality traffic, and a restriction-stable signed inverse through rank-complete
relations and blind descent.

## Disproof track

arrangements, lost occurrence identity, restriction rebuild, or a complete exponent
