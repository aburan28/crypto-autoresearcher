## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query work excluding output `N^q,N^q_m`, verified rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

With `0<=r<=o`, setup/state must be at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`; the total fresh online restriction/bisection sequence plus singleton verification must be at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`; and promotion needs `lambda<=0.45` and `mu<=0.45`. Here `q` charges every `O(log B)` positive-parent/negative-child query and singleton verification, while `o` charges final tuple or direct output. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Abelian-network theorems assume the processors, transition functions, and message routing; when halting, the odometer/final state is unique. The missing operation is an endpoint-only compiler whose halting/output predicate is biconditional with exact elliptic relation existence under every restriction. Making it faithful materializes source incidence or a completion oracle in the processors/rotor stacks. Firing order/provenance is not independently fatal because bisection suffices after an exact predicate. This meets IDEAs 017, 230, 352, 364, and 376 at the supplied-network and exact-stabilization boundary.

## Proof track

Construct compact endpoint-only processors, prove exact restriction-stable existence by stabilization, recover labels by charged bisection and singleton verification, then certify the full descent gates.

## Disproof track

Show one processor transition is a source lookup, exhibit equal retained stable states for restrictions with different existence bits, or prove stabilization/update work above the caps.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied finite abelian networks with unique labelled firing histories must stabilize and replay exactly.
- Negative: equal retained stable states for restrictions with different existence bits, duplicated labels, nonhalting inputs, signed cancellations, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 017/230/352/364/376, explicit rotor stacks, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only processors, exact restriction-stable existence/stabilization biconditional, charged `O(log B)` bisection and singleton verification, `1,000` independent rows, `100` blind descents, caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one source-bearing transition, equal-state/different-existence collision, nontermination, cap violation, or either exponent at least `0.50`.
