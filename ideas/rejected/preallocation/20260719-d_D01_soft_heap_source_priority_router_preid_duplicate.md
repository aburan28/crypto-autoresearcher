## Falsifiable hypothesis

Assign target-independent public priorities to partial elliptic sums, insert them into a Chazelle soft heap, and tolerate a bounded fraction of raised keys while still preserving every rare relation-bearing item. Repeated delete-min and meld would then expose exact signed five-source tuples for rank-complete relations and fresh blind descent below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The native operation deliberately raises some keys so meldable-priority operations beat comparison-heap costs. It counts only if public endpoint keys can be inserted without source enumeration and either corruption is disjoint from every accepting occurrence or a charged extraction bound still reaches every accepting occurrence under all restrictions; ordinary scheduling of a supplied source stream is a control.

## Assumptions

1. A public priority separates all accepting partial sums from nonaccepting items on every signed and exceptional chart.
2. Key construction, insertion, meld, corruption bookkeeping, restrictions, misses, replay, rank, logs, descent, verification, bit time, and memory are charged.
3. Raised keys never delay the unique accepting occurrence beyond the charged extraction budget in an adversarial dyadic restriction.
4. One frozen heap family serves known-log calibration and fresh scalar-blind `Q+[t]P` targets without target-trained rebuilds.
5. No explicit tuple table, post-hoc selector, dense resultant, scalar labels, or uncharged source oracle is admitted.

## Complete factor-base-to-target-descent path

1. Freeze the curve, signed decks, exceptional charts, public priority, corruption rule, restrictions, and verifier before targets.
2. Construct target-independent heap state within `B^(9/4+o(1))` without source-product materialization.
3. On known-log targets, answer exact restricted existence and recover five labelled occurrences by charged bisection and singleton verification.
4. Preserve failures and dependencies, collect at least `B` independent verified rows, and solve factor-base logarithms.
5. Reuse unchanged state for fresh scalar-blind `Q+[t]P`, verify a tuple, substitute logs, remove `t`, and verify `[x]P=Q`.
6. Charge construction, every inserted/corrupted item, negative queries, replay, rank, logs, descent, verification, bit operations, and peak memory.

For explicit scalar semantics, write each recovered factor-base occurrence as `A_i=[alpha_i]P` with sign `epsilon_i in {+1,-1}`. A known-log relation target `R=[kappa]P` yields the checked row `sum_i epsilon_i alpha_i = kappa (mod N)`. Source replay uses at most `5 ceil(log_2 B)+O(1)` charged positive-parent/negative-child restriction queries plus singleton checks. For a fresh masked target `R=Q+[t]P=[x+t]P`, a verified tuple yields `x=sum_i epsilon_i log_P(A_i)-t (mod N)`; the final check is `[x]P=Q`. Every failed restriction branch and scalar verification is charged.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, let `a,a_m` charge endpoint-item/key construction, item count, error-rate-dependent heap state, insert/meld/corruption bookkeeping, and restriction copies; let `q,q_m` charge every target-dependent key, delete-min/extraction through raised items, negative query, `O(log B)` restricted bisection, and replay. Let `delta,delta_t` be reciprocal verified relation/target densities after corruption-aware extraction, `r` verified independent-rank credit, `o` output, `u` ordering ambiguity plus extractions/rebuilds, and `ell,ell_m` factor-log time/state. Use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`, with `0<=r<=o`.

Require setup/state `<=B^(9/4+o(1))`, total fresh key construction/extraction/restriction/bisection/replay/verification `<=B^(5/4+o(1))`, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`. Raised-item scans, target-dependent priorities, and restriction rebuilds are charged, not hidden in amortized heap operations.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Soft heaps accelerate operations only after items and comparable keys are supplied, so inserting relation-bearing partial sums is the missing source stream. Any nonzero corruption allowance can raise the unique accepting item and delay it beyond the charged extraction prefix in a restricted subproblem; scanning/auditing enough raised items or restoring their order requires exact membership/source state. Target-dependent priorities and rebuilding heaps after restrictions must also be charged. This merges with IDEAs `053/120/350/374` and pre-ID `C01/C02` rather than removing P1553 R4.

## Proof track

Construct keys endpoint-only and prove a corruption-invariance theorem that preserves every accepting restricted singleton, then close replay, rank, blind descent, and full costs.

## Disproof track

Expose one source-bearing inserted item, or construct two equal public key multisets for which an allowed corruption pattern delays the only source beyond the charged extraction budget in one instance.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a supplied ordered stream with planted redundant minima must survive the stated corruption budget and replay labels.
- Negative: a unique planted minimum raised beyond the charged extraction prefix, equal endpoint priorities with different sources, target-dependent keys, restriction rebuilds, absent targets, exceptional charts, and fresh blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs `053/120/350/374`, pre-ID `C01/C02`, a binary heap over explicit tuples, P1553 R4, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only target-independent item construction, a charged extraction bound despite accepting-item corruption on every restriction, exact all-strata replay, `1,000` independent rows, `100` blind descents, both caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one supplied source item, one accepting singleton delayed beyond the charged extraction budget, target-dependent keys/rebuilds, cap violation, or either complete exponent at least `0.50`.
