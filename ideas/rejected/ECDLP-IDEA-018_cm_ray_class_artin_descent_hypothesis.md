## Falsifiable hypothesis

For a frozen family of ordinary CM curves whose reductions contain prime-order subgroups
`<P>` of order `N≈p`, public points lift to ray-class torsion invariants so that `[x]P`
corresponds to an explicitly labelable Artin action on an elliptic unit. A ray-ideal factor
base, relation phase, and individual Artin descent recover `x` with total exponent below
`1/2`, including field degree, height, lift ambiguity, and memory.

## Mechanism-new operation

Lift marked CM torsion to a class-field invariant and convert scalar multiplication into
**ray-class Artin action**, then solve the Artin label through ideal relations. This is not
a same-field isogeny walk, global Mordell–Weil height compression, or scalar-orbit period.
The restriction to CM curves is explicit and cannot support a generic-curve claim.

## Assumptions

1. The CM order, reduction prime, subgroup, and deterministic marked-point lift are public.
2. Lifted invariants for `P,Q` are unambiguous or every branch is charged.
3. Scalar multiplication matches a computable ray-class/Artin action with a known decoding map.
4. Field degree, discriminant, unit height, ideal factoring, relations, and descent are charged.
5. No known toy logarithm or target-selected class invariant enters construction.
6. All evidence remains toy, special-family, heuristic, model-bound, and novelty-unverified.

## Complete factor-base-to-target-descent path

1. Freeze the CM order, lift, ray modulus, embeddings, and marked-point lift rule.
2. Construct and verify elliptic-unit/class invariants `u_P,u_Q` and the scalar-to-Artin law.
3. Build a small-prime ideal factor base and collect target-independent ray-class relations.
4. Solve its relation matrix and certify every class/unit dependency.
5. Individually descend the Artin action carrying `u_P` to `u_Q`, recovering all candidate scalar labels.
6. Reduce the candidates modulo `N` and verify `[x]P=Q` on the original curve.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Let reciprocal CM-family applicability be `N^zeta_CM`, CM discriminant/conductor and ray
modulus construction `N^c`, ray-field degree `N^phi`, coefficient/unit height `N^h`,
marked-lift branching `N^b`, and invariant evaluation `N^e`. For ideal factor base
`N^beta`, reciprocal smoothness density `N^delta`, per-attempt cost `N^q`, sparse-LA
cost `N^(omega_s*beta)`, individual descent `N^tau`, class-label ambiguity `N^u`, and
bit-memory `N^s`, rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` and BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time/memory.
The candidate has time exponent
`lambda=max(zeta_CM+c,phi,h,b,e,beta+delta+q,omega_s*beta,tau,u)` and memory exponent
`mu=max(s,beta+phi+h,2*beta)`, with every ideal/invariant and relation-matrix entry
charged by actual degree and height. Generic-curve
inapplicability is explicit and cannot be hidden in a favorable-family selector.

## Likely fatal obstruction

The ray field generated by `N`-torsion normally has degree and invariant height at least
on the order of `N` (often `N^2`), while selecting the lift corresponding to a finite-field
point may itself be the original DLP. The Artin label can simply re-encode `x` in a class
group of comparable hardness.

## Proof track

Give a deterministic marked lift and reciprocity formula, prove sub-square-root ray-field
and height bounds for a declared CM family, and prove complete ideal-factor-base descent
with `lambda,mu<1/2`.

## Disproof track

Show field degree/height or lift branching has exponent at least `1/2`, that Artin labeling
is DLP-equivalent, or that relation/descent costs reach rho after discriminant accounting.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive control: tiny CM ray class fields with exhaustively labeled torsion and Artin actions.
- Positive instrumentation control: oracle-labeled lifts, excluded from promotion.
- Negative control: matched non-CM ordinary curves.
- Isogeny control: same-field CM neighbors under the ledger's PDP metrics.
- Leakage control: blind point labels before constructing ray invariants.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

The first gate freezes small CM discriminants and prime `N<=31`, constructs published ray
invariants and Artin orbits, and blinds exhaustive scalar labels until the unlabeled orbit
partition is frozen. Promotion only to a scaling study requires exact reciprocity, no use
of point logarithms, nontrivial public coordinate correspondence on at least 90% of the
declared CM family, and upper 95% `phi<=0.35`, `h<=0.35`, `lambda<=0.45`, and
`mu<=0.45`. Falsify if lift selection uses the toy DLP, every public partition is
target-independent, field degree/height has lower 95% exponent `>=0.50`, or any accepted
scalar fails verification. Non-CM failure is outside the special-family scope.
