## Falsifiable hypothesis

Public elliptic addition correspondences compile to a bounded Coxeter word and Bott–Samelson object whose light-leaves or double-leaves basis canonically indexes every exact factor-base source subexpression of the endpoint. Reading those basis labels without enumerating source words yields complete relations, factor logarithms, and masked target descent below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The proposed operation is **elliptic endpoint-to-Bott–Samelson compilation followed by Soergel light-leaves source-basis extraction**. It is mechanism-new only if the endpoint produces a canonical finite word and basis whose leaves carry exact point identities without receiving a source-labelled expression. Computing light leaves for a supplied Coxeter word, changing reduced expressions, recoloring generators with factor-base points, or swapping categorical software is a control.

Semantic review found that light leaves index subexpressions of an already supplied Bott–Samelson word; they do not recover a word from its endpoint morphism. Uncolored Coxeter generators do not identify elliptic points, while coloring them with factor-base identities supplies the missing source deck. Thus the categorical basis reorganizes an input decomposition and merges with prior monoidal/source-word inverses. This version is merged/rejected only at that compiler boundary.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Pollard rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time; BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Let realization, word-compiler, and diagrammatic setup cost `N^a,N^a_m`; reciprocal relation and target densities be `N^delta,N^delta_t`; one complete leaves query cost `N^q,N^q_m`; leaf/source output and target ambiguity be `N^o,N^u`; and factor-log linear algebra cost `N^ell,N^ell_m`. The complete exponents are

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

Every word, generator color, subexpression, braid branch, diagram, basis element, source tuple, failed endpoint, and target ambiguity candidate is charged.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Light leaves are a basis for morphisms between Bott–Samelson objects indexed by a chosen expression. The expression is source-side data: the endpoint does not canonically reveal which generators or subexpressions produced it. Abstract generator labels discard elliptic point identities; adding one color per factor-base point and all compatible words recreates the `B^m` source deck. Braid invariance can relate supplied words but cannot manufacture the missing one.

## Proof track

Construct a bounded target-uniform endpoint-to-word functor, prove its point-colored light-leaves basis is source-biconditional on all elliptic strata without source advice, and derive complete rank and blind-descent exponents `lambda,mu<=0.45`.

## Disproof track

Find distinct source words inducing the same endpoint object, show generator coloring requires source-point labels or `B^m` words, exhibit basis dependence that loses a source, or prove either complete exponent at least `0.5`.
