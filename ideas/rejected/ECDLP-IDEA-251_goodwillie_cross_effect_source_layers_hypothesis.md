But the cubical functor values require relation objects for many source subsets, and a source-faithful generator basis is precisely the missing relation deck; stable layers also forget point labels. ## Likely fatal obstruction

A cross-effect is computed from the functor on a cube of supplied inputs.  Making those values source-faithful requires the subset relation objects the proposal seeks to recover.  Abstract group addition is degree one, so its higher cross-effects vanish; a nonlinear coordinate lift can create a fifth layer only by restoring unbounded source state, and homotopy equivalence forgets exact finite-field labels.
