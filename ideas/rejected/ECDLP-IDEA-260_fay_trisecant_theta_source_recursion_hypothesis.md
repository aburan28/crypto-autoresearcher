## Likely fatal obstruction

Fay identities relate theta values after the curve, Jacobian data, Abel arguments, and marked points are supplied; they do not choose the unknown points or invert the Abel map.  Many distinct five-point divisors share one endpoint class.  A recursion over possible marked points branches over `B`, restoring the `B^5` source tree, while a specialization that names the correct points is source advice.  Compact class equality therefore survives after source ancestry has been forgotten.
