## Falsifiable hypothesis

A public endpoint comparison functor is monadic on the five-source relation category, and its Eilenberg–Moore algebra admits a compact, source-blind inverse that returns exact signed factor points for relation collection and fresh-target descent in complete time and memory exponents at most `0.45`.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **form the endpoint monad from a public adjunction, prove Barr–Beck monadicity, and reconstruct an exact source tuple as an algebra over that monad**. This is not a solver substitution: the proposed new object is a comparison equivalence between endpoint data and source-bearing descent algebras. The operation name is absent from the canonical corpus, but the comparison functor, coequalizer diagram, and algebra action must retain the source object or its full descent datum. It therefore merges semantically with IDEAs 088, 127, 176, 181, and 251.

## Assumptions

1. A target-independent source category, endpoint category, adjunction, and comparison functor are defined over the public finite-field curve without enumerating source tuples.
2. The comparison functor satisfies the required conservative and split-coequalizer hypotheses on every reduced, repeated, and signed source stratum.
3. One compact Eilenberg–Moore algebra canonically determines exact factor points, not only an isomorphism class or aggregate endpoint.
4. Construction, coequalizers, algebra actions, source output, relation density, rank, factor logs, descent, verification, and peak memory are charged.
5. The identical comparison and inverse apply to fresh masked targets without target-trained descent data.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

With setup time and memory `N^a,N^a_m`, factor-base size `N^beta`, reciprocal relation and target densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, one monad/algebra/source inverse `N^q,N^q_m`, independent-rank gain `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor-log completion `N^ell,N^ell_m`,

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

`q` includes construction of all coequalizers and algebra actions; `o` charges exact point-labelled outputs. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `1/2` and negligible memory; BSGS has time and memory exponents `1/2`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Barr–Beck reconstructs an object only after the adjunction, comparison functor, and compatible algebra/descent data are supplied. In this setting, a point-faithful algebra action or split coequalizer diagram contains the hidden source object; retaining only endpoint data leaves many nonisomorphic source tuples in one fiber. Building the complete Eilenberg–Moore object therefore materializes the source algebra or loses exact point labels.

## Proof track

Prove a public compact adjunction, all-strata monadicity, a source-free algebra constructor, a canonical exact point inverse, sufficient independent relation density, reusable factor logs, blind target replay, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.

## Disproof track

Exhibit two source objects with the same endpoint algebra, show that a comparison morphism or split coequalizer encodes a source-labelled incidence, or prove that categorical state/output or either complete exponent is at least `0.50`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a finite supplied monadic adjunction with known Eilenberg–Moore algebras must reconstruct its labelled toy objects.
- Negative: relabelled source categories with identical endpoint monads must not yield preferred elliptic points.
- Baselines: IDEAs 088/127/176/181/251, P1434, P1478, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only after an independent all-strata source biconditional, 1,000 verified rows and 100 blind descents at each large future toy size, and both complete exponents at most `0.45`.
- Falsify this arm if the monad or algebra needs source-labelled objects, if one endpoint admits indistinguishable source algebras, or if state/output or either exponent reaches `0.50`.
- Exponents in `(0.45,0.50)` are inconclusive and non-promoting.
