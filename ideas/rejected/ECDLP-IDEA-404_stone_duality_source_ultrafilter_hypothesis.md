## Falsifiable hypothesis

The Boolean algebra generated by endpoint-computable exact restricted-existence predicates has a compact Stone dual whose points canonically correspond to occurrence-labelled factor tuples, so clopen bisection recovers relations and blind targets below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **close exact endpoint predicates under Boolean operations, pass to the Stone space of ultrafilters, locate a target-compatible ultrafilter by clopen restrictions, and decode its point to factor occurrences**. The proposed information flow is algebra-to-zero-dimensional-space duality, not a post-hoc selector or explicit source table.

## Assumptions

1. A subgate public Boolean algebra supports exact membership for every signed deck restriction.
2. Its Stone space separates all valid source occurrences without one generator or atom per tuple.
3. Target compatibility determines a canonical ultrafilter and not merely a nonempty clopen set.
4. Clopen restriction updates and point decoding reuse target-independent state within the caps.
5. Predicate construction, Boolean closure, ultrafilter location, decode, output, rank, logs, descent, verification, bit time, and memory are charged.

For `B=N^beta`, `beta=1/5`, setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, query work excluding output `N^q,N^q_m`, verified rank credit `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor logs `N^ell,N^ell_m`, use

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

With `0<=r<=o`, setup/state must be at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`, a complete fresh restricted query at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`, and promotion needs `lambda<=0.45` and `mu<=0.45`. Pollard rho has expected time exponent `0.50`; BSGS has time and memory exponents `0.50`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Stone duality represents a Boolean algebra after its exact predicates are supplied. Computing the target/restriction predicate is already Query2P1. An algebra separating every labelled source has source-many atoms or source-distinct clopens; a smaller algebra merges tuples, and an ultrafilter identifies only the retained truth profile. This meets IDEAs 120, 125, 138, 156, 168, and 195 at the exact-predicate and selector-circuit boundary.

## Proof track

Construct a compact endpoint-only exact predicate algebra, prove its Stone points are source-biconditional under arbitrary restrictions, give a canonical occurrence decoder, and certify `lambda,mu<=0.45`.

## Disproof track

Show one generator is Query2P1 or source incidence, exhibit equal ultrafilter profiles with different sources, or prove algebra/Stone state/output above the caps.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied finite Boolean algebras with labelled atoms must reproduce ultrafilters and clopen bisection.
- Negative: quotient algebras merging atoms, relabelled atoms with fixed truth tables, missing negative predicates, all signed strata, restrictions, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 120/125/138/156/168/195, explicit source truth tables, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with endpoint-only compact exact predicates, source-biconditional Stone points, `1,000` independent rows, `100` blind descents, caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one Query2P1/source predicate, equal-profile/different-source collision, missing negative restriction, cap violation, or either exponent at least `0.50`.
- A correct toy Stone representation is only a control.
