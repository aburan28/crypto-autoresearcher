## Falsifiable hypothesis

The public coordinate-addition graph admits a bounded-rank coherent configuration whose target-based Terwilliger algebra decomposes the five-source relation fiber into thin subconstituent modules, with primitive source idempotents that invert biconditionally to exact factor-base tuples below rho. The hypothesis fails if coherent closure has rank comparable to the pair state space, if irreducible modules are not thin, or if idempotents aggregate source orbits.

## Mechanism-new operation

The proposed operation is **target-pointed Terwilliger decomposition with exact source idempotents**. Starting from public addition relations and a distinguished target, coherent closure would supply adjacency classes; the Bose-Mesner and dual target idempotents would generate a noncommutative algebra; thin irreducible modules would then expose one-dimensional coordinates for source tuples.

This is mechanism-new only if thinness and source inversion are proved from elliptic addition, not assumed from a Hamming, distance-regular, or group association-scheme control. Ordinary low rank, block diagonalization, additive-character projection, a fixed tensor basis, or source labels attached after enumeration are duplicates. The preflight rejects the generic claim because the frozen coordinate-addition matrices have full sampled rank and no theorem makes their coherent classes or Terwilliger modules thin and source-biconditional.

## Assumptions

1. `E/F_p` contains a public prime-order subgroup `<P>` of order `N=p^(1+o(1))`, with target `Q=[x]P` and factor base `F` of size `B=N^beta`.
2. A finite colored directed graph encodes complete projective addition, signs, infinity, repeated points, and factor-base membership without knowing source tuples.
3. Weisfeiler-Leman/coherent closure has `N^rho_c` classes and is constructible in time and memory below `N^1/2`.
4. The target-pointed Terwilliger algebra has thin modules whose primitive idempotents correspond one-to-one with exact signed source tuples, including multiplicity.
5. The idempotents and their source inverse are computable without a pair-state matrix, explicit tuple table, or post-hoc labels.
6. Closure, structure constants, module decomposition, source output, relation collection, factor logs, blind descent, verification, and peak memory are charged.

## Complete factor-base-to-target-descent path

1. Freeze `E,P,N,Q,F,B,beta`, the colored projective addition graph, target-pointing convention, closure algorithm, and exceptional-fiber policy.
2. Construct coherent closure and all structure constants from public graph predicates without materializing the full pair or tuple state space.
3. Build the target-pointed Terwilliger algebra, decompose its standard module, prove thinness, and map primitive idempotents to every exact signed factor-base tuple.
4. Independently verify every decoded tuple by direct elliptic addition and record orbit collisions, multiplicities, and missed sources.
5. Apply the same target-pointed decomposition to known multiples until `B+sigma` verified rows have rank `B`, charging all closures and outputs.
6. Solve and independently verify the factor-base logarithms.
7. Point the identical construction at blind `Q+[t]P`, decode tuples, substitute factor logs, subtract `t`, and retain all candidates.
8. Accept only `[x]P=Q` and serialize complete closure, module, output, rank, time, and memory receipts.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Pollard rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time with constant-state memory; BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Let `B=N^beta`; coherent closure construction time/memory be `N^a,N^a_m`; class/structure-constant and Terwilliger-module payload be `N^c,N^c_m`; reciprocal relation and target densities be `N^delta,N^delta_t`; idempotent construction, source inversion, and verification per query be `N^k`; source and target ambiguity exponents be `o,u`; and factor-log linear-algebra time/memory be `N^ell,N^ell_m`. Then

`lambda=max(a,c,beta+delta+k+o,ell,delta_t+k+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,c_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

The accounting includes adjacency input, coherent refinement, all structure constants, module bases, eigenspaces, failed targets, source tuples, factor rows, blind descent, and coefficient bit lengths. A polynomial algorithm in an explicit `N`-vertex or `B^2`-state graph is above rho when its charged exponent is at least `1/2`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Terwilliger thinness is exceptional structure, not a consequence of having an abelian group law. Adding sparse factor-base colors and directed rational addition usually destroys distance regularity and proliferates coherent classes. Even when a group association scheme has thin modules, primitive idempotents separate representation types or orbits, not the ordered source tuples inside an orbit. Refining until tuples are separated restores `B^2`, `B^3`, or larger state. The ledger's full-rank value, character, and phase matrices are direct warnings that the proposed bounded module payload is absent in the tested generic setting.

## Proof track

Historic survival would require an exact bounded-rank coherent-configuration theorem for generic prime-field elliptic addition, target-pointed thinness, a primitive-idempotent/source biconditional, and `lambda,mu<=0.45` through blind descent. No such theorem is present, and the frozen full-rank negatives contradict the naive coordinate and character realizations, so the declared generic mechanism is rejected.

## Disproof track

Show coherent rank or algebra dimension is `Omega(B^2)`; exhibit an irreducible module with endpoint multiplicity greater than one; give two distinct source tuples with the same pointed idempotent coordinates; show source-separating refinement materializes the pair/tuple state; or obtain `lambda>=1/2` or `mu>=1/2`. Any one condition closes the mechanism.

## Positive and negative controls

- Frozen transposed-value, additive-character, and full-phase matrices from the ledger as matched spectral negatives.
- Random colored directed graphs matched for vertex and color counts.
- Blind known-log targets with independent tuple/scalar verification and matched rho/BSGS accounting.
