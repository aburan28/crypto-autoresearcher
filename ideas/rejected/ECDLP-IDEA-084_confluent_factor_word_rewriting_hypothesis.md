## Falsifiable hypothesis

A target-independent finite set of local, source-labelled elliptic addition rules forms a terminating confluent rewriting system on words over a factor-base alphabet. Every subgroup point has a canonical normal form of length `N^(rho_w+o(1))`, `rho_w<1/2`, and rewrite ancestry gives an exact factor-base expression. Rule completion, normal-form evaluation, relation calibration, blind target reduction, output, and memory are all sub-rho.

## Mechanism-new operation

The operation is **finite confluent completion of local factor-word relations with ancestry-preserving canonical reduction**. A scalar-indexed dictionary, generic automaton, elliptic recurrence, fixed-curve lookup compiler, or post-hoc rewriting of already found relations is a control. Survival requires a bounded public rule basis and proof of confluence/short normal forms without DLP labels.

## Assumptions

1. Alphabet symbols are public factor-base points and signs.
2. Every rule is an independently verified elliptic equality with retained ancestry.
3. A public reduction order proves termination and confluence on the subgroup.
4. Rule completion and the complete rule set stay sub-rho.
5. Normal forms retain enough orientation to recover exact point sources and target scalars.
6. Completion pairs, rejected rewrites, normal-form length, rank, descent, and memory are charged.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Rho time and BSGS time/memory exponents are `1/2`. Let rule-set/completion exponent be `c`, normal-form length `rho_w`, per-rewrite/search exponent `k`, factor-base exponent `beta`, relation/target densities `delta,delta_t`, output `o`, linear algebra `ell`, and memory `mu`. Then `lambda=max(c,beta+delta+rho_w+k+o,ell,delta_t+rho_w+k+o)`. Peak rule storage is not amortized; an `N`-state rule dictionary has exponent `1`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Knuth-Bendix completion closes only the congruence generated by its seed relations; it cannot create missing elliptic relations. A complete factor-word presentation that preserves subgroup orientation must already contain the full Cayley/orientation information or grow to `N`-scale. Short normal forms can still have exponential ancestry, reproducing the fixed-curve compiler and automaton lanes `006/011/027/067/070` rather than removing their obstruction.

## Proof track

Construct a scalar-blind finite rule basis, prove completion/termination/confluence and short ancestry, then bound all relation and blind-descent costs below rho.

## Disproof track

Show critical-pair completion/rule count/ancestry exponent at least `1/2`, find irreducible distinct words for the same point, or show confluence order requires factor logs.

## Positive and negative controls

- Finite groups with known confluent presentations.
- Planted short rewriting systems.
- Random local elliptic rule sets.
- Elliptic-net/automaton and fixed-curve compiler controls.
- Complete tiny-group confluence checks.
- Blind masked targets with full rewrite traces.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

The theorem gate requires complete termination/confluence, scalar-blind rule construction, and symbolic sub-sqrt rule/ancestry bounds. Future promotion requires zero equality/source errors, 100 blind descents, and upper 95% `c,rho_w,lambda,mu<=0.45`. Falsify if lower 95% completion/ancestry exponent is at least `0.50` or confluence requires scalar labels.
