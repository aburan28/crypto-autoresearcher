## Falsifiable hypothesis

Elliptic addition correspondences and factor-base restrictions form a locally finite 2-Segal decomposition space. Its incidence coalgebra and weak antipode canonically invert a public endpoint into all atomic factor-base decompositions without traversing the decomposition fiber, enabling complete factor logarithms and masked unknown-target descent below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The proposed operation is **2-Segal/decomposition-space incidence convolution followed by antipode or Möbius source extraction**. It is mechanism-new only if the endpoint simplex has a compact, source-blind incidence representation and antipode evaluation emits exact signed point atoms without opening all flags. A supplied decomposition space with enumerated simplices, a different incidence-algebra evaluator, or cancellation of aggregate counts is a control.

Semantic review found that the antipode is an alternating sum over nondegenerate decompositions of the endpoint. Local finiteness makes the sum defined but does not construct its terms. Keeping the result aggregated loses source identities; opening the terms recreates the same target-fiber/source-edge deck already recorded. The version is therefore merged/rejected at the endpoint-to-decomposition interface, not as a criticism of decomposition-space theory.

## Complete factor-base-to-target-descent path

1. Freeze `E,P,N,Q,F,B=N^beta`, the elliptic addition correspondence, simplicial groupoids, incidence basis, arity, masks, and an independent verifier.
2. Map every known-log endpoint `R_j=[r_j]P` to the same scalar-blind endpoint simplex without using `r_j` or a source decomposition.
3. Evaluate the frozen incidence antipode and emit all exact signed factor-base atomic decompositions with multiplicity.
4. Verify point membership and elliptic sums; preserve cancellations, automorphisms, repeats, infinity, misses, false decompositions, and every emitted flag.
5. Collect `B+sigma` independently verified rows of rank `B`, solve factor-base logs, and verify every log by scalar multiplication.
6. Apply the identical endpoint map and antipode to fresh masked targets `Q+[t]P`.
7. Substitute verified factor logs, remove `t`, retain every ambiguity candidate, and accept only `x` satisfying `[x]P=Q`.
8. Charge simplicial construction, face maps, flag traversal, convolution, cancellation bookkeeping, all output, rank, linear algebra, descent, verification, time, and peak bit memory.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Pollard rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time; BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Let decomposition-space and incidence setup cost `N^a,N^a_m`; reciprocal relation and target densities be `N^delta,N^delta_t`; one antipode query cost `N^q,N^q_m`; emitted flags/tuples and target ambiguity be `N^o,N^u`; and factor-log linear algebra cost `N^ell,N^ell_m`. The complete exponents are

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

All face maps, flags, automorphism weights, cancellations, source outputs, failed queries, and target branches are charged; a symbolic antipode sign is not a unit-cost source oracle.

## Likely fatal obstruction

The antipode value is defined by summing over exactly the decomposition flags that encode the target fiber. If evaluated only in an aggregate incidence quotient, different exact point sources cancel or merge and cannot yield factor-base rows. If lifted to a basis fine enough to identify the sources, building or traversing that basis opens the original decomposition deck, with source-distinct size comparable to `B^m`. Möbius signs reorganize those terms but do not generate them from the endpoint.

## Proof track

Construct a finite target-uniform 2-Segal object from public elliptic data, prove an exact source-biconditional for its antipode basis on all strata, prove subenumerative evaluation and bounded output, and derive `lambda,mu<=0.45` through rank and blind descent.

## Disproof track

Produce two endpoint fibers merged by the incidence basis, reduce antipode evaluation to enumeration of nondegenerate flags, show cancellation destroys an exact source, expose source-indexed basis size `B^m`, or prove either complete exponent at least `0.5`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: finite posets and locally finite decomposition spaces with explicitly supplied decomposition flags and known Möbius values.
- Positive: exhaustive toy elliptic addition groupoids whose source fibers are deliberately enumerated.
- Negative: incidence bases quotiented to endpoint cardinalities, where distinct same-sum point tuples must remain indistinguishable.
- Negative: explicit flag decks, solver substitutions, post-hoc selectors, dense resultants, rho, BSGS, known-log leakage, and blind-target controls.
