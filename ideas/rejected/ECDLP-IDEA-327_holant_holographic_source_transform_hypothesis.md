## Likely fatal obstruction

Holant algorithms contract a supplied signature network and preserve an aggregate. The local elliptic transition tensors or neighbor iterators are the missing source object. An invertible basis does not convert the partition function into a preferred witness, and tags that prevent cancellation or permit replay materialize source provenance.
