## Likely fatal obstruction

A Chow–Lam form is a global equation for when a supplied projection meets a supplied
variety.  For five finite row decks, the relevant cycle degree counts the transversal
incidences the form claims to compress; its coefficient construction can therefore
encode the `B^5` deck.  Vanishing gives existence or a branch locus, while a polar gives
aggregate contact data and can have multiple contacts.  Recovering the five finite row
labels requires factoring or resolving precisely the source fibre omitted from the
compact equation.

## Proof track

Construct the form endpoint-only, prove a degree/coefficient bound inside the promotion
rectangle, and give a canonical all-strata polar inverse to exact signed rows with full
rank, blind descent, and complete exponents at most `0.45`.

## Disproof track

Show that form degree or coefficient traffic is `Omega(B^3)` or source-fibre sized,
exhibit two transversals sharing the same contact data, or prove that contact resolution
or either complete exponent is at least `0.50`.
