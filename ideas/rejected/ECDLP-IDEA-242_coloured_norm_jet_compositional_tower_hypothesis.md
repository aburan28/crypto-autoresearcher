Its blockwise constructor still starts with `B^5` leaves; sequential elimination returns a dense resultant, a balanced cut restores `B^3` state, and any nontrivial descended relation creates whole-fiber zeros whose complete first jet vanishes.  Lattes, ECFFT, power/Dickson, and extension-field controls supply no generic-prime compact deck.  The only residual finite-state-closure gate is already routed jointly to IDEA-195 and IDEA-102.
