## Falsifiable hypothesis

Signed factor points and endpoint fibers admit a compact target-uniform Tambara-functor encoding in which restrictions, additive transfers, and multiplicative norms obey distributivity strongly enough that Tambara reciprocity can invert an endpoint aggregate to its exact factor tuple, yielding reusable relations and blind target descent below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **compile factor-point data into a Tambara functor, apply multiplicative norms and additive transfers across finite `G`-sets, and invert the resulting norm-transfer reciprocity expression to exact signed source points**. This is not merely a same-field isogeny: it proposes a new equivariant multiplicative/additive calculus. But Tambara operations consume supplied equivariant elements and return orbit-symmetric sums and products. A point-faithful `G`-set, mark table, or orbit label names the source deck rather than discovering it, so the operation merges with IDEAs 072, 100, 127, 166, and 179.

## Assumptions

1. There is a public finite `G`-set and Tambara functor whose elements compactly encode all signed factor points without one orbit label per source atom.
2. Restriction, transfer, norm, and distributivity are biconditional with elliptic endpoint addition on every source stratum.
3. Tambara marks or reciprocity coefficients canonically invert orbit aggregates to exact factor points without factoring a source-sized polynomial or enumerating the fiber.
4. Functor construction, orbit/mark state, operations, inverse output, relation rank, factor logs, descent, verification, time, and peak memory are charged.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

With setup `N^a,N^a_m`, factor base `N^beta`, reciprocal densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, one norm-transfer/inverse `N^q,N^q_m`, rank gain `N^r`, output `N^o`, ambiguity `N^u`, and factor-log completion `N^ell,N^ell_m`,

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

`q` includes equivariant compilation, all restriction/transfer/norm evaluations, exact orbit inverse, and verification; `o` includes every orbit lift and exact factor tuple. Rho has expected time exponent `1/2` and negligible memory; BSGS has time and memory exponents `1/2`.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Tambara norms and transfers aggregate data already attached to a finite `G`-set; they do not recover the elements that generated a supplied norm or transfer value. Orbit symmetrization erases point labels, while a point-separating mark table, free `G`-set, or one orbit per factor point is an explicit source deck. For prime cyclic elliptic groups there is also no canonical nontrivial equivariant action that preserves scalar orientation. The norm-transfer calculus therefore reorganizes supplied source data instead of inverting the endpoint fiber.

## Proof track

Construct a compact target-uniform Tambara functor and prove an all-strata biconditional between its norm-transfer aggregates and exact signed elliptic sources, including a canonical point inverse; then prove independent relation rank, factor logs, blind descent, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.

## Disproof track

Exhibit distinct factor multisets with identical restrictions, transfers, norms, and accessible marks, or prove every point-separating `G`-set/mark refinement has source-sized rank or output; also test whether scalar automorphisms leave norm data invariant while changing the exact source labels.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: a supplied finite Tambara functor with labelled `G`-orbits must reproduce known restriction, transfer, norm, and reciprocity values.
- Negative: orbit-permuted factor tuples with identical Tambara aggregates must not be accepted as exact point recovery.
- Baselines: explicit orbit enumeration, a same-field transfer with no source inverse, IDEAs 072/100/127/166/179, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with an independent all-strata source biconditional, 1,000 verified relation rows and 100 blind descents per large size, and both full exponents at most `0.45`.
- Falsify if factor-point labels are supplied through the `G`-set or marks, distinct sources share all accessible Tambara data, any source stratum fails, or state/output reaches exponent `0.50`.
- Exponents in `(0.45,0.50)` are inconclusive and non-promoting.
