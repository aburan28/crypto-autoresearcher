## Falsifiable hypothesis

An endpoint-derived filter, overlapping cover, and exact clustering rule produce a compact Mapper nerve whose restriction-stable cells retain enough provenance to lift one cell path to an exact occurrence-labelled five-deck relation and complete blind descent below the frozen gates.

## Mechanism-new operation

The screened operation is **pull an overlapping cover back through an elliptic endpoint filter, cluster each pullback, form the Mapper nerve, traverse a compact quotient cell, and lift it canonically to factor occurrences**. It is distinct from generic topology or clustering only if the pullback components can be represented without sampling or enumerating the relation fibre.

4. Restrictions commute with the filter, clustering, nerve, and lift without target-specific rebuilding.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Mapper summarizes a supplied point cloud. Here the missing objects are precisely the rare point-labelled relation tuples, so constructing pullback clusters requires the source generator the method is meant to replace. A nerve retains intersection topology, not the identities of all samples witnessing each simplex; storing those identities restores explicit source incidence. Different labelled fibres can therefore have the same Mapper quotient. This merges with IDEAs 073, 218, 232, 233, and 384 unless an endpoint-only exact component oracle and provenance-preserving lift are proved.

## Proof track

Give an endpoint-only implicit pullback representation, prove that its nerve has a source biconditional and canonical occurrence lift under every restriction, and derive complete `lambda,mu<=0.45` exponents.

## Disproof track

Construct equal-nerve fibres with different source labels, show that one cluster edge requires a sampled tuple, or prove that exact pullback-component enumeration exceeds the setup/query caps.

## Positive and negative controls

- Positive: supplied labelled point clouds with planted components must reproduce their independently computed Mapper nerve and sample labels.
- Negative: label permutations with the same nerve, cover refinements, clustering perturbations, empty/spurious cells, all strata, arbitrary restrictions, and blind targets.
- Baselines: IDEAs 073/218/232/233/384, explicit source clouds, persistence/nerve summaries, Query2P1, rho, and BSGS.

## Quantitative promotion and falsification gates

- Promote only with an endpoint-only exact pullback oracle, provenance-preserving nerve lift, `1,000` independent rows, `100` blind descents, frozen state/query caps, and `lambda,mu<=0.45`.
- Falsify on one required sampled source, one equal-nerve/different-source collision, one missing/spurious tuple, a restriction rebuild above cap, or either exponent at least `0.50`.
- A correct toy nerve or clustering result is only a control.
