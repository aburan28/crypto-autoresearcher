## Falsifiable hypothesis

A colored operad generated by elliptic addition and factor-base membership has a quadratic PBW presentation and is Koszul. Its bar-cobar transform sends a target operation to a bounded family of primitive cooperations that can be inverted canonically to every exact signed factor-base leaf, enabling complete relations, factor logs, and masked target descent below rho and BSGS.

## Mechanism-new operation

The operation is **Koszul/PBW compression of the elliptic source-tree operad followed by primitive-cooperation inversion to leaves**. The obstruction removed would be the exponential parenthesized source-tree family: bounded primitives would expose leaves without enumerating trees. A bar complex built after source circuits are supplied, a generic circuit backend, or a relation-only homology class is a control; the exact primitive/leaf inverse is essential.

Independent review found no pre-source endpoint compiler, so the bar-cobar/Koszul step
currently acts only after the source tree or relation circuit exists. It therefore merges
with the supplied-tree/circuit and relation-certificate lanes IDEA-088/106/135/072. PBW
or Koszulity alone does not create or invert factor-base atoms.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Pollard rho is `N^(1/2+o(1))` time; BSGS is `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Let setup be `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal relation and target densities `N^delta,N^delta_t`, bar-cobar extraction/inversion `N^q,N^q_m`, output and ambiguity `N^o,N^u`, and factor-log algebra `N^ell,N^ell_m`. Then

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

These are complete time and peak-memory exponents; every tree, differential term, primitive, rewrite branch, and source output is charged.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Elliptic addition with membership and exceptional-chart relations may be nonquadratic or non-Koszul. Even if a Koszul model exists, its bar construction contains all source trees, while primitive/homology classes can forget the exact leaves. Inverting those classes may therefore recreate the original source-fiber enumeration.

## Proof track

Give the colored presentation, prove PBW and Koszulity including exceptional strata, bound target primitives, prove an exact primitive/leaf biconditional, and derive complete `lambda,mu<=0.45`.

## Disproof track

Exhibit an unavoidable higher relation or PBW ambiguity, exponential bar states, distinct leaf tuples with one primitive class, a source-seeded transform, or an exponent at least `0.5`.

## Positive and negative controls

- Known quadratic Koszul operads with supplied leaves.
- Non-Koszul presentations and bar complexes retaining all trees.
- Supplied elliptic source circuits and translated-circuit backends.
- Exhaustive toy fibers, direct enumeration, rho, BSGS, and blind-target verification.
