## Falsifiable hypothesis

For a declared family of ordinary prime-field curves, construct a public exact mirror
elliptic model in which each target-independent factor-base point corresponds to a marked
Lagrangian atom. Suppose a filtered pair-of-pants or higher product for a target point has
action-separated rigid triangles, and the lowest isolated triangle terms are biconditional
with exact signed five-source tuples. If those terms and all ambiguity can be computed and
inverted in complete exponent `alpha<3/2`, they yield `B+sigma` full-rank relation rows,
verified factor logs, and blind `Q+[t]P` descent below rho.

The record is merged/rejected. Published mirror and higher-product machinery aggregates
triangles into theta or categorical products, while the proposed finite-field action filter
and source inverse are only postulated. Without an explicit theorem separating this
operation from the cluster/scattering broken-line and existing categorical reporter lanes,
the mechanism is a relabelling rather than a new source generator.

## Mechanism-new operation

The proposed operation is **transport factor points to marked mirror Lagrangian atoms,
compute a filtered pair-of-pants product, isolate rigid triangles by action, and invert an
isolated triangle term to the exact factor-base vertices that generated it**. The
filtration, coefficient field, gradings, orientations, repetitions, and source inverse
must be frozen before target queries.

Evaluating an ordinary theta identity, translating a known divisor relation into the
Fukaya category, selecting a triangle after a source is known, or using a dense enumeration
of all polygons is a duplicate/control. Cluster/scattering broken-line coordinates,
matchgate or Pfaffian reporters, geometric LR/Satake reporters, and skein/quantum-trace
reporters are also merge controls. The new operation must generate sources, remove the
existing search obstruction, and have a theorem separating it from those lanes.

1. `E(F_p)` contains a public prime-order subgroup `<P>` of order `N=p^(1+o(1))`, target
   `Q=[x]P`, fixed arity five, and target-independent sign-canonical factor base
   `F={F_1,...,F_B}` with `B=L=N^ell`.
2. A public mirror elliptic model, coefficient/Novikov specialization, marked Lagrangians,
   gradings, orientations, filtration, and pair-of-pants products are uniform over the
   declared finite-field family.
3. Factor atoms are target-independent and target data enters only through the product
   terminal, never through post-hoc triangle selection.
4. Action separation isolates every accepted rigid triangle term, and a public inverse
   returns exact signed factor points with all multiplicities and exceptional branches.
5. The mirror/source correspondence is biconditional and independently verified on the
   original elliptic curve, including repeated points and infinity.
6. Mirror construction, extension fields, polygon counts, precision, output, misses, rank,
   linear algebra, descent, verification, and peak bit memory are fully charged.
7. All finite observations remain toy, heuristic, model-bound, and novelty-unverified.

## Complete factor-base-to-target-descent path

1. Freeze `E,P,N,F,B=L`, the mirror model, atom Lagrangians, coefficient specialization,
   filtration, polygon conventions, source inverse, exceptional branches, and an
   independent elliptic verifier.
2. Construct every factor atom target-independently and certify exact round-trip mapping to
   `F_i`; record coincident atoms, lift choices, filtration ties, and precision losses.
3. For known public `R_j=[r_j]P`, form the frozen target product, compute all isolated rigid
   triangle contributions, invert each accepted term to exact signed factor points, and
   independently verify every five-point elliptic sum.
4. Preserve misses, tied actions, duplicate polygons, and kernel branches; collect exactly
   `B+sigma` verified source rows whose coefficient matrix has rank `B` modulo `N`.
5. Solve every factor-base logarithm and independently verify
   `[log_P(F_i)]P=F_i` for all `i`.
6. Freeze all mirror state, choose fresh public masks `t`, and apply the identical filtered
   product and triangle/source inverse to blind targets `Q+[t]P`.
7. Substitute verified factor logs, subtract `t`, enumerate every lift/triangle scalar
   candidate, and accept only `x` satisfying `[x]P=Q`.

## Full rho/BSGS cost model

Pollard rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` expected group operations with constant-state memory;
BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Set `B=L=N^ell`. Let mirror construction,
factor atoms, coefficient specialization, and filtered-product setup cost `L^(s+o(1))`
time and `L^(s_m+o(1))` peak memory. Let one complete target product, polygon isolation,
all source output, ambiguity enumeration, and verification cost `L^(alpha+o(1))` time and
`L^(m_q+o(1))` memory.

Unless a theorem proves a changed density, use `pi=min(1,L^5/N)`. In the sparse regime,

`T_rel=N*L^(alpha-4+o(1))`

and

`T_desc=N*L^(alpha-5+o(1))`.

Sparse linear algebra costs `L^(2+o(1))` time and at least `L^(1+o(1))` memory. Hence

`lambda=max(s*ell,1+(alpha-4)*ell,2*ell,1+(alpha-5)*ell)`

and

`mu=max(s_m*ell,m_q*ell,ell)`

At `ell=1/5`, strict time below rho requires `alpha<3/2`; the promotion gate
`lambda,mu<=0.45` requires `alpha<=1.25` and `s,s_m,m_q<=2.25`. All polygon terms,
Novikov/extension precision, lift enumeration, failed targets, output, `B+sigma` rows,
factor logs, and original-curve verification are charged. A precomputed table of
`L^c` triangles contributes exponent `c` even if later lookup is constant time.

## Likely fatal obstruction

Elliptic mirror products are typically theta-series sums over many holomorphic triangles,
not canonical one-term factorizations. Action ties and wall crossing can merge distinct
source polygons, while the categorical equivalence forgets the individual geometric
provenance needed for factor-base labels. A uniform exact mirror lift from `F_p` to a
Novikov or characteristic-zero setting may be noncanonical, and selecting a target's
lowest source-labelled triangle may require enumerating the original incidence fiber.
IDEA-076 already occupies cluster/scattering broken-line theta coordinates, while
IDEA-050, IDEA-092, IDEA-095, and IDEA-108 occupy nearby categorical source-reporting
operations; no concrete non-merge operation is presently identified.

## Proof track

Prove a non-merge theorem identifying an operation unavailable to cluster/scattering

## Disproof track

Exhibit stable action collisions or wall-crossing ambiguity, prove triangle coefficients
only give aggregate theta sums, reduce the proposed operation to IDEA-076 or one of the
existing categorical reporter lanes, show exact mirror construction or source inversion
requires dense incidence enumeration, or derive complete time or memory exponent at least
`1/2`.
