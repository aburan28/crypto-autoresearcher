# A1 — New tracked propagation objects on the AES round function

Slice: **object-first cryptanalysis of the AES round function itself**, reduced round only.
Every entry names the round count `r` and the key size. The five propagation objects this
repository has already named — integral / division-property sets, key-schedule differential
relations, Demirci–Şelçuk matching objects, partial-sum cost terms, impossible-differential
contradictions — are **off-limits as the primary lens**; where an entry brushes one of them
it tracks a different functional of it (a second moment, an operator, a multiplicative
label) and says so in its adjacency line.

**No entry below claims, predicts or implies a break, a key recovery against a fielded
parameter set, an asymptotic exponent improvement, or any relation to the published state of
the art in either direction.** Claim ceilings are `control`, `toy` or `medium`. Every entry
carries `sota_delta: 0` and `novelty_status: unverified`; `eprint.iacr.org`, `arXiv` and
`nist.gov` are unreachable here, so novelty is not adjudicated in either direction.
Nothing in this file is a ledger record, no identifier is minted, no hypothesis status
changes, no experiment is approved.

### 0.2 Tracked objects and the lossy-projection test

Three of the ten entries share one object and three more share a second. Both are stated
here once so that each entry's projection test is not re-litigated.

**TO-TORUS — the multiplicative product-class label of an S-box difference or mask pair.**
Write `S = L ∘ Inv` with `L(y) = A(y) + 0x63`, `A` the `GF(2)`-linear part. Define, for an
input/output difference pair, `m(δ, γ) = δ · A⁻¹(γ) ∈ GF(2⁸)*`, and for a trace-form linear
mask pair `n(a, b) = a · A*(b)` with `A*` the trace-adjoint of `A`.

- **Lossy?** Yes, and by exactly 255-to-1. The fibre over a label is the full orbit
  `{(λδ, λ⁻¹γ)}` of the `GF(2⁸)*` torus acting with opposite weights. Neither difference is
  recoverable from the label; the two together are not recoverable from either.
- **Structural, not a change of coordinates.** `Inv(λx) = λ⁻¹Inv(x)` on all of `GF(2⁸)`
  including `x = 0`, so the torus acts on the difference-pair space and the DDT, BCT and
  (trace-form) LAT of the S-box are **constant on its orbits**. The discarded coordinate is
  therefore provably irrelevant to every first-order statistic of the S-box layer. That is
  the discard being *compatible with the operations*, in the sense `docs/inventor-protocol.md`
  §2 requires, rather than a relabelling.
- **Recomputed in this session, standard library only, no AES query, re-derivable in
  seconds** (script `sboxcheck.py`/`sboxcheck3.py`/`sboxcheck4.py` in this session's
  scratchpad; **not a ledger measurement, no evidence strength attaches, and every entry
  below re-checks it before believing it**):
  - `DDT_S[δ][γ]` is a function of `m` alone: **255 classes, 0 classes carrying more than
    one value.** The 4-entries are exactly the single class `m = 1` — **255 of them, one per
    `δ`**, i.e. the four-solution locus is the hyperbola `δ · A⁻¹(γ) = 1`. Row profile of
    every nonzero `δ`: 129 zeros, 126 twos, one four.
  - `BCT_S[δ][γ]` is a function of `m` alone: **255 classes, 0 violations**; class-value
    histogram `{0: 128, 2: 124, 4: 1, 6: 2}`; **boomerang uniformity 6, attained exactly at
    the two classes `m ∈ {0xbc, 0xbd}`**.
  - In the **trace form** `W(a,b) = Σ_x (−1)^{Tr(ax)+Tr(b·Inv(x))}`, `W_Inv` is a function
    of `a·b` alone: **255 classes, 0 violations**, 16 distinct values in `[−28, 32]`, max
    `|W| = 32` i.e. correlation `2⁻³`. For the AES S-box `|W_S(a,b)| = |W_Inv(a, A*(b))|`,
    again 255 classes, 0 violations. (This **discharges by measurement** the recall marker
    `CAND-ND-5` carried at confidence MEDIUM for the S-box maximum linear correlation.)
  - The coordinate-basis dot-product LAT does **not** collapse; only the trace form does.
    Recorded because the first attempt in this session used the dot product and read 255
    violated classes — a live example of the instrument, not the cipher, making the reading.

**TO-PATTERN — the byte-activity support of a state difference, tracked as a distribution
rather than as a witness.** The 16-bit word `supp(Δ) ∈ {0,1}^16`.

- **Lossy?** Maximally: `2^128 → 2^16`, and then `N` observations → one distribution.
- **Structural.** SubBytes is bytewise bijective, so it preserves the zero set of a
  difference *exactly*; ShiftRows permutes the 16 bits; AddRoundKey acts trivially on
  differences. Only MixColumns moves support, and it does so column-locally. The discard
  (all byte values) is compatible with three of four layers exactly and with the fourth in
  the value-averaged sense that A1-10 makes explicit and then tests.
- **Not a change of coordinates.** The fibre over a weight-`w` pattern has `255^w` elements.
- **Verified here:** MixColumns weight-1 input → weight-4 output, **1020/1020**; weight-2
  input → weight-3 output **6120 of 390150** (= `C(4,2)·C(4,3)·255`), weight-4 output
  otherwise. Branch number 5 met with equality exactly on those.

### 0.3 CEILING-A1 — the one bound that binds every entry in this slice

> **The product-class label is exactly conserved by a SubBytes layer and exactly destroyed by
> the additive part of MixColumns.** `Inv` is torus-equivariant, so the label survives the
> S-box; `MC` is `GF(2⁸)`-*linear*, not multiplicative, so the next layer's difference
> `δ' = (MC γ)_i` is a **sum** of previous `γ`'s and the label algebra does not close across
> the interface. Consequently **TO-TORUS buys enumeration, never depth.** Entries A1-1,
> A1-2, A1-3 reduce a `255^{2n}` trail space to `255^n` and make an exact two-round hull or
> super-box DDT computable without MILP; **none of them extends the reach of any
> distinguisher by one round, and none may be written as if it did.**
>
> Symmetrically, **TO-PATTERN carries no value information by construction**, so any entry
> built on it is bounded above by the value-averaged law; A1-10's deliverable is precisely
> the round at which that law becomes uninformative, which is a **ceiling**, not an attack.
>
> And the reference does not move: **REF-A does not shrink with round count.** Exhaustive
> search at `r = 5` for AES-128 is still `2^127` five-round encryptions. Nothing below is a
> key recovery and nothing below is compared to one.

## 1. The ten ideas

### A1-1. The four-solution hyperbola: the DDT of the AES S-box is a function of one 8-bit multiplicative label, and the trail space collapses by `255^n`

**Round count and key size.** The object lives at `r = 2` (one AES super-box, i.e. the
`SB · ARK · MC · SB` factor of two rounds after ShiftRows), AES-128. Its consumers are the
`r = 4`–`r = 5` trail counts that every other entry in this slice needs.

**Tracked object + what it discards.** TO-TORUS, `m(δ,γ) = δ · A⁻¹(γ)`, one byte per active
S-box. Discards: which of the 2 or 4 values `x` realises the transition, the additive
structure entirely, and 254 of every 255 difference pairs (the torus orbit). The discard is
structural because the DDT is constant on the orbit — the discarded coordinate carries **no**
first-order differential information, which is a theorem about `Inv`, not a convenience.

**Mechanism.** `Inv(λx) = λ⁻¹Inv(x)` gives `DDT_Inv[λδ][λ⁻¹γ] = DDT_Inv[δ][γ]`, and
`DDT_S[δ][γ] = DDT_Inv[δ][A⁻¹γ]`, so the whole `255 × 255` DDT is one function on 255
classes. The 4-entries are one class, `m = 1`. Hence a two-round differential trail's exact
probability is a function of the **label vector** of its active S-boxes only. For a super-box
with one active input byte, the intermediate difference `γ` ranges over the 127 nonzero
entries of one DDT row, `MC γ` fixes the four round-2 input differences, and the condition
"all four round-2 S-boxes sit on the 4-locus" reads `β_i = A(((MCγ)_i)⁻¹)`: **exactly one
output difference `β*(γ)` per `γ`.** So the super-box DDT restricted to one-active-in has a
completely explicit list of 127 candidate maximisers per input difference, computable with
no search.

**Prediction (two-sided, numeric).** For each active input byte position and each of the
`4·255` one-active input differences `α`: (i) the exact super-box DDT restricted to
one-active `α` is `P(α,β) = Σ_γ DDT[α][γ]·∏_i DDT[(MCγ)_i][β_i] / 256^5`; (ii) the maximum
over `β` is attained at some `β*(γ)` of the explicit list, and (iii) the value at the
best `β*` exceeds the best `β` **off** the list by a factor `≥ (4/2)^4 = 16` before the
row-sum correction. Prediction (ii)+(iii) is the falsifiable content; (i) is arithmetic.

**Minimal discriminating test.** Pure table arithmetic in C, **no AES query at all**.
Enumerate `α` over `4·255`, `γ` over the 127-entry DDT row, `β` over `4·255` one-active
outputs plus the 127 listed `β*(γ)`; report the full `P(α,β)` maximiser and the on-list /
off-list ratio. *Known-answer gate:* replace `S` by `Inv` alone (drop `A`) — the four-locus
must move to `δγ = 1` exactly, all 255 of them, and the class count must stay 255/0. Second
gate: replace `S` by a uniform random bijection — the class count must **collapse** (the
255-class structure must be destroyed, expected ≈ 65025 distinct classes needed), which is
the sibling null proving this is the AES S-box's algebra and not AES-shaped geometry.

**Falsifier.** F1-a: any of the 255 four-entries off the class `m = 1` → the mechanism is
wrong at line one and the entry is withdrawn, not repaired. F1-b: the best `β` off the
explicit list beats the best on-list `β*` for any `α` → prediction (ii) is false and the
locus does not control the super-box maximiser. F1-c: on/off ratio `< 4` → the leverage is
under 2 bits and this is bookkeeping, not a lever.

**Charged cost.** Table build `2^16` for the DDT, `2^16` for `A`/`A⁻¹`; enumeration
`4·255 × 127 × (1020 + 127) ≈ 1.5e8` GF(2⁸) multiplications ≈ seconds in C, ≤ 10 MB. No
oracle calls, no queries, no preprocessing beyond the two tables. Machine model: single
core, in-cache. Nothing is uncharged.

**Ceiling.** `control`. It is an enumeration collapse and a structural derivation; by
CEILING-A1 it cannot extend depth and is not written as if it could.

**Risk band.** `representation_changing` — it changes how a trail is represented, from
`2n` difference bytes to `n` label bytes.

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `AES_S_box_low_degree_relations|39_independent_deg2_equations|
rank_98_of_137_monomials|random_bijection_null_0_quadrics` (MEAS-RT-A, `redteam/findings.yaml`).
**Why it is not that:** MEAS-RT-A counts the *algebraic* relations of the S-box graph over
`GF(2)`; this tracks a *differential* multiplicative label over `GF(2⁸)` and its consequence
is an exact trail enumeration, not an equation count. Second neighbour: `KN-FIND-028` Fact 2
(`Inv` preserves collinearity, `L` breaks it) — that is about a preserved *relation on
values*; this is about the *multiplicity* function on difference pairs, and here the affine
layer `A` does **not** break the structure, it merely twists it.

`sota_delta: 0` / `novelty_status: unverified`

### A1-2. The boomerang switch locus: the AES S-box BCT is the same one-byte label, and exactly two classes carry multiplicity 6

**Round count and key size.** `r = 4` and `r = 5` boomerang on AES-128, with a degenerate
`r = 1` single-S-box arm as the known-answer gate.

**Tracked object + what it discards.** TO-TORUS again, but the tracked *statistic* is the
switch multiplicity `BCT_S[δ][γ]`, not the differential multiplicity. Discards the same
254/255 of the difference-pair space and, additionally, the identity of which quartet
realises the switch.

**Mechanism.** `S⁻¹(S(x)+γ) = Inv(Inv(x) + A⁻¹γ)` (the `0x63` cancels), so
`BCT_S[δ][γ] = BCT_Inv[δ][A⁻¹γ]` and the torus argument applies verbatim. Recomputed here:
**255 classes, 0 violations; histogram `{0:128, 2:124, 4:1, 6:2}`; the two classes attaining
6 are `m ∈ {0xbc, 0xbd}`.** This makes the boomerang switch prescription completely
explicit: for any input difference `δ`, the *only* two output differences with switch
multiplicity 6 are `γ = A(0xbc·δ⁻¹)` and `γ = A(0xbd·δ⁻¹)`. Nothing has to be searched.

**Prediction.** Build two matched 4-round boomerang arms on AES-128 whose middle differences
are identical in **activity pattern and Hamming weight** and differ **only** in whether the
`k` switch S-boxes sit on the locus. Predict the return-count ratio `on/off ≥ 3^k / 1.2`
for `k ∈ {1,2}` forced S-boxes (per-S-box factor `6/2 = 3`), and predict the ratio to be
**1.00 ± resolution** in the random-bijective-S-box arm, where the locus is meaningless.

**Minimal discriminating test.** Tier 1: `2^30` quartets per arm at `r ∈ {2,3,4,5,10}`; per
quartet 2 encryptions and 2 decryptions. *Known-answer gate:* the degenerate one-S-box arm —
apply the quartet construction to a single S-box and count returns; it must reproduce
`BCT_S[δ][γ]/256` to three digits for 20 pre-registered `(δ,γ)`, including one at 6, one at
4, one at 2 and one at 0. A zero-count at the `BCT = 0` pair is a hard gate: a nonzero
reading there voids the instrument. Controls: matched PRP arm at identical quartet count
through the identical decryption path; `r = 10` decay arm; random-bijective-S-box arm;
degenerate-quartet counter (`∇ = 0`, `δ = 0`, `C3 = C2`) reported separately — a nonzero
uncounted occurrence voids the arm, because a trivial shift manufactured a spurious hit in
this campaign before.

**Falsifier.** F2-a: on/off ratio `≤ 1.2` at `2^30` quartets at `r = 4` → the S-box-level
switch gain does not survive to the cipher-level return event; recorded as a scoped negative
with its `2^-28.6` rare-event resolution stated in the same sentence. F2-b: the random-S-box
arm reproduces the on/off ratio → attribution to the AES S-box withdrawn; the effect is
AES-shaped geometry, exactly the `EV-AES-048545` outcome shape. F2-c: any ratio at `r = 10`
→ artifact tell, void.

**Charged cost.** Tier 1: `2^30 × 4 = 2^32` AES operations per (arm, round) ≈ 60 s per core
at the measured throughput; 5 rounds × 4 arms ≈ 20 core-minutes, plus `2^12` key schedules
charged, memory < 100 MB. The **decryption path is charged in kind, not only in count**:
this is a chosen-ciphertext access model, strictly stronger than A1-1's, and the inverse key
schedule is the dominant implementation risk (one false null in this campaign came from an
inverse-side index convention). BCT table build `2^24`, one-time.

**Ceiling.** `toy`. A measured on/off separation at `r = 4`–`5` is a distinguisher-quality
observation at toy tier and is not compared to REF-A.

**Risk band.** `high_risk` — the S-box-level factor of 3 may be entirely absorbed by the
`2^-96`-scale return event.

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `truncated_boomerang_quartet|two_probability_one_halves|
96_bit_return_event|shift_is_not_an_involution` (CAND-ND-3, `newdist/candidates.yaml`).
**Why it is not that:** CAND-ND-3 fixes the shift `∇` inside an inverse-SR-diagonal
*subspace* and reads a truncated support, deriving its probability-one halves from
ShiftRows geometry; this one chooses `∇` from a *computed value-level locus* of the S-box's
BCT and reads a graded multiplicity ratio, so its sibling null is a random S-box rather than
a non-diagonal byte set. The two would return opposite verdicts if the effect is geometric.

### A1-3. Coherent linear-hull blocks: the trace-form LAT is a function of one label, so a two-round hull is an exact 255-term sum

**Round count and key size.** Exact hull at `r = 2` (one super-box), AES-128; measured
correlation arms at `r = 2, 3, 4` only. `r ≥ 5` is declared **untestable-here in advance**
by the resolution argument below, so no null at `r ≥ 5` can be misread as a closure.

**Tracked object + what it discards.** TO-TORUS in its mask form: `n(a,b) = a · A*(b)`, one
byte per active S-box, and the **class vector** of a linear trail. Discards the individual
masks (255-to-1 per S-box), the sign convention absorbed into `Tr(b·0x63)`, and the identity
of the trail.

**Mechanism.** Verified here: `W_Inv(a,b)` in the trace form is a function of `a·b` alone —
**255 classes, 0 violations**, values in `{−28,…,32}`, `max|W| = 32` (correlation `2⁻³`,
now measured rather than recalled); and `|W_S(a,b)| = |W_Inv(a, A*(b))|`, again 255 classes,
0 violations. A linear trail's correlation is `∏_i W(a_i,b_i)/256`, hence a function of the
class vector alone. **Distinct trails sharing a class vector have identical correlation
magnitude and a sign determined by the class plus a `Tr(b·0x63)` term.** The two-round hull
between a one-active input mask `α` and an output mask `β` is therefore an *exact* sum over
the 255 intermediate mask values, decomposable into coherent blocks — the hull is a
structured sum, not a random walk. This is a direct instrument for the **hull-versus-
characteristic gap** that `O-1` names as its only crack and for which this repo has no tool.

**Prediction.** Define the coherence factor `F(α,β) = |hull(α,β)| / max_γ |single-trail(α,γ,β)|`.
Predict `F > 1` for a nonempty set of `(α,β)`, and predict the *distribution* of `F` over
`(α,β)` differs from its random-bijective-S-box counterpart at the same wiring — because in
the random arm the 255 terms have independent signs and `E[F] ≈ √255/‖·‖` behaviour, whereas
here the signs are class-determined. Freeze both the predicted `F` histogram and its digest
before the AES arm.

**Minimal discriminating test.** Pure table arithmetic; **no AES query for the hull part**.
For all `4·255` one-active `α` and all `4·255` one-active `β`, sum the 255 intermediate
terms and report the `F` histogram. *Known-answer gate:* replace `S` by the identity — the
hull must contain exactly one nonzero term and `F = 1.000` for every `(α,β)`; anything else
is an instrument fault. Second gate: replace `S` by `Inv` alone — the class count must stay
255/0 with the untwisted product `ab`. Sibling null: a uniform random bijective S-box at
identical wiring, which negates *the S-box's algebra* while preserving *the branch number*,
so it is not a null built by negating a sufficient hypothesis. Then, and only then, measure
the actual correlation at `r = 2, 3, 4` over `2^32` pairs for the pre-frozen mask set and
compare to the computed hull.

**Falsifier.** F3-a: `F ≤ 1.05` for every `(α,β)` → hull coherence is not exploitable at two
rounds; **expected verdict CLOSED**, and that is a legitimate deliverable under
`docs/inventor-protocol.md` §8, not a failure. F3-b: the random-S-box arm reproduces the `F`
histogram → attribution to the AES S-box withdrawn, only the branch-number-generic part is
claimed. F3-c: the measured `r = 2` correlation exceeds the computed hull → the class
collapse or the sign accounting is wrong, and the derivation is withdrawn.

**Charged cost.** LAT build `2^16 · 256 = 2^24` sign accumulations (~10 s in Python, <1 s in
C); hull enumeration `1020 × 1020 × 255 ≈ 2.7e8` ≈ seconds; measurement arms `2^33` AES ops
per round count ≈ 30 s/core. Memory < 200 MB. **Resolution charged up front:** correlation
resolution is `≈2^-16` at `2^32` pairs and `≈2^-19` at the `2^38` per-task cap, and the
derived hybrid ceiling at `r ≥ 5` sits below that — hence the untestable-here declaration.

**Ceiling.** `control`. Its most likely honest verdict is a **CLOSED** ceiling on two-round
hull coherence, plus the discharge of a recalled constant by measurement.

**Risk band.** `conservative`.

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `differential_linear_hybrid|probability_one_truncated_head|
untestable_here_beyond_r4|resolution_declared_up_front` (CAND-ND-5) and the `O-1`
hull-versus-characteristic open problem. **Why it is not that:** CAND-ND-5 *measures* a
correlation and recalls the LAT maximum; this *computes* the LAT's class structure, uses it
to sum an entire hull exactly, and treats the measurement only as a check on the computed
sum. CAND-ND-5's deliverable is a calibration; this one's is a coherence ceiling.

`sota_delta: 0` / `novelty_status: unverified`

### A1-4. The super-box top-degree coefficient matrix: 1024 bits distilled from a `2^32` truth table in one streaming pass

**Round count and key size.** The two-round AES super-box `Φ_k = SB ∘ ARK_k ∘ MC ∘ SB` on
one 32-bit column, AES-128 (the subkey `k` is 32 bits and is swept).

**Tracked object + what it discards.** The `32 × 32` `GF(2)` matrix `D(k)` whose `(i,c)`
entry is the ANF coefficient of the weight-31 monomial `∏_{j≠i} x_j` in coordinate `c` of
`Φ_k` — equivalently the parity of coordinate `c` summed over the hyperplane `x_i = 0`.
Discards `2^37 − 2^10` bits: the entire truth table except its top ANF layer, all lower-degree
structure, and the key. **Not invertible by any map:** `D` has `2^1024` possible values
against `(2^32)!` super-boxes, and the fibre is astronomically large.

**Mechanism.** Every coordinate of a permutation is balanced, so the degree-32 coefficient is
exactly 0 and the degree-31 layer is the top nonvanishing one. It is computed by a **single
streaming pass over all `2^32` inputs with 32 32-bit XOR accumulators** — one accumulator per
omitted variable — with no table stored. The AES super-box is not a random 32-bit permutation:
`SB` acts bytewise and `MC` is `GF(2)`-linear, so `D(k)` inherits a block structure whose
existence is the falsifiable claim. `D`'s **dependence on `k`** is the second, sharper
question: `ARK` sits between two S-box layers, so a `k`-independent `D` would be a
deterministic key-free invariant of the AES super-box that this program has never looked for.

**Prediction (two-sided).** Over 128 uniformly drawn subkeys, either (i) `mean rank-defect of
D(k)` falls outside `[0.68, 1.02]`, the 3-sd band around the uniform random `32×32 GF(2)`
value `0.850` (sd 0.66, sd of the mean 0.058, `P(full rank) = 0.2888`); or (ii) `D(k)` is
constant in `k` across all 128 draws. Either outcome is a finding; agreement with the random
law inside the band and non-constant in `k` is the null.

**Minimal discriminating test.** *Known-answer gate A:* replace `SB` by the identity — the
super-box is `GF(2)`-affine, so **`D` must be exactly the zero matrix, all 1024 bits**, and

### A1-7. Across-key overdispersion: `φ = Var_K(N)/E_K(N)` as a direct instrument for the differential hull

**Round count and key size.** `r ∈ {3,4,5,6,10}`, AES-128, with an AES-256 arm named as an
open gap rather than assumed (reduced-round depth for AES-192/256 is currently carried over
from AES-128 **without measurement**).

**Tracked object + what it discards.** The **overdispersion index** `φ = Var_K(N)/E_K(N)` of
a truncated-differential hit count `N`, taken **across keys**. Discards: which pairs hit,
which key produced which count, the trail identities, and `N` itself — the object is a single
dimensionless ratio. The discard is structural: `φ` is invariant under any relabelling of
keys and of pairs, and a Poisson mixture cannot be inverted to recover the trail set that
produced it. This is a **second moment across the key**, which is a functional no entry in
this repository tracks; every existing statistic is a first moment at fixed key.

**Mechanism.** If a truncated differential's probability is carried by essentially **one**
characteristic, `p(K)` is near-constant across keys and `N | K ~ Poisson(Mp)`, giving
`φ = 1` exactly. If it is carried by a **cluster** whose signed contributions depend on the
key, `p(K)` varies and `φ = 1 + M·Var_K(p)/E_K(p) > 1`, with the excess growing **linearly in
the data per key `M`**. That linear-in-`M` growth is the signature and is what separates the
hull from the characteristic — the gap `O-1` names as its only crack and for which this
program has had no instrument.

**Prediction.** At `r = 5` and `r = 6`, with `M = 2^20` pairs per key and `m = 2^12` keys,
predict `φ − 1` to be **positive and to scale as `M`** across `M ∈ {2^16, 2^18, 2^20}`; the
scaling, not the value, is the falsifiable content, because a constant offset in `φ` is an
instrument artifact and a linear one cannot be.

**Minimal discriminating test.** Reuse the `CAND-ND-1` readout (`collision on the inverse-SR
diagonal CW[j]`) so the code path is shared and its deterministic `r = 4` zero runs inside
the same binary as a positive control. *Known-answer gate A:* at `r = 3` with a diagonal input
difference the statistic **saturates by derivation** (D6), so the count is key-independent and
`φ` must read **0.000** — no variance at all; a nonzero `φ` at `r = 3` is an instrument fault
and voids the session. *Known-answer gate B:* the matched-PRP arm must read
`φ = 1.000 ± 3√(2/(m−1)) = 1.000 ± 0.066` at `m = 2^12`. *Degenerate flag:* at `r = 4` the
count is exactly 0 for every key, so `φ` is `0/0`; the instrument must emit the degenerate
flag, **not a number** — this is exactly the failure mode that produced the withdrawn
influence-density closure and it is trapped by construction here. Controls: `r = 10` decay
arm; independent-round-key arm; a resampling arm at **fixed key** with disjoint pair seeds,
to separate across-key variance from within-key sampling variance (without which `φ` is
uninterpretable).

**Falsifier.** F7-a: `φ ≤ 1.07` at `r = 5` and `r = 6` across all three `M` values → no
measurable hull enhancement at `M = 2^20`, `m = 2^12`; reported as a bound **with those two
numbers in the same sentence**. F7-b: `φ − 1 > 0` but **flat** in `M` → an offset, i.e. an
artifact or a key-schedule effect, not a hull; reported as such and never re-explained after
the fact. F7-c: gate A returns nonzero, or gate B falls outside its band → instrument void.

**Charged cost.** `m × M × |rounds| × |arms| = 2^12 × 2^20 × 5 × 3 = 2^35.9` encryptions ≈
`2^35.9 / 2^26.17 ≈ 850 s` per core-equivalent, ~15 min on 4 cores per `M` setting, ~45 min

quantile comparison is hand-written and unit-tested, which is where a silent bug would live.

**Ceiling.** `toy`.

**Risk band.** `conservative`.

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `r5_truncated_differential_collision_ratio|
mechanism_computed_predicted_value_frozen_first|~2_pow_-8_sketch|value_vs_support_level_discriminator`
(CAND-ND-2). **Why it is not that:** CAND-ND-2 measures a **first-moment ratio at a fixed

**Tracked object + what it discards.** TO-PATTERN as a **distribution**: the probability
vector `π_r ∈ ℝ^{2^16}` on byte-activity patterns of the state difference, evolved by the
operator `P = Σ_SR ∘ (C ⊗ C ⊗ C ⊗ C)`, where `Σ_SR` is the bit-permutation induced by
ShiftRows on the 16-bit pattern and `C` is the `16 × 16` value-averaged MixColumns
support-transition matrix on one column. Discards: **all byte values**, all inter-column
correlation, and the identity of any witness pair. The discard is structural for three of
four layers exactly (SubBytes preserves the zero set of a difference; ShiftRows permutes it;
AddRoundKey acts trivially) and is a declared **idealisation** at the fourth — and the
experiment's whole purpose is to measure where that idealisation breaks.

**Mechanism.** The absence of MILP and SAT is recorded in this program as foreclosing the
automated truncated-differential and division-property methodology "essentially in its
entirety". But the truncated-pattern transition **factorises**: MixColumns acts
column-locally and identically on all four columns, and ShiftRows is a fixed bit-permutation.
So the `2^16 × 2^16` operator — `2^32` entries, 4 GB dense, and therefore out of envelope if
materialised — is **never materialised**: one step costs `2^16 × 16` scalar operations on a
`2^16`-vector. `C` is built exhaustively, not sampled: rows for input weight 1, 2, 3, 4 cost
`4·255`, `6·255²`, `4·255³`, `255⁴` GF operations respectively, the largest being `4.2e9`.
Rows 1 and 2 are already verified here (`1020/1020` to weight 4; `6120/390150` to weight 3).

**Prediction.** (i) The stationary distribution is the uniform-on-nonzero-support law
`π(v) ∝ 255^{|v|}`, exactly. (ii) The total-variation distance `‖π_r − π‖_TV` falls below
`2^-8` at a computed round `r*`; predict `r* = 3`. (iii) **The ceiling:** no statistic that
is a function of the activity pattern alone can separate AES from a random permutation
beyond round `r* + 1`, because by construction it sees only `π_r`. (iv) The measured pattern
distribution at `r = r*` deviates from `π_{r*}` on at least one of the 17 weight bins by more
than `3σ` — because the value-independence idealisation is false and the deviation is
precisely the clustering the operator cannot see. Predictions (ii) and (iv) point in opposite
directions and are both measured by the same run.

**Minimal discriminating test.** Stage 0, no AES: build `C` exhaustively, verify rows 1 and 2
against the values above, power-iterate to `r = 12`, report `‖π_r − π‖_TV` per round and
`r*`. Stage 1: `2^30` pairs at `r ∈ {2,…,6,10}`, histogram the measured 16-bit patterns,
compare bin-by-bin against `π_r`. *Known-answer gate A:* replace `MC` by the identity — the
operator becomes the pure `Σ_SR` permutation, which has order 4, so the TV distance must be
**periodic and must never fall below its initial value**; an instrument reporting mixing here
is void. *Known-answer gate B:* the weight-1 row of `C` must be `(0,0,0,1)` exactly
(1020/1020) and the weight-2 row must read `6120/390150` to six digits. *Known-answer gate C:*
at `r = 4`, the measured pattern distribution restricted to diagonal-difference inputs must
reproduce the D4 statement that **no column of `Δ_3` is zero** — a deterministic zero in a
named set of bins. Controls: matched PRP arm (whose pattern law is exactly `π`, closed form);
`r = 10` decay arm; random-bijective-S-box arm; sibling null with `MC` replaced by a random
all-nonzero MDS matrix, which must give an **identical** `C` (MDS-ness alone determines the
weight transitions), so a difference between the arms would falsify the operator's premise.

**Falsifier.** F10-a: measured pattern distribution matches `π_r` on all 17 weight bins at
every `r ≥ r*` within `3σ` → prediction (iv) is false, the value-averaged law is complete at
this readout, and **the ceiling (iii) stands as a CLOSED statement** about the entire
truncated-pattern lane in this envelope, with its quantifier written out. F10-b: `r*` computed
as `≥ 5` → prediction (ii) is false and the truncated lane is deeper than assumed, which is
the more interesting outcome. F10-c: gate A reports mixing, or gate B misses either verified
row → instrument void. F10-d: the MDS sibling null gives a different `C` → the operator's
premise (MDS-ness determines the weight transitions) is false and the whole construction is
withdrawn.

**Charged cost.** Stage 0: `≈4.3e9` GF multiplications for `C` (~1 min in C, one time),
power iteration `12 × 2^16 × 16 ≈ 1.3e7` floating-point operations (instant, pure Python),
memory **`2^16` doubles = 512 KB** — the `2^32`-entry matrix is never allocated and that is
the entire reason this is in envelope. Stage 1: `2^31` AES operations per (round, arm) ≈
30 s/core; 6 rounds × 5 arms ≈ 15 core-minutes; memory one `2^16` counter array, 512 KB.
No MILP, no SAT, no numpy, no sage, no dense linear algebra.

**Ceiling.** `medium` — it delivers a computed baseline law that other entries in this slice
consume, and a ceiling statement about an entire lane. It is **not** a distinguisher and is
not compared to REF-A.

**Risk band.** `representation_changing`.

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `truncated_support_miss_in_the_middle|16_bit_activity_pattern_conservative|
sievable_form_measured|impossibility_itself_not_samplable` (CAND-RR78-D) and
`MixColumns_circulant_propagation_graph|1020_node_(lambda,k)_graph|two_step_graph|
deterministic_propagation_for_every_state` (Proposition 801-1, WITHHELD and not citable as
established). **Why it is not that:** CAND-RR78-D uses the 16-bit pattern **conservatively**,
as a support that may or may not be reachable, to argue an impossibility; Proposition 801-1
is a **deterministic** graph on `(λ,k)` pairs for a single column and a single state. This
builds the **value-averaged stochastic operator on the full 16-bit pattern space**, exploits
its exact tensor factorisation to avoid the 4 GB matrix, and produces a **mixing round** and a
lane ceiling rather than an impossible pattern or a deterministic propagation.

## 2. Objects considered and dropped at the lossy-projection test

Recorded so the omissions are deliberate rather than oversights. None of these consumed
compute.

- **The `⟨M⟩`-orbit label of a column difference.** `KN-FIND-028` Fact 1 establishes
  `ord(M) = 4` and `1 073 758 335` orbits on `2^32 − 1` nonzero vectors. The projection is
  therefore **4-to-1** — an invertible map up to a factor of four. Under house rule 4 that is
  a repackaging, not an object. Dropped.
- **The `GF(2⁸)`-univariate coefficient support of one output byte on a one-byte input line.**
  Verified here: the AES S-box has exactly **9 nonzero univariate coefficients**, at exponents
  `255 − 2^i` for `i = 0..7` plus `255` — i.e. it is a linearised polynomial in `x⁻¹`, which
  is why the sparsity is 9 and not 255. But `MC` is `GF(2⁸)`-**linear** and `ARK` is a
  translation, so at `r = 1` the support is exactly the S-box's own 9 terms (a perfect
  known-answer gate) and at `r = 2` the composition of two S-boxes saturates it. The object
  has a **structural reach of one round** and is dropped as too shallow to carry an entry;
  the 9-term fact is recorded here because it is the cleanest available gate for any future
  univariate instrument. Adjacent to `univariate_GF256_interpolation_coefficients|...`
  (H-AES-ecf3ad), which owns the coset-restricted version.
- **The multiset of rotation-classes of the four state rows.** Invariant under ShiftRows
  exactly (`SR` rotates row `t` by `t`), translated by `ARK`, destroyed by `MC` at the first
  interface. One-layer reach. Dropped.
- **The full ciphertext difference of a mixture quadruple, and the 255-byte delta-set output
  sequence.** Both already dropped in `newdist/candidates.yaml` for the same reason —
  not lossy, hence changes of coordinates rather than objects. Not re-litigated.
- **Invariant-subspace and nonlinear-invariant objects over a weak-key class.** Closed on
  `NC-3` cost arithmetic (`E[total] = 2^-d·2^{128-d-g} + (1−2^-d)·2^128`, so only constant
  density moves anything) and `NC-7`. A1-5 deliberately asks the **subspace** question in the
  single-key, all-keys setting where that arithmetic does not apply, and says so.
