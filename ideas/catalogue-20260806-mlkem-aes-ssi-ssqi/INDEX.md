# Idea catalogue, 2026-08-06 — 120 raw ideas, 12 slices, ML-KEM / AES / SSI / SSQI

Generated by twelve independent idea-generator sessions over disjoint slices (10 entries
each), then screened for duplication against the prior catalogue and the ledger (`DEDUP.md`)
and adversarially reviewed (`REDTEAM.md`).

**Nothing in this catalogue is a ledger record.** No `IDEA-*` identifier has been minted, no
hypothesis status has changed, no experiment has been approved. Every entry carries
`sota_delta: 0` and `novelty_status: unverified` — `eprint.iacr.org`, `arXiv` and `nist.gov`
are unreachable from this environment, so **novelty is not adjudicated in either direction**:
nothing here is asserted new and nothing here is dismissed as known. Claim ceilings are
`control`, `toy` or `medium` throughout. No entry claims, predicts or implies a break, a key
recovery against a fielded parameter set, or an asymptotic exponent improvement.

**Name collision warning.** The prior catalogue `ideas/catalogue-20260805/` uses `A1..A4` for
*ECDLP* slices and `B*`/`C*` for SSI/SSQI. This catalogue's `A1..A3` are **AES** and are
unrelated to them. Prior-art references below that read `B1-9`, `B2-7`, `C1-3`, `C2-9` etc.
are 2026-08-05 entries.

### 4d. Systemic defects (all four domains)

1. **The lossy-projection test is passed on paper while the instrument, not the target, is what gets measured.** *(headline)* The catalogue asks "is the map invertible?" and never asks "is the map a property of the primitive or of my harness?"
2. **Headline predictions that are theorems or tautologies, presented as falsifiable** — the direct cause of five of the nine kills.
3. **Three-point log-log fits with two free parameters** (`S1-1`, `S1-3`, `Q1-1`), where `log p` varies by well under a factor 2 and the parameters are not jointly identifiable.
4. **Data complexity never checked against the event rate** (`A3-7` at `2^{-126}`, `A1-2` at 96 bits, `A1-6` at `2^{-90}`).
5. **Unit slippage between entropy, log-variance and cost** — each instance a factor-of-2-to-4 error in a headline bit count.
6. **Ceilings asserted over families strictly larger than the invariant can bound.** A ceiling naming the wrong invariant is worse than no ceiling: it retires proposals it does not cover.
7. **Cross-entry contradictions inside a single slice** (`S2-1` vs `S2-7`; `S3-9` vs `S3-8`). Slice-level consistency was never checked.
8. **Adversary models with no adversary** (`M2-6`, `M2-1`, `A2-8`).
9. **Duplicate closures across slices with no cross-reference** (`S1-6` and `Q2-5` close two families by the same Ramanujan argument; the MixColumns/DDT enumeration is claimed by three places). Work is being paid for three times.
10. **Charged-cost tables that do not cover the test actually proposed** — the charging discipline is applied to the machine and not to the quantifier.

## 7. What this catalogue does not establish

This document establishes nothing about the security of ML-KEM, AES, SQIsign, CSIDH, or any
supersingular-isogeny problem, and it advances no cost, exponent, or claim tier: `sota_delta`
is zero on every axis for every one of the 120 entries. It is pre-ledger throughout — no
identifier is minted, no hypothesis status changes, no experiment is approved, and every
verdict in `DEDUP.md` and `REDTEAM.md` is advisory to the Coordinator, not a decision. It does
not adjudicate novelty in either direction, because `eprint.iacr.org`, `arXiv` and `nist.gov`
are unreachable from this environment; an entry surviving the screen is not thereby new, and
none is dismissed as known. It does not establish that any of the 102 survivors will produce
a result: the red team's own headline finding is that the catalogue systematically measures
the analyst's harness rather than the target, and 56 survivors persist only at a reduced
ceiling — several reduced to "the arithmetic is correct, the inference is not". The 15
strongest entries are entries **one adversarial pass failed to break**, which is weaker than
correct, and the arithmetic re-verification covers only the identities the reviewer chose to
recompute. Several entries rest on unverified recollections or on documents the environment
cannot read (`S3-3` is outright environment-blocked; `S1-3`'s "free side" rests on
Gross–Zagier / Gross–Keating recollections its own slice flags as unverified), and an
infrastructure failure is never mathematical evidence. Finally, the count of 102 is a count of
**distinct falsifiable proposals**, not of results, findings, or evidence; nothing in this
catalogue has been run.
