# A3 — AES (FIPS 197) idea catalogue: automated search, solver tooling, and measured-execution attack construction

Slice: mechanisms in **how an attack is found** and **how its cost is measured**. Model-and-encoding
choices that dominate the tractability of an automated search; constraint-propagation and abstraction
strength as measurable quantities rather than modelling folklore; search-guidance with an honest
baseline arm; automatic key-recovery-cost evaluation; bitsliced / instrumented execution of a
distinguisher with a measured false-positive behaviour against a matched control; differential
cluster enumeration at the scale this machine can actually reach; and the conversion of a counted
operation into a measured second. GOAL-AES-003 asks for **measured cost against references this repo
can adjudicate itself**; every entry below is built to produce a number this machine emits, not a
number this machine models.

**No entry below claims a break, a key recovery against a fielded parameter set, an asymptotic
exponent, or any relation to the published state of the art in either direction.** Ceilings are
`control`, `toy` or `medium`. Novelty is not adjudicable in this environment: eprint.iacr.org,
arXiv and nist.gov are unreachable, so nothing here is claimed new and nothing here is dismissed as
known. Every entry carries `sota_delta: 0` and `novelty_status: unverified`.

## 0. Frame

### 0.1 Off-limits as the primary lens for this slice

- **OL-1 Full-round / deployed AES.** Owned exclusively by RQ-AES-002 at `analyzed` forever. Nothing
  in this slice is phrased as a statement about 10/12/14 rounds, under any outcome. Where an entry
  measures a per-round quantity (A3-7 especially) the per-round factor is reported **at the rounds
  measured and nowhere else**; extrapolating it is barred inside the entry itself, not just in the
  headline.
- **OL-2 Side-channel, fault, implementation.** Out of scope as a category error. Note the near-miss
  this slice must actively avoid: A3-1 and A3-9 both touch *timing* and *fault injection*, and both
  are aimed at **the instrument and the cost model**, never at the cipher. Injecting a fault into a
  measurement harness to test the harness is instrument hygiene; injecting it into a target to
  extract a key is DFA and is not proposed anywhere here.
- **OL-3 Quantum, related/known/chosen-key as a completing model, multi-target, AES-derived
  constructions.** Closed by the goal record and, for multi-target, additionally by NC-2 arithmetic.
- **OL-4 The established families as the *novel* lens.** Differential/truncated, linear, integral /
  division property, impossible differential, MITM/DS, boomerang, biclique, algebraic, yoyo,
  mixture, subspace trail, partial sums. They remain fully admissible here as the **baseline arm**
  and as the **implementation target of the thing being instrumented** — which is most of this
  slice's content, since one cannot measure the cost of finding a trail without a trail-finding
  procedure to measure.
- **OL-5 Re-deriving GOAL-AES-002's committed measurements.** MEAS-GOAL-AES-002-001 (throughput),
  -002 (integral death rounds), -005 (TMTO), -006/-007/-008 (O-3 correction) are **inputs**, cited
  with their identifiers and their coordinator-side provenance caveat, never re-derived. The
  7.64/7.81/8.11-bit amortization figure is never cited as a justification for anything (RQ-AES-003
  R4(2)).
- **OL-6 Anything whose cheapest executable gate needs MILP, SAT, SMT, CP, numpy, sage, dense
  large-dimension GF(2) linear algebra, more than ~15 GB, or more than ~2^38 queries in one task.**
  This is the single hardest constraint on this particular slice and it is dealt with in §0.4 rather
  than hedged per entry.

### 0.2 Tracked objects and the lossy-projection test

Two objects are shared across the slice. Both are projections **of an execution**, not of the
cipher, which is what makes this slice structurally different from A1's propagation objects.

---

**TO-COST.** The map
`(attack or instrument A, machine M, working-set footprint W) ↦ (counted resource vector, measured resource vector)`,
where the counted vector is what an instrumented counter increments (table reads, S-box lookups,
XORs, queries) and the measured vector is `(wall-clock seconds, peak RSS bytes, queries issued)`.

- **Lossy?** Yes, and in two independent directions at once.
  *Forward:* from `(2.4 s, 4.1 GB, 2^32)` you cannot recover the attack, the key, the plaintext set,
  which candidates survived, or even which family the attack belongs to. Infinitely many distinct
  attacks map to the same cost pair. Nothing about the cipher is retained.
  *Backward:* one attack does **not** map to one cost pair. The same counted vector maps to wildly
  different measured vectors as `W` crosses a cache boundary. TO-COST is therefore not even a
  function of the mathematics — it is a function of the mathematics **and** the memory hierarchy.
- **Is the discard structural, or a change of coordinates?** Structural, and the second direction is
  the proof. A change of coordinates is invertible; TO-COST is not merely non-invertible, it is
  **not single-valued in the direction that matters**. The quantity every cost model in this
  repository silently assumes — that counted operations determine measured time up to one global
  scalar — is exactly the assumption that TO-COST is a function of the attack alone. A3-1 makes that
  assumption falsifiable.
- **Compatible with the operations of the target?** Deliberately not, and that is the point. TO-COST
  does not propagate through rounds; it propagates through *implementations*. It is closed under
  implementation substitution (rewrite the inner loop, the cost pair moves; the attack does not),
  which is precisely the closure property a cost model needs and a propagation object does not have.
- **Consequence used throughout.** REF-A is a **counted** reference (2^(k-1) r-round encryptions) and
  every executed attack in this repository reports a **measured** figure. The comparison mandated by
  RQ-AES-003 R2(a) therefore runs through a conversion, and that conversion is currently an
  unmeasured scalar. A3-1, A3-3, A3-8 and A3-10 are all about that conversion; A3-5 replaces the
  scalar with a currency.

---

**TO-SEARCH.** The map
`(search procedure S over a declared abstract space, evaluation oracle O) ↦ (best-so-far objective trace B(n), reachable-state profile |R(r)|, realizable fraction φ(r))`,
i.e. a monotone integer sequence plus two integer sequences indexed by round.

- **Lossy?** Yes. `B(n)` records *that* an objective value was reached at call `n`, never *which*
  point reached it. The trail, the mask, the activity pattern, the differences and the key are all
  discarded; a 4-round trail achieving 25 active S-boxes and a different 4-round trail achieving 25
  produce the identical trace. `|R(r)|` and `φ(r)` retain two integers per round from a set of up to
  2^16 patterns each carrying a 128-bit realization.
- **Is the discard structural?** Yes, and it is the *reason the object is interesting*. `R_A(r)`, the
  set an abstraction declares reachable, is **closed under the round map by construction**;
  realizability is **not** closed under anything. The gap between them is not recoverable from
  either one alone and cannot be removed by re-coordinatising: it is the price of the abstraction,
  and it is precisely what makes an automated search's optimum a *bound* rather than a *trail*.
  A3-2 measures that gap; A3-4 measures whether a search procedure's reported advantage survives a
  competent baseline.
- **Not a repackaging of the trail.** A trail determines its trace; a trace determines no trail. The
  fibre over a single trace value is exponentially large — that fibre is exactly what A1's
  propagation objects track, and it is exactly what this slice discards.

### 0.3 Ceilings that bind every entry in this slice, stated up front

> **CEILING-A3-A (measurement cannot move REF-A).** REF-A does not shrink with round count: reducing
> `r` reduces the cost of one trial, never the number of trials. At `r = 5` for AES-128 it is 2^127
> five-round encryptions against a measured machine reach of 2^42.6 across an entire campaign
> budget. **No entry in this slice can produce a key recovery, and none claims to.** The most any
> entry here can do is (i) retire an unmeasured assumption that a cost claim currently rests on,
> (ii) close a lane with a measured obstruction, or (iii) supply an instrument whose fidelity is
> itself measured. Entries A3-4, A3-9 and A3-10 are ceiling/control entries whose **expected verdict
> is CLOSED**, and that is stated as a design property, not discovered later as a disappointment.

> **CEILING-A3-B (no solver, and this slice is the one that feels it).** There is no MILP, no SAT, no
> SMT, no CP solver, no numpy and no sage on this machine, which forecloses the automated
> division-property / differential / linear search methodology essentially in its entirety
> (DEC-20260731-019 ruling 10). Consequently **not one entry below proposes running a solver.**
> Every entry converts a question that would ordinarily be answered by a solver run into a question
> answered by a self-built enumerator, a DP over ≤ 2^16 states, or a direct measurement — and where
> that conversion is impossible the entry says OUT OF ENVELOPE and stops, rather than estimating
> (AGENTS.md rule 9). A3-2 and A3-4 are the two entries that carry the most of this weight: they
> study *the modelling and baseline choices that dominate a solver's tractability* without needing
> the solver, because those choices are properties of the encoding, not of the search.

> **CEILING-A3-C (infrastructure is not a result).** A throughput receipt, a pinned harness, a
> repaired mutation control and a re-verified round-count convention are INFRASTRUCTURE and never
> count as a result. Several entries below **produce** a receipt as a by-product; each one states
> separately what the *result* is (a locus, a sign, a gap, a closure) and what the *receipt* is, and
> the receipt is never offered as the deliverable.

> **CEILING-A3-D (the resolution clause, inherited verbatim).** Every distinguisher and death-round
> number produced by anything in this slice carries, in the same sentence: a measured false-positive
> rate against a matched random-permutation control at the same data complexity, a round-count decay
> check, **the instrument's resolution in bits**, and a sibling null that isolates the named
> property. The standing example of the failure this prevents is a density statistic with no power
> by construction (random function 0.4995 vs AES 0.4996) once read as a closure, and a bias probe
> resolving to ~2^-8.8 per cell against a 2^-40 threshold — about 30 bits too blunt.

## 1. The ten ideas

### A3-1. The counted-op → measured-second conversion is not a scalar: the amortization-inversion locus in working-set size

**Tracked object → what it discards.** For two implementations `X` (direct) and `Y` (reorganised —
fold-then-reuse, i.e. the partial-sum-shaped rewrite) of **one identical candidate-filtering
subproblem**, the pair
`ρ(W) = ( ρ_ops(W), ρ_time(W) ) = ( counted table-reads of X / of Y , measured seconds of X / of Y )`
as a function of the working-set footprint `W`. This is TO-COST at two points, differenced. It
discards the filter, the key, the data, which candidates survive, and the correctness of either
implementation — two numbers per `W`. It is not invertible in either direction (§0.2): the same
`ρ_ops` occurs at every `W`, while `ρ_time` moves.

**Mechanism.** Every "cost reorganisation" in this repository, and every cost model that converts
counted operations into REF-A's unit, assumes `ρ_time ≈ ρ_ops` up to one global scalar. That
assumption is a claim about the memory hierarchy, and it is false somewhere: `Y` trades a large
number of cheap sequential reads for a smaller number of *scattered* reads into a histogram, and its
advantage in counted operations is bought with a worse access pattern. As `W` grows past L1, L2, L3
and into DRAM, the per-access cost of `Y` rises faster than that of `X`. The interesting content is
not that constants exist; it is that the *sign* of the advantage can flip while the counted-op
advantage is unchanged. `sq_null.c mode_compare6` already prints `measured_speedup` and
`op_count_ratio` side by side — at exactly one `W`. One point cannot exhibit a sign change.

**Prediction (pre-registered).** There exists `W* ≤ 2^33` bytes at which `ρ_ops(W*) > 1.5` (the
reorganisation still wins decisively on counted operations) while `ρ_time(W*) < 1.0` (it loses on the
clock). Equivalently, the *conversion factor* `κ(W) = ρ_time(W)/ρ_ops(W)` falls below `1/1.5` before
the 15 GB envelope is reached. Sharper: `κ` is monotone non-increasing in `W` and spans at least a
factor of 4 across `W ∈ [2^12, 2^33]` bytes.

**Minimal discriminating test.** Build `X` and `Y` over one frozen subproblem whose *output* is
checked identical between them (candidate-set equality, as `compare6` already checks). Sweep `W`
over ~22 geometrically spaced footprints from 4 KB to 8 GB by varying the histogram/table width
only, holding the counted-op ratio analytically fixed and *verifying* it from the counters at every
point. Five repeats per point, median and IQR reported, `getrusage` peak RSS reported per point.
- **Known-answer gate (degenerate setting, answer known in advance).** At `W ≤ 24 KB` the entire
  working set is L1-resident on this machine, so time must track counted operations:
  `κ(W) ∈ [0.9, 1.1]` is required. If it is not, the timer, the counter or the compiler (a hoisted
  loop, a vectorised inner) is broken and **no other point in the sweep may be read**. A second
  degenerate gate: set `X = Y` (same binary, same flags, different argv label); `κ` must be
  `1.00 ± 0.03` at every `W`, which catches a scheduler or thermal artifact masquerading as a
  hierarchy effect.
- **Sibling control.** Repeat the whole sweep with the AES S-box replaced by the identity map. The
  memory-hierarchy effect must be **unchanged** (it is a property of the access pattern, not of the
  table values); if it moves, the measurement is picking up something other than what it names.

**Falsifier.** `κ(W) ∈ [0.75, 1.33]` at every one of the 22 sweep points, with the IQR small enough
to resolve a 25% deviation → the scalar conversion CM-3(a) assumes is faithful across this entire
envelope, the entry closes itself, and the closure is the deliverable: a measured licence to convert
counted operations to seconds with one constant at reduced-round scale. Conversely a single point
with `ρ_ops > 1.5` and `ρ_time < 1.0` establishes the inversion and every "operation-count saving" in
this repository must thereafter be re-quoted with its footprint.

**Charged cost.** Implementation ~300 lines C, two inner loops plus a counter and a `clock_gettime`
harness (the `compare6` skeleton is reusable but must be re-instrumented per point). Compute: 22
points × 5 repeats × 2 arms; the large-`W` points are the ~150 s memory-bound shape, small-`W` points
are seconds; **budget ≈ 3.5 h single core**, and it must be single core because four-core scaling is
unmeasured *and* the shared L3 would confound the sweep — that restriction is a charge, not a saving.
Memory: peak 8 GB at the largest point, inside 15 GB, measured not modelled. Preprocessing: one table
build per point, charged. Data: zero cipher queries beyond the frozen fold input. Machine model:
single core, `-O3 -maes -msse4.1`, THP and turbo state recorded (unrecorded, they are a confounder).

**Ceiling `medium`** — a claim about this machine's cost conversion, not about AES. **Risk
`conservative`.**

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `partial_sum_candidate_ladder|2_pow_20_(k3,k4)_pairs_over_4_diagonals_x_4_MC_rows|2_pow_-8S_wrong_candidate_survival|true_vs_falsified_hint_arms`
(H-AES-d6405d, `sq_null.c` `mode_compare6`). **Why it is not that:** `compare6` measures the
naive-vs-partial-sums ratio at one table size and reports it as a speedup; this tracks the
*ratio of the two ratios* as a function of working-set size and pre-registers a **sign change**,
which a single operating point cannot exhibit and which decides whether an operation count may be
converted to seconds by a constant at all.

`sota_delta: 0` · `novelty_status: unverified`

### A3-2. Abstraction soundness gap: measuring how conservative a propagation model is, and where the false-positive mass appears

**Tracked object → what it discards.** For an abstraction `A` of the state (candidates: 16-bit
byte-activity pattern; 4×3-bit per-column weight vector; per-column branch class), the pair of
integers per round
`( |R_A(r)| , φ_A(r) )` where `R_A(r)` is the forward closure of a fixed start pattern under `A`'s
propagation rules and `φ_A(r) = |{p ∈ R_A(r) : at least one real pair realizes p}| / |R_A(r)|`.
It discards every value, every difference, every trail, every probability and the key: two integers
per `(A, r)` from a set whose members each carry 128 bits of realization.

**Mechanism, and why the discard is structural.** `R_A(r)` is closed under the round map **by
construction** — that is what makes an automated model a model. Realizability is closed under
nothing. The gap `1 - φ_A(r)` is therefore not removable by re-coordinatising and is not recoverable
from `R_A(r)` alone; it is the price of the abstraction, and it is *exactly* the reason a solver's
optimum over `A` is a bound and not a trail. In the literature-free setting this repository operates
in, the modelling choice is normally defended by assertion ("the activity-pattern encoding is
conservative"). *How* conservative is a measurable number, and it has never been measured here.
CAND-RR78-D built a sieve **on** the 16-bit activity pattern precisely because it is conservative;
it did not measure the conservatism.

**Prediction (pre-registered).** (i) `φ_byte-activity(r) = 1.000` exactly at `r ≤ 2` — the
byte-activity abstraction is *tight* for two rounds, which follows from D1–D3-type support algebra
and is re-derivable in seconds. (ii) `φ_byte-activity(3) ≤ 0.5`: at least half the patterns the
activity model declares reachable at `r = 3` have zero realizations at the sampling resolution used.
(iii) The coarser column-weight abstraction has `φ < 1` already at `r = 2`, i.e. **coarsening buys
false positives one full round earlier than it buys state-count savings**, and the state-count
saving at `r = 2` is under 3 bits. Consequence, which is the actual deliverable: on this cipher at
these rounds, the finer abstraction is *both* smaller in effective search space *and* sounder — the
modelling choice dominates and the ranking is measured rather than asserted.

**Minimal discriminating test.** (1) Enumerate `R_A(r)` for `r = 1..4` by exhaustive forward closure
over ≤ 2^16 patterns in pure C — a few milliseconds, no solver. (2) Estimate realizability by
sampling: draw `2^24` pairs with the declared input difference per `(A, r)`, mark the abstract image
of every realized output, and count marked patterns. Sampling gives a **lower bound** on the
realizable set, so `φ` is reported as an upper bound on the gap with its sample resolution attached:
at `2^24` samples a pattern realized with probability below `2^-24` is invisible, so
`φ_measured ≥ φ_true` and the number is quoted as `φ ≤ x at resolution 2^-24`, never as a point
value. (3) Report `|R_A(r)|`, `φ_A(r)`, and the *identity of the largest false-positive cluster*
(which coarse class contributes most unrealizable mass), because that is what a model-builder acts
on.
- **Known-answer gate.** `r = 1` from a single active byte: `R_byte-activity(1)` must be exactly the
  set derivable in advance from "SubBytes preserves support, ShiftRows permutes it, MixColumns has no
  zero entry" — a specific, small, independently computable set — and `φ(1)` must be exactly
  `1.000`, since every one-round pattern in that set is realized by construction. Any deviation
  voids the enumerator and no other round may be read. Second degenerate gate: run the enumerator
  with MixColumns replaced by the identity; `|R(r)|` must then be constant in `r`, which catches a
  propagation rule silently applied twice.
- **Sibling null.** Repeat with the AES S-box replaced by a uniform random bijection. `φ` must be
  **unchanged** to within resolution, because the byte-activity abstraction does not see S-box
  values. If it moves, the enumerator is leaking value information into a support-only model — a
  defect, reported as one.

**Falsifier.** `φ_byte-activity(3) ≥ 0.95` at resolution `2^-24` → the activity abstraction is
essentially tight at `r = 3`, the "abstraction gap dominates tractability" thesis is refuted at this
depth, and the honest conclusion is that at these rounds the encoding choice is *not* where the
difficulty lives. Equally falsifying in the other direction: `φ_column-weight(2) = 1.000`, which
would refute prediction (iii).

**Charged cost.** Implementation ~250 lines C (closure + marker bitmap + sampler). Compute:
3 abstractions × 4 rounds × `2^24` encryptions ≈ `2^27.6` ≈ **3 s of AES work**, plus a few ms of
enumeration; ×8 independent keys and ×2 sibling arms ≈ **under 2 minutes single core**. Memory:
2^16-bit bitmaps, under 100 MB — this entry is the cheapest in the slice and is deliberately ranked
where cost is negligible so that a null costs nothing. Data: chosen-plaintext pairs only, `2^24` per
cell, counted. Preprocessing: none. No solver, no numpy.

**Ceiling `medium`** — a claim about propagation-model encodings on this cipher at `r ≤ 4`, not about
AES security. **Risk `conservative`.**

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `truncated_support_miss_in_the_middle|16_bit_activity_pattern_conservative|sievable_form_measured|impossibility_itself_not_samplable`
(IDEA-20260801-904 / CAND-RR78-D). **Why it is not that:** RR78-D *uses* the conservatism of the
16-bit activity pattern to build a sievable form of an impossibility; it treats "conservative" as a
qualitative licence. This entry makes conservatism the tracked quantity and measures the
false-positive mass it costs, with the round at which it appears as the falsifiable output — which
RR78-D assumed and never measured.

### A3-3. Domination predictor: the measured margin function `D(h)`, calibrated on this repository's own committed domination point

**Tracked object → what it discards.** The scalar function
`D(h) = log2( measured seconds of exhaustive search over the residual key space left by the structured attack given h hint bytes ) − log2( measured seconds of the structured attack itself at h )`,
for `h` in the range where **both** arms are measurable. TO-COST at two points, differenced across a
parameter. It discards the attack's internals, the hint contents, the key, the surviving candidate
set and the certificate — one signed real per `h`.

**Mechanism.** This repository holds exactly one datum on this axis and it is an anecdote: the `r = 6`
hinted partial-sums recovery works and is **DOMINATED**, 69.39 s against ~25 s for exhaustive search
over the same hinted residual at 12 hint bytes. An anecdote at one `h` cannot distinguish "this
construction is dominated" from "this construction is dominated *at this hint budget*", and the
distinction is the whole content of an automatic key-recovery-complexity evaluator. `D(h)` is
monotone in `h` for a structural reason worth stating: increasing `h` shrinks the residual (helping
the brute-force arm as `2^(-8)` per byte) while helping the structured arm at most linearly in the
filtering it removes, so the *shape* of `D` is a prediction with a mechanism, not a curve fit.

**Prediction (pre-registered).** (i) `D(h)` is strictly decreasing in `h` over the measurable window.
(ii) `D(12) < 0` reproducing the committed point to within run-to-run variance. (iii) Any zero
crossing `h*` lies **below** the measurable window, i.e. `D(h) < 0` for every `h ∈ {12,...,15}`;
consequently the hinted lane at `r = 6` is dominated everywhere this machine can look, and the
region `h ≤ 11` is recorded **OUT OF ENVELOPE / DEFERRED_UNBOUNDED** rather than estimated. That
last clause is the entry's discipline: the honest deliverable is a monotone measured curve **plus an
explicit, bounded, named unmeasurable region**, not an extrapolated crossing.

**Minimal discriminating test.** For each `h ∈ {12, 13, 14, 15}` (residual `2^32, 2^24, 2^16, 2^8`
last-round-key candidates respectively): run the existing `attack6n` pipeline at that hint count with
its instrumented counters, and run a matched brute-force-over-residual arm implemented in the same
binary with the same AES-NI path and the same confirmation criterion. Three independent
(key, seed) instances per `h`, median reported with IQR.
- **Known-answer gate (two degenerate settings).** `h = 16`: the whole last round key is given, so the
  residual is a single candidate and the brute-force arm must measure **exactly one** confirmation —
  a time known in advance to within timer noise. If it does not, the residual accounting is wrong and
  nothing else may be read. Second: `h = 12` must reproduce the committed `69.39 s / ~25 s` pair to
  within the measured instance-to-instance spread; the evaluator that cannot reproduce the one point
  it generalises is void (the C1-2 discipline, applied here to an executed run rather than to a
  table of rows).
- **Sibling control.** A *falsified* hint arm at every `h` — the pipeline already supports true vs
  falsified hint arms — which must produce an empty survivor set. A falsified-hint arm that produces
  survivors means the filter is not doing what the evaluator charges it for.
- **Decay/decoy control.** Run the same `D(h)` measurement at `r = 5`, where a certified,
  unhinted recovery exists at 25.66 s. `D(h)` there must be dominated by the *unhinted* attack at
  every `h` — a structural sanity condition: adding hints to an attack that does not need them cannot
  help.

**Falsifier.** (a) `D(h)` non-monotone over `{12,...,15}` → the `h = 12` domination reading is an
artifact of that hint budget and the committed verdict must be re-scoped. (b) `D(h) > 0` at any
measurable `h` → the first non-dominated hinted construction in this repository, reported with
REF-B named in the same sentence and the published frontier declared unadjudicable. (c) The `h = 16`
gate failing → instrument void.

**Charged cost.** The structured arm is ~70 s per instance (measured); the brute-force arm at
`h = 12` is `2^32` six-round trials ≈ `2^32 / 2^26.17` ≈ **57 s single core** — note this is *not*
the committed "~25 s", which was measured under its own conditions, and the discrepancy is itself a
reason to re-measure both arms in one binary rather than to compare across records. Total:
4 values of `h` × 3 instances × 2 arms ≈ **35 min single core**, plus the falsified-hint and `r = 5`
controls ≈ 20 min. Memory: as measured for the existing pipeline (fold table 4×4×1024×8 B per
thread, plus the 2^16-bit parity table); peak RSS measured per run, not modelled. Data: `2^32`
chosen plaintexts per structure, counted and charged in kind. Preprocessing: key schedules charged,
not amortised, since the arms differ in how many they need. Certificate re-verification charged.

**Ceiling `medium`** — a claim about the measured cost of two constructions on this machine, with the
AES-facing part capped at `toy` and no full-round transcription. **Risk `conservative`.**

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `hinted_partial_sums_r6|12_hint_bytes|69.39s_vs_~25s_trivial_alternative|DOMINATED`
(EV-AES-005 / EV-AES-d33b1c OBS-B2-1). **Why it is not that:** that record is one measured point read

### A3-4. Search-guidance honesty control: calls-to-optimum against a competent greedy baseline on a self-computed known optimum

**Tracked object → what it discards.** The best-so-far trace `B_S(n)` — a monotone step function of
evaluation count — for three searchers `S ∈ {guided, greedy, uniform-random}` over the identical
abstract space with the identical evaluation oracle. TO-SEARCH's first component. It discards which
point achieved the value, the trail, the pattern, the differences and the key: a monotone integer
sequence, from which no searcher's *output* is recoverable and under which any two searchers reaching
the same value at the same call are indistinguishable.

**Mechanism.** Claims that a guided (learned, heuristic, annealed) search "finds trails far faster
than random search" are claims about a **baseline**, and the baseline is almost never the strongest
cheap one. Uniform random search over a 2^64-ish abstract trail space is a straw baseline: greedy
hill-climbing on the same objective exploits the *layered* structure of the space (an SPN round
function makes the objective decomposable per round), and decomposability is exactly what makes
greedy competitive. This entry does not propose a guided searcher; it proposes the **control that any
guided searcher must clear**, and it measures how much of a typical advantage the baseline choice
alone accounts for. It is a control entry and its expected verdict is that the advantage is largely
baseline-attributable — a CLOSED-shaped, valuable outcome.

**Prediction (pre-registered).** With the objective "minimum number of active S-boxes over `r`
rounds" and the optimum computed independently by an exhaustive per-round DP over ≤ 2^16 activity
patterns: (i) greedy reaches the exact DP optimum within `2^12` objective evaluations for every
`r ≤ 4`; (ii) uniform random needs more than `2^20` evaluations for `r = 4`; (iii) the gap
`log2(random calls) − log2(greedy calls) ≥ 8 bits`, and that 8+ bits is **entirely attributable to
the baseline choice**, before any guidance is introduced. Any future guided searcher in this
repository must therefore report its trace against greedy, and a guided-vs-random comparison alone is
recorded as uninformative.

**Minimal discriminating test.** (1) Compute the exact optimum by DP: states are 16-bit activity
patterns, transitions are the abstraction's propagation rules with per-column active-count cost,
`r ≤ 4`, ≤ 2^16 states per layer — milliseconds in C, no solver. (2) Run each searcher with a hard
budget of `2^22` evaluations, 32 seeds each, recording `B_S(n)` and the first `n` at which the DP
optimum is attained (or `>2^22` if never). (3) Report the three traces on one axis with their
inter-seed spread.
- **Known-answer gate (degenerate setting with an independently derivable answer).** At `r = 2` the
  DP optimum must equal the MixColumns branch number, **computed here by exhaustive enumeration**
  over weight-1..4 column inputs rather than recalled — a number this repository has twice listed as
  "still not done, minutes of work". If the DP disagrees with the exhaustive enumeration, the DP is
  void. Second degenerate gate: a **planted** objective on a synthetic space with a known unique
  optimum at known Hamming distance; guided and greedy must both find it, random must not within
  budget — this confirms the harness can express an advantage at all, so that a null on AES is a null
  about AES and not about the meter.
- **Sibling control.** Shuffle the objective's per-round decomposition (evaluate the same total cost
  through a randomly permuted round order). Greedy's advantage must **degrade**, because greedy's
  competence is a claim about decomposability specifically. If greedy is equally good on the shuffled
  objective, the mechanism attributed to it is wrong and the entry says so.

**Falsifier.** (a) Greedy fails to reach the DP optimum within `2^16` evaluations at `r ≤ 4` → greedy
is not a competent baseline on this space, the straw-baseline thesis fails, and a guided-vs-random
comparison is not automatically suspect. (b) `log2(random) − log2(greedy) < 4 bits` → the baseline
choice accounts for little and the entry closes itself. (c) The planted-optimum gate showing no
guided/random separation → harness void.

**Charged cost.** Implementation ~200 lines C plus ~80 lines for the DP. Compute: 3 searchers × 32
seeds × ≤ `2^22` evaluations of an O(16) objective ≈ `2^29` cheap ops ≈ **seconds**; the DP and the
branch-number enumeration are milliseconds. Memory under 50 MB. **Zero AES queries** — this entry
consumes no cipher data at all, which is why it is the right thing to run before any guided-search
work is dispatched. Machine model: single core, no solver, no numpy.

**Ceiling `control`** — this is an instrument-calibration entry, it can never satisfy a completion
criterion, and it is proposed on exactly that basis. **Risk `conservative`.**

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `mutation_control_instrument|three_repairs_five_false_green_escapes|coverage_escapes_closable_evasion_escapes_not|in_process_instrument_limit`
(KN-FIND-029). **Why it is not that:** KN-FIND-029 measures escape rates of a *correctness* control —
whether a broken instrument is caught. This measures *attribution*: whether a reported advantage
belongs to the method or to a weak comparison arm. Different failure mode, different tracked object
(a best-so-far trace, not an escape count), and it additionally discharges the twice-named open item
of computing the MixColumns branch number and the S-box DDT/LAT maxima by enumeration rather than
recall.

### A3-5. Evidence bits per measured second: a common currency in which distinguishers of different shapes can be ranked at all

**Tracked object → what it discards.** For an instrument `I`, the pair `(Λ_I, T_I)` where `Λ_I` is the
**measured** expected log-likelihood ratio in bits that one experiment of `I` accumulates in favour
of the AES arm against its matched random-permutation control, and `T_I` is the **measured** seconds
that one experiment costs on this machine; the currency is `Λ_I / T_I`. It discards the statistic's
identity, the tracked cipher object, the round count, the data, the key and the direction of the
effect — one non-negative real per instrument. Two entirely different instruments with the same
evidence rate are identified, which is the point.

**Mechanism, and why the discard is structural.** This repository currently ranks distinguishers by
**depth** ("survives to `r`"), which is an ordinal on one axis and cannot compare a deterministic
`r = 4` zero (integral: 16/16 balanced, 53 s per `2^32` sweep) with a `r = 5` rate excess (yoyo:
59 hits against a null of 4.0 at `N = 2^32`). Those are not the same kind of evidence and depth
cannot see the difference. Λ is the unique additive functional of a measurement that answers "how
much did I learn", and dividing by measured seconds is what makes it a *cost* statement rather than a
statistical one — which is exactly GOAL-AES-003's mandate. The discard is structural because Λ is
invariant under any sufficient re-encoding of the statistic (relabelling buckets, changing the test's
parameterisation) while the instrument is not: the fibre over a Λ value is the whole space of
statistics with that power.

**Prediction (pre-registered).** (i) Ranking by `Λ/T` **differs** from ranking by depth on at least
one pair of this program's own measured instruments. (ii) Specifically: the `r = 4` full-diagonal
integral delivers at least `2^5` more evidence bits per measured second than the `r = 5` yoyo at its
measured exposure, because a deterministic all-or-nothing readout at a `2^-184`-scale null tail
concentrates enormous Λ into 53 s, while a 15x rate excess on a Poisson mean of 4 concentrates a few
bits into a much longer run. (iii) Consequently "deeper" and "stronger per unit of machine" are
**measurably different orderings** in this repository, and any campaign ranking that uses only depth
is leaving measured evidence on the table.

**Minimal discriminating test.** Mostly re-analysis of committed receipts, which is why it is cheap.
(1) For each of four instruments with committed counts (full-diagonal integral at `r = 4`; 1-byte
delta-set integral at `r = 3`; `r = 5` yoyo excess; per-cell influence bias at `r = 3`), compute Λ
from the *measured* null and alternative statistics using the exact Poisson/Bernoulli LLR, with the
null taken from the matched control arm and never from a model. (2) Re-measure `T` for each on
today's machine at a reduced but identical exposure so the seconds are comparable and current.
(3) Report `(Λ, T, Λ/T)` with the resolution of each Λ in bits.
- **Known-answer gate (two degenerate settings).** (a) A synthetic biased-coin instrument with
  analytically known Λ: the meter must return it to within 1% at `10^7` draws. (b) **The
  zero-information setting**: feed the meter two streams from the *same* distribution (fair coin vs
  fair coin). It must return `Λ = 0` to within its stated resolution. This second gate is not
  decoration — it is the direct guard against the failure this campaign already made once, where a
  statistic with no power by construction (0.4995 vs 0.4996) was read as a closure. A currency that
  cannot meter zero is not a currency.
- **Sibling null.** Meter the *same* instrument against a control that differs only in the property
  being isolated (e.g. random-bijective-S-box arm rather than random-permutation arm). Λ against the
  wrong control must be materially smaller; if Λ is the same against both, the instrument is not
  measuring the property its label names.

**Falsifier.** If the `Λ/T` ranking coincides with the depth ranking on **all four** measured
instruments, the currency adds nothing at this scale and the entry closes itself — a legitimate
CLOSED verdict, and a useful one, because it would licence continuing to rank by depth. Numeric
form: `|rank_Λ/T − rank_depth| = 0` for all four, with each Λ resolved to better than 0.5 bits.

**Charged cost.** Implementation ~150 lines (LLR meter + two synthetic gates) plus a re-timing
wrapper. Compute: re-timing four instruments at reduced exposure ≈ **under 10 minutes single core**;
the synthetic gates are seconds. Memory: bounded by the largest instrument re-timed; the integral
sweep's counter is the binding one and is charged at its measured footprint. Data: no new cipher data
beyond the re-timing runs, counted. Preprocessing: none. **Declared limitation, charged not hidden:**
Λ computed from a committed receipt inherits that receipt's coordinator-side provenance and its
exposure; where the committed null is itself modelled rather than measured, the instrument is
excluded from the ranking rather than metered on a modelled null.

**Ceiling `medium`** — a claim about how this repository's own measured instruments compare per unit
of machine, not about AES. **Risk `representation_changing`** — it proposes a new unit of account for
every distinguisher claim in the campaign.

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** RQ-AES-003 R6's binding requirement that every distinguisher claim
carry a measured false-positive rate against a matched random-permutation control at the same data
complexity, plus its resolution in bits. **Why it is not that:** R6 fixes the *control* and the *data
complexity* so that a claim is honest; it does not supply a way to compare two honest claims of
different shapes. This fixes the *unit of account* and denominates it in measured seconds, which is
the axis GOAL-AES-003 exists to adjudicate.

`sota_delta: 0` · `novelty_status: unverified`

### A3-6. ROC, not ratio: the per-instance decision power of a rate excess, and the overdispersion nobody has measured

**Tracked object → what it discards.** The measured operating curve
`{ (τ, FPR(τ), TPR(τ)) : τ ∈ ℤ≥0 }` of the **single-instance** decision rule "declare AES if the
instrument's count `≥ τ`", estimated over `N` independent `(key, seed)` draws of the AES arm and `N`
matched control draws, together with the two arms' **overdispersion** `Var/Mean`. It discards the
counts' provenance, the key, the probe geometry, the texts — a three-column table plus two scalars.
It is not recoverable back to any count stream.

**Mechanism.** Every headline object in this campaign is a **rate excess**: 59 hits against a null of
4.0 at `N = 2^32`; a 13.5–15.6× rate at `r = 5`; 14 vs 1 at matched exposure against an ideal
permutation. A rate excess is an *aggregate* statement and says nothing about whether a **single**
instance can be classified, because classification depends on the spread, not on the ratio of the
means. The campaign's own S-box-substitution arms are the evidence that the spread matters and has
not been quantified: a 27-arm random-S-box reference distribution with **mean 15.48 and range 6–25**
places all five AES readings in its interior. A ratio of means computed against a distribution that
wide is a different object from a decision rule, and the two have never been separated here.

**Prediction (pre-registered), with its arithmetic frozen first.** Under pure Poisson behaviour at an
exposure of `2^30` — where ~14 events are expected under BATCH-009's measured rate against a control
mean of ~1 — the ideal operating point is `τ = 6` giving `TPR > 0.99` at `FPR < 2^-10`. The
pre-registered prediction is that the **measured** curve falls materially short of that ideal, and
that the entire shortfall is overdispersion: `Var/Mean ≥ 2.0` on the AES arm across keys and seeds.
Equivalently: the effective number of independent bits the instrument delivers per instance is
strictly less than the Poisson calculation implies, and the deficit is the measurable deliverable.

**Minimal discriminating test.** 32 AES draws and 32 control draws at the largest exposure the arms
can both reach, each with an independent key and seed; build the empirical ROC and AUC; report
`Var/Mean` per arm; report the resolution of the AUC estimate at `N = 32` (binomial, and it is
coarse — that coarseness is stated in the same sentence as the AUC, never after it).
- **Known-answer gate (two degenerate settings).** (a) Feed the meter two synthetic Poisson streams
  with known means `λ1 = 14`, `λ0 = 1`: the measured ROC must match the analytic ROC within binomial
  error at `N = 32`. (b) Feed it **two identical** streams: AUC must be `0.5 ± resolution`. Gate (b)
  is the anti-self-deception gate — an ROC meter that cannot return chance on identical inputs will
  manufacture a distinguisher from any pair of arms.
- **Controls, all mandatory.** Round-count decay arm at `r = 6` and `r = 10`; sibling null isolating
  the property (random-bijective-S-box arm, which is *not* the same control as the random-permutation
  arm and must be reported separately); structure-destroyed arm.
- **Named envelope boundary, charged not hidden.** The *matched random-permutation* arm at `2^30` is
  currently **OUT OF ENVELOPE**: the pair-storage architecture costs 8.590 GB at `2^26` and cannot
  reach `2^26`, let alone `2^30`, within any plausible memory budget. This entry therefore either
  waits on the O(1)-memory-per-query ideal-permutation construction that GOAL-AES-003 ranks first, or
  runs with the control arm declared missing and reports **only** the AES arm's overdispersion —
  which is itself the un-measured quantity and is worth the run. Using `r = 10` AES as the
  permutation surrogate conflates the decay control with the null and is explicitly not done.

**Falsifier.** (a) Measured `Var/Mean ∈ [0.8, 1.2]` on both arms → the counts are Poisson, the
Poisson ROC applies, the rate excess *does* carry per-instance decision power at reachable exposure,
and the prediction is refuted (an outcome that would strengthen the existing object, and is recorded
as such). (b) Measured AUC within `[0.5 − ε, 0.5 + ε]` at the achievable exposure → the excess
carries **no** per-instance decision power at this exposure; a scoped closure with its resolution in
bits stated in the same sentence. (c) Gate (b) failing → meter void, nothing reported.

**Charged cost.** Implementation ~200 lines (ROC/AUC in pure C — no numpy — plus the two synthetic
gates, which must be unit-tested by hand precisely because there is no library to check against).
Compute: 32 + 32 draws at `2^30` per draw ≈ `2^36` cipher operations for the AES arm ≈ **~17 min on
one core** at 2^26.17/s, or one 1600 s producer task; the control arm's cost is **not estimated** and
is recorded as blocked. Memory: O(1) per query on the AES arm, under 100 MB; the control arm is the
memory-bound one and is exactly what is blocked. Data: `2^35`–`2^36` adaptive queries counted and
charged in kind (this instrument uses a decryption oracle; that is a stronger access model and is
named here, not later).

**Ceiling `toy`** — an AES-facing distinguisher-power claim at `r = 5`, scoped to the tested rounds,
keys, seeds and harness; no full-round transcription. **Risk `high_risk`** — the prediction may go
either way and the control arm may not be buildable inside the envelope.

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `yoyo_word_exchange_orbit|amask_smask_probe_geometry|W_ge_1_counting_predicate|13.5-15.6x_at_r5_dead_at_r6`
(EV-AES-9794e1 OBS-B6-1; EV-AES-a47618). **Why it is not that:** that tracks an aggregate rate ratio
against a null mean and reports the ratio. This tracks the operating curve of a **per-instance**
decision rule and the arms' overdispersion, which a ratio of means cannot expose and which the
27-arm random-S-box spread (mean 15.48, range 6–25) already hints is the binding quantity.

### A3-7. Hull minus characteristic, measured: the per-round differential clustering factor at `r = 2, 3, 4` with an exact `r = 1` gate

**Tracked object → what it discards.** The scalar
`κ(Δ_in, Δ_out, r, K) = ( measured differential probability Pr_x[E_K^r(x) ⊕ E_K^r(x⊕Δ_in) = Δ_out] ) / ( probability of the best single characteristic Δ_in → Δ_out over r rounds, computed here by a self-built per-column DP )`.
One positive real per `(Δ_in, Δ_out, r, K)`. It discards which trails contribute, how many there are,
their intermediate differences, the values, and the key material — the entire internal structure of
the hull collapses to one ratio. Two hulls with the same total mass and completely different trail
sets are identified; the fibre is exponentially large.

**Mechanism, and why the discard is structural.** Wide-trail accounting bounds a *characteristic*;
security arguments then treat the characteristic as a proxy for the *differential*. The gap between
them is named in this repository as **the only crack in O-1's depth bound**, and it has never been
touched with a number in either direction. It is structurally lossy rather than a re-coordinatisation
because the characteristic probability is computed in a model where round transitions are independent
(the Markov idealisation) while the measured probability is not; the two differ by exactly the
quantity the idealisation throws away, and no invertible map recovers the trail decomposition from
the ratio.

**Prediction (pre-registered), with the ladder chosen so exactness is available where affordable.**
(i) `κ = 1.000` **exactly** at `r = 1`: a single S-box transition admits one trail, so hull equals
characteristic by definition. (ii) `κ ≥ 2` at `r = 3` for the pre-registered `(Δ_in, Δ_out)` set with
`Δ_in` supported in one byte. (iii) The per-round growth factor `κ(r+1)/κ(r)` is measurable at
`r = 2 → 3` and is **reported only for the rounds measured**; extrapolating it to `r ≥ 5` or to full
round is barred inside this entry, and no such extrapolation appears in the deliverable. (iv) Sibling
prediction: `κ` measured with a uniform random bijective S-box of the same wiring is **within a
factor of 1.5** of the AES value, i.e. clustering at these rounds is SPN-shape-generic rather than
AES-table-specific — the campaign's own recurring lesson, here made a pre-registered prediction that
can fail.

**Minimal discriminating test.** (1) Compute the DDT exhaustively over `2^16` pairs (seconds; this
also discharges the twice-named open item of computing the S-box DDT maximum by enumeration rather
than recall). (2) Compute the best characteristic by a per-column DP over the DDT — no solver, ≤ 2^16
states per layer. (3) Measure the differential probability: at `r = 2` with `Δ_in` in one byte the
output difference depends on a bounded set of state bytes and the measurement is **exact** by
enumeration; at `r = 3` it is exact over a `2^32` sweep (the 53 s AES-NI shape); at `r = 4` it is
**sampled** at `2^34` with the resolution declared as `≈ 2^-17` and every `r = 4` number quoted with
that resolution in the same sentence. (4) Repeat over ≥ 8 keys — the hull is key-dependent and a
single key cannot separate clustering from a key-specific fluctuation.
- **Known-answer gate.** `r = 1`: the measured differential probability must equal `DDT[Δ_in][Δ_out]/256`
  **exactly**, and `κ` must be exactly `1.000`. Both sides are independently computable; disagreement
  voids the instrument and no higher round may be read. Second degenerate gate: `Δ_out` chosen with
  `DDT[Δ_in][Δ_out] = 0`; the measured probability must be exactly 0 at `r = 1` and the DP must return
  probability 0, catching an off-by-one in the trail enumerator.
- **Sibling null.** The uniform-random-bijective-S-box arm with identical wiring (prediction (iv)),
  and a second sibling replacing MixColumns by a random invertible 4×4 GF(2^8) matrix with all
  entries nonzero — which negates the specific matrix while preserving the *sufficient* hypothesis,
  the KN-FIND-017 discipline about nulls built by negating a sufficient hypothesis.
- **Decay control.** `r = 10` arm at the `r = 4` data complexity; a `κ` materially above 1 at `r = 10`
  is the artifact tell and voids every arm.

**Falsifier.** `κ ≤ 1.05` at `r = 3` across the pre-registered `(Δ_in, Δ_out)` set at the achieved
resolution → clustering is empirically inert at this depth and the crack is not opened at this scale;
recorded as a scoped negative **with its resolution in bits**, and explicitly not as a statement about
any round count not measured. Second falsifier: `κ(1) ≠ 1.000` → instrument void. Third: the
random-S-box sibling differing from AES by more than 1.5× → prediction (iv) refuted and the clustering
*is* table-specific at these rounds, which would be the interesting outcome.

**Charged cost.** Implementation ~350 lines C (DDT, per-column DP, exact and sampled measurement
paths, 32-bit projected difference counter). Compute: `r = 3` exact sweeps at `2^32` ≈ 53 s each
× 8 keys × 4 `Δ_in` ≈ **~30 min**; `r = 4` sampled at `2^34` ≈ 4 min per (key, Δ_in) ≈ **~2 h**;
sibling and decay arms roughly double it. Total ≈ **5–6 h single core**, i.e. multiple producer tasks
— charged, not compressed. Memory: the output-difference histogram is restricted to a 32-bit
projection giving a ~4 GB counter, the ~150 s memory-bound shape, inside 15 GB; the full 128-bit
difference histogram is **OUT OF ENVELOPE** and is not attempted. Data: chosen-plaintext pairs,
`2^32`–`2^34` per cell, counted. Preprocessing: DDT and DP, charged (seconds).

**Ceiling `toy`** — an AES-facing measurement at `r ≤ 4`, scoped to the tested rounds, differences,
keys and resolution. **No statement about `r ≥ 5`, none about full round, and no extrapolation of the
per-round factor appears anywhere in the deliverable.** **Risk `high_risk`.**

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** The open problem "the HULL-versus-CHARACTERISTIC gap in O-1 — the
only named crack in the depth bound; requires >150 bits and no known aggregation approaches it".
**Why it is not that:** the open problem is stated at full round and concludes that no aggregation
approaches the needed 150 bits. This entry makes **no** full-round statement and does not aggregate;
it measures the per-round clustering factor where it is exactly computable, delivering the first
number in either direction, and it treats the >150-bit figure as belonging to a question it does not

### A3-8. Cost-tail charging: the attack-cost quantile object, and whether a single-run margin has an unstated ±

**Tracked object → what it discards.** The empirical distribution over independent
`(key, plaintext-set, seed)` instances of the attack's measured cost, projected to
`(Q50, Q90, Q99, Q99/Q50)` in both wall-clock seconds and instrumented operation count, together with
the distribution of the **survivor count at the decision point**. TO-COST sampled and reduced to four
order statistics. It discards which key, which candidates survived, the certificate, and the run
order; a distribution of thousands of measurements becomes four numbers, and it is not invertible.

**Mechanism.** CM-3(d) charges an attack at `cost/p` with `p` measured over independent instances.
That convention prices the *success probability* but not the *cost dispersion*, and for a
filter-based attack the cost is itself random: the number of candidates surviving the filter and
requiring confirmation is a random variable, and if that variable is heavy-tailed the mean is not the
right charge and a single-run figure is a median dressed as a budget. The one certified result in
this repository has a single reported figure — 25.66 s — and one independent re-execution on one
other key. A re-execution establishes reproducibility of *existence*; it cannot expose a tail, because
two draws cannot.

**Prediction (pre-registered).** (i) `Q99/Q50 ≥ 1.5` in wall clock over 64 independent instances of
the `r = 5` certified recovery, driven by the survivor-count tail rather than by machine noise.
(ii) The survivor-count distribution is over-dispersed relative to the Poisson its filtering rate
implies (`Var/Mean ≥ 1.5`). (iii) The committed 25.66 s lies within 15% of `Q50` but outside `Q99`,
so any REF-A/REF-B margin quoted from a single run carries an unstated upward uncertainty that must
thereafter be carried explicitly.

**Minimal discriminating test.** 64 independent instances of the existing `r = 5` recovery, each with
its own key, plaintext set and seed, each with instrumented counters, measured wall clock, measured
peak RSS, and the survivor count logged at the decision point; certificate re-verified per instance
by the independent wrapper (charged). Report the four order statistics and the survivor-count
histogram, plus the machine-noise floor measured separately.
- **Known-answer gate (two degenerate settings).** (a) A synthetic attack whose survivor count is
  drawn from a **known** `Poisson(λ)` and whose per-survivor confirmation cost is a known constant:
  the meter must reproduce the analytic `Q99/Q50` to within the 64-draw sampling error. (b) A
  **zero-variance** setting: survivor count pinned to exactly 1 and the same confirmation performed
  64 times; `Q99/Q50` must be `1.00 ± timer noise`. Gate (b) separates the tail being measured from
  the machine's own jitter, and without it a thermal-throttling artifact reads as a cryptanalytic
  tail.
- **Sibling control.** Re-run the 64 instances with the **same** key and 64 different seeds. The
  dispersion attributable to the key must separate from the dispersion attributable to the text
  sample; if they are indistinguishable, the tail is not the survivor-count mechanism claimed and the
  attribution is withdrawn.

**Falsifier.** `Q99/Q50 ≤ 1.10` over 64 instances with the noise floor below 3% → cost dispersion is
negligible, single-run charging is safe, and the entry **closes itself while retiring a live objection
to the one certified result in this repository** — a valuable CLOSED outcome, stated as the expected
one is not: the prediction here is that the tail exists. Second falsifier: gate (b) failing → meter
void.

**Charged cost.** Implementation ~120 lines of wrapper plus survivor logging inside the existing
pipeline. Compute: 64 × ~25.66 s ≈ **28 min single core** for the primary arm, plus 64 × the
fixed-key sibling ≈ another 28 min, plus certificate re-verification per instance (charged, not
amortised) ≈ 10 min. Total **under 1.5 h**. Memory: as measured for the existing attack, peak RSS
recorded per instance rather than once. Data: `2^32` chosen plaintexts per instance, counted and
charged in kind; **the data charge is 64× the single-run charge and that is the real cost of knowing
the tail**, stated rather than absorbed. Preprocessing: 64 key schedules, charged.

**Ceiling `medium`** — a claim about the cost distribution of an executed construction on this
machine; the AES-facing content stays at `toy` and no margin is transcribed to any other round count.
**Risk `conservative`.**

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `executed_certified_key_recovery|r5_AES128_full_unrestricted_key_space|25.66s_measured_instrumented|independently_re_executed_certificate`
(EV-AES-005 OBS-5; EV-AES-d33b1c/EV-AES-d8a13e). **Why it is not that:** those records establish
existence, correctness and reproducibility on a second key. This tracks the cost **distribution**
across 64 instances and its tail ratio, which two runs cannot expose, and it makes the single
reported second-count auditable as a quantile rather than as a budget.

### A3-9. The escape spectrum: which fidelity gate has power over which fault class, and the specific zero this predicts

**Tracked object → what it discards.** The **escape matrix** `E[i][g] ∈ {0,1}`, recording whether
injected fault class `i` survives gate `g` undetected, for a declared catalogue of fault classes
partitioned into *cipher faults* (wrong S-box entry, wrong ShiftRows offset, wrong round count, final
round with MixColumns) and *readout faults* (wrong `CW[j]` index, wrong `j`, off-by-one in the
statistic's round index, wrong bucket width, duplicate-draw miscount), against three gates:
`g1` FIPS-197 known-answer vectors; `g2` cross-implementation differential test against an
independently written **bitsliced** path; `g3` the object's own saturating positive control. It
discards the fault's source location, the code, the statistic's value and the cipher's output — a
small 0/1 matrix.

**Mechanism, and the specific prediction that makes it falsifiable.** This campaign has already
produced one false null from a wrong index convention, and its own instrument-limit finding records
three repairs and five false-green escapes with coverage escapes closable and evasion escapes not.
The sharp, checkable claim here is a **structural zero**: `g1` has *provably* no power over readout
faults, because a readout fault leaves the cipher exactly correct and known-answer vectors test only
the cipher. `g2` inherits the same blindness **whenever the readout is shared between the two
implementations**, which is the default and is a design defect that symbol-level independence audits
do not catch (two instruments can share zero symbols and still share a convention). Therefore the
only gate with power over the readout is `g3`, the saturating positive control — and an object with
no saturating control has, by this argument, **no fidelity gate at all**. That is a statement about
instrument design that can be measured and can fail.

**Prediction (pre-registered).** `E[cipher fault][g1] = 0` for all cipher faults (all caught);
`E[readout fault][g1] = 1` for **all** readout faults (none caught) — the structural zero;
`E[readout fault][g2] = 1` when the readout is shared and `= 0` when it is independently rewritten,
which makes the *scope of the independence audit* the deciding variable; `E[readout fault][g3] = 0`
for at least 80% of readout faults. Consequence: symbol-level instrument independence is necessary
and **not sufficient**, and the sufficient condition is an independently authored readout plus a
saturating control.

**Minimal discriminating test.** Build the fault catalogue (≈ 20 classes, each a one-line patch,
each with its own inference stanza per the artifact-provenance gate); apply each to a small
distinguisher pipeline; run all three gates; fill the matrix. The bitsliced path is written once and
is the real implementation cost; it doubles as the second instrument for any future measurement, but
**that reuse is infrastructure and is not the result**.
- **Known-answer gate (two degenerate settings).** (a) Inject a fault known in advance to be caught
  by `g1` — flip one S-box table entry — `g1` must fire. (b) Inject the **identity fault** (a patch
  that changes nothing): **no** gate may fire. Gate (b) is the false-positive check on the gates
  themselves; a gate that fires on the identity fault would make the whole matrix meaningless in the
  favourable direction.
- **Control.** Two independently authored readouts for one object, differing only in convention
  choice, cross-checked on a saturating input where the correct output is derivable in advance
  (e.g. an `r = 2` arm where a statistic saturates by theorem). This is the arm that decides whether
  `g2` with an independent readout actually recovers the lost power.

**Falsifier.** (a) `g1` catches **any** readout fault class → the claimed structural zero is wrong and
the whole power-separation thesis collapses; reported as written. (b) `g3` catches fewer than 50% of
readout faults → the saturating control is not the backstop it is claimed to be, which would be the
most consequential negative available here, since several proposed instruments in this repository
rely on exactly that backstop. (c) The identity-fault gate firing → catalogue void.

**Charged cost.** Implementation: the bitsliced AES path ~400 lines and is the dominant cost; the
catalogue and harness ~250 lines. Compute: 20 classes × 3 gates × small runs at ≤ `2^22` encryptions
each ≈ `2^28` total ≈ **~4 s of AES work**; wall clock is dominated by compilation, ~20 builds. Memory
negligible. Data: none beyond the harness's own. Machine model: single core; the bitsliced path is
written for portable 64-bit words rather than AVX2, because AVX2 availability on this machine is
**unmeasured** and assuming it would be exactly the uncharged resource this slice forbids.

**Ceiling `control`** — instrument-fidelity content, cannot satisfy a completion criterion, and is
proposed on that basis. **Risk `conservative`.**

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `independent_yoyo_instrument|rc8probe_zero_shared_symbols_with_v2_v4_lineage|byte_locality_replicated|cipher_substitution_uninformative_at_2_pow_24`
(EV-AES-acddd0 OBS-B11-1), with `mutation_control_instrument|...` (KN-FIND-029) as the secondary
neighbour. **Why it is not that:** rc8probe established *symbol-level* independence of one whole
instrument and used it to replicate a finding; KN-FIND-029 measured escapes of a mutation control
over a proof machine. This measures the **power of each gate against a partitioned fault catalogue**
and predicts a specific structural zero — that cipher-correctness gates have exactly zero power over
readout faults, so symbol-level independence without readout independence buys nothing on the class
of faults that has actually bitten this campaign.

### A3-10. Ceiling entry: the anytime-progress axis, on which the definitional reference dominates every construction in this repository — expected verdict CLOSED

**Tracked object → what it discards.** The **elimination trace**
`E_A(t) = log2( number of master keys that attack A's own emitted certificate-of-exclusion has ruled out after t measured seconds )`,
a monotone real function of measured time. It discards which keys were excluded, the attack's
structure, the data, the round count and the key: one monotone curve, from which no attack and no
certificate is recoverable. Two completely different attacks with the same elimination profile are
identified.

**Mechanism.** REF-A eliminates keys at exactly the measured trial rate, so
`E_REF-A(t) = log2(7.555e7 · t)` on one core — strictly positive for every `t > 0`, and it is the
*only* construction here with that property. Every filter-based attack in this repository emits
nothing at all until its fold completes: it either returns the key or it returns nothing, so
`E(t) = 0` for all `t < T_complete`. On the anytime axis — evidence per elapsed second when the budget
may be truncated at any point — the definitional reference is therefore not merely competitive but
**strictly dominant over every structured construction this campaign has built**, and that is a
measurement rather than an argument. The consequence that bites: a structured attack's advantage is
entirely an end-effect, so a partially-run structured attack has literally zero value, whereas a
partially-run REF-A has measurable value. Any campaign-budget reasoning that pro-rates a structured
attack across producer tasks is invalid, and this entry says so with a curve.

**Prediction (pre-registered), and its expected verdict.** `E_A(t) = 0` for all `t < T_complete` for
every construction currently in this repository — the `r = 5` certified recovery, the `r = 6` hinted
recovery, the `r = 7` probe — while `E_REF-A(t) > 0` for every `t > 0`. **Expected verdict: CLOSED,
the ceiling holds.** This is a legitimate and valuable entry precisely because the expected verdict is
CLOSED: it converts an implicit assumption about budget accounting into a measured statement, and it
names, in the same breath, the one modification that would open the axis (below).

**Minimal discriminating test.** (1) Measure `E_REF-A(t)` directly: run the trial loop for 60 s with
an exclusion counter, on the same binary and the same AES-NI path, and report the measured slope
beside the committed 2^26.17/s figure — a decay-free, machine-current anchor. (2) Instrument each
existing attack to emit a certificate-of-exclusion at every internal checkpoint it *can* honestly
emit one, and record `E(t)` for 8 instances at `r = 5`. (3) Report both curves on one axis with
measured wall clock.
- **Known-answer gate (degenerate setting whose answer is known in advance).** Build an artificially
  **interruptible** variant of the `r = 5` attack that emits partial exclusions after each of the four
  diagonal folds — by construction it *must* show `E(t) > 0` at the three intermediate checkpoints,
  with the number of excluded keys at each checkpoint computable in advance from the filter's own
  arithmetic. If the meter reads zero on the interruptible variant, the meter is broken and the main
  reading is void. This gate does double duty: it is also the named repair, because an attack that
  can emit partial exclusions is an attack with a nonzero anytime profile.
- **Control.** A trivially anytime construction (a partial exhaustive search over a declared key
  subset) must show the analytic slope; a trivially non-anytime construction (a fixed-cost function
  that returns only at the end) must show a step. Both are known in advance and both must read
  correctly before any real attack's curve is interpreted.

**Falsifier.** Any existing construction in this repository showing `E(t) > 0` strictly before
completion, at a level above the meter's resolution → the ceiling is **false as stated**, the anytime
axis is live, and the entry is withdrawn rather than repaired. Numeric form: `E(t) ≥ 1 bit` at any
`t ≤ 0.9 · T_complete` in any of 8 instances.

**Charged cost.** Implementation ~150 lines (checkpoint emission, exclusion counter, interruptible
variant). Compute: 60 s for the REF-A slope, 8 × ~25.66 s for the `r = 5` instances, plus the two
control constructions ≈ **under 10 minutes single core**. Memory: unchanged from the existing
pipeline, peak RSS recorded. Data: as the existing attacks, counted. **Charged honestly and stated
plainly: this entry is cheap because it measures a shape, not a magnitude, and its value lies in
closing an accounting question rather than in producing a cryptanalytic number.**

**Ceiling `control`** — an accounting/ceiling entry; it cannot satisfy a completion criterion and does
not try to. **Risk `representation_changing`** — it adds an axis to the Pareto comparison CM-3 R2(e)
mandates (time, memory, data, success probability), and an added axis changes what "dominated" means.

**Nearest prior-art neighbour.** `Hellman_rainbow_TMTO|table_covers_2_pow_128_key_space|precomputation_alone_equals_reference|dominated_on_memory_axis_too`
(MEAS-GOAL-AES-002-005 / NC-1). **Why it is not that:** NC-1 closes TMTO by arithmetic on total
precomputation, on constructions that *have* precomputation, and its axis is memory. This entry is
about constructions with no precomputation at all and its axis is the **time-profile of evidence
emission**; the two closures share the conclusion "the reference is not beaten" and share no
mechanism, no object and no arithmetic.

## 2. Dependencies, ordering, and what this slice deliberately does not contain

**Ordering.** A3-2, A3-4 and A3-9 consume **no cipher data and no measurable AES budget** (seconds,

modesty. No four-core budget is declared anywhere, because four-core scaling is assumed and not
