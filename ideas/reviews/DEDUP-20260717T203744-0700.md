## Semantic exclusion rules

The following are duplicates or controls unless a new mathematical operation removes
the recorded obstruction through exact source output and the full descent:

- same-field isogeny, curve-model, factor-base, term-order, solver, or parameter changes;
- explicit large-prime or source tables, fixed-curve advice, and post-hoc selectors;
- dense resultants, quotient-algebra backends, reporter substitutions, or aggregate
  certificates whose source lift remains the original search;
- relation correctness without `B+sigma` independent rows, rank `B`, verified factor
  logs, masked target descent, and final `[x]P=Q` verification;
- count, trace, phase, cycle, characteristic-class, or categorical objects whose exact
  decorations materialize the full source incidence table.

For `B=N^beta`, every candidate charges setup, inverse relation density, per-attempt
work, exact source lift/output, `B+sigma` rows, linear algebra, blind descent, candidate
ambiguity, verification, and memory. Pollard rho has time exponent `1/2`; memory-matched
BSGS has time and memory exponents `1/2`. Promotion requires every complete charged
exponent to be strictly below `1/2`, not merely one local kernel.

duplicates, censored attempts, output, rank, factor-log linear algebra, and peak memory
