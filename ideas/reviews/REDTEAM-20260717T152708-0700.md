## Replacement `048` theorem audit

The repaired Fourier algebra is correct for nonzero `x`:

`hat(F_Q)(k)=hat(F_P)(k*x^(-1))`, so a matched nonzero pair `j,k` gives
`x=k*j^(-1)`.

That correctness does not make the contracted predicate family viable. For the frozen
bounded-degree coordinate-character predicates, standard Weil–Deligne/elliptic hybrid
character-sum bounds give

`sum_(R in E(F_p)) chi(f(R))*psi_k(R) = O(cond(f)*sqrt(p))`

when the associated sheaf is nontrivial. Fixed conductor therefore gives normalized
nontrivial coefficients `O(N^(-1/2))`, contradicting the contract's required
`N^(-0.20)` scale. At that threshold, generic coefficient estimation is dense-scale and
cannot beat rho.

`048` is distinct from `001`'s addition-incidence witness extraction and `045`'s affine
hard-bit decoder, and it is a close dual-frequency sibling of `011`. Semantic distinction
does not overcome the theorem. A future successor would require a degree-growing
predicate family and must charge conductor, degree, coefficient/SLP construction,
evaluation, query complexity, memory, and coefficient multiplicity; that is a new
hypothesis, not a repair to `048`.

Additional contract defects preserved in the review evidence:

- the AGS significant-coefficient theorem was not yet bound to exact query, precision,
  failure, memory, and polynomial-in-`1/tau` costs;
- fixed numeric thresholds do not establish an `N^(-delta)` asymptotic;
- `x=0`, zero-frequency anchors, real-signal conjugate symmetry, and the resulting
  `+/-j` candidates were not completely specified;
- the declared target-cell product substantially exceeded `maximum_runs: 6000` unless a
  run was redefined as a documented batch; and
- curve enumeration, family aggregation, confidence accounting, collision output, and
  cached-query memory needed exact definitions.

These defects make contract `048` non-dispatchable independently of the theorem-level
spectral-flatness rejection.
