## Semantic decision rule

A candidate counted as operation-distinct only when its mathematical operation and
information flow differed from every ledger owner and canonical idea. The following
remained duplicates or controls unless a new operation removed the recorded obstruction:

- same-field isogeny, coordinate, solver, backend, or parameter variants;
- explicit large-prime, source, edge, matrix, tensor, or witness tables;
- post-hoc selectors and correctness/relation-only certificates;
- dense resultants or aggregate approximations without exact source replay;
- data structures whose construction consumes the relation-source incidence they query.

The minimum accepted source interface is a target-labelled, subset-stable **exact
restricted-existence decision** under arbitrary dyadic deck restrictions, followed by
charged `O(log B)` bisection and singleton verification. Counts, all witnesses,
multiplicities, and canonical sections are unnecessary stronger controls. Every route
must also collect independent factor rows, solve and verify factor logarithms, apply the
same operation to scalar-blind `Q+[t]P`, remove `t`, and verify `[x]P=Q`.

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

`B^(5/4)`, complete time and memory exponents at most `0.45`, `1,000` independently
verified rows, and `100` blind descents. Rho time and BSGS time/memory remain exponent
