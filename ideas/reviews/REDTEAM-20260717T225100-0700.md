## Primary-literature audit

Every new record has at least two direct primary-source URLs. The second read checked the
constructive framework and the strongest nearby obstruction, not merely ECDLP keyword
overlap. No checked source supplies the missing complete source-generating operation:

- Maltsev CSP algorithms do not evade the exact coset closure theorem;
- Terwilliger, bispectrum, Tannakian, vanishing-cycle, mirror, and turnpike results expose
  aggregate structure rather than an endpoint-computable exact source inverse;
- Denef–Pas cells do not imply a uniform symmetric-fiber Skolem selector;
- folded AG and HDX/sheaf decoders assume the received word or local views that the ECDLP
  mechanism must construct;
- Lubin–Tate theory gives formal torsion and local extensions, but its formal logarithm
  annihilates finite torsion; and
- flat-extension theorems begin with moments supplied and do not construct IDEA-133's
  compact elliptic target-local functional.

The late IDEA-121 theorem receipt correctly distinguishes a scoped route negative from an
unconditional arithmetic-circuit lower bound. No literature absence is presented as proof
of novelty.
