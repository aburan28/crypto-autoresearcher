   Jacobian/Fitting ideal. A separately defined polar center requires a versioned record.
2. `099` treats primitive `S_d/A_d` monodromy only as a conditional sufficient
   obstruction. Its unconditional disposition is a semantic backend merge plus a missing
   charged block-to-source lift.
3. `100` rejects a target-to-source path that starts after relation-algebra construction;
   it makes no dimension-only runtime or memory lower-bound claim.
4. `101` scopes its `N`-state argument to the proposed finite-Heisenberg
   Schrodinger/delta-source realization and explicitly permits faithful one-dimensional
   cyclic characters outside that model.
5. `103` defines the positive-dimensional signed addition family, its geometric fibers,
   and the discrete accepted factor-base subsets separately. Faithful tropicalization of
   a skeleton is a nearby control, not proof of source injectivity or inversion.
6. `104` charges a public witness-free initializer; a supplied supported warm state is a
   forbidden decomposition oracle rather than a zero-cost starting condition.
7. `107` permits a continuation branch to leave and later return to the finite factor
   base, then charges return density, branch switching, full-orbit continuation, output,
   ambiguity, extension, and verification.
8. `108` includes Muller's primary skein-algebra source in addition to Bonahon-Wong and
   does not transfer their state-sum results to finite-field elliptic addition.

All twelve records retain at least two nearby primary sources. The checked literature
supports the named neighboring operation, not a complete generic prime-field ECDLP
algorithm below rho. Every novelty claim therefore remains unverified.

complexities. A local exponent below `1/2` receives no promotion if any complete-path time
