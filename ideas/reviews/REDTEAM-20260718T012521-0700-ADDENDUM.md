## Corrected findings

### `146`: rejected at an all-arity scoped no-go

The affine/WNU/Cauchy–Davenport proof is sound for every arity. If the arity is
divisible by `N`, idempotence and equal WNU coefficients are inconsistent; otherwise
the operation is barycentric averaging and cannot preserve a proper non-singleton
factor base. Growing arity is not an escape. Only a signature that neither exposes nor
primitive-positive defines the group-addition graph remains outside scope. The retired
contract records the proved no-go, classifies the existing theorem receipt separately
from prospective artifacts, and permits zero runs.

### `147`: rejection narrowed to the frozen event encoding

The rare-satisfaction versus bad-event obstruction is supportable for the declared
independent-leaf/projective-addition event system. It is not a theorem against every
conceivable dependency encoding. Any successor must provide its own equivalent local
event family and violation oracle without importing source completion.

### `148`: ordered signed slot encoding repaired

The original `B` multilinear variables could not distinguish repeated points across
slots and could merge or cancel permutations. The record now freezes `10B` variables
`z_(j,S,epsilon)` for five ordered slots, factor-base points, and signs. Standard
isolation uses weights in `{1,...,20B}`; the degree-five substitution fits in
`F_p[t]/(t^(100B+1))`; and `10B` typed logarithmic-direction evaluations are charged.
These are review parameters for a future new-ID successor, not an executed experiment.

### `149`: one type-correct source branch only

The disjunctive “scalar digit or source atom” claim mixed two cost models. The retained
negative now fixes exact signed source-atom output. A direct scalar-digit decoder is a
different mechanism. A successor must also type the affine code domain and alphabet:
the order-`N` scalar index and naturally `F_p`-valued observable do not inherit the
cited lifted-code field structure without an explicit charged conversion.

### `150`: moved from deferred to rejected/scoped

Standard Gabidulin decoding can recover a correctable error vector; that is not the
missing operation. Under the declared separable addition-compatible point encoding,
a target-determined linear syndrome induces a homomorphism from the order-`N` subgroup
to a characteristic-`p` additive space. Since `N` is prime to `p`, the map is zero.
This is the IDEA-140 prime-to-`p` additive obstruction in a Moore wrapper. A joint
nonlinear encoder avoids that theorem only by reinstating the missing P1434 source
compiler and therefore needs a new ID.

The rank-metric representation cost is also explicit: Gabidulin length `n_K` over
`F_(p^m_K)` requires `n_K<=m_K`; indexing `B` positions needs `n_K>=B`, so
`m_K>=B`, and an explicit length-`B` word carries at least `B^2` base-field
coordinates. The retired contract records the scoped no-go and remains unapproved with
zero runs.

### `151`: FI equivariance narrowed correctly

Simultaneously relabelling labels and attached coordinates is equivariant. What fails
is treating `S_B` as a symmetry of one fixed numeric specialization while holding its
coordinates fixed. FI finite generation can describe representation type; it does not
compress the coordinate payload or labelled source output needed for factor logs and
descent.

### `152`: deferred only with canonical kernel atomization

A vector-space kernel basis is not a set of point atoms: arbitrary basis changes mix
sources. The theorem gate now requires a multiplication/module action, joint
eigenspectrum on reduced fibers, and full primary/nilpotent local-algebra splitting on
nonreduced fibers, with the splitter charged in `q,q_m`. The multigraded neighbor
Eisenbud–Erman–Schreyer, *Tate Resolutions for Products of Projective Spaces*, is now
cited. `152` remains the cohort's sole deferred record.

### `153`–`157`: scoped wording and literature repairs

- `153` retains the corrected boundary: one faithful character can separate cyclic
  torus elements, but scalar-blind embedding, typed readout, and inversion relocate the
  torus DLP; no `|T|`-character lower bound is claimed.
- `154` separates logical state count from succinct description, transition evaluation,
  endpoint-coordinate, and inverse costs. `N` logical states alone do not imply `N`
  explicit memory.
- `155` now requires one explicit affine tuple geometry and projection family. Switching
  components are family-specific controls, not a universal Radon nullspace theorem.
- `156` attributes the grid coefficient formula to Schauz rather than Alon and labels

  evaluator.
- `157` records that the degree-two component containing a known odd start is a path,
  not a cycle. The record supplies no locally computable elliptic pairing theorem, and
