## Deduplication rule

A solver substitution, parameter change, same-field isogeny variant, explicit
large-prime or source table, post-hoc selector, dense resultant, aggregate
invariant, or relation-only certificate was classified as a duplicate or
control unless a new mathematical operation removed the recorded
endpoint-to-exact-source obstruction. A different vocabulary, representation,
or faster backend did not count as a new mechanism by itself.

The following current boundaries were applied:

- P1523: an exact globally composable label on the prime-order target group is
  constant or injective; nonhomomorphic/list-restricted support laws remain
  outside that theorem.
- P1524: raw pairwise Kummer trace/norm is the complete `S3` branch polynomial;
  source-complete composition is the occupied norm/resultant backend.
- P1525: exact linear one-witness target/source incidence satisfies
  `M>=S`, `T>=B*N/S`, and hence `max(T,M)>=N^0.6` at `beta=1/5`; nonlinear
  implicit-batch/multirow output remains outside scope.
- P1526: ECFFT's auxiliary tree does not transport target addition, while a
  target isogeny is injective on the prime subgroup; explicit support occupancy
  returns the `B^3` boundary.
- P1527: for the frozen canonical `psi_c(x)=x+c+1/x` family on
  `y^2=x^3+1`, simultaneous transformed trace/norm invariance has only the
  deck-fixed curve component and a bounded nonfixed residue at `c=0`.
- P1528: a same-field rational target-isogeny kernel preserving the near-prime
  subgroup has size `N^o(1)`, and kernel-coset preimages collapse to duplicate
  factor-log columns. Extension-field, non-group, and genuinely non-Cartesian
  intertwiners remain outside that scoped theorem.

## Cost and complete-path audit

Every record freezes `E,P,N,Q,F,B=N^beta`; constructs its operation without
source advice; applies it to known-log endpoints; independently verifies all
exact signed sources and multiplicities; collects at least `B+sigma` ranked
rows; solves and verifies factor-base logarithms; applies the identical frozen
operation to fresh masks `Q+[r]P`; subtracts the mask; retains ambiguity; and
accepts only after `[x]P=Q` verifies.

For setup `N^a,N^a_m`, reciprocal relation/target densities
`N^delta,N^delta_t`, query and exact inverse `N^q,N^q_m`, independently ranked
rows/query `N^r`, output and ambiguity `o,u`, and factor-log linear algebra
`N^ell,N^ell_m`, the common complete model is

`lambda=max(a,beta+delta+q-r+o,ell,delta_t+q+o+u,beta)`

`mu=max(a_m,q_m,beta+o,ell_m,u)`.

Promotion requires both exponents at most `0.45`; values strictly between
`0.45` and `0.50` are inconclusive and non-promoting; either exponent at least
`0.50`, one missed or false source, hidden source advice, or an omitted setup,
output, rank, factor-log, descent, verification, or memory term falsifies the
scoped version. No candidate meets a promotion gate.
