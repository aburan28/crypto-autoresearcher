# ECDLP-IDEA-121 semantic deduplication and assignment

## Scope

This review resolves the unassigned shared-bivariate common-norm receipt identified by
the `109`-`120` corpus closeout. It compares the exact operation, source obligations,
cost model, and full descent path against the closest existing hypotheses and ledger
boundaries. It is research triage, not experiment approval or breakthrough evidence.

## Candidate operation

The candidate keeps one symbolic `Theta(B^2)` source-marked P1510 circuit `H(U,W)` and
asks for the source-labelled common factors of `Res_U(T,H)` and `Res_U(F,H)` without
specializing at `Theta(B)` selector roots or expanding either degree-`Theta(B^3)` norm.

The load-bearing novelty is not a resultant identity or generic factorization. It is a
circuit-preserving common-norm operation with an exact inverse to target, start, and four
within-transition source labels.

## Literature and cost screen

The current algebraic two-relation-matrix modular-composition cost
`soft-O(n^((omega+3)/4))` becomes `B^((omega+3)/2)` at `n=B^2`, which cannot be
strictly below `B^(5/2)` for `omega>=2`. Fixed-point truncated resultants and all dense
norm/fiber-product routes are cubic or worse. Kedlaya-Umans near-linear finite-field bit
complexity is the only surviving literature exception, but no checked source reduces a
bivariate product-circuit common norm with exact source jets to a bounded number of
ordinary modular compositions.

## Verdict

Assign the mechanism a new ID, `ECDLP-IDEA-121`, because its shared symbolic selector
and common-norm operation are outside the semantic fingerprints of `053`, `068`, `106`,
`117`, and `120`.

Disposition is `deferred_ku_circuit_common_norm_reduction_required`, not active or
approved. The exact identities and planted controls establish only that the input can be
described with `Theta(B^2)` leaves. Standard consuming routes are independently scoped
negative. The missing circuit-to-Kedlaya-Umans reduction, source inverse, rank, factor
logs, blind descent, and complete exponents prevent dispatch.

Any future contract must reference IDEA-121, retain `review_required` status, and permit
only the theorem action in the hypothesis. No elliptic scaling run is admissible.

## Claim boundary

IDEA-121 is toy, heuristic, model-bound, novelty-unverified, and theorem-deferred. A
compact circuit, exact norm identity, standard-route negative, planted common factor,
valid relation, or recovered toy source is not a generic below-rho ECDLP algorithm or a
breakthrough.
