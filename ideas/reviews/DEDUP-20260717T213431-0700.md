## Semantic exclusion rules

The screen treats the following as duplicates or controls unless a new mathematical
operation removes the recorded obstruction through exact source output and the full
descent:

- same-field isogeny, curve-model, factor-base, solver, term-order, or parameter
  variants;
- explicit large-prime/source tables, fixed-curve advice, and post-hoc selectors;
- dense resultants, quotient-algebra backends, membership reporters, or solver
  substitutions whose source lift is still the original search;
- relation-only certificates, correlations, traces, regulators, or identities without
  exact source inversion;
- compressed aggregate states whose provenance decoration materializes the full
  incidence object;
- correctness without `B+sigma` rows of rank `B`, independently verified factor logs,
  frozen masked-target descent, and final `[x]P=Q` verification.

For every candidate, Pollard rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` expected group operations with
constant-state memory, and BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Setup, inverse
success density, complete per-attempt work, exact source output, linear algebra, blind
descent, ambiguity, verification, and peak memory are all charged. Promotion requires
every complete exponent to be below `1/2`; a local identity or reporter is insufficient.
