unless a new mathematical operation explicitly removes the recorded obstruction through
exact source output and complete descent:

## Complete factor-base-to-target gate

Every record freezes `E,P,N,F`, factor-base size, arity, signs, masks, setup, exceptional
branches, and ambiguity policy before outcomes. For known public `R_j=[r_j]P`, a
candidate must emit exact signed tuples in `F`, independently verify their elliptic sums,
preserve misses and duplicates, retain exactly `B+sigma` rows of rank `B`, solve and
independently verify every factor-base logarithm, freeze all state, and then apply the
identical operation to fresh `Q+[t]P`. It must enumerate ambiguity, subtract `t`, and
accept only `x` satisfying `[x]P=Q` on the original curve.

Pollard rho costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` expected group operations with constant-state memory;
BSGS costs `N^(1/2+o(1))` time and memory. Setup/advice, inverse density, failed queries,
source output, relation rank, factor-log linear algebra, blind descent, verification,
serialized bytes, field/precision conversions, and peak memory are charged. Promotion
requires complete time and memory exponents at most `0.45`; a proved or lower-95% bound
at least `0.50`, or one source error, falsifies the corresponding arm. A local identity,
reporter, or verifier pass is insufficient.
