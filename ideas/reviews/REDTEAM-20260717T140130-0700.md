## Critical correctness and deduplication findings

1. `013`, `014`, `020`, and `022` collect relations using
   `R=[a]P+[b]Q` and then claim ordinary factor-base logarithm recovery without placing
   the unknown `x` in the linear system. This is the target-dependent base-log error
   previously corrected in the first cohort. Relations must instead use known-log
   `R=[a]P`, or explicitly formulate and charge a joint solve.
2. Strict semantic deduplication does not support `013`, `014`, or `022` as separate
   mechanisms from the occupied `001/012` support-oracle cluster; `020` is a Cartier
   restatement of the `007/012` function/support lane absent a new decoder theorem; and
   `023` is another level/deck-orbit labeling form of `010/011`.
3. `017` is mathematically self-defeating: a homomorphism from an order-`N` group to a
   smaller component group of coprime order is trivial, while a nontrivial image requires
   a component carrying an `N`-factor. It is a useful negative control for `016`, not an
   attack hypothesis.
4. `024` fails in the stated geometric Chow quotient. For `N` prime to `p`,
   `alpha_R=[R]-[O]` is `N`-torsion and its external square has zero Albanese class on
   `E x E`; prime-to-`p` torsion in `CH_0^0` is detected by the Albanese map, so the
   proposed nonzero standard regulator cannot exist. See Milne,
   [Zero cycles on algebraic varieties in nonzero characteristic](https://www.numdam.org/article/CM_1982__47_3_271_0.pdf).
