## Semantic novelty gate

A candidate was allocated an ID only after its named mathematical operation was distinct
enough to state and falsify. Allocation is not promotion. A record remains active only
if the operation removes a recorded obstruction, emits exact factor-base sources,
collects at least `B=N^beta` independent relations, solves and verifies factor logs,
descends an individual blind target, and retains a plausible complete time and memory
exponent below both rho and BSGS.

Same-field isogeny variants, solver substitutions, parameter changes, explicit
large-prime tables, post-hoc selectors, dense resultants, and relation-only certificates
were duplicates or controls unless a new mathematical operation supplied the missing
biconditional and removed its charged obstruction.

The common accounting surface was

`T = C_setup + ((B+sigma)/pi_rel)(C_trial+C_report+C_source+C_output)
     + C_LA(B) + C_descent/pi_desc + C_verify`,

with every setup, coefficient-height, state, branch, output, rank, linear-algebra,
target-descent, verification, and peak-memory exponent compared with matched
`N^(1/2+o(1))` Pollard-rho and BSGS controls.
