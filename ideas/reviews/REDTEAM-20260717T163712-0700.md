## Claim boundary

No experiment ran and no run artifact was fabricated. Every surviving statement remains
toy, heuristic, model-bound, and novelty-unverified. Exact identities, common roots,
short recurrences, smaller unsaturated polytopes, valid relations, or recovered toy
scalars are not breakthroughs. Promotion requires independently verified source
recovery and complete precomputation, relation-density, rank, linear-algebra,
target-descent, verification, and memory exponents below both Pollard rho and BSGS.
