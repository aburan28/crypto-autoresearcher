## Deduplication rule

A candidate can receive a canonical ID only if its mathematical operation changes the
information path, not merely the representation or backend. It must construct from
public elliptic endpoints a target-uniform, restriction-aware, exact existence
operation, preserve signed occurrence provenance under charged replay, complete
factor-base rank and logs, and perform the identical scalar-blind descent. Same-field
isogenies, solver substitutions, parameter changes, explicit tables, post-hoc
selectors, dense resultants, and relation-only certificates remain duplicates or
controls unless they remove the recorded obstruction.

The typed surviving frontier remains:

```text
BuildPairIndex(F_1,F_2), BuildPairIndex(F_3,F_4)
Query2P1(R,I_1,I_2,I_3,I_4,I_5) -> exact Exists
```

with all setup/advice and source state at most `B^(9/4+o(1))`, the complete fresh
target and every dyadic replay query at most `B^(5/4+o(1))`, exact all-strata source
inversion, `Theta(B)` independent factor-base rows, factor-log completion, and blind
descent. It is an unconstructed typed exception, not a lower bound and not P1554.
