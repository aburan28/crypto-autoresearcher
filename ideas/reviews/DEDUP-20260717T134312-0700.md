# ECDLP idea semantic deduplication report — 2026-07-17T13:43:12-07:00

## Scope and claim boundary

This report records the second 12-idea generation cohort, `ECDLP-IDEA-013` through
`ECDLP-IDEA-024`. Before selection, the audit read all 16 substantive entries in
`ledger/` and all 21 substantive files in the stable pre-existing `ideas/` corpus:
12 hypotheses, the README and registry, the artifact instructions, three contracts,
the prior deduplication and red-team reports, and the validator. AppleDouble sidecars
are metadata, not research evidence, and were removed after the audit.

Every survivor is `toy`, `heuristic`, `model-bound`, and `novelty-unverified`.
Primary-source proximity checks bound the claims; they do not prove novelty. No
correctness result, relation, correspondence, or toy scalar recovery is a breakthrough.

## Deduplication method

Each seed was compared by the tuple

`source object | new mathematical operation | target representation | obstruction removed | complete target descent | asymptotic bottleneck`.

Different terminology, coordinates, solvers, parameters, curve models, or factor-base
shapes do not create a new mechanism. A survivor had to name a mathematical operation
not occupied by the ledger or ideas `001–012`, return usable witnesses or a scalar
coordinate, charge applicability and bit memory, and finish with independent
`[x]P=Q` verification. Same-field isogenies, solver substitutions, parameter changes,
explicit large-prime tables, post-hoc selectors, dense resultants, and relation-only
certificates were treated as duplicates or controls unless a new operation removed the
recorded obstruction.

## Rejected as duplicates or controls

Twelve generated seeds were rejected before ID assignment:

1. Longer or adaptive same-field isogeny walks — duplicate of the closed `ISO` lane.
2. Edwards, Hessian, Kummer, or alternate affine-coordinate PDPs — representation control.
3. Gröbner, F4/F5, SAT, XL, tensor, or linear-solver substitutions — solver control unless the mathematical quotient changes.
4. Larger arity, a different `beta`, or another summation-polynomial parameter sweep — parameter control.
5. Interval, progression, endomorphism-orbit, or other ordinary structured bases — duplicate of the tested factor-base lane unless a decoder removes sparsity/search cost.
6. Explicit large-prime and double-large-prime tuple tables — storage-shift control, not a new operation.
7. Post-hoc favorable-curve, favorable-target, or favorable-relation selectors — invalid selection, not a mechanism.
8. Dense resultants, determinant zero tests, and zero-minor searches — membership certificates without witnesses.
9. Relation-only divisor, pairing, tame-symbol, or higher-Chow certificates — no factor-base-log or target-scalar descent.
10. Prym or split-Jacobian variants with the same projection/smoothness path — duplicate of `002`.
11. Character-sheaf sparse Fourier restatements of spectral incidence — duplicate of `001` absent a new atom decoder.
12. Lattès deck trees, Kummer ladders, and orbit-polynomial relabelings — duplicate of `006`, `010`, or `011` when inversion remains orbit enumeration.
