## Mathematical and cost-model review

Every record contains a complete declared path from frozen public inputs through exact
source output, verified rows, rank, factor logarithms, frozen blind masking, target
descent, ambiguity enumeration, and final `[x]P=Q` verification, or explicitly names the
direct coordinate's required scalar-blind source atomizer. Pollard rho and BSGS remain
the matched `N^(1/2+o(1))` baselines. Setup, advice, source output, inverse density,
`B+sigma` rows, rank, linear algebra, blind descent, verification, and peak memory are
charged.

For `115`, let the oriented factor deck have size `B=N^beta` and relation arity `t`.
Under the strict-Ulrich square-linear fingerprint, one independent kernel/cokernel atom
per accepted source makes the determinant divisor carry
`L_t(B)=binomial(B+t-1,t)=Theta(B^t)` source length. Local Smith form gives matrix
dimension `s>=L_t(B)/3`. The smallest frozen arm has `t*beta=3*0.18=0.54`, above rho's
`0.50` exponent before kernel work, rank, or descent. This is not a general runtime or
peak-memory lower bound for multiterm cancellation, rectangular Fitting schemes,
succinct implicit operators, generalized kernels, or nonlinear target-specialized
decoders; each excluded representation needs a new ID and full cost audit.

The `117` cost equations remain verbatim coherent between hypothesis and contract:

`lambda=max(s*ell,1+(alpha-4)*ell,2*ell,1+(alpha-5)*ell)`

`mu=max(s_m*ell,m_q*ell,ell)`

The first receipt establishes `alpha=2` for the current explicit/dense routes. The second
shows that the exact source-marked P1510 product-circuit continuation instantiates
`Theta(L^3)` leaves before semijoin work. With `N=Theta(L^5)`, this is setup exponent
`3/5`, above rho's `1/2`. The independent reviewer replayed the stated planted-root
family over `F_65537` for `L={4,6,8,12,16,24,32}`: every case had `L^3` leaves and
dense degree per side, gcd degree `L`, exact recovery of all `L` planted labelled source
pairs, dense-expanded coefficient-vector length `L^3+1`, thin `L^2` control below rho,
and leaf/rho ratios
`2, 2.449, 2.828, 3.464, 4, 4.899, 5.657`. The declared `117` mechanism is therefore
rejected at this scope. No unconditional lower bound is claimed for arbitrary succinct
or target-uniform representations acting before P1510 leaf emission, and streaming peak
memory is not lower-bounded by the cubic generation/traffic count.
