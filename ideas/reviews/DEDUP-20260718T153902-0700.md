

verified rank credit satisfies `0<=r<=o`. Promotion would require campaign
`beta+delta+q-r+o<=0.45`, fresh-target
`delta_t+q+o+u<=0.25`, setup/state/factor-log exponents at most `0.45`, and the
complete path above. Pollard rho remains time exponent `0.50`, memory exponent `0`;
BSGS remains time and memory exponent `0.50`.

disjoint actual-point decks. Colliding known targets must be replaced; blind masks
