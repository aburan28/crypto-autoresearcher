## Semantic deduplication rule

Names, ambient categories, solvers, or coordinate systems did not establish novelty.
Same-field isogeny variants, solver substitutions, parameter changes, explicit
large-prime tables, post-hoc selectors, dense resultants, and relation-only certificates
were classified as duplicates or controls unless an explicit operation removed the
known obstruction. The comparison fingerprint was:

`input scope | representation | new operation | information exposed | descent decoder | dominant charged obstruction`
