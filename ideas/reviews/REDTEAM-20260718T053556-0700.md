## IDEA-212 parity, topology, and exchange audit

For an odd-characteristic skew-symmetric matrix, every odd-order principal
determinant is zero. A principal pivot can be taken only on a nonsingular,
hence even-size, principal block and preserves skew-symmetry/parity. Five source
atoms therefore require a mandatory gadget before they can correspond to a
nonsingular principal minor.

That gadget does not fix the main obstruction. For a universal bounded-degree
kernel, principal-minor feasibility is the determinant-nonzero locus and is
Zariski open on each defined chart. Exact signed five-sum equality to one
endpoint is a proper Zariski-closed condition. They cannot be generically
biconditional. Over `F_p`, reversing zero/nonzero by an indicator such as a
Fermat-power test requires field-size degree/state and violates the proposed
bounded kernel and complete cost gate.

Basis exchange is an additional, conditional obstruction: if the decoded
size-five family is the only feasible layer or a minimum feasible layer, two
generic distinct decompositions of the same endpoint cannot exchange one
source and preserve the sum. Arbitrary auxiliary feasible layers can avoid
that single exchange statement, but then their coding, decoding, and ancestry
state are a new operation that must be specified and charged. The revised
record correctly scopes the rejection to the direct kernel and leaves exactly
one executable theorem/no-go action; it does not preserve an algorithmic
survivor.
