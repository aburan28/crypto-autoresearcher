### IDEA-146 — reject at an exact scoped gate

The initial deferred WNU proposal did not survive. On the prime-cyclic addition
relation, polymorphisms are affine. Idempotent WNU identities force equal coefficients
`c` with `m*c=1 mod N`, hence fixed-arity barycentric averaging. Repeated averaging
closes a nonempty subset containing two points to the whole one-dimensional prime
cyclic group, so a sparse factor base `1<B<N` cannot be preserved. Bounded width also
requires the appropriate operation family, not merely one displayed WNU term.

Disposition: `rejected_exact_sparse_factor_base_WNU_no_go`. The conservative contract
is retained only as retired, unapproved, zero-run theorem evidence. Growing arity,
non-group languages, or a non-affine operation are outside scope and require a new ID.

### IDEA-149 — merge the disjunctive decoder

symbol. It does not produce the hidden shift or a factor-base atom. The proposed
“scalar digit or source atom” message therefore assumes the missing inverse and merges
with the occupied public-predicate/orbit/local-view lanes (`048`, `070`, `110`, `130`).
The path was split so a future source-atom route must build exact tuples and relation
rank, while a mutually exclusive digit route must specify radix, carries, calibration,
candidate assembly, and direct scalar verification.

Disposition: `merged_rejected_public_symbol_not_hidden_shift_or_source_inverse`. The
high-risk contract remains retired, unapproved, and zero-run. One concrete `Phi`, one
output type, and a complete typed inverse would require a new ID.
