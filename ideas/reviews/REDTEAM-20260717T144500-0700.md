## Cost and control audit

- Every record includes a syntactic rho/BSGS model, but several unknown exponents are the
  original DLP under a new name: absolute-distance extraction, module orientation,
  covariant labeling, Artin-word decoding, or zeta-partition refinement.
- Correctness controls with planted logs or oracle torsion coordinates test plumbing only.
  They cannot promote a mechanism whose non-oracle decoder is absent.
- Toy mutual information, low numerical tensor rank, a valid lift, or a relation
  certificate cannot establish an asymptotic speedup.
- Output lists, field degree, coefficient height, full invariant/panel construction,
  applicability failures, and bit memory must remain charged. Omitting any one of these
  can create a false sub-rho exponent.
- The rank proof for `035` and the torsion/target arguments for `017`, `021`, `024`, `030`,
  and `031` are mathematical scoped negatives, not implementation failures.

## Literature and novelty audit

The literature checks in the hypothesis files are mostly appropriate as proximity
boundaries, but they do not prove novelty. Independent checking confirmed especially that
`032` is covered by the known elliptic/infrastructure DLP equivalence, `033` gains no
point-coordinate evaluator from quaternion-order computation, `034` must be contrasted
with the exact anomalous case `N=p`, `035` cannot infer an elliptic-specific exact rank
collapse from general tensor-train machinery, and `036` asks genus-one covariants to
provide a scalar label not present in the cited invariant theory.

Accordingly, all claims remain `novelty-unverified`; for rejected records this label means
only that no exhaustive priority search was attempted, not that a viable mechanism
survived.
