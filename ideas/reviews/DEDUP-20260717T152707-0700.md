## Semantic deduplication rule

The comparison fingerprint was:

`input scope | representation | new mathematical operation | information exposed | complete decoder | dominant charged obstruction`

Names, categories, solvers, and coordinates were not evidence of novelty. Same-field
isogeny variants, solver substitutions, parameter changes, explicit large-prime tables,
post-hoc selectors, dense resultants, and relation-only certificates were duplicates or
controls unless a new operation removed the recorded obstruction. A candidate also
failed if its map killed prime-order information, moved the DLP to an order-`N` target,
returned only a count/relation without atoms, or omitted an applicability, output,
degree/height, target-descent, verification, or bit-memory exponent.
