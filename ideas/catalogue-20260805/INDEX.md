## 2. Screen criteria applied before any identifier is minted

This session filed six proposals on 2026-08-03. **Four have since had a premise
overturned by independent review**, every one caught by a check costing minutes,
and none by the author at writing time:

| proposal | defect | found by |
|---|---|---|
| `82b2b7` | bottleneck misdiagnosed; the projection π it presumes frozen is not in the freeze | Coordinator audit → `DEC-20260805-0e1c91` |
| `202a15` | 17,947 is the *sem's* deficit, not a second *null* deficit | Coordinator conversion → `CORR-20260805-9d2e17` |
| `fa9839` | `R = B` should be `Θ((B/m)·ln B)`; `D_trial` depends on `B` | slice A1 (A1-8, A1-2) |
| `48e258` | known-answer gate cannot detect the error it exists to catch | slice C2 (C2-1) |

That is a measured hit rate, and it is the whole argument for screening before
minting: identifiers are immutable, and 102 unscreened records would carry this
error rate permanently into the ledger.

The screen an entry must pass:

1. **Reachable negative.** Some outcome must count as negative. `DEC-20260805-cc2b32`
   rejected a frozen contract precisely because its positive branch was unreachable
   at freeze while it advertised "decidable with a reachable negative".
2. **No tautological gate.** *A gate must be evaluated somewhere the candidate
   readings actually disagree.* This is the C2-1 lesson generalised: `48e258` gates
   at `w = 2^30`, where all three committed vOW readings coincide, while they
   diverge only in the `w ≥ M ≥ 2^93.3` cap branch that the tested grid never
   exercises. A "PASS by construction" on an unexercised branch is not a check.
   `EXP-SDEG-f7faa8`'s G2 and G5 failed the same way.
3. **Premise verified at source, not at quotation.** Every load-bearing number
   re-read from the committed artifact rather than from another record's citation
   of it. Three of the four defects above were quotation errors propagating.
4. **Instrument fidelity before measurement.** A positive control that can fail,
   evaluated *before* the primary run, not alongside it.
5. **Environment honesty.** SageMath is unavailable and uninstallable here; eprint
   and arXiv are unreachable. An entry needing either must say so and supply a
   fallback, or be marked environment-blocked. An infrastructure failure is never
   mathematical evidence (`AGENTS.md` rule 5).

## 5. What this catalogue does not do

- It does **not** mint identifiers, create ledger records, change any hypothesis
  status, or approve any experiment. Everything here is pre-ledger.
- It does **not** claim novelty for any entry, and does not dismiss any as known.
  Primary sources are unreachable; novelty is unadjudicated in both directions.
- It does **not** advance any exponent, cost, or claim tier. `sota_delta` is zero
  on every axis for every entry, and each carries an explicit `dominated_by`.
- It does **not** resolve any open problem. `KN-OPEN-001`, `-002`, `-013`, `-014`,
  `-015`, `-019`, `-020` and `-024` are all untouched by this document.
- Several entries rest on **unverified recollections** flagged in their slice files
  — AOV-forced skewness of `(P, Nrd/p)`, the `D^{3/2}p^{1/2}` displacement count,
  Ford's divisor-window density at `δ ≈ 0.086`, the Kuhn–Struik `sqrt(L·N)` figure,
  and the Corrigan-Gibbs/Kogan `ST² = Ω(εN)` bound. Each gates specific entries and
  each requires primary-source transcription before use.
