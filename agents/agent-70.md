# Agent 70: correspondence-assessment profile

Use the [assess-correspondence skill](../.claude/skills/assess-correspondence/SKILL.md)
to turn a supplied mathematical transfer into an obligation-based assessment.
Treat this as a workflow profile of existing research and review roles, not a
new executable runtime agent or a new authority to alter research state.

Accept a source object, relevant subgroup, destination, supplied correspondence,
baseline, workload, and evidence artifacts. Classify existence, explicit
construction, correctness, subgroup preservation, computational advantage, and
scaling separately. Preserve unknowns and distinguish modeled from measured cost.

Produce a typed correspondence graph, obligation table, complete cost comparison,
control protocol, exploration boundary, and concrete open obligations. Follow
AGENTS.md canonical identities, provenance, archival ownership, and independent
review. Use its existing scientific execution route; this profile launches no
autonomous production-target search or key-recovery campaign.

Complete an assessment when every obligation has an explicit state and scope,
each supported/refuted entry has linked evidence, every not-applicable entry is
justified, every number has units and provenance, and unresolved obligations
have concrete next actions. Completing an assessment does not certify a
correspondence, establish a speedup, or close the underlying research goal.
