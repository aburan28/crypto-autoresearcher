# Proposed single-target stopping experiment

This is an input for Coordinator design, not a protocol, experiment approval, implementation, or result. It follows the second-ranked route in DEC-20260921-6572be.

The reviewed parent RUN-KIC-e3dbed has an exact target-determining prefix in every one of its 24 held-out transcripts. Median prefix 73 rows, median full-rank prefix 79.5 rows; paired gap median 7.5, range 1–35. These are posthoc transcript properties with no measured runtime saving. The parent diagnostic and its hash are bound by the decision. All inputs have internal provenance.

The current coefficient system is A f + b d = a modulo r. A consists of the first K stored coefficients; b is the final stored coefficient (r - coefficient_b) mod r; a is the separately stored coefficient_a. The target is uniquely determined when a linear combination w satisfies w A = 0 and w b = 1. The same combination yields d = w a. The proposal is to carry RHS and row-combination coefficients through elimination and terminate on a pivot in the final target-variable column. No full factor-base log vector is needed to certify this output.

Candidate production behavior should differ only by RHS/certificate-aware elimination, target-only stopping, and the necessary truthful status/receipt/verification path. Keep original K=75 eta=1/256 slot capacity and Bloom sizing from the R1 allocation-only ARM baseline. Do not depend on or select a P2 capacity outcome. Preserve field arithmetic, factor-base membership and orbit labels, relation search order and policy, deterministic RNG, all source guards, and one-worker settings. The collector must not read the known fixture scalar. Use the scalar only to construct the public target and check the returned answer.

Charge all online elimination and certificate work. Emit and independently recompute certificate weights against every admitted original row, checking w A=0, w b=1 and w a=returned d. Preserve full independent factor-base membership validation, all relation group-identity validation, and final [d]G=Q validation. Do not claim FULL_RANK or that factor-base logs were computed when only the target was identified. The former full-solution validation can be replaced only by a complete certificate and group verification for the requested single-target output, with the cost included and the difference explicit.

Proposed finite test: 24 fresh public known scalars, disjoint from all R1 and P2 cases; three arms (unchanged R1 IC baseline, target-only IC, unchanged common-ARM rho); six permutations cycled four times for 72 serial fresh processes. No density/capacity selection or prior-target pooling. Derive scalars from full SHA256 of CSIC53-TARGETSTOP-v1-case-XX, 1 + digest_little_endian mod (r-1). Derive independent direct/rho uint64 seeds from first eight little-endian digest bytes of CSIC53-TARGETSTOP-v1-direct-seed-XX and CSIC53-TARGETSTOP-v1-rho-seed-XX. Same seed for the two direct arms. Stop on collision without substitution.

Primary candidate/rho median wall ratio <0.95 with fixed 10,000-resample paired-bootstrap 95% upper endpoint <1; all 24 pairs must have valid known-answer and telemetry checks. Secondary candidate/baseline wall, CPU, RSS, actual admitted row counts and stages. Use random.Random('CSIC53-TARGETSTOP-BOOT-v1'), 24 draws per resample, type-7 percentiles at .025 and .975. Whole-process spawn-to-wait4-reap boundary, one worker, fresh directory/process, no retained state, 240-second watchdog, 8 GiB cooperative Darwin footprint guard, 50 ms footprint sampling, requested 1 ms wait4 polling, no retries/drops. Build and source-preparation costs separately retained.

Controls before measurement: exact synthetic linear systems with known solutions and target identifiable before full factor rank; zero target column never identifies; target unit row does; duplicate/nonzero-scaled rows preserve the result; inconsistent RHS fails explicitly; certificate is independently recomputed. Use deterministic generated singular and full-rank systems at the actual scalar modulus and explicit small prime controls. Tiny n13 group cases must verify all curve relations and the target certificate. Do not demand equal final transcripts after the target-only arm stops; do demand exact common relation-prefix equality against the unchanged baseline for matched n13 controls and, offline, for all measured direct pairs up to the earlier stopping prefix. Preserve every prefix mismatch as a failed comparison, with no rerun. No extra n53 timing experiments outside the frozen panel.

Require a new source/case/runner/analyzer admission, immutable snapshot, independent blind numeric review and Coordinator disposition. This proposal makes no runtime, asymptotic, novelty, SOTA, security, or cryptanalytic claim. No target-only implementation or scalar calculation has been performed here.
