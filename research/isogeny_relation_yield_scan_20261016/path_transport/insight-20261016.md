## Interpretation and limits

These results support using the saved maps and checkpoint format for the
next implementation stage. The two independent implementations share the
archived graph input and the stored polynomial map coefficients; the integer
verifier recomputes the map action, but it does not rederive the complete
isogeny rational map from the kernel. Sage checks 17 fixed sample points and
the dual identity at each edge. The full 2,048-edge P29 and 256-edge P37
chains remain incomplete. No target stream, random base, decomposition,
matrix rank, linear algebra, same-point solve, or matched rho work was run.

`chain.svg` shows exact checked vertices and model identities; `edge_times.svg`
graphs the recorded diagnostic edge times with their limited scope. Their
editable source is `render_report.py`, and `report.pdf` embeds both. Existing
canonical study graphs were checked: `../graph.svg` describes the archived
full graph plan and has no new yield or verified full-path result to add yet.

The next gate is full-path checkpoint transport with resource monitoring and
independent replay. The prespecified yield experiment then applies the
same-conductor E2/E3 checks, exact random-base sampling, rank prefixes,
simultaneous hypergeometric intervals, and coordinate controls before making
any yield comparison. [internal: `../PROTOCOL-original-draft.md`]
