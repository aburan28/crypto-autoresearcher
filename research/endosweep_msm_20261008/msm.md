# Pippenger MSM with endomorphisms: modelled operation counts

All costs in base-field multiplications M of each curve (S = 0.8 M, I = 100 M, costmodel.py). Modelled expectations, not timings; the model is validated against a counted implementation on secp256k1 (last section).  `mixed`: Jacobian buckets, mADD accumulation.  `batch_affine`: affine buckets, 5.8 M per accumulation addition plus a shared inversion per pairwise-tree round.
