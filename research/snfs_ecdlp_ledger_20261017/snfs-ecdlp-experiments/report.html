What changed the assessment

E3 remains the best open research question, with no demonstrated fast blind solver. Eight target-independent rank-eight cubic pencils were checked. The 92-section interpolation method found 8 of 160 fresh targets and 24 of 24 planted low-section targets. Rho verified every one of the 184 toy target logs. Oracle MITM recovered many logs, but median cold costs were above rho at each size. Query success on a small fixed section set is not a general section-finding algorithm.

The M=1 oracle hybrid was faster after amortization on the three smaller 20-target batches, but not on the largest tested size. This is ordinary bounded group search with shared preprocessing; it is not a new blind section algorithm. Both M=1 and M=2 costs and success counts are retained in summary.json.

E4: exact short-vector counts are 241, 2401, 9121 and 26641 at squared heights 2,4,6,8. At p=16381, 26641 candidates still reached only about 53–70% of the fibre. Candidate count above group order does not guarantee coverage.

A3: 54 completed target attempts: 17 verified SAT answers, 4 verified unsatisfiable cases and 33 censored/unknown attempts. Among resolved cases the minimum cold SAT/enumeration wall-time ratio was 225.3×, including full curve-point table construction, factor-base filtering and verified target solving. This pilot uses a full point table; a production implementation could build a factor base more cheaply. This is one bit-vector implementation on small parameters, not a general SAT or algebraic lower bound.

A2: the selected weak lattice prototype recovered no S3 plants in 18 cases; all nine univariate controls passed. The separate required bivariate validation is missing, so this does not establish a Coppersmith threshold or a negative result on the full method.

D4: the original independence test is mathematically invalid. For nonzero integral W and B<p, B-smoothness excludes p divisibility. The pilot verified zero overlap. A nonzero mutual information caused by that exclusion is not an attack signal. Further work needs a different feature and a costed held-out prediction test.

Controls: torsion-section, Smart, Tate-pairing, split-cover, EDS and class-number controls passed within their stated scopes. Compact zero-dimensional factor-base descriptions exist, but no cheap decomposition follows from that fact. All nine fitted-lift small relations were single-point 2-torsion.
