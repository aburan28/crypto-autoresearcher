# Research direction and remaining execution

E3, supported by E1/E2/E4, remains the most useful conditional research lead in this ledger: finding a bounded-height section through a prescribed fibre point exposes a concrete algorithmic bottleneck. This is a research priority, not a demonstrated attack advantage or a canonical scientific decision. The imported [follow-up](snfs-ecdlp-followup/summary.json) and [audit](snfs-ecdlp-intake/audit-findings.json) give the observations and corrections behind that assessment.

The immediate question is whether a blind solver can improve a completed operation relative to the frozen index-calculus baseline at measured coverage. Record the distance to rho separately. Finding a section alone is insufficient: its coordinates in a certified Mordell-Weil basis, torsion, relation independence, kernel index/saturation, specialization subgroup coverage, and the final verified logarithm all require accounting. A low-height count does not establish surjectivity. Rational-function/projective sections, including specialization to O, require appropriate charts and saturation.

The classical split cubic pencil supplies the rank-eight control already constructed locally. The realization question is now rank growth through generic prescribed ordinary fibres with certified independent sections and checked height conventions. Rank estimates from insufficient point counts, geometric rank substituted for arithmetic rank, or reuse of a known rank-eight example would not answer that question.
