# Four-level m=51 native-rho results

**Execution and integrity: PASS. Inferential validity: FAIL.** The sealed run
completed all 20,480 prespecified solves, but the predeclared held-out A/A
practical-equivalence gate failed. Therefore **all dependent primary-contrast
inferential and equivalence labels are withheld**. The intervals below are reported only as computed
diagnostics; they are not admitted confidence/equivalence conclusions.

This is an observations report. It records one bounded native-endpoint
experiment and makes no hypothesis transition. It does not establish a weak
curve, a causal conductor effect, a transfer advantage, an end-to-end ECDLP
cost, a security-bit change, or the absence of any of those things.

## Executed scope

`2026-10-13T13:11:58.576041Z` in 608.035806 seconds. It executed 3,556 native
setup units over 3,537 distinct models and produced 20,480/20,480 successful
records:

- 12,288 primary inference solves: 3,072 for each of `C`, `A`, `P`, and `B`;
- 8,192 held-out A/A solves: 2,048 for each arm;
- 32 selected parent/bottom pair clusters, every one of the 16 `f_E=271`
  orbits, and all 51 conjugates for every selected `P/B` pair; and
- one native signed-negation action (`mode=0`) throughout, with no point or
  walk-state transport.

All four arms have ambient order `2,251,799,852,369,764`, prime subgroup order
`17,192,986,687`, and cofactor `130,972`. The four endomorphism conductors are
`1`, `271`, `120871`, and `32756041 = 271*120871`.

The frozen gate requires every simultaneous 99% A/A interval to lie strictly
inside the `[-0.15,+0.15]` bit practical-equivalence margin. It failed on A and
B:

| B | +0.039732294 | [-0.079573195, +0.159037783] | **no** |

A crossed the lower margin by `0.003035934` bit and B crossed the upper margin
by `0.009037783` bit. All four intervals include zero, and all four point
estimates have absolute value below `0.040` bit. The failed gate cannot
distinguish bias from insufficient precision and establishes neither drift nor
its absence. The protocol therefore requires the whole primary family to remain
unlabeled. A fresh preregistered replication can add evidence, but cannot
retroactively validate this run.

Exact gate values are in [`aa-summary.csv`](aa-summary.csv), and the preserved
decision is [`setup_robustness_decision.json`](setup_robustness_decision.json).
Because the A/A gate failed, the conditional setup-robustness lane did not

## Interpretation boundary

The narrow result is: four native endpoint levels were measured successfully,
and their calibrated point estimates differed by at most 0.026350098 bit in
the pairwise comparisons. The prespecified inference gate failed narrowly, so
the result remains inferentially indeterminate.

The experiment supplies no executable isogeny evaluator or dual, no transported
subgroup point, and no map construction/evaluation/recovery cost. See
[`transfer-assessment.json`](transfer-assessment.json). It therefore supports
neither a source-transfer weakness nor any security-bit statement. One child
per sampled parent also means within-parent child heterogeneity is not
identified.

[`ARCHIVE.md`](ARCHIVE.md); independent and post-seal integrity observations
are in [`AUDIT.md`](AUDIT.md); unsuccessful or non-triggered paths are retained
in [`ATTEMPTS.md`](ATTEMPTS.md). The frozen pre-run `README.md`, `PROTOCOL.md`,
and `TRANSFER_BOUNDARY.md` remain unchanged as historical freeze records.
