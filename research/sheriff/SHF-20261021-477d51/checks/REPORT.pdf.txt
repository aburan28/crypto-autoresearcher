Question
ic plan and ic bench --auto choose every ic.pipeline stage from earlier measurements: the factor base and
its size, the oracle, the stop rule, the target source. On prime-order curves none of that evidence was measured on,
each plan ran against the alternatives one change away from it (8 targets × 3 rounds + 1 warm-up per arm and
curve). Primary (amendment 1): the registered curves icv1-fp24-tm5099-a6f137c6, icv1-fp26-tm1775-7e8fb6df and
icv1-fp28-t28201-65099091 (log2 r 24.0, 25.3, 28.0). Supplementary: the original protocol’s prime_search curves
find_prime_order_curve(24, 3), (28, 1) and (30, 1) (log2 r 23.5, 27.7, 29.6), unregistered and therefore not named
by slug.

Result
In the counted unit, every choice the planner made was the better one, on all six curves.

Classification
Tooling validation, engineering (AGENTS.md §3); no S improves on a measured baseline and no leaderboard row moves.
Predictions P1–P4 held on all six curves; P5 failed for the doubled base. The oracle, stop-rule and target choices are
confirmed at every band weight, and the size rule in the counted unit. Follow-up, as its own change: a weight-aware
base-size rule. Deviations: amendment 1 (the original curves are unregistered; the registry on main is stale at 2,572
of 8,034 curves and the cover-transfer ledger pins that count); replay counts not fixed by the protocol (12 of 256 per
session); a first launch on a pre-freeze binary was stopped within seconds and discarded unread.
