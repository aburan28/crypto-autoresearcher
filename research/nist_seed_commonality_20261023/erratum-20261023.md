# Additive environment-metadata correction

**Date:** 2026-10-23 UTC. **Applies to:** [report.md](report.md) and
[report.pdf](report.pdf) at commit
`1cd35e7371f27a0f2bfe52cad6b9ddd8f9ff5131`. The original report and
factor receipts remain unchanged.

The sentence in the report's **Completed twist factorizations** section saying
that software versions are preserved in the two factor receipts is too broad.
The `gp_version` field in each receipt is empty. The exact factor commands,
stdin, stdout, stderr, process status, elapsed wall time, host, script digest,
input digest, factors, and product check *are* preserved there. The runner
used `gp --version` but stored only its stdout; this GP installation writes
its version banner to stderr.

[gp_environment_correction.json](gp_environment_correction.json), produced by
[capture_gp_environment.py](capture_gp_environment.py), binds each immutable
factor receipt by SHA-256. A **post-run** check observed PARI/GP 2.17.3 at
`/opt/homebrew/Cellar/pari/2.17.3/bin/gp-2.17` and recorded its binary SHA-256
`1b3464452036a97072a2514f5094bb49188e2ee3089a61514443494f4875ceb5`.
That check does not establish the exact binary identity at the earlier factor
calls. The separate [Sage verifier](sage_verification.json) records Sage
10.10.rc0 and rechecks the factors with proof-enabled primality tests and the
full twist-order products. Sage may use PARI libraries internally, so these
checks are not a claim of independent primality algorithms.

**Unchanged arithmetic conclusion:** Both reported factorizations multiply to
their exact quadratic-twist orders, and their largest prime factors have 147
and 140 bits. The correction changes provenance precision, not the factor
values or the selection-rule result. It adds no historical generator trace or
ordinary-group attack.
