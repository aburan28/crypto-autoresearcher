# Cartier joint: independent proof and control

TASK-20261025-9b6553, 2026-10-25. Derived mathematics, independently checked locally; no formal proof certificate or official scientific transition. The blind geometric argument is retained unchanged in blind-derivation.md. This note adds an independent direct Cartier calculation after comparison with the root proposal.

Let C be the smooth completion of u^4=f(x), f=x^3-3x+b, in odd characteristic p=4m+3. Its regular differential basis is omega1=dx/u^2, omega2=dx/u^3, omega3=x dx/u^3. At a finite branch point u is a uniformizer and ord(dx)=3; at infinity the orders of these three forms are 1,4,0. Their independence and genus3 establish that this is a basis. The involution u->-u acts as +1,-1,-1; the last two forms describe the Prym summand under the degree-power-of-two isogeny E x P -> J_C.

There are two mutually consistent checks that Cartier kills omega2 and omega3.

1. Over the algebraic closure, tau:u->i*u has eigenvalues -1,i,i on these forms. Naturality and p^-1-semilinearity give tau^*C(omega)=C(tau^*omega)=i^(1/p)C(omega)=-i*C(omega) for either Prym form. The -i eigenspace in H0(C,Omega) is zero. It is legitimate to extend scalars even though tau is defined over Fp2; Cartier remains semilinear there.
2. Directly, omega2=f(x)^m dx/u^p and omega3=x*f(x)^m dx/u^p. The rule C(g^p eta)=g C(eta), together with C(sum a_j x^j dx)=sum a_(pn+p-1)^(1/p) x^n dx, applies because x is a separating variable. The polynomial numerators have degrees 3m and 3m+1; both are less than p-1=4m+2. No selected coefficient exists. For omega1 the corresponding expression is f(x)^((p-1)/2)dx/(u^2)^p; the only selected exponent is p-1, and its coefficient is the elliptic Cartier coefficient a.

Thus C_C has matrix diag(a,0,0) in an Fp-defined basis. This does not identify a Cartier matrix with a Hasse-Witt matrix over a general extension: they are semilinear adjoints, and iterates require the appropriate twists. Over Fp, the corrected Achter–Howe §2.5 congruence is chi_X(T) ≡ T^g det(TI-[C]) mod p; its displayed e=1 case applies here. For E it yields a=t mod p; for C it yields chi_C(T) ≡ T^5(T-a).

For completeness, tau preserves P because pi o tau=[-1]_E o pi. On P, tau^2=-1 follows from pi^*pi_*=1+deck and norm zero. Fp-Frobenius satisfies F tau=tau^-1 F=-tau F on P. On an ell-adic realization (ell!=2,p), F is therefore similar to -F, so its degree-four polynomial is even. Reciprocity gives chi_P(T)=T^4+A T^2+p^2. The isogeny decomposition gives chi_C=(T^2-tT+p)chi_P. Reducing this expression modulo p, its T^4 coefficient is A mod p. Comparison with T^5(T-a) proves p|A.

Pairing the four complex Weil roots as alpha,-alpha,beta,-beta gives A=-(alpha^2+beta^2), hence |A|<=2p. Consequently A=k*p for k=-2,-1,0,1,2. These are necessary candidates, with no assertion that all five occur for this family. The actual P-256 residues and gcds in independent-results.json exclude r from all five orders p^2+k*p+1. Thus r has valuation one in #J_C(Fp). Since pi^* embeds the order-r rational source subgroup, this subgroup is the entire rational r-primary subgroup; recovery is [2^-1]pi_* on it. This conclusion concerns Fp-rational points. P[r](Fbar_p) still has r^4 elements.

The degree-two factor decomposition can have a finite 2-primary intersection, which has no r-torsion because r is odd. P denotes the connected identity component of the norm kernel; it is not the finite etale kernel of an elliptic ell-isogeny. All four checked F5 kernels have ell!=p and are finite etale, so geometric connectedness would be a false claim for them. Frobenius stability and rationality of every individual point are different properties.

A concrete control at p=5,b=1: f=x^3+2x+1 is squarefree and Cartier on (dx/u^3,x dx/u^3) is [[2,1],[1,0]] with determinant4. Here p=1 mod4, so Cartier preserves character i instead of swapping it. Independent exact point counts over F5,F25,F125 give 5,29,68 and chi_P(T)=T^4-2T^3-T^2-10T+25. The polynomial is not even, and its T^2 coefficient is not divisible by5. The forbidden extension of the p=3 mod4 argument is thereby falsified. Four additional smooth controls at p=7,11,13,19 are retained; each b=2 companion is singular and is rejected explicitly by the smoothness test.
