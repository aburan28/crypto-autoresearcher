# Batch 054 digest

## Overview
- Strong core theme: ECDLP/curve-DLP cryptanalysis — index calculus (Gaudry–Thomé–Thériault–Diem; Diem; Laine–Lauter), Weil descent and Gröbner-basis methods (Joux–Vitse, Petit–Quisquater, Huang–Kosters–Yeo, Nagao), and GHS-attack classifications (Momose–Chao group).
- A second large cluster of 2025–2026 ePrint-style papers (2026-1000 through 2026-1018) covering MPC/VSS/PVSS, post-quantum constructions (UPKE from FESTA, POKÉ weak keys, PQ e-voting, symmetric ABE), quantum cryptanalysis, and blockchain/messaging protocols.
- Classics present: Canetti–Goldreich–Halevi random-oracle critique, Bellare on negligible functions, Renner–Wolf on information reconciliation/privacy amplification, and the Koblitz–Koblitz–Menezes ECC history paper.
- Includes the 768-bit prime-field discrete logarithm computation (Kleinjung et al., 2017), a landmark NFS-DL record relevant to DLP cost modeling.
- Anomalies: 19.pdf is a garbage one-line extract; 1997-015.pdf and 2005-277.pdf are slash/glyph-encoded (font-remapped) text, only partially readable; 2026-1017.pdf extract is a graphical-abstract/highlights page; several 2026 papers carry future (2026) dates.

## Papers
- **19.pdf** — Not a readable paper: extract is a single garbage line ("¤ + ¤ + ¤ + 100"). No title or content recoverable.
- **192.pdf** — Simple and Tight Bounds for Information Reconciliation and Privacy Amplification (Renato Renner, Stefan Wolf). Tightens information-theoretic key-agreement bounds for general distributions, matching the i.i.d. case.
- **1997-004.pdf** — A Note on Negligible Functions (Mihir Bellare). Defines two notions of negligibility for collections of functions, proves them equivalent, and reconciles variants in the cryptographic literature.
- **1997-015.pdf** — Title unclear (?) — text is slash/glyph-encoded (font remapping). Partially decodable: appears to describe a multi-party computation protocol using a trusted third party and homomorphic encryption. Content not fully recoverable.
- **1998-011.pdf** — The Random Oracle Methodology, Revisited (Ran Canetti, Oded Goldreich, Shai Halevi). Shows schemes secure in the random oracle model that are insecure under any concrete hash implementation.
- **20.pdf** — Dual-Rail Random Switching Logic: A Countermeasure to Reduce Side Channel Leakage (Zhimin Chen, Yujie Zhou). Gate-transition power model shows masked dual-rail gates still leak; proposes DRSL with synchronized inputs.
- **2004-153.pdf** — A double large prime variation for small genus hyperelliptic index calculus (Gaudry, Thomé, Thériault, Diem). Two double-large-prime index calculus algorithms; genus-3 DLP beats Pollard rho at small field sizes.
- **2005-119.pdf** — Index Calculus in Class Groups of Plane Curves of Small Degree (Claus Diem). Heuristic Õ(q^{2−2/(d−2)}) DLP algorithm; genus-3 non-hyperelliptic DLP in Õ(q).
- **2005-277.pdf** — Forms for Elliptic/Hyperelliptic Curves with Weak Weil Restrictions (?) (Fumiyuki Momose, Jinhui Chao). Text slash-encoded; lists classes of low-genus curves over extension fields with weak coverings (GHS-vulnerable).
- **2008-390.pdf** — Elliptic Curve Cryptography: The Serpentine Course of a Paradigm Shift (A. H. Koblitz, N. Koblitz, A. Menezes). Historical/sociological account of ECC's path from mistrust to acceptance.
- **2010-157.pdf** — ECDLP over Small Degree Extension Fields (Antoine Joux, Vanessa Vitse). Improved index calculus on E(F_{q^n}); practical relations on E(F_{p^5}) and oracle-assisted static-DH break on a 130-bit field.
- **2010-158.pdf** — A variant of the F4 algorithm (Antoine Joux, Vanessa Vitse). F4 variant for same-shape polynomial systems avoiding reductions to zero, competing with F5 in algebraic cryptanalysis.
- **2012-146.pdf** — On Polynomial Systems Arising from a Weil Descent (Christophe Petit, Jean-Jacques Quisquater). Heuristic analysis implying subexponential ECDLP over F_{2^n} (n ≳ 2000); current key sizes not threatened.
- **2013-487.pdf** — Classification of Elliptic/Hyperelliptic Curves with Weak Coverings against the GHS Attack under an Isogeny Condition (Iijima, Momose, Chao). Complete classification of genus-2,3 curves with (2,...,2)-coverings in odd characteristic, adding 17 new classes.
- **2014-346.pdf** — Time-Memory Trade-offs for Index Calculus in Genus 3 (Kim Laine, Kristin Lauter). Implements and optimizes Diem's Õ(q) genus-3 algorithm; C++ implementation with memory and parallelization analysis.
- **2015-573.pdf** — Last fall degree, HFE, and Weil descent attacks on ECDLP (Huang, Kosters, Yeo). Introduces last fall degree; solves HFE unconditionally in polynomial time for fixed parameters; casts doubt on first-fall-degree ECDLP claims.
- **2015-805.pdf** — A classification of elliptic curves with respect to the GHS attack in odd characteristic (Iijima, Momose, Chao). Systematic classification of elliptic curves with (2,...,2)-coverings for prime extension degrees d = 2,3,5,7. Companion extension of 2013-487.
- **2015-984.pdf** — Complexity of ECDLP under the First Fall Degree Assumption (Draft) (Koh-ichi Nagao). Using disjoint factor bases, derives polynomial-time ECDLP bounds O(n^{8ω+1}) over F_{2^n} under the assumption.
- **2017-067.pdf** — Computation of a 768-bit prime field discrete logarithm (Kleinjung, Diem, Lenstra, Priplata, Stahlke). Reports NFS 768-bit DL record, parameter choices, and implications for DSA/ElGamal key sizes.
- **2017-1120.pdf** — A Ciphertext-Size Lower Bound for Order-Preserving Encryption with Limited Leakage (David Cash, Cong Zhang). Proves ciphertext expansion lower bound separating OPE from ORE under limited-leakage security.
- **2017-372.pdf** — A crossbred algorithm for solving Boolean polynomial systems (Antoine Joux, Vanessa Vitse). Hybrid method for solving multivariate Boolean systems over F_2 (NuTMiC 2017, HAL deposit).
- **2018-649.pdf** — No-signaling Linear PCPs (Susumu Kiyoshima). First PCP for P that is both linear and no-signaling sound; yields 2-message delegation of computation.
- **2018-921.pdf** — Round Optimal Black-Box "Commit-and-Prove" (Khurana, Ostrovsky, Srinivasan). First round-optimal black-box commit-and-prove: 4-round ZK and 3-round WI from injective OWFs.
- **2018-927.pdf** — Adaptively Secure Distributed PRFs from LWE (Libert, Stehlé, Titiu). First fully non-interactive adaptively secure DPRF in the standard model, with robustness extension.
- **2020-442.pdf** — Fine-Grained Cryptography: A New Frontier? (Invited Talk) (Alon Rosen). One-page abstract surveying fine-grained crypto where adversaries are only polynomially stronger than honest parties.
- **2021-1141.pdf** — Round-Efficient Byzantine Agreement and MPC with Asynchronous Fallback (Deligios, Hirt, Liu-Zhang). Round-efficient BA/MPC secure under both synchronous and asynchronous network thresholds.
- **2021-598.pdf** — Proof of Assets in the Diem Blockchain (Chatzigiannis, Chalkias). Formalizes solvency proof-of-assets for account-based blockchains (Diem/Libra), with light-client size optimizations preserving 66.67% BFT.
- **2022-1573.pdf** — Solving Small Exponential ECDLP in EC-based Additively Homomorphic Encryption and Applications (Tang, Ling, Cai, Shan, Liu, Tang, Qiu). FastECDLP speeds small-exponent ECDLP decryption for EC-AHE at 128-bit security.
- **2022-746.pdf** — Efficient Proofs of Knowledge for Threshold Relations (Avitabile, Botta, Friolo, Visconti). Modular transformation yielding efficient Sigma-protocols for k-out-of-l threshold relations with improved communication.
- **2024-1081.pdf** — Practical Non-interactive Multi-signatures, and a Multi-to-Aggregate Signatures Compiler (Rambaud, Levrat). Addresses verification/setup cost bottlenecks of pairing-based multi- and aggregate signatures used in Ethereum/Diem consensus.
- **2025-1560.pdf** — On the Termination of the HotStuff Protocol Within the UC Framework (Zeng, Dong, Xu). First UC-framework formalization and termination proof of HotStuff under partial synchrony and delay attacks.
- **2026-1000.pdf** — Information-Theoretic Optimistic Verifiable Secret Sharing (Hirt, Liu-Zhang, Marsicano). Matching feasibility/impossibility for VSS adaptive round complexity: 2 rounds iff 3t+k<n (perfect) or 2t+k<n (statistical).
- **2026-1001.pdf** — Distributed Simon's Algorithm with Less Per-Node Qubit Overhead (Li, Zhen, Fan, Hao, Gao). XOR-type periodic function cuts per-node qubits from 2^{t+1}m to m+n−t for distributed Simon's algorithm.
- **2026-1002.pdf** — On weak keys of POKÉ (Tomoki Moriya). Identifies weak keys in the isogeny-based PKE POKÉ; POKÉ-4D is threatened, requiring new parameters that erase its efficiency edge.
- **2026-1003.pdf** — A Blockchain-Based Pre-Verification Access Control Scheme with Vector Commitments and Bulletproofs (Liang, Li, Hu, Yan, Li, Wu). Pairing-free, setup-free on-chain access-eligibility proofs via inner-product arguments for IoT data sharing.
- **2026-1004.pdf** — PQKryvos: Post-Quantum Secure E-Voting With Flexible Ballot Formats and Public Tally-Hiding (Huber, Reisert, Küsters). Lattice-commitment plus hash-based ZK e-voting supporting many ballot formats with tally-hiding privacy.
- **2026-1005.pdf** — More from Less: Composable General MPC with Global Public Verifiability from a Single Enclave Only (Bayreuther et al., KIT). Impossibility result plus new simulation technique giving publicly verifiable TEE-based MPC from one enclave.
- **2026-1006.pdf** — Current trends in AI-Aided Cryptography (Höbbel, Kavalir, Knoblauch, Wiesmaier). Trend survey of 90 publications (2021–2025); cryptanalysis and hashing dominate, protocols and PQC underexplored.
- **2026-1007.pdf** — Quantum Circuit Implementation and Grover's Search on the Lightweight Block Cipher KLEIN Family (Mukherjee, Dutta, Singh, Alexandar, Mandal). Quantum circuits for all KLEIN variants with Grover resource estimates.
- **2026-1008.pdf** — Unified FPGA Design of Kyber and Dilithium with Provable Fault Tolerance (Chowdhury, Mishra, Bhattacharya, Mukhopadhyay). Microcoded shared datapath for both schemes plus probabilistic rejection-sampling fault countermeasure.
- **2026-1009.pdf** — Constant-Online PVSS from CCA2-Secure Threshold Encryption (Zhang, Cai, Kan, Zhang, Yung). Generic framework making PVSS distribution and verification cost independent of shareholder count.
- **2026-1010.pdf** — MINGLE: In-Band Gossip for Key Transparency Split-View Detection in E2EE Messengers (Fasllija, Heimberger, Paul). Opportunistic in-band gossip lets clients detect KT equivocation without third-party auditors.
- **2026-1011.pdf** — Can We Tolerate Small Side-Channel Leakages: The Role of Registers in Glitch-Stopping Circuits (Ovchinnikov, Biesmans, Myny, Nikov, Nikova). Glitch-propagation patterns can hide leakage from TVLA in theoretically insecure masked designs.
- **2026-1012.pdf** — Linear self-equivalence of the known families of APN functions (Baudrin, Canteaut, Perrin). Introduces multivariate projective mappings unifying structure across almost all known APN families.
- **2026-1013.pdf** — Sequence-Level Security for Active Weighted Signature Reconfiguration (Sunghyeon Jo). Rank-exposure guard compiler lifting weighted threshold signature updates to sequence-level unforgeability; implemented over ADAPT.
- **2026-1014.pdf** — Updatable Public-Key Encryption from FESTA (Basso, Fouotsa, Kouider, Kutas, Maino, Marco). Isogeny-based UPKE from dimension-4 FESTA with unbounded updates and formal security proof.
- **2026-1015.pdf** — Comments on "Server-Aided Public Key Authenticated Searchable Encryption..." (Yoshida, Emura). Short note giving keyword-guessing attacks against Cheng–Meng's SA-PAEKS scheme.
- **2026-1016.pdf** — Efficient Homomorphic String Search via TFHE (Narisada, Okada, Nishide, Fukushima). Binary-search pattern matching over TFHE ciphertexts: O(|P|log|T|) comparisons; million-character genomic search in minutes.
- **2026-1017.pdf** — Quantum and Post-Quantum Blockchain: A Systematic Survey (Konara, Nimuthumana, Sayakkara, Mahasinghe, De Zoysa). Survey of quantum/PQ blockchain literature; extract is the graphical-abstract/highlights page.
- **2026-1018.pdf** — Symmetric Attribute-Based Encryption from Minimal Hardness Assumptions (Longo, Sorbera). CP-ABE in a symmetric setting from CRHFs and PRFs, natively post-quantum, with extended access-tree notation.
