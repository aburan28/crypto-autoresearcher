# Batch 071 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by mid-2000s symmetric cryptanalysis: eSTREAM stream-cipher breaks (Achterbahn, Grain, DECIM, SNOW 2.0, Py, WG/LEX, MOSQUITO, HBB) and Wang-style hash collision work (MD4/MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256/HAVAL/Tiger), apparently FSE 2006-era papers.
- A second block of CRYPTO 2006-era theory papers: NIZK/zaps (Groth-Ostrovsky-Sahai), signatures of knowledge, blind signatures, randomized hashing (Halevi-Krawczyk), non-malleable encryption, anonymous HIBE (Boyen-Waters), fuzzy extractors.
- Number-theoretic highlights: Joux-Lercier-Smart-Vercauteren on NFS for medium-prime finite fields, and "Inverting HFE is Quasipolynomial" (Granboulan-Joux-Stern) — the only DLP-relevant items in a batch otherwise light on elliptic-curve/ECDLP content.
- Regev's "Lattice-based Cryptography" is a survey, not a research paper.
- Anomalies: three files are extraction garbage (40470185.pdf token soup; 40470296.pdf mojibake, likely misencoded CJK text; 40470313.pdf hex-font garbage), and 407.pdf has an anomalous short filename.
- File ordering suggests conference-proceedings sequences (4047xxxx ≈ FSE 2006, 4117xxxx ≈ CRYPTO 2006).

## Papers
- **40040601.pdf** — Simplified Threshold RSA with Adaptive and Proactive Security (Almansa, Damgård, Nielsen). Presents a simple, optimally resilient, proactively and adaptively secure threshold RSA scheme via a new rewinding strategy, with UC-framework security proof.
- **40470001.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Achterbahn (Johansson, Meier, Muller). Breaks the eSTREAM stream cipher Achterbahn, including the full version, with about 2^61 steps, exposing flaws in its nonlinear combining design.
- **40470015.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Grain (Berbain, Gilbert, Maximov). Key-recovery attack on the eSTREAM cipher Grain needing 2^43 computations and 2^38 keystream bits via linear approximations of NFSR feedback and filter functions.
- **40470030.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the Stream Cipher DECIM (Wu, Preneel). Finds an initialization flaw recovering half the key bits with 2^20 IVs and a strong keystream bias enabling message recovery.
- **40470042.pdf** — On Feistel Structures Using a Diffusion Switching Mechanism (Shirai, Shibutani). Generalizes active-S-box lower-bound proofs for Feistel ciphers with switched diffusion matrices, removing optimality restrictions and covering arbitrary round counts.
- **40470059.pdf** — Pseudorandom permutation families over abelian groups (Granboulan, Levieil, Piret). Extends differential and linear cryptanalysis to block ciphers over non-bitstring domains, proves generalized piling-up lemmas, and analyzes a toy decimal cipher.
- **40470080.pdf** — A Zero-Dimensional Gröbner Basis for AES-128 (Buchmann, Pyshkin, Weinmann). Efficiently computes a degree-lex Gröbner basis for the AES-128 key-recovery ideal from one plaintext/ciphertext pair and discusses (lack of) security implications.
- **40470093.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the Full HAVAL with 4 and 5 Passes (Yu, Wang, Yun, Park). Practical 2-block collision attacks on 4-pass HAVAL (2^36 computations) and the first better-than-birthday attack on 5-pass HAVAL (2^123).
- **40470115.pdf** — Collisions and Near-Collisions for Reduced-Round Tiger (Kelsey, Lucks). Adapts MD4-family message-modification techniques to Tiger, finding collisions for 17 of 24 rounds and near-collisions for 20 rounds.
- **40470131.pdf** — Analysis of Step-Reduced SHA-256 (Mendel, Pramstaller, Rechberger, Rijmen). Introduces a new perturbation-vector technique yielding collision characteristics for step-reduced SHA-256, without threatening full SHA-256.
- **40470149.pdf** — Improved Linear Distinguishers for SNOW 2.0 (Nyberg, Wallén). Sharper bias estimates of SNOW 2.0's FSM yield a linear distinguisher with bias 2^-86.9, the strongest known at the time.
- **40470169.pdf** — Reducing the Space Complexity of BDD-based Attacks on Keystream Generators (Krause, Stegemann). Divide-and-conquer strategies cut memory of BDD attacks on LFSR ciphers, with experiments on reduced E0, A5/1, and self-shrinking generator.
- **40470185.pdf** — Not a readable paper: one-page extraction of meaningless token fragments ("1IV 2IV 3IV 1m…"), likely a broken figure/diagram page.
- **40470198.pdf** — A New Dedicated 256-bit Hash Function: FORK-256 (Hong et al.). Proposes FORK-256, a software-efficient 256-bit hash claimed at least 30% faster than SHA-256 and secure against known attacks.
- **40470213.pdf** — Some Plausible Constructions of Double-Block-Length Hash Functions (Hirose). Builds 2n-bit compression functions from n-bit components with birthday-bound collision security, plus a new "indistinguishability in the iteration" notion.
- **40470229.pdf** — Provably Secure MACs From Differentially-uniform Permutations and AES-based Implementations (Minematsu, Tsunoo). MACs combining a block cipher with a low-differential permutation; AES-based versions provably secure and 1.4–2.5× faster than CBC-MAC-AES.
- **40470245.pdf** — Searching for Differential Paths in MD4 (Schläffer, Oswald). Automates Wang-style differential path search for MD4, finding over 1000 paths, some with fewer conditions than Wang et al.'s original.
- **40470265.pdf** — A Study of the MD5 Attacks: Insights and Improvements (Black, Cochran, Highland). Explains and improves Wang et al.'s MD5 collision techniques, yielding a faster attack and releasing an open-source "MD5 Toolkit".
- **40470281.pdf** — The Impact of Carries on the Complexity of Collision Attacks on SHA-1 (Mendel, Pramstaller, Rechberger, Rijmen). Refined carry analysis shows the true SHA-1 collision-attack complexity is slightly lower than all published estimates.
- **40470296.pdf** — Not readable: extraction is mojibake (misencoded non-Latin, likely CJK text); title and content cannot be recovered from the extract.
- **40470313.pdf** — Not readable: extraction is hex-glyph font garbage ("/C6/CT/DB…"); underlying text unrecoverable from the extract.
- **40470331.pdf** — The Ideal-Cipher Model, Revisited: An Uninstantiable Blockcipher-Based Hash Function (Black). Exhibits a blockcipher-based hash provably secure in the ideal-cipher model yet insecure under any instantiation, paralleling random-oracle uninstantiability.
- **40470344.pdf** — How Far Can We Go on the x64 Processors? (Matsui). Software optimization of AES (170 cycles/block on Athlon 64), first bitsliced AES and Camellia, and SHA-256/512/Whirlpool performance analysis on x64 CPUs.
- **40470362.pdf** — Computing the algebraic immunity efficiently (Didier, Tillich). Near-optimal O(m^d) algorithm proving a Boolean function has no annihilators or low-degree multiples up to degree d, plus a practical variant.
- **40470378.pdf** — Upper Bounds on Algebraic Immunity of Boolean Power Functions (Nawaz, Gong, Gupta). Gives closed-form upper bounds on algebraic immunity of power functions (Inverse, Kasami, Niho exponents) without running algorithms.
- **40470393.pdf** — Chosen-Ciphertext Attacks against MOSQUITO (Joux, Muller). Breaks MOSQUITO, the last dedicated self-synchronizing stream cipher, with ~2^70 work versus claimed 96-bit security.
- **40470408.pdf** — Distinguishing Attacks on the Stream Cipher Py (Paul, Preneel, Sekar). Exploits output-word bias in eSTREAM cipher Py for a distinguisher needing 2^84.7 key/IVs, well below the design security level.
- **40470425.pdf** — Resynchronization Attacks on WG and LEX (Wu, Preneel). Differential attack recovers 48 key bits of WG with 2^31.3 chosen IVs; slide attack breaks strong LEX given ~2^60.8 random IVs.
- **407.pdf** — A Near-Practical Attack against B mode of HBB (Mitra). First attack on the basic mode of cellular-automata stream cipher HBB: known-plaintext guess-then-determine with 2^50 time complexity.
- **41170001.pdf** — Rigorous Bounds on Cryptanalytic Time/Memory Tradeoffs (Barkan, Biham, Shamir). Formalizes TMTOs via stateful random graphs, proves rigorous lower bounds on online time T=Ω(N²/M² ln N), and gives improved rainbow tradeoffs.
- **41170021.pdf** — On the Power of the Randomized Iterate (Haitner, Harnik, Reingold). Uses the randomized-iterate technique to give more security-preserving reductions for building PRGs from one-way functions and weak-to-strong OWF amplification.
- **41170039.pdf** — Strengthening Digital Signatures via Randomized Hashing (Halevi, Krawczyk). A message-randomization hashing mode that bases signature security on weaker-than-collision-resistance properties without changing hash or signature algorithms.
- **41170058.pdf** — Round-Optimal Composable Blind Signatures in the Common Reference String Model (Fischlin). Two-move concurrently executable blind signatures from general assumptions, extended to universally composable blind signatures.
- **41170076.pdf** — On Signatures of Knowledge (Chase, Lysyanskaya). Formalizes signatures of knowledge (signing as holder of an NP witness), proves UC-style and game-based definitions equivalent, and constructs schemes under standard assumptions.
- **41170094.pdf** — Non-interactive Zaps and New Techniques for NIZK (Groth, Ostrovsky, Sahai). Constructs non-interactive zaps for all NP languages from the Decision Linear assumption via new NIZK techniques.
- **41170109.pdf** — Rankin's Constant and Blockwise Lattice Reduction (Gama, Howgrave-Graham, Koy, Nguyen). Improves analysis of Schnorr's block reduction (approximation factors to their (ln 2)-th power) and shows intrinsic limits via Rankin's constant.
- **41170129.pdf** — Lattice-based Cryptography (Regev). Survey of lattice-based cryptography from Ajtai's seminal work to recent efficient constructions; expository, not new research.
- **41170140.pdf** — A Method for Making Password-Based Key Exchange Resilient to Server Compromise (Gentry, MacKenzie, Ramzan). Generic compiler making any PAKE scheme resilient to server compromise with one extra round and one signature.
- **41170158.pdf** — Mitigating Dictionary Attacks on Password-Protected Local Storage (Canetti, Halevi, Steiner). Password-based storage encryption hardened with human-solvable puzzles (CAPTCHAs) so offline attackers cannot mount dictionary attacks.
- **41170177.pdf** — Rationality and Adversarial Behavior in Multi-Party Computation (Lysyanskaya, Triandopoulos). Introduces a mixed-behavior MPC model with rational and adversarial parties and a mediator-emulating protocol robust against ⌈n/2⌉−2 corruptions.
- **41170195.pdf** — When Random Sampling Preserves Privacy (Chaudhuri, Mishra). Quantifies when random sampling of anonymized tables preserves privacy, linking safe sample size to the number of rare values.
- **41170212.pdf** — Tight Bounds for Unconditional Authentication Protocols in the Manual Channel and Shared Key Models (Naor, Segev, Smith). log*n-round manual-channel authentication with 2log(1/ε)+O(1) manually authenticated bits, proven essentially optimal.
- **41170230.pdf** — Robust Fuzzy Extractors and Authenticated Key Agreement from Close Secrets (Dodis, Katz, Reyzin, Smith). Improved fuzzy extractors: key agreement from correlated secrets at the minimal |W|/2 entropy threshold and first standard-model constructions with errors.
- **41170248.pdf** — On Forward-Secure Storage (Dziembowski). Defines forward-secure storage — huge ciphertexts whose small adversarially computed functions leak nothing even if the key is later revealed — with schemes and composition.
- **41170268.pdf** — Construction of a Non-Malleable Encryption Scheme from Any Semantically Secure One (Pass, shelat, Vaikuntanathan). Transforms any semantically secure encryption scheme into one non-malleable for arbitrarily many messages.
- **41170287.pdf** — Anonymous Hierarchical Identity-Based Encryption (Without Random Oracles) (Boyen, Waters). First fully anonymous HIBE with security in the standard model under the Decision Linear assumption, with applications to keyword search.
- **41170305.pdf** — Fast Algorithms for the Free Riders Problem in Broadcast Encryption (Ramzan, Woodruff). Efficient O(|R||F|) algorithms for optimal free-rider assignment in complete-subtree and other subset-cover broadcast encryption schemes.
- **41170323.pdf** — The Number Field Sieve in the Medium Prime Case (Joux, Lercier, Smart, Vercauteren). NFS variants giving L(1/3) discrete-log algorithms in F_{p^n} for medium p; with function field sieve results, covers all finite fields; includes a 120-digit F_{p^3} record.
- **41170342.pdf** — Inverting HFE is Quasipolynomial (Granboulan, Joux, Stern). Shows asymptotic quasipolynomial complexity of Gröbner-basis attacks inverting the HFE multivariate public-key scheme.
- **41170356.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of 2R− schemes (Faugère, Perret). Efficient Gröbner-basis decomposition of two-round "minus variant" multivariate schemes, breaking proposed challenges in hours and undermining the 2R− design principle.
