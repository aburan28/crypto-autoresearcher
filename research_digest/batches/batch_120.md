# Batch 120 digest

## Overview
- Batch splits cleanly into two halves: ~36 Eurocrypt/Crypto-era 2015–2016 research papers (numeric IDs), followed by 10 LNCS conference proceedings front-covers (Springer ISBN files, ASIACRYPT/PKC/CRYPTO 2018–2022).
- Dominant themes: secure multi-party computation and its round/interaction complexity, obfuscation (diO, iO, post-zeroizing, graded encodings), and lattice cryptography (basis reduction, BKZ, LWE IBE, ring signatures, ideal lattices).
- Notable papers: the Stevens–Karpman–Peyrin freestart collision for full SHA-1, Groth's 3-group-element pairing SNARK, Cramer–Ducas–Peikert–Regev on short generators of principal ideals, and Renes–Costello–Batina complete addition formulas for prime-order Weierstrass curves.
- Several 1–2 page items are position/talk abstracts rather than full papers (Preneel, Collberg, Bhargavan, Prouff).
- Anomaly: 96650356.pdf is unreadable mojibake (encoding garbage); no title or content recoverable.

## Papers
- **96650217.pdf** — Improved Differential-Linear Cryptanalysis of 7-round Chaskey with Partitioning (Gaëtan Leurent). First single-user cryptanalysis of the ARX MAC Chaskey; partitioning techniques cut data/time complexity of differential-linear attacks on 6–7 rounds.
- **96650220.pdf** — New Negative Results on Differing-Inputs Obfuscation (Mihir Bellare, Igors Stepanovs, Brent Waters). Shows sub-exponentially secure diO for Turing machines cannot exist if sub-exponentially secure OWFs (and iO) exist.
- **96650231.pdf** — Reusable Fuzzy Extractors for Low-Entropy Distributions (Canetti, Fuller, Paneth, Reyzin, Smith). First reusable fuzzy extractor tolerating arbitrary correlations between noisy readings, for Hamming noise with near-linear error rates.
- **96650235.pdf** — Recovering Short Generators of Principal Ideals in Cyclotomic Rings (Cramer, Ducas, Peikert, Regev). Rigorously proves the log-unit lattice is efficiently decodable, enabling quantum polynomial-time attack on short-generator principal ideal problems.
- **96650239.pdf** — Indifferentiability of Confusion-Diffusion Networks (Dodis, Stam, Steinberger, Liu). First positive indifferentiability results for confusion-diffusion networks; constant rounds suffice to extend domain of a public random permutation.
- **96650241.pdf** — Multi-Input Functional Encryption in the Private-Key Setting (Brakerski, Komargodski, Segev). Builds adaptively secure multi-input FE from any single-input private-key FE for constant inputs, from varied assumptions.
- **96650247.pdf** — Post-Zeroizing Obfuscation (Badrinarayanan, Miles, Sahai, Zhandry). Initiates post-zeroizing obfuscation study; new tool restricting adversarial encodings from matrix branching programs, applied to evasive circuits.
- **96650251.pdf** — Practical, Predictable Lattice Basis Reduction (Daniele Micciancio, Michael Walter). Experiments showing theoretical lattice reduction algorithms are practical; replaces simulation with closed-form prediction, via dual SVP without dual basis.
- **96650256.pdf** — Secure Computation from Elastic Noisy Channels (Khurana, Maji, Sahai). Feasibility of unconditionally secure MPC from one-sided unreliable ("elastic") noisy channels via correlated private randomness.
- **96650259.pdf** — Essentially Optimal Robust Secret Sharing with Maximal Corruptions (Bishop, Pastro, Rajaraman, Wichs). First robust secret sharing with n=2t+1 achieving share size m+Õ(k), independent of the number of parties.
- **96650260.pdf** — All Complete Functionalities are Reversible (Khurana, Kraschewski, Maji, Prabhakaran, Sahai). Any 2-party functionality enabling general MPC securely realizes oblivious transfer in a fixed direction, hence is reversible.
- **96650262.pdf** — Provably Robust Sponge-Based PRNGs and KDFs (Peter Gaži, Stefano Tessaro). Seeded sponge-based PRNG with input proven robust in the ideal permutation model; also an efficient sponge-based KDF.
- **96650263.pdf** — Randomness Complexity of Private Circuits for Multiplication (Belaïd, Benhamouda, Passelègue, Prouff, Thillard, Vergnaud). Characterizes randomness needed for d-probing-secure multiplication; linear lower bound, quasi-linear constructions, verification tool.
- **96650266.pdf** — Computationally Binding Quantum Commitments (Dominique Unruh). Introduces collapse-binding commitments for the quantum setting, with random-oracle constructions and 3-round statistical ZK quantum arguments of knowledge for NP.
- **96650272.pdf** — On the Size of Pairing-based Non-interactive Arguments (Jens Groth). SNARK for arithmetic circuit satisfiability with 3 group elements and one pairing-product verification equation; plus lower bounds for linear interactive proofs.
- **96650274.pdf** — Nonce-Based Cryptography: Retaining Security when Randomness Fails (Mihir Bellare, Björn Tackmann). Extends nonce-based design to PKE and signatures; hedged extractors keep IND-CCA unless RNG and seed are both compromised.
- **96650278.pdf** — Freestart Collision for Full SHA-1 (Stevens, Karpman, Peyrin). First practical freestart collision for all 80 SHA-1 steps, costing ~2^57.5 compression calls on GPUs.
- **96650279.pdf** — Improved Masking for Tweakable Blockciphers with Applications to Authenticated Encryption (Granger, Jovanovic, Mennink, Neves). Introduces tweakable Even-Mansour construction MEM with efficient masking, yielding parallelizable AE schemes OPP.
- **96650288.pdf** — Tightly Secure CCA-Secure Encryption without Pairings (Gay, Hofheinz, Kiltz, Wee). First DDH-based CCA PKE with tight security independent of challenge/decryption queries, halving ciphertext overhead; extends to tightly secure NIZKs.
- **96650292.pdf** — Faster Algorithms for Solving LPN (Bin Zhang, Lin Jiao, Mingsheng Wang). Improved BKW-style LPN algorithms using cascaded perfect codes, breaking claimed 80-bit security of HB+, HB#, LPN-C and Lapin instances.
- **96650300.pdf** — Constrained Pseudorandom Functions for Unconstrained Inputs (Deshpande, Koppula, Waters). Constrained PRFs for Turing-machine constraints on unbounded inputs from iO and injective PRGs, avoiding knowledge assumptions.
- **96650306.pdf** — Zero-Knowledge Arguments for Lattice-Based Accumulators (Libert, Ling, Nguyen, Wang). Efficient Stern-type ZK proofs of hash-chain knowledge under SIS; yields first lattice logarithmic-size ring signatures and trapdoor-free group signatures.
- **96650310.pdf** — Honey Encryption Beyond Message Recovery Security (Jaeger, Ristenpart, Tang). Strengthens honey encryption with target-distribution semantic security and non-malleability, hiding partial plaintext info under brute-force key search.
- **96650318.pdf** — Structural Lattice Reduction (Gama, Izabachène, Nguyen, Xie). Generalizes SIS/LWE worst-case to average-case reductions to arbitrary finite abelian groups; simpler GSW-style homomorphic scheme with mux gates.
- **96650323.pdf** — Fair and Robust Multi-Party Computation using a Global Transaction Ledger (Kiayias, Zhou, Zikas). Formal UC model with global ledger for financially compensated fair MPC with dishonest majority, plus composition theorem.
- **96650325.pdf** — Improved Progressive BKZ Algorithms and their Precise Cost Estimation by Sharp Simulator (Aono, Wang, Hayashi, Takagi). Optimizes progressive BKZ parameters with a Gram–Schmidt length simulator and cost model; ~50× faster than BKZ 2.0 on SVP challenges.
- **96650336.pdf** — Unconditionally Secure Computation with Reduced Interaction (Damgård, Nielsen, Ostrovsky, Rosén). Proves Ω(nt) message-complexity lower bounds for unconditional MPC of difficult function classes, with near-matching upper bounds for t=1.
- **96650340.pdf** — Automated Unbounded Analysis of Cryptographic Constructions in the Generic Group Model (Ambrona, Barthe, Schmidt). Master theorem and constraint-solving tool gga∞ proving unbounded generic-group-model security automatically.
- **96650345.pdf** — Adaptively Secure Identity-Based Encryption from Lattices with Asymptotically Shorter Public Parameters (Shota Yamada). Two lattice IBE schemes with best asymptotic efficiency; anonymous variant and polynomial-factor LWE variant, plus ABE for branching programs.
- **96650347.pdf** — Complete Addition Formulas for Prime Order Elliptic Curves (Renes, Costello, Batina). First efficient complete addition law for every prime-order short Weierstrass curve, enabling exception-free ECC on NIST-type curves.
- **96650348.pdf** — On the Complexity of Scrypt and Proofs of Space in the Parallel Random Oracle Model (Alwen, Chen, Kamath, Kolmogorov, Pietrzak, Tessaro). Entangled pebbling framework proving Ω(w(n/log n)²) cumulative memory complexity for scrypt against parallel adversaries.
- **96650356.pdf** — Unreadable: extracted text is mojibake/encoding garbage (escaped byte sequences); title and content not recoverable from extract.
- **96650357.pdf** — The Exact Round Complexity of Secure Computation (Garg, Mukherjee, Pandey, Polychroniadou). Relates round complexity of MPC and non-malleable commitments; k-round NMC gives max(4,k+1)-round two-party and multi-party protocols.
- **96650360.pdf** — Online/Offline OR Composition of Sigma Protocols (Ciampi, Persiano, Scafuro, Siniscalchi, Visconti). Efficient (k,n)-proofs of partial knowledge where instances can be specified late, solving an open question on delayed-input OR composition.
- **96650364.pdf** — Non-Malleable Codes for Bounded Depth, Bounded Fan-in Circuits (Ball, Dachman-Soled, Kulkarni, Malkin). Unconditionally secure non-malleable codes resilient to bounded-output-locality tampering, including NC0.
- **96650366.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation from Constant-Degree Graded Encoding Schemes (Huijia Lin). Builds IO for all poly-size circuits from constant-degree graded encodings, constant-degree PRGs, and subexponential LWE.
- **96650382.pdf** — The Future of Cryptography (Bart Preneel). 2-page position essay contrasting cryptography's academic success with deployment failures against mass surveillance; calls on community to respond.
- **96650383.pdf** — Engineering Code Obfuscation (Christian Collberg). 2-page overview of man-at-the-end (MATE) adversary scenarios and the role of code obfuscation/tamper-proofing for software asset protection.
- **96650384.pdf** — Protecting Transport Layer Security from Legacy Vulnerabilities (Karthikeyan Bhargavan). 1-page talk abstract arguing TLS agility leaves broken legacy crypto enabled and downgrade protection assumptions fail in practice.
- **96650385.pdf** — Securing Cryptography Implementations in Embedded Systems (Emmanuel Prouff). 1-page abstract surveying masking countermeasures against side-channel analysis and links to MPC and coding theory.
