# Batch 062 digest

## Overview
- Era/venues: almost entirely 2002–2003 academic cryptography — a large FSE 2003 block (stream ciphers Turing/Rabbit/Helix, block-cipher cryptanalysis), plus ASIACRYPT/PKC/CHES-style papers; filenames look like proceeding article IDs.
- Dominant topics: symmetric cipher design & cryptanalysis (MQ/algebraic attacks, HAVAL collisions, Khazad, traceable ciphers), RSA & factoring (generalized Wiener attack, NFS polynomial selection, RSA-1024/TWIRL estimates, tight reductions, ESIGN security), and ECC/hyperelliptic work (Koblitz scalar multiplication, Picard-curve arithmetic, Tate pairing, index calculus for small genus, AGM point counting).
- Protocol cluster: group signatures (three papers), broadcast encryption / trace-and-revoke / key predistribution (four papers), authenticated key establishment, oblivious transfer, and zero-knowledge arguments.
- Notable papers: Al-Riyami–Paterson "Certificateless Public Key Cryptography" (seminal CL-PKC paper); Coron–Naccache showing Boneh et al.'s k-element aggregate extraction assumption is just CDH; Lenstra et al.'s RSA-1024 factoring estimates.
- Anomalies: 3 extracts are undecodable font-encoding garbage (28870366, 29470042, 29470129); 2 more required hex-glyph decoding but were recovered (28870277 = Wallén, 28870324 = Rabbit); one off-topic item (ancient Chinese padlocks, 28940325); one odd filename ("29.pdf").

## Papers
- **28870277.pdf** — Linear Approximations of Addition Modulo 2^n (Johan Wallén; hex-encoded extract, decoded). Gives more accurate linear approximations of the carry function of integer addition, improving linear cryptanalysis of ciphers using modular addition.
- **28870290.pdf** — Block Ciphers and Systems of Quadratic Equations (Alex Biryukov, Christophe De Cannière). Compares multivariate quadratic systems defining Khazad, Misty1, Kasumi, Camellia, Rijndael and Serpent with respect to algebraic re-linearization attacks.
- **28870306.pdf** — Turing: a Fast Stream Cipher (Gregory Rose, Philip Hawkes). Proposes a software-fast stream cipher (up to 256-bit keys) combining a SOBER-style LFSR with a keyed block-cipher-like mixing function.
- **28870324.pdf** — Rabbit: A New High-Performance Stream Cipher (Boesgaard, Vesterager, Pedersen, Christiansen, Scavenius; hex-encoded extract, decoded). Stream cipher iterating coupled non-linear functions; ~3.7 cycles/byte on Pentium III, with security analysis.
- **28870348.pdf** — Helix: Fast Encryption and Authentication in a Single Cryptographic Primitive (Ferguson, Whiting, Schneier, Kelsey, Lucks, Kohno). High-speed stream cipher with built-in MAC, about twice as fast as Rijndael, suitable for small messages.
- **28870366.pdf** — Unreadable: extract is undecoded custom-font glyph garbage; title and content cannot be determined from the text.
- **28870382.pdf** — Practical Symmetric On-line Encryption (Pierre-Alain Fouque, Gwenaelle Martinet, Guillaume Poupard). Gives a generic countermeasure to blockwise adversaries and the first security proof for CFB mode against such attackers.
- **28870396.pdf** — The Security of "One-Block-to-Many" Modes of Operation (Henri Gilbert). Luby–Rackoff analysis of block-cipher expansion modes; proves the MILENAGE (UMTS) expansion function pseudorandom.
- **28940001.pdf** — Chosen-Ciphertext Security without Redundancy (Duong Hieu Phan, David Pointcheval). Full-Domain Permutation encryption and 3-round OAEP achieve IND-CCA without plaintext redundancy at near-optimal bandwidth.
- **28940019.pdf** — Some RSA-based Encryption Schemes with Tight Security Reduction (Kaoru Kurosawa, Tsuyoshi Takagi). Exactly tight one-wayness for Rabin-Paillier and the first IND-CPA scheme whose one-wayness equals factoring general n=pq.
- **28940037.pdf** — A Simple Public-Key Cryptosystem with a Double Trapdoor Decryption Mechanism and its Applications (Bresson, Catalano, Pointcheval). Revisits a Cramer–Shoup Paillier variant in a different subgroup, yielding perfectly hiding commitments with on-line/off-line tradeoff.
- **28940055.pdf** — Rotations and Translations of Number Field Sieve Polynomials (Jason Gower). CRT-based rotation and translation algorithms producing NFS polynomials with good root properties and small size.
- **28940064.pdf** — On Class Group Computations Using the Number Field Sieve (Mark Bauer, Safuat Hamdy). Explains obstructions to an NFS for class-group computation; such an NFS would imply subexponential algorithms for elliptic-curve-related problems.
- **28940079.pdf** — Parallelizing Explicit Formula for Arithmetic in the Jacobian of Hyperelliptic Curves (Pradeep Mishra, Palash Sarkar). Methodology for provably round-optimal parallel versions of Lange's genus-2 formulas, including side-channel-resistant encapsulated add-and-double.
- **28940097.pdf** — Tate Pairing Implementation for Hyperelliptic Curves y^2 = x^p − x + d (Iwan Duursma, Hyang-Sook Lee). Closed formula for the Tate pairing in characteristic p, improving prior implementations for p = 3.
- **28940110.pdf** — The AGM-X0(N) Heegner Point Lifting Algorithm and Elliptic Curve Point Counting (David Kohel). p-adic point counting for small-characteristic elliptic curves via lifts of modular-curve invariants; implemented for p = 2,3,5,7,13.
- **28940123.pdf** — Key Management Schemes for Stateless Receivers Based on Time Varying Heterogeneous Logical Key Hierarchy (Miodrag Mihaljevic). Broadcast-encryption key management using a reconfigurable key hierarchy with divide-and-conquer subset covering.
- **28940141.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Authenticated Key Establishment Protocols (SeongHan Shin, Kazukuni Kobara, Hideki Imai). Categorizes AKE protocols by resilience to stored-secret leakage and proposes protocols immune to client- and server-side leakage.
- **28940159.pdf** — Untraceable Fair Network Payment Protocols with Off-line TTP (Chih-Hung Wang). Fair e-commerce payment protocol combining off-line untraceable cash with a Restrictive Confirmation Signature Scheme for buyer privacy.
- **28940195.pdf** — New Parallel Domain Extenders for UOWHF (Lee, Chang, Lee, Sung, Nandi). Two tree-based parallel domain-extension algorithms for universal one-way hash functions with near-optimal key-length expansion.
- **28940215.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of 3-pass HAVAL (Van Rompay, Biryukov, Preneel, Vandewalle). Practical collision attack on 3-pass HAVAL requiring about 2^29 compression-function computations and negligible memory.
- **28940233.pdf** — Efficient Group Signatures without Trapdoors (Giuseppe Ateniese, Breno de Medeiros). First constant-size group signature scheme in which no party, including managers, holds trapdoor secrets, enabling shared public parameters.
- **28940254.pdf** — Accumulating Composites and Improved Group Signing (Gene Tsudik, Shouhuai Xu). Dynamic accumulator over composite values plus efficient proof of factorization knowledge yield a provably secure group signature with one-exponentiation Join.
- **28940272.pdf** — Almost Uniform Density of Power Residues and the Provable Security of ESIGN (Tatsuaki Okamoto, Jacques Stern). Proves near-uniform distribution of e-th powers modulo an RSA modulus, repairing gaps in ESIGN's random-oracle security proof.
- **28940287.pdf** — Factoring Estimates for a 1024-bit RSA Modulus (Lenstra, Tromer, Shamir, Kortsmit, Dodson, Hughes, Leyland). Estimates NFS yield for RSA-1024 under TWIRL hardware parameters, justifying improved parameter choices.
- **28940307.pdf** — Index Calculus Attack for Hyperelliptic Curves of Small Genus (Nicolas Theriault). Variant of Gaudry's index calculus beating the original attack and Rho for Jacobian DLP in genus ≥ 3, plus a memory-lean variant for genus ≥ 4.
- **28940325.pdf** — The Secret and Beauty of Ancient Chinese Padlocks (Hong-Sen Yan, Hsing-Hui Huang). Historical/mechanical study of ancient Chinese locks, analogizing splitting-spring padlocks to asymmetric and combination locks to symmetric cryptosystems (off-topic curiosity).
- **28940331.pdf** — A Traceable Block Cipher (Olivier Billet, Henri Gilbert). Multivariate block cipher with many equivalent per-user keys but a hard-to-recover meta-key, giving inherent traitor tracing for broadcast content.
- **28940347.pdf** — A New Attack Against Khazad (Frederic Muller). Extension of the Square attack exploiting key-schedule redundancy breaks 5 rounds of the NESSIE candidate Khazad faster than exhaustive search.
- **28940359.pdf** — An Efficient Public Key Trace and Revoke Scheme Secure against Adaptive Chosen Ciphertext Attack (Kim, Hwang, Lee). Improves the Dodis–Fazio scheme, halving the enabling block under DDH plus collision-resistant hashing alone.
- **28940376.pdf** — Sequential Key Derivation Patterns for Broadcast Encryption and Key Predistribution Schemes (Attrapadung, Kobara, Imai). Generic PRSG-based construction framework unifying SD/LSD broadcast encryption and yielding collusion-resistant KPS meeting a proven key-size lower bound.
- **28940395.pdf** — Boneh et al.'s k-Element Aggregate Extraction Assumption Is Equivalent to the Diffie-Hellman Assumption (Jean-Sebastien Coron, David Naccache). Shows the aggregate-signature extraction problem from Eurocrypt 2003 is the Computational Diffie-Hellman problem in disguise.
- **28940402.pdf** — On Diophantine Complexity and Statistical Zero-Knowledge Arguments (Helger Lipmaa). Practical honest-verifier statistical zero-knowledge arguments of knowledge for bounded-arithmetic languages, applied to voting and auction schemes.
- **28940420.pdf** — Verifiable Homomorphic Oblivious Transfer and Private Equality Test (Helger Lipmaa). Modifies the Aiello–Ishai–Reingold OT into a weakly secure HOT protocol, building verifiable OT and private equality tests for cryptographic auctions.
- **28940439.pdf** — Generalized Powering Functions and their Application to Digital Signatures (Sato, Takagi, Tezuka, Takaragi). Optimized 3-to-1 powering function over a p^2*q modulus, one-way as hard as factoring, with efficient inversion and provably secure signatures.
- **28940457.pdf** — Certificateless Public Key Cryptography (Sattam Al-Riyami, Kenneth Paterson). Seminal paper introducing certificateless PKC (no escrow, no certificates) with a pairing-based CL-PKE scheme secure under a BDH-related assumption.
- **28940479.pdf** — A Complete and Explicit Security Reduction Algorithm for RSA-based Cryptosystems (Kurosawa, Schmidt-Samoa, Takagi). Euclidean-variant algorithm finding small solutions of ax+y=c mod N whenever they exist, yielding tighter RSA-OAEP and RSA-Paillier proofs.
- **28940501.pdf** — The Insecurity of Esign in Practical Implementations (Fouque, Howgrave-Graham, Martinet, Poupard). Partially-known-nonce attack: leaking 8 bits of the 768-bit nonce across 57 signatures recovers the ESIGN secret key in minutes.
- **28940517.pdf** — Efficient One-time Proxy Signatures (Huaxiong Wang, Josef Pieprzyk). Two one-time proxy signature schemes built from trapdoor-free one-way functions, one-time signatures, oblivious transfer and combinatorial objects.
- **29.pdf** — Read-Proof Hardware from Protective Coatings (Tuyls, Schrijen, Skoric, van Geloven, Verhaegh, Wolters). Read-proof hardware via a random protective coating whose measured fingerprint feeds a fuzzy extractor, so no key sits in memory (early PUF work).
- **29470001.pdf** — A Generalized Wiener Attack on RSA (Johannes Blomer, Alexander May). Extends Wiener's small-d attack to keys with ex+y = 0 mod phi(N) for small x,y; breaks the Yen–Kim–Lim–Moon RSA-type scheme.
- **29470014.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of a Public-key Encryption Scheme Based on the Polynomial Reconstruction Problem (Jean-Sebastien Coron). Polynomial-time break of the Augot–Finiasz Eurocrypt 2003 scheme and its eprint repair via a Berlekamp–Welch variant.
- **29470028.pdf** — Faster Scalar Multiplication on Koblitz Curves Combining Point Halving with the Frobenius Endomorphism (Avanzi, Ciet, Sica). Novel scalar decomposition mixing halve-and-add with tau-and-add, up to ~14.3% faster than the Frobenius method.
- **29470042.pdf** — Unreadable: extract is undecoded custom-font glyph garbage; title and content cannot be determined from the text.
- **29470056.pdf** — Fast Arithmetic on Jacobians of Picard Curves (Stephane Flon, Roger Oyono). Fast addition/doubling algorithms for Picard-curve Jacobians (char != 3) with a chord-and-tangent-like geometric interpretation; 144M+12SQ+2I per addition.
- **29470070.pdf** — Undeniable Signatures Based on Characters: How to Sign with One Bit (Jean Monnerat, Serge Vaudenay). Undeniable signature scheme from residue characters allowing arbitrarily short signatures with quadratic asymptotic complexity and full security proof.
- **29470087.pdf** — Efficient Extension of Standard Schnorr/RSA Signatures into Universal Designated-Verifier Signatures (Steinfeld, Wang, Pieprzyk). Converts existing Schnorr/RSA signature infrastructure into UDVS schemes with random-oracle security proofs.
- **29470101.pdf** — Constructing Committed Signatures From Strong-RSA Assumption In The Standard Complexity Model (Huafei Zhu). First committed signature scheme provably secure in the standard model under strong RSA, for non-interactive fair exchange.
- **29470115.pdf** — Constant Round Authenticated Group Key Agreement via Distributed Computation (Emmanuel Bresson, Dario Catalano). Practical constant-round authenticated group key exchange under DDH, combining ElGamal encryption with secret sharing.
- **29470129.pdf** — Unreadable: extract is undecoded custom-font glyph garbage; title and content cannot be determined from the text.
