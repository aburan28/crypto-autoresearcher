# Batch 033 digest

## Overview
- All 50 files are consecutive ePrint-style extracts (1304201xx–1304202xx) from a single theory-crypto proceedings volume, almost certainly TCC 2021: foundations, MPC, zero knowledge, commitments, and quantum cryptography.
- Dominant themes: secure multi-party computation (round complexity, adaptivity, communication efficiency — ~12 papers), zero-knowledge/proof systems (SNARGs, ZAPs, sigma-protocols, PoK), and quantum/post-quantum crypto (software leasing, resettable ZK, quantum FHE, key-length extension).
- Notable papers: Dory (transparent inner-product/polynomial commitments, Lee); Chiesa–Yogev tight bounds for Micali's SNARGs; Devadas et al. iO from LWE + succinct sampling; Peikert et al. lattice vector/functional commitments.
- Recurring author clusters: Kamath–Klein–Pietrzak (two papers on adaptive security/garbling), Badertscher (three papers), Hirt–Liu-Zhang–Maurer (adaptive MPC).
- Anomaly for this corpus: zero ECC/ECDLP/side-channel/pairing-attack content — the batch is pure theoretical cryptography despite the corpus focus on elliptic-curve research.
- No duplicates, no non-paper items; all 50 extracts are legitimate research paper first pages.

## Papers
- **130420133.pdf** — Forward Secret Encrypted RAM: Lower Bounds and Applications (Bienstock, Dodis, Yeo). Proves an Ω(log n) lower bound for forward-secret encrypted RAMs via a new symbolic model, and shows overhead-free forward secrecy for ORAMs, memory checkers, and multicast encryption.
- **130420134.pdf** — Cryptographic Shallots: A Formal Treatment of Repliable Onion Encryption (Ando, Lysyanskaya). Gives the first ideal functionality, game-based definition, and provably secure construction for two-way (repliable) onion encryption.
- **130420136.pdf** — Distributed Merkle's Puzzles (Dinur, Hasson). Extends Merkle's 1974 key-agreement puzzles to M players with amortized per-player cost, proving an optimal Ω(M·T²) eavesdropper query gap.
- **130420138.pdf** — Secure Quantum Computation with Classical Communication (Bartusek). Constructs constant-round composable protocols for blind, verifiable classical delegation of quantum computation from QLWE, enabling MPC between classical clients and a quantum server.
- **130420144.pdf** — Black-Box Impossibilities of Obtaining 2-Round Weak ZK and Strong WI from Polynomial Hardness (Kiyoshima). Shows black-box impossibility of 2-round delayed-input weak ZK and strong WI arguments under polynomially hard falsifiable assumptions.
- **130420145.pdf** — Two-Round Maliciously Secure Computation with Super-Polynomial Simulation (Agarwal, Bartusek, Goyal, Khurana, Malavolta). First two-round maliciously secure MPC for general functionalities without trusted setup, using super-polynomial simulation and a bilinear-map conditional disclosure of secrets.
- **130420148.pdf** — Continuously Non-Malleable Secret Sharing: Joint Tampering, Plain Model and Capacity (Brian, Faonio, Venturi). First plain-model threshold secret sharing resilient to joint noisy leakage and continuous tampering, with matching capacity bounds.
- **130420149.pdf** — Tight Security Bounds for Micali's SNARGs (Chiesa, Yogev). Gives a tight security analysis of Micali's PCP-to-SNARG compiler in the random oracle model, reducing oracle output size and argument length.
- **130420153.pdf** — Secure Software Leasing from Standard Assumptions (Kitagawa, Nishimaki, Yamakawa). Builds finite-term secure software leasing for pseudorandom functions from LWE, removing the quantum-money assumption of prior work.
- **130420154.pdf** — Simple Constructions from (Almost) Regular One-Way Functions (Mazor, Zhang). Gives efficient, near-optimal black-box constructions of PRGs and UOWHFs from (almost) regular one-way functions, closing gaps between bounds.
- **130420155.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Purely Algebraic Signatures (Döttling, Hartmann, Hofheinz, Kiltz, Schäge, Ursu). Shows wide classes of algebraic signature schemes fall to simple linear-algebra attacks in known- and hidden-order groups.
- **130420157.pdf** — Acyclicity Programming for Sigma-Protocols (Abe, Ambrona, Bogdanov, Ohkubo, Rosen). Composes sigma-protocols for compound statements via a new "acyclicity program" model, with verifier cost incomparable to CDS span-program composition.
- **130420159.pdf** — Statistical ZAPs from Group-Based Assumptions (Couteau, Katsumata, Sadeghi, Ursu). Template compiling hidden-bit-model NIZKs into statistical ZAPs via interactive hidden-bit generators, instantiated from explicit DDH-type assumptions.
- **130420160.pdf** — Adaptive Security of Multi-Party Protocols, Revisited (Hirt, Liu-Zhang, Maurer). Clean-slate treatment of adaptive MPC security via constructive cryptography, addressing the commitment problem without non-committing encryption overheads.
- **130420165.pdf** — Generalized Proofs of Knowledge with Fully Dynamic Setup (Badertscher, Jost, Maurer). Provides theoretical foundations for a generalized proof-of-knowledge notion covering dynamic, setup-dependent relations and interactive statement derivation.
- **130420167.pdf** — Multi-Party Functional Encryption (Agrawal, Goyal, Tomida). Unifies multi-input/client/authority FE notions into one framework and constructs the first multi-authority ABE with inner-product computation from pairings.
- **130420170.pdf** — On Treewidth, Separators and Yao's Garbling (Kamath, Klein, Pietrzak). Shows Yao's garbling is adaptively indistinguishable for circuits of treewidth w with 2^O(w) security loss, via a new pebble game using graph separators.
- **130420176.pdf** — Policy-Compliant Signatures (Badertscher, Matt, Waldner). Introduces signatures producible only when signer and receiver attribute sets jointly satisfy a global policy, with attribute privacy; applications to compliant financial transactions.
- **130420182.pdf** — Post-quantum Resettably-Sound Zero Knowledge (Bitansky, Kellner, Shmueli). Studies ZK soundness against quantum resetting provers; proves black-box barriers ruling out constant-round public-coin black-box post-quantum ZK.
- **130420183.pdf** — Simple and Efficient Batch Verification Techniques for Verifiable Delay Functions (Rotem). Compiles any proof of correct exponentiation into a batch PoCE, cutting VDF batch-verification communication and time, plus an improved low-order-assumption variant.
- **130420184.pdf** — On Actively-Secure Elementary MPC Reductions (Applebaum, Goel). Shows that actively secure dishonest-majority "elementary" MPC reductions (single call to a non-cryptographic functionality) are unlikely to exist.
- **130420187.pdf** — Succinct LWE Sampling, Random Polynomials, and Obfuscation (Devadas, Quach, Vaikuntanathan, Wee, Wichs). Constructs iO from LWE plus a new succinct LWE-sampling notion, with a candidate sampler based on a falsifiable random-polynomial assumption.
- **130420188.pdf** — Laconic Private Set Intersection and Applications (Alamati, Branco, Döttling, Garg, Hajiabadi, Pu). Studies two-round PSI with near-optimal communication; feasibility from CDH or LWE, plus an efficient black-box semi-honest-receiver construction.
- **130420189.pdf** — Secure Software Leasing Without Assumptions (Broadbent, Jeffery, Lord, Podder, Sundaram). Achieves secure software leasing for compute-and-compare circuits information-theoretically, with no setup or computational assumptions.
- **130420192.pdf** — Oblivious Transfer from Trapdoor Permutations in Minimal Rounds (Choudhuri, Ciampi, Goyal, Jain, Ostrovsky). Three- and four-round black-box OT from trapdoor permutations, yielding the first round-optimal 2PC from TDPs.
- **130420193.pdf** — Environmentally Friendly Composable Multi-Party Computation in the Plain Model from Standard (Timed) Assumptions (Broadnax, Mechler, Müller-Quade). First fully environmentally friendly, constant-round black-box composable commitments and MPC in the plain model from timed assumptions.
- **130420194.pdf** — Dory: Efficient, Transparent Arguments for Generalised Inner Products and Polynomial Commitments (Lee). Transparent-setup inner-pairing-product argument with O(log n) proof/verifier under SXDH, yielding efficient batchable polynomial commitments.
- **130420195.pdf** — On Communication-Efficient Asynchronous MPC with Adaptive Security (Chopard, Hirt, Liu-Zhang). Adaptively secure asynchronous MPC with optimal resilience t<n/3 and O(n²κ) bits per multiplication, a quadratic improvement.
- **130420196.pdf** — Efficient Perfectly Secure Computation with Optimal Resilience (Abraham, Asharov, Yanai). Perfectly secure malicious MPC with t<n/3 sharing only O(n) values per multiplication, matching semi-honest costs.
- **130420198.pdf** — The Cost of Adaptivity in Security Games on Graphs (Kamath, Klein, Pietrzak, Walter). Proves near-matching lower bounds on adaptive security loss for proxy re-encryption, prefix-constrained PRFs, and generalized selective decryption.
- **130420199.pdf** — ABE for DFA from LWE against Bounded Collusions, Revisited (Wee). New public-key ABE for DFA from LWE with ciphertext size Õ(ℓ+B) against bounded collusions, via a new lattice trapdoor sampling technique.
- **130420200.pdf** — Non-Malleable Vector Commitments via Local Equivocability (Rotem, Segev). First rigorous non-malleability framework for vector commitments with local openings and private updates, with blockchain applications.
- **130420202.pdf** — Non-Malleable Time-Lock Puzzles and Applications (Freitag, Komargodski, Pass, Sirkin). Introduces non-malleable time-lock puzzles, yielding the first fair non-interactive multi-party coin flipping and auctions in the plain model.
- **130420204.pdf** — On Communication Models and Best-Achievable Security in Two-Round MPC (Goel, Jain, Prabhakaran, Raghunath). Characterizes which communication channels are necessary for two-round honest-majority MPC, showing broadcast-only MPC implies 2-message OT.
- **130420206.pdf** — Concurrent Composition of Differential Privacy (Vadhan, Wang). Initiates composition theory for interactive differentially private mechanisms, proving concurrent composition matches optimal non-interactive bounds for pure DP.
- **130420207.pdf** — Grafting Key Trees: Efficient Key Management for Overlapping Groups (Alwen et al.). Efficient key-tree management for many overlapping groups (multicast/TreeKEM settings), reducing rotation cost beyond independent per-group trees.
- **130420209.pdf** — Rate-1 Quantum Fully Homomorphic Encryption (Chardouvelis, Döttling, Malavolta). Constructs rate-1 quantum FHE from QLWE, giving maliciously secure quantum SFE with near-optimal communication.
- **130420210.pdf** — Direct Product Hardness Amplification (Lanzenberger, Maurer). Simpler, more general, near-optimal concrete direct-product hardness amplification theorem, applicable to interactive games like MAC/signature forgery.
- **130420212.pdf** — Amortizing Rate-1 OT and Applications to PIR and PSI (Chase, Garg, Hajiabadi, Li, Miao). Amortizes many rate-1 OTs to four group elements per instance from pairings, improving PIR and PSI communication.
- **130420215.pdf** — Unifying Presampling via Concentration Bounds (Guo, Li, Liu, Zhang). Unifies presampling techniques for auxiliary-input idealized models via concentration bounds and explores extensions to quantum models.
- **130420216.pdf** — On the (Ir)Replaceability of Global Setups, or How (Not) to Use a Global Ledger (Badertscher, Hesse, Zikas). Shows UC global setups (e.g., a Bitcoin ledger) cannot generically be replaced by their implementing protocols without strong conditions.
- **130420217.pdf** — Disappearing Cryptography in the Bounded Storage Model (Guan, Zhandry). Introduces online obfuscation for streamed, un-storable programs; VBB impossibility but VGB candidates yielding disappearing ciphertexts and signatures.
- **130420221.pdf** — Trojan-Resilience without Cryptography (Chakraborty, Dziembowski, Gałązka, Lizurej, Pietrzak, Yeo). Detects hardware Trojans for stateless circuits via simple master-circuit cross-checking of manufactured chips, no MPC/VC overhead.
- **130420222.pdf** — Vector and Functional Commitments from Lattices (Peikert, Pepin, Sharp). Post-quantum statelessly updatable vector commitments and functional commitments from standard SIS, with shorter proofs than prior work.
- **130420227.pdf** — Quantum Key-length Extension (Jaeger, Song, Tessaro). Tight concrete security bounds for FX and double-encryption key-length extension against quantum attackers in ideal models.
- **130420228.pdf** — Fully-succinct Publicly Verifiable Delegation from Constant-Size Assumptions (González, Zacharakis). Publicly verifiable delegation with SNARK-like proof size from falsifiable k-MDDH assumptions, via no-signaling somewhere-statistically-binding commitments.
- **130420230.pdf** — BKW Meets Fourier: New Algorithms for LPN with Sparse Parities (Dachman-Soled, Gong, Kippen, Shahverdi). New algorithms for sparse-secret LPN combining BKW with Fourier analysis, improving exponents in constant- and low-noise regimes.
- **130420235.pdf** — Computational Robust (Fuzzy) Extractors for CRS-dependent Sources with Minimal Min-entropy (Feng, Tang). Robust (fuzzy) extractors for general CRS-dependent sources at minimal entropy in the computational setting, via a new κ-MAC primitive.
- **130420236.pdf** — Updatable Public Key Encryption in the Standard Model (Dodis, Karthikeyan, Wichs). Forward-secure updatable PKE constructions in the standard model for secure messaging, avoiding HIBE-based FS-PKE complexity.
- **130420240.pdf** — Generalized Pseudorandom Secret Sharing and Efficient Straggler-Resilient Secure Computation (Benhamouda, Boyle, Gilboa, Halevi, Ishai, Nof). Generalizes PRSS for non-interactive generation of pseudorandom correlations, yielding efficient strong-honest-majority and straggler-resilient MPC.
