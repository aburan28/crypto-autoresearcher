# Batch 084 digest

## Overview
- Two clean venue clusters: files 56770xxx are CRYPTO 2009 papers (foundations, ZK, symmetric cryptanalysis, lattices, pairings-related PKC); files 57470xxx are CHES 2009 papers (implementations, side-channel attacks/defenses, pairing and ECC hardware).
- Side-channel work dominates the CHES half: EM/template attacks on HMAC, algebraic SCA on AES, attacks on RSA-CRT and RSA prime generation, plus masking/shuffling/RSL countermeasures.
- Notable papers: Biryukov–Khovratovich–Nikolić related-key attack on full AES-256; Waters' Dual System Encryption; Barak–Mahmoody optimal O(n²) attack on random-oracle key exchange; Bernstein's batch binary Edwards speed records; practical forgery of ISO/IEC 9796-2/EMV signatures (Coron et al.).
- Elliptic-curve/pairing content is implementation-focused: hashing into curves (Icart), BN-curve Fp arithmetic and ASIPs, Tate pairing in characteristic 3, ECC coprocessor with SCA countermeasures.
- Anomalies: 57470031 has hex-escaped text extraction (decodable title); 56770459 is a 1-page position essay; 57470063 a 1-page keynote abstract; 57470220 a 5-page invited-talk extended abstract.

## Papers
- **56770158.pdf** — On the Composition of Public-Coin Zero-Knowledge Protocols (Pass, Tseng, Wikström). Shows only BPP languages have public-coin black-box ZK secure under unbounded parallel repetition; gives a protocol secure under a-priori bounded concurrency.
- **56770175.pdf** — On the Amortized Complexity of Zero-knowledge Protocols (Cramer, Damgård). General technique improving efficiency of ZK protocols over cut-and-choose baselines, amortizing proof size and error probability.
- **56770190.pdf** — Linear Algebra with Sub-linear Zero-Knowledge Arguments (Groth). Sub-linear size ZK arguments for committed matrix relations (product, Hadamard product), yielding O(√N) circuit-satisfiability arguments.
- **56770207.pdf** — New Birthday Attacks on Some MACs Based on Block Ciphers (Yuan, Wang, Jia, Xu, Xiaoyun Wang). New distinguishers and forgery/state-recovery attacks on Alred/Alpha-MAC, plus first impossible-differential attacks on related MACs.
- **56770229.pdf** — Distinguisher and Related-Key Attack on the Full AES-256 (Biryukov, Khovratovich, Nikolić). q-multicollision chosen-key distinguisher and first related-key key-recovery attack on full 14-round AES-256 (2^131 time).
- **56770248.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of C2 (Borghoff, Knudsen, Leander, Matusiewicz). Practical attacks on the Cryptomeria cipher used in DVD-Audio/SD DRM, recovering the secret S-box and 56-bit key well below brute force.
- **56770265.pdf** — Message Authentication Codes from Unpredictable Block Ciphers (Dodis, Steinberger). SS-NMAC mode achieving birthday-security MAC from an unpredictable block cipher, with leakage-resilience properties; solves a Eurocrypt 2008 open problem.
- **56770300.pdf** — How to Hash into Elliptic Curves (Icart). Deterministic, constant-operation encoding from F_{p^n} into elliptic curves via cube roots, enabling timing-attack-resistant hash-to-curve.
- **56770315.pdf** — Batch binary Edwards (Bernstein). Software speed records for 251-bit binary-field scalar multiplication using binary Edwards curves with complete addition formulas and vectorized field arithmetic.
- **56770333.pdf** — Solving Hidden Number Problem with One Bit Oracle and Advice (Akavia). HNP algorithm using only k≥1 bits with polynomial advice, robust to noise, via a local universal Fourier-coefficient finding algorithm.
- **56770350.pdf** — Computational Indistinguishability Amplification (Maurer, Tessaro). Systematic tight product theorems for amplifying security of PRGs/PRFs/PRPs under composition.
- **56770369.pdf** — Merkle Puzzles Are Optimal (Barak, Mahmoody-Ghidary). Any n-query random-oracle key exchange can be broken in O(n²) queries, matching Merkle's protocol and answering Impagliazzo–Rudich's open question.
- **56770386.pdf** — Position Based Cryptography (Chandran, Goyal, Moriarty, Ostrovsky). Initiates position-based crypto; proves secure positioning impossible in the plain model and constructs it (plus key exchange) in the bounded-storage model.
- **56770403.pdf** — Improving the Security of Quantum Protocols via Commit-and-Open (Damgård, Fehr, Lunemann, Salvail, Schaffner). Compiler upgrading BB84-style two-party protocols from almost-honest to arbitrary adversary security, preserving bounded-quantum-storage security.
- **56770423.pdf** — Practical Cryptanalysis of ISO/IEC 9796-2 and EMV Signatures (Coron, Naccache, Tibouchi, Weinmann). Algorithmic refinements enable practical existential forgery of amended ISO/IEC 9796-2, computed on RSA-2048 in two days for ~$800.
- **56770440.pdf** — How Risky is the Random-Oracle Model? (Leurent, Nguyen). Shows standard large-output hash instantiations (BR93, P1363, PKCS) are weaker than random oracles and studies impact on ROM signature schemes.
- **56770459.pdf** — Abstraction in Cryptography (Maurer). One-page position essay advocating higher levels of abstraction in cryptographic definitions and proofs.
- **56770460.pdf** — Asymptotically Good Ideal Linear Secret Sharing with Strong Multiplication over Any Fixed Finite Field (Cascudo, Chen, Cramer, Xing). Coding-theoretic framework yielding asymptotically good ideal LSSS with strong multiplication over any fixed Fq, for efficient MPC.
- **56770481.pdf** — The Round Complexity of Verifiable Secret Sharing Revisited (Patra, Choudhary, Rabin, Rangan). Circumvents VSS round lower bounds with negligible reconstruction error: 2-round VSS for n=3t+1 and 1-round VSS for t=1, with matching optimality results.
- **56770499.pdf** — Somewhat Non-Committing Encryption and Efficient Adaptively Secure Oblivious Transfer (Garay, Wichs, Zhou). Introduces semi-adaptive security plus a compiler to full adaptive security via a new non-committing encryption primitive; efficient adaptive OT.
- **56770517.pdf** — Collusion-Free Multiparty Computation in the Mediated Model (Alwen, Katz, Lindell, Persiano, shelat, Visconti). Strengthens mediated-model definitions and constructs collusion-free protocols for any multiparty functionality.
- **56770534.pdf** — Privacy-Enhancing Auctions Using Rational Cryptography (Miltersen, Nielsen, Triandopoulos). Hybrid utility model (money plus privacy) with cryptographic protocols implementing rational auction equilibria without trusted mediators.
- **56770551.pdf** — Utility Dependence in Correct and Fair Rational Secret Sharing (Asharov, Lindell). Proves rational secret sharing requires knowledge of players' utilities in the basic setting; shows utility-independent protocols under relaxed utility assumptions.
- **56770568.pdf** — On Bounded Distance Decoding, Unique Shortest Vectors, and the Minimum Distance Problem (Lyubashevsky, Micciancio). Proves near-equivalence of uSVP, BDD, and GapSVP, basing Ajtai–Dwork and Regev cryptosystems on worst-case GapSVP.
- **56770585.pdf** — Fast Cryptographic Primitives and Circular-Secure Encryption Based on Hard Learning Problems (Applebaum, Cash, Peikert, Sahai). Efficient LWE-based key-dependent-message/circular-secure cryptosystems and near-linear-time pseudorandom objects.
- **56770608.pdf** — Dual System Encryption (Waters). New proof methodology yielding first fully secure IBE and HIBE with short parameters under simple assumptions (decisional BDH and Linear).
- **56770625.pdf** — The Group of Signed Quadratic Residues and Applications (Hofheinz, Kiltz). Studies the signed-quadratic-residues gap group; proves Strong DH under factoring and CCA security of hybrid ElGamal under higher residuosity.
- **56770642.pdf** — Short and Stateless Signatures from the RSA Assumption (Hohenberger, Waters). First short, stateless, standard-model signature scheme secure under plain RSA, via a new prefix-guessing proof technique also re-proving Waters signatures.
- **56770659.pdf** — Smooth Projective Hashing for Conditionally Extractable Commitments (Abdalla, Chevalier, Pointcheval). Builds SPHFs for disjunctions/conjunctions of languages and applies them to extractable commitments, certification, and PAKE without random oracles.
- **57470001.pdf** — Faster and Timing-Attack Resistant AES-GCM (Käsper, Schwabe). Bitsliced AES-CTR at 7.59 cycles/byte plus first reasonably fast constant-time AES-GCM on 64-bit Intel.
- **57470016.pdf** — Accelerating AES with Vector Permute Instructions (Hamburg). First constant-time AES software efficient in sequential modes, using SSSE3/AltiVec vector-permute instructions and composite fields.
- **57470031.pdf** — SSE Implementation of Multivariate PKCs on Modern x86 CPUs (?) (Chen, Chen, Chen, Cheng, Ding, Kuo, Lee, Yang). Hex-escaped extraction artifact; decodable title indicates an SSE-optimized implementation of multivariate public-key cryptosystems on x86 CPUs.
- **57470048.pdf** — MicroEliece: McEliece for Embedded Devices (Eisenbarth, Güneysu, Heyse, Paar). First McEliece public-key encryption implementations on an 8-bit AVR microcontroller and a Spartan-3AN FPGA, addressing large-key storage.
- **57470063.pdf** — Physical Unclonable Functions and Secure Processors (Devadas). One-page keynote abstract on PUF-based device identity, RFID authentication, and certified-execution secure processors.
- **57470064.pdf** — Practical Electromagnetic Template Attack on HMAC (Fouque, Leurent, Réal, Valette). Profiled EM template attack recovering the HMAC-SHA-1 key from a single execution, validated on FPGA.
- **57470078.pdf** — First-Order Side-Channel Attacks on the Permutation Tables Countermeasure (Prouff, McEvoy). Breaks Coron's permutation-table masking on AES with first-order CPA/MIA attacks, verified in simulation and on a smart card.
- **57470096.pdf** — Algebraic Side-Channel Attacks on the AES (Renauld, Standaert, Veyrat-Charvillon). Algebraic SCA recovers the AES key from a single observed plaintext on an 8-bit controller; shows all rounds' leakage is exploitable.
- **57470111.pdf** — Differential Cluster Analysis (Batina, Gierlichs, Lemke-Rust). Cluster-analysis-based collision side-channel attack combining DPA and collision techniques; demonstrated on DES software and AES hardware.
- **57470127.pdf** — Known-Plaintext-Only Attack on RSA-CRT with Montgomery Multiplication (Hlaváč). Turns final-subtraction leakage counts in Montgomery RSA-CRT into an HNP instance, enabling passport Active Authentication cloning without chosen plaintexts.
- **57470141.pdf** — A New Side-Channel Attack on RSA Prime Generation (Finke, Gebhardt, Schindler). BSI attack exploiting power leakage of trial divisions during RSA prime generation, with 10–15% success for realistic parameters.
- **57470156.pdf** — An Efficient Method for Random Delay Generation in Embedded Software (Coron, Kizhvatov). New, more secure and lightweight random-delay countermeasure against side-channel/fault attacks, validated with practical attacks on an 8-bit platform.
- **57470171.pdf** — Higher-order Masking and Shuffling for Software Implementations of Block Ciphers (Rivain, Prouff, Doget). Quantifies DPA against masking-plus-shuffling and designs a scalable higher-order masking/shuffling scheme for AES with adjustable resistance.
- **57470189.pdf** — A Design Methodology for a DPA-Resistant Cryptographic LSI with RSL Techniques (Saeki, Suzuki, Shimizu, Satoh). Random Switching Logic implemented with standard CMOS cells; 130-nm AES LSI resists DPA/CPA over a million traces.
- **57470205.pdf** — A Design Flow and Evaluation Framework for DPA-resistant Instruction Set Extensions (Regazzoni et al.). CAD flow and evaluation environment for hybrid CMOS/protected-logic (MCML) instruction-set extensions against power analysis.
- **57470220.pdf** — Invited Talk Crypto Engineering: Some History and Some Case Studies (Paar). Five-page extended abstract reflecting on a decade of CHES and real-world crypto implementation case studies.
- **57470225.pdf** — Hardware Accelerator for the Tate Pairing in Characteristic Three (Beuchat, Detrey, Estibals, Okamoto, Rodríguez-Henríquez). Parallel Tate-pairing accelerator over F_{3^m} built on a pipelined Karatsuba-Ofman multiplier with VHDL generator.
- **57470240.pdf** — Faster Fp-arithmetic for Cryptographic Pairings on Barreto-Naehrig Curves (Fan, Vercauteren, Verbauwhede). BN-modulus-aware Fp multiplication via polynomial Montgomery reduction with pseudo-Mersenne coefficient reduction; 5.4× speedup in hardware pairing processor.
- **57470255.pdf** — Designing an ASIP for Cryptographic Pairings over Barreto-Naehrig Curves (Kammler, Zhang, Schwabe, Scharwaechter, Langenberg, Auras, Ascheid, Mathar). Design-space exploration of a C-programmable ASIP with scalable Fp functional units for BN-curve pairings; 15.8 ms pairing.
- **57470273.pdf** — KATAN & KTANTAN — A Family of Small and Efficient Hardware-Oriented Block Ciphers (De Cannière, Dunkelman, Knežević). Six lightweight 80-bit-key block ciphers (32/48/64-bit blocks) down to 462 GE, targeting RFID.
- **57470287.pdf** — Programmable and Parallel ECC Coprocessor Architecture (Guo, Fan, Schaumont, Verbauwhede). Scalable programmable ECC coprocessor integrating side-channel and fault countermeasures with area/speed/security trade-offs.
