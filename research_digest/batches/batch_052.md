# Batch 052 digest

## Overview
- The batch is a coherent slice of recent conference papers: files 14438240–14438394 appear to be ASIACRYPT 2023 proceedings (LNCS style), and 14602003–14602039 continue in the same format, likely another 2023 LNCS volume.
- Heavy concentration on lattice-based/post-quantum cryptography: LWE attacks and security analysis, FHE bootstrapping, threshold FHE, quantum circuits for AES, and quantum lattice enumeration.
- A notable isogeny cluster: polynomial-time attack on M-SIDH/FESTA (Castryck–Vercauteren), new SIDH countermeasures (binSIDH/terSIDH), CSIDH proof systems, and genus-2 superspecial isogeny cryptanalysis.
- Strong zero-knowledge/proof-systems thread: NIZK frameworks, weak ZK via Goldreich-Levin, MPC-in-the-Head improvements, ZKVM memory consistency, functional proofs.
- Anomaly: none — all 50 entries are legitimate research papers; no duplicates, slides, or garbage found. ECC-specific work is sparse (mainly the Edwards448/E448 point-operation paper and isogeny papers).

## Papers
- **14438240.pdf** — Concrete Analysis of Quantum Lattice Enumeration (Bai, van Hoof, Johnson, Lange, Ngo). Concrete quantum-circuit implementation of BKZ enumeration via Montanaro's quantum backtracking, with parallelization and resource estimates.
- **14438251.pdf** — Lattice-Based Functional Commitments: Fast Verification and Cryptanalysis (Wee, Wu). Two lattice functional commitments with sublinear verification from the ℓ-succinct SIS assumption, plus cryptanalytic discussion.
- **14438263.pdf** — Just How Fair is an Unreactive World? (Raghuraman, Yang). Impossibility results: no unreactive primitive of cardinality t is complete for fair n-party computation with t > n/2 corruptions.
- **14438265.pdf** — Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge Functional Proofs (Zeng, Lai, Huang, et al.). Introduces fNIZK letting provers authorize verifiers to learn functions of the witness; generic NP construction plus efficient set-membership instantiation.
- **14438279.pdf** — Weak Zero-Knowledge via the Goldreich-Levin Theorem (Khurana, Malavolta, Tomer). Three-round weak zero-knowledge for NP against non-adaptive verifiers from doubly-enhanced injective trapdoor functions, using GL list decoding.
- **14438280.pdf** — Improved Quantum Circuits for AES: Reducing the Depth and the Number of Qubits (Liu, Preneel, Zhao, Wang). Generic techniques for AES quantum oracles, reducing qubit/depth costs of Grover-based key search.
- **14438282.pdf** — Amortized bootstrapping revisited: Simpler, asymptotically-faster, implemented (Guimarães, Pereira, van Leeuwen). Simpler, cheaper amortized FHE bootstrapping with RNS-GSW instantiation and C++ implementation.
- **14438284.pdf** — Rotation Key Reduction for Client-Server Systems of Deep Neural Network on FHE (Lee, Lee, Kim, No). Hierarchical rotation keys let servers derive required CKKS/BFV rotation keys from a small client-sent set, cutting communication.
- **14438289.pdf** — Simple Threshold (Fully Homomorphic) Encryption From LWE With Polynomial Modulus (Boudgoust, Scholl). Threshold LWE-based FHE with polynomial modulus, using Rényi divergence and a one-way-to-IND transformation.
- **14438290.pdf** — A Simple and Efficient Framework of Proof Systems for NP (Wang, Su, Pan, Chen). Pairing-based NIZK for NP improving on Groth–Ostrovsky–Sahai proof size and cost, extended to improved batch arguments.
- **14438295.pdf** — The Indifferentiability of the Duplex and its Practical Applications (Degabriele, Fischlin, Govinden). Proves the Duplex construction indifferentiable from an online random oracle; full-state variant fails the notion.
- **14438296.pdf** — Efficient Secure Storage with Version Control and Key Rotation (Chen, Guo, Li, Tang). Updatable Secure Storage combining key rotation with integrity against malicious servers for dynamic databases.
- **14438298.pdf** — Towards compressed permutation oracles (Unruh). Defines a compressed permutation oracle for quantum proofs and proves a curious 2-for-1 soundness theorem.
- **14438299.pdf** — LERNA: Secure Single-Server Aggregation via Key-Homomorphic Masking (Li, Lin, Polychroniadou, Tessaro). Single-server secure aggregation with reusable setup, fewer rounds, from DCR and (R)LWR.
- **14438300.pdf** — Memory-Efficient Attacks on Small LWE Keys (Esser, Girme, Mukherjee, Sarkar). Polynomial-memory combinatorial attacks on small-secret LWE as used in NTRU, Kyber, Dilithium.
- **14438302.pdf** — Populating the Zoo of Rugged Pseudorandom Permutations (Degabriele, Karadžić). New RPRP constructions validating the CRYPTO 2022 modular AEAD framework, plus weaker sufficient variants.
- **14438305.pdf** — Generalized Fuzzy Password-Authenticated Key Exchange from Error Correcting Codes (Bootle, Faller, Hesse, Hostáková, Ottenhues). Breaks Dupont et al.'s fuzzy PAKE via per-character MITM guesses and gives a fixed ECC-based scheme.
- **14438309.pdf** — Unified View for Notions of Bit Security (Watanabe, Yasunaga). Shows equivalence (up to constant bits) of the two bit-security frameworks and resolves open problems on tight GL reductions.
- **14438311.pdf** — On Gaussian sampling, smoothing parameter and application to signatures (Espitau, Wallet, Yu). General framework for lattice Gaussian sampling over lattice extensions, generalizing Klein's sampler, with signature applications.
- **14438312.pdf** — Generic Security of the SAFE API and Its Applications (Khovratovich, Marhuenda Beltrán, Mennink). Identifies SAFECore sponge variant and proves indifferentiability, securing SAFE-based hashing, AE, and ZK uses.
- **14438313.pdf** — Two-Round Concurrent 2PC from Sub-Exponential LWE (Abdolmaleki, Badrinarayanan, Fernando, Malavolta, Rahimi, Sahai). Round-optimal concurrently secure two-party computation in the plain model from subexponential LWE.
- **14438314.pdf** — Too Many Hints – When LLL Breaks LWE (May, Nowakowski). Efficient single-stroke integration of many perfect/modular hints into lattices; breaks Kyber-512 with 449 mod-q hints using only LLL.
- **14438319.pdf** — Non-Interactive Commitment from Non-Transitive Group Actions (D'Alconzo, Flamini, Gangemi). First non-interactive bit commitment from group actions (isogeny-like assumptions), secure under decisional Group Action Inversion.
- **14438322.pdf** — Bicameral and Auditably Private Signatures (Nguyen, Roy, Susilo, Xu). Signature system hiding both attribute and policy from verifiers while proving certification, with auditable selective disclosure.
- **14438324.pdf** — An Efficient Strong Asymmetric PAKE Compiler Instantiable from Group Actions (McQuoid, Xu). 2-message, 7-exponentiation PAKE-to-saPAKE compiler with UC security; CSIDH instantiation gives first post-quantum saPAKE.
- **14438334.pdf** — Registered (Inner-Product) Functional Encryption (Francati, Friolo, Maitra, Malavolta, Rahimi, Venturi). Generalizes registered encryption to registered functional encryption, including inner-product functionality without key escrow.
- **14438340.pdf** — Verifiable Decentralized Multi-Client Functional Encryption for Inner Product (Nguyen, Phan, Pointcheval). Computational verifiability notion and construction for multi-client inner-product FE against malicious users.
- **14438344.pdf** — Polynomial IOPs for Memory Consistency Checks in Zero-Knowledge Virtual Machines (Zhang, Sun, Zhang, Gu). Formalizes ZKVM memory consistency checks in the polynomial IOP model with security proofs.
- **14438345.pdf** — Algebraic Attacks on Round-Reduced Rain and Full AIM-III (Zhang, Wang, Yu, Guo, Cui). Algebraic cryptanalysis of MPC-in-the-Head-friendly one-way functions Rain and AIM used in post-quantum signature candidates.
- **14438346.pdf** — Cuckoo Commitments: Registration-Based Encryption and Key-Value Map Commitments for Large Spaces (Fiore, Kolonelos, de Perthuis). First efficient pairing-based RBE supporting large (arbitrary-string) identities via cuckoo-hashing-style commitments.
- **14438354.pdf** — A polynomial time attack on instances of M-SIDH and FESTA (Castryck, Vercauteren). Generalized "lollipop" attack breaking M-SIDH and some FESTA instances in polynomial time despite masked torsion images.
- **14438357.pdf** — Quantum Speed-Up for Multidimensional (Zero Correlation) Linear Distinguishers (Hosoyamada). Correlation Extraction Algorithm from a modified Simon subroutine speeds up multidimensional linear/zero-correlation/integral distinguishers.
- **14438365.pdf** — Exploiting the Symmetry of Zn: Randomization and the Automorphism Problem (Jiang, Wang, Luo, Liu, Yu, Wang). Studies ZSVP/ZLIP on rotations of Z^n lattices via symmetry, clarifying complexity relevant to new lattice primitives.
- **14438369.pdf** — New SIDH Countermeasures for a More Efficient Key Exchange (Basso, Fouotsa). Artificially oriented curves yield binSIDH/terSIDH key exchange protocols leaking less information than prior post-attack SIDH fixes.
- **14438373.pdf** — Fine-Grained Proxy Re-Encryption: Definitions & Constructions from LWE (Zhou, Liu, Han, Zhang). FPRE lets proxies re-encrypt to functions f(m) of the message, with LWE-based constructions.
- **14438376.pdf** — A new approach based on quadratic forms to attack the McEliece cryptosystem (Couvreur, Mora, Tillich). Quadratic-form distinguisher for Goppa/alternant codes and polynomial-time key recovery via MinRank on distinguishable instances.
- **14438378.pdf** — Oblivious Transfer from Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Colisson, Muguruza, Speelman). Generic transform of classical ZK protocols into composable quantum OT; first round-optimal 2-message quantum OT in the ROM.
- **14438382.pdf** — Threshold Linear Secret Sharing to the Rescue of MPC-in-the-Head (Feneuil, Rivain). Using threshold LSSS in MPCitH improves soundness from 1/N to 1/(N^ℓ) and speeds up verification.
- **14438390.pdf** — A Generic Construction of Tightly Secure Password-based Authenticated Key Exchange (Pan, Zeng). Tightly secure PAKE from OW-PCA KEMs in the BPR model, instantiable from DH and lattices (Kyber).
- **14438394.pdf** — A Generic Construction of an Anonymous Reputation System and Instantiations from Lattices (Blömer, Bobolz, Porzenheim). Generic anonymous reputation system from signatures, NIZK, and linking tags; first module-lattice instantiation.
- **14602003.pdf** — Chosen-Ciphertext Secure Dual-Receiver Encryption in the Standard Model Based on Post-Quantum Assumptions (Benz, Beskorovajnov, et al.). Shows existing post-quantum IND-CCA2 DRE schemes lack soundness, gives attacks and classification of applications.
- **14602006.pdf** — Towards Practical Multi-key TFHE: Parallelizable, Key-Compatible, Quasi-linear Complexity (Kwak, Min, Song). Multi-key TFHE with two-phase blind rotation reducing gate bootstrapping from quadratic to near-linear in key count.
- **14602009.pdf** — New proof systems and an OPRF from CSIDH (Delpech de Saint Guilhem, Pedersen). ZK proofs of arithmetic on CSIDH group elements via sigma protocols and MPC-in-the-Head, yielding a competitive OPRF.
- **14602014.pdf** — Fast and Simple Point Operations on Edwards448 and E448 (Li, Yu, Xu). New explicit formulas for d = −1 twisted Edwards curves: 8M full addition, 7M mixed addition for the NIST/TLS Edwards448/E448 curves.
- **14602023.pdf** — Multi-Signatures for Ad-hoc and Privacy-Preserving Group Signing (Lehmann, Özbay). Shows deterministic key aggregation breaks signer privacy across groups; proposes probabilistic-aggregation multi-signatures.
- **14602026.pdf** — SoK: Learning With Errors, Circular Security, and Fully Homomorphic Encryption (Micciancio, Vaikuntanathan). Systematizes circular-security notions needed by LWE-based FHE, with conjectures, reductions, and open problems.
- **14602036.pdf** — Probabilistic Hash-and-Sign with Retry in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Kosuge, Xagawa). Fixes a flawed classical proof and gives first QROM proof for hash-and-sign-with-retry used in code/MQ-based signatures.
- **14602037.pdf** — Public-key Encryption with Keyword Search in Multi-user, Multi-challenge Setting under Adaptive Corruptions (Ling, Zhang, Chen, Huang, Qian). First multi-user PEKS schemes secure under adaptive corruptions in the standard model.
- **14602038.pdf** — R3PO: Reach-Restricted Reactive Program Obfuscation and its Applications (Bhushan, Obbattu, Prabhakaran, Raghunath). New obfuscation abstraction unifying recent garbled-circuit constructions; yields black-box MA-ABE from CP-ABE plus DDH.
- **14602039.pdf** — An algorithm for efficient detection of (N,N)-splittings and its application to the isogeny problem in dimension 2 (Corte-Real Santos, Costello, Frengley). Fast detection of optimally split genus-2 Jacobians speeds the Costello–Smith dimension-2 isogeny attack by 25–160×.
