# Batch 078 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ~2008-era theory-of-cryptography papers (TCC/Eurocrypt style): secure computation (MPC, covert adversaries), zero-knowledge, commitments, and game theory–cryptography connections.
- A second cluster covers symmetric/primitive cryptanalysis and design: KeeLoq attack, HMAC/NMAC-MD4/MD5 key recovery, LPS expander hash collisions, sponge indifferentiability, permutation-based hashing bounds.
- ECDLP-relevant highlight: 49650162 (Smith) uses explicit isogenies to move genus-3 hyperelliptic Jacobian DLP instances to easier non-hyperelliptic Jacobians (~18.6% of curves).
- Other notable papers: Groth–Sahai efficient NIZKs for bilinear groups (49650412), Cash–Kiltz–Shoup twin Diffie-Hellman (49650126), Katz–Sahai–Waters predicate encryption (49650145).
- Anomalies: two files (49650108, 49650268) are hex/garbled extraction failures, not readable research text.
- Game-theoretic protocols (mediators, rational secret sharing, correlated equilibria) form a conspicuous sub-batch of ~5 papers.

## Papers
- **49480151.pdf** — Efficient Protocols for Set Intersection and Pattern Matching with Security Against Malicious and Covert Adversaries (Carmit Hazay, Yehuda Lindell). Builds set-intersection and pattern-matching protocols from secure PRF evaluation (incl. Naor–Reingold), achieving malicious and covert security.
- **49480171.pdf** — Fast Private Norm Estimation and Heavy Hitters (Joe Kilian, André Madeira, Martin Strauss, Xuan Zheng). Semi-honest protocols for Euclidean norm of vector differences and heavy hitters with quasilinear computation and polylog communication.
- **49480189.pdf** — Matroids Can Be Far From Ideal Secret Sharing (Amos Beimel, Noam Livne, Carles Padró). Improves lower bounds on share size for matroid-induced (e.g., Vámos) access structures, separating them from ideal secret sharing.
- **49480207.pdf** — Perfectly-Secure MPC with Linear Communication Complexity (Zuzana Beerliová-Trubíniová, Martin Hirt). Perfectly secure MPC with O(n) field elements per multiplication against t<n/3 adaptive adversaries, via hyper-invertible matrices.
- **49480225.pdf** — MPC vs. SFE: Perfect Security in a Unified Corruption Model (Beerliová-Trubíniová, Fitzi, Hirt, Maurer, Zikas). Studies separation between secure function evaluation and reactive MPC under mixed/general corruption models with perfect security.
- **49480245.pdf** — Bridging Game Theory and Cryptography: Recent Results and Future Directions (Jonathan Katz). Survey of replacing trusted mediators with cryptographic protocols and designing protocols for rational players; proposes new definitions.
- **49480267.pdf** — Verifiably Secure Devices (Sergei Izmalkov, Matt Lepinski, Silvio Micali). Introduces a stronger notion of secure computation realized in the ballot-box model, giving perfect correlated equilibrium and interdependent function evaluation.
- **49480296.pdf** — Lower Bounds on Implementing Robust and Resilient Mediators (Ittai Abraham, Danny Dolev, Joseph Halpern). Tight lower bounds on implementing equilibria via cheap talk, linking them to Byzantine agreement impossibility results.
- **49480314.pdf** — Cryptography and Game Theory: Designing Protocols for Exchanging Information (Gillat Kol, Moni Naor). Rational secret sharing and SMPC protocols immune to backward induction with polynomial (not exponential) iteration structure.
- **49480334.pdf** — Equivocal Blind Signatures and Adaptive UC-Security (Aggelos Kiayias, Hong-Sheng Zhou). Defines equivocal blind signatures and a methodology for adaptively UC-secure blind signatures using special ZK functionalities.
- **49480352.pdf** — P-signatures and Noninteractive Anonymous Credentials (Belenkiy, Chase, Kohlweiss, Lysyanskaya). Introduces P-signatures with proofs on committed values, yielding practical non-interactive anonymous credentials without random oracles.
- **49480370.pdf** — Multi-Property Preserving Combiners for Hash Functions (Marc Fischlin, Anja Lehmann). Hash combiners simultaneously preserving collision resistance, pseudorandomness, random-oracle-ness, TCR, and MAC security.
- **49480388.pdf** — OT-Combiners via Secure Computation (Harnik, Ishai, Kushilevitz, Nielsen). General framework building oblivious-transfer combiners from secure computation protocols, yielding first constant-rate OT-combiners.
- **49480407.pdf** — Semi-Honest to Malicious Oblivious Transfer The Black-Box Way (Iftach Haitner). Black-box reduction from malicious OT to semi-honest OT, generalizing the Ishai et al. breakthrough.
- **49480422.pdf** — Black-Box Construction of a Non-Malleable Encryption Scheme from Any Semantically Secure One (Choi, Dachman-Soled, Malkin, Wee). Black-box semantic-security-to-non-malleability transform with quasi-linear ciphertext blow-up, using error-correcting codes.
- **49480440.pdf** — A Linear Lower Bound on the Communication Complexity of Single-Server PIR (Haitner, Hoch, Segev). Ω(n) server-communication lower bound for black-box trapdoor-permutation-based PIR, making naive download optimal up to constants.
- **49480460.pdf** — Randomness Extraction via δ-Biased Masking in the Presence of a Quantum Attacker (Serge Fehr, Christian Schaffner). Proves δ-biased-mask randomness extraction secure against quantum adversaries, with entropic encryption and error-correction applications.
- **49480477.pdf** — An Equivalence between Zero Knowledge and Commitments (Shien Jin Ong, Salil Vadhan). Shows an NP language has zero-knowledge proofs iff it has instance-dependent commitments, confirming commitments are necessary for ZK.
- **49480496.pdf** — Interactive and Noninteractive Zero Knowledge are Equivalent in the Help Model (Chailloux, Ciocan, Kerenidis, Vadhan). Unconditional equivalence of interactive and noninteractive ZK with a statement-dependent dealer, incl. quantum case.
- **49480549.pdf** — On Constant-Round Concurrent Zero-Knowledge (Rafael Pass, Muthuramakrishnan Venkitasubramaniam). Constructs constant-round concurrent ZK when adversaries are modeled as quasi-polynomial-time machines.
- **49480567.pdf** — Concurrent Non-malleable Commitments from Any One-way Function (Huijia Lin, Rafael Pass, Venkitasubramaniam). Black-box concurrent non-malleable commitments from one-way functions, simplifying stand-alone non-malleable commitment proofs.
- **49480585.pdf** — Faster and Shorter Password-Authenticated Key Exchange (Rosario Gennaro). Improves KOY/GL-framework PAKE by replacing one-time signatures with MACs and labeled ciphertexts; standard-model proof under DDH/QR/N-residuosity.
- **49480603.pdf** — Saving Private Randomness in One-Way Functions and Pseudorandom Generators (Dedić, Harnik, Reyzin). Studies reducing secret input length of OWFs; positive results via pairwise-independent hashing, impossibility for black-box reductions.
- **49480621.pdf** — Degradation and Amplification of Computational Hardness (Shai Halevi, Tal Rabin). Generic framework for hardness amplification/degradation under sequential repetition of interactive protocols matching information-theoretic bounds.
- **49650001.pdf** — A Practical Attack on KeeLoq (Indesteege, Keller, Dunkelman, Biham, Preneel). Practical key-recovery on the KeeLoq car-key cipher: 2^16 known plaintexts, 2^44.5 work, ~2 days on a small cluster.
- **49650019.pdf** — Key Recovery on Hidden Monomial Multivariate Schemes (Fouque, Macario-Rat, Stern). Polynomial-time equivalent-key recovery for C*-type and higher-degree hidden-monomial multivariate schemes (IP problem instances).
- **49650031.pdf** — Predicting Lattice Reduction (Nicolas Gama, Phong Nguyen). Extensive NTL experiments yielding conjectures on actual LLL/BKZ behavior to predict lattice attack costs and set lattice key sizes.
- **49650052.pdf** — Efficient Sequential Aggregate Signed Data (Gregory Neven). Sequential aggregate signature/signed-data schemes from uncertified claw-free permutations (low-exponent RSA), cutting signing and verification costs.
- **49650070.pdf** — Proving tight security for Rabin–Williams signatures (Daniel J. Bernstein). Tight random-oracle-model security reductions to factoring for lowest-cost Rabin–Williams signature variants, including the surprising fixed unstructured B=0 case.
- **49650088.pdf** — Threshold RSA for Dynamic and Ad-Hoc Groups (Gennaro, Halevi, Krawczyk, Rabin). Variants of Shoup's threshold RSA suited to MANETs: non-interactive, no broadcast, member addition by threshold of existing members.
- **49650108.pdf** — Not a readable research paper: extraction produced hex-encoded garbage (likely font/encoding failure); content unrecoverable from this extract.
- **49650126.pdf** — The Twin Diffie-Hellman Problem and Applications (David Cash, Eike Kiltz, Victor Shoup). Introduces twin DH, hard even with a decision oracle via trapdoor test; yields short-ciphertext ElGamal, Cramer–Shoup and IBE variants.
- **49650145.pdf** — Predicate Encryption Supporting Disjunctions, Polynomial Equations, and Inner Products (Katz, Sahai, Waters). Predicate encryption for inner products over Z_N, enabling disjunctions, polynomials, CNF/DNF, and threshold predicates.
- **49650162.pdf** — Isogenies and the Discrete Logarithm Problem in Jacobians of Genus 3 Hyperelliptic Curves (Benjamin Smith). Explicit (Z/2Z)^3 isogenies transfer genus-3 hyperelliptic Jacobian DLP to non-hyperelliptic Jacobians with faster index calculus, for ~18.57% of curves.
- **49650180.pdf** — On the Indifferentiability of the Sponge Construction (Bertoni, Daemen, Peeters, Van Assche). Proves sponge construction indifferentiable from a random oracle with a random permutation/transformation, foundation for Keccak-type designs.
- **49650197.pdf** — A New Mode of Operation for Block Ciphers and Length-Preserving MACs (Dodis, Pietrzak, Puniya). Enciphered-CBC mode giving first constant-rate VIL-MAC from FIL-MAC and secure PRF domain extension without re-keying.
- **49650218.pdf** — Security/Efficiency Tradeoffs for Permutation-Based Hashing (Phillip Rogaway, John Steinberger). Ideal-permutation-model bounds: rate-α permutation-based hashes admit collision/preimage attacks in ~N^(1−α) queries, limiting possible designs.
- **49650235.pdf** — New Key-Recovery Attacks on HMAC/NMAC-MD4 and NMAC-MD5 (Lei Wang, Kazuo Ohta, Noboru Kunihiro). Near-collision-based improvements to HMAC/NMAC-MD4 key recovery (queries 2^88→2^72) and related-key NMAC-MD5 attacks.
- **49650252.pdf** — Collisions for the LPS expander graph hash function (Jean-Pierre Tillich, Gilles Zémor). Quasi-linear collision-finding algorithm against the Charles–Goren–Lauter LPS Ramanujan-graph hash, with concrete 100-digit-prime collision.
- **49650268.pdf** — Not a readable research paper: hex-encoded garbled extraction; appears to be a Turkish-authored paper with affiliations, but content unrecoverable.
- **49650287.pdf** — Efficient Two Party and Multi Party Computation against Covert Adversaries (Vipul Goyal, Payman Mohassel, Adam Smith). Efficient covert-adversary MPC secure with dishonest majority; constant-round two-party protocol improving Aumann–Lindell.
- **49650305.pdf** — Almost-everywhere Secure Computation (Juan Garay, Rafail Ostrovsky). Circumvents connectivity lower bounds for MPC by achieving security for most nodes in low-degree networks (e.g., expanders).
- **49650322.pdf** — Truly Efficient 2-Round Perfectly Secure Message Transmission Scheme (Kaoru Kurosawa, Kazuhiro Suzuki). First 2-round PSMT for n=2t+1 with O(n) transmission rate and polynomial computation, solving a CRYPTO 2006 open problem.
- **49650339.pdf** — Protocols and Lower Bounds for Failure Localization in the Internet (Boaz Barak, Sharon Goldberg, David Xiao). First formal security definitions and constructions for secure failure-localization path-quality monitoring, plus lower bounds (keys required, OWFs implied).
- **49650358.pdf** — HB#: Increasing the Security and Efficiency of HB+ (Henri Gilbert, Matthew Robshaw, Yannick Seurin). LPN-based RFID authentication protocols random-HB#/HB# resisting man-in-the-middle attacks that break HB+.
- **49650376.pdf** — Sub-linear Zero-Knowledge Argument for Correctness of a Shuffle (Jens Groth, Yuval Ishai). First practical shuffle argument with sub-linear communication for mix-nets/voting, combining PCP ideas with prior shuffle arguments.
- **49650394.pdf** — Precise Concurrent Zero Knowledge (Pandey, Pass, Sahai, Tseng, Venkitasubramaniam). First precise concurrent ZK with essentially optimal simulation overhead, including statistically precise protocols for unbounded concurrency.
- **49650412.pdf** — Efficient Non-interactive Proof Systems for Bilinear Groups (Jens Groth, Amit Sahai). The Groth–Sahai methodology: simple efficient NIZK/NIWI proofs working directly in bilinear groups without circuit reductions.
- **49650430.pdf** — Zero-Knowledge Sets with short proofs (Dario Catalano, Dario Fiore, Mariagrazia Messina). Trapdoor q-mercurial commitments from the Strong Diffie–Hellman assumption yield zero-knowledge sets with short proofs.
- **49650448.pdf** — Strongly Multiplicative Ramp Schemes From High Degree Rational Points On Curves (Chen, Cramer, de Haan, Cascudo Pueyo). Algebraic-geometry ramp secret-sharing schemes with strong multiplication and near-optimal privacy for efficient atomic secure multiplication.
