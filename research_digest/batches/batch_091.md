# Batch 091 digest

## Overview
- Dominant theme: theoretical cryptography circa 2010–2011 (mostly EUROCRYPT/TCC-era work), with heavy coverage of leakage-resilient crypto, homomorphic encryption/lattices, and secure multi-party computation.
- Strong lattice/FHE cluster: Gentry–Halevi FHE implementation, FHE over the integers, bonsai trees, lattice (H)IBE, BGN-type LWE cryptosystem, extreme-pruning lattice enumeration.
- Symmetric/crypto-analysis cluster: practical related-key attacks on AES-256, automatic related-key search, RC4/WPA statistical attacks, algebraic attack on compact-key McEliece variants, Stam's conjecture proof.
- Notable impact papers: Boneh–Freeman homomorphic signatures for polynomials, Lewko et al. fully secure functional encryption, Freeman's composite-to-prime-order pairing conversion, Ristenpart et al. indifferentiability limitations.
- Anomaly: 66320133.pdf extract is unreadable hex-encoded garbage (no recoverable title). One item (66320296) is a 2-page position essay, not a research paper. No obvious duplicates.
- Very little elliptic-curve/ECDLP content directly; pairings appear mainly as bilinear-group assumptions for ABE/VRF/signatures.

## Papers
- **66320087.pdf** — Fully Leakage-Resilient Signatures (Boyle, Segev, Wichs). First fully leakage-resilient signature schemes without random oracles, tolerating (1−o(1))L bits of leakage on the signing key.
- **66320107.pdf** — A Formal Study of Power Variability Issues and Side-Channel Attacks for Nanoscale Devices (Renauld, Standaert, Veyrat-Charvillon, Kamel, Flandre). Measurements on 20 AES S-box chips show technology scaling invalidates common leakage models and affects profiled attacks.
- **66320116.pdf** — Secure Obfuscation for Encrypted Signatures (Satoshi Hada). Obfuscator, secure in the standard model, for an encrypted-signature functionality composing Waters signatures with Boneh–Boyen–Shacham linear encryption.
- **66320127.pdf** — Implementing Gentry's Fully-Homomorphic Encryption Scheme (Gentry, Halevi). First implementation of full FHE including bootstrapping, via faster key generation and batching optimizations.
- **66320129.pdf** — Encryption Schemes Secure Against Chosen-Ciphertext Selective Opening Attacks (Fehr, Hofheinz, Kiltz, Wee). New SO-CCA-secure encryption construction from hash proof systems and cross-authentication codes, without lossy encryption.
- **66320133.pdf** — Unreadable: extract is hex-escaped garbage (font-encoding failure); title and content not recoverable.
- **66320147.pdf** — Homomorphic Signatures for Polynomial Functions (Boneh, Freeman). First homomorphic signature scheme evaluating multivariate polynomials on signed data, using ideal lattices.
- **66320149.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Three-Move Blind Signature Schemes (Fischlin, Schröder). Meta-reduction showing standard-model black-box security proofs for Chaum/Pointcheval–Stern blind signatures would solve hard problems.
- **66320154.pdf** — Multi-property-preserving Domain Extension Using Polynomial-based Modes of Operation (Lee, Steinberger). Double-piped mode preserving MAC/PRF/PRO properties at twice Lucks' speed, with O(2^n/n) unforgeability.
- **66320167.pdf** — Semi-Homomorphic Encryption and Multiparty Computation (Bendlin, Damgård, Orlandi, Zakarias). Relaxed semi-homomorphic encryption notion yields efficient UC-secure dishonest-majority MPC with a crypto-free online phase.
- **66320173.pdf** — Constant-Round Non-Malleable Commitments from Sub-Exponential One-Way Functions (Pass, Wee). First constant-round non-malleable commitment from one-wayness alone, with a black-box security proof.
- **66320176.pdf** — Bonsai Trees, or How to Delegate a Lattice Basis (Cash, Hofheinz, Kiltz, Peikert). Lattice "bonsai tree" structure giving standard-model hash-and-sign signatures and the first non-pairing hierarchical IBE.
- **66320180.pdf** — Adaptive Trapdoor Functions and Chosen-Ciphertext Security (Kiltz, Mohassel, O'Neill). Introduces adaptive TDFs as the weakest known TDF condition implying black-box CCA-secure PKE, unifying prior constructions.
- **66320184.pdf** — Plaintext-Dependent Decryption: A Formal Security Treatment of SSH-CTR (Paterson, Watson). Proves SSH binary packet protocol in counter mode secure under buffered stateful CCA, reconciling prior proofs with Albrecht et al. attacks.
- **66320187.pdf** — Tight Proofs for Signature Schemes without Random Oracles (Schäge). First tight security proofs for Strong-RSA signature classes (Cramer-Shoup, Camenisch-Lysyanskaya, Zhu, Fischlin) plus short SDH variants.
- **66320189.pdf** — Adaptively Secure Broadcast (Hirt, Zikas). Shows property-based broadcast definitions do not imply simulation-based ones and studies adaptively secure composable broadcast.
- **66320198.pdf** — Key Recovery Attacks of Practical Complexity on AES-256 Variants With Up To 10 Rounds (Biryukov, Dunkelman, Keller, Khovratovich, Shamir). Experimentally verifiable related-key attacks, e.g. recovering a 9-round AES-256 key with 2 related keys in 2^39 time.
- **66320199.pdf** — Stam's Collision Resistance Conjecture (Steinberger). Proves Stam's CRYPTO 2008 conjectured collision-resistance bound for multi-call compression functions up to a constant factor, covering most r=1 cases.
- **66320201.pdf** — Constructing Verifiable Random Functions with Large Input Spaces (Hohenberger, Waters). Practical bilinear-group VRF secure for exponentially large inputs under the non-interactive ℓ-DDHE assumption.
- **66320202.pdf** — Fully Secure Functional Encryption: Attribute-Based Encryption and (Hierarchical) Inner Product Encryption (Lewko, Okamoto, Sahai, Takashima, Waters). First fully (adaptively) secure ABE and inner-product predicate encryption via dual system encryption.
- **66320207.pdf** — Adaptive Pseudo-Free Groups and Applications (Catalano, Fiore, Warinschi). Defines adaptive pseudo-freeness, proves the RSA group satisfies it, and derives generic digital/network-coding signature schemes.
- **66320220.pdf** — Automatic Search for Related-Key Differential Characteristics in Byte-Oriented Block Ciphers (Biryukov, Nikolić). Search tool for related-key characteristics yielding best attacks on AES-192 (full), byte-Camellia (full), and Khazad.
- **66320221.pdf** — Partial Fairness in Secure Two-Party Computation (Gordon, Katz). New partial-fairness definition with broad feasibility results for polynomial-size domain/range functionalities under standard assumptions.
- **66320227.pdf** — Commuting Signatures and Verifiable Encryption (Fuchsbauer). Primitive where signing and verifiable encryption commute, built on automorphic signatures and Groth–Sahai proofs; yields simpler delegatable anonymous credentials.
- **66320228.pdf** — Secure Message Transmission with Small Public Discussion (Garay, Givens, Ostrovsky). First SMT-PD protocol with sublinear (logarithmic) public-channel communication and optimal private-channel complexity and rounds.
- **66320229.pdf** — Public-Key Encryption in the Bounded-Retrieval Model (Alwen, Dodis, Naor, Segev, Walfish, Wichs). First BRM public-key encryption tolerating arbitrary bounded key leakage, via identity-based hash proof systems from bilinear groups, lattices, and QR.
- **66320233.pdf** — Converting Pairing-Based Cryptosystems from Composite-Order Groups to Prime-Order Groups (Freeman). Framework converting composite-order pairing schemes (BGN, traitor tracing, ABE) to prime-order groups with comparable assumptions.
- **66320243.pdf** — Efficient Device-Independent Quantum Key Distribution (Hänggi, Renner, Wolf). Efficient QKD whose security is fully device-independent, resting only on non-local correlations and no-signaling between labs.
- **66320244.pdf** — Bounded Key-Dependent Message Security (Barak, Haitner, Hofheinz, Ishai). Standard-model PKE secure against KDM attacks for bounded keys/circuits under DDH or LWE, with application to symbolic protocol instantiation.
- **66320248.pdf** — Secure Authentication from a Weak Key, Without Leaking Information (Bouman, Fehr). Four-round information-theoretic message authentication from a weak session key that leaks nearly nothing about a reusable long-term key; quantum-secure variant.
- **66320253.pdf** — Computational Soundness, Co-induction, and Encryption Cycles (Micciancio). Co-inductive symbolic adversary-knowledge definitions yield computational soundness without banning encryption cycles.
- **66320254.pdf** — Fully Homomorphic Encryption over the Integers (van Dijk, Gentry, Halevi, Vaikuntanathan). Conceptually simple FHE using only modular arithmetic over integers; security reduces to approximate integer gcd.
- **66320257.pdf** — Lattice Enumeration using Extreme Pruning (Gama, Nguyen, Regev). Extreme-pruning technique gives exponential speedups for SVP/CVP enumeration, with the first sound analysis of pruning.
- **66320264.pdf** — Perfectly Secure Multiparty Computation and the Computational Overhead of Cryptography (Damgård, Ishai, Krøigaard). Perfectly secure honest-majority MPC with near-polylogarithmic amortized overhead, tolerating (1/3−ε) corruptions.
- **66320266.pdf** — A Simple BGN-type Cryptosystem from LWE (Gentry, Halevi, Vaikuntanathan). LWE-based encryption supporting many additions and one multiplication, with better expansion than BGN and IBE/leakage-resilient variants.
- **66320268.pdf** — Secret Keys from Channel Noise (Ahmadi, Safavi-Naini). Unconditionally secure key establishment over eavesdropped noisy channels without pre-shared randomness, with SK-capacity bounds.
- **66320273.pdf** — Universal One-Way Hash Functions via Inaccessible Entropy (Haitner, Holenstein, Reingold, Vadhan, Wee). Simpler, more efficient construction of UOWHFs from any one-way function via inaccessible entropy.
- **66320276.pdf** — Efficient Lattice (H)IBE in the Standard Model (Agrawal, Boneh, Boyen). LWE-based adaptively secure IBE and hierarchical IBE in the standard model using lattices with two distinct short-vector trapdoors.
- **66320284.pdf** — Protecting Circuits from Leakage: the Computationally-Bounded and Noisy Cases (Faust, Rabin, Reyzin, Tromer, Vaikuntanathan). General compiler making circuits leakage-resilient using one small stateless leak-proof component.
- **66320288.pdf** — Almost Optimum t-Cheater Identifiable Secret Sharing Schemes (Obana). Cheater-identifying secret sharing with share size |S|/ε, nearly optimal and independent of n, k, t.
- **66320290.pdf** — Algebraic Cryptanalysis of McEliece Variants with Compact Keys (Faugère, Otmani, Perret, Tillich). Algebraic key-recovery attack breaking quasi-cyclic and dyadic compact-key McEliece variants using bi-homogeneous equations from alternant codes.
- **66320296.pdf** — Cryptography between Wonderland and Underland (Yung). 2-page position essay contrasting theoretical cryptography with industrial cryptographic system development; not a research paper.
- **66320328.pdf** — Domain Extension for MACs Beyond the Birthday Barrier (Dodis, Steinberger). First variable-length MAC from fixed-length non-compressing primitives with security meaningful beyond the 2^(n/2) birthday bound.
- **66320348.pdf** — Statistical Attack on RC4 Distinguishing WPA (Sepehrdad, Vaudenay, Vuagnoux). RC4 bias-pool tools yielding a 2^43 WPA distinguisher and best-known WPA temporary-key recovery using 2^38 packets.
- **66320391.pdf** — Two-output Secure Computation With Malicious Adversaries (shelat, Shen). Compiles Yao garbled circuits to malicious security via witness indistinguishability with lower overhead than cut-and-choose; includes implementation.
- **66320411.pdf** — Efficient Non-Interactive Secure Computation (Ishai, Kushilevitz, Ostrovsky, Prabhakaran, Sahai). First general black-box two-party protocols using only parallel ideal-OT calls and no further interaction, even against malicious senders.
- **66320431.pdf** — Towards a Game Theoretic View of Secure Computation (Asharov, Canetti, Hazay). Captures secrecy, correctness, and fairness of two-party fail-stop protocols as Nash-equilibrium properties; four fairness notions shown equivalent.
- **66320451.pdf** — Highly-Efficient Universally-Composable Commitments based on the DDH Assumption (Lindell). Non-interactive UC commitments from DDH in the CRS model costing O(1) exponentiations, with static and adaptive variants.
- **66320471.pdf** — Concurrent Composition in the Bounded Quantum Storage Model (Unruh). Defines BQS-UC model and gives a statistically secure concurrently composable commitment, yielding setup-free two-party computation in bounded quantum storage.
- **66320491.pdf** — Careful with Composition: Limitations of the Indiﬀerentiability Framework (Ristenpart, Shacham, Shrimpton). Counterexample showing indifferentiability composition fails for multi-stage games; defines stronger reset indifferentiability that practical hashes cannot meet.
