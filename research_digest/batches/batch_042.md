# Batch 042 digest

## Overview
- Entire batch appears to be ASIACRYPT 2022 proceedings papers (uniform LNCS style, ~30 pages each, sequential numbering; an "asiacrypt22" contact email appears in 137910237).
- Dominant themes: zero-knowledge proofs/NIZKs and SNARKs, functional encryption, lattice/post-quantum cryptography (NTRU, LWE, Hawk, SPHINCS+, FO transform), and symmetric cryptanalysis (LowMC, differential-neural, rectangle/linear attacks, OMAC bounds).
- Also represented: secure messaging (ratcheting, AEAD key identification), distributed consensus/blockchain networking (Byzantine agreement, SMR, flooding), searchable/sum-preserving encryption, and isogeny crypto (SIDH proofs, group-action CDH≡DLog).
- Notable: 137910237 shows the De Feo–Jao–Plût SIDH identification scheme's soundness proof is invalid; 137910165 introduces the Hawk lattice signature; 137910083's Mimblewimble fix was deployed in Litecoin MWEB.
- Anomalies: none in content — all 50 files are genuine research papers, no duplicates. (Source chunk file contained 2 stray NUL bytes; a cleaned UTF-8 copy was used.)

## Papers
- **137910070.pdf** — On Module Unique-SVP and NTRU (Felderhoff, Pellet-Mary, Stehlé). Reduces worst-case module-uSVP to worst-case NTRU and gives a random self-reduction, showing NTRU is computationally representative of mod-uSVP.
- **137910075.pdf** — Random Sources in Private Computation (Couteau, Rosén). Studies how many parties need randomness in information-theoretic private computation, showing t random sources suffice for t-private computation with t < n/2.
- **137910076.pdf** — Non-Interactive Secure Computation of Inner-Product from LPN and LWE (Couteau, Zarezadeh). Introduces a non-interactive inner-product primitive via public encodings, built from a twist on Alekhnovich-based NIKE, with linear communication.
- **137910083.pdf** — Non-interactive Mimblewimble transactions, revisited (Fuchsbauer, Orrù). Fixes Yu's flawed non-interactive Mimblewimble transaction scheme with rigorous security analysis; a variant is deployed as Litecoin's MWEB.
- **137910089.pdf** — Algebraic Meet-in-the-Middle Attack on LowMC (Liu, Sarkar, Wang, Meier, Isobe). New algebraic MITM technique exploiting partial nonlinear layers improves CRYPTO 2021 attacks and breaks some LowMC instances for the first time.
- **137910094.pdf** — Cryptographic Primitives with Hinting Property (Alamati, Patranabis). Simpler hinting PRG constructions from cyclic-group/isogeny assumptions, plus hinting weak PRFs and a functional hinting property, enabling CCA encryption and KDM security.
- **137910096.pdf** — Efficient Searchable Symmetric Encryption for Join Queries (Jutla, Patranabis). JXT extends the OXT protocol to support conjunctive queries over joins of encrypted tables without pre-computation, with proven adaptive simulation-based security.
- **137910106.pdf** — Synthesizing Quantum Circuits of AES with Lower T-depth and Less Qubits (Huang, Sun). Generic synthesis/optimization techniques for quantum oracles of block ciphers yield lower T-depth, fewer-qubit AES circuits for Grover/Simon-based attacks.
- **137910111.pdf** — Multi-Client Functional Encryption with Fine-Grained Access Control (Nguyen, Phan, Pointcheval). Framework for MCFE combining function evaluation with LSSS-based access control, with constructions for inner products in single- and multi-client settings.
- **137910114.pdf** — Knowledge Encryption and Its Applications to Simulatable Protocols With Low Round-Complexity (Deng, Zhang). New "knowledge encryption" notion from two-round OT yields the first three-round (weakly) simulatable OT and other low-round protocols.
- **137910121.pdf** — Identity-Based Matchmaking Encryption from Standard Assumptions (Chen, Li, Wen, Weng). First IB-ME scheme secure in the standard model under SXDH, with constant-size parameters, built from a variant of two-level anonymous IBE.
- **137910133.pdf** — Enhancing Differential-Neural Cryptanalysis (Bao, Guo, Liu, Ma, Tu). Improved neutral-bit techniques give better 12-round and first practical 13-round neural key-recovery attacks on Speck32/64.
- **137910136.pdf** — Continuously Non-Malleable Codes against Bounded-Depth Tampering (Brian, Faust, Micheli, Venturi). First continuously non-malleable codes for decision-tree and bounded-depth tampering families, strengthening RKA protection without memory erasures.
- **137910138.pdf** — Failing gracefully: Decryption failures and the Fujisaki-Okamoto transform (Hövelmanns, Hülsing, Majenz). Tighter QROM security bounds for the FO transform handling decryption failures, also covering the explicit-reject variant.
- **137910142.pdf** — On Secure Ratcheting with Immediate Decryption (Pijnenburg, Poettering). Analyzes ratcheting protocols (as in Signal) that support immediate decryption under out-of-order ciphertext delivery, with forward and post-compromise security.
- **137910157.pdf** — Nostradamus goes Quantum (Benedikt, Fischlin, Huppert). Gives a quantum herding algorithm for the Nostradamus hash attack with ~2^{3n/7} evaluations and proves near-optimality against random compression functions.
- **137910160.pdf** — SNACKs: Leveraging Proofs of Sequential Work for Blockchain Light Clients (Abusalah, Fuchsbauer, Gaži, Klein). Defines succinct non-interactive arguments of chain knowledge from graph-labeling PoSWs to build trustless light-client protocols.
- **137910162.pdf** — Large-Precision Homomorphic Sign Evaluation using FHEW/TFHE Bootstrapping (Liu, Micciancio, Polyakov). Sign/comparison algorithms scaling logarithmically in the ciphertext modulus via iterative homomorphic floor evaluation, enabling high-precision FHE comparisons.
- **137910164.pdf** — Full Quantum Equivalence of Group Action DLog and CDH, and More (Montgomery, Zhandry). Shows CDH for abelian group actions (e.g., isogenies) is quantumly equivalent to discrete log for any non-negligible advantage.
- **137910165.pdf** — Hawk: Module LIP makes Lattice Signatures Fast, Compact and Simple (Ducas, Postlethwaite, Pulles, van Woerden). Module-LIP signature scheme with no floats or rejection sampling; four times faster and ~15% more compact than Falcon.
- **137910169.pdf** — Witness Encryption and Null-IO from Evasive LWE (Vaikuntanathan, Wee, Wichs). Proves security of the Chen–Vaikuntanathan–Wee witness encryption and null-iO candidate under the new evasive LWE assumption.
- **137910170.pdf** — Instantiability of Classical Random-Oracle-Model Encryption Transforms (Murphy, O'Neill, Zaheri). Using obfuscation, instantiates OAEP and Fujisaki-Okamoto with standard-model hash functions for specific PKE schemes achieving IND-CCA.
- **137910174.pdf** — Strongly Anonymous Ratcheted Key Exchange (Dowling, Hauck, Riepel, Rösler). First ratcheted key exchange adding strong anonymity guarantees under temporary secret exposure, extending RKE definitions.
- **137910176.pdf** — Collusion-Resistant Functional Encryption for RAMs (Ananth, Chung, Fan, Qian). First public-key collusion-resistant FE where keys represent RAM programs, from FE for circuits plus doubly-efficient PIR, with input-specific decryption time.
- **137910180.pdf** — Nonmalleable Digital Lockers and Robust Fuzzy Extractors in the Plain Model (Apon, Cachet, Fuller, Hall, Liu). First plain-model non-malleable digital lockers and robust fuzzy extractors for sources with under half entropy rate.
- **137910185.pdf** — Towards Tight Security Bounds for OMAC, XCBC and TMAC (Chattopadhyay, Jha, Nandi). Closes the gap between OMAC's security upper bound and the birthday attack, proving tight O(q²/2ⁿ + qℓ²/2ⁿ) PRF bounds.
- **137910186.pdf** — Counting Vampires: From Univariate Sumcheck to Updatable ZK-SNARK (Lipmaa, Siim, Zając). One-group-element univariate sumcheck argument yields Vampire, an updatable universal zk-SNARK with shortest known argument length.
- **137910187.pdf** — A Modular Approach to the Security Analysis of Two-Permutation Constructions (Chen). Public-permutation mirror theory framework simplifies multi-user security proofs for two-permutation constructions like TEM, pEDM, and nEHtMp.
- **137910191.pdf** — Compact and Tightly Selective-Opening Secure Public-key Encryption Schemes (Pan, Zeng). Four PKE schemes with the first combination of tight SIM-SO-CCA security and compact ciphertexts/public keys in the random oracle model.
- **137910192.pdf** — Recovering the tight security proof of SPHINCS+ (Hülsing, Kudinov). Repairs the flawed tight proof of SPHINCS+ via a new WOTS-TW security analysis and new quantum query lower bounds, losing only a factor w.
- **137910199.pdf** — Authenticated Encryption with Key Identification (Len, Grubbs, Ristenpart). First formalization of AEAD supporting key identification (AEAD-KI), with new robustness and indistinguishability notions and proven-secure constructions.
- **137910203.pdf** — Practical Provably Secure Flooding for Blockchains (Liu-Zhang, Matt, Maurer, Rito, Thomsen). Provably secure flooding protocol matching the bounded-delay network assumption of consensus proofs under weight-based corruption thresholds.
- **137910204.pdf** — State Machine Replication under Changing Network Conditions (Alexandru, Blum, Katz, Loss). Two network-agnostic SMR protocols with improved corruption thresholds or communication, plus impossibility of purely asynchronous proactive secret sharing.
- **137910208.pdf** — Efficient Adaptively-Secure Byzantine Agreement for Long Messages (Bhangale, Liu-Zhang, Loss, Nayak). Byzantine agreement for long messages with O(nℓ + n·poly(κ)) communication in synchronous and asynchronous models via subcommittee election.
- **137910210.pdf** — Triply Adaptive UC NIZK (Canetti, Sarkar, Wang). First multi-instance UC NIZK with full adaptive soundness and adaptive zero-knowledge from LWE (or LPN+DDH), via triply adaptive Sigma protocols and CI hashing.
- **137910215.pdf** — Compact FE for Unbounded Attribute-Weighted Sums for Logspace from SXDH (Datta, Pal, Takashima). First FE for attribute-weighted sums in the uniform model with unbounded attribute lengths and Logspace weight functions, from SXDH.
- **137910217.pdf** — New Algorithms and Analyses for Sum-Preserving Encryption (Miracle, Yilek). Proves a mixing-time bound for the Tajik et al. construction and gives new sum-preserving schemes via practical rank/unrank methods.
- **137910220.pdf** — Optimizing Rectangle Attacks: A Unified and Generic Framework for Key Recovery (Song, Zhang, Yang, Shi, Zhao, Hu, Weng). Unified generic key-recovery algorithm for rectangle attacks covering and outperforming the four previous algorithms.
- **137910226.pdf** — Improved Straight-Line Extraction in the Random Oracle Model With Applications to Signature Aggregation (Kondi, shelat). Improves computation cost of straight-line extractable proofs by 70–200×, motivated by Schnorr/EdDSA signature aggregation.
- **137910231.pdf** — Efficient NIZKs from LWE via Polynomial Reconstruction and "MPC in the Head" (Ghosal, Lou, Sahai). Builds LWE-based NIZKs on MPC-in-the-head instead of parallel repetition, using more efficient CI hash functions from polynomial reconstruction.
- **137910232.pdf** — GUC-Secure Commitments via Random Oracles: New Impossibility and Feasibility (Zhou, Zhang, Zhou, Ren). Shows 2-round GUC-secure commitments impossible in the global observable RO model, then gives a round-optimal scheme from one-way functions.
- **137910236.pdf** — EvalRound Algorithm in CKKS Bootstrapping (Kim, Park, Kim, Kim, Min). Optimizes the EvalRound step of CKKS bootstrapping to reduce level consumption in the standard Cheon et al. bootstrapping pipeline.
- **137910237.pdf** — SIDH Proof of Knowledge (De Feo, Dobson, Galbraith, Zobernig). Shows the De Feo–Jao–Plût identification scheme soundness proof is invalid and gives new sigma protocols proving knowledge of isogeny walks and torsion-point images.
- **137910240.pdf** — A Non-heuristic Approach to Time-space Tradeoffs and Optimizations for BKW (Liu, Yu). BKW variant via Wagner's generalized birthday on ac-ary trees achieves Esser et al.'s LPN/LWE time-space tradeoffs without heuristics.
- **137910244.pdf** — An Analysis of the Algebraic Group Model (Zhang, Zhou, Katz). Shows the claimed strict weakening of GGM by AGM fails as currently formalized: AGM hardness and reductions need not imply GGM analogues.
- **137910246.pdf** — Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge Proofs to Multiple Verifiers (Yang, Wang). Efficient multi-verifier ZK protocols in the RO model with sublinear per-gate communication per verifier, streamable and honest-majority secure.
- **137910248.pdf** — General Properties of Quantum Bit Commitments (Extended Abstract) (Yan). Any computational quantum bit commitment converts to canonical non-interactive form, and hiding/binding flavors are equivalent, making QBC symmetric.
- **137910250.pdf** — Optimising Linear Key Recovery Attacks with Affine Walsh Transform Pruning (Flórez-Gutiérrez). Affine pruning of the Walsh transform speeds linear key recovery, yielding an improved DES attack and the first 29-round PRESENT-128 attack.
- **137910266.pdf** — Functional Encryption with Secure Key Leasing (Kitagawa, Nishimaki). Defines SKFE with secure key leasing and transforms standard SKFE into it without extra assumptions, capturing arbitrary pirate strategies.
- **137910269.pdf** — Privacy-Preserving Authenticated Key Exchange in the Standard Model (Lyu, Liu, Han, Gu). Adds robustness to PPAKE to resolve recipient ambiguity; generic construction from KEM/SIG/MAC/PRG/SE instantiated from DDH/CDH in the standard model.
