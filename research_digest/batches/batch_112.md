# Batch 112 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by theoretical cryptography from the 2014–2015 era, consistent with ePrint preprints for TCC 2015 / PKC 2015 / EUROCRYPT 2015 (file numbers 9014xxxx–9020xxxx).
- Two large thematic clusters: non-malleable codes and tamper/leakage resilience (90140377–90140534, 90200135), and obfuscation-based results (iO, diO, probabilistic obfuscation, multilinear maps) spanning 90150377–90150669.
- Also strong coverage of secure computation (adaptive/UC MPC, verifiable computation, oblivious polynomial evaluation), functional encryption, tight security reductions, and selective-opening security of PKE.
- Elliptic-curve/pairing content is sparse: only structure-preserving signatures from Type II pairings (90200154) and BGV bootstrapping mentioning elliptic-curve groups (90200107) touch EC-adjacent topics.
- Most relevant to ECDLP: 90200136 (rigorous analysis of Pollard Kangaroo/Rho and Gaudry-Schost random-walk DLP attacks) — the only paper directly about the discrete logarithm problem.
- No anomalies: all 50 extracts are legitimate research-paper first pages; no duplicates, slides, or non-paper content detected.

## Papers
- **90140321.pdf** — General Statistically Secure Computation with Bounded-Resettable Hardware Tokens (Döttling, Kraschewski, Müller-Quade, Nilges). Studies UC secure computation with hardware tokens that an adversary may reset only a bounded number of times, nearly matching non-resettable stateful-token results.
- **90140347.pdf** — Resettably Sound Zero-Knowledge Arguments from OWFs — the (semi) Black-Box way (Ostrovsky, Scafuro, Venkitasubramanian). Constructs constant-round resettably-sound ZK arguments of knowledge using black-box one-way functions via semi-black-box Barak simulation.
- **90140377.pdf** — A Rate-Optimizing Compiler for Non-malleable Codes Against Bit-wise Tampering and Permutations (Agrawal, Gupta, Maji, Pandey, Prabhakaran). Compiles zero-rate non-malleable codes into rate-1 codes against bit-wise tampering and permutations, subsuming Cheraghchi–Guruswami.
- **90140400.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Non-Malleable Codes (Aggarwal, Dziembowski, Kazana, Obremski). Builds the first information-theoretic non-malleable code in the 2-split-state model that also tolerates leakage, closing a gap in Aggarwal et al. STOC 2014.
- **90140429.pdf** — Locally Decodable and Updatable Non-Malleable Codes and Their Applications (Dachman-Soled, Liu, Shi, Zhou). Defines and constructs non-malleable codes with local decoding/updating, applied to securing RAM computation against memory tampering and leakage.
- **90140453.pdf** — Tamper Detection and Continuous Non-Malleable Codes (Jafargholi, Wichs). Initiates tamper-detection codes for bounded function families and gives a modular non-malleable code construction combining tamper-detection with leakage-resilient codes.
- **90140483.pdf** — Optimal Algebraic Manipulation Detection Codes in the Constant-Error Model (Cramer, Padró, Xing). Studies AMD codes with constant error probability and unbounded message space, achieving tag length meeting the log log M + Ω(1) lower bound.
- **90140504.pdf** — Non-Malleable Condensers for Arbitrary Min-Entropy, and Almost Optimal Protocols for Privacy Amplification (Li). First non-malleable condensers for arbitrary min-entropy, yielding 2-round privacy amplification with optimal entropy loss.
- **90140534.pdf** — From Single-Bit to Multi-Bit Public-Key Encryption via Non-Malleable Codes (Coretti, Maurer, Tackmann, Venturi). Builds multi-bit PKE from single-bit CCA-secure PKE by encoding plaintexts with continuous non-malleable codes, achieving a self-destruct variant of CCA security.
- **90140563.pdf** — Constructing and Understanding Chosen Ciphertext Security via Puncturable Key Encapsulation Mechanisms (Matsuda, Hanaoka). Introduces puncturable KEMs as a unifying framework capturing DDN-style CCA-security proofs for existing PKE/KEM constructions.
- **90140593.pdf** — Non-committing encryption from Φ-hiding (Hemenway, Ostrovsky, Rosen). First non-committing encryption with ciphertext expansion logarithmic in message length, 2 rounds, based on the Φ-hiding assumption.
- **90140612.pdf** — On the Regularity of Lossy RSA: Improved Bounds and Applications to Padding-Based Encryption (Smith, Zhang). New regularity bounds for RSA exponentiation on arithmetic progressions under Φ-hiding; improves PKCS#1 v1.5 CPA analysis and corrects a flawed prior lemma.
- **90140632.pdf** — Tightly-Secure Authenticated Key Exchange (Bader, Hofheinz, Jager, Kiltz, Li). First AKE protocol with tight security independent of user/session counts, from SXDH/DLIN, via new multi-user signature and KEM definitions.
- **90150031.pdf** — Key-Homomorphic Constrained Pseudorandom Functions (Banerjee, Fuchsbauer, Peikert, Pietrzak, Stevens). Constructs PRFs that are simultaneously constrained and key-homomorphic, from multilinear maps and LWE.
- **90150089.pdf** — Oblivious Polynomial Evaluation and Secure Set-Intersection from Algebraic PRFs (Hazay). Efficient malicious-secure two-party protocols for oblivious polynomial evaluation and set-intersection using algebraic PRFs.
- **90150119.pdf** — Verifiable Random Functions from Weaker Assumptions (Jager). Fully adaptive VRFs with large input space from q-type assumptions with q only logarithmic in the security parameter; essentially Lysyanskaya's VUF.
- **90150142.pdf** — Multi-Client Verifiable Computation with Stronger Security Guarantees (Gordon, Katz, Liu, Shi, Zhou). Systematic study of multi-client verifiable computation with a simulation-based security notion and protocols tolerating stronger corruption scenarios.
- **90150167.pdf** — Public Verification of Private Effort (Alberini, Moran, Rosen). Anonymous polling framework with publicly verifiable results via a responder certification graph and new "Private Proof of Effort" primitive.
- **90150197.pdf** — Primary-Secondary-Resolver Membership Proof Systems (Naor, Ziv). 3-party ZK membership/non-membership proof systems motivated by privacy-preserving DNSSEC denial-of-existence.
- **90150227.pdf** — Tight Parallel Repetition Theorems for Public-Coin Arguments using KL-divergence (Chung, Pass). Simpler tight parallel-repetition proof via KL-divergence, plus first Chernoff-type theorem reducing soundness and completeness errors simultaneously.
- **90150246.pdf** — Stretching Groth-Sahai: NIZK Proofs of Partial Satisfiability (Ràfols). Extends Groth–Ostrovsky–Sahai correlated CRS generation via monotone span programs, yielding NIZKs of partial satisfiability of bilinear equations.
- **90150276.pdf** — Outlier Privacy (Lui, Pass). Introduces tailored differential privacy where each individual's privacy parameter depends on their "outlierness," with accurate histogram release mechanisms.
- **90150304.pdf** — Function-Private Functional Encryption in the Private-Key Setting (Brakerski, Segev). Generic transformation giving function privacy to any sufficiently expressive private-key functional encryption scheme.
- **90150323.pdf** — Functional Encryption for Randomized Functionalities in the Private-Key Setting from Minimal Assumptions (Komargodski, Segev, Yogev). Private-key FE for randomized functionalities from any deterministic-FE scheme, using punctured-programming techniques without obfuscation.
- **90150377.pdf** — Separations in Circular Security for Arbitrary Length Key Cycles (Koppula, Ramchen, Waters). Uses iO to separate IND-CPA from n-circular security for any n, including bit encryption, and strengthens distinguishers to key-recovery attacks.
- **90150400.pdf** — ZAPs and Non-Interactive Witness Indistinguishability from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Bitansky, Paneth). ZAPs from iO + OWFs and NIWIs from iO + one-way permutations, replacing prior trapdoor-permutation and bilinear assumptions.
- **90150428.pdf** — Random-Oracle Uninstantiability from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Brzuska, Farshim, Mittelbach). Shows Encrypt-with-Hash, Fujisaki–Okamoto and other ROM transforms are uninstantiable in the standard model assuming iO.
- **90150457.pdf** — On Obfuscation with Random Oracles (Canetti, Kalai, Paneth). Robust unobfuscatable function families cannot be VBB-obfuscated even with random-oracle access, contrasting with structured idealized-model positive results.
- **90150469.pdf** — Obfuscation of Probabilistic Circuits and Applications (Canetti, Lin, Tessaro, Vaikuntanathan). Defines probabilistic iO (pIO) variants, constructs X-pIO from sub-exponential iO + OWFs, and derives FHE and other applications.
- **90150499.pdf** — Graph-Induced Multilinear Maps from Lattices (Gentry, Gorbunov, Halevi). New lattice-based multilinear encoding scheme where permitted operations are restricted by a directed graph; encodes LWE samples in short square matrices.
- **90150529.pdf** — Obfuscating Circuits via Composite-Order Graded Encoding (Applebaum, Brakerski). Candidate circuit obfuscator from composite-order graded encodings operating directly on circuits, with complexity proportional to circuit size.
- **90150586.pdf** — Adaptively Secure, Universally Composable, Multiparty Computation in Constant Rounds (Dachman-Soled, Katz, Rao). Constant-round UC MPC tolerating malicious adaptive corruption of any number of parties, from iO and a CRS.
- **90150614.pdf** — Two-Round Adaptively Secure MPC from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Garg, Polychroniadou). UC two-round MPC secure against active adaptive adversaries corrupting arbitrary parties, without erasures, using iO.
- **90150639.pdf** — Obfuscation-based Non-black-box Simulation and Four Message Concurrent Zero Knowledge for NP (Pandey, Prabhakaran, Sahai). Four-message fully concurrent ZK for NP from public-coin differing-inputs obfuscation via a new PCP-free non-black-box simulation technique.
- **90150669.pdf** — Public-Coin Differing-Inputs Obfuscation and Its Applications (Ishai, Pandey, Sahai). Proposes public-coin diO, a plausible diO variant avoiding known impossibility results, with applications to obfuscation and functional encryption.
- **90200107.pdf** — Bootstrapping BGV Ciphertexts with a Wider Choice of p and q (Orsini, van de Pol, Smart). BGV bootstrapping without special modulus structure, with depth O(log p + log log q), using algebraic-group representations (polynomial or elliptic curve).
- **90200108.pdf** — Divisible E-Cash Made Practical (Canard, Pointcheval, Sanders, Traoré). First efficient divisible e-cash system secure in the standard model, using a public global tree structure for coins; also an efficient ROM variant.
- **90200112.pdf** — Short Signatures With Short Public Keys From Homomorphic Trapdoor Functions (Alperin-Sheriff). Standard-model lattice signatures with constant-size public keys and single-vector signatures via puncturable homomorphic trapdoor functions.
- **90200116.pdf** — How Secure is Deterministic Encryption? (Bellare, Dowsley, Keelveedhi). Three findings on deterministic PKE: standard-to-ROM security transfer subtleties, impossibility of selective-opening security, and single-user security not implying multi-user security.
- **90200124.pdf** — Two-Server Password-Authenticated Secret Sharing UC-Secure Against Transient Corruptions (Camenisch, Enderlein, Neven). First TPASS protocol with UC security against adaptive/transient server corruptions, including compromise-recovery procedures.
- **90200129.pdf** — Simulation-based Selective Opening CCA Security for PKE from Key Encapsulation Mechanisms (Liu, Paterson). Generalizes the Fehr et al. approach to SIM-SO-CCA secure PKE via a special KEM-based framework.
- **90200135.pdf** — Continuous Non-Malleable Key Derivation and Its Application to Related-Key Security (Qin, Liu, Yuen, Deng, Chen). Extends non-malleable KDFs to the continuous setting for unbounded adaptive RKA queries; construction for polynomial RKD functions.
- **90200136.pdf** — Collision of random walks and a refined analysis of attacks on the discrete logarithm problem (Kijima, Montenegro). Rigorously analyzes expected collision time in Pollard Kangaroo (matching bounds), Rho (first non-trivial lower bound), and Gaudry–Schost DLP algorithms.
- **90200138.pdf** — Adaptive Witness Encryption and Asymmetric Password-based Cryptography (Bellare, Hoang). Shows GGSW13 witness-encryption soundness is insufficient, defines adaptively-sound WE, and applies it to asymmetric password-based encryption.
- **90200143.pdf** — Simpler Efficient Group Signatures from Lattices (Nguyen, Zhang, Zhang). Simpler lattice-based group signature improving key and signature sizes by an O(log N) factor, secure under SIS and LWE in the ROM.
- **90200146.pdf** — Packing Messages and Optimizing Bootstrapping in GSW-FHE (Hiromasa, Abe, Okamoto). First matrix-encrypting FHE with homomorphic matrix arithmetic; optimizes bootstrapping, reducing the lattice approximation factor from Õ(n³) to Õ(n^2.5).
- **90200149.pdf** — Tightly-Secure Signatures from Chameleon Hash Functions (Blazy, Kakvi, Kiltz, Pan). Generic standard-model transformation from chameleon hashes to tightly-secure tree-based signatures; first tight SIS-based signature scheme.
- **90200150.pdf** — Simple Functional Encryption Schemes for Inner Products (Abdalla, Bourse, De Caro, Pointcheval). Practical inner-product functional encryption from DDH, plus a generic scheme instantiable under LWE.
- **90200154.pdf** — Strongly-Optimal Structure Preserving Signatures from Type II Pairings: Synthesis and Lower Bounds (Barthe, Fagerholm, Fiore, Scedrov, Schmidt, Tibouchi). Proves lower bounds on pairing computations in verification of Type-II structure-preserving signatures and gives matching constructions.
- **90200156.pdf** — On the Selective Opening Security of Practical Public-Key Encryption Schemes (Heuer, Jager, Kiltz, Schäge). Proves RSA-OAEP and DHIES (with one-time pad) achieve simulation-based SO-CCA security in the random oracle model.
