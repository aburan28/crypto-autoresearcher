# Batch 023 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by what appear to be ~2020-era conference proceedings (PKC/CRYPTO style): two contiguous ID blocks (12110xxx, 12171xxx) of full papers, mostly 26-31 pages.
- Heavy lattice/post-quantum presence: LWE/RLWE constructions, lattice reduction algorithms (CVPP slicer, enumeration, ModuleSVP, Ideal-SVP), isogeny-based SIKE/CSIDH cryptanalysis and threshold schemes.
- Strong proof-theory and ZK contingent: NIZK extensions, inner-product arguments, compressed Sigma-protocols, SNARGs, witness encryption, memory-tight reductions, indifferentiability.
- DLP/factorization relevance for this corpus: 12171078 (DLP in pairing-relevant fields) and 12171079 (RSA-240 + 795-bit DLP records) are the most directly ECDLP-adjacent.
- Anomalies: 121109003 is a 1-page invited-talk abstract (Ishai, "How Low Can We Go?"), not a full paper; 12171069 is an anonymized submission ("No Institute Given", authors not shown).

## Papers
- **12110168.pdf** — Going Beyond Dual Execution: MPC for Functions with Efficient Verification (Hazay, shelat, Venkitasubramaniam). Extends dual-execution 1-bit-leakage security to multiparty computation for efficiently verifiable functions, improving on two-party dual execution.
- **12110170.pdf** — Linearly-Homomorphic Signatures and Scalable Mix-Nets (Hébant, Phan, Pointcheval). Proves correct shuffling of signed ElGamal ciphertexts with constant-size global proof, yielding publicly verifiable, highly scalable mix-nets for e-voting.
- **12110173.pdf** — Improved Classical Cryptanalysis of SIKE in Practice (Costello, Longa, Naehrig, Renes, Virdia). Optimized van Oorschot-Wiener parallel collision search implementation giving improved concrete classical attacks on SIKE Round-2 parameters.
- **12110174.pdf** — Bandwidth-efficient threshold EC-DSA (Castagnos, Catalano, Laguillaumie, Savasta, Tucker). Gennaro-Goldfeder variant eliminating range proofs, cutting signing bandwidth 4.4-9x with malicious security in the dishonest-majority setting.
- **12110176.pdf** — Generic Authenticated Key Exchange in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Hövelmanns, Kiltz, Schäge, Unruh). FOAKE: two-message AKE from any passively secure PKE (even imperfectly correct) in the QROM, generalizing Fujisaki-Okamoto.
- **12110178.pdf** — Privacy-Preserving Authenticated Key Exchange and the Case of IKEv2 (Schäge, Schwenk, Lauer). Formal PPAKE model guaranteeing identity confidentiality; applied to prove identity-hiding of signature-based IKEv2 including dynamic modes.
- **12110185.pdf** — Threshold Schemes from Isogeny Assumptions (De Feo, Meyer). First threshold schemes from hard homogeneous spaces: threshold CSIDH encryption and threshold CSI-FiSh signatures with compact keys and thresholds up to 56.
- **12110197.pdf** — Tweaking the Asymmetry of Asymmetric-Key Cryptography on Lattices (Zhang, Yu, Fan, Zhang, Yang). Introduces asymmetric module-LWE/SIS assumptions yielding KEMs and signatures with smaller keys and ciphertexts than standard lattice schemes.
- **12110202.pdf** — The randomized slicer for CVPP: sharper, faster, smaller, batchier (Ducas, Laarhoven, van Woerden). Sharp success-probability analysis of the randomized slicer for closest-vector-with-preprocessing, improving time/memory trade-offs including batched instances.
- **12110209.pdf** — Limits on the Efficiency of (Ring) LWE based Non-Interactive Key Exchange (Guo, Kamath, Rosen, Sotiraki). Proves impossibility barriers for non-interactive LWE-based key exchange with polynomial modulus under natural reconciliation strategies.
- **12110212.pdf** — On Black-Box Extensions of NIZK Arguments, and Signatures Directly from Simulation Soundness (Abe, Ambrona, Ohkubo). Impossibility results for black-box conjunctive/disjunctive NIZK extensions; efficient signatures from unbounded simulation-sound NIZKs.
- **12110215.pdf** — Updateable Inner Product Argument with Logarithmic Verifier and Applications (Daza, Ràfols, Zacharakis). Structured-CRS inner product argument giving logarithmic verification, improving Bulletproofs range proofs and circuit satisfiability under standard assumptions.
- **12110218.pdf** — Memory-Tight Reductions for Practical Key Encapsulation Mechanisms (Bhattacharyya). Refutes an impossibility conjecture with memory-tight IND-CCA reductions for Cramer-Shoup, ECIES/Hashed-ElGamal KEM, and Fujisaki-Okamoto variants.
- **12110219.pdf** — Master-Key KDM-Secure IBE from Pairings (Garg, Gay, Hajiabadi). First IBE achieving key-dependent-message security for messages depending on the master secret key, from standard pairing assumptions with tight reductions.
- **12110222.pdf** — Improved Discrete Gaussian and Subgaussian Analysis for Lattice Cryptography (Genise, Micciancio, Peikert, Walter). Modular linear-algebraic framework unifying discrete Gaussian convolution theorems; application to an LWE self-reduction.
- **12110230.pdf** — Hierarchical Identity-Based Encryption with Tight Multi-Challenge Security (Langrehr, Pan). First HIBE with tight adaptive multi-challenge security under Matrix-DDH, plus extensions to tight CCA security and anonymity.
- **12110236.pdf** — Sublinear-Round Byzantine Agreement under Corrupt Majority (Chan, Pass, Shi). First corrupt-majority BA with sublinear O((1/eps) log(1/delta)) rounds, near-matching the Omega(1/eps) lower bound, against adaptive corruption.
- **12110240.pdf** — On QA-NIZK in the BPK Model (Abdolmaleki, Lipmaa, Siim, Zając). Studies subversion-resistant quasi-adaptive NIZKs, linking Sub-ZK to bare-public-key non-black-box NIZK; proves Kiltz-Wee QA-NIZK subversion zero-knowledge.
- **12110242.pdf** — PAKEs: New Framework, New Techniques and More Efficient Lattice-Based Constructions (Jiang, Gong, He, Nguyen, Wang). New PAKE framework with standard-model LWE/Ring-LWE instantiations via approximate smooth projective hashing, more efficient than prior lattice PAKEs.
- **12110246.pdf** — Witness Maps and Applications (Chakraborty, Prabhakaran, Wichs). Introduces unique/compact witness maps as deterministic-prover proof systems; constructs dual-mode witness maps for NP from sub-exponential iO, implying witness encryption.
- **12110250.pdf** — Toward RSA-OAEP without Random Oracles (Cao, O'Neill, Zaheri). Partial and full standard-model instantiations of RSA-OAEP hash oracles under chosen-ciphertext security, using algebraic properties of RSA at practical parameters.
- **12110259.pdf** — A New Paradigm for Public-Key Functional Encryption for Degree-2 Polynomials (Gay). First public-key FE for quadratic functions with succinct ciphertexts and simulation security from standard assumptions, via partially function-hiding inner-product FE.
- **12110260.pdf** — Threshold Ring Signatures: New Definitions and Post-Quantum Security (Haque, Scafuro). First t-out-of-N threshold ring signature definitions against active adversaries, with a post-quantum construction from trapdoor commitments.
- **12110263.pdf** — MPSign: A Signature from Small-Secret Middle-Product Learning with Errors (Bai et al.). Fiat-Shamir signature from polynomial LWE over an exponential family of defining polynomials, with tight security proof in the QROM.
- **12110274.pdf** — Verifiable Inner Product Encryption Scheme (Soroush, Iovino, Rial, Roenne, Ryan). First efficient verifiable inner-product encryption with unconditional verifiability against colluding encryptors and authorities; privacy under DLin.
- **12110280.pdf** — Witness Indistinguishability for any Single-Round Argument with Applications to Access Control (Brakerski, Kalai). Converts any single-round argument into a witness-indistinguishable one from quasi-polynomial OT, yielding private attribute-based access control.
- **121109003.pdf** — How Low Can We Go? (Invited Talk) (Ishai). One-page invited-talk abstract surveying minimal-cost cryptography (computation, communication, rounds, randomness); not a full research paper.
- **12171010.pdf** — New Techniques for Traitor Tracing: Size N^{1/3} and More from Pairings (Zhandry). Breaks the decade-old O(sqrt(N)) barrier with pairing-based traitor tracing achieving O(N^{1/3})-sized parameters and other trade-offs.
- **12171011.pdf** — Security Analysis of NIST CTR-DRBG (Hoang, Shen). Proves the patched NIST CTR-DRBG pseudorandom generator satisfies the robustness security notion of Dodis et al., addressing previously noted vulnerabilities.
- **12171021.pdf** — LWE with Side Information: Attacks and Concrete Security Estimation (Dachman-Soled, Ducas, Gong, Rossi). Framework integrating side-channel "hints" into primal lattice reduction attacks on LWE, with a Sage toolkit for concrete security estimates.
- **12171023.pdf** — Indifferentiability for Public Key Cryptosystems (Zhandry, Zhang). Initiates indifferentiability for PKE, signatures, and non-interactive key agreement, giving constructions indifferentiable from ideal cryptosystems in the random oracle model.
- **12171024.pdf** — Automatic Verification of Differential Characteristics: Application to Reduced Gimli (Liu, Isobe, Meier). Automated model combining difference and value transitions to avoid invalid differential characteristics in permutation-based primitives; applied to reduced Gimli.
- **12171031.pdf** — Compressed Sigma-Protocol Theory and Practical Application to Plug & Play Secure Algorithmics (Attema, Cramer). Reconciles Bulletproofs-style compression with Sigma-protocol theory, achieving logarithmic-communication circuit ZK within established theory.
- **12171039.pdf** — A Tight Parallel Repetition Theorem for Partially Simulatable Interactive Arguments via Smooth KL-Divergence (Berman, Haitner, Tsfadia). Tightens soundness-error bounds for parallel repetition of partially simulatable interactive arguments, improving prior hardness-amplification rates.
- **12171041.pdf** — A Polynomial-Time Algorithm for Solving the Hidden Subset Sum Problem (Coron, Gini). Replaces BKZ in the Nguyen-Stern attack with a multivariate technique, yielding the first polynomial-time hidden subset sum algorithm, practical to n~250.
- **12171043.pdf** — On Succinct Arguments and Witness Encryption from Groups (Barta, Ishai, Ostrovsky, Wu). Designated-verifier SNARG with 2-group-element proofs in standard (generic) groups via 1-query linear PCPs, halving Groth's concrete proof size.
- **12171046.pdf** — A non-PCP Approach to Succinct Quantum-Safe Zero-Knowledge (Bootle, Lyubashevsky, Nguyen, Seiler). First poly-logarithmic post-quantum ZK arguments not based on PCPs, via succinct commitments from Ring-SIS.
- **12171049.pdf** — Round-optimal Black-box Commit-and-prove with Succinct Communication (Kiyoshima). Four-round black-box succinct commit-and-prove combining MPC-in-the-head with Kalai et al.'s two-round argument, from sub-exponential CRHFs, PIR, and OT.
- **12171069.pdf** — Non-Malleability against Polynomial Tampering (authors not shown; anonymous submission). First explicit non-malleable code for bounded-degree polynomial tampering, via seedless non-malleable extractors, with applications to robust secret sharing.
- **12171070.pdf** — Interactive Proofs for Social Graphs (Katzir, Shikhelman, Yogev). Doubly-efficient two-message protocols for verifiably estimating social-graph size and health metrics with few oracle queries, made non-interactive via Fiat-Shamir.
- **12171075.pdf** — The MALICIOUS Framework: Embedding Backdoors into Tweakable Block Ciphers (Peyrin, Wang). Method hiding high-probability related-tweak differentials as key-recovery backdoors in tweakable block ciphers; instantiates the LowMC-M family.
- **12171077.pdf** — Chosen Ciphertext Security from Injective Trapdoor Functions (Hohenberger, Koppula, Waters). Black-box CCA-secure public-key encryption from any injective trapdoor function, without extra properties like lossiness.
- **12171078.pdf** — Asymptotic complexities of discrete logarithm algorithms in pairing-relevant finite fields (De Micheli, Gaudry, Pierrot). Compares quasi-polynomial, NFS, and adapted FFS variants at the small/medium-characteristic boundary to identify pairing parameters of maximal security.
- **12171079.pdf** — Comparing the difficulty of factorization and discrete logarithm: a 240-digit experiment (Boudot, Gaudry, Guillevic, Heninger, Thomé, Zimmermann). Records for RSA-240 (795-bit) factorization and 795-bit prime-field DLP, showing DLP is not much harder than factorization at equal size.
- **12171088.pdf** — Cryptanalytic Extraction of Neural Network Models (Carlini, Jagielski, Mironov). Treats ML model extraction as cryptanalysis: a differential attack on ReLU networks recovering parameters to floating-point precision with far fewer queries than prior work.
- **12171096.pdf** — Faster Enumeration-based Lattice Reduction: Root Hermite Factor k^{1/(2k)} in Time k^{k/8+o(k)} (Albrecht, Bai, Fouque, Kirchner, Stehlé, Wen). Speeds enumeration-based lattice reduction from k^{k/(2e)} to k^{k/8}, matching a conjectured heuristic lower bound.
- **12171100.pdf** — Lattice Reduction for Modules, or How to Reduce ModuleSVP to ModuleSVP (Mukherjee, Stephens-Davidowitz). Generalizes slide reduction to module lattices, reducing rank-k approximate ModuleSVP to lower-rank instances with near-plain-lattice quality.
- **12171101.pdf** — Adaptively Secure Constrained Pseudorandom Functions in the Standard Model (Davidson, Katsumata, Nishimaki, Yamada, Yamakawa). First adaptively secure constrained PRFs for non-trivial predicate classes from standard assumptions in the standard model.
- **12171112.pdf** — Rounding in the Rings (Liu, Wang). Algebraic framework for (Module) Ring-LWR: search-to-decision reduction and Ring-LWE to Module-RLWR reduction via a new ring leftover hash lemma.
- **12171116.pdf** — Random Self-reducibility of Ideal-SVP via Arakelov Random Walks (de Boer, Ducas, Pellet-Mary, Wesolowski). Rapid mixing of random walks on the Arakelov class group yields a sharp worst-case to average-case reduction for Ideal-SVP.
