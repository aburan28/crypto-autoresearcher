# Batch 025 digest

## Overview
- Two coherent conference runs: files 12171357–12171410 are CRYPTO 2020 (LNCS 12170) papers; files 12491112–12491251 are ASIACRYPT 2020 (LNCS 12491) papers. All ~2020-era full research papers.
- Dominant themes: secure multi-party computation (edaBits, GMW branching, matrix multiplication, RSA modulus generation, round complexity, synchronous/asynchronous MPC), NIST post-quantum cryptanalysis (LEDAcrypt, HQC, Classic McEliece side-channel, quantum sieve costs), and lattice-based constructions (NIZK, IPE, e-cash, KDM-CCA2, M-LWE hardness).
- Strong symmetric-crypto cluster: cryptanalysis of Spook, MiMC, tweakable Even-Mansour, key-alternating ciphers, impossible differentials, division property/monomial prediction, ARX differential models, quantum collision attacks on AES-like hashing.
- Isogeny-based crypto appears twice (B-SIDH, SiGamal) — both later affected by the 2022 SIDH breaks, though the extracts predate that.
- Password-authenticated key exchange shows up twice (UC relaxed PAKE, fuzzy asymmetric PAKE); updatable encryption also twice (Jiang; Boneh et al.) — related but distinct papers, not duplicates.
- No anomalies: every entry is a genuine research paper with title, authors, and abstract; no duplicates, slides, or garbage files detected.

## Papers
- **12171357.pdf** — Universally Composable Relaxed Password Authenticated Key Exchange (Abdalla, Barbosa, Bradley, Jarecki, Katz, Xu). Defines lazy-extraction PAKE in the UC framework and shows SPEKE, SPAKE2, TBPEKE UC-realize it in the ROM.
- **12171364.pdf** — Cryptanalysis Results on Spook: Bringing Full-round Shadow-512 to the Light (Derbez, Huynh, Lallemand, Naya-Plasencia, Perrin, Schrottenloher). Practical distinguishers on full-round Shadow-512/384 permutations and forgeries on 4-step S1P mode of NIST LWC candidate Spook.
- **12171368.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of LEDAcrypt (Apon, Perlner, Robinson, Santini). Identifies a large class of weak keys in NIST PQC candidate LEDAcrypt and gives practical key-recovery, e.g., 1 in 2^47.7 keys at 256-bit security with 2^18.7 guesses.
- **12171374.pdf** — NIZK from LPN and Trapdoor Hash via Correlation Intractability for Approximable Relations (Brakerski, Koppula, Mour). Builds NIZK arguments from LPN plus trapdoor hash functions via a new correlation-intractability strategy for approximable relations.
- **12171388.pdf** — Alzette: a 64-bit ARX-box (feat. CRAX and TRAX) (Beierle, Biryukov, Cardoso dos Santos, Großschädl, Perrin, Udovenko, Velichkov, Wang). Introduces a constant-time 64-bit ARX S-box with AES-comparable differential/linear properties for building (tweakable) block ciphers.
- **12171389.pdf** — Fully Deniable Interactive Encryption (Canetti, Park, Poburinnaya). First deniable communication protocol secure even when both parties are coerced, using subexponentially secure iO and one-way functions.
- **12171394.pdf** — Improved Primitives for MPC over Mixed Arithmetic-Binary Circuits (Escudero, Ghosh, Keller, Rachuri, Scholl). Introduces edaBits for efficient arithmetic-binary conversions, speeding truncation, comparison, and bit-decomposition with active security.
- **12171397.pdf** — Multiparty Generation of an RSA Modulus (Chen, Cohen, Doerner, Kondi, Lee, Rosefield, shelat). Maliciously secure distributed biprime RSA modulus generation via sampling-and-sieving, improving asymptotic and concrete efficiency over prior work.
- **12171401.pdf** — Efficient Pseudorandom Correlation Generators from Ring-LPN (Boyle, Couteau, Gilboa, Ishai, Kohl, Scholl). New PCGs from ring-LPN producing OLE, authenticated triples, and matrix-product correlations orders of magnitude more efficiently.
- **12171405.pdf** — Dynamic Decentralized Functional Encryption (Chotard, Dufour-Sans, Gay, Phan, Pointcheval). Defines DDFE letting users join dynamically without trusted setup; constructs inner-product FE from DDH/LWE in the ROM.
- **12171406.pdf** — PPAD-Hardness and Delegation with Unambiguous Proofs (Kalai, Paneth, Yang). Proves Nash-equilibrium hardness from a bilinear-map assumption via an unambiguous, updatable delegation scheme for super-polynomial-time computations.
- **12171410.pdf** — Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge Arguments for QMA, with Preprocessing (Coladangelo, Vidick, Zhang). Initiates NIZK for QMA: under quantum-hard LWE, any QMA language has a preprocessing NIZK with classical verification.
- **12491112.pdf** — Practical Exact Proofs from Lattices: New Techniques to Exploit Fully-Splitting Rings (Esgin, Nguyen, Seiler). Fast lattice ZK proof of knowledge of ternary linear-equation solutions, 8x shorter than Bootle–Lyubashevsky–Seiler CRYPTO 2019.
- **12491114.pdf** — MOTIF: (Almost) Free Branching in GMW via Vector-Scalar Multiplication (Heath, Kolesnikov, Peceny). GMW extension evaluating conditional branches nearly free by amortizing OTs across branches via vector-scalar gates, ~b-fold OT savings.
- **12491116.pdf** — Towards Classical Hardness of Module-LWE: The Linear Rank Case (Boudgoust, Jeudy, Roux-Langlois, Wen). First classical (non-quantum) worst-case hardness reduction for Module-LWE with polynomial modulus when rank ≥ number-field degree.
- **12491117.pdf** — Maliciously Secure Matrix Multiplication with Applications to Private Deep Learning (Chen, Kim, Razenshteyn, Rotaru, Song, Wagh). UC-secure SPDZ-style matrix multiplication/convolution protocols cutting communication over 100x versus LowGear.
- **12491122.pdf** — Finding Collisions in a Quantum World: Quantum Black-Box Separation of Collision-Resistance and One-Wayness (Hosoyamada, Yamakawa). Extends Simon's separation to the quantum setting: no quantum fully-black-box reduction from CRHF to one-way/trapdoor permutations.
- **12491130.pdf** — Estimating Quantum Speedups for Lattice Sieves (Albrecht, Gheorghiu, Postlethwaite, Schanck). Heuristic cost analysis and quantum circuits for near-neighbour-search sieve components; finds only small quantum speedups under optimistic assumptions.
- **12491131.pdf** — B-SIDH: Supersingular Isogeny Diffie-Hellman Using Twisted Torsion (Costello). SIDH variant using both (p+1)- and (p−1)-torsion of supersingular curves and twists, enabling smaller primes and smaller public keys.
- **12491133.pdf** — Mind the Propagation of States: New Automatic Search Tool for Impossible Differentials and Impossible Polytopic Transitions (Hu, Li, Jiao, Tian, Wang). State-propagation-based automatic search overcoming key-schedule and large-S-box limitations of prior impossible-differential tools.
- **12491136.pdf** — Minimizing the Two-Round Tweakable Even-Mansour Cipher (Dutta). Shows 2-round and 4-round tweakable Even-Mansour constructions retain 2n/3-bit security even when built from fewer independent permutations.
- **12491139.pdf** — Determining the Core Primitive for Optimally Secure Ratcheting (Balli, Rösler, Vaudenay). Revisits strong ratcheted key exchange security, unifying secret-exposure and randomness attacks, and identifies necessary building blocks.
- **12491140.pdf** — Beyond Birthday Bound Secure Fresh Rekeying: Application to Authenticated Encryption (Mennink). Fresh rekeying schemes achieving beyond-birthday-bound (2n/3 to optimal 2^n) side-channel-resistant security by borrowing tweakable-cipher design ideas.
- **12491141.pdf** — The Direction of Updatable Encryption Does Not Matter Much (Jiang). Proves uni- and bi-directional updatable-encryption security notions equivalent, and gives an efficient LWE-based post-quantum scheme.
- **12491151.pdf** — Incrementally Aggregatable Vector Commitments and Applications to Verifiable Decentralized Storage (Campanelli, Fiore, Greco, Kolonelos, Nizzardo). Subvector-commitment openings mergeable succinctly unboundedly many times; constructions in unknown-order groups with constant-size parameters.
- **12491152.pdf** — Lattice-Based E-Cash, Revisited (Deo, Libert, Nguyen, Sanders). First secure compact e-cash from lattices, fixing proofs invalidated by Bourse et al. (ASIACRYPT 2019), with two concrete instantiations.
- **12491153.pdf** — Privacy-Preserving Pattern Matching on Encrypted Data (Bkakria, Cuppens, Cuppens). Public- and private-key schemes for arbitrary pattern matching over encrypted data protecting both data and patterns, with constant-size trapdoors.
- **12491168.pdf** — SiGamal: A Supersingular Isogeny-based PKE and Its Application to a PRF (Moriya, Onuki, Takagi). ElGamal-like PKE from CSIDH with an added 2^r-order point, IND-CPA under new P-CSSDDH assumption, plus a Naor–Reingold-style PRF.
- **12491170.pdf** — Improving Speed and Security in Updatable Encryption Schemes (Boneh, Eskandarian, Kim, Shih). Stronger ciphertext-dependent security definitions and significantly faster updatable encryption constructions for cloud key rotation.
- **12491175.pdf** — Non-Committing Encryption with Constant Ciphertext Expansion from Standard Assumptions (Yoshida, Kitagawa, Xagawa, Tanaka). First NCE with constant ciphertext expansion from DDH or LWE without iO or CRS, via weak-NCE amplification with wiretap codes.
- **12491179.pdf** — Efficient Homomorphic Comparison Methods with Optimal Complexity (Cheon, Kim, Kim). Composite-polynomial approximation of the sign function giving optimal-complexity comparison in homomorphic encryption, faster for large inputs.
- **12491187.pdf** — Fuzzy Asymmetric Password-Authenticated Key Exchange (Erwig, Hesse, Orlt, Riahi). Combines asymmetric and fuzzy PAKE in a UC definition tolerating noisy passwords and untrusted servers; built from OT and robust secret sharing.
- **12491188.pdf** — Public-Key Generation with Verifiable Randomness (Blazy, Towa, Vergnaud). New model and generic protocols proving keys were generated from honest randomness; includes a faster zero-knowledge argument for double discrete log.
- **12491193.pdf** — Side Channel Information Set Decoding Using Iterative Chunking: Plaintext Recovery from the "Classic McEliece" Hardware Reference Implementation (Lahr, Niederhagen, Petri, Samardjiska). EM side-channel plus ISD iterative-chunking attack recovering plaintexts from Classic McEliece FPGA implementation in ~562 measurements.
- **12491195.pdf** — On the Exact Round Complexity of Best-of-Both-Worlds Multi-party Computation (Patra, Ravi, Singla). Nearly settles round complexity of BoBW MPC achieving GOD/fairness with honest majority and abort security with dishonest majority.
- **12491198.pdf** — Improved Security Analysis for Nonce-based Enhanced Hash-then-Mask MACs (authors not visible in extract). Proves nEHtM MAC security up to ~2^{3n/4} queries with graceful degradation, and stronger bounds for truncated variants.
- **12491199.pdf** — Simulation-Sound Arguments for LWE and Applications to KDM-CCA2 Security (Libert, Nguyen, Passelègue, Titiu). Direct LWE-based simulation-sound NIZK instantiating Naor–Yung in the standard model, yielding LWE-based KDM-CCA2 encryption.
- **12491200.pdf** — MPC with Synchronous Security and Asynchronous Responsiveness (Liu-Zhang, Loss, Maurer, Moran, Tschudi). Matching feasibility/impossibility results for MPC combining synchronous security with asynchronous responsiveness under t/T corruption thresholds.
- **12491205.pdf** — A New Decryption Failure Attack against HQC (Guo, Johansson). Secret-key recovery on hqc-256-1 via decryption failures of ~2^64 crafted ciphertexts after one-time precomputation; overall ~2^246 complexity.
- **12491206.pdf** — Possibility and Impossibility Results for Receiver Selective Opening Secure PKE in the Multi-Challenge Setting (Yang, Lai, Huang, Au, Xu, Susilo). Characterizes which simulation-based RSO security notions are achievable for PKE under multiple challenge ciphertexts.
- **12491207.pdf** — Cryptography from One-Way Communication: On Completeness of Finite Channels (Agrawal, Ishai, Kushilevitz, Narayanan, Prabhakaran, Prabhakaran, Rosen). Bit-ROT is complete with inverse-polynomial error, but no finite channel realizes string-ROT with negligible error.
- **12491224.pdf** — Tight Security Analysis of 3-Round Key-Alternating Cipher with a Single Permutation (Wu, Yu, Cao, Dong). Proves 3-round KAC built from one permutation is secure up to Θ(2^{3n/4}) queries, matching the independent-permutation bound.
- **12491227.pdf** — Quantum Collision Attacks on AES-like Hashing with Low Quantum Random Access Memories (Dong, Sun, Shi, Gao, Wang, Hu). Extends quantum rebound attacks on AES-like hash functions to work with low quantum RAM, improving Hosoyamada–Sasaki EUROCRYPT 2020.
- **12491230.pdf** — An Algebraic Formulation of the Division Property: Revisiting Degree Evaluations, Cube Attacks, and Key-Independent Sums (Hu, Sun, Wang, Wang). "Monomial prediction" framework algebraically detecting division-property trails, unifying cube attacks and degree evaluation.
- **12491231.pdf** — CCA-Secure (Puncturable) KEMs from Encryption with Non-Negligible Decryption Errors (Cini, Ramacher, Slamanig, Striecks). Practical compilers transforming PKE with large correctness error into CCA-secure KEMs, applicable to NIST PQC candidates.
- **12491233.pdf** — A Bit-Vector Differential Model for the Modular Addition by a Constant (Azimi, Ranea, Salmasizadeh, Mohajeri, Aref, Rijmen). First SMT-compatible differential model for constant modular addition, enabling automated differential analysis of more ARX ciphers.
- **12491237.pdf** — MoniPoly — An Expressive q-SDH-Based Anonymous Attribute-Based Credential System (Tan, Groß). Pairing-based ABC system with set commitments supporting complex show proofs over unrestricted attribute spaces, constant pairing cost for verifier.
- **12491239.pdf** — Collusion Resistant Trace-and-Revoke for Arbitrary Identities from Standard Assumptions (Kim, Wu). Trace-and-revoke scheme tolerating unbounded collusions and arbitrary identities from standard assumptions, overcoming prior bounded-collusion limits.
- **12491240.pdf** — An Algebraic Attack on Ciphers with Low-Degree Round Functions: Application to Full MiMC (Eichlseder, Grassi, Lüftenegger, Øygarden, Rechberger, Schofnegger, Wang). First key-recovery on full-round binary-field MiMC via generalized higher-order differential cryptanalysis with half the codebook.
- **12491251.pdf** — Adaptively Secure Inner Product Encryption from LWE (Katsumata, Nishimaki, Yamada, Yamakawa). First adaptively secure inner-product encryption from LWE with sub-exponential modulus, without complexity leveraging.
