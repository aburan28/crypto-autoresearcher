# Batch 065 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ASIACRYPT 2004 (LNCS 3329, the `3329xxxx` files) and TCC 2005 (LNCS 3378, the `3378_xxx` files) proceedings — era ~2004–2005, mostly symmetric crypto, provable security, and protocol theory.
- Notable cluster of algebraic-cryptanalysis papers: XSL analysis (Cid–Leurent), XL bounds (Diem), and XL-vs-Gröbner comparison (Ars–Faugère et al.), collectively deflating XSL/XL claims against AES.
- Strong hash-function mini-theme: three papers on UOWHF domain extenders and higher-order UOWHFs (Sarkar; Hong–Preneel–Lee), plus timestamping security (Buldas–Saarepera).
- Little elliptic-curve content: one curve-based hardware coprocessor paper (Sakiyama et al.) and one pairing-based group signature (Nguyen–Safavi-Naini); ECDLP itself is absent.
- Anomalies: three files (33290211, 33290445, 3378_151) are garbled mojibake extracts with unidentifiable titles; 33290425 is a Korean government IT-policy talk, not research; 32 and 33290076 are 1-page position/talk abstracts.

## Papers
- **31560441.pdf** — How to Disembed a Program? (Chevallier-Mames, Naccache, Paillier, Pointcheval). Proposes the ROM-less "Externalized Microprocessor" secure token, with RSA screening and provably secure protocols for executing and authenticating externally supplied code.
- **32.pdf** — Challenges for Trusted Computing (Ahmad-Reza Sadeghi). 1-page position paper surveying open problems in TCG/TPM deployment: TPM complexity, spec non-compliance, and missing analysis.
- **328.pdf** — An Analysis of the XSL Algorithm (Cid, Leurent). Systematic study giving strong evidence that XSL linearization does not efficiently solve the AES equation system in its current form.
- **33.pdf** — Superscalar Coprocessor for High-speed Curve-based Cryptography (Sakiyama, Batina, Preneel, Verbauwhede). Hardware coprocessor exploiting instruction-level parallelism speeds up ECC/HECC scalar multiplication 1.8–2.7×.
- **33290001.pdf** — On Feistel Ciphers Using Optimal Diffusion Mappings across Multiple Rounds (Shirai, Preneel). Proves active-S-box guarantees (R(m+1) in 3R rounds) for Feistel designs resisting both differential and linear cryptanalysis.
- **33290016.pdf** — Efficient Instantiations of Tweakable Blockciphers and Refinements to Modes OCB and PMAC (Rogaway). XE/XEX constructions turn any blockcipher into a cheap tweakable one, yielding simpler, faster OCB and PMAC variants.
- **33290031.pdf** — Eliminating Random Permutation Oracles in the Even-Mansour Cipher (Gentry, Ramzan). Replaces the random permutation oracle with a 4-round Feistel of random functions, proving the cipher super-pseudorandom.
- **33290046.pdf** — Towards Plaintext-Aware Public-Key Encryption without Random Oracles (Bellare, Palacio). Defines PA0/PA1/PA2 hierarchy; proves DEG and CS-lite plaintext-aware, yielding the most efficient proven IND-CCA1 scheme.
- **33290061.pdf** — OAEP 3-Round: A Generic and Secure Asymmetric Encryption Padding (Phan, Pointcheval). Three-round OAEP variant achieving IND-CCA2 with optimal bandwidth ratio, avoiding OAEP's loose quadratic reduction.
- **33290076.pdf** — Stream Ciphers: Dead or Alive? (Shamir). 1-page invited-talk abstract surveying the declining state, attacks on, and possible futures of stream ciphers.
- **33290077.pdf** — On the Generalized Linear Equivalence of Functions over Finite Fields (Breveglieri, Cherubini, Macchetti). Generalizes linear equivalence via geometric representation; finds APN functions not classically equivalent to power functions.
- **33290090.pdf** — Sieving Using Bucket Sort (Aoki, Ueda). Bucket-sort sieving for the number field sieve that drastically cuts cache misses, speeding the dominant factoring step.
- **33290102.pdf** — Right-Invariance: A Property for Probabilistic Analysis of Cryptography based on Infinite Groups (Eonkyung Lee). Builds a mathematical framework transplanting finite-group right-invariance reasoning to infinite-group cryptography.
- **33290118.pdf** — Practical Two-Party Computation based on the Conditional Gate (Schoenmakers, Tuyls). Introduces the conditional gate, efficiently realized from homomorphic threshold ElGamal, enabling practical MPC circuits.
- **33290136.pdf** — Privacy in Non-Private Environments (Bläser, Jakoby, Liśkiewicz, Manthey). Introduces "lossy private" protocols quantifying information leaked when privately computing on non-2-connected networks.
- **33290151.pdf** — Asynchronous Proactive Cryptosystems Without Agreement (Przydatek, Strobl). Proactive secret sharing and joint random sharing protocols avoiding asynchronous agreement, with bounded worst-case complexity.
- **33290168.pdf** — Lattice-Based Threshold-Changeability for Standard Shamir Secret-Sharing Schemes (Steinfeld, Wang, Pieprzyk). Raises Shamir thresholds post-setup without dealer/shareholder communication, using lattice reduction.
- **33290184.pdf** — Masking Based Domain Extenders for UOWHFs: Bounds and Constructions (Sarkar). Proves a ⌈log2 s⌉ mask lower bound and gives a new parallel UOWHF domain extender with near-optimal key expansion.
- **33290198.pdf** — Higher Order Universal One-Way Hash Functions (Hong, Preneel, Lee). Generalizes UOWHFs to order r, enabling hash constructions with much shorter keys in linear and tree modes.
- **33290211.pdf** — Title unidentifiable (?). Extract is garbled mojibake (broken CJK/embedded-font encoding); content unreadable, appears to discuss hash-function-like parameters.
- **33290228.pdf** — New Approaches to Password Authenticated Key Exchange based on RSA (Muxiang Zhang). PEKEP, an RSA-based PAKE allowing small exponents, proven secure against e-residue offline dictionary attacks in the random oracle model.
- **33290243.pdf** — Constant-Round Authenticated Group Key Exchange for Dynamic Groups (Kim, Lee, Lee). Provably secure constant-round contributory AGKE for ad hoc groups with joins/leaves; three exponentiations per user.
- **33290258.pdf** — A Public-Key Black-Box Traitor Tracing Scheme with Sublinear Ciphertext Size against Self-Defensive Pirates (Matsushita, Imai). Sublinear-ciphertext public-key tracing that still identifies a traitor even when pirate decoders self-destruct on detection.
- **33290273.pdf** — Batching Schnorr Identification Scheme with Applications to Privacy-Preserving Authorization (Gennaro, Leigh, Sundaram, Yerazunis). Runs many Schnorr proofs near the cost of one; enables selective-disclosure authorization on low-bandwidth smart cards.
- **33290290.pdf** — Secret Handshakes from CA-Oblivious Encryption (Castelluccia, Jarecki, Tsudik). Builds secret-handshake protocols under CDH (not BDH) using new CA-oblivious public-key encryption hiding the certifying authority.
- **33290305.pdf** — k-Times Anonymous Authentication (Teranishi, Furukawa, Sako). Anonymous authentication unlinkable up to k uses; anyone can publicly trace users exceeding the limit, no authority needed.
- **33290320.pdf** — The XL-Algorithm and a Conjecture from Commutative Algebra (Diem). Proves dimension upper bounds showing XL is not subexponential for slightly overdetermined systems, assuming/discussing Fröberg's conjecture.
- **33290335.pdf** — Comparison between XL and Gröbner Basis Algorithms (Ars, Faugère, Imai, Kawazoe, Sugita). Shows XL is a redundant variant of F4; F5 uses far smaller matrices on semi-regular systems.
- **33290350.pdf** — Generic Homomorphic Undeniable Signatures (Monnerat, Vaudenay). Defines a group-homomorphism interpolation problem generalizing DL/DH/RSA; converts private homomorphisms into undeniable signatures with arbitrarily short signatures.
- **33290367.pdf** — Efficient and Provably Secure Trapdoor-free Group Signature Schemes from Bilinear Pairings (Nguyen, Safavi-Naini). Trapdoor-free pairing group signatures with constant-size keys, proven secure in the BSZ04 model, half/third the size of ACJT00.
- **33290382.pdf** — On the Security of MOR Public Key Cryptosystem (Lee, Kim, Kwon, Nahm, Kwak, Baek). Reduces MOR security to DLP over G itself generically, and analyzes Inn(G) DLP via group extensions and central commutator attacks.
- **33290397.pdf** — Cryptanalyzing the Polynomial-Reconstruction based Public-Key System Under Optimal Parameter Choice (Kiayias, Yung). Uses list decoding to break the general Augot–Finiasz coding-theoretic cryptosystem approach, not just the original parameters.
- **33290412.pdf** — Colluding Attacks to a Payment Protocol and Two Signature Exchange Schemes (Bao). Simple collusion breaks fairness of Wang's Asiacrypt'03 payment protocol and two Ateniese fair-exchange schemes; fixes only the latter.
- **33290425.pdf** — Information Security in Korea IT839 Strategy (Ho-Ick Suk). Not a research paper: 2-page Korean government policy talk describing the IT839 strategy (8 services, 3 infrastructures, 9 growth engines).
- **33290427.pdf** — How Far Can We Go Beyond Linear Cryptanalysis? (Baignères, Junod, Vaudenay). Unified statistical framework for generalized linear attacks; constructs optimal distinguishers and shows partial inherited resistance.
- **33290445.pdf** — Title unidentifiable (?). Extract is garbled mojibake (broken CJK/embedded-font encoding); content unreadable.
- **33290462.pdf** — Time-Memory Trade-Off Attacks on Multiplications and T-functions (Mitra, Sarkar). Breaks Klimov–Shamir multi-word T-function proposals, recovering 128-bit states in 2^21–2^40 time; suggests conjugate-permutation fixes.
- **33290477.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Bluetooth Keystream Generator Two-level E0 (Lu, Vaudenay). Exploits resynchronization flaws and FSM bias for the best key-recovery attack on two-level E0: 2^40 operations from 2^35 short frames.
- **33290491.pdf** — On Provably Secure Time-Stamping Schemes (Buldas, Saarepera). Shows collision-resistance folklore proofs fail against precomputation; introduces chain-resistance condition yielding provably secure timestamping.
- **33290506.pdf** — Strong Conditional Oblivious Transfer and Computing on Intervals (Blake, Kolesnikov). Stronger COT definition plus simple one-round protocols for greater-than and union-of-intervals membership in the semi-honest model.
- **33290521.pdf** — Improved Setup Assumptions for 3-Round Resettable Zero Knowledge (Di Crescenzo, Persiano, Visconti). Introduces the counter public-key model enabling three-round concurrently sound resettable zero-knowledge arguments for NP.
- **3378_001.pdf** — Optimal Error Correction Against Computationally Bounded Noise (Micali, Peikert, Sudan, Wilson). Cryptographic public-key coding schemes uniquely decodable beyond classical bounds (near 1/2 error rate), proven qualitatively optimal.
- **3378_020.pdf** — Hardness Amplification of Weakly Verifiable Puzzles (Canetti, Halevi, Steiner). Proves direct-product hardness amplification when only the puzzle generator can verify solutions; relates to parallel repetition of arguments.
- **3378_037.pdf** — On Hardness Amplification of One-Way Functions (Lin, Trevisan, Wee). Lower bounds on fully black-box weak-to-strong one-way function constructions via hitting-set connections; better parameters imply OWFs unconditionally.
- **3378_053.pdf** — Cryptography in Subgroups of Z_n* (Groth). Uses small hidden-order subgroups of RSA groups for more efficient commitments, signatures (adaptive-CMA proof), and homomorphic encryption under new assumptions.
- **3378_069.pdf** — Efficiently Constructible Huge Graphs that Preserve First Order Properties of Random Graphs (Naor, Nussboim, Tromer). Efficient pseudorandom graph constructions satisfying 0/1 laws for bounded-quantifier-depth first-order properties.
- **3378_088.pdf** — Comparing Two Notions of Simulatability (Hofheinz, Unruh). Separates standard from universal simulatability for computational/statistical security (they coincide perfectly); defines time-lock puzzles for a stronger separation.
- **3378_106.pdf** — Relaxing Environmental Security: Monitored Functionalities and Client-Server Computation (Prabhakaran, Sahai). Monitored-functionalities framework drops extractability from UC-style definitions, enabling simpler plain-model client-server protocols.
- **3378_130.pdf** — Handling Expected Polynomial-Time Strategies in Simulation-Based Security Proofs (Katz, Lindell). Develops rigorous techniques for expected-polynomial-time adversaries/simulators within simulation-based security proofs.
- **3378_151.pdf** — Title unidentifiable (?). Extract is garbled mojibake (broken CJK/embedded-font encoding); content unreadable.
