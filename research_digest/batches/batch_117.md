# Batch 117 digest

## Overview
- Batch is dominated by 2015-era conference papers (mostly ASIACRYPT/TCC-style, ~25-page LNCS format) covering symmetric cryptography, provable security, and secure computation — almost no elliptic-curve/ECDLP content despite the corpus theme.
- Strong clusters: symmetric designs/cryptanalysis (Sprout, Midori, ASASA, AEZ/Marble, HMAC, Even–Mansour, hash known-key attacks), secure computation/MPC (UC black-box, OT-based SPDZ, ORAM, garbling, cut-and-choose), and advanced public-key primitives (IBE, ABE, functional encryption, obfuscation).
- Notable non-technical items: three anomalous short extracts (94520356, 94520357, 94520358) which are 1–2 page keynote/essay abstracts, not full papers — including Rogaway's "The Moral Character of Cryptographic Work".
- Side-channel/leakage theme present (key enumeration, power-analysis metric ILA, AES under leakage, memory-hard tradeoffs).
- Obfuscation lower bounds cluster at the end (95620001, 95620016, 95620046), with 95620016 explicitly building on 95620001 (Pass–Shelat) but not a duplicate.
- Anomalies found: 3 (short keynote/essay abstracts); no duplicates detected.

## Papers
- **94520196.pdf** — Improved security proofs in lattice-based cryptography: using the Rényi divergence (Bai, Langlois, Lepoint, Stehlé, Steinfeld). Replaces statistical distance with Rényi divergence in lattice security proofs, yielding smaller parameters and simpler proofs for search and some distinguishing problems.
- **94520198.pdf** — Solving Linear Equations Modulo Unknown Divisors: Revisited (Lu, Zhang, Peng, Lin). Generalizes Howgrave-Graham-style lattice attacks for small solutions modulo unknown divisors, with applications to RSA-variant cryptanalysis.
- **94520210.pdf** — Another Tradeoff Attack on Sprout-like Stream Ciphers (Zhang, Gong). Studies k-normality of augmented functions to build a flexible time/memory/data tradeoff attack breaking Sprout much faster than prior attacks.
- **94520211.pdf** — Beyond-Birthday-Bound Security for Tweakable Even-Mansour Ciphers with Linear Tweak and Key Mixing (Cogliati, Seurin). Gives a 4-round tweakable Even-Mansour construction using only linear key/tweak mixing, provably secure to ~2^{2n/3} queries.
- **94520213.pdf** — Design Principles for HFEv- based Multivariate Signature Schemes (Petzoldt, Chen, Yang, Tao, Ding). Derives HFEv- design principles from degree-of-regularity analysis and proposes Gui, over 100x faster than QUARTZ.
- **94520214.pdf** — Counting Keys in Parallel After a Side Channel Attack (Martin, O'Connell, Oswald, Stam). Efficient algorithms for key rank estimation and parallel key enumeration to quantify residual security after side-channel attacks.
- **94520216.pdf** — Idealizing Identity-Based Encryption (Hofheinz, Matt, Maurer). Formalizes IBE's goal as a "delivery controlled channel"; shows standard definitions insufficient in the standard model, with fixes in ROM or via weaker notion IND-ID1-CPA.
- **94520219.pdf** — On Black-Box Complexity of Universally Composable Security in the CRS model (Hazay, Venkitasubramaniam). First black-box UC-secure constructions (OT, commitments, computation) from PKE-style assumptions across static, one-sided, and adaptive corruptions.
- **94520227.pdf** — Security of Full-State Keyed Sponge and Duplex (Mennink, Reyhanitabar, Vizár). Unifies and proves security of full-state keyed Sponge/Duplex, enabling a SpongeWrap variant with nearly free associated-data authentication.
- **94520234.pdf** — On the Impact of Known-Key Attacks on Hash Functions (Mennink, Preneel). Formalizes the weak cipher model to assess known-key attack impact; shows PGV, Grøstl, and Shrimpton-Stam compression functions resist known differential known-key attacks.
- **94520236.pdf** — A Unified Metric for Quantifying Information Leakage under Power Analysis (Zhang, Ding, Fei, Luo). Proposes ILA, a unified leakage metric covering first/higher-order DPA/CPA and masked implementations, tied to attack success rates.
- **94520239.pdf** — Tradeoff Cryptanalysis of Memory-Hard Functions (Biryukov, Khovratovich). Breaks Catena's tradeoff-resilience proof and develops ranking tradeoffs reducing time–memory costs of Catena, yescrypt, and Lyra2.
- **94520240.pdf** — How to Sequentialize Independent Parallel Attacks? (Bogos, Vaudenay). Optimal scheduling of independent attacks shows a phase transition: for biased distributions (LPN, password search) the best strategy is one step per attack.
- **94520246.pdf** — Heuristic Tool for Linear Cryptanalysis with Applications to CAESAR Candidates (Dobraunig, Eichlseder, Mendel). Heuristic search tool finding linear characteristics for large-state, weakly aligned primitives, demonstrated on CAESAR candidate permutations.
- **94520248.pdf** — Generic Security of NMAC and HMAC with Input Whitening (Gaži, Pietrzak, Tessaro). Simple input-whitening variants of NMAC/HMAC admit tight generic PRF security bounds with minimal efficiency penalty.
- **94520249.pdf** — Selective Opening Security for Receivers (Hazay, Patra, Warinschi). Studies receiver-side selective-opening security: simulation-based is strictly stronger than indistinguishability-based; both feasible from non-committing encryption variants.
- **94520251.pdf** — Reverse-engineering of the cryptanalytic attack used in the Flame super-malware (Fillinger, Stevens). Reconstructs Flame's unpublished MD5 chosen-prefix collision attack, analyzing differential paths and proving a complexity lower bound.
- **94520253.pdf** — An Inverse-free Single-Keyed Tweakable Enciphering Scheme (Bhaumik, Nandi). FMix, a mixed-type Feistel tweakable enciphering scheme needing one key and no blockcipher inverse, TSPRP-secure from a PRP assumption.
- **94520260.pdf** — Compactly Hiding Linear Spans (Libert, Peters, Joye, Yung). Constant-size simulation-sound QA-NIZK proofs for linear subspaces with tight security reductions, improving prior loose bounds.
- **94520265.pdf** — Optimally Secure Block Ciphers from Ideal Primitives (Tessaro). Swap-or-Not-derived cipher with near-optimal PRP security from a single ideal component, plus generic related-key security amplification.
- **94520267.pdf** — A Unified Approach to MPC with Preprocessing using OT (Frederiksen, Keller, Orsini, Scholl). OT-extension-based multiplication triple generation for SPDZ/TinyOT/MiniMAC in characteristic-two fields, ~100x faster than prior SPDZ preprocessing.
- **94520273.pdf** — How Secure is AES under Leakage (Bogdanov, Isobe). Grey-box analysis of AES with explicit state leakage; new differential-bias key-recovery attacks and quantification of residual security under countermeasures.
- **94520285.pdf** — New Circular Security Counterexamples from Decision Linear and LWE (Bishop, Hohenberger, Waters). Builds 2-circular security counterexamples from decision k-linear and LWE assumptions, widening the class separating circular from CPA/CCA security.
- **94520287.pdf** — Composable & Modular Anonymous Credentials (Camenisch, Dubovitskaya, Haralambiev, Kohlweiss). Introduces unlinkable redactable signatures enabling the first efficient UC-secure multi-issuer anonymous credential system without random oracles.
- **94520288.pdf** — Secret Sharing and Statistical Zero Knowledge (Vaikuntanathan, Vasudevan). Connects SZK proof systems to secret sharing, characterizing access structures with efficient sharing and giving new schemes for problems like bounded-degree graph isomorphism.
- **94520294.pdf** — Key-Recovery Attacks on ASASA (Minaud, Derbez, Fouque, Karpman). Algebraic key-recovery breaks both ASASA public-key and secret-key schemes (2^39 and 2^63), plus LPN-based and fast white-box attacks.
- **94520296.pdf** — Oblivious Network RAM (Dachman-Soled, Liu, Papamanthou, Shi, Vishkin). Proposes ORAM with multiple memory banks where intra-bank obliviousness is free, using parallelism to beat the standard logarithmic ORAM cost barrier.
- **94520304.pdf** — Secure Computation From Millionaire (shelat, Venkitasubramaniam). Iterative secure-computation paradigm reducing matroid optimization, convex hulls, and scheduling problems to secure comparisons with gradual output release.
- **94520308.pdf** — Midori: A Block Cipher for Low Energy (Banik, Bogdanov, Isobe, Shibutani, Hiwatari, Akishita, Regazzoni). Lightweight block cipher explicitly optimized for energy per bit, targeting power-constrained devices like medical implants.
- **94520314.pdf** — New Realizations of Somewhere Statistically Binding Hashing and Positional Accumulators (Okamoto, Pietrzak, Waters, Wichs). New constructions of SSB hashing and positional accumulators, key tools for iO-based applications.
- **94520316.pdf** — On Cut-and-Choose Oblivious Transfer and Its Variants (Kolesnikov, Kumaresan). Simplified definitions and efficient OT-hybrid realizations of cut-and-choose OT, removing DDH reliance and enabling OT extension.
- **94520319.pdf** — Public Verifiability in the Covert Model (Almost) for Free (Kolesnikov, Malozemoff). PVC-compatible OT extension and practical improvements making publicly verifiable covert two-party computation nearly as cheap as plain covert protocols.
- **94520323.pdf** — Collision Attacks against CAESAR Candidates: AEZ and Marble (Fuhr, Leurent, Suder). Birthday-complexity mask-recovery attacks on OCB-inspired AE schemes, yielding forgeries and key recovery against Marble and AEZ v2/v3.
- **94520325.pdf** — Function-Hiding Inner Product Encryption (Bishop, Jain, Kowalczyk). Private-key function-hiding inner-product FE from SXDH over asymmetric bilinear groups, secure against unbounded collusion.
- **94520328.pdf** — Multi-Input Functional Encryption for Unbounded Arity Functions (Badrinarayanan, Gupta, Jain, Sahai). MI-FE supporting unbounded arity from differing-inputs obfuscation and CRHFs, enabling dynamic on-the-fly MPC.
- **94520329.pdf** — Conversions among Several Classes of Predicate Encryption (Attrapadung, Hanaoka, Yamada). Shows equivalences among KP/CP-ABE, doubly-spatial encryption, and ABE for arithmetic span programs, with compactness tradeoffs.
- **94520334.pdf** — Multilinear and Aggregate Pseudorandom Functions (Abdalla, Benhamouda, Passelègue). Proves aggregate security of Naor-Reingold PRF under DDH and constructs new multilinear PRFs via an extended linear-independent-polynomial framework.
- **94520337.pdf** — A Framework for Identity-Based Encryption with Almost Tight Security (Attrapadung, Hanaoka, Yamada). Broadcast-encoding framework yielding almost tightly secure IBE in multi-challenge settings, including sub-linear public parameters.
- **94520338.pdf** — Garbling Scheme for Formulas with Constant Size of Garbled Gates (Kempka, Kikuchi, Kiyoshima, Suzuki). Garbling scheme with four bits per gate for formulas using a generalized correlation-robust trapdoor permutation, proven in programmable ROM.
- **94520340.pdf** — Limits of Extractability Assumptions with Distributional Auxiliary Input (Boyle, Pass). Shows auxiliary-input extractability assumptions (PC-diO, extractable OWFs, SNARKs) cannot all hold simultaneously, via succinct punctured programs.
- **94520347.pdf** — Multi-Party Key Exchange for Unbounded Parties from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Khurana, Rao, Sahai). First non-interactive multiparty key exchange for unbounded parties without trusted setup or knowledge assumptions, from iO.
- **94520348.pdf** — Card-based Cryptographic Protocols Using a Minimal Number of Cards (Koch, Walzer, Härtel). Settles card minimality: four-card Las Vegas committed-format AND protocol, five cards for finite runtime.
- **94520351.pdf** — Three-Party ORAM for Secure Computation (Faber, Jarecki, Kentros, Wei). Honest-majority three-server SC-ORAM based on binary-tree ORAM, cheaper than two-party garbled-circuit/SPDZ approaches.
- **94520352.pdf** — Pure Differential Privacy for Rectangle Queries via Private Partitions (Dwork, Naor, Reingold, Rothblum). Private partition mechanism yielding improved pure-DP mechanisms for interval/rectangle queries and continual stream counting.
- **94520356.pdf** — Structure-Preserving Cryptography (Abe). Anomaly: 2-page keynote-style overview of structure-preserving cryptography over bilinear groups, not a full research paper.
- **94520357.pdf** — Computer-aided cryptography: status and perspectives (Barthe). Anomaly: 1-page abstract of a keynote on computer-aided/EasyCrypt-style verified cryptographic proofs.
- **94520358.pdf** — The Moral Character of Cryptographic Work (Rogaway). Anomaly: 1-page essay abstract arguing cryptography is inherently political and urging community response to mass surveillance.
- **95620001.pdf** — Impossibility of VBB Obfuscation with Ideal Constant-Degree Graded Encodings (Pass, shelat). Rules out general-purpose VBB obfuscation in ideal graded-encoding models supporting only constant-degree polynomials (assuming trapdoor permutations).
- **95620016.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Virtual Black-Box Obfuscation in Idealized Models (Mahmoody, Mohammed, Nematihaji). Extends VBB impossibility to generic group models for abelian groups and random trapdoor permutation oracles; builds on Pass–Shelat (not a duplicate).
- **95620046.pdf** — Lower Bounds on Assumptions behind Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Mahmoody, Mohammed, Nematihaji, Pass, shelat). Black-box separations: no iO from CRHFs unless PH collapses, and limits on iO from trapdoor-permutation/generic-group/graded-encoding primitives.
