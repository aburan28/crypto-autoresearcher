# Batch 090 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by theoretical crypto / provable-security papers, apparently from 2011-era ePrint proceedings: one block on signatures, multivariate cryptanalysis and impossibility results, a large TCC-style block on hardness amplification, leakage resilience, black-box separations, and secure computation, and a Eurocrypt-style block on lattices, pairings, and side channels.
- Major themes: leakage-resilient encryption/signatures (5 papers), black-box impossibility/separation results (6+ papers), secure two-party computation and coin tossing (6 papers), and differential/zero-knowledge privacy definitions (3 papers).
- Notable papers: Boneh-Sahai-Waters "Functional Encryption: Definitions and Challenges"; Stehlé-Steinfeld making NTRU provably secure under ideal-lattice assumptions; Aranha et al.'s sub-2M-cycle pairing implementation on BN curves.
- Little ECC/ECDLP content; the only elliptic-curve work is pairing implementation (66320047). Post-quantum cryptanalysis cluster (HFE, IP1S, skew polynomials, UOV) appears early in the batch.
- Anomalies: two 1-page invited-talk abstracts (65970536, 66320001), one 5-page survey talk (66320002), and an unusually short filename (659.pdf).

## Papers
- **65710345.pdf** — Linear Recurring Sequences for the UOV Key Generation (Petzoldt, Bulygin, Buchmann). Builds a UOV multivariate signature scheme with public key from a linear recurring sequence, cutting public key size by up to 86%.
- **65710359.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Instantiating PSS in the Standard Model (Bhattacharyya, Mandal). Proves black-box impossibility of proving PSS unforgeable even from ideal or lossy trapdoor permutations; extends to randomized signatures with recoverable randomness.
- **65710378.pdf** — On-line Non-transferable Signatures Revisited (Schuldt, Matsuura). Extends Liskov-Micali's on-line non-transferability model and gives strengthened constructions for undeniable/designated-confirmer-style signatures resistant to on-line attacks.
- **65710436.pdf** — Revocation for Delegatable Anonymous Credentials (Acar, Nguyen). Introduces homomorphic proofs and an accumulator with delegatable non-membership proofs, yielding a revocable delegatable anonymous credential system with provable security.
- **65710455.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Multivariate and Odd-Characteristic HFE Variants (Bettale, Faugère, Perret). Improves Kipnis-Shamir key recovery, generalizes it to Multi-HFE via MinRank, and practically breaks proposed 256-bit-security parameters in days.
- **65710474.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Cryptosystems Based on Non-commutative Skew Polynomials (Dubois, Kammerer). Shows the skew-polynomial Diffie-Hellman problem falls to an efficient attack reducing it to linear algebra via one-sided gcd.
- **65710499.pdf** — Practical Cryptanalysis of the Identification Scheme Based on the IP1S Problem (Bouillaguet, Faugère, Fouque, Perret). Gives two deterministic polynomial-time algorithms breaking Patarin's degree-two IP1S identification scheme parameters in seconds.
- **659.pdf** — Efficient Designated Confirmer Signatures Without Random Oracles or General Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Gentry, Molnar, Ramzan). Generic transformation from any signature scheme to designated confirmer signatures without random oracles or NP general ZK proofs.
- **65970001.pdf** — Input Locality and Hardness Amplification (Bogdanov, Rosen). New hardness-amplification results for input-local one-way functions, preserving locality; applications to Goldreich's one-way function.
- **65970019.pdf** — General Hardness Amplification of Predicates and Puzzles (Holenstein, Schoenebeck). Simpler non-rewinding proofs of Yao's XOR lemma and hardness amplification for weakly verifiable puzzles with arbitrary monotone checks.
- **65970037.pdf** — Security Amplification for the Cascade of Arbitrarily Weak PRPs (Tessaro). Proves essentially tight security amplification for cascades of weak PRPs via the first interactive hardcore lemma, settling a Luby-Rackoff open problem.
- **65970058.pdf** — Parallel Repetition for Leakage Resilience Amplification Revisited (Jain, Pietrzak). Strong counterexample: a signature scheme secure with log(k) leakage whose polynomially many independent copies are all broken with poly(k) leakage.
- **65970070.pdf** — Achieving Leakage Resilience Through Dual System Encryption (Lewko, Rouselakis, Waters). Generic modification making dual-system IBE, HIBE, and ABE schemes leakage-resilient, including continual leakage with key updates.
- **65970088.pdf** — Signatures Resilient to Continual Leakage on Memory and Computation (Malkin, Teranishi, Vahlis, Yung). First signature scheme tolerating continual leakage on key and randomness during signing, secure under SXDH without random oracles.
- **65970106.pdf** — After-the-Fact Leakage in Public-Key Encryption (Halevi, Lin). Formalizes entropic leakage-resilient PKE capturing leakage after the challenge ciphertext; realized by Naor-Segev hash-proof constructions.
- **65970124.pdf** — One-Time Computable Self-Erasing Functions (Dziembowski, Kazana, Wichs). Defines one-time computable PRFs self-erasing during evaluation, enabling password-protected storage secure against viruses; based on random oracles and graph pebbling.
- **65970142.pdf** — Perfectly Secure Oblivious RAM Without Random Oracles (Damgård, Meldgaard, Nielsen). Information-theoretically secure ORAM hiding access patterns without random oracles, plus a lower bound on required randomness.
- **65970162.pdf** — Unconditional and Composable Security Using a Single Stateful Tamper-Proof Hardware Token (Döttling, Kraschewski, Müller-Quade). First UC two-party computation and one-time programs with information-theoretic security from a single tamper-proof token.
- **65970180.pdf** — Correlated-Input Secure Hash Functions (Goyal, O'Neill, Rao). Studies hash security under correlated inputs, constructs it from q-DHI-type assumptions for polynomial relations, with applications to password login and searchable encryption.
- **65970198.pdf** — Black-Box Circular-Secure Encryption Beyond Affine Functions (Brakerski, Goldwasser, Kalai). PKE schemes KDM-secure for degree-d polynomials and bounded Turing machines of the secret key, from DDH or LWE.
- **65970216.pdf** — Homomorphic Encryption: from Private-Key to Public-Key (Rothblum). Transforms any compact additively homomorphic private-key encryption scheme into a public-key scheme, losing one hop of homomorphism.
- **65970232.pdf** — Identity-Based Encryption Secure Against Selective Opening Attack (Bellare, Waters, Yilek). First SOA-secure IBE schemes via a "one-sided public openability" property, instantiated from Boyen-Waters and dual-system IBE.
- **65970250.pdf** — Functional Encryption: Definitions and Challenges (Boneh, Sahai, Waters). Initiates formal study of functional encryption, shows game-based definitions inadequate and simulation-based ones impossible in the standard model.
- **65970270.pdf** — Concurrent Non-Malleable Zero Knowledge with Adaptive Inputs (Lin, Pass). First concurrent non-malleable ZK protocol without trusted setup secure against adversaries adaptively choosing proven statements.
- **65970288.pdf** — Round-Optimal Password-Based Authenticated Key Exchange (Katz, Vaikuntanathan). Framework for one-round (simultaneous message) PAKE in the standard model with a CRS, also yielding UC-secure variants.
- **65970306.pdf** — Bringing People of Different Beliefs Together to do UC (Garg, Goyal, Jain, Sahai). Studies UC security when parties hold different beliefs about multiple setup assumptions; gives general condition under which UC is achievable.
- **65970324.pdf** — Secure Two-Party Computation via Cut-and-Choose Oblivious Transfer (Lindell, Pinkas). More efficient and simpler malicious-secure Yao-based two-party computation using cut-and-choose OT under DDH, with concrete analysis.
- **65970342.pdf** — Practical Adaptive Oblivious Transfer from Simple Assumptions (Green, Hohenberger). First adaptive OT with efficient initialization, database-independent transfers, and standard-model security under a static bilinear assumption.
- **65970360.pdf** — Completeness Theorems with Constructive Proofs for Finite Deterministic 2-Party Functions (Kraschewski, Müller-Quade). Tight combinatorial criteria characterizing which finite deterministic 2-party functions imply OT, extended to the UC framework.
- **65970378.pdf** — A Zero-One Law for Secure Multi-Party Computation with Ternary Outputs (Kreitz). Complete characterization of honest-majority thresholds for functions with three outputs; the Boolean zero-one law extends to Z3.
- **65970396.pdf** — PCPs and the Hardness of Generating Private Synthetic Data (Ullman, Vadhan). Under one-way functions, no efficient differentially private algorithm can output synthetic data preserving all two-way marginals; proof uses PCP encodings.
- **65970413.pdf** — Limits of Computational Differential Privacy in the Client/Server Setting (Groce, Katz, Yerukhimovich). Shows computationally private client/server mechanisms can be converted to equally efficient statistically private ones for constant-dimensional queries.
- **65970428.pdf** — Towards Privacy for Social Networks: A Zero-Knowledge Based Definition of Privacy (Gehrke, Lui, Pass). Proposes zero-knowledge privacy, strictly stronger than differential privacy, achievable for averages, histograms, and graph statistics via sublinear algorithms.
- **65970446.pdf** — On the Black-Box Complexity of Optimally-Fair Coin Tossing (Dachman-Soled, Lindell, Mahmoody, Malkin). Rules out black-box optimally-fair (O(1/r)-bias) coin tossing from one-way functions for r below O(n/log n).
- **65970464.pdf** — Tight Bounds for Classical and Quantum Coin Flipping (Hänggi, Wullschleger). Unified definition of weak/strong coin flipping with aborts and tight bounds on achievable parameters in both classical and quantum settings.
- **65970482.pdf** — Exploring the Limits of Common Coins Using Frontier Analysis of Protocols (Maji, Ouppaphan, Prabhakaran, Rosulek). Shows common trusted randomness adds no power for information-theoretic secure evaluation of deterministic functions, via frontier analysis.
- **65970500.pdf** — Limits on the Stretch of Non-Adaptive Constructions of Pseudo-Random Generators (Bronson, Juma, Papakonstantinou). Black-box impossibility results showing non-adaptive constructions cannot amplify PRG stretch as adaptive ones do.
- **65970518.pdf** — On the Complexity of Non-Adaptively Increasing the Stretch of Pseudorandom Generators (Miles, Viola). No non-adaptive black-box construction outputs mere bits of oracle answers for linear stretch; matching positive result with affine output functions.
- **65970536.pdf** — Concurrent Security and Non-malleability (Pass). One-page invited-talk abstract surveying methods for concurrent security without trusted setup; not a full research paper.
- **65970537.pdf** — (Nearly) Round-Optimal Black-Box Constructions of Commitments Secure Against Selective Opening Attacks (Xiao). Tight round-complexity bounds for parallel and concurrent composition of commitments secure against selective opening attacks.
- **65970555.pdf** — Limits on the Power of Zero-Knowledge Proofs in Cryptographic Constructions (Brakerski, Katz, Segev, Yerukhimovich). New black-box framework allowing ZK proofs relative to oracles; captures Naor-Yung CCA paradigm while some separations persist.
- **65970575.pdf** — Towards Non-Black-Box Lower Bounds in Cryptography (Pass, Tseng, Venkitasubramaniam). Under average-case coNP-not-in-AM assumptions, rules out non-black-box constructions of OWPs, CRHFs, and others from OWFs.
- **65970593.pdf** — On Black-Box Separations among Injective One-Way Functions (Matsuda, Matsuura). No fully black-box construction of one-way permutations even from 1-bit-increasing adaptively secure injective OWFs.
- **65970611.pdf** — Impossibility of Blind Signatures From One-Way Permutations (Katz, Schröder, Yerukhimovich). Rules out black-box blind signature schemes from one-way functions, even from random permutation oracles and for 1-bit honest-but-curious security.
- **66320001.pdf** — The Arithmetic Codex: Theory and Applications (Cramer). One-page invited-talk abstract defining arithmetic codex/secret sharing unifying multiplicative LSSS used in information-theoretic MPC; not a full paper.
- **66320002.pdf** — Lattice Reduction Algorithms: Theory and Practice (Nguyen). Five-page invited survey on lattice reduction algorithms, their cryptanalytic applications, and gaps between theory and practice.
- **66320007.pdf** — Efficient Authentication from Hard Learning Problems (Kiltz, Pietrzak, Cash, Jain, Venturi). First efficient LPN-based MACs and two-round actively secure authentication protocols with tight reductions.
- **66320027.pdf** — Making NTRU as Secure as Worst-Case Problems over Ideal Lattices (Stehlé, Steinfeld). Modifies NTRUEncrypt with Gaussian-sampled keys so the public key is statistically uniform, giving standard-model security from worst-case ideal-lattice problems via R-LWE.
- **66320047.pdf** — Faster Explicit Formulas for Computing Pairings over Ordinary Curves (Aranha, Karabina, Longa, Gebotys, López). Lazy-reduction and compressed-squaring techniques speed BN-curve pairings 28-34%, first under 2M cycles on 64-bit platforms.
- **66320067.pdf** — Pushing the Limits: A Very Compact and a Threshold Implementation of AES (Moradi, Poschmann, Ling, Paar, Wang). 2400-GE AES-128 hardware implementation plus threshold-masking countermeasure against first-order side-channel attacks, evaluated on real power traces.
