# Batch 123 digest

## Overview
- Entire batch is CRYPTO 2016 (LNCS vols. 9813–9815, parts I–III): 50 full research papers, no duplicates or anomalies.
- Heavy symmetric-crypto focus: authenticated-encryption modes (SCT, EWCDM, XGCM), tweakable ciphers (XPX, SKINNY/MANTIS), and cryptanalysis (MISTY1 2^70 attack, FLIP, AES-like SPNs, division property).
- Strong theory/MPC cluster: obfuscation combiners and impossibility results, PPAD/Nash hardness, protocol transformations, network-hiding and OT-based MPC.
- ECDLP-relevant highlights: exTNFS lowers medium-prime NFS complexity (pairing key-size impact), efficient constant-time SIDH implementation, µKummer genus-2 hyperelliptic crypto on microcontrollers.
- Hardware/embedded security presence: Antikernel OS, vatiCAN automotive authentication, DRAM PUFs, parametric Trojans, ParTI combined side-channel/fault countermeasures.
- Notable cryptanalysis: subfield lattice attack on overstretched NTRU, practical break of Algebraic Eraser, polynomial-time break of GGH15 multilinear maps, quantum Simon's-algorithm attacks on symmetric schemes.

## Papers
- **98130227.pdf** — Antikernel: A Decentralized Secure Hardware-Software Operating System Architecture (Zonenberg, Yener). FPGA-prototyped OS architecture replacing the monolithic kernel with independent hardware state machines on a packet-switched NoC for least-privilege security and verifiability.
- **98130234.pdf** — vatiCAN: Vetted, Authenticated CAN Bus (Nürnberger, Rossow). Backward-compatible HMAC authentication for automotive CAN bus, preventing ECU impersonation with under 4 ms added latency.
- **98130236.pdf** — Run-time Accessible DRAM PUFs in Commodity Devices (Xiong et al.). First run-time access to decay-based DRAM PUFs in off-the-shelf Linux systems, enabling key storage and device authentication without extra hardware.
- **98130239.pdf** — Physical Layer Group Key Agreement for Automotive Controller Area Networks (Jain, Guajardo). Derives group keys among CAN ECUs from physical bus properties, achieving DH-like security without group-operation overhead.
- **98130241.pdf** — µKummer: efficient hyperelliptic signatures and key exchange on microcontrollers (Renes, Schwabe, Smith, Batina). Genus-2 Kummer-surface scalar multiplication bringing 128-bit-security hyperelliptic crypto to AVR ATmega and Cortex M0, beating ECC speed records.
- **98130250.pdf** — A Design Methodology for Stealthy Parametric Trojans and Its Application to Bug Attacks (Ghandali, Becker, Holcomb, Paar). SAT+genetic-algorithm method inserts delay-based parametric Trojans on rare paths, demonstrated on a multiplier enabling stealthy bug attacks.
- **98140001.pdf** — Key-alternating Ciphers and Key-length Extension: Exact Bounds and Multi-user Security (Hoang, Tessaro). Proves exact, attack-matching security bounds for key-alternating ciphers and XOR cascades, extended to the multi-user setting.
- **98140031.pdf** — Counter-in-Tweak: Authenticated Encryption Modes for Tweakable Block Ciphers (Peyrin, Seurin). SCT mode turns a tweakable block cipher into nonce-based AEAD with beyond-birthday security and birthday-bound nonce-misuse resistance.
- **98140061.pdf** — XPX: Generalized Tweakable Even-Mansour with Improved Security Guarantees (Mennink). Single-permutation tweakable cipher generalizing EM/XEX, proven strong TPRP with related-key security; yields related-key proofs for COPA, Minalpher, Chaskey variants.
- **98140091.pdf** — Indifferentiability of 8-Round Feistel Networks (Dai, Steinberger). Shows balanced 8-round Feistel networks are indifferentiable from a random permutation, improving prior 10-round results.
- **98140117.pdf** — EWCDM: An Efficient, Beyond-Birthday Secure, Nonce-Misuse Resistant MAC (Cogliati, Seurin). Two-block-cipher-call MAC based on a new Encrypted Davies-Meyer PRP-to-PRF conversion, beyond-birthday secure with nonce-misuse resilience.
- **98140147.pdf** — A subfield lattice attack on overstretched NTRU assumptions (Albrecht, Bai, Ducas). Resurrects and generalizes the Gentry–Szydlo subfield attack, giving sub-exponential attacks on overstretched NTRU underlying LTV and YASHE FHE schemes.
- **98140177.pdf** — A Practical Cryptanalysis of the Algebraic Eraser (Ben-Zvi, Blackburn, Tsaban). Practical key-recovery attack on the braid-group-based Algebraic Eraser key agreement (ISO/IEC 29167-20 candidate), recovering claimed 128-bit-security keys in under 8 CPU-hours.
- **98140188.pdf** — Lattice-Based Fully Dynamic Multi-Key FHE with Short Ciphertexts (Brakerski, Perlman). Multi-key FHE supporting unbounded parties joining dynamically, with ciphertext size and operation cost linear in the current party count via tailored bootstrapping.
- **98140212.pdf** — Cryptography with Auxiliary Input and Trapdoor from Constant-Noise LPN (Yu, Zhang). Shows constant-noise LPN resists sub-exponentially-hard auxiliary input, yielding leakage-resilient encryption, obfuscators, and extractors à la Dodis–Kalai–Lovett.
- **98140241.pdf** — The Multi-User Security of Authenticated Encryption: AES-GCM in TLS 1.3 (Bellare, Tackmann). Formalizes multi-user AE security, analyzes randomized-nonce GCM (RGCM) for TLS 1.3, and proposes better multi-user-secure XGCM.
- **98140271.pdf** — A Modular Treatment of Cryptographic APIs: The Symmetric-Key Case (Shrimpton, Stam, Warinschi). First compositional security models for token/HSM APIs, combining secure key management with secure primitive implementations under adaptive key corruption.
- **98140300.pdf** — Encryption Switching Protocols (Couteau, Peters, Pointcheval). Defines two-party protocols converting ciphertexts between encryption schemes, implying general 2PC; instantiates with Paillier plus a new multiplicatively homomorphic ElGamal-like scheme.
- **98140330.pdf** — Message Transmission with Reverse Firewalls (Dodis, Mironov, Stephens-Davidowitz). Reverse-firewall framework letting a third party sanitize traffic so Alice can communicate securely even on a corrupted machine.
- **98140360.pdf** — Big-Key Symmetric Encryption: Resisting Key Exfiltration (Bellare, Kane, Rogaway). Bounded-retrieval-model symmetric encryption with huge keys to defeat exfiltration, built on a sharp subkey-prediction lemma with concrete bounds.
- **98140390.pdf** — Backdoors in Pseudorandom Number Generators: Possibility and Impossibility Results (Degabriele, Paterson, Schuldt, Woodage). Stronger forward-secure backdoored PRGs where one output reveals the full state, plus results on robust PRNGs with input.
- **98140420.pdf** — A 2^70 Attack on the Full MISTY1 (Bar-On, Keller). Division-property attack with refined key recovery breaking full MISTY1 in 2^69.5 time (near-full codebook), showing only ~70-bit security.
- **98140443.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the FLIP Family of Stream Ciphers (Duval, Lallemand, Rotella). Guess-and-determine attack on FHE-oriented FLIP stream ciphers, recovering keys in 2^54/2^68 versus claimed 2^80/2^128 security.
- **98140463.pdf** — The Magic of ELFs (Zhandry). Introduces Extremely Lossy Functions from exponential DDH, yielding point-function obfuscation with auxiliary input and polynomially many hardcore bits for any OWF.
- **98140493.pdf** — Breaking the Circuit Size Barrier for Secure Computation Under DDH (Boyle, Gilboa, Ishai). DDH-based 2-of-2 secret sharing with compact evaluation of branching programs, giving 2PC and function secret sharing with communication independent of program size.
- **98140523.pdf** — Extended Tower Number Field Sieve: A New Complexity for the Medium Prime Case (Kim, Barbulescu). exTNFS variant lowering medium-prime DLP complexity in F_{p^n} from L(1/3, 2.15) to L(1/3, 1.71), forcing larger pairing-based key sizes.
- **98140553.pdf** — Efficient algorithms for supersingular isogeny Diffie-Hellman (Costello, Longa, Naehrig). First constant-time SIDH library, up to 2.9× faster than prior work with 564-byte keys, targeting 128-bit quantum security.
- **98140583.pdf** — New Insights on AES-Like SPN Ciphers (Sun, Liu, Guo, Qu, Rijmen). Exploits S-box and MDS matrix details to build 5-round zero-correlation hulls and integrals on AES-like ciphers, extending beyond S-box-agnostic 4-round limits.
- **98140602.pdf** — Lightweight Multiplication in GF(2^n) with Applications to MDS Matrices (Beierle, Kranz, Leander). Optimizes XOR-count of fixed-element GF(2^n) multiplication via basis choice, yielding record MDS matrices for round-based hardware.
- **98140631.pdf** — Another view of the division property (Boura, Canteaut). Parity-set reformulation of Todo's division property enabling better integral distinguishers on reduced-round PRESENT and S-box design criteria.
- **98150001.pdf** — Adversary-dependent Lossy Trapdoor Function from Hardness of Factoring Semi-smooth RSA Subgroup Moduli (Yamakawa, Yamada, Hanaoka, Kunihiro). Weaker ad-LTDF notion built from factoring semi-smooth RSA subgroup moduli, giving the first factoring-based deterministic encryption without decision assumptions.
- **98150031.pdf** — Optimal Security Proofs for Signatures from Identification Schemes (Kiltz, Masny, Pan). Tighter multi-user Fiat–Shamir signature reductions losing only Q_h (not number of users), with concrete multi-user treatment of Schnorr signatures.
- **98150061.pdf** — FHE Circuit Privacy Almost For Free (Bourse, Del Pino, Minelli, Wee). GSW-variant FHE for branching programs achieves circuit privacy without noise flooding or bootstrapping, under standard LWE.
- **98150091.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of a Theorem: Decomposing the Only Known Solution to the Big APN Problem (Perrin, Udovenko, Biryukov). Decomposes the 6-bit APN permutation via a new "butterfly" structure (quadratic/CCZ-equivalent pair), cutting its hardware cost.
- **98150121.pdf** — The SKINNY Family of Block Ciphers and its Low-Latency Variant MANTIS (Beierle et al.). Tweakable block-cipher family competing with SIMON on hardware/software efficiency while providing strong (related-)tweak differential/linear bounds.
- **98150151.pdf** — Automatic Search of Meet-in-the-Middle and Impossible Differential Attacks (Derbez, Fouque). Generic algorithm automatically finding best MitM and impossible-differential attacks across SPN, Feistel, and Lai-Massey ciphers, improving attacks on AES, SIMON, mCRYPTON.
- **98150203.pdf** — Breaking Symmetric Cryptosystems using Quantum Period Finding (Kaplan, Leurent, Leverrier, Naya-Plasencia). Applies Simon's algorithm to break symmetric constructions (Even-Mansour, CBC-MAC variants, AE modes) in polynomial quantum queries under superposition access.
- **98150233.pdf** — Efficiently Computing Data-Independent Memory-Hard Functions (Alwen, Blocki). New energy-complexity measure and evaluation algorithms for iMHFs, with attacks on DAG classes underlying prominent memory-hard functions.
- **98150263.pdf** — Towards Sound Fresh Re-Keying with Hard (Physical) Learning Problems (Dziembowski et al.). First formal reductions for fresh re-keying side-channel countermeasures, basing security on hard learning problems rather than heuristics.
- **98150293.pdf** — ParTI: Towards Combined Hardware Countermeasures against Side-Channel and Fault-Injection Attacks (Schneider, Moradi, Güneysu). Combines threshold-implementation masking with error detection in one construction, instantiated on LED cipher.
- **98150323.pdf** — Network-Hiding Communication and Applications to Multi-Party Protocols (Hirt, Maurer, Tschudi, Zikas). First topology-hiding communication protocol for incomplete networks using black-box PKE, tolerating passive corruption of arbitrary nodes.
- **98150353.pdf** — Network Oblivious Transfer (Kumaresan, Raghuraman, Sealfon). Characterizes which OT-channel graphs let all party pairs establish OT correlations against dishonest majority, matching the Harnik–Ishai–Kushilevitz lower bound.
- **98150383.pdf** — On the Power of Secure Two-Party Computation (Hazay, Venkitasubramaniam). Extends MPC-in-the-head to transform semi-honest 2PC in the OT-hybrid model into adaptive ZK proofs, including first linear-rate adaptive ZK, from OWFs.
- **98150413.pdf** — Secure Protocol Transformations (Ishai, Kushilevitz, Prabhakaran, Sahai, Yu). Formalizes black-box MPC protocol transformations, rederiving classical feasibility results and giving first constant-rate information-theoretic MPC for constant parties.
- **98150443.pdf** — On the Communication required for Unconditionally Secure Multiplication (Damgård, Nielsen, Polychroniadou, Raskin). Proves Ω(n) bits per multiplication gate are inherent for gate-by-gate information-theoretic MPC in honest-majority and preprocessing settings.
- **98150473.pdf** — Universal Constructions and Robust Combiners for Indistinguishability Obfuscation and Witness Encryption (Ananth, Jain, Naor, Sahai, Yogev). Efficient combiners for iO and WE producing constructions as secure as the best candidate, plus universal constructions.
- **98150503.pdf** — Obfuscation Combiners (Fischlin, Herzberg, Bin Noon, Shulman). Robust obfuscation combiners with tight quorum bounds: 3-out-of-4 possible, 2-out-of-3 black-box impossible, generalized to (2γ+1)-out-of-(3γ+1).
- **98150533.pdf** — On Statistically Secure Obfuscation with Approximate Correctness (Brakerski, Brzuska, Fleischhacker). Rules out statistical approximate iO under NP ⊄ AM∩coAM plus OWFs, closing the gap left by Goldwasser–Rothblum; introduces correlation obfuscation.
- **98150563.pdf** — Revisiting the Cryptographic Hardness of Finding a Nash Equilibrium (Garg, Pandey, Srinivasan). Bases PPAD hardness on polynomially hard assumptions: iO plus one-way permutations, and public-key functional encryption.
- **98150593.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of GGH15 Multilinear Maps (Coron, Lee, Lepoint, Tibouchi). Polynomial-time attack breaking GGH15 multipartite key agreement (even with safeguards) by computing an equivalent private key; also hits GGH13 variants with graph-induced constraints.
