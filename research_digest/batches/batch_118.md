# Batch 118 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by TCC 2016-A (9562xxxx, 42 papers) and PKC 2016 (9614xxxx, 8 papers) era work, ~2015–2016, mostly theoretical/foundational cryptography.
- Recurring themes: indistinguishability obfuscation (constructions, applications, and impossibility results), functional/predicate encryption, ORAM and oblivious parallel RAM, NIZK/PCP/sigma-protocol techniques, and non-malleable codes.
- Notable ECDLP/EC-relevant items: algebraic index-calculus attempts on prime-field ECDLP (96140156) and degenerate curve attacks extending invalid-curve attacks to Edwards curves (96140158); several pairing-based IBE/ABE papers.
- Other standouts: Onion ORAM (constant bandwidth blowup), VRFs from standard assumptions, ARMed SPHINCS (embedded hash-based signatures), LPN-based authentication.
- No duplicates, no non-paper anomalies; all 50 extracts are recognizable research papers.

## Papers
- **95620064.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation: from Approximate to Exact (Bitansky, Vaikuntanathan). Transforms subexponentially-secure approximate iO into exact iO, plus an approximate-to-exact result for functional encryption.
- **95620094.pdf** — Output-Compressing Randomized Encodings and Applications (Lin, Pass, Seth, Telang). Studies sublinear/compact randomized encodings: impossibility in the plain model, feasibility in the CRS model, with iO applications.
- **95620124.pdf** — Functional Encryption for Turing Machines (Ananth, Sahai). Adaptively secure FE for unbounded TMs from iO for circuits; yields first succinct adaptively secure garbling in the standard model.
- **95620154.pdf** — The Complexity of Computing the Optimal Composition of Differential Privacy (Murtagh, Vadhan). Shows optimal composition of heterogeneous DP algorithms is #P-complete; gives polynomial-time approximation algorithm.
- **95620172.pdf** — Order-Revealing Encryption and the Hardness of Private Learning (Bun, Zhandry). Uses ORE to separate efficient PAC learning from efficient differentially private PAC learning.
- **95620202.pdf** — On the Hardness of Learning with Rounding over Small Modulus (Bogdanov, Guo, Masny, Richelson, Rosen). Sample-preserving reductions from LWE to LWR without number-theoretic restrictions on the modulus.
- **95620219.pdf** — Two-Round Man-in-the-Middle Security from LPN (Cash, Kiltz, Tessaro). 2-round secret-key authentication with tight sequential-MIM security from LPN-type assumptions via a generic round-preserving transform.
- **95620243.pdf** — Algebraic partitioning: Fully compact and (almost) tightly secure cryptography (Hofheinz). New algebraic partitioning proof technique yielding fully compact, tightly secure signatures and PKE.
- **95620272.pdf** — Standard Security Does Imply Security Against Selective Opening for Markov Distributions (Fuchsbauer, Heuer, Kiltz, Pietrzak). Polynomial IND-CPA-to-selective-opening reduction parametrized by the message dependence graph.
- **95620296.pdf** — Non-Malleable Encryption: Simpler, Shorter, Stronger (Coretti, Dodis, Tackmann, Venturi). Improves rate of NM-CPA from IND-CPA, efficient multi-bit amplification, and a stronger achievable notion.
- **95620326.pdf** — Verifiable Random Functions from Standard Assumptions (Hofheinz, Jager). First VRFs with exponential input space and full adaptive security from constant-size assumptions (e.g., DLIN) in bilinear groups.
- **95620353.pdf** — Homomorphic evaluation requires depth (Bogdanov, Lee). Shows homomorphic evaluation of non-trivial functions under any CPA-secure HE cannot be done in AC^0, unlike basic encryption operations.
- **95620360.pdf** — On Basing Private Information Retrieval on NP-Hardness (Liu, Vaikuntanathan). Single-server single-round PIR cannot be based on NP-hardness unless PH collapses; breaks PIR with an SZK oracle.
- **95620376.pdf** — On the Correlation Intractability of Obfuscated Pseudorandom Functions (Canetti, Chen, Reyzin). Builds correlation-intractable ensembles for bounded-complexity relations from iO, puncturable PRFs, and input-hiding obfuscation.
- **95620405.pdf** — Reconfigurable Cryptography: A flexible approach to long-term security (Hesse, Hofheinz, Rupp). Introduces cryptosystems whose security can be upgraded at runtime by updating only the CRS; gives reconfigurable encryption and signatures.
- **95620435.pdf** — Multilinear Maps from Obfuscation (Albrecht, Farshim, Hofheinz, Larraia, Paterson). Constructs multilinear groups with hard DDH-type problems from iO, homomorphic encryption, and NIZKs.
- **95620464.pdf** — Perfect Structure on the Edge of Chaos: Trapdoor Permutations from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Bitansky, Paneth, Wichs). First trapdoor permutation candidate not based on factoring, built from iO and OWFs.
- **95620514.pdf** — Adaptive Security with Quasi-Optimal Rate (Hemenway, Ostrovsky, Richelson, Rosen). Non-committing encryption from (Ring-)LWE with polylog ciphertext expansion for adaptive MPC.
- **95620532.pdf** — On the Complexity of Additively Homomorphic UC Commitments (Frederiksen, Jakobsen, Nielsen, Trifiletti). Constant-round UC additively homomorphic commitments approaching rate 1, using erasure decoding instead of error correction.
- **95620557.pdf** — Simplified Universal Composability Framework (Wikström). Presents a simplified UC framework (fixed parties, static corruption) with modular composition and easy comparison to full UC.
- **95620587.pdf** — Characterization of Secure Multiparty Computation Without Broadcast (Cohen, Haitner, Omri, Rotem). Characterizes which symmetric functionalities are securely computable without broadcast under honest/dishonest majority.
- **95620609.pdf** — Making the Best of a Leaky Situation: Zero-Knowledge PCPs from Leakage-Resilient Circuits (Ishai, Weiss, Yang). Compiles PCPs into non-adaptively verifiable ZK PCPs via leakage-resilient circuits, avoiding locking schemes.
- **95620639.pdf** — Quasi-Linear Size Zero Knowledge from Linear-Algebraic PCPs (Ben-Sasson, Chiesa, Gabizon, Virza). 2-round (duplex) ZK PCPs of length Õ(K) for K malicious-verifier queries, improving on K^6.
- **95620669.pdf** — From Private Simultaneous Messages to Zero-Information Arthur-Merlin Protocols and Back (Applebaum, Raykov). Establishes two-way correspondences between ZAM and PSM complexity measures, improving ZAM bounds.
- **95620687.pdf** — A Transform for NIZK Almost as Efficient and General as the Fiat-Shamir Transform Without Programmable Random Oracles (Ciampi, Persiano, Siniscalchi, Visconti). Improves Lindell's NPRO-model transform toward FS-level efficiency and generality.
- **95620717.pdf** — Improved OR-Composition of Sigma-Protocols (Ciampi, Persiano, Scafuro, Siniscalchi, Visconti). OR-composition where only one statement must be fixed at proof start, aiding round-optimal protocols.
- **95620747.pdf** — Onion ORAM: A Constant Bandwidth Blowup Oblivious RAM (Devadas, van Dijk, Fletcher, Ren, Shi, Wichs). First constant-bandwidth-blowup ORAM under standard assumptions, using server-side AHE computation.
- **95620777.pdf** — Oblivious Parallel RAM and Applications (Boyle, Chung, Pass). Initiates Oblivious PRAM: compiles PRAMs to data-independent access patterns with polylog slowdown; applications to garbled PRAM.
- **95620807.pdf** — Oblivious Parallel RAM: Improved Efficiency and Generic Constructions (Chen, Lin, Tessaro). Path-ORAM-based OPRAM nearly matching single-client ORAM costs, plus a generic ORAM→OPRAM transform.
- **95620837.pdf** — Déjà Q: Encore! Un Petit IBE (Wee). Adaptively secure anonymous IBE in composite-order bilinear groups with one-element ciphertext overhead and one pairing decryption.
- **95620858.pdf** — A Study of Pair Encodings: Predicate Encryption in Prime Order Groups (Agrawal, Chase). Relaxed information-theoretic pair-encoding property yielding prime-order predicate encryption under SXDH/DLIN; first semi-adaptive CP-ABE with constant-size ciphertexts.
- **95620888.pdf** — Optimal Amplification of Noisy Leakages (Dziembowski, Faust, Skórski). Tight analysis of additive secret-sharing encodings in the noisy-leakage model for leakage-resilient crypto.
- **95620910.pdf** — Rational Sumchecks (Guo, Hubáček, Rosen, Vald). Rational-prover sumcheck protocols yielding single-round sublinear-verification delegation for all of P.
- **95620940.pdf** — Interactive Coding for Interactive Proofs (Bishop, Dodis). Shows IP = PSPACE still holds over adversarial channels corrupting a constant fraction of communication.
- **95620955.pdf** — Information-theoretic Local Non-malleable Codes and their Applications (Chandran, Kanukurthi, Raghuraman). Combines local decodability with non-malleability; constructions and applications to tamper resilience.
- **95620981.pdf** — Optimal Computational Split-state Non-malleable Codes (Aggarwal, Agrawal, Gupta, Maji, Pandey, Prabhakaran). Improves split-state non-malleable code rates toward the information-theoretic 1/2 barrier in the computational setting.
- **95621006.pdf** — How to Avoid Obfuscation Using Witness PRFs (Zhandry). Introduces witness PRFs from multilinear maps, replacing iO for multiparty key exchange, hardcore bits, and other applications.
- **95621035.pdf** — Cutting-Edge Cryptography Through the Lens of Secret Sharing (Komargodski, Zhandry). Introduces distributed (dealerless) and functional secret sharing, connecting to key exchange and functional encryption.
- **95621065.pdf** — Fully Secure Functional Encryption without Obfuscation (Garg, Gentry, Halevi, Zhandry). Adaptively secure FE from polynomial assumptions on composite-order multilinear maps, avoiding iO.
- **95621095.pdf** — On Constructing One-Way Permutations from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Asharov, Segev). Black-box impossibility: no domain-invariant OWP construction from OWF + iO for oracle-aided circuits.
- **95621125.pdf** — Contention in Cryptoland: Obfuscation, Leakage and UCE (Bellare, Stepanovs, Tessaro). Shows pairs of assumed primitives (VGBO vs AI-DHI, iO vs leakage-resilient SE, iO vs split-source UCE) cannot co-exist.
- **95621149.pdf** — Point-Function Obfuscation: A Framework and Generic Constructions (Bellare, Stepanovs). Definitional framework parameterized by target generators plus generic constructions from iO, deterministic PKE, and UCEs.
- **96140102.pdf** — Trading Plaintext-Awareness for Simulatability to Achieve Chosen Ciphertext Security (Matsuda, Hanaoka). Weakens Dachman-Soled's CCA construction by trading plaintext awareness against simulatability of components.
- **96140106.pdf** — Extended Nested Dual System Groups, Revisited (Gong, Chen, Dong, Cao, Tang). Prime-order instantiations of ENDSG giving the first almost-tight IBE in the multi-instance multi-ciphertext setting under d-Lin.
- **96140129.pdf** — Functional Encryption for Inner Product with Full Function Privacy (Datta, Dutta, Mukhopadhyay). Private-key inner-product FE with full function privacy from SXDH in asymmetric bilinear groups.
- **96140137.pdf** — On Public Key Encryption from Noisy Codewords (Ben-Sasson, Ben-Tov, Damgård, Ishai, Ron-Zewi). Under an additive-combinatorics conjecture, binary-field noisy-codeword PKE schemes admit 2^O(√n) attacks.
- **96140151.pdf** — ARMed SPHINCS: Computing a 41 KB signature in 16 KB of RAM (Hülsing, Rijneveld, Schwabe). Implements SPHINCS-256 on an ARM Cortex M3 with 16 KB RAM; benchmarks and XMSS^MT comparison.
- **96140156.pdf** — Algebraic approaches for the Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithm Problem over prime fields (Petit, Kosters, Messeng). Generalizes binary-curve index-calculus attacks to prime-field ECDLP; experiments show practicality only for small parameters but flag potential vulnerability.
- **96140158.pdf** — Degenerate Curve Attacks: Extending Invalid Curve Attacks to Edwards Curves and Other Models (Neves, Tibouchi). First invalid-curve-style attacks on (twisted) Edwards curves, Jacobi quartics and intersections via degenerate groups.
- **96140160.pdf** — CCA-Secure Keyed-Fully Homomorphic Encryption (Lai, Deng, Ma, Sakurai, Weng). Constructs CCA-secure keyed-FHE (evaluation-key-gated homomorphic computation) via convertible identity-based FHE.
