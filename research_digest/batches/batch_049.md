# Batch 049 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by theory/foundations work, apparently from a single proceedings-style ePrint cluster (likely CRYPTO 2023): NIZK, SNARGs, proofs of knowledge, and complexity-based foundations (OWFs, Kolmogorov complexity).
- Heavy MPC coverage: solitary-output MPC, proactive secret sharing, network-agnostic/statistical MPC, YOSO/mobile adversaries, topology-hiding computation, DORAM, Byzantine agreement.
- Strong quantum-crypto cluster: publicly-verifiable/certified deletion (three papers), quantum PKE, unclonable/copy-protection, pseudorandom states with proof of destruction.
- Lattice/post-quantum applied work: anonymous credentials, proof-of-knowledge from Hint-MLWE, DualMS multi-signature, chainable functional commitments, VOLE-in-the-head signatures.
- Very little elliptic-curve content: only the CSIDH/class-group self-pairings paper (140850419) and the ECDSA provable-security limits paper (14369093).
- Anomaly: 14369056 has no visible authors; no duplicates or non-paper files found.

## Papers
- **140850419.pdf** — Weak Instances of Class Group Action Based Cryptography via Self-Pairings (Castryck, Houben, Merz, Mula, van Buuren, Vercauteren). Characterizes isogeny-compatible self-pairings on oriented curves and uses them to find weak instances of CSIDH-style class group actions when the secret isogeny degree is known.
- **140850426.pdf** — A Note on Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge from CDH (Couteau, Jain, Jin, Quach). Builds NIZK and ZAP arguments for NP with infinitely-often uniform security from subexponential CDH (no pairings), plus a denser-parameter non-black-box technique.
- **140850429.pdf** — Random Oracle Combiners: Breaking the Concatenation Barrier for Collision-Resistance (Dodis, Ferguson, Goldin, Hall, Pietrzak). Circumvents collision-resistance combiner lower bounds by defining random-oracle combiners, giving a simple length-preserving salted-XOR construction.
- **140850431.pdf** — A Framework for Practical Anonymous Credentials from Lattices (Bootle, Lyubashevsky, Nguyen, Sorniotti). Introduces lattice-based anonymous credentials with prover/verifier cost independent of user count, via a new short-preimage assumption family also yielding blind and group signatures.
- **140850440.pdf** — Publicly-Verifiable Deletion via Target-Collapsing Functions (Bartusek, Khurana, Poremba). Introduces target-collapsing hashes (weaker than collapsing) enabling publicly-verifiable deletion for Dual-Regev/LWE encryption and commitments from one-way functions.
- **140850445.pdf** — How to Recover a Secret with O(n) Additions (Applebaum, Nir, Pinkas). Constructs near-threshold secret sharing whose reconstruction needs only O(n) field additions, beating Shamir and Cramer-Xing complexity for threshold cryptography.
- **140850447.pdf** — Fork-Resilient Continuous Group Key Agreement (Alwen, Mularczyk, Tselekounis). Generalizes CGKA to tolerate forks and out-of-order traffic, enabling scalable E2E encrypted group messaging over unreliable infrastructure, with two constructions.
- **140850448.pdf** — Security-Preserving Distributed Samplers: How to Generate any CRS in One Round without Random Oracles (Abram, Waters, Zhandry). New definitions and one-round standard-model distributed CRS samplers preserving security-game hardness, built on a new single-message ZK notion.
- **140850452.pdf** — Anonymous Tokens with Stronger Metadata Bit Hiding from Algebraic MACs (Chase, Durak, Vaudenay). Attacks the CRYPTO 2020 hidden-metadata-bit anonymous token protocol and builds a stronger algebraic-MAC-based token hiding issuer metadata for fraud detection.
- **140850454.pdf** — Individual Cryptography (Dziembowski, Faust, Lizurej). Formalizes "individual" algorithms where one machine must fully know the secret, constructing proofs of individual knowledge and individual secret sharing via MPC-hardness.
- **140850461.pdf** — Unifying Freedom and Separation for Tight Probing-Secure Composition (Belaïd, Cassiers, Rivain, Taleb). Establishes equivalences between non-interference and input-output separation definitions for masking, generalizes to multi-input gadgets, and proves security of known gadgets.
- **140850464.pdf** — SNARGs for Monotone Policy Batch NP (Brakerski, Farber Brodsky, Kalai, Lombardi, Paneth). Constructs LWE-based SNARGs for monotone-policy batch-NP via a new predicate-extractable hash, departing from the non-signaling PCP framework.
- **140850481.pdf** — Toward Practical Lattice-based Proof of Knowledge from Hint-MLWE (Kim, Lee, Seo, Song). Improves ZK proofs of knowledge for RLWE encryption and BDLOP commitments using the Hint-MLWE assumption, removing noise flooding and rejection sampling overhead.
- **140850482.pdf** — Publicly Verifiable Zero-Knowledge and Post-Quantum Signatures from VOLE-in-the-Head (Baum, Braun, Delpech de Saint Guilhem, Klooß, Orsini, Roy, Scholl). Converts designated-verifier VOLE-based ZK into publicly verifiable linear-size proofs and post-quantum signatures via a "VOLE-in-the-head" technique.
- **140850484.pdf** — Perfect MPC Over Layered Graphs (David, Deligios, Goel, Ishai, Konring, Kushilevitz, Liu-Zhang, Narayanan). Studies perfectly secure MPC under mobile adversaries/YOSO settings, aiming to match BGW-style guarantees with layered communication graphs.
- **140850489.pdf** — One-way Functions and the Hardness of (Probabilistic) Time-Bounded Kolmogorov Complexity w.r.t. Samplable Distributions (Liu, Pass). Shows equivalence between OWF existence and mild average-case hardness of MpKtP under any samplable distribution, matching the uniform-distribution case.
- **140850494.pdf** — DualMS: Efficient Lattice-Based Two-Round Multi-Signature with Trapdoor-Free Simulation (Chen). Proposes a Dilithium-G-based two-round multi-signature with smaller keys/signatures than DOTT and MuSig-L via trapdoor-free dual signing simulation.
- **14369014.pdf** — Pseudorandomness with Proof of Destruction and Applications (Behera, Brakerski, Sattath, Shmueli). Defines pseudorandom quantum states with classical proofs of destruction, constructible from any post-quantum OWF, enabling classically-communicated quantum primitives.
- **14369015.pdf** — Near-Optimal Private Information Retrieval with Preprocessing (Lazzaretti, Papamanthou). First single-server PIR with ~O(sqrt(n)) amortized server time and ~O(1) bandwidth, via a new adaptable pseudorandom set primitive.
- **14369017.pdf** — Anonymous Permutation Routing (Bunn, Kushilevitz, Ostrovsky). Improves the NIAR anonymous routing model to near-linear router computation while relaxing assumptions from decisional linear to weaker ones.
- **14369030.pdf** — Revisiting Updatable Encryption: Controlled Forward Security, Constructions and a Puncturable Perspective (Slamanig, Striecks). Investigates "controlled" forward security for ciphertext-independent updatable encryption and gives constructions from a puncturable viewpoint.
- **14369034.pdf** — Generalized Special-Sound Interactive Proofs and their Knowledge Soundness (Attema, Fehr, Resch). Generalizes special soundness to arbitrary access structures on challenge subsets, extending proof-of-knowledge extraction results to a much wider class of interactive proofs.
- **14369039.pdf** — Synchronizable Fair Exchange (Kumaresan, Raghuraman, Sealfon). Introduces the FSyX two-party primitive, shown complete for fair n-party computation with dishonest majority via pairwise FSyX instances and synchronized triggers.
- **14369040.pdf** — Memory Checking for Parallel RAMs (Mathialagan). Initiates memory checking for PRAMs: online checker with O(log N) overhead and offline with amortized O(1), from one-way functions.
- **14369041.pdf** — Multi-Instance Randomness Extraction and Security against Bounded-Storage Mass Surveillance (Guan, Wichs, Zhandry). Extends incompressible cryptography to multi-user settings so storage-bounded mass surveillance with later key compromise reveals only a proportional fraction of ciphertexts.
- **14369049.pdf** — Communication Lower Bounds of Key-Agreement Protocols via Density Increment Arguments (Huang, Mao, Yang, Zhang). Studies communication complexity of random-oracle-model key agreement, addressing the conjecture that Merkle's Puzzles are communication-optimal, via density increment arguments.
- **14369050.pdf** — Efficiently Testable Circuits Without Conductivity (Baig, Chakraborty, Dziembowski, Gałązka, Lizurej, Pietrzak). Builds tamper-detecting circuit compilers without the prior fan-out conductivity restriction, using random inner-product checks.
- **14369051.pdf** — On Secure Computation of Solitary Output Functionalities With and Without Broadcast (Alon, Omri). Characterizes three-party solitary-output functionalities computable with full security against one corruption in the point-to-point model.
- **14369053.pdf** — Towards Topology-Hiding Computation from Oblivious Transfer (Ball, Bienstock, Kohl, Meyer). First topology-hiding computation on arbitrary networks against all-but-one semi-honest corruptions from OT-based two-party computation, via locally simulatable MPC.
- **14369056.pdf** — Public-Key Encryption, Local Pseudorandom Generators, and the Low-Degree Method (authors not visible). Applies the low-degree method from statistics to design/analyze a PKE scheme based on Goldreich's local PRG, combining two Applebaum-Barak-Wigderson proposals.
- **14369057.pdf** — Composable Long-Term Security with Rewinding (Berger, Broadnax, Klooß, Mechler, Müller-Quade, Ottenhues, Raiber). Enables rewinding-based UC simulation for long-term security, constructing a long-term-secure composable commitment in the CRS model despite prior impossibility results.
- **14369059.pdf** — Proactive Secret Sharing with Constant Communication (Falk, Noble, Rabin). First PSS protocols with constant per-party communication per epoch using expander graphs, with various privacy/robustness trade-offs.
- **14369060.pdf** — Chainable Functional Commitments for Unbounded-Depth Circuits (Balbás, Catalano, Fiore, Lai). Introduces chainable functional commitments supporting multi-input openings, yielding first FCs for unbounded-depth circuits from pairings or lattices.
- **14369061.pdf** — Zombies and Ghosts: Optimal Byzantine Agreement in the Presence of Omission Faults (Loss, Stern). Shows Byzantine agreement with mixed Byzantine/send/receive omission faults is possible when n > 2t + r + s with cryptographic setup, matching the known lower bound.
- **14369062.pdf** — (Verifiable) Delay Functions from Lucas Sequences (Hoffmann, Hubáček, Kamath, Krňák). Studies sequential hardness of Lucas sequences over RSA moduli as delay functions, potentially weaker than iterated squaring, and builds verifiable delay functions.
- **14369068.pdf** — Network Agnostic MPC with Statistical Security (Appan, Choudhury). Initiates statistically secure network-agnostic MPC in the general-adversary model, giving protocols secure for Q(2) synchronous and Q(3) asynchronous adversary structures.
- **14369069.pdf** — Agile Cryptography: A Universally Composable Approach (Badertscher, Ciampi, Kiayias). Proposes a UC framework of updatable ideal functionalities capturing security retained across timely algorithm updates, exemplified for signatures and NIZK.
- **14369070.pdf** — Holographic SNARGs for P and Batch-NP from (Polynomially Hard) Learning with Errors (Kiyoshima). Public-verification holographic SNARGs for P and batch-NP from polynomial LWE, applied to weaken assumptions of a 3-round ZK argument.
- **14369072.pdf** — Immunizing Backdoored PRGs (Ball, Dodis, Goldin). Studies immunization of backdoored PRGs: rules out seeded 1-immunizers under falsifiable assumptions and analyzes seedless k-immunizers for k ≥ 2.
- **14369076.pdf** — Weakening Assumptions for Publicly-Verifiable Deletion (Bartusek, Khurana, Malavolta, Poremba, Walter). Generic compiler adding publicly-verifiable deletion to cryptosystems using only one-way functions (or one-way state generators).
- **14369079.pdf** — Publicly Verifiable Deletion from Minimal Assumptions (Kitagawa, Nishimaki, Yamakawa). Compiler adding publicly verifiable deletion to PKE, ABE, and quantum FHE from OWFs alone, upgrading private verification via digital signatures.
- **14369081.pdf** — 3-Party Secure Computation for RAMs: Optimal and Concretely Efficient (Ichikawa, Komargodski, Hamada, Kikuchi, Ikarashi). Three-party DORAM with optimal O(log N) local computation, 25x faster than prior work, extended to the first concretely efficient maliciously secure DORAM.
- **14369089.pdf** — On the Round Complexity of Fully Secure Solitary MPC with Honest Majority (Badrinarayanan, Miao, Mukherjee, Ravi). Determines round complexity of fully secure solitary-output MPC with honest majority: 3 rounds with broadcast (matching standard MPC), with additional settings analyzed.
- **14369091.pdf** — Your Reputation's Safe with Me: Framing-Free Distributed Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Hazay, Venkitasubramaniam, Weiss). Defines strong completeness for distributed ZK proofs so malicious verifiers cannot frame an honest prover, and constructs strongly-complete dZK protocols.
- **14369093.pdf** — Limits in the Provable Security of ECDSA Signatures (Hartmann, Kiltz). Meta-reduction result: algebraic security reductions for ECDSA require programming the conversion function, so meaningful proofs need strong idealized models.
- **14369094.pdf** — Public-Key Encryption with Quantum Keys (Barooti, Grilo, Huguenin-Dumittan, Malavolta, Sattath, Vu, Walter). Initiates quantum public-key encryption with quantum-state keys, giving security definitions and constructions from OWF-level assumptions (MiniQCrypt).
- **14369096.pdf** — One-out-of-Many Unclonable Cryptography: Definitions, Constructions, and More (Kitagawa, Nishimaki). Introduces relaxed one-out-of-many unclonability, yielding unclonable encryption, copy-protection for point functions, and unclonable predicate encryption from LWE.
- **14369102.pdf** — On Time-Space Lower Bounds for Finding Short Collisions in Sponge Hash Functions (Akshima, Duan, Guo, Liu). Studies auxiliary-input time-space trade-offs for finding short collisions in sponge hashes, closing gaps left by CRYPTO 2022 attacks.
- **14369106.pdf** — Semi-Quantum Copy-Protection and More (Chevalier, Hermouet, Vu). Dequantizes hidden-coset-state copy-protection via classically-instructed remote state preparation, yielding semi-quantum copy-protection and tokenized signatures.
- **14369107.pdf** — Rogue-Instance Security for Batch Knowledge Proofs (Segev, Sharabi, Yogev). Defines rogue-instance knowledge soundness for batch proofs (analogous to rogue-key security) and gives an efficient generic batch proof-of-knowledge for algebraic Sigma protocols like Schnorr.
