# Batch 088 digest

## Overview
- Two clean conference blocks: files 6223xxxxx are CRYPTO 2010 (LNCS 6223) papers; files 6225xxxxx are CHES 2010 (LNCS 6225) papers — an all-2010 theory-then-hardware batch.
- CRYPTO half is heavy on secure computation: verifiable/delegated computation (Gennaro-Gentry-Parno; Chung-Kalai-Vadhan), ORAM, NMZK, coin tossing, UC zero-one law, plus symmetric results (KASUMI sandwich attack, generalized Feistel, HKDF, RKA-secure PRFs).
- CHES half mixes side-channel/fault attacks (random delays, I-cache, collision attacks, Flash bumping, FSA, RSA exponent-randomization fault attack), lightweight crypto (Quark, PRINTcipher, ARMADILLO, GOST in 651 GE), PUFs/TRNGs, and several SHA-3 Round-2 hardware benchmarking papers.
- ECC-relevant entries: co-Z addition formulæ/ladders (Goundar-Joye-Miyaji), Longa-Gebotys x86-64 speed records, and an RNS FPGA scalar-multiplication coprocessor.
- Notable single papers: Dunkelman-Keller-Shamir practical related-key break of full KASUMI; Krawczyk's HKDF; Rivain-Prouff provable higher-order AES masking.
- Anomalies: 62230405.pdf and 62250225.pdf are unreadable font-encoding garbage ("/CD/D2..." mojibake), not summarizable.

## Papers
- **62230350.pdf** — Correcting Errors in RSA Private Keys (Henecka, May, Meurer). Las-Vegas algorithm correcting random bit-flip errors (not just erasures) in RSA secret keys with small e; tolerates error rate δ<0.237, and recovers factorization for δ<0.084.
- **62230369.pdf** — Improved Differential Attacks for ECHO and Grøstl (Thomas Peyrin). Better differential trails and a new internal differential attack yield best-known cryptanalysis of both SHA-3 candidates, including a distinguisher for the full Grøstl-256 compression function.
- **62230387.pdf** — A Practical-Time Related-Key Attack on the KASUMI Cryptosystem (Dunkelman, Keller, Shamir). New "sandwich attack" recovers the full 128-bit KASUMI key with 4 related keys and 2^32 time, verified experimentally in two hours on a PC.
- **62230405.pdf** — Not a readable paper: extracted text is font-encoding mojibake ("/CD/D2/CX/DA ..."); title and content unrecoverable from the extract.
- **62230423.pdf** — Concurrent Non-Malleable Zero Knowledge Proofs (Lin, Pass, Tseng, Venkitasubramaniam). First concurrent NMZK proof (not just argument) without setup assumptions, with improved round complexity and tighter reductions.
- **62230441.pdf** — Equivalence of Uniform Key Agreement and Composition Insecurity (Cho, Lee, Ostrovsky). Proves adaptive insecurity of composed non-adaptive PRFs is equivalent to the existence of uniform-transcript key agreement, strengthening Pietrzak's duality.
- **62230459.pdf** — Non-Interactive Verifiable Computing (Gennaro, Gentry, Parno). Formalizes verifiable computation and gives a non-interactive scheme letting weak clients outsource function evaluation with cheap verification plus input/output privacy.
- **62230477.pdf** — Improved Delegation of Computation using Fully Homomorphic Encryption (Chung, Kalai, Vadhan). Improves Gennaro-Gentry-Parno delegation: eliminates the huge public key and reduces the delegator's offline work.
- **62230495.pdf** — Oblivious RAM Revisited (Pinkas, Reinman). Rebuilds ORAM with Cuckoo hashing and new oblivious sorting, achieving O(n) external memory and O(log² n) requests per access, improving practicality.
- **62230513.pdf** — On Strong Simulation and Composable Point Obfuscation (Bitansky, Canetti). Introduces "Virtual Grey Box" relaxation of VBB obfuscation and constructs fully composable point-function obfuscators under a strong DDH variant.
- **62230531.pdf** — Protocols for Multiparty Coin Toss With Dishonest Majority (Beimel, Omri, Orlov). Extends Moran-Naor-Segev to multiparty coin tossing with <2/3 corruptions, achieving optimal O(1/r) bias in key parameter regimes.
- **62230551.pdf** — Multiparty Computation for Dishonest Majority: from Passive to Active Security at Low Cost (Damgård, Orlandi). Efficient cut-and-choose gives UC active security for arithmetic circuits at small constant overhead over passive security.
- **62230569.pdf** — Secure Multiparty Computation with Minimal Interaction (Ishai, Kushilevitz, Paskin). Shows two-round MPC is possible when only one party is corrupted (n≥5), matching known three-round lower bounds for t≥2.
- **62230587.pdf** — A Zero-One Law for Cryptographic Complexity w.r.t. Computational UC Security (Maji, Prabhakaran, Rosulek). Proves every trusted setup in the UC framework is either useless or all-powerful; develops automata-theoretic tools for reactive functionalities.
- **62230607.pdf** — On Generalized Feistel Networks (Hoang, Rogaway). Unified coupling-based proofs give beyond-birthday-bound CCA security for unbalanced, alternating, type-1/2/3, and numeric Feistel variants.
- **62230625.pdf** — Cryptographic Extraction and Key Derivation: The HKDF Scheme (Krawczyk). First rigorous definition of KDF security via computational extractors, justifying and analyzing the extract-then-expand HMAC-based HKDF design.
- **62230646.pdf** — Time-Space Tradeoffs for Attacks Against One-Way Functions and PRGs (De, Trevisan, Tulsiani). Tight time/space/advice tradeoffs for inverting functions on an ε-fraction of inputs and for distinguishing PRG outputs.
- **62230663.pdf** — Pseudorandom Functions and Permutations Provably Secure Against Related-Key Attacks (Bellare, Cash). First standard-model RKA-secure PRFs (Naor-Reingold-based, under DDH) and their conversion to RKA-secure block ciphers.
- **62230682.pdf** — Secure Two-Party Quantum Evaluation of Unitaries Against Specious Adversaries (Dupuis, Nielsen, Salvail). Private two-party quantum unitary evaluation against "specious" adversaries using private SWAP and classical AND-box; characterizes Clifford-group ease.
- **62230704.pdf** — On the Efficiency of Classical and Quantum Oblivious Transfer Reductions (Winkler, Wullschleger). New bounds on the efficiency of statistically secure two-party computation and OT reductions from distributed randomness and OT variants.
- **62230723.pdf** — Sampling in a Quantum Population, and Applications (Bouman, Fehr). Framework lifting classical sampling strategies to quantum systems, yielding simple new security proofs for BB84 QKD and quantum oblivious transfer.
- **62250001.pdf** — Quark: A Lightweight Hash (Aumasson, Henzen, Meier, Naya-Plasencia). Sponge-based lightweight hash family inspired by Grain/KATAN; u-Quark gives 64-bit security in 1379 GE for RFID-class devices.
- **62250016.pdf** — PRINTcipher: A Block Cipher for IC-Printing (Knudsen, Leander, Poschmann, Robshaw). Two block ciphers (PRINTcipher-48/96) designed to exploit IC-printing technology for very low-cost printed RFID tags.
- **62250031.pdf** — Sponge-Based Pseudo-Random Number Generators (Bertoni, Daemen, Peeters, Van Assche). PRNG construction on sponge functions allowing interleaved seeding and output; analyzed via indifferentiability, with a small-width Keccak instance.
- **62250046.pdf** — A High Speed Coprocessor for Elliptic Curve Scalar Multiplications over Fp (Guillermin). RNS-based FPGA architecture, reportedly the fastest public design for general prime fields; SPA-resistant and DPA-capable.
- **62250060.pdf** — Co-Z Addition Formulæ and Binary Ladders on Elliptic Curves (Goundar, Joye, Miyaji). Extends Meloni's co-Z arithmetic with new formulæ yielding efficient, side-channel-protected Montgomery-ladder and double-add scalar multiplication.
- **62250075.pdf** — Efficient Techniques for High-Speed Elliptic Curve Cryptography (Longa, Gebotys). Bottom-up field/point/scalar optimizations set new x86-64 ECC speed records, including GLS and Twisted Edwards, with up to ~30% gains.
- **62250090.pdf** — Analysis and Improvement of the Random Delay Countermeasure of CHES 2009 (Coron, Kizhvatov). Shows the CHES 2009 random-delay method is weaker than claimed, proposes an improved generator, a better security criterion, and practical DPA validation.
- **62250105.pdf** — New Results on Instruction Cache Attacks (Acıiçmez, Brumley, Grabher). HMM/vector-quantization framework enables real-world I-cache timing attack recovering OpenSSL DSA keys via lattice methods, plus kernel/compiler countermeasures.
- **62250120.pdf** — Correlation-Enhanced Power Analysis Collision Attack (Moradi, Mischke, Eisenbarth). Adapts collision attacks to break the masked Canright-Batina AES S-box implementation using only ~6× the traces of an unprotected attack.
- **62250135.pdf** — Side-Channel Analysis of Six SHA-3 Candidates (Benoît, Peyrin). Gives concrete DPA procedures and selection functions for six second-round SHA-3 candidates, comparing their side-channel resistance by simulation.
- **62250150.pdf** — Flash Memory 'Bumping' Attacks (Skorobogatov). New optical fault-injection technique presets data-path bits, cutting verification-search space for secure Flash memory from >2^100 to ~2^15 per block.
- **62250165.pdf** — Self-Referencing: A Scalable Side-Channel Approach for Hardware Trojan Detection (Du, Narasimhan, Chakraborty, Bhunia). Compares transient current across IC regions to cancel process variation, improving Trojan-detection sensitivity at scale.
- **62250180.pdf** — When Failure Analysis Meets Side-Channel Attacks (Di-Battista, Courrege, Rouzeyre, Torres, Perdu). Adapts failure-analysis methods (light emission, laser stimulation) into DPA/DEMA-style attacks and photocurrent-assisted leakage enhancement.
- **62250195.pdf** — Fast Exhaustive Search for Polynomial Systems in F2 (Bouillaguet, Chen, Cheng, Chou, Niederhagen, Shamir, Yang). Optimized CPU/GPU exhaustive search solves 48 quadratic equations in 48 variables in 21 minutes on a GTX 295.
- **62250210.pdf** — 256-bit Standardized Crypto for 650 GE — GOST Revisited (Poschmann, Ling, Wang). Compact ASIC implementation of GOST 28147-89 needing only 800 GE, reducible to 651 GE using one S-box while remaining standard-compliant.
- **62250225.pdf** — Not a readable paper: extracted text is font-encoding mojibake ("/C5/CX/DC/CT ..."); title and content unrecoverable from the extract.
- **62250240.pdf** — Developing a Hardware Evaluation Method for SHA-3 Candidates (Henzen, Gendotti, Guillet, Pargaetzi, Zoller, Gürkaynak). VLSI benchmarking methodology with 90nm CMOS post-layout results for all 14 Round-2 SHA-3 candidates.
- **62250253.pdf** — Fair and Comprehensive Methodology for Comparing Hardware Performance of Fourteen Round Two SHA-3 Candidates Using FPGAs (Gaj, Homsirikamol, Rogawski). Proposes fair FPGA benchmarking metrics, uniform interface, multi-vendor results, and a single-ranking procedure.
- **62250268.pdf** — Performance Analysis of the SHA-3 Candidates on Exotic Multi-Core Architectures (Bos, Stefan). Benchmarks all Round-2 SHA-3 candidates on Cell Broadband Engine and NVIDIA GPUs, with instruction-count-based estimates.
- **62250283.pdf** — XBX: eXternal Benchmarking eXtension for the SUPERCOP Framework (Wenzel-Benner, Gräf). Extends SUPERCOP beyond POSIX hosts via cross-compilation and hardware harness, enabling benchmarking on phones, PDAs, and smart cards.
- **62250296.pdf** — Public Key Perturbation of Randomized RSA Implementations (Berzati, Canovas-Dumas, Goubin). First fault attack defeating RSA exponent randomization, recovering 1024-bit private keys from about 1000 faulty signatures.
- **62250310.pdf** — Fault Sensitivity Analysis (Li, Sakiyama, Gomisawa, Fukunaga, Takahashi, Ohta). New fault attack using the onset condition of faults (e.g., critical clock frequency) rather than faulty values; breaks PPRM1 and WDDL AES on SASEBO-R.
- **62250325.pdf** — An Alternative to Error Correction for SRAM-Like PUFs (Hofer, Boehm). "Stable-PUF-marking" pre-selects high-mismatch cells to obtain reproducible PUF outputs without conventional error correction.
- **62250341.pdf** — New High Entropy Element for FPGA-Based True Random Number Generators (Varchola, Drutarovsky). Transition Effect Ring Oscillator harvests metastability-resolution noise; TERO-based TRNG passes NIST 800-22 tests.
- **62250356.pdf** — The Glitch PUF: A New Delay-PUF Architecture Exploiting Glitch Shapes (Suzuki, Shimizu). Delay-PUF using non-linear glitch behavior to resist delay-response prediction, with a design-stage simulation evaluation method.
- **62250373.pdf** — Garbled Circuits for Leakage-Resilience: Hardware Implementation and Evaluation of One-Time Programs (Järvinen, Kolesnikov, Sadeghi, Schneider). Generic GC/OTP architecture with two FPGA prototypes and efficiency analysis of leakage-resilient one-time programs.
- **62250388.pdf** — ARMADILLO: A Multi-Purpose Cryptographic Primitive Dedicated to Hardware (Badel et al.). Hardware-oriented primitive for RFID/sensors supporting hashing, encryption, and authentication; serial version needs 2923 GE.
- **62250403.pdf** — Provably Secure Higher-Order Masking of AES (Rivain, Prouff). First generic d-th-order masking scheme for AES with provable security and practical software overhead, extending Ishai-Sahai-Wagner.
- **62250418.pdf** — Algebraic Side-Channel Analysis in the Presence of Errors (Oren, Kirschbaum, Popp, Wool). Recasts single-trace side-channel key recovery as pseudo-Boolean optimization, tolerating measurement errors that break SAT-based approaches.
