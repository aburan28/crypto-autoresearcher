# Batch 108 digest

## Overview
- Dominant cluster: symmetric-key cryptography from FSE 2015 (LNCS 8540) — lightweight block/stream cipher design and cryptanalysis (Sprout, KATAN, PRINCE, Simon/Speck, MISTY1, LED-64), plus MDS matrix and S-box constructions.
- Heavy CAESAR-era authenticated-encryption presence: new schemes (p-OMD, APE, CLOC, COBRA, POE/POET) and attacks on candidates (FIDES, ICEPOLE), plus refined security bounds (GCM, keyed sponges, tweakable blockciphers).
- Second venue cluster: CRYPTO 2014 (LNCS 8616) — lattices/FHE (Gentry-Szydlo, key-homomorphic PRFs, faster bootstrapping), MPC/garbled circuits (FleXOR, dishonest-majority binary MPC), indistinguishability obfuscation (Boneh-Zhandry), and quantum one-time memories.
- Notable applied work: RC4/WPA-TKIP plaintext recovery and RC4 bias analyses, and algorithm-substitution (mass surveillance) security modeling.
- Anomalies: none — all 50 extracts are valid research-paper first pages; no duplicates, slides, or non-paper content found.

## Papers
- **85400108.pdf** — On Lightweight Stream Ciphers with Shorter Internal States (Frederik Armknecht, Vasily Mikhalev). Introduces Sprout, a lightweight stream cipher that mixes the fixed key into keystream generation to resist TMDTO attacks with sub-2x state size.
- **85400109.pdf** — Direct Construction of Recursive MDS Diffusion Layers using Shortened BCH Codes (Daniel Augot, Matthieu Finiasz). Gives a direct BCH-code-based construction of recursive MDS matrices for compact block-cipher diffusion layers, avoiding exhaustive companion-matrix search.
- **85400111.pdf** — Lightweight MDS Involution Matrices (Sim, Khoo, Oggier, Peyrin). New search methods and equivalence classes yield the lightest known (involutory) MDS matrices for dimensions 4–32, saving up to 50% XOR gates.
- **85400113.pdf** — Internal Differential Boomerangs: Practical Analysis of the Round-Reduced Keccak-f Permutation (Jérémy Jean, Ivica Nikolić). Combines internal differentials with boomerangs to give practical, verified distinguishers on 7–8 rounds of Keccak-f.
- **85400114.pdf** — A New Classification of 4-bit Optimal S-boxes and its Application to PRESENT, RECTANGLE and SPONGENT (Zhang, Bao, Rijmen, Liu). Classifies optimal 4-bit S-boxes into 183 categories and shows S-box choice strongly affects differential/linear resistance of these ciphers.
- **85400117.pdf** — Boosting OMD for Almost Free Authentication of Associated Data (Reyhanitabar, Vaudenay, Vizár). p-OMD simplifies the OMD AE scheme to pure Merkle-Damgård iteration, processing associated data almost free with unchanged security bound.
- **85400119.pdf** — Improved Linear Sieving Techniques with Applications to Step-Reduced LED-64 (Dinur, Dunkelman, Keller, Shamir). Introduces linear key sieve for MITM attacks, improving attacks on 2-step and 3-step LED-64 from 2^60 to ~2^48–2^49.
- **85400122.pdf** — Improved Slender-set Linear Cryptanalysis (Liu, Jin, Qi). Improves Borghoff et al.'s slender-set attack on PRESENT-like ciphers with secret S-boxes, practically recovering full-round Maya's S-box.
- **85400123.pdf** — A More Cautious Approach to Security Against Mass Surveillance (Degabriele, Farshim, Poettering). Shows a hidden assumption in the BPR algorithm-substitution-attack model makes security unsatisfiable when slightly weakened, and proposes a refined model.
- **85400125.pdf** — Rotational Cryptanalysis of ARX Revisited (Khovratovich, Nikolić, Pieprzyk, Sokołowski, Steinfeld). Shows chained additions in ARX ciphers are not Markovian for rotational analysis; corrects probability computation and fixes attacks on BLAKE2.
- **85400126.pdf** — Dependence in IV-related bytes of RC4 key enhances vulnerabilities in WPA (Sen Gupta, Maitra, Meier, Paul, Sarkar). Studies RC4 biases from WPA's IV-derived key bytes, yielding an improved WPA distinguisher and new keystream biases.
- **85400127.pdf** — Relaxing Full-Codebook Security: A Refined Analysis of Key-Length Extension Schemes (Gaži, Lee, Seurin, Steinberger, Tessaro). Analyzes cascade key-length-extension PRP security when the adversary sees less than the full codebook, with tight bounds.
- **85400130.pdf** — Optimally Secure Tweakable Blockciphers (Bart Mennink). Constructs tweakable blockciphers from one or two blockcipher calls achieving up to optimal 2^n security, beyond birthday bound.
- **85400131.pdf** — Improved Single-Key Attacks on 9-Round AES-192/256 (Li, Jia, Wang). Adds a key-dependent sieve to MITM attacks, improving 9-round AES-192/256 key-recovery complexities and memory.
- **85400133.pdf** — The Related-Key Security of Iterated Even–Mansour Ciphers (Farshim, Procter). Initiates related-key analysis of iterated Even–Mansour ciphers, showing one round resists some RKA classes and three rounds give CCA-level RKA security.
- **85400137.pdf** — Differential-Linear Cryptanalysis of ICEPOLE (Huang, Tjuawinata, Wu). Differential-linear attacks under nonce misuse recover state/key of ICEPOLE-128/128a at ~2^46 and ICEPOLE-256a at ~2^60, experimentally verified.
- **85400138.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of FIDES (Itai Dinur, Jérémy Jean). Guess-and-determine state-recovery attacks break FIDES-80/96 at 2^75/2^90 time with only 2^4 bytes of data, enabling universal forgery.
- **85400140.pdf** — Plaintext Recovery Attacks Against WPA/TKIP (Paterson, Poettering, Schuldt). Exploits large TSC-dependent RC4 keystream biases in WPA/TKIP for practical broadcast-setting plaintext recovery.
- **85400143.pdf** — Differential-Linear Cryptanalysis Revisited (Blondeau, Leander, Nyberg). Develops a concise exact theory of differential-linear cryptanalysis using the fundamental differential–linear link.
- **85400144.pdf** — Improved All-Subkeys Recovery Attacks on FOX, KATAN and SHACAL-2 Block Ciphers (Isobe, Shibutani). Improved ASR/MITM attacks give best-known single-key round coverage on FOX, KATAN32/48/64, and SHACAL-2.
- **85400148.pdf** — Improved Higher-Order Differential Attacks on MISTY1 (Achiya Bar On). Speeds up higher-order differential attacks on 7-round MISTY1 by 2^16 and gives first attack with all 5 FL layers.
- **85400150.pdf** — LS-Designs: Bitslice Encryption for Efficient Masked Software Implementations (Grosso, Leurent, Standaert, Varıcı). Proposes LS-design block ciphers whose bitslice structure yields efficient, regular Boolean-masked software implementations against side channels.
- **85400151.pdf** — Efficient Fuzzy Search on Encrypted Data (Boldyreva, Chenette). Defines efficiently fuzzy-searchable encryption with optimal security notion, shows space-inefficiency is inherent, and offers weaker-notion efficient schemes.
- **85400153.pdf** — Impact of ANSI X9.24-1:2009 Key Check Value on ISO/IEC 9797-1:2011 MACs (Iwata, Wang). Fully characterizes how the public key check value weakens five of nine ISO CBC MACs, with matching proofs of tightness.
- **85400157.pdf** — Meet-in-the-Middle Technique for Truncated Differential and Its Applications to CLEFIA and Camellia (Li, Jia, Wang, Dong). MITM-constructed truncated differentials yield best-known attacks on 14–15-round CLEFIA and 11–12-round Camellia.
- **85400158.pdf** — Security of Keyed Sponge Constructions Using a Modular Proof Approach (Andreeva, Daemen, Mennink, Van Assche). Derives improved indistinguishability bounds for inner- and outer-keyed sponges, including multi-target settings via multiplicity.
- **85400159.pdf** — Pipelineable On-Line Encryption (Abed, Fluhrer, Forler, List, Lucks, McGrew, Wenzel). Introduces POE, a CCA-secure pipelineable online cipher family, and POET, an online AE scheme inheriting its properties.
- **85400160.pdf** — Match Box Meet-in-the-Middle Attack against KATAN (Fuhr, Minaud). New "match box" MITM extension attacks 153 of 254 KATAN32 rounds with low data, best known on all KATAN versions.
- **85400163.pdf** — Multiple differential cryptanalysis of round-reduced PRINCE (Canteaut, Fuhr, Gilbert, Naya-Plasencia, Reinhard). Multiple-differential attack reaches 10 rounds of PRINCE (vs. 8 previously), extended to 11 rounds for a weak S-box variant.
- **85400168.pdf** — GCM Security Bounds Reconsidered (Niwa, Ohashi, Minematsu, Iwata). Shows GCM's 2^22 bound constant is near-optimal under the sum bound, then proves improved bounds (constant 32) avoiding it.
- **85400171.pdf** — APE: Authenticated Permutation-Based Encryption for Lightweight Cryptography (Andreeva et al.). First permutation-based AE scheme provably resistant to nonce misuse, instantiated with Quark, Photon, and Spongent permutations.
- **85400172.pdf** — Collision Spectrum, Entropy Loss, T-Sponges, and Cryptanalysis of GLUON-64 (Perrin, Khovratovich). Introduces collision probability spectrum for non-injective iterations and uses it for preimage attacks on GLUON-64 violating its claims.
- **85400173.pdf** — Equivalent Key Recovery Attacks against HMAC and NMAC with Whirlpool Reduced to 7 Rounds (Guo, Sasaki, Wang, Wang, Wen). Adapts AES MITM techniques to recover HMAC/NMAC equivalent keys for 7-round Whirlpool (previous best: 6 rounds).
- **85400174.pdf** — The Indistinguishability of the XOR of k permutations (Cogliati, Lampe, Patarin). Improves Lucks' security bounds for the XOR of k independent pseudorandom permutations as a PRF, beyond the birthday bound.
- **85400183.pdf** — SPRING: Fast Pseudorandom Functions from Rounded Ring Products (Banerjee, Brenner, Leurent, Peikert, Rosen). Practical instantiations of the BPR lattice-based PRF with BCH/CRT tricks, achieving software throughput within small factors of AES.
- **85400184.pdf** — Collision Attack on 5 Rounds of Grøstl (Mendel, Rijmen, Schläffer). New multi-block differential trails give collisions on 4- and 5-round Grøstl-256 (2^67/2^120), including chosen-prefix collisions.
- **85400186.pdf** — CLOC: Authenticated Encryption for Short Input (Iwata, Minematsu, Guo, Morioka). Provably secure AEAD mode optimized for short messages on small microprocessors, needing no precomputation or large memory.
- **85400193.pdf** — Branching Heuristics in Differential Collision Search with Applications to SHA-512 (Eichlseder, Mendel, Schläffer). Improved guess-and-determine branching heuristic yields practical semi-free-start collisions on 38 of 80 SHA-512 steps.
- **85400194.pdf** — Differential Cryptanalysis of Round-Reduced Simon and Speck (Abed, List, Lucks, Wenzel). Differential attacks reach slightly over half the rounds of NSA's lightweight Simon and Speck cipher families.
- **85400195.pdf** — The Related-Key Analysis of Feistel Constructions (Barbosa, Farshim). Shows Feistel with suitable round-key reuse achieves provable related-key security; analyzes Lucks' transform and its oracle-dependent limitations.
- **85400196.pdf** — COBRA: A Parallelizable Authenticated Online Cipher without Block Cipher Inverse (Andreeva, Luykx, Mennink, Yasuda). Misuse-resistant online AE from a Feistel network, GCM-like efficiency, no block-cipher inverse, privacy up to prefix under nonce reuse.
- **85400197.pdf** — Security Analysis of Key-Alternating Feistel Ciphers (Lampe, Seurin). Proves security bounds for key-alternating Feistel ciphers via coupling, generalizing Luby–Rackoff and improving prior coupling analysis.
- **86160106.pdf** — Revisiting the Gentry-Szydlo Algorithm (Lenstra, Silverberg). Places the Gentry-Szydlo lattice-with-symmetry algorithm in a general algebraic framework, giving deterministic poly-time orthonormal-basis finding with cryptographic applications.
- **86160109.pdf** — New and Improved Key-Homomorphic Pseudorandom Functions (Banerjee, Peikert). Key-homomorphic PRFs from much weaker LWE assumptions with dramatically improved key sizes, parameters, and runtime, plus ring-LWE variants.
- **86160110.pdf** — FleXOR: Flexible garbling for XOR gates that beats free-XOR (Kolesnikov, Mohassel, Rosulek). Garbled-circuit XOR technique needing weaker assumptions than free-XOR and yielding over 30% smaller circuits for many cases.
- **86160113.pdf** — Dishonest Majority Multi-Party Computation for Binary Circuits (Larraia, Orsini, Smart). Extends Tiny-OT to n-party dishonest-majority MPC for binary circuits via pairwise-to-global authentication transfer, improving two-party efficiency too.
- **86160117.pdf** — Amplifying Privacy in Privacy Amplification (Aggarwal, Dodis, Jafargholi, Miles, Reyzin). Studies optimal privacy amplification, simultaneously optimizing entropy loss, rounds, post-application robustness, and source privacy, including the bounded retrieval model.
- **86160121.pdf** — Faster Bootstrapping with Polynomial Error (Alperin-Sheriff, Peikert). New FHE bootstrapping with small-polynomial runtime and LWE approximation factor, using elementary arithmetic instead of Barrington's theorem.
- **86160128.pdf** — Multiparty Key Exchange, Efficient Traitor Tracing, and More from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Boneh, Zhandry). Uses iO for setup-free multiparty non-interactive key exchange, distributed-setup broadcast encryption, and fully collusion-resistant traitor tracing.
- **86160133.pdf** — Single-shot security for one-time memories in the isolated qubits model (Yi-Kai Liu). Improved one-time memory constructions from isolated qubits with single-shot min-entropy security against LOCC adversaries, efficiently implementable.
