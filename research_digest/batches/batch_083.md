# Batch 083 digest

## Overview
- Strongly clustered around 2008–2009 IACR conference work (Eurocrypt/Crypto/FSE 2009 era, Springer LNCS-style front pages), dominated by symmetric cryptanalysis and provable-security theory.
- Heavy SHA-3-competition-era hash cryptanalysis: attacks or proofs for MD6, RadioGatún, LAKE, EnRUPT, Whirlpool, Grøstl, Tiger, SHA-0/1/2, plus the famous MD5 rogue-CA chosen-prefix collision paper.
- A notable leakage-resilience/side-channel cluster: Goldwasser's invited-talk abstract, the Standaert–Malkin–Yung unified framework, Pietrzak's leakage-resilient mode, Naor–Segev, and Alwen–Dodis–Wichs.
- Elliptic-curve content is implementation-oriented rather than ECDLP-theoretic: ECM on GPUs (Bernstein et al.), GLV endomorphisms (Galbraith–Lin–Scott), double-base chains (Doche–Kohel–Sica), genus-2 curve generation (Satoh).
- Notable anomalies: 54790369.pdf is a 2-page invited-talk abstract, not a full paper; no duplicates or non-paper items found.

## Papers
- **54790279.pdf** — Cube Attacks on Tweakable Black Box Polynomials (Itai Dinur, Adi Shamir). Introduces cube attacks for solving tweakable GF(2) polynomials, breaking reduced-round Trivium far faster than prior techniques.
- **54790299.pdf** — Smashing SQUASH-0 (Khaled Ouafi, Serge Vaudenay). Key-recovery attack with 1024 chosen challenges on Shamir's RFID challenge-response protocol SQUASH-0 with linear mixing; non-linear SQUASH remains open.
- **54790313.pdf** — Practical Chosen Ciphertext Secure Encryption from Factoring (Dennis Hofheinz, Eike Kiltz). Practical Rabin-based public-key encryption with standard-model CCA security reduced to the factoring assumption.
- **54790333.pdf** — Realizing Hash-and-Sign Signatures under Standard Assumptions (Susan Hohenberger, Brent Waters). Stateful counter-based technique yielding standard-model hash-and-sign signatures from RSA and CDH in bilinear groups.
- **54790351.pdf** — PKE Secure Against Key Dependent Chosen Plaintext and Adaptive Chosen Ciphertext Attacks (Camenisch, Chandran, Shoup). Naor-Yung-style generic construction solving the open problem of KDM-CCA2 security, instantiated from BHHO and Cramer-Shoup.
- **54790369.pdf** — Cryptography Without (Hardly Any) Secrets (Shafi Goldwasser). Two-page invited-talk abstract surveying leakage/side-channel-resilient cryptography after Micali-Reyzin; not a full research paper.
- **54790371.pdf** — Salvaging Merkle-Damgård for Practical Applications (Dodis, Ristenpart, Shrimpton). Develops new idealized hash notions provably met by strengthened Merkle-Damgård, bridging random-oracle analysis and practical hash functions.
- **54790389.pdf** — On the Security of Padding-Based Encryption Schemes (Eike Kiltz, Krzysztof Pietrzak). Black-box impossibility result: OAEP-like padding schemes cannot be proven CCA-secure even from ideal trapdoor permutations.
- **54790407.pdf** — Simulation without the Artificial Abort (Mihir Bellare, Thomas Ristenpart). Removes the artificial-abort step from Waters' IBE proof, simplifying it and improving concrete security and efficiency.
- **54790425.pdf** — On the Portability of Generalized Schnorr Proofs (Camenisch, Kiayias, Yung). Defines protocol portability conditions under which efficient Sigma protocols retain ZKP properties when used as subroutines.
- **54790443.pdf** — A Unified Framework for the Analysis of Side-Channel Key Recovery Attacks (Standaert, Malkin, Yung). Information-theoretic plus security-metric framework enabling fair evaluation and comparison of side-channel attacks and countermeasures.
- **54790461.pdf** — A Leakage-Resilient Mode of Operation (Krzysztof Pietrzak). Shows weak PRFs are seed-incompressible and builds a simple leakage-resilient stream cipher secure under bounded per-round leakage.
- **54790481.pdf** — ECM on Graphics Cards (Bernstein, Chen, Cheng, Lange, Yang). Record-setting GPU implementation of elliptic-curve factorization using Edwards curves and parallel addition formulas on NVIDIA GTX 295 cards.
- **54790501.pdf** — Double-Base Number System for Multi-Scalar Multiplications (Doche, Kohel, Sica). Joint Double-Base Chains cut additions over 20% versus Joint Sparse Form for computing [n]P + [m]Q.
- **54790519.pdf** — Endomorphisms for Faster Elliptic Curve Cryptography on a Large Class of Curves (Galbraith, Lin, Scott). Extends Iijima et al. homomorphisms over Fp2 to the GLV method, speeding point multiplication to ~0.70–0.84 of prior best.
- **54790537.pdf** — Generating Genus Two Hyperelliptic Curves over Large Characteristic Finite Fields (Takakazu Satoh). Probabilistic polynomial-time point-counting test to generate Y²=X⁵+uX³+vX curves with almost-prime Jacobian order.
- **54790555.pdf** — Verifiable Random Functions from Identity-based Key Encapsulation (Abdalla, Catalano, Fiore). Constructs VRFs directly from "VRF-suitable" IB-KEMs, avoiding Goldreich-Levin hardcore-bit transformations.
- **54790573.pdf** — Optimal Randomness Extraction from a Diffie-Hellman Element (Chevalier, Fouque, Pointcheval, Zimmer). Proves truncation of DH elements/EC point abscissas is a near-optimal extractor via new exponential-sum bounds.
- **54790591.pdf** — A New Randomness Extraction Paradigm for Hybrid Encryption (Kiltz, Pietrzak, Stam, Yung). Generic 1-universal to 2-universal hash-proof-system transform giving standard-model IND-CCA2 hybrid encryption, including Damgård's ElGamal.
- **566.pdf** — A Simple Threshold Authenticated Key Exchange from Short Secrets (Abdalla, Chevassut, Fouque, Pointcheval). GTPAKE: threshold password-based AKE for gateway/back-end server settings, sharing only the private key among servers.
- **56650001.pdf** — Cube Testers and Key Recovery Attacks on Reduced-Round MD6 and Trivium (Aumasson, Dinur, Meier, Shamir). Recovers 14-round MD6 key in 2^22 and introduces cube testers distinguishing MD6 and Trivium at record rounds.
- **56650023.pdf** — An Efficient State Recovery Attack on X-FCSR-256 (Stankovski, Hell, Johansson). State recovery on the FCSR-based stream cipher X-FCSR-256 with total complexity at most 2^57.6.
- **56650041.pdf** — Key Collisions of the RC4 Stream Cipher (Mitsuru Matsui). Exhibits colliding RC4 key pairs (24-byte example) producing identical initial states, likely even below 20-byte keys.
- **56650054.pdf** — Intel's New AES Instructions for Enhanced Performance and Security (Shay Gueron). Describes Intel's six AES-NI instructions giving hardware AES support for performance and security.
- **56650070.pdf** — Blockcipher Based Hashing Revisited (Martijn Stam). Generalized analysis of rate-1 blockcipher-based hashing, including two single-call compression functions with beyond-birthday collision resistance.
- **56650090.pdf** — On the Security of Tandem-DM (Fleischmann, Gorski, Lucks). First security proof for the Tandem-DM double-block-length hash: ~2^120.4 collision bound when instantiated with AES-256.
- **56650110.pdf** — Indifferentiability of Permutation-Based Compression Functions and Tree-Based Modes, with Applications to MD6 (Dodis, Reyzin, Rivest, Shen). Indifferentiability proofs for MD6's compression function and tree mode of operation.
- **56650128.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of RadioGatún (Thomas Fuhr, Thomas Peyrin). Differential collision attacks on the RadioGatún hash family, with an explicit collision for the 2-bit word version.
- **56650146.pdf** — Preimage Attacks on Reduced Tiger and SHA-2 (Isobe, Shibutani). Meet-in-the-middle one-block preimage attacks on 16-round Tiger and 24-step SHA-256/SHA-512, the first for reduced-step SHA-512.
- **56650164.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the LAKE Hash Family (Biryukov, Gauravaram, Guo, Khovratovich, Ling, Matusiewicz, Nikolić, Pieprzyk, Wang). Round-independent collision (2^33, 2^42) and near-collision attacks on LAKE-256 exploiting non-injective primitives.
- **56650201.pdf** — Algebraic Techniques in Differential Cryptanalysis (Martin Albrecht, Carlos Cid). Combines algebraic relations from differential characteristics with statistical attacks to improve key recovery on reduced PRESENT-128.
- **56650218.pdf** — Multidimensional Extension of Matsui's Algorithm 2 (Hermelin, Cho, Nyberg). Statistically grounded multidimensional linear cryptanalysis methods (LLR and chi-squared), tested on reduced-round Serpent.
- **56650256.pdf** — Practical Collisions for EnRUPT (Sebastiaan Indesteege, Bart Preneel). Practical collision attacks on all seven SHA-3 candidate EnRUPT variants, with a demonstrated EnRUPT-256 collision.
- **56650270.pdf** — The Rebound Attack: Cryptanalysis of Reduced Whirlpool and Grøstl (Mendel, Rechberger, Schläffer, Thomsen). Introduces the rebound attack, yielding collisions on 4.5-round Whirlpool and attacks on reduced Grøstl.
- **56650287.pdf** — Revisiting the IDEA Philosophy (Pascal Junod, Marco Macchetti). Fast SIMD IDEA implementation (5.42 cycles/byte) and a new WIDEA cipher with nonlinear key schedule and fast compression function.
- **56650307.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the ISDB Scrambling Algorithm MULTI2 (Aumasson, Nakahara, Sepehrdad). Theoretical shortcut key recovery on full MULTI2 and linear attacks up to 20 of 32 rounds on Japan's broadcast cipher.
- **56650319.pdf** — Beyond-birthday-bound Security Based on Tweakable Block Cipher (Kazuhiko Minematsu). Builds a 2n-bit block cipher with beyond-birthday security from a tweakable block cipher, plus TBC constructions from ordinary ciphers.
- **56650356.pdf** — MAC Reforgeability (John Black, Martin Cochran). Defines MAC reforgeability, shows widely used MACs fail it, and proposes WMAC with a tight reduction for constrained devices.
- **56650374.pdf** — New Distinguishing Attack on MAC using Secret-Prefix Method (Xiaoyun Wang, Wei Wang, Keting Jia, Meiqin Wang). Truncated-differential distinguisher for secret-prefix MACs, applied to 43-step and 61-step reduced SHA-1.
- **56650386.pdf** — Fast and Secure CBC-Type MAC Algorithms (Mridul Nandi). GCBC1/GCBC2 CBC-MAC variants needing only s block-cipher calls, with unified PRF-security analysis of CBC-type constructions.
- **56650405.pdf** — HBS: A Single-Key Mode of Operation for Deterministic Authenticated Encryption (Iwata, Yasuda). Single-key deterministic AE mode combining vector-input polynomial hashing with a counter-like encryption mode.
- **56770001.pdf** — Reconstructing RSA Private Keys from Random Key Bits (Nadia Heninger, Hovav Shacham). Recovers small-exponent RSA private keys from a random 0.27 fraction of bits, applying to cold-boot attacks.
- **56770018.pdf** — Public-Key Cryptosystems Resilient to Key Leakage (Moni Naor, Gil Segev). Generic leakage-resilient PKE from any universal hash proof system, secure against bounded key-leakage functions.
- **56770036.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Public-Key Cryptography in the Bounded-Retrieval Model (Alwen, Dodis, Wichs). First leakage-resilient ID, signature, and AKA protocols, extensible to tolerate gigabytes of leakage via larger keys.
- **56770054.pdf** — Short Chosen-Prefix Collisions for MD5 and the Creation of a Rogue CA Certificate (Stevens, Sotirov, Appelbaum, Lenstra, Molnar, Osvik, de Weger). Refined MD5 chosen-prefix collisions (~2^49) enabling a rogue CA certificate from a commercial CA.
- **56770071.pdf** — Meet-in-the-Middle Preimage Attacks Against Reduced SHA-0 and SHA-1 (Kazumaro Aoki, Yu Sasaki). MitM preimage attacks handling linear-combination message schedules, reaching 52-step SHA-0 and 48-step SHA-1.
- **56770090.pdf** — Private Mutual Authentication and Conditional Oblivious Transfer (Stanislaw Jarecki, Xiaomin Liu). First O(1)-exponentiation unlinkable secret-handshake protocol, built on new private conditional oblivious transfer under DDH.
- **56770107.pdf** — Randomizable Proofs and Delegatable Anonymous Credentials (Belenkiy, Camenisch, Chase, Kohlweiss, Lysyanskaya, Shacham). Efficient delegatable anonymous credentials via randomizable Groth-Sahai NIZK proofs, O(Lk) proof size.
- **56770124.pdf** — Computational Differential Privacy (Mironov, Pandey, Reingold, Vadhan). Relaxes differential privacy to computationally bounded adversaries, proves equivalences via the dense model theorem, and enables more accurate protocols.
- **56770141.pdf** — Probabilistically Checkable Arguments (Yael Tauman Kalai, Ran Raz). PIR-based reduction converting public-coin interactive proofs to one-round arguments; defines computationally-sound PCAs with witness-size proofs for NP.
