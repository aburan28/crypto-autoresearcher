# Batch 032 digest

## Overview
- This batch is almost entirely the CRYPTO 2021 proceedings (Springer LNCS ~12825–12828 numbering), so the era is uniform: 2021 flagship-venue theory crypto.
- Dominant themes: secure multiparty computation (YOSO, Fluid MPC, Silver VOLE/OT, GMW-style compilers, unconditional MPC), zero-knowledge/SNARKs (Halo Infinite, state-restoration soundness, batch arguments, compressed Σ-protocols), and secret sharing / extractors / leakage resilience.
- A solid minority covers lattice/post-quantum crypto (LWE ring signatures, FE for Turing machines, compressed Σ-protocols on lattices, faster LLL) and symmetric cryptanalysis (superbox alignment, differential-linear, SPN t-wise independence).
- Notable papers: "Halo Infinite" (PCD without SNARK recursion), "Silver" (silent OT/VOLE from LDPC decoding), "Counterexamples to New Circular Security Assumptions Underlying iO", and the SIDH torsion-point attack paper — the only ECC/isogeny-adjacent work in the batch.
- Anomalies: `13.pdf` is an out-of-sequence older paper (Joye–Paillier prime generation on portable devices, ~2006, 15 pages); `12826339.pdf` is an anonymous submission; `12826431.pdf` shows no authors in the extract. No duplicates or non-paper content found.

## Papers
- **12826229.pdf** — YOSO: You Only Speak Once — Secure MPC with Stateless Ephemeral Roles (Gentry, Halevi, Krawczyk, Magri, Nielsen, Rabin, Yakoubov). Introduces the YOSO model where each MPC party sends a single message then disappears, enabling serverless-style, DoS-resistant MPC.
- **12826241.pdf** — Composition with Knowledge Assumptions (Kerber, Kiayias, Kohlweiss). Formal model for composing zk-SNARK knowledge assumptions; proves general impossibility but yields the first simultaneously succinct and composable zk-SNARK.
- **12826253.pdf** — Oblivious Key-Value Stores and Amplification for Private Set Intersection (Garimella, Pinkas, Rosulek, Trieu, Yanai). Formalizes OKVS, gives fastest constructions to date plus amplification of failure probability, speeding up modern PSI protocols.
- **12826257.pdf** — Game-Theoretic Fairness Meets Multi-Party Protocols: The Case of Leader Election (Chung, Chan, Wen, Shi). Achieves approximate game-theoretically fair leader election in O(log log n) rounds, nearly matching tournament-tree fairness sub-logarithmically.
- **12826271.pdf** — Functional Encryption for Turing Machines with Dynamic Bounded Collusion from LWE (Agrawal, Maitra, Vempati, Yamada). Introduces dynamic bounded-collusion FE (collusion bound chosen at encryption time) with first CPFE constructions for circuits.
- **12826279.pdf** — Adaptive Extractors and their Application to Leakage Resilient Secret Sharing (Chandran, Kanukurthi, Obbattu, Sekar). Defines extractors secure against post-output leakage; builds adaptively secure leakage-resilient secret sharing with constant rate and leakage rate.
- **12826280.pdf** — Silver: Silent VOLE and Oblivious Transfer from Hardness of Decoding Structured LDPC Codes (Couteau, Rindal, Raghuraman). Extremely fast silent OT extension/VOLE (10M OTs in 300 ms, 122 KB) from a new structured-LDPC decoding assumption.
- **12826286.pdf** — Hidden Cosets and Applications to Unclonable Cryptography (Coladangelo, Liu, Liu, Zhandry). Generalizes hidden subspace states to coset states; yields plain-model tokenized signatures and unclonable decryption under iO-based assumptions.
- **12826299.pdf** — Improved Computational Extractors and their Applications (Khurana, Srinivasan). Builds optimal computational two-source and non-malleable extractors in the CRS model under sub-exponential DDH, for balanced and adversarial sources.
- **12826302.pdf** — Towards faster polynomial-time lattice reduction (Kirchner, Espitau, Fouque). Recursive heuristic LLL variants exploiting fast matrix multiplication reduce rank-d lattices in ~Õ(d^ω·C), beating fplll by ~d²·B factors.
- **12826304.pdf** — Fluid MPC: Secure Multiparty Computation with Dynamic Participants (Choudhuri, Goel, Green, Jain, Kaptchuk). Initiates MPC where parties dynamically join and leave, formalizing "fluidity" (minimum rounds online) and giving constructions.
- **12826307.pdf** — Halo Infinite: Proof-Carrying Data from Additive Polynomial Commitments (Boneh, Drake, Fisch, Gabizon). Generalizes Halo: heuristically builds PCD/recursive proofs from any homomorphic (additive) polynomial commitment without SNARK recursion.
- **12826312.pdf** — Non-Interactive Batch Arguments for NP from Standard Assumptions (Choudhuri, Jain, Jin). First CRS-model publicly verifiable non-interactive batch argument for NP from standard assumptions, via dual-mode interactive batch arguments and correlation intractability.
- **12826319.pdf** — Computational Hardness of Optimal Fair Computation: Beyond Minicrypt (Maji, Wang). Studies which cryptographic assumptions are necessary for optimal (1/r-unfair) fair coin-tossing, extending impossibility results beyond Minicrypt.
- **12826320.pdf** — Tight State-Restoration Soundness in the Algebraic Group Model (Ghoshal, Tessaro). Methodology for tight state-restoration soundness bounds in the AGM, giving first non-trivial concrete Fiat-Shamir security analysis for Bulletproofs- and Sonic-style arguments.
- **12826323.pdf** — Thinking Outside the Superbox (Bordes, Daemen, Kuijsters, Van Assche). Formalizes "alignment" in cipher design and computationally compares aligned vs unaligned primitives' differential/linear propagation and clustering behavior.
- **12826334.pdf** — Counterexamples to New Circular Security Assumptions Underlying iO (Hopkins, Jain, Lin). Gives explicit counterexamples to strengthened circular-security assumptions behind recent lattice iO schemes (Gay–Pass, Wee–Wichs); schemes unbroken but assumptions need refinement.
- **12826339.pdf** — Targeted Lossy Functions and Applications (Anonymous). Relaxes lossy trapdoor functions to target-input lossiness without inversion trapdoors; builds them from Minicrypt assumptions with many applications.
- **12826341.pdf** — Tighter Security for Schnorr Identification and Signatures: A High-Moment Forking Lemma for Σ-Protocols (Rotem, Segev). Circumvents the square-root security-loss barrier for Schnorr and special-sound Σ-protocols via a high-moment forking lemma.
- **12826342.pdf** — On the Possibility of Basing Cryptography on EXP ≠ BPP (Liu, Pass). Shows equivalence between infinitely-often OWFs and mild average-case hardness of Levin-Kolmogorov complexity (MKtP), linking OWFs to EXP ≠ BPP up to an errorless/two-sided gap.
- **12826348.pdf** — Mac'n'Cheese: Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Boolean and Arithmetic Circuits with Nested Disjunctions (Baum, Malozemoff, Rosen, Scholl). VOLE-MAC-based interactive ZK for large circuits with cheap nested disjunctions, plus Fiat-Shamir non-interactive variant.
- **12826351.pdf** — Quadratic Secret Sharing and Conditional Disclosure of Secrets (Beimel, Othman, Peter). Studies secret sharing with degree-2 polynomial share generation, proving bounds and constructions beyond linear schemes.
- **12826352.pdf** — Two-Round Trip Schnorr Multi-Signatures via Delinearized Witnesses (Kılınç Alper, Burdges). Two-round Schnorr multi-signature secure in AGM+ROM under one-more-DL and a new m-entwined sum problem, simplifying delinearization proofs.
- **12826361.pdf** — Constructing Locally Leakage-resilient Linear Secret-sharing Schemes (Maji, Paskin-Cherniavsky, Suad, Wang). Proves random-linear-code Massey schemes with rate k/n > 1/2 resist arbitrary constant-bit local leakage per share, improving prior 0.907 threshold.
- **12826364.pdf** — Improved torsion-point attacks on SIDH variants (de Quehen, Kutas, Leonardi, Martindale, Panny, Petit, Stange). Strengthens Petit's torsion-point attacks on supersingular isogeny key exchange, affecting more SIDH parameterizations.
- **12826365.pdf** — Differential-Linear Cryptanalysis from an Algebraic Perspective (Liu, Lu, Lin). Introduces Differential Algebraic Transitional Form theory for more accurate bias estimation and key recovery; applied to Ascon, Serpent, Grain v1.
- **12826370.pdf** — Low-Complexity Weak Pseudorandom Functions in AC0[MOD2] (Boyle, Couteau, Gilboa, Ishai, Kohl, Scholl). New WPRF candidates computable by sparse F2 polynomials / AC0∘MOD2 circuits, with security analyses and implications for LPN-style assumptions.
- **12826375.pdf** — The t-wise Independence of Substitution-Permutation Networks (Liu, Tessaro, Vaikuntanathan). Proves almost pairwise independence of concrete SPN/key-alternating ciphers with enough rounds, implying provable resistance to differential and linear attacks.
- **12826376.pdf** — Compact Ring Signatures from Learning With Errors (Chatterjee, Garg, Hajiabadi, Khurana, Liang, Malavolta, Pandey, Shiehian). First ring signatures with logarithmic size from plain LWE in the standard model, post-quantum, via compact ZAP arguments.
- **12826377.pdf** — MPC-Friendly Symmetric Cryptography from Alternating Moduli: Candidates, Protocols, and Applications (Dinur, Goldfeder, Halevi, Ishai, Kelkar, Sharma, Zaverucha). New OWF/PRG/WPRF candidates alternating Z2/Z3 linear maps with fast MPC evaluation and Picnic-style signature applications.
- **12826378.pdf** — MHz2k: MPC from HE over Z2k with New Packing, Simpler Reshare, and Better ZKP (Cheon, Kim, Lee). Active-majority MPC over Z2k from somewhat homomorphic encryption; new packing and ZK proofs cut amortized communication 2.2–4.8×.
- **12826384.pdf** — SMILE: Set Membership from Ideal Lattices with Applications to Ring Signatures and Confidential Transactions (Lyubashevsky, Nguyen, Seiler). Logarithmic-size lattice set-membership proofs yielding smaller ring signatures and a more efficient Monero-like confidential transaction system.
- **12826388.pdf** — Large Message Homomorphic Secret Sharing from DCR and Applications (Roy, Singh). First HSS with negligible error and exponentially large plaintext space from DCR (not LWE/FHE), resolving a BGI16 open question; gives 2-server ORAM and rate-1 trapdoor hash.
- **12826393.pdf** — Sublinear GMW-Style Compiler for MPC with Preprocessing (Boyle, Gilboa, Ishai, Nof). New malicious-security compiler for preprocessing MPC where extra storage and online communication are only logarithmic in circuit size.
- **12826398.pdf** — Authenticated Key Exchange and Signatures with Tight Security in the Standard Model (Han, Jager, Kiltz, Liu, Pan, Riepel, Schäge). First tightly secure AKE in the standard model, generically from signatures and KEMs with suitable new security notions.
- **12826401.pdf** — Limits on the Adaptive Security of Yao's Garbling (Kamath, Klein, Pietrzak, Wichs). Shows the exponential-in-depth adaptive security loss of Jafargholi–Wichs' modified Yao garbling is essentially optimal via matching lower bounds.
- **12826403.pdf** — An Algebraic Framework for Universal and Updatable SNARKs (Ràfols, Zapico). Introduces Checkable Subspace Sampling Arguments unifying pairing-based universal/updatable SNARK constructions, enabling modular designs with new trade-offs.
- **12826412.pdf** — Unconditional Communication-Efficient MPC via Hall's Marriage Theorem (Goyal, Polychroniadou, Song). First unconditional MPC for a single arithmetic circuit with amortized O(1) field elements per gate.
- **12826416.pdf** — Time- and Space-Efficient Arguments from Groups of Unknown Order (Block, Holmgren, Rosen, Rothblum, Soni). Public-coin ZK arguments for NP with prover time/space T·polylog(T)/S·polylog(T) from hidden-order groups; also fixes a gap in the DARK polynomial commitment.
- **12826418.pdf** — SSE and SSD: Page-Efficient Searchable Symmetric Encryption (Bossuat, Bost, Fouque, Minaud, Reichle). Replaces locality with "page efficiency" as the right SSE performance metric for SSDs, via a Data-Independent Packing memory-allocation abstraction.
- **12826420.pdf** — A Compressed Σ-Protocol Theory for Lattices (Attema, Cramer, Kohl). Ports compressed Σ-protocol theory to lattices (SIS), giving the first non-PCP lattice-based commit-and-prove transparent circuit-ZK with polylog communication.
- **12826422.pdf** — Towards a Unified Approach to Black-Box Constructions of Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Liang, Pandey). Bypasses Rosulek's impossibility by constructing "proof-based" OWFs/PRGs/CRHFs with black-box ZK proofs from ordinary primitives.
- **12826423.pdf** — Threshold Schnorr with Stateless Deterministic Signing from Standard Assumptions (Garillot, Kondi, Mohassel, Nikolaenko). Dishonest-majority threshold Schnorr with deterministic EdDSA-style nonces requiring no fresh randomness or state continuity.
- **12826431.pdf** — Multi-theorem Designated-Verifier NIZK for QMA (?; no authors in extract). First multi-theorem secure MDV-NIZK for QMA under LWE, via a compiler from NIZK for NP plus quantum sigma protocols.
- **13.pdf** — Fast Generation of Prime Numbers on Portable Devices: An Update (Joye, Paillier). Speeds up prime generation for onboard RSA key creation on smart cards with coprocessors; an older (~2006) paper, out of sequence in this batch.
- **130420119.pdf** — Somewhere Statistical Soundness, Post-Quantum Security, and SNARGs (Kalai, Vaikuntanathan, Zhang). Unifies Kilian's protocol and the BMW heuristic via multi-extractable SSB hashing from LWE; converts BatchNP SNARGs into post-quantum SNARGs for non-signaling PCP languages.
- **130420120.pdf** — On Derandomizing Yao's Weak-to-Strong OWF Construction (Brzuska, Couteau, Karanko, Rohrbach). Proves any direct-product weak-to-strong OWF amplification needs input length Ω(p(n)), explaining the cost of Yao's construction.
- **130420122.pdf** — Information-Theoretically Secure MPC against Mixed Dynamic Adversaries (Damgård, Escudero, Ravi). Efficient IT-secure MPC where a dynamic adversary chooses mixed passive/active/fail-stop corruption thresholds after seeing the protocol.
- **130420124.pdf** — Random-Index PIR and Applications (Gentry, Halevi, Magri, Nielsen, Yakoubov). Defines random-index PIR (retrieved index is a random output), shows equivalence to PIR with rounds, gives noninteractive variants, and applies it to YOSO-style protocols.
- **130420128.pdf** — Ring-based Identity Based Encryption — Asymptotically Shorter MPK and Tighter Security (Abla, Liu, Wang, Wang). RLWE-based IBE with shorter master public keys and tighter reductions via a new ring homomorphic equality test and partition-based analysis improvements.
