# Batch 008 digest

## Overview
- This batch is drawn from two major conference proceedings: ASIACRYPT 2017 (files 10624xxxx, LNCS vol. 10624) and PKC 2018 (files 10770/10777xxxx), i.e. late-2017 era cryptography.
- Dominant themes: zero-knowledge proofs/SNARKs and secure computation (garbled circuits, MPC, OT), lattice-based and post-quantum cryptography (LWE/SIS/LWR constructions, Rényi divergence, quantum Fiat-Shamir), and obfuscation/multilinear maps.
- Symmetric cryptography is a solid minority thread: AES yoyo distinguishers, Even-Mansour attacks, OCB3 integrity, beyond-birthday MACs, iterated random functions.
- Only two papers touch elliptic curves directly: a faster SIDH isogeny algorithm (Costello–Hisil) and quantum resource estimates for Shor's ECDLP algorithm (Roetteler et al.) — both notable for the corpus's ECC focus.
- Anomalies: two 1-page invited-talk abstracts (Wang on combinatorics in IT-crypto; Moody on the NIST PQC competition) rather than full papers. No duplicates, no non-paper content; the chunk file itself contained 4 stray NUL bytes (cleaned copy used).

## Papers
- **106240255.pdf** — Zero-Knowledge Arguments for Lattice-Based PRFs and Applications to E-Cash (Libert, Ling, Nguyen, Wang). ZK arguments for correct evaluation of LWR-based PRFs, yielding the first compact lattice-based e-cash system.
- **106240260.pdf** — Adaptive Oblivious Transfer with Access Control from Lattice Assumptions (Libert, Ling, Mouhartem, Nguyen, Wang). OT-AC supporting any NC1 branching-program access policy, secure under LWE and SIS.
- **106240264.pdf** — JIMU: Faster LEGO-based Secure Computation using Additive Homomorphic Hashes (Zhu, Huang). LEGO cut-and-choose MPC replacing homomorphic commitments with additive homomorphic hashing, tolerating more faulty garbled gates.
- **106240265.pdf** — Towards a Classification of Non-interactive Computational Assumptions in Cyclic Groups (Ghadafi, Groth). Shows q-GDHE and q-SFrac families act as "uber" assumptions implying all target-type assumptions; proves CDH equivalent to 1-GDHE.
- **106240270.pdf** — Overlaying Conditional Circuit Clauses for Secure Computation (Kennedy, Kolesnikov, Wilfong). Heuristic small universal circuits for if/switch clauses, giving faster garbled-circuit and GMW protocols for conditional logic.
- **106240271.pdf** — New Key Recovery Attacks on Minimal Two-Round Even-Mansour Ciphers (Isobe, Shibutani). Meet-in-the-middle attacks breaking the birthday data barrier on two-round Even-Mansour constructions.
- **106240272.pdf** — Tightly-Secure Signatures from Five-Move Identification Protocols (Kiltz, Loss, Pan). Fiat-Shamir on five-move ID protocols yields tightly secure signatures from CDH and factoring; explains Chevallier-Mames tightness.
- **106240274.pdf** — Instantaneous Decentralized Poker (Bentov, Kumaresan, Miller). Amortized MPC with penalties over stateful Ethereum contracts for poker and off-chain cash distribution; includes duplex micropayment channels.
- **106240275.pdf** — Quantum Fully Homomorphic Encryption With Verification (Alagic, Dulek, Schaffner, Speelman). First non-interactive verifiable quantum FHE scheme with authenticated encryption for delegated quantum computation.
- **106240276.pdf** — Yoyo Tricks with AES (Rønjom, Bardeh, Helleseth). New SPN properties yield key-independent yoyo distinguishers for 3–6 round AES and a record key-recovery attack on 5-round AES.
- **106240285.pdf** — Faster packed homomorphic operations and efficient circuit bootstrapping for TFHE (Chillotti, Gama, Georgieva, Izabachène). Packing, weighted-automata evaluation, and 137ms LWE-to-RingGSW circuit bootstrapping speedups for TFHE.
- **106240288.pdf** — Hybrid Consensus: Efficient Consensus in the Permissionless Model (Pass, Shi). Proves responsive permissionless consensus cannot tolerate ≥1/3 corruption, and gives a matching hybrid protocol achieving that bound.
- **106240292.pdf** — Sharper Bounds in Lattice-Based Cryptography using the Rényi Divergence (Prest). Tight Rényi-divergence lemmas improve security/precision tradeoffs for Gaussian rejection and trapdoor sampling.
- **106240294.pdf** — Homomorphic Encryption for Arithmetic of Approximate Numbers (Cheon, Kim, Kim, Song). The CKKS scheme: HE for approximate real/complex arithmetic with rescaling and RLWE batching via canonical embedding.
- **106240296.pdf** — An Efficient Quantum Collision Search Algorithm and Implications on Symmetric Cryptography (Chailloux, Naya-Plasencia, Schrottenloher). First quantum collision algorithm cheaper than classical in gate count, revising symmetric security estimates.
- **106240298.pdf** — Access Control Encryption for General Policies from Standard Assumptions (Kim, Wu). First ACE scheme for arbitrary policies from standard assumptions via signatures, predicate encryption, and functional encryption.
- **106240299.pdf** — Succinct Spooky Free Compilers Are Not Black Box Sound (Brakerski, Kalai, Perlman). Defines spooky-free compilers for MIP-based delegation; shows succinct ones cannot have black-box security reductions to falsifiable assumptions.
- **106240301.pdf** — On the Depth of Oblivious Parallel RAM (Chan, Chung, Shi). Studies OPRAM depth with unbounded CPUs: logarithmic-depth upper bound and matching fundamental lower bounds.
- **106240305.pdf** — An Efficient Pairing-Based Shuffle Argument (Fauzi, Lipmaa, Siim, Zając). Most efficient known pairing-based NIZK shuffle argument for mix-nets; proves 100k-ciphertext shuffle in under a minute.
- **106240306.pdf** — Amortizing Randomness Complexity in Private Circuits (Faust, Paglialonga, Schneider). Reduces randomness consumption of masking countermeasures against side-channel probing by reusing randomness across gadgets.
- **106240307.pdf** — Post-Quantum Security of Fiat-Shamir (Unruh). Strengthened sigma-protocol conditions under which Fiat-Shamir proofs and signatures remain secure against quantum adversaries.
- **106240308.pdf** — A Subversion-Resistant SNARK (Abdolmaleki, Baghery, Lipmaa, Zając). Makes Groth's 2016 zk-SNARK knowledge-sound and Sub-ZK under CRS subversion with minimal CRS additions and verification.
- **106240309.pdf** — Two-Message Witness Indistinguishability and Secure Computation in the Plain Model from New Assumptions (Badrinarayanan, Garg, Ishai, Sahai, Wadia). Two-message WI and secure computation with super-polynomial simulation from Weak OT / sub-exponential DDH.
- **106240313.pdf** — Efficient Ring Signatures in the Standard Model (Malavolta, Schröder). First near-practical plain-model ring signatures from bilinear groups via re-randomizable keys and NIZK.
- **106240325.pdf** — Improved Security for OCB3 (Bhaumik, Nandi). Improved integrity bound for the OCB3 authenticated-encryption mode against adversaries making multiple/block-heavy forging attempts.
- **106240326.pdf** — Blockcipher-based MACs: Beyond the Birthday Bound without Message Length (Naito). More efficient MACs with beyond-birthday PRF security needing only one blockcipher call per message block.
- **106240329.pdf** — The Iterated Random Function Problem (Bhaumik, Datta, Dutta, Mouha, Nandi). First almost-tight adaptive bound for distinguishing iterates of a random function, relevant to password hashing.
- **106240331.pdf** — Preventing CLT Attacks on Obfuscation with Linear Overhead (Fernando, Rasmussen, Sahai). "Stamping" defense blocking zeroizing attacks on iO over CLT13 multilinear maps with additive branching-program blowup.
- **106240335.pdf** — The First Thorough Side-Channel Hardware Trojan (Ender, Ghandali, Moradi, Paar). Stealthy Trojan defeating provably secure masked SCA-protected implementations with zero gate-level changes, via sub-transistor or routing tweaks.
- **106240336.pdf** — The Minimum Number of Cards in Practical Card-based Protocols (Kastner, Koch, Walzer, Miyahara, Hayashi, Mizuki, Sone). Proves optimality of six-card AND protocols under closed-shuffle restrictions in card-based MPC.
- **106240338.pdf** — Linear-Time Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Arithmetic Circuit Satisfiability (Bootle, Cerulli, Ghadafi, Groth, Hajiabadi, Jakobsen). ZK proofs where prover uses O(N) field multiplications for N-gate circuits, via error-correcting-code commitments.
- **106240339.pdf** — A simple and compact algorithm for SIDH with arbitrary degree isogenies (Costello, Hisil). New Montgomery-form odd-degree isogeny formulas unifying curve/point evaluation, widening practical SIDH curve choices.
- **106240342.pdf** — Quantum Resource Estimates for Computing Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithms (Roetteler, Naehrig, Svore, Lauter). Precise qubit/Toffoli counts for Shor's ECDLP algorithm on NIST prime-field curves; ECC easier than RSA at equal classical security.
- **1062409006.pdf** — Combinatorics in Information-Theoretic Cryptography (invited talk) (Wang). One-page invited-talk abstract on applying combinatorial objects (hash families, graph colouring) to secret sharing and IT-secure schemes.
- **1062409007.pdf** — The ship has sailed: the NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography "competition" (Moody). One-page invited-talk abstract summarizing NIST's post-quantum standardization process and its motivation.
- **10770101.pdf** — Committed MPC: Maliciously Secure Multiparty Computation from Homomorphic Commitments (Frederiksen, Pinkas, Yanai). Dishonest-majority MPC over additively homomorphic commitments instead of MAC-ed shares, over arbitrary fields.
- **10770102.pdf** — Fast Garbling of Circuits over 3-Valued Logic (Lindell, Yanai). Efficient garbling of Kleene three-valued logic circuits (motivated by SQL with missing values) via Boolean encoding.
- **10770106.pdf** — Hybrid Encryption in a Multi-User Setting, Revisited (Giacon, Kiltz, Poettering). Analyzes multi-user KEM+DEM tightness weaknesses; introduces augmented DEMs robust to multi-instance key-collision attacks.
- **10770117.pdf** — Efficient Adaptively Secure Zero-knowledge from Garbled Circuits (Ganesh, Kondi, Patra, Sarkar). UC-secure constant-round adaptively secure ZK from garbled circuits with linear communication in statement size.
- **10770129.pdf** — Equational Security Proofs of Oblivious Transfer Protocols (Li, Micciancio). Equational-framework analysis of OT extension and the Chou–Orlandi "simplest OT", uncovering subtle definitional/timing shortcomings.
- **10770132.pdf** — Reusing Tamper-Proof Hardware in UC-Secure Protocols (Mechler, Müller-Quade, Nilges). Models tamper-proof hardware tokens as global untrusted setup, enabling safe reuse of simple signature tokens across protocols.
- **10770133.pdf** — New (and Old) Proof Systems for Lattice Problems (Alamati, Peikert, Stephens-Davidowitz). Statistical zero-knowledge proofs for GapSPP (smoothing parameter) with much smaller approximation factors than before.
- **10770134.pdf** — New Constructions of Identity-Based and Key-Dependent Message Secure Encryption Schemes (Döttling, Garg, Hajiabadi, Masny). Instantiates one-time signatures with encryption from LWE/LPN, giving first LPN-based IBE and KDM-secure PKE.
- **10770138.pdf** — Local Non-Malleable Codes in the Bounded Retrieval Model (Dachman-Soled, Kulkarni, Shahverdi). Locally decodable/updatable non-malleable split-state codes secure against bounded-leakage adversaries.
- **10770139.pdf** — Simple and Generic Constructions of Succinct Functional Encryption (Kitagawa, Nishimaki, Tanaka). Builds succinct functional encryption from exponentially-efficient IO (XIO) plus OWF, PKE, or IBE.
- **10770140.pdf** — Attribute-based Signatures for Unbounded Circuits in the ROM and Efficient Instantiations from Lattices (El Kaafarani, Katsumata). First lattice-based ABS for unbounded-circuit policies (in ROM), with a general multi-forking lemma.
- **10770142.pdf** — Full-Hiding (Unbounded) Multi-Input Inner Product Functional Encryption from the k-Linear Assumption (Datta, Okamoto, Tomida). First full-hiding MIFE for inner products in bilinear groups under k-LIN, supporting unbounded encryption slots.
- **10770144.pdf** — Interactively Secure Groups from Obfuscation (Agrikola, Hofheinz). Feasibility result: groups where an interactive Uber assumption holds, built from iO, FHE, and pairing-friendly groups.
- **10770145.pdf** — Graded Encoding Schemes from Obfuscation (Farshim, Hesse, Hofheinz, Larraia). Noise-free "dream version" graded encoding scheme from iO and pairings, provably resisting zeroizing attacks under standard assumptions.
- **10770147.pdf** — Tightly SIM-SO-CCA Secure Public Key Encryption from Standard Assumptions (Lyu, Liu, Han, Gu). Tight simulation-based selective-opening CCA-secure PKE from standard assumptions via strengthened KEM notions.
