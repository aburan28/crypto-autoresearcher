# Batch 045 digest

## Overview
- This batch is strikingly homogeneous: nearly all 50 files are EUROCRYPT 2023 papers (LNCS format, ~29-33 pages), spanning foundations, MPC, signatures, lattices, and symmetric cryptanalysis.
- Isogeny-based crypto is a major theme, dominated by the 2022 SIDH break: Robert's polynomial-time attack, the Maino-Martindale key-recovery attack, SQISign speedups via the Deuring correspondence, and the M-SIDH/MD-SIDH countermeasure proposal.
- Other notable papers: HyperPlonk (Plonk with linear-time prover), SNARGs from DDH, Chopsticks two-round multi-signatures, optimal single-server PIR, and the Batch Bootstrapping I/II series on FHE.
- Several papers address privacy/obliviousness lower bounds (DP data structures, batch PIR, differential obliviousness composition) and quantum-world feasibility/separation results (commitments, OT, Valiant's conjecture).
- Anomalies: none of the files are non-research content; 14004206.pdf (71p) is an unusually long automated-cryptanalysis paper; 14004255/14004256/14004257 form a related two-part series (not duplicates).

## Papers
- **14004057.pdf** — New Time-Memory Trade-Offs for Subset Sum – Improving ISD in Theory and Practice (Esser, Zweydinger). Pruning-based time-memory trade-offs for subset sum/ISD with record quasi-cyclic code decoding computation.
- **14004058.pdf** — Chopsticks: Fork-Free Two-Round Multi-Signatures from Non-Interactive Assumptions (Pan, Wagner). Two-round multi-signatures from DDH without rewinding, including a first tightly secure construction.
- **14004065.pdf** — Functional Commitments for All Functions, with Transparent Setup and from SIS (de Castro, Peikert). First functional commitments for all bounded-complexity functions under standard SIS with transparent setup.
- **14004075.pdf** — Multi-key and Multi-input Predicate Encryption from Learning with Errors (Francati, Friolo, Malavolta, Venturi). Defines and constructs multi-key/multi-input PE from sub-exponential LWE, with applications to NI-MPC and matchmaking encryption.
- **14004080.pdf** — On Differential Privacy and Adaptive Data Analysis with Bounded Space (Dinur, Stemmer, Woodruff, Zhou). First exponential space separation between private and non-private algorithms; reframes adaptive data analysis lower bounds as space bottlenecks.
- **14004084.pdf** — Pitfalls and Shortcomings for Decompositions and Alignment (Lambin, Leander, Neumann). Criteria for unique round-function decompositions in SPNs; shows alignment may have limited impact using PRESENT experiments.
- **14004086.pdf** — A Lower Bound on the Length of Signatures Based on Group Actions and Generic Isogenies (Boneh, Guan, Zhandry). Black-box Ω(λ²/log λ) signature-length lower bound for group-action signatures, matching state of the art.
- **14004096.pdf** — Breaking SIDH in Polynomial Time (Robert). Proven classical polynomial-time attack on SIDH/SIKE even with random starting curve, via dimension-8 abelian varieties.
- **14004102.pdf** — Improved Power Analysis Attacks on Falcon (Zhang, Lin, Yu, Wang). Side-channel key recovery on NIST-selected Falcon via covariance analysis of Gaussian sampler leakage, needing far fewer traces.
- **14004105.pdf** — New algorithms for the Deuring correspondence: Towards practical and secure SQISign signatures (De Feo, Leroux, Longa, Wesolowski). Faster ideal-to-isogeny translation yielding >2x speedup for SQISign key generation and signing.
- **14004109.pdf** — Short Signatures from Regular Syndrome Decoding in the Head (Carozza, Couteau, Joux). MPC-in-the-head signature from regular syndrome decoding with new 5-round ZK proof and ring-sharing conversion.
- **14004110.pdf** — Optimal Single-Server Private Information Retrieval (Zhou, Lin, Tselekounis, Shi). Preprocessing PIR from LWE with Õ(√n) computation and polylog bandwidth per query, improving Eurocrypt'22 scheme.
- **14004111.pdf** — Unbounded Quadratic Functional Encryption and More from Pairings (Tomida). First unbounded FE for quadratic functions plus partially-hiding extension, from MDDH on pairings.
- **14004112.pdf** — SNARGs and PPAD Hardness from the Decisional Diffie-Hellman Assumption (Kalai, Lombardi, Vaikuntanathan). First SNARGs for bounded-depth computation from DDH via Fiat-Shamir instantiation of GKR; yields PPAD hardness.
- **14004114.pdf** — Reverse Firewalls for Oblivious Transfer Extension and Applications to Zero-Knowledge (Chakraborty, Ganesh, Sarkar). First OT extension and efficient ZK protocols secure under machine tampering in the reverse-firewall model.
- **14004125.pdf** — Rai-Choo! Evolving Blind Signatures to the Next Level (Hanzlik, Loss, Wagner). Round-optimal, stateless, concurrently secure blind signatures with interaction-independent cost, from CDH in pairing groups.
- **14004126.pdf** — Sublinear-Communication Secure Multiparty Computation does not require FHE (Boyle, Couteau, Meyer). Sublinear-communication MPC framework from function secret sharing, avoiding FHE for 3- and 5-party settings.
- **14004127.pdf** — Constrained Pseudorandom Functions from Homomorphic Secret Sharing (Couteau, Meyer, Passelègue, Riahinia). Generic 1-key CPRF construction from HSS; first CPRFs for inner products and NC1 from DCR.
- **14004137.pdf** — A Direct Key Recovery Attack on SIDH (Maino, Martindale, Panny, Pope, Wesolowski). Subexponential (polynomial with known endomorphism ring) key-recovery attack on SIDH via isogenies of abelian surfaces.
- **14004158.pdf** — SuperPack: Dishonest Majority MPC with Constant Online Communication (Escudero, Goyal, Polychroniadou, Song, Weng). Actively secure dishonest-majority MPC whose per-gate online cost improves with more honest parties.
- **14004164.pdf** — Better Steady than Speedy: Full break of SPEEDY-7-192 (Boura, David, Heim Boissier, Naya-Plasencia). Optimized differential key recovery fully breaking the 7-round SPEEDY-7-192 block cipher.
- **14004169.pdf** — Oblivious Transfer with Constant Computational Overhead (Boyle, Couteau, Gilboa, Ishai, Kohl, Resch, Scholl). Malicious-security OT with constant computational overhead from correlation-robust local PRGs and sparse LPN.
- **14004170.pdf** — On Valiant's Conjecture: Impossibility of Incrementally Verifiable Computation from Random Oracles (Hall-Andersen, Nielsen). Proves Valiant's 2008 conjecture for IVC with long incremental witnesses and zero knowledge.
- **14004171.pdf** — HyperPlonk: Plonk with Linear-Time Prover and High-Degree Custom Gates (Chen, Bünz, Boneh, Zhang). Multilinear-commitment Plonk variant eliminating FFTs and supporting high-degree custom gates; much faster prover.
- **14004174.pdf** — Speak Much, Remember Little: Cryptography in the Bounded Storage Model, Revisited (Dodis, Quach, Wichs). Streaming bounded-storage-model key agreement and OT circumventing the Dziembowski-Maurer quadratic lower bound.
- **14004188.pdf** — Unique-Path Identity Based Encryption With Applications to Strongly Secure Messaging (Rösler, Slamanig, Striecks). Restricted HIBE variant (UPIBE) enabling efficient strongly secure messaging/KU-KEMs without full HIBE cost.
- **14004191.pdf** — The Return of the SDitH (Aguilar-Melchor, Gama, Howe, Hülsing, Joseph, Yue). Code-based signature scheme using Hypercube-MPCitH to amplify soundness of syndrome-decoding-in-the-head proofs cheaply.
- **14004193.pdf** — Black-Box Separations for Non-Interactive Commitments in a Quantum World (Chung, Lin, Mahmoody). Impossibility of black-box non-interactive commitments from OWFs with quantum computation and classical communication.
- **14004196.pdf** — Optimal Security for Keyed Hash Functions: Avoiding Time-Space Tradeoffs for Finding Collisions (Freitag, Ghoshal, Komargodski). Variable-input-length hash constructions from fixed idealized functions resisting time-space tradeoff collision attacks.
- **14004201.pdf** — On Non-uniform Security for Black-box Non-Interactive CCA Commitments (Garg, Khurana, Lu, Waters). Black-box non-interactive CCA commitments secure against non-uniform adversaries via a new tag-amplification compiler.
- **14004206.pdf** — Exploiting Non-Full Key Additions: Full-Fledged Automatic Demirci-Selçuk Meet-in-the-Middle Cryptanalysis of SKINNY (Shi, Sun, Song, Hu, Yang). Automated DS-MITM framework producing direct key-recovery attacks, applied to SKINNY; unusually long at 71 pages.
- **14004208.pdf** — Lower Bound Framework for Differentially Private and Oblivious Data Structures (Persiano, Yeo). First logarithmic lower bounds for DP RAMs regardless of block/cell sizes, plus bounds for sets, predecessor, union-find.
- **14004209.pdf** — Complete Characterization of Broadcast and Pseudo-Signatures from Correlations (Narayanan, Prabhakaran, Sangwan, Watanabe). Corrects CRYPTO 2004 gap, fully characterizing correlations enabling three-party broadcast/pseudo-signatures.
- **14004210.pdf** — Fully Adaptive Decentralized Multi-Authority ABE (Datta, Komargodski, Waters). First multi-authority ABE with full adaptive security tolerating authority corruptions, from k-linear in prime-order bilinear groups.
- **14004212.pdf** — Finding the Impossible: Automated Search for Full Impossible-Differential, Zero-Correlation, and Integral Attacks (Hadipour, Sadeghi, Eichlseder). Unified CP-based framework finding full key-recovery attacks, not just distinguishers, for block ciphers.
- **14004214.pdf** — A New Framework for Quantum Oblivious Transfer (Agarwal, Bartusek, Khurana, Kumar). "Fixed basis" quantum OT template yielding round-efficient malicious-secure OT protocols in the QROM, from EPR pairs or BB84 states.
- **14004217.pdf** — Lower Bounds for (Batch) PIR with Private Preprocessing (Yeo). Tight hint-size/access trade-off lower bounds tr = Ω(nk) for batch PIR with private preprocessing, closing logarithmic gap for single queries.
- **14004219.pdf** — Detect, Pack and Batch: Perfectly-Secure MPC with Linear Communication and Constant Expected Time (Abraham, Asharov, Patil, Patra). Perfectly secure optimally resilient MPC with linear per-gate communication and constant expected per-layer time via detectable secret sharing.
- **14004221.pdf** — Minimizing Setup in Broadcast-Optimal Two Round MPC (Damgård, Ravi, Siniscalchi, Yakoubov). Characterizes two-round MPC with selective identifiable abort across broadcast patterns, closing open honest-majority-without-PKI question.
- **14004222.pdf** — Revisiting BBS Signatures (Tessaro, Zhu). Proves security of the original shorter BBS signature under q-SDH and gives simplified ZK proofs for anonymous credentials.
- **14004223.pdf** — Registered Attribute-Based Encryption (Hohenberger, Lu, Waters, Wu). Introduces registered ABE: users register keys with a transparent curator, removing the trusted authority, with polylog-size parameters.
- **14004225.pdf** — Generic Attack on Duplex-Based AEAD Modes using Random Function Statistics (Gilbert, Heim Boissier, Khati, Rotella). O(2^{3c/4}) generic forgery attack on duplex AEAD modes, breaking Xoodyak's security claim.
- **14004239.pdf** — M-SIDH and MD-SIDH: countering SIDH attacks by masking information (Fouotsa, Moriya, Petit). Proposed SIDH variants masking torsion-point information to resist the 2022 Castryck-Decru/Maino-Martindale/Robert attacks.
- **14004240.pdf** — A Theory of Composition for Differential Obliviousness (Zhou, Shi, Chan, Maimon). First composition theory for differentially oblivious algorithms via new neighbor-preserving DO notion.
- **14004243.pdf** — Non-Interactive Blind Signatures for Random Messages (Hanzlik). New NIBS primitive: non-interactive blind signatures on random messages from signatures on equivalence classes, using existing Schnorr/ECDSA keys.
- **14004246.pdf** — Proof of Mirror Theory for a Wide Range of ξmax (Cogliati, Dutta, Nandi, Patarin, Saha). First complete proof of Patarin's "P_i⊕P_j theorem" for a wide range of ξmax, fixing gaps in the ICISC'05 proof.
- **14004255.pdf** — Batch Bootstrapping I: A New Framework for SIMD Bootstrapping in Polynomial Modulus (Liu, Wang). Algebraic batch-bootstrapping framework achieving Õ(λ^0.75) amortized FHE multiplications; part one of a series.
- **14004256.pdf** — Speed-Stacking: Fast Sublinear Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Disjunctions (Goel, Hall-Andersen, Kaptchuk, Spooner). Compiler giving sublinear-size, sublinear-time disjunctive ZK by exploiting fast simulation in IOPs and folding arguments.
- **14004257.pdf** — Batch Bootstrapping II: Bootstrapping in Polynomial Modulus Only Requires Õ(1) FHE Multiplications in Amortization (Liu, Wang). Sequel improving batch framework to Õ(1) amortized FHE multiplications per bootstrapped ciphertext (companion to 14004255.pdf).
- **14004260.pdf** — Polynomial-Time Cryptanalysis of the Subspace Flooding Assumption for Post-Quantum iO (Jain, Lin, Lou, Sahai). Polynomial-time distinguisher breaking the "final assumption" underlying a candidate post-quantum iO construction.
