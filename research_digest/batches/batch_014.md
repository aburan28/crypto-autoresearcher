# Batch 014 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ~2018-era IACR conference papers (CRYPTO/EUROCRYPT/ASIACRYPT ePrint style, 30p LNCS format throughout).
- Strong cluster of pairing-based/attribute-based and tightly-secure primitives: ABS, IPE, ID-KEM, QA-NIZK, tight IBE, ring signatures, functional encryption.
- Large post-quantum cluster: lattices (LWE/RLWR, BKZ, BLISS side-channel), isogenies (CSIDH, ordinary-isogeny key exchange), and cryptanalysis of NIST PQC submissions (RankSign, DAGS, WalnutDSA).
- Notable papers: CSIDH (11272266); Galbraith–Massimo–Paterson on malicious DH/EC parameter validation (114420140); SIFA fault attacks on masked AES (11272244).
- Symmetric crypto and implementation security well represented: MORUS and Keccak/KMAC cryptanalysis, white-box attacks, probing-security tooling, quantum k-xor algorithms.
- No anomalies: all 50 files are genuine research-paper first pages; no duplicates, slides, or non-paper content.

## Papers
- **11272203.pdf** — Attribute-Based Signatures for Unbounded Languages from Standard Assumptions (Sakai, Katsumata, Attrapadung, Hanaoka). First ABS for all deterministic Turing machines from SXDH, plus first ABS supporting NFA policies, via a "history of computations" abstraction.
- **11272207.pdf** — Free IF: Omitting Inactive Branches in S-Universal Garbled Circuits (Kolesnikov). Evaluates one circuit from a set S at cost of the largest circuit only, by reusing garbled tables to emulate inactive branches.
- **11272214.pdf** — Constructing Ideal Secret Sharing Schemes based on CRT (Ning, Miao, Huang, Meng, Xiong, Wang). Ideal (t,n)-threshold scheme from CRT over polynomial rings; shows Shamir's scheme is a special case.
- **11272218.pdf** — Improved Inner-product Encryption with Adaptive Security and Full Attribute-hiding (Chen, Gong, Wee). Two prime-order IPE schemes improving Okamoto–Takashima efficiency under k-LIN/XDLIN.
- **11272221.pdf** — Simple and More Efficient PRFs with Tight Security from LWE and Matrix-DDH (Jager, Kurek, Pan). Tightly secure PRFs with logarithmic loss and short keys; yields most efficient LWE-based PRF with small modulus, via all-prefix universal hash functions.
- **11272228.pdf** — Optimal Linear Multiparty Conditional Disclosure of Secrets Protocols (Beimel, Peter). Linear k-party CDS for arbitrary functions with optimal message size O(N^{(k-1)/2}).
- **11272229.pdf** — Measuring, Simulating and Exploiting the Head Concavity Phenomenon in BKZ (Bai, Stehlé, Wen). Refined BKZ simulator capturing shorter-than-expected first vectors, plus a new BKZ variant exploiting the phenomenon.
- **11272231.pdf** — LWE Without Modular Reduction and Improved Side-Channel Attacks Against BLISS (Bootle, Delaplace, Espitau, Fouque, Tibouchi). Shows reduction-free LWE is efficiently solvable, enabling exploitation of linear leakage in BLISS rejection sampling.
- **11272233.pdf** — Short Digital Signatures and ID-KEMs via Truncation Collision Resistance (Jager, Kurek). Standard-model, fully adaptive one-group-element signatures and ID-KEMs from a plausible hash assumption, without random oracles.
- **11272238.pdf** — Simple and Efficient Two-Server ORAM (Gordon, Katz, Wang). One-round two-server ORAM combining tree-based ORAM with two-server PIR; cheap initialization and good concrete parameters.
- **11272244.pdf** — Statistical Ineffective Fault Attacks on Masked AES with Fault Countermeasures (Dobraunig, Eichlseder, Gross, Mangard, Mendel, Primas). Single-fault SIFA breaks AES protected by both masking and detection-based fault countermeasures.
- **11272249.pdf** — Quantum Algorithms for the k-xor Problem (Grassi, Naya-Plasencia, Schrottenloher). Best-known quantum time complexities for k-xor in both low-qubit and exponential-quantum-memory settings, notably improving 3-xor.
- **11272250.pdf** — Concretely Efficient Large-Scale MPC with Active Security (TinyKeys for TinyOT) (Hazay, Orsini, Scholl, Soria-Vazquez). Actively secure MPC scaling to many parties with honest minority, via short-key information-theoretic MACs.
- **11272253.pdf** — Short Variable Length Domain Extenders With Beyond Birthday Bound Security (Chen, Mennink, Nandi). Proves LDT length doubler secure beyond birthday bound and extends it via new "harmonic permutation primitives."
- **11272256.pdf** — Decentralized Multi-Client Functional Encryption for Inner Product (Chotard, Dufour Sans, Gay, Phan, Pointcheval). DMCFE letting distrusting clients non-interactively encrypt for inner-product aggregation; adaptively secure in ROM.
- **11272265.pdf** — Homomorphic Secret Sharing for Low Degree Polynomials (Lai, Malavolta, Schröder). First plain-model HSS evaluating degree >2 polynomials from any degree-k multi-key homomorphic encryption.
- **11272266.pdf** — CSIDH: An Efficient Post-Quantum Commutative Group Action (Castryck, Lange, Martindale, Panny, Renes). Supersingular-isogeny group action over Fp giving practical non-interactive post-quantum key exchange with 64-byte keys at AES-128 level.
- **11272271.pdf** — Multi-Key Homomorphic Signatures Unforgeable under Insider Corruption (Lai, Tai, Wong, Chow). Generic M-HS unforgeable under signer corruption from zk-SNARKs; shows standard-assumption constructions unlikely.
- **11272273.pdf** — On the Concrete Security of Goldreich's Pseudorandom Generator (Couteau, Dupin, Méaux, Rossi, Rotella). Concrete security analysis of local PRGs applying d-ary predicates to random seed subsets.
- **11272277.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of MORUS (Ashur, Eichlseder, Lauridsen, Leurent, Minaud, Rotella, Sasaki, Viguier). Linear correlations in full MORUS keystream enable distinguishing and partial plaintext recovery, weakening the CAESAR finalist.
- **11272278.pdf** — State Separation for Code-Based Game-Playing Proofs (Brzuska, Delignat-Lavaud, Fournet, Kohbrok, Kohlweiss). Decomposes monolithic security games into stateful packages with algebraic composition, simplifying large reductions.
- **11272282.pdf** — Two attacks on rank metric code-based schemes: RankSign and an IBE scheme (Debris-Alazard, Tillich). Algebraic attack via low-weight codewords in augmented LRPC codes breaks all RankSign parameters and undermines the related IBE.
- **11272286.pdf** — An efficient structural attack on NIST submission DAGS (Barelli, Couvreur). Key-recovery attack on quasi-dyadic alternant codes with extension degree 2, breaking the DAGS NIST submission.
- **11272288.pdf** — Attacks and Countermeasures for White-box Designs (Biryukov, Udovenko). Generic attacks on masked white-box implementations extending DCA, yielding new constraints and a protection method.
- **11272291.pdf** — Improved (Almost) Tightly-Secure Simulation-Sound QA-NIZK with Applications (Abe, Jutla, Ohkubo, Roy). First almost-tight unbounded-simulation-sound QA-NIZK with compact CRS/proofs under SXDH, with tightly-secure SPS and CCA2-PKE applications.
- **11272296.pdf** — Identity-based Encryption Tightly Secure under Chosen-ciphertext Attacks (Hofheinz, Jia, Pan). First almost-tightly CCA-secure IBE, seven-group-element ciphertext overhead, from SXDH in pairing groups.
- **11272297.pdf** — Simulatable Channels: Extended Security that is Universally Composable and Easier to Prove (Degabriele, Fischlin). Simulation-based secure-channel definitions covering replay, reordering, fragmentation; proves SSH-CTR (Dropbear) channel-simulatable.
- **11272299.pdf** — On the Hardness of the Computational Ring-LWR Problem and its Applications (Chen, Zhang, Zhang). New computational RLWR assumption proven hard under RLWE, giving faster key exchange/PKE and supporting Saber/Round2/Lizard designs.
- **11272301.pdf** — Towards practical key exchange from ordinary isogeny graphs (De Feo, Kieffer, Smith). Algorithmic speedups revive Couveignes–Rostovtsev–Stolbunov ordinary-isogeny key exchange, with CK-model security proofs.
- **11272302.pdf** — SQL on Structurally-Encrypted Databases (Kamara, Moataz). Structured-encryption-only scheme supporting rich SQL queries on encrypted relational databases with lower leakage than PPE-based approaches.
- **11272303.pdf** — Non-Interactive Secure Computation from One-Way Functions (Badrinarayanan, Jain, Ostrovsky, Visconti). UC-secure NISC from one-way functions only, in the stateless hardware token model with a single token.
- **11272305.pdf** — Arya: Nearly Linear-Time Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Correct Program Execution (Bootle, Cerulli, Groth, Jakobsen, Maller). First ZK proof of knowledge for TinyRAM program execution with nearly linear-time prover.
- **11272308.pdf** — Building Quantum-One-Way Functions from Block Ciphers: Davies-Meyer and Merkle-Damgård (Hosoyamada, Yasuda). Hash functions almost optimally one-way against quantum adversaries in the ideal cipher model, without algebraic assumptions.
- **11272313.pdf** — A Universally Composable Framework for the Privacy of Email Ecosystems (Chaidos, Fourtounelli, Kiayias, Zacharias). UC model capturing email privacy notions (unobservability, anonymity, unlinkability) against global adversaries, with two analyzed constructions.
- **11272318.pdf** — Security of the Blockchain against Long Delay Attack (Wei, Yuan, Zheng). Proves Bitcoin-style blockchain consensus secure against network delays Δ ≥ 1/np, improving Pass–Seeman–shelat.
- **11272319.pdf** — ZCZ: n-bit SPRP Security with Minimal Tweakable-block-cipher Calls (Bhaumik, List, Nandi). Single-key variable-input-length SPRP from one tweakable block cipher, achieving full n-bit security.
- **11272328.pdf** — New MILP Modeling: Improved Conditional Cube Attacks on Keccak-based Constructions (Song, Guo, Shi, Ling). MILP-optimized conditional cube attacks giving best key-recovery results on KMAC128/256, Keyak, and Ketje.
- **11272329.pdf** — Tight Private Circuits: Achieving Probing Security with the Least Refreshing (Belaïd, Goudarzi, Rivain). tightPROVE tool decides t-probing security of composed masked gadgets with tight t+1 shares and minimal refresh insertion.
- **11272334.pdf** — More is Less: Perfectly Secure Oblivious Algorithms in the Multi-Server Setting (Chan, Katz, Nayak, Polychroniadou, Shi). Perfectly secure 3-server ORAM beating single-server schemes by a logarithmic factor, overcoming single-server lower bounds.
- **11272337.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Cryptography from Puncturable Primitives and Obfuscation (Chen, Wang, Zhou). Framework building bounded-leakage-resilient wPRFs/PEPRFs (hence encryption, KEM, PKE) from puncturable PRFs and iO.
- **114420105.pdf** — On Tightly Secure Primitives in the Multi-Instance Setting (Hofheinz, Nguyen). First universal definition of tight reductions; tightens OWF-based signatures, PRGs, and lossy trapdoor functions via multi-instance analysis.
- **114420109.pdf** — Reducing the Key Size of McEliece Cryptosystem from Automorphism-induced Goppa Codes via Permutations (Li, Xing, Yeo). General construction shrinking McEliece public keys using nontrivial automorphism subgroups of Goppa codes.
- **114420110.pdf** — Collusion Resistant Broadcast and Trace from Positional Witness Encryption (Goyal, Vusirikala, Waters). Broadcast with tracing whose key/ciphertext sizes are independent of user count, from positional witness encryption instead of iO.
- **114420121.pdf** — Factoring Products of Braids via Garside Normal Form (Merz, Petit). Braid-product decomposition algorithm yields universal forgery on NIST candidate WalnutDSA in seconds at both security levels.
- **114420123.pdf** — FE for Inner Products and Its Application to Decentralized ABE (Wang, Fan, Liu). Strengthened inner-product FE from LWE/DDH yields decentralized ABE for {0,1}-LSSS with bounded collusions in the plain model.
- **114420128.pdf** — Non-Zero Inner Product Encryption Schemes from Various Assumptions: LWE, DDH and DCR (Katsumata, Yamada). First efficient NIPE without bilinear maps: direct lattice construction plus generic construction from inner-product FE.
- **114420129.pdf** — Generic Constructions of Robustly Reusable Fuzzy Extractor (Wen, Liu, Gu). Two generic rrFE constructions from secure sketches plus key-shift-secure PRFs/AIAE; first instantiations from LWE and from DDH.
- **114420140.pdf** — Safety in Numbers: On the Need for Robust Diffie-Hellman Parameter Validation (Galbraith, Massimo, Paterson). Crafts DH and elliptic-curve parameters passing standard validation yet with easy DLP; enables offline dictionary attacks on PAKE.
- **114420142.pdf** — Shorter Ring Signatures from Standard Assumptions (González). Ring signatures of size Θ(n^{1/3}) group elements from bilinear Diffie-Hellman, improving prior standard-assumption constructions.
- **114420150.pdf** — Leakage-resilient IBE in Bounded Retrieval Model with Nearly Optimal Leakage-Ratio (Nishimaki, Yamakawa). First BRM PKE/IBE achieving leakage ratio 1−ε under standard assumptions, via inner-product predicate encryption.
