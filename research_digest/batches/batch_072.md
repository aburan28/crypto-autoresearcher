# Batch 072 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by mid-2000s CRYPTO/Eurocrypt/TCC-era theory cryptography: secure MPC, Byzantine agreement, secret sharing, UC security, and voting protocols form a large first cluster.
- A second strong cluster is symmetric cryptanalysis and hash-function research in the wake of the Wang et al. MD5/SHA-0/SHA-1 breaks: SHA-1 characteristics, SHA-0 collisions, HMAC/NMAC proofs and attacks, hash combiners, domain-extension transforms.
- RSA/factoring foundations (RSA vs factoring equivalence, Coppersmith attacks, OAEP, plaintext awareness, Paillier impossibility) and identity-/pairing-based constructions (HIBE, broadcast encryption, NIZK group signatures) also feature.
- Only two elliptic/hyperelliptic-curve papers: 2-adic CM method for genus 2 curves, and double-base scalar multiplication on Koblitz/supersingular curves.
- Three anomalous extracts with unreadable mojibake/broken encoding (41170422.pdf, 420.pdf, 42840317.pdf); the remaining 47 are genuine research papers, no obvious duplicates.

## Papers
- **41170368.pdf** — Receipt-Free Universally-Verifiable Voting With Everlasting Privacy (Moran, Naor). First universally verifiable receipt-free voting scheme from general assumptions with everlasting vote privacy; UC-secure, with a discrete-log instantiation.
- **41170388.pdf** — Cryptographic Protocols for Electronic Voting (David Wagner). One-page survey abstract on cryptographic e-voting protocols, open problems, and how cryptographers can improve election integrity.
- **41170389.pdf** — Asymptotically Optimal Two-Round Perfectly Secure Message Transmission (Agarwal, Cramer, de Haan). Two-round perfectly secure message transmission with O(ln) bits, plus a counterexample breaking a CRYPTO 2004 claim.
- **41170404.pdf** — Random Selection with an Adversarial Majority (Gradwohl, Vadhan, Zuckerman). First full-information-model protocols for joint random selection tolerating a dishonest majority, nearly optimal in rounds, randomness, and communication.
- **41170422.pdf** — (?) title unreadable. Extract is corrupted mojibake from a broken font encoding; not summarizable.
- **41170440.pdf** — On Expected Constant-Round Protocols for Byzantine Agreement (Katz, Koo). Expected constant-round authenticated Byzantine agreement with honest majority via new "moderated VSS", simplifying Feldman–Micali.
- **41170458.pdf** — Robust Multiparty Computation with Linear Communication Complexity (Hirt, Nielsen). Robust MPC for t<n/2 whose per-party communication is essentially independent of the number of parties — a first.
- **41170478.pdf** — On Combining Privacy with Guaranteed Output Delivery in Secure Multiparty Computation (Ishai, Kushilevitz, Lindell, Petrank). General MPC protocols that keep full security with honest majority yet retain privacy-with-abort when the majority is corrupted.
- **41170496.pdf** — Scalable Secure Multiparty Computation (Damgård, Ishai). First general MPC where amortized per-player work does not grow (and can vanish) with the number of players.
- **41170516.pdf** — Algebraic Geometric Secret Sharing Schemes and Secure Multi-Party Computations over Small Fields (Chen, Cramer). Ideal linear secret sharing from algebraic curves enabling strong-multiplication MPC over fields much smaller than the player count.
- **41170532.pdf** — Automated Security Proofs with Sequences of Games (Blanchet, Pointcheval). First automatic computational-model prover via a probabilistic process calculus; demonstrates machine proof of FDH signature unforgeability.
- **41170550.pdf** — On Robust Combiners for Private Information Retrieval and Other Primitives (Meier, Przydatek). Robust combiners for PIR, OT, and bit commitment, including the first A-to-B combiners between different primitives.
- **41170565.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Efficiently Combining Collision Resistant Hash Functions (Boneh, Boyen). Shows any collision-resistance combiner evaluating each hash once cannot beat concatenation in output length.
- **41170579.pdf** — On the higher order nonlinearities of algebraic immune functions (Carlet). Improved lower bounds relating a Boolean function's algebraic immunity to its higher-order nonlinearity profile.
- **41170597.pdf** — New Proofs for NMAC and HMAC: Security without Collision-Resistance (Bellare). Proves HMAC is a PRF assuming only that the compression function is a PRF, restoring proof support despite MD5/SHA-1 collisions.
- **420.pdf** — Not a readable paper extract: hexadecimal-encoded garbage text (broken PDF text extraction); not summarizable.
- **42840001.pdf** — Finding SHA-1 Characteristics: General Results and Applications (De Cannière, Rechberger). Automated search for SHA-1 differential characteristics, yielding the first published two-block collision for 64-step SHA-1.
- **42840021.pdf** — Improved Collision Search for SHA-0 (Naito, Sasaki, Shimoyama, Yajima, Kunihiro, Ohta). Improves Wang et al.'s SHA-0 collision attack to 2^36 operations via "submarine" message modification and corrected sufficient conditions.
- **42840037.pdf** — Forgery and Partial Key-Recovery Attacks on HMAC and NMAC Using Hash Collisions (Contini, Yin). Distinguishing, forgery, and partial key-recovery attacks on HMAC/NMAC exploiting MD4/MD5/SHA-0/reduced SHA-1 collisions.
- **42840053.pdf** — New Guess-and-Determine Attack on the Self-Shrinking Generator (Zhang, Feng). Flexible guess-and-determine attack on the self-shrinking generator beating generic time/memory/data tradeoffs, ~O(2^0.556L) time.
- **42840068.pdf** — On the (In)security of Stream Ciphers Based on Arrays and Modular Addition (Paul, Preneel). Unified distinguishing attacks on array-based stream ciphers (Py6, RC4A, ISAAC family) exploiting array-element/index dependence.
- **42840084.pdf** — Construction and Analysis of Boolean Functions of 2t+1 Variables with Maximum Algebraic Immunity (Li, Qi). Constructions of odd-variable Boolean functions with maximum algebraic immunity, analyzing nonlinearity and resilience.
- **42840099.pdf** — Secure Sketch for Biometric Templates (Li, Sutcu, Memon). Framework bounding relative entropy loss of secure sketches for continuous-domain biometrics, avoiding quantization-optimality problems.
- **42840113.pdf** — The 2-adic CM method for genus 2 curves with application to cryptography (Gaudry, Houtmann, Kohel, Ritzenthaler, Weng). 2-adic lifting algorithm for genus-2 CM invariants, a numerically stable alternative to complex analytic methods for crypto curve generation.
- **42840129.pdf** — Extending Scalar Multiplication Using Double Bases (Avanzi, Dimitrov, Doche, Sica). Double-base number systems speeding scalar multiplication on Koblitz and supersingular curves to o(log n) additions.
- **42840145.pdf** — HIBE with Short Public Parameters Without Random Oracle (Chatterjee, Sarkar). Extends Waters' IBE to hierarchical IBE secure in the full model with significantly shorter public parameters.
- **42840161.pdf** — Forward-Secure and Searchable Broadcast Encryption with Short Ciphertexts and Private Keys (Attrapadung, Furukawa, Imai). Introduces Hierarchical Identity-Coupling Broadcast Encryption yielding forward-secure, keyword-searchable broadcast encryption with user-count-independent sizes.
- **42840177.pdf** — On the Generic Construction of Identity-Based Signatures with Additional Properties (Galindo, Herranz, Kiltz). Generic transformation turning PKI signatures with extra properties (blind, proxy, VES, threshold, etc.) into identity-based analogues.
- **42840193.pdf** — On the Provable Security of an Efficient RSA-Based Pseudorandom Generator (Steinfeld, Pieprzyk, Wang). Tighter analysis shows RSA-based PRG outputting Omega(n) bits per multiply is secure under a partial-plaintext RSA variant, answering Gennaro's open question.
- **42840209.pdf** — On the Security of OAEP (Boldyreva, Fischlin). Security arguments for OAEP beyond the full random oracle model, including partial instantiations and a first standard-model non-malleability result.
- **42840225.pdf** — Relationship between Standard Model Plaintext Awareness and Message Hiding (Teranishi, Ogata). Shows standard-model PA2 plaintext awareness plus one-wayness implies IND-CCA2, and computational PA2 is strictly stronger than statistical.
- **42840239.pdf** — On the Equivalence of RSA and Factoring regarding Generic Ring Algorithms (Leander, Rupp). Any efficient generic ring algorithm solving low-exponent RSA can be converted into a factoring algorithm — evidence for RSA≡factoring.
- **42840253.pdf** — Trading One-Wayness against Chosen-Ciphertext Security in Factoring-Based Encryption (Paillier, Villar). Proves factoring-equivalent one-wayness and CCA security are incompatible for single-key schemes, blocking standard-model instantiations of OAEP-type constructions.
- **42840270.pdf** — A Strategy for Finding Roots of Multivariate Polynomials with New Applications in Attacking RSA Variants (Jochemsz, May). Generalized Coppersmith lattice strategy yielding polynomial-time attacks on Qiao–Lam CRT-RSA and Common Prime RSA.
- **42840286.pdf** — Indifferentiable Security Analysis of Popular Hash Functions with Prefix-free Padding (Chang, Lee, Nandi, Yung). Indifferentiability proofs and attacks for prefix-free MD hash constructions; sixteen PGV schemes shown indifferentiable from a random oracle.
- **42840301.pdf** — Multi-property-preserving Hash Domain Extension and the EMD Transform (Bellare, Ristenpart). Shows Coron et al.'s PRO-Pr transforms can destroy collision resistance; proposes EMD, a multi-property-preserving domain-extension transform.
- **42840317.pdf** — (?) title unreadable. Extract is mojibake from a broken CJK font encoding; paper content not identifiable.
- **42840334.pdf** — A Scalable Password-based Group Key Exchange Protocol in the Standard Model (Abdalla, Pointcheval). Constant-round password-based group key exchange proven secure in the standard model via smooth projective hash functions.
- **42840350.pdf** — A Weakness in Some Oblivious Transfer and Zero-Knowledge Protocols (Nikov, Nikova, Preneel). Attack on OT/NIZK protocols built on Paillier-type homomorphic encryption when decryption leaks encryption randomness over small input spaces, with a fix.
- **42840366.pdf** — Almost Optimum Secret Sharing Schemes Secure against Cheating for Arbitrary Secret Distribution (Obana, Araki). Universal-hash-based cheat detection giving nearly optimal share sizes, secure for arbitrary secret distributions.
- **42840384.pdf** — KFC - The Krazy Feistel Cipher (Baignères, Finiasz). Three-round Feistel block cipher with decorrelation-theory-based tight provable resistance against large cryptanalysis classes.
- **42840400.pdf** — Generic Attacks on Unbalanced Feistel Schemes with Contracting Functions (Patarin, Nachef, Berbain). KPA/CPA-1 generic attacks showing at least 2k rounds needed to secure unbalanced Feistel schemes with contracting functions.
- **42840416.pdf** — New Cryptanalytic Results on IDEA (Biham, Dunkelman, Keller). First relation involving all three IDEA operations; best known attacks on 5-round and 7.5-round (related-key) IDEA.
- **42840433.pdf** — New Approach for Selectively Convertible Undeniable Signature Schemes (Kurosawa, Takagi). Two efficient RSA-based selectively convertible undeniable signature schemes, one provably secure without random oracles.
- **42840449.pdf** — Simulation-Sound NIZK Proofs for a Practical Language and Constant Size Group Signatures (Groth). Pairing-based NIZK under the decision linear assumption yielding the first constant-size group signatures secure in the standard model.
- **42840465.pdf** — Analysis of One Popular Group Signature Scheme (Cao). Shows the ACJT group signature scheme fails exculpability: the group manager can sign on behalf of any member.
- **43920001.pdf** — Does Privacy Require True Randomness? (Bosley, Dodis). Resolves the question for information-theoretic encryption: keys allowing encryption of b>log n bits imply deterministic extraction of nearly b random bits.
- **43920021.pdf** — Tackling Adaptive Corruptions in Multicast Encryption Protocols (Panjwani). Soundness theorem for analyzing adaptive corruptions via key graphs; proves an LKH variant adaptively secure.
- **43920041.pdf** — Long-term Security and Universal Composability (Müller-Quade, Unruh). Initiates long-term-secure UC protocols; shows CRS setups insufficient but coin tossing and signature cards enable commitments and zero knowledge.
- **43920061.pdf** — Universally Composable Security with Global Setup (Canetti, Dodis, Pass, Walfish). Extends UC security to protocols sharing global setup, restoring deniability and showing ZK/commitment impossibility in this stronger model.
