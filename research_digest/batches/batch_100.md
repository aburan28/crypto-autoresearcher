# Batch 100 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ~2012–2013 conference papers (ASIACRYPT 2012, PKC/TCC 2013 vintage) on provable-security public-key cryptography: signatures, encryption notions, and UC-secure protocols.
- Recurring themes: functional/attribute-based encryption (Waters, Hohenberger–Waters, Boyen, Okamoto–Takashima, Barbosa–Farshim), homomorphic encryption limits and packing (Brakerski, Gentry, Katz), and zero-knowledge variants (concurrent, public-coin, malleable NIZKs).
- A smaller but notable cluster of side-channel / leakage papers: shuffling analysis (Veyrat-Charvillon et al.), inner-product masking (Balasch et al.), CRT-RSA combined attack (Barbu et al.), RSA key recovery from noisy bits.
- Only one elliptic-curve-specific paper: Longa–Sica's four-dimensional GLV-GLS scalar multiplication.
- Anomalies: three items are short invited-talk abstracts/essays rather than full papers (77780050 Waters, 77780250 Lindell, 77850120 Gentry); no obvious duplicates.

## Papers
- **76580591.pdf** — The Five-Card Trick Can Be Done with Four Cards (Mizuki, Kumamoto, Sone). Shows Boer's card-based secure AND computation needs only four cards instead of five, which is optimal.
- **76580600.pdf** — A Mix-Net From Any CCA2 Secure Cryptosystem (Khazaei, Moran, Wikström). Builds a provably secure, robust mix-net from any CCA2-secure cryptosystem, tolerating static corruption of mix-servers.
- **76580619.pdf** — How not to Prove Yourself: Pitfalls of the Fiat-Shamir Heuristic and Applications to Helios (Bernhard, Pereira, Warinschi). Shows the weak Fiat-Shamir variant is unsound against adaptive statement choice, enabling real attacks on the Helios voting system.
- **76580637.pdf** — Sequential Aggregate Signatures with Lazy Verification from Trapdoor Permutations (Brogle, Goldberg, Reyzin). RSA-style sequential aggregate signatures where signers need not verify or even know others' keys before signing.
- **76580656.pdf** — Commitments and Efficient Zero-Knowledge Proofs from Learning Parity with Noise (Jain, Krenn, Pietrzak, Tentes). LPN-based string commitments with efficient sigma-protocol proofs for arbitrary circuit relations among committed values.
- **76580674.pdf** — Calling out Cheaters: Covert Security With Public Verifiability (Asharov, Orlandi). Extends covert security so honest parties get a publicly convincing certificate of detected cheating, with an efficient two-party protocol.
- **76580692.pdf** — A Unified Framework for UC from Only OT (Pass, Lin, Venkitasubramaniam). Weakens the unified UC framework's assumption to semi-honest OT, yielding new feasibility and constant-round SPS-secure protocols.
- **76580710.pdf** — Four-Dimensional Gallant-Lambert-Vanstone Scalar Multiplication (Longa, Sica). Merges GLV and GLS into 4-dimensional scalar decomposition on twists over Fp2, giving up to ~50% faster scalar multiplication.
- **76580728.pdf** — Shuffling Against Side-Channel Attacks: a Comprehensive Study with Cautionary Note (Veyrat-Charvillon, Medwed, Kerckhof, Standaert). Reassesses shuffling countermeasures, showing simplified variants can be as weak as unprotected implementations.
- **76580746.pdf** — Theory and Practice of a Leakage Resilient Masking Scheme (Balasch, Faust, Gierlichs, Verbauwhede). Shows inner-product masking combines provable leakage resilience with practical efficiency, demonstrated on 8-bit AES.
- **77780001.pdf** — Packed Ciphertexts in LWE-based Homomorphic Encryption (Brakerski, Gentry, Halevi). Observes PVW packing enables SIMD homomorphic operations for plain LWE schemes, complementing ring-LWE CRT packing.
- **77780014.pdf** — Feasibility and Infeasibility of Adaptively Secure Fully Homomorphic Encryption (Katz, Thiruvengadam, Zhou). Proves compact FHE cannot be adaptively secure, but gives a non-compact adaptively secure FHE with circuit privacy.
- **77780032.pdf** — Chosen Ciphertext Secure Keyed-Homomorphic Public-Key Encryption (Emura, Hanaoka, Ohtake, Matsuda, Yamada). Introduces keyed-homomorphic PKE, restricting homomorphic evaluation to key holders so CCA security becomes achievable.
- **77780050.pdf** — Functional Encryption: Origins and Recent Developments (Waters). Short invited-talk abstract surveying functional encryption and multilinear-map-based circuit functionality; not a full research paper.
- **77780054.pdf** — Vector Commitments and their Applications (Catalano, Fiore). Defines concise position-binding vector commitments from RSA/CDH, with applications to verifiable databases and more.
- **77780072.pdf** — Efficient, Adaptively Secure, and Composable Oblivious Transfer with a Single, Global CRS (Choi, Katz, Wee, Zhou). UC-secure OT framework reusing one global CRS, round-optimal under DLIN/SXDH with adaptive security.
- **77780089.pdf** — Cryptography Using Captcha Puzzles (Kumarasubramanian, Ostrovsky, Pandey, Wadia). Formalizes Captcha-based cryptography, yielding constant-round concurrent non-malleable ZK via human-extractable Captchas.
- **77780107.pdf** — Improved Zero-knowledge Proofs of Knowledge for the ISIS Problem, and Applications (Ling, Nguyen, Stehlé, Wang). Closes the extraction gap in ISIS infinity-norm proofs via generalized Stern protocols, enabling weaker assumptions for lattice schemes.
- **77780125.pdf** — Decentralized Attribute-Based Signatures (Okamoto, Takashima). First multi-authority ABS with no central authority or trusted setup, fully secure under DLIN in the random oracle model.
- **77780143.pdf** — On the Semantic Security of Functional Encryption Schemes (Barbosa, Farshim). Revisits FE security definitions, fixes shortcomings, and derives new feasibility/impossibility results for adaptive token extraction.
- **77780161.pdf** — Attribute-Based Encryption with Fast Decryption (Hohenberger, Waters). First key-policy ABE with constant-pairing decryption (2 pairings for GPSW ciphertexts) plus tunable efficiency tradeoffs.
- **77780178.pdf** — Recovering RSA Secret Keys from Noisy Key Bits with Erasures and Errors (Kunihiro, Shinohara, Izu). Polynomial-time RSA key recovery from side-channel/cold-boot leakage containing both erasures and errors, with success-condition analysis.
- **77780196.pdf** — Combined Attack on CRT-RSA — Why Public Verification Must Not Be Public? (Barbu et al.). Combines fault injection with side-channel analysis on the verification countermeasure to factor the modulus and recover the RSA private key.
- **77780214.pdf** — Revocable Identity-Based Encryption Revisited: Security Model and Construction (Seo, Emura). Adds decryption-key-exposure security to revocable IBE, showing prior schemes fail it, and gives the first scalable resistant construction.
- **77780232.pdf** — Improved (Hierarchical) Inner-Product Encryption from Lattices (Xagawa). Lattice IPE over GF(q^n) with much smaller parameters than AFV, plus hierarchical IPE and fuzzy IBE extensions.
- **77780250.pdf** — Techniques for Efficient Secure Computation Based on Yao's Protocol (Lindell). One-page talk abstract surveying recent practical garbled-circuit techniques; not a full research paper.
- **77780251.pdf** — Non-Interactive Key Exchange (Freire, Hofheinz, Kiltz, Paterson). Revives NIKE with security models, factoring- and pairing-based constructions, and a generic NIKE-to-IND-CCA-PKE transform.
- **77780269.pdf** — Efficient UC-Secure Authenticated Key-Exchange for Algebraic Languages (Ben Hamouda, Blazy, Chevalier, Pointcheval, Vergnaud). General LAKE framework unifying PAKE and secret handshakes, UC-secure via smooth projective hashing on pairing languages.
- **77780289.pdf** — Tighter Reductions for Forward-Secure Signature Schemes (Abdalla, Ben Hamouda, Pointcheval). Gives factoring-based Fiat-Shamir and forward-secure signatures tight reductions to decisional assumptions via lossy identification.
- **77780309.pdf** — Tagged One-Time Signatures: Tight Security and Optimal Tag Size (Abe, David, Kohlweiss, Nishimaki, Ohkubo). Structure-preserving tagged one-time signatures with tight DLIN security, yielding record-short 14-element signatures and tightly secure PKE.
- **77780329.pdf** — Key Encapsulation Mechanisms from Extractable Hash Proof Systems, Revisited (Matsuda, Hanaoka). Relaxes Wee's ABO-XHPS framework to explain more CCA-secure KEMs and adds constrained-CCA variants.
- **77780348.pdf** — Robust Encryption, Revisited (Farshim, Libert, Paterson, Quaglia). Shows even strongly robust PKE leaves Sako's auction protocol vulnerable, and introduces complete robustness with constructions.
- **77780365.pdf** — Sender-Equivocable Encryption Schemes Secure against Chosen-Ciphertext Attacks Revisited (Huang, Liu, Qin). Breaks the NC-CCA security proof of Fehr et al.'s sender-equivocable scheme by exploiting exposed challenge randomness against its cross-authentication code.
- **77780382.pdf** — Efficient Completely Context-Hiding Quotable and Linearly Homomorphic Signatures (Attrapadung, Libert, Peters). First completely context-hiding quotable and linearly homomorphic signature schemes in the standard model, with linear-size signatures.
- **77780400.pdf** — Verifiably Encrypted Signatures with Short Keys based on the Decisional Linear Problem and Obfuscation for Encrypted VES (Nishimaki, Xagawa). First efficient VES with short keys under standard DLIN assumption, plus ES/EVES obfuscators.
- **77780420.pdf** — Sequential Aggregate Signatures with Short Public Keys: Design, Analysis and Implementation Studies (Lee, Lee, Yung). Sequential aggregate signatures with constant-size public keys in the standard model, answering an open problem of Lu et al.
- **77780440.pdf** — New Constructions and Applications of Trapdoor DDH Groups (Seurin). New trapdoor DDH group constructions from composite residuosity/RSA/factoring, applied to convertible undeniable signatures.
- **77780458.pdf** — Rate-Limited Secure Function Evaluation: Definitions and Constructions (Dagdelen, Mohassel, Venturi). Defines RL-SFE letting parties privately meter counterpart input usage across SFE executions, with compilers from commit-first SFE.
- **77780476.pdf** — Verifiable Elections That Scale for Free (Chase, Kohlweiss, Lysyanskaya, Meiklejohn). Verifiable shuffle and threshold decryption with proof size O(k(L+M+N)) instead of O(kLM+kLN), making large elections verifiable.
- **77780494.pdf** — On the Connection between Leakage Tolerance and Adaptive Security (Nielsen, Venturi, Zottarel). Proves tolerating even poly-log leakage essentially requires full adaptive security, and relates multi-party leakage to semi-adaptivity.
- **77850001.pdf** — Overcoming Weak Expectations (Dodis, Yu). An elementary inequality bounding expectations over weak sources, simplifying non-malleable extractors, leakage-resilient encryption, and leftover-hash-lemma bounds.
- **77850023.pdf** — A Counterexample to the Chain Rule for Conditional HILL Entropy (Krenn, Pietrzak, Wadia). Proves no meaningful chain rule exists for conditional HILL entropy under one-way permutations, with implications for deniable encryption.
- **77850040.pdf** — Hardness Preserving Reductions via Cuckoo Hashing (Berman, Haitner, Komargodaki, Naor). Uses cuckoo hashing to beat the birthday barrier in PRF domain extension and non-adaptive-to-adaptive PRF transforms.
- **77850060.pdf** — Concurrent Zero Knowledge in the Bounded Player Model (Goyal, Jain, Ostrovsky, Richelson, Visconti). Introduces the bounded-player model, achieving sub-logarithmic-round concurrent ZK, while showing concurrent secure computation remains impossible there.
- **77850080.pdf** — Public-Coin Concurrent Zero-Knowledge in the Global Hash Model (Canetti, Lin, Paneth). First public-coin concurrent ZK for NP in the global-hash-function model, with O(log^{1+eps} n) rounds.
- **77850100.pdf** — Succinct Malleable NIZKs and an Application to Compact Shuffles (Chase, Kohlweiss, Lysyanskaya, Meiklejohn). Generic controlled-malleable proofs from SNARGs supporting general transformations, applied to compact verifiable shuffles.
- **77850120.pdf** — Encrypted Messages from the Heights of Cryptomania (Gentry). Two-page invited-talk essay contrasting FHE's computation flexibility with access-control flexibility of ABE/witness encryption; not a full research paper.
- **77850122.pdf** — Attribute-Based Functional Encryption on Lattices (Boyen). Lattice-based key-policy ABE for access structures, selectively secure under LWE in the standard model via a new lattice manipulation technique.
- **77850142.pdf** — When Homomorphism Becomes a Liability (Brakerski). Shows majority-homomorphic schemes cannot have weakly-learnable decryption, ruling out low-noise LPN-style FHE and breaking the Bogdanov–Lee candidate.
- **77850161.pdf** — Garbling XOR Gates "For Free" in the Standard Model (Applebaum). Realizes free-XOR garbled circuits without random oracles, under LPN via RK/KDM-secure symmetric encryption.
