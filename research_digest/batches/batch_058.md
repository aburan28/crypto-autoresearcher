# Batch 058 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by 2002-era conference papers: the 2442xxxx series is Crypto 2002, the 2501xxxx series appears to be Asiacrypt 2002; a classic early-2000s provable-security vintage.
- Recurring themes: block/stream cipher design and cryptanalysis (AES algebraic structure, tweakable block ciphers, RC4 shuffles, SNOW/Scream, Boolean functions), and the limits of provable security (proof flaws in ESIGN/ECDSA, RO-vs-CT separation, generic-group-model weaknesses).
- ECC/pairing content relevant to the corpus: supersingular abelian varieties (Rubin–Silverberg), fast Tate pairing algorithms (Barreto et al.), zeta functions of hyperelliptic curves in char 2, XTR/LUC bit security via the hidden number problem, and Wagner's generalized birthday attack including EC blind signatures.
- Strong RSA/factoring cluster: Bernstein's factorization circuit analysis, unbalanced RSA with small CRT exponent, RSA padding (PSS, OAEP variants), TLS-RSA security.
- Anomaly: 24.pdf is garbled mojibake (broken font/encoding), not a readable research paper; no summary possible.

## Papers
- **23650264.pdf** — Compression and Information Leakage of Plaintext (John Kelsey). Shows lossless compression leaks plaintext information via output length, an algorithmic side-channel enabling surprisingly powerful attacks.
- **24.pdf** — Not a readable research paper: extract is garbled mojibake from broken font encoding; title and content unrecoverable. Anomaly.
- **24420001.pdf** — Essential Algebraic Structure Within the AES (Sean Murphy, Matthew Robshaw). Defines the BES cipher so AES can be expressed purely over GF(2^8), yielding a sparse overdetermined MQ system whose solution recovers the key.
- **24420017.pdf** — Blockwise-Adaptive Attackers: Revisiting the (In)security of Some Provably Secure Encryption Modes (Antoine Joux, Gwenaëlle Martinet, Frédéric Valette). Shows chunk-wise message processing breaks provably secure modes like CBC and IACBC via blockwise-adaptive attacks.
- **24420031.pdf** — Tweakable Block Ciphers (Moses Liskov, Ronald Rivest, David Wagner). Proposes block ciphers with a third "tweak" input, simplifying design and proofs of modes of operation.
- **24420061.pdf** — Dynamic Accumulators and Application to Efficient Revocation of Anonymous Credentials (Jan Camenisch, Anna Lysyanskaya). Introduces dynamic accumulators with constant-cost add/delete under strong RSA, enabling efficient credential and group-signature revocation.
- **24420077.pdf** — Provably Secure Steganography (Nicholas Hopper, John Langford, Luis von Ahn). Gives complexity-theoretic definitions of steganographic security and shows one-way functions imply secure steganography.
- **24420093.pdf** — Flaws in Applying Proof Methodologies to Signature Schemes (Jacques Stern, David Pointcheval, John Malone-Lee, Nigel Smart). Exhibits gaps in the ESIGN security proof and duplicate-signature attacks on ECDSA, cautioning that proofs need public scrutiny.
- **24420111.pdf** — Separating Random Oracle Proofs from Complexity Theoretic Proofs: The Non-committing Encryption Case (Jesper Buus Nielsen). Shows non-interactive non-committing encryption exists in the RO model but not the standard model, via RO programmability.
- **24420127.pdf** — On the Security of RSA Encryption in TLS (Jakob Jonsson, Burton Kaliski). Relates TLS RSA-handshake security to RSA inversion with a partial-RSA decision oracle, extending to tagged key-encapsulation.
- **24420143.pdf** — Security Analysis of IKE's Signature-based Key-Exchange Protocol (Ran Canetti, Hugo Krawczyk). Proves IKE's signature-authenticated DH exchange secure in a new "post-specified peer" model hiding identities.
- **24420162.pdf** — GQ and Schnorr Identification Schemes: Proofs of Security against Impersonation under Active and Concurrent Attacks (Mihir Bellare, Adriana Palacio). First proofs of GQ and Schnorr ID security under active/concurrent attack, from one-more-RSA and one-more-DL assumptions.
- **24420178.pdf** — On 2-Round Secure Multiparty Computation (Rosario Gennaro, Yuval Ishai, Eyal Kushilevitz, Tal Rabin). Proves 3 rounds is the exact round complexity for general secure computation; identifies a class of functions computable in 2 rounds.
- **24420194.pdf** — Private Computation: k-connected versus 1-connected Networks (Markus Bläser, Andreas Jakoby, Maciej Liśkiewicz, Bodo Siebert). Characterizes which functions admit information-theoretic private computation on non-2-connected networks, with randomness bounds.
- **24420210.pdf** — Analysis and Improvements of NTRU Encryption Paddings (Phong Nguyen, David Pointcheval). Shows one NTRU padding is not even IND-CPA; proves others IND-CCA2 only under stronger assumptions and proposes fixes under the original assumption.
- **24420226.pdf** — Universal Padding Schemes for RSA (Jean-Sébastien Coron, Marc Joye, David Naccache, Pascal Paillier). Shows PSS works for encryption too, allowing one RSA key pair for both encryption and signature concurrently.
- **24420242.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Unbalanced RSA with Small CRT-Exponent (Alexander May). Lattice-based Coppersmith attacks factoring unbalanced RSA moduli when the CRT exponent is small, up to q < N^0.382.
- **24420257.pdf** — Hyper-Encryption against Space-Bounded Adversaries from On-Line Strong Extractors (Chi-Jen Lu). Builds bounded-storage-model encryption with everlasting security using on-line strong randomness extractors.
- **24420272.pdf** — Optimal Black-Box Secret Sharing over Arbitrary Abelian Groups (Ronald Cramer, Serge Fehr). Constructs black-box secret sharing for threshold structures with near-optimal expansion factor, improving Desmedt–Frankel.
- **24420288.pdf** — A Generalized Birthday Problem (David Wagner). Gives subexponential k-list xor algorithms with applications: one-more forgeries on Schnorr/Okamoto–Schnorr blind signatures over elliptic curves, incremental hash breaks, correlation attacks.
- **24420305.pdf** — (Not So) Random Shuffles of RC4 (Ilya Mironov). Models RC4 via random-shuffle theory and recommends discarding at least 512 initial output bytes rather than 256.
- **24420321.pdf** — Black-Box Analysis of the Block-Cipher-Based Hash-Function Constructions from PGV (John Black, Phillip Rogaway, Thomas Shrimpton). Proves tight collision-resistance bounds for the 12 PGV-secure constructions and adds 8 more.
- **24420337.pdf** — Supersingular Abelian Varieties in Cryptology (Karl Rubin, Alice Silverberg). Determines achievable security parameters of supersingular abelian varieties and gives optimal constructions for pairing-based applications.
- **24420355.pdf** — Efficient Algorithms for Pairing-Based Cryptosystems (Paulo Barreto, Hae Kim, Ben Lynn, Michael Scott). Speeds Tate pairing evaluation ~55x in characteristic 3, reaching RSA-comparable performance in larger characteristics.
- **24420371.pdf** — Computing Zeta Functions of Hyperelliptic Curves over Finite Fields of Characteristic 2 (Frederik Vercauteren). Extends Kedlaya's point-counting algorithm to characteristic 2, O(g^5 n^3) time for genus-g curves over F_{2^n}.
- **24420387.pdf** — Threshold Password-Authenticated Key Exchange (Philip MacKenzie, Thomas Shrimpton, Markus Jakobsson). Distributes password verification across a server threshold so sub-threshold compromise prevents offline dictionary attacks; proven secure under DDH.
- **24420403.pdf** — A Threshold Pseudorandom Function Construction and Its Applications (Jesper Buus Nielsen). First practical threshold PRF, used to convert random-oracle Byzantine agreement into an efficient standard-model protocol.
- **24420419.pdf** — Efficient Computation Modulo a Shared Secret with Application to the Generation of Shared Safe-Prime Products (Joy Algesheimer, Jan Camenisch, Victor Shoup). Distributed protocols for modular computation and shared safe-prime generation, enabling efficient shared RSA key generation.
- **24420435.pdf** — Hidden Number Problem with the Trace and Bit Security of XTR and LUC (Wen-Ching Li, Mats Näslund, Igor Shparlinski). Uses exponential-sum bounds to prove the most significant bits of XTR secrets and hard-core bits of LUC are secure.
- **24420467.pdf** — Threshold Ring Signatures and Applications to Ad-hoc Groups (Emmanuel Bresson, Jacques Stern, Michael Szydlo). Weakens assumptions for ring signatures and gives an efficient provably secure threshold ring signature scheme.
- **24420483.pdf** — Deniable Ring Authentication (Moni Naor). Combines deniable authentication with ring signatures: source-hidden, no-paper-trail authentication from any strong encryption scheme, with threshold extensions.
- **24420501.pdf** — SiBIR: Signer-Base Intrusion-Resilient Signatures (Gene Itkis, Leonid Reyzin). Generalizes forward-secure and key-insulated signatures; stays secure after arbitrarily many non-simultaneous compromises, from strong RSA.
- **24420517.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Stream Ciphers with Linear Masking (Don Coppersmith, Shai Halevi, Charanjit Jutla). Distinguishing technique for ciphers combining nonlinear and linear processes; applied to SNOW (~2^95 words) and Scream-0 (~2^43 bytes).
- **24420534.pdf** — The Filter-Combiner Model for Memoryless Synchronous Stream Ciphers (Palash Sarkar). New stream-cipher model combining combiner and filter generators for optimal correlation-attack resistance, realizable with Boolean functions and cellular automata.
- **24420550.pdf** — A Larger Class of Cryptographic Boolean Functions via a Study of the Maiorana-McFarland Construction (Claude Carlet). Characterizes Maiorana–McFarland resilient functions' nonlinearity, notes a weakness, and defines a superclass with high nonlinearity.
- **24420566.pdf** — Linear VSS and Distributed Commitments Based on Secret Sharing and Pairwise Checks (Serge Fehr, Ueli Maurer). Unified linear-algebraic treatment of information-theoretic VSS and distributed commitment schemes, covering all known constructions.
- **24420583.pdf** — Perfect Hiding and Perfect Binding Universally Composable Commitment Schemes with Constant Expansion Factor (Ivan Damgård, Jesper Buus Nielsen). First UC commitments with constant expansion, from Paillier, Okamoto–Uchiyama, or DDH.
- **24420599.pdf** — Unique Signatures and Verifiable Random Functions from the DH-DDH Separation (Anna Lysyanskaya). Constructs unique signatures and VRFs in groups where DDH is easy but CDH is hard, without RSA-type assumptions.
- **24420615.pdf** — Security Proof for Partial-Domain Hash Signature Schemes (Jean-Sébastien Coron). Proves Rabin-based partial-domain hash signatures secure in the RO model when the hash exceeds 2/3 of modulus size, covering ISO 9796-2/PKCS#1 v1.5 variants.
- **248.pdf** — Spreading Alerts Quietly and the Subgroup Escape Problem (James Aspnes, Zoë Diamadi, Kristian Gjøsteen, René Peralta, Aleksandr Yampolskiy). Introduces AND-homomorphic blind coupon commitments for undetectable alert propagation; security rests on a new subgroup escape problem on pairing groups and EC over Z_n.
- **25.pdf** — Offline Hardware/Software Authentication for Reconfigurable Platforms (Eric Simpson, Patrick Schaumont). Lightweight mutual authentication of FPGA IP modules and platforms using a symmetric cipher and PUF, with no non-volatile storage.
- **25010001.pdf** — Analysis of Bernstein's Factorization Circuit (Arjen Lenstra, Adi Shamir, Jim Tomlinson, Eran Tromer). Re-evaluates Bernstein's NFS matrix-step circuits, finding only 1.17x (not 3.01x) larger factorable integers; practical RSA security rests on relation collection.
- **25010027.pdf** — A Variant of the Cramer–Shoup Cryptosystem for Groups of Unknown Order (Stefan Lucks). Extends Cramer–Shoup to quadratic residues mod composite N, CCA-secure under DDH over QR_N and factoring.
- **25010046.pdf** — Looking beyond XTR (Wieb Bosma, James Hutton, Eric Verheul). Tests the Brouwer–Pellikaan–Verheul conjecture on compact trace-based representations in degree-30 extension fields; experiments suggest it likely fails.
- **25010064.pdf** — Bounds for Robust Metering Schemes and Their Relationship with A^2-codes (Wakaha Ogata, Kaoru Kurosawa). Derives tight lower bounds on communication and secret size for robust web-metering schemes via near-equivalence with multi-use A^2-codes.
- **25010081.pdf** — Unconditionally Secure Anonymous Encryption and Group Authentication (Goichiro Hanaoka, Junji Shikata, Yumiko Hanaoka, Hideki Imai). Defines unconditionally secure asymmetric encryption hiding sender identity, with tight memory bounds and polynomial-based constructions.
- **25010099.pdf** — Adapting the Weaknesses of the Random Oracle Model to the Generic Group Model (Alexander Dent). Constructs schemes provably secure in the generic group model but insecure under any concrete group encoding, mirroring RO-model counterexamples.
- **25010109.pdf** — On the Impossibilities of Basing One-Way Permutations on Central Cryptographic Primitives (Yan-Cheng Chang, Chun-Yun Hsiao, Chi-Jen Lu). Shows no black-box reductions from one-way permutations to trapdoor functions or private information retrieval, completing the OWP relationship map.
- **25010124.pdf** — A Statistically-Hiding Integer Commitment Scheme based on Groups with Hidden Order (Ivan Damgård, Eiichiro Fujisaki). Fixes proof gaps in Fujisaki–Okamoto integer commitments over hidden-order groups, with efficient ZK protocols for multiplicative relations.
- **25010142.pdf** — Efficient Oblivious Transfer in the Bounded-Storage Model (Dowon Hong, Ku-Young Chang, Heuisu Ryu). Efficient unconditionally secure 1-out-of-N OT against storage-bounded receivers using extended interactive hashing.
