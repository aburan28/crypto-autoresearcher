# Batch 020 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ASIACRYPT 2019 proceedings-style papers (long 26–32 page LNCS formats, single-affiliation author blocks), with a few from EUROCRYPT/CRYPTO 2019 era.
- Strong symmetric-key cryptanalysis and side-channel/leakage cluster: MILP division property, AES exchange attack, A5/1 GSM break, S-box anomaly analysis, duplex/sponge leakage resilience, ISO 17825 TVLA critique, location-based SCA.
- Heavy post-quantum coverage: isogeny crypto (CSIDH/CSI-FiSh, SIDH/SIKE key compression, isogeny VDFs, AKE, Edwards isogenies), lattice crypto (Order-LWE, module-LLL, quantum sieving), LAC attack, code-based signatures and ZK.
- MPC/advanced-protocol cluster: fair/robust MPC round complexity, broadcast message complexity, MCFE/inner-product FE, UC frameworks and commitments, private set union, card-based crypto formal verification.
- Notable papers: De Feo et al. VDFs from isogenies and pairings; Beullens et al. CSI-FiSh with record 154-digit class group; Bardeh–Rønjom first 6-round AES distinguisher; Zhang's rainbow-table-free A5/1 attack.
- No anomalies: all 50 are genuine research-paper first pages; no duplicates detected.

## Papers
- **119210170.pdf** — MILP-aided Method of Searching Division Property Using Three Subsets and Applications (Wang, Hu, Guan, Zhang, Shi). First MILP modeling of three-subset division property with pruning/fast propagation; finds improved integral distinguishers for SIMON64, PRESENT, RECTANGLE, LBlock.
- **119210172.pdf** — How to Correct Errors in Multi-Server PIR (Kaoru Kurosawa). Gives an efficient O(ℓ³) decoding algorithm for b-error-correcting ℓ-server information-theoretic PIR, replacing an inefficient prior decoding procedure.
- **119210173.pdf** — Optimized Method for Computing Odd-Degree Isogenies on Edwards Curves (Kim, Yoon, Park, Hong). New w-coordinate isogeny formulas on Edwards curves beating Montgomery for degree ≥5; CSIDH implementation ~20% faster.
- **119210176.pdf** — Leakage Resilience of the Duplex Construction (Dobraunig, Mennink). Models leakage-resilient duplexing and derives fine-grained security bounds for keyed duplex, mapping to existing side-channel-secure duplex designs.
- **119210182.pdf** — Valiant's Universal Circuits Revisited: an Overall Improvement and a Lower Bound (Zhao, Yu, Zhang, Liu). Computer-aided 4-way supernode of size 18 gives EUG size 4.5n log n, ~5% smaller universal circuits after 40 years.
- **119210187.pdf** — A Novel CCA Attack using Decryption Errors against LAC (Guo, Johansson, Yang). Secret-key recovery attack exploiting decryption failures in error-correcting-code LWE schemes; breaks NIST PQC round-2 candidate LAC.
- **119210188.pdf** — Structure-Preserving Signatures on Equivalence Classes From Standard Assumptions (Khalili, Slamanig, Dakhilalian). First EUF-CMA-secure SPS-EQ under standard assumptions, beyond generic-group-model constructions; shows weakness of prior weaker variant.
- **119210190.pdf** — The Exchange Attack: How to Distinguish Six Rounds of AES with 2^88.2 Chosen Plaintexts (Bardeh, Rønjom). New exchange-equivalence technique yields first secret-key 6-round AES distinguisher and record 2^30 5-round attack.
- **119210197.pdf** — Forkcipher: a New Primitive for Authenticated Encryption of Very Short Messages (Andreeva et al.). Formalizes forkcipher primitive expanding n bits to 2n, with iterate-fork-iterate design and ForkSkinny instance targeting short-message AEAD (IoT, CAN FD).
- **119210204.pdf** — Quantum Random Oracle Model with Auxiliary Input (Hhan, Xagawa, Yamakawa). Introduces QROM-AI capturing both quantum queries and preprocessing; proves bounds for OWFs, PRGs, PRFs, MACs in this model.
- **119210208.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of GSM Encryption in 2G/3G Networks without Rainbow Tables (Bin Zhang). Fast near-collision attack on A5/1 recovering key in 2^31.79 ticks from 64 keystream bits, 1 MB memory, tiny precomputation—no rainbow tables.
- **119210215.pdf** — Simple and Efficient KDM-CCA Secure Public Key Encryption (Kitagawa, Matsuda, Tanaka). Two KDM-CCA PKE schemes (affine and polynomial function classes) from DCR plus IND-CCA PKE, with tight proofs and minimal overhead.
- **119210228.pdf** — Multi-Client Functional Encryption for Linear Functions in the Standard Model from LWE (Libert, Tiţiu). First standard-model, adaptive-corruption MCFE for linear functions, proved under LWE, removing random oracles of prior DDH schemes.
- **119210231.pdf** — Algebraic Cryptanalysis of STARK-Friendly Designs: Application to MARVELlous and MiMC (Albrecht et al.). Gröbner basis attacks break Jarvis/Friday round counts; MiMC resists due to different algebraic structure.
- **119210235.pdf** — Quisquis: A New Design for Anonymous Cryptocurrencies (Fauzi, Meiklejohn, Mercer, Orlandi). Provably anonymous cryptocurrency with concise blockchain state, no trusted setup, addresses used at most twice, via DDH-based updatable keys.
- **119210237.pdf** — Structure-Preserving and Re-randomizable RCCA-secure Public Key Encryption and its Applications (Faonio, Fiore, Herranz, Ràfols). Perfectly re-randomizable structure-preserving Rand-RCCA PKE in Type-3 pairing groups; applications to malleable NIZKs and efficient UC mix-nets.
- **119210252.pdf** — iUC: Flexible Universal Composability Made Simple (Camenisch, Krenn, Kusters, Rausch). New UC framework combining soundness, flexibility, and usability, fixing modeling burdens of UC/GNUC/IITM.
- **119210255.pdf** — Efficient UC Commitment Extension with Homomorphism for Free (and Applications) (Cascudo, Damgård, David, Döttling, Dowsley, Giacomelli). Near-optimal additively homomorphic UC commitments from any extractable commitment, rate 1, no OT; extends to multiplicative homomorphism.
- **119210265.pdf** — Strongly Secure Authenticated Key Exchange from Supersingular Isogenies (Xu, Xue, Wang, Au, Tian). First CK+-secure AKEs from supersingular isogenies via double-key PKE/KEM constructions, answering Galbraith's open problem.
- **119210266.pdf** — Location, location, location: Revisiting Modeling and Exploitation for Location-based Side Channel Leakages (Andrikos et al.). Spatial leakage model for near-field microprobe attacks plus information-theoretic evaluation of scenarios and countermeasures.
- **119210269.pdf** — Anomalies and Vector Space Search: Tools for S-Box Analysis (Bonnetain, Perrin, Tian). Introduces "anomaly" metrics quantifying S-box randomness, including first boomerang connectivity table statistics, plus vector-space search algorithm for structure recovery.
- **119210271.pdf** — A Critical Analysis of ISO 17825 (Whitnall, Oswald). Statistical critique of ISO/IEC 17825's TVLA-only side-channel conformance testing, finding grave issues with leakage-detection-only certification.
- **119210272.pdf** — Decisional Second-Preimage Resistance: When Does SPR Imply PRE? (Bernstein, Hülsing). Fills SPR→PRE gap for length-preserving hashes, enabling tight non-interactive reductions for multi-target preimage problems in hash-based signatures.
- **119210274.pdf** — CSI-FiSh: Efficient Isogeny based Signatures through Class Group Computations (Beullens, Kleinjung, Vercauteren). Record 154-digit class group computation enables practical CSIDH-based signatures: 263-byte signatures, 390 ms, 300× faster than SeaSign.
- **119210279.pdf** — An LLL Algorithm for Module Lattices (Lee, Pellet-Mary, Stehlé, Wallet). Generalizes LLL to modules over number-field rings, reducing rank-n to rank-2 and rank-2 to a quantum CVP oracle.
- **119210284.pdf** — Order-LWE and the Hardness of Ring-LWE with Entropic Secrets (Bolboceanu, Brakerski, Perlman, Sharma). Defines Order-LWE with worst-case hardness, yielding new PLWE analysis and RLWE hardness for non-uniform/entropic secrets.
- **119210289.pdf** — Simple Refreshing in the Noisy Leakage Model (Dziembowski, Faust, Żebrowski). Proves a very simple, efficient masking refresh scheme secure in the noisy leakage model with new analysis methodology.
- **119210294.pdf** — Algebraic XOR-RKA-Secure Pseudorandom Functions from Post-Zeroizing Multilinear Maps (Abdalla, Benhamouda, Passelègue). First RKA-secure PRF for XOR key relations, a feasibility result built on multilinear maps.
- **119210296.pdf** — Beyond Honest Majority: The Round Complexity of Fair and Robust Multi-party Computation (Patra, Ravi). Tight ⌈n/2⌉+1 round bounds for fair and robust MPC under dynamic/boundary corruption settings allowing majority dishonesty.
- **119210298.pdf** — Quantum Attacks without Superposition Queries: the Offline Simon's Algorithm (Bonnetain, Hosoyamada, Naya-Plasencia, Sasaki, Schrottenloher). New offline Simon-based quantum algorithm with classical queries breaks Even-Mansour in 2^(n/3) quantum time and data.
- **119210313.pdf** — Shorter Pairing-based Arguments under Standard Assumptions (González, Ràfols). Non-interactive arguments for circuits with O(d)-element proofs from falsifiable assumptions, mixing SNARK and QA-NIZK techniques.
- **119210315.pdf** — From Single-Input to Multi-Client Inner-Product Functional Encryption (Abdalla, Benhamouda, Gay). Generic standard-model MCFE for inner products from single-input FE plus PRFs; instantiable from DDH, LWE, Paillier; decentralized variant.
- **119210319.pdf** — 4-Round Luby-Rackoff Construction is a qPRP (Hosoyamada, Iwata). Proves 4-round Feistel secure against quantum superposition CPAs up to O(2^(n/12)) queries, with matching distinguishing upper bound.
- **119210321.pdf** — Verifiable Delay Functions from Supersingular Isogenies and Pairings (De Feo, Masson, Petit, Sanso). Two new VDF constructions from isogeny/pairing assumptions with proof-of-concept implementation showing practicality.
- **119210324.pdf** — Homomorphic Encryption for Finite Automata (Genise, Gentry, Halevi, Li, Micciancio). GSW-like matrix-encrypting scheme for fast encrypted NFA evaluation; security from new iNTRU assumption; studies NFA minimization for regexes.
- **119210335.pdf** — QFactory: Classically-Instructed Remote Secret Qubits Preparation (Cojocaru, Colisson, Kashefi, Wallden). Malicious-server-secure protocol letting a classical client have a quantum server prepare secret BB84 qubits; security from LWE.
- **119210336.pdf** — Dual Isogenies and Their Application to Public-key Compression for Isogeny-based Cryptography (Naehrig, Renes). Dual-isogeny speedups roughly halve public-key compression overhead for SIDH/SIKE, mainly via faster pairing computation.
- **119210340.pdf** — Sponges Resist Leakage: The Case of Authenticated Encryption (Degabriele, Janson, Struck). Reduces leakage-resilient AEAD to leakage-resilient FIL function families; instantiates with T-sponge (Slae), proven secure against non-adaptive leakage.
- **119210350.pdf** — Scalable Private Set Union from Symmetric-Key Techniques (Kolesnikov, Rosulek, Trieu, Wang). OT-hybrid, symmetric-key-only PSU protocol built on new reverse private membership test; handles 2^20-element sets in ~238 s single-threaded.
- **119210352.pdf** — Wave: A New Family of Trapdoor One-Way Preimage Sampleable Functions Based on Codes (Debris, Sendrier, Tillich). Code-based trapdoor PSA functions from generalized (U,U+V)-codes, yielding EUF-CMA hash-and-sign code-based signatures in ROM.
- **119210354.pdf** — Fine-Grained Cryptography Revisited (Egashira, Wang, Tanaka). Constructs fine-grained one-way permutations, hash proof systems (implying CCA PKE), and trapdoor OWFs in NC1 secure against NC1 under NC1 ⊊ L/poly.
- **119210361.pdf** — The Local Forking Lemma and its Application to Deterministic Encryption (Bellare, Dai, Li). New single-point-reprogramming forking lemma proves Encrypt-with-Hash message-recovery security for public-key-dependent deterministic encryption, bypassing impossibility results.
- **119210362.pdf** — The Broadcast Message Complexity of Secure Multiparty Computation (Garg, Goel, Jain). Tight upper/lower bounds on broadcast message complexity of MPC for every corruption threshold, showing non-simultaneous broadcast beats 2n messages.
- **119210375.pdf** — New Code-Based Privacy-Preserving Cryptographic Constructions (Nguyen, Tang, Wang, Zeng). First code-based statistically hiding commitments, logarithmic ZK range arguments, and set-membership proofs, advancing code-based privacy tools.
- **119210379.pdf** — Rate-1 Trapdoor Functions from the Diffie-Hellman Problem (Döttling, Garg, Hajiabadi, Liu, Malavolta). Rate-1 TDF from CDH and simpler rate-1 block-source-secure deterministic PKE from DDH, with implementation.
- **119210383.pdf** — Anonymous AE (Chan, Rogaway). Formalizes anonymous nonce-based AE (anAE) where ciphertexts conceal origin despite carrying decryption metadata; proves efficient NonceWrap construction from nAE plus blockcipher.
- **119210385.pdf** — Public-Key Function-Private Hidden Vector Encryption (and More) (Bartusek et al.). Public-key function-private predicate encryption for small-superset functionality, answering Boneh–Raghunathan–Segev's open HVE problem; covers point functions, conjunctions, d-CNFs.
- **119210391.pdf** — Quantum Algorithms for the Approximate k-List Problem and their Application to Lattice Sieving (Kirshanova, Mårtensson, Postlethwaite, Roy Moulik). Quantum k-Sieve in QRAM model solves SVP in 2^0.2989d time with 2^0.1395d memory, trading time for memory.
- **119210399.pdf** — Output Compression, MPC, and iO for Turing Machines (Badrinarayanan, Fernando, Koppula, Sahai, Waters). Output-compressing randomized encodings for TMs from iO plus LWE/DDH/Nth-residuosity; implies compact MPC and TM obfuscation feasibility results.
- **119210407.pdf** — Card-based Cryptography Meets Formal Verification (Koch, Schrempp, Kirsten). Uses software bounded model checking with SAT to find card-minimal protocols with distinct-symbol decks and prove tight lower bounds on card counts.
