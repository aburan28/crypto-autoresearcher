# Batch 059 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by two 2002–2003 conference proceedings: ASIACRYPT 2002 (2501xxxx files) and PKC 2003 (2567xxxx files); overwhelmingly public-key cryptography, signatures, and provable security.
- Strong block-cipher cryptanalysis cluster: Rijndael/AES dual ciphers and algebraic (XSL) attacks, differential-linear enhancements, boomerang attacks on SHACAL.
- Notable ECC relevance: side-channel/fault attacks on scalar multiplication (Goubin refined DPA, Izu–Takagi exceptional procedure attack, invalid-curve key-validation attacks), Montgomery arithmetic over GF(2^k), char-2 point counting, and Vaudenay's ECDSA curve-certification bypass.
- Pairing-based/ID-based crypto is prominent (Gentry–Silverberg HIBE, Cha–Cheon, Boldyreva GDH threshold/blind signatures, Zhang–Kim blind/ring signatures).
- Anomalies: 25010566 is an opinion/position paper (Crypto-Integrity), and 25010573 is a 2-page biometric fingerprint-spoofing note (gummy fingers) — off-topic for a crypto-theory corpus.
- Classic results present: Dodis VRFs, Groth's verifiable shuffle, Golle et al. exit-poll mixing, Courtois–Pieprzyk XSL, Klimov–Mityagin–Shamir break of neural key exchange.

## Papers
- **25010159.pdf** — In How Many Ways Can You Write Rijndael? (Barkan, Biham). Shows replacing Rijndael's constants (polynomial, MixColumn, S-box affine map) yields equivalent "dual ciphers," including square and logarithm variants, plus self-dual ciphers.
- **25010177.pdf** — On the Security of Rijndael-like Structures against Differential and Linear Cryptanalysis (Park, Sung, Chee, Yoon, Lim). Analyzes resistance of Rijndael-like SPN structures to differential and linear cryptanalysis via bounds on characteristic probabilities.
- **25010207.pdf** — Non-interactive Distributed-Verifier Proofs and Proving Relations among Commitments (Abe, Cramer, Fehr). Simplifies non-interactive commitment multiplication proofs using Pedersen VSS and extends to distributed-verifier proofs of partial knowledge.
- **25010225.pdf** — Asynchronous Secure Communication Tolerating Mixed Adversaries (Srinathan, Ashwin Kumar, Pandu Rangan). Characterizes network connectivity needed for perfect/unconditional secure communication in asynchronous networks with mixed adversaries and broadcast.
- **25010243.pdf** — Amplified Boomerang Attack Against Reduced-Round SHACAL (Kim, Moon, Lee, Hong, Lee, Jung). Uses short high-probability differentials to build a 36-step boomerang distinguisher, attacking 39-step (256-bit key) and 47-step (512-bit key) SHACAL.
- **25010254.pdf** — Enhancing Differential-Linear Cryptanalysis (Biham, Dunkelman, Keller). Generalizes differential-linear cryptanalysis to linear approximations with probability < 1, yielding best known attack on 9-round DES and breaking COCONUT98.
- **25010267.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Block Ciphers with Overdefined Systems of Equations (Courtois, Pieprzyk). Introduces the XSL algebraic attack exploiting sparse overdefined S-box equations in Serpent and Rijndael; argues security need not grow exponentially with rounds.
- **25010286.pdf** — Analysis of Neural Cryptography (Klimov, Mityagin, Shamir). Explains why mutually learning neural networks converge to a common key, then breaks the proposed key-exchange protocol three different ways, deeming it insecure.
- **25010297.pdf** — The Hardness of Hensel Lifting: The Case of RSA and Discrete Logarithm (Catalano, Nguyen, Stern). Fixes a flawed proof that one-wayness of the RSA-Paillier (RSAP) scheme equals RSA, using lattice-based Hensel-lifting arguments.
- **25010309.pdf** — A Comparison and a Combination of SST and AGM Algorithms for Counting Points of Elliptic Curves in Characteristic 2 (Gaudry). Improves the SST point-counting algorithm with AGM ideas and shows it outperforms AGM even at cryptographic sizes.
- **25010327.pdf** — A General Formula of the (t,n)-Threshold Visual Secret Sharing Scheme (Koga). Gives a general construction of basis matrices for (t,n) visual secret sharing via a bijection with homogeneous polynomials and lattice points.
- **25010345.pdf** — On Unconditionally Secure Robust Distributed Key Distribution Centers (D'Arco, Stinson). Builds an unconditionally secure distributed KDC robust against mobile adversaries, fixing proactive security of the Stinson–Wei VSS.
- **25010363.pdf** — Short Signatures in the Random Oracle Model (Granboulan). Proposes OPSSR padding achieving minimal message expansion for signatures with partial message recovery, with exact security proof in the ideal cipher model.
- **25010378.pdf** — The Provable Security of Graph-Based One-Time Signatures and Extensions to Algebraic Signature Schemes (Hevia, Micciancio). Gives standard-model security conditions for Bleichenbacher–Maurer graph-based one-time signatures and builds algebraic signature schemes.
- **25010396.pdf** — Transitive Signatures based on Factoring and RSA (Bellare, Neven). New transitive signature schemes under factoring/RSA assumptions, improving Micali–Rivest and answering their open question on the RSA-based scheme's security.
- **25010414.pdf** — 1-out-of-n Signatures from a Variety of Keys (Abe, Ohkubo, Suzuki). Constructs 1-out-of-n (ring-like) signatures mixing different key types (RSA and DL), more efficient and shorter than prior single-type schemes.
- **25010432.pdf** — A Revocation Scheme with Minimal Storage at Receivers (Asano). Two broadcast-encryption revocation methods using a-ary key trees for stateless receivers, minimizing receiver key storage (one key in Method 1).
- **25010450.pdf** — Optimistic Mixing for Exit-Polls (Golle, Zhong, Boneh, Jakobsson, Juels). Mix network that is fastest when all servers behave, converting inputs for slower backup mixing upon cheating; suited to instant election tallies.
- **25010465.pdf** — Improved Construction of Nonlinear Resilient S-Boxes (Gupta, Sarkar). Two constructions of resilient S-boxes: a modified Zhang–Zheng method beating Cheon's via the Griesmer bound, and a sharpened Maiorana–McFarland technique.
- **25010483.pdf** — An Upper Bound on the Number of m-Resilient Boolean Functions (Carlet, Gouget). New upper bound on the count of m-resilient Boolean functions in n variables, partially improving all previously known bounds.
- **25010497.pdf** — Group Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange Secure Against Dictionary Attacks (Bresson, Chevassut, Pointcheval). First formal security model and proven-secure password-based group key exchange resistant to dictionary attacks (random oracle and ideal cipher models).
- **25010515.pdf** — Secure Channels based on Authenticated Encryption Schemes: A Simple Characterization (Namprempre). Reduces Canetti–Krawczyk secure-channel security to two simple new security notions on the underlying authenticated encryption scheme.
- **25010533.pdf** — ID-Based Blind Signature and Ring Signature from Pairings (Zhang, Kim). First ID-based blind signature and ID-based ring signature schemes from bilinear pairings on elliptic/hyperelliptic curves, with security and efficiency analysis.
- **25010548.pdf** — Hierarchical ID-Based Cryptography (Gentry, Silverberg). Hierarchical ID-based encryption and signatures with total collusion resistance at arbitrary levels, CCA-secure in the random oracle model under BDH.
- **25010566.pdf** — Crypto-Integrity (Yung). Position paper arguing cryptographic components need built-in, real-time integrity assurance ("crypto-integrity") with minimal security/performance impact; reviews approaches and directions. Not a research-result paper.
- **25010573.pdf** — Gummy and Conductive Silicone Rubber Fingers — Importance of Vulnerability Analysis (Matsumoto). Short 2-page note urging vulnerability evaluation of fingerprint biometric systems against fake ("gummy") fingers. Off-topic short paper, not a crypto research paper.
- **25670001.pdf** — Efficient Construction of (Distributed) Verifiable Random Functions (Dodis). First simple efficient VRF construction (near Naor–Reingold PRF cost) and first distributed VRF, based on a new DDH variant in certain groups.
- **25670018.pdf** — An Identity-Based Signature from Gap Diffie-Hellman Groups (Cha, Cheon). ID-based signature provably secure in the random oracle model; with pairings it shares parameters with Boneh–Franklin IBE, giving a complete ID-based PKC.
- **25670031.pdf** — Threshold Signatures, Multisignatures and Blind Signatures Based on the Gap-Diffie-Hellman-Group Signature Scheme (Boldyreva). Builds robust proactive threshold, multi-, and blind signatures from BLS GDH signatures, simpler and more efficient than prior constructions.
- **25670047.pdf** — An Efficient Two-Party Public Key Cryptosystem Secure against Adaptive Chosen Ciphertext Attack (MacKenzie). Two-party shared Cramer–Shoup decryption, CCA2-secure under DDH in the standard model without random oracles or honest majority.
- **25670062.pdf** — On the Bit Security of NTRUEncrypt (Näslund, Shparlinski, Whyte). Shows that in natural computational models every bit of an NTRUEncrypt-encrypted message is as hard to recover as the whole message.
- **25670071.pdf** — Equivalence between Semantic Security and Indistinguishability against Chosen Ciphertext Attacks (Watanabe, Shikata, Imai). Introduces "non-dividability" and proves it equivalent to both semantic security and indistinguishability under any attack model, unifying the notions.
- **25670085.pdf** — Randomness Re-use in Multi-recipient Encryption Schemes (Bellare, Boldyreva, Staddon). Models new multi-recipient attacks and gives a general test determining when re-using randomness across recipients yields secure encryption.
- **25670100.pdf** — Public Key Trace and Revoke Scheme Secure against Adaptive Chosen Ciphertext Attack (Dodis, Fazio). First CCA2-secure and adaptively secure trace-and-revoke (broadcast encryption + traitor tracing) scheme, under DDH; also separates CCA2 from gCCA2.
- **25670116.pdf** — The Cramer-Shoup Strong-RSA Signature Scheme Revisited (Fischlin). Modifies Cramer–Shoup signatures for faster signing/verification and half-size signatures under strong RSA, plus stateful variant and group identification protocols.
- **25670130.pdf** — Strong Key-Insulated Signature Schemes (Dodis, Katz, Xu, Yung). Constructs strong (t,N)-key-insulated signature schemes limiting damage of key exposure on insecure devices, from various assumptions, with fixed public key.
- **25670145.pdf** — A Verifiable Secret Shuffle of Homomorphic Encryptions (Groth). Efficient honest-verifier zero-knowledge proof of correctness for shuffles of homomorphic encryptions; for ElGamal the proof is smaller than the ciphertexts, useful for mix-nets.
- **25670161.pdf** — Round-Optimal Contributory Conference Key Agreement (Boyd, González Nieto). First one-round contributory group key agreement protocol meeting the Becker–Wille bound, proven secure in the Bellare–Rogaway model, but without forward secrecy.
- **25670175.pdf** — Security Analysis of the MOR Cryptosystem (Tobias). Cryptanalyzes the MOR public-key scheme over SL(2,Z_p)⋊Z_p, giving attacks recovering plaintexts via the conjugacy problem without finding the secret key.
- **25670187.pdf** — A Practical Attack on Some Braid Group Based Cryptographic Primitives (Hofheinz, Steinwandt). Heuristic attack on the braid conjugacy problem showing many proposed braid-based key parameters are insecure, breakable in minutes on a PC.
- **25670199.pdf** — A Refined Power-Analysis Attack on Elliptic Curve Cryptosystems (Goubin). Shows random-projective-coordinates/curve-isomorphism DPA countermeasures are insufficient; new chosen-message power attack defeats them on many standardized curves.
- **25670211.pdf** — Validation of Elliptic Curve Public Keys (Antipa, Brown, Menezes, Struik, Vanstone). Practical invalid-curve attacks (combining small-subgroup and fault attack ideas) on EC key establishment when point validation is skipped; demonstrates on an IEEE 802.15 protocol.
- **25670224.pdf** — Exceptional Procedure Attack on Elliptic Curve Cryptosystems (Izu, Takagi). Attack exploiting errors from exceptional cases in EC addition formulas; infeasible on prime-order standard curves but feasible against Brier–Joye formulas.
- **25670240.pdf** — On Montgomery-Like Representations for Elliptic Curves over GF(2^k) (Stam). New x-coordinate-only representation for binary-field EC arithmetic with faster addition; concludes projective y-coordinate methods beat Montgomery forms on non-constrained devices.
- **25670254.pdf** — A Dedicated Sieving Hardware (Geiselmann, Steinwandt). Designs mesh-based special-purpose hardware for the sieving step of QS/NFS factoring; preliminary analysis suggests it could outperform TWINKLE for 512-bit numbers.
- **25670267.pdf** — A Fast and Secure Implementation of Sflash (Akkar, Courtois, Duteuil, Goubin). Optimized Sflash multivariate signature implementation on 8-bit smart cards (59 ms at 10 MHz) with DPA countermeasures; claimed fastest known signature scheme.
- **25670279.pdf** — A Practical Public Key Cryptosystem from Paillier and Rabin Schemes (Galindo, Martín, Morillo, Villar). IND-CPA encryption in the standard model with one-wayness based on factoring, via Rabin/Williams-style modification of RSA–Paillier; much faster than Okamoto–Uchiyama.
- **25670292.pdf** — A Lattice Based Public Key Cryptosystem Using Polynomial Representations (Paeng, Jung, Ha). GGH-style lattice cryptosystem using polynomial representations to shrink key sizes, aiming at a practical CVP-based scheme.
- **25670309.pdf** — The Security of DSA and ECDSA — Bypassing the Standard Elliptic Curve Certification Scheme (Vaudenay). Surveys DSA/ECDSA security and shows how to bypass ECDSA's curve-certification scheme to certify maliciously chosen curves (esp. characteristic two), proposing a fix.
