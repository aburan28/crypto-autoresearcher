# Batch 017 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by Eurocrypt 2019 (files 114760xxx) and Crypto 2019 (files 116940xxx) papers: MPC, zero-knowledge, secret sharing, leakage resilience, watermarking, and quantum/post-quantum cryptanalysis.
- Notable papers: zk-STARK ("Scalable Zero Knowledge with no Trusted Setup"), Libra, Gohr's deep-learning attack on Speck32/64, Zhandry's compressed-oracle QROM technique, and the Jaques–Schanck quantum claw-finding cost analysis of SIKE/SIDH.
- Corpus-relevant items (ECC/ECDLP-adjacent): Two-Party ECDSA from Hash Proof Systems; fixed-vs-random generator distinction in group-based assumptions (DL/CDH/DDH preprocessing bounds); quantum cryptanalysis of SIKE.
- Little elliptic-curve or ECDLP material overall; this batch skews toward general theoretical cryptography and symmetric/quantum cryptanalysis.
- Anomalies: two files (11535218, 11593447) are LNCS proceedings front-matter/editorial-board pages, not research papers; one file (116940179) is garbled hex/glyph-encoded extraction, unreadable.
- No obvious duplicates found.

## Papers
- **114760387.pdf** — Secret-Sharing Schemes for General and Uniform Access Structures (Applebaum, Beimel, Farràs, Nir, Peter). Improves general secret-sharing share-size exponent to 0.892 (0.942 for linear schemes) and studies uniform access structures.
- **114760406.pdf** — A Quantum-Proof Non-Malleable Extractor with Application to Privacy Amplification against Active Quantum Adversaries (Aggarwal, Chung, Lin, Vidick). First non-malleable extractor secure against quantum adversaries, enabling privacy amplification over unauthenticated channels.
- **114760413.pdf** — Degree 2 is Complete for the Round-Complexity of Malicious MPC (Applebaum, Brakerski, Tsabary). Non-interactive reduction showing MPC for degree-2 functions implies same round complexity for general functions, yielding new round-optimal protocols.
- **114760420.pdf** — Tight Proofs of Space and Replication (Ben Fisch). Concretely practical, arbitrarily tight proof-of-space from stacked depth-robust graphs, with a ZigZag PoRep variant enabling fast data extraction.
- **114760426.pdf** — A Modular Treatment of Blind Signatures from Identification Schemes (Hauck, Kiltz, Loss). General framework for blind signatures from linear ID schemes via a new One-More-MITM notion and a generalized forking lemma.
- **11535218.pdf** — Not a research paper: LNCS 3621 front matter (editorial board listing only).
- **11593447.pdf** — Not a research paper: LNCS 3788 front matter (editorial board listing only).
- **116940107.pdf** — How to Record Quantum Queries, and Applications to Quantum Indifferentiability (Mark Zhandry). New compressed-oracle QROM technique overcoming the recording barrier; proves quantum indifferentiability of Merkle-Damgård and security of Fujisaki-Okamoto.
- **116940115.pdf** — Efficient Collision Attack Frameworks for RIPEMD-160 (Liu, Dobraunig, Mendel, Isobe, Wang, Cao). Two new collision-attack frameworks (DLSR/SLDR) exploiting message-expansion weaknesses in reduced RIPEMD-160.
- **116940125.pdf** — Statistical Zeroizing Attack: Cryptanalysis of Candidates of BP Obfuscation over GGH15 Multilinear Map (Cheon, Cho, Hhan, Kim, Lee). Distinguishes obfuscations of equivalent branching programs via sample variance, breaking the Chen et al. CRYPTO'18 candidate at optimal parameters.
- **116940133.pdf** — Leakage Certification Revisited: Bounding Model Errors in Side-Channel Security Evaluations (Bronchain, Hendrickx, Massart, Olshevsky, Standaert). First quantitative leakage-certification bounds on true mutual information using perceived and hypothetical information.
- **116940138.pdf** — Tight Leakage-Resilient CCA-Security from Quasi-Adaptive Hash Proof System (Han, Liu, Lyu, Gu). Introduces quasi-adaptive hash proof systems giving generic tightly leakage-resilient CCA-secure PKE, instantiated from k-LIN over pairing groups.
- **116940142.pdf** — Improving Attacks on Round-Reduced Speck32/64 Using Deep Learning (Aron Gohr, BSI). Deep-residual-network distinguishers outperform classical differential ones, reducing 11-round Speck32/64 security to roughly 38 bits via Bayesian key search.
- **116940143.pdf** — Adaptively Secure and Succinct Functional Encryption (Kitagawa, Nishimaki, Tanaka, Yamakawa). Generic transformation upgrading weakly-selective single-key sublinearly-succinct functional encryption to adaptive, collusion-resistant, succinct FE.
- **116940145.pdf** — Data-Independent Memory Hard Functions: New Attacks and Stronger Constructions (Blocki, Harsha, Kang, Lee, Xing, Zhou). Shows greedy pebbling attacks on the DRSample iMHF and proposes stronger constructions for password hashing.
- **116940149.pdf** — Quantum Cryptanalysis in the RAM Model: Claw-Finding Attacks on SIKE (Jaques, Schanck). Establishes gate-count and depth-times-width quantum cost models, revising (upward) security estimates for SIDH/SIKE claw-finding attacks.
- **116940161.pdf** — On the Shortness of Vectors to be Found by the Ideal-SVP Quantum Algorithm (Ducas, Plançon, Wesolowski). Experimentally estimates concrete approximation factors of quantum Ideal-SVP algorithms on cyclotomic ideals, with heuristic improvements and provable lower bounds.
- **116940164.pdf** — Leakage Resilient Secret Sharing and Applications (Srinivasan, Vasudevan). Compiler turning any monotone-access-structure secret sharing into a local-leakage-resilient scheme with constant share-size blow-up and optimal leakage rate.
- **116940171.pdf** — Watermarking Public-Key Cryptographic Primitives (Goyal, Kim, Manohar, Waters, Wu). Watermarkable signatures from one-way functions and watermarkable PKE from standard algebraic assumptions, with public marking/extraction and collusion resistance.
- **116940176.pdf** — Two-Party ECDSA from Hash Proof Systems and Efficient Instantiations (Castagnos, Catalano, Laguillaumie, Savasta, Tucker). Generalizes Lindell's two-party ECDSA via hash proof systems, dropping non-standard interactive assumptions; instantiated with class groups.
- **116940179.pdf** — Unreadable: first page is hex/glyph-encoded extraction garbage (fragments suggest an obfuscation/iO-related paper); title and content cannot be determined from the extract.
- **116940187.pdf** — Lattice-Based Zero-Knowledge Proofs: New Techniques for Shorter and Faster Constructions (Esgin, Steinfeld, Liu, Liu). One-shot lattice ZK proofs for non-linear relations plus CRT-packing and NTT-friendly speedups.
- **116940188.pdf** — Adaptively Secure MPC with Sublinear Communication Complexity (Cohen, shelat, Wichs). Two-round adaptively secure SFE with sublinear communication from LWE and iO, plus Bob-optimized 2PC and sublinear NIZKs.
- **116940192.pdf** — Correlation of Quadratic Boolean Functions: Cryptanalysis of All Versions of Full MORUS (Shi, Sun, Sasaki, Li, Hu). Polynomial-time algorithm computing exact correlations of quadratic Boolean functions, yielding linear trails with correlation 2^-38 for full MORUS.
- **116940195.pdf** — Watermarking PRFs from Lattices: Stronger Security via Extractable PRFs (Kim, Wu). Lattice-based PRF watermarking secure against extraction-query adversaries without giving the authority power to break unmarked keys.
- **116940196.pdf** — The Communication Complexity of Threshold Private Set Intersection (Ghosh, Simkin). First upper and lower bounds for threshold PSI: Ω(t) lower bound, Õ(t) via FHE, and Õ(t²) from additively homomorphic encryption.
- **116940199.pdf** — Match Me if You Can: Matchmaking Encryption and its Applications (Ateniese, Francati, Nuñez, Venturi). New encryption primitive where sender and receiver both specify policies, with generic constructions from functional encryption.
- **116940201.pdf** — Scalable Zero Knowledge with no Trusted Setup (Ben-Sasson, Bentov, Horesh, Riabzev). zk-STIK/zk-STARK: concretely efficient IOP-based transparent zero-knowledge arguments from collision-resistant hashing alone.
- **116940203.pdf** — Seedless Fruit is the Sweetest: Random Number Generation, Revisited (Coretti, Dodis, Karthikeyan, Tessaro). New robustness notions enabling seedless PRNGs that tolerate primitive-dependent adversarial entropy sources.
- **116940207.pdf** — Revisiting Post-Quantum Fiat-Shamir (Liu, Zhandry). Gives mild conditions under which Fiat-Shamir is secure against quantum adversaries, implying unmodified lattice-based signatures remain secure.
- **116940213.pdf** — Broadcast and Trace with N^ε Ciphertext Size from Standard Assumptions (Goyal, Quach, Waters, Wichs). Broadcast-and-trace scheme with O(N^ε) ciphertexts from LWE plus bilinear maps, improving on Boneh–Waters' O(N^{1/2}).
- **116940215.pdf** — Exploring Constructions of Compact NIZKs from Various Assumptions (Katsumata, Nishimaki, Yamada, Yamakawa). Progress toward NIZKs for NP with proof size below O(|C|·κ) from falsifiable pairing-based and group-based assumptions.
- **116940216.pdf** — Highly Efficient Key Exchange Protocols with Optimal Tightness (Cohn-Gordon, Cremers, Gjøsteen, Jacobsen, Jager). Nearly-tight security reductions for Signal/Noise-style authenticated key exchange, with proven optimality of the linear loss.
- **116940219.pdf** — Communication-Efficient Unconditional MPC with Guaranteed Output Delivery (Goyal, Liu, Song). First unconditional MPC with guaranteed output delivery achieving linear-in-n communication per multiplication gate for t < n/3.
- **116940225.pdf** — Quantum Indistinguishability of Random Sponges (Czajkowski, Hülsing, Schaffner). Proves random sponges are quantum-indistinguishable from random functions, yielding quantum-secure PRFs from sponge constructions.
- **116940227.pdf** — Simultaneous Amplification: The Case of Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge (Goyal, Jain, Sahai). Amplifies soundness and zero-knowledge simultaneously for weak NIZK candidates via parallel repetition and a new MPC-based repetition.
- **116940229.pdf** — Libra: Succinct Zero-Knowledge Proofs with Optimal Prover Computation (Xie, Zhang, Zhang, Papamanthou, Song). GKR-based ZK system with linear prover time, succinct proofs, and one-time input-size-only trusted setup.
- **116940242.pdf** — Stronger Leakage-Resilient and Non-Malleable Secret Sharing Schemes for General Access Structures (Aggarwal, Damgård, Nielsen, Obremski, Purwanto, Ribeiro, Simkin). Generic compilers producing leakage-resilient and non-malleable secret sharing for arbitrary access structures.
- **116940247.pdf** — Security of the Fiat-Shamir Transformation in the Quantum Random-Oracle Model (Don, Fehr, Majenz, Schaffner). Generic reduction proving Fiat-Shamir preserves soundness and proof-of-knowledge against quantum adversaries.
- **116940260.pdf** — Non-Interactive Non-Malleability from Quantum Supremacy (Kalai, Khurana). Non-interactive non-malleable commitments in the plain model via a new tag-amplification technique, using quantum-supremacy-style assumptions.
- **116940267.pdf** — Communication Lower Bounds for Statistically Secure MPC, with or without Preprocessing (Damgård, Larsen, Nielsen). Proves Ω(ng) communication lower bounds for statistically secure MPC, showing the O(n) overhead of known protocols is inherent.
- **116940274.pdf** — The Distinction Between Fixed and Random Generators in Group-Based Assumptions (Bartusek, Ma, Zhandry). Separates fixed- vs random-generator variants of DDH/CDH and proves tight preprocessing lower bounds for random-generator DL/CDH.
- **116940275.pdf** — Non-Malleable Secret Sharing in the Computational Setting (Faonio, Venturi). Computationally private threshold secret sharing with continuous non-malleability, noisy-leakage resilience, and improved rate from one-to-one one-way functions.
- **116940277.pdf** — Unifying Leakage Models on a Rényi Day (Prest, Goudarzi, Martinelli, Passelègue). Unifies noisy-leakage metrics via pointwise mutual information, giving tighter reductions from noisy leakage to the probing model for masked implementations.
- **116940281.pdf** — Low Memory Attacks against Two-Round Even-Mansour using the 3-XOR Problem (Leurent, Sibleyras). Connects two-round Even-Mansour to the 3-XOR problem, yielding the first attacks with both data and memory well below 2^n.
- **116940286.pdf** — Cryptographic Sensing (Ishai, Kushilevitz, Ostrovsky, Sahai). Initiates the study of embedding cryptographic secrets in naturally occurring measurement or learning functions, with definitions and feasibility results.
- **116940289.pdf** — Fully Secure Attribute-Based Encryption for t-CNF from LWE (Tsabary). First fully secure lattice-based ciphertext-policy ABE for t-CNF policy classes, via a fully secure constrained PRF from OWF.
- **116940291.pdf** — Universally Composable Secure Computation with Corrupted Tokens (Chandran, Chongchitmate, Ostrovsky, Visconti). Introduces the corrupted token model and achieves UC-secure MPC from one-way functions when at most n−1 of n tokens are corrupted.
- **116940296.pdf** — Non-Malleable Codes for Decision Trees (Ball, Guo, Wichs). Efficient unconditional non-malleable codes secure against decision-tree tampering of depth n^{1/4−o(1)}, via reductions to split-state tampering.
- **116940301.pdf** — Non-Uniformly Sound Certificates with Applications to Concurrent Zero-Knowledge (Freitag, Komargodski, Pass). Succinct single-message certificates with best-possible security against non-uniform attackers, yielding constant-round fully concurrent ZK.
