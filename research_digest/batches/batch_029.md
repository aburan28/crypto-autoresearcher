# Batch 029 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ~2020–2021 top-venue papers (file numbering and cited venues strongly suggest EUROCRYPT 2021 LNCS vol. 12696 and nearby proceedings); mostly EU/US/IL theory cryptography.
- Big theme: secure computation — MPC (actively secure, covert, NISC, mrNISC, unbounded MPC, constant-overhead binary-field MPC), garbled circuits, function secret sharing, VOLE-PSI, silent OT/Paillier HSS.
- Strong cryptanalysis cluster: FF3 FPE attacks, GEA-1/GEA-2 GPRS break (likely intentional 40-bit backdoor), UOV/Rainbow parameter-breaking attacks, AES key-schedule structure, MITM preimage search, neural distinguisher analysis.
- Side-channel/leakage cluster: masking verification in F2, dummy shuffling in white-box, random probing expansion, Shamir leakage-resilience.
- Post-quantum and lattice cluster: GPU lattice sieving record (dim 180), Ring-LWE ideal SVP, LWE-based iO/mrNISC/MPC, SIDH arithmetic, QROM KEM tightness, lattice VOPRF, quantum proofs (compressed oracle, classical proofs of quantum knowledge).
- No anomalies: all 50 files appear to be genuine research-paper first pages; no duplicates, no non-paper content detected.

## Papers
- **126960189.pdf** — Fast verification of masking schemes in characteristic two (Bordes, Karpman). Generalizes the NI/SNI probing matrix model to any finite field with glitch robustness; verification tool is ~1000× faster, proving SNI gadgets secure up to order 11.
- **126960196.pdf** — Three Third Generation Attacks on the Format Preserving Encryption Scheme FF3 (Amon, Dunkelman, Keller, Ronen, Shamir). Presents three new attacks on the FF3 format-preserving encryption scheme, substantially weakening this NIST FPE standard.
- **126960202.pdf** — New Representations of the AES Key Schedule (Leurent, Pernot). Shows AES-128 key schedule splits into four independent 32-bit computations; yields a practical forgery on mixFeed, an attack on ALE, and improved impossible-differential attacks.
- **126960203.pdf** — On the Compressed-Oracle Technique, and Post-Quantum Security of Proofs of Sequential Work (Chung, Fehr, Huang, Liao). Clean exposition and parallel-query framework for Zhandry's compressed oracle, turning quantum query lower bounds into classical reasoning; applied to sequential work proofs.
- **126960205.pdf** — Candidate Obfuscation via Oblivious LWE Sampling (Wee, Wichs). Candidate indistinguishability obfuscation in the split-FHE framework, proven from LWE plus a new falsifiable oblivious-LWE-sampling assumption; plausibly post-quantum.
- **126960210.pdf** — Large Scale, Actively Secure Computation from LPN and Free-XOR Garbled Circuits (Ben-Efraim, Cong, Omri, Orsini, Smart, Soria-Vazquez). Actively secure multi-party garbled-circuit protocol with free-XOR and O(n) communication per party, plus a lighter preprocessing variant with implementation.
- **126960214.pdf** — Non-Interactive Zero Knowledge from Sub-exponential DDH (Jain, Jin). First NIZK and Zap arguments for NP from sub-exponential DDH without pairings, via a new interactive trapdoor hashing notion.
- **126960215.pdf** — Dummy Shuffling against Algebraic Attacks in White-box Implementations (Biryukov, Udovenko). Extends shuffling with dummy slots and proves security against linear/higher-degree algebraic attacks in the BU white-box model, with near-optimal refreshing.
- **126960217.pdf** — Improved cryptanalysis of UOV and Rainbow (Beullens). New intersection and rectangular MinRank attacks cut UOV/Rainbow key-recovery cost by 2^17–2^73, showing NIST PQC round-2/3 parameter sets miss their security targets.
- **126960219.pdf** — Advanced Lattice Sieving on GPUs, with Tensor Cores (Ducas, Stevens, van Woerden). GPU/Tensor-Core implementations of G6K sieve algorithms plus a dual-hash technique; sets SVP challenge record at dimension 180, ~100× better in time and energy.
- **126960226.pdf** — Password Hashing and Preprocessing (Farshim, Tessaro). Formal multi-instance analysis of password hashing with preprocessing, resolving an open BRT 2012 question via transcript counting; defines auxiliary-info KDF security and composition.
- **126960229.pdf** — Threshold Garbled Circuits and Ad Hoc Secure Computation (Ciampi, Goyal, Ostrovsky). Studies garbled circuits evaluable with missing or duplicate wire keys, connecting to NIMPC and ad hoc secure computation with collusion.
- **126960235.pdf** — The Rise of Paillier: Homomorphic Secret Sharing and Public-Key Silent OT (Orlandi, Scholl, Yakoubov). Perfect distributed-DLOG in Paillier groups yields error-free homomorphic secret sharing and PCFs for OT/VOLE under DCR/QR, enabling public-key silent OT.
- **126960236.pdf** — Round-Optimal Blind Signatures in the Plain Model from Classical and Quantum Standard Assumptions (Katsumata, Nishimaki, Yamada, Yamakawa). First round-optimal blind signature in the plain model from standard polynomial-time assumptions, classically and post-quantum instantiable.
- **126960240.pdf** — On the ideal shortest vector problem over random rational primes (Pan, Xu, Wadleigh, Cheng). Links prime-ideal SVP complexity to decomposition groups; for power-of-two cyclotomics most primes have poly-time ideal SVP — does not break Ring-LWE.
- **126960243.pdf** — Automatic Search of Meet-in-the-Middle Preimage Attacks on AES-like Hashing (Bao, Dong, Guo, Li, Shi, Sun, Wang). Automated tooling for MITM preimage attacks on AES-based hashing modes, extending attacks that exploit internal-state and key freedoms.
- **126960250.pdf** — Compactness of Hashing Modes and Efficiency beyond Merkle Tree (Andreeva, Bhattacharyya, Roy). Introduces a "compactness" efficiency metric for hash modes; two tree-based constructions give compact large-domain fixed-input-length hashing beyond Merkle trees.
- **126960255.pdf** — Mind the Middle Layer: The HADES Design Strategy Revisited (Keller, Rosemarin). Shows MDS matrix choice greatly affects HADES security: good choices raise differential/linear bounds, weak ones give invariant subspaces through the middle; applied to Poseidon/Starkad.
- **126960266.pdf** — Towards Accountability in CRS Generation (Ananth, Asharov, Dahari, Goyal). Defines accountable CRS authorities: if the authority leaks private inputs from protocol transcripts, a publicly verifiable proof of misbehavior can be produced.
- **126960280.pdf** — Cryptanalytic Applications of the Polynomial Method for Solving Multivariate Equation Systems over GF(2) (Dinur). Concretely efficient polynomial-method solver for GF(2) systems (~2^0.815n for quadratic); breaks 2 of 3 new LowMC-based Picnic parameter sets.
- **126960290.pdf** — Function Secret Sharing for Mixed-Mode and Fixed-Point Secure Computation (Boyle, Chandran, Gilboa, Gupta, Ishai, Kumar, Rathee). Improves FSS key sizes (~4× for comparison) and extends FSS-based preprocessing to mixed-mode and fixed-point secure computation gates.
- **126960292.pdf** — Non-interactive Distributional Indistinguishability (NIDI) and Non-Malleable Commitments (Khurana). Introduces NIDI arguments fixing NIWI's weakness for unique-witness languages; yields first plain-model hiding commit-and-prove and CCA-secure commitments from iO-type assumptions.
- **126960293.pdf** — Robust Property-Preserving Hash Functions for Hamming Distance and More (Fleischhacker, Simkin). Robust PPH for exact Hamming distance (O(tλ) digests) from q-strong bilinear DLOG, via a set-intersection PPH with near-matching lower bound.
- **126960303.pdf** — Oblivious Transfer is in MiniQCrypt (Grilo, Lin, Song, Vaikuntanathan). Constructs simulation-secure OT from quantum-secure one-way functions using quantum communication; constant-round UC-secure OT in the CRS model, enabling MiniQCrypt MPC.
- **126960305.pdf** — Leakage-resilience of the Shamir Secret-sharing Scheme against Physical-bit Leakages (Maji, Nguyen, Paskin-Cherniavsky, Suad, Wang). Analyzes Shamir sharing over prime fields when an adversary probes m physical bit-locations per share, aiming for low-threshold resilience.
- **126960310.pdf** — Efficient Range Proofs with Transparent Setup from Bounded Integer Commitments (Couteau, Klooß, Lin, Reichle). Transforms field commitments into bounded-integer commitments, yielding transparent-setup range proofs with less communication/computation than state of the art.
- **126960317.pdf** — Non-Interactive Anonymous Router (Shi, Wu). New NIAR primitive: a single untrusted router permutes encrypted messages using a one-time setup token, without interactive mix-net/DC-net protocols.
- **126960322.pdf** — Multiparty Reusable Non-Interactive Secure Computation from LWE (Benhamouda, Jain, Komargodski, Lin). Two-round MPC whose first-round input commitments are reusable across arbitrary computations by any subset of parties, from LWE.
- **126960325.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the GPRS Encryption Algorithms GEA-1 and GEA-2 (Beierle, Derbez, Leander, Leurent, Raddum, Rotella, Rupprecht, Stennes). Recovers GEA-1 keys in 2^40 from 65 keystream bits via a suspicious LFSR weakness (apparently an intentional 40-bit backdoor); breaks GEA-2 in 2^45.1.
- **126960326.pdf** — Breaking the Circuit Size Barrier for Secure Computation under Quasi-Polynomial LPN (Couteau, Meyer). New HSS and pseudorandom correlation generator for log-local circuits gives two-party computation with sublinear O(s/log log s) communication under LPN variants.
- **126960357.pdf** — Unbounded Multi-Party Computation from Learning with Errors (Ananth, Jain, Jin, Malavolta). Round-optimal unbounded MPC (no a-priori party/function bound) in the plain model from LWE — the first post-quantum such result — via attribute-based SFE.
- **126960361.pdf** — Generic Compiler for Publicly Verifiable Covert Multi-Party Computation (Faust, Hazay, Kretzler, Schlosser). Compiler upgrading multi-party protocols (e.g., MPC preprocessing) to publicly verifiable covert security with certificates of cheating, using time-lock encryption.
- **126960366.pdf** — VOLE-PSI: Fast OPRF and Circuit-PSI from Vector-OLE (Rindal, Schoppmann). Batched OPRF from VOLE and PaXoS yields record-efficiency PSI (under 450 bits/element maliciously secure) plus a Circuit-PSI extension for secret-shared output.
- **126960377.pdf** — Constant-Overhead Unconditionally Secure Multiparty Computation over Binary Fields (Polychroniadou, Song). First honest-majority malicious-secure MPC evaluating a single boolean circuit with amortized O(n) communication per gate, no computational assumptions.
- **126960385.pdf** — A Deeper Look at Machine Learning-Based Cryptanalysis (Benamira, Gerault, Peyrin, Tan). Reverse-engineers Gohr's neural distinguisher on SPECK, explaining what patterns the network learns and how it relates to classical differential features.
- **126960386.pdf** — On the Power of Expansion: More Efficient Constructions in the Random Probing Model (Belaïd, Rivain, Taleb). Improves the expanding-compiler framework for random probing security, achieving better tolerated leakage probability and lower complexity.
- **126960389.pdf** — Bifurcated Signatures: Folding the Accountability vs. Anonymity Dilemma into a Single Private Signing Scheme (Libert, Nguyen, Peters, Yung). Single signing scheme that applies anonymity or accountability per-message based on predicates over message context and content.
- **126960403.pdf** — TARDIS: A Foundation of Time-Lock Puzzles in UC (Baum, David, Dowsley, Nielsen, Oechsner). UC-secure modeling of time-lock puzzles and timed primitives, proving random oracles necessary and enabling composable proofs for blockchain protocols.
- **126960410.pdf** — Classical proofs of quantum knowledge (Vidick, Zhang). Defines proofs of knowledge with classical verifier and quantum prover holding quantum witnesses; builds quantum extractors and shows Mahadev's protocol is an argument of quantum knowledge.
- **127110104.pdf** — Single-to-Multi-Theorem Transformations for Non-Interactive Statistical Zero-Knowledge (Fischlin, Rohrbach). FLS-like transformations upgrading single-theorem to multi-theorem NISZK in the common random string model, from one-way permutations or lattice assumptions.
- **127110107.pdf** — QCCA-Secure Generic Key Encapsulation Mechanism with Tighter Security in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Liu, Wang). Tighter QROM reduction for the KC transformation using the Measure-Rewind-Measure O2H lemma, yielding IND-qCCA KEMs from OW-CPA/IND-CPA PKEs.
- **127110108.pdf** — Improving Revocation for Group Signature with Redactable Signature (Sanders). Extends time-bound-key group signatures to fine-grained, reactivatable revocation via redactable signatures; also shrinks the PKC 2020 redactable scheme's public key.
- **127110110.pdf** — Bootstrapping fully homomorphic encryption over the integers in less than one second (Pereira). New AGCD-variant GSW-like scheme encrypting polynomials natively enables sub-second bootstrapping for FHE over the integers with ~400 MB keys.
- **127110111.pdf** — Group Signatures with User-Controlled and Sequential Linkability (Diaz, Lehmann). Group signatures where users alone decide, before or after signing, whether signatures are linkable, with sequential-linkability guarantees preventing selective omission.
- **127110112.pdf** — An Alternative Approach for SIDH Arithmetic (Bouvier, Imbert). Polynomial representation with bounded coefficients speeds SIKE field arithmetic; proposes a 736-bit prime giving 1.17× speedup over SIKEp751 at comparable security.
- **127110113.pdf** — Impossibility on Tamper-Resilient Cryptography with Uniqueness Properties (Wang, Matsuda, Hanaoka, Tanaka). Black-box separations showing tamper-resilience cannot be derived from common assumptions for unique signatures, VRFs, injective OWFs, and unique-message encryption.
- **127110122.pdf** — On the CCA Compatibility of Public-Key Infrastructure (Khurana, Waters). Defines X-compatible key generation; shows any sub-exponentially CPA-compatible KeyGen is CCA1/CCA2-compatible under hinting PRGs and non-interactive CCA2 commitments.
- **127110125.pdf** — Rate-1 Key-Dependent Message Security via Reusable Homomorphic Extractor against Correlated-Source Attacks (Lai, Liu, Wang). Reusable homomorphic extractors yield rate-1 KDM-secure PKE/IBE for block-affine functions, amplified to bounded circuits, from LWE or DDH; also leakage resilient.
- **127110128.pdf** — Round-optimal Verifiable Oblivious Pseudorandom Functions from Ideal Lattices (Albrecht, Davidson, Deo, Smart). First round-optimal (online) VOPRF secure under sub-exponential ideal-lattice assumptions, built with lattice NIZK arguments of knowledge.
- **127110132.pdf** — Two-Party Adaptor Signatures From Identification Schemes (Erwig, Faust, Hostáková, Maitra, Riahi). Generic transformation from identification-scheme-based signatures to adaptor signatures, plus aggregated two-party signing for smaller blockchain footprint.
