# Batch 055 digest

## Overview
- All 50 files are recent research papers (filenames carry 2026 ePrint-style numbers, many dated May 2026); no legacy ECDLP-era material in this batch.
- Dominant theme is post-quantum cryptography: lattice schemes (CKKS/BFV bootstrapping, ML-DSA/ML-KEM, threshold FHE, discrete Gaussian sampling, lattice estimators), isogeny-based crypto (SQIsign, pushforward attacks, Coppersmith bounds for isogeny HNP), and code-based crypto (Gabidulin EGMC break, QC-MDPC decoding).
- Strong symmetric-cryptanalysis cluster: AES related-differential and 7-round key-independent distinguishers, low-round Feistel plaintext recovery (classical + quantum), a Rijndael-256 SoK, and a Vistrutah FPGA evaluation.
- Side-channel and implementation security is prominent: single-trace attack on LESS, profiling-device-free SASCA on ML-KEM, masked Gaussian sampling, and an ML-DSA reduction-placement audit that found a wolfSSL defect.
- Notable breaks: 2026-972 breaks all 16 EGMC parameter sets (128-bit set reduced to ~35 bits); 2026-1030 gives practical attacks on an isogeny threshold signature scheme; 2026-1019 breaks two PAUKS keyword-search schemes.
- Anomalies: 2026-1043 is an ML-for-autonomous-systems literature review, not a cryptography paper; 2026-979 and 2026-984 are closely related (quantum rejection sampling for lattice Gaussian sampling and dual attacks) by different author teams — overlapping contributions, not duplicates.

## Papers
- **2026-1019.pdf** — On the Security of Public Key Authenticated Encryption with Keyword Search with Sender-independent Search Complexity (Takeshi Yoshida, Keita Emura). Short note showing keyword leakage from updated ciphertexts in Li et al.'s proxy-free PAUKS, also breaking their 2023 PAUKS scheme.
- **2026-1020.pdf** — On the Formal Verification of Authenticated Encryption of the MQTT Protocol (Varsha Jarali, Shashi Kant Pandey). Proposes E2EE MQTT using nested AES-GCM and Schnorr/HMQV-style key exchange, verified with ProVerif.
- **2026-1021.pdf** — Schnorr-like Proofs of Knowledge for Hidden Oil Subspaces in UOV (Zhiwei Wang). Sigma protocol proving knowledge of the hidden oil subspace in UOV keys, with a uniqueness theorem in the random UOV model.
- **2026-1022.pdf** — Thorns in Polynomial Convolution: Correlation, Large Deviations, and Applications (Cai, Liu, Wang, Lu). First systematic characterization of coefficient correlations in convolved Gaussian polynomials, showing DFR underestimation in structured lattice schemes.
- **2026-1023.pdf** — Faster CoeffToSlot and SlotToCoeff for Sparsely Packed Ciphertexts with Application to CKKS Bootstrapping (Xiaopeng Zheng). Exploits slot repetition patterns to cut cost of CKKS bootstrapping linear transforms at multiplicative depth 1.
- **2026-1024.pdf** — Separating the Pebbling Model from the Random Oracle Model (de Rezende, Engström, Reyzin). Resolves a decade-old question by showing the pebbling model is unrealistically restrictive for Proofs of Space and Memory-Hard Functions.
- **2026-1025.pdf** — Geometric Critical Point Screening: Clustering-Free Cryptanalytic Extraction of Neural Network Models (Ming Duan, Peiyao Tang). Reduces query complexity of neural-network signature extraction via geometric critical-point screening, avoiding clustering.
- **2026-1026.pdf** — Sparse Hermite Interpolation Method for Discrete-CKKS Functional Bootstrapping (Alexandru, Kim, Polyakov, Zheng). New sparse trigonometric Hermite interpolation overcoming noise-reduction limits of prior functional bootstrapping methods.
- **2026-1027.pdf** — Computing Asymptotic Bounds for the Automated Coppersmith Method via Linear Programming (Ding, Dai, Wu, Zhang, Zhang). Turns Automated Coppersmith bound computation into LP; improves bounds for isogeny, modular-inversion, and elliptic-curve hidden number problems.
- **2026-1028.pdf** — Collusion-Resistant Asymmetric Anamorphic Encryption: Framework, Generic Construction, and Concrete Instantiations (Xie, Yang, Au, Yu, Susilo). First framework for collusion-resistant asymmetric anamorphic encryption via a new witness-PRF-for-PKE abstraction.
- **2026-1029.pdf** — Towards a Unified Memory-Less Framework for TCitH (Chi-Domínguez, Gazzoni Filho, Palumbi, Rivera-Zamarripa). Embedded-friendly TCitH ZKP framework cutting stack usage up to 99%, plus constant-time bitsliced Rijndael-256.
- **2026-1030.pdf** — Pushforward Problems and Applications to Isogeny-based Cryptography (Luciano Maino, Christophe Petit). Pushforward-oracle framework for isogeny recovery yielding practical attacks on a threshold signature scheme and weak OPRF instances.
- **2026-1031.pdf** — Compact Quaternion Algorithms for SQIsign (Won Kim, Changmin Lee, Hyunwoo Yoo). Reduces fixed-precision memory requirements of SQIsign quaternion arithmetic for constrained devices.
- **2026-1032.pdf** — When Removing Reductions Goes Wrong: Auditing Reduction Placement in Production ML-DSA Implementations (Lee, Lim, Yoon). Certificate-backed audit of lazy Montgomery reductions across eight ML-DSA libraries, uncovering a wolfSSL defect.
- **2026-1033.pdf** — A New Construction Method for More Efficient Quadratic One-Time Noisy Multi-Client Functional Encryption Schemes (Zalonis, Scheu-Hachtel, Armknecht). Generic label-supporting construction (QUILT) giving 4.8–6.8x speedups for private logistic regression training.
- **2026-1034.pdf** — Vistrutah on FPGA: High-Throughput Pipelined Architecture and Comparison with Wider AES Variant (Ahmet Malal, Oğuz Yayla). First FPGA evaluation of the Vistrutah wide-block cipher, reaching 211.57 Gbps and comparing favorably with WAES-256.
- **2026-1035.pdf** — SoK: Rijndael-256 (Ayalneh, Hlayhel, Sharifian, Tereschenko). Systematizes Rijndael-256 as a wide-block cipher overcoming birthday-bound limits, including vectorized AES-NI implementations.
- **2026-1036.pdf** — Resettable Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge: Attacks and Defenses (Abdolmaleki, Campanelli, Dao, Mohammadi, Roustaeifar). First systematic study of resettable security for NIZKs: new srZK definition plus concrete resetting attacks on zkSNARKs.
- **2026-1037.pdf** — On Publicly Verifiable Tokens in Group Signatures with Message-Dependent Opening (Takuma Watanabe, Keita Emura). Formally defines public verifiability of admitter tokens in GS-MDO and analyzes existing pairing- and lattice-based schemes.
- **2026-1038.pdf** — Scaling Intelligence: Verifiable Decision Forest Inference with Remainder (Arsenis et al.). Scalable ZKML via structured data-parallel GKR proving 128-tree decision-forest inference in under 54 seconds total.
- **2026-1039.pdf** — Related-Differential Distinguishers on up to 7 Rounds of AES (Xueping Yan, Lin Tan, Wenfeng Qi). New related-differential non-random properties and improved secret-key distinguishers for 5- and 7-round AES.
- **2026-1040.pdf** — Classical and Quantum Full Plaintext Recovery for Low-Round Feistel-Type Designs (Guo, Wang, Jing, Mao, Liu). Upgrades low-round Feistel distinguishers to full plaintext recovery classically, and uses Simon's algorithm for 3-/4-round quantum recovery.
- **2026-1041.pdf** — CoNAN: A Structure-Aware Framework for Lattice Cryptanalysis (Raya, Kumar, Dey, Gangopadhyay). Exploits algebra-induced homomorphisms to derive lower-dimensional lattices, lowering security estimates for NTRU-like and structured-LWE schemes.
- **2026-1042.pdf** — Doubly Aggregatable Signatures (Fuchsbauer, Garimidi, Neu, Policharla, Resnick, Tas). New two-layer signature primitive with succinct publicly aggregatable certificates; two random-oracle constructions with different pairing/key-size trade-offs.
- **2026-1043.pdf** — Safe and Secure Autonomy by Machine Learning Techniques: A Systematic Literature Review (Hassania, Alidoost Nia, Ebrahimi Atani). Literature review of ML for autonomous-system safety/security (129 papers, 2018–2024); not a cryptography paper — out of corpus scope.
- **2026-1044.pdf** — Finding Random Collisions for Random Degree-2 Functions (Xinyu Mao). Efficient algorithm sampling near-ideal collision distributions for random degree-2 maps, refuting their distributional collision resistance when p^{N−M} is superpolynomial.
- **2026-1045.pdf** — Ciphertext-Updatable Attribute-Based and Predicate Encryption from Lattices (Schädlich, Scheu-Hachtel, Tairi, Wang). Defines CU-ABE/CU-PE with token-based ciphertext updates; constructions from LWE via inner-product FE and lockable obfuscation.
- **2026-1046.pdf** — AWARE: A Non-Interactive Anonymous Whistleblowing System against Recipient Corruption (Gao, Yu, Yang, Zhan, Jia). Whistleblowing protocol separating authentication from submission with committee threshold opening to resist recipient corruption.
- **2026-1047.pdf** — Round-Optimal Subversion-Resilient UC PAKE from Malleable Trapdoor Smooth Projective Hash Functions (Abdolmaleki et al.). Introduces M-TSPHF to build round-optimal UC-secure PAKE that tolerates undetectable code subversion.
- **2026-950.pdf** — Beyond the Anonymous Inbox: Secure Whistleblowing for All (Wechta, Kutyłowski, Lizurej, Syta). Compliance-driven whistleblowing system design derived from EU Directive 2019/1937, with formalized threat model and security goals.
- **2026-971.pdf** — Explicit cost analysis of Toom-4 multiplication for incomplete NTT in lattice-based cryptography (Sakura Oku, Momonari Kudo). Derives explicit Toom-4 operation counts for incomplete-NTT hybrids and identifies advantageous parameter ranges.
- **2026-972.pdf** — Breaking ACDGV MinRank Gabidulin encryption schemes over matrix codes (Thai Hung Le). Key-recovery attack breaking all 16 EGMC parameter sets, e.g. dropping a claimed 128-bit set to ~35 bits in under 10 minutes.
- **2026-973.pdf** — Asynchronous Lagrange-Based Threshold FHE with Smaller Modulus Overhead (Kim, Lee, Lee, Passelègue, Stehlé). Rigorous noise analysis of Shamir-based threshold FHE, cutting modulus overhead up to 90% for large committees.
- **2026-974.pdf** — LoTRS: Practical Post-Quantum Structured Threshold Ring Signatures from Lattices (Jagganath, Esgin, Steinfeld, Sakzad, Saarinen, Liu). Formalizes structured threshold ring signatures and builds a two-round lattice-based construction from a multisignature layer.
- **2026-975.pdf** — Functional Bootstrapping for a Single LWE Ciphertext with Õ(1) Polynomial Multiplications (Zheng, Li, Wang). First non-amortized Õ(1) BFV functional bootstrapping for one ciphertext over large plaintext spaces via sparse packing.
- **2026-976.pdf** — Revisiting DKLs Threshold ECDSA: Enhanced OT-based VOLE and Two-Party Signing (Gilad Asharov). Fixes VOLE parameter issues in DKLs threshold ECDSA and gives a two-party signing protocol with a 0.2KB online phase (~600x reduction).
- **2026-977.pdf** — ThriftyMPC: Reducing the Cost of Large-Scale MPC in the Cloud (Inyangson et al.). Framework combining MPC with cloud spot instances for cost-effective privacy-preserving computation, e.g. GWAS workloads.
- **2026-978.pdf** — Verifying Consensus Protocols from LLM-assisted TLA+: A Case Study of Byzantine Reliable Broadcast (Cao, Wang, Wang, Sui, Duan). TLAssist tool generates TLA+ specs for Byzantine broadcast protocols, reportedly finding deep design flaws.
- **2026-979.pdf** — Improved Dual Attack and Trapdoor Sampling via Quantum Rejection Sampling (Cong Ling, Hao Yan, Nicholas Zhao). Quantum rejection sampling accelerates lattice Gaussian sampling, cutting dual-attack costs on Kyber-512/768/1024 by 9/4/13 bits. (Overlaps with 2026-984.)
- **2026-980.pdf** — Key-Independent Secret-Key Distinguisher for 7-Round AES based on the Joint Generalized Zero-Difference Property (Shin et al.). First key-independent distinguisher for 7-round AES using only round-function structure.
- **2026-981.pdf** — Profiling-Device-Free SASCA Framework for ML-KEM (Yuxuan Wang). Soft-analytical side-channel attack on ML-KEM using chosen ciphertexts and adversarial domain adaptation instead of a matched profiling device.
- **2026-982.pdf** — Zero-shot deep-unfolding decoder for QC-MDPC McEliece cryptosystems (Kukita, Iseki, Namatame, Watanabe). Weight-homogenised deep-unfolding BP decoder transferring from small to large QC-MDPC codes without retraining.
- **2026-983.pdf** — A Formal Analysis of FLEX and FLEX2 (Ramses Fernandez). Formalizes FLEX Bitcoin bridge protocols with ideal functionalities, proving enforceability, CDS secrecy, and UC realization.
- **2026-984.pdf** — Quantum algorithm for Discrete Gaussian Sampling (Chevignard, Shen, Schrottenloher). Quantum rejection-sampling DGS with quadratic speedup, yielding improved quantum dual attacks and SIS algorithms. (Overlaps with 2026-979.)
- **2026-985.pdf** — A New Insight into Constructing Cryptographic Boolean Functions via Walsh Spectral Analysis (He, Ren, Chen, Yan, Hou). Iterative Walsh-recovery algorithms (OIWR) for constructing Boolean functions with desired spectra, applied to low-weight correlation-immune functions.
- **2026-986.pdf** — VeinoCert: Binding an Object to an Owner (Serge Vaudenay). Ownership-verification protocol combining RFID chips with fingervein biometrics, avoiding biometric storage at rest.
- **2026-987.pdf** — Impact of Post-Quantum Signatures on InnoDB B+-Trees and Efficient Batch Signing (Lee, Kim, Kim, Kang, Seo). Shows large PQC signatures collapse MySQL B+-tree fan-out; split-table plus Merkle batch signing restores throughput 28x.
- **2026-988.pdf** — Maskaglia: A New, Efficient Approach to Masked Discrete Gaussian Sampling (Abou Haidar, Espitau, Hoffmann, Tibouchi). Efficient masked (power-analysis-resistant) discrete Gaussian sampling improving on CDT-based masked samplers.
- **2026-989.pdf** — BumbleBee: Best-of-Both-Worlds MVBA with Optimal Communication, Latency and Resilience Tradeoffs (Fatima Elsheimy, Simon Holmgaard Kamp). Multi-valued Byzantine agreement achieving tight synchronous/asynchronous/responsiveness resilience with optimal communication.
- **2026-990.pdf** — Single-Trace Power Analysis of LESS Key Generation (Akın, Talayhan, Öztürk). Profiled single-trace horizontal attack recovering full secret monomial matrices of the LESS signature scheme on ARM Cortex.
