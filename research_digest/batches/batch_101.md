# Batch 101 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by TCC 2013 (77850xxx) and EUROCRYPT 2013 (78810xxx) proceedings papers; ~2012–2013 theoretical cryptography.
- Heavy focus on secure computation / MPC foundations: fairness, reactive functionalities, UC synchrony, OT extension, correlated randomness, constant-overhead MPC, ORAM.
- Strong black-box separation / impossibility thread: Fiat-Shamir, Fischlin's paradigm, random oracles, NIZK, circular security, RDM security, UOWHF lower bounds.
- Notable high-impact papers: Garg–Gentry–Halevi candidate multilinear maps from ideal lattices; Joux's faster medium-prime index calculus (1175/1425-bit DLP records); Bos–Costello–Hisil–Lauter genus-2 speed records; Stevens' improved SHA-1 collisions.
- Side-channel / leakage cluster: masking security proof, key-rank estimation, leakage-resilient crypto from minimal assumptions.
- Anomalies: four very short items (1–2 pages): a talk abstract (77850354), a survey talk abstract (77850597), an errata notice (77850719, responding to 77850557), and a 2-page Keccak/SHA-3 summary (78810311).

## Papers
- **77850181.pdf** — Why "Fiat-Shamir for Proofs" Lacks a Proof (Bitansky, Dachman-Soled, Garg, Jain, Kalai, López-Alt, Wichs). Broad black-box separation showing Fiat-Shamir security for statistically sound proofs cannot be proven under standard assumptions.
- **77850201.pdf** — On The (In)security Of Fischlin's Paradigm (Ananth, Bhaskar, Goyal, Rao). Analyzes Fischlin's alternative to Fiat-Shamir for NIZK proofs of knowledge and signatures, questioning its instantiability in the standard model.
- **77850221.pdf** — Signatures of Correct Computation (Papamanthou, Shi, Tamassia). New model for verifiable outsourced computation with efficient updates; constructions for multivariate polynomial evaluation/differentiation, secure in the ROM.
- **77850241.pdf** — A Full Characterization of Functions that Imply Fair Coin Tossing (Asharov, Lindell, Rabin). Complete characterization of finite Boolean functions implying fair coin toss, refining which functions cannot be computed with fairness.
- **77850261.pdf** — Characterizing the Cryptographic Properties of Reactive 2-Party Functionalities (Jeffs, Rosulek). Combinatorial (finite-automata) characterizations of passively-secure and complete reactive two-party functionalities, information-theoretically.
- **77850279.pdf** — Feasibility and Completeness of Cryptographic Tasks in the Quantum World (Fehr, Katz, Song, Zhou, Zikas). Shows classical UC feasibility/completeness results carry over to quantum computational setting; differences remain in information-theoretic case.
- **77850295.pdf** — Languages with Efficient Zero-Knowledge PCPs are in SZK (Mahmoody, Xiao). Resolves open question: any language with efficient ZK-PCPs (even adaptive) lies in SZK, ruling them out for NP-complete problems unless PH collapses.
- **77850315.pdf** — Succinct Non-Interactive Arguments via Linear Interactive Proofs (Bitansky, Chiesa, Ishai, Paneth, Ostrovsky). Builds preprocessing SNARGs from linear interactive proofs, yielding publicly-verifiable succinct arguments without PCPs.
- **77850334.pdf** — Unprovable Security of Perfect NIZK and Non-interactive Non-malleable Commitments (Pass). Black-box barriers to proving adaptive soundness of statistical NIZK and non-malleability of non-interactive commitments; proposes taxonomy of assumptions.
- **77850354.pdf** — Secure Computation for Big Data (Malkin). One-page invited-talk abstract on making secure computation practical for big-data applications; not a full research paper.
- **77850355.pdf** — Communication Locality in Secure Multi-Party Computation (Boyle, Goldwasser, Tessaro). MPC protocols where each party communicates with only polylog(n) others; supports secure sublinear distributed algorithms.
- **77850375.pdf** — Distributed Oblivious RAM for Secure Two-Party Computation (Lu, Ostrovsky). Two-server ORAM with O(log n) amortized overhead avoiding oblivious sorting; enables RAM-model secure two-party computation without circuit conversion.
- **77850435.pdf** — Limits on the Usefulness of Random Oracles (Haitner, Omri, Zarosim). Extends Impagliazzo–Rudich-style separations, showing limits on what can be built black-box in the random oracle model.
- **77850455.pdf** — Analyzing Graphs with Node Differential Privacy (Kasiviswanathan, Nissim, Raskhodnikova, Smith). Node-differentially-private graph algorithms via degree-bounded projections, with accuracy analysis on realistic networks.
- **77850475.pdf** — Universally Composable Synchronous Computation (Katz, Maurer, Tackmann, Zikas). New UC formulation of synchrony via bounded-delay network and clock functionalities, fixing shortcomings of prior models.
- **77850497.pdf** — Multi-Client Non-Interactive Verifiable Computation (Choi, Katz, Kumaresan, Cid). Extends Gennaro et al.'s verifiable computation to multiple weak clients outsourcing joint computations without interacting with each other.
- **77850517.pdf** — On the Feasibility of Extending Oblivious Transfer (Lindell, Zarosim). Nearly complete answers on OT extension: necessity of one-way functions, adaptive security, and extending from O(log n) base OTs.
- **77850537.pdf** — Computational Soundness of Coinductive Symbolic Security under Active Attacks (Hajiabadi, Kapron). First steps extending Micciancio's coinductive symbolic analysis to active adversaries under IND-CCA2 encryption.
- **77850557.pdf** — Revisiting Lower and Upper Bounds for Selective Decommitments (Ostrovsky, Rao, Scafuro, Visconti). Points out errors in Xiao's TCC 2011 selective-opening commitment claims and gives new lower/upper bounds.
- **77850577.pdf** — On the Circular Security of Bit-Encryption (Rothblum). Obstacles to proving circular security of bit encryption: counterexample under multilinear SXDH-type assumption and black-box impossibility.
- **77850597.pdf** — Cryptographic Hardness of Random Local Functions – Survey (Applebaum). One-page survey/talk abstract on constant-parallel cryptography via random local functions (Goldreich-style); not a full paper.
- **77850598.pdf** — On the Power of Correlated Randomness in Secure Computation (Ishai, Kushilevitz, Meldgaard, Orlandi, Paskin-Cherniavsky). Positive/negative results on communication-minimal and perfectly secure MPC with correlated randomness, no honest majority.
- **77850618.pdf** — Constant-Overhead Secure Computation of Boolean Circuits using Preprocessing (Damgård, Zakarias). First constant-overhead dishonest-majority MPC for Boolean circuits via homomorphic authentication from good codes.
- **77850639.pdf** — Implementing Resettable UC-functionalities with Untrusted Tamper-proof Hardware-Tokens (Döttling, Mie, Müller-Quade, Nilges). Protocols realizing general UC functionalities from untrusted resettable tokens, minimizing interaction and token count.
- **77850659.pdf** — A Cookbook for Black-Box Separations and a Recipe for UOWHFs (Barhum, Holenstein). General framework for fully black-box lower bounds; yields Ω(n/log n) query lower bound for UOWHF from one-way functions.
- **77850679.pdf** — Algebraic (Trapdoor) One-Way Functions and their Applications (Catalano, Fiore, Gennaro, Vamvourellis). Formalizes homomorphic "algebraic" OWFs from RSA/factoring/CDH, with applications to verifiable computation and more.
- **77850699.pdf** — Randomness-Dependent Message Security (Birrell, Chung, Pass, Telang). Systematic study of RDM security: full RDM impossible, bounded-RDM achievable via randomness preprocessing, black-box barriers for PKE.
- **77850719.pdf** — Errata to (Nearly) round-optimal black-box constructions of commitments secure against selective opening attacks (Xiao). Two-page errata notice summarizing proof errors found by Ostrovsky et al. (companion to 77850557.pdf).
- **78810001.pdf** — Candidate Multilinear Maps from Ideal Lattices (Garg, Gentry, Halevi). Landmark plausible multilinear-map constructions from ideal lattices (NTRU-like), enabling multipartite Diffie–Hellman and other applications.
- **78810018.pdf** — Lossy Codes and a New Variant of the Learning-With-Errors Problem (Döttling, Müller-Quade). First worst-to-average-case reduction for LWE with uniform errors via lossy codes, at cost of bounded sample count.
- **78810035.pdf** — A Toolkit for Ring-LWE Cryptography (Lyubashevsky, Peikert, Regev). Fast modular algorithms and analysis for arbitrary cyclotomic rings, broadening ring-LWE applications beyond power-of-two cyclotomics.
- **78810054.pdf** — Regularity of Lossy RSA on Subdomains and its Applications (Lewko, O'Neill, Smith). Bounds on regularity of lossy RSA map under Φ-Hiding, implying hardcore-bit, PKCS #1 v1.5, and RSA-OAEP security results.
- **78810071.pdf** — Efficient Cryptosystems From 2^k-th Power Residue Symbols (Joye, Libert). Generalizes Goldwasser–Micali using 2^k-th power residue symbols for bandwidth-efficient encryption; yields efficient QR-based lossy TDF.
- **78810088.pdf** — Deterministic Public-Key Encryption for Adaptively Chosen Plaintext Distributions (Raghunathan, Segev, Vadhan). Deterministic PKE security when adversaries adaptively choose unpredictable plaintext distributions after seeing the public key.
- **78810105.pdf** — How to Watermark Cryptographic Functions (Nishimaki). Defines and constructs watermarking for cryptographic functions; DLIN-based lossy trapdoor function watermarking via dual pairing vector spaces.
- **78810122.pdf** — Security Evaluations Beyond Computing Power (Veyrat-Charvillon, Gérard, Standaert). Key-rank estimation algorithm giving tight bounds on post-leakage security of devices under standard side-channel attacks.
- **78810139.pdf** — Masking against Side-Channel Attacks: a Formal Security Proof (Prouff, Rivain). Formal proof that masking order-d block cipher implementations leak negligible information under noisy-leakage model with refreshing.
- **78810158.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Cryptography from Minimal Assumptions (Hazay, López-Alt, Wee, Wichs). First leakage-resilient PKE from any PKE and leakage-resilient symmetric primitives from any one-way function.
- **78810175.pdf** — Faster index calculus for the medium prime case (Joux). "Pinpointing" technique speeds relation construction in medium-characteristic finite fields; demonstrated with 1175-bit and 1425-bit DLP records.
- **78810192.pdf** — Fast Cryptography in Genus 2 (Bos, Costello, Hisil, Lauter). Genus-2 curve implementations on Kummer surfaces with 4-D GLV; sets constant-time 128-bit-security scalar multiplication speed records.
- **78810209.pdf** — Graph-Theoretic Algorithms for the "Isomorphism of Polynomials" Problem (Bouillaguet, Fouque, Véber). New birthday-paradox algorithms breaking IP problem instances in time q^{2n/3} (rigorous) and q^{n/2} (heuristic).
- **78810226.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Full RIPEMD-128 (Landelle, Peyrin). First collision attack on full RIPEMD-128 compression function and first distinguisher on the full hash, via branch-separated differential paths.
- **78810243.pdf** — New collision attacks on SHA-1 based on optimal joint local-collision analysis (Stevens). Optimal combination of dependent local collisions; near-collision at 2^57.5, identical-prefix collision at ~2^61, chosen-prefix at ~2^77.1.
- **78810260.pdf** — Improving Local Collisions: New Attacks on Reduced SHA-256 (Mendel, Nad, Schläffer). Practical 28-step collision and 31-step two-block collision (2^65.5) on SHA-256 via larger local collisions and improved automated search.
- **78810277.pdf** — Dynamic Proofs of Retrievability via Oblivious RAM (Cash, Küpçü, Wichs). First proofs of retrievability supporting dynamic updates, built on oblivious RAM techniques.
- **78810294.pdf** — Message-Locked Encryption and Secure Deduplication (Bellare, Keelveedhi, Ristenpart). Formalizes MLE for secure cloud deduplication with privacy and tag-consistency definitions; ROM analyses and standard-model constructions.
- **78810311.pdf** — Keccak (Bertoni, Daemen, Peeters, Van Assche). Two-page summary of Keccak's selection as SHA-3, the sponge and duplex constructions; overview, not a full research paper.
- **78810313.pdf** — Batch Fully Homomorphic Encryption over the Integers (Cheon, Coron, Kim, Lee, Lepoint, Tibouchi, Yun). SIMD-style batching for DGHV integer FHE under approximate-GCD assumptions; AES homomorphic evaluation at ~12 min amortized.
- **78810334.pdf** — Practical Homomorphic MACs for Arithmetic Circuits (Catalano, Fiore). Efficient homomorphic message authenticators for bounded-degree arithmetic circuits, from OWFs or Diffie–Hellman inversion assumptions.
- **78810351.pdf** — Streaming Authenticated Data Structures (Papamanthou, Shi, Tamassia, Yi). Verifiable computation over data streams with small verifier state; exponentially faster prover (O(log M log n)) than prior streaming schemes.
