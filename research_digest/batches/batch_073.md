# Batch 073 digest

## Overview
- Batch consists almost entirely of TCC 2007 (filenames 43920xxx) and PKC 2007 (44500xxx) proceedings papers — 2007-era provable-security cryptography.
- Dominant themes: signature variants (blind, ring, group, designated confirmer, threshold, fair exchange), program obfuscation (3 papers), secret sharing, and secure computation protocols.
- Notable papers: Hohenberger–Rothblum–shelat–Vaikuntanathan on obfuscating re-encryption; Shacham–Waters ring signatures without random oracles; Boyen–Waters constant-size group signatures; Boneh–Waters queries on encrypted data.
- Strong cryptanalysis cluster: attacks on NTRU, the PJH lattice system, braid/Thompson-group key exchange, MFE and HFE multivariate schemes.
- Five files are extraction failures (mojibake/CID-encoded garbage): 43920118, 43920174, 43920311, 43920478, and 440 (the last also has an anomalous short filename).
- No elliptic-curve/ECDLP content; pairing-based constructions appear (group/ring signatures, ABE, PKIE).

## Papers
- **43920086.pdf** — Parallel Repetition of Computationally Sound Protocols Revisited (Pietrzak, Wikström). Gives the first computationally sound protocol where k-fold parallel repetition fails to decrease soundness error, settling a question left by Bellare–Impagliazzo–Naor.
- **43920103.pdf** — Lower Bounds for Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge (Hoeteck Wee). Establishes lower bounds and impossibility results for NIZK in the common random string, registered public key, and bare public key models.
- **43920118.pdf** — Extraction failure: text is mojibake with control characters; title and content unreadable. Cannot summarize.
- **43920137.pdf** — Security Against Covert Adversaries: Efficient Protocols for Realistic Adversaries (Aumann, Lindell). Introduces the covert-adversary model between semi-honest and malicious, with efficient multiparty protocols secure under deterrence-based definitions.
- **43920157.pdf** — On the Necessity of Rewinding in Secure Multiparty Computation (Backes, Müller-Quade, Unruh). Shows stand-alone information-theoretic security with black-box simulators does not imply concurrent composition, via a rewinding-vs-straight-line separation.
- **43920174.pdf** — Extraction failure: text is mojibake with control characters; title and content unreadable. Cannot summarize.
- **43920194.pdf** — On Best-Possible Obfuscation (Goldwasser, Rothblum). Defines best-possible obfuscation, relaxing black-box virtual-simulation so an obfuscated program leaks no more than any equivalent program of similar size.
- **43920214.pdf** — Obfuscation for Cryptographic Purposes (Hofheinz, Malone-Lee, Stam). Proposes a new obfuscation definition usable for cryptography, showing obfuscated secret-key encryption yields public-key encryption and random point functions are obfuscatable.
- **43920233.pdf** — Securely Obfuscating Re-Encryption (Hohenberger, Rothblum, shelat, Vaikuntanathan). First positive obfuscation result for a nontrivial cryptographic functionality: a re-encryption program, under a new security-aware obfuscation definition.
- **43920253.pdf** — Weakly-Private Secret Sharing Schemes (Beimel, Franklin). Studies secret sharing where unauthorized sets cannot rule out any secret; gives surprisingly small shares, (ℓ+c) bits, for general access structures.
- **43920273.pdf** — On Secret Sharing Schemes, Matroids and Polymatroids (Martí-Farré, Padró). Connects matroid ports to ideal access structures; proves schemes with information rate above 2/3 are matroid-related and derives polymatroid bounds.
- **43920291.pdf** — Secure Linear Algebra Using Linearly Recurrent Sequences (Kiltz, Mohassel, Weinreb, Franklin). Two-party protocols deciding singularity, rank, determinant of encrypted matrices in O(log n) rounds via Wiedemann-style recurrences.
- **43920311.pdf** — Extraction failure: text is mojibake with control characters; title and content unreadable. Cannot summarize.
- **43920323.pdf** — Concurrently-Secure Blind Signatures without Random Oracles or Setup Assumptions (Hazay, Katz, Koo, Lindell). First blind signature protocol proven secure under concurrent executions from standard assumptions, without random oracles or trusted setup.
- **43920342.pdf** — Designated Confirmer Signatures Revisited (Wikström). Fixes incomplete prior security definitions and gives a practical scheme under strong RSA, DCR, and DDH assumptions without random oracles.
- **43920362.pdf** — From Weak to Strong Watermarking (Hopper, Molnar, Wagner). Provides complexity-theoretic security definitions for watermarking and shows how to strengthen schemes meeting weaker practical goals.
- **43920382.pdf** — Private Approximation of Clustering and Vertex Cover (Beimel, Hallak, Nissim). Proves infeasibility of private approximation: vertex cover and k-center/k-median approximations must leak Ω(n) or related numbers of bits.
- **43920403.pdf** — Robuster Combiners for Oblivious Transfer (Meier, Przydatek, Wullschleger). Introduces asymmetric robust combiners for two-party primitives, yielding stronger OT combiners tolerating one-sidedly flawed candidates.
- **43920418.pdf** — One-Way Permutations, Interactive Hashing and Statistically Hiding Commitments (Hoeteck Wee). Proves an Ω(n/log n) round lower bound for black-box constructions of statistically hiding commitments from one-way permutations, matching Naor et al.
- **43920433.pdf** — Towards a Separation of Semantic and CCA Security for Public Key Encryption (Gertner, Malkin, Myers). Black-box separation: CCA constructions whose decryption never queries the semantic-security primitive's encryption cannot be CCA secure.
- **43920455.pdf** — Unifying Classical and Quantum Key Distillation (Christandl, Ekert, Horodecki, Horodecki, Oppenheim, Renner). Unifies key distillation from classical correlations and pure quantum states, giving upper bounds on key rates for tripartite states.
- **43920478.pdf** — Extraction failure: CID-encoded glyph codes (/C1/D2/…) with no font mapping; title and content unreadable. Cannot summarize.
- **43920498.pdf** — (Password) Authenticated Key Establishment: From 2-Party To Group (Abdalla, Bohli, González Vasco, Steinwandt). Compiler turning any secure 2-party key establishment into group key establishment in 2 extra rounds, using CRS-based non-malleable commitments.
- **43920514.pdf** — Multi-Authority Attribute Based Encryption (Chase). First ABE scheme where any polynomial number of independent authorities distribute attributes, tolerating arbitrary authority corruption.
- **43920534.pdf** — Conjunctive, Subset, and Range Queries on Encrypted Data (Boneh, Waters). Public-key systems supporting comparison, subset, and conjunctive queries on encrypted data via tokens, with a general analysis framework.
- **43920554.pdf** — How To Shuffle in Public (Adida, Wikström). Obfuscates decryption/re-encryption shuffles (BGN and Paillier based) so mixing becomes public, yielding a UC mix-net via verifiable joint decryption.
- **43920574.pdf** — Evaluating Branching Programs on Encrypted Data (Ishai, Paskin). Public-key scheme evaluating branching programs on encrypted inputs with ciphertext size independent of program size; also hides the program.
- **440.pdf** — Extraction failure: CID-encoded glyph codes, no readable text (anomalous short filename). Cannot summarize.
- **44500001.pdf** — Full-Domain Subgroup Hiding and Constant-Size Group Signatures (Boyen, Waters). Short constant-size group signatures fully secure in the standard model in bilinear groups, via a new NIZK technique hiding full-domain integers.
- **44500016.pdf** — A Direct Anonymous Attestation Scheme for Embedded Devices (Ge, Tate). Efficient DAA construction with all TCG features, suited to constrained devices, secure under strong RSA and DDH.
- **44500031.pdf** — Anonymous Signatures Made Easy (Fischlin). General, efficient transform building anonymous signatures (hiding signer identity) from ordinary schemes, with both random-oracle and standard-model instantiations.
- **44500043.pdf** — On the Generic and Efficient Constructions of Secure Designated Confirmer Signatures (Wang, Baek, Wong, Bao). Identifies two flaws in Gentry–Molnar–Ramzan's DCS transform and proposes a secure repair plus new constructions.
- **44500061.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Group-based Key Agreement Protocols Using Subgroup Distance Functions (Ruinskiy, Shamir, Tsaban). Breaks roughly half of keys of the Shpilrain–Ushakov Thompson-group protocol in seconds using subgroup distance functions.
- **44500076.pdf** — Length Based Attack and Braid Groups: Cryptanalysis of Anshel-Anshel-Goldfeld Key Exchange Protocol (Myasnikov, Ushakov). Implements the length-based attack against AAG, breaking random instances of the simultaneous conjugacy problem for certain parameters.
- **44500089.pdf** — New Chosen-Ciphertext Attacks on NTRU (Gama, Nguyen). Efficient key-recovery CCA attacks on NTRUencrypt exploiting decryption failures; a single failure can reveal the key for NTRU-1998 parameters.
- **44500107.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the Paeng-Jung-Ha Cryptosystem from PKC 2003 (Han, Kim, Yeom). Lattice key-recovery attack on the GGH/NTRU-hybrid PJH system, recovering keys for n=1001 in minutes.
- **44500118.pdf** — Optimistic Fair Exchange in a Multi-User Setting (Dodis, Lee, Yum). Separates single-user from multi-user security for optimistic fair exchange, defines the first complete multi-user model, and validates known paradigms.
- **44500134.pdf** — Multi-party Stand-alone and Setup-free Verifiably Committed Signatures (Zhu, Susilo, Mu). Shows a two-party/multi-party security gap for verifiably committed signatures and gives a setup-free multi-party construction with proofs.
- **44500150.pdf** — Knowledge-Binding Commitments with Applications in Time-Stamping (Buldas, Laur). Proves bounded list and set commitments are knowledge-binding, simplifying time-stamping security definitions and giving √N-more-efficient reductions.
- **44500166.pdf** — Efficient Ring Signatures Without Random Oracles (Shacham, Waters). First efficient ring signature secure under standard assumptions without random oracles, in bilinear groups, with 2ℓ+2 group elements per signature.
- **44500181.pdf** — Traceable Ring Signature (Fujisaki, Suzuki). Ring signatures with tag-based linkability: double-signing on an issue exposes identity, suitable for anonymous voting; includes formal security definitions.
- **44500201.pdf** — Two-Tier Signatures, Strongly Unforgeable Signatures, and Fiat-Shamir without Random Oracles (Bellare, Shoup). Standard-model Fiat-Shamir yielding two-tier signatures, generic strong-unforgeability transforms, and new one-time signatures.
- **44500217.pdf** — Improved On-Line/Off-Line Threshold Signatures (Bresson, Catalano, Gennaro). Alternative threshold version of hash-sign-switch on-line/off-line signatures avoiding the extra assumptions of Crutchfield et al.
- **44500233.pdf** — High Order Linearization Equation (HOLE) Attack on Multivariate Public Key Cryptosystems (Ding, Hu, Nie, Li, Wagner). Breaks the MFE multivariate system via second-order linearization equations and generalizes to higher-order attacks.
- **44500249.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of HFE with Internal Perturbation (Dubois, Granboulan, Stern). Differential attack exposing the perturbation kernel of internally perturbed HFE, enabling inversion of practical parameter sets.
- **44500266.pdf** — ℓ-Invertible Cycles for Multivariate Quadratic (MQ) Public Key Cryptography (Ding, Wolf, Yang). Proposes the ℓIC trapdoor, first new MQ trapdoor since Oil-and-Vinegar, extending Matsumoto–Imai with competitive speed.
- **44500282.pdf** — Chosen-Ciphertext Secure Key-Encapsulation Based on Gap Hashed Diffie-Hellman (Kiltz). Practical CCA-secure KEM proven in the standard model under the new GHDH assumption, yielding very efficient hybrid encryption.
- **44500298.pdf** — Parallel Key-Insulated Public Key Encryption Without Random Oracles (Libert, Quisquater, Yung). First standard-model parallel key-insulated encryption, built from bilinear maps via a connection to aggregate signatures.
- **44500315.pdf** — Multi-Bit Cryptosystems Based on Lattice Problems (Kawachi, Tanaka, Xagawa). Universal technique giving multi-bit versions of Ajtai–Dwork/Regev/Ajtai lattice cryptosystems without ciphertext expansion; identifies pseudohomomorphism.
- **44500330.pdf** — Practical and Secure Solutions for Integer Comparison (Garay, Schoenmakers, Villegas). Logarithmic- and constant-round protocols for comparing encrypted integers (Yao's millionaires' variant) based on threshold homomorphic encryption.
