# Batch 004 digest

## Overview
- This batch appears to be drawn from the EUROCRYPT 2017 proceedings (LNCS volume 10210): nearly all 50 files are full research papers from that venue/era.
- Dominant themes: secure multiparty computation (cut-and-choose, round complexity, UC models), indistinguishability obfuscation and functional encryption, and lattice-based cryptography (attacks and constructions).
- Notable for this corpus's ECDLP focus: 10210143 (768-bit prime-field discrete log record via NFS) and 10210146 (kilobit trapdoored-SNFS prime field DL computation); one elliptic-curve arithmetic paper (10210211, Kohel's twisted µ4-normal form over binary fields) and one SIDH/isogeny paper (10210251).
- Symmetric/side-channel cluster present: masking schemes (10210168, 10210174), cube attack on Keccak (10210176), 5-round AES property (10210215).
- Anomaly: 10210179.pdf is a 1-page file with no extractable text — content unknown.

## Papers
- **10210124.pdf** — High-Throughput Secure Three-Party Computation for Malicious Adversaries and an Honest Majority (Furukawa, Lindell, Nof, Weinstein). Malicious-secure 3PC from the Araki et al. semi-honest protocol, using cut-and-choose Beaver triple verification with very low communication.
- **10210130.pdf** — Amortized Complexity of Zero-Knowledge Proofs Revisited: Achieving Linear Soundness Slack (Cramer, Damgård, Xing, Yuan). ZK proofs of knowledge of short preimages under homomorphic functions with O(n) amortized communication and improved extraction bounds; applies to (Ring-)LWE.
- **10210134.pdf** — Concurrently Composable Security With Shielded Super-polynomial Simulators (Broadnax, Döttling, Hartung, Müller-Quade, Nagel). New composable framework where super-polynomial resources are modeled as shielded oracles, sitting strictly between SPS and Angel-based security.
- **10210137.pdf** — Improved Private Set Intersection against Malicious Adversaries (Rindal, Rosulek). Fixes a bug in Dong–Chen–Wen's Bloom-filter PSI via efficient cut-and-choose; 14–110× faster than prior work with implementation.
- **10210140.pdf** — Non-Interactive Secure 2PC in the Offline/Online and Batch Settings (Mohassel, Rosulek). Round-optimal (2-message) malicious-secure 2PC combining batch cut-and-choose with an O(log N) garbled-circuit reduction.
- **10210143.pdf** — Computation of a 768-bit prime field discrete logarithm (Kleinjung, Diem, Lenstra, Priplata, Stahlke). Records the NFS computation of a 768-bit prime-field DL, compares effort to 768-bit RSA factorization, and discusses security implications for DSA/ElGamal.
- **10210146.pdf** — A kilobit hidden SNFS discrete logarithm computation (Fried, Gaudry, Heninger, Thomé). First 1024-bit prime-field SNFS DL record on a deliberately trapdoored DSA-style prime; shows hidden-SNFS backdoored primes are feasible and hard to detect.
- **10210149.pdf** — Patchable Indistinguishability Obfuscation: iO for Evolving Software (Ananth, Jain, Sahai). Defines iO where obfuscated software can be updated by arbitrary Turing-machine patches, including multi-program patching.
- **10210151.pdf** — Random Sampling Revisited: Lattice Enumeration with Discrete Pruning (Aono, Nguyen). Generalizes Schnorr's random sampling via discrete pruning and gives the first sound analysis under the Gaussian heuristic.
- **10210160.pdf** — Adaptive partitioning (Hofheinz). New tight-reduction strategy yielding compact tightly-CCA-secure PKE from DLIN and the first tightly CCA-secure scheme from DCR.
- **10210168.pdf** — Parallel Implementations of Masking Schemes and the Bounded Moment Leakage Model (Barthe, Dupressoir, Faust, Grégoire, Standaert, Strub). Introduces the bounded-moment leakage model; proves serial probing security implies bounded-moment security for parallel masked implementations.
- **10210169.pdf** — On dual lattice attacks against small-secret LWE and parameter choices in HElib and SEAL (Albrecht). BKW-informed dual-attack variants on short-secret LWE that lower concrete security estimates of HElib and SEAL parameter sets (e.g., 80→62–68 bits).
- **10210174.pdf** — How Fast Can Higher-Order Masking Be in Software? (Goudarzi, Rivain). Optimized ARM assembly implementations of ISW/CPRR masking and s-box polynomial decomposition to make higher-order masking practical.
- **10210176.pdf** — Conditional Cube Attack on Reduced-Round Keccak Sponge Function (Huang, Wang, Xu, Wang, Zhao). New conditional cube tester distinguishing technique improving key-recovery attacks on Keccak-MAC and Keyak.
- **10210179.pdf** — Anomaly: 1-page file with no extractable text; content unknown, cannot summarize.
- **10210182.pdf** — Relativistic (or 2-prover 1-round) zero-knowledge protocol for NP secure against quantum adversaries (Chailloux, Leverrier). Proves the Hamiltonian-Cycle ZK construction secure against quantum adversaries in the relativistic setting, with bounds for parallel string/bit commitment.
- **10210186.pdf** — The Multi-User Security of Double Encryption (Hoang, Tessaro). Tight ideal-cipher-model bounds showing double encryption degrades only mildly with user count, unlike single encryption; general lifting technique.
- **10210189.pdf** — Decentralized Anonymous Micropayments (Chiesa, Green, Liu, Miao, Miers, Mishra). Constructs decentralized anonymous micropayments via offline probabilistic payments extending Zerocash techniques over a public ledger.
- **10210192.pdf** — 0-RTT Key Exchange with Full Forward Secrecy (Günther, Hale, Jager, Lauer). 0-RTT key exchange (QUIC/TLS 1.3 style) achieving forward secrecy and replay protection for the first payload, contrary to folklore impossibility.
- **10210196.pdf** — Breaking the Sub-Exponential Barrier in Obfustopia (Garg, Pandey, Srinivasan, Zhandry). New techniques to avoid sub-exponential loss in security reductions when applying iO, with implications for functional encryption.
- **10210201.pdf** — Analysis of the Blockchain Protocol in Asynchronous Networks (Pass, Seeman, shelat). Proves Nakamoto consensus achieves consistency and liveness in asynchronous networks with bounded adversarial delays and adaptive player churn.
- **10210202.pdf** — Public-Seed Pseudorandom Permutations (Soni, Tessaro). Introduces standard-model public-seed PRPs, relates them to UCE notions, and gives instantiations from block ciphers and keyless permutations.
- **10210205.pdf** — Quantum-Secure Symmetric-Key Cryptography Based on Hidden Shifts (Alagic, Russell). Replaces XOR with alternate finite-group operations in qCPA-broken symmetric schemes, grounding security in the quantum Hidden Shift problem.
- **10210206.pdf** — Toward Fine-Grained Blackbox Separations Between Semantic and Circular-Security Notions (Hajiabadi, Kapron). Rules out fully blackbox constructions of 1-seed-circular bit encryption from CPA security and t-circular from (t−1)-circular security.
- **10210211.pdf** — Twisted µ4-normal form for elliptic curves (Kohel). New twisted normal form for binary-field elliptic curves with 9M+2S addition and near-optimal doubling, plus side-channel-friendly Montgomery scalar multiplication.
- **10210212.pdf** — Modifying an Enciphering Scheme after Deployment (Grubbs, Ristenpart, Yarom). Zig-Zag construction to alter deployed length-preserving ciphers (e.g., FPE) while preserving decryption of old ciphertexts, with SPRP-based proofs.
- **10210215.pdf** — A New Structural-Differential Property of 5-Round AES (Grassi, Rechberger, Rønjom). Key-independent differential property for up to 5 rounds of AES (output-pair differences hitting a subspace a multiple of 8 times), proved and applied to distinguishers.
- **10210216.pdf** — Boolean Searchable Symmetric Encryption with Worst-Case Sub-Linear Complexity (Kamara, Moataz). IEX construction giving non-interactive SSE for arbitrary Boolean queries with worst-case sub-linear search and optimal communication.
- **10210232.pdf** — Lattice-Based SNARGs and Their Application to More Efficient Obfuscation (Boneh, Ishai, Sahai, Wu). First lattice-based quasi-optimal SNARG candidate via linear-only vector encryption and linear PCPs, with applications to obfuscation efficiency.
- **10210233.pdf** — Scrypt is Maximally Memory-Hard (Alwen, Chen, Pietrzak, Reyzin, Tessaro). Proves scrypt's cumulative memory complexity is Ω(n²w) in the parallel random oracle model, i.e., optimally memory-hard.
- **10210234.pdf** — Functional Encryption: Deterministic to Randomized Functions from Simple Assumptions (Agrawal, Wu). Black-box transformation turning any public-key FE for deterministic functions into FE for randomized functions from DDH/RSA-type assumptions.
- **10210240.pdf** — A Note on Perfect Correctness by Derandomization (Bitansky, Vaikuntanathan). General compiler converting noticeably-correct PKE/iO/MPC schemes into perfectly correct ones via reverse randomization and NW-style derandomization.
- **10210241.pdf** — Computing generator in cyclotomic integer rings (Biasse, Espitau, Fouque, Gélin, Kirchner). Heuristic subexponential L(1/2) algorithm for the Principal Ideal Problem in prime-power cyclotomic fields, with cryptanalytic application to a FHE scheme.
- **10210251.pdf** — Efficient compression of SIDH public keys (Costello, Jao, Longa, Naehrig, Renes, Urbanik). Speeds up SIDH public-key compression by over an order of magnitude, making compressed keys nearly free relative to an SIDH round.
- **10210256.pdf** — Formal Abstractions for Attested Execution Secure Processors (Pass, Shi, Tramèr). Formalizes attested-execution secure processors; shows they enable stateful obfuscation but that composable 2PC needs secure processors on both sides.
- **10210257.pdf** — Cryptanalyses of Candidate Branching Program Obfuscators (Chen, Gentry, Halevi). Attacks on GGHRSW-type branching-program obfuscation over GGH13/GGH15 graded encodings, recovering plaintext ideals and bundling-scalar ratios for total breaks.
- **10210258.pdf** — Separating Semantic and Circular Security for Symmetric-Key Bit Encryption from the LWE Assumption (Goyal, Koppula, Waters). Separates 1-circular from semantic security for bit encryption using only LWE, via oblivious branching-program evaluation.
- **10210270.pdf** — Cryptography with Updates (Ananth, Cohen, Jain). Systematic updatable-cryptography framework via updatable randomized encodings, applying to ABE, FE, iO, ZK proofs, and MPC.
- **10210276.pdf** — Robust Transforming Combiners from Indistinguishability Obfuscation to Functional Encryption (Ananth, Jain, Sahai). Robust combiners that build secure iO (then FE/NIKE) from a set of candidates of which only one must be secure, under one-way functions.
- **10210278.pdf** — Unconditional UC-Secure Computation with (Stronger-Malicious) PUFs (Badrinarayanan, Khurana, Ostrovsky, Visconti). Unconditional UC-secure computation even when adversaries create bounded-state or PUF-encapsulating malicious PUFs.
- **10210281.pdf** — Private Puncturable PRFs From Standard Lattice Assumptions (Boneh, Kim, Montgomery). First privately puncturable PRF from LWE and 1D-SIS, hiding the punctured point, extending the Brakerski–Vaikuntanathan PRF.
- **10210287.pdf** — Ad Hoc PSM Protocols: Secure Computation Without Coordination (Beimel, Ishai, Kushilevitz). Private Simultaneous Messages protocols where any unknown subset of k out of n parties may participate; constructions for NC1 and general functions.
- **10210288.pdf** — Hashing Garbled Circuits for Free (Fan, Ganesh, Kolesnikov). "Free Hash" obtains garbled-circuit hashes at no extra cost during GC generation, removing up to 6× overhead in cut-and-choose SFE.
- **10210291.pdf** — On the Exact Round Complexity of Self-Composable Two-Party Computation (Garg, Kiyoshima, Pandey). 5-round SPS-secure 2PC under concurrent self-composition from quasi-polynomially hard injective OWFs (7 rounds from CRHFs).
- **10210292.pdf** — Simplifying Design and Analysis of Complex Predicate Encryption Schemes (Agrawal, Chase). "Symbolic security" property for pair encodings enabling concise proofs and conversions, yielding fully secure predicate encryption under a new q-type assumption.
- **10210293.pdf** — Revisiting Lattice Attacks on overstretched NTRU parameters (Kirchner, Fouque). Improved subfield variant of the Albrecht–Bai–Ducas/Cheon–Jeong–Lee attacks on overstretched NTRU, breaking concrete YASHE FHE instances.
- **10210299.pdf** — Multi-Input Inner-Product Functional Encryption from Pairings (Abdalla, Gay, Raykova, Wee). First MIFE for inner products from the standard k-Lin assumption in bilinear groups, adaptively secure against unbounded collusion.
- **10210300.pdf** — Fixing Cracks in the Concrete: Random Oracles with Auxiliary Input, Revisited (Dodis, Guo, Katz). Disproves Unruh's presampling conjecture and gives new bounds for security against preprocessing adversaries in the auxiliary-input random-oracle model.
- **10210302.pdf** — Constraint-Hiding Constrained PRFs for NC1 from LWE (Canetti, Chen). First constraint-hiding constrained PRFs for NC1 circuits from LWE via GGH15-style graph-induced maps, with application to reusable garbled circuits.
- **10210310.pdf** — Projective Arithmetic Functional Encryption and Indistinguishability Obfuscation From Degree-5 Multilinear Maps (Ananth, Sahai). Introduces projective arithmetic FE with a degree-preserving construction from multilinear maps, yielding iO from degree-5 maps.
