# Batch 066 digest

## Overview
- Two coherent conference blocks: `3378_*` files are TCC 2005 (LNCS 3378) theory papers — steganography, simulation-based/UC security (incl. quantum QKD), secure computation, privacy; `3386*` files are PKC 2005 (LNCS 3386) papers — RSA analysis, threshold RSA, password-based key exchange, multivariate cryptanalysis, identity-/certificateless-based encryption.
- Notable papers: Boneh–Goh–Nissim "Evaluating 2-DNF Formulas on Ciphertexts" (foundational partially-homomorphic encryption); Cramer–Damgård–Ishai share conversion; Renner–König UC privacy amplification against quantum adversaries; Bleichenbacher's pseudoprime attack on GNU Crypto SRP.
- Nice pair: 33860246.pdf proposes the Tractable Rational Map signature scheme and 33860261.pdf (Joux et al.) cryptanalyzes it — same venue, proposal and break.
- Anomalies: 4 of 50 files unusable — 3378_425.pdf has no extracted text (0p), and 3378_170.pdf, 3378_494.pdf, 3378_530.pdf are garbled font-mapped extractions; titles unrecoverable from the text.
- Little elliptic-curve/ECDLP content in this batch; pairing-based work appears only in the identity-based papers at the end of the PKC block.

## Papers
- **3378_170.pdf** — (?). Garbled, non-readable font-mapped extraction; title and content cannot be determined from the text.
- **3378_189.pdf** — Chosen-Ciphertext Security of Multiple Encryption (Yevgeniy Dodis, Jonathan Katz). Formalizes CCA security for multiple (layered) encryption and gives generic standard-model constructions, plus an efficient one from hierarchical IBE.
- **3378_211.pdf** — Public-Key Steganography with Active Attacks (Michael Backes, Christian Cachin). Introduces complexity-theoretic model for public-key steganography with chosen-covertext attacks (SS-CCA/SS-PDR-CCA) and constructions from PDR-CCA-secure encryption.
- **3378_228.pdf** — Upper and Lower Bounds on Black-Box Steganography (Nenad Dedić, Gene Itkis, Leonid Reyzin, Scott Russell). Shows sampling lower bound exponential in stegosystem rate and gives the first secret-key stegosystems essentially matching it, including a stateless one for high-entropy channels.
- **3378_246.pdf** — Fair-Zero Knowledge (Matt Lepinski, Silvio Micali, abhi shelat). Multi-verifier ZK guaranteeing zero-knowledge toward all verifiers even under collusion; built from quadratic residuosity with one-time private envelopes, using new Unique Zero Knowledge tools.
- **3378_265.pdf** — How To Securely Outsource Cryptographic Computations (Susan Hohenberger, Anna Lysyanskaya). Formalizes secure outsourcing to untrusted helpers and shows how to outsource modular exponentiation, reducing device load from O(n) to O(log² n) multiplications.
- **3378_284.pdf** — Secure Computation of the Mean and Related Statistics (Eike Kiltz, Gregor Leander, John Malone-Lee). Two-party protocol for securely computing the mean of a shared database; shows prior solutions leak information.
- **3378_304.pdf** — Keyword Search and Oblivious Pseudorandom Functions (Michael J. Freedman, Yuval Ishai, Benny Pinkas, Omer Reingold). Privacy-preserving keyword search via a new connection to oblivious PRF evaluation, with improved OPRF constructions.
- **3378_325.pdf** — Evaluating 2-DNF Formulas on Ciphertexts (Dan Boneh, Eu-Jin Goh, Kobbi Nissim). Homomorphic public-key encryption allowing public evaluation of 2-DNF formulas/quadratic polynomials on ciphertexts, with applications to PIR, voting, and verifiable computation.
- **3378_342.pdf** — Share Conversion, Pseudorandom Secret-Sharing and Applications to Secure Distributed Computing (Ronald Cramer, Ivan Damgård, Yuval Ishai). Local conversion of shares between secret-sharing schemes enables non-interactive pseudorandom secret-sharing and non-interactive threshold crypto protocols.
- **3378_363.pdf** — Toward Privacy in Public Databases (Shuchi Chawla, Cynthia Dwork, Frank McSherry, Adam Smith, Hoeteck Wee). Initiates theoretical study of the census problem; defines privacy compromise for statistical databases and analyzes sanitization techniques.
- **3378_385.pdf** — The Universal Composable Security of Quantum Key Distribution (Ben-Or, Horodecki, Leung, Mayers, Oppenheim). Derives a composable security definition for QKD and proves the usual unconditional security definition implies it, closing a composition gap.
- **3378_406.pdf** — Universally Composable Privacy Amplification Against Quantum Adversaries (Renato Renner, Robert König). Shows two-universal hashing yields UC-secure keys even against quantum adversaries, with optimal bounds on extractable key length; implies UC security of many QKD protocols.
- **3378_425.pdf** — No text extracted (0-page extract); content cannot be determined.
- **3378_444.pdf** — Sufficient Conditions for Collision-Resistant Hashing (Yuval Ishai, Eyal Kushilevitz, Rafail Ostrovsky). Constructs collision-resistant hash functions from homomorphic encryption, one-round PIR, and homomorphic one-way commitments.
- **3378_456.pdf** — The Relationship between Password-Authenticated Key Exchange and Other Cryptographic Primitives (Minh-Huyen Nguyen). Shows PAK and public-key encryption are black-box incomparable; builds key agreement and OT from GL-secure PAK and contrasts BPR vs GL definitions.
- **3378_475.pdf** — On the Relationships Between Notions of Simulation-Based Security (Anupam Datta, Ralf Küsters, John C. Mitchell, Ajith Ramanathan). Clarifies equivalences and separations among UC, black-box simulatability, and variants via a general computational framework.
- **3378_494.pdf** — (?). Garbled, non-readable font-mapped extraction; title and content cannot be determined from the text.
- **3378_511.pdf** — Further Simplifications in Proactive RSA Signatures (Stanisław Jarecki, Nitesh Saxena). New robust proactive/threshold RSA signature scheme with optimal threshold t < n/2, using prime-modulus sharing and integer reconstruction, with ~2x efficiency gain.
- **3378_530.pdf** — (?). Garbled, non-readable font-mapped extraction; title and content cannot be determined from the text.
- **3378_554.pdf** — Entropic Security and the Encryption of High Entropy Messages (Yevgeniy Dodis, Adam Smith). Strengthens entropic security to hide all functions of high-entropy inputs, proves equivalence to indistinguishability, and gives constructions from expanders and XOR-universal hashing.
- **3378_576.pdf** — Error Correction in the Bounded Storage Model (Yan Zong Ding). General paradigm for error-resilient private-key encryption in Maurer's bounded storage model tolerating a constant fraction of errors, via local fuzzy extractors.
- **3378_597.pdf** — Characterizing Ideal Weighted Threshold Secret Sharing (Amos Beimel, Tamir Tassa, Enav Weinreb). Characterizes all ideal weighted-threshold access structures: exactly the hierarchical threshold structures (Simmons) and certain tripartite ones.
- **33860001.pdf** — A New Related Message Attack on RSA (Oded Yacobi, Yacov Yacobi). Given e related RSA cryptograms, deterministically recovers the message in O(e) operations after amortizable preprocessing, using numerical-analysis tools on formal polynomials.
- **33860010.pdf** — Breaking a Cryptographic Protocol with Pseudoprimes (Daniel Bleichenbacher). Exploits GNU Crypto's fixed-base Miller-Rabin test by crafting pseudoprimes that pass SRP parameter checks, enabling a dictionary attack.
- **33860017.pdf** — Experimenting with Faults, Lattices and the DSA (Naccache, Nguyen, Tunstall, Whelan). First publicly reported physical glitch attack zeroing low bytes of DSA nonces on smart-cards, combined with lattice reduction to extract DSA private keys.
- **33860030.pdf** — Securing RSA-KEM via the AES (J. Jonsson, M.J.B. Robshaw). AES-based KDF for RSA-KEM whose CCA security reduces to RSA inversion assuming AES behaves as an ideal random permutation per key.
- **33860048.pdf** — One-time Verifier-based Encrypted Key Exchange (Michel Abdalla, Olivier Chevassut, David Pointcheval). Proves forward secrecy of the AuthA password-based key exchange and modifies it to resist attacks using captured passwords or server data.
- **33860066.pdf** — Password-Based Authenticated Key Exchange in the Three-Party Setting (Abdalla, Fouque, Pointcheval). Security notions and a generic construction for three-party password-based AKE where users share passwords only with a trusted server.
- **33860084.pdf** — On the Optimization of Side-Channel Attacks by Advanced Stochastic Methods (Werner Schindler). General stochastic-decision framework to optimize side-channel attack efficiency, illustrated with attacks on Montgomery multiplication.
- **33860105.pdf** — Symmetric Subgroup Membership Problems (Kristian Gjøsteen). Defines symmetric subgroup membership problems (related to DDH) and modifies the Cramer-Shoup framework to yield standard-model CCA-secure hybrid encryption from them.
- **33860121.pdf** — Optimizing Robustness while Generating Shared Secret Safe Primes (Emil Ong, John Kubiatowicz). Robust, efficient distributed generation of shared safe primes for Shoup-style threshold RSA, optimizing proofs of knowledge and modular arithmetic.
- **33860139.pdf** — Fast Multi-computations with Integer Similarity Strategy (Wu-Chuan Yang, D. J. Guan, Chi-Sung Laih). New integer-similarity strategy improving multiexponentiation/multi-scalar multiplication for ElGamal-like schemes, improving BSD sparse-form performance factors.
- **33860155.pdf** — Efficient Proofs of Knowledge of Discrete Logarithms and Representations in Groups with Hidden Order (Bangerter, Camenisch, Maurer). First efficient zero-knowledge proofs of knowledge of discrete logarithms and representations in hidden-order groups such as RSA groups.
- **33860173.pdf** — Efficient k-out-of-n Oblivious Transfer Schemes with Adaptive and Non-Adaptive Queries (Cheng-Kang Chu, Wen-Guey Tzeng). Two-round k-out-of-n OT with optimal message costs, unconditional receiver security, DDH-based sender security, and an adaptive variant.
- **33860185.pdf** — Converse Results to the Wiener Attack on RSA (Steinfeld, Contini, Wang, Pieprzyk). Proves Wiener's small-exponent attack succeeds only negligibly for d beyond N^{1/4+ε}, showing the bound is essentially tight, plus converses for natural extensions.
- **33860200.pdf** — RSA with Balanced Short Exponents and Its Application to Entity Authentication (Hung-Min Sun, Cheng-Ta Yang). Analyzes RSA variants with simultaneously short e and d, revisiting broken unbalanced-prime variants to derive secure balanced trade-offs.
- **33860218.pdf** — The Sampling Twice Technique for RSA-based Cryptosystems with Anonymity (Ryotaro Hayashi, Keisuke Tanaka). "Sampling twice" yields uniform ciphertext/signature distributions over RSA moduli, giving anonymous encryption, undeniable/confirmer, and ring signatures.
- **33860236.pdf** — From Fixed-Length to Arbitrary-Length RSA Encoding Schemes Revisited (Cathalo, Coron, Naccache). Builds secure arbitrary-length RSA signature encodings from fixed-length ones without requiring the encoding input to exceed the modulus size.
- **33860246.pdf** — Tractable Rational Map Signature (Wang, Hu, Lai, Chou, Yang). Proposes TRMS, a multivariate signature scheme based on tractable rational maps, with security analysis and implementation comparisons.
- **33860261.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the Tractable Rational Map Cryptosystem (Joux, Kunz-Jacques, Muller, Ricordel). Breaks the tractable rational map scheme via combinatorial weaknesses and an XL variant ("Linear Method"), inverting the trapdoor and rebuilding private keys (cryptanalysis of 33860246.pdf).
- **33860279.pdf** — Large Superfluous Keys in Multivariate Quadratic Asymmetric Systems (Christopher Wolf, Bart Preneel). Identifies equivalent private keys in MQ schemes (HFE, C*, UOV), shrinking effective key space by an order of magnitude with cryptanalytic and implementation implications.
- **33860292.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of HFEv and Internal Perturbation of HFE (Jintai Ding, Dieter Schmidt). New cryptanalysis of the HFEv multivariate scheme and a countermeasure: the internally perturbed HFE (IPHFE) cryptosystem.
- **33860306.pdf** — A Generic Scheme Based on Trapdoor One-Way Permutations with Signatures as Short as Possible (Louis Granboulan). Generic chained construction giving k-bit signatures with nearly k-bit security from any k-bit trapdoor permutation, tightly secure in the ideal-cipher model.
- **33860317.pdf** — Cramer-Damgård Signatures Revisited: Efficient Flat-Tree Signatures Based on Factoring (Dario Catalano, Rosario Gennaro). Modifies the Cramer-Damgård tree signature scheme to base security on factoring (weaker than RSA inversion) at equal efficiency.
- **33860332.pdf** — The Security of the FDH Variant of Chaum's Undeniable Signature Scheme (Ogata, Kurosawa, Heng). Classifies security of FDH-Chaum undeniable signatures across goals, attacks, and ZK levels; proves NIZK-variant security equivalent to CDH, not GDH as previously claimed.
- **33860350.pdf** — Efficient Threshold RSA Signatures with General Moduli and No Extra Assumptions (Ivan Damgård, Kasper Dupont). Robust threshold RSA signatures without trusted dealer, special moduli, or random oracles, matching best prior efficiency with an optimistic fast path.
- **33860366.pdf** — Improved Identity-Based Signcryption (Liqun Chen, John Malone-Lee). Efficient provably-secure identity-based signcryption in the random oracle model under the bilinear Diffie-Hellman assumption.
- **33860384.pdf** — Efficient Multi-Receiver Identity-Based Encryption and Its Application to Broadcast Encryption (Baek, Safavi-Naini, Susilo). Multi-receiver IBE needing only one pairing for n receivers, extended to adaptive CCA security and applied to subset-cover broadcast encryption.
- **33860402.pdf** — CBE from CL-PKE: A Generic Construction and Efficient Schemes (Sattam S. Al-Riyami, Kenneth G. Paterson). New efficient certificateless PKE under BDHP, repaired CBE definitions, and a generic CL-PKE→CBE conversion improving on Gentry's scheme.
