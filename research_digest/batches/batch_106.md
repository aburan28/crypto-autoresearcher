# Batch 106 digest

## Overview
- Two clear venue clusters: files 83830xxx are PKC 2014-era public-key papers (leakage resilience, CCA security, lattices/LWE/LPN, ABE, signatures, obfuscation); files 8424xxxx are FSE 2014-era symmetric papers (block-cipher and hash cryptanalysis, RC4/WEP attacks, MACs, masking).
- Heavy symmetric cryptanalysis coverage: Keccak/SHA-3 (3 papers), PRINCE (2 papers), LED, WIDEA, GOST/Camellia, Skein, plus generic results on Matsui's Algorithm 2, near-collisions, and known-key security.
- ECC relevance is thin: one paper (83830154) does concrete Pollard-rho cost estimates for NIST P-256, a BN curve, and genus-2 analogues — the only discrete-log work in the batch.
- Notable lattice/PQ thread: Gauss-sieve SVP record, LWE proxy re-encryption, lattice VLR group signatures, LPN CCA encryption, RKA-KDM schemes from DDH/LWE/QR/DCR.
- Anomalies: none — all 50 extracts are genuine research papers; no duplicates, no non-paper content. Two PRINCE papers (84240065, 84240085) overlap topically but are independent analyses.

## Papers
- **83830144.pdf** — Leakage-Flexible CCA-secure Public-Key Encryption: Simple Construction and Free of Pairing (Baodong Qin, Shengli Liu). Extends bounded key-leakage CCA-secure PKE to leakage rate 1-o(1) via a new Refined Subgroup Indistinguishability assumption, pairing-free.
- **83830145.pdf** — Practical Cryptanalysis of a Public-Key Encryption Scheme Based on New Multivariate Quadratic Assumptions (Albrecht, Faugère, Fitzpatrick, Perret, Todo, Xagawa). Disproves the hardness hypothesis of the HLY (PKC'12) multivariate quadratic encryption scheme.
- **83830151.pdf** — Re-encryption, functional re-encryption, and multi-hop re-encryption (Chandran, Chase, Liu, Nishimaki, Xagawa). Relaxed-correctness obfuscation framework yielding DLWE-based re-encryption obfuscators without FHE, plus first multi-hop construction.
- **83830154.pdf** — Elliptic and Hyperelliptic Curves: a Practical Security Analysis (Bos, Costello, Miele). Systematic Pollard-rho cost estimates (with automorphism speedups) for 128-bit ECDLP on NIST P-256, a BN curve, and two genus-2 curves.
- **83830155.pdf** — Achieving privacy in verifiable computation with multiple servers (Ananth, Chandran, Goyal, Kanukurthi, Ostrovsky). Input-private verifiable computation without FHE or pre-processing, using multiple non-colluding servers.
- **83830158.pdf** — On the Security of the Pre-Shared Key Ciphersuites of TLS (Li, Schäge, Yang, Kohlar, Schwenk). Defines ACCE security for pre-shared-key protocols and proves all TLS-PSK ciphersuite families secure without random oracles.
- **83830167.pdf** — Parallel Gauss Sieve Algorithm: Solving the SVP Challenge over a 128-Dimensional Ideal Lattice (Ishiguro, Kiyomoto, Miyake, Takagi). Improved parallel Gauss sieve solves the 128-dimensional TU Darmstadt ideal-lattice SVP challenge, then a record.
- **83830182.pdf** — Traceable Group Encryption (Libert, Yung, Joye, Peters). Group encryption with user-specific tracing trapdoors, letting an authority publicly trace all ciphertexts to one user without harming others' anonymity.
- **83830188.pdf** — Rounding and Chaining LLL: Finding Faster Small Roots of Univariate Polynomial Congruences (Bi, Coron, Faugère, Nguyen, Renault, Zeitoun). First significant speedups to Coppersmith's small-root algorithm via rounding and chaining in the LLL bottleneck.
- **83830189.pdf** — Related Randomness Attacks for Public Key Encryption (Paterson, Schuldt, Sibborn). Models adversarially reused/related encryption randomness; gives necessary/sufficient conditions and constructions tied to related-key PRFs and CIH.
- **83830195.pdf** — Lattice-based Group Signature Scheme with Verifier-local Revocation (Langlois, Ling, Nguyen, Wang). First VLR group signature from lattices (quantum-resistant), with logarithmic-size signatures under SIVP in the ROM.
- **83830197.pdf** — Encryption schemes secure under related-key and key-dependent message attacks (Böhl, Davies, Hofheinz). RKA-KDM secure SKE from DDH, LWE, QR, and DCR, generalizing Applebaum's LPN-based scheme; enables cheap XOR garbling.
- **83830201.pdf** — Bounded-Collusion Identity-Based Encryption from Semantically-Secure Public-Key Encryption (Tessaro, Wilson). Generic BC-IBE with no ciphertext blow-up from semantic security alone; instantiations from DDH, QR, NTRU, LWE.
- **83830202.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Signatures with Graceful Degradation (Nielsen, Venturi, Zottarel). New unforgeability notion where leakage ≥ signature size degrades gracefully to limiting the number of forgeries, not total collapse.
- **83830204.pdf** — Proxy Re-encryption from Lattices (Elena Kirshanova). Unidirectional, collusion-safe LWE-based proxy re-encryption without trusted authority, CCA-1 secure in the selective model.
- **83830206.pdf** — Verifiable Oblivious Storage (Apon, Katz, Shi, Thiruvengadam). Formalizes VOS generalizing ORAM with server-side computation and integrity; achieves constant bandwidth per query for large blocks, beating ORAM lower bounds.
- **83830209.pdf** — A Black-Box Construction of a CCA2 Encryption Scheme from a Plaintext Aware (sPA1) Encryption Scheme (Dana Dachman-Soled). Upgrades plaintext-aware weakly-simulatable PKE (e.g., Damgård ElGamal) to full CCA2 security, improving cNM-CCA1 results.
- **83830210.pdf** — On Minimal Assumptions for Sender-Deniable Public Key Encryption (Dana Dachman-Soled). Studies minimal assumptions for sender-deniable encryption, addressing the iO-based Sahai-Waters construction's reliance on obfuscation.
- **83830211.pdf** — Policy-Based Signatures (Mihir Bellare, Georg Fuchsbauer). Introduces signatures restricted to authority-specified policies with unforgeability and policy privacy; unifies prior signature variants.
- **83830214.pdf** — Enhanced Chosen-Ciphertext Security and Applications (Dachman-Soled, Fuchsbauer, Mohassel, O'Neill). Defines ECCA security (decryption oracle also returns randomness), builds it from adaptive TDFs, and applies it to PKENO.
- **83830216.pdf** — Simple Chosen-Ciphertext Security from Low-Noise LPN (Kiltz, Masny, Pietrzak). Simpler, more efficient IND-CCA PKE from low-noise LPN via a Micciancio-Peikert-style double-trapdoor adapted to LPN.
- **83830221.pdf** — Cross-Domain Secure Computation (Cho, Garg, Ostrovsky). New cross-domain MPC model with per-domain key registration; constant-round two-domain protocol, impossibility for ≥3 domains.
- **83830226.pdf** — A Framework and Compact Constructions for Non-monotonic Attribute-Based Encryption (Yamada, Attrapadung, Hanaoka, Kunihiro). Non-monotonic KP/CP-ABE with shortest ciphertext overhead, DBDH security, and first unbounded-attribute scheme.
- **83830229.pdf** — Chosen Ciphertext Security via UCE (Matsuda, Hanaoka). Studies feasibility of UCE-based CCA-secure PKE; shows negative results for instantiating Fujisaki-Okamoto with UCE plus positive constructions.
- **83830235.pdf** — Verifiable Set Operations over Outsourced Databases (Canetti, Paneth, Papadopoulos, Triandopoulos). Verifiable delegation of dynamic set operations (union/intersection/difference) with verification cost independent of set sizes.
- **83830237.pdf** — Practical Covert Authentication (Stanislaw Jarecki). First practical two-party covert (steganographic) mutual authentication: 5 rounds, O(1) exponentiations, under strong RSA/DQR/DDH.
- **83830238.pdf** — Generalizing Homomorphic MACs for Arithmetic Circuits (Catalano, Fiore, Gennaro, Nizzardo). Abstracts homomorphic MACs via limited-malleability encodings and graded encodings, improving succinctness and composability.
- **83830242.pdf** — Functional Signatures and Pseudorandom Functions (Boyle, Goldwasser, Ivan). Defines functional signatures and functional PRFs delegating signing/evaluation to ranges of functions; applications to SNARGs and delegation.
- **84240001.pdf** — Complementing Feistel Ciphers (Biryukov, Nikolić). Relaxed complementation-property related-key distinguishers; practical full-key recovery on GOST (31 related keys, 2^38) and multicollisions on Camellia-128 hash mode.
- **84240017.pdf** — On the Wrong Key Randomisation and Key Equivalence Hypotheses in Matsui's Algorithm 2 (Bogdanov, Tischhauser). Corrected statistical model for linear cryptanalysis showing prior success/data-complexity estimates overestimated attack efficiency.
- **84240037.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of WIDEA (Gaëtan Leurent). Truncated-differential distinguisher on full WIDEA (2^65), key recovery at w·2^68, and collision attack on WIDEA-8-based hashing.
- **84240052.pdf** — Towards Secure Distance Bounding? (Boureanu, Mitrokotsa, Vaudenay). Surveys distance-bounding protocols and attack models, exposes pitfalls, and proposes the provably secure SKI protocol.
- **84240065.pdf** — Reflection Cryptanalysis of PRINCE-like Ciphers (Soleimany, Blondeau, Yu, Wu, Nyberg, Zhang, Zhang, Wang). Exploits PRINCE's α-reflection to fold rounds; key recovery on full 12-round cipher for weak α classes (2^72.39 time).
- **84240085.pdf** — Security Analysis of PRINCE (Jean, Nikolić, Peyrin, Wang, Wu). First third-party analysis of PRINCE: related-key attack on full cipher, full-PRINCEcore attacks for several α, and TMD tradeoffs.
- **84240105.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Round-Reduced LED (Nikolić, Wang, Wu). Slidex, multicollision, and new random-difference chosen-key distinguishers reaching 10 of 12 steps of LED-128.
- **84240124.pdf** — Tweakable Blockciphers with Asymptotically Optimal Security (Lampe, Seurin). Extends the CRYPTO 2012 two-round TBC to r rounds, approaching the 2^n information-theoretic security bound via coupling arguments.
- **84240143.pdf** — Smashing WEP in A Passive Attack (Sepehrdad, Sušil, Vaudenay, Vuagnoux). Optimized RC4-in-WEP attacks: active key recovery in <5s with 22,500 packets; passive attack with 27,500 packets, as an Aircrack-ng patch.
- **84240167.pdf** — Full Plaintext Recovery Attack on Broadcast RC4 (Isobe, Ohigashi, Watanabe, Morii). New strong biases in the first 257 RC4 keystream bytes enable broadcast plaintext recovery with 2^32 ciphertexts.
- **84240191.pdf** — Time-memory Trade-offs for Near-collisions (Gaëtan Leurent). Generic near-collision algorithms combining truncation and covering codes; 10-near-collision on MD5 at 2^45.4 with 1TB memory.
- **84240206.pdf** — Collision Attacks on Up to 5 Rounds of SHA-3 Using Generalized Internal Differentials (Dinur, Dunkelman, Shamir). First published collisions on 3-round Keccak-384/512 and attacks up to 5 rounds via squeeze attacks.
- **84240228.pdf** — Rotational cryptanalysis of round-reduced Keccak (Morawiecki, Pieprzyk, Srebrny). Rotational techniques give a 4-round Keccak preimage attack and a 5-round distinguisher on Keccak-f[1600].
- **84240251.pdf** — Partial-Collision Attack on the Round-Reduced Compression Function of Skein-256 (Yu, Chen, Wang). Rebound plus modular differential techniques yield free-start partial collisions on 32-round Skein-256 (2^85).
- **84240269.pdf** — On Weak Keys and Forgery Attacks against Polynomial-based MAC Schemes (Procter, Cid). Algebraic structure of polynomial-evaluation MACs yields general forgeries; unifies GCM attacks and greatly expands weak-key classes.
- **84240283.pdf** — Secure Message Authentication against Related-Key Attack (Bhattacharyya, Roy). Formalizes related-key unpredictability for MACs; attacks XCBC/TMAC and gives first provably secure RKA domain-extension construction.
- **84240303.pdf** — Attacks and Security Proofs of EAX-Prime (Minematsu, Lucks, Morita, Iwata). Efficient attacks on ANSI C12.22's EAX′ for short inputs, but proves security when cleartexts exceed one block.
- **84240324.pdf** — Towards Understanding the Known-Key Security of Block Ciphers (Andreeva, Bogdanov, Mennink). Introduces known-key indifferentiability; proves Feistel and multiple Even-Mansour secure under it for enough rounds.
- **84240342.pdf** — On Symmetric Encryption with Distinguishable Decryption Failures (Boldyreva, Degabriele, Paterson, Stam). Security models where decryption-failure types leak, exposing surprising weaknesses in standard symmetric-encryption notions.
- **84240365.pdf** — Minimalism of Software Implementation (Matsui, Murakami). Benchmarks four block ciphers and seven hash functions on the RL78 microcontroller across ROM/RAM categories; e.g., AES at 3,855 cycles/block, 453-byte Keccak.
- **84240382.pdf** — Higher-Order Side Channel Security and Mask Refreshing (Coron, Prouff, Rivain, Roche). Shows the CHES 2010 mask-refreshing method breaks d-th order security (attackable at order ⌈d/2⌉+1) and proposes fixes.
- **84240398.pdf** — Masking Tables—An Underestimated Security Risk (Tunstall, Whitnall, Oswald). Attacks the masked-table precomputation phase of software masking schemes to recover masks; secure implementation needs substantial fresh randomness.
