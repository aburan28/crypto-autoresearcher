# Batch 022 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by recent top-venue conference papers (Eurocrypt/CRYPTO-era, ~2020, Springer LNCS style): heavy on post-quantum cryptography, especially isogeny-based (CSIDH appears four times: quantum analysis, c-sieve cost, endomorphism-ring reduction, Lossy CSI-FiSh).
- Strong zero-knowledge / proof-systems cluster: Fractal (transparent recursive SNARKs), SPARKs, statistical Zaps/ZAPRs, sequential-OR signatures, NIZKs in pairing-free groups.
- Substantial lattice/FHE/iO theory block: entropic LWE hardness, candidate iO from split-FHE, sparsifiable-input piO, tight lattice security, lattice FE impossibility, constrained/watermarked PRFs.
- ECC-relevant papers: CSIDH/CSI-FiSh isogeny work, pairing-friendly curves vs Special TNFS (Guillevic), ECM cofactorization speedups, iMessage signcryption analysis, Noise framework fACCE proofs.
- Applied/side-channel presence: Friet fault-detecting AE, Tornado masking compiler, slide attacks, quantum hash collision attacks, NIST PQC KEM oracle-cloning attacks.
- Anomalies: 12105473.pdf is Springer LLNCS LaTeX class instructions, not a research paper; 121059011.pdf is an invited-lecture essay (Silverberg) rather than a technical result.

## Papers
- **12105330.pdf** — TNT: How to Tweak a Block Cipher (Bao, Guo, Guo, Song). Builds a tweakable block cipher from three independent block ciphers by XOR-ing the tweak twice, proving beyond-birthday-bound 2^{2n/3} security with TNT-AES instantiation.
- **12105332.pdf** — Quantum Security Analysis of CSIDH (Bonnetain, Schrottenloher). Non-asymptotic cost analysis of quantum hidden-shift attacks on CSIDH; evaluates CSIDH-512 with under 40,000 logical qubits to assess proposed parameters.
- **12105335.pdf** — Continuous Verifiable Delay Functions (Ephraim, Freitag, Komargodski, Pass). Introduces cVDFs whose intermediate iterations are publicly verifiable, with applications to randomness beacons, outsourceable VDFs, and complexity-theoretic consequences.
- **12105340.pdf** — Generic-Group Delay Functions Require Hidden-Order Groups (Rotem, Segev, Shahaf). Proves no generic-group delay function exists in cyclic groups of known order: an attacker given the order breaks sequentiality.
- **12105343.pdf** — Fractal: Post-Quantum and Transparent Recursive Proofs from Holography (Chiesa, Ojha, Spooner). Recursive SNARK composition without pairing-friendly curve cycles; post-quantum, transparent preprocessing zkSNARK for R1CS via holographic proofs.
- **12105347.pdf** — Finding Hash Collisions with Quantum Computers by Using Differential Trails with Smaller Probability than Birthday Bound (Hosoyamada, Sasaki). Quantum rebound-style collision attacks exploiting low-probability trails against AES-MMO and Whirlpool.
- **12105351.pdf** — Signatures from Sequential-OR Proofs (Fischlin, Harasser, Janson). Sequential OR-proofs yield signatures secure in the non-programmable random oracle model, unlike traditional parallel OR-proofs; QROM variant also argued.
- **12105360.pdf** — Extracting Randomness from Extractor-Dependent Sources (Dodis, Vaikuntanathan, Wichs). Studies seeded extraction when the source can query the extractor with the reused seed; PRFs with high security serve as extractors.
- **12105364.pdf** — Measure-Rewind-Measure: Tighter QROM Proofs for One-Way to Hiding and CCA Security (Kuchta, Sakzad, Stehlé, Steinfeld, Sun). New MRM technique removes square-root loss in O2H, giving tighter Fujisaki-Okamoto KEM proofs.
- **12105367.pdf** — Friet: an Authenticated Encryption Scheme with Built-in Fault Detection (Simon, Batina, Daemen, Grosso, Massolino, Papagiannopoulos, Regazzoni, Samwel). Duplex AE scheme whose permutation embeds an error-correcting code to detect fault injections, with soft/hardware evaluation.
- **12105368.pdf** — New Slide Attacks on Almost Self-Similar Ciphers (Dunkelman, Keller, Lasry, Shamir). Four new slide-attack variants overcoming the last-round asymmetry problem, extending slide attacks from Feistel structures to SP networks.
- **12105380.pdf** — Formalizing Data Deletion in the Context of the Right to be Forgotten (Garg, Goldwasser, Vasudevan). Cryptographic definitional framework specifying what deletion compliance should mean for data collectors.
- **12105381.pdf** — PSI from PaXoS: Fast, Malicious Private Set Intersection (Pinkas, Rosulek, Trieu, Yanai). Malicious-secure two-party PSI with linear communication via a new probe-and-XOR data structure enabling cuckoo hashing with malicious security.
- **12105382.pdf** — Efficient Simulation of Random States and Random Unitaries (Alagic, Majenz, Russell). Stateful simulators for Haar-random states/unitaries convincing to unbounded adversaries; yields information-theoretically unforgeable quantum money.
- **12105385.pdf** — Compact Adaptively Secure ABE from k-Lin: Beyond NC1 and towards NL (Lin, Luo). Compact adaptively secure ABE for arithmetic branching programs, finite automata, and logspace Turing machines from k-Lin in pairing groups.
- **12105386.pdf** — Evolving Ramp Secret Sharing with a Small Gap (Beimel, Othman). Evolving ramp secret-sharing schemes with gap j^β and near-optimal share sizes for unbounded numbers of parties.
- **12105397.pdf** — Quantum-Access-Secure Message Authentication via Blind-Unforgeability (Alagic, Majenz, Russell, Song). Defines blind unforgeability for MACs against quantum-query adversaries, coinciding with EUF-CMA classically and supporting constructions.
- **12105400.pdf** — He Gives C-Sieves on the CSIDH (Peikert). Generalizes Kuperberg's collimation sieve to arbitrary cyclic groups, simulates and experiments up to CSIDH-512, quantifying concrete c-sieve attack costs.
- **12105401.pdf** — Hardness of LWE on General Entropic Distributions (Brakerski, Döttling). Resolves Entropic LWE hardness for arbitrary long secrets via an entropy bound tied to a lossiness property, shown essentially tight.
- **12105407.pdf** — Candidate iO From Homomorphic Encryption Schemes (Brakerski, Döttling, Garg, Malavolta). Introduces split-FHE (decryption via short hint plus public step) as sufficient for iO, with a generic candidate from LWE-FHE, DCR-based linear HE, and hashing.
- **12105411.pdf** — Rational Isogenies from Irrational Endomorphisms (Castryck, Panny, Vercauteren). Polynomial-time connecting-ideal algorithm reducing CSIDH security to computing endomorphism rings; also locates CM curves in the supersingular isogeny graph.
- **12105413.pdf** — Resource-Restricted Cryptography: Revisiting MPC Bounds in the Proof-of-Work Era (Garay, Kiayias, Ostrovsky, Panagiotakos, Zikas). Reconciles the t < n/3 BA/MPC bound with Nakamoto-style resource-majority consensus via a resource-restriction framework.
- **12105424.pdf** — Statistical ZAPR Arguments from Bilinear Maps (Lombardi, Vaikuntanathan, Wichs). Reusable two-message statistical witness-indistinguishable ZAPR arguments from quasi-polynomial DLIN on bilinear groups.
- **12105434.pdf** — An Algebraic Attack on Rank Metric Code-Based Cryptosystems (Bardet, Briaud, Bros, Gaborit, Neiger, Ruatta, Tillich). Augments the Ourivski-Johansson polynomial modelling so Gröbner bases solve at low degree, threatening ROLLO/RQC-type parameters.
- **12105435.pdf** — SPARKs: Succinct Parallelizable Arguments of Knowledge (Ephraim, Freitag, Komargodski, Pass). Arguments with prover parallel time essentially T, polylog processors and communication; interactive from CRH, non-interactive with a SNARK.
- **12105441.pdf** — Statistical Zaps and New Oblivious Transfer Protocols (Goyal, Jain, Jin, Malavolta). First statistical Zaps from quasi-polynomial LWE, plus round-optimal three-round receiver-private OT in the plain model.
- **12105444.pdf** — Impossibility Results for Lattice-Based Functional Encryption Schemes (Ünal). Proves barriers for function-hiding lattice FE under algebraic restrictions, explaining gaps versus pairing-based constructions.
- **12105446.pdf** — Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge in Pairing-Free Groups from Weaker Assumptions (Couteau, Katsumata, Ursu). NIZKs for NP from CDH plus weaker, more falsifiable KDM-style assumptions on ElGamal, without pairings.
- **12105459.pdf** — Security under Message-Derived Keys: Signcryption in iMessage (Bellare, Stepanovs). Formalizes encryption under message-derived keys and proves quantitative security of Apple's iMessage signcryption scheme.
- **12105461.pdf** — Succinct Non-Interactive Secure Computation (Morgan, Pass, Polychroniadou). First maliciously secure succinct non-interactive two-party computation with single short messages, from subexponential LWE with SPS security.
- **12105462.pdf** — Separate Your Domains: NIST PQC KEMs, Oracle Cloning and Read-Only Indifferentiability (Bellare, Davis, Günther). Key-recovery attacks on NIST PQC KEM submissions from oracle-cloning mistakes, plus a read-only indifferentiability theory of domain separation.
- **12105473.pdf** — Not a research paper: Springer LLNCS LaTeX2e document-class usage instructions (Version 2.20).
- **12105475.pdf** — Broadcast-Optimal Two-Round MPC (Cohen, Garay, Zikas). Complete feasibility/infeasibility characterization of broadcast vs point-to-point rounds for two-round MPC under identifiable, unanimous, and selective abort.
- **12105479.pdf** — Tornado: Automatic Generation of Probing-Secure Masked Bitsliced Implementations (Belaïd, Dagand, Mercadier, Rivain, Wintersdorff). Compiler integrating Usuba with tightPROVE+ to emit masked bitsliced code proven secure in the register probing model.
- **121059011.pdf** — Mathematics and Cryptography: A Marriage of Convenience? (Silverberg). Invited-lecture essay reflecting on the interplay between mathematics and cryptography communities; non-technical opinion piece.
- **12110103.pdf** — Efficient Redactable Signature and Application to Anonymous Credentials (Sanders). Constant-size redactable signatures verifiable in O(k) for k disclosed messages, with unlinkable shows yielding efficient anonymous credentials.
- **12110105.pdf** — Topology-Hiding Computation for Networks with Unknown Delays (LaVigne, Liu-Zhang, Maurer, Moran, Mularczyk, Tschudi). Introduces a model of unknown random channel delays under which topology-hiding computation is both definable and achievable.
- **12110112.pdf** — Faster Cofactorization with ECM Using Mixed Representations (Bouvier, Imbert). ECM implementation for medium-size NFS cofactors using batched primes, fast tripling, double-base chains, and mixed Edwards/Montgomery forms; fewer modular multiplications.
- **12110117.pdf** — Constraining and Watermarking PRFs from Milder Assumptions (Peikert, Shiehian). Builds (constraint-hiding) constrained PRFs and watermarking from LWE with much smaller modulus-noise ratios than prior work.
- **12110120.pdf** — The Usefulness of Sparsifiable Inputs: How to Avoid Subexponential iO (Agrikola, Couteau, Hofheinz). Probabilistic iO variant from polynomially secure iO and extremely lossy functions, yielding FHE, multi-key FHE, KDM security, spooky encryption, and FSS.
- **12110122.pdf** — Flexible Authenticated and Confidential Channel Establishment (fACCE): Analyzing the Noise Protocol Framework (Dowling, Rösler, Schwenk). First computational security proofs for Noise protocol patterns via a flexible generalization of the ACCE model.
- **12110123.pdf** — Almost Tight Security in Lattices with Polynomial Moduli — PRF, IBE, All-but-Many LTF, and More (Lai, Liu, Wang). Removes super-polynomial modulus requirements for almost tightly secure lattice PRFs, IBE, lossy trapdoor functions, and signatures.
- **12110129.pdf** — Bringing Order to Chaos: The Case of Collision-Resistant Chameleon-Hashes (Derler, Samelin, Slamanig). Systematizes collision-resistance notions for chameleon hashes, relates them, and proposes stronger notions motivated by redactable blockchains.
- **12110138.pdf** — MonZ2ka: Fast Maliciously Secure Two Party Computation on Z2k (Catalano, Di Raimondo, Fiore, Giacomelli). Dishonest-majority 2PC over Z2k using Joye-Libert encryption in preprocessing, avoiding the sacrifice step; efficient for large k.
- **12110144.pdf** — Fast, Compact, and Expressive Attribute-Based Encryption (Tomida, Kawahara, Nishimaki). KP/CP-ABE with unbounded attributes, negation, multi-use, fast decryption, adaptive security under standard assumptions, with PC/smartphone benchmarks.
- **12110145.pdf** — Public-Key Puncturable Encryption: Modular and Compact Constructions (Sun, Sakzad, Steinfeld, Liu, Gu). Generic puncturable KEM construction from key-homomorphic revocable IBE, supporting unbounded punctures and compact standard-model ciphertexts.
- **12110152.pdf** — Adaptive Simulation Security for Inner Product Functional Encryption (Agrawal, Libert, Maitra, Titiu). Resolves full adaptive SIM-based security for IPFE, beyond prior semi-adaptive or pre-challenge-only results, from DDH/DCR/LWE-type schemes.
- **12110160.pdf** — Lossy CSI-FiSh: Efficient Signature Scheme with Tight Reduction to Decisional CSIDH-512 (El Kaafarani, Katsumata, Pintore). Fiat-Shamir isogeny signature with tight security proof via lossy keys, ~280-byte signatures at ~800ms.
- **12110162.pdf** — A Short-List of Pairing-Friendly Curves Resistant to Special TNFS at the 128-Bit Security Level (Guillevic). Revises STNFS cost estimates with new polynomial selection; proposes Brezing-Weng family curves restoring 128-bit pairing security.
- **12110166.pdf** — Boosting Verifiable Computation on Encrypted Data (Fiore, Nitulescu, Pointcheval). Verifiable computation on encrypted data supporting degree >2 circuits with public delegatability and public verifiability, improving on Fiore-Gennaro-Pastro.
