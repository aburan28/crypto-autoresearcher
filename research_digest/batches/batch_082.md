# Batch 082 digest

## Overview
- Batch is two LNCS proceedings runs: TCC 2009 (LNCS 5444, 34 papers, 54440019–54440593) and EUROCRYPT 2009 (LNCS 5479, 15 papers, 54790001–54790261), plus one older standalone key-agreement paper (546.pdf).
- TCC 2009 block is dominated by secure-computation theory: complete fairness, rational secret sharing, MPC without honest majority, UC security, adaptive security, oblivious transfer, zero-knowledge, garbled circuits.
- EUROCRYPT 2009 block mixes symmetric cryptanalysis (full-MD5 preimage, HMAC/NMAC-MD5, MDC-2) with public-key results (selective opening, generic RSA vs factoring, broadcast encryption, order-preserving encryption, NICE break).
- Notable papers: Sasaki–Aoki first preimage attack on full MD5; Aggarwal–Maurer "Breaking RSA Generically is Equivalent to Factoring"; Gentry–Halevi HIBE with polynomially many levels; Nielsen–Orlandi LEGO garbled circuits.
- Anomalies: 54440162.pdf extraction was font-garbled (decoded as "Authenticated Adversarial Routing", Amir–Bunn–Ostrovsky); 54440072.pdf is only a 1-page invited-talk abstract (Peikert lattice survey); 546.pdf has a non-standard filename.
- Essentially no elliptic-curve/ECDLP content in this batch despite the corpus theme; it is almost entirely theory-of-cryptography and symmetric/public-key cryptanalysis.

## Papers
- **54440019.pdf** — Complete Fairness in Multi-Party Computation Without an Honest Majority (S. Dov Gordon, Jonathan Katz). First completely-fair MPC protocols for non-trivial functions without honest majority; evidence fairness is harder in round complexity than two-party case.
- **54440036.pdf** — Fairness with an Honest Minority and a Rational Majority (Shien Jin Ong, David C. Parkes, Alon Rosen, Salil Vadhan). Simple fair secret-reconstruction protocol for threshold sharing when many rational parties coexist with a small honest minority.
- **54440054.pdf** — Purely Rational Secret Sharing (Silvio Micali, abhi shelat). Rational secret sharing scheme removing reliance on players' beliefs, improving efficiency over prior game-theoretic constructions.
- **54440072.pdf** — Some Recent Progress in Lattice-Based Cryptography (Chris Peikert). 1-page invited-talk abstract surveying worst-case-hardness lattice crypto; not a full research paper.
- **54440073.pdf** — Non-Malleable Obfuscation (Ran Canetti, Mayank Varia). Defines two flavors of non-malleability for program obfuscation and gives constructions preventing related-program attacks.
- **54440091.pdf** — Simulation-Based Concurrent Non-Malleable Commitments and Decommitments (Rafail Ostrovsky, Giuseppe Persiano, Ivan Visconti). Commitment schemes secure against concurrent man-in-the-middle attacks under simulation-based definitions.
- **54440109.pdf** — Proofs of Retrievability via Hardness Amplification (Yevgeniy Dodis, Salil Vadhan, Daniel Wichs). Builds proofs of retrievability for outsourced files from weak primitives via hardness amplification.
- **54440127.pdf** — Security Amplification for Interactive Cryptographic Primitives (Yevgeniy Dodis, Russell Impagliazzo, Ragesh Jaiswal, Valentine Kabanets). Converts weakly secure interactive primitives into strongly secure ones.
- **54440145.pdf** — Composability and On-Line Deniability of Authentication (Yevgeniy Dodis, Jonathan Katz, Adam Smith, Shabsi Walfish). Studies deniable authentication under composition, including on-line deniability guarantees.
- **54440162.pdf** — Authenticated Adversarial Routing (Yair Amir, Paul Bunn, Rafail Ostrovsky). Feasibility of authenticated, throughput-efficient routing in unreliable, dynamically changing synchronous networks. (First-page text was font-garbled; decoded from glyph mapping.)
- **54440181.pdf** — Adaptive Zero-Knowledge Proofs and Adaptively Secure Oblivious Transfer (Yehuda Lindell, Hila Zarosim). Constructs adaptive zero-knowledge proofs yielding adaptively secure oblivious transfer.
- **54440199.pdf** — On the (Im)Possibility of Key Dependent Encryption (Iftach Haitner, Thomas Holenstein). Separation results on encryption secure for messages depending on the secret key, in private- and public-key settings.
- **54440217.pdf** — On the (Im)Possibility of Arthur-Merlin Witness Hiding Protocols (Iftach Haitner, Alon Rosen, Ronen Shaltiel). Identifies languages/distributions where constant-round public-coin protocols fail or achieve witness hiding.
- **54440235.pdf** — Secure Computability of Functions in the IT Setting with Dishonest Majority and Applications to Long-Term Security (Robin Künzler, Jörn Müller-Quade, Dominik Raub). Characterizes information-theoretic SFE possible despite corrupted majority, with long-term security applications.
- **54440253.pdf** — Complexity of Multi-party Computation Problems: The Case of 2-Party Symmetric Secure Function Evaluation (Hemanta K. Maji, Manoj Prabhakaran, Mike Rosulek). Classifies feasibility of two-party symmetric SFE across several security settings.
- **54440271.pdf** — Realistic Failures in Secure Multi-Party Computation (Vassilis Zikas, Sarah Hauser, Ueli Maurer). Models mixed corruption types (active/passive/fail) and analyzes MPC security under realistic failure assumptions.
- **54440291.pdf** — Secure Arithmetic Computation with No Honest Majority (Yuval Ishai, Manoj Prabhakaran, Amit Sahai). Complexity of securely evaluating arithmetic circuits over finite rings, mainly two-party malicious security.
- **54440311.pdf** — Universally Composable Multiparty Computation with Partially Isolated Parties (Ivan Damgård, Jesper Buus Nielsen, Daniel Wichs). Achieves UC-secure MPC by assuming some parties are partially isolated rather than full setup.
- **54440328.pdf** — Oblivious Transfer from Weak Noisy Channels (Jürg Wullschleger). Builds OT from unfair/weak noisy channels that give the adversary extra power, relaxing standard channel assumptions.
- **54440346.pdf** — Composing Quantum Protocols in a Classical Environment (Serge Fehr, Christian Schaffner). General security definition for quantum protocols implementing classical two-party tasks, with composition guarantees.
- **54440364.pdf** — LEGO for Two-Party Secure Computation (Jesper Buus Nielsen, Claudio Orlandi). Cut-and-choose garbled-circuit approach (LEGO) for actively secure two-party computation, log|C| efficiency gain on large circuits.
- **54440382.pdf** — Simple, Black-Box Constructions of Adaptively Secure Protocols (Seung Geol Choi, Dana Dachman-Soled, Tal Malkin, Hoeteck Wee). Compiler lifting adaptive semi-honest OT to adaptive malicious UC security via ideal commitments.
- **54440400.pdf** — Black-Box Constructions of Two-Party Protocols from One-Way Functions (Rafael Pass, Hoeteck Wee). Constant-round zero-knowledge arguments, trapdoor commitments, and parallel coin-tossing from black-box OWFs.
- **54440418.pdf** — Chosen-Ciphertext Security via Correlated Products (Alon Rosen, Gil Segev). Initiates study of one-wayness under correlated products to obtain CCA-secure encryption.
- **54440436.pdf** — Hierarchical Identity Based Encryption with Polynomially Many Levels (Craig Gentry, Shai Halevi). First HIBE with full security for non-constant depth, avoiding exponential reduction loss.
- **54440456.pdf** — Predicate Privacy in Encryption Systems (Emily Shen, Elaine Shi, Brent Waters). Studies privacy of predicates themselves in predicate encryption systems with token-based access control.
- **54440474.pdf** — Simultaneous Hardcore Bits and Cryptography Against Memory Attacks (Adi Akavia, Shafi Goldwasser, Vinod Vaikuntanathan). Simultaneous hardcore bits and public-key schemes resilient to secret-key leakage (memory attacks).
- **54440495.pdf** — The Differential Privacy Frontier (Cynthia Dwork). Extended abstract surveying the definition and recent advances in differential privacy.
- **54440502.pdf** — How Efficient can Memory Checking be? (Cynthia Dwork, Moni Naor, Guy N. Rothblum, Vinod Vaikuntanathan). Bounds and constructions for checking remote untrusted memory with small trusted local storage.
- **54440520.pdf** — Goldreich's One-Way Function Candidate and Myopic Backtracking Algorithms (James Cook, Omid Etesami, Rachel Miller, Luca Trevisan). Analysis of Goldreich's OWF candidate; shows myopic backtracking algorithms invert it (?) per extract's focus on attack algorithms.
- **54440538.pdf** — Secret Sharing and Non-Shannon Information Inequalities (Amos Beimel, Ilan Orlov). Uses non-Shannon information inequalities to improve lower bounds on share sizes in secret-sharing schemes.
- **54440557.pdf** — Weak Verifiable Random Functions (Zvika Brakerski, Shafi Goldwasser, Guy N. Rothblum, Vinod Vaikuntanathan). Defines weak VRFs and shows they suffice for applications while admitting simpler constructions.
- **54440575.pdf** — Efficient Oblivious Pseudorandom Function with Applications to Adaptive OT and Secure Computation of Set Intersection (Stanisław Jarecki, Xiaomin Liu). Efficient OPRF protocol applied to adaptive OT and private set intersection.
- **54440593.pdf** — Towards a Theory of Extractable Functions (Ran Canetti, Ronny Ramzi Dakdouk). Formalizes extractable functions where any range output implies an extractor recovering a preimage.
- **546.pdf** — Modular Security Proofs for Key Agreement Protocols (Caroline Kudla, Kenneth G. Paterson). Modular framework for constructing and simplifying security proofs of key agreement protocols.
- **54790001.pdf** — Possibility and Impossibility Results for Encryption and Commitment Secure under Selective Opening (Mihir Bellare, Dennis Hofheinz, Scott Yilek). Resolves existence of selective-opening-secure encryption and commitments, both positively and negatively.
- **54790037.pdf** — Breaking RSA Generically is Equivalent to Factoring (Divesh Aggarwal, Ueli Maurer). Generic ring algorithms breaking RSA imply factoring; any RSA break without factoring must exploit bit representations.
- **54790055.pdf** — Resettably Secure Computation (Vipul Goyal, Amit Sahai). Extends resettable zero-knowledge to general secure computation under resetting adversaries.
- **54790073.pdf** — On the Security Loss in Cryptographic Reductions (Chi-Jen Lu). Studies inherent security loss in reductions from protocols to underlying primitives.
- **54790091.pdf** — On Randomizing Hash Functions to Strengthen the Security of Digital Signatures (Praveen Gauravaram, Lars R. Knudsen). Analyzes RMX message randomization for hash-then-sign schemes, reducing reliance on collision resistance.
- **54790109.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of MDC-2 (Lars R. Knudsen, Florian Mendel, Christian Rechberger, Søren S. Thomsen). Collision and preimage attacks on the MDC-2 block-cipher-based double-length hash construction.
- **54790122.pdf** — Cryptanalysis on HMAC/NMAC-MD5 and MD5-MAC (Xiaoyun Wang, Hongbo Yu, Wei Wang, Haina Zhang, Tao Zhan). First distinguishing and related-key recovery attacks on HMAC/NMAC-MD5 and MD5-MAC.
- **54790136.pdf** — Finding Preimages in Full MD5 Faster than Exhaustive Search (Yu Sasaki, Kazumaro Aoki). First preimage attack on full MD5: pseudo-preimage 2^116.9, preimage 2^123.4.
- **54790154.pdf** — Asymmetric Group Key Agreement (Qianhong Wu, Yi Mu, Willy Susilo, Bo Qin, Josep Domingo-Ferrer). Defines asymmetric GKA where members share an encryption key rather than a common secret key, for confidential group channels.
- **54790172.pdf** — Adaptive Security in Broadcast Encryption Systems (with Short Ciphertexts) (Craig Gentry, Brent Waters). Techniques for adaptive security in broadcast encryption via a new semi-static security notion.
- **54790190.pdf** — Traitors Collaborating in Public: Pirates 2.0 (Olivier Billet, Duong Hieu Phan). New "Pirates 2.0" attack model where traitors publicly share key material, defeating traitor tracing schemes.
- **54790207.pdf** — Key Agreement from Close Secrets over Unsecured Channels (Bhavana Kanukurthi, Leonid Reyzin). Information-theoretic key agreement from low-entropy, noisy shared secrets via reconciliation and privacy amplification.
- **54790225.pdf** — Order-Preserving Symmetric Encryption (Alexandra Boldyreva, Nathan Chenette, Younho Lee, Adam O'Neill). First cryptographic treatment of OPE; shows relaxed security notions and constructions for range queries on encrypted data.
- **54790243.pdf** — A Double-Piped Mode of Operation for MACs, PRFs and PROs: Security beyond the Birthday Barrier (Kan Yasuda). Applies Lucks' double-pipe construction to secret-key primitives, achieving beyond-birthday security.
- **54790261.pdf** — On the Security of Cryptosystems with Quadratic Decryption: The Nicest Cryptanalysis (Guilhem Castagnos, Fabien Laguillaumie). First polynomial-time chosen-plaintext total break of the NICE family of cryptosystems.
