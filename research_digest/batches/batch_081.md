# Batch 081 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ~2008-era Springer LNCS papers (ASIACRYPT/PKC/EUROCRYPT style, 14–20 pages) spanning public-key encryption, IBE, signatures, cryptanalysis, and secure computation.
- Strong cryptanalysis cluster: preimage attacks on HAVAL, slide attacks on Grindahl/RadioGatún, impossible-differential attack on MISTY1, attacks on LEX, Sosemanuk/SNOW 2.0, TCHo, and the HB# RFID protocol.
- DLP/factoring cluster of direct ECDLP interest: two Pollard-rho speedups (prime fields and F_{p^m} via normal bases, with pairing-security implications), generic-group lower bounds (Rupp et al.), plus lattice factoring/RSA key-exposure work (May, Herrmann, Aono).
- Notable papers: Shacham–Waters "Compact Proofs of Retrievability", Hisil et al. "Twisted Edwards Curves Revisited" (fast ECC arithmetic), Moran–Naor–Segev optimally fair coin toss (resolving Cleve's Ω(1/r) bound tightness).
- Recurring themes: CCA-secure PKE with minimal overhead (Hanaoka–Kurosawa; Abe–Kiltz–Okamoto twice), IBE variants (Boneh–Hamburg, Chow escrow removal, Libert–Vergnaud accountable authority), anonymous credentials/revocation (Camenisch et al.).
- Anomalies: 53500394.pdf and 54430429.pdf are garbled/mis-encoded extractions and could not be summarized; no obvious duplicates detected.

## Papers
- **53500075.pdf** — Ambiguous Optimistic Fair Exchange (Huang, Yang, Wong, Susilo). Defines A-OFE where verifiers cannot prove authorship of partial signatures; gives security model and efficient construction without random oracles.
- **53500093.pdf** — Compact Proofs of Retrievability (Shacham, Waters). First PoR schemes with full security proofs in the Juels–Kaliski model; BLS-based public-verifiability scheme and PRF-based standard-model scheme.
- **53500111.pdf** — On the Security of HB# Against a Man-in-the-Middle Attack (Ouafi, Overbeck, Vaudenay). General MitM attack recovering HB#/Random-HB# secrets in ~2^25/2^34 rounds, applicable to earlier HB-like RFID protocols.
- **53500129.pdf** — Hash Functions from Sigma Protocols and Improvements to VSH (Bellare, Ristov). Generic collision-resistant hashing from Σ-protocols; fastest factoring-based hash and faster VSH* for short messages; chameleon-hash applications.
- **53500147.pdf** — Slide Attacks on a Class of Hash Functions (Gorski, Lucks, Peyrin). Applies slide attacks to sponge-like hash functions, attacking Grindahl-256/512 and a modified RadioGatún; discusses MAC forgeries and countermeasures.
- **53500166.pdf** — Basing PRFs on Constant-Query Weak PRFs (Maurer, Tessaro). Introduces constant-query weak PRFs and iterated constructions yielding efficient PRFs, encryption modes, and keyed-hash MACs from weaker assumptions.
- **53500184.pdf** — Universally Composable Adaptive Oblivious Transfer (Green, Hohenberger). Constructs adaptive k-out-of-N OT with UC security, avoiding rewinding-based proofs of earlier full-simulation protocols.
- **53500202.pdf** — A Linked-List Approach to Cryptographically Secure Elections Using Instant Runoff Voting (Keller, Kilian). Preferential-voting election protocol hiding unrevealed voter rankings with improved asymptotic performance for many candidates.
- **53500220.pdf** — Towards Robust Computation on Encrypted Data (Prabhakaran, Rosulek). Uses non-malleable homomorphic encryption for UC-secure protocols against active adversaries; handles multi-ciphertext homomorphic operations.
- **53500238.pdf** — Efficient Protocols for Set Membership and Range Proofs (Camenisch, Chaabouni, shelat). Bilinear-group set-membership/range zero-knowledge proofs beating prior O(k) protocols, an order of magnitude more efficient for DL settings.
- **53500257.pdf** — Preimage Attacks on 3, 4, and 5-pass HAVAL (Sasaki, Aoki). Meet-in-the-middle/local-collision preimage attacks: 3-pass at 2^225, 4-pass at 2^241, and reduced 5-pass HAVAL.
- **53500293.pdf** — Limits of Constructive Security Proofs (Backes, Unruh). Shows an existentially secure protocol that provably admits no constructive security proof, delimiting Rogaway's approach for unkeyed hash functions.
- **53500311.pdf** — Efficient Chosen Ciphertext Secure PKE under the CDH Assumption (Hanaoka, Kurosawa). CCA security under CDH without ciphertext expansion, via a generic broadcast-encryption-to-PKE transform.
- **53500329.pdf** — Twisted Edwards Curves Revisited (Hisil, Wong, Carter, Dawson). Faster twisted-Edwards addition (8M; 2M with four processors) speeding scalar multiplication and offering inherent SPA resistance.
- **53500358.pdf** — Chosen Ciphertext Security with Optimal Ciphertext Overhead (Abe, Kiltz, Okamoto). IND-CCA scheme matching the generic t-bit overhead lower bound via a four-round Feistel variant in the random oracle model.
- **53500376.pdf** — Concurrently Secure Identification Schemes Based on Worst-Case Hardness of Lattice Problems (Kawachi, Tanaka, Xagawa). Stern-variants secure against concurrent attacks under weaker lattice assumptions; yields ad-hoc anonymous identification.
- **53500394.pdf** — Garbled/mis-encoded text extraction; content unreadable, not summarizable.
- **53500412.pdf** — Solving Linear Equations Modulo Divisors: On Factoring Given Any Bits (Herrmann, May). Heuristic lattice algorithm factoring with known bits scattered in n blocks; ln(2)≈70% of bits suffice.
- **53500430.pdf** — An Infinite Class of Balanced Functions with Optimal Algebraic Immunity (Carlet, Feng). Boolean functions with optimal algebraic immunity and degree plus much better nonlinearity than previous classes, resisting fast algebraic/correlation attacks.
- **53500447.pdf** — An Improved Impossible Differential Attack on MISTY1 (Dunkelman, Keller). Attacks 5-round MISTY1 with FL layers (2^46.45) and 6-round with FL — best known result — plus 7-round without FL.
- **53500461.pdf** — Generalized Identity Based and Broadcast Encryption Schemes (Boneh, Hamburg). "Spatial encryption" framework unifying IBE/broadcast systems with constant-size ciphertexts; first broadcast HIBE with short ciphertexts.
- **53500477.pdf** — Speeding Up the Pollard Rho Method on Prime Fields (Cheon, Hong, Kim). r-adding walk evaluated with partial node information via precomputed tables, >10× practical speedup for 1024-bit primes.
- **53500495.pdf** — Sufficient Conditions for Intractability over Black-Box Groups (Rupp, Leander, Bangerter, Dent, Sadeghi). Criteria guaranteeing generic-model hardness of generalized DL/DH-type problems, easing lower-bound proofs.
- **53500513.pdf** — OAEP is Secure Under Key-Dependent Messages (Backes, Dürmuth, Unruh). Defines adKDM security with adaptive corruptions/active attacks and proves OAEP (partial-domain one-way) achieves it in the ROM.
- **53500530.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Sosemanuk and SNOW 2.0 Using Linear Masks (Lee, Lee, Park). Correlation attacks via linear approximations: Sosemanuk state recovery at 2^147.88; SNOW 2.0 at 2^204.38.
- **53500545.pdf** — A New Attack on the LEX Stream Cipher (Dunkelman, Keller). Key-recovery attack on leak-extraction cipher LEX needing 2^36.3 keystream bytes and 2^112 time; LEX dropped from eSTREAM portfolio.
- **54430001.pdf** — Implicit Factoring: On Polynomial Time Factoring Given Only an Implicit Hint (May, Ritzenhofen). Factors RSA moduli given only another modulus whose prime shares t least significant bits, via lattices; generalizes to multiple queries.
- **54430016.pdf** — The Security of All Bits Using List Decoding (Morillo, Ràfols). Extends list-decoding hard-core methodology to all bits of functions on cyclic groups; reproves bit security of RSA, DL, Paillier.
- **54430036.pdf** — A New Lattice Construction for Partial Key Exposure Attack for RSA (Aono). Improved Coppersmith-type lattice for small-d RSA with known LSBs of d, theoretically faster than Boneh–Durfee-line attacks.
- **54430056.pdf** — Subset-Restricted Random Walks for Pollard rho Method on F_{p^m} (Kim, Cheon, Hong). Iterating function with small image exploiting normal bases speeds rho on extension fields; implies larger subgroup orders for pairing systems via MOV.
- **54430070.pdf** — Signing a Linear Subspace: Signature Schemes for Network Coding (Boneh, Freeman, Katz, Waters). Homomorphic subspace signatures authenticating vectors in V against malicious network-coding nodes; matching length lower bound.
- **54430090.pdf** — Improving the Boneh-Franklin Traitor Tracing Scheme (Junod, Karlov, Lenstra). Tracing in Õ(k²) independent of user count, list-decoding critique, and fix for a 2k-traitor key-derivation attack.
- **54430108.pdf** — Modeling Key Compromise Impersonation Attacks on Group Key Exchange Protocols (Gorantla, Boyd, González Nieto). First KCI-resilience model for GKE; breaks existing protocols under outsider KCI and re-proves one protocol secure.
- **54430146.pdf** — Distributed Public-Key Cryptography from Weak Secrets (Abdalla, Boyen, Chevalier, Pointcheval). Virtual high-entropy private key from distributed passwords; UC protocols for distributed ElGamal and general DL-based/IBE systems.
- **54430166.pdf** — Asynchronous Multiparty Computation: Theory and Implementation (Damgård, Geisler, Krøigaard, Nielsen). Perfectly secure async MPC, active/adaptive <n/3 corruptions, O(n²|C|k) communication; VIFF framework with benchmarks.
- **54430186.pdf** — Multi-Party Computation with Omnipresent Adversary (Ghodosi, Pieprzyk). MPC model where all participants may be curious; examines bounds and privacy when no truly honest parties are assumed.
- **54430202.pdf** — Blind and Anonymous IBE and Authorised Private Searches on Public Key Encrypted Data (Camenisch, Kohlweiss, Rial, Sheedy). PEOKS keyword search with oblivious trapdoors and committed blind anonymous IBE for authorized private search.
- **54430241.pdf** — Towards Black-Box Accountable Authority IBE with Short Ciphertexts and Private Keys (Libert, Vergnaud). Efficient accountable-authority IBE combining Goyal's first scheme's efficiency with simple weak black-box tracing; adaptive-ID secure.
- **54430261.pdf** — Removing Escrow from Identity-Based Encryption (Chow). Formalizes KGC anonymous ciphertext indistinguishability, shows existing pairing IBEs fail it, and proposes anonymous key-issuance architecture mitigating escrow.
- **54430301.pdf** — Security of Blind Signatures Under Aborts (Fischlin, Schröder). Generic transform adding selective-failure blindness to any secure blind signature; three-move schemes stay unforgeable under user aborts.
- **54430321.pdf** — Security of Sanitizable Signatures Revisited (Brzuska et al.). First comprehensive formal treatment of sanitizable-signature properties, their relations, and a proven-secure modification of the original scheme.
- **54430361.pdf** — CCA-Secure Proxy Re-Encryption without Pairings (Shao, Cao). First unidirectional pairing-free PRE with CCA security and collusion resistance, via signatures of knowledge and Fujisaki–Okamoto in the ROM.
- **54430381.pdf** — Compact CCA-Secure Encryption for Messages of Arbitrary Length (Abe, Kiltz, Okamoto). ElGamal variant with one-group-element overhead for arbitrary-length messages; secure under strong Diffie–Hellman in the ROM.
- **54430397.pdf** — Verifiable Rotation of Homomorphic Encryptions (de Hoogh, Schoenmakers, Škorić, Villegas). Zero-knowledge proofs of hidden cyclic rotation of ciphertext lists, including a DFT/FFT-based protocol; mix and voting applications.
- **54430415.pdf** — A Practical Key Recovery Attack on Basic TCHo (Herrmann, Leander). Chosen-ciphertext key recovery on TCHo in ~d^{3/2} decryptions; breaks all proposed parameters within hours, forcing the hybrid construction.
- **54430429.pdf** — Garbled/encoded text extraction (slash-separated hex-like tokens); content unreadable, not summarizable.
- **54430468.pdf** — Revocable Group Signature Schemes with Constant Costs for Signing and Verifying (Nakanishi, Fujii, Hira, Funabiki). O(1) sign/verify revocation without member key updates, at cost of O(N) (extendable O(√N)) public key.
- **54430486.pdf** — An Accumulator Based on Bilinear Maps and Efficient Revocation for Anonymous Credentials (Camenisch, Kohlweiss, Soriente). Dynamic bilinear-map accumulator enabling cheap witness updates for revocation of anonymous credentials.
- **54430506.pdf** — Controlling Access to an Oblivious Database using Stateful Anonymous Credentials (Coull, Green, Hohenberger). Stateful anonymous credentials plus OT enforce per-user access policies (Brewer–Nash, Bell–LaPadula) on oblivious database queries.
- **54440001.pdf** — An Optimally Fair Coin Toss (Moran, Naor, Segev). Constructs r-round two-party coin flipping with bias O(1/r), proving Cleve's Ω(1/r) lower bound tight under standard assumptions.
