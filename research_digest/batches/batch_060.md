# Batch 060 digest

## Overview
- Batch is almost entirely Eurocrypt/Crypto 2003-era proceedings papers: dominant themes are zero-knowledge/concurrent ZK, secure two-party and multi-party computation, and provable-security foundations (UC model, group signatures, PAKE).
- Strong cryptanalysis cluster: RSA side-channel/partial-key-exposure attacks (Kühn, Fouque et al., Blömer–May, Shamir–Tromer TWIRL), HFE/Quartz multivariate attacks (Courtois et al., Faugère–Joux), block/stream cipher cryptanalysis (EMD mode, shrinking generator, LILI-128/Toyocrypt, related-key theory).
- Elliptic-curve content is a minority but notable: Lercier–Lubicz quasi-quadratic point counting, Hess's GHS attack generalization, Ciet–Lange–Sica–Quisquater fast-endomorphism arithmetic; pairing-based work includes Boneh–Gentry–Lynn–Shacham aggregate signatures and Canetti–Halevi–Katz forward-secure PKE.
- Well-known landmark papers present: CAPTCHA (von Ahn et al.), TWIRL factoring hardware, aggregate signatures from bilinear maps, foundations of group signatures (Bellare–Micciancio–Warinschi).
- Anomaly: 26.pdf is garbled misencoded text (apparent CJK-encoded PDF extraction failure mentioning RSA); no usable content. No duplicates detected.

## Papers
- **25670324.pdf** — Side-Channel Attacks on Textbook RSA and ElGamal Encryption (Ulrich Kühn). Efficient attacks on plain RSA/ElGamal decryption exploiting implementation assumptions about message length, showing textbook RSA padding must be checked carefully.
- **25670337.pdf** — On the Security of HFE, HFEv- and Quartz (Courtois, Daum, Felke). Studies inversion attacks on the HFEv- trapdoor behind Quartz, showing modified HFE systems remain attackable and estimating Quartz's best inversion-attack complexity.
- **25670351.pdf** — Generic Attacks and the Security of Quartz (Nicolas T. Courtois). Presents generic attacks (~2^80) on HFEv--based short signatures, examines provable-security bounds for Quartz-like constructions, and proposes an alternative with matching bounds.
- **26.pdf** — Not usable: garbled misencoded text (apparent CJK PDF extraction failure); RSA is mentioned but title, authors, and content are unreadable.
- **26560001.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the EMD Mode of Operation (Antoine Joux). Shows EMD (and parallelizable EME) disk-encryption modes are insecure as tweakable block ciphers, with efficient attacks and a refined generalized birthday algorithm.
- **26560017.pdf** — On the Optimality of Linear, Differential and Sequential Distinguishers (Pascal Junod). Applies statistical hypothesis testing to characterize optimal linear/differential distinguishers, improves Vaudenay's asymptotic results, and formalizes sequential distinguishers.
- **26560033.pdf** — A Toolbox for Cryptanalysis: Linear and Affine Equivalence Algorithms (Biryukov, De Cannière, Braeken, Preneel). Efficient algorithms for linear/affine equivalence of S-boxes yield new equivalent representations of Rijndael, DES, Camellia, and attacks on generalized Even-Mansour.
- **26560051.pdf** — Two-Threshold Broadcast and Detectable Multi-Party Computation (Fitzi, Hirt, Holenstein, Wullschleger). Introduces two-threshold security: full security below threshold t, degraded but detectable security up to larger threshold T, for broadcast and MPC.
- **26560068.pdf** — On the Limitations of Universally Composable Two-Party Computation Without Set-up Assumptions (Canetti, Kushilevitz, Lindell). Characterizes which deterministic functions admit UC-secure two-party evaluation in the plain model, showing very few do.
- **26560087.pdf** — Fair Secure Two-Party Computation (Benny Pinkas). Transforms Yao's protocol into a fair one using gradual-release timed commitments and blind signatures, preventing premature-termination advantage without a trusted third party.
- **26560123.pdf** — Resettable Zero-Knowledge in the Weak Public-Key Model (Zhao, Deng, Lee, Zhu). Introduces the WPK model generalizing UPK and gives a 3-round black-box resettable ZK argument with concurrent soundness for NP.
- **26560140.pdf** — Simulatable Commitments and Efficient Concurrent Zero-Knowledge (Micciancio, Petrank). Defines DDH-based simulatable commitments to transform any public-coin HVZK proof into concurrent ZK with near-optimal additive overhead.
- **26560160.pdf** — Simulation in Quasi-polynomial Time, and its Application to Protocol Composition (Rafael Pass). Relaxes ZK to quasi-polynomial-time simulation, yielding constant-round straight-line concurrent arguments composable without set-up assumptions.
- **26560177.pdf** — Strengthening Zero-Knowledge Protocols Using Signatures (Garay, MacKenzie, Yang). Uses secure signatures to convert Sigma-protocols into unbounded simulation-sound concurrent ZK, achieving non-malleability/UC for Omega-protocol variants.
- **26560195.pdf** — Nearly One-Sided Tests and the Goldreich-Levin Predicate (Gustav Hast). Gives an efficient reduction strengthening Goldreich-Levin hard-core bit security against nearly one-sided tests, with applications to PRG-based signature randomness.
- **26560211.pdf** — Efficient and Non-Malleable Proofs of Plaintext Knowledge and Applications (Jonathan Katz). Efficient non-malleable proofs of plaintext knowledge for RSA, Rabin, Paillier, ElGamal, yielding CCA-secure interactive encryption, PAKE, and deniable authentication.
- **26560229.pdf** — A Public Key Encryption Scheme Based on the Polynomial Reconstruction Problem (Augot, Finiasz). First PKE scheme built on the Polynomial Reconstruction problem, with attacks analyzed and parameter sizes given; low per-bit cost.
- **26560241.pdf** — A Simpler Construction of CCA2-Secure Public-Key Encryption Under General Assumptions (Yehuda Lindell). Simplifies CCA2-secure PKE from trapdoor permutations, only slightly more involved than Naor-Yung CCA1 security.
- **26560255.pdf** — A Forward-Secure Public-Key Encryption Scheme (Canetti, Halevi, Katz). First non-interactive forward-secure PKE, secure under decisional bilinear Diffie-Hellman in the standard model with logarithmic parameter growth.
- **26560272.pdf** — Certificate-Based Encryption and the Certificate Revocation Problem (Craig Gentry). Introduces certificate-based encryption, combining IBE implicit certification with PKE's no-escrow property to build efficient PKI with simple revocation.
- **26560294.pdf** — CAPTCHA: Using Hard AI Problems For Security (von Ahn, Blum, Hopper, Langford). Introduces CAPTCHAs formalized via hard AI problems, with constructions and a win-win link to steganographic communication.
- **26560312.pdf** — Concealment and its Applications to Authenticated Encryption (Dodis, An). Defines concealments (hider/binder split of a message), equivalent to collision-resistant hashing, enabling authenticated encryption of long messages from short-message AE.
- **26560330.pdf** — Predicting the Shrinking Generator with Fixed Connections (Ekdahl, Meier, Johansson). Practical distinguishing attack on the shrinking generator exploiting non-random output block distribution, applicable to arbitrary shrunken LFSRs of moderate length.
- **26560345.pdf** — Algebraic Attacks on Stream Ciphers with Linear Feedback (Courtois, Meier). Lowers algebraic equation degree by multiplying by chosen polynomials, breaking Toyocrypt in 2^49 and LILI-128 in 2^57; general attack on LFSR-based ciphers.
- **26560360.pdf** — Counting Points on Elliptic Curves over Finite Fields of Small Characteristic in Quasi Quadratic Time (Lercier, Lubicz). Canonical-lift/AGM-style point-counting algorithm reaching quasi-quadratic time, demonstrated with a 100002-bit computation.
- **26560374.pdf** — The GHS Attack Revisited (F. Hess). Generalizes GHS Weil descent to arbitrary Artin-Schreier extensions, almost squaring the number of vulnerable elliptic curves and further weakening curves over F_2^155.
- **26560388.pdf** — Improved Algorithms for Efficient Arithmetic on Elliptic Curves Using Fast Endomorphisms (Ciet, Lange, Sica, Quisquater). Extends tau-adic expansions to prime-field curves with efficient endomorphisms and introduces phi-Joint Sparse Form for fast double scalar multiplication.
- **26560401.pdf** — A Signature Scheme as Secure as the Diffie-Hellman Problem (Goh, Jarecki). Shows a Schnorr-like signature scheme with a tight reduction to CDH in the random oracle model, improving on forking-lemma-based guarantees.
- **26560416.pdf** — Aggregate and Verifiably Encrypted Signatures from Bilinear Maps (Boneh, Gentry, Lynn, Shacham). Defines aggregate signatures, builds them from BLS short signatures, and derives verifiably encrypted signatures for contract signing.
- **26560433.pdf** — Hypercubic Lattice Reduction and Analysis of GGH and NTRU Signatures (Michael Szydlo). New reduction technique for hypercubic lattices arising in GGH/NTRUSign transcript analysis, reducing key recovery to a lattice distinguishing problem.
- **26560449.pdf** — Why Provable Security Matters? (Jacques Stern). Essay on provable security methodology, random oracle caveats, and validation of proofs, using OAEP and ESIGN as case studies.
- **26560462.pdf** — On the Security of RDSA (Fouque, Poupard). Total break of the RDSA Schnorr variant in unknown-order groups via known-message attack with lattice reduction; recommends the GPS scheme instead.
- **26560477.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the Public-Key Encryption Based on Braid Groups (Lee, Park). Recovers private keys of the CRYPTO 2000 braid-group encryption scheme using the Burau representation, and discusses secure parameter requirements.
- **26560491.pdf** — A Theoretical Treatment of Related-Key Attacks: RKA-PRPs, RKA-PRFs, and Applications (Bellare, Kohno). Formalizes related-key security for PRPs/PRFs via RKD function classes, proving impossibility and possibility results and securing related-key constructions.
- **26560507.pdf** — Provably Secure Threshold Password-Authenticated Key Exchange (Di Raimondo, Gennaro). First threshold PAKE protocols provably secure in the standard model, distributing the password across n > 3t servers.
- **26560524.pdf** — A Framework for Password-Based Authenticated Key Exchange (Gennaro, Lindell). General PAKE framework in the CRS model abstracting Katz-Ostrovsky-Yung via smooth projective hashing, with instantiations under DDH, quadratic and N-residuosity.
- **26560545.pdf** — The Security of Many-Round Luby-Rackoff Pseudo-Random Permutations (Maurer, Pietrzak). Proves Feistel cascades with r rounds achieve security alpha = 1 - O(1/r), approaching the trivial bound and improving Patarin's 3/4.
- **26560563.pdf** — New Bounds in Secret-Key Agreement: The Gap Between Formation and Secrecy Extraction (Renner, Wolf). Introduces a conditional mutual information upper bound on secret-key rate and shows intrinsic information lower-bounds key formation cost.
- **26560579.pdf** — Round Efficiency of Multi-Party Computation with a Dishonest Majority (Katz, Ostrovsky, Smith). MPC with dishonest majority in O(log n) rounds from trapdoor permutations, and O(1) rounds using non-black-box techniques.
- **26560597.pdf** — Efficient Multi-Party Computation over Rings (Cramer, Fehr, Ishai, Kushilevitz). Unconditionally secure MPC over arbitrary finite rings using black-box ring access, extended to constant-round protocols with efficiency gains for fields.
- **26560615.pdf** — Foundations of Group Signatures: Formal Definitions, Simplified Requirements, and a Construction Based on General Assumptions (Bellare, Micciancio, Warinschi). Formal anonymity/traceability definitions for group signatures that imply prior informal requirements, plus a construction from trapdoor permutations.
- **26560631.pdf** — Extracting Group Signatures from Traitor Tracing Schemes (Kiayias, Yung). Reveals a duality between group signatures and traitor tracing, yielding the first provably secure constant-size group signature from DDH plus random oracle.
- **268.pdf** — A Sender Verifiable Mix-Net and a New Proof of a Shuffle (Douglas Wikström). First ElGamal mix-net without re-encryption, UC-secure with sender verifiability, plus a novel zero-knowledge proof of a decryption-permutation shuffle.
- **27.pdf** — Power Attack on Small RSA Public Exponent (Fouque, Kunz-Jacques, Martinet, Muller, Valette). SPA-based attack recovering RSA private keys with small public exponent despite exponent randomization and message blinding, exploiting non-consecutive leaked bits.
- **27290001.pdf** — Factoring Large Numbers with the TWIRL Device (Shamir, Tromer). Hardware design for the NFS sieving step 3-4 orders of magnitude more cost-effective, implying 512-bit RSA sieving in minutes and raising doubts about 1024-bit RSA.
- **27290027.pdf** — New Partial Key Exposure Attacks on RSA (Blömer, May). Extends Boneh-Durfee-Frankel partial key exposure attacks via Coppersmith's method to larger public exponents, covering MSB/LSB cases and CRT-RSA variants.
- **27290044.pdf** — Algebraic cryptanalysis of hidden field equation (HFE) cryptosystems using Gröbner bases (Faugère, Joux). Breaks the first HFE challenge in two days with F5, explains why HFE systems are easier than random, and argues polynomial-time cryptanalysis.
- **27290061.pdf** — On Constructing Locally Computable Extractors and Cryptosystems in the Bounded Storage Model (Salil Vadhan). Sample-then-extract approach to locally computable extractors with refined Nisan-Zuckerman lemma, improving bounded-storage-model cryptosystems.
- **27290078.pdf** — Unconditional Authenticity and Privacy from an Arbitrarily Weak Secret (Renner, Wolf). Interactive protocols achieving authentication and privacy amplification from a shared string of which the adversary knows an arbitrarily large fraction.
- **27290096.pdf** — On Cryptographic Assumptions and Challenges (Moni Naor). Classifies computational assumptions by falsifiability via challenges/competitions, and poses open problems, notably designing efficiently challengeable block ciphers.
