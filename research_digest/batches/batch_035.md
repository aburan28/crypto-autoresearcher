# Batch 035 digest

## Overview
- Batch is a coherent run of ~2021-era conference papers (sequential 130900xxx IDs; LNCS-style formatting, Asiacrypt-like mix), spanning symmetric cryptanalysis, FHE, MPC, and quantum cryptography.
- Dominant themes: symmetric-key cryptanalysis (linear/cube/fault/quantum attacks on Simon, Simeck, LowMC, LightMAC, OCB), homomorphic encryption (BGV/BFV, CKKS transciphering, TFHE), and provable-security foundations (QROM, AGM-in-UC, tight reductions).
- Post-quantum content is strong: NTRU hardness reductions, fault-injection and key-mismatch attacks on NIST PQC Round 3 KEMs, lattice group signatures, isogeny-based MPC and OPRF cryptanalysis.
- Closest to ECDLP/finite-field DL: a record 521-bit TNFS discrete-log computation in Fp6 (130900207); little elliptic-curve-specific material in this batch.
- Notable anomalies: 130900241 lists "No Author Given"; extracts contain NUL-byte artifacts (rendered "/x00") in author lists of 130900235 and 130900337. No duplicates or non-paper items found.

## Papers
- **130900158.pdf** — Tight Security for Key-Alternating Ciphers with Correlated Sub-Keys (Stefano Tessaro, Xihu Zhang). Proves optimal security for t-round key-alternating ciphers under limited-independence correlated sub-key distributions, via generalized Sum-Capture theorems.
- **130900159.pdf** — Revisiting Homomorphic Encryption Schemes for Finite Fields (Kim, Polyakov, Zucca). Revisits BGV and BFV schemes, improving BFV noise growth and giving theoretical and experimental comparisons of both.
- **130900160.pdf** — How to Build a Trapdoor Function from an Encryption Scheme (Garg, Hajiabadi, Malavolta, Ostrovsky). Generic compiler turning any pseudorandom-ciphertext encryption into a TDF using hinting PRGs, yielding first CDH-based identity-based and attribute-based TDFs.
- **130900164.pdf** — On the hardness of the NTRU problem (Pellet-Mary, Stehlé). Gives first reduction-based hardness evidence for NTRU: worst-case ideal-SVP reduces to average-case search NTRU, and search to decision variants.
- **130900168.pdf** — Better Security-Efficiency Trade-Offs in Permutation-Based Two-Party Computation (Chen, Tessaro). Improves tweakable correlation-robust hash functions from permutations, with one-call and two-call constructions strengthening garbling and OT-extension security bounds.
- **130900171.pdf** — A Geometric Approach to Linear Cryptanalysis (Tim Beyne). Unifies variants of linear cryptanalysis via a geometric interpretation, yielding new invariants, piling-up motivation, and rank-one approximations resolving an FSE 2019 open problem.
- **130900174.pdf** — Two-Round Adaptively Secure MPC from Isogenies, LPN, or CDH (Alamati, Montgomery, Patranabis, Sarkar). Builds round-optimal adaptively secure malicious MPC from a new r-iOT notion, instantiable from CDH, LPN, or CSIDH-type group actions.
- **130900177.pdf** — Franchised Quantum Money (Roberts, Zhandry). Introduces franchised quantum money with per-user verification keys, secure against counterfeiting and sabotage, constructed from one-way functions.
- **130900185.pdf** — Private Join and Compute from PIR with Default (Lepoint, Patel, Raykova, Seth, Trieu). Introduces PIR-with-default enabling two-party private join-and-compute with sublinear communication hiding intersection size; practical costs for ad-conversion measurement.
- **130900188.pdf** — Astrolabous: A Universally Composable Time-Lock Encryption Scheme (Arapinis, Lamprou, Zacharias). Formalizes time-lock encryption as a UC ideal functionality and gives Astrolabous, the first UC-secure TLE construction in the random oracle model.
- **130900191.pdf** — Transciphering Framework for Approximate Homomorphic Encryption (Cho et al.). Proposes RtF framework combining CKKS and FV via a stream cipher, plus the new HE-friendly cipher HERA, reducing CKKS ciphertext expansion.
- **130900196.pdf** — Beyond Software Watermarking: Traitor-Tracing for Pseudorandom Functions (Goyal, Kim, Waters, Wu). Exposes flaws in existing watermarking security definitions and recasts PRF watermarking as traitor tracing with strengthened notions.
- **130900199.pdf** — Quantum Computationally Predicate-Binding Commitments with Application in Quantum Zero-Knowledge Arguments for NP (Jun Yan). Shows parallel-composed quantum computationally-binding commitments satisfy a stronger predicate-binding property, yielding quantum ZK arguments for NP.
- **130900203.pdf** — (Compact) Adaptively Secure FE for Attribute-Weighted Sums from k-Lin (Datta, Pal). First adaptively simulation-secure functional encryption for attribute-weighted sums, with vector attributes and arithmetic-branching-program weight functions, from k-Lin.
- **130900204.pdf** — Quantum Encryption with Certified Deletion, Revisited (Hiroka, Morimae, Nishimaki, Yamakawa). Extends certified-deletion encryption to reusable-key PKE and ABE with public verifiability, plus classical-communication variants.
- **130900205.pdf** — Fault-Injection Attacks against NIST's Post-Quantum Cryptography Round 3 KEM Candidates (Xagawa, Ito, Ueno, Takahashi, Homma). Surveys and extends key-recovery fault attacks skipping FO re-encryption checks across all Round 3 KEMs, including a new NTRU Prime attack.
- **130900207.pdf** — Lattice Enumeration for Tower NFS: a 521-bit Discrete Logarithm Computation (De Micheli, Gaudry, Pierrot). First TNFS implementation using lattice enumeration over a spherical sieving area, setting a 521-bit DL record in Fp6 relevant to blockchain ZK fields.
- **130900209.pdf** — Batching Base Oblivious Transfers (McQuoid, Rosulek, Roy). Shows common batch optimization of 2-round OT is error-prone, catalogs flawed implementations, and gives correct batching plus performance improvements.
- **130900213.pdf** — FAST: Secure and High Performance Format-Preserving Encryption and Tokenization (Durak, Horst, Horst, Vaudenay). SPN-based FPE/tokenization scheme over small domains using random S-boxes, with adaptive super-PRP security and quantum-security parameter suggestions.
- **130900215.pdf** — Divided We Stand, United We Fall: Security Analysis of Some SCA+SIFA Countermeasures Against SCA-Enhanced Fault Template Attacks (Saha, Bag, Jap, Mukhopadhyay, Bhasin). Shows combined SCA+SIFA countermeasures fail against side-channel-enhanced fault template attacks.
- **130900223.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of an oblivious PRF from supersingular isogenies (Basso, Kutas, Merz, Petit, Sanso). Breaks the Boneh–Kogan–Woo SIDH-based OPRF via attacks on the auxiliary one-more assumption, including polynomial-time and subexponential variants.
- **130900228.pdf** — Identity-Based Encryption for Fair Anonymity Applications (Wang, Chen, Huang, Ning, Wang, Yung). Defines and constructs rerandomizable RCCA-secure anonymous IBE, reconciling ciphertext rerandomization with active security for fair-anonymity applications.
- **130900229.pdf** — Simulation-Based Bi-Selective Opening Security for Public Key Encryption (Lai, Yang, Huang, Weng). Introduces bi-selective opening security where adversaries corrupt senders and receivers simultaneously, with simulation-based definitions and constructions.
- **130900230.pdf** — Reverse Firewalls for Adaptively Secure MPC without Setup (Chakraborty, Ganesh, Pancholi, Sarkar). Initiates reverse firewalls for adaptively corrupted, subverted MPC, defining the model and constructing actively secure protocols without setup.
- **130900235.pdf** — Massive Superpoly Recovery with Nested Monomial Predictions (Hu, Sun, Todo, Wang, Wang). New monomial-prediction framework recovering exact superpolies for cube attacks, pushing rounds for Trivium, Grain-128AEAD, and Kreyvium beyond prior limits.
- **130900238.pdf** — Chain Reductions for Multi-Signatures and the HBMS Scheme (Bellare, Dai). Gives tight security proofs for DL-based multi-signatures (BN, MuSig) via chain reductions in AGM or from non-DL assumptions, plus new 2-round HBMS scheme.
- **130900239.pdf** — Generalized Channels from Limited Blockchain Scripts and Adaptor Signatures (Aumayr et al.). Formalizes generalized channels enabling arbitrary off-chain operations on script-limited blockchains using adaptor signatures.
- **130900241.pdf** — Tight adaptive reprogramming in the QROM (no author listed). Proves a tight bound on QROM adaptive reprogramming with a matching attack, yielding tighter proofs for XMSS, Fiat–Shamir, and hedged signatures.
- **130900249.pdf** — Fine-tuning the ISO/IEC Standard LightMAC (Chattopadhyay, Jha, Nandi). Reduces LightMAC to a single block-cipher key (1k-LightMAC) and variant LightMAC-ds while retaining O(q²/2ⁿ) query-length-independent bounds.
- **130900254.pdf** — Snarky Ceremonies (Kohlweiss, Maller, Siim, Volkhov). Provides a security framework for SNARK SRS ceremony protocols and simplifies/optimizes the Groth16 ceremony, removing the random-beacon assumption.
- **130900263.pdf** — Key Encapsulation Mechanism with Tight Enhanced Security in the Multi-User Setting: Impossibility Result and Optimal Tightness (Han, Liu, Gu). Formalizes ECPA/ECCA multi-user KEM security and proves impossibility plus optimal tightness loss via a new meta-reduction tool.
- **130900269.pdf** — Clustering Effect in Simon and Simeck (Leurent, Pernot, Schrottenloher). Exploits differential/linear trail clustering within fixed active-bit windows to build stronger distinguishers, improving attacks by up to 7 rounds on Simon/Simeck.
- **130900271.pdf** — Efficient Leakage-Resilient MACs without Idealized Assumptions (Berti, Guo, Peters, Standaert). Leakage-resilient MAC from a collision-resistant hash and one TBC call per message, with beyond-birthday bounds under standard assumptions.
- **130900272.pdf** — Shorter Lattice-Based Group Signatures via "Almost Free" Encryption and Other Optimizations (Lyubashevsky, Nguyen, Plançon, Seiler). Lattice group signatures of ~200KB independent of group size, a 3x reduction over prior quantum-safe schemes.
- **130900274.pdf** — QCB: Efficient Quantum-secure Authenticated Encryption (Bhaumik, Bonnetain, Chailloux, Leurent, Naya-Plasencia, Schrottenloher, Seurin). Generalizes superposition attacks on OCB-like modes and proposes QCB, a rate-one parallelizable AE mode secure against quantum queries.
- **130900276.pdf** — Efficient NIZKs for Algebraic Sets (Couteau, Lipmaa, Parisella, Ødegaard). General methodology for pairing-based NIZKs that an encrypted vector lies in an algebraic set, yielding compact arguments for ABPs, R1CS, and NP.
- **130900282.pdf** — Categorization of Faulty Nonce Misuse Resistant Message Authentication (Chen, Mennink, Preneel). Exhaustive classification of beyond-birthday MACs from two block-cipher calls plus universal hashing under nonce misuse and faulty nonces.
- **130900290.pdf** — ConTra Corona: Contact Tracing against the Coronavirus by Bridging the Centralized–Decentralized Divide for Stronger Privacy (Beskorovajnov et al.). Hybrid COVID contact-tracing protocol protecting infected users' status via upload-what-you-observed and server-side separation of duties.
- **130900291.pdf** — Quantum Linearization Attacks (Bonnetain, Leurent, Naya-Plasencia, Schrottenloher). Introduces quantum linearization using Simon's algorithm over multi-block inputs, breaking parallelizable MACs like LightMAC and PMAC in the superposition model.
- **130900292.pdf** — DEFAULT: Cipher Level Resistance Against Differential Fault Attack (Baksi, Bhasin, Breier, Khairallah, Peyrin, Sarkar, Sim). First cipher-level DFA-resistant design methodology, protecting without device/protocol assumptions or duplication.
- **130900294.pdf** — Algebraic Adversaries in the Universal Composability Framework (Abdalla, Barbosa, Katz, Loss, Xu). Formalizes the algebraic-group model within UC, proves a composition theorem, and applies it to several protocols.
- **130900295.pdf** — Cryptographic Analysis of the Bluetooth Secure Connection Protocol Suite (Fischlin, Sanina). Analyzes the full Bluetooth Secure Connections suite, showing key secrecy holds only under trust-on-first-use given cross-subprotocol attacks.
- **130900300.pdf** — Boosting the Security of Blind Signature Schemes (Katz, Loss, Rosenberg). First efficient blind signatures secure for polynomially many concurrent sessions from RSA, factoring, or DL in the ROM, generalizing Pointcheval's transform.
- **130900302.pdf** — Generic Framework for Key-Guessing Improvements (Broll, Canale, Flórez-Gutiérrez, Leander, Naya-Plasencia). Uses affine decision trees over S-box properties to optimize key-guessing, yielding best known attacks on Noekeon, GIFT, and RECTANGLE.
- **130900310.pdf** — A Systematic Approach and Analysis of Key Mismatch Attacks on Lattice-Based NIST Candidate KEMs (Qin, Cheng, Zhang, Pan, Hu, Ding). Develops a unified method deriving lower bounds on oracle queries needed for key-mismatch attacks against lattice KEMs.
- **130900314.pdf** — Symmetric Key Exchange with Full Forward Security and Robust Synchronization (Boyd, Davies, de Kock, Gellert, Jager, Millerjord). Lightweight pre-shared-key AKE with forward security via linear/non-linear key evolution, introducing synchronization robustness as a formal goal.
- **130900331.pdf** — Random Probing Expansion: Quasi Linear Gadgets & Dynamic Compilers (Belaïd, Rivain, Taleb, Vergnaud). Advances random-probing masking compilers with quasi-linear gadgets and dynamic compilers capturing horizontal side-channel attacks.
- **130900334.pdf** — Improved Programmable Bootstrapping with Larger Precision and Efficient Arithmetic Circuits for TFHE (Chillotti, Ligier, Orfila, Tap). Introduces WoP-PBS removing TFHE's padding-bit requirement via BFV-style multiplication and generalized bootstrapping, improving precision.
- **130900336.pdf** — New Attacks on LowMC instances with a Single Plaintext/Ciphertext pair (Banik, Barooti, Vaudenay, Yan). Refines linearization attacks and adds a 2-stage MITM key-recovery on LowMC with one known pair, relevant to Picnic signatures.
- **130900337.pdf** — Hierarchical Integrated Signature and Encryption (Chen, Tang, Wang). Introduces HISE letting one public key serve signature and encryption with delegation of decryption; generic constructions from IBE or uniform OWFs.
