# Batch 047 digest

## Overview
- Entire batch appears to be CRYPTO 2023 proceedings papers (Springer-style first pages, ~21–35p each), spanning the full modern cryptography spectrum rather than the corpus's nominal elliptic-curve focus.
- Post-quantum themes dominate: lattice cryptography (tight AKE, compact gadgets, LWR hardness barriers, fast lattice reduction, timed crypto), code-based cryptanalysis (Durandal, BIKE), isogeny blind signatures (CSI-Otter), and an attack on the ATFE signature scheme.
- Strong cluster of threshold/distributed signatures and MPC: Sparkle, Snowblind, two-party deterministic Schnorr, Fluid MPC, round-optimal black-box MPC, class-group threshold encryption, Bingo VSS/DKG.
- Symmetric crypto well represented: practical related-key attack on GOST, Griffin ZK-friendly hash, indifferentiability corrections for sum-of-permutations, Squared-Ratio multi-user method, backdoor-avoidance criteria for SPNs, Gaston permutation.
- Elliptic-curve-specific content is thin: only pairing-friendly curve cycles (140850167), the CSIDH class-group action (140850136), and class-group encryption (140850104) touch EC machinery.
- Notable "proof repair" theme: several papers fix flaws in prior security proofs (Dilithium Fiat-Shamir-with-aborts, XMSS/SPHINCS+, sum of permutations). Anomalies: source chunk contained 3 NUL bytes (cleaned for reading); no duplicates or non-paper items found.

## Papers
- **140850038.pdf** — Graph-Theoretic Algorithms for the Alternating Trilinear Form Equivalence Problem (Ward Beullens). New heuristic algorithms break two of three parameter sets of the Eurocrypt'22 ATFE-based signature; the third falls short of 128-bit security.
- **140850054.pdf** — Lattice-based Authenticated Key Exchange with Tight Security (Pan, Wagner, Zeng). First tightly secure AKE from a post-quantum assumption, via a new OW-ChCCA KEM notion built from LWE; also yields tightly BiSO-secure PKE.
- **140850060.pdf** — PAC Privacy: Automatic Privacy Measurement and Control of Data Processing (Xiao, Devadas). New instance-based privacy definition with a Monte-Carlo framework that automatically generates privacy parameters for black-box data processing, with composition bounds.
- **140850064.pdf** — New Bounds on the Local Leakage Resilience of Shamir's Secret Sharing Scheme (Klein, Komargodski). Improves the threshold under which Shamir's scheme over prime fields stays secure when each share leaks a little information.
- **140850067.pdf** — Efficient Hybrid Exact/Relaxed Lattice Proofs and Applications to Rounding and VRFs (Esgin, Steinfeld, Liu, Ruj). LANES+ framework for hybrid exact/relaxed lattice zero-knowledge proofs, giving shorter proofs of rounding and verifiable deterministic functions.
- **140850072.pdf** — MacORAMa: Optimal Oblivious RAM with Integrity (Mathialagan, Vafa). First maliciously secure ORAM with worst-case O(log N) overhead and O(1) client storage from one-way functions, matching the lower bound.
- **140850073.pdf** — Analysis of the security of the PSSI problem and cryptanalysis of the Durandal signature scheme (Aragon, Dyseryn, Gaborit). Key-recovery attack from signature leakage breaks all published parameters of the rank-metric signature Durandal.
- **140850075.pdf** — Round-Optimal Black-Box MPC in the Plain Model (Ishai, Khurana, Sahai, Srinivasan). First fully black-box round-optimal (4-round) MPC in the plain model, from sub-exponentially secure two-message statistical sender-private OT.
- **140850079.pdf** — Black-Hole Radiation Decoding is Quantum Cryptography (Brakerski). Shows an existential equivalence between hardness of decoding Hawking radiation and secure quantum primitives such as commitments and oblivious transfer.
- **140850086.pdf** — Horst Meets Fluid-SPN: Griffin for Zero-Knowledge Applications (Grassi, Hao, Rechberger, Schofnegger, Walch, Wang). New prime-field hash function Griffin combining Horst and Fluid-SPN ideas for efficient zero-knowledge proof systems.
- **140850092.pdf** — Compact Lattice Gadget and Its Applications to Hash-and-Sign Signatures (Yu, Jia, Wang). Develops a compact lattice gadget improving the practicality and efficiency of gadget-based hash-and-sign signatures.
- **140850096.pdf** — Revisiting Time-Space Tradeoffs for Function Inversion (Golovnev, Guo, Peters, Stephens-Davidowitz). New algorithms for black-box function inversion with preprocessing, improving the classic Fiat–Naor tradeoff, plus a simplified proof of the original result.
- **140850097.pdf** — Practical-Time Related-Key Attack on GOST with Secret S-boxes (Dunkelman, Keller, Weizmann). First practical (~2^27 encryptions) related-key differential attack on full 32-round GOST without knowing the S-boxes.
- **140850098.pdf** — On the Security of Keyed Hashing Based on Public Permutations (Fuchs, Rotella, Daemen). Universality analysis of Farfalle-style deck functions; shows parallel compression outperforms serial, demonstrated with Xoodoo.
- **140850099.pdf** — Revisiting the Indifferentiability of the Sum of Permutations (Gunsing, Bhaumik, Jha, Mennink, Shen). Uncovers serious flaws in prior n-bit indifferentiability proofs for the sum of two public permutations and revisits the whole line of research.
- **140850101.pdf** — On Optimal Tightness for Key Exchange with Full Forward Secrecy via Key Confirmation (Gellert, Gjøsteen, Jacobsen, Jager). Impossibility result: adding key confirmation for full forward secrecy incurs linear tightness loss for natural protocols, disproving a prior claim.
- **140850102.pdf** — Reductions from module lattices to free module lattices, and application to dequantizing module-LLL (De Micheli, Micciancio, Pellet-Mary, Tran). Reduces SVP-type problems on arbitrary rank≥2 modules to free modules, dequantizing the module-LLL algorithm.
- **140850103.pdf** — Tri-State Circuits: A Circuit Model that Captures RAM (Heath, Kolesnikov, Ostrovsky). New circuit model evaluating RAM programs with O(T log³T log log T) gates; enables authenticated garbled RAM and constant-round malicious 2PC of RAM programs.
- **140850104.pdf** — Secure Multiparty Computation from Threshold Encryption based on Class Groups (Braun, Damgård, Orlandi). First actively secure threshold version of the CL class-group cryptosystem, yielding UC-secure MPC with transparent setup and a YOSO adaptation.
- **140850109.pdf** — Arithmetic Sketching (Boneh, Boyle, Corrigan-Gibbs, Gilboa, Ishai). Formalizes arithmetic sketching for low-communication zero-knowledge verification of secret-shared vectors, with a general framework from algebraic varieties.
- **140850111.pdf** — Streaming Functional Encryption (Guan, Korb, Sahai). Introduces streaming FE supporting sequential encryption and partial decryption of prefixes; constructs it generically from compact FE for P/Poly.
- **140850114.pdf** — Correlation Intractability and SNARGs from Sub-exponential DDH (Choudhuri, Garg, Jain, Jin, Zhang). First SNARGs for Batch-NP and P from sub-exponential DDH, via new correlation-intractable hash functions for small-input product relations.
- **140850128.pdf** — Exploring Decryption Failures of BIKE: New Class of Weak Keys and Key Recovery Attacks (Wang, Wang, Wang). Characterizes QC-MDPC decryption failures via a "gathering property", identifies weak-key classes, and gives key-recovery attacks on BIKE.
- **140850131.pdf** — Two-Round Stateless Deterministic Two-Party Schnorr Signatures From Pseudorandom Correlation Functions (Kondi, Orlandi, Roy). First two-party deterministic Schnorr threshold protocol making black-box use of primitives, built on pseudorandom correlation functions.
- **140850136.pdf** — CSI-Otter: Isogeny-based (Partially) Blind Signatures from the Class Group Action with a Twist (Katsumata, Lai, LeGrow, Qin). First provably secure isogeny-based blind signature, exploiting quadratic twists of the CSIDH class group action; 128 B keys, 8 KB signatures.
- **140850137.pdf** — Universal Amplification of KDM Security: From 1-Key Circular to Multi-Key KDM (Waters, Wichs). Upgrades 1-key circular-secure bit encryption plus CPA-secure PKE to multi-key KDM security for general bounded-size functions.
- **140850152.pdf** — On Linear Communication Complexity for (Maximally) Fluid MPC (Bienstock, Escudero, Polychroniadou). Fluid MPC protocols with O(n) communication per gate in honest-majority and preprocessed dishonest-majority settings, with maximal fluidity.
- **140850158.pdf** — Fixing and Mechanizing the Security Proof of Fiat-Shamir with Aborts and Dilithium (Barbosa et al.). Identifies and repairs a subtle gap in CMA-to-NMA reductions for Fiat-Shamir-with-aborts signatures, with mechanized proofs and concrete Dilithium analysis.
- **140850161.pdf** — Machine-Checked Security for XMSS as in RFC 8391 and SPHINCS+ (Barbosa, Dupressoir, Grégoire, Hülsing, Meijers, Strub). EasyCrypt-verified tight security proof for XMSS, completing the fix of a flaw affecting SPHINCS+/XMSS proofs.
- **140850165.pdf** — Cloning Games: A General Framework for Unclonable Primitives (Ananth, Kaleoglu, Liu). Unifying "cloning games" framework relating quantum money, copy-protection, and unclonable encryption, with new information-theoretic and QROM constructions.
- **140850167.pdf** — Revisiting cycles of pairing-friendly elliptic curves (Bellés-Muñoz, Jiménez Urroz, Silva). Proves no new 2-cycles arise from known polynomial families of pairing-friendly curves and analyzes the density of pairing-friendly cycles.
- **140850176.pdf** — Fast Blind Rotation for Bootstrapping FHEs (Xiang, Zhang, Deng, Dai, Feng). New NTRU-based blind rotation outperforming AP and GINX in key size and efficiency, yielding faster LWE/RLWE bootstrapping.
- **140850178.pdf** — Attribute-Based Multi-Input FE (and more) for Attribute-Weighted Sums (Agrawal, Tomida, Yadav). First attribute-based multi-input functional encryption for attribute-weighted sums, extending Crypto 2020 AWS-FE to the multi-party setting.
- **140850182.pdf** — Fully Adaptive Schnorr Threshold Signatures (Crites, Komlo, Maller). Proves adaptive security of the three-round threshold Schnorr scheme Sparkle under AOMDL in ROM/AGM, without requiring secure erasures.
- **140850184.pdf** — Snowblind: A Threshold Blind Signature in Pairing-Free Groups (Crites, Komlo, Maller, Tessaro, Zhu). First threshold blind signatures in pairing-free groups, via a transformation preserving blindness with three-round signing.
- **140850185.pdf** — TreePIR: Sublinear-Time and Polylog-Bandwidth Private Information Retrieval from DDH (Lazzaretti, Papamanthou). Two-server PIR with sublinear amortized server time and polylogarithmic bandwidth based only on DDH.
- **140850186.pdf** — Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge from Non-Interactive Batch Arguments (Champion, Wu). Builds NIZK for NP from batch arguments combined with a local pseudorandom generator and dual-mode commitments.
- **140850188.pdf** — On Concurrent Multi-Party Quantum Computation (Goyal, Liang, Malavolta). Systematic study of concurrent secure quantum computation: impossibility of concurrent 2PC for quantum functionalities, positive results in the bounded-concurrent setting.
- **140850193.pdf** — Improved Multi-User Security Using the Squared-Ratio Method (Chen, Choi, Lee). New information-theoretic multi-user proof technique yielding improved bounds for Xor-of-two-permutations, Encrypted Davies-Meyer, and nEHtM.
- **140850201.pdf** — Algebraic Reductions of Knowledge (Kothapalli, Parno). Introduces reductions of knowledge as a compositional generalization of arguments of knowledge, unifying recursive argument techniques via a tensor reduction.
- **140850202.pdf** — Practical Settlement Bounds for Longest-Chain Consensus (Gaži, Ren, Russell). New analysis giving explicit rigorous settlement error/latency bounds for proof-of-stake longest-chain protocols, and improved bounds for proof-of-work.
- **140850206.pdf** — Completeness Theorems for Adaptively Secure Broadcast (Cohen, Garay, Zikas). Shows the adaptively secure broadcast impossibility is inherent (not an artifact of simulation), plus completeness results via resource-restricted cryptography.
- **140850208.pdf** — A Lower Bound for Proving Hardness of Learning with Rounding with Polynomial Modulus (Newton, Richelson). Identifies a barrier to proving LWR hardness from LWE when the modulus is polynomial and the adversary sees unboundedly many samples.
- **140850218.pdf** — Lattice-Based Timed Cryptography (Lai, Malavolta). New lattice-based sequentiality assumption enabling proofs of sequential work without repeated squaring in unknown-order groups.
- **140850219.pdf** — Combined Fault and Leakage Resilience: Composability, Constructions and Compiler (Berndt, Eisenbarth, Faust, Gourjon, Orlt, Seker). Polynomial-masking compiler achieving combined side-channel and fault resilience with linear complexity for affine functions.
- **140850221.pdf** — Twin Column Parity Mixers and Gaston: A New Mixing Layer and Permutation (El Hirch, Daemen, Rohit, Makarim). New circulant twin column parity mixer with differential branch number 12, instantiated in the Gaston permutation.
- **140850227.pdf** — Bingo: Adaptivity and Asynchrony in Verifiable Secret Sharing and Distributed Key Generation (Abraham, Jovanovic, Maller, Meiklejohn, Stern). Adaptively secure packed asynchronous VSS with O(λn²) words, yielding the first adaptive asynchronous DKG matching static-scheme complexity.
- **140850230.pdf** — On Perfect Linear Approximations and Differentials over Two-Round SPNs (Beierle, Felke, Leander, Neumann, Stennes). Design criteria on S-box and linear layer guaranteeing absence of probability-one differentials and perfect linear approximations (anti-backdoor).
- **140850234.pdf** — Fast Practical Lattice Reduction through Iterated Compression (Ryan, Heninger). New lattice basis reduction with LLL-like guarantees but far faster practical performance; validated with extensive benchmarks and a public implementation.
- **140850235.pdf** — Security Analysis of the WhatsApp End-to-End Encrypted Backup Protocol (Davies, Faller, Gellert, Handirk, Hesse, Horváth, Jager). First formal UC analysis of WhatsApp's OPAQUE-based E2EE backup; confirms strong guarantees but shows a corrupted server can make extra password guesses.
