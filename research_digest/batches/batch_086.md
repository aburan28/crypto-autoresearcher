# Batch 086 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by two 2010 conference proceedings: TCC 2010 (the 59780xxx block) and PKC 2010 (the 60560xxx block), plus two older outliers (5_21.pdf, 605.pdf, ~2005 era).
- Heavy theory-of-cryptography focus: secure computation / fairness / covert security, UC assumptions, zero-knowledge (concurrent, non-malleable, Σ-protocols), obfuscation, leakage resilience, and foundational assumptions (DDH, LWE, subset sum).
- The PKC block adds applied topics: pairing/ECC implementation (high-degree twists, Hessian curves, cyclotomic squaring, Groth–Sahai fixes), attribute-based and proxy re-encryption, network coding signatures, and a full break of the PKC'2009 Algebraic Surface Cryptosystem.
- Two files are one-page invited-talk abstracts (Ishai on MPC applications; Camenisch on privacy-enhancing crypto), not full papers.
- Anomaly: 60560053.pdf contains only chart-axis fragments ("Jochemsz–May / Our approach"), not a readable paper — likely a figure page from a small-RSA-exponent attack paper.

## Papers
- **59780037.pdf** — Almost Optimal Bounds for Direct Product Threshold Theorem (Charanjit S. Jutla). Improves hardness bounds for threshold parallel repetition of weakly-verifiable puzzles to nearly match the Chernoff bound.
- **59780052.pdf** — On Symmetric Encryption and Point Obfuscation (Canetti, Kalai, Varia, Wichs). Shows tight connections between weakly-random-key encryption, KDM security, and multi-bit point function obfuscation, porting constructions and impossibility results.
- **59780071.pdf** — Obfuscation of Hyperplane Membership (Canetti, Rothblum, Varia). Constructs an obfuscator for constant-dimension hyperplane membership testing under a strong DDH variant, with an application to digital signatures.
- **59780089.pdf** — Secure Computation and Its Diverse Applications (Yuval Ishai). One-page invited-talk abstract surveying connections between MPC and codes, parallel cryptography, private circuits, and MPC-in-the-head.
- **59780090.pdf** — On Complete Primitives for Fairness (Gordon, Ishai, Moran, Ostrovsky, Sahai). Initiates study of complete primitives for fair secure computation, proving no short (O(log k)) primitive is complete and exhibiting a fairness hierarchy.
- **59780108.pdf** — On the Necessary and Sufficient Assumptions for UC Computation (Damgård, Nielsen, Orlandi). Characterizes setup/computational assumptions for UC security; shows any non-trivial key-registration authority plus passive OT suffices, and OWFs suffice for UC commitments/ZK.
- **59780126.pdf** — From Passive to Covert Security at Low Cost (Damgård, Geisler, Nielsen). Compiles passively secure honest-majority protocols into covert-secure ones catching cheating with probability 1/4 at roughly twice the cost.
- **59780144.pdf** — A Twist on the Naor-Yung Paradigm and Its Application to Efficient CCA-Secure Encryption (Cramer, Hofheinz, Kiltz). Reworks Naor-Yung to yield practical IND-CCA encryption from DH-type two-move key exchange assumptions, avoiding expensive NIZKs.
- **59780162.pdf** — Two Is A Crowd? A Black-Box Separation of One-Wayness and Security Under Correlated Inputs (Yevgeniy Vahlis). Proves no black-box construction of correlation-secure injective trapdoor functions from classic trapdoor permutations.
- **59780180.pdf** — Efficient, Robust and Constant-Round Distributed RSA Key Generation (Damgård, Mikkelsen). First constant-round maliciously secure distributed RSA key generation with negligible error even with a single primality-test iteration.
- **59780198.pdf** — Threshold Decryption and Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Lattice-Based Cryptosystems (Bendlin, Damgård). Regev-variant LWE scheme with efficient non-interactive threshold decryption and constant-overhead ZK proofs of plaintext knowledge.
- **59780216.pdf** — Ideal Hierarchical Secret Sharing Schemes (Farràs, Padró). Completely characterizes which hierarchical access structures admit ideal secret sharing schemes, using matroid ports and integer polymatroids.
- **59780234.pdf** — A Hardcore Lemma for Computational Indistinguishability (Maurer, Tessaro). Proves a computational hardcore lemma for indistinguishability, giving security amplification for arbitrarily weak PRGs with optimal stretch.
- **59780252.pdf** — On Related-Secret Pseudorandomness (Goldenberg, Liskov). Develops a related-secret security framework and shows typical hard-core bits are not related-secret pseudorandom, a barrier to provably related-key-secure blockciphers.
- **59780270.pdf** — A Domain Extender for the Ideal Cipher (Coron, Dodis, Mandal, Seurin). Builds a 2n-bit ideal cipher from an n-bit one via 3-round Feistel, indifferentiable; shows 2 rounds insufficient and gives tweakable-cipher variants.
- **59780288.pdf** — Delayed-Key Message Authentication for Streams (Fischlin, Lehmann). Formalizes MACs where the key arrives only after the stream (as in SSL/TLS handshakes) and gives online schemes as secure as key-available ones.
- **59780306.pdf** — Founding Cryptography on Tamper-Proof Hardware Tokens (Goyal, Ishai, Sahai, Venkatesan, Wadia). Shows unconditional non-interactive secure computation and other plain-model-impossible tasks become feasible with simple tamper-proof tokens.
- **59780324.pdf** — Truly Efficient String Oblivious Transfer Using Resettable Tamper-Proof Tokens (Kolesnikov). String OT from stateless/resettable tokens needing only 6–27 symmetric operations, secure against covert sender and malicious receiver.
- **59780342.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Signatures (Faust, Kiltz, Pietrzak, Rothblum). Defines leakage-resilient signatures and constructs a tree-based stateful scheme from any 3-time signature, tolerating leakage of 1/3 of state per signature.
- **59780360.pdf** — Public-Key Encryption Schemes with Auxiliary Inputs (Dodis, Goldwasser, Kalai, Peikert, Vaikuntanathan). PKE secure even when the adversary gets any uninvertible function of the secret key, from DDH and LWE; extends Goldreich-Levin over large fields.
- **59780380.pdf** — Public-Key Cryptographic Primitives Provably as Secure as Subset Sum (Lyubashevsky, Palacio, Segev). Semantically secure PKE polynomially equivalent to random subset sum, plus leakage-resilient variant and semi-honest OT.
- **59780398.pdf** — Rationality in the Full-Information Model (Ronen Gradwohl). Studies faithful, resilient protocols for rational players in the full-information model, with results and impossibilities for leader election and random sampling.
- **59780416.pdf** — Efficient Rational Secret Sharing in Standard Communication Networks (Fuchsbauer, Katz, Naccache). Rational secret sharing over asynchronous point-to-point networks with new strict-Nash/trembling equilibrium notions preventing subliminal channels.
- **59780434.pdf** — Bounds on the Sample Complexity for Private Learning and Private Data Release (Beimel, Kasiviswanathan, Nissim). Gives tight sample-complexity bounds for differentially private PAC learning and private data release, narrowing the gap with VC-dimension characterizations.
- **59780452.pdf** — New Techniques for Dual System Encryption and Fully Secure HIBE with Short Ciphertexts (Lewko, Waters). First fully secure HIBE with short ciphertexts, via a new dual-system-encryption realization in composite-order groups under static assumptions.
- **59780477.pdf** — Robust Encryption (Abdalla, Bellare, Neven). Formalizes robustness (no ciphertext valid under two keys), shows naive recipient-tagging fails, and gives provable transforms plus patches for existing schemes.
- **59780495.pdf** — Privacy-Enhancing Cryptography: From Theory Into Practice (Jan Camenisch). One-page invited-talk abstract on using cryptographic tools to protect personal data, and on industry/government readiness.
- **59780496.pdf** — Concise Mercurial Vector Commitments and Independent Zero-Knowledge Sets with Short Proofs (Libert, Yung). Shorter trapdoor q-mercurial commitments yielding zero-knowledge sets with short membership and non-membership proofs.
- **59780514.pdf** — Eye for an Eye: Efficient Concurrent Zero-Knowledge in the Timing Model (Pass, Tseng, Venkitasubramaniam). Concurrent ZK in the timing model that delays messages only by each verifier's actual response time rather than the slowest network link.
- **59780532.pdf** — Efficiency Preserving Transformations for Concurrent Non-Malleable Zero Knowledge (Ostrovsky, Pandey, Visconti). Compiles any public-coin HVZK argument of knowledge into concurrent non-malleable ZK under DDH with small round/computation overhead.
- **59780550.pdf** — Efficiency Limitations for Σ-Protocols for Group Homomorphisms (Bangerter, Camenisch, Krenn). Generic-group-model evidence that efficient Σ-protocols in hidden-order groups cannot achieve negligible soundness error (bounded below by 1/2).
- **59780568.pdf** — Composition of Zero-Knowledge Proofs with Efficient Provers (Birrell, Vadhan). Revisits sequential/parallel composability of zero knowledge when provers are polynomial-time, showing closure and non-closure results under different definitions.
- **59780584.pdf** — Private Coins versus Public Coins in Zero-Knowledge Proof Systems (Pass, Venkitasubramaniam). Extends Goldreich–Krawczyk lower bounds to fully black-box private-coin constant-round ZK, with near-linear round lower bounds for concurrent ZK.
- **5_21.pdf** — Faster Correlation Attack on Bluetooth Keystream Generator E0 (Lu, Vaudenay). Best known key-recovery attack on Bluetooth E0: 2^39 time given 2^39 bits, using recursive correlation computation and fast Walsh transform decoding.
- **605.pdf** — Server-Aided Verification: Theory and Practice (Girault, Lefranc). Formalizes delegating signature/ID verification to an untrusted server, proves security of Lim-Lee/Schnorr and Girault-Quisquater variants, and gives a pairing-based generic method.
- **60560001.pdf** — Simple and Efficient Public-Key Encryption from Computational Diffie-Hellman in the Standard Model (Haralambiev, Jager, Kiltz, Shoup). Practical CCA-secure PKE from the CDH assumption in the standard model, simpler and more efficient than prior schemes.
- **60560019.pdf** — Constant Size Ciphertexts in Threshold Attribute-Based Encryption (Herranz, Laguillaumie, Ràfols). First threshold ABE with constant-size ciphertexts, extendable to weighted threshold policies, selective-security proof.
- **60560035.pdf** — Algebraic Cryptanalysis of the PKC'2009 Algebraic Surface Cryptosystem (Faugère, Spaenlehauer). Fully breaks the ASC multivariate scheme via ideal decomposition, recovering messages for recommended 2^102-security parameters in 0.05 seconds.
- **60560053.pdf** — Not a readable paper: extract contains only chart-axis fragments ("Dimension", "Jochemsz–May", "Our approach"), apparently a figure page from a small-exponent RSA attack comparison.
- **60560088.pdf** — On the Feasibility of Consistent Computations (Laur, Lipmaa). Strengthens covert security so honest parties detect output-altering misbehavior; gives consistent OT, conditional disclosure of secrets, and private inference control.
- **60560107.pdf** — Multi-Query Computationally-Private Information Retrieval with Constant Communication Rate (Groth, Kiayias, Lipmaa). Settles multi-query CPIR optimally up to constant factors: an information-theoretic lower bound plus a matching protocol.
- **60560124.pdf** — Further Observations on Optimistic Fair Exchange Protocols in the Multi-user Setting (Huang, Mu, Susilo, Wu, Xiang). Defines "strong resolution-ambiguity" under which single-user-secure optimistic fair exchange lifts to the multi-user setting, with a new random-oracle-free construction.
- **60560142.pdf** — Secure Network Coding Over the Integers (Gennaro, Katz, Krawczyk, Rabin). Homomorphic signature/hashing schemes for network coding pollution defense based on RSA and factoring, with notable efficiency gains.
- **60560161.pdf** — Preventing Pollution Attacks in Multi-Source Network Coding (Agrawal, Boneh, Boyen, Freeman). Security model and generic construction for integrity when routers mix packets from multiple sources, extending homomorphic-signature defenses.
- **60560179.pdf** — Groth–Sahai proofs revisited (Ghadafi, Smart, Warinschi). Corrects errors in two of the three Groth–Sahai NIWI instantiations and enables more efficient proofs via asymmetric-pairing assumptions.
- **60560196.pdf** — Constant-Round Concurrent Non-Malleable Statistically Binding Commitments and Decommitments (Cao, Visconti, Zhang). Statistically binding constant-round commitment, concurrent non-malleable in both commitment and decommitment, from claw-free permutation pairs.
- **60560212.pdf** — Faster Squaring in the Cyclotomic Subgroup of Sixth Degree Extensions (Granger, Scott). Very efficient squaring in the cyclotomic subgroup of F_{q^6} via Weil restriction, speeding pairing- and torus-based cryptography.
- **60560227.pdf** — Faster Pairing Computations on Curves with High-Degree Twists (Costello, Lange, Naehrig). Faster explicit Miller-loop addition/doubling formulas for twists of degree 2, 3, 4, 6, speeding Tate and ate pairing variants.
- **60560246.pdf** — Efficient Arithmetic on Hessian Curves (Farashahi, Joye). Unified/complete addition formulas for generalized Hessian curves (curves with Z/3Z torsion); fastest projective addition at 9M+3S and 5M+4S differential addition in binary fields.
- **60560264.pdf** — CCA Proxy Re-Encryption without Bilinear Maps in the Standard Model (Matsuda, Nishimaki, Tanaka). Bidirectional PRE-CCA-secure proxy re-encryption without pairings or random oracles, built from a new "re-applicable" variant of DDH-based lossy trapdoor functions.
