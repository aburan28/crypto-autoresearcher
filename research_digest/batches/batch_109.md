# Batch 109 digest

## Overview
- Strongly homogeneous batch: essentially all papers are CRYPTO 2014 proceedings (LNCS-style, ~18p) spanning MPC, obfuscation, pairings, lattices, and symmetric crypto; two CHES 2014 papers close the batch.
- Heavy concentration on secure computation (MPC round complexity, identifiable abort, cut-and-choose, garbled circuits, NIMPC) and on obfuscation/multilinear maps (iO feasibility and impossibility, witness encryption, broadcast encryption).
- ECC/ECDLP-relevant highlights: Granger–Kleinjung–Zumbrägel break '128-bit' supersingular binary curves via quasi-polynomial DLP; Ben-Sasson et al. scalable zk-SNARKs via cycles of elliptic curves; Abe–Groth–Ohkubo–Tibouchi structure-preserving signatures from Type II pairings.
- Notable side-channel papers: Genkin–Shamir–Tromer acoustic RSA key extraction, and Benger et al. Flush+Reload lattice attack on OpenSSL ECDSA over secp256k1.
- No anomalies: every file is a genuine research paper extract; no duplicates, slides, or non-paper content detected.

## Papers
- **86160135.pdf** — Round-Efficient Black-Box Construction of Composable Multi-Party Computation (Susumu Kiyoshima). Black-box MPC in the angel-based UC plain model with Õ(log² n) rounds from semi-honest OT, via constant-round CCA-secure commitments from one-way functions.
- **86160136.pdf** — Hardness of k-LWE and Applications in Traitor Tracing (Ling, Phan, Stehlé, Steinfeld). Introduces k-LWE with a polynomial-loss LWE reduction and builds the first lattice-based traitor tracing scheme with public traceability from worst-case assumptions.
- **86160146.pdf** — Minimizing the Two-Round Even-Mansour Cipher (Chen, Lampe, Lee, Seurin, Steinberger). Studies security of the two-round Even-Mansour (key-alternating) cipher when keys and permutations are not independent, minimizing its components.
- **86160148.pdf** — The Security of Multiple Encryption in the Ideal Cipher Model (Dai, Lee, Mennink, Steinberger). Gives matching attacks and proofs, determining exact security of ℓ-round multiple encryption in the ideal cipher model via Patarin's H-coefficient method.
- **86160149.pdf** — RSA Key Extraction via Low-Bandwidth Acoustic Cryptanalysis (Genkin, Shamir, Tromer). Extracts full 4096-bit GnuPG RSA keys within an hour from acoustic emanations of decryption, using a phone or microphone up to 10 m away.
- **86160154.pdf** — Switching Lemma for Bilinear Tests and Constant-size NIZK Proofs for Linear Subspaces (Jutla, Roy). Switching lemma for pairing-based tests yields constant-size quasi-adaptive NIZK proofs for linear subspaces, independent of witnesses and equations.
- **86160156.pdf** — Low Overhead Broadcast Encryption from Multilinear Maps (Boneh, Waters, Zhandry). First fully collusion-resistant public-key broadcast encryption with ciphertext, private key, and public key sizes poly-logarithmic in user count, from multilinear maps.
- **86160161.pdf** — On the Communication Complexity of Secure Computation (Data, Prabhakaran, Prabhakaran). Develops information-theoretic tools giving tight lower bounds on communication complexity of 3-party information-theoretic MPC for various functions.
- **86160165.pdf** — Structure-Preserving Signatures from Type II Pairings (Abe, Groth, Ohkubo, Tibouchi). Surprisingly constructs Type-II structure-preserving signatures with one verification equation and two group elements, beating Type I/III lower bounds; studies verification-key size bounds.
- **86160166.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Cryptography with Tamperable Randomness (Austrin, Chung, Mahmoody, Pass, Seth). Shows encryption, commitments, and zero-knowledge break under p-tampering of honest randomness via a Fourier biasing technique; signatures can resist 1/poly tampering.
- **86160173.pdf** — Secure Multi-Party Computation with Identifiable Abort (Ishai, Ostrovsky, Zikas). Initiates ID-MPC: first information-theoretic protocol with identifiable abort from correlated randomness, and first black-box computational ID-MPC from OT.
- **86160175.pdf** — How to Eat Your Entropy and Have it Too — Optimal Recovery Strategies for Compromised RNGs (Dodis, Shamir, Stephens-Davidowitz, Wichs). Designs RNGs-with-input that recover from state compromise in near-optimal time without knowing compromise timing or entropy amounts.
- **86160189.pdf** — On the Implausibility of Differing-Inputs Obfuscation and Extractable Witness Encryption with Auxiliary Input (Garg, Gentry, Halevi, Wichs). Shows general-purpose diO with auxiliary input implies a specific circuit cannot be obfuscated, casting doubt on diO's existence.
- **86160193.pdf** — Memento: How to Reconstruct your Secrets from a Single Password in a Hostile Environment (Camenisch, Lehmann, Lysyanskaya, Neven). First t-out-of-n threshold password-authenticated secret sharing that does not leak the password to malicious servers, proven UC-secure.
- **86160195.pdf** — Quantum position verification in the random oracle model (Dominique Unruh). Quantum position verification scheme in the random oracle model without bounded-storage/entanglement assumptions, plus efficient position-based authentication.
- **86160202.pdf** — Scalable Zero Knowledge via Cycles of Elliptic Curves (Ben-Sasson, Chiesa, Tromer, Virza). Achieves scalable zk-SNARKs by recursive proof composition over cycles of elliptic curves, avoiding quasilinear proving keys and memory blowup.
- **86160215.pdf** — The Exact PRF-Security of NMAC and HMAC (Gaži, Pietrzak, Rybář). Gives a simple uniform proof that NMAC is a PRF when f is a PRF, and shows the ε+ℓqδ bound is essentially tight via matching attacks.
- **86160218.pdf** — Block Ciphers – Focus On The Linear Layer (feat. PRIDE) (Albrecht, Driessen, Kavun, Leander, Paar, Yalçın). General methodology for constructing security/efficiency trade-off linear layers for SPN ciphers; instantiates PRIDE, a fast 8-bit-microcontroller cipher.
- **86160224.pdf** — Self-bilinear Map on Unknown Order Groups from Indistinguishability Obfuscation and Its Applications (Yamakawa, Yamada, Hanaoka, Kunihiro). Builds self-bilinear maps with auxiliary information from iO under factoring, yielding multilinear maps with unbounded level and compact elements.
- **86160225.pdf** — Client-Server Concurrent Zero Knowledge with Constant Rounds and Guaranteed Complexity (Canetti, Jain, Paneth). Constant-round concurrent ZK where each client learns an a-priori bound on its session's communication complexity.
- **86160226.pdf** — Witness Encryption from Instance Independent Assumptions (Gentry, Lewko, Waters). Introduces positional witness encryption, a framework proving witness encryption secure from instance-independent assumptions via hybrid isolation of witnesses.
- **86160232.pdf** — Polynomial Spaces: A New Framework for Composite-to-Prime-Order Transformations (Herold, Hesse, Hofheinz, Ràfols, Rupp). New polynomial framework for converting composite-order cryptosystems to prime-order, nearly doubling Groth–Sahai verification efficiency and circumventing lower bounds.
- **86160233.pdf** — Non-Interactive Secure Multiparty Computation (Beimel, Gabizon, Ishai, Kushilevitz, Meldgaard, Paskin-Cherniavsky). Introduces NIMPC: parties send single messages from correlated randomness; studies robustness notions and feasibility for general functions.
- **86160235.pdf** — Automated Analysis of Cryptographic Assumptions in Generic Group Models (Barthe, Fagerholm, Fiore, Mitchell, Scedrov, Schmidt). Automated tool that proves or attacks hardness assumptions in generic (leveled multilinear) group models via symbolic methods and master theorems.
- **86160238.pdf** — The Impossibility of Obfuscation with Auxiliary Input or a Universal Simulator (Bitansky, Canetti, Cohn, Goldwasser, Kalai, Paneth, Rosen). iO for general circuits implies impossibility of average-case VBB obfuscation with auxiliary input or universal simulator.
- **86160239.pdf** — Improved Short Lattice Signatures in the Standard Model (Ducas, Micciancio). Standard-model lattice signatures from ideal-SVP with single-vector signatures and O(log n)-vector public keys, improving prior key sizes.
- **86160240.pdf** — Related-Key Security for Pseudorandom Functions Beyond the Linear Barrier (Abdalla, Benhamouda, Passelègue, Paterson). Repairs and extends the Bellare–Cash framework to build RKA-secure PRFs for claw-containing, beyond-linear classes of RKD functions.
- **86160245.pdf** — Security of Symmetric Encryption against Mass Surveillance (Bellare, Paterson, Rogaway). Formalizes algorithm-substitution attacks on symmetric encryption, shows randomized stateless schemes are subvertable, and designs surveillance-resistant schemes.
- **86160248.pdf** — Optimal Non-Perfect Uniform Secret Sharing Schemes (Farràs, Hansen, Kaced, Padró). Extends polymatroid methods to non-perfect secret sharing, determines optimal information ratio for uniform access functions generalizing threshold structures.
- **86160252.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation and UCEs: The Case of Computationally Unpredictable Sources (Brzuska, Farshim, Mittelbach). Shows iO implies non-black-box attacks on the original BHK UCE definitions, so no concrete hash function can satisfy them.
- **86160261.pdf** — Homomorphic Signatures with Efficient Verification for Polynomial Functions (Catalano, Fiore, Warinschi). First homomorphic signature schemes for polynomial functions without random oracles, with adaptive security and amortized verification faster than recomputation.
- **86160271.pdf** — Improved Generic Attacks Against Hash-based MACs and HAIFA (Dinur, Leurent). First state-recovery attack on HMAC-HAIFA at 2^(4ℓ/5), improved message-length trade-offs, and first universal forgery attacks on HMAC-SHA-1/SHA-2.
- **86160272.pdf** — Converting Cryptographic Schemes from Symmetric to Asymmetric Bilinear Groups (Abe, Groth, Ohkubo, Tango). Automatable graph-based method converting pairing schemes from symmetric to asymmetric bilinear groups without isomorphisms between source groups.
- **86160273.pdf** — Efficient Three-Party Computation from Cut-and-Choose (Choi, Katz, Malozemoff, Zikas). Constant-round malicious-secure three-party protocol via cut-and-choose, costing only a small constant factor over state-of-the-art two-party protocols.
- **86160274.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation from Semantically-Secure Multilinear Encodings (Pass, Seth, Telang). Defines semantic security for graded encodings and constructs iO for all polynomial-size circuits from it plus LWE.
- **86160275.pdf** — Cryptography with Streaming Algorithms (Papakonstantinou, Yang). Studies cryptography on streaming devices with tiny internal memory: proves impossibility of super-linear-stretch PRGs and sub-linear-key PKE, gives LWE-based constructions.
- **86160276.pdf** — Amortizing Garbled Circuits (Huang, Katz, Kolesnikov, Kumaresan, Malozemoff). Malicious-secure two-party computation with amortized cut-and-choose over repeated executions of one circuit, achieving 2–5× efficiency improvements.
- **86160281.pdf** — Leakage-Tolerant Computation with Input-Independent Preprocessing (Bitansky, Dachman-Soled, Lin). General leakage-tolerant MPC with one-time input-independent preprocessing tolerating continual bounded leakage across unbounded executions.
- **86160282.pdf** — Feasibility and Infeasibility of Secure Computation with Malicious PUFs (Dachman-Soled, Fleischhacker, Katz, Lysyanskaya, Schröder). Unconditional OT impossible with stateful malicious PUFs; UC two-party computation possible with stateless malicious PUFs.
- **86160283.pdf** — (Hierarchical) Identity-Based Encryption from Affine Message Authentication (Blazy, Kiltz, Pan). Generic tightness-preserving transform from affine MACs to adaptively secure IBE/HIBE, yielding the first tightly secure HIBE in the standard model.
- **86160286.pdf** — Algorithms in HElib (Halevi, Shoup). Describes algorithms and optimizations in the HElib library implementing BGV homomorphic encryption with ciphertext packing and Gentry-Halevi-Smart techniques.
- **86160291.pdf** — Cut-and-Choose Yao-Based Secure Computation in the Online/Offline and Batch Settings (Lindell, Riva). Reduces amortized cut-and-choose cost to O(s/log N) garbled circuits per execution in batch and online/offline two-party settings.
- **86160292.pdf** — Physical Zero-Knowledge Proofs of Physical Properties (Fisch, Freund, Naor). First UC-based framework for physical zero-knowledge proofs about physical objects, with applications to DNA profiling and neutron radiography.
- **86160295.pdf** — Cryptography from Compression Functions: The UCE Bridge to the ROM (Bellare, Hoang, Keelveedhi). Uses UCE security and domain-extension transforms to turn VIL-ROM schemes into FIL-ROM ones, handling multi-stage games more efficiently than indifferentiability.
- **86160297.pdf** — Proving the TLS Handshake Secure (as it is) (Bhargavan, Fournet, Kohlweiss, Pironti, Strub, Zanella-Béguelin). Proves agile security of the deployed TLS handshake using the miTLS verified implementation and a 3,000-line EasyCrypt proof for the RSA/DH KEM.
- **86160302.pdf** — Breaking '128-bit Secure' Supersingular Binary Curves (Granger, Kleinjung, Zumbrägel). New field representations and descent methods make quasi-polynomial DLP practical: genus-1 F_2^1223 curve has ~59-bit security; genus-2 F_2^367 curve totally broken.
- **86160324.pdf** — On Virtual Grey Box Obfuscation for General Circuits (Bitansky, Canetti, Kalai, Paneth). Defines strong indistinguishability obfuscation, shows VGB obfuscation equals SIO unconditionally, and relates existing obfuscators to this notion.
- **86160326.pdf** — How to Use Bitcoin to Design Fair Protocols (Bentov, Kumaresan). Achieves fairness in secure computation via monetary penalties on abort, using a claim-or-refund abstraction realizable with two Bitcoin transactions.
- **87310103.pdf** — "Ooh Aah... Just a Little Bit": A small amount of side channel can go a long way (Benger, van de Pol, Smart, Yarom). Flush+Reload cache attack on OpenSSL ECDSA plus lattice reduction recovers secp256k1 private keys from as few as 200 signatures.
- **87310109.pdf** — Embedded Evaluation of Randomness in Oscillator Based Elementary TRNG (Fischer, Lubicz). Simple, small-area embedded method to measure ring-oscillator jitter within 5% error, enabling TRNG calibration and online entropy assessment.
