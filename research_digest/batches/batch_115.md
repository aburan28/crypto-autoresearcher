# Batch 115 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by CRYPTO 2015 proceedings papers (the 921602xx file range), spanning LWE/lattice algorithms, functional encryption and iO, MPC round complexity, and zero-knowledge.
- Strong symmetric-crypto cluster: Even-Mansour analyses, S-box reverse engineering (Skipjack), PRESENT/SIMON/Sprout cryptanalysis, keyed-sponge PRF bounds, online AE definitions.
- ECDLP-relevant paper: 92160209 (last fall degree, HFE, and Weil descent attacks on ECDLP in characteristic 2, casting doubt on first-fall-degree heuristics).
- Side-channel/masking cluster near the end: probing-security decomposition (92160288), masking consolidation (92160324), plus two CHES 2015 papers (92930001, 92930021) on profiling attacks and dimensionality reduction.
- Notable breaks: total break of CLT13 multilinear maps via zeroizing attacks (92160310), key recovery on ASASA (92160238), full Sprout attack (92160328).
- No anomalies: all 50 files are genuine research papers; no duplicates or non-paper content found.

## Papers
- **92160189.pdf** — Coded-BKW: Solving LWE Using Lattice Codes (Guo, Johansson, Stankovski). New BKW variant mapping subvectors to lattice codewords to cancel more positions per step; performs especially well for binary-LWE.
- **92160192.pdf** — Proofs of Space (Dziembowski, Faust, Kolmogorov, Pietrzak). Introduces proofs of space as disk-space alternative to proof-of-work, built from high-pebbling-complexity graphs and Merkle trees; discusses cryptocurrency applications.
- **92160193.pdf** — Arguments of Proximity (Kalai, Rothblum). Constructs one-round interactive arguments of proximity for time-t languages from sub-exponentially secure FHE, with near-optimal parameters and delegation implications.
- **92160200.pdf** — A Punctured Programming Approach to Adaptively Secure Functional Encryption (Waters). First adaptively secure FE for circuits from iO with polynomial-loss reduction, via new puncturable deterministic encryption primitive.
- **92160206.pdf** — Multi-Key Security: The Even-Mansour Construction Revisited (Mouha, Luykx). Proves Even-Mansour achieves ideal-cipher-like security in the practically relevant multi-key setting when few plaintexts are encrypted per key.
- **92160207.pdf** — The Exact PRF Security of Truncation: Tight Bounds for Keyed Sponges and Truncated CBC (Gaži, Pietrzak, Tessaro). Proves nearly tight concrete PRF bounds for keyed sponges (e.g., SHA-3/KECCAK) and truncated CBC-MAC.
- **92160208.pdf** — On Reverse-Engineering S-Boxes with Hidden Design Criteria or Structure (Biryukov, Perrin). Techniques to recover hidden S-box structure, applied to Skipjack's secret S-box; includes decomposition methods and DDT-embedding algorithm.
- **92160209.pdf** — Last fall degree, HFE, and Weil descent attacks on ECDLP (Huang, Kosters, Yeo). Introduces last fall degree complexity measure; proves polynomial-time HFE solving for fixed parameters and gives evidence against first-fall-degree heuristics underlying subexponential ECDLP claims in characteristic 2.
- **92160210.pdf** — Data Is a Stream: Security of Stream-Based Channels (Fischlin, Günther, Marson, Paterson). Initiates formal security study of streaming channels (TLS, SSH, QUIC), defining confidentiality/integrity notions and a composition result needing "error predictability".
- **92160211.pdf** — Bloom Filters in Adversarial Environments (Naor, Yogev). Analyzes Bloom filters against adaptive adversaries; shows non-trivial adversarially robust Bloom filters exist iff one-way functions exist.
- **92160215.pdf** — A Quasipolynomial Reduction for Generalized Selective Decryption on Trees (Fuchsbauer, Jafargholi, Pietrzak). Gives almost-polynomial security reductions for the GSD game restricted to tree-like graphs, improving on exponential-loss bounds relevant to LKH multicast.
- **92160218.pdf** — From Selective to Adaptive Security in Functional Encryption (Ananth, Brakerski, Segev, Vaikuntanathan). Black-box transformation upgrading any sufficiently expressive selectively secure FE to adaptive security without extra assumptions, using hybrid encryption.
- **92160220.pdf** — Distributions Attaining Secret Key at a Rate of the Conditional Mutual Information (no author given). Characterizes distributions whose secret-key rate equals conditional mutual information under three communication scenarios; solves key distillation with shared randomness via Gács–Körner common information.
- **92160223.pdf** — Online Authenticated-Encryption and its Nonce-Reuse Misuse-Resistance (Hoang, Reyhanitabar, Rogaway, Vizár). Argues OAE1 does not really provide nonce-reuse misuse resistance; proposes radically reformulated OAE2 notion capturing best-possible online AE security.
- **92160226.pdf** — Known-key Distinguisher on Full PRESENT (Blondeau, Peyrin, Wang). First known-key distinguisher on full-round PRESENT (80- and 128-bit), combining latest differential cryptanalysis with a meet-in-the-middle layer; verified on small-scale variant.
- **92160235.pdf** — PoW-Based Distributed Cryptography with no Trusted Setup (Andrychowicz, Dziembowski). Formal model and broadcast protocol for peer-to-peer distributed crypto assuming only bounded adversarial computing power; yields beacon generation and blockchain-style computation.
- **92160237.pdf** — Multi-Identity and Multi-Key Leveled FHE from Learning with Errors (Clear, McGoldrick). Extends GSW identity-based FHE to multi-identity setting and gives first multi-key FHE from standard LWE (avoiding non-standard assumptions).
- **92160238.pdf** — Key-Recovery Attack on the ASASA Cryptosystem With Expanding S-Boxes (Gilbert, Plût, Treger). Simple distinguisher and full key recovery on the ASASA public-key scheme (Asiacrypt 2014) using partial derivatives of public polynomials and linear algebra.
- **92160241.pdf** — Implicit Zero-Knowledge Arguments and Applications to the Malicious Setting (Benhamouda, Couteau, Pointcheval, Wee). Introduces lightweight implicit ZK arguments and simulation-sound variants from DDH; improves round efficiency of secure inner-product computation for biometric authentication.
- **92160244.pdf** — Impossibility of Black-Box Simulation Against Leakage Attacks (Ostrovsky, Persiano, Visconti). Proves impossibility of leakage-resilient black-box zero knowledge and of leakage-resilient MPC for several functionalities, via succinct-argument techniques.
- **92160248.pdf** — Secure Computation with Minimal Interaction, Revisited (Ishai, Kumaresan, Kushilevitz, Paskin-Cherniavsky). Positive and negative results for two-round MPC without setup for n=3 and n=4 parties with one malicious corruption, including efficient selective-abort protocols.
- **92160253.pdf** — Tweaking Even-Mansour Ciphers (Cogliati, Lampe, Seurin). Introduces tweakable Even-Mansour (TEM) construction; proves two-round TEM secure up to ~2^{2n/3} queries using H-coefficients technique.
- **92160255.pdf** — Efficient Zero-Knowledge Proofs of Non-Algebraic Statements with Sublinear Amortized Cost (Hu, Mohassel, Rosulek). ZK system where a prover repeatedly proves RAM-program relations over a large dataset with sublinear amortized cost per proof after one-time setup.
- **92160257.pdf** — Structure-Preserving Signatures from Standard Assumptions, Revisited (Kiltz, Pan, Wee). New simple pairing-based SPS constructions under standard assumptions, narrowing the gap to generic-group-model-optimal schemes.
- **92160259.pdf** — Reproducible Circularly-Secure Bit Encryption: Applications and Realizations (Hajiabadi, Kapron). Combines circular security with reproducibility to build k-wise TDFs, CCA2-secure and deterministic encryption; shows DDH- and subgroup-based schemes are reproducible.
- **92160262.pdf** — Constant-Round Concurrent Zero-knowledge from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Chung, Lin, Pass). First constant-round concurrent ZK protocol for NP, based on CRHFs, one-way permutations, and iO for P/poly.
- **92160264.pdf** — An Improved BKW Algorithm for LWE with Applications to Cryptography and Lattices (Kirchner, Fouque). BKW variant with quantization step generalizing modulus switching; solves binary-LWE instances far faster and gives subexponential algorithms for BDD/GapSVP variants.
- **92160273.pdf** — Constant-Round MPC with Fairness and Guarantee of Output Delivery (Gordon, Liu, Shi). Lower bound ruling out two-round fair protocols (via VBB obfuscation impossibility) plus matching three-round protocol using new "flexible" threshold FHE.
- **92160275.pdf** — Practical Round-Optimal Blind Signatures in the Standard Model (Fuchsbauer, Hanser, Slamanig). First practically efficient round-optimal blind signatures in the standard model from SPS-EQ, with extensions to partially blind signatures and anonymous credentials.
- **92160279.pdf** — Bilinear Entropy Expansion from the Decisional Linear Assumption (Kowalczyk, Lewko). Dual-system-encryption technique expanding entropy under DLIN, yielding fully secure key-policy ABE with short public parameters.
- **92160283.pdf** — Concurrent Secure Computation with Optimal Query Complexity (Canetti, Goyal, Jain). First MIQ-secure concurrent protocol with worst-case per-session guarantee: simulator makes only constant ideal queries per session, matching known lower bound.
- **92160287.pdf** — Explicit Non-malleable Codes against Bit-wise Tampering and Permutations (Agrawal, Gupta, Maji, Pandey, Prabhakaran). First explicit unconditional non-malleable codes for a non-compartmentalized tampering class allowing bit permutations plus flips/sets.
- **92160288.pdf** — Algebraic Decomposition for Probing Security (Carlet, Prouff, Rivain, Roche). Represents masked nonlinear functions as low-degree function sequences with new probing-secure evaluation methods, improving on Coron–Roy–Vivek.
- **92160295.pdf** — Round-Optimal Black-Box Two-Party Computation (Ostrovsky, Richelson, Scafuro). Black-box secure two-party protocol achieving the Katz–Ostrovsky 5-round lower bound, avoiding non-black-box access and cut-and-choose overhead.
- **92160298.pdf** — Incoercible Multi-Party Computation and Universally Composable Receipt-Free Voting (Alwen, Ostrovsky, Zhou, Zikas). First universally composable notion of incoercible MPC covering receipt-freeness and active coercion, without assuming knowledge of other coerced or corrupted parties.
- **92160300.pdf** — Short Group Signatures via Structure-Preserving Signatures: Standard Model Security from Simple Assumptions (Libert, Peters, Yung). Significantly shorter fully anonymous group signatures from simple assumptions (SXDH/DLIN), cutting previous 2.8 kB overhead.
- **92160306.pdf** — Capacity and Data Complexity in Multidimensional Linear Attack (Huang, Vaudenay, Lai, Nyberg). Formulates key-dependent capacity (Gamma) and data complexity (Inverse Gamma) distributions for multidimensional linear attacks; solves median-complexity estimation, verified on 5-round PRESENT.
- **92160309.pdf** — An Algebraic Framework for Pseudorandom Functions and Applications to Related-Key Security (Abdalla, Benhamouda, Passelègue). Unifying algebraic PRF framework capturing Naor–Reingold, Lewko–Waters, and related-key constructions; yields new related-key security for polynomial permutations.
- **92160310.pdf** — Zeroizing Without Low-Level Zeroes: New MMAP Attacks and Their Limitations (Coron, Gentry, Halevi, Lepoint, Maji, Miles, Raykova, Sahai, Tibouchi). Extends zeroizing attacks to multilinear maps without low-level zero encodings: total break of CLT13 and weak-DL attack on a GGH13 variant.
- **92160318.pdf** — Parallel Hashing via List Recoverability (Haitner, Ishai, Omri, Shaltiel). Collision-resistant hash of long messages via a single round of parallel non-adaptive calls to a small random function, using list-recoverable codes; implies two-round Fiat–Shamir arguments.
- **92160324.pdf** — Consolidating Masking Schemes (Reparaz, Bilgin, Nikova, Gierlichs, Verbauwhede). Shows Ishai–Sahai–Wagner private circuits, Threshold Implementations, and Trichina gate are closely related; points out higher-order TI weakness with mitigations and share-reduced constructions.
- **92160327.pdf** — Actively Secure OT Extension with Optimal Overhead (Keller, Orsini, Scholl). First practical actively secure OT extension matching passive IKNP cost: κ base OTs, additive O(κ) communication, within 5% of passive runtime.
- **92160328.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Full Sprout (Lallemand, Naya-Plasencia). Key-recovery attack on the full Sprout lightweight stream cipher, over 2^10 times faster than exhaustive search with low data complexity; verified on toy version.
- **92160333.pdf** — Efficient Multi-Party Computation: from Passive to Active Security via Secure SIMD Circuits (Genkin, Ishai, Polychroniadou). Efficient passive-to-active MPC compiler exploiting AMD circuits and SIMD structure to close the efficiency gap between semi-honest and malicious security.
- **92160339.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation from Compact Functional Encryption (Ananth, Jain). Reduces iO to compact single-key selectively secure FE for NC1 via novel arity amplification; also yields first multi-input FE for constant arity from standard assumptions.
- **92160345.pdf** — Observations on the SIMON block cipher family (Kölbl, Leander, Tiessen). Exact differential/linear expressions for Simon-like round functions; SAT/SMT-based search finds optimal characteristics, shows default rotation constants not always optimal.
- **92160352.pdf** — Efficient Pseudorandom Functions via On-the-Fly Adaptation (Döttling, Schröder). Framework turning small-domain PRFs into full PRFs via bounded PRFs and "on-the-fly adaptation" with tight reductions; fast k-LIN/DDH instantiation with short keys.
- **92160363.pdf** — Large-Scale Secure Computation: Multi-party Computation for (Parallel) RAM Programs (Boyle, Chung, Pass). First polylogarithmic-overhead MPC for PRAM programs tolerating (1/3−ε) malicious parties, with polylog communication locality per party.
- **92930001.pdf** — Robust Profiling for DPA-Style Attacks (Whitnall, Oswald). Designs profiled side-channel distinguishers (machine learning and mean-trace/PCA methods) robust to mismatches between profiling and attack traces, validated across distortion sets.
- **92930021.pdf** — Less is More: Dimensionality Reduction from a Theoretical Perspective (Bruneau, Guilley, Heuser, Marion, Rioul). Mathematical analysis of dimensionality reduction in side-channel analysis; shows optimal attacks stay optimal after linear projection, asymptotically coinciding with LDA.
