# Batch 064 digest

## Overview
- Batch is almost entirely two 2004 conference proceedings: FSE 2004 (LNCS 3017, filenames 3017xxxx = page numbers) covering stream/block ciphers, modes of operation, and related-key analysis; and CHES 2004 (LNCS 3156, filenames 3156xxxx) covering side-channel attacks/countermeasures and cryptographic hardware.
- Dominant themes: power analysis (DPA/CPA/second-order, masking countermeasures), ECC hardware implementations (GF(p)/GF(2^m) arithmetic, smartcard coprocessors), stream-cipher design and cryptanalysis (HC-256, RC4/RC4A, SOBER-128).
- Notable papers: HC-256 (Wu); EAX authenticated-encryption mode (Bellare–Rogaway–Wagner); Correlation Power Analysis (Brier–Clavier–Olivier); ECC vs RSA on 8-bit CPUs (Gura et al., Sun Labs); fault analysis of stream ciphers (Hoch–Shamir).
- Anomalies: 4 extracts (30170261, 30170409, 30170454, 31560176) are unreadable due to broken font encoding (glyph codes like /D6/CT); source chunk contained stray NUL bytes (a cleaned UTF-8 copy was used); 30170483 is only a 1-page extended abstract; the two Maximov papers (30170483, 308.pdf) overlap in topic but are distinct works.

## Papers
- **30170226.pdf** — A New Stream Cipher HC-256 (Hongjun Wu). Proposes HC-256, a software-efficient stream cipher using two secret 1024-entry 32-bit tables as alternating S-boxes; ~4.2 cycles/byte on Pentium 4.
- **30170244.pdf** — A New Weakness in the RC4 Keystream Generator and an Approach to Improve the Security of the Cipher (Souradyuti Paul, Bart Preneel). New bias in RC4's first two output bytes distinguishable with 2^25 bytes; proposes strengthened variant RC4A.
- **30170261.pdf** — (unreadable extract) Font-encoded garbage; title and content not recoverable from extract. Likely an FSE 2004 paper (LNCS 3017, p. 261ff).
- **30170280.pdf** — ICEBERG: an Involutional Cipher Efficient for Block Encryption in Reconfigurable Hardware (Standaert, Piret, Rouvroy, Quisquater, Legat). 64-bit involutional block cipher optimized for FPGAs with on-the-fly key scheduling and side-channel resistance.
- **30170298.pdf** — Related Key Differential Attacks on 27 rounds of XTEA and Full-round GOST (Ko, Hong, Lee, Lee, Kang). Best-known related-key truncated differential attack on 27-round XTEA; distinguishing and key-recovery attacks on full-round GOST.
- **30170316.pdf** — On the Additive Differential Probability of Exclusive-Or (Lipmaa, Wallén, Dumas). Studies XOR differential probability under additive differences; gives linear-time algorithm via rational series for analyzing mixed XOR/addition ciphers.
- **30170331.pdf** — Two Power Analysis Attacks against One-Mask Methods (Akkar, Bévan, Goubin). Defeats Trichina et al.'s single-mask AES countermeasure with first-order DPA and breaks the Unique Masking Method for DES.
- **30170349.pdf** — Nonce-Based Symmetric Encryption (Phillip Rogaway). Formalizes deterministic encryption with surfaced nonce IV; gives definitions, constructions, and properties better matching real modes like CBC.
- **30170360.pdf** — Ciphers Secure Against Related-Key Attacks (Stefan Lucks). Proves PRF/PRP constructions secure against related-key attacks, improving on Bellare–Kohno; new PRFs from number-theoretic assumptions.
- **30170373.pdf** — Cryptographic Hash-Function Basics: Definitions, Implications, and Separations (Rogaway, Shrimpton). Gives seven definitions for preimage/second-preimage/collision resistance and works out all concrete-security implications and separations.
- **30170391.pdf** — The EAX Mode of Operation (Bellare, Rogaway, Wagner). Proposes EAX, an on-line AEAD block-cipher mode protecting message privacy and header authenticity with provable security.
- **30170409.pdf** — (unreadable extract) Glyph-code garbage (/BV/CF...); likely an FSE 2004 paper (LNCS 3017, p. 409ff) but title/content not recoverable.
- **30170427.pdf** — New Security Proofs for the 3GPP Confidentiality and Integrity Algorithms (Iwata, Kohno). Proves generalized f8/f9 secure assuming KASUMI is PRP-RKA secure, fixing earlier incorrect proofs.
- **30170445.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of a Message Authentication Code due to Cary and Venkatesan (Blackburn, Paterson). Finds MAC collisions in ~2^48.5 queries and forgeries in ~2^55, far below expected 128-bit security.
- **30170454.pdf** — (unreadable extract) Font-encoded garbage; appears to be an FSE 2004 paper (LNCS 3017, p. 454ff), possibly on block-cipher modes; content not recoverable.
- **30170472.pdf** — A MAC forgery attack on SOBER-128 (Watanabe, Furuya). Shows SOBER-128's MAC function is vulnerable to differential cryptanalysis with forgery success probability ~2^-6.
- **30170483.pdf** — On Linear Approximation of Modulo Sum (Alexander Maximov). One-page abstract; gives O(n·2^3(k−1)) algorithm for noise distribution of approximating modular sum by XOR.
- **308.pdf** — Fast Computation of Large Distributions and Its Cryptographic Applications (Maximov, Johansson). O(kn2^n) convolution algorithms for XOR/modular-sum distributions; yields improved distinguishing attack on SNOW 2.0.
- **31.pdf** — Automated Design of Cryptographic Devices Resistant to Multiple Side-Channel Attacks (Kulikowski, Smirnov, Taubin). Asynchronous pipeline synthesis flow producing power-balanced AES hardware, ~6x better balancing, 6.6x throughput gain.
- **31560001.pdf** — Towards Efficient Second-Order Power Analysis (Waddle, Wagner). Explores Zero-Offset 2DPA and FFT 2DPA variants to efficiently defeat masking with second-order techniques.
- **31560016.pdf** — Correlation Power Analysis with a Leakage Model (Brier, Clavier, Olivier). Introduces CPA using Hamming-distance leakage model; experimentally validated and explains DPA limitations.
- **31560030.pdf** — Power Analysis of an FPGA Implementation of Rijndael: Is Pipelining a DPA Countermeasure? (Standaert, Örs, Preneel). First successful power attacks on FPGA Rijndael; evaluates pipelining's effect on DPA resistance.
- **31560045.pdf** — Long Modular Multiplication for Cryptographic Applications (Laszlo Hars). DSP-like coprocessor with new column-sum Montgomery/interleaved modular multiplication variants, faster squaring, minimal memory.
- **31560060.pdf** — Leak Resistant Arithmetic (Bajard, Imbert, Liardet, Teglia). Uses Residue Number Systems as a natural defense against timing, SPA/DPA, and EM side-channel attacks (RNS Montgomery multiplication).
- **31560074.pdf** — Efficient Linear Array for Multiplication in GF(2^m) Using a Normal Basis for ECC (Kwon, Gaj, Kim, Hong). Low gate-complexity Gaussian normal basis multiplier for NIST-recommended binary fields up to GF(2^571).
- **31560089.pdf** — Low-Power Elliptic Curve Cryptography Using Scaled Modular Arithmetic (Öztürk, Sunar, Savaş). Modulus scaling yields efficient inversion and a low-power, small-footprint GF(p) ECC processor architecture.
- **31560104.pdf** — A Low-Cost ECC Coprocessor for Smartcards (Aigner, Bock, Hütter, Wolkerstorfer). Small-area GF(2^m) ECDSA coprocessor; efficient inversion enables affine coordinates; bitslice design adapts to key sizes.
- **31560117.pdf** — Comparing Elliptic Curve Cryptography and RSA on 8-bit CPUs (Gura, Patel, Wander, Eberle, Chang Shantz). Shows ECC viable on microcontrollers: 0.81s for 160-bit point multiplication on ATmega128; ECC advantage grows with key size.
- **31560132.pdf** — Instruction Set Extensions for Fast Arithmetic in Finite Fields GF(p) and GF(2^m) (Großschädl, Savaş). Five custom MIPS32 instructions accelerate finite-field arithmetic; 192-bit EC scalar multiplication in 36 ms at 33 MHz.
- **31560147.pdf** — Aspects of Hyperelliptic Curves over Large Prime Fields in Software Implementations (Avanzi). Optimized library shows genus-2 HEC performance within ~14–15% of elliptic curves at 192/256-bit group sizes.
- **31560162.pdf** — A Collision-Attack on AES Combining Side Channel- and Differential-Attack (Schramm, Leander, Felke, Paar). Detects internal AES collisions via side-channel leakage, reducing attack effort below known side-channel attacks.
- **31560176.pdf** — (unreadable extract) Glyph-code garbage; likely CHES 2004 paper (LNCS 3156, p. 176ff); title/content not recoverable.
- **31560191.pdf** — Simple Power Analysis of Unified Code for ECC Double and Add (Colin D. Walter). Shows unified addition formulae still leak via conditional subtraction in Montgomery multiplication; advocates constant-time MMM.
- **31560205.pdf** — DPA on n-bit sized Boolean and Arithmetic Operations and its Application to IDEA, RC6 and HMAC (Lemke, Schramm, Paar). Analyzes multi-bit selection DPA on XOR/addition/multiplication; experimental IDEA attack on AVR microcontroller.
- **31560220.pdf** — Side-Channel Attacks in ECC: A General Technique for Varying the Parametrization of the Elliptic Curve (Loren D. Olson). Uniform point-addition representations with per-scalar-multiplication reparametrization for curves in characteristic ≥ 5.
- **31560230.pdf** — Switching Blindings with a View Towards IDEA (Neiße, Pulkus). Space-efficient table-based conversion between arithmetic and boolean masking, including modulo 2^k+1 arithmetic for IDEA.
- **31560240.pdf** — Fault Analysis of Stream Ciphers (Hoch, Shamir). Systematic fault-attack techniques against LFSR-based stream ciphers plus specific attacks on RC4, LILI-128, and SOBER-t32.
- **31560255.pdf** — A Differential Fault Attack against Early Rounds of (Triple-)DES (Ludger Hemme). DFA exploiting induced faults in early Feistel rounds; recovers DES first-round key on a smart card within hours.
- **31560270.pdf** — An Offset-compensated Oscillator-based Random Bit Source for Security Applications (Bock, Bucci, Luzzi). Jitter-based RNG with compensation loop, fully digital, robust against forcing attacks, with built-in entropy self-test.
- **31560284.pdf** — Improving the Security of Dual-Rail Circuits (Sokolov, Murphy, Bystrov, Yakovlev). Two-spacer dual-rail protocol guarantees all gates switch each cycle; automated conversion tool; evaluated on AES benchmarks.
- **31560299.pdf** — A New Attack with Side Channel Leakage during Exponent Recoding Computations (Sakai, Sakurai). Recovers RSA/ECDSA secret exponents by attacking width-w NAF and fractional-window recoding stages.
- **31560313.pdf** — Defeating Countermeasures Based on Randomized BSD Representations (Fouque, Muller, Poupard, Valette). Breaks full Ha–Moon randomized scalar recoding via local-collision detection; argues randomized BSD is fundamentally flawed.
- **31560328.pdf** — Pipelined Computation of Scalar Multiplication in Elliptic Curve Cryptosystems (Pradeep Kumar Mishra). First pipelined ECC scalar multiplication overlapping point operations; can be made SCA-resistant.
- **31560343.pdf** — Efficient Countermeasures against RPA, DPA, and SPA (Mamiya, Miyaji, Morimoto). Proposes efficient countermeasures for ECC resisting refined/zero-value power analysis as well as SPA/DPA.
- **31560357.pdf** — Strong Authentication for RFID Systems Using the AES Algorithm (Feldhofer, Dominikus, Wolkerstorfer). Challenge-response RFID authentication with ultra-low-power AES hardware: 1000 cycles, under 9 µA on 0.35 µm CMOS.
- **31560371.pdf** — TTS: High-Speed Signatures on a Low-Cost Smart Card (Yang, Chen, Chen). Implements multivariate TTS signature scheme on 8051 microcontrollers; 170 ms signing on stock 8051, faster than SFLASHv2.
- **31560386.pdf** — XTR Implementation on Reconfigurable Hardware (Peeters, Neve, Ciet). FPGA implementation of XTR trace-based cryptosystem; exponentiation under 0.21 ms at >150 MHz, competitive area vs RSA/ECC.
- **31560400.pdf** — Concurrent Error Detection Schemes for Involution Ciphers (Joshi, Wu, Karri). Time-redundancy CED exploiting involution property to detect transient/permanent faults; ~24% area overhead on KHAZAD.
- **31560413.pdf** — Public Key Authentication with one (on-line) Single Addition (Girault, Lefranc). Low Hamming weight variants of GPS identification enable public-key authentication on processor-less chips with one serial addition.
- **31560428.pdf** — Attacking DSA Under a Repeated Bits Assumption (Leadbitter, Page, Smart). Lattice attack recovering DSA/ECDSA private keys when only equality relations between ephemeral-key bits leak, extending Howgrave-Graham–Smart/Nguyen–Shparlinski.
