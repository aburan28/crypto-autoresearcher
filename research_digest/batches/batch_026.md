# Batch 026 digest

## Overview
- This batch is a nearly contiguous run of ASIACRYPT 2020 proceedings papers (LNCS-style, ~30p each), spanning post-quantum crypto, MPC, ZK proofs, symmetric cryptanalysis, and side-channel masking; the tail (12550xxx) shifts to TCC-style theory papers.
- Strong isogeny-based cluster: CSIDH/group actions (12491283), radical isogenies (12491308), SQISign signatures (12491357), isogeny OPRFs (12491375), and isogeny ring signatures (12491294) — directly relevant to ECDLP-adjacent isogeny research.
- Heavy MPC/secret-sharing presence: threshold multi-key FHE, Galois-ring LSSS, CAFEs, round compression, distributed ZK proofs, and arithmetic secret sharing complexity.
- Side-channel/masking cluster: SILVER verification tool, cryptanalysis of masked ciphers via linear cryptanalysis, and packed-multiplication amortized masking.
- Anomaly: 12491342.pdf (1p) is a stray diagram fragment about a "punctured matrix" XOR scheme — not a paper first page.
- Notable results: SQISign's 204-byte post-quantum signatures, first full-permutation distinguisher on Gimli, and improved algebraic attacks breaking ROLLO NIST candidates' claimed security.

## Papers
- **12491258.pdf** — Scalable Ciphertext Compression Techniques for Post-Quantum KEMs and their Applications (Katsumata, Kwiatkowski, Pintore, Prest). Generic multi-recipient KEM construction from post-quantum assumptions; order-of-magnitude bandwidth savings and MLS/TreeKEM application.
- **12491261.pdf** — SILVER – Statistical Independence and Leakage Verification (Knichel, Sasdrich, Moradi). Framework using ROBDDs to formally verify masked hardware implementations against side-channel leakage at gate-level netlists.
- **12491264.pdf** — Lower Bounds on the Degree of Block Ciphers (Hebborn, Lambin, Leander, Todo). First meaningful lower bounds on algebraic degree of modern block ciphers via division property and parity sets.
- **12491265.pdf** — Improvements of Algebraic Attacks for solving the Rank Decoding and MinRank problems (Bardet, Bros, Cabarcas, Gaborit, Perlner, Smith-Tone, Tillich, Verbel). Improved algebraic MinRank attacks avoiding Gröbner bases; breaks ROLLO NIST round-2 parameters down to 71-bit complexity.
- **12491274.pdf** — Succinct Functional Commitment for a Large Class of Arithmetic Circuits (Lipmaa, Pavlyk). SFC scheme for semi-sparse polynomials via non-black-box SNARK techniques; aggregates vector and polynomial commitments under a new uber-assumption.
- **12491279.pdf** — Secure MPC: Laziness Leads to GOD (Badrinarayanan, Jain, Manohar, Sahai). Introduces threshold multi-key FHE from LWE for arbitrary monotone access structures, yielding MPC with guaranteed output delivery against "honest but lazy" parties.
- **12491283.pdf** — Cryptographic Group Actions and Applications (Alamati, De Feo, Montgomery, Patranabis). Generalizes Brassard–Yung/Couveignes group-action framework to isogeny assumptions (CSIDH, CSI-FiSh); builds dual-mode PKE, OT, and Naor-Reingold PRFs.
- **12491285.pdf** — Security Reductions for White-Box Key-Storage in Mobile Payments (Alpirez Bock, Brzuska, Fischlin, Janson, Michiels). Formalizes and proves secure hardware-bound white-box key derivation functions per Mastercard/EMVCo specifications.
- **12491290.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Masked Ciphers: A not so Random Idea (Beyne, Dhooghe, Zhang). Reduces bounded-query robust probing security of masked ciphers to linear cryptanalysis, yielding second-order masking with low randomness.
- **12491293.pdf** — A Combinatorial Approach to Quantum Random Functions (Döttling, Malavolta, Pu). Builds quantum-secure PRFs from small-domain classical PRFs via bipartite expanders; first key-homomorphic QPRF from LWE.
- **12491294.pdf** — Calamari and Falafel: Logarithmic (Linkable) Ring Signatures from Isogenies and Lattices (Beullens, Katsumata, Pintore). Logarithmic-size ring and linkable ring signatures from a group-action OR proof instantiated with CSIDH or MLWE.
- **12491295.pdf** — Crowd Verifiable Zero-Knowledge and End-to-end Verifiable Multiparty Computation (Baldimtsi, Kiayias, Zacharias, Zhang). Defines end-to-end verifiable MPC auditable even under total subversion, via new crowd-verifiable ZK from discrete-log assumptions.
- **12491298.pdf** — Towards Closing The Security Gap of Tweak-aNd-Tweak (TNT) (Guo, Guo, List, Song). Improved security analysis of the TNT tweakable block cipher construction (EUROCRYPT'20) and its TNT-AES instantiation.
- **12491303.pdf** — Improved Classical and Quantum Algorithms for Subset-Sum (Bonnetain, Bricout, Schrottenloher, Shen). New classical (2^0.283n) and quantum (2^0.236n) subset-sum algorithms improving Becker–Coron–Joux and quantum-walk results.
- **12491306.pdf** — KVaC: Key-Value Commitments for Blockchains and Beyond (Agrawal, Raghuraman). Constant-size key-value map commitments in unknown-order groups with 3-group-element membership proofs; yields efficient accumulators and vector commitments.
- **12491308.pdf** — Radical Isogenies (Castryck, Decru, Vercauteren). Deterministic radical-formula method for chains of small-degree isogenies without torsion-point generation; ~19% CSIDH speed-up.
- **12491309.pdf** — Subvert KEM to Break DEM: Practical Algorithm-Substitution Attacks on Public-Key Encryption (Chen, Huang, Yung). First practical ASAs on PKE: subverting the KEM of KEM/DEM hybrids to undetectably exfiltrate secrets.
- **12491311.pdf** — Inner-Product Functional Encryption with Fine-Grained Access Control (Abdalla, Catalano, Gay, Ursu). Public-key IPFE combined with ABE-style policies (monotone span programs) under LWE or pairing assumptions.
- **12491313.pdf** — Unbounded HIBE with Tight Security (Langrehr, Pan). First tightly secure unbounded hierarchical IBE under Matrix DDH in prime-order pairing groups, including multi-challenge setting.
- **12491319.pdf** — Asymptotically Good Multiplicative LSSS over Galois Rings and Applications to MPC over Z/p^k Z (Abspoel, Cramer, Damgård, Escudero, Rambaud, Xing, Yuan). Lifts strongly multiplicative secret sharing to Galois rings for asymptotically efficient MPC over Z/p^k Z.
- **12491324.pdf** — New results on Gimli: full-permutation distinguishers and improved collisions (Flórez Gutiérrez, Leurent, Naya-Plasencia, Perrin, Schrottenloher, Sibleyras). First full 24-round Gimli permutation distinguisher (2^64) plus 12/18-round collision attacks on Gimli-Hash.
- **12491327.pdf** — How to Build Optimally Secure PRFs Using Block Ciphers (Cogliati, Jha, Nandi). Birthday-bound-optimal variable-input-length PRFs from block ciphers via modified Benes; yields six optimal deterministic MACs.
- **12491333.pdf** — Security Limitations of Classical-Client Delegated Quantum Computing (Badertscher, Cojocaru, Colisson, Kashefi, Leichtle, Mantri, Wallden). Analyzes privacy loss of classical-client remote state preparation for delegated quantum computing in the Constructive Cryptography framework.
- **12491339.pdf** — Packed Multiplication: How to Amortize the Cost of Side-channel Masking? (Wang, Guo, Standaert, Yu, Cassiers). "Global" batching approach amortizing randomness and computation across masked multiplications to cut higher-order masking overheads.
- **12491341.pdf** — Catalic: Delegated PSI Cardinality with Applications to Contact Tracing (Duong, Phan, Trieu). First delegated PSI-cardinality protocol to untrusted servers via oblivious distributed key PRF; applied to lightweight-client contact tracing.
- **12491342.pdf** — Not a paper: 1-page stray diagram fragment describing XOR "punctured matrix" shares between parties P0/P1. No title, authors, or abstract.
- **12491354.pdf** — Multi-Client Oblivious RAM with Poly-Logarithmic Communication (Chow, Fech, Lai, Malavolta). First MCORAM with polylog communication/computation, hiding access patterns from both server and fellow clients.
- **12491357.pdf** — SQISign: Compact Post-Quantum Signatures from Quaternions and Isogenies (De Feo, Kohel, Leroux, Petit, Wesolowski). Supersingular-isogeny signature scheme with 204-byte signatures and 64-byte keys via a new quaternion isogeny-path algorithm.
- **12491361.pdf** — Post-Quantum Verification of Fujisaki-Okamoto (Unruh). First computer-verified post-quantum security proof of the Fujisaki-Okamoto transform, formalized in quantum relational Hoare logic (qrhl-tool).
- **12491365.pdf** — Circular Security Is Complete for KDM Security (Kitagawa, Matsuda). Shows circular security implies bounded KDM security for all bounded-size circuits, including a CPA-to-CCA amplification.
- **12491366.pdf** — Towards Efficiency-Preserving Round Compression in MPC (Ananth, Choudhuri, Goel, Jain). Round compression compiler preserving computational/communication efficiency, yielding round-optimal two-round semi-honest MPC with honest majority.
- **12491370.pdf** — Quantum Circuit Implementations of AES with Fewer Qubits (Zou, Wei, Sun, Liu, Wu). Qubit-reduced quantum AES circuits: 512/640/768 qubits for AES-128/192/256, over 30% fewer than prior work.
- **12491371.pdf** — ALBATROSS: publicly Attestable BATched Randomness based On Secret Sharing (Cascudo, David). PVSS-based multiparty randomness beacon generating Θ(n²) random values at Θ(log n) amortized exponentiations per value; UC variants under DDH/CDH.
- **12491375.pdf** — Oblivious Pseudorandom Functions from Isogenies (Boneh, Kogan, Woo). First OPRFs and verifiable OPRFs from supersingular isogenies, with new ZK protocols countering the Galbraith et al. attack.
- **12491376.pdf** — Non-Interactive Composition of Sigma-Protocols via Share-then-Hash (Abe, Ambrona, Bogdanov, Ohkubo, Rosen). Enhances CDS proofs of partial knowledge by hashing challenge shares; single transcript per statement and Fiat-Shamir security in non-programmable ROM.
- **12491379.pdf** — Twisted-PHS: Using the Product Formula to Solve Approx-SVP in Ideal Lattices (Bernard, Roux-Langlois). Twisted variant of the PHS algorithm for ideal-lattice Approx-SVP with full implementation showing better approximation factors in practice.
- **12491387.pdf** — Succinct Diophantine-Satisfiability Arguments (Towa, Vergnaud). First succinct honest-verifier ZK argument for Diophantine equation satisfiability with logarithmic communication, from hidden-order groups.
- **12491389.pdf** — On the Adaptive Security of MACs and PRFs (Morgan, Pass, Shi). Proves black-box impossibility of tight reductions for multi-user adaptively secure deterministic stateless MACs/PRFs; linear-in-users loss is necessary.
- **12491392.pdf** — Individual Simulations (Deng). Simulation technique exploiting adversary functionality structure; first two-round selective-opening commitments and three-round concurrent ZK under standard factoring.
- **12491396.pdf** — Circuit Amortization Friendly Encodings and their Application to Statistically Secure MPC (Dalskov, Lee, Soria-Vazquez). CAFEs generalize RMFEs over Galois rings GR(2^k, d) for concretely efficient parallel MPC over Z_2^k.
- **12491397.pdf** — Unbounded Dynamic Predicate Compositions in ABE from Standard Assumptions (Attrapadung, Tomida). Framework for adaptively secure unbounded dynamic ABE compositions under MDDH, resolving Attrapadung's Eurocrypt'19 open problem.
- **12491398.pdf** — Simpler Statistically Sender Private Oblivious Transfer from Ideals of Cyclotomic Integers (Micciancio, Sorrell). Two-message statistically sender-private OT from RLWE over cyclotomics with O(n log q) less communication.
- **12491404.pdf** — Efficient and Round-Optimal Oblivious Transfer and Commitment with Adaptive Security (Canetti, Sarkar, Wang). Most efficient two-round UC-secure adaptive bit-OT under DDH with O(1) exponentiations, plus related OT and commitment schemes.
- **12491414.pdf** — Two-Pass Authenticated Key Exchange with Explicit Authentication and Tight Security (Liu, Liu, Gu, Weng). Generic tightly secure 2-pass AKE from KEM+signature in a strengthened model, via new "IND-mCPA with adaptive reveals" KEM notion.
- **12491419.pdf** — Succinct and Adaptively Secure ABE for Arithmetic Branching Programs from k-Lin (Lin, Luo). Adaptively secure ABE for arithmetic branching programs with constant-size ciphertexts (KP) or keys (CP) under k-Lin.
- **12491420.pdf** — CCA Updatable Encryption Against Malicious Re-Encryption Attacks (Chen, Li, Tang). Strengthens updatable encryption models against maliciously injected ciphertexts; new ReCrypt+ construction with CCA and post-compromise security.
- **12491421.pdf** — Efficient Fully Secure Computation via Distributed Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Boyle, Gilboa, Ishai, Nof). Fully secure constant-party MPC with <1.5 ring elements communicated per party per multiplication gate, black-box in a PRG.
- **12550103.pdf** — Non-Malleable Codes, Extractors and Secret Sharing for Interleaved Tampering and Composition of Tampering (Chattopadhyay, Li). Explicit information-theoretic non-malleable codes for interleaved split-state and composed affine tampering classes.
- **12550110.pdf** — On Pseudorandom Encodings (Agrikola, Couteau, Ishai, Jarecki, Sahai). Initiates study of pseudorandom encodings for efficiently samplable distributions, linking invertible sampling to steganography and adaptive MPC.
- **12550113.pdf** — On the Complexity of Arithmetic Secret Sharing (Cramer, Xing, Yuan). Shows asymptotically good strongly multiplicative LSSS can be effectively constructed even without algebraic-geometry towers, partially answering the CRYPTO 2006 open question.
