# Batch 040 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by theory cryptography circa 2022: ~25 CRYPTO 2022 papers (135070xxx IDs) followed by ~25 TCC 2022 papers (137470xxx IDs); nearly all are full proceedings papers, no junk items.
- MPC is the single biggest theme (~10 papers): dishonest-majority packed secret sharing, fluid MPC, two-round/round-optimal MPC, randomized encodings, minimal-trust full security, broadcast via packed VSS.
- Second cluster is proof systems: succinct arguments, IOPs, lattice-based sublinear ZK for R1CS, Fiat-Shamir tightness, quantum rewinding, vector commitments over rings, compressed Sigma-protocols.
- Notable applied items: FROST/BLS threshold-signature security hierarchy (Bellare et al.), insider security analysis of MLS, Falcon-style shorter lattice signatures, CSIDH group-action PAKE, proof-of-useful-work blockchain (Ofelimos).
- Quantum cryptography is prominent (~6 papers): unclonable encryption, semi-quantum tokenized signatures, classical verification of quantum computation, PRS/PRFS generators, collusion-resistant copy-protection.
- Essentially no elliptic-curve/ECDLP content; closest items are CSIDH group actions, pairing-free vector-commitment impossibility, and generic-group (Uber/PGG) formalizations.

## Papers
- **135070358.pdf** — Sharing Transformation and Dishonest Majority MPC with Packed Secret Sharing (Goyal, Polychroniadou, Song). First dishonest-majority MPC with sublinear (O(1) for constant corruption fraction) per-gate communication, via packed secret sharing and a new sharing-transformation technique.
- **135070361.pdf** — Le Mans: Dynamic and Fluid MPC for Dishonest Majority (Rachuri, Scholl). Extends Fluid MPC to dishonest majority via universal preprocessing for SPDZ with dynamically changing participant sets and PCG-based preprocessing.
- **135070364.pdf** — Programmable Distributed Point Functions (Boyle, Gilboa, Ishai, Kolobov). New DPF framework for polynomial-size domains introducing reusable offline keys (programmable DPF) from one-way functions, yielding privately puncturable PRFs.
- **135070365.pdf** — Tight Bounds on the Randomness Complexity of Secure Multiparty Computation (Goyal, Ishai, Song). Proves Omega(t^2) randomness lower bound for semi-honest XOR MPC, nearly matching upper bounds, with explicit protocols and extensions to symmetric functions.
- **135070368.pdf** — Securing Approximate Homomorphic Encryption using Differential Privacy (Li, Micciancio, Schultz, Sorrell). Transforms IND-CPA approximate HE into IND-CPAD-secure schemes by adding differentially private (Gaussian) noise at decryption; applied to CKKS.
- **135070369.pdf** — Two-Round MPC without Round Collapsing Revisited (Lin, Liu). Simpler, more efficient two-round maliciously secure MPC from tensor-OLE correlated randomness and random oracles, for Boolean circuits and arithmetic branching programs.
- **135070373.pdf** — Verifiable Relation Sharing and Multi-Verifier Zero-Knowledge in Two Rounds (Applebaum, Kachlon, Patra). Introduces VRS (sharing plus ZK proof on shared data) with optimal two-round UC-secure protocols tolerating dishonest majority of verifiers.
- **135070375.pdf** — Succinct Interactive Oracle Proofs: Applications and Limitations (Nassar, Rothblum). Compiles succinct IOPs into zero-knowledge proofs via OWFs and gives limitations/lower bounds for succinct IOP constructions.
- **135070379.pdf** — Overloading the Nonce: Rugged PRPs, Nonce-Set AEAD, and Order-Resilient Channels (Degabriele, Karadzic). Introduces rugged PRPs between PRP and SPRP, enabling two-pass AEAD constructions and new nonce-set AEAD notions.
- **135070382.pdf** — CHIP and CRISP: Protecting All Parties Against Compromise through Identity-Binding PAKEs (Cremers, Naor, Paz, Ronen). New PAKE variants binding identity into the exchange to protect client storage and symmetric group settings beyond (s)aPAKE like OPAQUE.
- **135070383.pdf** — Shorter Hash-and-Sign Lattice-Based Signatures (Espitau, Tibouchi, Wallet, Yu). Techniques to shrink GPV-style hash-and-sign lattice signatures over NTRU lattices, targeting improvements to Falcon bandwidth.
- **135070388.pdf** — Password-Authenticated Key Exchange from Group Actions (Abdalla, Eisenhofer, Kiltz, Kunzweiler, Riepel). Provably secure PAKE from commutative group actions (CSIDH), using quadratic twists and a bit-by-bit approach to avoid offline dictionary attacks.
- **135070395.pdf** — Practical Sublinear Proofs for R1CS from Lattices (Nguyen, Seiler). Lattice-based ZK proof system for R1CS with square-root witness scaling, concretely 2-3x smaller than Ligero, via an interactive Schwartz-Zippel variant.
- **135070396.pdf** — On the Feasibility of Unclonable Encryption, and More (Ananth, Kaleoglu, Li, Liu, Zhandry). Shows unclonable-indistinguishability encryption exists unconditionally in the quantum random oracle model, with results on oracle necessity.
- **135070404.pdf** — MozZ2karella: Efficient Vector-OLE and Zero-Knowledge Proofs Over Z_2k (Baum, Braun, Munch-Hansen, Scholl). Maliciously secure VOLE extension over Z_2k powering the QuarkSilver ZK system for native CPU-ring arithmetic.
- **135070413.pdf** — Faster Sounder Succinct Arguments and IOPs (Holmgren, Rothblum). Succinct arguments for structured Boolean circuits with polylog(lambda) prover overhead and 2^-lambda soundness from linear-size collision-resistant hashing.
- **135070419.pdf** — Semi-Quantum Tokenized Signatures (Shmueli). Classical-sender tokenized signature schemes implying public-key quantum money with a classical stateless bank and direct classical transactions.
- **135070423.pdf** — Ofelimos: Combinatorial Optimization via Proof-of-Useful-Work (Fitzi, Kiayias, Panagiotakos, Russell). PoUW blockchain whose consensus doubles as a decentralized optimization solver built on a new Doubly Parallel Local Search algorithm, with security analysis.
- **135070427.pdf** — Succinct Classical Verification of Quantum Computation (Bartusek, Kalai, Lombardi, Ma, Malavolta, Vaikuntanathan, Vidick, Yang). First plain-model succinct classical argument for BQP from post-quantum iO and LWE, via a modular re-proof of Mahadev's protocol.
- **135070428.pdf** — Quadratic Multiparty Randomized Encodings Beyond Honest Majority and Their Applications (Applebaum, Ishai, Karni, Patra). First general degree-2 multiparty randomized encodings tolerating up to floor(2n/3) passive corruptions, with MPC applications.
- **135070429.pdf** — Correlated Pseudorandomness from Expand-Accumulate Codes (Boyle, Couteau, Gilboa, Ishai, Kohl, Resch, Scholl). Simple PCG design from expand-accumulate codes with provable security, offline-online mode, and extensions to pseudorandom correlation functions.
- **135070435.pdf** — On The Insider Security of MLS (Alwen, Jost, Mularczyk). First formal insider-security notion for CGKA/group messaging and a rigorous analysis of MLS Draft 12's TreeKEM core (ITK).
- **135070444.pdf** — Sustained Space and Cumulative Complexity Trade-offs for Data-Dependent Memory-Hard Functions (Blocki, Holman). Strengthens memory-hardness cost metrics beyond cumulative memory complexity to rule out attacker space-time trade-off flexibility.
- **135070451.pdf** — Dynamic Local Searchable Symmetric Encryption (Minaud, Reichle). First dynamic memory-efficient SSE via a Generic Local Transform from page-efficient to local schemes; matches static STOC'16 bounds with dynamism at no cost.
- **135070469.pdf** — Better than Advertised Security for Non-Interactive Threshold Signatures (Bellare, Crites, Komlo, Maller, Tessaro, Zhu). Unified security hierarchy for non-interactive threshold signatures; shows FROST1/BLS meet stronger notions and analyzes FROST2 with DKG.
- **137470009.pdf** — Doubly Efficient Interactive Proofs over Infinite and Non-Commutative Rings (Soria-Vazquez). GKR-style interactive proofs for layered circuits over arbitrary (possibly infinite, non-commutative) rings using black-box ring arithmetic.
- **137470016.pdf** — On the Optimal Communication Complexity of Error-Correcting Multi-Server PIR (Eriguchi, Kurosawa, Nuida). Determines asymptotically optimal communication for b-error-correcting multi-server PIR under perfect and relaxed statistical error correction.
- **137470018.pdf** — On Secret Sharing, Randomness, and Random-less Reductions for Secret Sharing (Aggarwal, Chung, Obremski, Ribeiro). Impossibility results for 2-out-of-2 leakage-resilient and non-malleable secret sharing without uniform randomness.
- **137470019.pdf** — Statistical Security in Two-Party Computation Revisited (Badrinarayanan, Patranabis, Sarkar). Framework for round-optimal one-sided statistically secure 2PC from weak OT plus commitments; instantiations from CDH and isogeny assumptions.
- **137470020.pdf** — Vector Commitments over Rings and Compressed Sigma-Protocols (Attema, Cascudo, Cramer, Damgard, Escudero). Generalizes compressed Sigma-protocol theory to arithmetic modulo any number (including powers of 2) via new homomorphic vector commitments.
- **137470021.pdf** — Fiat-Shamir Transformation of Multi-Round Interactive Proofs (Attema, Fehr, Klooss). Shows knowledge error degrades only linearly in oracle queries for special-sound multi-round protocols, with matching negative results.
- **137470022.pdf** — Round-optimal Honest-majority MPC in Minicrypt and with Everlasting Security (Applebaum, Kachlon, Patra). Three-round fully secure honest-majority MPC from Minicrypt assumptions, with optimal or near-optimal resiliency thresholds.
- **137470023.pdf** — Bet-or-Pass: Adversarially Robust Bloom Filters (Naor, Oved). Unifies and formalizes robustness notions for Bloom filters against adaptive adversaries, proposing the Bet-or-Pass test as the right definition.
- **137470026.pdf** — Achievable CCA2 Relaxation for Homomorphic Encryption (Akavia, Gentry, Halevi, Vald). Defines funcCPA security sufficient for client-aided HE outsourcing against malicious servers; links circuit privacy and circular security.
- **137470028.pdf** — Quantum Rewinding for Many-Round Protocols (Lai, Malavolta, Spooner). First multi-round quantum rewinding strategy, proving post-quantum knowledge soundness of Bulletproofs-style lattice succinct arguments.
- **137470034.pdf** — Fully-Secure MPC with Minimal Trust (Ishai, Patra, Patranabis, Ravi, Srinivasan). Studies guaranteed-output MPC using a small, single-call trusted party, proving exponential lower bounds for universal-decoder protocols and giving constructions.
- **137470036.pdf** — How to Sample a Discrete Gaussian (and more) from a Random Oracle (Lu, Waters). Framework of "explainable" sampling allowing discrete Gaussians and related distributions to be programmed into random oracles.
- **137470039.pdf** — Forward-Secure Encryption with Fast Forwarding (Dodis, Jost, Karthikeyan). Adds sublinear fast-forward key catch-up to forward-secure encryption, with efficient constructions in a bulletin-board model.
- **137470042.pdf** — Pseudorandom (Function-Like) Quantum State Generators: New Definitions and Applications (Ananth, Gulati, Qian, Yuen). Adaptive/superposition-secure PRFS variants from post-quantum OWFs; classical-communication commitments and encryption from short PRS.
- **137470045.pdf** — Collusion Resistant Copy-Protection for Watermarkable Functionalities (Liu, Liu, Qian, Zhandry). Initiates collusion-resistant quantum copy-protection in the plain model; first copy-protection of digital signature schemes.
- **137470051.pdf** — Adaptive Multiparty NIKE (Koppula, Waters, Zhandry). Adaptively secure multiparty non-interactive key exchange from polynomially hard iO and standard assumptions, improving prior sub-exponential requirements.
- **137470054.pdf** — Random-Index Oblivious RAM (Halevi, Kushilevitz). Introduces RORAM, a weakened ORAM for random-index access sufficient for sampling/MPC applications, with lighter hierarchical and tree-based constructions.
- **137470058.pdf** — Round-Optimal Black-Box Secure Computation from Two-Round Malicious OT (Ishai, Khurana, Sahai, Srinivasan). Round-optimal black-box two-party NISC and three-round MPC in the CRS model from any two-round malicious OT.
- **137470061.pdf** — Multi-Authority ABE from Lattices without Random Oracles (Waters, Wee, Wu). First plain-model multi-authority ABE for subset policies, based on evasive LWE, plus a modular related-trapdoor LWE framework.
- **137470063.pdf** — Beyond Uber: Instantiating Generic Groups via PGGs (Bauer, Farshim, Harasser, O'Neill). Standard-model pseudo-generic group definition capturing when real groups "look generic," generalizing the Uber assumption family.
- **137470064.pdf** — Multi-Input Quadratic Functional Encryption: Stronger Security, Broader Functionality (Agrawal, Goyal, Tomida). MIFE for quadratic functions from bilinear maps with fine-grained user-corruption security beyond prior all-or-nothing models.
- **137470068.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Algebraic Vector Commitments in Pairing-Free Groups (Catalano, Fiore, Gennaro, Giunta). Rules out large classes of concise algebraic vector commitments (and implied succinct polynomial commitments) in pairing-free prime-order groups.
- **137470070.pdf** — Bounded Functional Encryption for Turing Machines: Adaptive Security from General Assumptions (Agrawal, Kitagawa, Modi, Nishimaki, Yamada, Yamakawa). Adaptively simulation-secure bounded-collusion FE/ABE for Turing machines from general assumptions in the standard model.
- **137470073.pdf** — Public-Key Encryption from Homogeneous CLWE (Bogdanov, Cueto Noval, Hoffmann, Rosen). Four PKE schemes from the homogeneous continuous LWE assumption, plus an SZK-oracle algorithm for hCLWE highlighting statistical-computational gaps.
- **137470075.pdf** — Asymptotically Free Broadcast in Constant Expected Time via Packed VSS (Abraham, Asharov, Patil, Patra). Expected-constant-round broadcast with O(nL + n^4 log n) communication at optimal resilience, using packed verifiable secret sharing.
