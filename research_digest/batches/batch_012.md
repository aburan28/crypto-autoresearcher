# Batch 012 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by theory-of-cryptography papers from the ~2018 CRYPTO/TCC era (numbered ePrint-style files 10993xxx and 11239xxx): secure computation (MPC round complexity, incomplete networks, leakage resilience), obfuscation/functional encryption, and impossibility/black-box separation results.
- Recurring themes: tight security reductions (signatures, NIKE, LRW2), indistinguishability obfuscation and FE (constructions, quantum attacks, Turing-machine variants), ORAM/OPRAM bounds, and quantum-safe primitives (QFHE, pseudorandom quantum states, collapsing hash functions).
- Notable papers: Ball et al. on worst-case Proofs of Work; Pellet-Mary's quantum attack on GGH13-based obfuscators; Boneh et al. "Crypto Dark Matter" PRF candidates; Dodis et al. breaking Facebook's attachment franking.
- Very little elliptic-curve/ECDLP content in this batch despite the corpus focus; the one ECC-adjacent paper is an older smartcard pairing implementation (11.pdf).
- Anomalies: 10993372.pdf is a 1-page malformed math fragment (not a paper); 11.pdf has an odd short filename and is a ~2006 pairing-on-smartcards paper, out of place among 2018 theory papers.

## Papers
- **10993336.pdf** — Proofs of Work From Worst-Case Assumptions (Ball, Rosen, Sabin, Vasudevan). Builds PoWs provably hard under fine-grained worst-case assumptions (OV, 3SUM, APSP), proving non-amortizability and faster verification.
- **10993341.pdf** — Practical and Tightly-Secure Digital Signatures and Authenticated Key Exchange (Gjøsteen, Jager). First practical tightly-secure multi-user signature via a new Fiat-Shamir application, extended to a 1-RTT tightly-secure signed-DH-style AKE.
- **10993346.pdf** — Private Circuits: A Modular Approach (Ananth, Ishai, Sahai). Simpler modular construction of leakage-resilient circuits tolerating constant-rate random wire leakage, with basis trade-offs and a negative result.
- **10993350.pdf** — On Tightly Secure Non-Interactive Key Exchange (Hesse, Hofheinz, Kohl). First NIKE with n/2 reduction loss circumventing Bader et al.'s lower bound, plus a generalized lower bound showing the reduction is optimal.
- **10993359.pdf** — Quantum Attacks against Indistinguishability Obfuscators Proved Secure in the Weak Multilinear Map Model (Pellet-Mary). Quantum polynomial-time attack on GMMSSZ/DG-GMM obfuscators over GGH13 maps via the short principal ideal solver.
- **10993361.pdf** — On the Local Leakage Resilience of Linear Secret Sharing Schemes (Benhamouda, Degwekar, Ishai, Rabin). Shows additive and high-threshold Shamir sharing over large prime fields resist bounded local leakage, via Fourier analysis.
- **10993366.pdf** — GGH15 Beyond Permutation Branching Programs: Proofs, Attacks, and Candidates (Chen, Vaikuntanathan, Wee). Studies GGH15 with general branching programs: new constrained PRFs and lockable obfuscation from LWE, plus a "rank attack" breaking iO candidates.
- **10993372.pdf** — Not a research paper: 1-page extract is a garbled fragment of math notation with no title, authors, or text.
- **10993373.pdf** — A Simple Obfuscation Scheme for Pattern-Matching with Wildcards (Bishop, Kowalczyk, Malkin, Pastro, Raykova, Shi). Efficient obfuscation of wildcard pattern matching from the generic group heuristic, avoiding multilinear maps or LWE.
- **10993379.pdf** — Multi-Input Functional Encryption for Inner Products: Function-Hiding Realizations and Constructions without Pairings (Abdalla, Catalano, Fiore, Gay, Ursu). Generic pairing-free transform from single- to multi-input inner-product FE, instantiable from DDH, LWE, and DCR.
- **10993383.pdf** — Quantum FHE (Almost) As Secure As Classical (Brakerski). QFHE scheme with classical key generation from polynomial-modulus LWE, matching classical FHE assumptions up to polynomial factors.
- **10993384.pdf** — Structured Encryption and Leakage Suppression (Kamara, Moataz, Ohrimenko). Compiler framework eliminating query/search-pattern leakage in structured encryption, generalizing square-root ORAM with better query complexity.
- **10993396.pdf** — On the Complexity of Compressing Obfuscation (Asharov, Ephraim, Komargodski, Pass). Systematic study of compressing obfuscation (slightly compressed truth-table iO), giving positive and negative results on its power and plausibility.
- **10993397.pdf** — Pseudorandom Quantum States (Ji, Liu, Song). Defines quantum states indistinguishable from random to quantum poly-time adversaries; constructs them from quantum-secure OWFs and proves a cryptographic no-cloning theorem.
- **10993402.pdf** — Out-of-Band Authentication in Group Messaging: Computational, Statistical, Optimal (Rotem, Segev). Initiates out-of-band authentication for group messaging against MitM, with tight bounds on adversary success vs. authenticated message length.
- **10993405.pdf** — Indifferentiable Authenticated Encryption (Barbosa, Farshim). Studies AEAD via indifferentiability: classical schemes fail, a 3-round Feistel construction succeeds, and 2 rounds are impossible.
- **10993413.pdf** — Encrypt or Decrypt? To Make a Single-Key Beyond Birthday Secure Nonce-Based MAC (Datta, Dutta, Nandi, Yasuda). DWCDM replaces EWCDM's outer encryption with decryption, yielding a truly single-key beyond-birthday-bound MAC via mirror theory.
- **10993415.pdf** — Limits of Practical Sublinear Secure Computation (Boyle, Ishai, Polychroniadou). Framework separating practical from impractical sublinear-communication secure computation, identifying provably hard big-data problems.
- **10993416.pdf** — Round-Optimal Secure Multi-Party Computation (Halevi, Hazay, Polychroniadou, Venkitasubramaniam). Establishes exact round complexity of malicious-secure MPC, constructing four-round protocols from polynomial-time assumptions.
- **10993417.pdf** — Amortized Complexity of Information-Theoretically Secure MPC Revisited (Cascudo, Cramer, Xing, Yuan). New amortization paradigm trading field size rather than adversary threshold, keeping maximal threshold (n-1)/3 in the BGW model.
- **10993426.pdf** — Optimal Channel Security Against Fine-Grained State Compromise: The Safety of Messaging (Jaeger, Stepanovs). Formal definition and construction of a bidirectional channel with better state-compromise security than Signal's Double Ratchet.
- **10993428.pdf** — Fast Message Franking: From Invisible Salamanders to Encryptment (Dodis, Grubbs, Ristenpart, Woodage). Breaks Facebook Messenger's attachment franking, then builds fast compactly committing AE from the new "encryptment" primitive.
- **10993430.pdf** — Round-Optimal Secure Multiparty Computation with Honest Majority (Ananth, Choudhuri, Goel, Jain). Round-optimal honest-majority MPC: two rounds with abort from OWFs, three rounds with guaranteed output from PKE plus Zaps.
- **10993438.pdf** — Limits on the Power of Garbling Techniques for Public-Key Encryption (Garg, Hajiabadi, Mahmoody, Mohammed). Shows garbled circuits with OWF/random-oracle gates cannot yield PKE, suggesting known non-black-box techniques are insufficient.
- **10993440.pdf** — Simplifying Game-Based Definitions: Indistinguishability up to Correctness (Rogaway, Zhang). Introduces IND|C via oracle silencing to handle trivial wins, applied to clean definitions for stateful authenticated encryption.
- **10993444.pdf** — Combiners for Backdoored Random Oracles (Bauer, Farshim, Mazaheri). Models hash functions with backdoored tables; shows two independent backdoored functions suffice to bootstrap hardness, via communication-complexity reductions.
- **10993456.pdf** — Must the Communication Graph of MPC Protocols be an Expander? (Boyle, Cohen, Data, Hubáček). Foundational study of MPC over dynamic communication graphs, giving protocols whose induced graphs are not expanders.
- **10993459.pdf** — Sub-Linear Lattice-Based Zero-Knowledge Arguments for Arithmetic Circuits (Baum, Bootle, Cerulli, del Pino, Groth, Lyubashevsky). First SIS-based ZK argument with O(sqrt(N))-style sublinear communication for arithmetic circuit satisfiability.
- **11.pdf** — Implementing Cryptographic Pairings on Smartcards (Scott, Costigan, Abdulwahab). Implements Tate, Ate, and eta-T pairings on a 32-bit MIPS smartcard, achieving 0.15 s pairing times (older paper, anomalous filename/era).
- **11239107.pdf** — On the Complexity of Fair Coin Flipping (Haitner, Makriyannis, Omri). Studies whether o(1/sqrt(r))-fair coin flipping requires oblivious transfer/public-key primitives, extending black-box separation results.
- **11239111.pdf** — On the Structure of Unconditional UC Hybrid Protocols (Rosulek, Shirley). Characterizes when a function UC-reduces to an incomplete ideal g: exactly when a single-call, no-communication protocol exists.
- **11239116.pdf** — Topology-Hiding Computation Beyond Semi-Honest Adversaries (Lavigne, Liu-Zhang, Maurer, Moran, Mularczyk, Tschudi). Topology-hiding protocols tolerating fail-stop and semi-malicious corruptions with only arbitrarily small topology leakage.
- **11239118.pdf** — Towards Tight Security of Cascaded LRW2 (Mennink). Gives a 2^{3n/4}-query distinguishing attack on generalized Cascaded LRW2 and proves tight security when tweak repetitions are bounded.
- **11239124.pdf** — Oblivious Transfer in Incomplete Networks (Narayanan, Prabhakaran). Characterizes when two parties can realize information-theoretic OT against general adversary structures in incomplete private-channel networks.
- **11239133.pdf** — Secure Computation using Leaky Correlations: Asymptotically Optimal Constructions (Block, Gupta, Maji, Nguyen). First 2-message two-party protocol computing linear-size circuits despite linear leakage on preprocessed correlations.
- **11239137.pdf** — Is there an Oblivious RAM Lower Bound for Online Reads? (Weiss, Wichs). Studies whether the log(n) ORAM lower bound extends to online read-only or read-favorable settings beyond balls-and-bins models.
- **11239138.pdf** — Impossibility of Simulation Secure Functional Encryption Even with Random Oracles (Agrawal, Koppula, Waters). Exhibits a functionality (PRF-evaluation FE) for which simulation security is impossible even in the random oracle model.
- **11239143.pdf** — Impossibility of Order-Revealing Encryption in Idealized Models (Zhandry, Zhang). Black-box separates ideally-secure ORE from symmetric-key primitives, PKE, and generic groups, via an information-theoretic ORE impossibility.
- **11239144.pdf** — One-Message Zero Knowledge and Non-Malleable Commitments (Bitansky, Lin). Defines one-message ZK with weak soundness from multi-collision-resistant hashing; builds first one-message concurrent non-malleable commitments from falsifiable assumptions.
- **11239148.pdf** — Perfectly Secure Oblivious Parallel RAM (Chan, Nayak, Shi). First perfectly secure OPRAM with O(log N log log N) parallel-time blowup and O(1) space blowup, simplifying perfectly secure ORAM.
- **11239149.pdf** — Smooth NIZK Arguments (Jutla, Roy). Defines smooth NIZKs paralleling smooth projective hash functions; yields the first single-round asymmetric UC-PAKE secure under adaptive corruption.
- **11239155.pdf** — On the Power of Amortization in Secret Sharing: d-Uniform Secret Sharing and CDS with Constant Information Rate (Applebaum, Arkis). Constant information-ratio secret sharing for d-uniform access structures via a new 4-bits-per-secret-bit multiparty CDS.
- **11239157.pdf** — Information-Theoretic Secret-Key Agreement: The Asymptotically Tight Relation Between the Secret-Key Rate and the Channel Quality Ratio (Jost, Maurer, Ribeiro). Characterizes the satellite-setting secret-key rate asymptotically tightly as a function of the adversary's antenna advantage Q.
- **11239164.pdf** — A Simple Construction of iO for Turing Machines (Garg, Srinivasan). Simpler iO for Turing machines with obfuscation time independent of machine runtime/space, from iO for circuits and SSB hash functions.
- **11239174.pdf** — Classical Proofs for the Quantum Collapsing Property of Classical Hash Functions (Fehr). Framework giving purely classical, compositional proofs that hash domain-extension constructions are collapsing (quantum collision resistance analogue).
- **11239180.pdf** — Fine-Grained Secure Computation (Campanelli, Gennaro). Defines secure computation against moderately complex (NC1) adversaries; builds FHE and verifiable computation from NC1 != xorL/poly, without OWFs.
- **11239183.pdf** — Two-Round MPC: Information-Theoretic and Black-Box (Garg, Ishai, Srinivasan). Two-round MPC from OT correlations with black-box PRG use and information-theoretic variants, plus new DDH-based non-interactive OT constructions.
- **11239186.pdf** — FE and iO for Turing Machines from Minimal Assumptions (Agrawal, Maitra). Builds TM-model iO and single/multi-input FE from (sub-exponentially secure) compact FE for circuits, removing prior sub-exponential-loss barriers.
- **11239188.pdf** — Exploring Crypto Dark Matter: New Simple PRF Candidates and Their Applications (Boneh, Ishai, Passelègue, Sahai, Wu). Proposes extremely simple weak PRF candidates in depth-2 ACC0 (mod-2/mod-3 piecewise-linear maps) with theoretical connections and applications.
- **11239190.pdf** — Upgrading to Functional Encryption (Badrinarayanan, Khurana, Sahai, Waters). Studies whether existing PKE can be upgraded to FE without changing keys; shows negative results via FHE+LWE and random-oracle separations.
