# Batch 038 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ~2022-era Eurocrypt/Crypto-style proceedings papers (mostly 30p): secure multiparty computation and round/communication optimality, zero-knowledge, obfuscation, and functional/attribute-based encryption.
- Strong symmetric-cryptanalysis cluster: improved attacks on ChaCha, GIFT-64, AES-like hashing (rebound/Super-Inbound), sponge preimage tightness, and MITM modeling; plus white-box and side-channel work.
- Post-quantum cluster: trilinear-form signatures, lattice isomorphism problem, GeMSS/Rainbow rank attacks, partial key exposure on BIKE/Rainbow/NTRU, and LWE-based ZK.
- ECDLP-adjacent highlights: Groth–Shoup on ECDSA with additive key derivation and presignatures (GGM analysis), and Zhandry's generic-group model comparison (Shoup vs Maurer).
- Anomalies: 135070040.pdf has no extracted text; 134.pdf is an older (mid-2000s) GESS/SFE paper, an era outlier in the batch.

## Papers
- **132760243.pdf** — Highly Efficient OT-Based Multiplication Protocols (Haitner, Makriyannis, Ranellucci, Tsfadia). New OT-based two-party multiplication nearly as efficient as Gilboa's semi-honest protocol but maliciously secure without further compilation.
- **132760244.pdf** — COA-Secure Obfuscation and Applications (Canetti, Chakraborty, Khurana, Kumar, Poburinnaya, Prabhakaran). Introduces chosen-obfuscation-attack security for obfuscated programs, with applications to watermarking and completely CCA encryption.
- **132760251.pdf** — Practical Post-Quantum Signature Schemes from Isomorphism Problems of Trilinear Forms (Tang, Duong, Joux, Plantard, Qiao, Susilo). Signature scheme from alternating trilinear form equivalence, inspired by GMW graph-isomorphism ZK; NIST PQ candidate alternative.
- **132760254.pdf** — Dynamic Collusion Bounded Functional Encryption from Identity-Based Encryption (Garg, Goyal, Lu, Waters). Initiates dynamically bounded collusion-resistant FE, letting each encryptor choose the collusion bound.
- **132760256.pdf** — Rubato: Noisy Ciphers for Approximate Homomorphic Encryption (Ha, Kim, Lee, Lee, Son). Family of low-degree noisy symmetric ciphers for the RtF transciphering framework, improving throughput over HERA.
- **132760257.pdf** — Property-Preserving Hash Functions for Hamming Distance from Standard Assumptions (Fleischhacker, Larsen, Simkin). Robust PPH for Hamming distance from standard lattice assumptions, improving Eurocrypt 2021 work.
- **132760271.pdf** — Round-Optimal Black-Box Protocol Compilers (Ishai, Khurana, Sahai, Srinivasan). Black-box compilers from semi-honest to malicious security in ROM and OT-correlations models, yielding round-optimal MPC and batch OT.
- **132760277.pdf** — Guaranteed Output in O(√n) Rounds for Round-Robin Sampling Protocols (Cohen, Doerner, Kondi, shelat). Generic compiler reducing n-round round-robin sampling (e.g., powers-of-tau setups) to O(√n) broadcasts with guaranteed output.
- **132760278.pdf** — On IND-qCCA Security in the ROM and its Applications (Huguenin-Dumittan, Vaudenay). Shows IND-qCCA KEMs easily obtained from passive PKE in (Q)ROM; proves CPA-secure KEMs suffice for TLS 1.3 handshake security.
- **132760280.pdf** — Batch-OT with Optimal Rate (Brakerski, Branco, Döttling, Pu). Two-message rate-1 batch oblivious transfer from LPN plus DDH/QR/LWE, matching information-theoretic communication lower bounds without FHE.
- **132760288.pdf** — Unclonable Polymers and Their Cryptographic Applications (Almashaqbeh, Canetti, Erlich, et al.). Protein-polymer consumable storage with self-destruct properties enables secure vaults and one-time programs.
- **132760293.pdf** — On the Concrete Security of TLS 1.3 PSK Mode (Davis, Diemert, Günther, Jager). First tight security proofs for TLS 1.3 PSK handshake modes via a new indifferentiability-based key-schedule abstraction.
- **132760296.pdf** — Universally Composable Subversion-Resilient Cryptography (Chakraborty, Magri, Nielsen, Venturi). UC foundation for reverse firewalls, generalizing UC to subverted cores with sanitizing firewalls.
- **132760297.pdf** — Adaptively Secure Computation for RAM Programs (Bangalore, Ostrovsky, Poburinnaya, Venkitasubramaniam). First two-round adaptively secure two-party computation with communication proportional to square of RAM complexity.
- **132760306.pdf** — Revamped Differential-Linear Cryptanalysis on Reduced Round ChaCha (Dey, Garai, Sarkar, Sharma). Improved differential-linear attacks on ChaCha, reducing 256-bit time complexity to 2^221.95 and first 6.5-round ChaCha-128 attack.
- **132760308.pdf** — Asymptotically Quasi-Optimal Cryptography (de Castro, Hazay, Ishai, Vaikuntanathan, Venkitasubramaniam). First AQO batch OLE from RLWE and AQO zero-knowledge arguments with polylog overhead for all n and λ.
- **132760310.pdf** — Optimal Broadcast Encryption and CP-ABE from Evasive Lattice Assumptions (Wee). Candidate optimal broadcast encryption with poly(log N) parameters from a new evasive LWE variant; plausibly post-quantum.
- **132760313.pdf** — A Novel Completeness Test for Leakage Models (Gao, Oswald). Statistical test based on collapsed models to verify state completeness in side-channel leakage models and simulators; reveals new attack vectors.
- **132760321.pdf** — Round-Optimal Multi-Party Computation with Identifiable Abort (Ciampi, Ravi, Siniscalchi, Waldner). Shows four rounds suffice for MPC with identifiable abort under standard assumptions, closing a gap left by Garg et al.
- **132760323.pdf** — Embedding the UC Model into the IITM Model (Rausch, Küsters, Chevalier). Initiates study of relations between universal composability models, enabling reuse of results across UC variants.
- **132760333.pdf** — On the Security of ECDSA with Additive Key Derivation and Presignatures (Groth, Shoup). Generic-group-model security analysis of BIP32-style key derivation and presignatures, in isolation and combined, for threshold ECDSA.
- **132760345.pdf** — Towards Micro-Architectural Leakage Simulators (Gao, Oswald, Page). Shows grey-box reverse engineering of micro-architectural leakage on commercial ARM processors is practical; enhances ELMO simulator.
- **132760347.pdf** — Field Instruction Multiple Data (Aung, Lim, Sim, Tan, Wang, Yeo). Applies reverse multiplication friendly embedding to FHE to encode more data atop SIMD plaintext slots, with recoding techniques.
- **132760348.pdf** — Secure Multiparty Computation with Free Branching (Goel, Hall-Andersen, Hegde, Jain). Generic multi-party framework for branching circuits with communication proportional to the largest branch, generalizing stacked garbling.
- **132760354.pdf** — On the Lattice Isomorphism Problem, Quadratic Forms, Remarkable Lattices, and Cryptography (Ducas, van Woerden). Worst-to-average-case reductions for LIP and ZK proofs of lattice isomorphisms, enabling McEliece-style lattice cryptography.
- **132760362.pdf** — A Greater GIFT: Strengthening GIFT against Statistical Cryptanalysis (Sun, Preneel, Wang, Wang). Manual-plus-automated differential/linear analysis of GIFT-64, proving structural properties of optimal characteristics.
- **132760363.pdf** — Distributed (Correlation) Samplers: How to Remove a Trusted Dealer in One Round (Abram, Scholl, Yakoubov). One-round distributed samplers and public-key PCFs using IO, with an anti-rusher compiler for active security.
- **132760376.pdf** — Secure Multiparty Computation with Sublinear Preprocessing (Boyle, Gilboa, Ishai, Nof). First concretely efficient malicious MPC with sublinear-communication preprocessing of authenticated triples via pseudorandom correlation generators.
- **134.pdf** — Gate Evaluation Secret Sharing and Secure One-Round Two-Party Computation (Kolesnikov). Older paper proposing GESS, a secret-sharing generalization of Yao's garbled circuits for one-round SFE.
- **135070003.pdf** — To Label, or Not To Label (in Generic Groups) (Zhandry). Shows Shoup's and Maurer's generic group models are not equivalent; gives new un-instantiability results for Maurer and the algebraic group model.
- **135070004.pdf** — Augmented Random Oracles (Zhandry). Proposes AROM to capture ROM impossibility results; strengthens Fiat-Shamir, Fujisaki-Okamoto, and Encrypt-with-Hash transforms against them.
- **135070006.pdf** — Structure-Aware Private Set Intersection, With Applications to Fuzzy Matching (Garimella, Rosulek, Singh). PSI with communication scaling with description size of structured sets, via a new relaxed function secret-sharing paradigm.
- **135070013.pdf** — Quantum Commitments and Signatures without One-Way Functions (Morimae, Yamakawa). Non-interactive quantum commitments and signatures from pseudorandom quantum states, possibly without any one-way functions.
- **135070018.pdf** — Implicit White-Box Implementations: White-Boxing ARX Ciphers (Ranea, Vandersmissen, Preneel). New implicit-function white-box design resisting attacks on CEJO and self-equivalence schemes; open-source BoolCrypt tool.
- **135070027.pdf** — Triangulating Rebound Attack on AES-like Hashing (Dong, Guo, Li, Pham). Super-Inbound technique extends rebound inbound phase up to 3 rounds, improving classical and quantum collision attacks on AES-like hashes.
- **135070029.pdf** — Parallel Repetition of (k1,...,kµ)-Special-Sound Multi-Round Interactive Proofs (Attema, Fehr). Proves t-fold parallel repetition of special-sound multi-round proofs optimally reduces knowledge error to κ^t.
- **135070031.pdf** — Improving Support-Minors Rank Attacks: Applications to GeMSS and Rainbow (Baena, Briaud, Cabarcas, Perlner, Smith-Tone, Verbel). Greatly improved Support-Minors attack renders GeMSS irreparable; also improves XL strategies.
- **135070036.pdf** — Block-Cipher-Based Tree Hashing (Gunsing). Diagnoses a fundamental indifferentiability-proof error in Daemen et al.'s tree hashing and fixes the security bounds via generalized finalization.
- **135070037.pdf** — Tight Preimage Resistance of the Sponge Construction (Lefevre, Mennink). Derives tight preimage bound for sponges, showing e.g. Ascon-Hash achieves 2^192 rather than claimed 2^128 preimage security.
- **135070040.pdf** — No text extracted; cannot summarize.
- **135070046.pdf** — Simplified MITM Modeling for Permutations: New (Quantum) Attacks (Schrottenloher, Stevens). Simplified MILP model for MITM attacks improving distinguishers on Spongent/PRESENT-like and AES-like permutations, classically and quantumly.
- **135070065.pdf** — Secret Can Be Public: Low-Memory AEAD Mode for High-Order Masking (Naito, Sasaki, Sugawara). AEAD mode HOMA protecting only half the key-dependent state under masking; SKINNY-based instantiation with hardware evaluation.
- **135070070.pdf** — Partial Key Exposure Attacks on BIKE, Rainbow and NTRU (Esser, May, Verbel, Wen). Shows constant-fraction key leakage suffices for polynomial-time key recovery in BIKE, Rainbow, NTRU via information-set decoding techniques.
- **135070072.pdf** — Certified Everlasting Zero-Knowledge Proof for QMA (Hiroka, Morimae, Nishimaki, Yamakawa). ZK proof for QMA where a deletion certificate upgrades hiding to everlasting; new certified-everlasting commitment primitive.
- **135070074.pdf** — The Gap Is Sensitive to Size of Preimages: Collapsing vs Quantum Collision-Resistance (?) (Cao, Xue). Studies relations between collapsing and collision-resistant hash functions; equivalence for polynomially bounded preimages.
- **135070077.pdf** — Collision-Resistance from Multi-Collision-Resistance (Rothblum, Vasudevan). Non-black-box, non-constructive transformation of moderately shrinking t-MCRH (t∈{3,4}) into infinitely-often CRH.
- **135070086.pdf** — Formalizing Delayed Adaptive Corruptions and the Security of Flooding Networks (Matt, Nielsen, Thomsen). UC semantics for δ-delayed adaptive adversaries, enabling meaningful security proofs for blockchain flooding protocols.
- **135070096.pdf** — Public Randomness Extraction with Ephemeral Roles and Worst-Case Corruptions (Nielsen, Ribeiro, Obremski). Information-theoretic YOSO-style randomness extraction tolerating worst-case corruptions; optimal threshold protocols.
- **135070102.pdf** — Nearly Optimal Property Preserving Hashing (Holmgren, Liu, Tyner, Wichs). Simpler, near-optimal PPH/RPPH for Hamming distance via syndrome list-decoding, with matching lower bound and robust upgrade.
- **135070111.pdf** — Public-Coin 3-Round Zero-Knowledge from LWE and Keyless Multi-Collision-Resistant Hash (Kiyoshima). Constructs public-coin 3-round ZK argument for NP from sub-exponential LWE plus keyless MCRH.
