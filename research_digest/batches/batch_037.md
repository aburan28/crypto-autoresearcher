# Batch 037 digest

## Overview
- All 50 files are genuine research papers; consecutive IDs (132760xxx) and uniform style indicate one proceedings volume — Springer LNCS 13276, i.e., EUROCRYPT 2022 (part I).
- Dominant themes: zero-knowledge proofs/SNARKs (IOPs, Bulletproofs, elastic SNARKs, SNARGs), secure computation (MPC, garbled circuits/RAM, coin toss, PVSS), and post-quantum crypto (lattices, isogenies, NIST PQC KEM analysis).
- Strong quantum-security thread: quantum attacks/algorithms (filtering for SIS/LWE, Even-Mansour, watermarking PRFs, non-malleable commitments, blind verification of quantum sampling).
- Symmetric cryptanalysis highlights: first correlation attack on full SNOW-V/Vi, DFA breaking DEFAULT's security claim, refined GEA-1/GEA-2 attacks, committing AE (Bellare–Hoang).
- ECC relevance is thin: only 132760128 (SNARK-friendly 2-chains of pairing-friendly curves, El Housni–Guillevic) is directly elliptic-curve/pairing work; several others use DL-group assumptions generically.
- Anomaly: the source chunk contained one NUL byte (unreadable as strict UTF-8); a cleaned copy chunk_037_clean.txt was used for extraction. No duplicates or non-paper items.

## Papers
- **132760047.pdf** — On the Multi-User Security of Short Schnorr Signatures with Preprocessing (Blocki, Lee). Proves 3k-bit truncated Schnorr signatures give k-bit multi-user security in GGM+ROM, and analyzes key-prefixed variants against preprocessing attackers.
- **132760058.pdf** — CoCoA: Concurrent Continuous Group Key Agreement (Alwen, Auerbach, Cueto Noval, Klein, Pascual-Perez, Pietrzak, Walter). Group key agreement for large messaging groups allowing concurrent key rotations without the cost/damage of prior 2-round MLS-style updates.
- **132760073.pdf** — Quantum Algorithms for Variants of Average-Case Lattice Problems via Filtering (Chen, Liu, Zhandry). Polynomial-time quantum algorithms for special-parameter SIS, LWE-with-quantum-states, and EDCP variants via a filtering technique.
- **132760074.pdf** — A Correlation Attack on Full SNOW-V and SNOW-Vi (Shi, Jin, Zhang, Cui, Ding, Jin). SAT/SMT-found linear approximations yield the first attack on full SNOW-V/SNOW-Vi faster than exhaustive key search (~2^246 time).
- **132760083.pdf** — Constant-round Blind Classical Verification of Quantum Sampling (Chung, Lee, Lin, Wu). Extends Mahadev's classical verification of quantum computation to sampling problems (SampBQP) with blindness and constant rounds.
- **132760094.pdf** — Approximate Divisor Multiples – Factoring with Only a Third of the Secret CRT-Exponents (May, Nowakowski, Sarkar). Partial key exposure attack on CRT-RSA: knowing a third of bits of both dp,dq suffices to factor when e≈N^{1/12}.
- **132760101.pdf** — Zero-Knowledge IOPs with Linear-Time Prover and Polylogarithmic-Time Verifier (Bootle, Chiesa, Liu). IOP simultaneously achieving zero knowledge, linear-time proving, and polylog verification for arithmetic circuit satisfiability.
- **132760102.pdf** — Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge Proofs with Fine-Grained Security (Wang, Pan). First NIZKs in the NC1 fine-grained setting (adversaries as strong as honest users), without random oracles, plus fine-grained FHE.
- **132760105.pdf** — On Succinct Non-Interactive Arguments in Relativized Worlds (Chen, Chiesa, Spooner). Introduces low-degree/linear-code random oracle models where transparent SNARKs exist for all NP computations relative to the oracle.
- **132760106.pdf** — Anonymous, Robust Post-Quantum Public Key Encryption (Grubbs, Maram, Paterson). Systematic study of anonymity and robustness for post-quantum PKE/KEMs, showing implicit rejection invalidates prior KEM-DEM results.
- **132760109.pdf** — McEliece needs a Break – Solving McEliece-1284 and Quasi-Cyclic-2918 with Modern ISD (Esser, May, Zweydinger). First optimized implementations of representation-based ISD; record decodings used to re-estimate BIKE/HQC/McEliece bit security.
- **132760117.pdf** — EpiGRAM: Practical Garbled RAM (Heath, Kolesnikov, Ostrovsky). First practically feasible garbled RAM, with amortized O(w·log²n·κ) per access, beating linear scan from 512 elements.
- **132760125.pdf** — Garbled Circuits With Sublinear Evaluator (Haque, Heath, Kolesnikov, Lu, Ostrovsky, Shah). GCWise extends stacked garbling so the evaluator's work is also sublinear; application to Garbled PIR.
- **132760128.pdf** — Families of SNARK-friendly 2-chains of elliptic curves (El Housni, Guillevic). Generalizes BLS12/BW6 2-chain constructions for recursive SNARKs, compares with Cocks–Pinch curves, and derives fast optimal pairings.
- **132760130.pdf** — Information-Combining Differential Fault Attacks on DEFAULT (Nageler, Dobraunig, Eichlseder). Breaks the DFA-resistance claim of block cipher DEFAULT, recovering the full key with under 100 faults via equivalent-key classes.
- **132760133.pdf** — Round-Optimal and Communication-Efficient Multiparty Computation (Ciampi, Ostrovsky, Waldner, Zikas). Black-box compilers giving round-optimal maliciously secure MPC with circuit-scalable or circuit-independent communication from FE combiners.
- **132760134.pdf** — Anamorphic Encryption: Private Communication against a Dictator (Persiano, Phan, Yung). Introduces encryption allowing hidden private messages even when the adversary (state) can access keys and dictate plaintexts.
- **132760137.pdf** — Multi-Designated Receiver Signed Public Key Encryption (Maurer, Portmann, Rito). New PKE notion where a sender encrypts+signs to chosen receivers with consistency, off-the-record, and anonymity; built from standard assumptions via PKE-for-broadcast.
- **132760138.pdf** — A Fast and Simple Partially Oblivious PRF, with Applications (Tyagi, Celi, Ristenpart, Sullivan, Tessaro, Wood). Pairing-free POPRF from a new one-more gap SDH inversion assumption; applications to Privacy Pass, OPAQUE, breach alerting.
- **132760140.pdf** — Fiat–Shamir Bulletproofs are Non-Malleable (in the Algebraic Group Model) (Ganesh, Orlandi, Pancholi, Takahashi, Tschudi). Proves simulation-extractability (non-malleability) of Fiat–Shamir Bulletproofs in AGM via a weak unique response property.
- **132760141.pdf** — Gemini: Elastic SNARKs for Diverse Environments (Bootle, Chiesa, Hu, Orrù). Elastic SNARKs letting provers trade time vs memory per environment; FFT-free construction with Rust/arkworks implementation and benchmarks.
- **132760146.pdf** — Stacking Sigmas: A Framework to Compose Σ-Protocols for Disjunctions (Goel, Green, Hall-Andersen, Kaptchuk). Compiler turning Σ-protocols into disjunctive proofs with communication proportional to the largest clause, not all clauses.
- **132760154.pdf** — Authentication in the Bounded Storage Model (Dodis, Quach, Wichs). Unconditionally secure MACs and signatures in the streaming bounded-storage model, using local extractors and learning-parities space lower bounds.
- **132760155.pdf** — Round-Optimal Byzantine Agreement (Ghinea, Goyal, Liu-Zhang). Randomized BA matching the (c·r)^{-r} failure-probability lower bound up to constants, tolerating adaptive rushing adversary with t<(1−ε)n/2.
- **132760157.pdf** — A Complete Characterization of Game-Theoretically Fair, Multi-Party Coin Toss (Wu, Asharov, Shi). Characterizes when n-party Blum-style coin toss forms a coalition-resistant Nash equilibrium under corrupt majority.
- **132760162.pdf** — Post-Quantum Security of the Even-Mansour Cipher (Alagic, Bai, Katz, Majenz). Proves security of Even-Mansour when the attacker has classical access to the keyed permutation but quantum access to the public permutation.
- **132760166.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Candidate Obfuscators for Affine Determinant Programs (Yao, Chen, Yu). Attacks the Bartusek et al. iO candidate for ADPs by exploiting weakness in its randomization step; discusses countermeasures.
- **132760168.pdf** — Watermarking PRFs against Quantum Adversaries (Kitagawa, Nishimaki). Defines unremovability against quantum pirates and builds privately extractable watermarking PRFs from quantum-hard LWE.
- **132760170.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation from LPN over Fp, DLIN, and PRGs in NC0 (Jain, Lin, Sahai). iO from LPN over prime fields, DLIN, and NC0 PRGs, removing LWE; yields first non-lattice FHE and public-key FE.
- **132760174.pdf** — Efficient Schemes for Committing Authenticated Encryption (Bellare, Hoang). GCM/AES-GCM-SIV variants whose ciphertexts commit to the key (and all inputs) with zero ciphertext expansion.
- **132760185.pdf** — Incompressible Cryptography (Guan, Wichs, Zhandry). Simple constructions of incompressible public-key encryption and signatures from minimal assumptions, secure even if keys later leak unless the whole ciphertext is stored.
- **132760186.pdf** — Private Circuits with Quasilinear Randomness (Goyal, Ishai, Song). Reduces randomness complexity of ISW-style t-private circuits from O(t²·log ts) to O(t·log ts) bits, including the input encoder.
- **132760193.pdf** — Hiding in Plain Sight: Memory-tight Proofs via Randomness Programming (Ghoshal, Ghosal, Jaeger, Tessaro). New memory-tight reduction technique hiding state in random strings; applications to signatures and authenticated encryption.
- **132760194.pdf** — Secure Non-interactive Simulation: Feasibility & Rate (Khorasgani, Maji, Nguyen). Defines SNIS of correlated randomness; proves impossibility of BSS↔BES interconversion and characterizes feasibility/rate for BES-to-BES simulation.
- **132760203.pdf** — Non-malleable Commitments Against Quantum Attacks (Bitansky, Lin, Shmueli). First quantum-secure non-malleable commitments under standard assumptions; log*-round protocol from post-quantum OWFs via a generic extractable-commitment transform.
- **132760204.pdf** — Lightweight, Maliciously Secure Verifiable Function Secret Sharing (de Castro, Polychroniadou). Verifiable two-party FSS for (multi-)point functions using only symmetric-key operations, unconstrained verification, malicious security.
- **132760209.pdf** — Anonymity of NIST PQC Round-3 KEMs (Xagawa). Analyzes anonymity of all Round-3 KEMs in the QROM via strong disjoint-simulatability; notes QROM IND-CCA proof gaps for Kyber/Saber/NTRU Prime.
- **132760213.pdf** — One-Shot Fiat-Shamir-based NIZK Arguments of Composite Residuosity and Logarithmic-Size Ring Signatures (Libert, Nguyen, Peters, Yung). One-shot trapdoor Σ-protocols for DCR languages with large challenge spaces, yielding standard-model NIZKs and log-size ring signatures.
- **132760214.pdf** — Sine Series Approximation of the Mod Function for Bootstrapping of Approximate HE (Jutla, Manohar). Improved sine-series approximation of the mod function enabling practical CKKS bootstrapping with ~100-bit precision.
- **132760216.pdf** — Limits of Polynomial Packings for Zpk and Fpk (Cheon, Lee). Formalizes polynomial packing methods (HE packing, RMFE) and proves upper bounds/impossibility results on density, level-consistency, and surjectivity.
- **132760217.pdf** — Mitaka: A Simpler, Parallelizable, Maskable Variant of Falcon (Espitau, Fouque, Gérard, Rossi, Takahashi, Tibouchi, Wallet, Yu). NTRU-lattice hash-and-sign signature replacing Falcon's sampler with a simpler hybrid sampler, easing parallelization and side-channel protection.
- **132760223.pdf** — Practical Non-interactive Publicly Verifiable Secret Sharing with Thousands of Parties (Gentry, Halevi, Lyubashevsky). LWE-based non-interactive PVSS using multi-receiver PVW encryption, efficient enough for thousand-party blockchain committees.
- **132760224.pdf** — Short Pairing-Free Blind Signatures with Exponential Security (Tessaro, Zhu). First practical pairing-free three-move blind signatures with concurrent security and short signatures in GGM/AGM, via new WFROS problem lower bounds.
- **132760226.pdf** — A PCP Theorem for Interactive Proofs and Applications (Arnon, Chiesa, Yogev). Generalizes the PCP theorem: any k-round IP has a k-round public-coin IOP with O(1) bits read per message; applications to hardness of approximation and SNARKs.
- **132760227.pdf** — Refined Cryptanalysis of the GPRS Ciphers GEA-1 and GEA-2 (Amzaleg, Dinur). Reduces GEA-1 attack memory from 44 GiB to ~4 MiB at same 2^40 time (2.5 h on a laptop) and gives new time/data tradeoff attacks on GEA-2.
- **132760228.pdf** — SNARGs for P from Sub-exponential DDH and QR (Hulett, Jawale, Khurana, Srinivasan). Publicly verifiable SNARGs for deterministic (and bounded-space nondeterministic) computation from sub-exponential DDH and QR, without pairings.
- **132760233.pdf** — Group Signatures and More from Isogenies and Lattices: Generic, Simple, and Efficient (Beullens, Dobson, Katsumata, Lai, Pintore). Dynamic group/accountable ring signatures from group actions (CSIDH or MLWE), O(log N) size, via online-extractable OR-proofs.
- **132760236.pdf** — Asymmetric PAKE with low computation and communication (Dos Santos, Gu, Jarecki, Krawczyk). Two aPAKE protocols achieving optimal computation and round complexity via password-encrypted unilaterally authenticated key exchange (EKE-style compiler).
- **132760238.pdf** — Secure Non-Interactive Reduction and Spectral Analysis of Correlations (Agarwal, Narayanan, Pathak, Prabhakaran, Prabhakaran, Rehan). Studies SNIR between correlations, defining a correlation's spectrum as a necessary condition for reduction, plus a mirroring symmetry lemma.
- **132760239.pdf** — High-Precision Bootstrapping for Approximate Homomorphic Encryption by Error Variance Minimization (Lee, Lee, Kim, Kim, No, Kang). Direct polynomial approximation of modular reduction via error-variance minimization, improving CKKS bootstrapping precision and depth.
