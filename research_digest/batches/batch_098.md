# Batch 098 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ~2012-era CRYPTO/CHES-style proceedings: roughly two thematic blocks — theoretical/foundational crypto plus a very large side-channel/hardware-security cluster (DPA, masking, PUFs, fault attacks).
- A second block covers symmetric cryptanalysis (GOST, TEA/XTEA, Camellia, Grøstl rebound/preimage attacks) and lightweight-cipher implementation studies.
- Notable papers: Mahmoody–Pass on black-box vs non-black-box commitments; Gentry–Halevi–Smart homomorphic AES evaluation; Brakerski FHE without modulus switching; Skorobogatov & Woods FPGA backdoor discovery.
- Direct ECDLP/elliptic-curve content is thin here: only a pairing-bit-hardness result (74170827), an FPGA GF(2^m) scalar multiplier (74280493), and a CPA-protected DF-ECC processor (74280547).
- Anomalies: one empty extract (74280120), one page of plot-axis garbage (74280231), one IACR copyright form (74280338), and a talk abstract on banking security (74280318).

## Papers
- **74170694.pdf** — The Curious Case of Non-Interactive Commitments (Mohammad Mahmoody, Rafael Pass). Rules out black-box constructions of non-interactive commitments from general one-way functions, separating black-box from non-black-box power.
- **74170712.pdf** — Efficient Dissection of Composite Problems (Dinur, Dunkelman, Keller, Shamir). Introduces "dissection" algorithms with better time/memory tradeoffs for multiple-encryption key recovery, knapsacks, and combinatorial search.
- **74170734.pdf** — Resistance Against Iterated Attacks by Decorrelation Revisited (Bay, Mashatan, Vaudenay). Closes two open problems: 2d−1 decorrelation does not suffice against order-d iterated attacks, and repeated queries help distinguishers.
- **74170753.pdf** — Secure Identity-Based Encryption in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Mark Zhandry). First quantum-random-oracle security proofs for IBE, HIBE, and a signature scheme without extra assumptions, via semi-constant oracle distributions.
- **74170771.pdf** — Quantum to Classical Randomness Extractors (Berta, Fawzi, Wehner). Constructs extractors distilling classical randomness from quantum sources using mutually unbiased bases, with entropy upper bounds and applications.
- **74170789.pdf** — Actively Secure Two-Party Evaluation of Any Quantum Operation (Dupuis, Nielsen, Salvail). First actively secure protocol for two-party computation of arbitrary CPTP maps on joint quantum states.
- **74170807.pdf** — Impossibility of Efficient Key Encapsulation and Programmable Hash Functions in Prime Order Groups (Hanaoka, Matsuda, Schuldt). Rules out black-box CCA-secure KEMs with single-group-element overhead in prime-order groups.
- **74170827.pdf** — Hardness of Computing Individual Bits for One-way Functions on Elliptic Curves (Duc, Jetchev). Shows all input bits of pairing-based one-way functions are hard-core; predicting any bit solves fixed-argument pairing inversion.
- **74170845.pdf** — Homomorphic Evaluation of the AES Circuit (Gentry, Halevi, Smart). Working leveled-FHE implementation evaluating AES-128, achieving ~5-minute amortized per-block cost with SIMD and new key/modulus-switching tools.
- **74170863.pdf** — Fully Homomorphic Encryption without Modulus Switching from Classical GapSVP (Zvika Brakerski). Tensoring technique giving linear noise growth and scale-invariant FHE with classical worst-case GapSVP security reduction.
- **74280001.pdf** — 3D Hardware Canaries (Briais, Caron, Cioranesco, et al.). Uses Hamiltonian-path wire cages around circuits in 3D ICs as tamper-detection "canaries" with challenge-response integrity attestation.
- **74280019.pdf** — Breakthrough Silicon Scanning Discovers Backdoor in Military Chip (Skorobogatov, Woods). Reports detection of a silicon-level JTAG backdoor in Actel ProASIC3 military FPGAs using Pipeline Emission Analysis; key extraction enables full chip compromise.
- **74280037.pdf** — Simple Photonic Emission Analysis of AES (Schlösser, Nedospasov, Krämer, Orlic, Seifert). Low-cost optoelectronic setup capturing transistor photon emissions to recover a full AES key via S-box access monitoring.
- **74280054.pdf** — Compiler Assisted Masking (Moss, Oswald, Page, Tunstall). First step toward automating first-order Boolean masking insertion at the compiler level to protect software against DPA.
- **74280070.pdf** — Threshold Implementations of All 3×3 and 4×4 S-boxes (Bilgin, Nikova, Nikov, Rijmen, Stütz). Derives provably first-order-DPA-resistant shared functions for all small S-boxes and DES S-boxes, with cost analysis.
- **74280088.pdf** — How Far Should Theory Be from Practice? (Moradi, Mischke). Evaluates a ROM-based masking countermeasure beyond its assumed leakage model; practical security gain over conventional schemes is marginal.
- **74280103.pdf** — Efficient and Provably Secure Switching from Arithmetic to Boolean Masking (Blandine Debraize). Fixes a bugged prior conversion algorithm and gives faster, provably secure arithmetic-to-Boolean masking conversions.
- **74280120.pdf** — No text extracted; content unknown.
- **74280138.pdf** — Algebraic Side-Channel Attacks Beyond the Hamming Weight Leakage Model (Oren, Renauld, Standaert, Wool). Replaces SAT solvers with optimizers over a posteriori probabilities, improving robustness of algebraic side-channel key recovery.
- **74280153.pdf** — Selecting Time Samples for Multivariate DPA Attacks (Reparaz, Gierlichs, Verbauwhede). Black-box technique to identify informative time-sample tuples before higher-order DPA, cutting attack cost from months to days.
- **74280173.pdf** — Unified and Optimized Linear Collision Attacks (Gérard, Standaert). Recasts linear collision attacks as LDPC decoding with Bayesian detection, improving error tolerance; finds collision attacks often weaker than DPA in practice.
- **74280191.pdf** — Towards Super-Exponential Side-Channel Security with Efficient Leakage-Resilient PRFs (Medwed, Standaert, Joux). Shows bounded-data leakage-resilient PRFs breakable on 8-bit MCUs; proposes parallel tree-based PRF tweaks.
- **74280211.pdf** — Practical Leakage-Resilient Symmetric Cryptography (Faust, Pietrzak, Schipper). Simple constructions (3-round Feistel, GGM variant) achieve leakage resilience under relaxed non-adaptive security notions.
- **74280231.pdf** — Not a paper: single page of axis labels from a CPA success-rate plot; no extractable research content.
- **74280249.pdf** — Practical Security Analysis of PUF-based Two-Player Protocols (Rührmair, van Dijk). Quadratically reduces CRP readout needed to cheat CRYPTO 2011 PUF-based OT/BC protocols; interactive hashing as countermeasure.
- **74280266.pdf** — Soft Decision Error Correction for Compact Memory-Based PUFs (van der Leest, Preneel, van der Sluis). Single-enrollment soft-decision decoding reduces SRAM cells needed for 128-bit PUF key recovery to 3900.
- **74280281.pdf** — PUFs: Myth, Fact or Busted? (Katzenbeisser, Kocabaş, Rožić, Sadeghi, Verbauwhede, Wachsmann). First large-scale evaluation of five intrinsic PUF types across 96 TSMC 65nm ASICs with a common methodology.
- **74280300.pdf** — PUFKY: A Fully Functional PUF-based Cryptographic Key Generator (Maes, Van Herrewege, Verbauwhede). Complete FPGA ring-oscillator PUF key generator with BCH decoder, producing 128-bit keys with <10⁻⁹ failure rate.
- **74280318.pdf** — Banking Security: Attacks and Defences (Steven Murdoch). One-page talk abstract surveying EMV/Chip-and-PIN and online-banking vulnerabilities; not a full research paper.
- **74280338.pdf** — IACR Copyright Form; boilerplate rights-transfer policy text, not a research paper.
- **74280353.pdf** — Solving Quadratic Equations with XL on Parallel Architectures (Cheng, Chou, Niederhagen, Yang). Parallel XL/block-Wiedemann implementation solving 32-variable MQ systems over F16 in 5 days on an 8-node cluster.
- **74280371.pdf** — Efficient Implementations of MQPKS on Constrained Devices (Czypek, Heyse, Thomae). 8-bit microcontroller implementations of UOV, Rainbow, enTTS, and 0/1-UOV at matched security levels for direct comparison.
- **74280388.pdf** — Towards Green Cryptography (Kerckhof, Durvaux, Hocquet, Bol, Standaert). Energy-focused hardware comparison of lightweight block ciphers, highlighting key-schedule and voltage/frequency scaling effects.
- **74280406.pdf** — Lightweight Cryptography for the Cloud (Matsuda, Moriai). Bitslice PRESENT/Piccolo implementations reach ~4.6 cycles/byte on Xeon, offering timing-attack resistance for multi-tenant clouds.
- **74280424.pdf** — Low-Latency Encryption — Is "Lightweight = Light + Wait"? (Knežević, Nikov, Rombouts). Studies hardware latency of block ciphers and design tradeoffs; gives recommendations for low-latency cipher design.
- **74280444.pdf** — Attacking RSA–CRT Signatures with Faults on Montgomery Multiplication (Fouque, Guillermin, Leresteux, Tibouchi, Zapalowicz). Fault attacks effective against any padding including RSA-PSS when small registers can be zeroed or fixed.
- **74280462.pdf** — Reduce-by-Feedback: Timing Resistant and DPA-aware Modular Multiplication (Michael Vielhaber). Shows DPA break of RSA with standard Reduce-by-Feedback/Montgomery multiplication and a fixed variant resistant to timing and power analysis.
- **74280475.pdf** — Side Channel Attack to Actual Cryptanalysis: Breaking CRT-RSA with Low Weight Decryption Exponents (Sarkar, Maitra). Cold-boot error-correction heuristics break CRT-RSA schemes with low-Hamming-weight decryption exponents in minutes.
- **74280493.pdf** — Pushing the Limits of High-Speed GF(2^m) Elliptic Curve Scalar Multiplication on FPGAs (Rebeiro, Sinha Roy, Mukhopadhyay). Fastest reported generic binary-field ECC scalar multiplier via pipelining and overlapped scalar-bit scheduling.
- **74280511.pdf** — On the Design of Hardware Building Blocks for Modern Lattice-Based Encryption Schemes (Göttert, Feller, Schneider, Buchmann, Huss). Hardware/software LWE encryption implementation with FFT polynomial multiplication and discrete Gaussian samplers.
- **74280529.pdf** — Practical Lattice-Based Cryptography: A Signature Scheme for Embedded Systems (Güneysu, Lyubashevsky, Pöppelmann). FPGA-optimized lattice signature scheme with ~100-bit security, competitive area and performance versus classical schemes.
- **74280547.pdf** — Efficient Countermeasure against CPA Attacks with Randomized Montgomery Operations for DF-ECC Processor (Lee, Chung, Chang, Lee). Randomized Montgomery-domain field operations protect a 521-bit dual-field ECC processor with <4% overhead.
- **75490001.pdf** — "Provable" Security Against Differential and Linear Cryptanalysis (Kaisa Nyberg). Invited-talk survey of S-box nonlinearity criteria, bent functions, and countermeasures to differential/linear cryptanalysis.
- **75490009.pdf** — Improved Attacks on Full GOST (Dinur, Dunkelman, Shamir). New fixed-point property yields full 32-round GOST attacks reducing memory to 2^36 and time to 2^192.
- **75490029.pdf** — Zero Correlation Linear Cryptanalysis with Reduced Data Complexity (Bogdanov, Wang). Statistical technique cuts data needs of zero-correlation attacks; record key recoveries on 21-round TEA and 25-round XTEA.
- **75490049.pdf** — A Model for Structure Attacks, with Applications to PRESENT and Serpent (Wang, Sun, Tischhauser, Preneel). General complexity model for differential structure attacks, relaxing prior conditions on input-difference sets.
- **75490069.pdf** — A Methodology for Differential-Linear Cryptanalysis (Jiqiang Lu). New differential-linear framework without Biham et al.'s extra assumption; applied to CTC2, 13-round DES, and others.
- **75490090.pdf** — New Observations on Impossible Differential Cryptanalysis of Reduced-Round Camellia (Liu, Li, Gu, Wang, Liu, Chen, Li). Improved impossible-differential attacks on Camellia via new 7-round distinguishers covering 3/4 of the key space.
- **75490110.pdf** — Improved Rebound Attack on the Finalist Grøstl (Jean, Naya-Plasencia, Peyrin). Rebound distinguishers reaching 9 and 10 rounds on Grøstl-256/512 permutations using a three-active-state solving algorithm.
- **75490127.pdf** — (Pseudo) Preimage Attack on Round-Reduced Grøstl Hash Function and Others (Wu, Feng, Wu, Guo, Dong, Zou). First pseudo-preimage attacks on 5-round Grøstl-256 and 8-round Grøstl-512 via meet-in-the-middle; improves Whirlpool/AES-hash results.
