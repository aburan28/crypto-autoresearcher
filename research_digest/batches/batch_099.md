# Batch 099 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by 2012-era symmetric cryptanalysis: hash-function attacks (Keccak/SHA-3 finalists, SHA-2, RIPEMD-128, Whirlpool, ARMADILLO2), biclique/rebound techniques, and ARX differential analysis; mostly FSE 2012 and ASIACRYPT 2012 papers.
- Strong block-cipher/modes thread: ciphertext stealing, McOE online AE, GCM weak keys, iterated Even-Mansour bounds, LED, PRINCE, ZUC, Salsa20, HMAC related-key attacks.
- Second cluster of public-key theory: pairings (structure-preserving and dual form signatures, ηT pairing DLP record over GF(3^97)), lattices (NTRUSign cryptanalysis, Gaussian sampling), LPN-based schemes, leakage/RKA security.
- Most ECDLP-relevant items: Petit–Quisquater on Weil-descent polynomial systems for binary-curve ECDLP (subexponential heuristics), Hayashi et al. breaking ηT-pairing fields via FFS, and Bos–Kleinjung ECM factorization records.
- Anomalies: 76580001 (Boneh pairings) and 76580002 (Zong lattices) are 1-page invited-talk abstracts, not full papers. No duplicates or non-paper files found.

## Papers
- **75490147.pdf** — Practical Cryptanalysis of ARMADILLO2 (Naya-Plasencia, Peyrin). Practical distinguishers, free-start and semi-free-start collision attacks plus related-key recovery on the ARMADILLO2 primitive via weak diffusion and local-linearization.
- **75490184.pdf** — The Security of Ciphertext Stealing (Rogaway, Wooding, Zhang). Proves ind$-security of CBC with all NIST-recommended ciphertext-stealing variants, formalizes online encryption, and shows the 1982 Meyer–Matyas variant is insecure.
- **75490200.pdf** — McOE: A Family of Almost Foolproof On-Line Authenticated Encryption Schemes (Fleischmann, Forler, Lucks). Introduces McOE, an online AE design (member McOE-X) secure against nonce-reusing adversaries while remaining efficient and blockcipher-based.
- **75490220.pdf** — GCM, GHASH and Weak Keys (Saarinen). Shows GHASH has broad classes of weak keys in its 512 multiplicative subgroups beyond the trivial H=0 case, with experiments on AES-GCM.
- **75490228.pdf** — Collision Attacks on the Reduced Dual-Stream Hash Function RIPEMD-128 (Mendel, Nad, Schläffer). Practical collision attack on 38 of 64 steps, near-collisions for 44 steps, and non-randomness to 48 steps using automatic search for nonlinear differential characteristics.
- **75490248.pdf** — Bicliques for Preimages: Attacks on Skein-512 and the SHA-2 family (Khovratovich, Rechberger, Savelieva). Introduces bicliques as a preimage-attack tool, giving first cryptanalysis of round-reduced Skein and improved-round attacks on SHA-2.
- **75490268.pdf** — Converting Meet-in-the-Middle Preimage Attack into Pseudo Collision Attack: Application to SHA-2 (Li, Isobe, Shibutani). Converts partial-target preimage attacks into best-known pseudo-collisions on 43-step SHA-256 and 46-step SHA-512, extensible to Skein and BLAKE.
- **75490290.pdf** — UNAF: A Special Set of Additive Differences with Application to the Differential Analysis of ARX (Velichkov, Mouha, De Cannière, Preneel). Introduces UNAF differences for accurate multi-round differential probabilities in ARX designs, yielding a key-recovery attack on 5-round Salsa20.
- **75490310.pdf** — ElimLin Algorithm Revisited (Courtois, Sepehrdad, Sušil, Vaudenay). Characterizes ElimLin algebraically (order- and change-invariant), studies its limits, and applies it to LBlock, MIBS, and the CTC2 toy cipher.
- **75490330.pdf** — Short-output universal hash functions and their use in fast and secure data authentication (Nguyen, Roscoe). Proposes digest(), a short-output universal hash with near-optimal collision probability for fast MACs and manual authentication protocols.
- **75490350.pdf** — Lapin: An Efficient Authentication Protocol Based on Ring-LPN (Heyse, Kiltz, Lyubashevsky, Paar, Pietrzak). Two-round active-secure authentication from Ring-LPN, much more efficient than HB variants and comparable to AES for constrained devices.
- **75490370.pdf** — Higher-Order Masking Schemes for S-Boxes (Carlet, Goubin, Prouff, Quisquater, Rivain). First generic software masking schemes protecting any s-box at arbitrary order against side-channel attacks, with a new s-box efficiency criterion.
- **75490390.pdf** — Recursive Diffusion Layers for Block Ciphers and Hash Functions (Sajadieh, Dakhilalian, Mala, Sepehrdad). Constructs MDS-quality diffusion layers from Feistel-like linear rounds using only XORs and rotations, applicable to MMB and Hierocrypt.
- **75490407.pdf** — Unaligned Rebound Attack: Application to Keccak (Duc, Guo, Peyrin, Wei). Derives best differential paths for up to 5 Keccak rounds and builds distinguishers up to 8 rounds via an unaligned rebound attack.
- **75490427.pdf** — Differential propagation analysis of Keccak (Daemen, Van Assche). Designers' computer-aided trail search proving any 6-round differential trail in Keccak-f[1600] has weight at least 74 (82 in worst case).
- **75490447.pdf** — New attacks on Keccak-224 and Keccak-256 (Dinur, Dunkelman, Shamir). Practical collisions for 4-round Keccak-224/256 in minutes and near-collisions for 5 rounds, combining differential and algebraic techniques.
- **76580001.pdf** — Pairing-based Cryptography: Past, Present, and Future (Boneh). 1-page invited-talk abstract surveying pairing applications (IBE, functional encryption, short signatures) and open problems beyond bilinear maps.
- **76580002.pdf** — Some Mathematical Mysteries in Lattices (Zong). 1-page talk abstract on classical lattice mathematics (packing density, shortest vectors) and its relevance to cryptography; not a research paper.
- **76580003.pdf** — Constant-Size Structure-Preserving Signatures: Generic Constructions and Simple Assumptions (Abe, Chase, David, Kohlweiss, Nishimaki, Ohkubo). Frameworks refining Even–Goldreich–Micali yielding constant-size structure-preserving signatures (11–17 group elements) from simple non-q-type assumptions like DLIN.
- **76580021.pdf** — Dual Form Signatures: An Approach for Proving Security from Static Assumptions (Gerbush, Lewko, O'Neill, Waters). Dual Form Signature framework proving Camenisch–Lysyanskaya and Boldyreva et al. aggregate-signature variants secure under static rather than interactive assumptions.
- **76580039.pdf** — Breaking pairing-based cryptosystems using ηT pairing over GF(3^97) (Hayashi, Shimoyama, Shinohara, Takagi). Solves the 923-bit DLP in GF(3^(6·97)) via an improved medium-prime function field sieve in ~148 days on 252 cores, breaking ηT-pairing cryptosystems.
- **76580057.pdf** — On the (Im)possibility of Projecting Property in Prime-Order Setting (Seo). Proves lower bounds on image size and generic-operation cost of projecting bilinear pairings in prime-order groups from the k-linear assumption; asymmetric bounds are tight.
- **76580075.pdf** — Optimal Reductions of Some Decisional Problems to the Rank Problem (Villar). Gives a logarithmic-loss (optimal) reduction from the Rank Problem to DDH, improving tightness of matrix-based primitives like lossy trapdoor functions.
- **76580093.pdf** — Signature Schemes Secure against Hard-to-Invert Leakage (Faust, Hazay, Nielsen, Nordholt, Zottarel). First digital signature schemes secure in the auxiliary-input model, tolerating exponentially hard-to-invert leakage of the secret key, plus auxiliary-input identification.
- **76580111.pdf** — Completeness for Symmetric Two-Party Functionalities - Revisited (Lindell, Omri, Zarosim). Shows round-based (non-atomic) reductions change the trivial/complete characterization: some symmetric functionalities are neither under ideal-box reductions yet complete with constant-round protocols.
- **76580129.pdf** — Adaptively Secure Garbling with Applications to One-Time Programs and Secure Outsourcing (Bellare, Hoang, Rogaway). Defines adaptive security notions for garbling schemes and gives static-to-adaptive transforms, fixing gaps in one-time-program and outsourcing proofs.
- **76580149.pdf** — The Generalized Randomized Iterate and its Application to New Efficient Constructions of UOWHFs from Regular One-Way Functions (Ames, Gennaro, Venkitasubramaniam). Builds UOWHFs from regular one-way functions of unknown regularity with O(n log n) keys via reusable iterates and Shoup domain extension.
- **76580167.pdf** — Perfect Algebraic Immune Functions (Liu, Zhang, Lin). Characterizes Boolean functions with perfect immunity to (fast) algebraic attacks: variable counts must be (one more than) a power of two; shows Carlet–Feng constructions achieve it.
- **76580185.pdf** — Differential Analysis of the LED Block Cipher (Mendel, Rijmen, Toz, Varıcı). Attacks 12/16 rounds of LED-64 and 16/24 of LED-128 by extending Even–Mansour attacks and plateau characteristics to LED's step structure.
- **76580203.pdf** — PRINCE – A Low-latency Block Cipher for Pervasive Computing Applications (Borghoff et al.). Hardware-oriented cipher encrypting in one clock cycle with negligible decryption overhead, exploiting fully-unrolled non-iterative design choices.
- **76580221.pdf** — Analysis of Differential Attacks in ARX Constructions (Leurent). Introduces multi-bit and quartet constraints for more precise ARX differential/boomerang analysis, showing several published attacks use unsatisfiable characteristics.
- **76580239.pdf** — Integral and Multidimensional Linear Distinguishers with Correlation Zero (Bogdanov, Leander, Nyberg, Wang). Proves integrals imply zero-correlation approximations and that zero-correlation is a special multidimensional linear case; attacks Skipjack variant and reduced CAST-256.
- **76580257.pdf** — Differential Attacks Against Stream Cipher ZUC (Wu, Huang, Nguyen, Wang, Ling). Exploits non-injective initialization of ZUC 1.4 to obtain identical keystreams and key recovery (2^67–2^99.4), with a suggested injective fix; relevant to 3GPP 128-EEA3/EIA3.
- **76580273.pdf** — An Asymptotically Tight Security Analysis of the Iterated Even-Mansour Cipher (Lampe, Patarin, Seurin). Proves optimal O(N^{t/(t+1)}) non-adaptive and O(N^{t/(t+2)}) adaptive query bounds for t-round key-alternating ciphers via coupling arguments.
- **76580291.pdf** — 3kf9: Enhancing 3GPP-MAC beyond the Birthday Bound (Zhang, Wu, Sui, Wang). Combines f9 (3GPP-MAC) with EMAC into a CBC-based MAC provably PRF-secure beyond the birthday bound with three independent keys, suited to lightweight ciphers.
- **76580327.pdf** — RKA Security beyond the Linear Barrier: IBE, Encryption and Signatures (Bellare, Paterson, Thomson). Framework for RKA-secure IBE under affine and bounded-degree polynomial key derivation, yielding first such CCA PKE, symmetric encryption, and signatures in the standard model.
- **76580345.pdf** — Fully Secure Unbounded Inner-Product and Attribute-Based Encryption (Okamoto, Takashima). First unbounded, fully (adaptively) secure and attribute-hiding IPE and ABE schemes under DLIN, using indexing and consistent randomness amplification on dual pairing vector spaces.
- **76580363.pdf** — Computing on Authenticated Data: New Privacy Definitions and Constructions (Attrapadung, Libert, Peters). Refines privacy notions for homomorphic signatures (complete context hiding), shows relations among definitions, and gives constructions for linear and subset predicates.
- **76580381.pdf** — A Coding-Theoretic Approach to Recovering Noisy RSA Keys (Paterson, Polychroniadou, Sibborn). Recasts noisy RSA key recovery (cold boot) in coding-theoretic terms, explaining prior limits and giving a new algorithm that solves the motivating problem.
- **76580399.pdf** — Certifying RSA (Kakvi, Kiltz, May). Algorithm using Coppersmith's method that certifies whether RSA_{N,e} is a permutation for prime e ≥ N^{1/4+ε} without knowing the factorization, in time O(ε^-8 log²N).
- **76580410.pdf** — Faster Gaussian Lattice Sampling using Lazy Floating-Point Arithmetic (Ducas, Nguyen). Shows naive floating-point gives no asymptotic gain but a laziness technique speeds lattice Gaussian sampling to Õ(n²) or Õ(n) in cases like NTRUSign, mostly in double precision.
- **76580428.pdf** — Learning a Zonotope and More: Cryptanalysis of NTRUSign Countermeasures (Ducas, Nguyen). Revisits the Nguyen–Regev attack to show it learns zonotopes/deformed parallelepipeds, practically breaking NTRUSign perturbation and deformation countermeasures (8,000 signatures for NTRUSign-251).
- **76580446.pdf** — On Polynomial Systems Arising from a Weil Descent (Petit, Quisquater). Heuristic evidence that Weil-descent systems have low regularity, implying Diem's index-calculus solves binary-curve ECDLP in subexponential ~O(2^{c n^{2/3} log n}), threatening only n > ~2000.
- **76580464.pdf** — ECM at Work (Bos, Kleinjung). Optimizes Edwards-curve ECM with addition–subtraction chains, cutting memory up to 55× and setting a GTX 580 GPU throughput record for integer factorization cofactorization.
- **76580482.pdf** — IND-CCA secure Cryptography based on a variant of the LPN Problem (Döttling, Müller-Quade, Nascimento). First IND-CCA2 public-key encryption from Alekhnovich's LPN variant in the standard model via all-but-one simulation, a post-quantum candidate.
- **76580500.pdf** — Provable Security of the Knudsen-Preneel Compression Functions (Lee). Proves collision resistance of code-based Knudsen–Preneel wide-pipe compression functions up to bounds matching known attacks for MDS codes, plus tighter preimage proofs.
- **76580519.pdf** — Optimal Collision Security in Double Block Length Hashing with Single Length Key (Mennink). Shows 2-call single-key double-block-length compression functions admit 2^{n/2} collision attacks, and gives first 3-call construction with optimal collision and preimage security.
- **76580537.pdf** — Bicliques for permutations: collision and preimage attacks in stronger settings (Khovratovich). Extends bicliques to keyless permutations (sliced bicliques), giving first collision attacks on 11-round reduced Skein and preimage attacks on reduced Grøstl output transformation.
- **76580555.pdf** — Investigating Fundamental Security Requirements on Whirlpool: Improved Preimage and Collision Attacks (Sasaki, Wang, Wu, Wu). Improves Whirlpool cryptanalysis: 6-round hash preimage at 2^481 and 8-round compression-function collision at 2^120 via meet-in-the-middle and rebound techniques.
- **76580573.pdf** — Generic Related-key Attacks for HMAC (Peyrin, Sasaki, Wang). Generic single-related-key distinguishing and forgery attacks on HMAC with ~2^{n/2} queries, showing wide-pipe hashes don't help when key size equals block size, with a simple patch.
