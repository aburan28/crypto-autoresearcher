# Batch 053 digest

## Overview
- Two distinct strata: 44 recent (ePrint-style, ~2023–2024) papers on post-quantum/lattice cryptography, zero-knowledge proofs, signatures, and MPC, plus 6 older mid-2000s works on side-channel attacks, RFID, and zero-knowledge databases.
- Heavy concentration of lattice/post-quantum topics: LWE hardness/estimation, code-based signatures (ReSolveD, R-SDP), lattice MVC, Peregrine/HFERP cryptanalysis, isogeny group actions (SCALLOP-HD).
- Strong ZK/proof-systems cluster: lookup arguments (cq+/Locq), compressed sigma protocols, simulation-extractable KZG/HyperPlonk, vector commitments with range proofs.
- Notable cryptanalysis/backdoor papers: Micali-Schnorr PRG backdoor analysis, Peregrine break, HFERP attack, parallel-ROS attack on isogeny/lattice blind signatures.
- Anomalies: 15.pdf extract begins mid-paper (title not visible); 18.pdf is a 1-page position essay, not a full research paper; no obvious duplicates.

## Papers
- **14602046.pdf** — Threshold Structure-Preserving Signatures (Mitrokotsa, Mukherjee, Sedaghat, Slamanig, Tomy). Threshold SPS with strong, adaptive security under standard assumptions, removing random oracles and interactive assumptions of prior work.
- **14602048.pdf** — Lookup Arguments: Improvements, Extensions and Applications to Zero-Knowledge Decision Trees (Campanelli, Faonio, Fiore, Li, Lipmaa). Extends lookups to matrix and zero-knowledge variants (cq+, zkcq+, cq++), with ZK decision-tree application.
- **14602058.pdf** — On Structure-Preserving Cryptography and Lattices (Hofheinz, Hostáková, Langrehr, Ursu). First framework porting structure-preserving crypto (sets, encryption, signatures) from pairings to lattices, with a compatible ZK argument system.
- **14602060.pdf** — Quantum CCA-Secure PKE, Revisited (Alamati, Maram). Studies qCCA security for PKE, building it from key-dependent-message encryption and hash proof systems, including first LPN-based instantiation.
- **14602061.pdf** — Faster Amortized FHEW Bootstrapping Using Ring Automorphisms (De Micheli, Kim, Micciancio, Suhl). Practical amortized FHEW bootstrapping replacing Nussbaumer with NTT via ring automorphisms, cutting hidden constants and noise growth.
- **14602062.pdf** — On the Possibility of a Backdoor in the Micali-Schnorr Generator (Davis, Green, Heninger, Ryan, Suhl). Analyzes backdoor potential in ISO-standardized Micali-Schnorr/RSA PRGs; exhibits exploitable parameter families and insecure standard-permitted exponents.
- **14602064.pdf** — Updatable, Aggregatable, Succinct Mercurial Vector Commitment from Lattice (Wang, Yiu, Zhao, Jiang). First non-trivial lattice-based mercurial VC with updatability and aggregatability, built on the BASIS assumption, for ZK sets/databases.
- **14602065.pdf** — On Information-Theoretic Secure MPC with Local Repairability (Escudero, Tjuawinata, Xing). Constructs linear secret-sharing schemes that are simultaneously multiplicative and locally repairable, enabling MPC with party-state recovery in dynamic settings.
- **14602069.pdf** — Breaking Parallel ROS (Katsumata, Lai, Reichle). New polynomial-time attack on ℓ-concurrent unforgeability of parallel-repetition blind signatures, breaking isogeny-based CSI-Otter and lattice-based Blaze+/BlindOR.
- **14602075.pdf** — Selective Opening Security in the QROM, Revisited (Pan, Zeng). First correct QROM proofs of selective-opening CCA security for two Fujisaki-Okamoto variants, via adaptive QRO reprogramming; fixes a gap in prior work.
- **14602079.pdf** — Formalizing Hash-then-Sign Signatures (Poettering, Rastikian). Initiates provable-security treatment of Hash-then-Sign schemes, capturing attacks where hashing and signing are separated (e.g., smartcard signing of arbitrary hashes).
- **14602090.pdf** — Witness Encryption for Succinct Functional Commitments (Campanelli, Fiore, Khoshakhlagh). Introduces WE-FC, a pairing-based witness-encryption flavor where decryption is unlocked by a succinct functional-commitment opening.
- **14602096.pdf** — Parameter-Hiding Order-Revealing Encryption without Pairings (Peng, Chen, Wang, He, Huang). Efficient parameter-hiding ORE built from identification schemes, eliminating bilinear maps from ciphertext comparison.
- **14602098.pdf** — ReSolveD: Shorter Signatures from Regular Syndrome Decoding and VOLE-in-the-Head (Cui, Liu, Yan, Yang, Yu, Zhang). Code-based post-quantum signature from the RSD assumption via VOLE-in-the-head; 3.99 KB signatures at 128-bit security.
- **14602099.pdf** — Efficient KZG-based Univariate Sum-check and Lookup Argument (Zhang, Sun, Gu). Losum sum-check with one-MSM proving, plus Locq lookup argument with smaller proofs and fewer pairings than cq/cq+/cq++.
- **14602103.pdf** — Vector Commitments With Proofs of Smallness (Libert). VCs with constant-size proofs that committed vectors are small, yielding shortest-known 3-group-element range proofs and short proofs for ring-LWE statements.
- **14602110.pdf** — Ring/Module LWE under Linear Leakage (Wang, Lai, Liu). Reduces standard search MLWE to decision MLWE with linear leakage, validating hardness assumptions behind efficient lattice ZK proofs, with refined tradeoffs.
- **14602112.pdf** — Tagged Chameleon Hash from Lattices (Li, Liu). Introduces tagged chameleon hashes with post-quantum full collision resistance in the standard model, targeting redactable blockchains without random oracles.
- **14602119.pdf** — Zero Knowledge Protocols and Signatures from the Restricted Syndrome Decoding Problem (Baldi, Bitzer, Pavoni, Santini, Wachter-Zeh, Weger). Adapts SDP-based ZK protocols to R-SDP for much lower communication; introduces compact R-SDP(G) variant.
- **14602120.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the Peregrine Lattice-Based Signature Scheme (Lin, Suzuki, Zhang, Espitau, Yu, Tibouchi, Abe). Breaks Peregrine (Korean PQC competition candidate) by exploiting information leakage from its centered-binomial sampler replacing Falcon's Gaussian sampler.
- **14602122.pdf** — Improved Cryptanalysis of HFERP (Cartor, Cartor, Furue, Smith-Tone). New attack on the multivariate HFERP encryption scheme combining Simple-Attack insights and support-minors/MinRank techniques, pushing parameters below claimed security.
- **14602123.pdf** — Succinct Verification of Compressed Sigma Protocols in the Updatable SRS Setting (Dutta, Ganesh, Jawalkar). Compressed sigma protocols with logarithmic verifier, yielding circuit ZK with O(log n) proof and verification in the updatable-SRS setting.
- **14602125.pdf** — More Efficient Public-Key Cryptography with Leakage and Tamper Resilience (Han, Liu, Gu). Formalizes strong leakage-and-tamper-resilient signatures; gives direct MDDH-based signature and CCA-PKE constructions avoiding heavy NIZK machinery.
- **14602128.pdf** — On Sigma-Protocols and (packed) Black-Box Secret Sharing Schemes (Bartoli, Cascudo). Universal sigma protocols for group-homomorphism preimages over arbitrary finite abelian groups, built from a new packed black-box secret sharing scheme.
- **14602133.pdf** — On Proving Equivalence Class Signatures Secure from Non-interactive Assumptions (Bauer, Fuchsbauer, Regen). Negative result: original-security EQS cannot be proven secure under standard/non-interactive assumptions with standard techniques.
- **14602134.pdf** — SCALLOP-HD: Group Action from 2-Dimensional Isogenies (Chen, Leroux, Panny). Reimplements the SCALLOP class-group action using dimension-2 isogenies via Kani's lemma, simplifying and potentially speeding up the framework.
- **14602137.pdf** — Fully Dynamic Attribute-Based Signatures for Circuits from Codes (Ling, Nguyen, Phan, Tang, Wang, Xu). First code-based ABS, supporting circuits and full dynamicity (simultaneous enroll/revoke), with shorter signatures than lattice counterparts.
- **14602140.pdf** — Laconic Branching Programs from the Diffie-Hellman Assumption (Garg, Hajiabadi, Miao, Murphy). Two-round 2PC where communication depends only on the short input and program depth, extending laconic OT/PSI to branching programs from DH.
- **14602141.pdf** — SoK: Public Key Encryption with Openings (Brunetta, Heum, Stam). Systematizes PKE security under key/randomness compromise (multi-user, SOA, NCE) into four philosophies, mapping a hierarchy from IND-CPA to NCE.
- **14602148.pdf** — Dynamic Collusion Functional Encryption and Multi-Authority ABE (Garg, Goyal, Lu). Generic compiler upgrading bounded-collusion FE from static to dynamic collusion bounds, with applications to multi-authority ABE.
- **14602151.pdf** — A Refined Hardness Estimation of LWE in Two-step Mode (Xia, Wang, Wang, Gu, Wang). Shows two-step (lattice reduction + enumeration/sieving) LWE solving beats BKZ-only; provides refined estimator affecting security-level estimates.
- **14602152.pdf** — Oblivious Accumulators (Baldimtsi, Karantaidou, Raghuraman). Defines accumulators hiding elements and set size from all parties (element hiding, add-delete indistinguishability), with generic construction from key-value commitments.
- **14602155.pdf** — A Simpler and More Efficient Reduction of DLog to CDH for Abelian Group Actions (Galbraith, Lai, Montgomery). Tighter, fully black-box reduction needing ~1/ε⁴ CDH-oracle calls versus ~1/ε²¹, relevant to isogeny-based crypto.
- **14602159.pdf** — Registered Attribute-Based Signature (Zhang, Zhao, Zhu, Gong, Chen). Defines registered ABS (transparent key curator, user-generated keys) and gives first fully secure constructions from k-Lin in the standard model.
- **14602162.pdf** — Compact Selective Opening Security From LWE (Hofheinz, Hostáková, Kastner, Klein, Ünal). First SO-CCA-secure PKE with constant ciphertext expansion from a standard assumption, via new compact lossy trapdoor functions from LWE.
- **14602165.pdf** — Short Code-based One-out-of-Many Proofs and Applications (Liu, Wang). Succinct code-based one-out-of-many proofs extending Stern/Veron frameworks, yielding logarithmic-size ring signatures and the most compact code-based group signature (~144 KB).
- **14602168.pdf** — Rate-1 Fully Local Somewhere Extractable Hashing from DDH (Branco, Döttling, Srinivasan, Zanotto). First rate-1 fully local somewhere-extractable hash from DDH, matching the prior LWE-based construction of Devadas et al.
- **14602169.pdf** — On Algebraic Embedding for Unstructured Lattices (Bolboceanu, Brakerski, Sharma). Establishes a hardness gradient for Order-LWE, clarifying relations between unstructured-lattice problems and structured (ideal/module) variants.
- **14602170.pdf** — On Instantiating Unleveled Fully-Homomorphic Signatures from Falsifiable Assumptions (Gay, Ursu). First unleveled fully homomorphic signature scheme in the standard model from falsifiable assumptions (iO, FHE, NIZK), with unbounded context-hiding.
- **14602173.pdf** — Simulation-Extractable KZG Polynomial Commitments and Applications to HyperPlonk (Libert). Simulation-extractable multivariate KZG commitments enabling an SE-secure HyperPlonk instantiation with straight-line extraction in AGM+ROM.
- **14602179.pdf** — Private Set Operations from Multi-Query Reverse Private Membership Test (Chen, Zhang, Zhang, Dong, Liu). Framework for private set intersection/union built on mqRPMT from new cwPRF/pOPRF notions under DDH-like assumptions, with practical implementations.
- **14602181.pdf** — Updatable Policy-Compliant Signatures (Badertscher, Maitra, Matt, Waldner). Extends policy-compliant signatures so policies can be updated without redistributing all keys, preserving attribute privacy.
- **14602182.pdf** — Network-Agnostic Multi-Party Computation Revisited (Bhimrajka, Choudhury, Varadarajan). Network-agnostic MPC over secret-shared data from plain PKI, avoiding ZK proofs; includes first network-agnostic VSS with optimal thresholds.
- **14602186.pdf** — Multi-Hop Fine-Grained Proxy Re-Encryption (Zhou, Liu, Han). Formalizes multi-hop fine-grained PRE with function-associated re-encryption keys, giving CPA- and HRA-secure schemes.
- **15.pdf** — High-resolution waveform matching for DPA using Phase-Only Correlation (?) (authors not visible). Proposes POC-based alignment to correct displacement errors in power waveforms for side-channel analysis. Extract starts mid-paper; title/authors uncertain.
- **154.pdf** — Parallel Multi-Party Computation from Linear Multi-Secret Sharing Schemes (Zhang, Liu, Xiao). Defines secure parallel MPC computing multiple functions against multiple adversary structures, built generically from linear multi-secret sharing schemes.
- **16.pdf** — Cache-Collision Timing Attacks Against AES (Bonneau, Mironov). Cache-collision timing attacks on table-driven AES; recovers a full 128-bit key with ~2¹³ samples, four orders of magnitude better than prior attacks.
- **17.pdf** — Provably Secure S-Box Implementation Based on Fourier Transform (Prouff, Giraud, Aumonier). Fourier-transform-based masking countermeasure for S-Boxes with proven DPA resistance, applied efficiently to FOX and AES.
- **172.pdf** — Updatable Zero-Knowledge Databases (Liskov). Defines transparently updatable zero-knowledge databases and updatable commitments, with a practical construction from VRFs and mercurial commitments.
- **18.pdf** — The Outer Limits of RFID Security (Juels). One-page position essay arguing RFID security/privacy extends beyond protocol design to side channels, covert channels, and physical-layer issues; not a full research paper.
