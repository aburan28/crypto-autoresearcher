# Batch 107 digest

## Overview
- Era/venues: almost entirely FSE 2013 (84240xxx series) and EUROCRYPT 2014 (84410xxx series) conference papers, plus a few likely CRYPTO-era 2014 items (85400xxx).
- Two strong thematic clusters: (1) symmetric cryptanalysis and design — stream ciphers (Trivium, Grain v1, Hummingbird-2, GMR-2), lightweight block ciphers (KLEIN, LED, Zorro), AES meet-in-the-middle and biclique attacks, and authenticated-encryption design/theory (ALE, Minematsu's AE mode, Namprempre–Rogaway–Shrimpton composition); (2) theory of cryptography — obfuscation (Barak et al., Hohenberger–Sahai–Waters), garbled RAM/circuits, functional and attribute-based encryption, non-malleable codes, UC commitments.
- Notable for ECDLP relevance: Barbulescu–Gaudry–Joux–Thomé quasi-polynomial DLP in small-characteristic fields; Faugère–Huot–Joux–Renault–Vitse on symmetrized summation polynomials for EC index calculus; Costello–Hisil–Smith fast constant-time ECDH on the x-line.
- Side-channel/leakage cluster: Coron higher-order masking of S-box tables, Duc–Dziembowski–Faust unifying probing and noisy leakage, Durvaux–Standaert–Veyrat-Charvillon on certifying chip leakage.
- Anomaly: 84410115.pdf is a garbage/hex-escaped extraction (likely a CJK-encoded PDF whose text layer failed) — title and content unreadable.
- No duplicates detected among the 50 files.

## Papers
- **84240416.pdf** — ALE: AES-Based Lightweight Authenticated Encryption (Bogdanov, Mendel, Regazzoni, Rijmen, Tischhauser). Proposes ALE, an online single-pass AE built on AES rounds, with 2.5 kGE ASIC area and 1.19 cpb AES-NI throughput.
- **84240436.pdf** — Related-key Attacks Against Full Hummingbird-2 (Markku-Juhani O. Saarinen). First sub-exhaustive (2^64) chosen-IV key-recovery attack on the full Hummingbird-2 AE cipher using related keys, plus a proposed design fix.
- **84240451.pdf** — A Low Data Complexity Attack on the GMR-2 Cipher Used in Satellite Phones (Li, Li, Li, Sun). Dynamic guess-and-determine attack recovering the 64-bit GMR-2 satellite-phone session key with very low data.
- **84240470.pdf** — Improving Key Recovery to 784 and 799 rounds of Trivium using Optimized Cube Attacks (Fouque, Vannet). Extends cube attacks on Trivium via Moebius transforms and quadratic superpolys, reaching 784 rounds (2^39) and 799 rounds.
- **84240483.pdf** — Near Collision Attack on the Grain v1 Stream Cipher (Zhang, Li, Feng, Lin). Proposes a near-collision key-recovery attack on Grain v1 exploiting internal-state near collisions, extrapolated to ~2^71.4 ticks.
- **84240504.pdf** — Exhausting Demirci-Selçuk Meet-in-the-Middle Attacks against Reduced-Round AES (Derbez, Fouque). Automates exhaustive search of Demirci–Selçuk MITM attacks on AES, yielding improved attacks on 8-round AES-192/256.
- **84240524.pdf** — A Framework for Automated Independent-Biclique Cryptanalysis (Abed, Forler, List, Lucks, Wenzel). Introduces the Janus Java framework that finds optimal bicliques and gives first full-round biclique attacks on BKSQ.
- **84240544.pdf** — A new criterion for avoiding the propagation of linear relations through an Sbox (Boura, Canteaut). Defines (v,w)-linearity of S-boxes to quantify affine-relation propagation; slightly improves Fuhr's attack on Hamsi.
- **84410115.pdf** — Not usable: the extract is hex-escaped glyph codes (probably a CJK-font PDF with broken text extraction); title and content unreadable.
- **84410117.pdf** — A Bound For Multiparty Secret Key Agreement (Tyagi, Watanabe). Single-shot, distribution-free upper bound on multiparty secret key length, linking key agreement to binary hypothesis testing.
- **84410118.pdf** — Replacing a Random Oracle: Full Domain Hash From Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Hohenberger, Sahai, Waters). Instantiates full-domain-hash random oracles with concrete iO-based hash functions, evading Dodis et al. impossibility results.
- **84410124.pdf** — A Full Characterization of Completeness for Two-party Randomized Function Evaluation (Kraschewski, Maji, Prabhakaran, Sahai). Settles which finite randomized 2-party functions are complete for actively-secure 2PC via a polynomial-time test.
- **84410126.pdf** — Higher Order Masking of Look-up Tables (Jean-Sébastien Coron). New algorithm masking S-box tables at arbitrary order with O(n²) time and improved share bound n ≥ 2t+1 under probing security.
- **84410127.pdf** — Universally Composable Symbolic Analysis for Two-Party Protocols based on Homomorphic Encryption (Dahl, Damgård). Compiles two-party protocols using commitments/homomorphic encryption into symbolic processes with a UC computational-soundness theorem verifiable by ProVerif.
- **84410132.pdf** — A heuristic quasi-polynomial algorithm for discrete logarithm in finite fields of small characteristic (Barbulescu, Gaudry, Joux, Thomé). Landmark BJGT algorithm giving quasi-polynomial n^O(log n) DLP complexity in small-characteristic fields, surpassing Function Field Sieve.
- **84410134.pdf** — Generic Universal Forgery Attack on Iterative Hash-based MACs (Peyrin, Wang). First generic universal forgery on HMAC/NMAC via functional-graph analysis, showing resistance is below the long-believed 2^n.
- **84410135.pdf** — Key Derivation Without Entropy Waste (Dodis, Pietrzak, Wichs). Positive and negative results on beating the RT-bound entropy loss in KDFs, resolving the samplable-RT conjecture.
- **84410138.pdf** — How to Certify the Leakage of a Chip? (Durvaux, Standaert, Veyrat-Charvillon). Statistical methodology to quantify side-channel leakage and certify how close an evaluation is to a perfect leakage model.
- **84410150.pdf** — Links Between Truncated Differential and Multidimensional Linear Properties of Block Ciphers (Blondeau, Nyberg). Establishes the missing link between truncated differential and multidimensional linear attacks; shows saturation attacks equal truncated differentials, applied to PRESENT.
- **84410155.pdf** — The Locality of Searchable Symmetric Encryption (Cash, Tessaro). Proves storage-vs-locality lower bounds for SSE and gives an O(N log N) index with O(log N) reads per search.
- **84410158.pdf** — Symmetrized Summation Polynomials: Using Small Order Torsion Points to Speed up Elliptic Curve Index Calculus (Faugère, Huot, Joux, Renault, Vitse). Builds compact symmetrized summation polynomials via small torsion points, easing relation search for EC index calculus on small-degree extension fields.
- **84410161.pdf** — Why Proving HIBE Systems Secure is Difficult (Lewko, Waters). Shows simple black-box reductions for checkable HIBE/ABE inherently suffer exponential security degradation in hierarchy depth.
- **84410164.pdf** — Key-Versatile Signatures and Applications: RKA, KDM and Joint Enc/Sig (Bellare, Meiklejohn, Thomson). Introduces key-versatile signatures letting keys be reused across purposes, yielding RKA-secure and KDM-secure constructions.
- **84410165.pdf** — Unifying Leakage Models: from Probing Attacks to Noisy Leakage (Duc, Dziembowski, Faust). Reduces noisy-leakage security to probing security, fixing shortcomings of Prouff–Rivain without leak-free gates.
- **84410168.pdf** — Salvaging Indifferentiability in a Multi-stage Setting (Mittelbach). Defines "unsplittability" of games, salvaging the MRH indifferentiability composition theorem for HMAC/NMAC and Merkle-Damgård variants.
- **84410175.pdf** — Garbled RAM Revisited (Gentry, Halevi, Lu, Ostrovsky, Raykova, Wichs). Identifies a circular-security flaw in Lu–Ostrovsky garbled RAM and gives two constructions (one IBE-based) that remove it.
- **84410177.pdf** — Protecting Obfuscation Against Algebraic Attacks (Barak, Garg, Kalai, Paneth, Sahai). Simplifies the GGH+13 obfuscator and proves VBB security in the generic multilinear map model, ruling out algebraic attacks.
- **84410181.pdf** — Honey Encryption: Security Beyond the Brute-Force Bound (Juels, Ristenpart). Introduces honey encryption, which yields decoy plaintexts under wrong keys to protect low-entropy password encryption beyond brute-force bounds.
- **84410190.pdf** — Déjà Q: Using Dual Systems to Revisit q-Type Assumptions (Chase, Meiklejohn). Shows many q-type bilinear assumptions are implied by subgroup hiding via dual-system techniques, e.g. Dodis–Yampolskiy PRF with arbitrary domain.
- **84410201.pdf** — Efficient Round Optimal Blind Signatures (Garg, Gupta). First blind signature scheme that is round-optimal, standard-model, and efficient (6.5 KB signatures, ~100 KB communication, <1 KB amortized).
- **84410203.pdf** — Non-Malleability from Malleability: Simulation-Sound Quasi-Adaptive NIZK Proofs (Libert, Peters, Joye, Yung). Constant-size unbounded simulation-sound QA-NIZK proofs from homomorphic structure-preserving signatures, with CCA2-encryption applications.
- **84410207.pdf** — Efficient Non-Malleable Codes and Key-Derivation for Poly-Size Tampering Circuits (Faust, Mukherjee, Venturi, Wichs). Rate-approaching-1 information-theoretic non-malleable codes secure against any a-priori poly-size tampering family.
- **84410214.pdf** — Tight Security Bounds for Key-Alternating Ciphers (Chen, Steinberger). Proves the conjectured tight 2^{tn/(t+1)} indistinguishability bound for t-round iterated Even–Mansour ciphers.
- **84410219.pdf** — Identity-Based Encryption Secure Against Selective Opening Chosen-Ciphertext Attack (Lai, Deng, Liu, Weng, Zhao). Introduces extractable IBE with one-sided public openability and generically builds SO-CCA-secure IBE.
- **84410226.pdf** — Dual System Encryption via Doubly Selective Security (Attrapadung). Generic pair-encoding framework extending dual-system encryption; first fully secure FE for regular languages, unbounded large-universe ABE, and constant-size-ciphertext ABE.
- **84410239.pdf** — Parallelizable Rate-1 Authenticated Encryption from Pseudorandom Functions (Minematsu). OCB-like parallelizable, one-pass, rate-1 AE mode using only the forward blockcipher via a two-round tweakable Feistel idea.
- **84410241.pdf** — Polynomial Time Attack on Wild McEliece Over Quadratic Extensions (Couvreur, Otmani, Tillich). Polynomial-time structural filtration attack on wild-Goppa McEliece over quadratic extensions via code distinguishability.
- **84410245.pdf** — Distributed Point Functions and their Applications (Gilboa, Ishai). Constructs efficient DPFs from one-way functions, enabling polylogarithmic-query 2-server PIR and PIR-writing.
- **84410253.pdf** — Reconsidering Generic Composition (Namprempre, Rogaway, Shrimpton). Revisits AE generic composition under IV/nonce-based definitions, showing "only Encrypt-then-MAC works" is oversimplified and ISO 19772's EtM is wrong.
- **84410261.pdf** — GGHLite: More Efficient Multilinear Maps from Ideal Lattices (Langlois, Stehlé, Steinfeld). Improves GGH re-randomization via Rényi divergence, cutting public parameter size and randomizer count for ideal-lattice multilinear maps.
- **84410275.pdf** — Faster Compact Diffie–Hellman: Endomorphisms on the x-line (Costello, Hisil, Smith). Constant-time, twist-secure x-only ECDH at 128-bit security using endomorphism-driven 2D addition chains on Q-curves over Fp2, p=2^127−1.
- **84410276.pdf** — Non-Interactive Secure Computation Based on Cut-and-Choose (Afshar, Mohassel, Pinkas, Riva). Single-round 2PC protocol with reusable first message, matching best interactive cut-and-choose concrete efficiency.
- **84410291.pdf** — Sometimes-Recurse Shuffle: Almost-Random Permutations in Logarithmic Expected Time (Morris, Rogaway). Card-shuffle-based PRF-to-PRP conversion tolerating N queries with Θ(log N) expected random-function calls, for format-preserving encryption.
- **84410295.pdf** — On the Complexity of UC Commitments (Garay, Ishai, Kumaresan, Wee). Rate-1 computationally efficient UC commitments from few OT calls plus black-box PRG, under DDH and other assumptions.
- **84410298.pdf** — Fully Key-Homomorphic Encryption, Arithmetic Circuit ABE and Compact Garbled Circuits (Boneh et al.). First ABE with secret keys short in circuit depth, yielding the first reusable garbled circuits with additive-only size overhead.
- **84410299.pdf** — Multi-Input Functional Encryption (Goldwasser, Gordon, Goyal, Jain, Katz, Liu, Sahai, Shi, Zhou). Defines multi-input FE, links it to obfuscation notions, and builds indistinguishability-secure schemes from iO.
- **85400104.pdf** — Analysis of Impossible, Integral and Zero-Correlation Attacks on Type-II Generalized Feistel Networks using the Matrix Method (Blondeau, Minier). Uses a matrix round-function representation to relate key-recovery attacks behind impossible/integral/zero-correlation distinguishers on Type-II GFNs.
- **85400105.pdf** — On the Minimum Number of Multiplications Necessary for Universal Hash Functions (Nandi). Proves (d−1)+⌈n/2⌉ multiplications are necessary for d-block universal hashing and gives the matching-optimal EHC construction for d ≤ 4.
- **85400106.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of KLEIN (Lallemand, Naya-Plasencia). First attacks on all 12 rounds of KLEIN-64 via truncated differentials, with a practical 10-round full key-recovery implementation.
- **85400107.pdf** — Probabilistic Slide Cryptanalysis and Its Applications to LED-64 and Zorro (Soleimany). Probabilistic slide technique overcoming round constants on single-key Even–Mansour ciphers; recovers the key for 16 rounds of Zorro and best known-plaintext results on 2-step LED-64.
