# Batch 094 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ASIACRYPT 2011–era papers (the 7073xxxx/7194xxxx ID runs): a strong theory-heavy mix of provable-security constructions (functional encryption, lossy/deniable encryption, UC commitments, zero-knowledge) and cryptanalysis.
- Notable landmark results: Bogdanov–Khovratovich–Rechberger biclique cryptanalysis of full AES; Chen–Nguyen "BKZ 2.0" lattice security estimates; Agrawal–Freeman–Vaikuntanathan LWE functional encryption; Gaudry–Kohel–Smith genus-2 point counting with real multiplication.
- A substantial SHA-3-competition and hash cryptanalysis cluster (SHA-3 FPGA benchmarking, JH rebound, SHA-256 higher-order differential collisions, SHA-2 characteristic search, ARMADILLO2).
- Side-channel/leakage-resilience and physical-security themes recur (related-key/tampering, leakage-resilience limits, inner-product extractor, tamper-resilient hardware tokens, TLS tag-size attack).
- Only one ECC-adjacent paper: genus-2 curve point counting; the batch is otherwise symmetric-crypto, lattices, and protocol theory rather than ECDLP.
- Anomaly: 70730563.pdf is a 1-page fragment containing only figure labels (Pri-Expo/Bit-Expo variants), not an extractable paper.

## Papers
- **69170475.pdf** — Small Public Keys and Fast Verification for Multivariate Quadratic Public Key Systems (Petzoldt, Thomae, Bulygin, Wolf). Shrinks UOV-based MQ signature public keys ~88% and verification time ~59% by combining reduced and equivalent keys, argued not to harm security.
- **69170491.pdf** — Throughput vs. Area Trade-offs in High-Speed Architectures of Five Round 3 SHA-3 Candidates (Homsirikamol, Rogawski, Gaj). FPGA implementations of all SHA-3 finalists plus SHA-2 using folding/unrolling/pipelining, ranked by throughput-to-area ratio.
- **69170507.pdf** — Efficient Hashing using the AES Instruction Set (Bos, Özen, Stam). First comprehensive software benchmark of 256-bit blockcipher-based hash functions using AES-NI, covering double-block-length and RIJNDAEL-256 constructions.
- **70730001.pdf** — BKZ 2.0: Better Lattice Security Estimates (Chen, Nguyen). State-of-the-art BKZ implementation with pruning plus a simulation algorithm revising lattice security estimates, e.g. NTRUSign's claimed 93-bit set offers at most ~65 bits.
- **70730021.pdf** — Functional Encryption for Inner Product Predicates from Learning with Errors (Agrawal, Freeman, Vaikuntanathan). First lattice-based functional encryption beyond IBE, enabling range/subset queries and polynomial evaluation on encrypted data, weakly attribute-hiding.
- **70730041.pdf** — Random Oracles in a Quantum World (Boneh, Dagdelen, Fischlin, Lehmann, Schaffner, Zhandry). Separates classical from quantum-accessible random oracle models and gives history-free reduction conditions under which classical ROM proofs transfer to quantum adversaries.
- **70730070.pdf** — Lossy Encryption: Constructions from General Assumptions and Efficient Selective Opening CCA Security (Hemenway, Libert, Ostrovsky, Vergnaud). General lossy-encryption constructions from re-randomizable encryption, OT, and hash proof systems, yielding selective-opening-secure schemes from DDH/DCR/QR.
- **70730088.pdf** — Structure Preserving CCA Secure Encryption and Applications (Camenisch, Haralambiev, Kohlweiss, Lapon, Naessens). First structure-preserving (purely algebraic, hash-free) CCA-secure PKE scheme, with example joint-computation protocols.
- **70730106.pdf** — Decoding Random Linear Codes in Õ(2^0.054n) (May, Meurer, Thomae). New information-set decoding algorithm using the Howgrave-Graham–Joux representation technique, improving over Stern and ball-collision decoding.
- **70730125.pdf** — Lower and Upper Bounds for Deniable Public-Key Encryption (Bendlin, Nielsen, Nordholt, Orlandi). Proves impossibility of non-interactive receiver-/bi-deniable encryption with better than polynomial security, plus matching constructions.
- **70730143.pdf** — Bridging Broadcast Encryption and Group Key Agreement (Wu, Qin, Zhang, Domingo-Ferrer, Farràs). Introduces contributory broadcast encryption, merging GKA and BE so a negotiated group public key lets senders encrypt to arbitrary subsets.
- **70730161.pdf** — On the Joint Security of Encryption and Signature, Revisited (Paterson, Schuldt, Stam, Thomson). Standard-model constructions for jointly secure combined public-key schemes sharing one keypair for encryption and signature, including an IBE-based generic build.
- **70730179.pdf** — Polly Cracker, Revisited (Albrecht, Farshim, Faugère, Perret). Formalizes Polly-Cracker-style schemes via the ideal remainder problem; shows symmetric versions achieve only bounded CPA security, but noisy variants yield somewhat homomorphic encryption.
- **70730197.pdf** — Oblivious RAM with O((log N)^3) Worst-Case Cost (Shi, Chan, Stefanov, Li). Tree-based ORAM achieving poly-logarithmic worst-case (not just amortized) cost with constant client storage for outsourced storage privacy.
- **70730215.pdf** — Noiseless Database Privacy (Bhaskar, Bhowmick, Goyal, Laxman, Thakurta). Defines "noiseless privacy" giving exact query answers under distributional and auxiliary-information assumptions, with composition rules, as an alternative to differential privacy.
- **70730233.pdf** — The Preimage Security of Double-Block-Length Compression Functions (Armknecht, Fleischmann, Krause, Lee, Stam, Steinberger). Near-optimal preimage-resistance bounds (~2^{2n−5} to 2^{2n−10} queries) for Abreast-DM, Tandem-DM, and Hirose's scheme.
- **70730251.pdf** — Rebound Attack on JH42 (Naya-Plasencia, Toz, Varıcı). New 32-round differential path yielding semi-free-start near-collisions up to 37 rounds and the first distinguishers on the full 42-round JH internal permutation.
- **70730269.pdf** — Second-Order Differential Collisions for Reduced SHA-256 (Biryukov, Lamberger, Mendel, Nikolić). Practical second-order differential collision for SHA-256's compression function reduced to 47/64 steps, plus corrected rectangle attacks on SHACAL-2.
- **70730287.pdf** — Finding SHA-2 Characteristics: Searching Through a Minefield of Contradictions (Mendel, Nad, Schläffer). First automated tool for SHA-2 differential characteristics; practical semi-free-start collisions for 32 steps and a 27-step collision attack on SHA-256.
- **70730305.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of ARMADILLO2 (Abdelraheem, Blondeau, Naya-Plasencia, Videau, Zenner). Meet-in-the-middle parallel-matching attack inverting ARMADILLO2, enabling key recovery (FIL-MAC) and (second-)preimage attacks, experimentally validated.
- **70730322.pdf** — An Experimentally Verified Attack on Full Grain-128 Using Dedicated Reconfigurable Hardware (Dinur, Güneysu, Paar, Shamir, Zimmermann). First single-key attack on full Grain-128 (~2^38 faster than brute force) via an improved cube tester, verified on RIVYERA FPGA hardware.
- **70730339.pdf** — Biclique Cryptanalysis of the Full AES (Bogdanov, Khovratovich, Rechberger). First single-key recovery attacks on full AES-128/192/256 with complexities 2^126.1 / 2^189.7 / 2^254.4 using bicliques, no related keys needed.
- **70730368.pdf** — Tag Size Does Matter: Attacks and Proofs for the TLS Record Protocol (Paterson, Ristenpart, Shrimpton). New distinguishing attack on TLS with truncated MACs and variable padding; longer tags provably yield length-hiding AE security stronger than IND-CCA.
- **70730386.pdf** — Resettable Cryptography in Constant Rounds — the Case of Zero Knowledge (Deng, Feng, Goyal, Lin, Sahai, Yung). Constant-round protocols achieving resettable zero-knowledge in the bare public key model where prover and verifier may reuse fixed random tapes.
- **70730403.pdf** — Two Provers in Isolation (Crépeau, Salvail, Simard, Tapp). Shows the BGKW two-prover bit commitment breaks with entangled provers, and develops classical "isolation" conditions needed to guarantee binding.
- **70730421.pdf** — Efficient Zero-Knowledge Arguments from Two-Tiered Homomorphic Commitments (Groth). Practical sublinear-communication ZK arguments; application to range proofs communicating only O(N^{1/3}) group elements.
- **70730439.pdf** — A Framework for Practical Universally Composable Zero-Knowledge Protocols (Camenisch, Krenn, Shoup). A CKY-like specification language compiling discrete-log ZK proofs of knowledge into efficient UC-secure protocols.
- **70730457.pdf** — Non-Interactive and Re-Usable Universally Composable String Commitments with Adaptive Security (Fischlin, Libert, Manulis). First UC commitments (pairing-based) simultaneously non-interactive, string-based, CRS-reusable, and adaptively secure under erasures.
- **70730475.pdf** — Cryptography Secure Against Related-Key Attacks and Tampering (Bellare, Cash, Miller). Studies when RKA security transfers between primitives; achieves RKA security for fault-injection-style RKD classes via identity-collision-resistant PRGs.
- **70730493.pdf** — Counting Points on Genus 2 Curves with Real Multiplication (Gaudry, Kohel, Smith). Schoof-type point counting accelerated to Õ(log^5 q) for genus-2 curves with RM endomorphisms; computes a 256-bit prime-order Jacobian.
- **70730511.pdf** — On the Efficiency of Bit Commitment Reductions (Ranellucci, Tapp, Winkler, Wullschleger). Proves Ω(kn) lower bound for building n bit commitments from OT/noisy channels (constant rate impossible), circumvented via restricted UC commitments.
- **70730529.pdf** — Secure Communication in Multicast Graphs (Yang, Desmedt). Gives tight necessary and sufficient connectivity conditions for secure communication in multicast graphs under general adversary structures, resolving a decade-old open problem.
- **70730547.pdf** — Constant-Round Private Function Evaluation with Linear Complexity (Katz, Malka). First constant-round two-party PFE protocol with linear complexity avoiding universal circuits, even for semi-honest adversaries.
- **70730563.pdf** — Not a readable paper extract: 1-page fragment containing only figure labels ("Pri-Expo-BD … Pub-Expo"); title and content unrecoverable from the text.
- **70730581.pdf** — Computational Verifiable Secret Sharing Revisited (Backes, Kate, Patra). Shows homomorphic commitments are unnecessary for computational VSS, with new schemes having lower communication complexity in synchronous and asynchronous models.
- **70730601.pdf** — Natural Generalizations of Threshold Secret Sharing (Farràs, Padró, Xing, Yang). New families of access structures admitting ideal linear secret sharing, found systematically via integer polymatroids.
- **70730619.pdf** — Separating Short Structure-Preserving Signatures from Non-Interactive Assumptions (Abe, Groth, Ohkubo). Meta-reduction result: optimally short (3-group-element) structure-preserving signatures cannot be proven secure via algebraic reduction to non-interactive assumptions.
- **70730638.pdf** — Short Signatures From Weaker Assumptions (Hofheinz, Jager, Kiltz). (m,1)-programmable hash functions yielding standard-model signatures from plain (non-strong) RSA and q-Diffie-Hellman assumptions with shorter signatures.
- **70730658.pdf** — Practical Key-recovery For All Possible Parameters of SFLASH (Bouillaguet, Fouque, Macario-Rat). Practical key recovery against SFLASH across the full parameter range using pencils of matrices and quadratic forms.
- **70730677.pdf** — The Leakage-Resilience Limit of a Computational Problem is Equal to its Unpredictability Entropy (Aggarwal, Maurer). Proves the maximum tolerable adaptive leakage bits for a search problem equals its unpredictability entropy (negative log of best poly-time success probability).
- **70730694.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Cryptography From the Inner-Product Extractor (Dziembowski, Faust). Refreshable randomized encodings with additive homomorphism securing Okamoto ID and ElGamal-based encryption against continuous bounded leakage.
- **70730714.pdf** — Program Obfuscation with Leaky Hardware (Bitansky, Canetti, Goldwasser, Halevi, Kalai, Rothblum). Obfuscation using a small set of universal, reusable hardware devices that stays secure even if all devices leak short-output functions of their state.
- **70730732.pdf** — BiTR: Built-in Tamper Resilience (Choi, Kiayias, Malkin). Defines and constructs protocols whose UC security is unaffected by hardware-token tampering, including general UC computation tolerating affine tampering.
- **71940105.pdf** — On Efficient Zero-Knowledge PCPs (Ishai, Mahmoody, Sahai). Mostly negative results: under PH non-collapse, efficient ZK-PCPs for NP against arbitrary malicious verifiers do not exist for non-adaptive verifiers; implications for token-based ZK.
- **71940110.pdf** — Confidentiality and Integrity: A Constructive Perspective (Maurer, Rüedlinger, Tackmann). Re-evaluates game-based symmetric-encryption notions via constructive cryptography, finding some (INT-PTXT, INT-CTXT, IND-CCA) too weak or ill-fitting, favoring composable channel-based definitions.
- **71940112.pdf** — On the Public Indifferentiability and Correlation Intractability of the 6-Round Feistel Construction (Mandal, Patarin, Seurin). Six-round Feistel with random round functions is publicly indifferentiable from a random permutation, via a new "sequential indifferentiability" notion implying correlation intractability.
- **71940120.pdf** — Computing on Authenticated Data (Ahn, Boneh, Camenisch, Hohenberger, shelat, Waters). Unified framework of P-homomorphic ("slightly homomorphic") signatures letting third parties derive unlinkable signatures on related messages, unifying redactable/transitive/quotable signatures.
- **71940122.pdf** — Computational Extractors and Pseudorandomness (Dachman-Soled, Gennaro, Krawczyk, Malkin). Studies existence and efficiency of computational extractors, proving one-way functions are necessary and giving matching black-box efficiency bounds.
- **71940123.pdf** — On the Security of the "Free-XOR" Technique (Choi, Katz, Kumaresan, Zhou). Shows garbled-circuit free-XOR cannot be proven from correlation robustness alone; defines the required hash security notion (with circular security) and proves it sufficient.
- **71940124.pdf** — Lower Bounds in Differential Privacy (De). Sharpens and unifies noise lower bounds for differential privacy, separating counting queries from general low-sensitivity queries and (ε,0)- from (ε,δ)-privacy.
