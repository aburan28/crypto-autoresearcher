# Batch 031 digest

## Overview
- This batch is dominated by theoretical cryptography, apparently a single conference proceedings block (file IDs 12826044–12826226, ~30p each, style matches CRYPTO 2021): MPC, zero-knowledge, lattices, and quantum cryptography.
- Heavy quantum/post-quantum theme: concurrent quantum ZK, post-quantum constant-round ZK, quantum copy-protection, quantum secure computation, QROM security of HMAC, quantum collision attacks on SHA-2, impossibility of quantum VBB obfuscation.
- Strong MPC/secret-sharing cluster: arithmetic secret sharing over Z/p^lZ, broadcast-optimal two-round MPC, ATLAS honest-majority MPC, non-commutative-ring MPC, garbled circuits beating half-gates, black-box round complexity results.
- Notable applied/results papers: MuSig2 two-round Schnorr multi-signatures, KHAPE aPAKE, FIDO2 provable security analysis, linear cryptanalysis of FF3-1/FEA, cryptanalysis of full LowMC, key recovery on all HFE variants (GeMSS), improved MITM on ternary LWE keys.
- Anomaly: despite the corpus being described as mostly ECC/ECDLP/side-channel material, this batch contains essentially no elliptic-curve or ECDLP papers; closest DL-related items are MuSig2, k-out-of-n DL proofs, and DualRing ring signatures. No non-paper artifacts found.

## Papers
- **12826044.pdf** — Asymptotically-Good Arithmetic Secret Sharing over Z/p^lZ with Strong Multiplication (Cramer, Rambaud, Xing). Arithmetic secret sharing with strong multiplication over rings Z/p^lZ, enabling efficient BGW-style MPC with constant-size shares.
- **12826049.pdf** — Fine-grained Secure Attribute-based Encryption (Wang, Pan, Chen). First fine-grained ABE, adaptively secure under NC1 ≠ ⊕L/poly, built generically from predicate encodings.
- **12826061.pdf** — Subquadratic SNARGs in the Random Oracle Model (Chiesa, Yogev). First improvement to Micali's ROM SNARG in 25 years, achieving sub-quadratic argument size via strong PCP soundness notions.
- **12826067.pdf** — On the Concurrent Composition of Quantum Zero-Knowledge (Ananth, Chung, La Placa). Initiates concurrent QZK: bounded-concurrent QZK for NP and QMA, plus quantum proofs of knowledge from QLWE.
- **12826074.pdf** — Lattice Reduction with Approximate Enumeration Oracles (Albrecht, Bai, Li, Rowell). Systematic study of relaxed enumeration in BKZ yielding a faster polynomial-space lattice reduction algorithm with concrete performance data.
- **12826078.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Full LowMC and LowMC-M with Algebraic Techniques (Liu, Isobe, Meier). New algebraic key-recovery techniques breaking full LowMC instances, with negligible memory versus prior difference-enumeration attacks.
- **12826081.pdf** — Broadcast-Optimal Two Round MPC with an Honest Majority (Damgård, Magri, Ravi, Siniscalchi, Yakoubov). Settles which security guarantees two-round honest-majority MPC achieves depending on broadcast availability per round.
- **12826083.pdf** — Linear Cryptanalysis of FF3-1 and FEA (Beyne). Practical distinguishing and message-recovery attacks on the FF3-1 and FEA-1/2 format-preserving encryption standards via generalized linear cryptanalysis.
- **12826084.pdf** — Three Halves Make a Whole? Beating the Half-Gates Lower Bound for Garbled Circuits (Rosulek, Roy). Garbling scheme with free XOR and 1.5κ+5 bits per AND gate, bypassing the half-gates 2κ lower bound via "slicing and dicing".
- **12826091.pdf** — Subtractive Sets over Cyclotomic Rings (Albrecht, Lai). Introduces (s,t)-subtractive sets to build Schnorr-like lattice proofs of knowledge with small knowledge error, plus matching impossibility results.
- **12826097.pdf** — A Black-Box Approach to Post-Quantum Zero-Knowledge in Constant Rounds (Chia, Chung, Yamakawa). Constant-round statistical-soundness arguments for NP with black-box ε-zero-knowledge against quantum verifiers from collapsing hash functions.
- **12826100.pdf** — MuSig2: Simple Two-Round Schnorr Multi-Signatures (Nick, Ruffing, Seurin). First two-round multi-signature scheme secure under concurrent sessions that outputs ordinary Schnorr signatures with key aggregation.
- **12826102.pdf** — KHAPE: Asymmetric PAKE from Key-Hiding Key Exchange (Gu, Jarecki, Krawczyk). OPAQUE variant achieving aPAKE security without an OPRF, avoiding offline dictionary attacks on OPRF compromise, near-optimal exponentiation cost.
- **12826103.pdf** — Does Fiat-Shamir Require a Cryptographic Hash Function? (Chen, Lombardi, Ma, Quach). Shows sound Fiat–Shamir instantiations for signatures and NIZKs using non-cryptographic hash functions like sum-mod-p in some settings.
- **12826107.pdf** — Non-Interactive Secure Multiparty Computation for Symmetric Functions, Revisited (Eriguchi, Ohara, Yamada, Nuida). Improved communication complexity for NIMPC protocols computing abelian programs/symmetric functions.
- **12826111.pdf** — Puncturable Pseudorandom Sets and Private Information Retrieval with Near-Optimal Online Bandwidth and Time (Shi, Aqeel, Chandrasekaran, Maggs). Two-server preprocessing PIR with ~O(√n) online cost via puncturable pseudorandom sets.
- **12826117.pdf** — Broadcast Encryption with Size N^{1/3} and More from k-Lin (Wee). Pairing-based CP-ABE for degree-3 polynomials with compact parameters, yielding broadcast encryption breaking the √N barrier.
- **12826122.pdf** — On the Round Complexity of Secure Quantum Computation (Bartusek, Coladangelo, Khurana, Ma). First constant-round maliciously secure two-party and multi-party quantum computation protocols in the CRS model.
- **12826135.pdf** — Efficient Information-Theoretic Multi-Party Computation over Non-Commutative Rings (Escudero, Soria-Vazquez). First efficient unconditionally secure MPC black-box over non-commutative rings via generalized Shamir sharing.
- **12826136.pdf** — New Approaches for Quantum Copy-Protection (Aaronson, Liu, Liu, Zhandry, Zhang). Copy-protection for unlearnable programs relative to classical oracles, and copy-detection from watermarking plus quantum money.
- **12826137.pdf** — A Rational Protocol Treatment of 51% Attacks (Badertscher, Lu, Zikas). Rational protocol design analysis of 51% double-spend attacks on PoW chains, with utility fixes and protocol remedies that deter majority attackers.
- **12826142.pdf** — Provable Security Analysis of FIDO2 (Barbosa, Boldyreva, Chen, Warinschi). First provable security analysis of WebAuthn and CTAP2, identifying CTAP2 design flaws and proposing the stronger sPACA protocol.
- **12826145.pdf** — ATLAS: Efficient and Scalable MPC in the Honest Majority Setting (Goyal, Li, Ostrovsky, Polychroniadou, Song). 33% communication improvement over Damgård–Nielsen semi-honest MPC, halved round complexity, extended to malicious security.
- **12826148.pdf** — Witness Authenticating NIZKs and Applications (Feng, Tang). Introduces waNIZKs letting a witness holder identify proofs made with that witness, with entropic ZK and applications to group signatures and non-malleable hashing.
- **12826150.pdf** — Meet-in-the-Middle Attacks Revisited: Key-recovery, Collision, and Preimage Attacks (Dong, Hua, Sun, Li, Wang, Hu). Constraint-based framework extending automatic MITM preimage attacks to key-recovery and collision settings.
- **12826151.pdf** — Compressing Proofs of k-Out-Of-n Partial Knowledge (Attema, Cramer, Fehr). Compressed DL-setting proofs of partial knowledge generalizing Groth–Kohlweiss logarithmic 1-out-of-n proofs to general k.
- **12826155.pdf** — A Logarithmic Lower Bound for Oblivious RAM (for all parameters) (Komargodski, Lin). New ORAM lower bound removing the w/b factor, giving logarithmic amortized probe bounds across all parameter regimes.
- **12826156.pdf** — One-Way Functions Imply Secure Computation in a Quantum World (Bartusek, Coladangelo, Khurana, Ma). Black-box construction of simulation-secure quantum OT from quantum-hard OWFs via extractable/equivocal quantum commitments.
- **12826160.pdf** — Receiver-Anonymity in Rerandomizable RCCA-Secure Cryptosystems Resolved (Wang, Chen, Yang, Huang, Wang, Yung). First anonymous rerandomizable RCCA-secure PKE, resolving a CRYPTO 2007 open problem via extended smooth projective hashing.
- **12826164.pdf** — Upslices, Downslices, and Secret-Sharing with Complexity of 1.5n (Applebaum, Nir). Secret-sharing schemes for downslice access structures with total share size 2^{0.585n}, improving general access-structure complexity.
- **12826168.pdf** — Efficient Key Recovery for all HFE Signature Variants (Tao, Petzoldt, Ding). Key-recovery attack on HFEv- showing Minus/Vinegar modifications add little security; proposed GeMSS parameters fall short of claimed levels.
- **12826169.pdf** — On Tight Quantum Security of HMAC and NMAC in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Hosoyamada, Iwata). Proves tight Θ(2^{n/3}) quantum query bound for distinguishing HMAC/NMAC, closing the gap with known attacks.
- **12826170.pdf** — Oblivious RAM with Worst-Case Logarithmic Overhead (Asharov, Komargodski, Lin, Shi). First ORAM with worst-case O(log N) overhead and constant client memory, via a new de-amortization framework.
- **12826171.pdf** — Multi-Input Quadratic Functional Encryption from Pairings (Agrawal, Goyal, Tomida). First MIFE for quadratic functions from pairings under the bilateral matrix Diffie–Hellman assumption.
- **12826178.pdf** — Impossibility of Quantum Virtual Black-Box Obfuscation of Classical Circuits (Alagic, Brakerski, Dulek, Schaffner). Under quantum-hard LWE, VBB obfuscation of classical circuits into quantum states is generally impossible.
- **12826180.pdf** — Non-Malleable Codes for Bounded Parallel-Time Tampering (Dachman-Soled, Komargodski, Pass). First non-malleable codes against all polynomial-size bounded-parallel-time tampering, covering non-uniform NC, in the plain model.
- **12826183.pdf** — Quantum Collision Attacks on Reduced SHA-256 and SHA-512 (Hosoyamada, Sasaki). First dedicated quantum collision attacks reaching 38/39 steps of SHA-256/SHA-512, improving on classical 31/27 steps.
- **12826185.pdf** — MoSS: Modular Security Specifications Framework (Herzberg, Leibowitz, Syta, Wrotniak). Framework separating security requirements from models/assumptions for reusable, modular protocol security specifications.
- **12826188.pdf** — Secure Computation from One-Way Noisy Communication (Agrawal, Ishai, Kushilevitz, Narayanan, Prabhakaran, Prabhakaran, Rosen). Circumvents impossibility of information-theoretic one-way OT-style computation via ideal obfuscation and anti-concentration.
- **12826190.pdf** — DualRing: Generic Construction of Ring Signatures with Efficient Instantiations (Yuen, Esgin, Liu, Au, Ding). Generic ring signature from canonical identification schemes; DL instantiation compresses to logarithmic size with an improved Bulletproofs-style sum argument.
- **12826191.pdf** — Towards Tight Random Probing Security (Cassiers, Faust, Orlt, Standaert). New PDT composability notion and STRAPS tool tightening random-probing security analysis of masked circuits such as AES S-boxes.
- **12826193.pdf** — A New Simple Technique to Bootstrap Various Lattice Zero-Knowledge Proofs to QROM Secure NIZKs (Katsumata). Semi-generic transform compiling lattice Σ-protocols into QROM-secure straight-line extractable NIZKs via extractable linear homomorphic commitments.
- **12826196.pdf** — Traceable Secret Sharing and Applications (Goyal, Song, Srinivasan). Secret sharing where cheating servers who sell shares can be traced, deterring cryptographic share-collection bribery schemes.
- **12826200.pdf** — Three-Round Secure Multiparty Computation from Black-Box Two-Round Oblivious Transfer (Patra, Srinivasan). Three-round MPC making black-box use of two-round OT, resolving round complexity for semi-honest black-box MPC from minimal assumptions.
- **12826201.pdf** — Lower bounds on lattice sieving and information set decoding (Kirshanova, Laarhoven). New lower bounds for nearest-neighbor subroutines underlying lattice sieving and ISD cryptanalytic algorithms.
- **12826204.pdf** — Separating Adaptive Streaming from Oblivious Streaming using the Bounded Storage Model (Kaplan, Mansour, Nissim, Stemmer). First general separation: an adversarially-robust streaming problem needing polynomial space with a polylog-space oblivious algorithm.
- **12826205.pdf** — On the Round Complexity of Black-Box Secure MPC (Ishai, Khurana, Sahai, Srinivasan). Round-optimal 4-round black-box pairwise MPC from PKE with pseudorandom public keys, improving prior high-constant-round black-box protocols.
- **12826219.pdf** — Deniable Fully Homomorphic Encryption from Learning With Errors (Agrawal, Goldwasser, Mossel). First compact deniable FHE from LWE, with ciphertext/public-key size independent of detection probability.
- **12826223.pdf** — Round Efficient Secure Multiparty Quantum Computation with Identifiable Abort (Alon, Chung, Chung, Huang, Lee, Shen). First multi-party quantum computation with identifiable abort and circuit-independent quantum communication rounds, from quantum-resistant FHE.
- **12826226.pdf** — How to Meet Ternary LWE Keys (May). Improved meet-in-the-middle attack using representation technique reaching ~S^0.25 asymptotically, with concrete estimates for NTRU, NTRU Prime, BLISS, and GLP.
