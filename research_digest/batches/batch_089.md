# Batch 089 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by Asiacrypt/Eurocrypt/PKC 2010-era papers (IACR ePrint numbering), spanning symmetric cryptanalysis, provable-security protocol design, and lattice/pairing-based constructions.
- Heavy cluster of SHA-3 candidate cryptanalysis (Skein, Hamsi, ECHO, Grøstl), plus classical attacks on Tiger/MD4/SHA-2, AES-192/256, SOSEMANUK, and Grain/KATAN.
- Strong presence of zero-knowledge and proof-system papers (Groth ×2, polynomial commitments, SGGM, random-oracle programmability) and leakage-resilience work.
- ECDLP-relevant items: Granger's Static-DHP index-calculus on ECs over extension fields, Bernstein–Lange–Schwabe Pollard rho negation-map speedup, and Avanzi–Heuberger char-3 scalar multiplication.
- Anomalies: 6477379.pdf and 6477561.pdf have garbled/obfuscated text layers (hex-encoded titles and undecodable content respectively); 65710296.pdf is a 1-page talk abstract, not a full paper.

## Papers
- **62250433.pdf** — Coordinate Blinding over Large Prime Fields (Michael Tunstall, Marc Joye). Multiplicative coordinate blinding for scalar multiplication on short-Weierstrass curves over prime fields, shown to generalize/randomly-isomorph curve countermeasures, implemented efficiently with Montgomery multiplication.
- **641.pdf** — Universal Designated Verifier Signature Proof (Joonsang Baek, Reihaneh Safavi-Naini, Willy Susilo). New UDVS scheme removing the unrealistic requirement that designated verifiers create certified keys compatible with the signer's parameters.
- **6477001.pdf** — Rotational Rebound Attacks on Reduced Skein (Khovratovich, Nikolić, Rechberger). Combines rotational cryptanalysis with rebound attack to distinguish ~53/57 rounds of Skein-256/512 compression function and Threefish.
- **6477020.pdf** — Finding Second Preimages of Short Messages for Hamsi-256 (Thomas Fuhr). Exploits affine relations on 3-round Hamsi-256 compression to give second-preimage attack at ~2^251.3 for short messages, below generic cost.
- **6477038.pdf** — Non-Full-Active Super-Sbox Analysis: Applications to ECHO and Grøstl (Sasaki, Li, Wang, Sakiyama, Ohta). Detects non-ideal properties of full 8-round ECHO permutation at 2^182 time/2^37 memory, plus practical distinguishers on Grøstl-256.
- **6477056.pdf** — Advanced Meet-in-the-Middle Preimage Attacks: First Results on Full Tiger, and Improved Results on MD4 and SHA-2 (Guo, Ling, Rechberger, Wang). First shortcut preimage attack on full Tiger (2^188.8) and improved MD4/SHA-2 results via extended MITM framework.
- **6477076.pdf** — Collision Attacks against the Knudsen-Preneel Compression Functions (Özen, Stam). New collision attacks on code-based wide-pipe compression functions, disproving Knudsen–Preneel's proven lower bound for most parameter sets.
- **6477094.pdf** — Improved Generic Attacks on Unbalanced Feistel Schemes with Expanding Functions (Volte, Nachef, Patarin). Systematic computer-aided re-analysis of generic attacks on unbalanced Feistel schemes with expanding round functions, finding more efficient attack conditions.
- **6477112.pdf** — The World is Not Enough: Another Look on Second-Order DPA (Standaert, Veyrat-Charvillon, Oswald, Gierlichs, Medwed, Kasper, Mangard). Shows equivalence of standard univariate distinguishers fails for multivariate attacks on masked implementations; refined analysis under Hamming-weight leakage.
- **6477130.pdf** — Conditional Differential Cryptanalysis of NLFSR-based Cryptosystems (Knellwolf, Meier, Naya-Plasencia). Conditional differentials give best-known distinguishers/key recovery on Grain v1 (104/160 rounds), Grain-128 (215/256), and KATAN/KTANTAN.
- **6477146.pdf** — A Byte-Based Guess and Determine Attack on SOSEMANUK (Feng, Liu, Zhou, Wu, Feng). Byte-granularity guess-and-determine recovers SOSEMANUK internal state at O(2^176), beating exhaustive search for keys over 176 bits.
- **6477159.pdf** — Improved Single-Key Attacks on 8-round AES-192 and AES-256 (Dunkelman, Keller, Shamir). Three new techniques yield first non-marginal single-key attack on 8-round AES-192 and improve all 7/8-round AES-192/256 complexities.
- **6477178.pdf** — Constant-Size Commitments to Polynomials and Their Applications (Kate, Zaverucha, Goldberg). Defines polynomial commitment schemes with constant-size commitments and openings (KZG commitments), applied to VSS, zero-knowledge sets, credentials.
- **6477196.pdf** — Computationally Secure Pattern Matching in the Presence of Malicious Adversaries (Hazay, Toft). Secure two-party pattern matching with full simulation against malicious adversaries, O(n+m) cost, plus wildcard/approximate/secret-length variants.
- **6477232.pdf** — Generic Compilers for Authenticated Key Exchange (Jager, Kohlar, Schäge, Schwenk). Generic, provably secure compilers (Bellare–Rogaway model) combining separate key agreement and authentication protocols, relevant to TLS.
- **6477267.pdf** — Efficient String-Commitment from Weak Bit-Commitment (Chung, Liu, Lu, Yang). Security amplification: ω(log s) black-box calls to weak bit-commitment suffice for full string commitment, improving prior ω(log² s) bounds.
- **6477285.pdf** — On the Static Diffie-Hellman Problem on Elliptic Curves over Extension Fields (Robert Granger). Index-calculus-style algorithm solving Static DHP over E(F_{q^n}) with Õ(q^{1-1/(n+1)}) queries/time; reduces security of EUROCRYPT'09 curves over F_{p²} and F_{p⁴}.
- **6477305.pdf** — Random Oracles With(out) Programmability (Fischlin, Lehmann, Ristenpart, Shrimpton, Stam, Tessaro). Formalizes three levels of ROM programmability; proves FDH needs full programming while Shoup's KEM achieves CCA with limited programming.
- **6477323.pdf** — Short Pairing-based Non-interactive Zero-Knowledge Arguments (Jens Groth). Sub-linear-size (constant with large CRS) pairing-based NIZK arguments for circuit satisfiability from new knowledge-of-exponent assumptions, no random oracles.
- **6477343.pdf** — Short Non-interactive Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Jens Groth). Uses PCPs to build quasi-linear-size NIZK proofs for circuit satisfiability with adaptive unconditional soundness; shortest known at the time, e.g., via Naccache–Stern.
- **6477379.pdf** — Faster Fully Homomorphic Encryption (Damien Stehlé, Ron Steinfeld) (?). Extract is hex-encoded garbage; only title/authors/affiliations decodable — abstract content unavailable from text layer.
- **6477399.pdf** — A Group Signature Scheme from Lattice Assumptions (Gordon, Katz, Vaikuntanathan). First group signature from LWE (random oracle model), via new trapdoor basis-sampling algorithm for orthogonal lattices.
- **6477435.pdf** — The Round Complexity of Verifiable Secret Sharing: The Statistical Case (Kumaresan, Patra, Rangan). Settles statistical VSS round complexity: 3-round VSS iff t<n/2, with efficient 4-round protocol.
- **6477452.pdf** — General Perfectly Secure Message Transmission Using Linear Codes (Yang, Desmedt). PSMT in general adversary-structure model via linear codes; first constant-round (2–3 round) interactive protocols with improved transmission complexity.
- **6477470.pdf** — On Invertible Sampling and Adaptive Security (Ishai, Kumarasubramanian, Orlandi, Sahai). Evidence that some randomized functionalities cannot be realized with adaptive security (no erasure), via hardness of invertible sampling.
- **6477505.pdf** — A Closer Look at Anonymity and Robustness in Encryption Schemes (Mohassel). Hybrid encryption can destroy anonymity even with anonymous components; weak robustness of the KEM restores it; gives efficient robust-PKE/IBE transforms.
- **6477523.pdf** — Limitations on Transformations from Composite-Order to Prime-Order Groups: The Case of Round-Optimal Blind Signatures (Meiklejohn, Shacham, Freeman). Shows prime-order DLIN pairings cannot be simultaneously cancelling and projecting, limiting composite-to-prime-order translations.
- **6477543.pdf** — The Semi-Generic Group Model and Applications to Pairing-Based Cryptography (Jager, Rupp). Proposes SGGM as middle ground between GGM and standard model; master theorems reduce most bilinear assumptions to two finite-field ones.
- **6477561.pdf** — Unreadable: text layer is obfuscated/encoded gibberish with no decodable title or abstract; content cannot be determined from extract.
- **6477581.pdf** — Structured Encryption and Controlled Disclosure (Chase, Kamara). Generalizes searchable symmetric encryption to arbitrarily structured data (matrices, labeled data, graphs) with efficient queries, e.g., adjacency and subgraph queries.
- **6477599.pdf** — Leakage Resilient ElGamal Encryption (Kiltz, Pietrzak). Proves multiplicatively blinded ElGamal over bilinear prime-order groups is CCA-secure under continuous leakage in the generic-group model.
- **6477617.pdf** — Efficient Public-Key Cryptography in the Presence of Key Leakage (Dodis, Haralambiev, López-Alt, Wichs). Leakage-resilient encryption, signatures, ID and AKA achieving efficiency, strong security, and flexible leakage bounds simultaneously, in standard model.
- **65710001.pdf** — Linearly Homomorphic Signatures over Binary Fields and New Tools for Lattice-Based Signatures (Boneh, Freeman). First homomorphic signature authenticating vector subspaces over F₂, from new k-SIS lattice assumption (random oracle model).
- **65710019.pdf** — Homomorphic Network Coding Signatures in the Standard Model (Attrapadung, Libert). First standard-model homomorphic network-coding signatures signing individual vectors on-the-fly with constant per-packet overhead, via Waters dual encryption.
- **65710036.pdf** — Efficient Attribute-Based Signatures for Non-Monotone Predicates in the Standard Model (Okamoto, Takashima). First fully secure ABS supporting non-monotone predicates (NOT/AND/OR/threshold), proven under DLIN and CR hashes.
- **65710055.pdf** — Ciphertext-Policy Attribute-Based Encryption: An Expressive, Efficient, and Provably Secure Realization (Brent Waters). CP-ABE with linear-size ciphertexts and standard-model security under new decisional PBDHE (and weaker BDHE variants).
- **65710074.pdf** — Generic Constructions for Chosen-Ciphertext Secure Attribute Based Encryption (Yamada, Attrapadung, Hanaoka, Kunihiro). Generic CPA-to-CCA conversions for ABE needing only delegatability or new "verifiability" property; covers KP/CP, small/large universe.
- **65710093.pdf** — Expressive Key-Policy Attribute-Based Encryption with Constant-Size Ciphertexts (Attrapadung, Libert, de Panafieu). First KP-ABE with non-monotonic access structures and constant-size ciphertexts, via identity-based broadcast encryption and a new revocation scheme.
- **65710112.pdf** — Faster and Lower Memory Scalar Multiplication on Supersingular Curves in Characteristic Three (Avanzi, Heuberger). New digit sets from Frobenius unit-group structure give up to 30–47% speedups and 3–8× memory reduction for pairing-relevant char-3 curves.
- **65710132.pdf** — On the Correct Use of the Negation Map in the Pollard Rho Method (Bernstein, Lange, Schwabe). Branchless negation-map Pollard rho plus faster modular arithmetic nearly halves the secp112r1 ECDLP cost on PlayStation 3.
- **65710152.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the RSA Subgroup Assumption from TCC 2005 (Coron, Joux, Mandal, Naccache, Tibouchi). Attack on hidden-order RSA subgroups at ~2^{ℓ/2} complexity shows Groth's 100-bit parameter choice overly aggressive.
- **65710163.pdf** — (If) Size Matters: Size-Hiding Private Set Intersection (Ateniese, De Cristofaro, Tsudik). Introduces SHI-PSI hiding the client's set size; efficient construction under RSA in the random oracle model.
- **65710181.pdf** — Sub-Linear, Secure Comparison With Two Non-Colluding Parties (Tomas Toft). Yao-millionaires-type comparison of secret values in sub-linear secure operations (log or constant rounds), suited to Paillier-based two-party computation.
- **65710200.pdf** — Oblivious Transfer with Hidden Access Control Policies (Camenisch, Dubovitskaya, Neven, Zaverucha). OT with access control where policies stay hidden from the database, supporting credential revocation and one-record-per-query limits.
- **65710257.pdf** — Parallel Decryption Queries in Bounded Chosen Ciphertext Attacks (Matsuda, Matsuura). Introduces "mixed CCA" security unifying notions between CPA and CCA via parallel decryption queries; studies relations among the notions.
- **65710277.pdf** — Secure Blind Decryption (Matthew Green). First practical blind decryption schemes with strong CCA security in the standard model under bilinear-group assumptions; applications to privacy-preserving storage and OT.
- **65710296.pdf** — New Developments in Leakage-Resilient Cryptography (Vinod Vaikuntanathan). 1-page survey/talk abstract on definitions and constructions for cryptography resilient to general information leakage; not a full research paper.
- **65710297.pdf** — On the Security of a Bidirectional Proxy Re-Encryption Scheme from PKC 2010 (Weng, Zhao, Hanaoka). Shows the PKC 2010 PRE scheme without bilinear maps is not CCA-secure, leaving that open problem unresolved.
- **65710310.pdf** — Fully Secure Accountable-Authority Identity-Based Encryption (Sahai, Seyalioglu). First fully secure accountable-authority IBE, tracing dishonest PKGs given only black-box decoder-box access, from DBDH.
- **65710326.pdf** — One-Pass HMQV and Asymmetric Key-Wrapping (Halevi, Krawczyk). Shows one-pass HMQV (HOMQV) suits asymmetric key-wrapping, degrading to DHIES without a server key, with forward secrecy and near-free performance.
