# Batch 009 digest

## Overview
- This batch is drawn almost entirely from the PKC 2018 and EUROCRYPT 2018 conference proceedings (file IDs 10770xxx and 10822xxx), i.e., top-tier 2017–2018 cryptography.
- Dominant topics: zero-knowledge proofs/arguments (Hamming-weight ZK, subversion-ZK SNARKs, DVNIZK, ZK round complexity), secure computation (OT extension, two-round MPC, covert 2PC, message complexity), and lattice/post-quantum cryptography (LWE equivalence to eDCP, tuple sieving, parallel basis reduction, rounded Gaussian sampling, lattice group signatures, MQ signatures SOFIA).
- Notable ECDLP-relevant paper: 10822110.pdf (Corrigan-Gibbs & Kogan) proves tight generic lower bounds for discrete-log with preprocessing and gives new preprocessing attacks; 10822135.pdf (Micciancio & Walter) formalizes bit security.
- Other highlights: practical cryptanalysis of WalnutDSA (10770207), bootstrapped blockchain consensus without trusted setup (10770257), PKE resistant to parameter subversion from elliptic-curve groups (10770208).
- No non-paper files, garbage extracts, or obvious duplicates found; all 50 entries are genuine research papers.

## Papers
- **10770151.pdf** — Compact Zero-Knowledge Proofs of Small Hamming Weight (Damgård, Luo, Oechsner, Scholl, Simkin). ZK proof that a committed vector has bounded Hamming weight, size independent of string length, with five applications including actively secure k-out-of-n OT.
- **10770155.pdf** — Subversion-zero-knowledge SNARKs (Georg Fuchsbauer). Shows leading SNARKs (Groth, Gennaro et al., Pinocchio) retain zero knowledge even under maliciously chosen CRS, with implications for Zcash parameter setup.
- **10770159.pdf** — Key Dependent Message Security and Receiver Selective Opening Security for Identity-Based Encryption (Kitagawa, Tanaka). First IBE schemes with KDM security and simulation-based RSO security, both from standard IBE building blocks.
- **10770164.pdf** — Revisiting Proxy Re-Encryption: Forward Secrecy, Improved Security, and Applications (Derler, Krenn, Lorünser, Ramacher, Slamanig, Striecks). Introduces forward-secret proxy re-encryption where evolved re-encryption keys prevent decryption of old ciphertexts.
- **10770171.pdf** — Non-Malleability vs. CCA-Security: The Case of Commitments (Broadnax, Fetzer, Müller-Quade, Rupp). Settles relations among non-malleability and CCA notions for commitments, showing parallel NM and parallel CCA are not equivalent for commitments.
- **10770182.pdf** — Efficient Batch Zero-Knowledge Arguments for Low Degree Polynomials (Bootle, Groth). Constant-round discrete-log ZK argument framework for polynomial relations with batch verification, yielding improved membership, polynomial evaluation, and range proofs.
- **10770183.pdf** — Hashing solutions instead of generating problems: On the interactive certification of RSA moduli (Auerbach, Poettering). Two efficient one-round protocols for proving RSA moduli well-formed, improving prior four-pass protocols to two passes.
- **10770185.pdf** — Hash Proof Systems over Lattices Revisited (Benhamouda, Blazy, Ducas, Quach). Explains and overcomes limitations of prior lattice SPHFs via harmonic analysis, constructing SPHFs for standard LWE ciphertext languages.
- **10770191.pdf** — Extending Oblivious Transfer with Low Communication via Key-Homomorphic PRFs (Peter Scholl). First OT extension with sublinear (logarithmic) communication and active security using only black-box primitives, via key-homomorphic PRFs from LWE.
- **10770193.pdf** — Privately Constraining and Programming PRFs, the LWE Way (Peikert, Shiehian). Unified LWE-based constructions of constraint-hiding constrained PRFs and privately programmable PRFs via a new "shift-hiding shiftable function" notion.
- **10770194.pdf** — Learning With Errors and Extrapolated Dihedral Cosets (Brakerski, Kirshanova, Stehlé, Wen). Shows LWE is quantum-polynomial-time equivalent to a relaxed "extrapolated" dihedral coset problem, generalizing Regev's reduction.
- **10770199.pdf** — Fully Homomorphic Encryption from the Finite Field Isomorphism Problem (Doröz, Hoffstein, Pipher, Silverman, Sunar, Whyte, Zhang). FHE scheme based on the new hardness assumption of recovering a secret isomorphism between finite fields.
- **10770204.pdf** — KEM Combiners (Giacon, Heuer, Poettering). Black-box constructions combining multiple KEMs into one that is CCA-secure as long as any single ingredient KEM is secure.
- **10770206.pdf** — A Unified Framework for Trapdoor-Permutation-Based Sequential Aggregate Signatures (Gentry, O'Neill, Reyzin). Unifies prior sequential aggregate signature schemes via ideal ciphers, yielding the first pairing-free scheme with lazy verification and constant-size signatures.
- **10770207.pdf** — A Practical Cryptanalysis of WalnutDSA (Hart, Kim, Micheli, Pascual Perez, Petit, Quek). Practical attack on SecureRF's braid-group-based signature scheme, forging signatures in about two minutes by bypassing its core hard problems.
- **10770208.pdf** — Public-Key Encryption Resistant to Parameter Subversion and its Realization from Efficiently-Embeddable Groups (Auerbach, Bellare, Kiltz). Defines PKE/KEM security under subverted parameters and builds elliptic-curve KEMs achieving it via efficiently-embeddable group families.
- **10770209.pdf** — On Composable Security for Digital Signatures (Badertscher, Maurer, Tackmann). Gives a composable, constructive-cryptography treatment of digital signatures as repositories for authenticated reading rather than monolithic ideal functionalities.
- **10770210.pdf** — Multi-Key Searchable Encryption, Revisited (Hamlin, shelat, Weiss, Wichs). Strengthens the MKSE definition (fixing Mylar's broken security) and constructs schemes meeting it, including a simple PRF-based construction.
- **10770212.pdf** — Rounded Gaussians: Fast and Secure Constant-Time Sampling for Lattice-Based Crypto (Hülsing, Lange, Smeets). Replaces discrete Gaussians with rounded Gaussians in BLISS-style signatures, enabling constant-time sampling twice as fast as prior CDT samplers.
- **10770214.pdf** — Two-Factor Authentication with End-to-End Password Security (Jarecki, Krawczyk, Shirvanian, Saxena). TFA scheme from device-enhanced PAKE and short-authenticated-string authentication, secure even when passwords leak and components are compromised.
- **10770217.pdf** — Hedged Nonce-Based Public-Key Encryption: Adaptive Security under Randomness Failures (Huang, Lai, Chen, Au, Peng, Li). Initiates hedged security for nonce-based PKE, giving best-possible security whenever randomness fails.
- **10770218.pdf** — Making Public Key Functional Encryption Function Private, Distributively (Fan, Tang). Defines distributed public-key functional encryption with shared function keys enabling function privacy, resolving an open problem on cloud-hosted services.
- **10770227.pdf** — Speed-ups and time–memory trade-offs for tuple lattice sieving (Herold, Kirshanova, Laarhoven). Improves tuple sieving for SVP with tunable time–memory trade-offs, e.g., triple sieve in time 2^0.3588n with quasi-linear memory.
- **10770229.pdf** — Related Randomness Security for Public Key Encryption, Revisited (Matsuda, Schuldt). Shows inherent limits of related-randomness security for PKE and proposes the weaker, achievable notion of related refreshable randomness security.
- **10770231.pdf** — Fast Lattice Basis Reduction Suitable for Massive Parallelization and Its Application to the Shortest Vector Problem (Teruya, Kashiwabara, Hanaoka). Massively parallel lattice basis reduction algorithm extending Fukase–Kashiwabara, applied to evaluate SVP hardness.
- **10770239.pdf** — Towards Characterizing Securely Computable Two-Party Randomized Functions (Data, Prabhakaran). Combinatorial characterizations of randomized functions admitting information-theoretic semi-honest 2PC, including ternary-output and 2-round cases.
- **10770252.pdf** — SOFIA: MQ-based signatures in the QROM (Chen, Hülsing, Rijneveld, Samardjiska, Schwabe). First multivariate-quadratic signature scheme proven secure in the quantum random oracle model, with 128-bit parameters and AVX2 implementation.
- **10770257.pdf** — Bootstrapping the Blockchain, with Applications to Consensus and Fast PKI Setup (Garay, Kiayias, Leonardos, Panagiotakos). PoW blockchain protocol that bootstraps genesis blocks from scratch under adversarial pre-computation, enabling consensus and PKI without trusted setup.
- **10770259.pdf** — On the Message Complexity of Secure Multiparty Computation (Ishai, Mittal, Ostrovsky). Proves 2n+k−3 point-to-point messages are necessary and sufficient for general semi-honest MPC with n inputs and k outputs.
- **10770262.pdf** — Improved (Almost) Tightly-Secure Structure-Preserving Signatures (Jutla, Ohkubo, Roy). Compresses SPS signatures by ~40% and reduces verification pairing equations while keeping an O(λ) reduction loss to SXDH.
- **10770264.pdf** — Weakly Secure Equivalence-Class Signatures from Standard Assumptions (Fuchsbauer, Gay). Builds structure-preserving signatures on equivalence classes from Matrix-Diffie-Hellman assumptions under a relaxed, efficiently verifiable unforgeability notion.
- **10770268.pdf** — Efficient Covert Two-Party Computation (Stanislaw Jarecki). Constant-round covert 2PC with black-box simulation under DDH in the CRS model, at efficiency close to standard cut-and-choose garbled-circuit protocols.
- **10770270.pdf** — On the Security of Classic Protocols for Unique Witness Relations (Deng, Song, Yu, Chen). Gives conditions under which classic public-coin protocols (incl. Schnorr) are witness hiding for unique-witness relations, via a relaxed one-more DL assumption.
- **10770286.pdf** — Constant-size Group Signatures from Lattices (Ling, Nguyen, Wang, Xu). First lattice-based group signature with signature size independent of the number of users, secure under Ring-SIS and Ring-LWE.
- **10822110.pdf** — The Discrete-Logarithm Problem with Preprocessing (Corrigan-Gibbs, Kogan). Proves the essentially tight bound ST² = Ω̃(εN) for generic discrete-log algorithms with preprocessing, plus new preprocessing attacks on multiple-discrete-log and decisional problems.
- **10822112.pdf** — An Improved Affine Equivalence Algorithm for Random Permutations (Itai Dinur). New affine-equivalence algorithm for n-bit permutations running in about n³2ⁿ bit operations, improving on Biryukov et al., with applications to S-box and white-box analysis.
- **10822135.pdf** — On the Bit Security of Cryptographic Primitives (Micciancio, Walter). Formalizes a quantitative notion of bit security for general cryptographic games, resolving paradoxes of the traditional definition for decision problems.
- **10822139.pdf** — Formal Verification of Masked Hardware Implementations in the Presence of Glitches (Bloem, Gross, Iusupov, Könighofer, Mangard, Winter). Method to formally verify masked hardware circuits directly on netlists in the glitch-extended probing model using Fourier coefficients of gates.
- **10822149.pdf** — Obfustopia Built on Secret-Key Functional Encryption (Kitagawa, Nishimaki, Tanaka). Constructs indistinguishability obfuscation for all circuits solely from collusion-resistant secret-key functional encryption, via relaxed puncturable SKFE.
- **10822151.pdf** — Synchronized Aggregate Signatures from the RSA Assumption (Hohenberger, Waters). Efficient RSA-based aggregate signatures in the synchronized (time-period) setting, supporting large period bounds with practical signing times.
- **10822154.pdf** — Random Oracles and Non-Uniformity (Coretti, Dodis, Guo, Steinberger). Improves Unruh's pre-sampling technique to optimal O(ST/P) loss in the auxiliary-input ROM, giving tight bounds for non-uniform and preprocessing security proofs.
- **10822162.pdf** — Efficient Designated-Verifier Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge Proofs of Knowledge (Chaidos, Couteau). Framework for designated-verifier NIZK proofs of knowledge for algebraic languages with statistical, unbounded soundness under standard assumptions.
- **10822166.pdf** — Towards Breaking the Exponential Barrier for General Secret Sharing (Liu, Vaikuntanathan, Wee). Refutes strengthened secret-sharing lower-bound conjectures with schemes of sub-exponential share size 2^O(√n log n) for large function families.
- **10822173.pdf** — Sustained Space Complexity (Alwen, Blocki, Pietrzak). Introduces sustained-memory complexity for memory-hard functions and constructs functions with near-optimal sustained memory usage.
- **10822174.pdf** — On the Existence of Three Round Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Fleischhacker, Goyal, Jain). Rules out three-round private-coin ZK proofs for NP, even with non-black-box simulation, under program-obfuscation assumptions.
- **10822176.pdf** — Homomorphic Lower Digits Removal and Improved FHE Bootstrapping (Chen, Han). Improved bootstrapping for FV and BGV via lowest-digit-removal polynomials, reducing depth and implemented in SEAL with practical timings.
- **10822177.pdf** — Multi-Collision Resistant Hash Functions and their Applications (Berman, Degwekar, Rothblum, Vasudevan). Defines MCRH, bases it on an Entropy Approximation variant, and shows it implies constant-round statistically hiding commitments.
- **10822182.pdf** — Two-Round Multiparty Secure Computation from Minimal Assumptions (Garg, Srinivasan). Two-round MPC protocols assuming only two-round oblivious transfer, matching security (semi-honest or malicious) of the underlying OT.
- **10822183.pdf** — Adaptively Secure Garbling with Near Optimal Online Complexity (Garg, Srinivasan). Adaptively secure garbling with online communication n+m+poly(log|C|,λ) from CDH, factoring, or LWE.
- **10822185.pdf** — Tightly-Secure Key-Encapsulation Mechanism in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Saito, Xagawa, Yamakawa). Tight conversion of weakly secure deterministic PKE into IND-CCA KEMs in the QROM via a new "disjoint simulatability" notion.
