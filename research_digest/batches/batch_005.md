# Batch 005 digest

## Overview
- Dominantly 2017-era CRYPTO/EUROCRYPT/TCC-style papers (IDs resemble IACR ePrint 2017/3xx–4xx); heavy focus on theoretical constructions: functional encryption, obfuscation, secure computation, and lattice-based cryptography.
- Strong symmetric-crypto subcluster: impossible-differential and cube cryptanalysis, Keccak/SHA-1 collisions, tweakable blockcipher and AE modes.
- Notable landmark paper: 10401289 — the first full SHA-1 collision (SHAttered, Stevens et al., CWI/Google).
- Notable applied paper: 10401105 — Lindell's fast two-party ECDSA signing, the only elliptic-curve-adjacent work in this batch; ECDLP/pairings/side-channel content otherwise absent.
- One anomaly: 10401132 has no extractable text (1 page).
- Anomalous duo of related papers: 10210328 and 10401284 are sequential works on topology-hiding computation by overlapping authors (not duplicates).

## Papers
- **10210313.pdf** — From Minicrypt to Obfustopia via Private-Key Functional Encryption (Komargodski, Segev). Shows quasi-polynomially-secure private-key FE suffices to build public-key encryption and indistinguishability obfuscation, strengthening Bitansky et al.'s bridge between Impagliazzo's worlds.
- **10210316.pdf** — New Impossible Differential Search Tool from Design and Cryptanalysis Aspects (Sasaki, Todo). MILP-based tool finding longer impossible differentials for Midori128, Lilliput, Minalpher, ARIA, MIBS, revealing new structural properties of those ciphers.
- **10210320.pdf** — On Removing Graded Encodings from Functional Encryption (Bitansky, Lin, Paneth). Under LWE, succinct FE from constant-degree graded encodings can be transformed to rely on bilinear groups, narrowing the gap to IO from better-understood assumptions.
- **10210328.pdf** — Topology-Hiding Computation Beyond Logarithmic Diameter (Akavia, Moran). Constructs topology-hiding MPC for large-diameter graphs (cycles, trees, log-circumference) under DDH, advancing beyond prior log-diameter feasibility results.
- **10210332.pdf** — Quantum Authentication with Key Recycling (Portmann). Full security proof that Barnum et al.'s quantum authentication recycles the entire key on success and part on tampering, with optimal recycling rate.
- **10210334.pdf** — Sublinear Zero-Knowledge Arguments for RAM Programs (Mohassel, Rosulek, Scafuro). UC-secure succinct ZK argument where per-proof prover/verifier cost is proportional to ORAM running time, sublinear in dataset size, without trusted setup.
- **10210340.pdf** — New Collision Attacks on Round-Reduced Keccak (Qiao, Song, Liu, Guo). Extends connector techniques to give first practical collision attacks on 5-round SHAKE128 and 5-round Keccak variants.
- **10210342.pdf** — One-Shot Verifiable Encryption from Lattices (Lyubashevsky, Neven). Ring-LWE verifiable encryption with negligible soundness error in a single proof, yielding compact proofs for individual ciphertexts; applications to key escrow and verifiably encrypted signatures.
- **10210359.pdf** — Small CRT-Exponent RSA Revisited (Takayasu, Lu, Peng). Improved Coppersmith-type lattice attacks on small CRT-exponent RSA, extending Bleichenbacher–May and Jochemsz–May bounds after a decade.
- **10210362.pdf** — Magic Adversaries Versus Individual Reduction: Science Wins Either Way (Deng). Win-win result: either non-uniform PKE/key agreement exist, or Feige–Shamir is distributional concurrent ZK; connects PKE complexity to concurrent-ZK round complexity.
- **10210363.pdf** — Short generators without quantum computers: the case of multiquadratics (Bauch, Bernstein, de Valence, Lange, van Vredendaal). Classical algorithm recovering short generators (private keys of Gentry-style ideal-lattice schemes) in multiquadratic fields with surprisingly low pre-quantum security.
- **10210364.pdf** — Group-Based Secure Computation: Optimizing Rounds, Communication, and Computation (Boyle, Gilboa, Ishai). Improves DDH-based secure computation: black-box group use, 2-round MPC for constant parties, near-optimal communication for NC1/log-space.
- **10401105.pdf** — Fast Secure Two-Party ECDSA Signing (Lindell). Two-party ECDSA protocol roughly 100x faster than prior work (~37 ms per P-256 signature), secure under standard assumptions plus a Paillier-based assumption.
- **10401112.pdf** — LPN Decoded (Esser, Kübler, May). New low-memory classical and first quantum algorithms for LPN, with best time-memory tradeoffs and medium-size instances (k=243) solved in practice.
- **10401119.pdf** — Privacy-Free Garbled Circuits for Formulas: Size Zero and Information-Theoretic (Kondi, Patra). Information-theoretic privacy-free garbling of Boolean formulas producing zero ciphertexts, breaking the one-ciphertext-per-AND lower bound and giving adaptive security for free.
- **10401124.pdf** — Black-Box Parallel Garbled RAM (Lu, Ostrovsky). Garbling of parallel RAM programs with poly-log overhead, removing the non-black-box IBE assumption of Boyle–Chung–Pass TCC 2016-A.
- **10401132.pdf** — Not a research paper: no text extracted (single blank or image-only page).
- **10401134.pdf** — Incremental Program Obfuscation (Garg, Pandey). Initiates study of incremental obfuscation; proves VBB/VGB impossibility even for point functions, defines indistinguishability-based notions and gives constructions.
- **10401137.pdf** — Insuperability of the Standard Versus Ideal Model Gap for Tweakable Blockcipher Security (Mennink). Proves that with the standard-to-ideal transformation proof technique, tweak-rekeying cannot yield optimal 2^n standard-model security.
- **10401151.pdf** — Time-Memory Tradeoff Attacks on the MTP Proof-of-Work Scheme (Dinur, Nadler). Sub-linear computation-memory tradeoff attack on the MTP (Argon2d-based) proof-of-work, undermining its claimed memory-hardness penalty.
- **10401155.pdf** — Secure Computation based on Leaky Correlations: High Resilience Setting (Block, Maji, Nguyen). Correlation extractors converting leaky correlated randomness into fresh OTs with high leakage resilience, improving constants of prior feasibility results.
- **10401159.pdf** — Asymptotically Compact Adaptively Secure Lattice IBEs and Verifiable Random Functions (Yamada). New partitioning techniques yielding adaptively secure lattice IBEs with poly-log master public keys and compact VRFs on bilinear groups.
- **10401160.pdf** — Laconic Oblivious Transfer and its Applications (Cho, Döttling, Garg, Gupta, Miao, Polychroniadou). OT where the receiver commits to a large input with a short message; DDH-based construction enabling non-interactive secure computation on big data and multi-hop RAM homomorphic encryption.
- **10401176.pdf** — PRF-ODH: Relations, Instantiations, and Impossibility Results (Brendel, Fischlin, Günther, Janson). Systematizes PRF-ODH assumption variants into a hierarchy; achievable in ROM under SDH but implausible in standard model via algebraic black-box reductions.
- **10401177.pdf** — All-But-Many Lossy Trapdoor Functions from Lattices and Applications (Boyen, Li). First LWE-based ABM-LTF, yielding the first lattice-based compact PKE with tight IND-SO-CCA2 security.
- **10401180.pdf** — Encryption Switching Protocols Revisited: Switching modulo p (Castagnos, Imbert, Laguillaumie). Simpler generic ESP construction instantiated modulo a prime (Elgamal variant plus Castagnos–Laguillaumie), improving efficiency for arithmetic 2-party computation.
- **10401185.pdf** — Cube Attacks on Non-Blackbox Polynomials Based on Division Property (Todo, Isobe, Hao, Meier). Division-property cube attacks allowing large cube sizes; best-known key recovery on 832-round Trivium, 183-round Grain128a, 704-round ACORN.
- **10401194.pdf** — A Formal Treatment of Multi-key Channels (Günther, Mazaheri). First formal model for channels with key updates (as in TLS 1.3), giving game-based confidentiality/integrity notions capturing forward security and phase-key insulation.
- **10401199.pdf** — The TinyTable protocol for 2-Party Secure Computation, or: Gate-scrambling Revisited (Damgård, Nielsen, Nielsen, Ranellucci). Maliciously secure 2PC in preprocessing model; practical (~1 ms AES latency) and theoretically optimal O(s) communication/preprocessing for general circuits.
- **10401206.pdf** — Compact Structure-preserving Signatures with Almost Tight Security (Abe, Hofheinz, Nishimaki, Ohkubo, Pan). First generic structure-preserving signature scheme whose reduction cost is independent of signing-query count, via adaptive partitioning.
- **10401209.pdf** — Breaking The FF3 Format-Preserving Encryption Standard Over Small Domains (Durak, Vaudenay). Practical total break of NIST's FF3 via a slide attack exploiting bad domain separation, reducing it to a new known-plaintext 4-round Feistel attack.
- **10401222.pdf** — Stronger Security for Reusable Garbled Circuits, General Definitions and Attacks (Agrawal). LWE-based FE for circuits unifying and improving reusable garbled circuits and predicate encryption; also shows existing LWE predicate encryption insecure in strong attribute-hiding games.
- **10401241.pdf** — Generic Transformations of Predicate Encodings: Constructions and Applications (Ambrona, Barthe, Schmidt). Algebraic characterization of predicate-encoding privacy, with more efficient encodings for boolean formulae/span programs and links to pair/tag-based encodings.
- **10401245.pdf** — Identity-Based Encryption from the Diffie-Hellman Assumption (Döttling, Garg). First IBE and HIBE from plain CDH (no pairings) or factoring, bypassing impossibility results via non-black-box use of garbled circuits.
- **10401246.pdf** — Kurosawa-Desmedt Meets Tight Security (Gay, Hofheinz, Kohl). Pairing-free PKE with almost tight DDH reduction, 3-group-element ciphertexts and only 6-group-element public keys, improving Gay et al. EUROCRYPT 2016.
- **10401248.pdf** — From Obfuscation to the Security of Fiat-Shamir for Proofs (Kalai, Rothblum, Rothblum). Constructs a hash function making Fiat–Shamir sound for proofs in the plain model, assuming sub-exponentially secure IO and input-hiding obfuscation.
- **10401256.pdf** — The Bitcoin Backbone Protocol with Chains of Variable Difficulty (Garay, Kiayias, Leonardos). First formal cryptographic analysis of Bitcoin's difficulty target recalculation, extending the q-bounded synchronous backbone model to variable difficulty.
- **10401269.pdf** — Middle-Product Learning With Errors (Roşca, Sakzad, Stehlé, Steinfeld). Introduces MP-LWE with a reduction from PLWE for any monic f, yielding a quasi-linear-time PKE secure if PLWE is hard for some polynomial in an exponential family.
- **10401273.pdf** — Proving Resistance against Invariant Attacks: How to Choose the Round Constants (Beierle, Canteaut, Leander, Rotella). Characterizes when invariant (subspace/nonlinear) attacks apply; proves Prince, Skinny-64, Mantis7 immune and shows how to pick round constants guaranteeing resistance.
- **10401278.pdf** — Encrypted Davies-Meyer and Its Dual: Towards Optimal Security Using Mirror Theory (Mennink, Neves). Improved security bounds for Encrypted Davies-Meyer and a dual construction with near-optimal 2^{n}/67 security via a modernized mirror theorem.
- **10401283.pdf** — Identity-based Encryption from Codes with Rank Metric (Gaborit, Hauteville, Phan, Tillich). Solves the long-open problem of code-based IBE using rank-metric codes, a post-quantum alternative to pairing/lattice IBE.
- **10401284.pdf** — Topology-Hiding Computation on All Graphs (Akavia, LaVigne, Moran). Resolves the open question: topology-hiding MPC feasible for all graphs under DDH, using multiple random walks atop the chain-graph broadcast of Akavia–Moran.
- **10401285.pdf** — Structure vs. Hardness through the Obfuscation Lens (Bitansky, Degwekar, Vaikuntanathan). Shows even IO does not imply NP∩coNP or SZK hardness via fully black-box reductions, extending to many primitives built from IO.
- **10401286.pdf** — Watermarking Cryptographic Functionalities from Standard Lattice Assumptions (Kim, Wu). First watermarkable PRF family with strong unremovability from LWE and 1D-SIS, via a new "translucent PRF" primitive, removing the IO assumption.
- **10401289.pdf** — The first collision for full SHA-1 (Stevens, Bursztein, Karpman, Albertini, Markov). Landmark SHAttered attack: first practical SHA-1 collision (~2^63.1 compression calls), producing two distinct PDFs with the same hash.
- **10401296.pdf** — Boosting Authenticated Encryption Robustness With Minimal Modifications (Ashur, Dunkelman, Luykx). Analyzes minimal tweaks to GCM and ChaCha20-Poly1305 to improve robustness (e.g., nonce-misuse, multi-user security) without sacrificing deployed efficiency.
- **10401297.pdf** — ZMAC: A Fast Tweakable Block Cipher Mode for Highly Secure Message Authentication (Iwata, Minematsu, Peyrin, Seurin). TBC-based MAC mode processing n+t bits per call with beyond-birthday-bound security; essentially optimal, parallelizable, and faster than PMAC1.
- **10401298.pdf** — Key Rotation for Authenticated Encryption (Everspaugh, Paterson, Ristenpart, Scott). Systematic study of updatable AE: strengthened security notions, exposes flaws in Boneh et al.'s scheme, and gives efficient schemes meeting them.
- **10401299.pdf** — Practical Functional Encryption for Quadratic Functions with Applications to Predicate Encryption (Baltico, Catalano, Fiore, Gay). Efficient pairing-based FE for quadratic functions (bilinear maps on encrypted vectors), with selective and adaptive security under standard assumptions.
- **10401307.pdf** — Quantum Security of NMAC and Related Constructions (Song, Yun). Proves NMAC, HMAC, AMAC, and fixed-length cascade are quantum-secure PRFs against superposition queries via a new direct hybrid argument.
