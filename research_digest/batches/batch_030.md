# Batch 030 digest

## Overview
- Nearly all 50 items are circa-2021 conference papers (LNCS-style preprints, likely PKC/CRYPTO/EUROCRYPT 2021 era) spanning lattice crypto, zero-knowledge, MPC/PSI, signatures, and post-quantum cryptography.
- Lattice/PQC themes dominate: BKZ/uSVP attack cost estimation, slide-reduction convergence, lattice multi-signatures, lattice ZK proofs, I-PLWE hardness, LPN worst-case reductions, and MinRank multivariate schemes.
- ECC/isogeny-adjacent highlights: compact ZK proofs for threshold ECDSA, DH-OPRF blinding weaknesses, SIKE hardware cryptanalysis cost model, and pairing-free SIDH key compression.
- Notable single-author theory results: Niehues' optimally tight VRFs, Zhandry's white-box traitor tracing, Ambrona's generic pair-encoding negation, Nuida's PRG-failure taxonomy.
- Anomaly: 127110258.pdf is a 1-page invited-talk abstract (Ducas, Lattices and Factoring), not a full paper; no non-paper artifacts or duplicates found.

## Papers
- **127110139.pdf** — The Convergence of Slide-type Reductions (Michael Walter). Applies Hanrot et al.'s dynamical systems analysis to slide/block-Rankin reduction, yielding sharper runtime bounds and faster practical variants competitive with BKZ.
- **127110142.pdf** — Revisiting (R)CCA Security and Replay Protection (Badertscher, Maurer, Portmann, Rito). Systematically maps the hierarchy of CCA-type PKE security notions relative to benchmark applications, clarifying RCCA and detectable variants.
- **127110144.pdf** — On the Success Probability of Solving Unique SVP via BKZ (Postlethwaite, Virdia). Extends Dachman-Soled et al.'s primal-attack estimator to BKZ 2.0 with experiments, re-estimating costs against NIST PQC KEM finalists.
- **127110151.pdf** — BETA: Biometric-Enabled Threshold Authentication (Agrawal, Badrinarayanan, Mohassel, Mukherjee, Patranabis). UC-secure fuzzy threshold tokenizer letting t devices jointly issue tokens from approximate biometrics, protecting templates and keys.
- **127110152.pdf** — Masked Triples: Amortizing Multiplication Triples across Conditionals (Heath, Kolesnikov, Peceny). Reuses Beaver triples across conditional branches in MPC, needing n triples instead of b·n for b branches.
- **127110154.pdf** — Multi-Party Threshold Private Set Intersection with Sublinear Communication (Badrinarayanan, Miao, Raghuraman, Rindal). Proves Ω(nT) communication lower bounds for multiparty threshold PSI and gives matching protocols from threshold FHE.
- **127110155.pdf** — Two-round n-out-of-n and Multi-Signatures and Trapdoor Commitment from Lattices (Damgård, Orlandi, Takahashi, Tibouchi). Fiat–Shamir-with-Aborts distributed signatures (Dilithium-G-like) secure under Module-SIS/LWE with low round complexity.
- **127110158.pdf** — On the (In)Security of the Diffie-Hellman Oblivious PRF with Multiplicative Blinding (Jarecki, Krawczyk, Xu). Reveals attacks on the faster multiplicative-blinding DH-OPRF variant and characterizes it as a weaker "Correlated OPRF" functionality.
- **127110161.pdf** — Compact Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Threshold ECDSA with Trustless Setup (Yuen, Cui, Xie). Smaller/faster ZK proofs for discrete-log relations and CL ciphertexts in unknown-order groups, cutting threshold ECDSA keygen bandwidth ~47-70%.
- **127110163.pdf** — An Efficient and Generic Construction for Signal's Handshake (X3DH) (Hashimoto, Katsumata, Kwiatkowski, Prest). Defines Signal-conforming AKE and builds it generically from KEMs and signatures with post-quantum, state-leakage, and deniability security.
- **127110165.pdf** — Isogeny-based key compression without pairings (Pereira, Barreto). Compresses SIDH/SIKE public keys without expensive pairing computations while reducing storage for related operations.
- **127110171.pdf** — Universal Proxy Re-Encryption (Döttling, Nishimaki). Introduces UPRE converting ciphertexts between arbitrary PKE schemes; constructions from probabilistic iO and garbled circuits.
- **127110180.pdf** — Analysis of Multivariate Encryption Schemes: Application to Dob (Øygarden, Felke, Raddum). Predicts degree-fall polynomials in Gröbner basis attacks on perturbation-modified MQ schemes, breaking the Dob encryption scheme at suggested parameters.
- **127110181.pdf** — On the Integer Polynomial Learning with Errors Problem (Devevey, Sakzad, Stehlé, Steinfeld). First polynomial-time average-case reductions proving search I-PLWE equivalent to search PLWE for a large class of polynomials.
- **127110183.pdf** — Master-Key KDM-Secure ABE via Predicate Encoding (Feng, Gong, Chen). First generic framework for master-key KDM-secure ABE for affine functions, yielding first such HIBE and arithmetic-branching-program ABE.
- **127110186.pdf** — Exact Lattice Sampling from Non-Gaussian Distributions (Plançon, Prest). Trapdoor lattice sampling framework producing perfectly (not statistically) indistinguishable outputs, supporting uniform and affine distributions.
- **127110187.pdf** — Efficient Adaptively-Secure IB-KEMs and VRFs via Near-Collision Resistance (Jager, Kurek, Niehues). Adaptively secure versions of Agrawal-Boneh-Boyen and Boneh-Boyen IBEs plus an efficient VRF from near-collision resistance.
- **127110191.pdf** — Subversion-Resilient Public Key Encryption with Practical Watchdogs (Bemmann, Chen, Jager). Refines Russell et al.'s watchdog model with concrete-efficiency requirements so watchdogs remain practical against subverted implementations.
- **127110193.pdf** — Cryptographic Pseudorandom Generators Can Make Cryptosystems Problematic (Nuida). Catalogs three mechanisms by which provably secure PRGs still destroy cryptosystem security, including visible seeds and non-uniform distinguishers.
- **127110194.pdf** — Non-Interactive CCA2-Secure Threshold Cryptosystems (Devevey, Libert, Nguyen, Peters, Yung). First adaptively secure, non-interactive CCA2 threshold PKE in the standard model without pairings.
- **127110195.pdf** — Publicly Verifiable Zero Knowledge from (Collapsing) Blockchains (Scafuro, Siniscalchi, Visconti). Builds pvZK from blockchain setup assumptions, exposing an "attack of the clones" against prior proof-of-stake-based constructions.
- **127110200.pdf** — Two-server Distributed ORAM with Sublinear Computation and Constant Rounds (Hamlin, Varia). First 2-server semi-honest DORAM achieving both sublinear server work and constant rounds, via square-root ORAM and efficient PIR.
- **127110202.pdf** — Updatable Signatures and Message Authentication Codes (Cini, Ramacher, Slamanig, Striecks, Tairi). Definitional framework and constructions for key-updatable signatures/MACs supporting key rotation via update tokens.
- **127110206.pdf** — Flexible and Efficient Verifiable Computation on Encrypted Data (Bois, Cascudo, Fiore, Kim). Combines verifiable computation with homomorphic encryption without restrictive HE parameter requirements, improving concrete delegation efficiency.
- **127110207.pdf** — Transferable E-cash: A Cleaner Model and the First Practical Instantiation (Bauer, Fuchsbauer, Qian). Simpler, stronger security model plus first practical bilinear-group transferable e-cash; notes a flaw in the PKC'15 scheme.
- **127110210.pdf** — Multi-Client Functional Encryption for Separable Functions (Ciampi, Siniscalchi, Waldner). Compiles single-input FE into multi-client FE for separable functions, and introduces outsourceable decentralized MCFE.
- **127110211.pdf** — More Efficient Digital Signatures with Tight Multi-User Security (Diemert, Gellert, Jager, Lyu). Generic tightly multi-user secure signatures with adaptive corruptions from lossy identification; DDH instantiation gives 3-exponent signatures.
- **127110213.pdf** — Shorter Lattice-Based Zero-Knowledge Proofs via One-Time Commitments (Lyubashevsky, Nguyen, Seiler). Cuts lattice ZK proof-of-knowledge sizes ~30% via one-time commitments, with savings propagating to larger protocols.
- **127110215.pdf** — Multiparty Cardinality Testing for Threshold Private Intersection (Branco, Döttling, Pu). N-party intersection cardinality test with Õ(Nt²) communication, yielding matching threshold PSI.
- **127110221.pdf** — Private Set Operations from Oblivious Switching (Garimella, Mohassel, Rosulek, Sadeghian, Singh). Oblivious-switching framework computing arbitrary functions over set intersections/unions, 2.5–3× less communication than prior art.
- **127110223.pdf** — Verifiable Random Functions with Optimal Tightness (Niehues). Meta-reduction proving factor-Q loss unavoidable for VRFs from non-interactive assumptions, plus the first construction achieving that bound.
- **127110224.pdf** — A Geometric Approach to Homomorphic Secret Sharing (Ishai, Lai, Malavolta). Compiler building HSS for multivariate polynomials from linear HE plus information-theoretic HSS, efficient for arbitrary server counts.
- **127110225.pdf** — Multivariate Public Key Cryptosystem from Sidon Spaces (Raviv, Langton, Tamo). New MinRank-based multivariate PKC from Sidon spaces, proven resistant to kernel and minor attacks, with software implementation.
- **127110230.pdf** — Generic Negation of Pair Encodings (Ambrona). Transformation converting any pair encoding scheme for predicate P into one for its negation, solving a problem open since 2015.
- **127110235.pdf** — Banquet: Short and Fast Signatures from AES (Baum, Delpech de Saint Guilhem, Kales, Orsini, Scholl, Zaverucha). MPC-in-the-head signatures using only AES/SHA-3, >50% shorter than BBQ with sub-10ms sign/verify.
- **127110239.pdf** — On Publicly-Accountable Zero-Knowledge and Small Shuffle Arguments (Fleischhacker, Simkin). Shuffle arguments with noticeable soundness but public accountability, from collision-resistant hashing alone, communication independent of vector length.
- **127110241.pdf** — Beyond Security and Efficiency: On-Demand Ratcheting with Security Awareness (Caforio, Durak, Vaudenay). Generic ratcheted-messaging constructions letting users detect attacks and mix light/heavy protocols on demand.
- **127110244.pdf** — On Selective-Opening Security of Deterministic Primitives (O'Neill, Zaheri). First standard-model positive SOA results for deterministic primitives; also proves one-more-RSA hard, yielding first proof of Chaum's blind signatures.
- **127110245.pdf** — Group Encryption: Full Dynamicity, Message Filtering and Code-Based Instantiation (Nguyen, Safavi-Naini, Susilo, Wang, Xu, Zeng). Formalizes fully dynamic group encryption with enrollment/revocation and gives a code-based instantiation.
- **127110252.pdf** — Steel: Composable Hardware-based Stateful and Randomised Functional Encryption (Bhatotia, Kohlweiss, Martinico, Tselekounis). Defines stateful/randomized FE and UC-realizes it via TEE attested execution, strengthening prior definitions.
- **127110258.pdf** — Lattices and Factoring (Invited Talk) (Léo Ducas). One-page invited-talk abstract surveying Schnorr–Adleman and Chor–Rivest connections between lattices, factoring, and BDD. Not a full research paper.
- **12826001.pdf** — White Box Traitor Tracing (Mark Zhandry). First white-box tracing schemes for general obfuscated decoder programs, achieving consistency and privacy properties black-box tracing cannot.
- **12826005.pdf** — Revisiting the Security of DbHtS MACs: Beyond-Birthday-Bound in the Multi-User Setting (Shen, Wang, Gu, Weng). Generic framework proving beyond-birthday-bound multi-user security for double-block Hash-then-Sum MAC variants.
- **12826020.pdf** — Proof-Carrying Data without Succinct Arguments (Bünz, Chiesa, Lin, Mishra, Spooner). Constructs PCD from split accumulation schemes without SNARKs, with a transparent DL-based argument for R1CS.
- **12826028.pdf** — Smoothing Out Binary Linear Codes and Worst-case Sub-exponential Hardness for LPN (Yu, Zhang). Establishes worst-case to average-case hardness reductions for constant-noise LPN via smoothing analysis of binary linear codes.
- **12826030.pdf** — Pushing the Limits of Valiant's Universal Circuits (Liu, Yu, Zhao, Zhang, Liu, Hu). Simpler, tighter, more compact universal circuit constructions improving constant factors and implementations of Valiant's recursive design.
- **12826033.pdf** — Sumcheck Arguments and their Applications (Bootle, Chiesa, Sotiraki). Unifies sumcheck protocols with commitment-folding techniques into one framework, recovering many prior succinct arguments and answering open questions.
- **12826034.pdf** — Secure Wire Shuffling in the Probing Model (Coron, Spignoli). Improves the ISW wire-shuffling side-channel countermeasure to O(t) time with simpler construction, beating masking for AES at t≥6000 probes.
- **12826035.pdf** — The Cost to Break SIKE: A Comparative Hardware-Based Analysis with AES and SHA-3 (Longa, Wang, Szefer). ASIC-based cost model for van Oorschot-Wiener attacks showing SIKE parameters exceed claimed security levels; explores smaller primes.
- **12826041.pdf** — No Time to Hash: On Super-Efficient Entropy Accumulation (Dodis, Guo, Stephens-Davidowitz, Xie). Rigorous analysis of rotation-based RNG state refresh, proving entropy accumulation for 2-monotone input distributions.
