# Batch 125 digest

## Overview
- Batch splits into two clear eras: a 2002–2004 block of applied cryptography (Boneh–Boyen IBE/signatures, signcryption, forward-secure signatures, stream-cipher cryptanalysis, braid-group attacks) and a ~2016 TCC-style block of theory papers (garbled RAM, MPC with identifiable abort, obfuscation, differential privacy, pseudoentropy).
- The first 15 files (99850xxx) form a coherent theory cluster: constant-round RAM-model 2PC, adaptive garbled RAM, secret-key functional encryption implying iO, and concentrated/computational differential privacy.
- ECDLP-relevant items: FGHR13 (symmetries in index calculus for ECDLP) and anon.pdf (breaking the Certicom ECC2K-130 challenge with parallel Pollard rho) — plus Koblitz's textbook on number theory and cryptography.
- Six files (AFproc, CameraDPV, CompressedPairings, DFSproc, ProcEC04, and one section of EUROCRYPT-adjacent material) have garbled/broken text extraction and cannot be summarized.
- Six files are LNCS proceedings front-matter/editorial-board pages (b104116, b105124, b11817, b72231, b75033, b94617) and one (authors.pdf) is a conference author index — none are research papers.
- Non-paper anomalies: PQShield 2022 white paper on post-quantum cryptography (industry white paper, not a research paper) and a 2-page AES/Rijndael extended abstract.

## Papers
- **99850176.pdf** — Constant-Round Maliciously Secure Two-Party Computation in the RAM Model (Carmit Hazay, Avishay Yanai). First constant-round maliciously secure 2PC protocol in the RAM model, built by hardening garbled RAM constructions (Gentry et al., Garg et al.) against malicious adversaries.
- **99850180.pdf** — Adaptive Succinct Garbled RAM or: How To Delegate Your Database (Ran Canetti, Yilei Chen, Justin Holmgren, Mariana Raykova). Garbles a persistent database plus adaptively chosen RAM programs with succinct garbled programs, using iO and a new "adaptive accumulators" primitive; enables database delegation with updates.
- **99850185.pdf** — Concentrated Differential Privacy: Simplifications, Extensions, and Lower Bounds (Mark Bun, Thomas Steinke). Reformulates concentrated differential privacy via Rényi divergence, yielding sharper composition bounds, lower bounds, and a unified "approximate CDP" definition.
- **99850187.pdf** — Separating Computational and Statistical Differential Privacy in the Client-Server Model (Mark Bun, Yi-Hsiu Chen, Salil Vadhan). Shows, from sub-exponential OWFs and zaps, a client-server task achievable with computational DP but infeasible with information-theoretic DP.
- **99850190.pdf** — Targeted Homomorphic Attribute-Based Encryption (Zvika Brakerski, David Cash, Rotem Tsabary, Hoeteck Wee). ABE supporting compact homomorphic evaluation across different attributes when the target policy is known to the evaluator; security from LWE with sub-exponential modulus-to-noise ratio.
- **99850195.pdf** — Cross&Clean: Amortized Garbled Circuits With Constant Overhead (Jesper Buus Nielsen, Claudio Orlandi). Shows that, amortized over many executions, actively secure garbled-circuit 2PC needs only a constant number of garbled circuits in the cut-and-choose approach.
- **99850196.pdf** — Efficient Secure Multiparty Computation with Identifiable Abort (Carsten Baum, Emmanuela Orsini, Peter Scholl). First efficient dishonest-majority MPC with identifiable abort, using information-theoretic homomorphic signatures and SPDZ-style preprocessing.
- **99850198.pdf** — Composable Adaptive Secure Protocols without Setup under Polytime Assumptions (Carmit Hazay, Muthuramakrishnan Venkitasubramaniam). First setup-free, black-box MPC with composable adaptive security from standard assumptions (under "UC with super-polynomial helpers"); also revisits the UC zero-one law.
- **99850202.pdf** — From Cryptomania to Obfustopia through Secret-Key Functional Encryption (Nir Bitansky, Ryo Nishimaki, Alain Passelègue, Daniel Wichs). Shows sub-exponentially secure secret-key functional encryption plus plain PKE implies indistinguishability obfuscation.
- **99850203.pdf** — Secure Obfuscation in a Weak Multilinear Map Model (Sanjam Garg, Eric Miles, Pratyay Mukherjee, Amit Sahai, Akshayaram Srinivasan, Mark Zhandry). New iO candidate proven secure in the weak multilinear map model, the first candidate resisting all known polynomial-time attacks on GGH13 maps.
- **99850205.pdf** — Composable Security in the Tamper-Proof Hardware Model under Minimal Complexity (Carmit Hazay, Antigoni Polychroniadou, Muthuramakrishnan Venkitasubramaniam). Reformulates tamper-proof hardware in the GUC framework and gives two-round GUC-secure 2PC with stateless tokens from one-way functions.
- **99850208.pdf** — Simulating Auxiliary Inputs, Revisited (Maciej Skorski). Improved boosting-based simulator for auxiliary-input problems, fixing a flaw in a TCC'14 paper and beating previous complexity bounds; applies to leakage-resilient stream ciphers.
- **99850210.pdf** — Pseudoentropy: Lower-bounds for Chain Rules and Transformations (Krzysztof Pietrzak, Maciej Skorski). Proves first lower bounds showing existing HILL/Metric entropy transformations are essentially optimal for non-adaptive black-box reductions.
- **99850211.pdf** — Virtual Grey-Boxes Beyond Obfuscation: A Statistical Security Notion for Cryptographic Agents (Shashank Agrawal, Manoj Prabhakaran, Ching-Hua Yu). Extends virtual-grey-box security beyond obfuscation to functional encryption, graded encodings, and bilinear maps, characterizing it via an indistinguishability-preserving notion.
- **99850214.pdf** — Delegating RAM Computations with Adaptive Soundness and Privacy (Prabhanjan Ananth, Yu-Chi Chen, Kai-Min Chung, Huijia Lin, Wei-Kai Lin). First RAM delegation scheme over persistent databases achieving both adaptive soundness and privacy, from iO and DDH.
- **AFproc.pdf** — Text extraction is garbled (broken font encoding); paper unreadable from extract, no summary possible.
- **BBibeEC04.pdf** — Efficient Selective-ID Secure Identity-Based Encryption Without Random Oracles (Dan Boneh, Xavier Boyen). Two efficient IBE schemes proven selective-identity secure without random oracles, one extending to selective-ID hierarchical IBE.
- **BBsigsEC04.pdf** — Short Signatures Without Random Oracles (Dan Boneh, Xavier Boyen). Short signatures provably secure without random oracles under the new Strong Diffie-Hellman assumption, nearly as short as BLS with limited message recovery.
- **CameraDPV.pdf** — Text extraction is garbled (broken font encoding); paper unreadable from extract, no summary possible.
- **CompressedPairings.pdf** — Text extraction is garbled (broken font encoding); presumably on compressed pairings per filename (?), but no summary possible from extract.
- **DFSproc.pdf** — Text extraction is garbled (broken font encoding); paper unreadable from extract, no summary possible.
- **DKea2002.pdf** — Generic Lower Bounds for Root Extraction and Signature Schemes in General Groups (Ivan Damgård, Maciej Koprowski). Proves exponential generic lower bounds for root extraction in unknown-order groups, implying generic security of (strong) RSA; revisits Cramer-Shoup signatures.
- **DRS-ec2004-final.pdf** — Fuzzy Extractors: How to Generate Strong Keys from Biometrics and Other Noisy Data (Yevgeniy Dodis, Leonid Reyzin, Adam Smith). Introduces fuzzy extractors and secure sketches to derive stable, near-uniform keys from noisy non-uniform sources like biometrics.
- **EC02-ClarkJacob.pdf** — Fault Injection and a Timing Channel on an Analysis Technique (John A. Clark, Jeremy L. Jacob). Applies fault-injection and timing ideas to a simulated-annealing attack on Pointcheval's PPP identification schemes, showing all recommended parameter sizes unsafe.
- **EC02final.pdf** — Efficient Generic Forward-Secure Signatures With an Unbounded Number of Time Periods (Tal Malkin, Daniele Micciancio, Sara Miner). First efficient forward-secure signature scheme without a pre-fixed bound on time periods, from any signature scheme, proven in the standard model.
- **EC04Concurrent.pdf** — Concurrent Signatures (Liqun Chen, Caroline Kudla, Kenneth G. Paterson). Introduces concurrent signatures that are ambiguous until a keystone is released, then bind both signers simultaneously; efficient scheme secure in ROM under discrete log.
- **EUROCRYPT.pdf** — Public Key Encryption with Keyword Search (Dan Boneh, Giovanni Di Crescenzo, Rafail Ostrovsky, Giuseppe Persiano). Defines and constructs PEKS, letting a gateway test whether encrypted email contains a keyword (e.g., "urgent") without learning anything else.
- **FGHR13.pdf** — Using Symmetries in the Index Calculus for Elliptic Curves Discrete Logarithm (Jean-Charles Faugère, Pierrick Gaudry, Louise Huot, Guénaël Renault). Exploits symmetries to speed point decomposition (PDP) in Gaudry-style index calculus for ECDLP over extension fields F_{q^n}; Journal of Cryptology 2013.
- **GroupDH_StandardModel.pdf** — Dynamic Group Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange under Standard Assumptions (Emmanuel Bresson, Olivier Chevassut, David Pointcheval). Authenticated dynamic group DH key exchange with refined security model (strong corruption, concurrent sessions), proven secure under DDH in the standard model.
- **HMS_final_1.pdf** — Complete Classification of Bilinear Hard-Core Functions (Thomas Holenstein, Ueli Maurer, Johan Sjödin). Introduces "exponential rank loss" characterizing exactly which bilinear functions are general hard-core functions for any one-way function.
- **Hawkes_Rose_FINAL_VERSION.pdf** — Rewriting Variables: the Complexity of Fast Algebraic Attacks on Stream Ciphers (Philip Hawkes, Gregory G. Rose). Corrects underestimated substitution complexity in fast algebraic attacks on LFSR ciphers, adds FFT speedups, and gives the fastest known pre-computation method.
- **Koblitz.2ndEd.pdf** — A Course in Number Theory and Cryptography, 2nd ed. (Neal Koblitz). Springer graduate textbook (GTM 114) covering number-theoretic foundations of cryptography, including elliptic-curve methods; a book, not a research paper.
- **LL02.pdf** — Potential Weaknesses of the Commutator Key Agreement Protocol based on Braid Groups (Sang Jin Lee, Eonkyung Lee). Reduces breaking the Anshel–Anshel–Goldfeld braid commutator key agreement to list-MSCP in permutation/matrix groups, exposing a weakness from the colored Burau key extractor.
- **Molland_Crypto2004_CorrAttack.pdf** — An Improved Correlation Attack Against Irregular Clocked and Filtered Keystream Generators (Håvard Molland, Tor Helleseth). New key-recovery correlation attack on irregularly clocked, Boolean-filtered LFSR generators, more efficient than prior analyses.
- **PQShield-Quantum-Threat-3-An-Overview-of-Post-Quantum-Cryptography-August-2022.pdf** — Quantum Computing Threat: An Overview of Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQShield Ltd). Industry white paper surveying classical public-key crypto, the quantum threat, and lattice-/code-/hash-based PQC families; not a research paper.
- **ProcEC04.pdf** — Text extraction is garbled (broken font encoding); paper unreadable from extract, no summary possible.
- **Rijndaeleurocrypt.pdf** — AES and the Wide Trail Design Strategy (Joan Daemen, Vincent Rijmen). Two-page extended abstract explaining how Rijndael's wide-trail design strategy bounds differential and linear trail probabilities/correlations.
- **UDVS_asia03_final_1.pdf** — Universal Designated-Verifier Signatures (Ron Steinfeld, Laurence Bull, Huaxiong Wang, Josef Pieprzyk). Defines UDVS, where any signature holder can designate a BLS-compatible signature to a chosen verifier; secure under BDH in the random oracle model.
- **Version courte Format Springer.pdf** — Security of Random Feistel Schemes with 5 or More Rounds (Jacques Patarin). Uses the "coefficients H" technique to prove random Feistel schemes secure against KPA/CPA-2/CPCA-2 at 4/5/6 rounds respectively for m ≪ 2^n.
- **ad_hoc_groups.pdf** — Anonymous Identification in Ad Hoc Groups (Yevgeniy Dodis, Aggelos Kiayias, Antonio Nicolosi, Victor Shoup). Introduces ad-hoc anonymous identification via accumulators with one-way domain; yields constant-size group/ring signatures with protocols independent of group size.
- **adr.pdf** — On the Security of Joint Signature and Encryption (Jee Hea An, Yevgeniy Dodis, Tal Rabin). Formalizes signcryption security (insider/outsider), proves EtS and StE compositions secure, and introduces parallel "commit-then-encrypt-and-sign" composition.
- **anon.pdf** — Breaking ECC2K-130 (authors not visible in extract, "?"). Describes solving Certicom's ECC2K-130 ECDLP challenge with parallelized Pollard rho across CPUs, PS3/Cell clusters, GPUs, and FPGAs, with ASIC cost estimates.
- **article.pdf** — Fast Correlation Attacks: an Algorithmic Point of View (Philippe Chose, Antoine Joux, Michel Mitton). Improves fast correlation attacks on LFSR stream ciphers via better asymptotic algorithms for finding and evaluating parity-checks.
