# Batch 119 digest

## Overview
- Mid-2010s IACR-style crypto papers (9614xxxx/9665xxxx ePrint numbering), heavy on public-key primitives: signature variants (sanitizable, aggregate, attribute-based, functional, hash-based XMSS-T), KDM/circular-security and leakage-resilient PKE, MPC, and functional encryption.
- Strong cryptanalysis thread: breaks of GGH and CLT15 multilinear maps, generalized Coppersmith attacks on RSA variants, NFS discrete-log polynomial selection in non-prime fields, hash-combiner attacks, weak Ring-LWE instances, PMAC analysis, and practical attacks (OpenSSL RNG, s2n "Lucky Microseconds" timing, Streebog/Kuznyechik S-box reverse engineering).
- Notable papers: Bootle et al.'s logarithmic-communication discrete-log ZK argument (inner-product technique, precursor to Bulletproofs); Hu–Jia GGH map break; Cheon et al. CLT15 break; Albrecht–Paterson s2n timing attack.
- Only mild elliptic-curve/pairing presence (SXDH-based schemes, bilinear-map ABS); no ECDLP-focused papers in this batch.
- Anomaly: 96140171.pdf is a 1-page file with no extractable text. No obvious duplicates.

## Papers
- **96140167.pdf** — Efficient Unlinkable Sanitizable Signatures from Signatures with Re-Randomizable Keys (Fleischhacker, Krupp, Malavolta, Schneider, Schröder, Simkin). First efficient unlinkable sanitizable signature scheme, via a new primitive of signatures with re-randomizable keys, instantiated with Schnorr.
- **96140168.pdf** — Nearly Optimal Verifiable Data Streaming (Krupp, Schröder, Simkin, Fiore, Ateniese, Nuernberger). First unbounded verifiable data streaming schemes in the standard model, via new Chameleon Vector Commitments with constant-size commitments.
- **96140169.pdf** — On the Hardness of Proving CCA-Security of Signed ElGamal (Bernhard, Fischlin, Warinschi). Gives meta-reduction evidence that proving Signed ElGamal IND-CCA in the random oracle model is hard, excluding large classes of reductions.
- **96140171.pdf** — Anomaly: 1-page PDF with no extractable text; cannot be identified.
- **96140173.pdf** — Robust Secret Sharing Schemes Against Local Adversaries (Bishop, Pastro). Near-optimal share-size robust secret sharing when corruptions are one less than honest players, under a non-communicating local adversary model.
- **96140174.pdf** — On the Key Dependent Message Security of the Fujisaki-Okamoto Constructions (Kitagawa, Matsuda, Hanaoka, Tanaka). Shows FO'99 transform is not KDM-CCA secure in general, while the 2013 hybrid variant is.
- **96140175.pdf** — Asynchronous Secure Multiparty Computation in Constant Time (Cohen). First constant-time asynchronous MPC protocol, using threshold FHE to remove king-slaves interaction, secure for t < n/3.
- **96140177.pdf** — Identity-Based Cryptosystems and Quadratic Residuosity (Joye). Reveals hidden algebraic structure in Cocks' QR-based IBE, enabling homomorphic evaluation and ciphertext anonymization, disproving its presumed non-homomorphicity.
- **96140179.pdf** — Mitigating Multi-Target Attacks in Hash-based Signatures (Hülsing, Rijneveld, Song). Introduces XMSS-T with tight security via new multi-target hash-function notions; smaller signatures, also improves SPHINCS; includes quantum security estimates.
- **96140180.pdf** — Deniable Functional Encryption (De Caro, Iovino, O'Neill). Initiates deniable functional encryption in two models (authority-assisted and multi-distributional), with constructions for general circuits.
- **96140184.pdf** — KDM-Security via Homomorphic Smooth Projective Hashing (Wee). Unified frameworks for KDM-secure PKE and dual-mode UC oblivious transfer from homomorphic smooth projective hashing under d-linear, QR, and DCR assumptions.
- **96140190.pdf** — Delegatable Functional Signatures (Backes, Meiser, Schröder). Introduces DFS unifying functional, policy-based, sanitizable, identity-based, and blind signatures; feasibility from one-way functions or doubly-enhanced TDPs, plus impossibility results.
- **96140194.pdf** — Easing Coppersmith Methods using Analytic Combinatorics (Benhamouda, Chevalier, Thillard, Vergnaud). Analytic-combinatorics toolbox for analyzing Coppersmith lattice attacks with many variables; applications to weak PRGs, RSA key generation, and randomness.
- **96140196.pdf** — Chosen-Ciphertext Security from Subset Sum (Faust, Masny, Venturi). IND-CCA public-key encryption polynomially equivalent to Subset Sum hardness, improving Lyubashevsky–Palacio–Segev's CPA scheme via Micciancio–Peikert trapdoors.
- **96140208.pdf** — Attribute-Based Signatures for Circuits from Bilinear Map (Sakai, Attrapadung, Hanaoka). Practically efficient ABS for arbitrary circuit predicates from SXDH, combining Groth–Sahai and Groth–Ostrovsky–Sahai proofs.
- **96140209.pdf** — Reducing Depth in Constrained PRFs: From Bit-Fixing to NC1 (Chandran, Raghuraman, Vinayagamurthy). Lowers required multilinearity degree for constrained PRFs/ABE and builds NC1 constrained PRFs from single-bit-fixing PRFs with key-homomorphic properties.
- **96140210.pdf** — On Generic Constructions of Circularly-Secure, Leakage-Resilient Public-Key Encryption Schemes (Hajiabadi, Kapron, Srinivasan). Generic transform from homomorphic reproducible private-key encryption to KDM-projection-secure, leakage-resilient, auxiliary-input-secure PKE.
- **96140217.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Public-Key Encryption from Obfuscation (Dachman-Soled, Gordon, Liu, O'Neill, Zhou). Modular obfuscation-based construction achieving continual leakage resilience including leakage on key-update randomness.
- **96140226.pdf** — Adaptively Secure Multi-Party Computation from LWE (via Equivocal FHE) (Damgård, Polychroniadou, Rao). Constant-round UC adaptively secure MPC against all-but-one corruptions from LWE, with communication independent of circuit size.
- **96140228.pdf** — Non-Malleable Functions and Their Applications (Chen, Qin, Zhang, Deng, Chow). Formalizes non-malleable deterministic functions (relaxing NMOWHFs), studies relations to one-wayness, and gives applications.
- **96140235.pdf** — Universally Composable Authentication and Key-Exchange with Global PKI (Canetti, Shahaf, Vald). UC framework for PKI-based authentication and key exchange where the PKI is a pre-existing global setup, proving security of signature-based protocols.
- **96140236.pdf** — Fault-Tolerant Aggregate Signatures (Hartung, Kaidel, Koch, Koch, Rupp). Aggregate signatures whose verification identifies the validly signed subset despite up to a bounded number of faulty signatures; generic construction from ordinary aggregate signatures.
- **96140237.pdf** — Identity-based Hierarchical Key-insulated Encryption without Random Oracles (Watanabe, Shikata). First hierarchical IKE secure in the standard model under SXDH; depth-one case gives first IKE with constant-size parameters.
- **96140239.pdf** — Very-efficient simulatable flipping of many coins into a well (Brandão). Near-optimal two-party parallel coin-flipping and a UC commitment scheme via an expand-mask-hash technique combining extractable and equivocable commitments.
- **96140240.pdf** — How to Generalize RSA Cryptanalyses (Takayasu, Kunihiro). Systematically transfers standard-RSA lattice attacks to N = p^r q variants (Takagi, prime-power RSA), yielding simpler proofs and improved small-exponent and partial-key-exposure attacks.
- **96650103.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of GGH Map (Hu, Jia). Efficient attacks on GGH multilinear maps: breaks multipartite key exchange and the K-GMDDH assumption via modified encoding/zero-testing, and an X3C-based witness encryption instance.
- **96650114.pdf** — Constant-round Leakage-resilient Zero-knowledge from Collision Resistance (Kiyoshima). Constant-round leakage-resilient ZK arguments from collision-resistant hashing alone, simultaneously secure for ZK and soundness under leakage.
- **96650118.pdf** — An Analysis of OpenSSL's Random Number Generator (Strenzke). Demonstrates OpenSSL RNG weaknesses: entropy not effectively mixed, low-entropy states recoverable, and key entropy capped near 80 bits despite large seed input.
- **96650120.pdf** — New Complexity Trade-Offs for the (Multiple) Number Field Sieve Algorithm in Non-Prime Fields (Sarkar, Singh). New NFS polynomial selection method generalizing GJL and Conjugation, improving discrete-log complexity in extension fields for certain p ranges.
- **96650121.pdf** — Provable Security Evaluation of Structures against Impossible Differential and Zero Correlation Linear Cryptanalysis (Sun, Liu, Guo, Rijmen, Li). Provable bounds on impossible differentials/zero-correlation hulls for SPN and SP-Feistel structures independent of S-box choices.
- **96650124.pdf** — Pseudorandom Functions in Almost Constant Depth from Low-Noise LPN (Yu, Steinberger). Parallelizable low-depth PRFs from the low-noise LPN assumption, simplifying LWE-based constructions to AND/XOR operations.
- **96650127.pdf** — From Improved Leakage Detection to the Detection of Points of Interests in Leakage Traces (Durvaux, Standaert). Compares t-test vs correlation leakage detection via SNR, and derives an improved t-test better localizing exploitable points of interest in side-channel traces.
- **96650132.pdf** — New Attacks on the Concatenation and XOR Hash Combiners (Dinur). First second-preimage attack faster than 2^n on the concatenation combiner (optimal 2^{3n/4}) and an improved 2^{2n/3} preimage attack on the XOR combiner.
- **96650133.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the New CLT Multilinear Map over the Integers (Cheon, Fouque, Lee, Minaud, Ryu). Polynomial-time zeroizing-style attacks recovering all secret parameters of CLT15, fully breaking it for virtually all applications.
- **96650134.pdf** — Sanitization of FHE Ciphertexts (Ducas, Stehlé). Efficient ciphertext sanitization giving circuit privacy for lattice-based FHE without noise flooding, via homomorphic decryption plus re-randomization.
- **96650135.pdf** — Reverse-Engineering the S-Box of Streebog, Kuznyechik and STRIBOBr1 (Biryukov, Perrin, Udovenko). Recovers the hidden Feistel-like structure of the Russian standard S-box, enabling 2.5×/8× hardware gains but revealing weak 4-bit components.
- **96650136.pdf** — Lucky Microseconds: A Timing Attack on Amazon's s2n Implementation of TLS (Albrecht, Paterson). Lucky 13 variant plus countermeasure bypass achieves plaintext recovery against s2n's CBC ciphersuites despite prior audits.
- **96650140.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Tight Cryptographic Reductions (Bader, Jager, Li, Schäge). New meta-reduction technique proving tightness impossibility beyond signatures, including unavoidable single-to-multi-user security loss and NIKE lower bounds.
- **96650150.pdf** — Towards Stream Ciphers for Efficient FHE with Low-Noise Ciphertexts (Méaux, Journault, Standaert, Carlet). New stream cipher design for FHE transciphering combining constant small noise of block ciphers with stream-cipher capacity, using Boolean filter functions.
- **96650166.pdf** — On the Influence of Message Length in PMAC's Security Bounds (Luykx, Preneel, Szepieniec, Yasuda). Analyzes generic PMAC length dependence and shows the Gray-code instantiation is comparatively insecure via a concrete attack.
- **96650173.pdf** — Circuit Compilers with O(1/log(n)) Leakage Rate (Andrychowicz, Dziembowski, Faust). Probing-secure circuit compilers with perfect security and O(1/log n) leakage rate, and constant rate for affine circuits and secret refreshing.
- **96650177.pdf** — On the Composition of Two-Prover Commitments, and Applications to Multi-Round Relativistic Commitments (Fehr, Fillinger). Composition theorem for two-prover commitments improving Lunghi et al.'s relativistic scheme error growth from double-exponential to linear.
- **96650178.pdf** — On the Power of Hierarchical Identity-Based Encryption (Mahmoody, Mohammed). Proves no fully black-box construction of collision-resistant hashing from HIBE, yielding separations for IBE/CCA-PKE versus FHE and CRH.
- **96650180.pdf** — Safely Exporting Keys from Secure Channels: On the Security of EAP-TLS and TLS Key Exporters (Brzuska, Jacobsen, Stebila). Proves EAP-TLS-style key export from "TLS-like" ACCE protocols yields secure AKE, via a semi-black-box encryption-based key-checking technique.
- **96650182.pdf** — Hash-Function based PRFs: AMAC and its Multi-User Security (Bellare, Bernstein, Tessaro). Proves PRF security of AMAC (keyed MD-hash plus truncation, as in Ed25519) with multi-user security nearly matching single-user.
- **96650184.pdf** — Polytopic Cryptanalysis (Tiessen). Generalizes differential cryptanalysis to interdependencies among larger text sets; impossible-polytopic attacks on round-reduced DES and AES compete with or beat prior results.
- **96650195.pdf** — Anonymous Traitor Tracing: How to Embed Arbitrary Information in a Key (Nishimaki, Wichs, Zhandry). Traitor tracing where user identity information is embedded directly in decryption keys and recoverable by tracing, removing the distributor's record-keeping.
- **96650200.pdf** — Efficient Zero-Knowledge Arguments for Arithmetic Circuits in the Discrete Log Setting (Bootle, Cerulli, Chaidos, Groth, Petit). Logarithmic-communication ZK argument for arithmetic circuit satisfiability from discrete log, via an inner-product argument on Pedersen commitments (Bulletproofs precursor).
- **96650208.pdf** — Provably Weak Instances of Ring-LWE Revisited (Castryck, Iliashenko, Vercauteren). Solves search RLWE for the Elias et al. weak polynomial families with 7 samples at 100% success, for arbitrary modulus, via skewed error distributions.
- **96650213.pdf** — 10-Round Feistel is Indifferentiable from an Ideal Cipher (Dachman-Soled, Katz, Thiruvengadam). Improves Coron et al.'s 14-round bound, showing 10 Feistel rounds with independent keyed random round functions suffice for indifferentiability.
