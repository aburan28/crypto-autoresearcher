# Batch 021 digest

## Overview
- The batch is dominated by what appears to be EUROCRYPT 2020 proceedings (the contiguous 12105xxx series): symmetric cryptanalysis, lattice/post-quantum crypto, ZK proofs/SNARKs, MPC, and secret sharing.
- Notable papers: Marlin (universal/updatable zkSNARKs), OptORAMa (optimal ORAM), Transparent SNARKs from DARK compilers (Supersonic), blind Schnorr signatures in the AGM, and Grover-oracle cost estimates for AES/LowMC relevant to NIST PQC security categories.
- ECC/ECDLP-specific work is sparse: double-base chains for EC scalar multiplication, SPA-resistant genus-2 HECC scalar multiplication, low-weight DLP/subset-sum algorithms, and memory-tightness/scalability analyses of (Hashed) ElGamal over elliptic curves.
- Several side-channel/fault papers: masking with PRGs, fault template attacks, hedged Fiat-Shamir under faults, Gram-Schmidt norm leakage key recovery against Falcon/DLP.
- Anomalies: 11935230.pdf is LNCS series front matter (editorial board page, not a research paper); 12105233.pdf has no extracted text.

## Papers
- **119210414.pdf** — Efficient Noninteractive Certification of RSA Moduli and Beyond (Goldberg, Reyzin, Sagga, Baldimtsi). Noninteractive ZK proof that an RSA key defines a permutation over Z_N, plus certifications for Paillier suitability, two-prime products, and Blum integers.
- **11935230.pdf** — Not a research paper: Lecture Notes in Computer Science vol. 4284 front matter (editorial board listing only).
- **12.pdf** — SPA-resistant Scalar Multiplication on Hyperelliptic Curve Cryptosystems (Akishita, Katagi, Kitamura). Genus-2 HECC scalar multiplication via divisor decomposition and joint regular form; ~19% faster with SPA resistance.
- **12105102.pdf** — Mind the Composition: Birthday Bound Attacks on EWCDMD and SoKAC21 (Mridul Nandi). Birthday-bound attacks invalidating the claimed n-bit and 2n/3-bit security of two composition-based MAC/PRF constructions.
- **12105107.pdf** — Double-Base Chains for Scalar Multiplications on Elliptic Curves (Yu, Al Musa, Li). First polynomial-time counting algorithm for DBCs, an average Hamming weight lower bound, and a faster optimal-DBC generation algorithm.
- **12105111.pdf** — Low Weight Discrete Logarithm and Subset Sum in 2^0.65n with Polynomial Memory (Esser, May). New polynomial-memory collision algorithms improving BCJ for low-Hamming-weight DLP and random subset sum.
- **12105113.pdf** — Private Information Retrieval with Sublinear Online Time (Corrigan-Gibbs, Kogan). First PIR with sublinear online lookups via an offline/online model; proves optimal communication/time trade-offs.
- **12105117.pdf** — Side-channel Masking with Pseudo-Random Generator (Coron, Greuet, Zeitoun). Reduces randomness of high-order ISW masking to Õ(t²) bits using multiple independent robust PRGs, faster generation.
- **12105121.pdf** — Blackbox Secret Sharing Revisited: A Coding-Theoretic Approach (Cramer, Xing). Coding-theoretic treatment of blackbox secret sharing yielding expansionless near-threshold schemes.
- **12105126.pdf** — Tight Security Bounds for Double-block Hash-then-Sum MACs (Kim, Lee, Lee). Proves 2^{3n/4}-query PRF security for DbHtS MACs (PMAC-Plus, 3kf9, LightMAC-Plus), closing the gap with best attacks.
- **12105134.pdf** — On the Quantum Complexity of the Continuous Hidden Subgroup Problem (de Boer, Ducas, Fehr). Detailed qubit/resource analysis of continuous HSP algorithms underlying ideal-lattice cryptanalysis.
- **12105164.pdf** — Statistical ZAP Arguments (Badrinarayanan, Fernando, Jain, Khurana, Sahai). First two-message public-coin statistical WI arguments for NP from quasi-polynomial LWE; also SPS statistical ZK.
- **12105165.pdf** — Tight Time-Space Lower Bounds for Finding Multiple Collision Pairs (Dinur). Proves parallel collision search optimal (T²·S ≈ C²·N); restricted-model optimality for double-encryption attacks.
- **12105173.pdf** — Integral Matrix Gram Root and Lattice Gaussian Sampling without Floats (Ducas, Galbraith, Prest, Yu). Lattice Gaussian sampling using integral matrix decompositions, eliminating floating-point arithmetic.
- **12105176.pdf** — The Retracing Boomerang Attack (Dunkelman, Keller, Ronen, Shamir). New boomerang variant discarding data to force ciphertext-side equalities, boosting distinguisher probability from p²q² to p²q.
- **12105178.pdf** — Optimal Merging in Quantum k-xor and k-sum Algorithms (Naya-Plasencia, Schrottenloher). Optimal "merging tree" framework for quantum/classical k-xor; improved algorithms for subset-sum, LPN, multiple encryption.
- **12105182.pdf** — Implementing Grover Oracles for Quantum Key Search on AES and LowMC (Jaques, Naehrig, Roetteler, Virdia). Depth-optimized Grover oracles lowering quantum key-search cost estimates behind NIST PQC security categories.
- **12105185.pdf** — Marlin: Preprocessing zkSNARKs with Universal and Updatable SRS (Chiesa, Hu, Maller, Mishra, Vesely, Ward). Algebraic holographic proofs yield preprocessing zkSNARKs improving on Sonic in all efficiency parameters.
- **12105188.pdf** — Private Aggregation from Fewer Anonymous Messages (Ghazi, Manurangsi, Pagh, Velingker). Improved analysis of split-and-mix anonymous aggregation cutting messages by Θ(log n); near-optimal lower bounds.
- **12105190.pdf** — Two-Round Oblivious Transfer from CDH or LPN (Döttling, Garg, Hajiabadi, Masny, Wichs). First malicious UC-secure two-round OT under CDH or LPN via "elementary OT" transformations.
- **12105193.pdf** — New Constructions of Statistical NIZKs (Libert, Passelègue, Wee, Wu). Statistical designated-verifier NIZKs from k-Lin in pairing-free groups and related new statistical NIZK techniques.
- **12105197.pdf** — Combiners for Functional Encryption, Unconditionally (Jain, Manohar, Sahai). First unconditional FE combiner, implying universal functional encryption secure if any FE scheme exists.
- **12105202.pdf** — On the Streaming Indistinguishability of a Random Permutation and a Random Function (Dinur). Tight, conjecture-free bound on streaming switching lemma via communication-complexity reduction.
- **12105206.pdf** — Fault Template Attacks on Block Ciphers Exploiting Fault Propagation (Saha, Bag, Basu Roy, Patranabis, Mukhopadhyay). Data-dependent fault observability enables blind middle-round fault template attacks defeating fault and power countermeasures.
- **12105226.pdf** — On the Memory-Tightness of Hashed ElGamal (Ghoshal, Tessaro). Lower bounds showing straightline black-box reductions for Hashed ElGamal need memory linear in adversary queries (generic group model).
- **12105227.pdf** — Optimal Broadcast Encryption from Pairings and LWE (Agrawal, Yamada). Fully collusion-resistant broadcast encryption with small parameters from bilinear maps + LWE via compact CP-ABE.
- **12105229.pdf** — On a Generalization of SPNs: The HADES Design Strategy (Grassi, Lüftenegger, Rechberger, Rotaru, Schofnegger). Non-uniform S-box layer design strategy with security arguments against differential/linear and algebraic attacks.
- **12105231.pdf** — Security of Hedged Fiat–Shamir Signatures under Fault Attacks (Aranha, Orlandi, Takahashi, Zaverucha). Formal bit-tampering fault model and security analysis of hedged (deterministic-with-nonce) Fiat–Shamir signatures like EdDSA variants.
- **12105233.pdf** — No text extracted; content could not be identified.
- **12105241.pdf** — Key Recovery from Gram–Schmidt Norm Leakage in Hash-and-Sign Signatures over NTRU Lattices (Fouque, Kirchner, Tibouchi, Wallet, Yu). Side-channel leakage of Gram–Schmidt norms in Falcon/DLP Gaussian sampling enables full secret-key recovery.
- **12105242.pdf** — Adaptively Secure ABE for DFA from k-Lin and More (Gong, Wee). First adaptively secure ABE for DFA from k-Lin, plus first ABE for NFA and compact ABE for branching programs.
- **12105250.pdf** — Blind Schnorr Signatures and Signed ElGamal Encryption in the Algebraic Group Model (Fuchsbauer, Plouviez, Seurin). Rigorous AGM+ROM proofs for Schnorr, blind Schnorr (under one-more DL and ROS), plus a ROS-hardened signing variant.
- **12105253.pdf** — Lower Bounds for Leakage-Resilient Secret Sharing (Nielsen, Simkin). Information-theoretic lower bounds on share size and randomness for leakage-resilient secret sharing; implications for Shamir's scheme.
- **12105255.pdf** — Secure Multi-party Quantum Computation with a Dishonest Majority (Dulek, Grilo, Jeffery, Majenz, Schaffner). Extends two-party quantum MPC to any k players secure against k−1 colluders, with efficient Clifford-code verification.
- **12105262.pdf** — Sigma Protocols for MQ, PKP and SIS, and Fishy Signature Schemes (Beullens). Sigma protocols with low soundness error for MQ/PKP/SIS yielding efficient Fiat–Shamir signatures (MUDFISH, SUSHSYFISH).
- **12105267.pdf** — Key-Homomorphic Pseudorandom Functions from LWE with Small Modulus (Sam Kim). New lattice PRFs with polynomial modulus and key-homomorphism, via a Learning-with-Rounding-and-Errors variant.
- **12105271.pdf** — Stacked Garbling for Disjunctive Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Heath, Kolesnikov). Garbled-circuit ZK where communication scales with the longest conditional branch, up to m× improvement for m branches.
- **12105280.pdf** — Lightweight Authenticated Encryption Mode Suitable for Threshold Implementation (Naito, Sasaki, Sugawara). TBC-based AE modes PFB_Plus and PFB breaking the 3-share barrier for first-order threshold implementations, with proofs.
- **12105281.pdf** — On Instantiating the Algebraic Group Model from Falsifiable Assumptions (Agrikola, Hofheinz, Kastner). Standard-model group (from iO) where every algorithm is algebraic, transporting AGM results to falsifiable assumptions.
- **12105287.pdf** — How to Extract Useful Randomness from Unreliable Sources (Aggarwal, Obremski, Ribeiro, Siniscalchi, Visconti). SHELA source model for extraction when many weak sources are adversarially corrupted; impossibility and construction results.
- **12105289.pdf** — Everybody's a Target: Scalability in Public-Key Encryption (Auerbach, Giacon, Kiltz). Analyzes multi-instance scaling factors of PKE (e.g., EC Hashed ElGamal granularity) quantifying mass-surveillance vulnerability.
- **12105292.pdf** — OptORAMa: Optimal Oblivious RAM (Asharov, Komargodski, Lin, Nayak, Peserico, Shi). First ORAM achieving O(log N) amortized overhead, matching the known lower bound.
- **12105293.pdf** — Improving Key-Recovery in Linear Attacks: Application to 28-round PRESENT (Flórez-Gutiérrez, Naya-Plasencia). Matrix-based FFT key-recovery generalization exploiting key schedule; first attack on 28-round PRESENT.
- **12105294.pdf** — (One) Failure Is Not an Option: Bootstrapping the Search for Failures in Lattice-Based Encryption (D'Anvers, Rossi, Virdia). Directional failure boosting accelerates finding decryption-failure ciphertexts in Kyber/Saber-like schemes.
- **12105299.pdf** — Which Languages Have 4-Round Fully Black-Box Zero-Knowledge Arguments from One-Way Functions? (Hazay, Pass, Venkitasubramaniam). 4-round fully black-box ZK from OWFs implies L ∈ MA; resolves round complexity for NP.
- **12105300.pdf** — Modeling for Three-Subset Division Property without Unknown Subset (Hao, Leander, Meier, Todo, Wang). Improved three-subset division-property modeling yielding better cube attacks on Trivium and Grain-128AEAD.
- **12105301.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation Without Maps: Attacks and Fixes for Noisy Linear FE (Agrawal, Pellet-Mary). Cryptanalysis of NLinFE lattice assumptions underlying map-free iO, with attacks and repaired parameters.
- **12105312.pdf** — Low Error Efficient Computational Extractors in the CRS Model (Garg, Kalai, Khurana). Explicit two-source and non-malleable extractors with negligible error for low min-entropy sources under CRS-model assumptions.
- **12105321.pdf** — The Price of Active Security in Cryptographic Protocols (Hazay, Venkitasubramaniam, Weiss). First constant-overhead actively secure dishonest-majority MPC over arbitrary fields via a passive-to-active compiler for F_MULT.
- **12105324.pdf** — Transparent SNARKs from DARK Compilers (Bünz, Fisch, Szepieniec). Polynomial commitment scheme (DARK) from groups of unknown order; transparent SNARKs via compiling Polynomial IOPs like PLONK.
