# Batch 041 digest

## Overview
- All 50 entries are genuine research papers, apparently from a 2022-era theory-crypto conference batch (CRYPTO/TCC/ASIACRYPT-style); no non-paper content or duplicates found.
- First block (137470xxx, 35 papers): foundations of cryptography — secure computation complexity (sublinear MPC, SNIR/SNIS decidability, OT complexity), zero-knowledge and proof systems (IOPs, SNARGs, NIZKs), and lower bounds (VRFs, RBE, CGKA, PPAD).
- Second block (137910xxx, 15 papers): symmetric-key cryptanalysis and design (Salsa/ChaCha, SKINNYe-64-256, 3kf9 MAC, deck functions, truncated permutation) plus applied/protocol papers (TLS 1.3 key schedule, puncturable key wrapping).
- Elliptic-curve-relevant highlights: ECFFT Part II (scalable transparent proofs over all large fields via elliptic curves), SwiftEC (faster indifferentiable hashing to elliptic curves), and improved Coppersmith bounds for the EC Hidden Number Problem in ECDH side-channel settings.
- Post-quantum / quantum themes recur: isogeny-based trapdoor claw-free functions, classically verifiable NIZK for QMA, post-quantum insecurity from LWE, quantum analysis of memory-hard functions.
- Processing note: the chunk file was not UTF-8 (MacRoman-encoded); a decoded copy was used for reading. Not a content anomaly.

## Papers
- **137470079.pdf** — Anonymous Whistleblowing over Authenticated Channels (Agrikola, Couteau, Maier). Introduces "anonymous transfer" for whistleblowing without trusted parties; proves impossibility of asymptotically secure versions and gives a fine-grained heuristic construction.
- **137470080.pdf** — Steganography-Free Zero-Knowledge (Abdolmaleki, Fleischhacker, Goyal, Jain, Malavolta). Circumvents the known impossibility of preventing steganographic leakage via a non-interactive preprocessing model, yielding steganography-free zero-knowledge from standard assumptions.
- **137470081.pdf** — Candidate Trapdoor Claw-Free Functions from Group Actions (Alamati, Malavolta, Rahimi). First non-lattice TCF candidate, built from isogeny-based group actions with deterministic evaluation; applied to classical verification of quantum computation (test of a qubit).
- **137470083.pdf** — The Price of Verifiability: Lower Bounds for Verifiable Random Functions (Brandt, Hofheinz, Kastner, Ünal). Framework for pairing-based VRFs proving that short proofs and reductions to simple assumptions cannot coexist, explaining limitations of known VRFs.
- **137470086.pdf** — On the Worst-Case Inefficiency of CGKA (Bienstock, Dodis, Garg, Grogan, Hajiabadi, Rösler). Shows known Continuous Group Key Agreement protocols (basis of MLS secure messaging) cannot achieve worst-case efficiency guarantees beyond trivial bounds.
- **137470089.pdf** — A Toolbox for Barriers on Interactive Oracle Proofs (Arnon, Bhangale, Chiesa, Yogev). IOP-to-IOP transformations (length/round reduction, derandomization) yielding barriers: low-error IOPs are as hard as low-error PCPs, plus limits on quasilinear and binary-alphabet IOPs.
- **137470096.pdf** — SCALES: MPC with Small Clients and Larger Ephemeral Servers (Acharya, Hazay, Kolesnikov, Prabhakaran). Modifies the YOSO model so only servers are ephemeral, enabling concretely efficient MPC outsourcing under adaptive corruption via rerandomizable garbling schemes.
- **137470097.pdf** — Sublinear Secure Computation from New Assumptions (Boyle, Couteau, Meyer). Sublinear-communication secure evaluation of layered circuits from decomposable rate-1 batch OT, yielding feasibility from Quadratic Residuosity plus LPN.
- **137470099.pdf** — Scalable and Transparent Proofs over All Large Fields, via Elliptic Curves (ECFFT Part II) (Ben-Sasson, Carmon, Kopparty, Levit). Constructs scalable, transparent IOPs over any sufficiently large finite field (not just FFT-friendly fields) using elliptic-curve-based FFT techniques.
- **137470100.pdf** — A Tight Computational Indistinguishability Bound of Product Distributions (Geier). Proves the computational analog of the tight statistical bound dX+dY−dX·dY for product distributions, with applications to amplifying weak oblivious transfer.
- **137470101.pdf** — Lower Bounds for the Number of Decryption Updates in Registration-Based Encryption (Mahmoody, Qi, Rahimi). Proves an almost-tight lower bound showing the logarithmic number of decryption updates in RBE schemes is essentially necessary.
- **137470105.pdf** — PPAD is as Hard as LWE and Iterated Squaring (Bitansky, Choudhuri, Holmgren, Kamath, Lombardi, Paneth, Rothblum). Bases PPAD hardness (Nash equilibrium computation) on standard LWE plus iterated squaring, improving prior sub-exponential or obfuscation-heavy assumptions.
- **137470109.pdf** — Universal Reductions: Reductions Relative to Stateful Oracles (Chan, Freitag, Pass). Framework modeling attackers with access to arbitrary unbounded stateful "Nature"; gives impossibility and feasibility results probing how much cryptography needs the extended Church-Turing hypothesis.
- **137470111.pdf** — Poly Onions: Achieving Anonymity in the Presence of Churn (Ando, Christ, Lysyanskaya, Malkin). First formal treatment of onion routing over multiple runs on dynamic networks with churn; defines security/anonymity and constructs the "Poly Onions" primitive realizing them.
- **137470112.pdf** — Fully Succinct Batch Arguments for NP from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Garg, Sheridan, Waters, Wu). Direct construction of fully succinct batch arguments for NP supporting unboundedly many statements, from iO and one-way functions.
- **137470113.pdf** — Adaptive versus Static Multi-oracle Algorithms, and Quantum Security of a Split-key PRF (Don, Fehr, Huang). Compiler converting adaptive multi-oracle algorithms to static ones with mild query blow-up; applied to prove QROM security of a hash-based split-key PRF for KEM combiners.
- **137470114.pdf** — IBE with Incompressible Master Secret and Small Identity Secrets (Döttling, Garg, Sekar, Wang). Initiates big-key identity-based encryption: an incompressible master secret resists exfiltration while per-identity secret keys stay small.
- **137470115.pdf** — Secure Sampling with Sublinear Communication (Choi, Dachman-Soled, Gordon, Liu, Yerukhimovich). Two-party protocols for secure L1/L2 sampling with sublinear communication; impossibility for general product sampling and application to differentially-private data release.
- **137470116.pdf** — Secure Non-interactive Simulation from Arbitrary Joint Distributions (Khorasgani, Maji, Nguyen). Characterizes which source correlations enable SNIS of binary symmetric/erasure correlations, with rate bounds and computer-assisted discovery of optimal non-linear protocols.
- **137470118.pdf** — Leakage-resilient Linear Secret-sharing against arbitrary Bounded-size Leakage Family (Maji, Nguyen, Paskin-Cherniavsky, Suad, Wang, Ye, Yu). Random Massey and Shamir schemes are leakage-resilient against bounded-size joint leakage families, enabling leakage-resilient secure multiplication with near-optimal parameters.
- **137470121.pdf** — Oblivious-Transfer Complexity of Noisy Coin-Toss via Secure Zero Communication Reductions (Goyal, Narayanan, Prabhakaran). Tight Θ(log 1/p) OT-complexity bound for p-noisy coin-tossing — the first OT lower bound independent of input/output length.
- **137470124.pdf** — Secure Non-Interactive Reducibility is Decidable (Bhushan, Misra, Narayanan, Prabhakaran). Shows existence of a secure non-interactive reduction between any pair of two-party correlations is algorithmically decidable, via spectral analysis and a new junta theorem.
- **137470128.pdf** — ABE for Circuits with Constant-Size Secret Keys and Adaptive Security (Li, Lin, Luo). First key-policy ABE for circuits with constant-size keys (three group elements) and first ciphertext-policy ABE for formulae with both constant keys and succinct ciphertexts.
- **137470130.pdf** — Parallelizable Delegation from LWE (Freitag, Pass, Sirkin). First time-tight non-interactive delegation scheme for P (SPARG) from polynomial hardness of LWE, with time-independent variant and applications to VDFs.
- **137470131.pdf** — Verifiable Private Information Retrieval (Ben-David, Kalai, Paneth). Introduces vPIR, where a server proves properties of its database to the client with sublinear communication; constructions from batch arguments, plus an equivalence between batch arguments and quasi-arguments.
- **137470132.pdf** — Permissionless Clock Synchronization with Public Setup (Garay, Kiayias, Shen). First proof-of-work-based permissionless clock synchronization protocol tolerating fluctuating participation, yielding a PoW ledger without a global clock.
- **137470133.pdf** — Universally Composable Σ-protocols in the Global Random-Oracle Model (Lysyanskaya, Rosenbloom). Identifies minimal properties for GUC-secure NIZK proofs of knowledge and gives efficient constructions in restricted programmable and observable global random-oracle models.
- **137470134.pdf** — Rate-1 Incompressible Encryption from Standard Assumptions (Branco, Döttling, Dujmović). Simple framework achieving rate-1 incompressible encryption (security despite later key exposure, given bounded adversary memory) from DDH plus DCR or LWE.
- **137470137.pdf** — On Perfectly Secure Two-Party Computation for Symmetric Functionalities with Correlated Randomness (Alon, Nissenbaum, Omri, Paskin-Cherniavsky, Patra). Studies feasibility of perfectly secure malicious-security two-party computation for symmetric functionalities in plain and correlated-randomness models.
- **137470139.pdf** — On Black-Box Constructions of Time and Space Efficient Sublinear Arguments from Symmetric-Key Primitives (Bangalore, Bhadauria, Hazay, Venkitasubramaniam). First complexity-preserving sublinear zero-knowledge argument black-box from collision-resistant hashing, with prover space matching the NP relation's space.
- **137470140.pdf** — Post-Quantum Insecurity from LWE (Lombardi, Mook, Quach, Wichs). Contrived constructions of PRFs, encryption, MACs, and signatures proven classically secure under LWE via black-box reductions yet broken by few-query quantum attacks, separating classical from post-quantum security.
- **137470143.pdf** — The Parallel Reversible Pebbling Game: Analyzing the Post-Quantum Security of iMHFs (Blocki, Holman, Lee). Introduces a reversible pebbling model to bound quantum circuit space-time cost for attacks on data-independent memory-hard functions; classical pebbling strategies do not directly transfer.
- **137470144.pdf** — One-Time Programs from Commodity Hardware (Eldridge, Goel, Green, Jain, Zinkus). Realizes one-time programs for general functionalities from widely available counter lockboxes (password-guessing-limited key stores), with techniques to minimize the number of lockboxes.
- **137470145.pdf** — How to Obfuscate MPC Inputs (McQuoid, Rosulek, Xu). Introduces input obfuscation for two-party computation (io2PC): Alice's encoded input yields only oracle access under compromise; constructions for VBB-obfuscatable functions in ROM and generic group models.
- **137470147.pdf** — Four-Round Black-Box Non-Malleable Schemes from One-Way Permutations (Ciampi, Orsini, Siniscalchi). First four-round non-malleable commitment from black-box one-to-one one-way functions (prior needed >16 rounds), via non-malleable zero-knowledge with respect to commitments.
- **137910004.pdf** — Latin Dances Reloaded: Improved Cryptanalysis against Salsa and ChaCha, and the proposal of Forró (Coutinho, Passos, Grados Vásquez, de Mendonça, de Sousa, Borges). Improved differential-linear distinguishers for ChaCha, a bidirectional linear expansion technique against Salsa, and a new ARX cipher proposal Forró.
- **137910006.pdf** — Short-lived zero-knowledge proofs and signatures (Arun, Bonneau, Clark). Proofs and signatures that naturally lose soundness after a time delay because anyone can forge them via sequential VDF computation; includes new re-randomizable and zero-knowledge VDFs.
- **137910007.pdf** — Classically Verifiable NIZK for QMA with Preprocessing (Morimae, Yamakawa). Three constructions of classically verifiable NIZKs for QMA in preprocessing models, including the first dual-mode NIZK for QMA from quantum-hard LWE.
- **137910014.pdf** — Flashproofs: Efficient Zero-Knowledge Arguments of Range and Polynomial Evaluation with Transparent Setup (Wang, Chau). Transparent-setup discrete-log range arguments with O(N^{2/3}) communication and gas-efficient on-chain verification, plus polynomial-evaluation arguments.
- **137910020.pdf** — Key-Reduced Variants of 3kf9 with Beyond-Birthday-Bound Security (Shen, Sibleyras). Repairs broken key-reduced 3kf9 MAC variants: a single doubling restores beyond-birthday-bound security; new n2kf9/n1kf9 proven secure up to 2^{2n/3} queries.
- **137910027.pdf** — SwiftEC: Shallue–van de Woestijne Indifferentiable Function To Elliptic Curves (Chavez-Saab, Rodríguez-Henríquez, Tibouchi). Faster indifferentiable hashing to elliptic curves, cutting the cost penalty of prior Shallue–van de Woestijne-based indifferentiable constructions.
- **137910032.pdf** — Key-schedule Security for the TLS 1.3 Standard (Brzuska, Delignat-Lavaud, Egger, Fournet, Kohbrok, Kohlweiss). Full reduction-based security proof of the TLS 1.3 key schedule as standardized, covering all key derivations, negotiated modes, and optional DH/PSK combinations.
- **137910038.pdf** — Unconditionally Secure NIZK in the Fine-Grained Setting (Wang, Pan). First NIZK with perfect soundness and unconditional zero-knowledge against AC0 adversaries, built around an OR-proof system in the fine-grained setting.
- **137910040.pdf** — Jammin' on the deck (Băcuieți, Daemen, Hoffert, Van Assche, Van Keer). Re-factors symmetric cryptography around deck functions instead of block ciphers, giving simpler authenticated-encryption modes with session support and a new "jammin cipher" ideal model.
- **137910042.pdf** — Security of Truncated Permutation Without Initial Value (Grassi, Mennink). Proves truncated-permutation hashing remains indifferentiable from a random oracle when the fixed initial value is replaced by a randomized one, repairing the omission case.
- **137910047.pdf** — Threshold Linearly Homomorphic Encryption on Z/2^kZ (Castagnos, Laguillaumie, Tucker). First threshold linearly homomorphic encryption scheme with message space Z/2^kZ, matching CPU-native arithmetic for MPC applications.
- **137910050.pdf** — Puncturable Key Wrapping and Its Applications (Backendal, Günther, Paterson). New primitive for fine-grained forward security in key hierarchies; applied to forward-secure TLS 1.3 0-RTT resumption and protected file storage with file shredding.
- **137910052.pdf** — Mind the TWEAKEY Schedule: Cryptanalysis on SKINNYe-64-256 (Qin, Dong, Wang, Hua, Wang). Formal cryptanalysis of the SKINNYe-64-256 tweakey schedule, finding unexpected differential cancellations (up to 8 within 30 rounds) undermining its security expectation.
- **137910058.pdf** — Log-S-unit Lattices: Using Explicit Stickelberger Generators to Solve Approx Ideal-SVP (Bernard, Lesavourey, Nguyen, Roux-Langlois). Extends Twisted-PHS ideal-lattice experiments to cyclotomic fields of degree up to 210 via explicit Stickelberger generators, outperforming CDW in the tested range and removing most quantum steps.
- **137910066.pdf** — Improving Bounds on Elliptic Curve Hidden Number Problem for ECDH Key Exchange (Xu, Sarkar, Wang, Hu). Revisits Coppersmith's method for the Diffie–Hellman EC-HNP, improving bounds on how few leaked bits of ECDH secrets suffice for key recovery in side-channel settings.
