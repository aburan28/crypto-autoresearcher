# Batch 105 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by theory-of-cryptography conference papers circa 2013–2014: the filename blocks map to Crypto 2013 (8271xxxx), TCC 2014 (8349xxxx), and PKC 2014 (8383xxxx) proceedings.
- Heavy focus on secure computation and its foundations: MPC round complexity, fairness, UC security, hardware tokens, black-box separations, and zero-knowledge variants.
- A strong obfuscation cluster (point/function/extractability/evasive/VBB obfuscation) reflecting the post-GGH13 multilinear-maps boom, plus non-malleable codes and tamper/leakage resilience.
- Only two elliptic-curve/DLP-relevant papers: Smith's Q-curve fast endomorphisms (GLV/GLS acceleration) and the GF(2^809) FFS discrete-log record; the batch is otherwise symmetric/lattice/foundations heavy.
- Anomalies: three 1-page invited-talk abstracts (Knudsen on block cipher history, Micali on mechanism design, Impagliazzo on general vs. specific assumptions) — no full papers.
- No obvious duplicates; no non-paper junk files detected.

## Papers
- **82710279.pdf** — A Modular Framework for Building Variable-Input-Length Tweakable Ciphers (Shrimpton, Terashima). Introduces the PIV construction for VIL tweakable ciphers with beyond-birthday-bound security (TCT2), plus robust AE conversion tolerating nonce misuse.
- **82710288.pdf** — Families of Fast Elliptic Curves from Q-curves (Benjamin Smith). Constructs elliptic curves over F_p^2 with efficiently computable endomorphisms by reducing quadratic Q-curves, generalizing GLS and enabling twist-secure GLV-style acceleration.
- **82710293.pdf** — Self-Updatable Encryption: Time Constrained Access Control with Hidden Attributes and Better Efficiency (Lee, Choi, Lee, Park, Yung). Modularizes revocable-storage ABE via a "self-updatable encryption" primitive supporting ciphertext updates over time.
- **82710298.pdf** — Function-Private Subspace-Membership Encryption and Its Applications (Boneh, Raghunathan, Segev). Extends function privacy to subspace-membership encryption with a generic construction from any inner-product encryption, yielding predicate-private search applications.
- **82710318.pdf** — Parallelizable and Authenticated Online Ciphers (Andreeva, Bogdanov, Luykx, Mennink, Tischhauser, Yasuda). Presents COPE, the first parallelizable online cipher, and COPA, a parallelizable online AE scheme with nonce-misuse resistance.
- **82710350.pdf** — Discrete Gaussian Leftover Hash Lemma over Infinite Domains (Agrawal, Gentry, Halevi, Sahai). Proves a lattice analog of the leftover hash lemma: Gaussian-weighted subset sums over lattices are statistically close to discrete Gaussians.
- **82710376.pdf** — SCARE of Secret Ciphers with SPN Structures (Rivain, Roche). Gives a generic side-channel reverse-engineering attack recovering the full secret design of SPN or Feistel ciphers, including under noisy leakage.
- **82710378.pdf** — Block ciphers – past and present (Lars R. Knudsen). One-page invited-talk abstract surveying block cipher cryptanalysis history and lightweight design (PRESENT, PRINCE); not a full paper.
- **83490107.pdf** — Towards Characterizing Complete Fairness in Secure Two-Party Computation (Gilad Asharov). Shows almost all Boolean functions with distinct domain sizes can be computed with complete fairness in the two-party setting.
- **83490108.pdf** — Constant-Round Black-Box Construction of Composable Multi-Party Computation Protocol (Kiyoshima, Manabe, Okamoto). First constant-round, black-box, angel-based UC-secure MPC, via constant-round CCA-secure commitments; shows sub-exponential assumptions are necessary for black-box reductions.
- **83490111.pdf** — Probabilistically Checkable Proofs of Proximity with Zero-Knowledge (Ishai, Weiss). Initiates zero-knowledge PCPs of proximity, giving query-efficient constructions via MPC-in-the-head-style transformations.
- **83490112.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Basing Public-Coin One-Way Permutations on Trapdoor Permutations (Takahiro Matsuda). Black-box separation showing one-way permutations cannot be constructed from even ideally secure trapdoor permutations.
- **83490117.pdf** — One-Sided Adaptively Secure Two-Party Computation (Hazay, Patra). Studies 2PC where at most one party is adaptively corrupted, achieving NCE and OT with constant public-key operations for large message spaces.
- **83490119.pdf** — Dual System Encryption via Predicate Encodings (Hoeteck Wee). Introduces predicate encodings as an information-theoretic primitive unifying adaptively secure predicate encryption schemes via Waters' dual system methodology.
- **83490121.pdf** — Non-Malleable Coding Against Bit-wise and Split-State Tampering (Cheraghchi, Guruswami). Constructs explicit non-malleable codes against bit-wise tampering and the more general two-split-state tampering model.
- **83490126.pdf** — Locally Updatable and Locally Decodable Codes (Chandran, Kanukurthi, Ostrovsky). Defines codes supporting both local decoding and local updates under a new Prefix Hamming metric, with constant rate and polylog update locality.
- **83490128.pdf** — Standard versus Selective Opening Security: Separation and Equivalence Results (Hofheinz, Rupp). Separates IND-CCA from IND-SO-CCA security while proving standard security implies selective-opening security in the passive (CPA) setting.
- **83490129.pdf** — Chosen Ciphertext Security via Point Obfuscation (Matsuda, Hanaoka). Builds CCA-secure PKE from CPA-secure PKE or lossy encryption plus multi-bit point-function obfuscators with auxiliary-input security.
- **83490133.pdf** — Achieving Constant Round Leakage-Resilient Zero-Knowledge (Omkant Pandey). First constant-round leakage-resilient zero-knowledge protocol achieving the ideal epsilon = 0 guarantee, via a public-coin preamble.
- **83490137.pdf** — Broadcast Amplification (Hirt, Maurer, Raykov). Characterizes when a small-domain broadcast primitive amplifies to larger domains: possible for n=3 parties from a single 3-broadcast, impossible for n>3.
- **83490138.pdf** — Multi-Linear Secret-Sharing Schemes (Beimel, Ben-Efraim, Padró, Tyomkin). Studies the power of multi-linear secret sharing, proving separations by secret length and the first super-polynomial share-size lower bounds beyond the linear case.
- **83490145.pdf** — How to Fake Auxiliary Input (Jetchev, Pietrzak). Shows any short auxiliary input can be simulated by an efficient deterministic function, yielding a simpler, tighter proof for leakage-resilient stream ciphers.
- **83490147.pdf** — Statistical Concurrent Non-Malleable Zero Knowledge (Orlandi, Ostrovsky, Rao, Sahai, Visconti). Constructs the first argument system simultaneously achieving statistical zero knowledge and concurrent non-malleability for NP.
- **83490158.pdf** — Leakage Resilient Fully Homomorphic Encryption (Berkoff, Liu). Builds the first leakage-resilient leveled FHE from DLWE-based techniques, extended to arbitrary depth by combining with multi-key FHE.
- **83490162.pdf** — Two-round Secure MPC from Indistinguishability Obfuscation (Garg, Gentry, Halevi, Raykova). Achieves two-round UC-secure general MPC in the CRS model using indistinguishability obfuscation, with communication independent of circuit size.
- **83490163.pdf** — Virtual Black-Box Obfuscation for All Circuits via Generic Graded Encoding (Brakerski, Rothblum). Constructs a general-purpose obfuscator proven VBB-secure in the generic graded-encoding model under a Bounded Speedup Hypothesis.
- **83490164.pdf** — On the Cryptographic Complexity of the Worst Functions (Beimel, Ishai, Kumaresan, Kushilevitz). Gives sublinear upper bounds on OT, correlated-randomness, communication, and share complexity for securely evaluating worst-case functions.
- **83490167.pdf** — Unified, Minimal and Selectively Randomizable Structure-Preserving Signatures (Abe, Groth, Ohkubo, Tibouchi). Optimal 3-element structure-preserving signatures with 1-element verification keys, working in all bilinear group types with selective randomizability.
- **83490169.pdf** — Securing Circuits and Protocols Against 1/poly(k) Tampering Rate (Dachman-Soled, Kalai). Compiles any circuit into one resilient to tampering with a 1/poly(k) fraction of wires, independent of circuit size and without sensitive leakage.
- **83490175.pdf** — Continuous Non-malleable Codes (Faust, Mukherjee, Nielsen, Venturi). Extends non-malleable codes to continuous tampering in the split-state model, identifying a uniqueness property as necessary for the construction.
- **83490176.pdf** — Lower Bounds in the Hardware Token Model (Agrawal, Ananth, Goyal, Prabhakaran, Rosen). Proves lower bounds on the number of bit-OT/stateless hardware tokens needed for secure two-party computation, ruling out constant-query protocols for some functions.
- **83490179.pdf** — On the Impossibility of Structure-Preserving Deterministic Primitives (Abe, Camenisch, Dowsley, Dubovitskaya). Shows deterministic primitives (e.g., certain signatures/VRFs) cannot be structure-preserving, limiting Groth-Sahai-friendly modular protocol design.
- **83490180.pdf** — Obfuscation for Evasive Functions (Barak, Bitansky, Canetti, Kalai, Paneth, Sahai). Studies obfuscation of evasive circuit families: equivalence of VBB/VGB notions, bootstrapping to all functions, and impossibility results.
- **83490181.pdf** — On the Power of Public-key Encryption in Secure Computation (Mahmoody, Maji, Prabhakaran). Oracle separation showing key-agreement/PKE is useless (beyond commitments) for black-box semi-honest secure two-party SFE.
- **83490182.pdf** — Can Optimally-Fair Coin Tossing be Based on One-Way Functions? (Dachman-Soled, Mahmoody, Malkin). Progress toward showing optimally-fair (O(1/r) bias) coin tossing cannot be black-box based on one-way functions.
- **83490186.pdf** — (Efficient) Universally Composable Oblivious Transfer Using a Minimal Number of Stateless Tokens (Choi, Katz, Schröder, Yerukhimovich, Zhou). UC-secure OT where two parties exchange one stateless token each for unbounded OTs; proven optimal for black-box token use.
- **83490187.pdf** — 4-Round Resettably-Sound Zero Knowledge (Chung, Ostrovsky, Pass, Venkitasubramaniam, Visconti). First 4-round resettably-sound zero-knowledge argument for NP from one-way functions, closing a round-complexity gap.
- **83490190.pdf** — On Extractability Obfuscation (Boyle, Chung, Pass). Initiates extractability obfuscation, bootstrapping from NC1 to all polynomial-time Turing machines and defining functional witness encryption.
- **83490192.pdf** — Collusion And Privacy In Mechanism Design (Silvio Micali). One-page invited-talk abstract advocating crypto–game-theory collaboration on collusion- and privacy-resilient mechanisms; not a full paper.
- **83490193.pdf** — Specific versus General Assumptions in Cryptography (Russell Impagliazzo). One-page invited-talk abstract on the history and benefits of basing security proofs on general rather than problem-specific assumptions.
- **83830105.pdf** — Online/Offline Attribute-Based Encryption (Hohenberger, Waters). Splits ABE encryption and key generation into an offline preparation phase and a fast online phase, targeting constrained devices.
- **83830107.pdf** — Identity-Based Lossy Trapdoor Functions: New Definitions, Hierarchical Extensions, and Implications (Escala, Herranz, Libert, Ràfols). Redefines lossiness for identity-based TDFs to achieve adaptive (not just selective) security, with hierarchical extensions.
- **83830113.pdf** — Solving Random Subset Sum Problem by lp-norm SVP Oracle (Hu, Pan, Zhang). Generalizes Lagarias-Odlyzko/Coster et al. subset-sum density bounds to lp-norm SVP oracles, solving almost all density-one instances for p ≥ 3.
- **83830115.pdf** — Efficient Delegation of Zero-Knowledge Proofs of Knowledge in a Pairing-Friendly Setting (Canard, Pointcheval, Sanders). Lets a weak prover delegate discrete-log-relation proof computations to an untrusted helper without leaking secrets.
- **83830119.pdf** — Scale-Invariant Fully Homomorphic Encryption over the Integers (Coron, Lepoint, Tibouchi). Scale-invariant variant of the DGHV integer FHE with modulus linear in circuit depth; implements full AES homomorphic evaluation in ~23 s/block.
- **83830123.pdf** — General Impossibility of Group Homomorphic Encryption in the Quantum World (Armknecht, Gagliardoni, Katzenbeisser, Peter). Proves IND-CPA-secure abelian group homomorphic encryption is impossible against quantum adversaries.
- **83830124.pdf** — Discrete logarithm in GF(2^809) with FFS (Barbulescu, Bouvier, Detrey, Gaudry, Jeljeli, Thomé, Videau, Zimmermann). Reports a record Function Field Sieve discrete-log computation in GF(2^809), benchmarking FFS against the 2013 quasi-polynomial advances.
- **83830130.pdf** — Fine-Tuning Groth-Sahai Proofs (Escala, Groth). Efficiency improvements for Groth-Sahai NIZK proofs in the SXDH setting, plus a type-based commit-and-prove scheme allowing commitment reuse.
- **83830133.pdf** — On the Lossiness of the Rabin Trapdoor Function (Yannick Seurin). Shows the Rabin trapdoor function is lossy over suitably restricted domains under a Phi-Hiding-type assumption, implying tight FDH signature security.
- **83830140.pdf** — Lazy Modulus Switching for the BKW Algorithm on LWE (Albrecht, Faugère, Fitzpatrick, Perret). BKW variant with lazy modulus switching that reduces the cost of solving binary- and small-secret LWE versus standard BKW and lattice reduction.
