# Batch 006 digest

## Overview
- Batch splits cleanly into two venue-era clusters: ~25 theory papers (looks like CRYPTO 2017 proceedings) on MPC, indistinguishability obfuscation, commitments, zero-knowledge, CDS, and lattice techniques; followed by ~25 CHES-style 2017 papers on masking, side-channel attacks, fault injection, and implementation security.
- Notable papers: Ouroboros (first provably secure proof-of-stake blockchain), a UC treatment of Bitcoin, Groth–Maller minimal signatures of knowledge / SE-SNARKs, and Bellare et al.'s ratcheted encryption formalization underlying Signal-style messaging.
- Lattice/post-quantum theme recurs in both halves: Gaussian sampling, LWE-based lossy trapdoor functions, side-channel attacks on QcBits and masked lattice encryption, McBits revisited.
- Side-channel cluster emphasizes masking theory and practice (Boolean↔arithmetic conversion, threshold sharing uniformity, very-high-order masking) plus physical attacks: X-ray fault injection, EM attacks on dual-rail FPGA logic, secure-boot compromise via FPGA SoCs, lidar spoofing.
- No duplicates, non-paper files, or anomalous extracts detected; all 50 entries are research-paper first pages with clear title/abstract text.

## Papers
- **10401309.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation for Turing Machines: Constant Overhead and Amortization (Ananth, Jain, Sahai). First iO for bounded-input Turing machines with constant (2×) size overhead, plus a bootstrapping theorem amortizing obfuscation of many machines.
- **10401311.pdf** — A New Approach to Round-Optimal Secure Multiparty Computation (Ananth, Choudhuri, Jain). New approach to malicious-secure MPC without trusted setup: five rounds from DDH, round-optimal four rounds from one-way permutations plus sub-exponential DDH.
- **10401313.pdf** — Hedging Public-Key Encryption in the Real World (Boldyreva, Patton, Shrimpton). Lifts hedged-PKE security (IND-CDA) to the CCA setting achievable from high-level library APIs, and proposes MM-CCA generalizing IND-CCA to imperfect randomness.
- **10401315.pdf** — Quantum non-malleability and authentication (Alagic, Majenz). Shows prior quantum non-malleability definition too weak, gives a stronger entropic definition implying secrecy, and improves quantum authentication using two-designs.
- **10401316.pdf** — Amortization with Fewer Equations for Proving Knowledge of Small Secrets (del Pino, Lyubashevsky). Lattice zero-knowledge proofs of knowledge of small secrets with amortized communication kicking in at far fewer equations than prior Cramer et al. protocols.
- **10401326.pdf** — Private Multiplication over Finite Fields (Belaïd, Benhamouda, Passelègue, Prouff, Thillard, Vergnaud). Algebraic characterization of probing-model privacy/non-interference for finite-field multiplication, with two generic lower-overhead masking constructions.
- **10401331.pdf** — Distinguisher-Dependent Simulation in Two Rounds and its Applications (Jain, Kalai, Khurana, Rothblum). New simulation technique black-box in both adversary and distinguisher, yielding round-optimal WI arguments, arguments of knowledge, and three-round protocols from DDH/QR.
- **10401332.pdf** — Four-Round Concurrent Non-Malleable Commitments from One-Way Functions (Ciampi, Ostrovsky, Siniscalchi, Visconti). First 4-round concurrent non-malleable commitment from any OWF, via a new simulation-witness-independence notion.
- **10401334.pdf** — Bitcoin as a Transaction Ledger: A Composable Treatment (Badertscher, Maurer, Tschudi, Zikas). Casts Bitcoin as a UC-realizable ledger functionality, giving the first universally composable security treatment of the Bitcoin protocol.
- **10401336.pdf** — Robust Non-Interactive Multiparty Computation Against Constant-Size Collusion (Benhamouda, Krawczyk, Rabin). First efficient robust NIMPC for NC1 (information-theoretic) and P (from OWFs) tolerating constant-size collusions, built from PSM protocols.
- **10401337.pdf** — The Price of Low Communication in Secure Multi-Party Computation (Garay, Ishai, Ostrovsky, Zikas). Studies sublinear-communication MPC; shows sublinear communication drastically reduces tolerable corruptions, with complete semi-honest feasibility bounds.
- **10401338.pdf** — Gaussian Sampling over the Integers: Efficient, Generic, Constant-Time (Micciancio, Walter). New generic, efficient, constant-time discrete Gaussian samplers for lattice crypto, resilient to timing side channels, plus precision-analysis techniques.
- **10401340.pdf** — Lower Bounds on Obfuscation from All-or-Nothing Encryption Primitives (Garg, Mahmoody, Mohammed). Black-box separations: IO cannot be built from witness encryption, predicate encryption, or FHE (assuming OWFs and PH not collapsing).
- **10401344.pdf** — Snarky Signatures: Minimal Signatures of Knowledge from Simulation-Extractable SNARKs (Groth, Maller). Pairing-based SE-SNARK of 3 group elements yielding minimal signatures of knowledge, matching proven lower bounds.
- **10401348.pdf** — New security notions and feasibility results for authentication of quantum data (Garg, Yuen, Zhandry). Stronger quantum authentication definitions characterizing adversary behavior; shows Wegman–Carter with 3-universal hashing resists superposition attacks.
- **10401357.pdf** — Conditional Disclosure of Secrets via Non-Linear Reconstruction (Liu, Vaikuntanathan, Wee). Breaks the N^1/2 "linear reconstruction" barrier for CDS with N^o(1) communication, improving secret-sharing share complexity for graph access structures.
- **10401362.pdf** — Non-Full Sbox Linearization: Applications to Collision Attacks on Round-Reduced Keccak (Song, Liao, Guo). First practical collisions for 5-round Keccak-224 and a 6-round challenge instance via partial linearization of the nonlinear layer.
- **10401365.pdf** — Conditional Disclosure of Secrets: Amplification, Closure, Amortization, Lower-bounds, and Separations (Applebaum, Arkis, Raykov, Vasudevan). Systematic study of CDS: closure under complement/formulas, error amplification, amortization, and lower bounds.
- **10401372.pdf** — All-But-Many Lossy Trapdoor Functions and Selective Opening Chosen-Ciphertext Security from LWE (Libert, Sakzad, Stehlé, Steinfeld). First all-but-many lossy trapdoor function from standard LWE, yielding SIM-SO-CCA2 secure encryption.
- **10401375.pdf** — Indifferentiability of Iterated Even-Mansour Ciphers with Non-Idealized Key-Schedules: Five Rounds are Necessary and Sufficient (Dai, Seurin, Steinberger, Thiruvengadam). Proves 5-round IEM with trivial key schedule indifferentiable from ideal cipher, with matching 4-round attack.
- **10401377.pdf** — Memory-Tight Reductions (Auerbach, Cash, Fersch, Kiltz). Initiates study of memory efficiency in security reductions; gives a toolbox and an impossibility result via data-streaming lower bounds.
- **10401378.pdf** — Degree Evaluation of NFSR-Based Cryptosystems (Liu). Numeric-mapping framework for algebraic-degree estimation; best known distinguishers on reduced Trivium/TriviA-SC and first cryptanalysis of Kreyvium.
- **10401387.pdf** — Non-Malleable Codes for Space-Bounded Tampering (Faust, Hostáková, Mukherjee, Venturi). Studies non-malleable codes where tampering adversaries are space-bounded and may include the decoding algorithm.
- **10401388.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation from Trilinear Maps and Block-Wise Local PRGs (Lin, Tessaro). IO from L-linear maps plus PRGs with block-wise locality L, plausibly instantiable for L≥3, weakening locality-5 requirements.
- **10401390.pdf** — Information-theoretic Indistinguishability via the Chi-squared Method (Dai, Hoang, Tessaro). New chi-squared-divergence proof method giving tighter bounds for XOR of permutations, encrypted Davies-Meyer, and Swap-or-not.
- **10401392.pdf** — Functional Graph Revisited: Updates on (Second) Preimage Attacks on Hash Combiners (Bao, Wang, Guo, Gu). Improved preimage attack on XOR combiner (2^{5n/8}) and first generic optimal second-preimage attack on Zipper hash (2^{3n/5}).
- **10401394.pdf** — Message Franking via Committing Authenticated Encryption (Grubbs, Lu, Ristenpart). Formalizes Facebook message franking via compactly committing AEAD; proves several in-use AEAD schemes committing with keys as openings.
- **10401397.pdf** — Ratcheted Encryption and Key Exchange: The Security of Messaging (Bellare, Camper Singh, Jaeger, Nyayapati, Stepanovs). Formal definitions and a proven-secure protocol for ratcheted key exchange/encryption underlying Signal-style messaging.
- **10401402.pdf** — Secure Arithmetic Computation with Constant Computational Overhead (Applebaum, Damgård, Ishai, Nielsen, Zichron). First semi-honest two-party arithmetic-circuit protocol with constant amortized field operations per gate; efficient vector-OLE with rate 1/3.
- **10401405.pdf** — Optimal Security Reductions for Unique Signatures: Bypassing Impossibilities with A Counterexample (Guo, Chen, Susilo, Lai, Yang, Mu). Shows prior q_s-loss impossibility results for unique/re-randomizable signatures can be bypassed, via a counterexample-based analysis.
- **10401412.pdf** — A New Distribution-Sensitive Secure Sketch and Popularity-Proportional Hashing (Woodage, Chatterjee, Dodis, Juels, Ristenpart). Layer-hiding hash secure sketch saving entropy for known secret distributions, with typo-tolerant password authentication constructions.
- **10401419.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation from SXDH on 5-Linear Maps and Locality-5 PRGs (Lin). Reduces multilinear-map degree needed for IO to 5, with security from SXDH, PRG security, and sub-exponential LWE.
- **10401425.pdf** — Ouroboros: A Provably Secure Proof-of-Stake Blockchain Protocol (Kiayias, Russell, David, Oliynykov). First proof-of-stake blockchain with rigorous Bitcoin-comparable security plus an incentive mechanism making honesty an approximate Nash equilibrium.
- **10401426.pdf** — Be Adaptive, Avoid Overcommitting (Jafargholi, Kamath, Klein, Komargodski, Pietrzak, Wichs). Unifying framework for selective-to-adaptive security transformations; new adaptively secure secret sharing for monotone-circuit access structures.
- **10529101.pdf** — Nanofocused X-Ray Beam To Reprogram Secure Circuits (Anceau, Bleuet, Clédière, Maingault, Rainard, Tucoulou). Synchrotron X-ray nanobeams corrupt single transistors in flash/EEPROM/RAM, enabling reversible fault attacks on microcontrollers.
- **10529102.pdf** — Reconciling d+1 Masking in Hardware and Software (Gross, Mangard). Unified masking algorithm for hardware and software reducing fresh-randomness costs, in some cases halving hardware randomness over state of the art.
- **10529114.pdf** — High-Order Conversion From Boolean to Arithmetic Masking (Coron). Boolean-to-arithmetic masking conversion with complexity independent of register size, proven ISW-secure; also attacks on Hutter–Tunstall conversions.
- **10529118.pdf** — Changing of the Guards: a simple and efficient method for achieving uniformity in threshold sharing (Daemen). Generic, simple method to achieve uniformity in threshold implementations with d+1 shares against first-order DPA.
- **10529119.pdf** — On the Security of Carrier Phase-based Ranging (Ólafsdóttir, Ranganathan, Capkun). Demonstrates distance-decreasing relay attacks on multicarrier phase-based ranging, reducing 50 m ranges to under 3 m; countermeasures shown insufficient.
- **10529126.pdf** — Hacking in the Blind: (Almost) Invisible Runtime User Interface Attacks (Malisa, Kostiainen, Knell, Sommer, Capkun). Peripheral-interposing device performs adaptive UI attacks via state estimation/fingerprinting; evaluated on e-banking PCs and medical terminals.
- **10529127.pdf** — Your Rails Cannot Hide From Localized EM: How Dual-Rail Logic Fails on FPGAs (Immler, Specht, Unterstein). High-resolution EM analysis defeats DPA-resistant dual-rail logic on FPGAs; custom placement more than doubles resistance.
- **10529128.pdf** — How to Break Secure Boot on FPGA SoCs through Malicious Hardware (Jacob, Heyszl, Zankl, Rolfes, Sigl). Malicious FPGA fabric on shared memory bus subverts secure boot on Xilinx Zynq-7000, booting attacker-controlled images.
- **10529132.pdf** — Illusion and Dazzle: Adversarial Optical Channel Exploits against Lidars for Automotive Applications (Shin, Kim, Kwon, Kim). Relay spoofing and saturation attacks against Velodyne VLP-16 lidar, inducing false or blinded perception for autonomous vehicles.
- **10529133.pdf** — A Side-Channel Assisted Cryptanalytic Attack Against QcBits (Rossi, Hamburg, Hutter, Marson). DPA on QC-MDPC decoding leaks partial key; solving noisy linear equations recovers the full QcBits private key, plus countermeasure.
- **10529146.pdf** — FourQ on embedded devices with strong countermeasures against side-channel attacks (Liu, Longa, Pereira, Reparaz, Seo). Record-speed constant-time FourQ ECDH on 8/16/32-bit microcontrollers with strong side-channel countermeasures.
- **10529147.pdf** — McBits Revisited (Chou). Constant-time fast code-based (McEliece/Niederreiter) implementation exploiting internal parallelism, achieving higher security level than original McBits.
- **10529149.pdf** — Very High Order Masking: Efficient Implementation and Security Evaluation (Journault, Standaert). 32-share masked AES/Fantomas bitslice implementations with new multi-model security evaluation showing >2^64-measurement resistance.
- **10529150.pdf** — Hiding Secrecy Leakage in Leaky Helper Data (Hiller, Önalan). Wiretap coset coding reduces secrecy leakage in PUF key-derivation helper data, cutting required PUF response bits by 60% versus debiasing.
- **10529154.pdf** — Back to Massey: Impressively fast, scalable and tight security evaluation tools (Choudary, Popescu). Bound-based guessing-entropy estimation scaling to arbitrarily large keys (e.g., 4096-bit RSA) in a fraction of a second.
- **10529155.pdf** — Single-Trace Side-Channel Attacks on Masked Lattice-Based Encryption (Primas, Pessl, Mangard). First single-trace key-recovery attack on masked lattice encryption, exploiting NTT leakage via templates, belief propagation, and lattice decoding.
