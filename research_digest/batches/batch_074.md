# Batch 074 digest

## Overview
- Batch is dominated by ~2007-era conference papers (PKC, EUROCRYPT, FSE eSTREAM years), with three main clusters: secure two-/multi-party computation and zero-knowledge, symmetric cryptanalysis of hash functions and stream ciphers (MD5, FORK-256, Panama, eSTREAM candidates), and pairing/signature-based public-key cryptography.
- Notable papers: Stevens–Lenstra–de Weger chosen-prefix MD5 collisions with rogue X.509 certificates; Lindell–Pinkas cut-and-choose malicious 2PC; Ong–Vadhan "Zero Knowledge and Soundness are Symmetric"; Enge–Gaudry L(1/3+ε) DLP algorithm for low-degree curves; Granger et al. generalization of the Ate pairing to hyperelliptic curves.
- Only a handful of ECDLP/ECC-specific items (batch ECDSA verification on Koblitz curves, Ate pairing, Jacobian DLP); most of the batch is protocols and symmetric cryptanalysis rather than curve cryptanalysis.
- Anomalies: 45150034.pdf and 459.pdf are undecodable garbage extracts (broken font/glyph encoding); 45930071.pdf is hex-encoded but decodes to a Naya-Plasencia paper on Achterbahn.

## Papers
- **44500343.pdf** — Multiparty Computation for Interval, Equality, and Comparison without Bit-Decomposition (Takashi Nishide, Kazuo Ohta). Simplifies constant-round bit-decomposition and builds more efficient constant-round comparison, interval, and equality tests on shared secrets without full bit-decomposition.
- **44500361.pdf** — Identity-Based Traitor Tracing (Abdalla, Dent, Malone-Lee, Neven, Phan, Smart). First identity-based traitor tracing scheme, secure in the standard model under DBDH in asymmetric pairings, built on wildcard IBE from Waters' scheme.
- **44500377.pdf** — Verifiable Shuffle of Large Size Ciphertexts (Jens Groth, Steve Lu). Two 3-move honest-verifier zero-knowledge arguments for shuffle correctness, improving round, communication, and computation complexity for mix-nets and voting.
- **44500393.pdf** — A Survey of Single-Database PIR: Techniques and Applications (Rafail Ostrovsky, William E. Skeith III). Surveys single-database private information retrieval constructions and their connections to collision-resistant hashing, oblivious transfer, and public-key encryption.
- **44500412.pdf** — Deterministic Polynomial Time Equivalence between Factoring and Key-Recovery Attack on Takagi's RSA (Noboru Kunihiro, Kaoru Kurosawa). Extends May's equivalence to Takagi's N=p^r q RSA variant using a new LLL technique for non-triangular matrices.
- **44500426.pdf** — Efficient Pseudorandom Generators Based on the DDH Assumption (Farashahi, Schoenmakers, Sidorenko). Provably secure modification of the insecure Dual EC generator, efficient under DDH with a new probabilistic randomness extractor.
- **44500442.pdf** — Fast Batch Verification of Multiple Signatures (Jung Hee Cheon, Jeong Hyun Yi). Batch verification of DSA/ECDSA from multiple signers via uniform width-w NAFs, with τ-adic extension to Koblitz curves needing ~6 EC additions per signature.
- **44500458.pdf** — A Closer Look at PKI: Security and Efficiency (Boldyreva, Fischlin, Palacio, Warinschi). Models CA plus key registration and users jointly, giving stronger security definitions and more efficient provably-secure encryption/signature constructions for PKI.
- **44500476.pdf** — Self-Generated-Certificate Public Key Encryption Without Pairing (Junzuo Lai, Weidong Kou). Pairing-free SGC-PKE resisting denial-of-decryption attacks, reaching Girault's trust level 3 with shorter keys than Liu–Au.
- **45150001.pdf** — Chosen-Prefix Collisions for MD5 and Colliding X.509 Certificates for Different Identities (Stevens, Lenstra, de Weger). Automated differential-path search yields chosen-prefix MD5 collisions at ~2^50 cost, enabling colliding X.509 certificates with different identities.
- **45150023.pdf** — Non-Trivial Black-Box Combiners for Collision-Resistant Hash-Functions don't Exist (Krzysztof Pietrzak). Proves no black-box robust combiner for CRHFs can have output significantly shorter than concatenation, answering Boneh–Boyen.
- **45150034.pdf** — (not a readable extract) First-page text is undecoded binary/glyph garbage; no title or content recoverable.
- **45150052.pdf** — An Efficient Protocol for Secure Two-Party Computation in the Presence of Malicious Adversaries (Yehuda Lindell, Benny Pinkas). Cut-and-choose on Yao garbled circuits yields practical malicious-security 2PC, proven in the standard model via simulation.
- **45150079.pdf** — Revisiting the Efficiency of Malicious Two-Party Computation (David P. Woodruff). Expander-graph modification of Mohassel–Franklin schemes, asymptotically improving known malicious 2PC efficiency and correcting an analysis error.
- **45150097.pdf** — Efficient Two-Party Secure Computation on Committed Inputs (Stanisław Jarecki, Vitaly Shmatikov). UC-secure garbled-circuit 2PC on committed inputs under DCR/strong RSA, using a new Camenisch–Shoup variant and efficient committed OT.
- **45150115.pdf** — Universally Composable Multi-Party Computation Using Tamper-Proof Hardware (Jonathan Katz). Proposes tamper-proof hardware tokens as a UC setup assumption to bypass impossibility of general computation without honest majority.
- **45150129.pdf** — Generic and Practical Resettable Zero-Knowledge in the Bare Public-Key Model (Moti Yung, Yunlei Zhao). Constant-round concurrently sound resettable ZK arguments for NP in the BPK model under one-way functions, with practical Σ-protocol instantiation.
- **45150148.pdf** — Instance-Dependent Verifiable Random Functions and Their Application to Simultaneous Resettability (Yi Deng, Dongdai Lin). Defines instance-dependent VRFs and uses them to build the first constant-round simultaneously resettable ZK arguments for NP.
- **45150169.pdf** — Conditional Computational Entropy, or Toward Separating Pseudoentropy from Compressibility (Hsiao, Lu, Reyzin). Separates conditional HILL and Yao entropies and introduces unpredictability entropy, generalizing extraction and hardcore-bit results.
- **45150187.pdf** — Zero Knowledge and Soundness are Symmetric (Shien Jin Ong, Salil Vadhan). Unconditional characterization of NP problems with ZK arguments, showing NP∩coNP ZK-argument closure under complement and SZK-argument/CZK-proof duality.
- **45150210.pdf** — Mesh Signatures: How to Leak a Secret with Unwitting and Unwilling Participants (Xavier Boyen). Ring-signature generalization with rich access structures and compulsory enrollment, efficient from bilinear maps with everlasting anonymity.
- **45150228.pdf** — The Power of Proofs-of-Possession: Securing Multiparty Signatures against Rogue-Key Attacks (Thomas Ristenpart, Scott Yilek). Registered-key model showing simple proofs of possession suffice for provable security of multisignature and ring signature schemes.
- **45150246.pdf** — Batch Verification of Short Signatures (Camenisch, Hohenberger, Pedersen). First multi-signer batch verifier without random oracles whose dominant cost is independent of signature count, plus a new very short signature scheme.
- **45150264.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of SFLASH with Slightly Modified Parameters (Dubois, Fouque, Stern). Simple linear-algebra forgery attack showing slightly modified SFLASH parameters are insecure, though standard SFLASH resists.
- **45150276.pdf** — Differential Cryptanalysis of the Stream Ciphers Py, Py6 and Pypy (Hongjun Wu, Bart Preneel). Chosen-IV differential attacks on eSTREAM candidates Py/Py6/Pypy reduce effective key size to a few bytes, rendering them practically insecure.
- **45150291.pdf** — Secure Computation from Random Error Correcting Codes (Chen, Cramer, Goldwasser, de Haan, Vaikuntanathan). Bases threshold secure computation on arbitrary error-correcting codes, deepening the code/secret-sharing connection.
- **45150311.pdf** — Round-Efficient Secure Computation in Point-to-Point Networks (Jonathan Katz, Chiu-Yuen Koo). Secure computation protocols using only a single broadcast round, achieving optimal thresholds and better round complexity when compiled to point-to-point networks.
- **45150329.pdf** — Atomic Secure Multi-Party Multiplication with Low Communication (Cramer, Damgård, de Haan). Shows Rabin-style Shamir-based multiplication can be made much more communication-efficient under mild restrictions, even for a single multiplication.
- **45150347.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the Sidelnikov Cryptosystem (Lorenz Minder, Amin Shokrollahi). Structural key-recovery attack on the Reed–Muller-code Sidelnikov cryptosystem, breaking the proposed 2048-length parameters in about an hour.
- **45150361.pdf** — Toward a Rigorous Variation of Coppersmith's Algorithm on Three Variables (Aurélie Bauer, Antoine Joux). Analyzes a rigorous three-variable Coppersmith variant using lattice reduction plus Gröbner bases, applied to short-RSA-exponent attacks.
- **45150379.pdf** — An L(1/3 + ε) Algorithm for the Discrete Logarithm Problem for Low Degree Curves (Andreas Enge, Pierrick Gaudry). Heuristic subexponential L(1/3+ε) DLP algorithm for Jacobians of low-degree unbalanced plane curves, improving on L(1/2) for that family.
- **45150394.pdf** — General Ad Hoc Encryption from Exponent Inversion IBE (Xavier Boyen). Extends exponent-inversion IBE schemes to general identity structures (hierarchical, fuzzy, attribute-based) without random oracles.
- **45150412.pdf** — Non-Interactive Proofs for Integer Multiplication (Ivan Damgård, Rune Thorbek). UC, practical non-interactive verifiable secret sharing of integers with ZK proofs of ab=c, without random oracles, useful for auctions/elections.
- **45150430.pdf** — Ate Pairing on Hyperelliptic Curves (Granger, Hess, Oyono, Thériault, Vercauteren). Generalizes the Ate pairing to arbitrary algebraic curves with loop length up to g times shorter than Tate and no final exponentiation.
- **45150448.pdf** — Ideal Multipartite Secret Sharing Schemes (Oriol Farràs, Jaume Martí-Farré, Carles Padró). Characterizes ideal multipartite access structures using discrete polymatroids and matroid connections, giving necessary and sufficient conditions.
- **45150466.pdf** — Non-Wafer-Scale Sieving Hardware for the NFS: Another Attempt to Cope with 1024-bit (Willi Geiselmann, Rainer Steinwandt). Moderate-size chip design for NFS sieving at 1024-bit RSA scale, 2–3.5× slower than TWIRL but far more technologically realistic.
- **45150482.pdf** — Divisible E-cash Systems can be Truly Anonymous (Sébastien Canard, Aline Gouget). First off-line divisible e-cash scheme with full unlinkability and anonymity without a trusted third party, using a binary-tree security tag.
- **45150498.pdf** — A Fast and Key-Efficient Reduction of Chosen-Ciphertext to Known-Plaintext Security (Ueli Maurer, Johan Sjödin). Optimal range extension for weak PRFs plus weak-PRF-to-PRF construction, yielding more efficient CCA-secure symmetric encryption.
- **45150517.pdf** — Range Extension for Weak PRFs; The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly (Krzysztof Pietrzak, Johan Sjödin). Classifies black-box range-extension constructions for weak PRFs by provability, proving the best known expansion (2m−1 with m keys) optimal.
- **45150534.pdf** — Feistel Networks made Public, and Applications (Yevgeniy Dodis, Prashant Puniya). Shows constant/log-round Feistel is insufficient when round functions are merely unpredictable or intermediate values leak, and gives new analysis handling such settings.
- **45150555.pdf** — Oblivious-Transfer Amplification (Jürg Wullschleger). Optimal protocols realizing OT from universal OT and bounds for OT from weak OT, using a new distributed leftover hash lemma, with computational analogues.
- **45150573.pdf** — Simulatable Adaptive Oblivious Transfer (Camenisch, Neven, shelat). Practical adaptive k-out-of-N OT with full simulatability against selective-failure attacks; one ROM construction and one efficient standard-model construction.
- **459.pdf** — (not a readable extract) First page is undecoded slash-glyph font-encoding garbage; no title or content recoverable.
- **45930001.pdf** — Producing Collisions for Panama, Instantaneously (Joan Daemen, Gilles Van Assche). Practical collision attack on the Panama hash function in ~2^6 state updates, drastically improving prior 2^82 attacks; stream cipher unaffected.
- **45930019.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of FORK-256 (Matusiewicz, Peyrin, Billet, Contini, Pieprzyk). Differential-based near-collision and collision attacks on FORK-256, breaking the full compression function and hash at ~2^109.6 with memory.
- **45930039.pdf** — The Grindahl Hash Functions (Knudsen, Rechberger, Thomsen). New Rijndael-based hash family Grindahl-256/512 designed so no low-weight characteristics form collisions, with low memory requirements.
- **45930055.pdf** — Overtaking VEST (Antoine Joux, Jean-René Reinhard). Chosen-IV collision attack on eSTREAM stream cipher family VEST recovering 53 state bits with ~2^22–2^29 IV setups, extendable to MAC forgery.
- **45930071.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Achterbahn-128/80 (María Naya-Plasencia; title recovered from hex-encoded extract). Two key-recovery attacks against the final eSTREAM candidate Achterbahn-128/80, breaking the 80-bit variant with complexity ~2^61(?).
- **45930085.pdf** — Differential-Linear Attacks against the Stream Cipher Phelix (Hongjun Wu, Bart Preneel). Recovers the Phelix key with ~2^41.5 operations using 2^34 chosen nonces and 2^37 chosen plaintext words via differential-linear cryptanalysis.
- **45930099.pdf** — How to Enrich the Message Space of a Cipher (Thomas Ristenpart, Phillip Rogaway). XLS construction extends a cipher's message space by s bits using a multipermutation, proven a strong pseudorandom permutation, working in tweakable/VIL settings.
