# Batch 043 digest

## Overview
- Uniform batch of ~2022-era conference papers (Springer LNCS first-page style, mostly ASIACRYPT 2022 and PKC 2022 vintage); all 50 are legitimate research papers, no duplicates or non-paper items.
- Heavy on public-key proof/signature primitives: zero-knowledge arguments, ring/blind/group signatures, vector and functional commitments.
- Strong isogeny-crypto cluster: new isogeny representation/pSIDH (Leroux), radical isogeny "racewalking", CSIDH-based OT/MPC, group-action KEM/NIKE in the QROM.
- FHE and MPC themes recur: NTRU/LWE FHE (FINAL), TFHE bootstrapping, hybrid HE (Elisabeth), YOSO-model encryption, PSI, secret leader election, topology-hiding computation.
- Symmetric cryptanalysis and side-channel work present: MILP boomerang on AES-192, SAT-based 6-round SHA-3 collisions, cube attacks on Trivium, division property for MiMC family, masked fixed-weight sampling.
- Anomalous/notable: "Certifying Giant Nonprimes" (13940083) applies crypto certificates to GIMPS/PrimeGrid prime searches — an unusual application paper; nothing else anomalous.

## Papers
- **137910273.pdf** — YOLO YOSO: Fast and Simple Encryption and Secret Sharing in the YOSO Model (Cascudo, David, Garms, Konring). Efficient encryption toward anonymous future committees for adaptively secure YOSO protocols.
- **137910274.pdf** — Anonymous Public Key Encryption under Corruptions (Huang, Lai, Han, Lyu, Weng). New formalizations and schemes giving PKE anonymity plus confidentiality under the same corruption types.
- **137910276.pdf** — DAG-Σ: A DAG-based Sigma Protocol for Relations in CNF (Zeng, Lai, Huang, Wang, Zheng). Sigma protocol for k-CNF partial-knowledge relations in the discrete-log setting via DAG conversion.
- **137910280.pdf** — Efficient Zero-Knowledge Arguments in Discrete Logarithm Setting (Kim, Lee, Seo). Three interactive ZK arguments for arithmetic circuits trading sublogarithmic proof size against sublinear verification.
- **137910281.pdf** — A Third is All You Need: Extended Partial Key Exposure Attack on CRT-RSA with Additive Exponent Blinding (Zhou, van de Pol, Yu, Standaert). Extends May et al.'s PKE attack to blinded CRT exponents, recovering keys from 1/3 known bits.
- **137910286.pdf** — On Rejection Sampling in Lyubashevsky's Signature Scheme (Devevey, Fawzi, Passelègue, Stehlé). Analyzes rejection sampling to minimize signature size; hyperball uniforms meet new compactness lower bounds.
- **137910287.pdf** — Towards Case-Optimized Hybrid Homomorphic Encryption Featuring the Elisabeth Stream Cipher (Cosseron, Hoffmann, Méaux, Standaert). Introduces Group Filter Permutator paradigm and Elisabeth cipher enabling server-side neural-network inference over HHE.
- **137910288.pdf** — Zero-Knowledge Protocols for the Subset Sum Problem from MPC-in-the-Head with Rejection (Feneuil, Maire, Rivain, Vergnaud). Quadratic-size ZK arguments for subset sum via small-integer secret sharing with rejection.
- **137910289.pdf** — Universal Ring Signatures in the Standard Model (Branco, Döttling, Wohnig). Formalizes and constructs ring signatures over rings of users with keys from arbitrary, differing signature schemes.
- **137910291.pdf** — FINAL: Faster FHE instantiated with NTRU and LWE (Bonte, Iliashenko, Park, Pereira, Smart). FHE from non-overstretched NTRU (and NTRU+LWE), beating TFHE bootstrapping speed and key sizes.
- **137910297.pdf** — Additive-Homomorphic Functional Commitments and Applications to Homomorphic Signatures (Catalano, Fiore, Tucker). Pairing-based additive-homomorphic FCs yielding expressive multi-input homomorphic signatures without lattices or multilinear maps.
- **137910298.pdf** — A New Isogeny Representation and Applications to Cryptography (Leroux). Suborder representation for prime-degree isogenies under new SOIP assumption, with pSIDH NIKE application.
- **137910301.pdf** — BLOOM: Bimodal Lattice One-Out-of-Many Proofs and Applications (Lyubashevsky, Nguyen). Lattice one-out-of-many proofs giving the most compact lattice ring/group signatures with 7–15× smaller public keys.
- **137910302.pdf** — Revisiting Related-Key Boomerang Attacks on AES Using Computer-Aided Tool (Derbez, Euler, Fouque, Nguyen). MILP model for nonlinear key schedules yields best related-key boomerang on AES-192 (2^124 time/data).
- **137910308.pdf** — Memory-Tight Multi-Challenge Security of Public-Key Encryption (Jaeger, Kumar). First PKE schemes with reductions tight in time, advantage, and memory, via augmented KEM-DEM/Hashed-ElGamal.
- **137910314.pdf** — PointProofs, Revisited (Libert, Passelègue, Riahinia). Security proof for PointProofs vector commitments without the algebraic group model, under the weaker n-DHE assumption.
- **137910317.pdf** — Multi-User Security of the Sum of Truncated Random Permutations (Choi, Kim, Lee, Lee). New Luby-Rackoff-backward PRF constructions (SaT1/SaT2) combining summation and truncation with multi-user bounds.
- **137910318.pdf** — Attaining GOD Beyond Honest Majority With Friends and Foes (Hegde, Koti, Kukkala, Patil, Patra, Paul). Guaranteed-output-delivery MPC in the Friends-and-Foes model; proves OT necessity and gives QuadSquad protocol.
- **137910323.pdf** — Traceable Receipt-Free Encryption (Devillez, Pereira, Peters). New TREnc primitive and traceable-CCA notion enabling receipt-free e-voting ballot submission with group-element messages.
- **137910327.pdf** — Statistical Decoding 2.0: Reducing Decoding to LPN (Carrier, Debris-Alazard, Meyer-Hilfiger, Tillich). Revives statistical decoding for code-based crypto by reducing generic decoding to LPN solved via Fourier techniques.
- **137910328.pdf** — Encryption to the Future (Campanelli, David, Khoshakhlagh, Konring, Nielsen). Formalizes EtF for sending secrets to future anonymous committees; shows links to witness encryption over blockchains.
- **137910332.pdf** — The Abe-Okamoto Partially Blind Signature Scheme Revisited (Kastner, Loss, Xu). Points out flaws in the original security proof and supplies a rigorous new proof with similar bounds.
- **137910341.pdf** — Stretching Cube Attacks: Improved Methods to Recover Massive Superpolies (He, Hu, Preneel, Wang). New techniques overcoming monomial-trail explosion in division-property cube attacks, pushing Trivium beyond round 845.
- **137910344.pdf** — On the Field-Based Division Property: Applications to MiMC, Feistel MiMC and GMiMC (Cui, Hu, Wang, Wei). Extends division property to F_{2^n} for degree estimation of arithmetization-oriented ciphers.
- **137910346.pdf** — A Modular Approach to the Incompressibility of Block-Cipher-Based AEADs (Hosoyamada, Isobe, Todo, Yasuda). Formalizes white-box AEAD incompressibility (whPRI), shows CCM/GCM/OCB insecure, gives secure modes.
- **137910349.pdf** — Linear-map Vector Commitments and their Practical Applications (Campanelli, Nitulescu, Ràfols, Zacharakis, Zapico). Unifying VC framework with constructions reusing existing powers-of-tau setups, improving state of the art.
- **137910354.pdf** — Concurrently Composable Non-Interactive Secure Computation (Morgan, Pass). First concurrently composable SPS-NISC in the plain model, identifying essentially necessary and sufficient primitives.
- **137910356.pdf** — Rotatable Zero Knowledge Sets (Chen, Dodis, Ghosh, Goldin, Kesavan, Marcedone, Mou). RZKS adds post-compromise security to auditable dictionaries for key transparency systems.
- **137910357.pdf** — Group Action Key Encapsulation and Non-Interactive Key Exchange in the QROM (Duman, Hartmann, Kiltz, Kunzweiler, Lehmann, Riepel). Shows active QROM security of GA-HEG/GA-HDH requires quantum-accessible strong CDH; gives variants from classical assumptions.
- **137910358.pdf** — Horizontal Racewalking Using Radical Isogenies (Castryck, Decru, Houben, Vercauteren). Extends radical isogeny formulae to N≤37, optimizes them, and solves radical selection over supersingular F_p curves.
- **137910362.pdf** — Exploring SAT for Cryptanalysis: (Quantum) Collision Attacks against 6-Round SHA-3 (Guo, Liu, Song, Tu). SAT-based trail search yields first 6-round classical/quantum collision attacks on SHAKE128/SHA3-224/SHA3-256.
- **137910371.pdf** — Towards Practical Topology-Hiding Computation (Li). Improves communication of topology-hiding broadcast on cycles from O(n²κ²) to O(n²κ), plus sum/general computation results.
- **13940002.pdf** — Sender-binding Key Encapsulation (Benz, Beskorovajnov, Eilebrecht, Müller-Quade, Ottenhues, Schwerdt). Shows KEMs weaker than CCA2 suffice for UC-secure hybrid encryption with authentication.
- **13940007.pdf** — Improved Private Set Intersection for Sets with Small Entries (Bui, Couteau). PSI from vector-OLE/ring-OLE with communication independent of security parameter, including a standard-model ring-LPN variant.
- **13940008.pdf** — Pseudorandom Correlation Functions from Variable-Density LPN, Revisited (Couteau, Ducros). Gives concrete, efficient PCF instantiations by strengthening analysis of the VDLPN assumption beyond prior asymptotics.
- **13940024.pdf** — Efficient and Universally Composable Single Secret Leader Election from Pairings (Catalano, Fiore, Giunta). UC definition of SSLE and efficient realization from distributable public-key encryption with keyword search.
- **13940030.pdf** — Extendable Threshold Ring Signatures with Enhanced Anonymity (Avitabile, Botta, Fiore). Strengthens anonymity of ETRS for "count me in" applications where partial signatures sit on public bulletin boards.
- **13940031.pdf** — Tracing a Linear Subspace: Application to Linearly-Homomorphic Group Signatures (Hébant, Pointcheval, Schädlich). Code-based tracing of full collusions in span of vectors, applied to linearly-homomorphic group signatures.
- **13940032.pdf** — Round-Optimal Oblivious Transfer and MPC from Computational CSIDH (Badrinarayanan, Masny, Mukherjee, Patranabis, Raghuraman, Sarkar). First round-optimal maliciously secure OT/MPC from the weakest CSIDH assumption, plus 3-round OT extension.
- **13940037.pdf** — Simple, Fast, Efficient, and Tightly-Secure Non-Malleable Non-Interactive Timed Commitments (Chvojka, Jager). Standard-model non-malleable timed commitments via Naor-Yung plus NIZK for simple algebraic languages.
- **13940038.pdf** — Functional Encryption against Probabilistic Queries (Wang, Sun, Wang, Gu). New pIND security notion between SIM and IND, with an all-circuits FE construction secure for unbounded queries.
- **13940040.pdf** — Discretization Error Reduction for High Precision Torus Fully Homomorphic Encryption (Lee, Yoon). Extended bootstrapping (EBS) for TFHE achieving high precision with small polynomial dimension, parallelizable.
- **13940048.pdf** — A Holistic Approach Towards Side-Channel Secure Fixed-Weight Polynomial Sampling (Krausz, Land, Richter-Brockmann, Güneysu). Timing- and power-protected fixed-weight sampling for BIKE/HQC/McEliece with new comparison-based masked method.
- **13940049.pdf** — A Map of Witness Maps: New Definitions and Connections (Chakraborty, Prabhakaran, Wichs). Studies compact/unique witness maps, situating them between witness PRFs and indistinguishability obfuscation.
- **13940055.pdf** — Pattern Matching in Encrypted Stream from Inner Product Encryption (Bouscatié, Castagnos, Sanders). Relates stream encryption with pattern matching to HVE/IPE, overcoming selective-security and non-standard-assumption limits.
- **13940061.pdf** — Unidirectional Updatable Encryption and Proxy Re-encryption from DDH (Miao, Patranabis, Watson). First unidirectional UE/PRE without FHE/iO/structure, via new Key-and-Plaintext Homomorphic Encryption from DDH.
- **13940065.pdf** — Security Analysis of RSA-BSSA (Lysyanskaya). Proves proposed RSA blind-signature standard strongly one-more-unforgeable; shows RSA-FDH variant fails blindness under malicious keys.
- **13940080.pdf** — Credibility in Private Set Membership (Garg, Hajiabadi, Jain, Jin, Pandey, Shiehian). Defines credible PSM with soundness against cheating senders; round-optimal constructions from DDH/LWE with log communication.
- **13940083.pdf** — Certifying Giant Nonprimes (Homann, Hubáček, Kamath, Pietrzak). Cryptographically sound certificates of non-primality for Proth numbers, protecting distributed prime searches from malicious volunteers.
- **13940084.pdf** — Almost Tightly-Secure Re-Randomizable and Replayable CCA-secure Public Key Encryption (Faonio, Hofheinz, Russo). Multi-user/multi-ciphertext Rand-RCCA PKE with almost tight reduction; yields first almost tightly-secure MixNet.
