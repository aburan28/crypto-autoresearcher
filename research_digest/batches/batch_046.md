# Batch 046 digest

## Overview
- The batch is essentially a contiguous run of Eurocrypt 2023 proceedings papers (LNCS-style numbering 14004xxx/14085xxxx), spanning MPC, garbled circuits/RAM, ZK/SNARKs, lattices, isogenies, and symmetric cryptanalysis.
- Most notable paper: the Castryck–Decru key-recovery attack on SIDH/SIKE (14004409.pdf), breaking the NIST round-4 candidate SIKEp434 in minutes; related isogeny works include CSIDH fault attacks (14004332.pdf) and trusted supersingular curve generation (14004354.pdf).
- Strong MPC cluster: garbled RAM (NanoGRAM), actively secure 2PC half-gates, reusable NISC, MrNISC, COT/DPF tree optimizations, arithmetic garbling, and constant-overhead VOLE.
- Symmetric/SCA cluster: truncated boomerang attacks on AES-based ciphers, SHA-3 collision attacks, quantum linear key recovery via QFT, low-noise masking with Mersenne-prime ciphers, and a revisit of Prouff–Rivain masking proofs.
- Anomaly: 14004458.pdf (Rothblum, "Indistinguishable Predictions and Multi-Group Fair Learning") is an algorithmic-fairness survey, not a cryptography paper per se.
- No duplicates or non-paper files found; all 50 extracts are genuine research papers.

## Papers
- **14004262.pdf** — NanoGRAM: Garbled RAM with Õ(log N) Overhead (Andrew Park, Wei-Kai Lin, Elaine Shi). New garbled RAM achieving near-optimal Õ(λ·W log N) per-access cost non-interactively, matching Circuit-ORAM-based baselines without interaction.
- **14004267.pdf** — Privacy-Preserving Blueprints (Markulf Kohlweiss, Anna Lysyanskaya, An Nguyen). Extends anonymous credentials with an auditor "blueprint" letting a user escrow f(x,y) against a secret watchlist x; gives general FHE+NIZK construction and efficient DDH-based watchlist instantiation.
- **14004270.pdf** — Another Round of Breaking and Making Quantum Money (Jiahui Liu, Hart Montgomery, Mark Zhandry). Breaks the KLS22 lattice quantum-money scheme via a general impossibility theorem, and proposes an "invariant money" framework with candidate instantiations from group actions, isogenies, and knots.
- **14004277.pdf** — Spartan and Bulletproofs are simulation-extractable (for free!) (Quang Dao, Paul Grubbs). Proves both transparent DL-based zk-SNARKs are simulation-extractable in the ROM under plain discrete-log assumptions, ruling out adaptive soundness and malleability attacks at no extra cost.
- **14004283.pdf** — Traitor Tracing with N^{1/3}-size Ciphertexts and O(1)-size Keys from k-Lin (Junqing Gong, Ji Luo, Hoeteck Wee). First pairing-based traitor tracing with pk·sk·ct = o(N), achieving private tracing and full collusion resistance under (bilateral) k-Lin.
- **14004284.pdf** — Proof-Carrying Data From Arithmetized Random Oracles (authors not visible). Introduces the arithmetized random oracle model with a plausible standard-model instantiation, yielding PCD for arbitrary-depth predicates from collision-resistant hashing alone.
- **14004289.pdf** — Fine-Grained Non-Interactive Key-Exchange: Constructions and Lower Bounds (Abtin Afshar, Geoffroy Couteau, Mohammad Mahmoody, Elahe Sadeghi). Initiates fine-grained K-party NIKE: random-oracle constructions for all constant K, a 4-party generic-group scheme with quadratic gap, and an algebraic lower bound for 3-NIKE.
- **14004293.pdf** — Threshold and Multi-Signature Schemes from Linear Hash Functions (Stefano Tessaro, Chenzhi Zhu). Generalizes MuSig2/FROST via linear hash functions to get two-round multi- and threshold signatures secure under plain DL or RSA instead of OMDL.
- **14004294.pdf** — Black-Box Reusable NISC with Random Oracles (Yuval Ishai, Dakshita Khurana, Amit Sahai, Akshayaram Srinivasan). First reusable non-interactive secure computation for general functions using only black-box two-message OT plus a random oracle, via a NISC-to-reusable compiler.
- **14004299.pdf** — Context Discovery and Commitment Attacks: How to Break CCM, EAX, SIV, and More (Sanketh Menda, Julia Len, Paul Grubbs, Thomas Ristenpart). New context-discoverability framework yields commitment attacks against major AEAD schemes including CCM, EAX, SIV, GCM, and OCB3.
- **14004302.pdf** — Actively Secure Half-Gates with Minimum Overhead under Duplex Networks (Hongrui Cui, Xiao Wang, Kang Yang, Yu Yu). Actively secure constant-round 2PC with 2κ+5 bits one-way communication per AND gate, essentially matching semi-honest half-gates.
- **14004305.pdf** — Succinct Vector, Polynomial, and Functional Commitments from Lattices (Hoeteck Wee, David J. Wu). Framework for non-interactive lattice commitments; gives SIS-based vector commitments with private openings and the first succinct functional commitments for bounded-depth circuits.
- **14004307.pdf** — Half-Tree: Halving the Cost of Tree Expansion in COT and DPF (Xiaojie Guo, Kang Yang, Xiao Wang, Wenhao Zhang, Xiang Xie, Jiang Zhang, Zheli Liu). Suite of GGM-tree optimizations halving computation/communication in COT, sVOLE, DPF, and DCF protocols.
- **14004309.pdf** — Efficient FHEW Bootstrapping with Small Evaluation Keys, and Applications to Threshold Homomorphic Encryption (Yongwoo Lee, Daniele Micciancio, Andrey Kim, et al.). New FHEW-style bootstrapping combining arbitrary secret-key distributions with small TFHE-like evaluation keys and lower noise growth.
- **14004311.pdf** — Efficient Detection of High Probability Statistical Properties of Cryptosystems via Surrogate Differentiation (Itai Dinur, Orr Dunkelman, Nathan Keller, Eyal Ronen, Adi Shamir). Top-down black-box method using surrogate differentiation to find all significant differential/linear properties of a primitive roughly 2^{n/2} faster than prior techniques.
- **14004312.pdf** — Maliciously-Secure MrNISC in the Plain Model (Rex Fernando, Aayush Jain, Ilan Komargodski). Constructs multiparty reusable non-interactive secure computation with malicious security in the plain model, achieving round-optimal, succinct, concurrency-friendly MPC.
- **14004315.pdf** — Password-Authenticated TLS via OPAQUE and Post-Handshake Authentication (Julia Hesse, Stanislaw Jarecki, Hugo Krawczyk, Christopher Wood). UC security analysis of TLS-OPAQUE, the IETF-track composition of the OPAQUE aPAKE with TLS 1.3 exported authenticators.
- **14004316.pdf** — Randomized Half-Ideal Cipher on Groups with Applications to UC (a)PAKE (Bruno Freitas Dos Santos, Yanqi Gu, Stanislaw Jarecki). Introduces half-ideal ciphers realizable by a 2-round Feistel with one hash-onto-group op; suffices as drop-in for IC in EKE and aPAKE UC proofs.
- **14004320.pdf** — On the Optimal Succinctness and Efficiency of Functional Encryption and Attribute-Based Encryption (Aayush Jain, Huijia Lin, Ji Luo). First partially-hiding FE for RAM programs from circuit FE, with constant-size keys, rate-2 ciphertexts, and near-optimal decryption time.
- **14004329.pdf** — Truncated Boomerang Attacks and Application to AES-based Ciphers (Augustin Bariant, Gaëtan Leurent). General truncated-boomerang framework yielding best-known attacks on 8-round Kiasu-BC, round-reduced Deoxys-BC, and a distinguisher on full TNT-AES.
- **14004332.pdf** — Disorientation faults in CSIDH (Gustavo Banegas, Juliane Krämer, Tanja Lange, Michael Meyer, Lorenz Panny, Krijn Reijnders, Jana Sotáková, Monika Trimoska). Fault-injection attacks that flip isogeny-step directions in CSIDH group-action evaluation, enabling full key recovery with few faults.
- **14004338.pdf** — Effective and Efficient Masking with Low Noise using Small-Mersenne-Prime Ciphers (Loïc Masure, Pierrick Méaux, Thorben Moos, François-Xavier Standaert). Shows arithmetic encodings over small Mersenne-prime fields keep masking secure even with almost no leakage noise, unlike Boolean masking.
- **14004341.pdf** — Witness-Succinct Universally-Composable SNARKs (Chaya Ganesh, Yashvanth Kondi, Claudio Orlandi, Mahak Pancholi, Akira Takahashi, Daniel Tschudi). Compiler lifting any simulation-extractable NIZK to UC security in the global ROM while preserving witness succinctness; yields first constant-proof UC zkSNARKs.
- **14004342.pdf** — New Ways to Garble Arithmetic Circuits (Marshall Ball, Hanjun Li, Huijia Lin, Tianren Liu). Improved arithmetic garbling schemes with better rate, modularity, and functionality over the AIK (FOCS'11) LWE-based approach.
- **14004351.pdf** — Finding Many Collisions via Reusable Quantum Walks — Application to Lattice Sieving (Xavier Bonnetain, André Chailloux, André Schrottenloher, Yixin Shen). Chained/reusable quantum-walk algorithm finding many collisions, matching known lower bounds over wider parameter ranges and improving quantum lattice sieving.
- **14004354.pdf** — Supersingular Curves You Can Trust (Andrea Basso, Giulio Codogni, Deirdre Connolly, Luca De Feo, et al.). Addresses generating supersingular elliptic curves with unknown endomorphism ring without trusted setup, a key need for isogeny-based protocols.
- **14004358.pdf** — End-to-End Secure Messaging with Traceability Only for Illegal Content (James Bartusek, Sanjam Garg, Abhishek Jain, Guru-Vamsi Policharla). Set pre-constrained group signatures enabling tracing of originators of predefined illegal content while remaining secure against malicious service providers; concretely efficient implementation.
- **14004370.pdf** — A New Algebraic Approach to the Regular Syndrome Decoding Problem and Implications for PCG Constructions (Pierre Briaud, Morten Øygarden). First algebraic attack exploiting the regular noise structure of RSD / regular-LPN, beating prior algorithms for some MPC/ZK-relevant parameters.
- **14004372.pdf** — Let Attackers Program Ideal Models: Modularity and Composability for Adaptive Compromise (Joseph Jaeger). Fixes adaptive-compromise definitions of Jaeger–Tyagi (Crypto'20) for composition; proves modular KEM/DEM and Fujisaki–Okamoto secure under full adaptive compromise.
- **14004383.pdf** — Actively Secure Arithmetic Computation and VOLE with Constant Computational Overhead (Benny Applebaum, Niv Konstantini). First actively secure two-party arithmetic computation with constant amortized field operations per gate, via a highly efficient VOLE of rate 1/4.
- **14004384.pdf** — Endemic Oblivious Transfer via Random Oracles, Revisited (Zhelei Zhou, Bingsheng Zhang, Hong-Sheng Zhou, Kui Ren). Systematic UC/GUC study of endemic OT: 1-round UC EOT from DDH, first GUC construction from CDH, an impossibility in the programmable GRO model, and a 2-round adaptive protocol.
- **14004390.pdf** — Impossibility of Indifferentiable Iterated Blockciphers from 3 or Less Primitive Calls (Chun Guo, Lei Wang, Dongdai Lin). Proves no iterated blockcipher making ≤3 calls to random functions/permutations is statistically indifferentiable from an ideal cipher, proving optimality of a prior 4-call construction.
- **14004393.pdf** — Efficient Laconic Cryptography from Learning With Errors (Nico Döttling, Dimitris Kolonelos, Russell W. F. Lai, Chuanwei Lin, Giulio Malavolta, Ahmadreza Rahimi). Fully algebraic (black-box) laconic encryption from LWE forming the basis of efficient laconic primitives, removing impractical non-black-box techniques.
- **14004401.pdf** — Worst-Case Subexponential Attacks on PRGs of Constant Degree or Constant Locality (Akın Ünal). First unconditional subexponential-time distinguishing attacks on polynomial-stretch algebraic PRGs of constant degree or constant locality.
- **14004405.pdf** — An Incremental PoSW for General Weight Distributions (Hamza Abusalah, Valerio Cini). Incremental proof-of-sequential-work from skiplist-based PoSW, secure in the ROM, suitable for SNACK-based blockchain light clients under general weight distributions.
- **14004409.pdf** — An Efficient Key Recovery Attack on SIDH (Wouter Castryck, Thomas Decru). Polynomial-time key recovery on SIDH via Kani's reducibility criterion using exchanged torsion-point images; Magma implementation breaks NIST-candidate SIKEp434 in about ten minutes on one core.
- **14004414.pdf** — Broadcast, Trace and Revoke with Optimal Parameters from Polynomial Hardness (Shweta Agrawal, Simran Kumari, Anshu Yadav, Shota Yamada). Broadcast-trace-revoke with all parameters independent of user/revocation-list size, from compact FE and KP-ABE (polynomial assumptions), with embedded identities and adaptive security.
- **14004415.pdf** — How to Compress Encrypted Data (Nils Fleischhacker, Kasper Green Larsen, Mark Simkin). Two homomorphic algorithms compressing n ciphertexts with ≤t nonzeros to ~O(tξ) bits using only additions and constant multiplications, improving asymptotic and concrete state of the art.
- **14004423.pdf** — XOCB: Beyond-Birthday-Bound Secure Authenticated Encryption Mode with Rate-One Computation (Zhenzhen Bao, Seongha Hwang, Akiko Inoue, Byeonghak Lee, Jooyoung Lee, Kazuhiko Minematsu). AE mode with OCB-like rate-1 efficiency but BBB security from the block cipher's standard PRP assumption, for any key length.
- **14004424.pdf** — End-to-End Encrypted Zoom Meetings: Proving Security and Strengthening Liveness (Yevgeniy Dodis, Daniel Jost, Balachandar Kesavan, Antonio Marcedone). First formal analysis of Zoom's E2EE protocol; introduces leader-based continuous group key agreement with liveness suited to synchronous video calls.
- **14004425.pdf** — Collision Attacks on Round-Reduced SHA-3 Using Conditional Internal Differentials (Zhongyi Zhang, Chengan Hou, Meicheng Liu). New internal-differential collision attacks on round-reduced SHA-3 (including SHA3-512 and first SHAKE256 results) via differential transition conditions and difference condition tables.
- **14004431.pdf** — Weighted Oblivious RAM, with Applications to Searchable Symmetric Encryption (Léonard Assouline, Brice Minaud). Weighted ORAM supporting variable-size blocks via a framework over tree-based ORAMs, yielding efficient SSE with stronger security properties.
- **14004458.pdf** — Indistinguishable Predictions and Multi-Group Fair Learning (Guy N. Rothblum). Survey (not a cryptography paper) on outcome indistinguishability and multi-group fairness in prediction algorithms, inspired by computational indistinguishability.
- **140850004.pdf** — Prouff & Rivain's Formal Security Proof of Masking, Revisited: Tight Bounds in the Noisy Leakage Model (Loïc Masure, François-Xavier Standaert). Revisits the Eurocrypt 2013 masking proof to give tight noisy-leakage security bounds, removing prior proof-artifact restrictions on noise level, security parameter, and adversary type.
- **140850006.pdf** — Tracing Quantum State Distinguishers via Backtracking (Mark Zhandry). "Back One Step" model yields post-quantum iO/diO equivalence for polynomially many differing points and traitor tracing schemes secure against quantum-state decoders from PKE.
- **140850008.pdf** — Coefficient Grouping for Complex Affine Layers (Fukang Liu, Lorenzo Grassi, Clémence Bouvier, Willi Meier, Takanori Isobe). Extends the coefficient-grouping technique to arbitrary-weight affine layers, bounding algebraic degree of SPN ciphers relevant to FHE-friendly symmetric design.
- **140850019.pdf** — Lattice Signature with Efficient Protocols, Application to Anonymous Credentials (Corentin Jeudy, Adeline Roux-Langlois, Olivier Sanders). Post-quantum signatures with efficient protocols on standard and structured lattices; proof of signature possession under 650 KB, enabling practical lattice anonymous credentials.
- **140850025.pdf** — Extractors: Low Entropy Requirements Colliding With Non-Malleability (Divesh Aggarwal, Eldon Chung, Maciej Obremski). Introduces collision-resistant randomness extractors to push two-source non-malleable extractors below the 0.99n min-entropy barrier of prior constructions.
- **140850029.pdf** — Quantum Linear Key-recovery Attacks Using the QFT (André Schrottenloher). Framework quantizing FFT-based linear key-recovery attacks via the Quantum Fourier Transform, giving super-quadratic quantum speedups with classical known-plaintext queries.
- **140850031.pdf** — Does the Dual-Sieve Attack on Learning with Errors even Work? (Léo Ducas, Ludo N. Pulles). Generalizes the FFT trick in dual-sieve LWE attacks and shows the underlying heuristics contradict formal theorems in some regimes, casting doubt on claimed improvements against NIST lattice candidates.
