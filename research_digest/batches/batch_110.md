# Batch 110 digest

## Overview
- Overwhelmingly CHES 2014 (8731xxxx) and ASIACRYPT 2014 (8873xxxx) papers: hardware crypto, side-channel attacks/countermeasures, and provable security work from the 2014 era.
- Dominant theme is side-channel analysis: static-power leakage, higher-order DPA models, EM sensors, masking conversions, gate-level masking, template attacks, and physical attacks on laptops and PUFs.
- Strong representation of efficient implementations: GPU-accelerated NFS cofactorization, Curve41417 ECDH on ARM, genus-2 Jacobian coordinates, Ring-LWE and BLISS lattice crypto on reconfigurable hardware, pairings/ECC for embedded systems.
- Notable papers: Genkin–Pipman–Tromer "Get Your Hands Off My Laptop" physical key-extraction; Bernstein–Chuengsatiansup–Lange Curve41417; Boneh–Corrigan-Gibbs bivariate polynomials mod composites; Jean–Nikolić–Peyrin TWEAKEY framework.
- Anomaly: 87310144.pdf is unextractable garbage (font-encoded character codes, no readable text) — likely a Chinese-language paper with broken extraction.

## Papers
- **87310110.pdf** — Cofactorization on Graphics Processing Units (Miele, Bos, Kleinjung, Lenstra). Offloads the NFS cofactorization step to a GTX 580 GPU acting as a cryptanalytic co-processor alongside CPUs, yielding more NFS relations in less time.
- **87310111.pdf** — EM Attack Is Non-Invasive? — Design Methodology and Validity Verification of EM Attack Sensor (Homma et al.). Standard-cell-based semi-automatic design of an LC-oscillator "EM attack sensor" countermeasure against EM analysis and fault-injection attacks.
- **87310113.pdf** — Side-Channel Leakage through Static Power — Should We Care about in Practice? (Moradi). First practical key-recovery side-channel attack exploiting static (leakage) power consumption of 65/45/28 nm Xilinx FPGAs.
- **87310121.pdf** — FOAM: Searching for Hardware-Optimal SPN Structures and Components with a Fair Comparison (Khoo, Peyrin, Poschmann, Yap). Proposes the "figure of adversarial merit" metric combining security and implementation properties to compare SPN designs fairly.
- **87310122.pdf** — Entropy Evaluation for Oscillator-based True Random Number Generators (Ma et al.). Studies entropy estimation for oscillator-sampling TRNGs, whose randomness quality directly affects cryptographic system security.
- **87310131.pdf** — A Statistical Model for Higher Order DPA on Masked Devices (Ding, Zhang, Fei, Luo). Formulates a statistical model for higher-order (multivariate) DPA attacks against masked block cipher implementations.
- **87310132.pdf** — FPGA Implementations of SPRING and Their Countermeasures against Side-Channel Attacks (Brenner et al.). Hardware implementations of the SPRING PRF family with side-channel countermeasures, targeting AES-competitive throughput.
- **87310140.pdf** — Destroying Fault Invariant with Randomization — A Countermeasure for AES against Differential Fault Attacks (Tupsamudre, Bisht, Mukhopadhyay). Improves a LatinCrypt 2012 randomness-based fault countermeasure so the faulty last-round byte is also masked.
- **87310143.pdf** — Fast Evaluation of Polynomials over Binary Finite Fields and Application to Side-channel Countermeasures (Coron, Roy, Vivek). New polynomial evaluation technique with heuristic O(2^(n/2)/√n) complexity, speeding masked S-box computations, plus a matching lower bound.
- **87310144.pdf** — Not readable: extraction is font-encoded character codes (appears to be a Chinese-language paper) with no recoverable title or text.
- **87310148.pdf** — Side-Channel Attack Against RSA Key Generation Algorithms (Bauer, Jaulmes, Lomné, Prouff, Roche). Extends side-channel attacks on embedded RSA prime generation beyond naive implementations to protected ones.
- **87310149.pdf** — Secure Lightweight Entity Authentication with Strong PUFs: Mission Impossible? (Delvaux, Gu, Schellekens, Verbauwhede). Questions feasibility of lightweight strong-PUF-based IC authentication protocols.
- **87310157.pdf** — Secure Conversion Between Boolean and Arithmetic Masking of Any Order (Coron, Großschädl, Vadnala). Extends Goubin's CHES 2001 masking conversion algorithms to arbitrary masking orders.
- **87310164.pdf** — Efficient Pairings and ECC for Embedded Systems (Unterluggauer, Wenger). Three small, side-channel-protected hardware/software designs making pairing-based cryptography practical on ARM embedded platforms.
- **87310166.pdf** — A New Framework for Constraint-Based Probabilistic Template Side Channel Attacks (Oren, Weisse, Wool). Combines SAT/Pseudo-Boolean constraint solving with probabilistic templates to extract keys from few side-channel traces.
- **87310170.pdf** — Making RSA–PSS Provably Secure Against Non-Random Faults (Barthe, Dupressoir, Fouque, Grégoire, Tibouchi, Zapalowicz). Gives provable security of RSA–PSS (CRT-based) signatures against non-random fault attacks.
- **87310171.pdf** — Enhanced Lattice-Based Signatures on Reconfigurable Hardware (Pöppelmann, Ducas, Güneysu). FPGA implementation of the BLISS lattice signature scheme, tackling high-precision Gaussian sampling in hardware.
- **87310174.pdf** — Early Propagation and Imbalanced Routing, How to Diminish in FPGAs (Moradi, Immler). Addresses early-propagation and routing-imbalance flaws that undermine DPA-resistant logic styles on FPGAs.
- **87310178.pdf** — ICEPOLE: High-speed, Hardware-oriented Authenticated Encryption (Morawiecki et al.). Introduces the ICEPOLE authenticated encryption family designed for high-speed hardware performance (CAESAR candidate).
- **87310183.pdf** — Compact Ring-LWE Cryptoprocessor (Sinha Roy, Vercauteren, Mentens, Chen, Verbauwhede). Compact Ring-LWE encryption processor with three NTT optimizations for efficient polynomial multiplication.
- **87310188.pdf** — Efficient Power and Timing Side Channels for Physical Unclonable Functions (Rührmair et al.). Shows power and timing side channels undermine the claimed physical-attack resilience of PUFs.
- **87310195.pdf** — How to Estimate the Success Rate of Higher-Order Side-Channel Attacks (Lomné, Prouff, Rivain, Roche, Thillard). Methods to estimate attack success rates when direct measurement is too costly for evaluators.
- **87310197.pdf** — Gate-Level Masking Under a Path-Based Leakage Metric (Leiserson, Marson, Wachs). New gate-level masking technique robust to glitches and early propagation, evaluated under a path-based leakage metric.
- **87310201.pdf** — Physical Characterization of Arbiter PUFs (Tajik et al.). Physical (e.g., laser/THz spectroscopy) characterization of arbiter PUFs, probing their resistance to invasive analysis.
- **87310202.pdf** — Get Your Hands Off My Laptop: Physical Side-Channel Key-Extraction Attacks on PCs (Genkin, Pipman, Tromer). Extracts RSA/ElGamal keys from laptops via chassis ground-potential fluctuations measurable by touch or cable.
- **87310203.pdf** — Good is Not Good Enough: Deriving Optimal Distinguishers from Communication Theory (Heuser, Rioul, Guilley). Treats the side channel as a communication channel to derive mathematically optimal distinguishers for any noise scenario.
- **87310207.pdf** — RSA meets DPA: Recovering RSA Secret Keys from Noisy Analog Data (Kunihiro, Honda). Two algorithms recovering RSA keys directly from noisy analog data (cold boot/side channel) without lossy quantization.
- **87310220.pdf** — Simple Power Analysis on AES Key Expansion Revisited (Clavier, Marion, Wurcker). Revisits Hamming-weight SPA on 8-bit AES key expansion, analyzing attacks despite common countermeasures.
- **87310222.pdf** — Bitline PUF: Building Native Challenge-Response PUF Capability into Any SRAM (Holcomb, Fu). Turns existing SRAM bitlines into a challenge-response PUF, avoiding dedicated idle silicon area.
- **87310223.pdf** — Constructing S-boxes for Lightweight Cryptography with Feistel Structure (Li, Wang). Constructs S-boxes balancing differential uniformity, nonlinearity, and low hardware cost via Feistel structures.
- **87310226.pdf** — Curve41417: Karatsuba Revisited (Bernstein, Chuengsatiansup, Lange). Constant-time ARM Cortex-A8 ECDH faster than OpenSSL's fastest option while exceeding 2^200 security using a >2^400 prime.
- **88730111.pdf** — Mersenne Factorization Factory (Kleinjung, Bos, Lenstra). Work in progress factoring seventeen Mersenne numbers via shared-sieving SNFS, the first practical "factorization factory," cutting effort over 50%.
- **88730115.pdf** — Automatic Security Evaluation and (Related-key) Differential Characteristic Search (Sun et al.). Systematic linear-inequality methods to describe S-box differential properties, applied to SIMON, PRESENT, LBlock, DES(L).
- **88730117.pdf** — Multi-Valued Byzantine Broadcast: the t<n Case (Hirt, Raykov). Broadcast protocols for t<n with O(ℓn) communication for large messages, matching the optimal complexity bound.
- **88730123.pdf** — Simulatable Leakage: Analysis, Pitfalls, and New Constructions (Longo Galea et al.). Critically analyzes Standaert et al.'s simulatable-leakage notion bridging leakage-resilience theory and side-channel practice.
- **88730132.pdf** — Jacobian Coordinates on Genus 2 Curves (Hisil, Costello). New projective coordinates and explicit formulas speeding genus-2 Jacobian scalar multiplication by ~1.25× over prime fields.
- **88730134.pdf** — Fairness Versus Guaranteed Output Delivery in Secure Multiparty Computation (Cohen, Lindell). Studies the relationship between fairness and guaranteed output delivery in MPC without honest majority.
- **88730144.pdf** — Multi-target DPA Attacks: Pushing DPA Beyond the Limits of a Desktop Computer (Mather, Oswald, Whitnall). Scales DPA to multi-target settings with computation beyond standard desktop capabilities.
- **88730148.pdf** — Actively Secure Private Function Evaluation (Mohassel, Sadeghian, Smart). First general framework for actively secure PFE without universal circuits, split into pre-processing and online stages.
- **88730163.pdf** — Composable Security of Delegated Quantum Computation (Dunjko, Fitzsimons, Portmann, Renner). Proves composable security for delegated (blind) quantum computation protocols.
- **88730167.pdf** — Secret-Sharing for NP (Komargodski, Naor, Yogev). Advances the open problem of which monotone functions admit computational secret-sharing, realizing schemes for NP access structures.
- **88730171.pdf** — Multi-user Collisions: Applications to Discrete Logarithm, Even-Mansour and PRINCE (Fouque, Joux, Mavromati). Studies multi-user cryptanalytic settings, applying collision techniques to discrete log, Even-Mansour, and PRINCE.
- **88730185.pdf** — Tweaks and Keys for Block Ciphers: the TWEAKEY Framework (Jean, Nikolić, Peyrin). Unifies tweakable block cipher and related-key-resistant design via the TWEAKEY framework with arbitrary tweak/key sizes.
- **88730188.pdf** — Structure-Preserving Signatures on Equivalence Classes and their Application to Anonymous Credentials (Hanser, Slamanig). New structure-preserving signatures allowing consistent randomization of messages and signatures for anonymous credentials.
- **88730191.pdf** — Forging Attacks on Two Authenticated Encryption Schemes COBRA and POET (Nandi). Shows universal forgery attacks on the FSE 2014/CAESAR AE modes COBRA and POET.
- **88730193.pdf** — Low Probability Differentials and the Cryptanalysis of Full-Round CLEFIA-128 (Emami, Ling, Nikolić, Pieprzyk, Wang). Shows the 2^-k probability bound assumption for related-key security is insufficient; cryptanalyzes full-round CLEFIA-128.
- **88730194.pdf** — Order-Preserving Encryption Secure Beyond One-Wayness (Teranishi, Yung, Malkin). Initiates semantic-security-style study for OPE, defining what plaintext information OPE can hide beyond one-wayness.
- **88730197.pdf** — XLS is not a Strong Pseudorandom Permutation (Nandi). Disproves the claimed SPRP security of the XLS length-preserving construction with a three-query distinguisher of advantage ~1/2.
- **88730211.pdf** — Homomorphic Authenticated Encryption Secure Against Chosen-Ciphertext Attack (Joo, Yun). Defines homomorphic AE security notions (including a meaningful IND-CCA analogue) and gives a construction.
- **88730215.pdf** — Bivariate Polynomials Modulo Composites and their Applications (Boneh, Corrigan-Gibbs). Studies hardness of bivariate polynomial congruences mod RSA composites, deriving a new assumption and an algebraic collision-resistant hash.
