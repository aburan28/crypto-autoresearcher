# Batch 027 digest

## Overview
- The entire batch is drawn from a single venue and era: TCC 2020 proceedings (LNCS volume 12550, filenames 12550xxx), i.e., modern theory-of-cryptography work — no toy curves or ECDLP experiments.
- Dominant themes: succinct arguments / zero knowledge (SNARKs, IOPs, NIZKs, witness hiding, deterministic-prover ZK), secure multiparty computation (round-optimal MPC, fairness, asynchronous BA), and quantum-era cryptography (classical verification of quantum computation, certified deletion, quantum traitor tracing).
- Secondary themes: blockchains and consensus (ledger combiners, blockchain from non-idealized hashes, secret-keeping blockchains), secret sharing lower/upper bounds, ORAM lower bounds, and foundational/primitive results (extractable OWFs, non-committing encryption, function inversion tradeoffs).
- Discrete-log/DDH appears as a hardness assumption in several constructions (e.g., reusable two-round MPC from DDH, range-trapdoor functions from power-DDH, algebraic group model analyses of decisional assumptions), but no paper is specifically about elliptic-curve cryptography or ECDLP.
- Anomaly: 12550135.pdf is not a research paper — it is the Springer LLNCS LaTeX class instruction document.
- Notable high-impact results: round-optimal (4-round) MPC from minimal assumptions (12550195), first symmetric-key CP-ABE for circuits from LWE (12550137), and the accumulation-scheme foundations for recursive proof composition later used in Halo-style systems (12550115).

## Papers
- **12550115.pdf** — Recursive Proof Composition from Accumulation Schemes (Bünz, Chiesa, Mishra, Spooner). Formalizes accumulation schemes as the foundation of recursive SNARK composition, yielding PCD without requiring a sublinear-time verifier.
- **12550117.pdf** — Linear-Time Arguments with Sublinear Verification from Tensor Codes (Bootle, Chiesa, Groth). Builds an IOP from tensor codes with linear-time prover and sublinear (O(N^ε)) verifier, in the holographic setting.
- **12550118.pdf** — On Perfect Correctness in (Lockable) Obfuscation (Goyal, Koppula, Vusirikala, Waters). Studies how to remove the one-sided correctness error of LWE-based lockable obfuscation, motivated by backdoor-injection concerns.
- **12550121.pdf** — Barriers for Succinct Arguments in the Random Oracle Model (Chiesa, Yogev). Shows succinct RO arguments imply IOPs, whose parameters in turn imply fast algorithms, yielding lower-bound barriers under ETH.
- **12550122.pdf** — Schrödinger's Pirate: How To Trace a Quantum Decoder (Zhandry). Defines traitor tracing against quantum pirate decoders, proves barriers for classical tracing techniques, and gives positive results for private linear broadcast encryption.
- **12550125.pdf** — A Lower Bound for One-Round Oblivious RAM (Cash, Drucker, Hoover). Proves one-round balls-in-bins ORAM needs Ω(√N) bandwidth or client memory, showing optimality of the one-round square-root ORAM.
- **12550126.pdf** — Can a Public Blockchain Keep a Secret? (Benhamouda, Gentry, Gorbunov, Halevi, Krawczyk, Lin, Rabin, Reyzin). Uses proactive secret sharing with small committees to let a public blockchain store and conditionally release secrets against mobile adversaries.
- **12550127.pdf** — New Techniques in Replica Encodings with Client Setup (Garg, Lu, Waters). Advances proofs of replication with client setup (Damgård–Ganesh–Orlandi style), analyzing and improving replica encoding constructions without timing assumptions.
- **12550130.pdf** — Quantum encryption with certified deletion (Broadbent, Islam). Encrypts classical data into quantum ciphertexts so a recipient can provably delete the plaintext; feasible with single-qubit devices and finite-key analysis.
- **12550134.pdf** — Secure Quantum Extraction Protocols (Ananth, La Placa). Defines and constructs classical interactive arguments of knowledge with extraction and zero-knowledge against quantum polynomial-time adversaries.
- **12550135.pdf** — Not a research paper: Springer LLNCS LaTeX2ε class usage instructions for proceedings papers.
- **12550136.pdf** — Efficient Range-Trapdoor Functions and Applications: Rate-1 OT and More (Garg, Hajiabadi, Ostrovsky). Constructs range-trapdoor hash functions with short public keys and O(n log n) evaluation from power-DDH, giving rate-1 lossy TDFs and OT.
- **12550137.pdf** — CP-ABE for Circuits (and more) in the Symmetric Key Setting (Agrawal, Yamada). First symmetric-key ciphertext-policy ABE for all polynomial-size circuits from LWE, with unbounded collusion resistance.
- **12550138.pdf** — Optimal Broadcast Encryption from LWE and Pairings in the Standard Model (Agrawal, Wichs, Yamada). Achieves optimal-parameter broadcast encryption from LWE plus the KOALA assumption, proven in the standard model.
- **12550140.pdf** — Equipping Public-Key Cryptographic Primitives with Watermarking (Nishimaki). General framework converting any PKC primitive with a canonical all-but-one reduction into an efficiently watermarkable variant.
- **12550141.pdf** — Robust Secret Sharing with Almost Optimal Share Size and Security Against Rushing Adversaries (Fehr, Yuan). Gives robust secret sharing for t < n/2 with optimal share-size overhead, rushing security, and polynomial-time procedures.
- **12550142.pdf** — The Share Size of Secret-Sharing Schemes for Almost All Access Structures and Graphs (Beimel, Farràs). Proves almost all access structures admit secret-sharing schemes with share size 2^Õ(√n), plus results for graph access structures.
- **12550147.pdf** — Batch Verification for Statistical Zero Knowledge Proofs (Kaslasi, Rothblum, Rothblum, Sealfon, Vasudevan). Gives efficient honest-verifier SZK batch verification for languages with non-interactive SZK protocols, with near-additive polylog communication per instance.
- **12550149.pdf** — Coupling of Random Systems (Lanzenberger, Maurer). Develops a simple theory of random systems where distinguishing advantage equals statistical distance of couplings, and proves threshold combiners amplify indistinguishability.
- **12550156.pdf** — Revisiting Fairness in MPC: Polynomial Number of Parties and General Adversarial Structures (Dachman-Soled). Constructs fair MPC without honest majority for polynomially many parties, covering symmetric Boolean functions and majority via combinatorial designs.
- **12550159.pdf** — On the Price of Concurrency in Group Ratcheting Protocols (Bienstock, Dodis, Rösler). Formally studies tradeoffs between post-compromise security, concurrency, and communication overhead in group ratcheting (Signal/MLS context).
- **12550162.pdf** — Stronger Security and Constructions of Multi-Designated Verifier Signatures (Damgård, Haagh, Mercer, Nitulescu, Orlandi, Yakoubov). Strengthens MDVS definitions (source hiding under partial collusion, consistency) for group off-the-record messaging and gives matching constructions.
- **12550163.pdf** — Non-interactive classical verification of quantum computation (Alagic, Childs, Grilo, Hung). Makes Mahadev's classical verification of quantum computation non-interactive and zero-knowledge, yielding the first classical NIZK for QMA.
- **12550166.pdf** — Accumulators in (and Beyond) Generic Groups: Non-Trivial Batch Verification Requires Interaction (Schul-Ganz, Segev). Proves tight Ω(t·λ/log λ) group-operation lower bound for non-interactive batch verification in generic-group accumulators.
- **12550167.pdf** — On the Power of an Honest Majority in Three-Party Computation Without Broadcast (Alon, Cohen, Omri, Suad). Characterizes which asymmetric three-party functionalities admit fully secure computation with honest majority but no broadcast channel.
- **12550171.pdf** — Characterizing Deterministic-Prover Zero Knowledge (Bitansky, Choudhuri). Shows deterministic-prover ZK is feasible against bounded-auxiliary-input verifiers (from iO/NIWI assumptions), characterizing when it can exist.
- **12550173.pdf** — Zero-Communication Reductions (Narayanan, Prabhakaran, Prabhakaran). Introduces zero-communication reductions in information-theoretic crypto, yielding new PSM/CDS/OT complexity bounds and an invertible-rank conjecture for super-linear OT lower bounds.
- **12550176.pdf** — NIZK from SNARG (Kitagawa, Matsuda, Yamakawa). Builds NIZK for NP from any mildly succinct SNARG plus one-way functions, and gives a generic SNARG-to-zkSNARG conversion from PKE.
- **12550179.pdf** — Weakly Extractable One-Way Functions (Bitansky, Eizenstadt, Paneth). Defines weakly extractable OWFs circumventing iO-based barriers and ties them to the existence of three-message zero knowledge.
- **12550183.pdf** — Algebraic Distinguishers: From Discrete Logarithms to Decisional Uber Assumptions (Rotem, Segev). Extends the algebraic group model to decisional problems, showing polynomial equivalence among DDH, Linear, and Uber-type assumptions algebraically.
- **12550184.pdf** — Continuous Group Key Agreement with Active Security (Alwen, Coretti, Jost, Mularczyk). Proposes three progressively stronger (passive to active/insider) security notions for CGKA, showing prior constructions fail even the weakest.
- **12550186.pdf** — Classical Verification of Quantum Computations with Efficient Verifier (Chia, Chung, Yamakawa). Gives constant-round negligible-soundness CVQC via parallel repetition, plus two-round protocols with efficient verifier in QROM/CRS models.
- **12550190.pdf** — Blockchains from Non-Idealized Hash Functions (Garay, Kiayias, Panagiotakos). Identifies three concrete hash properties (collision resistance, computational randomness extraction, iterated hardness) sufficient for standard-model blockchain security.
- **12550191.pdf** — Lower Bounds on the Time/Memory Tradeoff of Function Inversion (Chawin, Haitner, Mazor). Studies advice-plus-query tradeoffs for inverting random functions, focusing on the poorly understood non-adaptive setting.
- **12550193.pdf** — Ledger Combiners for Fast Settlement (Fitzi, Gaži, Kiayias, Russell). Black-box parallel composition of m longest-chain ledgers gives Θ(m)-fold latency reduction, achieving constant-time settlement for conflict-free transactions.
- **12550195.pdf** — Round Optimal Secure Multiparty Computation from Minimal Assumptions (Choudhuri, Ciampi, Goyal, Jain, Ostrovsky). Constructs four-round MPC in the plain model secure against dishonest majority, assuming only four-round oblivious transfer.
- **12550196.pdf** — Reusable Two-Round MPC from DDH (Bartusek, Garg, Masny, Mukherjee). Upgrades any two-round MPC to reuse its first message across computations, using DDH-based homomorphic secret sharing and NC1 PRFs.
- **12550199.pdf** — Transparent Error Correcting in a Computationally Bounded World (Grossman, Holmgren, Yogev). Builds uniquely decodable codes against computationally bounded channels with public-coin (transparent) setup, in the random oracle model.
- **12550202.pdf** — On Computational Shortcuts for Information-Theoretic PIR (Hong, Ishai, Kolobov, Lai). Studies when PIR servers can run sublinearly for structured databases (intervals, decision trees), with positive results for Reed–Muller PIR and hardness for matching-vector PIR.
- **12550209.pdf** — Functional Encryption for Quadratic Functions from k-Lin, Revisited (Wee). Simpler, shorter-ciphertext FE for quadratic functions and partially-hiding FE from the bilateral k-linear assumption in bilinear groups.
- **12550212.pdf** — Constant Ciphertext-Rate Non-Committing Encryption from Standard Assumptions (Brakerski, Branco, Döttling, Garg, Malavolta). First constant-rate non-committing encryption in the plain model from LWE, DDH, or QR.
- **12550214.pdf** — Super-Linear Time-Memory Trade-Offs for Symmetric Encryption (Dai, Tessaro, Zhang). Gives symmetric encryption from PRFs/PRPs with security beyond the birthday barrier against memory-bounded adversaries, via new XOR-code list-decoding bounds.
- **12550217.pdf** — A Secret-Sharing Based MPC Protocol for Boolean Circuits with Good Amortized Complexity (Cascudo, Gundersen). SPDZ-based dishonest-majority MPC evaluating many Boolean-circuit instances with ~10 bits online communication per party per multiplication gate, via reverse multiplication friendly embeddings.
- **12550219.pdf** — Asynchronous Byzantine Agreement with Subquadratic Communication (Blum, Katz, Liu-Zhang, Loss). First asynchronous BA protocols with expected subquadratic communication tolerating adaptive f < (1−ε)n/3 corruptions.
- **12550221.pdf** — Lower Bounds for Multi-Server Oblivious RAMs (Larsen, Simkin, Yeo). Proves Ω(log n) overhead lower bound for multi-server ORAM even with one corrupted server and weak distinguishing advantage, extended to other oblivious data structures.
- **12550223.pdf** — On the Security of Time-Lock Puzzles and Timed Commitments (Katz, Loss, Xu). Proves sequential-squaring hardness in a strong algebraic group model (speedup ⇒ factoring) and constructs non-malleable timed commitments.
- **12550224.pdf** — Lossiness and Entropic Hardness for Ring-LWE (Brakerski, Döttling). First entropic-hardness results for Ring-LWE (or under DSPR/NTRU-like assumptions), matching known ring-setting lower bounds.
- **12550225.pdf** — Towards Non-Interactive Witness Hiding (Kuykendall, Zhandry). Provides evidence that non-interactive witness-hiding proofs exist for wide language classes, via NIWI-based schemes and a new analysis of Pass's two-message argument.
- **12550227.pdf** — Multi-key Fully-Homomorphic Encryption in the Plain Model (Ananth, Jain, Jin, Malavolta). Constructs multi-key FHE with one-round decryption without trusted setup, from standard hardness assumptions.
- **12550229.pdf** — Expected-Time Cryptography: Generic Techniques and Applications to Concrete Soundness (Jaeger, Tessaro). Develops generic tight concrete-security tools for expected-time adversaries and derives the first quantitative soundness bounds for Bulletproofs.
