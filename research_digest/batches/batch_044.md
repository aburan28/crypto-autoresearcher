# Batch 044 digest

## Overview
- Almost all papers are 2022–2023 era, LNCS-style conference papers (consistent with PKC/CRYPTO/EUROCRYPT proceedings volumes), spanning post-quantum crypto, ABE/FE, NIZKs, and MPC.
- Strong lattice/post-quantum theme: NTRU efficiency, Kyber anonymity, Mitaka probing attack, LIP hull attacks, SCALLOP isogeny group action, FFI hardness, puncturable PRFs.
- Several applied-crypto attack papers: two independent MEGA key-recovery works (13940136 Heninger/Ryan, 14004044 Albrecht et al.), Chaghri and RIPEMD-160 cryptanalysis, sponge MitM preimage attacks.
- Zero-knowledge and advanced primitives are well represented: polynomial commitments (Dew, private PCS), subverted RSA group NIZKs, fine-grained verifier NIZK, laconic function evaluation, delegation of committed programs.
- Anomaly: `14.pdf` has a non-standard numeric filename and is an older (~2006-era) CEA-LETI side-channel power-analysis paper, out of step with the rest of the batch.
- No duplicates detected; all 50 entries are genuine research-paper first pages.

## Papers
- **13940090.pdf** — Generic Models for Group Actions (Duman, Hartmann, Kiltz, Kunzweiler, Lehmann, Riepel). Defines the Generic Group Action Model (GGAM) for CSIDH-like settings, proves lower bounds, and introduces a weaker Quantum Algebraic variant (QAGAM).
- **13940094.pdf** — POLKA: Towards Leakage-Resistant Post-Quantum CCA-Secure Public Key Encryption (Hoffmann, Libert, Momin, Peters, Standaert). Designs a PQ PKE scheme tailored to cheap side-channel protection via rigidity, randomized decryption, and key-homomorphic masking.
- **13940097.pdf** — Structure-Preserving Compilers from New Notions of Obfuscations (Campanelli, Francati, Orlandi). Proposes oracle-differing-input and oracle-indistinguishability obfuscation, weaker than VBB yet strong enough to compile secret-key primitives.
- **13940098.pdf** — A Thorough Treatment of Highly-Efficient NTRU Instantiations (Duman, Hövelmanns, Kiltz, Lyubashevsky, Seiler, Unruh). Gives NTRU instantiations/transformations with ROM/QROM proofs matching or beating Ring/Module-LWE schemes in speed and compactness.
- **13940099.pdf** — A Lightweight Identification Protocol Based on Lattices (Düzlü, Krämer, Pöppelmann, Struck). Builds a Kyber-based identification protocol avoiding FO transform and rejection sampling, targeting IoT/smart cards.
- **13940101.pdf** — Backward-Leak Uni-Directional Updatable Encryption from (Homomorphic) Public Key Encryption (Galteland, Pan). Shows backward-leak uni-directional UE is the strongest update notion and gives generic constructions from homomorphic or bootstrappable PKE.
- **13940102.pdf** — Post-Quantum Anonymity of Kyber (Maram, Xagawa). Resolves open problems from Grubbs et al. and Xagawa by proving anonymity of Kyber and hybrid PKE, needed for anonymous credentials and cryptocurrencies.
- **13940103.pdf** — GLUE: Generalizing Unbounded Attribute-Based Encryption for Flexible Efficiency Trade-Offs (Venema, Alpár). An unbounded CP-ABE scheme supporting negations and online/offline encryption with fast decryption via an encryption/decryption efficiency trade-off.
- **13940104.pdf** — A Generic Transform from Multi-Round Interactive Proof to NIZK (Fouque, Georgescu, Qian, Roux-Langlois, Wen). Fiat-Shamir-like transform with standard-model ZK and NPROM soundness, plus a new multi-round OR-composition with QROM security.
- **13940110.pdf** — Verifiable Capacity-bound Functions: A New Primitive from Kolmogorov Complexity (Ateniese, Chen, Francati, Papadopoulos, Tang). Initiates VCBFs, a "space" analog of VDFs with memory lower bounds and sublinear verification.
- **13940114.pdf** — Dew: A Transparent Constant-sized Polynomial Commitment Scheme (Arun, Ganesh, Lokam, Mopuri, Sridhar). First transparent PCS with constant-size proofs and logarithmic verification, from class groups, yielding constant-sized zk-SNARKs.
- **13940122.pdf** — Multi-Authority ABE for Non-Monotonic Access Structures (Ambrona, Gay). First MA-ABE from asymmetric prime-order pairings without trusted setup, with unbounded attribute universes and non-monotonic policies.
- **13940124.pdf** — CRAFT: Composable Randomness Beacons and Output-Independent Abort MPC From Time (Baum, David, Dowsley, Kishore, Nielsen, Oechsner). Extends TARDIS to UC-composable time-lock puzzles, randomness beacons, and OIA MPC from time-based primitives.
- **13940125.pdf** — Transparent Batchable Time-lock Puzzles and Applications to Byzantine Consensus (Srinivasan, Loss, Malavolta, Nayak, Papamanthou, Thyagarajan). Achieves transparent TLPs with batch solving of unbounded size via indistinguishability obfuscation, applied to consensus.
- **13940129.pdf** — Zero-Knowledge Arguments for Subverted RSA Groups (Kolonelos, Maller, Volkhov). New witness-extraction technique for unknown-order groups enabling NIZK range proofs over Paillier under subverted, maliciously generated setup.
- **13940130.pdf** — Private Polynomial Commitments and Applications to MPC (Bhadauria, Hazay, Venkitasubramaniam, Wu, Zhang). Introduces polynomial commitments hiding both polynomial and evaluation points, with batched verification and application to efficient private set intersection.
- **13940133.pdf** — QCCA-Secure Generic Transformations in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Shan, Ge, Xue). Formalizes oracle-masked PKE schemes and gives tighter IND-qCCA proofs for Fujisaki-Okamoto and first for REACT in the QROM.
- **13940135.pdf** — Threshold Private Set Intersection with Better Communication Complexity (Ghosh, Simkin). Threshold PSI protocols with quasilinear dependency on threshold t from additively homomorphic encryption and OT, avoiding FHE.
- **13940136.pdf** — The Hidden Number Problem with Small Unknown Multipliers: Cryptanalyzing MEGA in Six Queries (Heninger, Ryan). Solves a new hidden-number-problem variant to recover MEGA client RSA keys in six logins, improving the prior 512-login attack.
- **13940140.pdf** — Multi-Client Inner Product Encryption: Function-Hiding Instantiations Without Random Oracles (Shi, Vanjani). First function-hiding multi-client inner-product FE with adaptive security from bilinear groups, no random oracles.
- **13940145.pdf** — Decentralized Multi-Authority Attribute-Based Inner-Product FE: Large Universe and Unbounded (Datta, Pal). First decentralized MA-ABIPFE schemes supporting unbounded-length vectors under LSSS access policies.
- **13940150.pdf** — Hardening Signature Schemes via Derive-then-Derandomize: Stronger Security Proofs for EdDSA (Bellare, Davis, Di). Proves a derandomization transform against randomness failure and gives tighter, MD-hash-based security proofs for EdDSA.
- **13940161.pdf** — Non-Interactive Publicly-Verifiable Delegation of Committed Programs (Ghosal, Sahai, Waters). First fully non-interactive publicly verifiable delegation of committed programs from LWE, bypassing the apparent need for SNARGs for NP.
- **13940168.pdf** — EKE Meets Tight Security in the Universally Composable Framework (Liu, Liu, Han, Gu). Proposes 2DH-EKE, a tightly UC-secure PAKE variant from CDH, and an asymmetric version with provably optimal N-factor loss.
- **13940170.pdf** — A Universally Composable PAKE with Zero Communication Cost (And Why It Shouldn't Be Considered UC-Secure) (Roy, Xu). Shows UC PAKE does not imply correctness — a no-message "TrivialPAKE" qualifies — and studies fixes via a three-party modeling.
- **13940172.pdf** — Laconic Function Evaluation for Turing Machines (Döttling, Gajland, Malavolta). First LFE for Turing machines with asymptotically optimal parameters from iO and SSB hashing, yielding optimal-prover NIZKs and TM witness encryption.
- **13940177.pdf** — On Homomorphic Secret Sharing from Polynomial-Modulus LWE (Attema, Capitão, Kohl). New local share conversion yields the first HSS for branching programs from polynomial-modulus (Ring-)LWE with no correctness error.
- **13940179.pdf** — Hull Attacks on the Lattice Isomorphism Problem (Ducas, Gibbons). Studies the s-hull analogue of code hulls for LIP: useless for arithmetic distinguishing but halves the exponent in geometric attacks, countering a hardness conjecture.
- **13940182.pdf** — SCALLOP: Scaling the CSI-FiSh (De Feo, Fouotsa, Kutas, Leroux, Merz, Panny, Wesolowski). A supersingular isogeny group action over prime-conductor oriented curves whose class group is easy to compute, enabling scalable CSI-FiSh-like signatures.
- **13940187.pdf** — Multi-Instance Secure Public-Key Encryption (Brunetta, Heum, Stam). Defines and compares multi-instance security notions for PKE and shows when hybrid KEM/DEM encryption inherits multi-instance security.
- **13940192.pdf** — A Key-Recovery Attack against Mitaka in the t-Probing Model (Prest). Finds a flaw in masked Mitaka's t-probing proof and shows key recovery with as few as 3 probes given ~2^21 executions.
- **13940196.pdf** — Fine-grained Verifier NIZK and Its Applications (Liu, Liu, Han, Gu). Introduces FV-NIZK with master and derived-key verification, unbounded simulation soundness, and MDDH-based instantiations for linear subspaces.
- **14.pdf** — A Proposition for Correlation Power Analysis Enhancement (Le, Clédière, Canovas, Robisson, Servière, Lacoume). Older CEA-LETI side-channel paper unifying multi-bit DPA into Partitioning Power Analysis and improving CPA via restricted normalization. Anomalous bare-numeric filename.
- **14004015.pdf** — Deniable Authentication when Signing Keys Leak (Chakraborty, Hofheinz, Maurer, Rito). Studies deniable authentication where even the sender's leaked secret key must not convince third parties, via a scalable MDRS-PKE scheme.
- **14004016.pdf** — Privately Puncturing PRFs from Lattices: Adaptive Security and Collusion Resistant Pseudorandomness (Yang). First adaptively secure private puncturable PRF from standard lattices via a new "explainable hash" primitive.
- **14004017.pdf** — Almost Tight Multi-User Security under Adaptive Corruptions & Leakages in the Standard Model (Han, Liu, Gu). Generic MDDH-based constructions giving first almost-tight multi-user signatures, PKE, and signcryption under adaptive corruptions.
- **14004018.pdf** — One-Hot Conversion: Towards Faster Table-based A2B Conversion (D'Anvers). Speeds higher-order arithmetic-to-Boolean masking conversion ~15x on Cortex M4 via one-hot table techniques for lattice crypto.
- **14004021.pdf** — Non-uniformity and Quantum Advice in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Liu). Extends the multi-instance framework to quantum advice, matching classical-advice bounds for many QROM security games.
- **14004022.pdf** — From the Hardness of Detecting Superpositions to Cryptography: Quantum Public Key Encryption and Commitments (Hhan, Morimae, Yamakawa). Builds PKE from non-abelian group actions with quantum ciphertexts and a one-call flavor-conversion compiler for quantum bit commitments.
- **14004026.pdf** — Meet-in-the-Middle Preimage Attacks on Sponge-based Hashing (Qin, Hua, Dong, Yan, Wang). Generic MitM preimage framework for sponge hashes with MILP-automated search, applied to Keccak, Ascon, and Xoodyak.
- **14004027.pdf** — Asymmetric Group Message Franking: Definitions & Constructions (Lai, Zeng, Huang, Yiu, Mu, Weng). Formalizes message franking for group communication (AGMF) and builds it from a new HPS-KEMΣ primitive under DDH.
- **14004028.pdf** — On Polynomial Functions Modulo p^e and Faster Bootstrapping for Homomorphic Encryption (Geelen, Iliashenko, Kang, Vercauteren). Characterizes integer-polynomial-representable functions mod p^e and exploits null polynomials to speed up BGV/BFV bootstrapping.
- **14004030.pdf** — Non-Adaptive Universal One-Way Hash Functions from Arbitrary One-Way Functions (Mao, Mazor, Zhang). First non-adaptive UOWHF construction from arbitrary OWFs, using O(n^9) calls, plus a relaxed-UOWHF analysis of the HRV PRG.
- **14004039.pdf** — Coefficient Grouping: Breaking Chaghri and More (Liu, Anand, Wang, Meier, Isobe). Shows the FHE-friendly cipher Chaghri's algebraic degree grows linearly, yielding a 13-round distinguisher and key-recovery attacks on full 8 rounds.
- **14004040.pdf** — Analysis of RIPEMD-160: New Collision Attacks and Finding Characteristics with MILP (Liu, Wang, Sarkar, Anand, Meier, Li, Isobe). Extends RIPEMD-160 collision attacks to 36 of 80 rounds using new message-difference strategies and MILP-based characteristic search.
- **14004043.pdf** — From Farfalle to Megafono via Ciminion: The PRF Hydra for MPC Applications (Grassi, Øygarden, Schofnegger, Walch). Introduces Megafono, a keyed extendable PRF that removes Ciminion's expensive key schedule for MPC-friendly use.
- **14004044.pdf** — Caveat Implementor! Key Recovery Attacks on MEGA (Albrecht, Haller, Mareková, Paterson). Shows MEGA's post-attack RSA key sanity checks are themselves exploitable via an ECB oracle, enabling new key-recovery attacks.
- **14004048.pdf** — Just how hard are rotations of Z^n? Algorithms and cryptography with the simplest lattice (Bennett, Ganju, Peetathawatchai, Stephens-Davidowitz). Studies ZSVP (SVP on rotations of Z^n), reducing it to approximate uSVP and exploring consequences of its hardness.
- **14004050.pdf** — Public Key Encryption with Secure Key Leasing (Agrawal, Kitagawa, Nishimaki, Yamada, Yamakawa). Defines PKE with secure key leasing (quantum keys verifiably revocable) and constructs IND-KLA-secure schemes via quantum Goldreich-Levin.
- **14004053.pdf** — On the Hardness of the Finite Field Isomorphism Problem (Das, Joux). Gives a polynomial-time distinguisher for decisional FFI in large characteristic and breaks semantic security of the FFI-based FHE scheme.
