# Batch 087 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by circa-2010 top-tier conference papers (Crypto/Eurocrypt/PKC/TCC/FSE era) spanning both symmetric and public-key cryptography.
- A large cluster of SHA-3 competition cryptanalysis: JH, BLAKE, Grøstl, ECHO, BMW, Luffa, ESSENCE, Lesamnta, Tiger, plus AES implementation records.
- Strong leakage-resilience / side-channel group: Dodis–Pietrzak, Goldwasser–Rothblum, Juma–Vahlis, Brakerski–Goldwasser.
- Lattice/FHE cluster: Peikert's Gaussian sampler, Gentry's worst-case FHE basis, Boyen's lattice signatures, Agrawal–Boneh–Boyen HIBE.
- Notable record-setting cryptanalytic results: RSA-768 factorization (Kleinjung et al.) and 676-bit DLP in GF(3^6n) (Hayashi et al.), plus the DECT cipher break.
- ECDLP-relevant: Galbraith–Ruprai kangaroo acceleration in short intervals; hashing into ordinary elliptic curves (Brier et al.). No anomalies; all 50 are genuine research papers.

## Papers
- **60560282.pdf** — More Constructions of Lossy and Correlation-Secure Trapdoor Functions (Freeman, Goldreich, Kiltz, Rosen, Segev). New lossy/correlation-secure TDFs from quadratic residuosity, composite residuosity, and d-Linear assumptions, widening assumption bases and improving efficiency.
- **60560355.pdf** — Solving a 676-bit Discrete Logarithm Problem in GF(3^6n) (Hayashi, Shinohara, Wang, Matsuo, Shirase, Takagi). First practical JL06-FFS implementation, setting a new record for the DLP in GF(3^6·71) of 676 bits, relevant to ηT pairing security.
- **60560372.pdf** — Using Equivalence Classes to Accelerate Solving the DLP in a Short Interval (Galbraith, Ruprai). Kangaroo-type algorithm solving interval DLP in ~1.36√N group operations using equivalence classes (negation map), with experiments.
- **60560388.pdf** — Functional Encryption for Inner Product (Attrapadung, Libert). Constant-size-ciphertext inner-product encryption with adaptive security or negation support, implying broadcast encryption and revocation schemes in prime-order groups.
- **60560406.pdf** — Security of Encryption Schemes in Weakened Random Oracle Models (Kawachi, Numayama, Tanaka, Xagawa). Analyzes OAEP and Fujisaki-Okamoto schemes under weakened random oracle models (collision/SPT/FPT tractable), finding OAEP secure in all four.
- **60560424.pdf** — Fully Homomorphic Encryption with Relatively Small Key and Ciphertext Sizes (Smart, Vercauteren). Gentry-style FHE over characteristic-two fields with keys of two large integers and single-integer ciphertexts, reducing expansion versus Gentry's scheme.
- **60560448.pdf** — Unlinkability of Sanitizable Signatures (Brzuska, Fischlin, Lehmann, Schröder). Introduces unlinkability as a sixth security property for sanitizable signatures, shows it implies privacy, and gives a group-signature-based scheme meeting all six.
- **60560466.pdf** — Confidential Signatures and Deterministic Signcryption (Dent, Fischlin, Manulis, Stam, Schröder). Formalizes message-hiding confidentiality for signatures, relates it to deterministic signcryption, and gives constructions in ROM and standard model.
- **60560484.pdf** — Identity-Based Aggregate and Multi-Signature Schemes based on RSA (Bagherzandi, Jarecki). Two-round RSA-based identity-based multi/aggregate signatures via straight-line simulatable, aggregatable zero-knowledge proofs of knowledge.
- **60560503.pdf** — Lattice Mixing and Vanishing Trapdoors (Boyen). Framework for adaptively secure primitives from lattices using mixture lattices with simulation trapdoors vanishing on a secret subset; yields compact fully secure signatures and IBE.
- **61470001.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the DECT Standard Cipher (Nohl, Tews, Weinmann). Reverse-engineers the proprietary DECT stream cipher and gives a practical key-recovery attack from 2^15 keystreams within hours on a PC.
- **61470020.pdf** — Improving the Generalized Feistel (Suzaki, Minematsu). Replaces Type-II GFS cyclic shift with better permutations (de Bruijn graph based) to improve diffusion and reduce rounds needed for security.
- **61470041.pdf** — Nonlinear Equivalence of Stream Ciphers (Rønjom, Cid). Defines nonlinear equivalence classes of LFSR-based filter generators and shows algebraic immunity/nonlinearity are not class invariants, complicating security assessment.
- **61470056.pdf** — Lightweight Privacy Preserving Authentication for RFID Using a Stream Cipher (Billet, Etrog, Gilbert). RFID authentication with forward privacy and DoS resistance built solely from a stream cipher, with standard-model security proofs.
- **61470076.pdf** — Fast Software AES Encryption (Osvik, Bos, Stefan, Canright). New AES-128 speed records on AVR, ARM, Cell, and GPU platforms, plus the first GPU AES decryption implementation.
- **61470096.pdf** — Attacking the Knudsen-Preneel Compression Functions (Özen, Shrimpton, Stam). Query-optimal non-adaptive preimage attacks on code-based wide-pipe compression functions, falsifying the conjectured security bound.
- **61470118.pdf** — Finding Preimages of Tiger Up to 23 Steps (Wang, Sasaki). Meet-in-the-middle pseudo-preimage attack on 23-step Tiger compression (2^181), converted to a 2^187.5 preimage attack on the hash.
- **61470137.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of ESSENCE (Naya-Plasencia et al.). Collision attacks on full ESSENCE-256/-512 at 2^67.4 and 2^134.7 using advanced differential search, plus HMAC forgeries.
- **61470157.pdf** — Domain Extension for Enhanced Target Collision-Resistant Hash Functions (Mironov). Constructs eTCR domain extension with sublinear key expansion via a new composition theorem; shows eTCR exists iff one-way functions do.
- **61470173.pdf** — Security Analysis of the Mode of JH Hash Function (Bhattacharyya, Mandal, Nandi). Proves JH's chopMD mode indifferentiable from a random oracle with concrete bounds, showing its padding rule is essential.
- **61470197.pdf** — Enhanced Security Notions for Dedicated-Key Hash Functions (Reyhanitabar, Susilo, Mu). Defines enhanced variants of the seven Rogaway-Shrimpton hash security notions and maps their relationships.
- **61470217.pdf** — A Unified Method for Improving PRF Bounds for a Class of Blockcipher based MACs (Nandi). Framework of (secure) affine domain extensions giving improved O(tq/2^n) PRF bounds for CBC-MAC, GCBC*, OMAC, PMAC.
- **61470235.pdf** — How to Thwart Birthday Attacks against MACs via Small Randomness (Minematsu). Randomized MAC with n-bit IVs achieving beyond-birthday O(q³/2^2n) security, with blockcipher instantiations as efficient as CBC-MAC.
- **61470255.pdf** — Constructing Rate-1 MACs from Related-Key Unpredictable Block Ciphers (Zhang et al.). Systematic security analysis of 64 keyed-PGV rate-1 MACs under related-key unpredictability; 24 provably secure, others attacked or meaningless.
- **61470275.pdf** — Higher Order Differential Attack on Step-Reduced Variants of Luffa v1 (Watanabe, Hatano, Yamada, Kaneko). Shows slow algebraic degree growth of Luffa's permutation, yielding a 7-of-8-step distinguisher; no threat to full Luffa.
- **61470292.pdf** — Rebound Attack on Reduced-Round Versions of JH (Rijmen, Toz, Varıcı). First rebound analysis of JH: semi-free-start collisions for 16 of 35.5 rounds and near-collisions up to 22 rounds.
- **61470310.pdf** — Pseudo-cryptanalysis of the Original Blue Midnight Wish (Thomsen). Near-collision, pseudo-collision, and pseudo-preimage attacks on the original (pre-tweak) SHA-3 BMW compression function.
- **61470324.pdf** — Differential and invertibility properties of BLAKE (Aumasson, Guo, Knellwolf, Matusiewicz, Meier). Bottom-up analysis of BLAKE: round inversion, faster 1.5-round preimages, impossible differentials, and 4-round near-collisions.
- **61470339.pdf** — Rotational Cryptanalysis of ARX (Khovratovich, Nikolić). Introduces rotational cryptanalysis for addition-rotation-XOR primitives, giving the best known attack on reduced Threefish (Skein's core); proves ARX functional completeness.
- **61470353.pdf** — Another Look at Complementation Properties (Bouillaguet, Dunkelman, Leurent, Fouque). Generalizes DES complementation into symmetry-based distinguishers; gives a one-query distinguisher and fast collisions on full Lesamnta compression.
- **61470371.pdf** — Super-Sbox Cryptanalysis: Improved Attacks for AES-like Permutations (Gilbert, Peyrin). Super-Sbox technique improving rebound attacks on Grøstl and ECHO and the best 8-round known-key AES distinguisher.
- **621.pdf** — Errors in Computational Complexity Proofs for Protocols (Choo, Boyd, Hitchcock). Case studies exposing previously unpublished flaws in published security proofs of several protocols and a Bellare-Canetti-Krawczyk authenticator.
- **62230001.pdf** — Circular and Leakage Resilient Public-Key Encryption Under Subgroup Indistinguishability (Brakerski, Goldwasser). PKE from QR/DCR achieving key-dependent-message security and high leakage/auxiliary-input resilience.
- **62230021.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Pseudorandom Functions and Side-Channel Attacks on Feistel Networks (Dodis, Pietrzak). Positive and negative results: leakage-resilient PRFs/PRPs via Feistel with leakage-resilient round functions, plus generic side-channel attacks on Feistel.
- **62230041.pdf** — Protecting Cryptographic Keys Against Continual Leakage (Juma, Vahlis). Compiler using re-randomizable FHE plus one leak-free token to compute on encapsulated keys securely under continual leakage in the Micali-Reyzin model.
- **62230059.pdf** — Securing Computation Against Continuous Leakage (Goldwasser, Rothblum). General compiler making any algorithm resist unbounded continual "only computation leaks" attacks, instantiated under DDH with a simple key-independent hardware component.
- **62230080.pdf** — An Efficient and Parallel Gaussian Sampler for Lattices (Peikert). Efficient, parallelizable discrete Gaussian sampling algorithm for lattices with rigorous security, improving on the sequential GPV sampler.
- **62230098.pdf** — Lattice Basis Delegation in Fixed Dimension and Shorter-Ciphertext Hierarchical IBE (Agrawal, Boneh, Boyen). Fixed-dimension lattice basis delegation yielding HIBE schemes with shorter ciphertexts and keys from standard lattice assumptions.
- **62230116.pdf** — Toward Basing Fully Homomorphic Encryption on Worst-Case Hardness (Gentry). Key generation for ideal lattices with a worst-case/average-case connection basing Gentry's FHE in part on worst-case ideal-SIVP hardness.
- **62230138.pdf** — Additively Homomorphic Encryption with d-Operand Multiplications (Aguilar Melchor, Gaborit, Herranz). Defines chained encryption schemes evaluating degree-d polynomials on encrypted data, instantiated with uSVP worst-case security.
- **62230156.pdf** — i-Hop Homomorphic Encryption and Rerandomizable Yao Circuits (Gentry, Halevi, Vaikuntanathan). Defines and constructs i-hop/multi-hop HE where Eval can iterate on its own outputs, including an efficient DDH-based multi-hop scheme.
- **62230174.pdf** — Interactive Locking, Zero-Knowledge PCPs, and Unconditional Cryptography (Goyal, Ishai, Mahmoody, Sahai). Unconditional two-prover zero-knowledge proofs for NP in the interactive PCP model via a new interactive locking commitment primitive.
- **62230192.pdf** — Fully Secure Functional Encryption with General Relations from the Decisional Linear Assumption (Okamoto, Takashima). Fully secure FE for non-monotone access structures combined with inner-product relations under DLIN in the standard model.
- **62230210.pdf** — Structure-Preserving Signatures and Commitments to Group Elements (Abe, Fuchsbauer, Groth, Haralambiev, Ohkubo). Bilinear-group signatures/commitments whose verification is pairing-product equations, enabling modular protocol composition with NIZK proofs.
- **62230238.pdf** — Efficient Indifferentiable Hashing into Ordinary Elliptic Curves (Brier, Coron, Icart, Madore, Randriam, Tibouchi). First indifferentiable hash into ordinary elliptic curves based on Icart's encoding, plus a characteristic-3 deterministic encoding.
- **62230256.pdf** — Credential Authenticated Identification and Key Exchange (Camenisch, Casati, Gross, Shoup). UC-framework identification/key exchange using credential possession under arbitrary policies, including server-compromise-resilient PAKE without random oracles.
- **62230276.pdf** — Password-Authenticated Session-Key Generation on the Internet in the Plain Model (Goyal, Jain, Ostrovsky). First PAKE secure under concurrent self-composition in the plain model without trusted setup, using precise zero-knowledge ideas.
- **62230294.pdf** — Instantiability of RSA-OAEP under Chosen-Plaintext Attack (Kiltz, O'Neill, Smith). First positive standard-model IND-CPA result for RSA-OAEP, via padding-based encryption and lossiness of RSA under the Φ-hiding assumption.
- **62230313.pdf** — Efficient Chosen-Ciphertext Security via Extractable Hash Proofs (Wee). Introduces extractable hash proof systems yielding modular CCA-secure encryption from factoring and CDH, plus adaptive trapdoor relations.
- **62230332.pdf** — Factorization of a 768-bit RSA modulus (Kleinjung et al.). Reports the December 2009 NFS factorization of RSA-768, a new general-integer factoring record, and discusses implications for RSA key sizes.
