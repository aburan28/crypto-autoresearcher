# Batch 095 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by two 2012 IACR proceedings: file IDs 7194xxxx are TCC 2012 papers (foundations: zero knowledge, leakage resilience, PRFs, black-box separations); 7237xxxx are EUROCRYPT 2012 papers (symmetric cryptanalysis, pairings, block-cipher provable security).
- Strong leakage-resilience cluster: leakage-tolerant UC protocols, parallel repetition for leakage, leakage-resilient circuits, IBE with continual auxiliary leakage.
- Directly ECDLP-relevant: Joux–Vitse cover-and-decomposition index calculus over Fp6 (real 151-bit computation) and Faugère–Perret–Petit–Renault binary-field ECDLP index calculus via Gröbner bases.
- Notable symmetric-crypto results: biclique attack on full IDEA, Slidex attack/tight Even–Mansour bounds, key-alternating ciphers, LPMAC generic attacks.
- Anomalies: 72370136.pdf is a garbled/character-encoded extract (undecipherable); 72370009.pdf is a 1-page invited-talk abstract (Menezes, "Another Look at Provable Security"); 72370001.pdf is an invited-talk tutorial abstract (Joux).

## Papers
- **71940128.pdf** — Leakage-Tolerant Interactive Protocols (Bitansky, Canetti, Halevi). UC-style framework for security under arbitrary leakage, with leakage-tolerant constructions for message transmission, commitments, OT, and zero knowledge.
- **71940130.pdf** — From Non-Adaptive to Adaptive Pseudorandom Functions (Berman, Haitner). First direct construction of an adaptive PRF from a non-adaptive one, simply composing with a pairwise-independent hash, defying prior negative results.
- **71940133.pdf** — Functional Re-encryption and Collusion-Resistant Obfuscation (Chandran, Chase, Vaikuntanathan). Defines functional re-encryption and collusion-resistant obfuscation; gives constructions hiding both secret key and policy function.
- **71940138.pdf** — Lossy Functions Do Not Amplify Well (Pietrzak, Rosen, Segev). Shows black-box amplification of relative lossiness is limited to an additive O(log n)/n term, and proves this bound tight.
- **71940140.pdf** — On the Instantiability of Hash-and-Sign RSA Signatures (Dodis, Haitner, Tentes). Studies RSA-FDH uninstantiability when RSA is treated as generic-but-multiplicative, narrowing the gap with standard-model RSA analyses.
- **71940141.pdf** — A Parallel Repetition Theorem for Leakage Resilience (Brakerski, Kalai). Under a strengthened leakage-resilience definition, parallel repetition amplifies CPA leakage resilience, circumventing the Lewko–Waters counterexample.
- **71940142.pdf** — Identifying Cheaters Without an Honest Majority (Ishai, Ostrovsky, Seyalioglu). Unconditional identifiable secret sharing tolerating arbitrary corruptions, with applications to complete primitives for MPC without honest majority.
- **71940148.pdf** — Uniqueness is a Different Story (Fiore, Schröder). Black-box separation showing verifiable random functions cannot be built from adaptive trapdoor permutations.
- **71940156.pdf** — Round-Optimal Privacy-Preserving Protocols with Smooth Projective Hash Functions (Blazy, Pointcheval, Vergnaud). SPHF-based round-optimal protocols in the CRS model, yielding efficient oblivious signature-based envelope and blind signature schemes.
- **71940158.pdf** — Non-Interactive CCA-Secure Threshold Cryptosystems with Adaptive Security (Libert, Yung). General framework for adaptively secure, non-interactive, robust threshold CCA encryption without composite-order groups.
- **71940159.pdf** — Secure Two-Party Computation with Low Communication (Damgård, Faust, Hazay). Two-message UC-secure 2PC with poly-logarithmic communication via new extractable hash notions and succinct ZK arguments.
- **71940166.pdf** — Subspace LWE (Pietrzak). Introduces adaptive subspace LWE assumption and proves it nearly as hard as standard LWE, strengthening security of LPN/LWE-based schemes.
- **71940169.pdf** — Resettable Statistical Zero Knowledge (Garg, Ostrovsky, Visconti, Wadia). Shows rSZK = SZK under sub-exponential one-way functions, settling simultaneous resettability and statistical zero knowledge.
- **71940170.pdf** — Beyond the Limitation of Prime-Order Bilinear Groups, and Round Optimal Blind Signatures (Seo, Cheon). Proves security of a prime-order blind signature via a new "translating" property, invalidating a Meiklejohn–Shacham–Freeman impossibility claim.
- **71940175.pdf** — Collisions are not Incidental (Jetchev, Özen, Stam). Double-block-length compression function from two ideal-primitive calls with near-optimal collision resistance, proved via Szemerédi–Trotter over finite fields.
- **71940179.pdf** — The Knowledge Tightness of Parallel Zero-Knowledge (Chung, Pass, Tseng). Essentially tight bounds on black-box simulator queries for parallel ZK; new lower bounds for constant-round concurrent ZK.
- **71940181.pdf** — On Black-Box Reductions between Predicate Encryption Schemes (Goyal, Kumar, Lokam, Mahmoody). First black-box impossibility from IBE: no black-box construction of threshold predicate encryption from identity-based encryption.
- **71940184.pdf** — How to Delegate and Verify in Public (Parno, Raykova, Vaikuntanathan). Builds verifiable computation with public delegation and public verifiability from any attribute-based encryption scheme.
- **71940191.pdf** — A Dichotomy for Local Small-Bias Generators (Applebaum, Bogdanov, Rosen). Every local predicate either yields a small-bias generator on almost all graphs or fails on almost all — a sharp dichotomy.
- **71940195.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient Circuits without Computational Assumptions (Dziembowski, Faust). Information-theoretic compiler making any circuit resilient to continual bounded, independent leakage without public-key assumptions.
- **71940198.pdf** — Progression-Free Sets and Sublinear Pairing-Based NIZK Arguments (Lipmaa). Uses progression-free sets to cut Groth's NIZK CRS length and prover cost from quadratic to quasilinear in circuit size.
- **71940201.pdf** — Bounded-Collusion IBE from Key Homomorphism (Goldwasser, Lewko, Wilson). Transforms key-homomorphic PKE into bounded-collusion IBE; instantiations from quadratic residuosity and DDH without random oracles.
- **71940204.pdf** — Hardness Preserving Constructions of Pseudorandom Functions (Jain, Pietrzak, Tentes). Hardness-preserving PRF from any length-doubling PRG needing only O(log q) PRG calls per query, beating GGM-with-Levin.
- **71940208.pdf** — Iterative Constructions and Private Data Release (Gupta, Roth, Ullman). Unifying "iterative database construction" framework for differentially private query release, with improved bounds via low-rank decomposition.
- **71940209.pdf** — Point Obfuscation and 3-round Zero-Knowledge (Bitansky, Paneth). 3-round weak-ZK and witness-hiding protocols from auxiliary-input point obfuscation used non-black-box in reductions.
- **71940214.pdf** — A Unified Approach to Deterministic Encryption (Fuller, O'Neill, Reyzin). General deterministic-encryption construction from hardcore-bit trapdoor functions and lossy TDFs, with new computational-entropy tools.
- **71940221.pdf** — Counterexamples to Hardness Amplification Beyond Negligible (Dodis, Jain, Moran, Wichs). Shows direct-product hardness amplification for one-way functions, puzzles, and signatures stalls below exponentially-small levels.
- **71940223.pdf** — Simultaneously Resettable Arguments of Knowledge (Cho, Ostrovsky, Scafuro, Visconti). Constant-round simultaneously resettable WI argument of knowledge for NP; applications to BPK-model ZK and identification.
- **72370001.pdf** — A Tutorial on High Performance Computing applied to Cryptanalysis (Joux). Invited-talk abstract on using large-scale HPC for cryptanalytic record computations; not a research paper.
- **72370009.pdf** — Another Look at Provable Security (Menezes). One-page invited-talk abstract arguing provable-security proofs need cryptanalysis and engineering judgment; joint work with Koblitz et al.
- **72370010.pdf** — Cover and Decomposition Index Calculus on Elliptic Curves made practical (Joux, Vitse). Combines Weil descent with decomposition index calculus; computes a 151-bit ECDLP over Fp6 previously out of reach.
- **72370028.pdf** — Improving the Complexity of Index Calculus Algorithms in Elliptic Curves over Binary Fields (Faugère, Perret, Petit, Renault). Exploits multihomogeneous structure of Semaev-type systems via Gröbner bases to speed binary-field ECDLP index calculus.
- **72370046.pdf** — Key-Alternating Ciphers in a Provable Setting (Bogdanov, Knudsen, Leander, Standaert, Steinberger, Tischhauser). Proves generalized Even–Mansour key-alternating ciphers secure up to ~2^(2n/3) queries; tight for t=2.
- **72370064.pdf** — Efficient and Optimally Secure Key-Length Extension for Block Ciphers via Randomized Cascading (Gaži, Tessaro). Two-call randomized cascade gives κ+n/2-bit security, beating triple encryption, with matching generic attacks.
- **72370100.pdf** — Concurrently Secure Computation in Constant Rounds (Garg, Goyal, Jain, Sahai). Constant-round concurrently secure computation in the plain model under super-polynomial simulation, via new rewinding extraction.
- **72370118.pdf** — Identity-Based Encryption Resilient to Continual Auxiliary Leakage (Yuen, Chow, Zhang, Yiu). First IBE secure against continual auxiliary-input leakage, fully secure in the standard model; extends to HIBE.
- **72370136.pdf** — Title undecipherable (?). Extract is character-encoded garbage (slash-separated glyph codes); content cannot be determined from the text.
- **72370154.pdf** — On Round-Optimal Zero Knowledge in the Bare Public-Key Model (Scafuro, Visconti). Points out flaws in prior BPK-model cZK/rZK simulations and gives round-optimal concurrently sound cZK and rZK arguments.
- **72370172.pdf** — Robust Coin Flipping (Kopp, Wiltshire-Gordon). Characterizes achievable coin biases from remote random sources: rational with dishonest majority, algebraic otherwise; algebraic-geometry proof.
- **72370190.pdf** — Unconditionally-Secure Robust Secret Sharing with Compact Shares (Cevallos, Fehr, Ostrovsky, Rabani). Polynomial-time robust secret sharing for n/3≤t<n/2 with near-optimal Õ(κ+n) share-size overhead.
- **72370204.pdf** — All-But-Many Lossy Trapdoor Functions (Hofheinz). Tag-based LTFs with unbounded lossy tags generatable only via trapdoor; yields selective-opening-secure encryption.
- **72370240.pdf** — Dual Projective Hashing and its Applications (Wee). Invertibility-based analogue of Cramer–Shoup projective hashing; unifies known lossy TDF constructions and gives deterministic encryption incl. from LWE.
- **72370275.pdf** — Malleable Proof Systems and Applications (Chase, Kohlweiss, Lysyanskaya, Meiklejohn). Defines controlled-malleable NIZKs; compact proofs for multi-step verifiable shuffles and strong encryption notions.
- **72370295.pdf** — Group to Group Commitments Do Not Shrink (Abe, Haralambiev, Ohkubo). Lower bounds for structure-preserving commitments in bilinear groups — no shrinking, ≥2 pairing equations — plus optimal constructions.
- **72370312.pdf** — Tools for Simulating Features of Composite Order Bilinear Groups in the Prime Order Setting (Lewko). Dual pairing vector space tools converting composite-order pairing schemes (e.g., Lewko–Waters IBE/HIBE) to prime order under DLIN.
- **72370330.pdf** — Minimalism in Cryptography: The Even-Mansour Scheme Revisited (Dunkelman, Keller, Shamir). New Slidex attack matches the known-plaintext lower bound; single-key Even–Mansour is equally secure.
- **72370349.pdf** — Message Authentication, Revisited (Dodis, Kiltz, Pietrzak, Wichs). Probabilistic MACs from CCA encryption, hash proof systems, and weak PRFs; generic uf-cma to uf-cmva transformation.
- **72370369.pdf** — Property Preserving Symmetric Encryption (Pandey, Rouselakis). Defines symmetric encryption allowing public testing of a fixed-arity property over independently generated ciphertexts, for encrypted-data processing.
- **72370386.pdf** — Narrow-Bicliques: Cryptanalysis of Full IDEA (Khovratovich, Leurent, Rechberger). First key-recovery speed-up on full 8.5-round IDEA via narrow bicliques; impractical but technique verified on 7.5 rounds.
- **72370405.pdf** — Cryptanalyses on a Merkle-Damgård Based MAC (Sasaki). Generic O(2^(n/2)) distinguishing-H and almost-universal-forgery attacks on LPMAC over any narrow-pipe Merkle–Damgård hash.
