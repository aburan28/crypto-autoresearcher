# Batch 085 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by ~2009 conference proceedings: CHES 2009 (57470xxx: hardware security, PUFs, Trojans, side-channel/fault attacks), Asiacrypt 2009 (5912xxx: hash cryptanalysis, MPC, lattices), TCC 2010 (5978xxx: parallel repetition).
- Heavy SHA-3 competition cryptanalysis cluster: rebound attacks on Lane and Whirlpool, plus Skein, CubeHash, MD6, and MD5 combiners.
- Only two elliptic-curve-relevant items: double-base scalar multiplication (57470302) and cache-timing attacks on OpenSSL ECC (59120664); the batch skews to symmetric and protocol crypto.
- Notable theory results: hedged PKE against bad randomness (Bellare et al.), practical garbled-circuit 2PC (Pinkas et al.), lattice Fiat-Shamir signatures (Lyubashevsky).
- Anomalies: 59120206.pdf is font-encoded garbage (unreadable); 59120614.pdf is hex-escaped text but decodable (Stehlé et al., ideal-lattice PKE).

## Papers
- **57470302.pdf** — Elliptic Curve Scalar Multiplication Combining Yao's Algorithm and Double Bases (Nicolas Méloni, M. Anwar Hasan). Generalizes double-base chains via modified Yao's algorithm, adds binary/Zeckendorf representation, and compares scalar multiplication efficiency across curve shapes.
- **57470316.pdf** — The Frequency Injection Attack on Ring-Oscillator-Based True Random Number Generators (A. Theodore Markettos, Simon W. Moore). Injects frequencies into power supply to lock TRNG oscillators, collapsing entropy; demonstrated against an EMV payment card.
- **57470331.pdf** — Low-Overhead Implementation of a Soft Decision Helper Data Algorithm for SRAM PUFs (Roel Maes, Pim Tuyls, Ingrid Verbauwhede). First soft-decision helper data algorithm for SRAM PUFs, cutting FPGA resource use ~45% and required PUF size up to 58%.
- **57470346.pdf** — CDs Have Fingerprints Too (Ghaith Hammouri, Aykutlu Dana, Berk Sunar). Extracts unique fingerprints from identical CDs via manufacturing variability in land/pit lengths using a standard drive's photodetector, deriving 128-bit keys with fuzzy extractors.
- **57470361.pdf** — The State-of-the-Art in IC Reverse Engineering (Randy Torrance, Dick James). Industry survey of chip reverse-engineering techniques: teardowns, circuit extraction, process analysis, and challenges from embedded hardware encryption.
- **57470383.pdf** — Trojan Side-Channels: Lightweight Hardware Trojans through Side-Channel Engineering (Lang Lin, Markus Kasper, Tim Güneysu, Christof Paar, Wayne Burleson). Proposes foundry-inserted Trojans that leak secrets via engineered below-noise power side-channels, demonstrated on Xilinx FPGAs.
- **57470397.pdf** — MERO: A Statistical Approach for Hardware Trojan Detection (Rajat Subhra Chakraborty et al.). Test-pattern generation based on multiple excitation of rare logic nodes, achieving Trojan detection coverage with ~85% fewer test vectors.
- **57470412.pdf** — On Tamper-Resistance from a Theoretical Viewpoint: The Power of Seals (Paulo Mateus, Serge Vaudenay). Models tamper-proof hardware as "trusted agents," showing they break deniability, anonymity, and vote privacy in several protocol classes.
- **57470430.pdf** — Mutual Information Analysis: How, When and Why? (Nicolas Veyrat-Charvillon, François-Xavier Standaert). Systematizes MIA side-channel distinguishers, identifying when they beat correlation attacks and their limits for comparing leaking devices.
- **57470445.pdf** — Fault Attacks on RSA Signatures with Partially Unknown Messages (Jean-Sébastien Coron, Antoine Joux, Ilya Kizhvatov, David Naccache, Pascal Paillier). Extends RSA-CRT fault attacks to randomized/partially unknown message paddings using Coppersmith's multivariate small-roots algorithm.
- **57470460.pdf** — Differential Fault Analysis on DES Middle Rounds (Matthieu Rivain). Shows DFA faults injected in DES rounds 9–12 still break the cipher, determining how many rounds duplication countermeasures must protect.
- **585.pdf** — Examining Indistinguishability-Based Proof Models for Key Establishment Protocols (Kim-Kwang Raymond Choo, Colin Boyd, Yvonne Hitchcock). Compares Bellare–Rogaway, BPR2000, and Canetti–Krawczyk models with implication/non-implication proofs and a flaw in the BPR Corrupt-query formulation.
- **59120001.pdf** — Related-key Cryptanalysis of the Full AES-192 and AES-256 (Alex Biryukov, Dmitry Khovratovich). First related-key boomerang attacks on full AES-192 and full AES-256 (2^99.5 complexity for all keys).
- **59120019.pdf** — The Key-Dependent Attack on Block Ciphers (Xiaorui Sun, Xuejia Lai). Formalizes key-dependent-distribution attacks with hypothesis testing, yielding best-known attacks on 5.5- and 6-round IDEA.
- **59120035.pdf** — Cascade Encryption Revisited (Peter Gaži, Ueli Maurer). New indistinguishability lemma for random systems showing longer cascades improve security beyond triple encryption when key space is smaller than message space (e.g., DES).
- **59120050.pdf** — Quantum-Secure Coin-Flipping and Applications (Ivan Damgård, Carolin Lunemann). Proves classical coin-flipping secure against quantum adversaries via Watrous' quantum rewinding, with applications to quantum zero-knowledge.
- **59120082.pdf** — Security Bounds for the Design of Code-based Cryptosystems (Matthieu Finiasz, Nicolas Sendrier). Gives lower bounds on work factors of idealized ISD and generalized-birthday decoding attacks to guide durable parameter selection for code-based schemes.
- **59120100.pdf** — Rebound Attack on the Full Lane Compression Function (Krystian Matusiewicz, María Naya-Plasencia, Ivica Nikolić, Yu Sasaki, Martin Schläffer). Builds semi-free-start collisions for full Lane-256 and Lane-512 SHA-3 candidate compression functions using new rebound techniques.
- **59120118.pdf** — Rebound Distinguishers: Results on the Full Whirlpool Compression Function (Mario Lamberger, Florian Mendel, Christian Rechberger, Vincent Rijmen, Martin Schläffer). First distinguishing attack on the full 10-round Whirlpool compression function via extended inbound rebound phases.
- **59120136.pdf** — MD5 is Weaker than Weak: Attacks on Concatenated Combiners (Florian Mendel, Christian Rechberger, Martin Schläffer). First collision attack on hash combiners below the smaller function's birthday bound, answering Joux's 2004 question; targets MD5 combiners.
- **59120171.pdf** — Group Encryption: Non-Interactive Realization in the Standard Model (Julien Cathalo, Benoît Libert, Moti Yung). First fully non-interactive group encryption scheme in the standard model, using a new minimal-interaction certification building block.
- **59120189.pdf** — On Black-Box Constructions of Predicate Encryption from Trapdoor Permutations (Jonathan Katz, Arkady Yerukhimovich). Combinatorial impossibility results for black-box predicate encryption from TDPs, plus positive constructions from CPA-secure encryption under identified conditions.
- **59120206.pdf** — Title unreadable (?): extract is font-encoded slash-hex garbage with no recoverable title, authors, or abstract. Not summarizable from the provided text.
- **59120224.pdf** — Hedged Public-Key Encryption: How to Protect Against Bad Randomness (Mihir Bellare et al.). PKE schemes that retain a weaker IND-CDA security when randomness is poor, with RO-based and LTDF-based constructions.
- **59120242.pdf** — Secure Two-Party Computation is Practical (Benny Pinkas, Thomas Schneider, Nigel P. Smart, Stephen C. Williams). Optimized garbled-circuit two-party computation implementation with empirical evaluation, including an AES circuit benchmark.
- **59120260.pdf** — Secure Multi-party Computation Minimizing Online Rounds (Seung Geol Choi, Ariel Elbaz, Tal Malkin, Moti Yung). Defines the MP-CED model and gives round-efficient UC-secure MPC protocols with input-independent preprocessing.
- **59120278.pdf** — Improved Non-Committing Encryption with Applications to Adaptively Secure Protocols (Seung Geol Choi, Dana Dachman-Soled, Tal Malkin, Hoeteck Wee). Two-round non-committing encryption from new trapdoor-simulatable cryptosystems, first such schemes from factoring and lattice assumptions.
- **59120296.pdf** — Non-Malleable Statistically Hiding Commitment from Any One-Way Function (Zongyang Zhang, Zhenfu Cao, Ning Ding, Rong Ma). Black-box construction of non-malleable statistically hiding commitments from one-way functions using DDN message scheduling.
- **59120314.pdf** — Proofs of Storage from Homomorphic Identification Protocols (Giuseppe Ateniese, Seny Kamara, Jonathan Katz). Framework building homomorphic linear authenticators from ID protocols, yielding publicly verifiable, unbounded-use proofs of storage including a factoring-based one.
- **59120329.pdf** — Simple Adaptive Oblivious Transfer Without Random Oracle (Kaoru Kurosawa, Ryo Nojima). First pairing-free, fully simulatable adaptive OT under plain DDH in the standard model.
- **59120342.pdf** — Improved Generic Algorithms for 3-Collisions (Antoine Joux, Stefan Lucks). Memory-efficient parallelizable multicollision algorithms: 3-collisions in time N^(2/3) with N^(1/3) memory, generalized to r-collisions.
- **59120396.pdf** — On the Analysis of Cryptographic Assumptions in the Generic Ring Model (Tibor Jager, Jörg Schwenk). Shows Jacobi symbol computation is generically equivalent to factoring, proving generic-ring-model evidence of hardness is unreliable.
- **59120414.pdf** — Zero Knowledge in the Random Oracle Model, Revisited (Hoeteck Wee). Presents a ZK hierarchy in the ROM, separations tied to oracle programmability, composition failures and fixes, and round-optimal proofs of knowledge.
- **59120432.pdf** — A Framework for Universally Composable Non-Committing Blind Signatures (Masayuki Abe, Miyako Ohkubo). Shows UC non-committing blind signatures unrealizable in the plain model, characterizes them in the CRS model, and gives a generic Fischlin-based construction.
- **59120450.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of the Square Cryptosystems (Olivier Billet, Gilles Macario-Rat). Total break of the square-vinegar signature and square encryption multivariate schemes at ~2^35 operations, verified in Magma.
- **59120468.pdf** — Factoring pq² with Quadratic Forms: Nice Cryptanalyses (Guilhem Castagnos, Antoine Joux, Fabien Laguillaumie, Phong Q. Nguyen). Quadratic-form factoring algorithm giving polynomial-time key recovery against both imaginary and real NICE cryptosystems.
- **59120486.pdf** — Attacking Power Generators Using Unravelled Linearization (Mathias Herrmann, Alexander May). Lattice-based "unravelled linearization" recovers iterated power-generator sequences when ~ (1−1/e)·log N bits per iteration leak; bounds safe BBS/RSA-generator output sizes.
- **59120504.pdf** — Security Notions and Generic Constructions for Client Puzzles (L. Chen, P. Morrissey, N.P. Smart, B. Warinschi). Formal security model for DoS-defense client puzzles, an attack on a prior scheme, and a generic provably secure construction.
- **59120522.pdf** — Foundations of Non-Malleable Hash and One-Way Functions (Alexandra Boldyreva, David Cash, Marc Fischlin, Bogdan Warinschi). Initiates formal study of non-malleable hash/one-way functions with definitions, constructions from perfectly one-way hashing plus NIZKPoK, and separations.
- **59120540.pdf** — Improved Cryptanalysis of Skein (Jean-Philippe Aumasson et al.). First third-party analysis of Skein/Threefish: near-collisions, impossible differentials, and related-key boomerang key-recovery up to 32 of 72 rounds; full Skein unaffected.
- **59120558.pdf** — Linearization Framework for Collision Attacks: Application to CubeHash and MD6 (Eric Brier, Shahram Khazaei, Willi Meier, Thomas Peyrin). Linearization/dependency-table differential framework yielding best-known collision attacks on reduced-round SHA-3 candidates CubeHash and MD6.
- **59120596.pdf** — Fiat-Shamir With Aborts: Applications to Lattice and Factoring-Based Signatures (Vadim Lyubashevsky). Rejection-sampling Fiat-Shamir framework yielding the most efficient lattice-based ID and signature schemes to date, secure under worst-case ideal-lattice assumptions.
- **59120614.pdf** — Efficient Public-Key Encryption Based on Ideal Lattices (Extended Abstract) (Damien Stehlé, Ron Steinfeld, Keisuke Tanaka, Keita Xagawa) (?). Extract is hex-escaped but decodable; abstract body unavailable, so contribution not summarizable beyond the title.
- **59120632.pdf** — Smooth Projective Hashing and Password-Based Authenticated Key Exchange from Lattices (Jonathan Katz, Vinod Vaikuntanathan). CCA-secure LWE-based encryption admitting smooth projective hashing, giving the first PAKE protocol secure under a lattice assumption in the standard model.
- **59120649.pdf** — PSS is Secure against Random Fault Attacks (Jean-Sébastien Coron, Avradip Mandal). Proves the Bellcore RSA-CRT fault attack cannot extend to PSS encoding: PSS is provably secure against random fault attacks in the ROM.
- **59120664.pdf** — Cache-Timing Template Attacks (Billy Bob Brumley, Risto M. Hakala). Combines vector quantization and hidden Markov models for automated cache-timing cryptanalysis, recovering OpenSSL ECC keys via lattice attacks in hours on live traces.
- **59120682.pdf** — Memory Leakage-Resilient Encryption based on Physically Unclonable Functions (Frederik Armknecht, Roel Maes, Ahmad-Reza Sadeghi, Berk Sunar, Pim Tuyls). Introduces PUF-PRFs binding cryptography to hardware physical structure, building a block cipher resilient to memory attacks.
- **59120700.pdf** — Signature Schemes with Bounded Leakage Resilience (Jonathan Katz, Vinod Vaikuntanathan). Standard-model signature schemes tolerating leakage of n−n^ε bits, plus one-time schemes from one-way functions tolerating (1/4−ε)n and (1/2−ε)n leakage.
- **59780001.pdf** — An Efficient Parallel Repetition Theorem (Johan Håstad, Rafael Pass, Douglas Wikström, Krzysztof Pietrzak). General parallel-repetition theorem with efficient reduction, showing exponential soundness-error decay for public-coin and simulatable-verifier arguments.
- **59780019.pdf** — Parallel Repetition Theorems for Interactive Arguments (Kai-Min Chung, Feng-Hao Liu). Tight δ→δⁿ parallel repetition for public-coin arguments without Raz's Sampling Lemma, near-tight δ^(n/2) for broader classes, and FHE-based soundness amplification.
