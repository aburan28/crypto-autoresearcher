# Batch 104 digest

## Overview
- Dominated by 2013-era LNCS-style papers (Asiacrypt/FSE/TCC venues): secure multiparty computation, UC security, commitments, and zero-knowledge are the single largest cluster.
- Strong secondary clusters in hash-function cryptanalysis (HMAC/Whirlpool, RIPEMD-160, HAS-160, diamond structures) and block-cipher cryptanalysis (Feistel, Even-Mansour, LED-128, LBlock/TWINE).
- Side-channel and leakage work is well represented: masking schemes, leakage-detection statistics, tamper resilience, leakage-resilient PKE, and practical SPA on binary Huff curves.
- Notable high-impact items: Boneh–Waters constrained PRFs, Jutla–Roy quasi-adaptive NIZK, Bernstein–Lange "non-uniform cracks in the concrete", and the Taiwan smart-card RSA factoring study.
- ECC/ECDLP-adjacent material is thin: only binary Huff curve implementation/SPA and the 4-dimensional GLV via Weil restriction (genus-2 / elliptic curves over Fp2).
- Anomaly: 82700101.pdf is a 1-page invited-talk abstract (Danezis), not a full research paper.

## Papers
- **80860205.pdf** — A New Model for Error-Tolerant Side-Channel Cube Attacks (Li, Zhang, Fan, Verbauwhede). Recasts side-channel cube key recovery as binary linear decoding over a noisy channel; recovers full 80-bit PRESENT key with noisy measurements.
- **80860212.pdf** — On the Implementation of Unified Arithmetic on Binary Huff Curves (Ghosh, Kumar, Das, Verbauwhede). FPGA implementation of unified binary Huff arithmetic; shows actual SPA leakage contradicting claimed uniformity and proposes a fix.
- **80860223.pdf** — Analysis and Improvement of the Generic Higher-Order Masking Scheme of FSE 2012 (Roy, Vivek). Studies masking complexity of Carlet et al.'s generic S-box masking via cyclotomic-class addition chains, with lower bounds and improvements.
- **82700001.pdf** — New Generic Attacks Against Hash-based MACs (Leurent, Peyrin, Wang). Shows HMAC/NMAC give far less security above the birthday bound: Õ(2^{l/2}) distinguishing-H/state-recovery and Õ(2^{3l/4}) key recovery for checksum hashes.
- **82700021.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of HMAC/NMAC-Whirlpool (Guo, Sasaki, Wang, Wu). Universal forgery and first "original" key recovery on 6-round HMAC-Whirlpool, plus distinguishing-H on the full construction.
- **82700041.pdf** — Lattice-Based Group Signatures with Logarithmic Signature Size (Laguillaumie, Langlois, Libert, Stehlé). First lattice group signatures with signature/public-key size logarithmic in group size, secure under SIS/LWE in ROM.
- **82700061.pdf** — The Fiat–Shamir Transformation in a Quantum World (Dagdelen, Fischlin, Gagliardoni). Shows black-box extractors cannot exist for Fiat–Shamir in the quantum random-oracle model under natural conditions; discusses workarounds.
- **82700081.pdf** — On the Security of One-Witness Blind Signature Schemes (Baldimtsi, Lysyanskaya). Meta-reduction ("personal nemesis adversary") ruling out standard ROM security proofs for Schnorr/Brands blind signatures, impacting U-Prove.
- **82700101.pdf** — Engineering Privacy-Friendly Computations (Danezis). 1-page invited-talk abstract arguing crypto primitives for private computation are mismatched with engineering practice; not a full paper.
- **82700102.pdf** — Unconditionally Secure and Universally Composable Commitments from Physical Assumptions (Damgård, Scafuro). Constant-round black-box compiler giving first unconditional UC commitments from malicious PUFs and stateless tamper-proof tokens.
- **82700122.pdf** — Functional Encryption from (Small) Hardware Tokens (Chung, Katz, Zhou). Uses identical stateless hardware tokens to circumvent impossibility results for simulation-secure functional encryption; extensions to hierarchy and function privacy.
- **82700142.pdf** — Bounded Tamper Resilience: How to go Beyond the Algebraic Barrier (Damgård, Faust, Mukherjee, Venturi). Achieves RKA security against arbitrary key relations by bounding tampering queries; applies to Σ-protocol ID/signature schemes.
- **82700162.pdf** — Tamper Resilient Circuits: The Adversary at the Gates (Kiayias, Tselekounis). Introduces gate-tampering model, proves it strictly stronger than wire-tampering, gives impossibility results and a compiler achieving resilience.
- **82700182.pdf** — Efficient General-Adversary Multi-Party Computation (Hirt, Tschudi). Improves MPC for general (non-threshold) adversary structures: O(|Z|²) perfect and O(|Z|) statistical communication versus prior O(|Z|³).
- **82700202.pdf** — Fair and Efficient Secure Multiparty Computation with Reputation Systems (Asharov, Lindell, Zarosim). Formalizes MPC when reputation scores estimate party honesty; nearly tight feasibility results for fair computation.
- **82700222.pdf** — Between a Rock and a Hard Place: Interpolating Between MPC and FHE (Choudhury, Loftus, Orsini, Patra, Smart). Parameter-L protocol trading interaction for computation via interactive bootstrapping of SHE, interpolating MPC and FHE.
- **82700242.pdf** — Building Lossy Trapdoor Functions from Lossy Encryption (Hemenway, Ostrovsky). Derandomizes lossy encryption with pairwise-independent hashing to obtain lossy/injective trapdoor functions; links to OT equivalence.
- **82700262.pdf** — Pseudorandom Generators from Regular One-way Functions (Yu, Li, Weng). Tighter PRG constructions from known/unknown-regular OWFs with optimal call complexity matching Holenstein–Sinha lower bounds.
- **82700281.pdf** — Constrained Pseudorandom Functions and Their Applications (Boneh, Waters). Introduces constrained PRFs enabling evaluation only on authorized subsets; applications to ID-based key exchange and optimal broadcast encryption.
- **82700301.pdf** — Fully Homomorphic Message Authenticators (Gennaro, Wichs). Defines and builds homomorphic authenticators from FHE: cloud computes over authenticated data and returns a short verifiable tag.
- **82700321.pdf** — Non-uniform cracks in the concrete: the power of free precomputation (Bernstein, Lange). Argues standard concrete-security definitions ignore non-uniform precomputation, exhibiting sub-2^128 "attacks" on AES-128, P-256, DSA/RSA-3072.
- **82700341.pdf** — Factoring RSA keys from certified smart cards: Coppersmith in the wild (Bernstein, Chang, Cheng, Chou, Heninger, Lange, van Someren). Factors 184 of 2M+ Taiwan Citizen Digital Certificate RSA-1024 keys via batch-GCD and Coppersmith-type attacks.
- **82700361.pdf** — Naturally Rehearsing Passwords (Blocki, Blum, Datta). Formal usability/security models for password management based on human memory rehearsal schedules; shows password reuse boosts natural rehearsal.
- **82700381.pdf** — Leakage-Resilient CCA Secure PKE from Hash Proof System and One-Time Lossy Filter (Qin, Liu). Generic LR-CCA PKE construction from HPS + OT-LF achieving leakage rate 1/2 − o(1) under DDH/DCR.
- **82700401.pdf** — On Continual Leakage of Discrete Log Representations (Agrawal, Dodis, Vaikuntanathan, Wichs). DL representations tolerate continual bounded leakage; yields simple CLR one-way functions and PKE in the invisible-key-update model.
- **82700421.pdf** — Hiding the Input-Size in Secure Two-Party Computation (Lindell, Nissim, Orlandi). Initiates definitional study of input-size-hiding secure computation, treating cases with no a priori polynomial input-size bound.
- **82700441.pdf** — Secure Two-Party Computation with Reusable Bit-Commitments, via Cut-and-Choose with Forge-and-Lose (Brandão). C&C garbled-circuit 2PC needing only one correct evaluated circuit (~1/3 fewer GCs) plus reusable homomorphic commitments.
- **82700461.pdf** — A heuristic for finding compatible differential paths with application to HAS-160 (Kircanski, AlTawy, Youssef). Heuristic search for compatible differential paths yielding first practical second-order collision over all HAS-160 steps.
- **82700479.pdf** — Improved Cryptanalysis of Reduced RIPEMD-160 (Mendel, Peyrin, Schläffer, Wang, Wu). Non-linear path search plus branch merging yields 42-step semi-free-start collision on RIPEMD-160 compression function (was 36).
- **82700499.pdf** — Limited-birthday Distinguishers for Hash Functions (Iwamoto, Peyrin, Sasaki). Formalizes limited-birthday problem via dTCR, proves FSE 2010 generic attack optimal, and shows beyond-birthday collision attacks remain meaningful.
- **82700519.pdf** — On Diamond Structures and Trojan Message Attacks (Kortelainen, Kortelainen). New diamond-structure construction with O(2^{(n+d)/2}) message complexity and improved Trojan-message attack variants on Merkle–Damgård hashes.
- **82710121.pdf** — Random Projections, Graph Sparsification, and Differential Privacy (Upadhyay). Efficient differentially private sanitizers for cut queries on sparse graphs via preconditioned spectral sparsification extending Blocki et al.
- **82710137.pdf** — Leaked-State-Forgery Attack Against the Authenticated Encryption Algorithm ALE (Wu, Wu, Huang, Wang, Wu). New differential-style forgery exploiting leaked state; reduces ALE's claimed 128-bit authentication security to 97 bits (verified on a small variant).
- **82710143.pdf** — Constructing Confidential Channels from Authenticated Channels—Public-Key Encryption Revisited (Coretti, Maurer, Tackmann). Recasts PKE security in the constructive-cryptography framework, defining which channel properties schemes actually provide.
- **82710158.pdf** — Reset Indifferentiability and its Consequences (Baecher, Brzuska, Mittelbach). Characterizes reset-indifferentiable simulators as stateless/pseudo-deterministic; shows ROM and ideal-cipher model incomparable under reset indifferentiability.
- **82710160.pdf** — New Insight into the Isomorphism of Polynomial Problem IP1S and its Use in Cryptography (Macario-Rat, Plut, Gilbert). Explains why random IP1S instances are easy: polar-form matrix cyclicity gives a polynomial-time solver without Gröbner bases.
- **82710163.pdf** — Shorter Quasi-Adaptive NIZK Proofs for Linear Subspaces (Jutla, Roy). Defines quasi-adaptive NIZK for parametrized languages; shorter proofs than Groth–Sahai for linear subspaces under k-linear/XDH assumptions.
- **82710172.pdf** — Does My Device Leak Information? An a priori Statistical Power Analysis of Leakage Detection Tests (Mather, Oswald, Bandenburg, Wójcik). Compares t-test and mutual-information leakage-detection tests via a priori power analysis; finds t-test and continuous-MI comparably powerful.
- **82710196.pdf** — Generic Key Recovery Attack on Feistel Scheme (Isobe, Shibutani). All-subkeys-recovery meet-in-the-middle attacks on 8/9/11-round Feistel variants; best attack on 8-round CAST-128; implies round-count lower bounds.
- **82710201.pdf** — Efficient One-Way Secret-Key Agreement and Private Channel Coding via Polarization (Renes, Renner, Sutter). Polar-code schemes achieving strong-secrecy one-way secret-key and wiretap rates with near-linear complexity, though code construction is not efficient.
- **82710203.pdf** — Key Recovery Attacks on 3-round Even-Mansour, 8-step LED-128, and Full AES² (Dinur, Dunkelman, Keller, Shamir). First o(2^n) attack on one-key 3-round Even-Mansour; improves LED-128 to 8 steps and gives first attack on full AES².
- **82710219.pdf** — Four-Dimensional GLV via the Weil Restriction (Guillevic, Ionica). Exploits (2,2)-isogenies between genus-2 Jacobians and elliptic-curve products to get 4-dimensional GLV scalar multiplication on Freeman–Satoh curves and new Fp2 curves.
- **82710226.pdf** — How to Construct an Ideal Cipher from a Small Set of Public Permutations (Lampe, Seurin). Proves 12-round single-key iterated Even-Mansour (key-alternating cipher) indifferentiable from an ideal cipher; 4 rounds shown necessary.
- **82710236.pdf** — Behind the Scene of Side Channel Attacks (Lomné, Prouff, Roche). Bridges theory and practice of SCA evaluation; improves attacks (including stochastic) when the sensitive time instant is unknown among many leakage points.
- **82710239.pdf** — Notions of Black-Box Reductions, Revisited (Baecher, Brzuska, Fischlin). Extends the RTV framework with finer-grained notions capturing meta-reduction-targeted and "almost black-box" reductions.
- **82710245.pdf** — Adaptive and Concurrent Secure Computation from New Adaptive, Non-Malleable Commitments (Dachman-Soled, Malkin, Raykova, Venkitasubramaniam). Unified framework for adaptive UC secure computation; first adaptively secure concurrent protocols in timing and non-uniform simulation models.
- **82710253.pdf** — Constant-Round Concurrent Zero Knowledge in the Bounded Player Model (Goyal, Jain, Ostrovsky, Richelson, Visconti). First constant-round concurrent ZK in the bounded-player model from CRHFs with straight-line simulation, subsuming Barak's protocol.
- **82710258.pdf** — Key Difference Invariant Bias in Block Ciphers (Bogdanov, Boura, Rijmen, Wang, Wen, Zhao). Identifies linear approximations whose bias is invariant under key difference; yields related-key attacks on LBlock and TWINE breaking 2–3 more rounds.
- **82710265.pdf** — SPHF-Friendly Non-Interactive Commitments (Abdalla, Benhamouda, Blazy, Chevalier, Pointcheval). New UC-secure commitment formalism compatible with SPHFs; gives first one-round PAKE and efficient OT secure against adaptive adversaries.
- **82710268.pdf** — Succinct Non-Interactive Zero Knowledge Arguments from Span Programs and Linear Error-Correcting Codes (Lipmaa). Simplifies GGPR Circuit-SAT NIZK via standard span programs plus high-distance linear codes, improving efficiency.
