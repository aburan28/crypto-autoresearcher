# Batch 018 digest

## Overview
- Almost entirely theoretical cryptography, circa CRYPTO/TCC 2019 era: zero knowledge, secure computation, encryption (ABE, FE, FHE), proof systems, and non-malleability dominate.
- Recurring threads: lattice/LWE-based constructions (NIZK, ABE, ZK arguments), quantum security in the QROM (O2H theorem, SNARGs, delegated quantum computation), and storage-proofs (Filecoin replication, proofs of space-time).
- Notable applied results: devastating practical cryptanalysis of OCB2 (ISO-standardized AE mode), Bellare–Ng–Tackmann's AEAD-nonce-privacy revision, and asymmetric message franking for Signal-like messaging.
- No elliptic-curve or ECDLP papers appear in this batch; only tangential number theory (modular inversion hidden number problem).
- Three anomalies: two Springer LNCS volume front-matter pages (11745853, 11818175) and one hex-encoded unreadable extract (11891120).

## Papers
- **116940305.pdf** — On the Plausibility of Fully Homomorphic Encryption for RAMs (Hamlin, Holmgren, Weiss, Wichs). Initiates RAM-FHE; identifies rewindable ORAM as a necessary prerequisite and builds it from symmetric-key doubly-efficient PIR.
- **116940311.pdf** — Attribute Based Encryption (and more) for Nondeterministic Finite Automata from LWE (Agrawal, Maitra, Yamada). First symmetric-key ABE for NFAs from LWE, supporting unbounded inputs and unbounded key requests.
- **116940312.pdf** — Noninteractive Zero Knowledge for NP from (Plain) Learning With Errors (Peikert, Shiehian). Closes the open problem of NIZK for NP from plain LWE via a correlation-intractable hash family for arbitrary polynomial-size circuits.
- **116940313.pdf** — SpOT-Light: Lightweight Private Set Intersection from Sparse OT Extension (Pinkas, Rosulek, Trieu, Yanai). Two-party semi-honest PSI with lowest monetary cost using sparse OT extension and polynomial techniques.
- **116940316.pdf** — Proofs of Replicated Storage Without Timing Assumptions (Damgård, Ganesh, Orlandi). Formalizes Filecoin-style proof of replication and gives the first construction not relying on timing assumptions.
- **116940326.pdf** — Memory-Hard Functions from Cryptographic Primitives (Chen, Tessaro). Provably memory-hard constructions built from block ciphers, compression functions, and permutations via hard-to-pebble graphs.
- **116940327.pdf** — Unconditionally Secure Computation Against Low-Complexity Leakage (Bogdanov, Ishai, Srinivasan). Leakage-resilient circuit compilers unconditionally secure against AC0 and low-complexity global leakage, stateless and continuous.
- **116940330.pdf** — ABE for DFA from k-Lin (Gong, Waters, Wee). First selectively secure ABE for DFAs from the static k-linear assumption, resolving Waters' CRYPTO 2012 open problem.
- **116940333.pdf** — New Results on Modular Inversion Hidden Number Problem and Inversive Congruential Generator (Xu, Sarkar, Hu, Wang, Pan). Revisits Coppersmith techniques for the MIHNP and attacks on the inversive congruential generator.
- **116940339.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of OCB2: Attacks on Authenticity and Confidentiality (Inoue, Iwata, Minematsu, Poettering). Practical universal forgeries and full plaintext recovery against ISO-standardized OCB2, triggering its removal from standards.
- **116940342.pdf** — Explicit Rate-1 Non-malleable Codes for Local Tampering (Gupta, Maji, Wang). First explicit rate-1 non-malleable codes against ξ·log n-local and NC0 tampering in the plain model.
- **116940343.pdf** — Symmetric Primitives with Structured Secrets (Alamati, Montgomery, Patranabis). Black-box separations suggesting key-homomorphic weak PRFs, updatable encryption, and proxy re-encryption inherently need public-key assumptions.
- **116940347.pdf** — Trapdoor Hash Functions and Their Applications (Döttling, Garg, Ishai, Malavolta, Mour, Ostrovsky). Introduces TDHs with one-bit hints from DDH/QR/DCR/LWE, yielding rate-optimizing two-message secure computation protocols.
- **116940351.pdf** — Nonces are Noticed: AEAD Revisited (Bellare, Ng, Tackmann). New AEAD syntax and security definitions hiding nonces as well as messages, including misuse-resistant schemes.
- **116940354.pdf** — Homomorphic Time-Lock Puzzles and Applications (Malavolta, Thyagarajan). Puzzles whose hidden solutions can be homomorphically combined, enabling e-voting, coin flipping, and fair contract signing.
- **116940356.pdf** — New Constructions of Reusable Designated-Verifier NIZKs (Lombardi, Quach, Rothblum, Wichs, Wu). Reusable DV-NIZKs from LWE and LPN plus malicious-verifier variants with a common random string.
- **116940358.pdf** — Synchronous, with a Chance of Partition Tolerance (Guo, Pass, Shi). Consensus model bridging synchronous and partially synchronous settings, tolerating lazy or faulty honest nodes and partitions.
- **116940359.pdf** — Quantum security proofs using semi-classical oracles (Ambainis, Hamburg, Unruh). Improved one-way-to-hiding theorem via semi-classical oracles, giving tighter quantum ROM bounds for public-key encryption.
- **116940361.pdf** — Subvector Commitments with Application to Succinct Arguments (Lai, Malavolta). Position-independent subvector and linear-map commitments yielding small CS-proof-style SNARKs from class groups and pairings.
- **116940362.pdf** — Reusable Non-Interactive Secure Computation (Chase, Dodis, Ishai, Kraschewski, Liu, Ostrovsky, Vaikuntanathan). Maliciously secure reusable two-message NISC overcoming selective-failure attacks of prior OT-based protocols.
- **116940363.pdf** — Realizing Chosen Ciphertext Security Generically in Attribute-Based Encryption and Predicate Encryption (Koppula, Waters). Black-box CPA-to-CCA transform for ABE/predicate encryption using a hinting PRG from CDH or LWE.
- **116940365.pdf** — Zero-Knowledge Proofs on Secret-Shared Data via Fully Linear PCPs (Boneh, Boyle, Corrigan-Gibbs, Gilboa, Ishai). Fully linear PCPs enable succinct ZK proofs over secret-shared or homomorphically encoded inputs for aggregate statistics and MPC.
- **116940369.pdf** — Continuous Space-Bounded Non-Malleable Codes from Stronger Proofs-of-Space (Chen, Chen, Hostáková, Mukherjee). Non-malleable codes resilient to unbounded space-bounded tampering via new proof-extractable non-interactive proofs of space.
- **116940376.pdf** — Efficient Lattice-Based Zero-Knowledge Arguments with Standard Soundness (Yang, Au, Zhang, Xu, Yu, Whyte). ZK arguments for matrix-vector and integer relations with standard soundness and small soundness error, avoiding relaxation.
- **116940386.pdf** — Public-Key Cryptography in the Fine-Grained Setting (LaVigne, Lincoln, Williams). Fine-grained key exchange from structural properties of average-case hard problems like Orthogonal Vectors, if P=NP-era crypto fails.
- **116940389.pdf** — The Privacy Blanket of the Shuffle Model (Balle, Bell, Gascón, Nissim). Optimal single-message summation protocol and privacy amplification analysis for the shuffle model of differential privacy.
- **116940391.pdf** — Security in the Presence of Key Reuse: Context-Separable Interfaces and their Applications (Patton, Shrimpton). Framework for security under key reuse across shared interfaces; analyzes EdDSA and the Noise protocol framework.
- **116940397.pdf** — Algebraic Techniques for Short(er) Exact Lattice-Based Zero-Knowledge Proofs (Bootle, Lyubashevsky, Seiler). First exact short-vector proof system significantly outperforming Stern-type protocols, with 1/n per-round soundness error.
- **116940405.pdf** — Asymmetric Message Franking: Content Moderation for Metadata-Private End-to-End Encryption (Tyagi, Grubbs, Len, Miers, Ristenpart). AMF primitive enabling accountable reporting with deniability in Signal-like metadata-private messaging.
- **116940409.pdf** — On Round Optimal Statistical Zero Knowledge Arguments (Bitansky, Paneth). First three-message statistical ZK arguments for NP from keyless multi-collision-resistant hashing and LWE, matching the round lower bound.
- **116940417.pdf** — CCA Security and Trapdoor Functions via Key-Dependent-Message Security (Kitagawa, Matsuda, Tanaka). IND-CCA PKE and trapdoor functions from IND-CPA PKE plus projection-KDM-secure secret-key encryption.
- **116940422.pdf** — Simple Proofs of Space-Time and Rational Proofs of Storage (Moran, Orlov). Simple, practical PoST protocol letting a prover show storage of data over time, with incremental difficulty adjustment.
- **116940423.pdf** — Unifying computational entropies via Kullback–Leibler divergence (Agrawal, Chen, Horel, Vadhan). Hardness in relative entropy unifies pseudoentropy and inaccessible entropy, yielding modular OWF-to-commitment/PRG proofs.
- **116940429.pdf** — Strong Asymmetric PAKE based on Trapdoor CKEM (Bradley, Jarecki, Xu). Two-flow UC strong asymmetric PAKE from DDH, comparable cost to OPAQUE without hashing onto a group.
- **116940442.pdf** — Batching Techniques for Accumulators with Applications to IOPs and Stateless Blockchains (Boneh, Bünz, Fisch). Batched accumulator membership/non-membership proofs in unknown-order groups enabling stateless blockchains and smaller IOPs.
- **116940450.pdf** — How to Build Pseudorandom Functions From Public Random Permutations (Chen, Lambooij, Mennink). Tight 2n/3-bit security for SoEM and SoKAC PRF constructions from public permutations; birthday-bound limits for single-call designs.
- **116940471.pdf** — Efficient Pseudorandom Correlation Generators: Silent OT Extension and More (Boyle, Couteau, Gilboa, Ishai, Kohl, Scholl). PCGs from LPN enabling silent preprocessing for OT correlations in MPC with minimal communication.
- **116940474.pdf** — It wasn't me! Repudiability and Claimability of Ring Signatures (Park, Sealfon). Formalizes whether non-signers can repudiate and signers can later claim ring signatures; gives definitions and constructions.
- **11745853.pdf** — LNCS volume 3958 front matter only; editorial board cover page, no research paper content.
- **11818175.pdf** — LNCS volume 4117 front matter only; editorial board cover page, no research paper content.
- **11891102.pdf** — Statistical Difference Beyond the Polarizing Regime (Berman, Degwekar, Rothblum, Vasudevan). Extends SD polarization lemmas beyond β<α², links statistical difference hardness to one-way functions.
- **11891103.pdf** — Characterizing Collision and Second-Preimage Resistance in Linicrypt (McQuoid, Swope, Rosulek). Decidable characterization showing collision and second-preimage resistance asymptotically equivalent for nonce-separated Linicrypt programs.
- **11891119.pdf** — Lower and Upper Bounds on the Randomness Complexity of Private Computations of AND (Kushilevitz, Ostrovsky, Prouff, Rosén, Thillard, Vergnaud). First non-trivial lower bound: private AND needs more than one random bit; 8-bit upper bound.
- **11891120.pdf** — Unreadable hex-encoded glyph extract; title and content cannot be determined from the text (appears to be a paper on space-time tradeoffs for function inversion).
- **11891121.pdf** — Optimal Bounded-Collusion Secure Functional Encryption (Ananth, Vaikuntanathan). First public-key FE with encryption complexity linear in the collusion bound, from minimal PKE assumption; OWF suffices in private-key setting.
- **11891123.pdf** — Obfuscated Fuzzy Hamming Distance and Conjunctions from Subset Product Problems (Galbraith, Zobernig). VBB obfuscator for fuzzy Hamming matching and conjunctions from number-theoretic subset-product assumptions.
- **11891124.pdf** — Delegating Quantum Computation in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Zhang). Non-interactive delegation of Toffoli-plus-diagonal quantum circuits with light client quantum work, no FHE needed.
- **11891135.pdf** — Continuously Non-Malleable Secret Sharing for General Access Structures (Brian, Faonio, Venturi). Leakage-resilient continuously non-malleable secret sharing for arbitrary access structures in plain and CRS models.
- **11891138.pdf** — Estimating Gaps in Martingales and Applications to Coin-Tossing: Constructions & Hardness (Khorasgani, Maji, Mukherjee). Optimal-insecurity coin-tossing protocols via martingale gap analysis with geometric inductive techniques.
- **11891143.pdf** — Succinct Arguments in the Quantum Random Oracle Model (Chiesa, Manohar, Spooner). Proves Micali's SNARG unconditionally secure in the QROM, yielding the first post-quantum zkSNARK of knowledge.
