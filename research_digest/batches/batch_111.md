# Batch 111 digest

## Overview
- This batch is dominated by conference proceedings papers, apparently ASIACRYPT 2014 (88730xxx files) and TCC 2015 (9014xxx files), covering a broad sweep of theoretical and applied cryptography.
- ECC/ECDLP-relevant highlights: Joux–Pierrot on small-characteristic finite-field DLP precomputation, Doche on double-base chains for scalar multiplication, and Aranha et al. on GLV/GLS decomposition side-channel attacks against ECDSA with single-bit nonce bias.
- Strong side-channel cluster: soft analytical attacks, higher-order optimal distinguishers, higher-order threshold implementations, and SCA of GF(2^128) multiplication in AES-GCM.
- Large theory contingent: obfuscation (iO, diO, AIPO, UCEs), black-box separations, zero-knowledge variants, secure computation (MPC, ORAM, fairness, topology-hiding, dual execution), and SNARKs via square span programs.
- Symmetric crypto also well represented: Feistel/Even-Mansour/impossible-differential cryptanalysis, sponge AE modes (NORX et al.), RC4 bias exploitation, Catena password scrambling.
- Anomaly: 88730361.pdf is a 1-page abstract of a legal-policy talk on information-security law in Asia, not a technical research paper.

## Papers
- **88730217.pdf** — Improving the Polynomial Time Precomputation of Frobenius Representation Discrete Logarithm Algorithms (Antoine Joux, Cécile Pierrot). Simplified setting and new ideas reduce the polynomial-time precomputation in small-characteristic DLP algorithms from O(q^7) to O(q^6).
- **88730222.pdf** — On the Enumeration of Double-Base Chains with Applications to Elliptic Curve Cryptography (Christophe Doche). Algorithms to enumerate and find optimal double-base chains for scalar multiplication, plus a controlled-DBC method generating random scalars directly as chains.
- **88730225.pdf** — Solving LPN Using Covering Codes (Qian Guo, Thomas Johansson, Carl Löndahl). New LPN algorithm using covering-code approximations that breaks proposed 80-bit-security instances of HB variants, LPN-C, and Lapin.
- **88730227.pdf** — All-But-Many Encryption: A New Framework for Fully-Equipped UC Commitments (Eiichiro Fujisaki). Introduces all-but-many encryption abstraction yielding the first fully-equipped UC commitment scheme with optimal expansion factor, from DCR.
- **88730230.pdf** — Black-Box Separations for One-More (Static) CDH and Its Generalization (Jiang Zhang, Zhenfeng Zhang, Yu Chen, Yanfei Guo, Zongyang Zhang). First impossibility results on basing hardness of one-more (static) CDH over non-bilinear groups on other problems via black-box reductions.
- **88730232.pdf** — GLV/GLS Decomposition, Power Analysis, and Attacks on ECDSA Signatures with Single-Bit Nonce Bias (Aranha, Fouque, Gérard, Kammerer, Tibouchi, Zapalowicz). Analyzes security of GLV/GLS scalar decomposition and mounts side-channel attacks on ECDSA when nonces have a single-bit bias.
- **88730234.pdf** — Adaptive Security of Constrained PRFs (Fuchsbauer, Konstantinov, Pietrzak, Rao). New reduction achieving adaptive security for constrained PRFs with only quasipolynomial loss instead of exponential complexity leveraging.
- **88730238.pdf** — Meet-in-the-Middle Attacks on Generic Feistel Constructions (Guo, Jean, Nikolić, Sasaki). MITM key-recovery attacks on generic balanced Feistel ciphers reaching up to 14 rounds, with new lower bounds on rounds needed for security.
- **88730239.pdf** — Soft Analytical Side-Channel Attacks (Veyrat-Charvillon, Gérard, Standaert). Recasts algebraic side-channel attacks as LDPC-style decoding, combining template-attack probability vectors with low time/memory complexity.
- **88730241.pdf** — Efficient, Oblivious Data Structures for MPC (Marcel Keller, Peter Scholl). Oblivious arrays, dictionaries, and priority queues with polylog overhead built on ORAM protocols, enabling MPC Dijkstra shortest paths; benchmarked with SPDZ.
- **88730244.pdf** — Kummer Strikes Back: New DH Speed Records (Bernstein, Chuengsatiansup, Lange, Schwabe). New constant-time Diffie–Hellman speed records using genus-2 Kummer-surface arithmetic with CPU vectorization, avoiding GLV patents.
- **88730245.pdf** — Authenticating Computation on Groups: New Homomorphic Primitives and Applications (Catalano, Marcedone, Puglisi). Introduces linearly homomorphic authenticated encryption with public verifiability (instantiated for Paillier) and linearly homomorphic signatures for bilinear groups.
- **88730246.pdf** — Beyond 2^{c/2} Security in Sponge-Based Authenticated Encryption Modes (Jovanovic, Luykx, Mennink). Proves sponge-based AE (NORX and other CAESAR submissions) achieves up to min{2^{b/2}, 2^c, 2^κ} security, enabling faster parameter tweaks.
- **88730252.pdf** — Memory-Demanding Password Scrambling (Forler, Lucks, Wenzel). Mounts cache-timing and garbage-collector attacks on scrypt and presents Catena, a memory-hard password scrambler with password-independent memory access.
- **88730254.pdf** — Concise Multi-Challenge CCA-Secure Encryption and Signatures with Almost Tight Security (Libert, Joye, Yung, Peters). Multi-user/multi-challenge CCA-secure encryption and short signatures with almost tight reductions under simple assumptions, with compact ciphertexts.
- **88730256.pdf** — Cryptanalysis of Iterated Even-Mansour Schemes with Two Keys (Dinur, Dunkelman, Keller, Shamir). New multibridge technique shows all two-key iterated Even-Mansour schemes up to four rounds give at most n-bit security; improves LED-128 attack.
- **88730258.pdf** — On Tight Security Proofs for Schnorr Signatures (Fleischhacker, Jager, Schröder). Meta-reduction technique proving unconditionally that no tight generic reduction from natural algebraic-group problems to Schnorr security exists.
- **88730260.pdf** — Compact VSS and Efficient Homomorphic UC Commitments (Damgård, David, Giacomelli, Nielsen). Compact verifiable secret sharing yielding homomorphic UC commitments from symmetric crypto with O(k) amortized communication.
- **88730261.pdf** — Bootstrapping Obfuscators via Fast Pseudorandom Functions (Benny Applebaum). Upgrades obfuscators for weak classes (NC1/TC0) to general-purpose obfuscation if the weak class computes PRFs, under standard assumptions.
- **88730263.pdf** — Masks Will Fall Off: Higher-Order Optimal Distinguishers (Bruneau, Guilley, Heuser, Rioul). Derives optimal profiled higher-order distinguishers against masking schemes, explaining empirical second-order results and extending to any order.
- **88730267.pdf** — Cryptographic Schemes Based on the ASASA Structure: Black-box, White-box, and Public-key (Biryukov, Bouillaguet, Khovratovich). Designs public-key/white-box encryption from alternating affine and nonlinear S-box layers, with instructive attacks on weak variants.
- **88730272.pdf** — Efficient Identity-Based Encryption over NTRU Lattices (Ducas, Lyubashevsky, Prest). First practical lattice-based IBE using a particular NTRU distribution; kilobyte-size keys/ciphertexts and ~1 ms operations, plus shorter signatures.
- **88730275.pdf** — Side-Channel Analysis of Multiplications in GF(2^128): Application to AES-GCM (Belaïd, Fouque, Gérard). Recovers the AES-GCM authentication key from leakage of 128-bit multipliers by reducing key recovery to coding/LPN problems without key guesses.
- **88730287.pdf** — Algebraic Attack against Variants of McEliece with Goppa Polynomial of a Special Form (Faugère, Perret, de Portzamparc). Algebraic key-recovery attack on special cases of Wild McEliece Incognito, reducing to an efficiently solvable polynomial system plus a Sidelnikov-Shestakov variant.
- **88730288.pdf** — Higher-Order Threshold Implementations (Bilgin, Gierlichs, Nikova, Nikov, Rijmen). Extends the Threshold Implementation countermeasure to provable higher-order DPA security, validated with 300M FPGA traces of KATAN-32.
- **88730291.pdf** — Black-Box Separations for Differentially Private Protocols (Khurana, Maji, Sahai). Shows fully black-box constructions from key agreement cannot achieve optimal accuracy for distributed differentially private two-party boolean functions.
- **88730294.pdf** — Square Span Programs with Applications to Succinct NIZK Arguments (Danezis, Fournet, Groth, Kohlweiss). New NP characterization via square span programs yielding SNARKs with 4-group-element proofs verified in 6 pairings.
- **88730299.pdf** — Poly-Many Hardcore Bits for Any One-Way Function and a Framework for Differing-Inputs Obfuscation (Bellare, Stepanovs, Tessaro). Extracts polynomially many hardcore bits from any OWF using iO/diO; gives a definitional framework for differing-inputs obfuscation.
- **88730309.pdf** — Using Indistinguishability Obfuscation via UCEs (Christina Brzuska, Arno Mittelbach). First standard-model construction of a powerful UCE class from iO, puncturable PRFs, and AIPO, instantiating correlation-secure hashing and hardcore-bit extraction.
- **88730310.pdf** — Better Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Lattice Encryption and Their Application to Group Signatures (Benhamouda, Camenisch, Krenn, Lyubashevsky, Neven). More efficient ZK proofs of plaintext knowledge for lattice encryption, yielding a hybrid lattice/DL group signature.
- **88730318.pdf** — Indistinguishability Obfuscation versus Multi-Bit Point Obfuscation with Auxiliary Input (Christina Brzuska, Arno Mittelbach). Studies the relationship between iO and multi-bit point obfuscation with auxiliary input, including equivalence and separation results (?).
- **88730329.pdf** — How to Securely Release Unverified Plaintext in Authenticated Encryption (Andreeva, Bogdanov, Luykx, Mennink, Mouha, Yasuda). First formalization of releasing unverified plaintext (RUP) with plaintext-awareness notions PA1/PA2 characterizing safe AE behavior.
- **88730347.pdf** — Round-Optimal Password-Protected Secret Sharing and T-PAKE in the Password-Only Model (Jarecki, Kiayias, Krawczyk). First round-optimal (t,n) PPSS secure in the password-only model, built on a UC-defined oblivious PRF.
- **88730348.pdf** — Scrutinizing and Improving Impossible Differential Attacks: Applications to CLEFIA, Camellia, LBlock and Simon (Boura, Naya-Plasencia, Suder). Formalizes and optimizes impossible differential cryptanalysis, improving attacks on CLEFIA-128/Camellia and giving new attacks on LBlock and Simon.
- **88730350.pdf** — A Simplified Representation of AES (Henri Gilbert). New "untwisted" representation of two AES rounds enabling improved known-key distinguishers up to 10 rounds / full AES-128.
- **88730354.pdf** — Statistically-Secure ORAM with Õ(log^2 n) Overhead (Chung, Liu, Pass). Simple statistically secure ORAM with Õ(log^2 n) overhead, analyzed via a supermarket-problem bound.
- **88730360.pdf** — Big Bias Hunting in Amazonia: Large-scale Computation and Exploitation of RC4 Biases (Invited Paper) (Paterson, Poettering, Schuldt). Cloud-scale computation of RC4 keystream distributions enables improved plaintext recovery against WPA/TKIP and Microsoft MPPE.
- **88730361.pdf** — The Legal Infrastructure Around Information Security in Asia (Helaine Leggat). Not a research paper: 1-page abstract of an invited legal-policy talk on e-commerce/e-signature law in Asia.
- **90140001.pdf** — On Basing Size-Verifiable One-Way Functions on NP-Hardness (Andrej Bogdanov, Christina Brzuska). Proves that basing size-verifiable OWF inversion on NP-hardness via adaptive reductions implies NP ⊆ coAM, repairing a retracted AGGM claim.
- **90140007.pdf** — The Randomized Iterate, Revisited: Almost Linear Seed Length PRGs from a Broader Class of One-way Functions (Yu, Gu, Li, Weng). Simpler modular proof of Haitner-Harnik-Reingold PRGs and O(n log n)-seed PRGs from weakly-regular OWFs.
- **90140036.pdf** — The Power of Negations in Cryptography (Guo, Malkin, Oliveira, Rosen). Studies monotonicity and negation complexity of cryptographic primitives, proving e.g. one-way permutations cannot be monotone and lower bounds on negations for PRFs.
- **90140066.pdf** — From Weak to Strong Zero-Knowledge and Applications (Chung, Lui, Pass). Shows weak and strong zero-knowledge are inequivalent in general but equivalent for meaningful variants, via new non-black-box simulation.
- **90140093.pdf** — An Efficient Transform from Sigma Protocols to NIZK with a CRS and Non-Programmable Random Oracle (Yehuda Lindell). Fiat-Shamir-type transform giving NIZK proofs with ZK in the CRS model and soundness in the non-programmable ROM, at near-Fiat-Shamir cost.
- **90140134.pdf** — A Little Honesty Goes a Long Way: The Two-Tier Model for Secure Multiparty Computation (Garay, Gelles, Johnson, Kiayias, Yung). Introduces a two-tier MPC model where a small guaranteed-honest first tier anonymously performs computation among untrusted servers.
- **90140159.pdf** — Topology-Hiding Computation (Moran, Orlov, Richelson). Initiates MPC over networks whose topology itself is sensitive, with definitions plus impossibility and feasibility results.
- **90140183.pdf** — Secure Physical Computation Using Disposable Circuits (Fisch, Freund, Naor). Uses destructible hardware tokens to achieve UC-secure multiparty computation on physical inputs leaking at most 1 bit.
- **90140200.pdf** — Complete Characterization of Fairness in Secure Two-Party Computation of Boolean Functions (Asharov, Beimel, Makriyannis, Omri). Settles which symmetric Boolean functions admit fair two-party computation via a simple affine-span criterion.
- **90140230.pdf** — Richer Efficiency/Security Trade-offs in 2PC (Kolesnikov, Mohassel, Riva, Rosulek). Improves dual-execution garbled-circuit 2PC by restricting leakage to computation-dependent predicates and adding light cheating detection, cutting communication ~35%.
- **90140261.pdf** — Round-Efficient Concurrently Composable Secure Computation via a Robust Extraction Lemma (Goyal, Lin, Pandey, Pass, Sahai). First Õ(log n)-round plain-model protocol with angel-based concurrent composable security, via an Õ(log n)-round CCA-secure commitment from OWFs.
- **90140291.pdf** — An Alternative Approach to Non-black-box Simulation in Fully Concurrent Setting (Susumu Kiyoshima). New, arguably simpler proof of public-coin concurrent zero-knowledge for NP using a straight-line variant of Barak's non-black-box simulation.
