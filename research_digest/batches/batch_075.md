# Batch 075 digest

## Overview
- Dominant themes: symmetric-key cryptanalysis (slide attacks, weak keys, related-key rectangle attacks on AES/IDEA/Blowfish/GOST) and hash-function cryptanalysis of the MD4/MD5/SHA-1 family following Wang et al.'s 2004–05 breakthroughs.
- Strong showing of eSTREAM-era stream cipher work (QUAD, Pomaranch, Trivium, IV-setup security) and provable-security/MAC theory (PMAC bounds, randomized preprocessing, hash combiners).
- Second half shifts to public-key and protocol theory: lattice attacks on NTRU and small-exponent RSA (Coppersmith-type), MPC/UC protocols, oblivious transfer, PIR, bounded-quantum-storage QKD, and the NIST SP 800-90 Dual-EC-style RNG analysis by Brown & Gjøsteen.
- Era appears to be ~2006–2007 (FSE/CRYPTO/EUROCRYPT vintage), judging by cited works; mostly IACR-conference-style papers, not elliptic-curve/ECDLP focused.
- Notable papers: Practical Cryptanalysis of SFLASH (Dubois–Fouque–Shamir–Stern), Full Key-Recovery on HMAC/NMAC-MD4, and Information Security Economics (Anderson & Moore) — the latter a non-technical outlier.
- Anomaly: 46220130.pdf extracted as garbled mojibake (likely CJK-encoded PDF); content unidentifiable.

## Papers
- **45930117.pdf** — Security Analysis of Constructions Combining FIL Random Oracles (Yannick Seurin, Thomas Peyrin). Gives tight security bounds and generic attacks for compression functions built by combining smaller fixed-input-length random oracles, applied to block-cipher-based hash proposals.
- **45930135.pdf** — Bad and Good Ways of Post-Processing Biased Physical Random Numbers (Markus Dichtl). Shows quasigroup-based post-processing of biased physical RNG output is easily attacked, and proposes better entropy-extracting algorithms with bounded input length.
- **45930152.pdf** — Improved Slide Attacks (Eli Biham, Orr Dunkelman, Nathan Keller). Speeds up finding slid pairs via cycle-structure analysis and applies it to attack 24-round GOST with unknown S-boxes in ~2^63 encryptions.
- **45930168.pdf** — A New Class of Weak Keys for Blowfish (Orhun Kara, Cevat Manap). Introduces reflection attacks on r-round Blowfish that exploit "reflectively weak keys," recovering subkeys cheaply once such a key is identified with 2^34 known plaintexts.
- **45930182.pdf** — The 128-bit Blockcipher CLEFIA (Taizo Shirai, Kyoji Shibutani, Toru Akishita, Shiho Moriai, Tetsu Iwata). Proposes CLEFIA, an AES-compatible 128-bit block cipher using a generalized Feistel structure with strong hardware and software performance.
- **45930197.pdf** — New Lightweight DES Variants (Gregor Leander, Christof Paar, Axel Poschmann, Kai Schramm). Proposes DESL, a DES variant using a single repeated S-box, resistant to linear/differential/Davies-Murphy attacks and suited to RFID in 1848 GE.
- **45930212.pdf** — A New Attack on 6-Round IDEA (Eli Biham, Orr Dunkelman, Nathan Keller). First attack on 6-round IDEA faster than exhaustive search, combining Square-like techniques with linear cryptanalysis via the weak key schedule.
- **45930226.pdf** — Related-Key Rectangle Attacks on Reduced AES-192 and AES-256 (Jongsung Kim, Seokhie Hong, Bart Preneel). New related-key rectangle attacks on 8/10-round AES-192 and 9-round AES-256 with fewer related keys, also fixing a flaw in a prior EUROCRYPT 2005 attack.
- **45930243.pdf** — An Analysis of XSL Applied to BES (Chu-Wee Lim, Khoongming Khoo). Argues the XSL algebraic attack on AES via the BES embedding is far more expensive than claimed (≥2^401 for BES-128), undermining its effectiveness.
- **45930256.pdf** — On the Security of IV Dependent Stream Ciphers (Côme Berbain, Henri Gilbert). Formalizes security of composing a key/IV-setup PRF with a keystream PRNG, gives a tree-based construction, and extends QUAD's proof to cover its IV setup.
- **45930276.pdf** — Two General Attacks on Pomaranch-like Keystream Generators (Håkan Englund, Martin Hell, Thomas Johansson). A low-complexity distinguisher and an IV-resynchronization attack that work on all versions of the eSTREAM candidate Pomaranch.
- **45930292.pdf** — Analysis of QUAD (Bo-Yin Yang, Owen Chia-Hsin Chen, Daniel J. Bernstein, Jiun-Ming Chen). Practical and theoretical cryptanalysis of the provably-secure stream cipher QUAD, e.g. breaking QUAD(256,20,20) in ~2^66 cycles via XL-Wiedemann.
- **45930311.pdf** — Message Freedom in MD4 and MD5 Collisions: Application to APOP (Gaëtan Leurent). Shows how to control parts of colliding MD4/MD5 messages, defeating a CT-RSA'06 countermeasure and partially recovering APOP passwords.
- **45930331.pdf** — New Message Difference for MD4 (Yu Sasaki, Lei Wang, Kazuo Ohta, Noboru Kunihiro). Improves Wang et al.'s MD4 collision attack with a better local collision and differential-path construction algorithm, lowering complexity.
- **45930351.pdf** — Algebraic Cryptanalysis of 58-round SHA-1 (Makoto Sugita, Mitsuru Kawazoe, Ludovic Perret, Hideki Imai). Improves Wang's SHA-1 attack using algebraic message modification (semi-neutral bits, adjusters), reaching 2^31 for 58 rounds.
- **45930370.pdf** — Algebraic Immunity of S-boxes and Augmented Functions (Simon Fischer, Willi Meier). Studies algebraic immunity of augmented stream-cipher functions, enabling low-data attacks on some filter generators; tests suggest Trivium resists such attacks.
- **45930386.pdf** — Generalized Correlation Analysis of Vectorial Boolean Functions (Claude Carlet, Khoongming Khoo, Chu-Wee Lim, Chuan-Wen Loe). Introduces generalized correlation analysis for vector-output Boolean functions, yielding higher-bias linear approximations than Zhang-Chan attacks.
- **45930404.pdf** — An Analytical Model for Time-Driven Cache Attacks (Kris Tiri, Onur Aciicmez, Michael Neve, Flemming Andersen). Builds an analytical model predicting time-driven cache-attack strength on AES from table count, table size, and cache-line length, verified on three platforms.
- **45930419.pdf** — Improving the Security of MACs via Randomized Message Preprocessing (Yevgeniy Dodis, Krzysztof Pietrzak). Shows a short random salt restores optimal O(q²/2^k) security for hash-then-encrypt MACs built from practical suboptimal universal hash functions.
- **45930439.pdf** — New Bounds for PMAC, TMAC, and XCBC (Kazuhiko Minematsu, Toshiyasu Matsushima). Improves provable security bounds for these block-cipher MAC modes from σ²/2ⁿ to roughly ℓq²/2ⁿ, better when no message is exceptionally long.
- **45930457.pdf** — Perfect Block Ciphers With Small Blocks (Louis Granboulan, Thomas Pornin). Gives an algorithm for uniformly selecting and efficiently evaluating a pseudo-random permutation over small, possibly non-binary, domains using O(log n) space.
- **46220001.pdf** — Practical Cryptanalysis of SFLASH (Vivien Dubois, Pierre-Alain Fouque, Adi Shamir, Jacques Stern). Practical public-key-only forgery attack on the multivariate signature scheme SFLASH (v2 and v3) in about a second after minutes of precomputation.
- **46220013.pdf** — Full Key-Recovery Attacks on HMAC/NMAC-MD4 and NMAC-MD5 (Pierre-Alain Fouque, Gaëtan Leurent, Phong Q. Nguyen). First full key-recovery on HMAC/NMAC instantiated with MD4 (~2^88 queries) and a related-key full recovery on NMAC-MD5.
- **46220031.pdf** — How Should We Solve Search Problems Privately? (Amos Beimel, Tal Malkin, Kobbi Nissim, Enav Weinreb). Proposes stronger privacy definitions for secure multiparty computation of search problems with many valid outputs, plus protocol machinery.
- **46220050.pdf** — Public Key Encryption that Allows PIR Queries (Dan Boneh, Eyal Kushilevitz, Rafail Ostrovsky, William E. Skeith III). First public-key encryption scheme supporting private information retrieval over encrypted data with sublinear communication, hiding access patterns.
- **46220068.pdf** — Information Security Economics – and Beyond (Ross Anderson, Tyler Moore). Position/survey paper arguing incentives and economics explain security failures as much as technical design, spanning privacy, spam, phishing, DRM, and dependability.
- **46220092.pdf** — Cryptography with Constant Input Locality (Benny Applebaum, Yuval Ishai, Eyal Kushilevitz). Characterizes which primitives admit constant input locality: non-malleable primitives cannot, while OWFs/PRGs/commitments/PKE can under coding-based assumptions.
- **46220111.pdf** — Universally-Composable Two-Party Computation in Two Rounds (Omer Horvitz, Jonathan Katz). Constructs two-round UC-secure two-party computation for any functionality in the CRS model, matching round-complexity lower bounds; implies a two-round UC blind signature.
- **46220130.pdf** — Not a readable research paper: extraction is garbled mojibake (likely CJK-encoded PDF); title and content unidentifiable from the text.
- **46220150.pdf** — A Hybrid Lattice-Reduction and Meet-in-the-Middle Attack against NTRU (Nick Howgrave-Graham). Combines lattice reduction with meet-in-the-middle to cut the attack on NTRUEncrypt's k=80 parameter set from 2^84.2 to 2^60.3 loops.
- **46220170.pdf** — Improved Analysis of Kannan's Shortest Lattice Vector Algorithm (Guillaume Hanrot, Damien Stehlé). Improves the twenty-year-old complexity upper bounds of Kannan's SVP/CVP algorithms, refining practical cost estimates for attacking lattice-based cryptosystems.
- **46220206.pdf** — Random Oracles and Auxiliary Input (Dominique Unruh). Introduces a random-oracle model allowing oracle-dependent auxiliary input, provides a lazy-sampling theorem for proofs, and proves RSA-OAEP IND-CCA2 in the new model.
- **46220224.pdf** — Security-Amplifying Combiners for Collision-Resistant Hash Functions (Marc Fischlin, Anja Lehmann). Constructs hash combiners whose collision resistance exceeds the classical concatenation bound despite Joux's multi-collision attack, at near-classical efficiency.
- **46220242.pdf** — Hash Functions and the (Amplified) Boomerang Attack (Antoine Joux, Thomas Peyrin). Adapts the boomerang attack from block ciphers to hash functions as a neutral-bits tool, lowering the complexity of collision attacks on SHA-1.
- **46220260.pdf** — Amplifying Collision Resistance: A Complexity-Theoretic Treatment (Ran Canetti, Ron Rivest, Madhu Sudan, Luca Trevisan, Salil Vadhan, Hoeteck Wee). Gives a complexity-theoretic framework for turning weakly collision-resistant hash functions into strongly collision-resistant ones with near-optimal parameters.
- **46220279.pdf** — How Many Oblivious Transfers are Needed for Secure Multiparty Computation? (Danny Harnik, Yuval Ishai, Eyal Kushilevitz). Shows O(n) OT channels between party pairs are necessary and sufficient for general secure computation tolerating up to (1−ε)n corruptions.
- **46220298.pdf** — Simulatable VRFs with Applications to Multi-Theorem NIZK (Melissa Chase, Anna Lysyanskaya). Defines simulatable verifiable random functions, constructs them from NIZK and bilinear composite-order groups, and applies them to upgrade single-theorem NIZK to multi-theorem.
- **46220317.pdf** — Cryptography in the Multi-string Model (Jens Groth, Rafail Ostrovsky). Proposes the multi-string model where a majority of several authorities honestly generate common random strings, replacing the single trusted CRS party, with applications to fundamental protocols.
- **46220335.pdf** — Secure Identification and QKD in the Bounded-Quantum-Storage Model (Ivan B. Damgård, Serge Fehr, Louis Salvail, Christian Schaffner). Password-based identification and QKD schemes secure in the bounded-quantum-storage model, requiring no quantum memory for honest parties and tolerating noise.
- **46220353.pdf** — A Tight High-Order Entropic Quantum Uncertainty Relation With Applications (Ivan B. Damgård, Serge Fehr, Renato Renner, Louis Salvail, Christian Schaffner). Derives a tight min-entropy uncertainty relation and uses it to prove security of quantum OT, bit commitment, and QKD tolerating up to 17% error.
- **46220372.pdf** — Finding Small Roots of Bivariate Integer Polynomial Equations: a Direct Approach (Jean-Sébastien Coron). Simplifies Coppersmith's bivariate small-roots algorithm while keeping its asymptotic complexity; much faster in practice for factoring RSA with known high bits.
- **46220388.pdf** — A Polynomial Time Attack on RSA with Private CRT-Exponents Smaller Than N^0.073 (Ellen Jochemsz, Alexander May). First polynomial-time attack on RSA with small CRT exponents dp, dq < N^0.073, via Coppersmith-type lattice methods.
- **46220405.pdf** — Invertible Universal Hashing and the TET Encryption Mode (Shai Halevi). Presents TET, a tweakable length-preserving enciphering mode for disk-sector-like applications, built on efficient invertible universal hashing over wide blocks.
- **46220423.pdf** — Reducing Trust in the PKG in Identity Based Cryptosystems (Vipul Goyal). Introduces traceable identity-based encryption so a malicious PKG that sells a user's decryption key risks being caught, mitigating key escrow without multiple authorities.
- **46220441.pdf** — Pirate Evolution: How to Make the Most of Your Traitor Keys (Aggelos Kiayias, Serdar Pehlivanoglu). Formalizes "pirate evolution" attacks on trace-and-revoke schemes and shows the NNL scheme from CRYPTO 2001 is susceptible to decoder multiplication.
- **46220459.pdf** — A Security Analysis of the NIST SP 800-90 Elliptic Curve Random Number Generator (Daniel R. L. Brown, Kristian Gjøsteen). Proves the Dual-EC-style ECRNG secure under three conjectures, but shows the truncated-point problem is solvable at NIST truncation levels, making output distinguishable.
- **46220475.pdf** — A Generalization of DDH with Applications to Protocol Analysis and Computational Soundness (Emmanuel Bresson, Yassine Lakhnech, Laurent Mazaré, Bogdan Warinschi). Introduces the (P,Q)-DDH assumption subsuming prior DDH generalizations, with tight reductions simplifying proofs of group key exchange and Abadi-Rogaway soundness.
- **46220493.pdf** — Chernoff-type Direct Product Theorems (Russell Impagliazzo, Ragesh Jaiswal, Valentine Kabanets). First proof that parallel repetition with threshold acceptance amplifies security of challenge-response protocols such as CAPTCHAs, via a Chernoff-like direct product theorem.
- **46220510.pdf** — Rerandomizable RCCA Encryption (Manoj Prabhakaran, Mike Rosulek). First perfectly rerandomizable replayable-CCA-secure encryption scheme ("Double-strand Cramer-Shoup") under DDH, with UC functionality characterization.
- **46220528.pdf** — Deterministic and Efficiently Searchable Encryption (Mihir Bellare, Alexandra Boldyreva, Adam O'Neill). Defines strongest-possible privacy for deterministic public-key encryption enabling logarithmic-time database search; includes the length-preserving RSA-DOAEP construction.
