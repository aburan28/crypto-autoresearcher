# Semaev conservation and monodromy: publication proof dossier

**Status:** publication-oriented analysis; no ledger promotion, open-problem closure,
or cryptographic claim is made by this directory.

This dossier narrows the proposed paper to one theorem package:

> For a nonsingular short-Weierstrass elliptic curve in characteristic zero
> or greater than three, the generic one-variable Semaev polynomial is a signed-sum Kummer
> cover. Its splitting field is the fixed field of simultaneous sign reversal,
> its monodromy is the regular elementary abelian group
> \((C_2)^{m-2}\), and its finite-field specializations obey an all-or-nothing
> linear-versus-quadratic factorization law. A witness-count conservation law
> then separates field-of-definition effects from actual relation yield.

The detailed statements and proofs are in
[`theorem-dossier.md`](theorem-dossier.md). Exact convention checks are in
[`verify.py`](verify.py).

## What this work adds

The proof audit produced more than the original “monodromy is
\(C_2^{m-2}\)” headline.

1. **Correct splitting field.** If
   \(L=F(\sqrt{f(x_2)},\ldots,\sqrt{f(x_m)})\), the roots do not generate all of
   \(L\). Simultaneous sign reversal fixes every root. The splitting field is
   exactly \(L^{\langle\delta\rangle}\), and this gives the desired group
   without the contradictory “full field, then quotient” argument.

2. **Regular action and irreducibility.** The group acts regularly on the
   \(2^{m-2}\) generic roots. The normalized one-variable Semaev polynomial is
   therefore generically irreducible even though the action is imprimitive for
   \(m\ge 4\).

3. **Only two good finite-field factorization types.** At an unramified
   specialization over \(\mathbb F_q\), the polynomial either:
   - splits into \(2^{m-2}\) linear factors; or
   - factors into \(2^{m-3}\) irreducible quadratics.

   There are no mixed linear/quadratic patterns and no factors of degree above
   two on the good locus.

4. **Exact character criterion.** Complete splitting occurs precisely when all
   quadratic characters \(\chi(f(x_i))\) agree. Therefore both the
   all-square locus and the all-nonsquare (quadratic-twist) locus split
   completely. Complete splitting is not unique to rational-point factor
   bases.

5. **Square discriminant for every \(m\ge4\).** Every nonidentity element in
   the regular action is a product of an even number of transpositions, so the
   generic Galois group lies in the alternating group. The discriminant is
   consequently a square in the base function field for all \(m\ge4\).

6. **Exact split-density formula.** If \(r=m-1\), \(t=q+1-\#E(\mathbb F_q)\),
   and \(Z=\#\{x:f(x)=0\}\), then among tuples with \(f(x_i)\ne0\), the number
   whose Frobenius class is trivial is
   \[
     \left(\frac{q-Z-t}{2}\right)^r+
     \left(\frac{q-Z+t}{2}\right)^r.
   \]
   For fixed \(m\), the density is
   \(2^{2-m}+O_m(q^{-1})\), before removing the lower-dimensional collision
   locus.

7. **All block systems are classified.** In a regular action, blocks are
   exactly cosets of subgroups. The standard resultant recursion realizes one
   flag, but there are \(2^{m-2}-1\) quadratic intermediate fields and many
   alternative flags. It is therefore not yet justified to say that the
   recursion has exhausted every algorithmic use of imprimitivity.

8. **A bridge to conservation.** For an \(x\)-coordinate factor base, count
   signed witnesses rather than only distinct Semaev tuples. The total witness
   mass over all group targets is exactly fixed by the base size and arity.
   Geometry can change support, collision multiplicity, rank, and solving cost,
   but not the whole-group mean witness count.

## Corrections to the current internal account

This directory deliberately does not rewrite immutable research records.
Instead, it identifies corrections that a later Coordinator record should
consider:

- “the splitting field is the full multiquadratic field” is false; it is the
  diagonal-sign fixed field;
- a proof cannot first assert that every individual square root is generated by
  the roots and then quotient out simultaneous sign reversal;
- the rational-liftable \(x\)-coordinate condition is an arithmetic
  quadratic-character condition of constant density for fixed \(m\), not a
  “measure-zero factor-base locus” in the relevant finite-field counting model;
- complete splitting also occurs on the all-nonsquare/twist locus;
- “there is no hidden block structure beyond the standard recursion” is too
  strong: all subgroup-coset block systems exist, and only one flag has been
  linked to the recursion;
- generic complete-splitting statistics and relation yield must not be equated
  until the sign-witness multiplicity convention is fixed.

## Reproduction

From the repository root:

```bash
python3 analysis/semaev-conservation-monodromy/verify.py
```

The checker uses only the repository’s existing `sympy` dependency. It verifies:

- \(\operatorname{disc}_{x_3}S_3=16f(x_1)f(x_2)\);
- the regular-action cycle profile through \(m=8\);
- four good \(S_4\) specializations over \(\mathbb F_{101}\), including the
  all-nonsquare split case and both mixed quadratic cases;
- the square-discriminant corollary at those \(S_4\) specializations;
- the trace/character count formulas; and
- signed-witness conservation for two different equal-size factor bases whose
  support distributions differ.

## Publication path

A defensible manuscript should be theorem-first:

1. signed-sum root description and generic distinctness;
2. fixed-field splitting theorem and regular monodromy;
3. finite-field Frobenius/factorization law and exact density;
4. block-system classification;
5. signed-witness conservation and cryptanalytic scope;
6. computational convention checks;
7. primary-literature novelty review.

The current high-upside question is no longer whether the group is
\((C_2)^{m-2}\). It is whether the exact finite-field factorization law,
discriminant consequence, block-system classification, and conservation bridge
have already been assembled in the Semaev literature—and whether an alternative
subspace flag yields a measurably sparser elimination than the standard
resultant recursion.
