# Pilot: bounded-degree closure refutation at m=3 (chained S_3), n = 9..19

**This is an exploratory, hypothesis-generating pilot. It is not program evidence.** It ran no contract, wrote no run record, and changed no status. Its role is to say which contract is worth designing.

## Question
The c_trial harvest (`../ctrial-harvest/`) shows that SAT and enumeration solvers spend about 2^(m·l) on each failed decomposition attempt. Index calculus can beat rho only if that per-attempt exponent is below m/2 − 1. Only a bounded-degree algebraic refutation could achieve that: a closure at a fixed degree D that derives 1 from an unsatisfiable descended system. This pilot asks whether such a refutation holds at m=3, and with what degree.

## Object
- **System:** chained S_3 with m = t = 3. The equations are S_3(u,x1,x2) = 0 and S_3(u,x3,x_R) = 0, where u ∈ F_(2^n) is auxiliary.
- **Factor base:** x_i ∈ V = {deg < k}, with k = ceil(n/3).
- **Curves:** y² + xy = x³ + x² + b, with #E = 2q and q prime. Targets are x(2P).
- **Builders:** the repository's `boolsys` from EXP-SEMBIN-7e1371 and the chained builder in `vendor/`. `vendor/SOURCES.sha256` records sources and hashes; `vendor/PATCH_boolsys.diff` is the only edit, an import path.
- **Labels:** every target is labelled SAT/UNSAT by two independent exhaustive enumerators, one at curve level and one a Boolean C solver. They agree on 1,099 of 1,099 targets.

## Closures
- **M_D:** the plain Macaulay matrix.
- **W_D:** the mutant closure iterated to fixpoint, using the repository's `closure.c` with Ubuntu M4RI 0.0.20200125.
- **myW_D / myM_D:** an independent bit-packed GF(2) eliminator that does not use M4RI (`myclosure.c`).

- **Soundness:** there were no elimination faults and no evaluation-check mismatches.

## Reading (derived; not a decision)
1. **Degree.** On these toy sizes the minimal refuting closure degree is flat at 4 for n = 9, 11 and 15. Plain Macaulay needs degree 6 at n=9, and the semi-regular estimate is 7 to 11. This is a sound, independently cross-checked reading consistent with Semaev's Assumption 1 (degree ≤ 4) at m = t = 3. It agrees with the single in-repo msolve reading of d_F4 = 4 at (17,3,3,7), RUN-SEMBIN-b6eb9f. It does not stand in for the quarantined KN-FIND-9cba50.
2. **Mechanism.** The support-matched null is refuted while the dense matched null is not. So the low-degree refutation follows the sparse support of the descended system: u enters affinely, and the blocks are bilinear. It does not follow Semaev's particular coefficients, and it is not generic.
3. **No speedup is shown.**
   - At fixed D the per-attempt cost is polynomial in N, about C(N,4)^ω with N = n + 3k ≈ 2n. The toy-range wall time grows steeply (0.7 s, 7 s, 322 s), and n=19 exceeds this host's memory.
   - m=3 is in any case linear-algebra-bound: a factor base of about 2^(n/3) gives roughly 2^(2n/3) > 2^(n/2).
   - An exponent-level gain would need m ≥ 5 together with a degree that stays bounded as m grows. That is the regime of the Semaev 2015 cost family, whose crossover sits at n ≈ 460–520 (EV-SEMBIN-4125ec).

## Limitations
- One curve per n and one choice of V (polynomial basis). The random-V cells were labelled but never run.
- n=15 has 8 UNSAT instances; n=13 and n=17 are single probes; n=19 is censored by memory.
- The nulls were run only at n=9.
- The container restarted mid-ladder at about 18:30Z. The drivers stopped, and the planned n = 11/13/15 nulls and random-V cells were never run. `summarize.py` fails on the partial ladder, so `aggregate.py` reads the raw `results/*.jsonl` directly.
- Wall times were measured with up to 5 processes sharing 4 CPUs.
- The M4RI used here is 0.0.20200125, the version with the documented large-elimination defect. The independent eliminator agrees on every comparison run, at n = 9 and 11.

## Successor measurement (what a contract should freeze)
- W_D refutation degree and cost on chained S_3 for t = m ∈ {3, 4, 5}.
- n ladder up to the memory wall, with several curves and both polynomial and random V.
- Support-matched and dense nulls at every n.
- A fixed M4RI (≥ release-20240729) as the main eliminator, with the independent eliminator as cross-check.
- Ground truth from enumeration.
- A preregistered reading: does D stay at 4 as m grows (Assumption 1 at m ≥ 4), and does the sparse-support mechanism persist?
