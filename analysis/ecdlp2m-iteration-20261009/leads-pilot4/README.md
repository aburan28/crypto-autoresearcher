# Leads pilot #4: where the degree-4 closure's cost could come down, and the regime it was measured in

**Exploratory, hypothesis-generating pilot. Not program evidence.** No contract, no run
record, no ledger write, no status change. Toy sizes only (n = 7..11, m = 3..6); nothing here
is a statement about ECC2K-130 at any cryptographic size. One prediction was pre-registered
before its run (`PREDICTIONS.md`, commit 8322884378); everything else is post hoc and is
labelled so. All numbers below come from `results/*.jsonl` through `aggregate.py`
(`aggregate.md`), except the n = 131 table, which is a derivation (`loc_cost.py`,
`loc_table.py`, `locality_cost_n131.txt`) on top of `../wwit-ecc2k130/wwit_model.py`.

Host: the same 4-CPU / 15 GB machine as pilots #1 to #3, shared with the running
EXP-SEMBIN-d9767f trial and with up to two other pilot jobs; every job ran under `nice 19`
with an address-space cap (3 GB; 5 GB at m = 6). Wall times are upper bounds; products
(row x variable multiplications) and column counts are the reproducible cost figures.

## Questions

Pilot #3 found the degree-4 refutation survives a locality-2 column restriction up to m = 5.
Four follow-ups, each a candidate way to shrink the per-attempt cost that every closure lead
depends on (`../NEXT-STEPS.md`, step 1; `../wwit-ecc2k130/`):

1. **What is locality worth at n = 131?** (derivation)
2. **Can the columns be cut further** by capping how many u-bits (intermediate sums) a
   monomial may carry? (kind `cap`)
3. **Can the closure run window by window**, two adjacent blocks at a time, passing only
   polynomials on the shared block? (kind `win`)
4. **Can the target-independent part be closed once** and reused for every target?
   (kind `pre`)

and one pre-registered test: **does the locality-2 closure still refute at m = 6?**

## Object and instruments

Chained S_3 with t = m, as in pilots #1 to #3 (`pilot3.build_chain`, an unchanged copy).
Instances are byte-identical copies of pilot #3's labelled files, plus one new cell:
`m6-n9k3.json` and the check cell `m5-n9k3-regen.json`, generated by `gen_cell.py` with the
curve, field, basis and target list of pilot #3's m5-n9k3 cell. `gen_cell.py` labels by an
explicit chain enumeration with closed-form roots of the quadratic S_3(u, x, y) = 0 in u;
on the m = 5 cell it reproduces pilot #3's labels on 80/80 targets and contains every pilot
solution mask; every solution it emits is checked against the descended Boolean system.

`myclosure4.c` is pilot #3's `myclosure_local.c` (sha256 21f3648e...) with three additions:

- allowed monomials can also be restricted to a variable window and to at most `du` u-bits;
- a two-phase mode: the z-free generators (every block except the last) are closed to
  fixpoint first, then the last block's generators are inserted and the closure continues;
- `my_project`: the closure intersected with the span of monomials on a variable subset
  (a re-echelon with those monomials ordered lowest).

The degree-D closure is canonical (the smallest space containing the generators that is
closed under multiplication by a variable for every element of degree < D), so the
two-phase closure equals the one-phase closure; both are run and agree on every verdict.

Windowed closure (`worker4.py`, kind `win`): window a = blocks a and a+1, with all degree <= 4
monomials in the variables of those blocks; its generators are the two blocks' equations plus
the messages received; the message to a neighbour is the window's closure intersected with the
monomials on the shared block. Schedule: forward over the windows, then backward, repeated
until no message grows or 1 appears.

Soundness checks: every SAT instance must not be refuted (it never was); every closure row
and every message polynomial is evaluated at up to 16 known solutions (0 non-vanishing).

## Results (`aggregate.md`)

### 1. Locality at n = 131 (derivation)

`loc_cost.py` counts locality-restricted monomials exactly; it reproduces all six of pilot
#3's column counts. At each m's budget-optimal l (wwit model, D = 4, omega = 2):

| m | budget (bit-ops) | log2 cols full / L2 / largest window | margin full | margin L2 global | margin windowed, one pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 49.2 | 31.6 / 31.0 / 30.7 | -14.0 | -12.8 | -12.4 |
| 8 | 58.6 | 34.9 / 32.2 / 30.4 | -11.2 | -5.9 | -4.2 |
| 10 | 61.6 | 36.3 / 32.7 / 30.3 | -11.0 | -3.8 | -1.6 |
| 12 | 63.6 | 37.5 / 33.0 / 30.3 | -11.4 | -2.5 | +0.1 |
| 16 | 66.0 | 39.3 / 33.5 / 30.2 | -12.6 | -1.1 | +2.0 |

Locality is worth 5 to 12 bits over the full chained closure at m >= 8, and the windowed
form 2 to 3 more. It still needs omega = 2 (at omega = 2.37 every row is 9 bits or more
short) and m >= 12, and it assumes locality 2 and degree 4 keep refuting at those m,
which result 5 below contradicts at m = 6.

### 2. u-degree caps: the requirement rises with m

| m, n, k | smallest u-bit cap that refutes | columns there / full W_4 |
|---|---|---|
| 3, 9, 3 | <= 2 | 3,082 / 4,048 |
| 3, 11, 4 | <= 2 | 8,428 / 10,903 |
| 4, 7, 2 | 2 (with L2) | 3,450 / 9,109 |
| 4, 9, 3 | 3 (with L2; L2u2 refutes 0/10) | 23,198 / 31,931 |
| 5, 9, 3 | 4 = no cap (L2u3 refutes 0/5) | 65,649 / 124,314 |

A u-bit cap gives nothing on top of locality from m = 5 on. At m = 5 the refutation already
uses monomials of full degree 4 in the u-bits.

### 3. Windowed closure refutes everything that L2 refutes, in the last window

| cell | UNSAT refuted | SAT refuted | where 1 appeared | largest window cols (full W_4) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| m=4 n=7 | 25/25 | 0/4 | last window, first forward pass | 6,196 (9,109) |
| m=4 n=9 | 10/10 | 0/4 | last window, first forward pass | 20,854 (31,931) |
| m=5 n=9 k=3 | 5/5 | 0/1 (1 censored at 1,800 s) | last window, first forward pass | 46,938 (124,314) |

Every refutation happened on the first forward pass, in the last window. The forward
messages come only from z-free blocks, so they are the same for every target: the
per-target work is one closure of the last window (blocks m-3 and m-2, 2n + 2k variables,
12,951 columns at n = 9, independent of m) seeded with a precomputed message. That is a
concrete, measured form of Semaev's "one block" cost heuristic, which `../wwit-ecc2k130/`
showed is the conjunct every time-only winner needs.

### 4. Precomputing the target-independent closure

Medians per target. Phase 1 (z-free closure) is paid once per (curve, V).

| cell | UNSAT products, scratch / per target | UNSAT seconds, scratch / per target | SAT seconds, scratch / per target |
|---|---|---|---|
| m=3 n=9 | 8,925 / 170 | 0.023 / 0.000 | 0.17 / 0.06 |
| m=3 n=11 | 28,008 / 2,903 | 0.77 / 0.17 | 4.4 / 5.0 |
| m=4 n=7 | 18,456 / 109 | 0.053 / 0.001 | 1.2 / 0.18 |
| m=4 n=9 | 77,427 / 3,886 | 6.6 / 2.2 | 76 / 88 |

The product count falls 10 to 170 times, the UNSAT wall time 3 to 50 times, and the SAT wall
time not at all at the larger cells (phase 2 reduces against an almost full-rank basis, which
costs more per product). At m = 3 the gain shrinks from n = 9 to n = 11.

### 5. Pre-registered: m = 6, locality 2 (`PREDICTIONS.md`, P-M6)

| cell | columns | UNSAT refuted [CP95] | SAT refuted | median wall, RSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| m=6 n=9 k=3, L2 | 105,040 | **1/5 [0.01, 0.72]** | 0/2 | 369 s, 1.06 GB |

Prediction: at most 1 of 5 (falsified at 3 or more). **Outcome: 1 of 5, consistent.** The
four non-refuted UNSAT closures reached their fixpoint at rank 79,393 to 85,978 of 105,040.
At m = 5 the same restriction refuted 5/5 (pilot #3). This does not show that full W_4
fails at m = 6 (342,541 columns, beyond this host); it shows that locality 2 stops sufficing
between m = 5 and m = 6 at |V| = 8.

## Reading (derived; not a decision)

1. **The regime variable every closure pilot shares.** In all toy cells the z-free part of
   the system has about |V|^m solutions, and that number is comparable to the closure's
   column count: log2(columns / z-free solutions) is +5.3 (m=4 n=7), +3.2 (m=3 n=9),
   +2.0 (m=5 n=9 k=3), 0.0 (m=3 n=15 k=5) and -2.1 (m=3 n=17 k=6) (`zfree_fast.py`).
   Phase 1 of the precomputation shows what this means: the z-free closure's codimension is
   close to that solution count (231 of 236 at m=4 n=7; 412 of 456 at m=3 n=9), so at toy
   sizes the closure can nearly interpolate the solution set of the target-independent part.
   At n = 131 the same ratio is about 2^33 columns against about 2^(mk) solutions, i.e.
   below -100 bits. Every closure result so far, including EXP-SEMBIN-d9767f's cells, sits
   in the first regime and is read toward the second. It is the factor that is missing from
   the variable ledgers.
2. **It is not a sufficient explanation**, and at m = 3 not a necessary one: W_3 fails at
   m = 3 and 4 with ample room, and pilot #1 refuted at n = 15, k = 5 (ratio 0.0) and in an
   unrecorded single probe at n = 17, k = 6 (ratio -2.1). At m >= 4 no refutation so far used
   fewer columns than z-free solutions, and the one cell that crossed below, L1 at m = 5
   (17,061 columns against 30,688), refuted 0/10.
3. **The windowed and precomputed forms inherit that question.** Their per-target cost is
   small exactly because the precomputed closure or message captures the z-free solution
   set. Whether a degree-4 message still carries the needed information when that set is
   astronomically larger than the window is the open question, and the m = 6 L2 failure is
   the first data point on the wrong side of it.

## Limitations

- Toy sizes, one curve, polynomial basis V only; 5 to 25 UNSAT instances per cell; m = 6
  has 5 UNSAT and 2 SAT. Everything except P-M6 is post hoc.
- The windowed SAT run at m = 5 was censored at 1,800 s on one of two instances; per-window
  seconds were not recorded (an instrument slip: the single-phase timer field was read), so
  only the outer wall time of window runs is valid.
- Products and wall times were measured on a shared host; the precomputation's per-target
  saving in wall time (3x at m=4 n=9 UNSAT, none on SAT) is the figure to quote, not the
  product ratio.
- At the time of this commit lane A was still running the windowed m=4 n=11 cell and the
  two-phase m=5 L2 cell; they are absent from `results/`, not censored.
