## Exact statement and proof

`NCAlgebra B` is the free associative `ℤ`-algebra on translation letters
`tᵢ` and negation letter `v`. `commutatorIdeal B` is the **two-sided** ideal
spanned by *all* `xy − yx`. The codomain of `evalRho B n s` is the group
algebra over the subgroup of permutations of `ZMod n` generated by **all**
translations and negation. Its generators map as specified in
[`evalRho_trans` and `evalRho_neg`](../../../formal/CryptoResearch/NCP/CommutatorIdeal.lean).
Lean proves the following for **every** factor-base size `B`, modulus `n`,
and translation list `s : Fin B → ZMod n`:

```text
TwoSidedIdeal.ker (evalRho B n s) ≠ commutatorIdeal B.
```

The explicit witness is `w = v² − 1`. Negation squared is the identity, so
`w ∈ ker(evalRho)`. The algebra homomorphism `augmentation` sends every free
generator to zero. It kills all commutators and thus the entire ideal, while
it sends `w` to `−1`. Therefore `w ∉ commutatorIdeal B`. [The diagram](diagram.svg)
shows both maps. This augmentation argument proves that `w` survives the
commutative quotient without constructing a polynomial-ring isomorphism.

The target describes `rho` as onto the affine group algebra. The Lean
theorem proves kernel inequality for **any** `s`, so it applies to each
surjective instance. It does **not** prove that arbitrary chosen `sᵢ`
generate every translation in the codomain. This distinction is part of the
remaining independent semantic review. The additional witness
`(v · tᵢ)² − 1` mentioned in the source is not formalized; `v² − 1` already
proves the named proposition.

presentation of `ker(evalRho)`, prove surjectivity of every generator list,
verify the NC search algorithm or elliptic-curve implementation, or establish

official claim promotion. The hosted Lean verification workflow is currently
disabled; the local source-bound verifier receipt is the available machine
