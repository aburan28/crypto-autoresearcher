**Status:** scoped validation finding. The frozen measurement, formal target,
and existing Lean trace theorem are unchanged. No cryptanalytic timing,

## Archive reproduction and source-code defect

The original `ffd_semaev_measure.py` reproduced the committed
[`ffd_semaev_results.json`](../../../research/verification/ffd_semaev_results.json)
**byte for byte** (SHA-256 `64f6e34ceb16bd817c61c5974d2295c60197cf2b49f299663c3bc054333c99e7`).
Its eight cells cover `n=4..7`, `n'=3,4`, ten Semaev seeds and 25
support-matched null seeds per cell. The original archived counts are 70
Semaev draws at degree two and ten at degree three. The exact command, working
directory, output hash and stdout are in the [archive receipt](evidence/archive-replay.json)
and [log](evidence/archive-replay.log).

The source's `fall_profile` constructs a monomial multiple with a **set of
OR-combined masks**: `{mm | m for mm in f}`. In the Boolean polynomial ring
over F₂, repeated resulting monomials must cancel. Set construction keeps one
copy instead. This is a source-code error in the archived rank observable,
independent of the separate, correctly parity-aware equation builder.

A concrete witness is the first component equation of `n=4,n'=4,s=1`
multiplied by monomial mask `1` (`u₀`). Masks `1`, `17`, and `129` appear in
the archived product but cancel in the F₂ product. Across the complete 256
assignment truth table, the archived product disagrees with evaluation of
`f·u₀` on **64 assignments**; the corrected parity product agrees on all 256.
The [checker](../../../research/verification/replay_ffd_semaev_20261022.py)
asserts these exact facts and retains the witness in the [per-draw
record](evidence/draws.json).

## Trace witness and finite counterexample

The historical observable is
`new_falls(D) = dim(V_D ∩ R_{≤D−1}) − dim(V_{D−1})`, with `V_D` generated by
degree-compatible Boolean monomial multiples. The checker computes the
intersection dimension as `rank(full rows) − rank(degree-D projection)` using
an independent binary elimination routine. With Boolean F₂ multiplication,
68 of 80 draws first fall at degree two and 12 at degree three. Seven of those
degree-three draws have nonzero `x_R`, so the premise of the existing Lean
trace theorem is insufficient for a universal first-fall conclusion.

The smallest nonzero-target degree-three example is `n=4,n'=4,s=1`: seed
`32201`, `x_R=1`, basis `[9,3,2,12]` in the field with modulus `0b10011`.
Its trace multiplier is `μ=x_R⁻²=1`. The trace residual is the nonzero linear
polynomial `u₀+u₃+w₀+w₃`, already the fourth input equation. In both archived
and corrected degree-two calculations, `dim V₁=1` and
`dim(V₂∩R_{≤1})=1`; thus `new_falls(2)=0`. Under correct Boolean parity,
`dim V₂=11`, `dim V₃=53`, and `dim(V₃∩R_{≤2})=12`; therefore
`new_falls(3)=1` and first fall is degree three. The archived set rule gave
`dim V₃=59` and `new_falls(3)=7`, a different numeric result but the same
grade. The exact four component equations are in the record. The new
[Lean finite certificate](../../../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParityFiniteCertificate.lean)
checks these parity-row ranks and the three canceled monomial masks by kernel
reduction under the pinned Lean 4.35.0-rc1 toolchain. The [Lean
receipt](evidence/lean-certificate.json) binds the source hash and
[axiom output](evidence/lean-certificate.log); the three audited theorems use
only `propext`. A separate [literal check](../../../research/verification/check_ffd_semaev_lean_literal_20261022.py)
confirmed that all four Lean equation lists equal the exact source-bound
per-draw record; its [log](evidence/lean-literal-check.log) records the hashes.
The additive [formal target](../../../formal/targets/semaev-s3-boolean-parity-finite-20261022.yaml)
passed the repository's [target-spec validator](evidence/target-validation.log).
An additional [finite equation-semantics theorem](../../../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/FiniteEquationSemantics.lean)
evaluates the direct `S₃=e₂²+e₃+1` expression in
`F₂[X]/(X⁴+X+1)` and the four transcribed Boolean components on **all 256
assignments**. It proves their values coincide for the exact `x_R=1`, basis
`[9,3,2,12]` sample. Its separate
[target](../../../formal/targets/semaev-s3-finite-equation-semantics-20261022.yaml)
and [local verifier receipt](evidence/finite-equation-target-verification.json)
are machine verified; the [proof receipt](evidence/finite-equation-proof.json)
binds source hashes, the 2,013-job workspace build, 477-theorem axiom audit,
and exact theorem axiom output. This finite extensional bridge does not prove
a generic symbolic `S₃` descent or the row-rank semantics.
The [Lean evaluator](evidence/finite-equation-lean-eval.lean) exported its
16×16 field product table and all 256 direct `S₃` values. A separate
[Python checker](../../../research/verification/check_semaev_finite_lean_semantics_20261022.py)
compared them with the independently implemented field reference; all 512
values matched ([log](evidence/finite-equation-cross-check.log)).
The [semantic rank certificate](../../../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParitySemanticRankCertificate.lean)
then checks this instance's degree-one and degree-two Boolean rows against
explicit F₂ matrices. Lean verifies invertible row transformations and
echelon pivots, and Mathlib proves the **actual row-space dimensions** are
`1` for degree one, `11` for degree two, and `10` for the degree-two
projection. Its `semanticNoFallTwo` theorem therefore proves the rank
difference `11−10−1=0`. A companion theorem proves that the earlier
executable `rankOn` values equal these three matrix ranks for this sample.
The [generator](../../../research/verification/generate_semaev_semantic_rank_certificate_20261022.py)
regenerates the Lean source byte for byte from the frozen equation record;
the [proof receipt](evidence/semantic-rank-proof.json) binds every input and
result. This is a finite degree-two bridge, not generic `rankOn` correctness.
The separate [degree-three rank certificate](../../../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParityDegreeThreeRankCertificate.lean)
checks the same frozen system's full 80-row degree-three matrix and its
degree-three projection. A generic Lean lemma identifies XOR of packed
Boolean rows with addition in F₂; Lean then checks explicit elimination
transforms, inverses, and echelon pivots. Mathlib gives actual row-space
dimensions **53** and **41**. With the proved degree-two rank 11, its
`semanticOneNewFallThree` theorem establishes the rank increment
`53−41−11=1` for this sample. The [generator](../../../research/verification/generate_semaev_degree_three_rank_certificate_20261022.py)
rebuilds the Lean source from the archived equations, and the
[target](../../../formal/targets/semaev-s3-semantic-rank-degree3-20261022.yaml)
states this finite result. The [filtration certificate](../../../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParityFiltrationCertificate.lean)
then proves the missing rank-nullity link for this exact sample. A generic
row-space theorem identifies the kernel of a linear projection with the
full-rank minus projected-rank dimension. Lean checks that these 80 rows
have no support above degree three, so the degree-three projection kernel
is precisely `V₃ ∩ R≤2`, of dimension **12**. It also checks `V₂ ≤ V₃ ∩ R≤2`
and `dim V₂=11`. Its `existsNewLowDegreeVector` theorem proves that a vector
in the intersection lies outside `V₂`; this is an existential witness to a
new low-degree class. The [target](../../../formal/targets/semaev-s3-finite-filtration-20261022.yaml)
and [proof receipt](evidence/filtration-proof.json) bind this finite theorem.
Generic `rankOn` correctness and a symbolic descent remain open.

Among 75 nonzero-target draws, the trace residual is a new class modulo
`V₁` in 67 corrected degree-two draws. One other degree-two draw has an old
trace class but another new relation. Six corrected degree-three draws have a
nonzero trace residual already in `V₁`; one has a zero trace relation. Five
zero-target degree-three draws remain outside the Lean theorem premise. These
are finite results for the stated toy Boolean model, not solving-degree or
ECDLP-cost results.

## Proof and performance boundary

[`DegreeTwoFall.lean`](../../../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/DegreeTwoFall.lean)
proves field and trace cancellation under `x_R≠0`; its scope comment says a
general nonzero quotient class is unproved. The new finite Lean file proves exact
values of an executable Boolean row-elimination function for one transcribed
equation system. The additional finite equation-semantics theorem proves
that the four literal polynomials agree with direct `S₃` evaluation on all
256 assignments of this instance. It does **not** prove that `rankOn` equals
vector-space dimension in general or derive a generic symbolic descent from
abstract `S₃`. The semantic-rank certificates now prove that the executable
ranks agree with actual F₂ row-space dimensions at degrees one through three
and the degree-two and degree-three projections for this instance. The new
filtration certificate formalizes the intersection/rank-nullity identity,
inclusion of `V₂`, and existence of a new low-degree class for this one sample.
These links require independent review. The
[frozen target](../../../formal/targets/semaev-s3-degree2-fall-witness.yaml)
and [historical note](../../../research/FFD_SEMAEV_MEASUREMENT1.md) use broader
wording. The corrected finite example contradicts a **uniform degree-two
first-fall interpretation in this Boolean model**. It does not invalidate the
narrower Lean identity or establish a general statement about solving degree,
other field sizes, or attack cost. A generic rank-semantics theorem and
reviewed additive correction remain open in the [problem
record](../../../problems/semaev-degree-two-fall-nonzero-bridge.md).

No numerical performance dashboard changed because this replay measured no
time or attack cost. The new chart corrects a first-fall classification, not
an end-to-end performance metric. The new semantic rank proof changes no
per-draw grade, so the existing corrected chart and renderer remain current.
A committed-tree search of `ui/` for
`semaev`, `first.fall`, and `ffd_semaev` found no dashboard consumer of these
toy counts (`git grep -ni -E 'semaev|first.fall|ffd_semaev' HEAD -- ui`).

## Reproduction and limits

Run Python 3.12 from a scratch directory for the original script to avoid
rewriting the committed archive. Then run the corrected checker and chart
renderer from the repository root:

```sh
python3.12 /path/to/repo/research/verification/ffd_semaev_measure.py
python3.12 research/verification/replay_ffd_semaev_20261022.py \
  --output docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/draws.json
python3.12 docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/render.py
python3.12 research/verification/generate_semaev_semantic_rank_certificate_20261022.py \
  --output /tmp/BooleanParitySemanticRankCertificate.lean
cmp /tmp/BooleanParitySemanticRankCertificate.lean \
  formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParitySemanticRankCertificate.lean
python3.12 research/verification/generate_semaev_degree_three_rank_certificate_20261022.py \
  --output /tmp/BooleanParityDegreeThreeRankCertificate.lean
cmp /tmp/BooleanParityDegreeThreeRankCertificate.lean \
  formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParityDegreeThreeRankCertificate.lean
# From the repository root, with the pinned Lean toolchain installed:
cd formal && lean CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParityFiniteCertificate.lean
```

Source hashes, exact working directories and invocation arguments are bound in
the evidence receipts. The checker reuses the original sampler and
Semaev equation builder, but checks their field arithmetic and direct S₃ truth
tables independently. Its corrected Boolean multiplier uses XOR cancellation
and independently computed ranks. This is stronger than replaying the archive
alone and weaker than an independent Sage implementation or a Lean proof of
the whole filtration. The Lean equation, matrix and filtration certificates cover one exact
instance, with generic rank semantics still open. The [PDF](report.pdf) embeds the chart and the same
claim boundary.
