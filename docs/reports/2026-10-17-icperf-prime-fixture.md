## Why the order certificate is independent of point counting

For each saved curve, the plain-Python verifier checks primality of `p` and `N`, nonsingularity, canonical affine coordinates, the curve equation, `N P = O` for the nonidentity saved point `P`, the exact Hasse inequality `(N-(p+1))² <= 4p`, and `N² > 16p`. Since `N` is prime and `P` is nonidentity, `P` has order `N`; therefore `N` divides `#E(F_p)`. The Hasse interval is narrower than `N` and contains `N`, so `#E(F_p)=N`. The verifier does not call Sage cardinality. A separate Sage `Integer(...).is_prime(proof=True)` cross-check returned true for both `p` and `N` on all three records. `python3 tools/icperf_prime_fixture.py verify <record>` passed for each; four unit tests covered a known 83-point curve and tampered order, point, Hasse and coordinate cases.
