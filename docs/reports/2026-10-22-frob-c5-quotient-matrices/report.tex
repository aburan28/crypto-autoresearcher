\textbf{Certificate construction.} A standard-library Python generator reads
the committed matrix replay receipt only after checking its SHA-256 and the
replay checker's source hash. It independently divides each $X^{qj}$ by
$g_q$ for $j=1,\ldots,4$, and requires the remainder coefficients to match
the recorded matrix column. Lean checks all 12 explicit identities
$X^{qj}=R_{qj}+g_qQ_{qj}$, the remainder degree bounds, and the constant
column. Mathlib's \texttt{AdjoinRoot} power-basis representation then turns
these into 75 exact matrix-entry equalities. The generator's \texttt{--check}
compares generated Lean bytes; CRLF and receipt-hash mutations are rejected.
An altered q11 matrix entry is also rejected after the test receipt is rehashed.
