## Frozen input construction

The [emitter](../../experiments/EXP-ICPERF-018b0c/implementation/run.py) derives scalar candidates from `SHA256(b'ICPERF-k11-natural-v1\0' || fixture_sha256_bytes || uint32_be(counter))`, then admits the first eight distinct subgroup points whose four cofactor preimages are affine and avoid the torsion-coordinate pole. The [target manifest](../../experiments/EXP-ICPERF-018b0c/inputs/target_manifest.json), SHA-256 `973655e9329f060364d77511b490833066193e1f22a910fbdd65fa59d706d285`, records eight accepted candidates at counters 0–7, their public known-answer scalars, all 32 raw channel points, and the exact rejection rule. Both factor bases and their selection scans predate these targets. Each set of four raw channels represents **one** subgroup target.

For every target, the builder produced four seven-link `Pφ,3` chained XCNF files for each of the two preselected 316-column k11 bases. The [formula manifest](../../experiments/EXP-ICPERF-018b0c/inputs/formula_manifest.json), SHA-256 `82b0129ad0e9879c629414d24966161246435856b9d14cdbe1310ce477629368`, binds 64 compressed formulas, their uncompressed hashes, the base hashes, the input generator and the target manifest. The [positive-control manifest](../../experiments/EXP-ICPERF-018b0c/inputs/positive_manifest.json), SHA-256 `87095048bb6717eb3ec1d94acb34d9ee3cc9b0257a7432cf0e68d593a9bed068`, binds the two earlier *different-target* known-point formula/model pairs. Those positive targets test the instrument and cannot compare natural yield across bases.

## Independent checks and limits

The serialized-input [checker](../../experiments/EXP-ICPERF-018b0c/implementation/check.py) replayed every target scalar and channel, every formula's CNF/XOR gate structure and exact membership truth table, one seeded target-constant gate evaluation per formula, both complete signed-DIMACS positive models, their full-point certificates, and first-bit rejection controls. Its [write-once replay record](../../experiments/EXP-ICPERF-018b0c/inputs/independent_replay.json), SHA-256 `a929bfd286e920692dafe1d05a2ad3ba60f87cacac981f9063d39b43171a3171`, reports 8 targets, 2 positives and 64 natural formulas checked. A separate [Sage checker](../../experiments/EXP-ICPERF-018b0c/implementation/check_sage.py) independently rebuilt the `GF(2^61)` curve and verified all eight subgroup points and 32 raw channels, including cofactor projection and torsion-coordinate values; its [record](../../experiments/EXP-ICPERF-018b0c/inputs/sage_replay.json) has SHA-256 `2c5cd62fe9ecd9478b67e296b9a0d02b8a804d137b8ea020716b47690b990fc1`. Sage's first two invocations failed before import because its managed cache path was outside the default sandbox; the approved unchanged invocation passed. The checker metadata mismatch and Sage access failures are preserved in [preparation_failures.md](../../experiments/EXP-ICPERF-018b0c/inputs/preparation_failures.md).
