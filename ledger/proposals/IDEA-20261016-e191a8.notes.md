# Notes for idea-1 (carry-fall locality, shift covariance, exact null)

All derivations are by hand in this session; nothing here was executed. Stage A of the minimal test exists to check them.

## 1. Weight (carry) calculus, p = 2

A monomial x^a in a slot x = v + L(Y) (L F_2-linear in the k bits Y, or n bits for a free slot) descends, mod field equations Y_j^2 = Y_j, to a polynomial of degree <= wt(a) (binary Hamming weight): x^{2^i} = v^{2^i} + L^{(2^i)}(Y) is affine, and x^a = prod over set bits of a. Lucas: (x+v)^a = sum_{b subset of a} x^b v^{a-b}, so replacing x by x+v adds only monomials of weight <= wt(a). Coefficients change, weights do not.

S_3(u,x,u') = u^2x^2 + u^2u'^2 + x^2u'^2 + u x u' + B (char 2). Times u:
u^3x^2 (wt 2+1), u^3u'^2 (2+1), u x^2u'^2 (1+1+1), u^2 x u' (1+1+1), B u (1). Max 3 for every v. Naive degree of the product is deg u + deg S_3 = 1 + 3. So the n polynomials [u S_3^v]_j, j = 1..n, are Sum_i [alpha_i u]_j [S_3^v]_i with each term of degree 4 and the sum of degree <= 3. This is Semaev's x1*S_3 identity (lines 570-597 of inputs/SEMAEV-2015-310/paper_fulltext.md) with the shift carried along. The same holds with x or u' as the multiplier.

Last block S_3(U,X,R) with R constant has degree 2 and the multiplier argument does not apply (Semaev lines 591-598: bound 5 from [11], experiments give 4). Hence m = 2 (a single block with a constant slot) is the contrast cell: no carry fall predicted there, and a matched pair at m = 2 is not predicted invariant. At m >= 3 there are m-2 blocks with three variable slots.

A fall of a subsystem is a fall of the system (HKYY Prop 2.3(ii): F subset G gives V_{F,i} subset V_{G,i}); non-vanishing of u*S_3^v as a function on the domain is immediate. "New fall" in the HPS/HKYY sense (V_{F,d} intersect R_{<=d-1} != V_{F,d-1}) additionally needs [uS]_j outside the degree-<=3 Macaulay span: that is the matrix obligation (H1).

## 2. Saturation by the trace carrier

KR-IC-f5c584 (Kosters-Yeo): a group morphism E -> F_2 gives a linear form after descent; d_ff(S_3 descent) = 2. The morphism is F_2-linear in the bits, so under x -> v + L(Y) the form becomes affine. Either carrier keeps a d'_F <= 4 statement true for every v. A matched-pair d'_F test therefore cannot distinguish "Prop. 5 true because of a robust mechanism" from "Prop. 5 true and v is irrelevant"; it can only catch an implementation error. Informative observables sit above both carriers: see idea-3 (residual excess) and idea-2 (last fall).

## 3. Exact null (T1) and the flawed null in IDEA-20260913-352163

If v_i in V, X_i = L(Y_i + c_i) with c_i the coordinate vector of v_i in the basis of V. Substituting Y' = Y + c is a bijection of Boolean variables that preserves total degree and the field equations (c^2 = c), so EQS4(Y) = EQS2(Y'). HKYY Prop 2.3(iv) (A V_{F,i} = V_{AF,i} for A in Aff_m(k)) and Prop 2.6(v) (d_{AF} = d_F) make this a theorem for the last fall degree and for V_{F,i}, hence for every Macaulay rank at degree <= i. So EQS4 with v_i in V reproduces EQS2 exactly at every degree.

352163's minimal_test control says "all v_i equal, so EQS4 degenerates to EQS2 up to a global shift". For a common v not in V, x_i -> x_i + v for all i is NOT a symmetry of S_3 (the quadratic terms pick up v^2 and the cubic term gains v-dependent quadratics), and the slot set V+v is not V. The control is exact only for v in V. Recommend the exact null v_i = 0 mod V and treat "all v_i equal, not in V" as an ordinary shifted data point.

352163 P2 ("if invariance fails it fails upward") is not a theorem: the lift of a top-form syzygy is a nonzero lower-degree polynomial or a genuine syzygy depending on lower terms, and either can flip under a shift. See idea-3.

## 4. Odd p (for IDEA-20261006-486aa2)

For p odd, x^a descends to degree <= s_p(a) (base-p digit sum) because x^p is affine in the F_p bits. S_3 for y^2 = x^3 + A4 x + A6 has six top monomials of exponent patterns (2,0,2), (1,1,2), (0,2,2), (2,1,1), (1,2,1), (2,2,0), digit-sum degree 4. A multiplier x1^a x2^b x3^c drops the degree of a top monomial when some variable i has e_i >= 1 and e_i + a_i >= p (a carry). A fall at D = deg m + 4 needs every top monomial to carry in some variable (lower monomials of degree <= 3 stay below D).
- p = 3: a = (1,1,1) or (2,0,1) covers all six, deg m = 3, D = 7 (product degree 5 < 7). Nagao's bound 3p+1 = 10.
- p = 5: a = (4,0,3) or (4,3,0) covers all six, deg m = 7, D = 11. Nagao's bound 16.
These are upper bounds on the carry-mechanism first fall (other multipliers or the trace form may give lower), hand-derived, to be certified by Stage A. Implication: IDEA-20261006-486aa2 should score the control against "<= 7 (p=3), <= 11 (p=5)" and treat 3p+1 as a loose upper bound. Nagao's Lemma 6 is labelled heuristic by its author (footnote 4) and uses the monomial multiplier prod X_i^{p-1}.
