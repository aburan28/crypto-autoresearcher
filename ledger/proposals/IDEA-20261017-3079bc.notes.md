## Recount algorithm
W = rational points with x in V (both lifts). T1 = all floor(t/2)-multisets of W with their
sums (hash by x and y or by canonical affine key); T2 likewise for ceil(t/2). Given R:
for each A in T1, look up R-A and -R-A in T2 (sorted array + searchsorted). Dedupe the
resulting t-multisets by sorted tuple of point indices; map to x-multisets by dropping signs
and quotient by global negation; the sampled multiset itself is excluded. Cost |T1| lookups.
Sizes: (4,8): T=33k | (6,7): 358k | (8,6): 766k | (10,6): 1.0e7 | (12,5): 2.3e6 (all |W|~|V|).
Sampling: draw t entries uniformly from W (with replacement), random signs implicit
in W; R=sum; reject R=O and R with x in V (excluded from the target population).

## Null bound
Under Poisson the number of others is Poisson(lambda_class), lambda_class=d*C(|W|+t-1,t)/N
~ d*2^-g/t!. Bound for 2e4 targets per cell: expected events 2e4*lambda_class <= 0.2 at
(4,8) g=12 (lambda ~1.0e-5), far below at larger t. Flag a cell if events > mean+4 sqrt(mean)+3.

## Positive control
Base from V and b/V (b=sqrt B): translation by the rational 2-torsion T maps P to P+T,
x -> b/x; flipping any even subset of entries leaves the sum unchanged; so every sampled
multiset with distinct x_i and b/x_i not in the multiset has exactly 2^{t-1}-1 siblings.
Requires b/V to be given as an explicit list (no subspace needed for the recount).
A second control that costs nothing extra: Koblitz curve B=1 with V = ker g(tau)
(IDEA-20260915-8fe0ef lane): Frobenius orbits of size n give nu = n-1.
