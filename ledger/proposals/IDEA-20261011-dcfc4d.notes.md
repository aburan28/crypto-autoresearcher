## Cost identity

With exact engines, attempts per relation do not depend on the engine, so

total cost per relation = (C_build + C_engine + C_lift + C_verify) / (y x c)

with y the per-attempt relation yield and c the engine's completion fraction
(timeouts and unsupported-memory preflights count as non-completion; a setup
failure hits every engine on that target). Solver-stage median seconds report
C_engine alone. They can reorder the engines only if C_engine dominates; they
omit c entirely. The record tests whether that holds on the frozen inputs, and
the result may well be that the solver-stage ranking stands (the falsification
condition says so).

Per-relation cost is not defined when y = 0: the reported natural targets gave 0
relations (8 natural targets, 4 per seed, in the fixed-phase table and again in
the exhaustive 83-phase meet-in-the-middle), so natural-target cost can only be per attempt with a rule-of-three upper bound on
yield (3/8 = 0.375 per target at 95%). Finite per-relation costs come from the
planted controls, which are constructed and therefore not representative of
natural yield. The record says so rather than extrapolate.
