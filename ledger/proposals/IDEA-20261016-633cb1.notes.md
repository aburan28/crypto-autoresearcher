## Circularity remark (why a live-vector lift family is not an attack)
For an F_p-live vector (a,b) (aP^+bQ^=O^) and Bezout (u,v), H^=vP^-uQ^ lifts to a rational H on a curve through H^, and (P,Q)=(bH,-aH) reduces to the
prescribed pair with rational (non-integral) s,t. But a live vector is the discrete log of the pair; a lift family parametrised by it contains the answer.
Dependence inside a box fixed independently of (a,b) is the non-circular question.
