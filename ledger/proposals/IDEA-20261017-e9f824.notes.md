## Why mixture vs cluster is a first-order question
compound Poisson: c = sum of Poisson(rho) clusters of mean size kappa, mu=kappa*rho,
P = 1-e^{-mu/kappa} ~ mu/kappa: first-order loss, f_disp -> 1/kappa as mu->0.
mixture: P ~ mu - E[mu_class^2]/2: f_disp -> 1.
