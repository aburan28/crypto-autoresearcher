## Rank lemma for the constant-multiplier trace certificate (the lambda = r^{-2} witness)
With constant multiplier, lambda corresponds to a field element nu and lambda T_i is the i-th dual
coordinate of Theta_nu(u,x) = nu^{1/2} (u+x) + nu u x. A dependence among the n coordinates means
Tr(kappa Theta_nu) = 0 as a Boolean polynomial. Its bilinear part Tr(kappa nu u x) vanishes for all
u in F, x in V iff kappa nu x = 0 for all x in V, so kappa = 0. Rank n, hence Omega = a single target.
This certificate is the degree-1 deficit of IDEA-20260916-71ab94 / EV-CERTBIN-6c3e0a "linear
consequence", not the degree-4 refutation; the multi-multiplier case is NOT covered and E-point is a
prediction, not a theorem.

## Planted positive
