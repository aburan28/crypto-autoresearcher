# Notes for idea-3 (layer theorem, residual fall, census)

## 1. Layer theorem

Work in the Boolean ring B = F_2[Y]/(Y_j^2 - Y_j) with the degree filtration; its associated graded gr B = F_2[Y]/(Y_j^2). Fix field basis, V, R. Descend each S_3 block (Nagao Def. 3-4, 8).
- Cubic part of a block with three variable slots comes only from the monomial x x' u (the other monomials of S_3 are Frobenius squares, weight <= 2): T_j(Y,Y',U) = sum_{a,b,c} Y_a Y'_b U_c [alpha_a alpha_b alpha_c]_j, the structure tensor. No v, no B, no A.
- v enters through x -> v + L(Y): v*L(Y')L(U) terms, degree 2, plus linear and constant terms. B is the constant vector of each block.
- The last block S_3(U,X,R) is quadratic; its top form is u x R (coefficient bits of R) plus the squares; R, not v, is in it.
Macaulay rows are m*f, m a monomial. Order columns by degree descending and eliminate. A row's degree-D part is top(m)*top(f) computed in gr B (v-, B-independent), possibly zero. The pivots in degree-D columns are the rank of the matrix of these degree-D parts: pi_D(D). Rows whose degree-D part is zero, and combinations that cancel it, fall to lower layers, where v and B enter. Total rank = sum_e pi_D(e). The kernel of the top block (top-form syzygies: Koszul-type plus the carry syzygies) has v-independent dimension; its lifts are the new polynomials, counted by the lower pivots.
For v in V the substitution Y -> Y + c is a filtered automorphism, so every pi_D(e) is preserved (HKYY Prop 2.3(iv)).

## 2. Why the lower layers can move either way

A top-form syzygy lifts to a combination of degree < D that is either independent of the lower Macaulay span (a new fall) or inside it (no new fall; the lift is a genuine syzygy modulo lower rows). Which happens depends on the lower terms, i.e. on v and B. Rank is lower semicontinuous in parameters (generic >= special), but that orders a generic point of the family over F_2(v-bits) against a specialization, not two F_2-points, so no direction is guaranteed.

## 3. Residual definition

N_local(D) = dimension of the span of the local carry and trace consequences times monomials up to degree D, built from idea-1's ablation arms. Excess_D(v) = rank M_D(v) - pi_D(D) - N_local(D). If N_local cannot be computed reliably, report the raw lower-layer count rank M_D(v) - pi_D(D) and the ablated arms side by side.

## 4. Census sizes

Classes: v mod V gives (2^{n-k})^m. (n,k,m) = (7,2,3): 32,768, N = km + n(m-2) = 13 variables, Boolean columns sum_{j<=5} C(13,j) = 2,380: each rank is cheap. (8,2,3): 262,144 classes, N = 14. Disjoint classes (all v_i distinct mod V) are the Nagao-relevant stratum; the rest are labelled. Satisfiability is decided by enumerating the 2^{km} tuples against R.

## 5. Unsatisfiable instances

At small n with yield near 1 some (R, v) are unsatisfiable. Then 1 is in the ideal and literal Def. 6 gives d'_F = 1 (EV-SEMBIN-c71cad, DA-8). The same R can be satisfiable for v=0 and unsatisfiable for v != 0, so a naive pair comparison confounds satisfiability with shift. Stratify, or pick R satisfiable in both arms.
