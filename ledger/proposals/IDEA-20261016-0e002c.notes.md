## Gaussian two-norm identity
Curve equations for P=(s^2,sw), Q=(-t^2,tz): w^2=s^4+As^2+B, z^2=-t^4+At^2-B. Then
(2s^2+A)^2-(2w)^2 = 4s^4+4As^2+A^2-4s^4-4As^2-4B = D, and (2t^2-A)^2+(2z)^2 = 4t^4-4At^2+A^2+4(-t^4+At^2-B) = D.
So D=N^2-(2w)^2=M^2+(2z)^2 with N=2s^2+A, M=2t^2-A, N+M=2(s^2+t^2). D is a sum of two squares: primes 3 mod 4 occur to even power.
With D=q^e, e odd: q=2 or q=1 mod 4. N=(f_++f_-)/2, 2w=(f_+-f_-)/2.

## Slice equations
f_-=q^b=c^2 (b even, possibly 0), f_+=q^a. x(S)=X, x(S+T)=Y with X Y=B, X+Y=c^2-A, X-Y=2cs (IDEA-20261016-3fe347 G1).
D=A^2-4B=(c^2-X-Y)^2-4XY. Solve for Y: Y^2-2(c^2+X)Y+(c^2-X)^2=D gives Y=(c^2+X)+-sqrt(D+4c^2X).
B=+-r^i with B=XY: X=+-r^j, Y=+-r^k. s=(X-Y)/(2c) must be an integer; w=s^2-(X+Y)/2 (from s^2-w=(X+Y)/2).
Q on the curve: z^2=-t^4+At^2-B = c^2t^2-(t^2+X)(t^2+Y).
