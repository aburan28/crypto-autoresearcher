## 2. Counting

E(F_p) has N points. A uniformly random pair (x, y) in F_p^2 lies on E with
density ~1/p, so the box [0,B1) x (-B2, B2) holds ~ B1 * 2 B2 / p points, i.e.
~B1 B2 / p modulo +-. Matched cardinality B: B1 B2 = p B. For B = (6N)^{1/3}
~ 3.3e5 at pair 46 (p ~ 5.9e15): B1 B2 ~ 2e21; square side ~ 4.5e10 ~ p^{0.67}.

Bombieri-Weil: #{(x,y) in box: y^2 = f(x)} = B1 B2 / p + O(sqrt(p) log^2 p)
(quoted from a talk slide; unread at source). The main term exceeds the error
iff B1 B2 > p^{3/2} log^2 p. With B1 B2 = pB this is B > sqrt(p) log^2 p,
far above the operating point B ~ p^{1/3}. So the base size is a model
statement here. (Chang-Cilleruelo-Garaev-Hernandez-Shparlinski-Zumalacarregui,
arXiv:1111.1543, give upper bounds in small boxes; the abstract does not state
the range, and the body was not read.)

## 3. Enumeration price

x-scan: one sqrt-lift per candidate x in [0,B1); keep those with |y| < B2.
Lifts per kept element = B1 / (B1 B2 / p) = p / B2; for B2 = p/2 this is 2
(half the abscissae do not lift), the qr_window positive control divides it
back to 1 per scanned candidate. A thin window (B2 << p) pays p/B2.
A lattice enumeration of points of y^2 = f(x) mod p in a box would write
y^2 - f(x) = k p with k bounded by max(B2^2, B1^3)/p; for the operating point
k ranges up to ~p^{1}, so no linearisation; c_box between B (output size) and
B1 is the open number.

## 4. Information bookkeeping (arity m)

Unknowns: (x_i, y_i), i = 1..m, integers in [0,B1) x (-B2,B2). Equations
mod p: m curve equations and the group relation sum P_i = T (one equation on
the m-fold product after eliminating the sum; counted as 1 for the threshold).
Expected solutions (B1 B2)^m / p^{m+1}; with |F| = B1 B2 / p and |F|^m ~ p
this is 1 for every m. Same threshold phenomenon as H-ECDLP-b97b72 (2m
unknowns, one equation, product of bounds p).

## 5. Why a dispersion index

Under the random model the count in a translate of a fixed-shape box is
Poisson. Exponential-sum large coefficients (structure in the additive
characters of E(F_p) points) would show up as over-dispersion across
translates at shapes where Weil is silent. Planted dense sub-box: add a
known fraction of extra points in one box; dispersion must rise above 1.3,
confirming sensitivity. Family members (CM, maximal conductor) vs random
curves is the same contrast as in IDEA-20261015-cba035.
