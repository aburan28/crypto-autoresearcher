## Scope and what the tool is not

This is audit tooling, class `tooling`. The object-frame block of
docs/object-frame-ideation.md exists to stop "new arithmetic" proposals from
regressing to a known family; a transfer is an injective homomorphism on the
order-r subgroup, hence a change of coordinates by IDEA-20260806-c5d183 (a
deterministic projection propagating under the full translation action is
constant or injective), so the record does not offer a new tracked object. Its
value is audit throughput: one certificate and cost-table format across GHS,
isogeny, cover and pairing candidates, with the weakest unresolved obligation
kept visible on composed paths (transfer skill, step 9).

Verdict vocabulary (AGENTS.md): class-weak, weak representative exists,
source-transfer-weak, implementation-weak, no weakness found in scope. A bounded
miss is "not found within this family and boundary".
