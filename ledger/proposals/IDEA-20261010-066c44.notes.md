## 2. Why the m axis is not a repeat of the pilot

At m = 3 the only chain node is u. The descended S_3 = a^2 b^2 + a^2 c^2 + b^2 c^2 + abc + B gives monomials each containing at most one coordinate of u (squaring is F_2-linear), i.e. u enters affinely and blocks are bilinear: the pilot's mechanism reading. At m >= 4 the middle block S_3(u_{j-1}, x_{j+1}, u_j) contains u_{j-1}^2 u_j^2 and u_{j-1} x u_j, so two n-bit coordinate groups multiply. The support shape of the system changes qualitatively with m. Whether the sparse-support refutation survives is exactly what the support-matched null (same monomials, random coefficients) measures; the dense null (same per-degree counts, uniform monomials) says whether anything sparse is needed at all.

Why the enumerated-leg hybrid cannot stand in: IDEA-20260930-b26bbc / H-ECDLP-1f1111 enumerate m-2 legs by group arithmetic and decide a two-leg inner object (m = 2) by W_D. That costs 2^((m-2) l) per attempt before any closure, so alpha >= m-2, and m-2 > m/2-1 for every m > 2. Only a full-symbolic bounded-D refutation of the whole chain (no enumeration) can have alpha < m/2-1. This is the object measured here.

## 3. H1: D_W <= d_F4 on UNSAT (derivation sketch, unchecked)

W_D = least subspace of the multilinear ring B_{<=D} containing the generator multiples of degree <= D and closed under g -> v g for deg g <= D-1. Take an F4 run with a degree-compatible order and normal selection, Boolean field equations built in (or explicit, reducing to the same ring), in which every polynomial ever formed has degree <= d. Induction on steps: a reducer m*g used in symbolic preprocessing has lm(m g) = m lm(g) squarefree, so deg(m g) = deg m + deg g <= d; if g is in W_d then so is m g (multiply by the variables of m one at a time, intermediate degrees <= d). Hence every polynomial F4 forms is in W_d, and if F4 derives 1 at step degree d then 1 in W_d, i.e. D_W <= d_F4 on UNSAT. Consequence: a W_4 fixpoint without 1 on an enumeration-certified UNSAT instance certifies that no degree-4-bounded F4 run derives 1, so d_F4 >= 5 for that instance. The converse (d_F4 <= D_W + 1) is the heuristic H1: it needs completeness of degree-d pair processing, imitating Lazard's truncation theorem in the homogeneous case, and may fail for affine systems. Stage 2 (msolve/M2 step degrees) is the check; it is feasible only where F4 completes (n <= 11, m <= 4). msolve reading d_F4 = 4 at (17,3,3,7) is RUN-SEMBIN-b6eb9f (cited by the pilot README; not opened).
