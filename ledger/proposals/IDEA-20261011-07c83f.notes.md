## Transport inequality

phi is an F_q-isogeny of degree d = f_E1 / f_E2 coprime to r, so phi maps the
order-r subgroup isomorphically (KR-RHO-755e35; IDEA-20260807-5cabfc argues the
vertical closure at length). A solver run on the cheaper curve then transports
the answer at cost C_phi. A conductor-driven gain exists only if
C(E_hard) > C(E_easy) + C_phi. The paired test therefore reports C_phi beside
the log ratio. Without a cost gap larger than C_phi the difference, if any, is a
property of the equation, not a usable weakness; AGENTS.md: conductors are leads,
not ECDLP-bit discounts.
