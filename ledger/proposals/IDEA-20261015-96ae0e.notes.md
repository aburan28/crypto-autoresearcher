conductor-361 curve; Executor confirms model, j and End). The crater is
isomorphic over F_p to the reduction of E_d for d in exactly one class of

E_d(K) (x) Q = E_d(Q) (x) Q + E_{-19d}(Q) (x) Q has rank 2 r_d.

## 2. Box-DLP cost law

Let B_1..B_s be a Z-basis of E_d(K) mod torsion (s = 2 r_d) and
b_i = red(B_i) in G (public, free). red: Z^s -> G is a homomorphism.
Unknowns: ell_i = log_P b_i and k. A "lift of P in a box" is a in Z^s,
|a_i| <= A, with P = sum a_i b_i; V = (2A+1)^s.
- Finding it: generalised BSGS/kangaroo in the box, ~sqrt(V) group ops
  (split the coordinates in two halves; only group operations on G are used).
- Existence: a random target lies in the box image with probability V/N
  (if red is injective on the box, which needs V <= N).
- Each representation is one linear equation sum a_i ell_i = log P; with
  targets T_j = a_j P + b_j Q, s + 1 independent equations in
  (ell_1..ell_s, k) suffice.
Total expected cost: (s + 1) * sqrt(V) * (N / V) = (s + 1) N / sqrt(V),
minimised at V = N: (s + 1) sqrt(N). This is the lift analogue of the
generic-group bound KN-FIND-b7e091: the lifts give s known points of G, not
an oracle.

Where it could break: a lift description that is not a coefficient box (the
lift of T computed algebraically from T alone). That is the function-field /
prescribed-section xedni of EV-XEDN-001 and IDEA-20260805-d426f9, untouched.

## 3. Heights (IDEA-20260807-6533fd identity, applied)

Covering a constant fraction of G by reductions of points of canonical height
<= H needs V_s H^{s/2} / sqrt(Reg) >= N, i.e. H ~ (N sqrt(Reg))^{2/s}.
log2 N = 52.4 for pair 46 (N = 5947697996161643). log2 H = 52.4 * 2 / s:
s = 2: 52.4; s = 4: 26.2; s = 6: 17.5; s = 8: 13.1; s = 10: 10.5.
The coordinates of a point of canonical height H have ~H bits (up to
log|Delta|), so the representation cost of one lift is ~H bit operations:
2^52.4, 2^26.2, 2^17.5, 2^13.1 for s = 2, 4, 6, 8. Against sqrt(N) = 2^26.2:
for s <= 4 (Q-rank <= 2) representation alone already loses to rho; for
s >= 6 (Q-rank >= 3) it does not, and only (B) of the record keeps the route
closed, at cost >= (s + 1) sqrt(N).

## 4. The explicit kernel

N = N(pi - 1) is prime, so n = (pi - 1) O_K is the prime of norm N with
O_K / n = F_N. On an O_K-module E_d(K) of K-rank 1, ker(red) is exactly
(pi - 1) E_d(K) (index N), and pi - 1 = vw - 2 is a shortest generator by
norm divisibility. For K-rank rho > 1 the kernel is larger (index only N)
with shortest vectors ~ N^{1/(2 rho)}. This is the same fact as
IDEA-20260808-5158fa (pi = 1 mod n): rationality of G and triviality of the
Artin symbol at p coincide.
