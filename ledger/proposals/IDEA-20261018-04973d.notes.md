## Coupling of rho and UNSAT availability
The expected solution count per target is mu ~ c_m 2^{mk-n}, and the UNSAT fraction falls roughly as
e^{-mu}. Raising k at fixed n therefore removes UNSAT targets. Reaching rho < 0 with UNSAT targets at
m=4 needs larger n, hence large N:

| n | k | N | cols | rho |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | 4 | 44 | ~150k | +1.35 |
| 18 | 5 | 56 | ~397k | -1.25 |
| 22 | 6 | 68 | ~867k | -4.1 |

Dense W_4 does not reach these on the 4-CPU/15 GB host. The calibration is uncertain because the
m=5 n=9 k=3 cell has UNSAT targets although 2^{mk-n} = 64, so c_m is small; the Step-0 census decides
the rungs. That is why the Delta_H metric on SAT instances is included: it needs only the closure rank
and the enumerated solutions, so it covers every rho.
