## 4E criterion (closes O1 of IDEA-20260915-8fe0ef; derived, to be unit-tested at n=11,13)
x(2Q)=x_Q^2+B/x_Q^2. For P in 2E (Tr(x_P)=Tr(a)) the two rational halves are Q, Q+T
with x(Q+T)=b/x_Q (b=sqrt B). u=x_Q^2 solves u^2+x_P u+B=0, the other root B/u is
x_{Q+T}^2. P in 4E iff a half lies in 2E iff Tr(x_Q)=Tr(a) or Tr(x_{Q+T})=Tr(a), i.e.
Tr(u)=Tr(a) or Tr(u')=Tr(a), u'=B/u, u+u'=x_P.
 - Tr(a)=1: Tr(u)+Tr(u')=Tr(x_P)=1, exactly one equals 1=Tr(a): P in 4E always, 2E=4E,
   h=2 (matches #E=2 mod 4 for a=1).
 - Tr(a)=0: Tr(u)=Tr(u'), P in 4E iff Tr(u)=0; with u=x_P w, w^2+w=B/x_P^2 this is
   Tr(x_P w)=0, an Artin-Schreier-inverse (nonlinear) function of x_P. So no linear V is
   forced into 4E; d(V)<=2 for subspaces unless the bias of this function on V is
   1-O(1/t) (multiplier 2(1+eps^t)).
