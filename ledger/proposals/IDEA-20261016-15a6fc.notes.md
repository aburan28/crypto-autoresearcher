## Route and scalar

phi: E -> E' rational of degree ell, ell not dividing n. phi_hat phi = [ell], so
k P = phi_hat(k' phi(P)) with k' = k ell^-1 mod n. For depth 2 the maps compose.
phi restricted to <P> is injective, so phi(P) has order n on E'.

## a = -3 criterion

(x,y) -> (u^2 x, u^3 y) sends (a,b) -> (u^4 a, u^6 b). E' is isomorphic to an
a = -3 curve iff a'/(-3) is a fourth power, i.e. -3/a' is one. a' = 0 never
qualifies. Quartic twists change the group order, so are not allowed. Density
of fourth powers in F_p^* is 1/gcd(4, p-1): 1/4 for p = 1 mod 4, 1/2 for
p = 3 mod 4.

## Why Montgomery and Edwards are closed on prime order

Montgomery and twisted Edwards need a point of order 4 (recalled; BBJLP, not
opened here), so 4 | #E. #E is shared across the class (Tate), so a prime-order
class has no such neighbour. Where 4 | #E, the 2-Sylow structure and the
volcano level decide which of Legendre and Edwards exists
(IDEA-20260811-fe0934). Only composite-order curves can use arm 1 beyond
a = -3, and deployed prime-order curves are out of that scope. A rational
2-isogeny needs a rational 2-torsion point, so an odd-order curve has none.

## Neighbour counts (H2)

Ordinary E, ell not dividing f: ell-neighbours in the crater are 1 + (D_K/ell)
in {0, 2}. If ell divides f: 1 or ell + 1 depending on the level. With about
half of ell in {3,5,7,11,13} split and 2 neighbours each, depth 1 has about 3
neighbours (m in the prediction). P(some hit at depth 1) = 1-(1-q)^m: m = 3
gives 58% at q = 1/4 and 87% at q = 1/2; the 0.4 floor allows for m < 3.

## Arm 2

t^2 - 4p = f^2 D_K. Ascending the ell-volcano for each ell | f raises the
endomorphism order to conductor f/ell^v. Residual discriminant after ascending
all ell <= 13 factors: D_res = D_K * f_res^2. Hit criterion: |D_res| <= 20
(D = -3, -4 give a free lambda; small norms are otherwise cheap). nist.py
already reports D_K and f; all NIST entries have huge |D_K|, so the prediction
is zero hits. The positive control descends a j = 0 curve ell^v steps, giving
D = -3 ell^(2v), and must ascend back in v steps.
