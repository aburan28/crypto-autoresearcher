## 1. Supply and names

Schoof 1987 (recalled): the number of F_p-isomorphism classes of elliptic curves with trace t is the Hurwitz class
number H(t^2 - 4p). With 4p = t^2 + D0 f^2 and K = Q(sqrt(-D0)) of small discriminant this is
sum_{g | f} h(-D0 g^2), and h(-D0 g^2) ~ h(-D0) g prod_{l | g}(1 - (-D0/l)/l) / [units]. So the count is
~ h(-D0) * sigma(f) up to bounded factors, f ~ sqrt(4p/D0): Theta~(sqrt p). A class is the pair (g, [a]),
g | f, [a] in Cl(O_g): log2(count) ~ (1/2) log2 p bits. Computing the name needs factoring f (128-bit scale, feasible)
and class group data of imaginary quadratic orders of small D0; nothing in it uses a discrete logarithm in E.

Many K: the trace t_K differs per (D0, f); the union over all K of such sets is the set of all ordinary curves
(p of them), and each additional K costs a fresh sqrt(p) evaluations. This is the counting argument of
EXP-CANL-1064e0 restated: |S| / N <= (number of K) * sqrt(p) / p.

## 2. Hecke relations

For E_0/Q modular of conductor M, phi: X_0(M) -> E_0 (base cusp infinity) is Hecke-equivariant,
phi_* T_l = a_l phi_*, and T_l(infinity) = (l+1)(infinity), so for every point x not a cusp
sum_{x' in T_l(x)} phi(x') = a_l phi(x). Over F_p the l+1 points of T_l(x) are the pairs (E_1', E_2') obtained from the
l+1 subgroups C of E_1[l] (E_2 follows through the M-isogeny). A subgroup is F_p-rational iff it is an eigenline of
Frobenius on E_1[l]. For l | f (conductor of Z[pi] divisible by l) and E_1 not on the l-floor, Frobenius is scalar on
E_1[l] and all l+1 subgroups are rational (the l+1 edges of the l-volcano). On the l-floor only the ascending
edge is rational; the other l subgroups form one Frobenius orbit of length l, whose phi-images sum to a point of
E_0(F_p) that is not in V: one new unknown per floor vertex per relation, so floor relations add no rank.
Corrections: Atkin-Lehner w_M identifies x with w_M x (halves the unknowns, does not change the corank fraction);
U_l for l | M is a different operator; horizontal edges (l split in K) are the same relation with 2 + (l-1).

## 3. Tensor kernel (claim 3)

Model: f = prod l_i^{e_i}, all l_i inert in K for definiteness, h = 1. Vertices of the forest are
V = prod_i T_i, T_i the l_i-tree with level 0 of size 1, level j >= 1 of size (l_i + 1) l_i^(j-1), depth e_i.
Relation operator in direction i: R_i f(x) = sum_{children} f - a_i f(x) + [parent term for depth >= 1], for interior
vertices only. R_i has full row rank (triangular: each row has its own -a_i entry on a vertex not used by a later
row of a lower level), so dim ker R_i = |floor_i| = F_i. The stacked system over all i has solution space
the intersection of the subspaces V_1 x ... x ker R_i x ... x V_s, which is tensor of the kernels, dimension
prod_i F_i. F_i / |T_i| = floor fraction >= 1 - 1/l_i (equality in the limit of large depth; larger at depth 1).
So corank fraction >= prod_{l | f} (1 - 1/l) = phi(f)/f. For f <= 2^128 the minimum is attained by a primorial
(primes up to ~97), phi(f)/f ~ e^(-gamma)/ln 97 ~ 0.123. So at least 12% of the unknown logs are free, for every f.
The sum Sum_{l | f} 1/l (average Sum l^(-2) = 0.452 over random f) is the first-order approximation and an upper
bound on the rank fraction.

## 4. Literature question of class (iv), as far as recalled

Heegner points are CM points on X_0(M) pushed to a fixed modular E_0/Q; the canonical (Serre-Tate) lift of E_p is a
CM curve with End = O over the ring class field. They are different objects and meet only if E_0 itself is that CM
curve. So Gross-Zagier/Kolyvagin do not act on the canonical lift; they act on E_0, which exists with small M only for
small-coefficient E_p. A reader must still confirm both statements against Gross 1991 (Stage 0); until then this is
recalled, not retrieved.

## 5. Good places are isotropic (class ii, recalled derivation)

For p not dividing N * conductor, the Kummer image of E(K_p)/N in H^1(K_p, E[N]) is the unramified subgroup, which is
its own annihilator under local Tate duality. In Poitou-Tate / Greenberg-Wiles reciprocity the local term at p is
<a_p, b_p> = 0 for unramified a_p, b_p, so no global reciprocity relation contains the discrete-log coordinate
lambda_p. Relations that do contain it are only those inside the global group (Galois-module structure, which is
free of rank r over the group ring for generic points: no relation among generators) -- hence class (ii) needs a
non-local source, and the volcano relations above are the only explicit one found. IDEA-20261009-8d09c0 (iii) tests the
complementary independence of Lambda_p and Lambda_q.

## 6. Ceiling

worse in memory. The record is a closure with a named measured quantity (corank fraction), priority low.
