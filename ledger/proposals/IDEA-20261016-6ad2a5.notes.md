## Why a plain 2-divisibility gate is vacuous for D^ nonsquare
D^=1-64=-63 mod p. By hand Legendre: nonsquare for p=31,59,83,353; square for p=43. If D^ is nonsquare then
ker(alpha_p)=2E^(F_p), so the plus point (square x^) is already in 2E^(F_p): no information beyond sign eligibility.
The informative content is the vector parity, not the 2-divisibility of one point. For p=43 (full F_p 2-torsion) an extra
alpha'_p test applies.

## S4 four cases (opposite-sign arm, B=+-prime, D=prime)
D=f_+f_-, f_pm>0 integers, D prime => {f_+,f_-}={1,D}; swapping P->-P exchanges them, so take f_-=1. With u=w-s^2:
A=1-2s^2+2w=2u+1, B=w^2-s^4-A s^2=u^2-s^2=(u-s)(u+s). |B| prime: one factor is +-1:
(a) u=s+1: A=2s+3,B=2s+1, A-B=2; (b) u=s-1: A=2s-1,B=1-2s, A+B=0; (c) u=1-s: A=3-2s,B=1-2s, A-B=2;
(d) u=-1-s: A=-2s-1,B=2s+1, A+B=0. Against (A,B)=(-1,16): A-B=-17=2 needs p|19; A+B=15=0 needs p|15.
Hence no such lift for p in {31,43,59,83,353}. Not claimed for prime-power B or D (the full Handbook rank-1 slice w(B)=w(D)=1),
nor for p in {3,5,19}. Enumerating that slice is a cheap follow-up: f_+=q^a, f_-=q^b, w=(q^a-q^b)/4, z^2=m q^b-(w-m)^2.
