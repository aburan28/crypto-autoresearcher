## Z and its cocycle
Weierstrass: zeta(u+v) = zeta(u) + zeta(v) + (1/2)(wp'(u)-wp'(v))/(wp(u)-wp(v)). With wp=x, wp'=2y on
y^2=x^3+ax+b this is lam(R,S) = (y_R-y_S)/(x_R-x_S), the chord slope of the group law.
eta (quasi-period map) is Q-linear on torsion, so Z = zeta - eta is algebraic on torsion points,
odd, and satisfies Z(R+S)=Z(R)+Z(S)+lam(R,S) for R+S != O.
Averaging: fix R != O. Sum the relation over S in G \ {O,-R} (n-2 terms):
  sum_S Z(R+S)  = (sum over G\{O} of Z) - Z(R)      [R+S runs over G \ {R, O}]
  sum_S Z(S)    = (sum over G\{O} of Z) - Z(-R) = (sum over G\{O} of Z) + Z(R)   [Z odd]
  so  sum_S lam(R,S) = [T - Z(R)] - (n-2)Z(R) - [T + Z(R)] = -n Z(R),  T = sum over G\{O} of Z.
Hence Z(R) = -(1/n) sum_{S != O,-R} lam(R,S). Uniqueness: two primitives differ by a homomorphism G->F_p, zero.
Char p arithmetic only: Z is the splitting of the universal vector extension E^natural(F_p) -> E(F_p) (cocycle lam).

## K1 (jet formula)
psi_n(z)=sigma(nz)/sigma(z)^(n^2). At an n-torsion u, h(z)=psi_n/(wp(z)-wp(u)) is regular nonzero.
(log psi_n)' = n zeta(nz) - n^2 zeta(z); near z=u+e, n zeta(nz) = 1/e + n eta(nu) + O(e), eta(nu)=n eta(u).
wp'/(wp-wp(u)) = 1/e + wp''(u)/(2wp'(u)) + O(e). So h'/h(u) = n^2(eta(u)-zeta(u)) - wp''(u)/(2wp'(u)), i.e.
Z(u) = -(1/n^2)[h'/h(u) + wp''(u)/(2wp'(u))], with h'/h(u) = wp'(u) f_n''(x_u)/(2 f_n'(x_u)) for odd n.
wp'=2y, wp''=6x^2+2a: Z(R) = -(1/n^2)[ y f_n''/f_n'(x) + (3x^2+a)/(2y) ].
n=3: f_3'=12y^2, f_3''=12(3x^2+a) at the roots, y f''/f' = (3x^2+a)/y = 2 lam, Z = -(1/9)(3 lam) = -lam/3;
cocycle at R=S: Z(2R) = -Z(R) = 2Z(R) + lam, so Z = -lam/3. Consistent.
Jets: run the division-polynomial recursion over F_p[e]/(e^3) at x+e: O(log n) steps.
