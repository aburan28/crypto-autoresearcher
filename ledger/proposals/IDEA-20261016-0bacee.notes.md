## Amplifier
Input: P, Q=dP, oracle O(P,R)->c in Z/n (or F_p). Draw e_i uniform, R_i=Q+e_iP uniform in <P>.
O(P,R_i) = d+e_i with prob rho, else wrong. Keep t_i = c_i - e_i. True d appears Binomial(m, rho) times.
A wrong value v appears at least twice with probability ~ m^2/(2n) in total over all v (birthday on m
uniform values). Output any value with multiplicity >=2; ties resolved by verifying [t]P=Q (cost one
scalar multiplication per candidate; this verification is NOT free in a cost model comparing to rho, but
there are O(1) candidates).
P(true d seen >=2) ~ 1 - e^{-rho m}(1+rho m) >= 0.59 at rho m = 2, 0.8 at rho m = 3.
P(no spurious pair) ~ exp(-m^2/(2n)); at m = 2/rho and rho = 2 sqrt(2/n): m^2/2n = 1 /... = 4/(rho^2 2n)= 4n/(8n*... )
compute: rho^2 = 8/n, m^2 = 4/rho^2 = n/2, m^2/(2n) = 1/4, exp(-1/4)=0.78. So the stated threshold.
Cost vs rho: rho-algorithm 1.25 sqrt(n) additions; amplifier 2/rho * T_O = 2 T_O sqrt(n)/ (2 sqrt 2) = 0.71 T_O sqrt(n)
at the threshold. Break-even rho* = 1.6 (T_O/T_add)/sqrt(n) stated in the claim uses 1.25 sqrt(n) vs 2 T_O/rho.
