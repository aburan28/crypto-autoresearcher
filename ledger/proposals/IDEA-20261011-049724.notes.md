## Why RQ-FROB-7d8dd4 / GOAL-FROB-6333a9

The question asks whether the q-power Frobenius lowers the fully charged index
calculus cost beyond the trivial n-fold factor-base symmetry, and its decision
target names a Frobenius-invariant decomposition system. A phase unknown inside
the system is that object. RQ-CERTBIN-836ce2 (orbit-aware bases on ECC2K) and
RQ-BINSTD-b6f698 were considered; both are indexed by the target curve, and the
test here lives on toy Koblitz cells at n of 13 and 19. The ladder m of 51, 53,
83 and 131 is outside RQ-FROB's toy range (n prime, 5 to 41); if the
Coordinator wants it ranked it belongs to RQ-CERTBIN / RQ-BINSTD.
m = 51 = 3 x 17 is composite: F_{2^51} has subfields F_{2^3} and F_{2^17}, so a
51-bit cell is not a no-subfield cell and is not comparable with 53, 83, 131.

## Derivation: width stays, base grows

Let R be the number of orbit representatives of the seeded subspace. Phase-blind
(arm A) searches R x-coordinates. With a one-hot phase per summand (arm B) it
searches R x n x-coordinates, and the matrix still has R columns. At n = 13,
s = 4 the committed README has R = 5 and 65 admissible x-coordinates; without
phases only 4 of its 8 targets are decomposable (the planted ones), with phases
all 8 are. Per attempt arm B is slower (README, 4 targets, subgroup_xor: 16 s
without phases against 164 s with, planted), so seconds per independent relation
can go either way. That is the discriminating content of the test.

Relation coefficient: on the order-r subgroup pi acts as lambda with
lambda^2 + lambda + 2 = 0 mod r, so a summand at phase k and sign e contributes
e * lambda^k to its representative's column. The checker recomputes this
independently of the solver.

## Controls and ceilings

Ceiling: rank cannot exceed R. The local protocol uses s = 4, R = 5; if both
arms saturate rank 5 within budget the comparison is inconclusive, not support.
Choose the frozen subspace so that R exceeds what either arm collects. Generic
control: the same relation matrix with columns relabelled over a random Z/r
replica, to catch coefficient bookkeeping that works by accident.
