## Phase-weighted yield and proposed discrimination

Let $p_j$ be valid decomposition probability at rank deficit$j$, $\eta_j$ the
conditional probability of a new reduced row, and $C_j$ the complete attempt cost.
A model with repeated independent attempts would spend roughly

$$C_{\rm build}+\sum_j C_j/(p_j\eta_j)+C_{\rm LA}+C_{\rm descent}+C_{\rm verify}.$$

This is a conditional model; neither independence nor a uniform lower bound on
$p_j\eta_j$ is established. A simpler $UC/(p\eta)$ form must name its uniform
lower-bound assumptions. Report zero-rank batches explicitly. Per-phase totals
divided by newly verified reduced rank identify whether savings are in membership,
solver work, orbit handling or LA. Shorter equations may cost more after added
variables; smaller matrices can be offset by canonicalization.

tiny decomposition census, same-base list/map/chain arms, identity and random-label
