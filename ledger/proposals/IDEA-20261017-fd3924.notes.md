Sibling of `IDEA-FROB-nb.yaml`. Derivations, positioning and honest accounting that do not fit the 7,900-byte cap. Nothing here was run. Every pilot number is from `analysis/ecdlp2m-iteration-20261009/tau-factor-base-pilot/` (exploratory, not evidence).

## 1. Corrections to the handoff framing (read first)

1. **Net gain is 0.6-0.8 bit, not ~1.** The pilot's "matched" gain is measured against V_poly with negation only, and V_poly already has 0.18-0.22 bit of its own tau coincidence. V_nb minus V_poly is 0.98-0.22 = 0.76 bit at m=2 and 0.81-0.18 = 0.63 bit at m=3 (pilot "Summary by V family", >=300 rows). The record's `B_net` is defined against V_poly for that reason.
2. **"Usable conjugates" and "orbit shrink" are one effect, not two gains.** Both come from the window overlap (an element with end zero-runs s, t lies in a+1 = s+t+1 shifted windows). Adding log2(usable) to log2(g) double counts; the pilot README says so explicitly (rows x conjugates AND unknowns / orbits). B_net uses g only; usable conjugates are a diagnostic.
3. **Metric (i) alone is weakly discriminating.** The exhaustive-core law (IDEA-20260915-0d8e90: conflicts = c*2^(ml), c in [0.9,1.0]; IDEA-20260904-3c7a91 with symmetry group G) predicts R = 1 for ANY V of dimension k, so R in [0.7,1.4] is the null's prediction, not a risky one. The record is risky through (a) the V_rand arm (E2 vs E3), (b) D_W / columns, where the set V enters through V.V, and (c) the symmetry-pruned mode, where propagation can differ. KN-FIND-7c1e94 section 3 is the warning: on one random 9-dim V' plain M_4 went 144/144 -> 0/25 while W_4 stayed 25/25, so closure outcomes ARE sensitive to the set V even when the W_4 rate is at its ceiling.
4. **D_W = 4 at m=3 is the handoff's figure, which this session did not verify.** KN-FIND-c3a917 section 4 item 6: the mutant-closure cost "is unmeasured at any m >= 3", and item 2: W_4 is saturated at m=2 and cannot rank systems. The design therefore records D_W over D = 3,4,5, the matrix columns at D_W and the first refuting iteration, not only a pass/fail at D = 4. If the W_D instrument cannot be run at m=3 within budget, the D_W prediction is dropped for m=3 cells and the record says so (infrastructure, not a negative).

## 2. Derivations (zero compute)

Window geometry. Let x be uniform in the coordinate subspace V_nb (k coordinates, k <= n/2, so no wrap-around). Let s, t be its leading and trailing zero runs; for large k they are independent with P(s = i) = 2^-(i+1). The Frobenius orbit of x is the n cyclic shifts of its coordinate vector; exactly a+1 = s+t+1 of them lie in the window. Hence
- #orbits = sum over x in V of 1/(a+1) = |V| * E[1/(a+1)], and P(a) = (a+1) 2^-(a+2), so E[1/(a+1)] = sum_a 2^-(a+2) = 1/2. So g = |V|/#orbits -> 2.
  Pilot: g = 1.93-2.02 at n = 23, m = 2 (k = 11-13); larger at small n/k by finite-size effects (2.14-2.47 at n = 11).
- The Tr(1/x) condition and the subgroup (halving) condition are Frobenius-invariant, so orbits are wholly in or out of the factor base and do not change g.
- Usable conjugates of an m-summand relation: shift j is usable iff j in [-min s_i, min t_i]. P(min of m runs >= i) = 2^-(m i), so E[min] = 1/(2^m - 1) and E[count] = 1 + 2/(2^m - 1): 1.667 at m = 2 (pilot 1.55-1.89 for n >= 17), 1.286 at m = 3 (pilot 1.1-1.5). This is H2's formula and is checkable at n = 131 with field arithmetic only, no group operations.

Product space. For V_poly = {deg < k}, V.V = {deg < 2k-1}, dimension 2k-1, and products of degree < n never reduce modulo f when 2k-2 < n. For a generic V, dim V.V = min(n, k(k+1)/2). At m=3, k = 8: 2k-1 = 15 < n = 23 while the generic value is n. This is the KR-IC-73db3f lever and the reason E2 is a live alternative.

Normal-basis structure. In a normal basis x^2 is a cyclic shift, so for the S_3 descent the squared bilinear part contributes a shifted copy of the bilinear coordinates (the structure behind IDEA-20260922-7ab503). Optimal normal basis exists for n = 11 and 23 (2n+1 = 23, 47 prime and 2 generates the quadratic residues: order of 2 is 11 and 23); its multiplication table has 2n-1 nonzeros. n = 13, 17, 19: type not asserted here; the executor must check existence and fall back to random normal elements only.

## 3. Design details and cost

- 20 cells = 5 n x 2 m x 2 k. Arms per cell: V_poly (1), V_nb (3 random normal elements; 1 extra optimal normal basis at n = 11, 23), V_rand (1; fixed seed).
- UNSAT-for-all-arms rejection: at cells with decomposition probability near 0.6-0.9 (pilot: n = 17 m = 3 k = 8 has p about 0.99) most targets are SAT; draw up to 2,000 targets, record a short cell rather than relax the 20. Two independent enumerators: the pilot's log-table meet-in-the-middle and a second implementation sharing no code.
- Pairing: same x_R across arms; analyse log2 of per-target conflict ratios on targets UNSAT in both arms; report median and bootstrap CI per cell and mode.
- WDSat: the vendored, sha-bound build named in the design task (not located by this session; `analysis/frobenius-orbit-union/run_wdsat.py` is the nearest driver). Modes: plain with `-b` symmetry pruning (the "complete symmetry-pruned configuration") and XG-ext (EV-FROB-b6e1e9 observed XG-ext cuts the baseline 69x-220x, so both modes are needed or the result is a solver-mode artefact).
- Cost: conflicts per UNSAT instance scale as c*2^(mk)/|G|. The sum over k0, k0+1 of 2^(mk) is dominated by n=23: 2^24 + 2^26 (m=2) and 2^24 + 2^27 (m=3), so about 2.4e8 raw leaves per (cell, arm); after symmetry pruning (about 3x) and 100 instances (20 targets x 5 arms) about 8e9 conflicts at n=23 plus about 1.5e9 at n=19, i.e. about 7-10 CPU-hours at 3e5 conflicts/s in plain mode, much less in XG-ext. This is the handoff's 10 CPU-h figure and it is dominated by one cell; the cap is lifted by dropping k0+1 at n = 23, m = 3 (the executor records that as a scope reduction, not a result).
