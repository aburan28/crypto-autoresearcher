## Derivation: the correctness condition is a balance condition

Let V' be orbit labels, one per signed Frobenius orbit of F'. A partial with
large primes u, w has the relation
S + a ell(u) + b ell(w) = 0 over Z/r, with a, b in {+-lambda^k}, where S sums
small-leg columns. Orbit canonicalisation moves each large prime to its
representative and puts the factor +-lambda^k on the edge, so the edge gains lie
in the cyclic group <-lambda> of order 2n (lambda has order n modulo r because
pi^n = 1 on E(F_{2^n})).

Around a cycle u_0, ..., u_{k-1}, combine c_i times the i-th edge relation. The
coefficient of each ell(u_i) vanishes iff c_{i+1} = -c_i a_i / b_{i+1} (suitably
indexed); closing the loop forces c_1 to equal itself times the product of gains,
so a nonzero solution exists iff that product is 1 (a balanced cycle). For
uniform gains in a group of order 2n this has probability about 1/(2n); with
sign-only canonicalisation the gains are +-1 and the probability is about 1/2.
An unbalanced component still produces relations from bicycles (theta graph or
two cycles joined), so the space of large-prime-free combinations of edge
relations has dimension |E| - |V'| + b, where b is the number of balanced
components (frame matroid of the gain graph; their independence as final matrix
rows is a further, measured question). Consequences:

1. The vertex quotient by 2n starts relations about 2n times earlier in |E|, but
   unbalanced components need a bicycle, which has higher weight (cost of
   elimination, density of the final matrix).
2. "Cycle yield" in a plain census overstates relations in the orbit graph; the
   record's metric is independent verified relations, and the prediction
   includes the rank identity as an internal consistency check.

The identity assumes generic position (no extra coincidences among gains) and
the order-r part only; the cofactor 4 and the E[4] translates must be projected
away before canonicalisation. Whether H-621974-3's weight band survives bicycles
is exactly what the arm-versus-arm comparison would show; this record does not
claim it.
