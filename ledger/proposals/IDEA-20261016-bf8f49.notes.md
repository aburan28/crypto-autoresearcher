## S1/S2 (pattern (+,-); hypotheses (i) -1,B independent in Q*/Q*^2, (ii) D nonsquare)
Torsion: D nonsquare => E(Q)[2]=<T>; a 4-torsion g would give alpha(T)=alpha(g)^2=1, contradicting B nonsquare;
odd torsion lies in 2E. So alpha(E(Q)_tors) subset {1,B}.
Dependence mod torsion => P=kH+t1, Q=lH+t2 (E(Q)/tors free, rank-1 subgroup cyclic). With g=alpha(H), additive F_2
notation, e=class of -1, b=class of B (independent): k g+e1 b=0, l g+e2 b=e. If k odd: g=e1 b, then (l e1+e2)b=e,
impossible. So k even, t1 has alpha=1 (odd torsion), hence P in 2E. Also l odd, e2... gives the parity (a,b)=(l,-k)/gcd =
(odd on plus point, even on minus point). S1 is the contrapositive; P,Q are non-torsion since alpha(Q)=-1 is not in
{1,B} and f_+ not in {1,D}Q*^2 excludes torsion P.
(-,-): k g+e1 b=e and l g+e2 b=e force k,l odd. (+,+): k g=e1 b, l g=e2 b: no parity condition when g in {0,b}.

## Generic algorithm (all four arms)
V=<P,Q,T> in E(Q)/2E(Q). Compute alpha on generators; list v in V with alpha(v)=1 (v=P+Q in (-,-), v=P,Q in (+,+),
v=P or Q in (+,-)); for each take the dual preimage u-class in Q*/<Q*^2,D>; dim V = dim alpha(V) + dim(span of those
classes). dim V=3 certifies independence mod torsion for all coefficients. Factoring-free when only squareness of
f_pm, B, D is tested (opposite-sign arms).
(+,+) closed form (n=s^2-t^2, B nonsquare): f_+(P)=((w+n)^2-z^2)/n, f_+(Q)=(w^2-(z-n)^2)/n, common factor (w-z+n), so
