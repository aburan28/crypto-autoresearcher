# Notes for idea-4 (cut-describability gate)

## 1. Closure leak

For a subset P of the Boolean cube F_2^n and degree D, ev_D(q) = (monomial(q))_{|monomial| <= D}, a vector of length sum_{j<=D} C(n,j). I(P)_{<=D} = polynomials of degree <= D vanishing on P = the left kernel of the evaluation matrix of P. Z(I(P)_{<=D}) = {q : ev_D(q) in span ev_D(P)} = the D-closure of P. P is D-closed iff the closure equals P, and d_zero(P) = least such D (<= n always). Examples: an affine subspace of dimension j has d_zero = 1; a random subset of size s has d_zero about min{D: sum_{j<=D} C(n,j) > s} (generic evaluation vectors are independent until the space is exhausted). The counting formula is a lower bound for interpolation and the typical value for random sets; structured sets can be far below it. phi_D (fraction of outside points in the closure) is the continuous readout; d_zero = first D with phi_D = 0.
Cost at n = 18: 2^18 cube points, span of |P| <= 2^9 vectors, D <= 6: bitset reduction, about 10^8 operations; fine in pure Python minutes per cell.

## 2. Counting figure at n = 571

sum_{j<=D} C(571,j) >= 2^{285} first at D about 64 (log2 C(571,64) approx 284.6 by the entropy estimate, log2 C(571,65) approx 288). This is the generic-set prediction for the middle cut |P_{m/2}| ~ 2^{n/2}; it is arithmetic, not a measurement, and applies to a strategy (describe the partial-sum set by low-degree polynomials), not to every Macaulay derivation.

## 3. The transfer-matrix step and why the gate is necessary but not sufficient

Variables per link: u (n bits), y in V+v_{i+1} (k bits), u' (n bits): 2n+k. Message M_i: a basis of I(P_i)_{<=d} in the u-bits. Step: from M_i(u), S_3(u,y,u') = 0 (cubic) and the coset equations, derive I(P_{i+1})_{<=d} in u'. A degree-D' Macaulay matrix in 2n+k variables has about (2n)^{D'}/D'! columns; D' = d+3 is the first degree at which M_i * (cubic) products appear. Cost per link columns^w, memory columns^2, m links, O(n) trials (#Fb = m 2^{C_0} = O(n)): time n^{(d+3)w+2}, memory n^{2(d+3)}. The derivation at degree d+3 is the unproved stage 2; closure of P_i at degree d (the gate) does not imply it.
The explicit map (Y_1,Y_2) -> x(P_1+P_2) involves inversion (algebraic degree about n), while S_3 gives it implicitly at degree 3; eliminating the implicit variables to describe P_2 in u alone is the step whose degree this idea measures.

## 4. Controls and their meaning

Positive control: P_1 = V+v_1 (affine, d_zero = 1) and random affine subspaces of larger size: the instrument must report d_zero = 1 there, else it cannot see structure. Negative control: random subsets, d_zero equals the counting formula. At toy n the counting formula is 3 to 5, so absolute degrees cannot separate "constant" from "slowly growing"; the ratio of phi curves to size-matched nulls, and the trend of that ratio in n, is the readout.
