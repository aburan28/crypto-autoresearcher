### Corrections required (C1–C8)

**C1 — H012 (most important).** The ledger states: *"n=17 null D=5 126,922 == pred —
first direct null measurement past n=15, 16th exact cell."* In the staged artifacts
(`h012b_denseT_n17.json`, `h012b_denseT_n17r.json`) the number 126,922 appears **only** as
`runs[0].sr_pred_D` — the semi-regular *prediction*. The measured n=17 ranks are
`sem.rank = 108,392` (strided subset) and `99,071` (random subset), and the session's own
finding (iv) declares subset-column rank **invalid for sem** (−19% at n=15: 55,968 vs
69,073). No null-arm rank key exists in either file. So: the "first direct null
measurement past n=15" is **not evidenced in the artifacts**; if a full-exact n=17 null
rank exists elsewhere it must be cited explicitly, otherwise the claim downgrades to
"prediction only." Sub-claims (i) peeling dead (40+43 pivots of 143,882) and (iv) subset
invalid are supported; (ii) rank 69,073 at n=15 is supported but "== t2" is not checkable
from staged artifacts; (v) the deficit series is checkable only at n=15 (1,862) and n=18
(1,999) — the n=12 cell (1,322) has no staged artifact.

**C2 — H067.** The ledger's "δ = 0.000 ± 0.001 bits" uses the **null-subtracted**
convention without saying so. The raw r=4 signal is substantial (`delta_mm` = 0.0054 /
0.0106 bits, paired-diff CIs exclude 0) and is erased only because the permutation null is
equally large — i.e., the learnable-looking signal is fully explained by the featurization
null, which is the correct KILL logic, but the write-up must state the convention. Two
further omissions: the "100-rep permutation null" rep count is **not recorded anywhere in
the artifact** (only `perm_null_mean/std/p95`), so that specification is unverifiable; and
`decision.per_r["4"].xor_positive_control_ok = false` despite δ_xor ≈ 1.0000 — an
unexplained flag anomaly (r=2 flag is true). The KILL itself stands (transfer retention
0.32 < 0.5; negative slope on 2 points; pilot scale) — this is a framing correction, not
a reversal.

**C3 — H023.** "CP/slice/treewidth proxies inside 100-permutation null band" omits that at
the largest config (N = 21,341, B = 28) **two proxies sit marginally outside the band**:
`nuclear_over_fro` = 2.6677 vs band ≤ 2.6598, and `cp_relres_r8` = 0.4868 vs ≤ 0.4851
(`flags.any_outside = true`). Both excesses are < 0.5% over the band edge and appear only
at the largest B — exactly where a follow-up is cheapest and most informative. KILL
direction stands; the cell should be disclosed and re-run at one larger B before the
family is called closed. Also: GF(2) LSB flattening rank is B−1 (not full) at both 15-bit
configs, inside bands that themselves dip below B — cosmetic, but the ledger says "FULL at
all configs."

**C4 — H016.** The small-multiples arm is mis-quantified: coverable-target fractions
recomputed from the raw `counts` arrays are **0.5%, 0.375%, 0.25%, 0.0%** (4/800, 3/800,
2/800, 0/800), not "0.1–0.4%" (one curve covers literally zero targets). Omitted and more
interesting: at curve 1017 the small-multiples base produced **more total decompositions
than random (272 vs 152) piled onto only 2 targets** — extreme concentration, the opposite
of coverage. The QR-arm numbers (ratios 0.88–1.05, CIs contain 1, inside permutation
bands) verify exactly.

**C5 — H040.** The negation constants (0.949 / 0.943) are **conditional-on-success**
estimates: 17/200 and 29/200 negation runs ended in `escape_overflow` and were excluded.
Censoring is reported in the artifact but not in the ledger line. The plain-rho anchor
(theory 1.2533 inside both CIs) is unaffected.

**C6 — H002.** Two issues. (a) Against its own frozen pre-reg — "WIN: lower solving
degree at matched size" — the result is **not** a WIN: solving degree/exponent was
unchanged; what improved is wall-clock formulation quality. The session re-labeled the
outcome on a different metric than the pre-registered primary one (criterion drift; the
house statistical-discipline rule exists precisely for this). Correct tag: "formulation
gain, pre-reg criterion not met, exponent unchanged." (b) "~10×/doubling both arms" is
asserted in the artifact only for the original arm; the symmetrized tail grows ×24.5 then
×83 per |F|-doubling (1.08 s → 89.5 s), and the |F|=64 cell has no original-arm
measurement (skipped; comparison extrapolated). The 25→110 wall shift is the midpoints of
the artifact's stated ranges (~20–30 → ~96–128).

**C7 — H032.** The fitted shrink-exponent range is **−0.69 … −1.09** (9 fits), not the
ledger's "−0.7..−0.97" — the steepest cell (lsb_b4 = −1.09) falls outside the quoted
interval. Supported otherwise (21/32 Holm cells, ~1/p decay). Note: the 2^20 extension
used 500 null draws vs the 2,000 pre-registered.

**C8 — H041.** "~2× worse than plain rho" undersells it: effective constants 2.30 and
3.22·√N are **1.83× and 2.57×** the 1.2533 theory. All other numbers (239–439 two-cycles
vs null [0,2]; 5–7% useful collisions) verify.
