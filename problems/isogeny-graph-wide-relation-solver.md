## Problem

The P29/P37 graph now has 2,304 checked degree-7 edges and 2,306 vertices, but the requested comparison of relation yield, useful rank, and minimum/maximum complete same-point ECDLP cost cannot yet be established. Source-base transport has been independently replayed through all 2,048 P29 edges and all 256 P37 edges. Exact targetwise ordered-count equality passed on 128 fixed public control targets at P29 index 8 and P37 indices 4, 24, and 256. The graph-wide run still lacks equality on the committed discovery stream, a frozen admitted collector schedule over the fixed vertices and bases, and a complete index-calculus solve and matched-cost pipeline. The existing exact triple collector measures mathematical availability on prepared inputs; that alone does not time relation solving or recover an unknown scalar through the full pipeline.

The prospective design fixes three main bases on every vertex, a fourth on prespecified indices, discovery and fresh confirmation targets, finalist selection, and full cost accounting. It remains a draft rather than an approved `EXP-*` contract. The [inventory assessment](../research/isogeny_relation_yield_scan_20261016/discovery_capacity_20261022/report.md) materializes and independently checks 6,918 prospective main-base discovery arm identities. The draft does not explicitly assign target quotas to the check arms or decide whether the `u=2` transported-base oracle runs as a separate full-quota arm from the graph-transported source base. The additive inventory distinguishes three- and four-control scenarios; the Coordinator must freeze the mode and quotas before approval. The first 20-arm conductor-step study is a distinct frozen experiment and is not substituted for these 2,306 vertices.
