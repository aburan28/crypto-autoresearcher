# Semaev degree-two cancellation lacks a nonzero fall bridge

- **Status:** `open`
- **Kind:** proof gap, source-code defect, and interpretation gap
- **Affected work:** [historical measurement](../research/FFD_SEMAEV_MEASUREMENT1.md), [frozen formal target](../formal/targets/semaev-s3-degree2-fall-witness.yaml), [Lean trace theorem](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/DegreeTwoFall.lean), [finite Lean certificate](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParityFiniteCertificate.lean), [finite equation-semantics theorem](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/FiniteEquationSemantics.lean), [finite row-space theorem](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParitySemanticRankCertificate.lean), [concrete finite witness](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/ConcreteFirstFallWitness.lean), [2026-10-20 validation report](../docs/reports/2026-10-20-formal-validation/report.md), [2026-10-22 corrected replay](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/report.md), [2026-10-23 concrete witness report](../docs/reports/2026-10-23-semaev-concrete-witness/report.md)
- **Next action owner:** formal research lane and Coordinator for claim wording
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-24

## Problem

The uniform claim that the descended Semaev `S₃` system has a nontrivial first fall at degree two is not proved by its Lean theorem. That theorem cancels degree-two coefficients for nonzero target abscissa `x_R`; it does not show that the residual gives a new nonzero class. A checked finite example contradicts the uniform conclusion in the stated toy Boolean model without refuting the narrower trace identity.

The historical first-fall implementation has a separate defect: `fall_profile` represents a monomial multiple as `{mm | m for mm in f}`. Boolean polynomial multiplication over F₂ requires duplicate monomials to cancel. The frozen summary is exactly reproducible as written, but its ranks and grades are not generally the ranks and grades of the Boolean F₂ polynomial system.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Archived observable:** The eight frozen cells report 70 of 80 Semaev draws at degree two and ten at degree three. The original script reproduced its JSON byte for byte on October 22. Its 200 support-matched null grades reproduced as well. The [archive receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/archive-replay.json) binds the command, work directory and output hash.
- **Source defect witness:** In `n=4,n'=4,s=1`, the first equation multiplied by `u₀` has masks `1`, `17`, `129` in the archived set product; all three cancel in F₂. The archived product disagrees with direct product evaluation on 64 of 256 Boolean assignments; the corrected product agrees on all 256. The [checker](../research/verification/replay_ffd_semaev_20261022.py) asserts this and records it in [draws.json](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/draws.json).
- **Corrected exact data:** With XOR cancellation and independent binary ranks on the same 80 systems, 68 draws first fall at degree two and 12 at degree three. Two originally degree-two draws become degree three: `(n,n',s,x_R)=(5,3,4,8)` and `(5,3,8,8)`. Twelve of 200 null grades change. Seven corrected Semaev degree-three draws have `x_R≠0`; five have `x_R=0`. All 280 systems and both rank profiles are retained per draw.
- **Finite explanation:** At `n=4,n'=4,s=1,x_R=1`, the trace residual `u₀+u₃+w₀+w₃` is already the fourth linear generator. Correct Boolean ranks give `dim V₁=1`, `dim(V₂∩R_{≤1})=1`, so `new_falls(2)=0`; `dim V₂=11`, `dim V₃=53`, and `dim(V₃∩R_{≤2})=12`, so `new_falls(3)=1`. The trace theorem's nonzero-target premise holds, yet the stronger first-fall claim fails in this finite model.
- **Proved scope:** [`DegreeTwoFall.lean`](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/DegreeTwoFall.lean) proves Artin–Schreier normalization and trace annihilation of quadratic coefficients. It does not prove a general nonzero quotient class or universal first fall.
- **Finite Lean evidence:** [`BooleanParityFiniteCertificate.lean`](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParityFiniteCertificate.lean) kernel-checks the three extra archived product masks and executable ranks `1`, `11`, `53` with degree-projection ranks `1`, `10`, `41`. Its theorems give `newFalls(1)=0`, `newFalls(2)=0`, `newFalls(3)=1` for the transcribed system. The pinned-toolchain [receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/lean-certificate.json) records only `propext` in the audited theorem axioms. The [full workspace replay](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/full-workspace-replay.json) built 2,012 jobs and audited 475 project theorems with standard axioms only, using the unchanged Mathlib pin. The repository's single-target verifier produced a local [machine-verified receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/local-target-verification.json); its scratch-root `source_commit: null` is accompanied by [exact branch and content hashes](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/local-target-binding.json).
- **Finite equation bridge:** [`FiniteEquationSemantics.lean`](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/FiniteEquationSemantics.lean) proves by complete 256-assignment evaluation that the four literal equations equal direct `S₃=e₂²+e₃+1` field evaluation for this one `n=4` sample. The [expanded pinned workspace](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/finite-equation-proof.json) built 2,013 jobs, audited 477 theorems with standard axioms only, and produced a local machine-verified target receipt. This is a finite extensional bridge, not a generic symbolic descent.
- **Finite row-space bridge:** [`BooleanParitySemanticRankCertificate.lean`](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParitySemanticRankCertificate.lean) proves actual F₂ row-space dimensions `1`, `11`, and `10` for the degree-one rows, degree-two rows, and degree-two projection of this sample. Lean checks the generated literal rows against the parity routine, invertible elimination matrices, and echelon pivots. It proves the degree-two rank difference is zero and that the earlier executable ranks match these actual matrix ranks. The [source-bound receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/semantic-rank-proof.json) records a byte-identical regeneration, 8,959-job pinned build, 488-theorem standard-axiom audit, and local machine-verified target.
- **Finite degree-three row-space bridge:** [`BooleanParityDegreeThreeRankCertificate.lean`](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParityDegreeThreeRankCertificate.lean) proves actual F₂ row-space dimensions `53` and `41` for degree-three full and projected matrices of the same source-bound sample. It checks the row transforms using a generic XOR-to-F₂ lemma, invertible matrices, and echelon pivots; it proves the actual rank increment `53−41−11=1` and agreement with the executable ranks. Its [source-bound receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/degree-three-rank-proof.json) records byte-identical regeneration, an 8,960-job pinned build, 496-theorem standard-axiom audit, and a local machine-verified target. The verifier's scratch-root `source_commit` is null; the receipt binds the exact theorem, generator, target, toolchain, manifest and frozen draw hashes.
- **Finite filtration bridge:** [`BooleanParityFiltrationCertificate.lean`](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/BooleanParityFiltrationCertificate.lean) proves a generic row-space projection rank-nullity lemma and specializes it to the same source-bound sample. It identifies the degree-three projection kernel with `V₃∩R≤2`, proves its dimension is 12, proves `V₂` of dimension 11 lies inside it, and proves an existential vector in the intersection outside `V₂`. The [source-bound receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/filtration-proof.json) records the 8,961-job pinned build, 500-theorem standard-axiom audit, exact theorem axioms and local machine-verified target. It is an existence theorem for this finite sample, without a concrete packed-vector witness.
- **Concrete finite witness:** [`ConcreteFirstFallWitness.lean`](../formal/CryptoResearch/Semaev/ConcreteFirstFallWitness.lean) selects 23 source-generated degree-three rows by an explicit bitmask and proves their XOR has degree at most two. A linear functional at monomial masks 80 and 192 vanishes on every degree-two row but evaluates to one on the witness, proving it is outside `V₂`. The independent [source-binding receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-23-semaev-concrete-witness/evidence/source-binding.json) checks the archived equations, Lean literal, row order, XOR, separator and rank extension `11→12`. The [proof receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-23-semaev-concrete-witness/evidence/concrete-proof.json) records its pinned workspace build, axiom audit and local target verification. It refines the same finite sample and does not establish a general first-fall law.
- **Second finite implementation:** The [independent replay](../docs/reports/2026-10-23-semaev-concrete-witness/verify_independent.py) re-derives all four component equations from 256 direct `S₃` evaluations by a Möbius transform, verifies the 16-element field, and uses low-column binary elimination to reproduce the finite ranks, intersection dimensions, row combination and separator. Its [receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-23-semaev-concrete-witness/evidence/independent-replay.json) binds the source bytes. This checks one model with different arithmetic and elimination code; it does not substitute for independent human claim review or prove a generic first-fall statement.
- **Unresolved:** Generic correctness of `rankOn`, a generic descent from abstract `S₃`, and a general statement about first fall remain unproved. Hosted current-head CI, independent semantic review, and a reviewed additive correction to the frozen target remain necessary. A dated correction to the historical research note is proposed in PR #2504 but has not been merged. Official hypothesis status is unchanged. The finite data do not establish solving degree or ECDLP cost.

- [x] Prove that degree-one, degree-two, and degree-two-projection executable ranks equal actual F₂ row-space dimensions for the fixed sample.
- [x] Prove the degree-three full and projected matrix ranks for the fixed sample, and their equality with its executable rank values.
- [x] Prove the filtration intersection/rank-nullity identity and existence of a new low-degree class for the fixed sample.
- [x] Construct and machine-check an explicit degree-three row combination with degree at most two and a separating linear functional for the same fixed sample.
- [ ] Prove generic `rankOn` correctness if a general theorem is needed. State a qualified positive theorem only with hypotheses that exclude the checked exceptions, or publish the semantically reviewed finite counterexample. A generic symbolic `S₃` descent is a further theorem if a claim needs it.
- [x] Replay every frozen seed, target, basis and equation; reproduce the archived table; compute the corrected parity ranks for all 80 Semaev and 200 null systems; classify all exceptional grades; retain exact equation hashes and source revision.
- [ ] Add a reviewed, additive correction to the frozen target and historical uniform wording. Keep the original record immutable and distinguish archived counts from corrected Boolean counts in every graph and dashboard.

Meeting these conditions would settle the toy Boolean claim. General solving-degree and cryptanalytic cost claims are separate obligations.
