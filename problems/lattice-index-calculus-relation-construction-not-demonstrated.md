## Problem

These proposals do not yet supply a relation-construction pipeline simultaneously
certifying density, valid roots/points, independent rank and complete cost for any
claimed improvement. A public ideal lattice, low-degree map chain or short vector
does not fill those missing interfaces.

The requested scope is all three authorized F4/F2/F6 routes, with named F3 components,
on explicit prime cyclic subgroups. The first fixtures are tiny F43 pairing, F97
auxiliary/target and F43/F97/F101 polynomial systems, plus a rank-two F25 negative
control. No cryptographic-scale performance acceptance condition has been met or
weakened. There are no new scientific runs in this task.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed artifacts:** committed source/read receipts, two known-results maps and
  fresh compact dedup context. This task produces only proposals, notes/report and
  diagram sources. No measured relation yield or timing is asserted.
- **Derived, review pending:** fixed$q,r$ force the same embedding degree; scalar
  orbit families collapse after action-log constraints; the ordered-tuple first
  moment is only a success upper bound; strict achieved weighted norm certifies an
  integer equation but not sufficient independent equations or rational points.
- **Known context:** PKM already supplies auxiliary-map bases; FGHR symmetry is prior
  work. H-BINSTD-ce4f38 is analyzed, but its evidence was not audited here.
  H-ECDLP-c3d48a and H-ECDLP-a00090 retain their Stage0 boundaries.
- **Unknown:** actual uniform-target valid yield, fresh reduced-rank curve, sigma,
  complete finite-field descent cost, extraction complexity and cross-scale transfer.
- **Missing interfaces:** certified pairing witness/solver; auxiliary rational
  kernel/coset and target subgroup incidence; independent short-polynomial extraction;
  density-preserving low-height chain encoding; paid discovery and all failure costs.
- **Access limits:** KB unavailable, experiment inventory unmaterialized; neither
  implies repository or prior-evidence absence. FGHR/Semaev PDFs were403 for root;
  abstract receipts do not certify full-paper claims.

No timeout, bounded failure, impossibility theorem or security conclusion is
recorded. Completing this problem record does not resolve the missing construction.

## What would unblock it

- [ ] A separately frozen executable toy protocol with exact curve/field/subgroup,
  candidate law, all map exceptions, controls, outputs and complete phase accounting.
- [ ] Pairing lane: nondegenerate order$r$ images with compatible map identities,
  fixed$q,r,k$ and an actually specified finite-field relation/descent interface.
- [ ] Auxiliary lane: independent equality of list/map/chain bases, rational kernels
  and auxiliary lifts, actual target subgroup checks, certified scalar action and
  rank growth after known log constraints; explicitly preserve kernel poles or
  justify finite-nonkernel-coset exclusions.
- [ ] Polynomial lane: exact shift lattice/support/box, strict norm certificates,
  sufficient independent equations and bounded-root extraction; rational/sign/subgroup
  relation certificates checked against an exhaustive tiny oracle.
- [ ] Count uniform-target density and new-rank yield, failures, setup, memory,
  membership, solves, filtering, LA, descent and verification. State cold and genuine
  amortized workloads. Independently review any proposed performance interpretation.

These checks would establish scoped toy interfaces, not novelty, cryptographic
exponents or generic ECDLP advantage. A later speed claim requires matched evidence,
progress-fidelity records and ordinary independent review. One route's success
does not authorize silently closing or promoting another.
